## Identity
Ketta is a serving wench at the Boatman's Tavern in Nulb. [ch 018 / entry; ch 039 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Ketta is portrayed as anxious and concerned about the safety and consequences of the party's actions. In Chapter 039, she explicitly warns the party that they are "playing a dangerous game" and urges them to minimize violence. [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.04]

## History with the Party
**Chapter 018:**
- Ketta was present at the Boatman's Tavern as one of three serving wenches when the party approached the bar to inquire about the missing Rhennee woman, Serena. [ch 018 / 018.02; ch 018 / 018.05]
- During the party's interrogation of the wenches, another woman deflected questions by stating she did not work there and was only present to "talk to Ketta," indicating Ketta may have been a target of inquiry or conversation by others in the tavern at that time. [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.05]

**Chapter 039:**
- Ketta acted as a messenger for Zephyr, delivering a beer and a specific message from Zephyr to Captain Tolub. [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.04]
- Following the delivery of the message, Ketta warned the party about the danger of their confrontation with Tolub and asked them to keep bloodshed to a minimum. [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.04]
