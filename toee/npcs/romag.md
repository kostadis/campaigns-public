## Identity
**Name:** Romag
**Aliases:** High Priest of the Earth Temple, Prophet Romag, High Cleric of the Earth Temple [ch 019 / entry; ch 020 / entry; ch 021 / 021.07]
**Role:** High Priest/Prophet of the Earth Temple, one of the four elemental cults within the Temple of Elemental Evil [ch 019 / entry; ch 020 / 020.10]
**Status:** Deceased (Assassinated by the party) [ch 028 / entry; ch 028 / 028.05]

Romag is a powerful and influential figure within the Temple of Elemental Evil, serving as the leader of the Earth Temple [ch 020 / entry]. He styles himself as the High Cleric of the Earth Temple and claims authority over the earth-based followers in the temple complex [ch 021 / 021.07]. He resides in a lavishly decorated, well-furnished chamber within the temple [ch 020 / entry; ch 021 / entry]. He was a successor to Barkinar, who was promoted to the Deep Temple, and Romag had been in the role for a relatively short time, struggling to establish his authority compared to his predecessor [ch 022 / entry; ch 026 / entry]. He claims the Temple of Elemental Evil was constructed almost 15 years ago [ch 020 / entry; ch 020 / 020.10].

## Personality and Motivations
Romag presents himself as a leader who prefers to direct others rather than fight personally, comparing himself to the "king in a game of chess" who sends others to battle while standing back to defend himself at the end [ch 027 / moment]. Hartsch describes Romag's leadership style as leading "from under the earth," implying passivity and hidden machinations [ch 027 / 027.05]. Romag advocates for a slow, erosive approach to power, invoking the teachings of the former chief Barkinar: "we must erode, not strike with earthen violence" [ch 028 / entry; ch 028 / 028.04]. He believes dust is the ultimate power [ch 028 / moment].

However, Romag displays intense rage and brutality when his authority is challenged or when betrayal is discovered, demanding public sacrifices of traitors to instill fear [ch 021 / 021.07; ch 022 / 022.07]. He possesses a skewed perception of success, celebrating what is actually a defeat as a "great victory" [ch 022 / moment]. He is a micromanager who lacks the efficiency of his predecessor, often blaming others for problems he creates [ch 022 / entry; ch 022 / 022.01]. He engages in brutal rituals, using prisoners to feed gnolls, sacrifice to the elder elemental earth god, or dispose of rivals in an Otyugh he keeps in his territory [ch 025 / 025.03; ch 025 / 025.08; ch 026 / entry]. He is depicted as "kind of a clown" by the DM, having left large chunks of the dungeon empty and unused due to poor management [ch 025 / 025.06].

He is secretive, withholding critical information about the temple's deeper secrets and the Orb of Golden Death unless trust is earned through acts of violence and loyalty [ch 024 / 024.02]. He frames the party's missions as "cleanup" of messes they or his predecessors created [ch 022 / 022.01].

## History with the Party
*   The party first learned of Romag through a captured cultist, who revealed that a missing chest was meant for Romag and was likely located near Gallows Corner [ch 012 / 012.01].
*   The party attempted to contact the "prophets" of the temple by asking Captain Tolub to introduce them to Romag, specifically mentioning that they had something Romag desperately wanted [ch 018 / 018.05; ch 018 / 018.08].
*   Dick Rentsch arranged a meeting with Romag after the party proved their worth by killing Fire Temple gnoll recruits, a mission Romag desired [ch 019 / 019.10; ch 020 / 020.02].
*   The party met Romag in his chamber, presented him with the Chest of Rao, and received their first mission to kill Fire Temple gnolls inside the temple to earn the "badge of earth" [ch 020 / 020.10; ch 020 / moment].
*   Romag was pleased when the party returned alive from a mission involving werewolves instead of gnolls, approving of their suspicion of "angelic figures," and granted them badges and living space in the Earth Temple [ch 021 / 021.04].
*   Romag tasked the party with capturing Lieutenant Krell, a traitor, alive for a public sacrifice after Krell had set the party up to kill an Earth Temple ogre [ch 021 / 021.06; ch 021 / 021.07].
*   The party captured Krell and delivered him to Romag, who performed a ritual sacrifice where earth elementals tore Krell apart while Romag chanted to the elder elemental earth god [ch 022 / 022.05; ch 022 / 022.07].
*   Romag granted the party their own territory within the Earth Temple as a reward for their service, warning them that holding territory made them targets [ch 022 / 022.08; ch 022 / 022.10].
*   Romag tasked the party with recruiting the Broken Blades mercenary company to guard critical checkpoints, as he desired disciplined professionals over unreliable gnolls [ch 024 / 024.03].
*   Romag revealed the existence of the Orb of Golden Death, describing it as the key to the temple's vault and the only object capable of forcing the chaotic elements to submit to earth, though he withheld details of its contents [ch 024 / 024.02; ch 024 / moment].
*   Romag hosted a banquet to celebrate the party's success in recruiting the mercenaries and clearing territory, specifically noting Calmer's conversion from St. Cuthbert to the Elder Elemental God [ch 024 / 024.10].
*   The party discovered a prison chamber where Romag kept prisoners to feed his gnolls or sacrifice, and freed them, planning to blame the Fire Temple for the escape [ch 025 / 025.03; ch 025 / 025.04].
*   The party killed Romag's Otyugh, which he used to dispose of rivals, and found evidence of his brutal practices [ch 025 / 025.07; ch 025 / 025.08].
*   Romag scheduled a loyalty ritual where the party would help rip prisoners apart, but the prisoners had already been freed by the party [ch 026 / 026.07].
*   Romag ordered the party to attack Alrrem (Fire Temple) after a chimera attack, claiming the moment is now, but refused to lead the charge himself [ch 027 / 027.04].
*   The party, influenced by Hartsch's ambition and Romag's perceived passivity, planned a coup to assassinate Romag and install Hartsch as the new leader [ch 027 / 027.05; ch 028 / 028.03].
*   Romag was assassinated in his chambers by the party. He was isolated by Zephyr's magic, attacked by Zinnia and Sequoia, and finished by Calmer's *Toll the Dead* spell [ch 028 / 028.05].
*   After his death, Barkinar praised the party for eliminating Romag along with Hartsch, Alrrem, and Belsornig [ch 031 / 031.06; ch 032 / 032.01].
*   Post-death, it was revealed that the necromancer opposing the party was a former associate of Barkinar who disappeared when Romag received the promotion he wanted [ch 037 / 037.06; ch 040 / 040.05].

- In Chapter 42, the party found the necromancer's notes rejecting Romag, alongside Hartsch and Calmer, as false prophets. The writer claimed to be the true prophet and insisted that a ritual victim must be willing [ch 042 / 042.04].

## Last Observed State
Romag is dead. He was assassinated in Chapter 028 [ch 028 / entry; ch 028 / 028.05]. At the moment of his death, he was in his private chambers, believing he was holding a meeting with the party and Hartsch. He was killed by a combination of physical damage and necrotic magic, withering and dying after failing a Wisdom save against *Toll the Dead* [ch 028 / 028.05]. His body was left in his chamber, and Hartsch used his blood to mark his hands before declaring himself Supreme Prophet [ch 028 / 028.05].

## Relationships
**Hartsch:** Romag's sub-general who outwardly flattered him while inwardly despising his "passive" leadership style [ch 027 / 027.05; ch 028 / 028.04]. Hartsch signaled to the party that he wanted Romag removed and succeeded him after the assassination [ch 027 / 027.05; ch 028 / 028.05].
**Dick Rentsch:** Romag's agent/handler who recruited the party and mediated their interactions with Romag, often taking blame for intelligence failures [ch 019 / 019.10; ch 021 / 021.04].
**The Party (Zephyr, Zinnia, Sequoia, Calmer):** Romag's "new recruits" and later elite agents ("Obsidian Edge" under Hartsch, but formerly loyal to Romag). He trusted them enough to grant them territory and missions but remained suspicious of their loyalty until they proved their ruthlessness [ch 020 / 020.10; ch 022 / 022.10; ch 028 / 028.07].
**Barkinar:** The former leader of the Earth Temple who was promoted to the Deep Temple, leaving Romag in charge. Romag invoked Barkinar's teachings to justify his erosive strategy [ch 022 / entry; ch 028 / 028.04].
**Lieutenant Krell:** A traitor within Romag's ranks whom Romag had kept under watch but failed to prevent from betraying the party. Romag sacrificed him publicly [ch 021 / 021.07; ch 022 / 022.07].
**Alrrem:** The Prophet of the Fire Temple. Romag viewed him as a rival and sought to destroy him, claiming Alrrem was weak after losing monsters and money [ch 027 / 027.04].
**The Necromancer:** A former associate of Barkinar who disappeared when Romag took the leadership position, implying Romag's appointment displaced this rival [ch 037 / 037.06; ch 040 / 040.05].

**The Necromancer (Chapter 42 notes):** Rejects Romag's prophetic authority, treating him, Hartsch and Calmer as false prophets. This is the note writer's claim, learned after Romag's death; it does not establish a new meeting or change his deceased status [ch 042 / 042.04].
