## Identity
Sarge is a cult fanatic sergeant who served as the guard captain for the Moathouse [ch 017 / entry]. He held an Armor Class of 11 [ch 017 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Sarge was a fanatical devotee of a goddess, motivated by religious zeal. During combat, he shouted about the "revenge of the temple" and the "purging fire of his goddess" [ch 017 / entry]. He refused to surrender, maintaining his aggressive assault until his death [ch 017 / entry]. He demonstrated tactical awareness by prioritizing the enemy cleric, Calmer, understanding that eliminating him would significantly weaken the party's overall durability [ch 017 / entry].

## History with the Party
*   Sarge participated in the battle within the Moathouse halls, defending the premises alongside Lareth the Beautiful [ch 017 / 017.01].
*   During the initial exchange of blows, Sarge struck Calmer for 6 damage [ch 017 / 017.01].
*   When a confused guard attacked Calmer, Sarge simultaneously struck him for another 9 damage, specifically targeting him to reduce the party's healing capability [ch 017 / 017.01].
*   Sarge continued to harass Calmer with repeated attacks, dropping the cleric's hit points to 16 at one stage [ch 017 / 017.01].
*   Near the end of the conflict, Sarge landed a critical hit on Calmer for 12 damage, reducing the cleric to 4 hit points before Calmer healed himself [ch 017 / 017.01].
*   Zephyr attacked Sarge for 13 damage, and Sequoia followed up with an attack for 17 damage, leaving Sarge at 2 hit points [ch 017 / 017.01].
*   Sequoia delivered the final blow to finish off Sarge [ch 017 / 017.01].

## Last Observed State
Sarge was cut down and killed by Sequoia (with support from Zephyr) after fighting to the death in the Moathouse [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.01].

## Relationships
*   **Lareth the Beautiful:** Sarge served under Lareth, defending him during the party's assault on the Moathouse [ch 017 / 017.01].
*   **Calmer:** Sarge viewed Calmer as the primary tactical threat, deliberately focusing his attacks on the cleric to neutralize the party's healing [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.01].
*   **Zephyr:** Sarge was struck by Zephyr during the final moments of the fight [ch 017 / 017.01]. Zephyr earlier mentioned letting "Lareth and Sarge" kill the party [ch 017 / moment].
*   **Sequoia:** Sequoia landed the killing blow on Sarge [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.01].
