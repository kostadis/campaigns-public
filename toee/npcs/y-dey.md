## Identity
Y'dey, the former canoness of Hommlet, operates in Nulb as Mother Screng. This identity is established by the campaign's entity registry. The summaries record the party uncovering her disguise and resistance role, without establishing when they connected her to the former Hommlet canoness [ch 002 / entry; ch 039 / 039.09].

In Hommlet, she is described as the "former canoness and head cleric" who "disappeared mysteriously" [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.04].

In Nulb, she operates under the name "Mother Screng." She runs an herb shop and is disguised as an "old hag" [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.09]. She is revealed to be an "agent of good" and the "leader of the local resistance" against the Temple of Elemental Evil [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.09]. She is the superior of Otis, the blacksmith and spy [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.08].

## Personality and Motivations
**Y'dey (Hommlet Era):**
Information on her personality during her time in Hommlet is absent. Her disappearance is described as a "tragedy" by Terjon, while the blacksmith suspects foul play [ch 002 / entry].

**Mother Screng (Nulb Era):**
She is cautious and strategic. She first deflects the party to Otis to vet them before revealing herself [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.08]. She is perceptive, noticing a change in Calmer's spiritual nature despite his disguise [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.09].

She is motivated by the defeat of the Temple of Elemental Evil and the preservation of good in the region. She admits her information gathering is limited by the danger of the temple, stating her intelligence is "all secondhand" because no one else would brave the dungeon [ch 040 / 040.01]. She is pragmatic but compassionate, offering prayers for Sequoia's dead sister while expressing anger at her killer [ch 040 / 040.01].

## History with the Party
- **Chapter 002:** Y'dey was the head cleric of Hommlet prior to the party's arrival. She disappeared mysteriously before the party reached the village [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.04]. The party did not meet her; she was only discussed by the blacksmith and Terjon [ch 002 / 002.04; ch 002 / 002.05].
- **Chapter 039:** The party, operating under aliases in Nulb, visited Mother Screng's herb shop seeking information about the TZGY scarab. Vethra directed them to her [ch 039 / 039.07]. She deflected them to Otis to identify the scarab [ch 039 / 039.08]. After Otis vouched for them, the party returned. Screng dropped her hag disguise, revealing she was the resistance leader. She interrogated Calmer about his faith and then revealed that Prince Thrommel is alive and imprisoned in the temple [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.09].
- **Chapter 040:** Screng provided further details on Prince Thrommel, identifying him as a Paladin Lord and heir to Furyondy. She tasked the party with rescuing him, warning them not to trust the temple's presentation of him [ch 040 / 040.01]. She identified Sequoia's puzzle pieces as Thrommel's emblem, suggesting a personal connection to Sequoia's family [ch 040 / 040.01; ch 040 / moment]. She acknowledged Calmer's return to sanity in Chapter 40 through Calmer's recounting of her questions which prompted his contrition [ch 040 / 040.04].

## Last Observed State
In Chapter 040, Mother Screng continues leading the Nulb resistance while maintaining her herb-shop cover. She is currently allied with the party, having just assigned them the mission to rescue Prince Thrommel and provided them with identification of the emblem. She is located in Nulb, where she maintains a cover as an herb shop owner but reveals her true nature to vetted allies [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.01].

Status: Active in Nulb [ch 040 / entry].

## Relationships
- **Otis:** She is his superior and the leader of the local resistance. He defers to her for final intelligence and reveals [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.08].
- **Terjon:** In Hommlet, Terjon is the current head cleric. The blacksmith suspects Terjon had a role in Y'dey's disappearance [ch 002 / 002.04]. Terjon claims to have investigated Y'dey's disappearance and voices suspicions about Burne and Rufus [ch 002 / 002.05].
- **Prince Thrommel:** She claims to have intelligence that he is alive and imprisoned in the Temple of Elemental Evil [ch 039 / 039.09; ch 040 / 040.01].
- **Sequoia's Sister:** Screng speculates that the emblem pieces held by Sequoia indicate a secret gift from Thrommel to his sister, implying a prior connection between Thrommel and Sequoia's family [ch 040 / 040.01].
- **Calmer:** Screng questioned his identity, sensing he had risen in power within the Earth Temple, which forced Calmer to re-evaluate his choices [ch 039 / 039.09; ch 040 / 040.04].
