## Identity
Hestort 'Tort' Flintshine is a commoner, described as five feet tall and thin as a rail [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.01]. He was hired by a figure known as "the Duke" to gather information about a local castle in exchange for money [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Hestort is highly self-preserving and easily frightened. He deliberately avoids learning the identity of his employer’s superior because he believes knowing that person could lead to his death [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.02]. He is terrified of violence and authority figures; when threatened with magically enhanced intimidation, he wetted himself and confessed despite claiming to work for a cleric [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.02].

## History with the Party
*   The party tracked Hestort at night and witnessed him being ambushed and shot by four bandits near the river [ch 006 / 006.01].
*   Zephyr stabilized Hestort with a Potion of Healing during the combat, saving him from imminent death [ch 006 / 006.01].
*   Upon waking and seeing the party, Hestort feared he was in Hell or the Abyss and begged for protection [ch 006 / 006.01].
*   The party interrogated Hestort; he initially lied, claiming to work for Terjon, but confessed to working for "the Duke" after Zephyr used Thaumaturgy-enhanced intimidation [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.02].
*   Hestort revealed that the Duke hires bandits and works for an unknown individual in the village, a fact Hestort intentionally avoids knowing to stay alive [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.02].
*   Hestort stated that his intention to speak about meeting Zephyr was the likely trigger for the bandits' assassination attempt [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.02].
*   Hestort declared he was leaving town and that the party would never see him again [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.02].

## Last Observed State
Status not established in the summaries. Hestort stated his intention to leave town immediately after the interrogation [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.02; ch 006 / moment].

## Relationships
*   **The Duke:** Hestort’s employer, who hired him to spy on the castle [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.02]. Hestort fears the Duke’s associates and the unknown person the Duke works for [ch 006 / 006.02].
*   **Terjon:** A cleric of St. Cuthbert. Hestort falsely claimed to work for Terjon during his initial interrogation [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.02].
*   **Zephyr:** The party member who saved Hestort’s life with a potion and subsequently terrified him into confessing [ch 006 / 006.01; ch 006 / 006.02].
