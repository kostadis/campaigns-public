## Identity

Captain Dren Halveth is the half-orc captain of the Broken Blades, a company of veteran mercenaries and deserters from the Wild Coast. He has broad shoulders, scars beneath a worn leather coat, and one ruined white eye; his voice is low and sharp [ch 024 / entry]. Calmer appointed him Supreme Commander of the Earth Temple Forces [ch 033 / 033.04]. He serves as the party’s military operator, coordinating troops, security, supplies, and reinforcements [ch 040 / entry; ch 043 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations

- **Pragmatic and transactional:** Territory, personal support, and 100 gold pieces secured his recruitment; he subsequently receives a ten percent treasure share [ch 024 / 024.08; ch 035 / 035.03].
- **Protective of his troops:** He warns that excessive Earth Temple casualties would invite rival attacks. He places the Broken Blades behind the Earth Guards because he trusts his own men [ch 043 / 043.01; ch 043 / 043.02].
- **Politically calculating:** He recommends the Air Temple’s soldiers as the vanguard partly because their army threatens his position [ch 043 / 043.02].
- **Blunt about command limits:** He demands clear standing orders, distinguishes corpse disposal from attacking the necromancer, and argues that the party should confront major threats personally [ch 040 / 040.02; ch 040 / 040.05].
- **Cautious around unfamiliar dangers:** He worries about exploding casks and mimics, and admits when threats exceed his capabilities [ch 035 / 035.04; ch 036 / 036.01].

## History with the Party
*   **Recruitment:** The party first met Dren at the Waterside Hostel, where he was taking bids from both the Earth and Water Temples. After a failed "errand boy" task involving retrieving stolen weapons from the Crimson Reapers, the party persuaded him to join the Earth Temple by offering territory and gold, convincing him the Water Temple was misleading with money alone [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.05; ch 024 / 024.06; ch 024 / 024.08].
*   **Integration:** Dren and his Broken Blades settled into the Earth Temple's territory. He was used as a diversion during a prisoner escape to prevent his crew from hearing the noise [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.04].
*   **Temple War:** Dren was enlisted to set up a meeting with Romag to facilitate his assassination. Impressed by the party’s defeat of a Chimera, he agreed to the plan and rallied his men for war with promises of plunder [ch 028 / entry; ch 028 / 028.03; ch 028 / 028.07].
*   **Raid Aftermath:** After Hartsch ordered a failed raid, Dren confronted the party, accusing them of leading his men into a "meat grinder" and calling Hartsch a madman. He helped rescue the surviving Broken Blades and forcibly removed a wounded Calmer from a gnoll ambush to save him [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.04; ch 030 / 030.07].
*   **Command Promotion:** Dren was formally promoted to lead the Earth Temple forces under Calmer’s command. He explained that only clerics could rule temples and highlighted a one-week window to plunder the Fire Temple before the Greater Temple demanded order [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.02].
*   **Administrative Role:** Dren became the party’s primary contact for military and logistical matters. He managed supply chains, warned of undead threats, and provided tactical intelligence on trolls and the necromancer. He often complained about the administrative burden and the danger of the party’s schemes [ch 035 / entry; ch 036 / entry; ch 038 / 038.04; ch 040 / entry].
- **Reinforcement planning:** After hearing screams, Dren found the battered party outside the bronze door. He warned against exhausting the Earth Temple army and sent Torlin to summon Air and Fire Temple assistance, excluding the Water Temple because its representative had previously attempted to kill the party [ch 043 / 043.01].
- **Securing the doorway:** Before the party visited the library, Dren fetched the Broken Blades, Earth Guards, and Commander Joradin to guard the entrance, positioning his own soldiers behind the Earth Guards [ch 043 / 043.02].
- **Crypt assault:** The party adopted Dren’s proposal to send Air Temple troops first. He held Earth troops and the Broken Blades in reserve and supplied Zephyr with a shortbow for the Fire Temple’s fire arrows [ch 043 / 043.02; ch 043 / 043.03].

## Last Observed State

During the crypt assault, Dren keeps the Earth troops and Broken Blades in reserve, ready to reinforce if called. He supplies Zephyr with a shortbow while the Air Temple vanguard engages the undead [ch 043 / 043.03]. The supplied summaries do not establish the assault’s outcome [ch 043 / 043.03].

## Relationships

- **Calmer:** Dren recognizes him as his commanding authority. He previously carried the wounded cleric to safety and later explicitly compares Calmer’s battered appearance with that rescue [ch 032 / 032.02; ch 030 / 030.07; ch 043 / moment].
- **Zephyr:** Dren previously asserted that they were peers; Calmer subsequently delegated operational matters to his companions. Dren now discusses deception tactics with Zephyr and supplies his bow [ch 035 / 035.04; ch 043 / 043.01; ch 043 / 043.03].
- **Sequoia and Zinnia:** Sequoia entrusts him with maintaining order while warning against betrayal. Zinnia rallied his battered mercenaries after the disastrous Water Temple raid [ch 038 / 038.04; ch 030 / 030.04].
- **Broken Blades and Commander Joradin:** The Blades remain his personal company; Joradin is a lieutenant reporting to him. Dren explicitly favors protecting the soldiers he trusts [ch 043 / 043.02].
- **Torlin:** His black-clad runner carries requests for reinforcements to the Air and Fire Temples [ch 043 / 043.01].
- **Rival temples:** He considers the Air Temple army a threat to his position and excludes Water Temple assistance because of its representative’s attempted betrayal [ch 043 / 043.01; ch 043 / 043.02].
- **Barkinar:** Dren admitted him as a superior officer, then demanded clearer instructions for handling future claims of rank [ch 040 / 040.02].
