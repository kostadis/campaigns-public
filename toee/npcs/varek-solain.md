## Identity
Varek Solain is the Captain of the Fire Temple's forces and formerly its leader before the party's intervention [ch 033 / entry; ch 036 / entry; ch 038 / entry]. He is a cultist at heart who proclaims "And fire shall burn again!" when enthusiastic [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / moment]. The DM initially marked him on the map simply as "the captain" [ch 033 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
*   **Resilient but Resentful:** After an initial defeat and forced submission, Varek regains his composure. He complains that the party "took all my money and resources, dude!" and notes he has no army or food left [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / 038.02].
*   **Pragmatic Power Broker:** He operates on the belief that "He who controls food controls everything," visibly delighting when given the food depot to guard [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / moment].
*   **Cultist Zeal:** Despite his pragmatic side, he cannot suppress his fanatical nature, often slipping into cultist declarations like "And fire shall burn again!" [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / moment].
*   **Disillusioned Leader:** He blames the party for the destruction of his temple and the death of Alrrem, though he accepts his subordinate status to Zephyr and Dren Halveth [ch 033 / entry].

## History with the Party
*   The party found Varek wounded and defeated in his chambers after the destruction of the Fire Temple. He recognized the party as the "Obsidian Edge," prepared to fight, but submitted after being outnumbered, paying a tribute of two gold coins [ch 033 / entry].
*   Zephyr allowed Varek to remain a Fire Temple soldier, subordinate to Dren Halveth. Varek answered Zephyr's threat of "I will not forget this" with "Nor will I." [ch 033 / entry].
*   Maeris Dorn, the new head of the Fire Temple, was instructed by Zephyr to find Varek, whom the DM had marked as "the captain" [ch 033 / 033.05].
*   Varek arrived at a meeting with Vurakhal as if he had been listening at the door. He informed the party about the trolls, naming **Oohlgrist**, and explained that the trolls had refused recruitment by both Alrrem and Belsornig, scavenging the battlefield instead. He provided the trolls' location, stating, "I will be glad to see them gone" [ch 036 / entry; ch 036 / 036.02].
*   Varek told Vurakhal that Maeris Dorn was the head of the Fire Temple [ch 036 / 036.02].
*   The party sought Varek rather than Vurakhal or the local priest to burn a refuse-filled chamber to prevent a necromancer from using the bones. Varek was initially puzzled, noting the temple simply threw garbage there [ch 038 / 038.02].
*   Varek revealed that a disgruntled necromancer formerly under Barkinar "got pissed, and disappeared" when Romag got the job. He stated he had warned Alrrem, who failed to act, closing the subject with "Whatever. The war is over. So be it" [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / 038.02].
*   When asked to hunt the necromancer, Varek protested his lack of resources. He provided fire arrows and instructions to use oil, noting that someone needed to sort out the supply chain through Nulb [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / 038.02].
*   Zephyr assigned Varek the northern food supply depot to guard. Varek was clearly pleased, declaring "He who controls food controls everything." After Zephyr clarified the party controlled the food and Varek was only guarding it, Varek took it as a sign of trust and declared "And fire shall burn again!" [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / 038.02; ch 038 / moment].
*   Zephyr resolved to plant an Earth Temple agent in the Fire Temple to keep Varek in check [ch 038 / 038.02; ch 038 / moment].

## Last Observed State
Status not established in the summaries beyond Chapter 038, where he is alive, acting as the Captain of the Fire Temple guard, and in possession of the northern food supply depot [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / 038.02].

## Relationships
*   **Zephyr:** Varek submitted to Zephyr's authority after being defeated. He exchanges veiled threats with Zephyr ("I will not forget this" / "Nor will I"). Zephyr later assigns him the food depot to guard and considers him a potential threat requiring an infiltrator [ch 033 / entry; ch 038 / 038.02; ch 038 / moment].
*   **Dren Halveth:** Varek is subordinate to Dren Halveth following his surrender [ch 033 / entry].
*   **Maeris Dorn:** Varek is the Fire Guard captain whom Maeris Dorn was told to find; Varek informed Vurakhal that Maeris Dorn is the new head of the Fire Temple [ch 033 / 033.05; ch 036 / 036.02].
*   **Alrrem:** Varek blames the party for killing Alrrem. He notes that Alrrem begged the trolls to join him and was betrayed by them. Varek claims he warned Alrrem about the necromancer, but Alrrem failed to act [ch 033 / entry; ch 036 / entry; ch 038 / entry].
*   **Vurakhal:** Varek interacts with Vurakhal regarding the command structure of the Fire Temple and provides tactical information on the trolls [ch 036 / 036.02].
*   **The Trolls (Oohlgrist):** Varek holds a grudge against the trolls, noting they sat out the war and scavenged the bodies. He provides their location to the party with the express hope that they will be destroyed [ch 036 / entry; ch 036 / 036.02; ch 036 / moment].
