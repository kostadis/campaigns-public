## Identity
Hedrack is the Supreme Commander of the lower temple forces [ch 035 / entry; ch 040 / 040.02]. He is a high-ranking authority within the temple hierarchy who holds ultimate oversight over magical inventory and expenditures [ch 035 / entry]. He was referred to as "Lord Hedrack" in earlier contexts regarding magical item costs [ch 035 / entry; ch 035 / 035.05; ch 040 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Hedrack maintains strict financial discipline regarding temple resources, specifically insisting that magical items are not distributed for free to cover temple expenses [ch 035 / entry; ch 035 / 035.05; ch 035 / moment]. He asserts authority over other high-ranking temple figures, as noted by Dren when determining who has the power to bar officers from the party’s quarters [ch 040 / entry]. He takes an active interest in the affairs of the lower temple, specifically noticing the party’s recent accomplishments and requesting a direct meeting [ch 040 / 040.02].

## History with the Party
*   Lucius Graeme cited Hedrack’s explicit instructions to refuse giving away magical items, ensuring the party understood the cost of their acquisitions [ch 035 / entry; ch 035 / 035.05; ch 035 / moment].
*   Hedrack requested a meeting with the party for the day after their return, having taken notice of their recent actions [ch 040 / 040.02; ch 040 / entry].
*   The party agreed to ask Hedrack why the temple’s leadership is concerned with destroying the Chest of Rao, as part of Calmer’s strategy for the Earth Temple ritual [ch 040 / 040.05].
*   The party chose to meet Hedrack before addressing other threats, such as the necromancer’s bronze door [ch 040 / 040.08].
