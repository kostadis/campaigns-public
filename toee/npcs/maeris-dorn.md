## Identity

Maeris Dorn is a fire cleric appointed head of the Fire Temple under the party’s authority. At her appointment, she confirmed that no other fire clerics survived [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.05]. She later provides magical support for the party’s assault force [ch 043 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations

She initially expressed fury at the party’s destruction of her temple, but accepted their terms while restrained with a dagger beside her throat. She admitted she never liked Alrrem [ch 033 / 033.05; ch 033 / moment].

Before the later assault, she describes healing and supporting others as her intended role and prepares to serve behind the fighting [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.02].

## History with the Party
- The party discovered Maeris looting a lavishly decorated chamber in the Fire Temple while attempting to flee with valuables after the temple's takeover [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.05].
- She prepared to attack the party in fury over the destruction of her temple, but was subdued by Zephyr, Zinnia, and Sequoia in a "group-hug grapple" with a dagger held at her throat [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.05].
- Zephyr offered her a choice between leaving freely or staying as a subordinate; Calmer added death as an option, which Zephyr overruled [ch 033 / 033.05].
- Maeris acknowledged the Earth Temple's supremacy over the Fire Temple as demanded by Calmer and agreed to the terms [ch 033 / 033.05].
- The party appointed her as the new head of the Fire Temple and instructed her to find the captain of the Fire Guard (presumably Varek Solain) and organize the troops [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.05].
- She was spoken of by Varek Solain to Vurakhal as the new head of the Fire Temple, and the party confirmed her appointment [ch 036 / 036.02].
- Maeris Dorn arrived from the Fire Temple with Vurakhal as the party assembled its assault force [ch 043 / 043.02].
- She explained that she would remain behind the fighting to heal and provide support, then cast Bless and Aid before the assault [ch 043 / 043.02].
- She blessed Vurakhal as well as the party, excluded the air goblins from her blessing, and brought fire arrows suited to the enemy’s newly discovered weakness [ch 043 / entry].

## Last Observed State

Alive and preparing to support the assault force in chapter 043: she arrived with Vurakhal, cast Bless and Aid, and stated that she would remain behind the fighting to heal. Her participation in the ensuing battle and its outcome are not established in the supplied summaries [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.02].

## Relationships

- **The party:** Appointed her under their authority after restraining her and securing her acceptance of their terms. She later supplies blessings and healing support [ch 033 / 033.05; ch 043 / entry].
- **Alrrem:** Her former leader; she said she never really liked him [ch 033 / 033.05; ch 033 / moment].
- **Varek Solain:** Reported her appointment to Vurakhal; the party confirmed it [ch 036 / 036.02].
- **Vurakhal:** Arrived alongside her for the assault preparations and received her blessing [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.02].
- **Air Temple troops:** She did not extend her blessing to the air goblins; no reason is supplied [ch 043 / entry].
