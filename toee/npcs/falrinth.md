## Identity
Falrinth is a wizard who is currently in hiding from the Greater Temple for reasons of his own [ch 031 / entry; ch 032 / entry]. He possesses the Orb of Golden Death and refuses to surrender it to any temple faction, including the Greater Temple, Lord Wizard Senshock, or the party [ch 038 / entry; ch 040 / entry]. He communicates with the party via an illusory hooded projection because he cannot risk sending a physical runner, nor can the party be seen receiving one [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / 038.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Falrinth presents himself as someone who values discretion and patience, noting that he handles his friends "with discretion" but warning that "other friends are less patient" [ch 038 / moment]. He is highly cautious, maintaining his safety by using illusionary communication and refusing to meet in person [ch 038 / 038.05]. He appears motivated by self-preservation and perhaps a desire to disrupt the Greater Temple’s stability from the shadows, as evidenced by his warning that awakening a specific sleeper could bring the forces of Verbobonc down upon the temple [ch 038 / 038.05; ch 040 / entry]. He is also knowledgeable about the political dangers within the temple, specifically warning the party about the manipulative nature of Lucius Graeme and the recognizability of Calmer’s scarab to powerful enemies like Senshock [ch 038 / 038.05; ch 040 / entry].

## History with the Party
- Falrinth was first mentioned by Barkinar, the leader of the Greater Temple, as a wizard hiding from them who would be discussed at a later time [ch 031 / entry; ch 031 / 031.06].
- Barkinar reiterated the existence of Falrinth as a "matter for later" when he officially recognized the party as the new rulers of the Upper Temple [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.01].
- Falrinth made direct contact with the party via an illusory hooded figure while they were resting in their new quarters [ch 038 / 038.05; ch 038 / moment].
- During this interaction, Falrinth warned the party about Lucius Graeme, describing him as a man with golden eyes and soft hands who "collects the ones he gives you" [ch 038 / 038.05; ch 038 / moment].
- Falrinth warned Calmer to be careful with his scarab bearing the letters T-Z-G-Y, stating that powerful enemies like Senshock and Lucius Graeme would recognize it and use it against him [ch 038 / 038.05; ch 040 / entry].
- Falrinth thanked the party for handling Lareth with discretion and revealed that he possesses the Orb of Golden Death, which he refuses to surrender [ch 038 / 038.05; ch 038 / moment].
- He revealed the existence of a "sleeper" kept by "the holy gentleman" in an iron coffin on the level below the party's quarters, noting that waking it would bring the forces of Verbobonc down upon the temple [ch 038 / 038.05; ch 040 / entry].
- Falrinth established a communication method for the party to reach him: placing "A candle in the West" [ch 038 / 038.05; ch 040 / entry].
- The party debated whether to contact Falrinth via the western-wall candle signal to discuss the sleeper or Prince Thrommel, but chose to rest instead [ch 040 / 040.03].

## Last Observed State
Status not established in the summaries. The most recent reference to Falrinth is an entry noting his prior warnings and the party's decision not to contact him through the western-wall candle signal [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.03].

## Relationships
- **The Party (Specifically Calmer, Lareth, and Sequoia):** Falrinth calls Lareth a friend and appreciates the party's discretion with him. He specifically warns Calmer about the scarab he carries [ch 038 / 038.05; ch 038 / moment].
- **Lucius Graeme:** Falrinth identifies Graeme as a dangerous, flattering man who uses gifts to create obligations and collects those he influences [ch 038 / 038.05; ch 040 / entry].
- **Lord Wizard Senshock:** Falrinth identifies Senshock as one of the "holy gentlemen" who would like him dead and notes that Senshock would recognize Calmer’s scarab [ch 038 / 038.05; ch 040 / entry].
- **Barkinar/Greater Temple:** Falrinth is hiding from the Greater Temple and Barkinar. Barkinar has previously mentioned Falrinth as a person of interest to the party [ch 031 / entry; ch 038 / 038.05].
- **Hedrack:** Falrinth refuses to surrender the Orb of Golden Death to Hedrack [ch 038 / 038.05].
