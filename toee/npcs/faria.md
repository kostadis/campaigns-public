## Identity
Faria is a human druid. [ch 018 / entry] She is part of a group of druids based at Camp Number One in the Hall of the Scarlet Moon. [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.10]

## Personality and Motivations
Faria is described as friendly. [ch 018 / entry] She expressed excitement about the 'rite of the wicker giant'. [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.10] The group, including Faria, expressed hope that the rite would lead to greater cooperation among the druids of the Gnarley Forest. [ch 018 / 018.10] They were excited about being initiated into "some big ascension and some very positive thing." [ch 018 / 018.10]

## History with the Party
- Faria was present at Camp Number One when the party arrived, chanting around a bonfire with two other humans and one other female human. [ch 018 / 018.10]
- Verigo introduced Faria to the party alongside Mahoon and Iniri. [ch 018 / 018.10]
- Faria, along with the other druids at Camp Number One, expressed interest in the party’s presence and the upcoming rite. [ch 018 / 018.10]
- The party attempted to discern the druids' true intentions while interacting with Faria’s group, but failed to notice anything amiss due to low rolls. [ch 018 / 018.10]
- The party subsequently moved to Camp Number Two and discovered that the druids there were aligned with Elemental Evil; it is not established in the evidence whether Faria or her camp shares this allegiance. [ch 018 / 018.10]

## Last Observed State
Faria was last seen at Camp Number One in the Hall of the Scarlet Moon, offering the party food, drink, and brandy from a small cask, and inviting them to stay the night to witness the rite. [ch 018 / 018.10] Status not established in the summaries.

## Relationships

- Mahoon and Iniri: Faria is part of the same group as Mahoon and Iniri. [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.10]
