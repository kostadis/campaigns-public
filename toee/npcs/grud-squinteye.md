## Identity
Grud Squinteye is Captain Tolub's second-in-command [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.04]. He was present during the confrontation at the Boatman's Tavern in chapter 39 [ch 039 / 039.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Grud is loyal to Captain Tolub, standing by his side during tense situations [ch 039 / entry]. He maintains composure under pressure, smiling and laughing when his captain succeeds, though internal tension is evident in his physical actions, such as grinding his teeth [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.04].

## History with the Party
*   Grud stood by Captain Tolub when the party entered the Boatman's Tavern to confront him about their alleged deaths [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.04].
*   When the party surrounded Tolub's table, Grud made space for Zephyr, allowing him to sit between Grud and Tolub [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.04].
*   Grud was present during the standoff where Tolub offered a single supply run to keep the party's survival secret [ch 039 / 039.04].
*   After the deal was struck, Grud ground his teeth while Tolub boasted, indicating displeasure at the situation despite outward compliance with his captain's behavior [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.04].

## Last Observed State
Grud was last seen at the Boatman's Tavern during the confrontation with the party in Chapter 39 [ch 039 / 039.04]. Status not established in the summaries.
