## Identity
Thalsor is a druid and the leader of the Black Earth Raiders, a faction aligned with the Earth Temple [ch 010 / entry]. He wields a stone staff adorned with jagged crystals and commands guardians including mud mephits and a stone golem [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.04]. His voice is described as gravelly and resembling a landslide in slow motion [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Thalsor is fanatically devoted to the Earth Mother [ch 010 / entry]. He views surface-dwellers as inferior, comparing them to insects and asserting that the stones they trample are older, stronger, and wiser than any gods [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / moment; ch 010 / 010.04]. He is grandiose and theatrical, delivering long threats and sermons about the power of the earth and honey [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.05]. When his initial combat plan fails, he abruptly shifts to diplomacy, attempting to negotiate or intimidate the party into giving up their objectives [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.05]. He believes death is merely a transition to becoming part of the earth, claiming he will survive through reincarnation into rocks and dirt [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / moment; ch 010 / 010.05].

## History with the Party
- The party attempted to infiltrate the Black Earth Raiders' camp using stealth and deception, but the deception failed when Calmer’s clerical insignia was noticed [ch 010 / 010.04].
- Thalsor emerged from the temple to confront Calmer at the entrance, identifying him as a follower of St. Cuthbert and declaring the party’s presence a violation of sacred ground [ch 010 / 010.04].
- While Thalsor engaged Calmer in rhetoric, Zephyr, Zinnia, and Sequoia climbed the walls to secure surprise [ch 010 / 010.04].
- Thalsor summoned two mud mephits as guards fell back, reacting with disbelief when the party’s ambush rapidly destroyed his defenders [ch 010 / 010.04; ch 010 / moment].
- He animated a stone golem by touching a statue in the temple when the party demanded the honey [ch 010 / 010.04].
- During the subsequent battle, Thalsor delivered a lengthy sermon about the Earth Mother and honey, exhausting the party [ch 010 / 010.05].
- Seeing his forces wounded, Thalsor attempted to negotiate, claiming the honey was a tribute and a powerful aphrodisiac, and offering an exchange for something of value [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.05].
- When intimidation failed, Thalsor conjured a hail of sharp stones, leaving himself with four hit points by the end of the first session [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.05; ch 010 / moment].
- In the following session, Thalsor knocked out Calmer while the party attempted to rescue their companions, then killed Calmer when Zephyr tried to recover his body [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.01].
- Thalsor offered to spare Calmer’s body from mutilation in exchange for information on who sent the party [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.01].
- The party pretended to agree to negotiate the next morning, allowing them to escape while Thalsor remained wounded in his compound, screaming threats throughout the night [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.02; ch 011 / moment].
- Thalsor guarded Calmer’s body, expecting the party to attempt a rescue [ch 011 / 011.03].
- Sequoia, using gaseous form, retrieved Calmer’s body unnoticed by Thalsor [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.04; ch 011 / moment].
- Zinnia and Zephyr killed Thalsor quickly the following morning, allowing the party to recover Calmer’s intact body [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.04].

## Last Observed State
Thalsor was killed by Zinnia and Zephyr in the morning following the night he guarded Calmer's body [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.04].
