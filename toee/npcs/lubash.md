## Identity
Lubash is an ogre [ch 005 / entry; ch 005 / 005.01]. He announces his name when encountered by the party [ch 005 / 005.01; ch 005 / moment].

## Personality and Motivations
Lubash expresses hostility toward the party, stating they are "not members of the temple!" [ch 005 / entry]. He maintains six prisoners specifically to use as food [ch 005 / entry].

## History with the Party
Lubash was encountered by the party in the lower moathouse after they descended a staircase from the great hall and moved east through a door with greased hinges [ch 005 / 005.01]. Upon the party making a noise, Lubash announced himself, swung his club, and attacked [ch 005 / 005.01]. The party members Zinnia, Zephyr, and Sequoia killed Lubash during the encounter [ch 005 / 005.01].

## Last Observed State
Lubash is dead; he was killed by Zinnia, Zephyr, and Sequoia during the confrontation in the lower moathouse [ch 005 / 005.01].

## Relationships
