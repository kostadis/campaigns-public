## Identity
Elvanna Thorne is the proprietor of the Iron Lash brothel [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.06]. She is a gorgeous woman with orange-red hair [ch 018 / 018.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Elvanna conducts business with a shrewd, calculating demeanor [ch 018 / entry]. She speaks with a posh accent [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.06]. She values profit and is surprised by parties who do not attempt to negotiate, noting that she might have increased her demands had she known they were desperate [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.06]. She holds captives as collateral for debts or damages, specifically citing "beauty, debt, and disobedience" as factors in pricing [ch 018 / 018.06].

## History with the Party
- The party entered the Iron Lash seeking to speak with the boss, whom the guard identified as Elvanna [ch 018 / 018.06].
- Elvanna greeted the party as "new clients" and asked for their pleasure [ch 018 / 018.06].
- Elvanna confirmed that Serena was in her possession as compensation for damages caused by the Rhennee at the Boatman's Tavern [ch 018 / 018.06].
- Elvanna demanded 500 gold pieces for Serena's release [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.06].
- The party accepted the price immediately, paying 125 gold pieces each without negotiation [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.06].
- Elvanna ordered a brute to retrieve Serena from "the pits" [ch 018 / 018.06].
- As Serena was handed over, Elvanna mockingly expressed hope to see her again soon [ch 018 / 018.06].

## Last Observed State
Elvanna was last seen in Chapter 018 [ch 018 / 018.06]. Status not established in the summaries.
