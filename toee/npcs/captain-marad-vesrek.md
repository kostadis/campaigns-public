## Identity
Captain Marad Vesrek is the Captain of the *Crimson Trader*. He is identified in a wax-sealed ledger as the seller of a chimera to the Fire Temple, with the transaction paid in full [ch 027 / 027.03; ch 028 / entry]. The ledger also indicates his connection to Belsornig, the prophet of the Water Temple [ch 027 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Evidence regarding Vesrek's personal psychology or direct actions is absent. He is described solely through his role in the chimera transaction and his alleged alliance with the Water Temple [ch 027 / entry; ch 028 / 028.02]. Hartsch explicitly stated that Vesrek's identity was less important than what his involvement signified [ch 027 / 027.05].

## History with the Party
- The party discovered a ledger bearing a wax-sealed receipt from Captain Vesrek of the *Crimson Trader* while looting the fallen warlock in Chapter 27. The receipt detailed the purchase of "one tri-headed exotic asset paid in full by the fire temple" [ch 027 / 027.03].
- The party reviewed the ledger with Hartsch, who explained that Vesrek, an ally of the Water Temple prophet Belsornig, arranged the chimera purchase. Hartsch interpreted this as evidence of Water Temple manipulation to weaken the Fire Temple [ch 027 / 027.05].
- In Chapter 28, the party again examined the ledger's evidence, confirming Vesrek's role in linking the Chimera's purchase to the Water Temple's prophet Belsornig [ch 028 / 028.02].

## Last Observed State
Status not established in the summaries. Vesrek has not been seen acting or speaking in the provided evidence; he has only been mentioned in documents or by other NPCs [ch 027 / 027.03; ch 028 / 028.02].
