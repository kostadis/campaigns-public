## Identity
Hartsch is the self-proclaimed Supreme Prophet of the Earth Temple [ch 028 / moment; ch 031 / entry]. He rose to power after the party assassinated his predecessor, Romag; Hartsch marked his hands with Romag’s blood to declare his succession [ch 028 / entry]. Barkinar, leader of the Greater Temple, refers to him as "The Earth Pretender" [ch 031 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Hartsch is ambitious and deeply contemptuous of passive leadership. He explicitly rejects the philosophy of slow erosion and conquest, preferring to "destroy and rend and crack" [ch 027 / moment]. He harbored concealed disdain for Romag, rolling his eyes while flattering the previous prophet [ch 028 / 028.04], and signaled his willingness to take command if Romag’s position suddenly opened itself [ch 027 / moment].

While eager to project strength—declaring, "Yes, yes, I am powerful! Yes, I am!" [ch 028 / 028.06]—Hartsch lacks the temperament for sustained command. When the war he instigated devolved into chaos, he became panicked and exhausted, rejecting the suggestion to wait out the conflict as "Romag's passive strategy" [ch 030 / moment]. He is prone to impulsive, disastrous decisions, such as sending the Broken Blades on a doomed raid without proper authority [ch 030 / moment]. Under pressure, he displays incompetence and hysteria, repeatedly begging the party for help with the phrase, "What do you want me to do?" [ch 030 / moment].

## Last Observed State
Hartsch is dead: Belsornig's men assassinated him [GM]. During the temple war, Kelno showed the party Belsornig's assassination instructions, and Dren reported that the assassins were at Hartsch's door; the party chose not to rescue him [ch 031 / 031.02; ch 031 / 031.03]. Barkinar subsequently included Hartsch among the four problems gone by sundown [ch 031 / moment; ch 032 / moment]. In Chapter 32, Calmer proposed moving the party's headquarters to Hartsch's more comfortable chambers and later read rituals there [ch 032 / 032.02].

## Relationships
*   **Romag:** Subordinate who despised him. Hartsch dismissed Romag’s leadership as a "joke" and a "passive" failure, eventually orchestrating his replacement [ch 027 / moment; ch 028 / 028.04].
*   **The Party (Obsidian Edge):** Initially flattered and promoted as his "elite vanguard" [ch 028 / moment]. Hartsch relied on them to launch his war against the Fire Temple but failed to provide sound tactical guidance, leading to a bottleneck [ch 028 / 028.07]. The party ultimately disciplined him physically (Zephyr slapped him) for his incompetence [ch 030 / entry] and refused to rescue him when he was assassinated [ch 031 / 031.02].
*   **Kelno:** Viewed Hartsch as a tool. Kelno referred to Hartsch as the party’s "puppet" and noted that the party's promotion of Hartsch triggered the war between prophets [ch 031 / 031.02; ch 031 / entry].
*   **Belsornig:** Enemy who ordered his assassination. Belsornig dismissed Hartsch’s authority as "paper" and sent forces to kill him while his own agents were instructed to avoid the party [ch 031 / entry; ch 031 / 031.05].
*   **Barkinar:** Superior who views Hartsch with cold disdain, listing him as a problem gone by sundown [ch 032 / moment].
*   **Calmer:** Successor to Hartsch’s political space. Calmer now claims the Earth Temple and utilizes Hartsch’s personal chambers and books [ch 032 / 032.02].

**The Necromancer (Chapter 42 notes):** Rejects Hartsch's prophetic authority, treating him, Romag and Calmer as false prophets. This is the note writer's claim, learned after Hartsch's death; it does not establish a new meeting or change his deceased status [ch 042 / 042.04].
