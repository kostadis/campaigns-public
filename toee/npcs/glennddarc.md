## Identity
Head gnome of Sheernobb who resides at the Moot Hall and oversees village affairs [ch 008 / entry]. Physical description includes brown wrinkled skin, long white wild hair, and the habit of dozing with a weasel in his lap [ch 009 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Grouchy and dismissive, particularly regarding local politics; he specifically dislikes Gnonsbim for running for re-election and developing romantic interest in Pasmarie [ch 009 / entry]. He prioritizes immediate external threats, such as giants, over minor disturbances like bandits [ch 009 / entry]. Despite his initial gruffness, he is capable of warmth and hospitality, sharing meals and speaking kindly of the party in the evenings [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.01].

## History with the Party
The party traveled to Sheernobb and was halted by guards at the entrance; the guards identified Glennddarc as the appropriate authority for newcomers and directed the party to the Moot Hall, though the party did not meet him during this session [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.04].

The party was led to the Moot Hall by Sequoia, where Glennddarc was awakened by a weasel, welcomed them, and then annoyed by their questions about bandits; he dismissed the inquiry, commented on Gnonsbim, pointed them toward Pasmarie's beehives, and fell asleep before they could protest [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.03].

In the evening, Glennddarc was happy to see the party at the Moot Hall, spoke warmly about them, and shared a fine meal with the group [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.01].

## Last Observed State
Glennddarc was observed in the evening at the Moot Hall, sharing a fine meal with the party and speaking warmly about them [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.01].

## Relationships
**Gnonsbim:** Glennddarc is dismissive of Gnonsbim, noting he is running for re-election and has "gone sweet on Pasmarie" [ch 009 / entry].
**Pasmarie:** Glennddarc directs the party to her beehives; his dismissal of Gnonsbim implies a tension involving her [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.03].
**Weasel:** A weasel resides in his lap and wakes him from dozing [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.03].
