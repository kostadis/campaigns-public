## Identity
Pasmarie Clee is a petite gnome woman and the keeper of Sheernobb's beehives, also referred to as the owner of the Honey Haven Inn [ch 009 / entry; ch 015 / 015.05]. She is described as having a kind, weathered face, unruly curls, oversized boots, strong work-worn hands, and an apron smeared with honeycomb wax and pollen [ch 010 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Pasmarie expresses concern for the integrity of her business and the safety of her caravans, noting that taking secret shortcuts rather than the ordinary road leads to danger [ch 010 / 010.03]. She is willing to offer gratitude and enchanted honey as payment for assistance [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.04]. Others describe her as "very nice" [ch 015 / 015.05].

## History with the Party
*   Grindin Well-Read, a bookseller in Sheernobb, confirms to Zephyr and Zinnia that Pasmarie Clee has information on local bandits [ch 009 / 009.02].
*   Glennddarc, the Headgnome of Sheernobb, directs the party to Pasmarie's beehives when they inquire about the bandits [ch 009 / 009.03].
*   Pasmarie introduces herself to the party at her beehives, identifying herself as the keeper of Sheernobb's finest beehives [ch 009 / 009.04].
*   She tasks the party with recovering a stolen honey shipment, offering gratitude and enchanted honey as a reward [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.04; ch 011 / entry].
*   She informs the party that survivors of the caravan ambush spoke of the 'Black Earth' and stone weapons [ch 009 / 009.04].
*   She marks the last raid location, a few miles to the north east, on Zephyr's map [ch 009 / 009.04].
*   Before the party sets out, Pasmarie marks the caravan's location on the map and explains that it had taken a secret shortcut from Hommlet [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.03].
*   The party discovers broken crates marked with Pasmarie's seal at the ambush site near the Gnarley Forest [ch 010 / 010.03].
*   Zephyr and Zinnia realize that by securing the honey Pasmarie was looking for, they have inadvertently done work for Lareth the Beautiful, who had also sought to eliminate the Earth Temple cultists [ch 011 / 011.05].
*   The party returned the stolen honey to Pasmarie Clee in Sheernobb and received their reward [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.06].
*   Ostler Gundigoot, a tavern keeper in Hommlet, mentions that he now carries Honey Haven mead and had heard about "very nice people" from Glennon and Pasmarie Clee, crediting them with clearing bandit attacks [ch 015 / 015.05].
