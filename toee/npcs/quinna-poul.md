## Identity
Quinna Poul is a half-orc cook at the Honey Haven Inn in Sheernobb. She is described as enthusiastic and is currently preparing for the annual harvest festival. `[ch 008 / entry; ch 009 / entry]`

## Personality and Motivations
*   **Enthusiastic and Food-Motivated:** Quinna is eager to prepare high-quality meals and uses food as a primary incentive for the party. `[ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.05]`
*   **Pragmatic regarding Magic:** After an incident with animated ingredients, she offers a mild, understated explanation ("excited in the magic") rather than panic or complex lore explanations. `[ch 010 / moment]`
*   **Quest-Giving:** She actively assigns tasks to the party to obtain specific ingredients for her feasts. `[ch 008 / 008.05; ch 009 / 009.06]`

## History with the Party
*   **Ch 008:** Quinna approaches the party at the Honey Haven Inn, asking them to hunt a rare, dangerous, and elusive gnarled boar in the Gnarley Forest. She promises that a successful hunt will result in an extraordinary feast granting five additional hit points for one day. `[ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.05]`
*   **Ch 009:** Quinna points out the boar's location on a map for Zephyr after breakfast. She expresses delight when the party successfully captures the boar. She promises the party a feast on the 30th of the month and another feast the following day. `[ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.06]`
*   **Ch 010:** On the morning of the feast, Quinna reports to the party that several enchanted ingredients she purchased have animated and escaped into Sheernobb, causing chaos. She thanks the party after they recover the stabbed, shot, and bludgeoned vegetables. `[ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.02]`
*   **Ch 010:** After the ingredients are recovered, Quinna prepares a honey-glazed forest stew. This meal acts as a blessing, granting each diner five bonus hit points. She concludes by promising there will be no more quests from her. `[ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.02]`

## Last Observed State
Quinna successfully prepared the feast and served the honey-glazed forest stew. She has explicitly stated that she will provide no further quests to the party. `[ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.02]`

## Relationships
*   **Honey Haven Inn:** She works as the cook for the inn, alongside Mari Clee (proprietor) and Glennon (owner). `[ch 008 / 008.05]`
*   **The Party:** The party has completed two major tasks for her: hunting a boar and retrieving animated vegetables. `[ch 008 / 008.05; ch 009 / 009.06; ch 010 / 010.02]`
