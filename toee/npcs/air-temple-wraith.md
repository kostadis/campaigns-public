## Identity
*   An undead creature (wraith) that materializes in the octagonal room of the Air Temple when the party lands in the pit [ch 020 / entry].
*   Described as a terrifying undead spirit of malevolent power [ch 021 / entry], a spectral undead guardian [ch 028 / entry], and a malevolent undead entity [ch 029 / entry].
*   Physical attributes: AC 13, attacks deal necrotic damage [ch 020 / entry].
*   Defenses: Immune to necrotic damage; resistant to acid, bludgeoning, cold, fire, piercing, and slashing from non-magical weapons [ch 020 / entry]. Later evidence notes resistance to piercing damage, halving Zephyr’s pistol shot [ch 029 / entry].
*   Weaknesses: Can be turned by divine power [ch 020 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
*   Behaves with malevolence and mockery, calling the party "my little pets" and "poor little delightful little fools" [ch 030 / entry].
*   Motivations involve feeding on casualties from the temple war; it thanks the party for providing bodies as "tasty food" [ch 030 / entry].
*   Seeks to increase its power by commanding spectral servants raised from dead Earth Temple soldiers [ch 030 / entry].
*   Exhibits a desire to bargain for resources, asking for live bodies or advance word of the next attack while complaining about the lack of Earth Temple blood [ch 030 / entry].

## History with the Party
*   Chapter 020: The wraith materialized in the pit and hit Zephyr for 22 necrotic damage, reducing his maximum hit points [ch 020 / entry].
*   Chapter 020: Calmer used divine turning, dealing 17 damage and leaving the wraith frightened [ch 020 / entry].
*   Chapter 021: The wraith was still frightened at the start of the session; once the fear wore off, the party sneaked past it using a group stealth check [ch 021 / entry].
*   Chapter 028: The wraith briefly pursued the party before they fled its domain and it turned back; Kelno mistakenly identified it as an air elemental [ch 028 / entry].
*   Chapter 029: The wraith resisted Calmer’s attempts to turn it twice [ch 029 / entry].
*   Chapter 029: It struck Calmer with a blow dealing 16 damage as he fled, laughing hysterically at him [ch 029 / entry].
*   Chapter 029: Zephyr fired a pistol shot that was halved to 5 damage due to the wraith's piercing resistance [ch 029 / entry].
*   Chapter 029: The party passed the wraith again on their later descent, at which point it was noted as "staring at you again" [ch 029 / entry].
*   Chapter 030: The wraith guards the passage to the second level of the temple and commands seven specters raised from dead Earth Temple soldiers [ch 030 / entry].
*   Chapter 030: During combat, Spirit Guardians slowed and burned the wraith [ch 030 / entry].
*   Chapter 030: The wraith landed a critical necrotic hit that reduced Calmer to 3 hit points [ch 030 / entry].
*   Chapter 030: Calmer used Turn Undead to destroy the wraith [ch 030 / entry].

## Last Observed State
