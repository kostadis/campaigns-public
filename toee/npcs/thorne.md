## Identity
Thorne, known as "the Duke," is a mercenary and man-at-arms hired by the merchants Rannos Davl and Gremag to protect the party and guard their camp [ch 006 / entry; ch 003 / 003.02]. He describes himself as a hired hand who is "all about the money" rather than a true believer in the cult, though he acknowledges being of "questionable ethics" and willing to do anything for pay [ch 015 / 015.07; ch 015 / moment]. He later transitions into a role as a professional "cleaner" for the party, handling crime scene disposal and evidence removal [ch 016 / 016.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Thorne is characterized by dry practicality and cynical detachment [ch 003 / moment]. He prioritizes his payment and safety over ideology, explicitly stating he does not believe in the cult's goal to "corrupt the universe" and viewing his employers Rannos and Gremag as "lunatics" [ch 015 / 015.07]. He is pragmatic to the point of ruthlessness regarding crime scenes, advising the party to remove tongues from corpses to prevent *Speak with Dead* spells and offering professional advice on body disposal [ch 016 / 016.01; ch 016 / moment]. He expresses frustration with the party's inexperience in covering up crimes, noting they "suck at this whole cleaning business" [ch 016 / moment].

## History with the Party
*   **Initial Hire and Moathouse:** Thorne was hired by Rannos and Gremag to accompany the party; he guarded their horses outside the Moathouse during the first expedition, later making sarcastic remarks about the party's near-death experience with giant frogs [ch 003 / 003.02; ch 003 / moment]. He was surprised they survived and noted the short duration of their trip upon their return [ch 004 / 004.01].
*   **Interrogation of Tort:** The party learned via the commoner Tort that "the Duke" had hired Tort to gather information on the Moathouse in exchange for payment, had connections to bandits, and worked for an unknown master in the village [ch 006 / 006.02]. Despite this betrayal, the party did not confront him directly at that time [ch 006 / 006.02].
*   **Moathouse Expeditions:** Thorne guarded the party's belongings during subsequent trips, including after the rescue of Tort and a return to the Moathouse where he waited by a campfire until the party emerged, offering dry greetings like "Oh, you're alive" [ch 006 / 006.03; ch 008 / 008.02]. He attempted to extract information about cult evidence from them, which they concealed [ch 008 / 008.02].
*   **Capture and Interrogation:** After the party ambushed him at the merchants' stables, Thorne surrendered and revealed he was merely a hired hand for Rannos and Gremag, who were desperate to recover the Chest of Rao [ch 015 / 015.06; ch 015 / 015.07]. He agreed to help the party against his former employers, suggesting they knock him out to provide him with plausible deniability if the cult came looking [ch 015 / 015.07].
*   **Transition to Cleaner:** Following the party's killing of Rannos and Gremag, Thorne offered his services as a professional cleaner to dispose of the bodies [ch 016 / 016.01]. He advised them on hiding the evidence, including disguising Zinnia and Calmer to fake the merchants' departure, and warned that the "Deep Temple" was in charge above the local operatives [ch 016 / 016.01; ch 016 / moment].
*   **Retainer and Services:** The party hired Thorne on a retainer for future "messes" and crime scenes, with terms set at 20 gp per month and 100 gp per job [ch 016 / 016.01]. Later, after the party cleared the Moathouse, Zephyr contacted Thorne via a magical device to arrange for the disposal of bodies and evidence there, negotiating a fee of 50 gp for the incident [ch 017 / 017.02].
*   **Consideration for Hire:** In Chapter 24, Zephyr mentioned hiring Thorne to run their rooms at Nulb, but no action was taken [ch 024 / 024.03].

## Last Observed State
Status not established in the summaries beyond his role as a hired cleaner and merchant associate [ch 024 / 024.03]. In Chapter 024, he was mentioned as a potential hire for managing the party's rooms in Nulb, but the party did not act on this during the session [ch 024 / 024.03].

## Relationships
*   **Rannos Davl and Gremag:** His former employers. He described them as paranoid fanatics who answered to a "Deep Temple" [ch 015 / 015.07; ch 016 / 016.01]. He helped the party eliminate them and clean up the aftermath [ch 016 / 016.01].
*   **Tort:** A commoner Thorne had previously hired to spy on the party. Tort feared Thorne's associates enough to flee town [ch 006 / 006.02].
*   **The Party:** Initially a hired guard, he became a reluctant ally and eventually a professional contractor for the party's criminal cleanup needs [ch 016 / 016.01]. Zephyr jokes about forming "Zephyr and Duke Enterprises," but Thorne prefers the name "the cleaners" [ch 016 / moment].
