## Identity
A gnome and tavern keeper at "The Cheap Forester" in Ostverk [ch 011 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Insisted on food purchases before engaging in conversation [ch 011 / entry]. Dismissed rumors of a "big evil power" as lies, characterizing the threat as merely bandits [ch 011 / 011.07]. Proposed a theory that the chest would be used to secure a treaty with the Kingdom of Celene, enabling the Viscounty (Verbobonc) to wage war on gnomes and seize their gem mines [ch 011 / 011.07].

## History with the Party
- The party gathered rumors from Deakin in Ostverk [ch 011 / 011.07].
- Deakin insisted the party buy food before he would talk [ch 011 / 011.07].
- Deakin shared rumors that the chest was lost to bandits and that many are searching for it [ch 011 / 011.07].
- Deakin shared his theory regarding the chest's potential role in a treaty with Celene and subsequent war against gnomes [ch 011 / 011.07].
- Deakin noted that elves at Caer Ostverk and Lord Denelu were rumored to pay well for information on the chest [ch 011 / 011.07].
