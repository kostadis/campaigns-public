## Identity
Glennon Clee is the owner and proprietor of the Honey Haven Inn in Sheernobb [ch 008 / entry; ch 010 / entry]. He is a gnome who operates the establishment alongside Mari Clee and Pasmarie Clee [ch 008 / 008.05; ch 015 / 015.05]. The inn is famous for its exclusive local brew, Honey Haven mead [ch 008 / 008.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Glennon is direct, impatient, and focused on the safety of his business. He expects immediate action from those he hires, asking the party why they have not already departed to solve his problem once they agreed to help [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.05]. He is enthusiastic and expressive, reacting to the return of his stolen goods with loud excitement [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.01]. His primary motivation is the security and reputation of the Honey Haven, particularly regarding his inventory of mead [ch 008 / entry; ch 015 / 015.05].

## History with the Party
- Glennon approached the party upon learning they were adventurers, asking them to investigate barrels of mead stolen from his cellar, noting suspicious tracks leading into the forest [ch 008 / entry].
- He offered a reward of a week's free lodging and a small keg of enchanted honey mead for the recovery of the barrels [ch 008 / entry; ch 010 / 010.01].
- When the party hesitated to leave after agreeing, Glennon urgently asked why they had not departed already, prompting Zinnia to tell him to calm down [ch 008 / 008.05].
- Glennon personally checked in with Zinnia regarding the investigation, asking if the barrels had been found, to which Zinnia answered ambiguously [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.07].
- After the party returned the barrels (recovered by Zinnia in a glade), Glennon greeted the return of his beer with enthusiastic cries of "Yes, yes, yes, you found my beer." [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.01].
- He fulfilled his promise, providing the party with a week of free lodging at the inn and a keg of enchanted honeymead that grants advantage on Charisma checks [ch 008 / entry; ch 010 / 010.01].
- Glennon later spoke positively of the party to other contacts, referring to them as "very nice people" [ch 015 / 015.05].
- The party's assistance in clearing bandit attacks indirectly benefited Glennon's business, allowing Honey Haven mead to be restocked in Hommlet [ch 015 / 015.05].

## Last Observed State
Status not established in the summaries. He was last mentioned in Chapter 015 by Ostler Gundigoot, who quoted him speaking highly of the party [ch 015 / 015.05]. Glennon himself did not appear in Chapter 015.
