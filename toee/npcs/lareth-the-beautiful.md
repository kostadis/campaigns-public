## Identity
Lareth the Beautiful was a leader within the Moathouse dungeon and a member of the Temple of Elemental Evil’s Fire Temple [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.05]. He maintained control over fire territory while engaging in territorial plays against the Earth Temple, potentially seeking to gain a "seat at the table" within the temple hierarchy [ch 014 / 014.01]. Lareth employed guards, including a guard sergeant, and maintained relationships with local monsters, specifically hiring gnolls through a local gnoll king and employing bugbears who expected plunder and food [ch 008 / 008.01; ch 017 / 017.03; ch 017 / 017.05]. He was known to possess a chest of Rao and was aware that other factions, particularly the Deep Temple, disliked him and attempted to subvert his authority [ch 014 / entry; ch 019 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Lareth was characterized by extreme paranoia and dangerous competence in combat magic [ch 017 / entry]. He preferred to operate through deception and proxies, explicitly refusing to send his own guard sergeant on missions so that hired adventurers could act as "professionals" without his direct involvement [ch 008 / 008.01]. He was easily suspicious and prone to violence if his plans failed, stating, "I suppose I could kill you anyway," and later declaring the party had failed him when they could not produce a stolen chest [ch 016 / 016.06]. Lareth sought to expand his power by eliminating rival elemental factions, using the party to destroy the Black Earth Raiders (Earth Temple-aligned) to make himself more powerful [ch 011 / 011.05]. He valued self-preservation over loyalty or negotiation, taunting attackers with illusory duplicates and refusing to surrender even when at one hit point [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.01].

## Last Observed State
Lareth the Beautiful is dead, slain by Sequoia during a battle in the Moathouse [ch 017 / 017.01; ch 017 / entry]. During the final confrontation, he resisted a *Toll the Dead* spell at one hit point and cast *hold person*, paralyzing two party members before being killed [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.01]. His body and private chambers were looted by the party for items including a mace of smiting, magical plate mail, a ring of free action, a fire opal necklace, and significant gold [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.01]. Following his death, the party deceived his gnoll and bugbear employees into believing he was still alive or planning pay raises to manipulate them, eventually revealing his death to incite them to pillage the dungeon [ch 017 / 017.03; ch 017 / 017.05]. The Council of Hommlet acknowledged his defeat as a service that degraded the local bandits but did not end the threat of the Temple of Elemental Evil [ch 017 / 017.06].
