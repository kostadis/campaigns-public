## Identity
Spugnoir Balkof is a magic user and aspiring wizard [ch 001 / 001.02; ch 004 / entry; ch 006 / entry]. His name was misread and mispronounced by the DM for decades; "Balkof" was an addition by the DM, and his true name ends in S [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.01]. He is described as a famous mage known across many dimensions [ch 002 / entry], though elsewhere he is portrayed as self-important and knowledgeable [ch 004 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Spugnoir is self-important and complains that nobody pays attention when he speaks [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.06]. He enjoys being flattered [ch 005 / entry]. He is motivated by arcane knowledge, specifically seeking first choice of arcane scrolls and spellbooks in exchange for information [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.06]. He tends to dismiss local rumors with laughter, though he may possess credible intelligence on other matters [ch 004 / 004.06; ch 004 / moment].

## History with the Party
*   Met the heroes upon their arrival in Hommlet at the Inn of the Welcome Wench [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.02].
*   The party learned his name had been misread by the DM; Zephyr declared a preference for "Spugnoir" over "Balkof" [ch 002 / 002.01].
*   Raised an eyebrow and appeared impressed when the party exposed a cheat named Furnok at the inn [ch 004 / 004.05].
*   Dismissed rumors of a magical complex beneath the inn as nonsense, mocking the party for believing it [ch 004 / 004.06; ch 004 / moment].
*   Proposed an expedition to a lost wizard’s laboratory near Emridy Meadows, on the condition that he receive first choice of arcane scrolls and spellbooks [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.06].
*   The party accepted his proposal, though they ultimately chose to take Airgid home first [ch 004 / 004.06; ch 004 / 004.08].
*   Traveled with the party to the tower at Emridy Meadows [ch 005 / 005.05].
*   Spoke about the Circle of Eight and the Battle of Emridy Meadows during the trip [ch 005 / 005.05].
*   Was thrilled by the scrolls and spell book found in the leader's gnome-scale bedroom [ch 005 / 005.05].
*   Waited at the Inn of the Welcome Wench with Calmer, hoping to be chosen for the next expedition; the party chose Calmer instead [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.03].
*   Was chosen by the party for a subsequent expedition, but the party ultimately selected Calmer again because they needed a cleric [ch 008 / 008.03].
*   The party recalled his story about the Circle of Eight sealing the Temple of Elemental Evil when approaching the temple in Nulb [ch 020 / 020.03].
*   The party recalled his information regarding the seals on the temple doors, which were established by Bigby, Tenser, and Mordenkainen [ch 024 / 024.01].

## Last Observed State
Status not established in the summaries. The party last interacted with him directly in Chapter 008, when they chose not to take him on an expedition [ch 008 / 008.03]. He was mentioned in Chapters 020 and 024 as the source of lore regarding the Circle of Eight and the sealing of the Temple of Elemental Evil [ch 020 / 020.03; ch 024 / 024.01].
