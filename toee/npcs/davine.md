## Identity
Davine is one of four farm boys turned mercenaries who served as part of the Glimmerfields' crew [ch 025 / entry]. He is characterized as a member of the household staff and mercenary contingent accompanying the Glimmerfields, distinct from elite soldiers [ch 025 / 025.05].

## Personality and Motivations
The evidence describes Davine simply as a cheap mercenary rather than an elite soldier [ch 025 / 025.05]. No specific personal motivations, personality traits, or ambitions are recorded in the available summaries.

## History with the Party
*   Davine was part of the group of prisoners held by the party, including the Glimmerfields and their household staff [ch 025 / 025.05].
*   He surrendered and was imprisoned in the dungeon prior to the party's arrival [ch 025 / entry].
*   The party unlocked the prisoners' chains, freeing Davine along with Jorin Pike Kale, Master Aldous Glimmerfield, Mistress Lenora Glimmerfield, Kalen, Brask, and Horgus [ch 025 / 025.05].
*   Upon being freed, Davine and the other prisoners did not know how to exit and asked the party for guidance [ch 025 / 025.05].
*   The party guided Davine and the other prisoners to an escape route via a secret passageway and stairway [ch 025 / 025.05].
*   Davine successfully escaped the temple alongside the group [ch 025 / 025.05].

## Last Observed State
Davine was last seen during the prisoners' escape from the temple in Chapter 25 [ch 025 / 025.05]. He was freed from his chains and guided to an exit by the party [ch 025 / 025.05].

Status not established in the summaries.
