## Identity
Gremag was a trader in Hommlet who, alongside Rannos Davl, controlled much of the village’s commerce and manufacturing [ch 002 / entry]. While publicly presenting as a merchant, he was secretly a dangerous and powerful operative for the Temple of Elemental Evil, serving as Rannos Davl's henchman and bodyguard [ch 015 / entry]. The DM described him as "the real beefy, dangerous guy" and a "true believer of the cult" [ch 015 / entry]. He spoke with a nasal, aristocratic voice [ch 002 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Gremag was a fanatic, deeply committed to the cult's ideology of corrupting the universe, a sentiment contrasted by his employee (the Duke) who was motivated only by money [ch 015 / 015.07]. He displayed a short temper and impatience with negotiation, losing patience quickly and initiating combat with laughter at the party's defiance [ch 015 / 015.08]. He was physically imposing, described as a "beefy, strong" individual whose attacks could cause massive damage, such as knocking a monk from 31 to 0 hit points in two strikes [ch 015 / 015.08; ch 015 / moment].

## History with the Party
*   **Initial Contact:** Gremag and Rannos visited the heroes and convinced them to hire men-at-arms for protection [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.04].
*   **Expedition Support:** An employee of Rannos and Gremag accompanied the party to guard their camp during their first trip to the Moathouse [ch 002 / 002.06]. This man-at-arms, Thorne (also known as the Duke), remained outside while the party entered the ruins [ch 003 / 003.02].
*   **Surveillance and Trap:** Upon the party's return to Hommlet, the party realized Rannos and Gremag were controlling their movements. The Duke revealed that Rannos and Gremag were paranoid and desperate to recover the Chest of Rao, having previously set up an ambush at a tavern using the Duke [ch 015 / 015.07].
*   **Negotiation:** The party lured Rannos and Gremag to a meeting by sending a note. During the encounter in the merchants' office, Gremag sized up the party for an attack before Rannos demanded the Chest of Rao in exchange for their lives [ch 015 / 015.08].
*   **Combat:** Gremag initiated combat, knocking Zinnia unconscious with two brutal counterattacks after Zinnia struck him twice. Calmer eventually delivered the killing blow to Gremag [ch 015 / 015.08; ch 015 / moment].
*   **Aftermath:** The party looted Gremag's body, finding magical items, coin, and a mysterious scarab [ch 016 / 016.01]. The Duke helped cover up the death, and the party spread the cover story that Gremag and Rannos had left town urgently on business [ch 016 / 016.01; ch 016 / 016.02].

## Last Observed State
Gremag was slain by Calmer during the battle in the merchants' office [ch 015 / entry; ch 015 / moment]. His body was subsequently looted by the party [ch 016 / 016.01] and disposed of by the Duke, who dug holes for the corpses [ch 016 / 016.01]. A cover story was fabricated by the party to explain his absence, claiming he had departed for Verbobonc on private business [ch 016 / 016.01].

## Relationships
*   **Rannos Davl:** Gremag was Rannos's henchman and bodyguard [ch 015 / entry]. They jointly controlled Hommlet's trade [ch 002 / entry] and acted as agents for the Temple of Elemental Evil [ch 015 / entry]. Both were considered true believers or fanatics by their employee, the Duke [ch 015 / 015.07].
*   **The Duke (Thorne):** Gremag employed the Duke, who acted as a bodyguard and spy. The Duke stated he just works for Rannos and Gremag and was told to help set up ambushes [ch 015 / 015.07]. The Duke later admitted he "never liked" Gremag and Rannos, viewing them as fanatics [ch 016 / 016.01].
*   **Temple of Elemental Evil:** Gremag was an operative for the Temple, described as a true believer [ch 015 / entry]. He reported to higher-ups in the "Deep Temple" according to the Duke [ch 016 / 016.01].
