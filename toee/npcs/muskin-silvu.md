## Identity
Muskin Silvu is the evil cleric and necromancer in charge of the crypt, dressed in stonescale vestments. The DM identifies him as Muskin; the party has not learned his name. His notes claim that he is the true prophet of the Earth Temple [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.04; ch 042 / 042.04].

## Personality and Motivations
He claims the Earth Temple as his home, treats the party as usurpers, and threatens to exploit the corpses they leave behind. His notes denounce Romag, Hartsch, and Calmer as false prophets and insist that ritual victims must be willing [ch 037 / entry; ch 038 / entry; ch 042 / 042.04].

He has prepared additional undead to take the temple from Calmer. His opening plan falters when allied Air Temple troops absorb the attack he intended for the party or Fire Temple forces [ch 043 / 043.03; ch 043 / 043.04].

## History with the Party
- The party found his taunting note on a ghoul’s corpse. They deduced that he was likely a former associate of Barkinar displaced by Romag’s appointment [ch 037 / entry].
- Varek Solain later reported that the necromancer had served under Barkinar and disappeared after Romag received the promotion. Taking prisoners’ corpses and raising the dead remained suspicions in that account [ch 038 / entry].
- In the laboratory, the party defeated three ordinary ghasts and two ferocious ghasts. The necromancer was not part of that confrontation [ch 042 / 042.01; ch 042 / 042.02].
- The party recognized a freed prisoner’s carved-up body on a bench. They found the necromancer’s notes expressing frustration that this prisoner refused to cooperate. The laboratory held prisoners tortured in an attempt to make them willing ritual participants; no completed sacrifice is established [ch 042 / 042.04].
- Sequoia proposed making the necromancer a sacrifice, then withdrew the idea when Religion checks established that ritual sacrifice would cost Calmer St. Cuthbert’s favour. The proposal was not carried out [ch 042 / 042.04].
- When the party breached the crypt, Muskin laughed at the Air Temple vanguard and unleashed a necrotic emanation against the goblins. Their unexpected deployment consumed his opening attack [ch 043 / 043.03].
- After Calmer broke the first defensive line, Muskin fired three Grave Bursts at him. One struck, but Calmer maintained concentration. Muskin retreated deeper into the hallway, where further prepared undead awaited; the battle remained unresolved [ch 043 / 043.04].

## Last Observed State
Muskin was active in combat and retreated deeper into the hallway after attacking Calmer. The outer defenses were largely broken, but more dangerous enemies remained ahead when play stopped; his ultimate fate is not established [ch 043 / 043.04].

## Relationships
- **Calmer:** A rival whom his notes call a false prophet. Muskin prepared undead for a takeover and directly attacked him during the crypt battle [ch 042 / 042.04; ch 043 / 043.04].
- **Romag and Hartsch:** Denounced as false prophets in his notes [ch 042 / 042.04].
- **Barkinar:** His former superior, according to Varek Solain, who reported that Muskin disappeared after Romag received the promotion [ch 038 / entry].
- **The freed prisoner:** The party recognized the prisoner's carved-up body in the laboratory; the necromancer's notes expressed frustration at his refusal to cooperate [ch 042 / 042.04].
