## Identity
Iniri is a human druid encountered at Camp Number One at the Hall of the Scarlet Moon [ch 018 / entry]. She is one of four human figures (two male, two female) observed by the party chanting around a bonfire with two massive elk nearby [ch 018 / 018.10].

## Personality and Motivations
Iniri is described as friendly and excited about the "rite of the wicker giant" [ch 018 / entry]. Along with her companions Verigo, Mahoon, and Faria, she expresses hope that the rite will lead to "greater cooperation among the druids of the Gnarley Forest" [ch 018 / 018.10]. The group is excited to be initiated into "some big ascension and some very positive thing" [ch 018 / 018.10].

## History with the Party
The party approached Camp Number One, where Iniri was part of a group of four druids chanting around a bonfire [ch 018 / 018.10]. Verigo introduced the group, including Iniri, to the party [ch 018 / 018.10]. The druids, including Iniri, offered the party food, drink, and brandy, inviting them to stay the night and witness the rite [ch 018 / 018.10]. The party attempted to discern the druids' true intentions but failed to notice anything amiss during this interaction [ch 018 / 018.10].

## Last Observed State
Iniri was last seen at Camp Number One, engaging in conversation with the party about the upcoming rite and sharing brandy [ch 018 / 018.10]. The party subsequently moved to Camp Number Two and engaged in combat with different NPCs (Albreth and Ingulf), then fled the area using invisibility and a distraction involving a bear [ch 018 / 018.10]. Status not established in the summaries regarding her condition after the party's departure from the general area.

## Relationships
Iniri is a fellow druid with Verigo, Mahoon, and Faria at Camp Number One [ch 018 / 018.10].
