## Identity
Brakka Vusk is a silver-tongued gambler and member of Dren Halveth's gang, the Broken Blades [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.06]. He is described as crossbow-loving [ch 024 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Brakka is pragmatic and profit-driven, prioritizing survival and gain over blind loyalty or unnecessary conflict. He explains that his group avoids direct confrontation with powerful enemies like the Crimson Reapers because they "have enough enemies as it is" and there is "no profit in stealing it if you're going to die" [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.06]. His reaction to the party's questions was laughter, suggesting a relaxed or amused demeanor regarding danger [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.06].

## History with the Party
- The party met Brakka during a confrontation with Captain Dren Halveth on the second floor of the Waterside Hostel [ch 024 / 024.06].
- When the party member Calmer asked why Dren's crew did not retrieve their stolen weapons from the Crimson Reapers themselves, Brakka laughed and provided the explanation [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.06].
