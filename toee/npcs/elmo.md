## Identity
Elmo is a local man in Hommlet who presents himself as disheveled, somewhat drunk, and a buffoon [ch 004 / entry; ch 016 / entry]. He is a ranger by profession and a member of the town committee [ch 004 / 004.06; ch 016 / entry]. In Chapter 016, it is revealed that his persona is a cover; he is actually a highly focused and perceptive intelligence agent working for the Viscount of Verbobonc [ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / moment]. His father is the captain of the Hommlet militia [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 004 / 004.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Elmo shares a pragmatic attitude toward heroism, believing that doing good requires payment [ch 004 / 004.06; ch 004 / moment]. While maintaining his drunken facade, he is observant and perceptive, particularly regarding the actions of the party's members [ch 016 / moment]. As an agent for Verbobonc, he is motivated by political stability, urging the party to deal with the Moathouse threat independently to avoid triggering a conflict with the local gnomes, who would view Verbobonc troops as an act of war [ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / 016.03].

## History with the Party
*   Elmo intervened at the Welcome Wench to drive the cheating gambler Furnok out of town, threatening to summon his father and the militia [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.05].
*   He expressed approval of the party, calling them "good guys," and offered to serve as a fourth adventurer for future hunts in the woods [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.06].
*   The party considered recruiting Elmo to hunt a beast near the Moathouse or in the Gnarley Forest, but ultimately chose Calmer as their companion for the trip to the Lortmil Mountains [ch 004 / 004.08].
*   In Chapter 016, Elmo approached the party at the inn, where Zinnia and Zephyr identified his drunkenness as a front for his role as a spy for Verbobonc [ch 016 / 016.02; ch 016 / moment].
*   The party confronted Elmo in a private room, where he dropped his act and admitted to being a focused agent who had been watching the merchants Rannos and Gremag [ch 016 / 016.03].
*   Elmo instructed the party to destroy the evil presence at the Moathouse as independent contractors to avoid political fallout with the gnomes [ch 016 / 016.03].
*   Elmo sent rangers north to pursue Rannos and Gremag after the party falsely claimed they had fled with the Chest of Rao [ch 016 / 016.03; ch 016 / 016.04].

## Last Observed State
In Chapter 016, Elmo resumed his drunken persona and left the inn to alert his rangers, stating he had to "take a wee" [ch 016 / 016.03]. Status not established in the summaries.
