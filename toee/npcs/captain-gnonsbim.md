## Identity
Captain Gnonsbim is the leader of the Sheernobb militia [ch 008 / entry]. He arrived at the Honey Haven Inn after hearing about the party from the gate guards [ch 008 / 008.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Gnonsbim is pragmatic and politically motivated. He views the coordinated bandit attacks as proof that the brigands have a central leader [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.05]. His primary motivation is securing this intelligence and eliminating the threat to stabilize the region, as his militia is too thinly stretched to act without weakening village defenses [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.05]. He is also up for re-election, which drives his requirement that the party publicly credit his leadership before receiving a specific reward [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.05]. He values tangible results, explicitly asking for "proof, evidence, violence, dead bandits" [ch 008 / 008.05; ch 008 / moment].

## History with the Party
- Gnonsbim arrived at the Honey Haven Inn to meet the party, having heard about them from the gate guards [ch 008 / 008.05].
- He hired the party to locate the bandit camp northeast of town, offering 25 gold for the location, 10 gold per captured or killed bandit, 50 gold for the leader, and a custom-forged piece of equipment for returning with the leader or equivalent proof [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.05].
- He attached a political condition to the custom reward: the party must attend a speech crediting his leadership to receive the item [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.05].
- The party accepted the deal, and Gnonsbim bought them a round of Honey Haven mead, placing it on the captain's bill [ch 008 / 008.05].
- When Calmer reached for the mead, Gnonsbim agreed with Sequoia that consuming too much unfamiliar alcohol was a bad idea and ordered the drink stored for later [ch 008 / 008.05].
- Gnonsbim complimented Sequoia as the sort of person who can be trusted to look after the others [ch 008 / 008.05].

## Last Observed State
Captain Gnonsbim was present at the Honey Haven Inn during the party's stay, engaging in negotiation and social interaction with the adventurers [ch 008 / 008.05]. Status not established in the summaries.

- **Calmer:** Gnonsbim indirectly influenced Calmer by agreeing with the decision to store the mead rather than let Calmer consume it immediately [ch 008 / 008.05].
