## Identity
- Lucius Graeme is a soft-looking, grey man who smiles frequently [ch 035 / entry; ch 036 / entry; ch 038 / entry].
- He acts as a conduit and middleman between Nulb and the temple leadership, sent by Barkinar to support the new management [ch 035 / entry; ch 035 / 035.04].
- He introduces himself as a facilitator who procures magical items, luxury goods, and supplies for the temple [ch 035 / entry; ch 035 / 035.04].
- Falrinth describes him as having golden eyes and soft hands [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / 038.05].

## Personality and Motivations
- Lucius is cheerful, flattering, and polite, often laughing off security checks such as pat-downs [ch 035 / entry; ch 036 / entry].
- He insists that shopping for magical items is beneath the leadership and positions himself as a necessary intermediary [ch 035 / entry; ch 035 / 035.04].
- He is firm that items are not free, citing Lord Hedrack’s insistence on covering temple expenses and his own suppliers [ch 035 / entry; ch 035 / 035.05].
- He operates with a hidden agenda to collect favors and information by tracking the gifts he provides to targets [ch 038 / 038.05].
- He advises stalls and non-committal behavior when dealing with powerful enemies, suggesting he plays multiple sides or seeks leverage [ch 038 / 038.05].

## History with the Party
- Lucius arrived at the party's lair in Chapter 35, claiming to be sent by Barkinar to ensure the new leadership had what they needed [ch 035 / 035.04].
- He gifted the party a cask of fine wine and potions of superior healing, though he maintained this was a gesture of goodwill while other items had costs [ch 035 / 035.04; ch 036 / entry].
- He presented a chest of magical wares, including rings of protection, boots of levitation, and staffs of striking, explaining that special orders took 2–3 days [ch 035 / entry; ch 035 / 035.04; ch 035 / 035.05].
- Sequoia and Zephyr purchased shortswords +1 from Lucius for 1,000 gold pieces each [ch 035 / 035.05].
- Lucius offered to hold the Ring of Invisibility for Sequoia and the Ring of Free Action for Zephyr for a 4,000 gold piece deposit, which Zephyr refused [ch 035 / 035.05].
- In Chapter 37, Calmer identified Lucius as the person to contact regarding the cleanup of the temple's ruined food stores [ch 037 / entry; ch 037 / 037.04].
- In Chapter 38, Falrinth warned the party that Lucius secretly catalogs what he gives to collect favors and information later [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / 038.05].
- In Chapter 40, Lucius suggested the presentation of Tolub’s severed head on the party’s table, an arrangement Selentis facilitated and Barkinar delivered [ch 040 / 040.02].

## Last Observed State
- Lucius was last seen in Chapter 40, where he was suggested to have arranged the gruesome display of Tolub's head, though he was not directly described as being physically present in the room at that specific moment beyond the arrangement of the event [ch 040 / 040.02].
- Status not established in the summaries regarding his current location or immediate status following Chapter 40.

## Relationships
- **Barkinar**: Lucius is sent by Barkinar to act as a conduit to the temple [ch 035 / entry; ch 035 / 035.04]. Barkinar also mentioned Lucius's role in the presentation of Tolub's head [ch 040 / 040.02].
- **Falrinth**: Falrinth warns the party about Lucius, describing him as a flattering man who collects favors [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / 038.05].
- **Calmer**: Calmer views Lucius as the contact for logistics like food store cleanup [ch 037 / 037.04].
- **Sequoia and Zephyr**: They are customers who have purchased items from Lucius and debated deposits for rare items [ch 035 / 035.05].
