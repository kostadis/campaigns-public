## Identity
Garrick is a grizzled ex-mercenary with a missing ear and a perpetual scowl. He operates an armory stall in the town of Nulb [ch 019 / entry]. He is characterized by selling rusted weapons that are described as having "seen action" [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.07].

## Personality and Motivations
Garrick is loud and hails potential customers directly ("You there, adventurer! … I heard you want weapons!") [ch 019 / 019.07]. He maintains a pragmatic, slightly cynical sales pitch, acknowledging that his rusted weapons may have had "a little bit too much action" but insisting they "it still cuts when it counts. For the right price, of course." [ch 019 / 019.07]. When discussing local events, such as the hanging of Elfar Twell, he speaks with ambiguity regarding the victim's specific crimes but is certain about the political motive: "Madame Selentis, Lady Selentis, wanted him dead" [ch 019 / 019.07].

## History with the Party
- Zephyr, having asked Magda for weapons recommendations, was directed to Garrick [ch 019 / 019.07].
- Garrick hailed the party loudly to initiate sales [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.07].
- Garrick attempted to sell the party rusted weapons, specifically a rusted short sword, noting their condition with dark humor [ch 019 / 019.07].
- Zephyr declined the rusted gear, indicating a lack of interest in the merchandise [ch 019 / 019.07].
- Garrick provided information to the party regarding the hanged body visible from his stall, identifying the man as Elfar Twell and explaining that the hanging occurred because Twell "crossed Madame Selentis" [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.07].

## Last Observed State
Garrick was last observed running his armory stall in Nulb, attempting to sell rusted weapons to the party and discussing the recent execution of Elfar Twell [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.07]. Status not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
- **Elfar Twell**: Garrick identified the hanged man on display as Elfar Twell. Garrick stated Twell "broke the rule, the only rule in Nulb" by crossing Madame Selentis [ch 019 / 019.07].
- **Madame Selentis**: Garrick acknowledged her authority in Nulb, stating she wanted Elfar Twell dead, which led to his execution [ch 019 / 019.07].
- **Zephyr**: Zephyr sought Garrick out based on Magda's recommendation but did not purchase items from him [ch 019 / 019.07].
