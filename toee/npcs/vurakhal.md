## Identity
Vurakhal is a fire salamander and former Fire Temple guardian, now bound by a magical service contract to the party. He formerly held Frostbrand prisoner. [ch 033 / entry; ch 043 / entry]

He does not speak Common; the party initially used mime and later relied on Frostbrand to translate. He does not speak Abyssal. [ch 033 / 033.02; ch 036 / 036.02; ch 037 / entry]

His heat burns creatures touching him, and he fights with a flaming spear and a constricting tail. [ch 033 / entry; ch 036 / 036.04; ch 043 / 043.04]

## Personality and Motivations
Vurakhal treats service as a business arrangement: he surrendered when the cause appeared lost, accepted amendments removing soul-claiming clauses, and offered Greater Temple information if the relationship improved. [ch 033 / 033.02]

He carefully clarifies his obligations and was pleased that the party’s authority exempted him from reporting the troll mission to Fire Temple leadership. [ch 036 / 036.02]

He shows enthusiasm for combat and bored irritation toward Frostbrand’s speeches, including eye-rolling and retaliatory swats. [ch 036 / 036.04; ch 033 / 033.03]

## History with the Party
*   Vurakhal surrendered to the party after seeing the tide of battle turn at the Fire Temple and presented a magical contract to serve as a sentry and informant. [ch 033 / entry]
*   He communicated with the party entirely through mime because none of them spoke the salamander tongue. [ch 033 / 033.02]
*   He agreed to guard the Fire Temple and fight Water Temple survivors, hinting at Greater Temple secrets if the business relationship improved. [ch 033 / 033.02]
*   He bickered with the sentient sword Frostbrand, whom he had previously imprisoned in a fire pit. [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.03]
*   He was recruited by the party to help eliminate trolls that had been scavenging the battlefield. [ch 036 / 036.02]
*   He confirmed he did not need to report the troll-elimination job to the Fire Temple leadership because the party was the ruling authority. [ch 036 / 036.02; ch 036 / moment]
*   He participated in the ambush of two trolls, using his heat aura to prevent their regeneration and killing one with his flaming spear. [ch 036 / 036.04; ch 036 / moment]
*   He took significant damage (37 points) during the troll fights. [ch 036 / entry; ch 036 / 036.04]
*   He assisted in the stealthy approach to the troll chieftain’s lair, contributing a roll of 22 to the group stealth check that granted the party a surprise round. [ch 036 / entry; ch 036 / 036.05]
*   He used his fire aura to prevent the troll chieftain from regenerating during the final fight. [ch 036 / 036.05]
*   After the troll encounters, he returned to his lair due to injury. [ch 036 / entry; ch 036 / 036.07]
*   During the burning of the "garbage room," the party considered asking Vurakhal for help but ultimately approached Captain Varek Solain for fire arrows instead. [ch 038 / 038.02]
*   He remained stationed at the Fire Temple altar. [ch 038 / entry]
- Vurakhal arrived with Maeris Dorn to support the assault on the necromancer’s forces. Zephyr supplied his token to establish Dren’s authority to summon him. [ch 043 / 043.02]
- He received Bless from Maeris before the assault. [ch 043 / 043.03]
- He constricted a mummy priest with his tail and dragged it out of the passage, burning it through continued contact. The priest escaped and subsequently died to Calmer’s magic. [ch 043 / 043.04]

## Last Observed State
Vurakhal was actively fighting alongside the party in the Earth Temple crypts. His most recent recorded action was burning the priest held in his tail; it escaped before Calmer’s magic killed it. The battle remained unresolved when play stopped. [ch 043 / 043.04]
