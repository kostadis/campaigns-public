## Identity
Dick Rentsch is the proprietor of the Waterside Hostel in Nulb and serves as the local recruitment contact and liaison for Prophet Romag of the Earth Temple [ch 019 / entry; ch 024 / entry]. He acts as the gatekeeper for the Temple of Elemental Evil, controlling access to Prophet Romag and managing the flow of new recruits into the organization [ch 019 / entry; ch 020 / entry]. He is responsible for vetting potential assets, such as the mercenary company known as the Broken Blades, and facilitating their integration or recruitment on behalf of the Earth Temple [ch 024 / 024.03; ch 024 / 024.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Rentsch presents a duality of behavior: he is described as gruff and demanding when vetting new recruits, insisting on tests of trustworthiness and refusing immediate access to leadership, stating, "that's not how it works. I need to know that you're somebody I can trust" [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.08]. However, he also displays a "charming smile" and affability when dealing with established allies or in social settings at the hostel [ch 024 / 024.04]. He is pragmatic and ruthless, willing to send recruits into dangerous situations, such as ambushing gnoll handlers or navigating the Temple of Elemental Evil, and expresses disappointment in those who do not meet his expectations of competence [ch 019 / 019.10; ch 020 / moment].

He is fiercely loyal to the Earth Temple’s cause, boasting about his recruits' successes to Romag and expressing ambition for the temple's dominance, telling the party, "with your work, we're going to rule over all of the temples in no time" [ch 020 / 020.10; ch 024 / 024.05]. Rentsch operates with a belief in necessary complicity and pragmatic alliances, noting that even those with divided loyalties, like the barkeep Wat, must be kept close because "until complete power is secured, everyone has to work together" [ch 024 / 024.09]. He maintains a vigilant and secretive operational style, moving sensitive discussions to private rooms because "the walls have ears" [ch 024 / 024.05].

## Last Observed State
In Chapter 024, Rentsch was observed acting behind the bar of the Waterside Hostel while the party prepared to leave with the recruited Broken Blades [ch 024 / 024.09]. He facilitated the meeting between the party and Dren Halveth, knocking on the door to introduce them as having a business opportunity, and had previously taken the party to a private back room to discuss the recruitment terms [ch 024 / 024.05; ch 024 / 024.06]. When questioned by Calmer about retaining Wat despite suspicions of Water Temple loyalty, Rentsch reaffirmed his strategy of temporary cooperation with all factions until absolute power was achieved [ch 024 / 024.09].

In Chapter 025, Rentsch was not physically present but was the subject of testimony from a prisoner, Jorin Pike Kale. Kale stated, "I was brought here by Dick Rentsch and his men as punishment for making a pass at Dala," implicating Rentsch in the abduction and imprisonment of civilians who offend staff at the Waterside Hostel [ch 025 / 025.03; ch 025 / moment]. The party was not in Rentsch's presence during this event, and his current physical location or active status at this specific moment is not established in the summaries, though his ongoing role as a recruiter and enforcer is confirmed by the prisoners' account.

## Relationships
*   **Romag (Prophet of the Earth Temple):** Rentsch serves as Romag's primary contact in Nulb, reporting intelligence such as the presence of the Broken Blades and recruiting operatives like Joradin and the party to expand the Earth Temple's influence [ch 019 / entry; ch 022 / entry; ch 024 / 024.03]. Romag relies on Rentsch for vetting and acquiring higher-level operators to secure critical checkpoints [ch 024 / 024.03].
*   **Wat (Barkeep at Waterside Hostel):** Rentsch employs Wat, despite knowing that Wat is suspected of working for other factions, specifically the Water Temple. Rentsch retains him as a pragmatic measure to ensure cooperation from all potential actors until the Earth Temple secures total control [ch 024 / 024.09].
*   **The Party (Sequoia, Zephyr, Calmer, Zinnia):** Rentsch initially acted as their handler, guiding them into the Temple of Elemental Evil and setting their initial tasks [ch 020 / entry]. He later expressed satisfaction with their success ("I told you these guys were… these guys were the guys!") and tasked them with recruiting the Broken Blades to support their growing territory [ch 020 / 020.10; ch 024 / 024.05].
*   **Dren Halveth (Captain of the Broken Blades):** Rentsch acted as the intermediary between Dren Halveth and the Earth Temple, arranging meetings and facilitating the recruitment negotiations [ch 024 / 024.05; ch 024 / 024.06].
*   **Dala (Staff at Waterside Hostel):** Rentsch is implicated in the imprisonment of Jorin Pike Kale, who was captured by Rentsch and his men for making a pass at Dala, suggesting Rentsch enforces strict, brutal protection of the hostel's staff [ch 025 / 025.03].
*   **Jorin Pike Kale:** Rentsch is the captor who had Jorin imprisoned in the Earth Temple's prison chamber for an offense against Dala [ch 025 / 025.03].
