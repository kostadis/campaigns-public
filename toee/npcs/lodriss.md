## Identity
Lodriss is the actual owner of the Boatman's Tavern in Nulb [ch 019 / entry]. Skole describes her as the one "who's in charge of this, who actually owns this place" [ch 019 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Status not established in the summaries. No personality traits or personal motivations are attributed to Lodriss in the provided evidence; she is defined solely by her ownership of the tavern and her conflict with the bartender, Skole [ch 019 / 019.06].

## History with the Party
- Lodriss was mentioned by Skole during a private conversation in his back room, where he expressed his desire to remove her from the tavern's ownership [ch 019 / 019.06].
- Skole indicated that getting rid of Lodriss would allow him to regain control of his bar, framing her as an obstacle he wished to eliminate [ch 019 / 019.06].
