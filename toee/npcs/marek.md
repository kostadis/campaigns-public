## Identity
Marek is a local teenager living in Hommlet [ch 004 / entry]. His name is confirmed when he introduces himself during the brewery stakeout [ch 004 / 004.07].

## Personality and Motivations
Marek is terrified of discovery and seeks to protect his secret relationship with Althea from her father, Filliken [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.07]. He is motivated by the social and religious divide in Hommlet, specifically the conflict between the Church of St. Cuthbert and the Old Faith (druidic worship of Beory), which prevents his union with Althea [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.07]. He strongly denies any affiliation with the Temple of Elemental Evil [ch 004 / 004.07].

## History with the Party
*   Marek was part of the pair identified as "cloaked figures" behind the Braumeister’s house, whom the party initially suspected of being evil spies [ch 004 / 004.07].
*   When Zephyr approached their meeting spot, Marek and Althea were revealed to be two teenagers engaged in a romantic encounter [ch 004 / 004.07].
*   Marek pleaded with Zephyr and the party not to tell his parents about the meeting [ch 004 / 004.07].
*   He explained to the party that Althea’s father, Filliken, would oppose their relationship due to their differing religions [ch 004 / 004.07].
*   Marek denied belonging to the Temple of Elemental Evil when questioned by the party [ch 004 / 004.07].
*   Following the party's advice to be more discreet, Marek and Althea fled the scene in their cloaks [ch 004 / 004.07].

## Last Observed State
Marek fled the brewery grounds with Althea in their cloaks after being discovered and questioned by the party [ch 004 / 004.07]. Status not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
*   **Althea**: Marek is in a secret romantic relationship with Althea, meeting her behind the brewery at night [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.07].
*   **Filliken**: Althea’s father, whose religious opposition forces Marek and Althea to hide their relationship [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.07].
*   **Zephyr**: The party member who discovered Marek and Althea; Marek begged Zephyr for discretion [ch 004 / 004.07].
