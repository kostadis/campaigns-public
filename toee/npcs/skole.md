## Identity
Skole is the bartender of the Boatman's Tavern in Nulb. He presents himself to patrons as the owner of the establishment, though the evidence reveals that Lodriss actually owns the tavern [ch 019 / entry]. He is described as an informant who maintains a low profile while gathering intelligence on the local power dynamics [ch 038 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Skole is pragmatic and financially motivated, expressing frustration when forced to serve drinks at no profit, such as when Captain Tolub demands free rounds for the party [ch 018 / 018.05]. He dislikes both Captain Tolub and Lodriss, stating that he came to Nulb for "peace and quiet" but became entangled with "very sketchy people," with Tolub being the "sketchiest" [ch 019 / 019.06]. His primary motivation is to regain autonomy and ownership of his bar, asking the party for a "path to getting rid of Tolub" and Lodriss so he can "get my bar back" [ch 019 / 019.06]. He is cautious and discreet, speaking in whispers and hushed tones, and prefers to conduct sensitive business in his back office [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.06]. He possesses a dry, sarcastic wit, evidenced by his deadpan handling of the party's presumed deaths [ch 039 / moment].

## History with the Party
- The party first encountered Skole at the Boatman's Tavern, where he served as the bartender alongside the serving wench Ketta [ch 018 / 018.02].
- When the party returned to the tavern later that evening, they observed Skole talking to a man before interacting with Captain Tolub, during which Skole was forced to provide free drinks, grinding his teeth in annoyance [ch 018 / 018.05].
- Upon the party's return to Nulb in a later session, Skole was shocked to see them alive, having believed Tolub’s claim that they had been disposed of [ch 019 / 019.06].
- Skole took the party into his back office to speak privately, revealing that Tolub had been boasting about killing them and warning that Tolub dominated the local criminal elements [ch 019 / 019.06].
- He informed the party that if they wished to contact the Prophet of the Earth Temple, they should speak with Dick Rentsch at the Waterside Hostel, noting that Tolub worked for the Prophet of Fire [ch 019 / 019.06].
- Skole expressed his desire to be free of Tolub and Lodriss, asking the party for help in removing them so he could regain full control of the tavern [ch 019 / 019.06].
- Later, Skole sent a covert note to Sequoia via a dock hand, informing the party that Captain Tolub was still telling people they were dead, stating, "He thought you might enjoy the update" [ch 038 / 038.06; ch 039 / moment].
- When the party entered the tavern to confront Tolub, Skole served them free drinks, quipping that "dead people drink free" and instructing the server to send the bill to Tolub [ch 039 / 039.04; ch 039 / moment].
- Skole looked visibly disappointed when the party chose to accept a deal with Tolub rather than removing him, signaling his lost opportunity for independence [ch 039 / 039.04].

## Last Observed State
Status not established in the summaries. The party last interacted with Skole during a confrontation at the Boatman's Tavern, where he was actively serving customers and expressing disappointment at the party's decision to let Tolub remain in power [ch 039 / 039.04].

## Relationships
- **Captain Tolub:** Skole dislikes Tolub and considers him a dangerous nuisance who dominates Nulb's criminal elements and forces free drinks upon his patrons [ch 018 / 018.05; ch 019 / 019.06]. Tolub uses the tavern as a meeting place, which Skole tolerates but resents [ch 019 / 019.06].
- **Lodriss:** Lodriss is the actual owner of the Boatman's Tavern, and Skole views him as one of the "sketchy people" he is stuck with, wishing to be free of him [ch 019 / 019.06; ch 019 / entry].
- **The Party:** Skole views the party as a potential tool to remove his rivals, Tolub and Lodriss. He has provided them with information, warnings, and free drinks to maintain goodwill [ch 019 / 019.06; ch 039 / 039.04].
- **Ketta:** Ketta works as a server at the tavern and collaborates with Skole; she delivered a beer to Tolub during the confrontation, warning the party about the danger of their actions [ch 018 / 018.05; ch 039 / 039.04].
