## Identity
Gurdy is a gnome bard who resides at the Honey Haven Inn. He claims to know everyone who matters in town [ch 008 / 008.05; ch 008 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Gurdy trades information for alcohol, specifically accepting a mug of mead in exchange for local intelligence [ch 008 / 008.05; ch 008 / entry]. His demeanor inspires eye-rolls from the party upon his arrival, and he leans on their table while offering his services [ch 008 / 008.05]. He seeks to join the party as a musician and blade, motivated by a share of the treasure they expect to find [ch 008 / 008.05; ch 008 / entry].

## History with the Party
*   Gurdy approached the party at the Honey Haven Inn and offered information about bandit raids in exchange for a small fee or drink [ch 008 / 008.05; ch 008 / entry].
*   Zinnia bought him a mug of mead after noticing his empty cup and parched voice [ch 008 / 008.05].
*   Gurdy provided context on the local defense strategy, noting that gnomes favor defending farms over hunting brigands, and connected the raids to a previously missing investigator rescued by the party [ch 008 / 008.05; ch 008 / entry].
*   He directed the party to the Moot Hall for further intelligence regarding the bandits' location [ch 008 / 008.05].
*   Calmer firmly rejected Gurdy's offer to join the expedition [ch 008 / 008.05; ch 008 / entry].

## Last Observed State
Gurdy made an offer to join the party for a share of the treasure, which Calmer rejected. Status not established in the summaries [ch 008 / 008.05; ch 008 / entry].
