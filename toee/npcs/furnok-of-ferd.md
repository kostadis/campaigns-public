## Identity
Furnok of Ferd is a rogue and dishonest gambler known as "the fabulous rogue." [ch 001 / entry; ch 004 / entry] He operates within the Inn of the Welcome Wench in Hommlet. [ch 001 / 001.02; ch 004 / 004.05]

## Personality and Motivations
Furnok is characterized by dishonesty, specifically using nicked cards and loaded dice to swindle patrons at games. [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.05] His primary motivation, as stated during his exposure, is simply to make a living. [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / moment] When confronted, he acts defensively and denies wrongdoing until physically forced to leave. [ch 004 / 004.05]

## History with the Party
- The heroes first met Furnok at the Inn of the Welcome Wench upon their arrival in Hommlet. [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.02]
- The party assisted Ostler in exposing Furnok’s cheating. Ostler suspected Furnok was a crook and asked the party for proof. [ch 004 / 004.05]
- Zephyr attempted to steal evidence from Furnok but failed, though he remained undetected. He created a distraction by knocking over a table, causing Furnok to rise and angerously ask, "What have you done?" [ch 004 / 004.05]
- While Furnok was distracted, Zinnia successfully stole his dice. Zinnia immediately recognized them as loaded. [ch 004 / 004.05]
- The party presented the loaded dice to Ostler as proof. Ostler publicly confronted Furnok, accusing him of stealing from customers. [ch 004 / 004.05]
- The party received three nights of free lodging, a slight discount on future food, and Ostler’s confidence as a result of exposing Furnok. [ch 004 / 004.05]

## Last Observed State
In Chapter 004, Furnok was publicly confronted by Ostler and threatened by Elmo. He protested his actions but was finally driven out of the Inn of the Welcome Wench. [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.05]

Status not established in the summaries.

- **Ostler Gundigoot**: The innkeeper who exposed Furnok and banned him from the Inn of the Welcome Wench. Ostler viewed Furnok as a crook stealing from customers. [ch 004 / 004.05]
