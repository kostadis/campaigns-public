## Identity
Prince Thrommel is a Paladin Lord, Grand Marshall of Furyondy, Provost of Veluna, and heir apparent to Furyondy [ch 040 / 040.01; ch 041 / entry]. His planned marriage was intended to unite Furyondy and the Archclericy of Veluna against northern threats, specifically the Horned Society and Iuz [ch 040 / 040.01]. His personal emblem is a crown and crescent moon [ch 040 / 040.01; ch 040 / moment]. Long thought dead since the Battle of Emridy Meadows, he is revealed to be alive and held prisoner within the temple [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / moment]. He is suspected to be a sleeper in an iron coffin on the level below the party's quarters, potentially within the Eastern Tomb [ch 040 / entry; ch 041 / entry].

## History with the Party
The party has not encountered Prince Thrommel directly; all interactions involve discussions about his location, status, and the political implications of his rescue.

*   In the town of Nulb, Mother Screng revealed to the party that Thrommel is alive and imprisoned within the temple, tasking them with rescuing him [ch 039 / 039.09; ch 040 / 040.01].
*   Otis expressed skepticism that Thrommel would have allowed himself to be captured or remained a prisoner for ten years, though he accepted the mission based on Mother Screng’s evidence [ch 040 / 040.01; ch 040 / moment].
*   Sequoia produced four puzzle pieces from his sister’s corpse, which Mother Screng identified as forming Thrommel’s crown-and-crescent emblem, suggesting a past secret connection between the prince and Sequoia’s family [ch 040 / 040.01; ch 040 / moment].
*   Zinnia excluded Calmer from a private discussion with Zephyr and Sequoia regarding Thrommel, deciding to withhold the intelligence from his spy network until the prince was physically found to ensure he received credit for the discovery [ch 040 / 040.03; ch 040 / moment].
*   Using ancient divination tools in the Exhaulted Chamber of High Augury, the party asked the oracle where Thrommel could be found; the oracle answered, “Beware the Eastern Tomb” [ch 040 / 040.08; ch 040 / moment].
*   Zinnia suspects that when Thrommel is found, he may not look like a prince, and Zinnia links his waking to the prediction that “Verbobonc’s banners come down on the temple” [ch 041 / entry].
