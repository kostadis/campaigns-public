## Identity

Wat is the bartender at the Waterside Hostel in Nulb. He is described as a wiry man of medium height with short, unkempt hair, deep-set observant eyes, and a complexion darkened by years of working in dimly lit environments [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.09].

## Personality and Motivations

Wat is observant and wary, often attempting to extract information from patrons regarding their business with the proprietor, Dick Rentsch [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.09]. He appears to have conflicting loyalties; while employed by the hostel, he may not fully work for Dick Rentsch, with the DM noting he "might work for Dick, but he doesn't work for Dick" [ch 019 / 019.09]. The party suspects he works for the Water Temple due to his visible displeasure at their recruitment of the Broken Blades [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.09]. He is pragmatic and transactional, overcharging patrons and enforcing house rules strictly [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.09].

## History with the Party

*   Wat was instructed by Dick Rentsch to keep an eye on the bar while Rentsch conducted private business with the party [ch 019 / 019.08].
*   During the wait, Wat approached the party to ask about their impression on Dick Rentsch and attempted to pump them for information on what the boss was interested in [ch 019 / 019.09].
*   When the party deflected, asking if their business involved Dala, a serving wench; Wat appeared visibly relieved when they said no [ch 019 / 019.09].
*   Wat charged the party one silver piece per drink instead of the listed prices and served them gin, warning them they "don't want the beer" [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.09].
*   Upon the party's return to Nulb, Wat was seen preparing the bar, scrubbing vomit from floors, and arranging tables [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.04].
*   Wat watched the party recruit the Broken Blades with visible unhappiness, leading Zinnia to become reasonably convinced he was the Water Temple person [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.09].
*   Zephyr taunted Wat, offering him the chance to join "the winning side," which the DM corrected to reflect the evil alignment of the factions [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.09; ch 024 / moment].
*   As the party prepared to leave, Wat approached Dren to whisper something; Dren shoved Wat away, and Wat stumbled off [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.09].

## Last Observed State

Status not established in the summaries. The last recorded interaction was in Chapter 024, where Wat was shoved by Dren after attempting to whisper to him [ch 024 / 024.09].

## Relationships

*   **Dick Rentsch:** Wat's employer and the proprietor of the Waterside Hostel. Dick keeps Wat on the payroll to maintain a fragile peace among competing factions in Nulb [ch 019 / 019.08; ch 024 / 024.09].
*   **Dala:** A serving wench at the hostel. Wat specifically asked if the party's business was about her and was relieved when it was not [ch 019 / 019.09].
*   **Dren:** Leader of the Broken Blades. Wat attempted to whisper to Dren, resulting in Dren shoving him away [ch 024 / 024.09].
*   **Water Temple:** The party suspects Wat works for the Water Temple, though this is not explicitly confirmed by Wat himself [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.09].
