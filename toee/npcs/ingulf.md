## Identity
Ingulf is a human man in robes, identifying himself as a "druid of the Circle of the Scarlet Moon" [ch 018 / entry]. He is stationed at Camp Number Two within the Hall of the Scarlet Moon [ch 018 / 018.10]. His allegiance is confirmed to be elemental evil, specifically fire [ch 018 / 018.10; ch 018 / moment].

## Personality and Motivations
Ingulf presents himself initially as a teacher, stating, "We are here to teach" when asked about the druids' purpose [ch 018 / 018.10]. However, he becomes immediately hostile and threatening when questioned about the Temple of Elemental Evil [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.10]. The evidence does not detail his specific personal motivations beyond his devotion to the Circle and elemental fire.

## History with the Party
- Ingulf was present at Camp Number Two alongside Albreth when the party approached after scouting Camp Number One [ch 018 / 018.10].
- Ingulf and Albreth introduced themselves to the party as members of the "Circle of the Scarlet Moon" and druids [ch 018 / 018.10].
- When the party asked if they were related to the "Temple of Elemental," Ingulf and Albreth adopted threatening poses and stated, "you came to the wrong place to ask the wrong questions" [ch 018 / 018.10].
- Ingulf revealed their true allegiance by listing "fire, water, earth, or air," confirming their connection to elemental evil [ch 018 / 018.10; ch 018 / moment].
- During the resulting combat, Ingulf (identified as one of the "priests" or "fire clerics" in the scene description, likely acting in concert with Albreth) cast a fireball. One fireball caused significant damage, and a second knocked Calmer unconscious [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.10]. Note: The scene text refers to the first priest and the second priest casting fireballs; the entry confirms Ingulf casts fireball during the escape [ch 018 / entry].
- The party escaped using Dust of Disappearance and unleashing the leashed bears, leaving Ingulf and Albreth furious [ch 018 / 018.10].

## Last Observed State
Ingulf was last seen at Camp Number Two at the Hall of the Scarlet Moon during a combat encounter where he attempted to attack the party with fire magic but was evaded by invisibility and a released bear [ch 018 / 018.10]. The text notes "the fire clerics are furious" after the party escapes [ch 018 / 018.10]. Status not established in the summaries beyond this encounter.
