## Identity
**Name:** Mistress Selentis (also referred to as Madame Selentis and Nysera) [ch 018 / 018.02; ch 018 / 018.03]
**Role:** The true power in Nulb, described as the "queen of the underworld" and a "spider in a web" [ch 018 / 018.03]. She controls the roads, supply lines, and food supply running through Nulb [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.05].
**Appearance:** A blonde woman with dark brown eyes and an aura of fallen nobility [ch 039 / entry].
**Origin:** Member of an old, powerful, but disgraced family from Verbobonc [ch 018 / 018.03].
**Agents:** Wear a black and white cord [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.07].

## Personality and Motivations
**Pragmatic and Controlling:** Selentis is pragmatic and quickly aligns herself with whoever holds authority to maintain her control over logistics [ch 039 / entry]. She bluntly emphasizes her leverage over the party’s needs, stating she owns the road between their requests (meat, oil, coin) and their location [ch 039 / moment; ch 039 / 039.06].
**Intimidating:** She is known to kill people for lesser infractions than crossing her [ch 018 / 018.03]. Her mere attention to someone is considered a sign that things have "gone very bad" for them [ch 018 / 018.03].
**Observant:** She studies individuals closely, particularly Calmer, trying to discern their true nature [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.06]. She recognizes Sequoia’s signature forgeries as "extraordinarily hard to copy" [ch 039 / 039.06].
**Deceptive but Functional:** While she coerces groups like the Rhennee into service with threats, she also honors agreements once leverage is established, such as re-establishing supply chains for the temple [ch 039 / 039.03; ch 039 / 039.06].

## History with the Party
*   **Ch 018:** The party was warned about Madame Selentis by Eryk Velkori. She was identified as "Nysera," the leader of Nysera's Talons, and the true power behind Nulb’s underworld [ch 018 / 018.02; ch 018 / 018.03]. Eryk advised that she should not pay attention to the party, as she has killed people for less [ch 018 / 018.03].
*   **Ch 019:** The party witnessed the extent of her power when they passed the gallows where Elfar Twell was hanged on her direct orders for "crossing her" [ch 019 / 019.07]. Merchants confirmed she wanted him dead and the constable merely carried it out [ch 019 / 019.07].
*   **Ch 039:**
    *   The party learned that Selentis had coerced the Rhennee into a mysterious caravan run, using a man with her black and white cord to deliver the threat [ch 039 / 039.03].
    *   Zinnia recalled that Selentis ran the temple's supply chain [ch 039 / 039.04].
    *   The party infiltrated the mayor’s house by hiding in the Rhennee caravan. Selentis met them, initially speaking smoothly to Serena about the caravan use, then turning to the party when they revealed themselves [ch 039 / 039.06].
    *   Selentis recognized the party as the new leaders of the Upper Temple. She negotiated directly with them, acknowledging their need for supplies and their control over the temple. She accepted Sequoia’s new supply signatures and agreed to re-establish the supply chain for the Fire, Water, and Earth Temples under the party's management [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.06].
    *   When asked about Captain Tolub, the party ordered him "gone." Selentis later facilitated his removal [ch 039 / 039.06; ch 040 / 040.02].
    *   Selentis has the party followed by an agent in a black and white cord to ensure they do not get into trouble or to monitor them [ch 039 / 039.07].
*   **Ch 040:** Selentis arranged the removal and presentation of Captain Tolub's severed head to the party, a presentation suggested by Lucius and delivered by Barkinar [ch 040 / 040.02].

## Last Observed State
Status not established in the summaries. Selentis was last seen in Chapter 039 negotiating with the party and arranging supplies. In Chapter 040, she was mentioned as having arranged the removal of Tolub, but did not appear personally.
