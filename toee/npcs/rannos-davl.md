## Identity
Rannos Davl is a local trader in Hommlet who, along with his partner Gremag, controls most of the village’s trade and manufacturing, including the sale of all goods produced by the blacksmith [ch 002 / entry]. While publicly a merchant, Rannos is secretly an operative for the Temple of Elemental Evil, specifically working for the "Deep Temple," the shadowy organization that oversees the four elemental cults [ch 014 / 014.01; ch 016 / 016.01; ch 019 / 019.05]. The DM described him as a "general-purpose cult fanatic" [ch 015 / entry] and "the guy in purple" on the world map [ch 014 / 014.01]. He was killed by the party in Chapter 016 [ch 016 / 016.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Rannos is characterized as cold, dismissive, and fanatical. He views negotiations with the party as an opportunity to intimidate rather than compromise, offering only their lives in exchange for the Chest of Rao [ch 015 / 015.08]. He is described by the Duke as a "lunatic" who believes in the cult's goal to corrupt the universe [ch 015 / 015.07]. His primary motivation in the later chapters is the acquisition of the Chest of Rao, driven by desperation to appease his superiors at the Deep Temple [ch 015 / 015.07; ch 014 / 014.01]. He possesses no wife or children [ch 015 / 015.04].

## History with the Party
*   **Ch 001:** Rannos and Gremag persuaded the heroes to hire men-at-arms for protection upon their arrival in Hommlet [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.04; ch 001 / moment].
*   **Ch 002:** The party learned from the blacksmith that Rannos and Gremag control Hommlet’s trade [ch 002 / 002.04]. The party hired a man-at-arms through them [ch 002 / entry].
*   **Ch 003:** The party employed Thorne, a man-at-arms supplied by Rannos and Gremag, to guard their camp at the Moathouse [ch 003 / 003.02].
*   **Ch 006:** Tort, a captured commoner, revealed that "the Duke" (a hireling of Rannos) was gathering information on the castle and working with bandits [ch 006 / 006.02]. The party suspected Rannos might be involved in the conspiracy [ch 006 / 006.02].
*   **Ch 012:** An interrogation of a cultist revealed that Rannos Davl worked for a larger competing organization and that the chest meant for Romag was likely near Gallows Corner [ch 012 / 012.01].
*   **Ch 014:** The DM confirmed that Rannos and the Duke work for the Temple of Elemental Evil [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.01].
*   **Ch 015:** The party returned to Hommlet and set a trap for Rannos, Gremag, and the Duke. Rannos arrived at the stables/office area, expressing surprise at the mention of the chest [ch 015 / 015.04]. The party ambushed the Duke, then confronted Rannos and Gremag in their office. Rannos dismissed the Duke and demanded the Chest of Rao, threatening the party's lives [ch 015 / 015.07; ch 015 / 015.08]. Combat erupted after negotiations failed [ch 015 / 015.08].
*   **Ch 016:** The party finished the fight. Calmer’s spiritual weapon landed the killing blow on Rannos Davl, who was at 2 hit points [ch 016 / 016.01; ch 016 / moment]. The party then faked Rannos's departure to cover up the murder [ch 016 / 016.02].

## Last Observed State
Rannos Davl was killed in Chapter 016 during the confrontation at the merchant's office [ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / 016.01]. Calmer’s spiritual weapon delivered the killing blow after Rannos was reduced to 2 hit points by Zephyr [ch 016 / 016.01; ch 016 / moment]. The party subsequently looted his body and, with the Duke's help, disposed of the corpses, establishing a cover story that he had left town urgently with a magical chest [ch 016 / 016.01; ch 016 / 016.02].
