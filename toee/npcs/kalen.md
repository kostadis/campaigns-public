## Identity
Kalen is one of four farm boys turned mercenaries who were part of the Glimmerfields' crew [ch 025 / entry]. He is described by the DM as a cheap mercenary and "not elite soldier" [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Evidence is insufficient to determine Kalen's specific personality traits or individual motivations. He is characterized primarily by his social status as a former farm boy and his role as hired protection for the Glimmerfields [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.05].

## History with the Party
- Kalen was part of the Glimmerfields' protection detail alongside Brask, Horgus, and Davine, hired as cheap mercenaries [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.05].
- The party successfully freed Kalen and other prisoners from their chains in the dungeon [ch 025 / 025.05].
- After being freed, Kalen was among the group guided to an escape route via a secret passageway discovered by Zephyr [ch 025 / 025.05].
- The party guided the escaped prisoners, including Kalen, to the exit [ch 025 / 025.05].
