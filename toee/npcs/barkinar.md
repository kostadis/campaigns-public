## Identity
Barkinar is a high-ranking figure and the Commander of the Greater Temple's Troops [ch 031 / entry; ch 032 / entry; ch 036 / entry; ch 038 / entry]. He is a former head of the Earth Temple who was promoted to the Deep Temple to make way for Romag [ch 022 / entry; ch 037 / entry; ch 038 / entry]. He is currently described as being "down in the bottom levels" [ch 037 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Barkinar is characterized as a cold, composed, and powerful leader [ch 032 / entry]. He is described as a man who "never smiles" [ch 035 / entry]. He exhibits a sense of cold admiration for efficiency [ch 031 / moment] and possesses a breezy indifference toward the ritualistic slaughter of prisoners [ch 040 / moment]. He has expressed a personal dislike for the man Tolub [ch 040 / moment].
