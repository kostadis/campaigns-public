## Identity
*   An aging merchant who wears a patchwork cloak of mismatched fabrics [ch 019 / entry].
*   Operates a stall named Magda's Trinket Trove in Nulb [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.07].
*   The stall sells strange odds and ends, charms, baubles, cursed ink, and other curiosities [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.07].
*   Does not sell weapons [ch 019 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
*   Presents herself as an eager and slightly rambling salesperson, greeting customers with repeated hellos and asking what brings them to the stall [ch 019 / 019.07].
*   Willing to sell dangerous or powerful magical items, such as cursed ink, to adventurers [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.07].
*   Knows of other merchants in town, specifically directing those seeking weapons to Garrick [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.07].

## History with the Party
*   The party encountered Magda's stall, Magda's Trinket Trove, while walking through Nulb on their way to the Waterside Hostel [ch 019 / 019.07].
*   Zephyr interacted with Magda at her stall [ch 019 / 019.07].
*   Magda offered the party various items, including a jar of glowing mushrooms, an old map, a bottle of cursed ink, and a leather thong [ch 019 / 019.07].
*   Zephyr asked Magda about weapons [ch 019 / 019.07].
*   Magda directed Zephyr to Garrick, a nearby armorer [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.07].
