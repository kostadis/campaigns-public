## Identity
Elazar is described as a druid and the leader at the Hall of the Scarlet Moon [ch 018 / entry]. He is said to know the 'rite of the wicker giant' and promises to teach it to others [ch 018 / entry]. His true nature and allegiance remain unclear [ch 018 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Elazar is characterized by others as a figure who has convinced those gathered at his location that he belongs to the Circle of the Scarlet Moon [ch 018 / 018.09]. Rumors describe him as "mysterious but powerful" [ch 018 / 018.09]. No personal actions or direct dialogue from Elazar are present in the evidence; all personality traits are derived from second-hand accounts [ch 018 / 018.09].

## History with the Party
- Elazar was mentioned by Eryk Velkori during a consultation about the Hall of the Scarlet Moon [ch 018 / 018.09].
- The party traveled to the Hall of the Scarlet Moon, where Elazar is said to reside, and attempted to sneak up on the encampments below the site [ch 018 / 018.09].
- The evidence does not record any direct interaction, sighting, or communication between the party and Elazar [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.09].
