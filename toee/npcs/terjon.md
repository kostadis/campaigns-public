## Identity
Terjon is the head cleric (or chief cleric) of the Church of St. Cuthbert in Hommlet [ch 002 / entry; ch 004 / entry; ch 006 / entry]. He is the superior of the cleric Calmer [ch 006 / entry; ch 008 / 008.03; ch 011 / entry]. Terjon regards himself as the rightful authority in Hommlet, citing the religious government of Verbobonc which is led by clerics of St. Cuthbert [ch 002 / 002.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Terjon expects conspicuous religious fervor and conviction from followers of St. Cuthbert, particularly regarding conversions [ch 002 / 002.05]. He is described as stern, close-mouthed, and dismissive of incompetence or weak evidence [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.01]. He is politically motivated to undermine the current town leadership, specifically Burne and Rufus, whom he believes have neglected town defenses and the castle’s construction [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.05]. He believes he should receive intelligence regarding the Temple of Elemental Evil’s return before secular authorities, creating a conflict of interest with Rufus and Burne [ch 002 / 002.05; ch 002 / moment].

## History with the Party
*   **Chapter 002:** Terjon met with the party to grant Calmer permission to accompany them, provided he converted a newcomer. He congratulated Calmer on converting Zinnia and demanded that any evidence of the Temple’s return be brought to him first, not to Burne or Rufus [ch 002 / 002.05].
*   **Chapter 004:** The party returned from the Moathouse and reported their findings to Terjon. Terjon was unimpressed by the "eye rock" evidence and disgusted that the party failed to bring back a prisoner for questioning. He dismissed Calmer until he could produce useful information. When Terjon attempted to keep the rock despite dismissing its value, Zephyr successfully argued that if it were worthless, the party should keep it, forcing Terjon to return the stone [ch 004 / 004.01; ch 004 / moment].
*   **Chapter 006:** During the interrogation of Hestort "Tort" Flintshine, Tort falsely claimed to work for Terjon. Zephyr’s intimidation exposed the lie, confirming Tort was lying [ch 006 / 006.02].
*   **Chapter 008:** While the rest of the party met with Jaroo, Calmer visited Terjon to report on their findings. This visit reinforced Calmer’s loyalty to the church but did not result in Terjon taking an active role with the party [ch 008 / 008.03].
*   **Chapter 011:** After Calmer died, the party revived him at the Temple of St. Cuthbert. Zinnia gave a magical trident to Terjon as a tithe to secure the resurrection. Jaroo warned that Terjon and his followers might withhold healing magic from non-followers, though the resurrection of Calmer was successful [ch 011 / 011.06].
*   **Chapter 037:** Calmer reflected on his disillusionment with the church, specifically blaming Terjon ("the Canon Terjon of Hommlet") for being willing to let him die and doing nothing to save him when the order had the chance [ch 037 / 037.03].
*   **Chapter 040:** Calmer apologized to the party, contrasting Zephyr’s demonic appearance with Terjon’s saintly look. He reiterated that while Terjon looked like a saint, he was willing to let Calmer die [ch 040 / 040.04; ch 040 / moment].

## Last Observed State
Status not established in the summaries. The party and Calmer have interacted with Terjon or his temple as recently as Chapter 040, but Terjon’s physical location or condition is not described in the final scenes [ch 040 / 040.04].

## Relationships
*   **Calmer:** Calmer is Terjon’s subordinate. Terjon initially approved of Calmer’s conversion efforts but later dismissed Calmer for incompetence. Calmer grew deeply resentful of Terjon for failing to resurrect him, viewing Terjon’s inaction as a betrayal of their "working relationship" [ch 002 / 002.05; ch 004 / 004.01; ch 037 / 037.03; ch 040 / 040.04].
*   **Burne and Rufus:** Terjon views them as incompetent and corrupt, believing they have neglected their duties. He seeks to remove them from office and demands precedence over them in handling intelligence [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.05].
*   **Y’dey:** Y’dey was the former head cleric who disappeared. Terjon is close-mouthed about her disappearance, and the local blacksmith suspects Terjon played "no small role" in it [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / moment].
*   **The Blacksmith:** The blacksmith suspects Terjon’s involvement in Y’dey’s disappearance and notes that Terjon is now in charge [ch 002 / 002.04].
*   **Jaroo:** Jaroo has a professional relationship with Terjon as a fellow member of the village council, though Jaroo expresses disapproval of the Church of St. Cuthbert’s expansionism [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 008 / 008.03; ch 011 / 011.06].
*   **Zinnia:** Terjon interacted with Zinnia regarding her conversion and the tithe for Calmer’s resurrection. Zinnia noted Terjon’s prior dismissiveness [ch 002 / 002.05; ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.06].
*   **Hestort Flintshine (Tort):** Tort falsely claimed to work for Terjon as a cover story [ch 006 / 006.02].
