## Identity
Mahoon is a human druid who is part of the group at Camp Number One at the Hall of the Scarlet Moon. [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.10]

## Personality and Motivations
Mahoon is described as friendly and excited about the "rite of the wicker giant." He hopes the rite will lead to greater cooperation among the druids of the Gnarley Forest. [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.10]

## History with the Party
- Mahoon was introduced by Verigo when the party approached Camp Number One. [ch 018 / 018.10]
- Mahoon, along with the other druids at Camp Number One, offered the party food, drink, and brandy, inviting them to stay the night to witness the rite. [ch 018 / 018.10]
- The party attempted to discern the druids' true intentions at Camp Number One but failed to notice anything amiss. [ch 018 / 018.10]

## Last Observed State
Status not established in the summaries. The last evidence places him at Camp Number One offering hospitality before the party moved to other camps. [ch 018 / 018.10]

## Relationships
- **Verigo**: Verigo introduces Mahoon to the party. [ch 018 / 018.10]
- **Iniri and Faria**: Mahoon is part of the group of four humans at Camp Number One, alongside Verigo, Iniri, and Faria. [ch 018 / 018.10]
