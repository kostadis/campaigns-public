## Identity
Canon Belsornig was the Prophet of the Water Temple [ch 019 / 019.05; ch 028 / entry; ch 031 / entry]. He was referred to as the "big shot" within the Temple of Elemental Evil [ch 000 / 000.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Belsornig's machinations were described as "vicious" [ch 024 / 024.02]. He was known to have orchestrated the purchase of a chimera, a move that potentially manipulated the Fire Temple's finances [ch 027 / 027.05; ch 028 / 028.02]. Before his death, he had planned to abscond with a hidden treasury [ch 034 / 034.05; ch 034 / 034.06].
