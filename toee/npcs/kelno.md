## Identity
Kelno is the ancient, cunning, tired-looking leader of the Air Temple, also called its prefect and cleric. He maintains goblins, troops, informants, and a bribery network [ch 030 / entry; ch 031 / entry; ch 031 / 031.02].

He agreed to remain the party’s subordinate in exchange for his life and his office [ch 031 / 031.02].

## Personality and Motivations
- **Survival and office:** Kelno explicitly prioritizes living and retaining his position, offering the party his resources rather than fighting them [ch 031 / 031.02].
- **Preparation and opportunism:** He anticipated the party’s arrival for two weeks, recruited defectors during the war, and expanded into former Water Temple territory afterward [ch 030 / 030.06; ch 031 / 031.02; ch 036 / 036.06].
- **Visible loyalty, personal caution:** He wants Barkinar informed of his support. He enthusiastically assigns his soldiers the honor of the vanguard, then places himself far from danger [ch 043 / 043.02; ch 043 / 043.03].

## History with the Party
- Kelno was mentioned in Chapter 027 as the leader of the Air Temple, whom Hartsch dismissed as a "joke" with reduced territory and no real power [ch 027 / 027.05].
- In Chapter 028, Calmer identified a wraith haunting the Air Temple, noting that Kelno was too ignorant to recognize it as anything other than an air elemental [ch 028 / 028.09].
- In Chapter 029, the party discovered that Kelno was using goblins to scavenge dead bodies from the temple wars, signaling his involvement in the conflict [ch 029 / 029.04].
- A freed Air Temple prisoner in Chapter 029 revealed that Kelno knew the location of a secret door in the Air Temple leading to the Lower Temple [ch 029 / 029.06].
- In Chapter 030, the party observed Kelno’s forces bribing Earth Temple bugbears to defect, and defeated gnolls sent by Kelno to reclaim the bribe money [ch 030 / 030.06; ch 030 / 030.07].
- Kelno was first encountered in person in Chapter 031, where he greeted the party in his spartan quarters, having anticipated their arrival for two weeks [ch 031 / 031.02].
- In Chapter 031, Kelno explained that the war between the prophets would end only when two of the four were dead, and noted that Belsornig’s assassins had recently reached Hartsch [ch 031 / 031.02].
- Kelno offered the party an alliance in Chapter 031: if they killed Belsornig, he would serve as their number two, granting control over the Air Temple’s goblins and informants in exchange for his life [ch 031 / 031.02].
- During the party’s infiltration of the Water Temple in Chapter 031, they used Kelno’s name in a bluff to gain entry, though the guards did not fully trust them [ch 031 / 031.05].
- In Chapter 031, Kelno provided the party with a Dagger of Venom, which Zephyr used to deliver the killing blow to Belsornig [ch 031 / 031.05].
- Kelno accompanied the party when they were summoned by the Greater Temple in Chapter 031 and was present when Barkinar officially recognized the party as the new rulers of the Upper Temple [ch 031 / 031.06].
- In Chapter 032, Kelno was brought before Barkinar, who acknowledged him, and "deal with Kelno" became a task on the party’s administrative list [ch 032 / 032.01; ch 032 / entry].
- In Chapter 036, the party discovered that Kelno had quietly expanded his territory into the former Water Temple holdings, and they chose to concede this area to him rather than contest his claim [ch 036 / 036.06].
- In Chapter 039, the party reviewed dispatch cases and noted that Kelno commanded the only other surviving army and had already moved to expand his domain, with Zephyr stating he trusted Kelno while Sequoia did not [ch 039 / 039.05].
- In Chapter 039, the party instructed Madame Selentis to continue supplying Kelno, confirming their decision to keep him in a subservient but supported position [ch 039 / 039.06].
- In Chapter 040, Kelno’s informants provided intelligence stating that a necromancer currently troubling the party was a former Earth Temple cleric who had disappeared after Romag’s rise [ch 040 / 040.05].
- Kelno immediately accepted Torlin’s request for assistance and personally arrived with all twelve air goblins and a bugbear leader. He declared support for Calmer as prophet and asked that Barkinar hear of it [ch 043 / 043.02].
- When Zephyr proposed entering as a turncloak, Kelno suggested bringing a dead Earth Guard as a gift to make the deception convincing. Sequoia challenged his motives [ch 043 / 043.02].
- The party adopted Dren’s proposal to send the Air Temple troops first. Kelno rallied them with promises of honor and glory, then withdrew to a distant position; his vanguard absorbed the necromancer’s opening attack [ch 043 / 043.03].

## Last Observed State
Kelno was personally present at the crypt assault. After sending his goblins and bugbear forward, he positioned himself as far from the others as possible [ch 043 / 043.03].

His leading goblins suffered the necromancer’s opening attack; surviving goblins and the bugbear continued fighting. The supplied scene does not establish the assault’s final outcome or Kelno’s subsequent condition [ch 043 / 043.03].

## Relationships
- **The party:** Kelno’s agreement makes him their subordinate, retaining his office while offering goblins and informants. Zephyr expresses trust; Sequoia expresses distrust and later questions his motives during assault planning [ch 031 / 031.02; ch 039 / 039.05; ch 043 / 043.02].
- **Calmer:** Kelno addresses him as prophet and publicly declares the Air Temple’s support [ch 043 / 043.02].
- **Barkinar:** Kelno stood beside the party during Barkinar’s recognition of their rule and later specifically requested that Barkinar be told of his support [ch 031 / 031.06; ch 043 / 043.02].
- **Dren:** Dren regards the Air Temple army as a threat to his own force and advocates using it as the vanguard for political as well as tactical reasons [ch 043 / 043.02].
- **Air Temple troops:** Kelno commands the goblins and their bugbear leader, motivating their advance with promises of honor and glory [ch 043 / 043.02; ch 043 / 043.03].
- **Madame Selentis:** The party instructed her to continue supplying Kelno; she expressed surprise that he survived the old order [ch 039 / 039.06].
- **Belsornig:** Kelno supplied the Dagger of Venom intended for Belsornig, which Zephyr used to kill him [ch 031 / 031.05].
