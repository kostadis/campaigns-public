## Identity
Burne is a magic-user and one of the leaders of Hommlet, serving alongside Captain Rufus as a member of the village council [ch 004 / entry; ch 016 / entry]. He is described as fabulously groomed with perfect hair and a refined manner [ch 002 / 002.03]. A successful observation by the party suggests that Burne and Rufus are a couple [ch 002 / 002.03].

## Personality and Motivations
Burne is portrayed as annoyed by reports of bandit activity, preferring to conduct experiments than address regional threats [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.03]. He and Rufus appear to be enjoying a comfortable, richly appointed life funded by Verbobonc while the government-funded castle project languishes [ch 002 / 002.03]. Despite his initial apathy, Burne takes security seriously when the evidence warrants it, urging secrecy regarding potential sabotage within the construction camps [ch 004 / 004.02]. He appreciates subtlety and deniability in operations, praising the party’s deception of Lareth the Beautiful because it weakened bandit factions without expending government resources or implicating Verbobonc [ch 008 / 008.03; ch 008 / moment]. Burne eventually admits that the authorities were too hasty in dismissing reports of the Temple of Elemental Evil’s reactivation [ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / 016.04]. He agrees that the village militia must be prepared "at all costs" against the rising threat [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.06].

## History with the Party
*   In Chapter 0, Jijohn the Tinkerer advised the party to seek out Burne and Rufus upon reaching Hommlet [ch 000 / 000.03].
*   The party reported bandit activity to Burne and Rufus at their tower. Burne reacted to the news with "Oh, not that again" and asked Rufus to send the party away so he could conduct experiments [ch 002 / 002.03; ch 002 / moment]. Rufus dismissed the adventurers, stating they had "important material to discuss and activities to pursue" [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / moment].
*   High Cleric Terjon informed the party that Burne and Rufus had failed to do what was necessary to protect Hommlet and that their activities suggested something "shady" was happening since Canoness Y’dey’s disappearance [ch 002 / 002.05].
*   After returning from the Moathouse, the party met Burne and Rufus at the Inn of the Welcome Wench. Burne greeted them as his "most recently deputized troops" [ch 004 / 004.02]. Rufus revealed that the keep’s blueprints had been copied, and Burne asked the party to identify the suspected spy in the work encampment, instructing them to keep it "on the down low" [ch 004 / 004.02; ch 004 / moment]. Burne and Rufus paid for the party’s drinks [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.02].
*   Burne attended a council meeting where Rufus stated there was no conclusive evidence of the Temple’s re-emergence [ch 005 / 005.04].
*   The party debated whether to inform Burne and Rufus about the identity of the map thief (the Duke). They ultimately chose to conceal this information, suspecting Burne and Rufus might also be evil [ch 006 / 006.02].
*   The party chose to report to Burne and Rufus first upon returning from a later Moathouse expedition. They applauded the party’s deception of Lareth the Beautiful, noting that the resulting violence looked like internal conflict rather than government action [ch 008 / 008.03; ch 008 / moment].
*   Burne and Rufus confirmed their interest in the Chest of Rao, which Jaroo noted was important because Terjon might withhold healing magic from non-followers [ch 011 / 011.06].
*   The party maintained a cover story that the merchants Rannos and Gremag had left town with the Chest of Rao. Burne and Rufus visibly relaxed upon hearing a consistent story that matched Elmo’s report, confirming that rangers had been sent to pursue the supposed thieves [ch 016 / 016.02; ch 016 / 016.04]. Burne admitted they had been too hasty in dismissing Temple reactivation reports and urged the party to clear the Moathouse [ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / 016.04].
*   The DM identified Burne as one of the factions wanting the Chest of Rao [ch 014 / entry]. Zephyr ruled out selling the chest to Burne and Rufus because they were "already poor" [ch 015 / 015.03].
*   During a secret council meeting following the defeat of Lareth, Burne responded to the confirmation of the Temple’s return with, "Hmm. I see. I suppose… I suppose we'll have to look into that" [ch 017 / 017.06; ch 017 / entry]. He agreed that the militia must be prepared "at all costs" and that the castle construction must accelerate [ch 017 / 017.06].

## Last Observed State
Burne is an active leader in Hommlet who has agreed that the village must militarize and prepare its militia against the rising threat of the Temple of Elemental Evil [ch 017 / entry]. He is present at a secret meeting with the party and other council members where the threat is acknowledged [ch 017 / 017.06].

## Relationships
*   **Rufus:** Burne’s partner and co-leader of Hommlet. They are observed to be a couple [ch 002 / 002.03]. They act in concert regarding the party's deputization and public statements [ch 004 / 004.02; ch 008 / 008.03]. Rufus dismisses the party on behalf of both of them [ch 002 / moment].
*   **Terjon:** The High Cleric of St. Cuthbert. Terjon believes Burne and Rufus have failed to protect Hommlet and wants them fired [ch 002 / 002.05].
*   **Jaroo Ashstaff:** The Druid. Burne sits on the village council alongside Jaroo [ch 004 / 004.04].
*   **The Party:** Burne deputized the party to investigate threats [ch 004 / entry]. He appreciates their ability to conduct deniable operations [ch 008 / 008.03].
