## Identity
Elder Kenter (also referred to as The Village Elder or Village Elder) is a retired farmer who heads Hommlet’s ruling council [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.04; ch 017 / entry]. He lives in a fortified estate protected by an eight-foot-high stone wall and heavy gate, which he shares with his wife, four grown sons, two daughters-in-law, three grandchildren, two servant girls, and a hired farmhand [ch 004 / 004.04]. His sons and the hired hand serve in the local militia, and the entire household follows the Old Faith [ch 004 / 004.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Kenter is portrayed as wise and concerned with the security and proper governance of Hommlet [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / moment]. He places a high value on formal procedure, insisting that intelligence regarding threats be presented to the full council rather than individual members to prevent secrets and ensure appropriate action [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 004 / moment]. He is pragmatic and hospitable, offering a free meal to the party once their agreement to his terms was secured [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 004 / entry]. He is aligned with the Druid Jaroo and holds positive reports of the party’s character, though he remains vigilant regarding village safety [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.04].

## History with the Party
Kenter established a formal reporting procedure with the party, requiring that all future intelligence about the Moathouse be presented to the complete council at once [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 004 / moment]. He hosted the party for a meal after they agreed to these terms [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.04]. The party was directed to present evidence to the elders, including Kenter, after consulting with Jaroo [ch 005 / 005.04]. Kenter attended a secret meeting in the Inn of the Welcome Wench's back room with Burne, Rufus, Jaroo, and Ostler, where the party provided indisputable evidence of the Temple of Elemental Evil's resurgence [ch 017 / 017.06]. During this meeting, the council expressed deep concern and agreed to keep the information secret while preparing the local militia and accelerating castle construction [ch 017 / 017.06].
