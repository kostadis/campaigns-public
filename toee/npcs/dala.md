## Identity
Dala is a serving wench and table cleaner. She works at the Boatman's Tavern in Chapter 018 [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.05] and later appears working at the Waterside Hostel in Chapters 019, 024 [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.09; ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.04; ch 024 / 024.09].

## Personality and Motivations
The evidence contains no direct interactions or actions by Dala, preventing an assessment of her personality, motivations, or agency.

## History with the Party
- In Chapter 018, Dala was one of the serving wenches (along with Pearl and Ketta) present at the Boatman's Tavern when the party approached them to inquire about a woman named Serena. The party observed her, but another wench spoke on the group's behalf, citing fear of a pirate captain and revealing Serena had been taken to the Iron Lash brothel [ch 018 / 018.05].
- In Chapter 019, Wat, the bartender at the Waterside Hostel, directly asked the party if their business with the boss was about Dala. Zephyr explicitly denied this ("No, no"), relieving Wat [ch 019 / 019.09].
- In Chapter 024, the party saw Dala cleaning tables upon their arrival at the Waterside Hostel, and again later when preparing to leave with the Broken Blades. She made no contact with the party during these visits [ch 024 / 024.04; ch 024 / 024.09].

## Last Observed State
The last explicit visual observation of Dala occurred in Chapter 024, where she was cleaning tables at the Waterside Hostel [ch 024 / 024.09]. She is mentioned again in Chapter 025, but only as part of a prisoner's statement [ch 025 / 025.03; ch 025 / moment]. Status not established in the summaries regarding her whereabouts or condition after Chapter 024.

## Relationships
- **Dick Rentsch / His Men:** Jorin Pike Kale was imprisoned by Dick Rentsch and his men specifically as punishment for making a pass at Dala [ch 025 / 025.03; ch 025 / moment].

- **Jorin Pike Kale:** Jorin is a river sailor held captive in the Earth Temple. He stated he was imprisoned for making a pass at Dala [ch 025 / 025.03; ch 025 / moment].
