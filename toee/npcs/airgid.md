Airgid is a baby silver dragon [ch 000 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Airgid was found in a ravenous state while chained to a statue [ch 000 / entry; ch 000 / 000.06]. She is described as playful and intensely affectionate [ch 004 / entry]. Upon seeing the Lortmil Mountains, she expressed extreme joy and excitement to return home [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / moment].

## History with the Party
- The party found Airgid, a captive baby silver dragon, chained to a statue in a defaced chapel where she was ravenous [ch 000 / entry; ch 000 / 000.06].
- Airgid stated her mother was in the Lortmil Mountains [ch 000 / entry; ch 000 / 000.06].
