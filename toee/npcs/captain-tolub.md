## Identity
**Name:** Captain Tolub
**Role:** Captain of the ship *Iron Dagger*, leader of a group of river pirates, and a dominant figure in Nulb's criminal underworld [ch 018 / 018.05; ch 019 / 019.06; ch 025 / 025.05].
**Status:** Deceased. His severed head was presented to the party in Chapter 040 after Madame Selentis arranged his removal, fulfilling the party's request that he be "gone" [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Tolub is a boisterous, intimidating, and arrogant figure who demands respect and enjoys mocking those he perceives as weaker [ch 018 / entry]. He prioritizes drinking and social dominance over business, often forcing others to buy drinks for his crew or insisting on introductions before negotiations [ch 018 / 018.05]. He is deceptive and treacherous, willing to betray allies or send targets to their deaths to further his own alignment or save face [ch 018 / 018.10; ch 019 / entry]. When confronted, he attempts to save face through lies and false boasts rather than admitting defeat [ch 039 / 039.04]. He is described by others as the "sketchiest" person in Nulb [ch 019 / 019.06].

## History with the Party
*   **Initial Contact (Ch 18):** Tolub operated out of the Boatman's Tavern, demanding the party buy drinks before sharing information [ch 018 / 018.05]. He revealed that Serena was held at the Iron Lash brothel and later directed the party to the "Hall of the Scarlet Moon," claiming it was a path to the Earth Temple [ch 018 / 018.05; ch 018 / 018.08].
*   **Betrayal (Ch 18):** The party discovered Tolub had sent them to a Fire Temple encampment where they were ambushed by fire clerics, revealing his alignment with the Temple of Fire [ch 018 / 018.10; ch 019 / entry].
*   **Presumed Dead (Ch 19-25):** After the party survived, Tolub spread rumors that they were "meddlers" he had gotten rid of, claiming they didn't belong [ch 019 / 019.06]. He was confirmed to dominate criminal elements in Nulb, though Skole warned he was dangerous [ch 019 / 019.06]. Jorin Pike Kale provided information on Tolub's ship schedules [ch 025 / 025.05].
*   **Reappearance of Lies (Ch 38-39):** While the party was away, Tolub continued to tell people in Nulb that the party was dead, forcing townsfolk to act as if they didn't recognize the party members [ch 038 / 038.06; ch 039 / 039.02].
*   **Confrontation (Ch 39):** The party confronted Tolub at the Boatman's Tavern. Intimidated by Zephyr, Tolub offered a single free supply run to keep the party "dead" to save face, though he continued to boast and call them "fakes" and "clowns" after they left [ch 039 / 039.04]. Sequoia stole Tolub’s dispatch case, revealing his secret alignment with the Water Temple (reporting to Belsornig, then Brother Eelrich) and that the fire temple ambush had been a distraction [ch 039 / 039.04; ch 039 / 039.05].
*   **Death (Ch 40):** The party requested Madame Selentis make Tolub "gone," which she did. His severed head was delivered to the party’s quarters by Barkinar as a confirmation of order [ch 039 / 039.06; ch 040 / 040.02].

## Last Observed State
Dead. His severed head was presented on a plate to the party in their temple quarters in Chapter 040 [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.02].

## Relationships
*   **Barkinar:** Tolub had previously mocked Barkinar’s attempt to recruit him into the Earth Temple and boasted about the supremacy of Water. Barkinar expressed no regret at Tolub’s death [ch 040 / 040.02; ch 040 / moment].
*   **Madame Selentis:** Tolub was an agent who controlled supply lines in Nulb. Selentis eventually had him removed at the party's behest [ch 039 / 039.05; ch 039 / 039.06].
*   **Skole:** The bartender of the Boatman's Tavern. Skole warned the party that Tolub was "seriously bad business" and dominated Nulb's criminal elements. Skole was disappointed the party didn't remove Tolub sooner in Chapter 39 [ch 019 / 019.06; ch 039 / 039.04].
*   **Grud:** Tolub’s second-in-command. Grud was present during the confrontation in Chapter 39 and ground his teeth when the party accepted Tolub’s offer [ch 039 / 039.04].
*   **Brother Eelrich:** Tolub reported to Belsornig (now dead) and was aligned with Brother Eelrich, the head of the Water Temple [ch 039 / 039.05].
