## Identity
Grindin Well-Read is a gnome who operates as a bookseller. He maintains an establishment known as "Grindin Books," distinguished by a studded oak door and a sign reading “Welcome to the Grindin Books.” [ch 009 / 009.02] The interior features a cluttered display of books and maps. [ch 009 / 009.02]

## Personality and Motivations
Grindin was perceived by Zinnia as being annoyed at the party's intrusion into his shop. [ch 009 / 009.02] He is currently motivated by personal grievance regarding a stolen item, specifically complaining loudly about the theft of his conference invitation. [ch 009 / 009.02] He is helpful but perhaps overly enthusiastic or misdirected in his offers of assistance, suggesting enchanted wards and protective charms for caravans. [ch 009 / 009.02]

## History with the Party
- Zephyr and Zinnia visited Grindin Books and questioned Grindin Well-Read regarding local bandits. [ch 009 / 009.02]
- During this interaction, Grindin confirmed that Pasmarie Clee possesses information on the bandits. [ch 009 / 009.02]
- Grindin suggested that the party (or their caravans) use wards and protective charms for safety. [ch 009 / 009.02]
- Zinnia reacted with disdain to his suggestions, labeling him an "ignorant fool" internally or verbally, though Grindin remained engaged in the conversation. [ch 009 / 009.02]

## Last Observed State
