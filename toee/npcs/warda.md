## Identity
Warda is a gnome nationalist who opposes Verbobonc's territorial expansion. She identifies herself as fighting "for the free gnomes." [ch 014 / entry] She was held as a prisoner alongside Jerry, a soldier of Verbobonc. [ch 014 / 014.04]

## Personality and Motivations
Warda holds strong political convictions, viewing Jerry as "imperialist scum" and the cultists as "a symptom of Verbobonc imperialism." [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.04] She prioritizes her cause over personal safety, scorning Jerry's initial desire to flee and notify authorities as cowardly, instead joining the party to fight the cultists directly. [ch 014 / entry] Despite her hatred for Verbobonc imperialism, she accepts a truce with Jerry, defining their relationship as "allies of convenience." [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.04] In later interactions, she displays caution, arguing that the only safe option is to leave the temple rather than explore further. [ch 015 / entry; ch 015 / 015.02]

## History with the Party
*   Warda was discovered imprisoned with Jerry in a cell block containing a torture rack. [ch 014 / 014.04]
*   She engaged in a political argument with Jerry regarding Verbobonc's expansionism while the party attempted to secure their cooperation. [ch 014 / 014.04]
*   When Jerry expressed a desire to leave and alert authorities, Warda criticized his cowardice ("typical, typical, typical") and declared she would join the party to fight the cultists. [ch 014 / 014.04]
*   The party armed Warda and Jerry with scimitars taken from dead cultists. [ch 014 / 014.04]
*   Warda accompanied the party into the ritual chamber and fought alongside them against High Cultist Tharok. [ch 014 / 014.05]
*   During the first phase of the battle, Warda attacked Tharok, dealing 12 points of damage in one instance and approximately 5 points in another. She declared her actions were for the free gnomes. [ch 014 / 014.05]
*   Warda was present when Tharok attacked Zephyr, missing Warda with a subsequent attack. [ch 014 / 014.05]
*   Her follow-up attacks in the first session missed. [ch 014 / 014.05]
*   In the second session, Warda shouted a battle cry and attacked Tharok, missing twice with a longsword and then hitting with a shortsword for six points of damage. [ch 015 / 015.01]
*   Tharok struck Warda for 29 points of damage, though his second and third attacks missed her. [ch 015 / 015.01]
*   The party sustained injuries during the confrontation, with Warda noted as having sustained heavy injuries from Tharok. [ch 015 / entry]
*   After the ritual cavern began to collapse, Warda urged the party to flee, stating, "The only safe place is the exit." [ch 015 / 015.02]
*   The party followed her advice and exited the abbey, finding survivors in the library. [ch 015 / 015.02]
