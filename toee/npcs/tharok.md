## Identity
Tharok is identified as the High Templar [ch 013 / entry] or High Cultist of the Earth Temple [ch 014 / entry; ch 015 / entry]. He possesses an imposing and statuesque physique, with skin mottled like cracked stone and eyes that burn with fervent intensity [ch 014 / entry]. He was the leader of the Earth Temple ritual [ch 014 / entry] which aimed to summon a Titan [ch 013 / entry] by using the Chest of Rao as a magical battery [ch 014 / entry; ch 015 / entry]. He possesses devastating combat abilities, including AC 16, the ability to fling targets 10 feet, reaction strikes via "elemental claw," and earth magic that causes quakes [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Tharok is characterized by fervent intensity and a belief that his ritual is sacred [ch 014 / entry]. He views intruders as defilers of his rite and expresses rage through threats that "the earth itself will swallow you for your insolence" [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.05]. He displays arrogance, dismissing significant damage as "but a scratch" [ch 014 / 014.05] and taunting opponents with metaphors about stone and time [ch 014 / entry]. He also attempted to recruit Zephyr, claiming he was "one of the demon spawn" and belonged on Tharok's side [ch 015 / entry].

## History with the Party
- Tharok was first mentioned by a captive monk as the orchestrator of the Titan-summoning ritual [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.02].
- Cultists in the monastery were overheard wishing to see Tharok's ritual unfold, indicating his prominence within the Earth Cult [ch 014 / 014.03].
- The party entered the ritual chamber where Tharok was performing the ritual with the Chest of Rao and three kneeling cultists [ch 014 / 014.05].
- Tharok declared the party defilers and engaged them in combat, killing the NPC Jerry with three attacks totaling 44 damage [ch 014 / 014.05].
- The party killed Tharok's three cultist guards before engaging him directly [ch 014 / 014.05].
- Sequoia used a Religion check to determine that knocking the Chest of Rao off the altar would disrupt the ritual [ch 014 / 014.05].
- Zephyr dealt significant damage to Tharok, leading Tharok to target him specifically; Tharok knocked Zephyr back 10 feet [ch 014 / 014.05].
- Tharok remained heavily damaged at the end of Chapter 14 [ch 014 / 014.05].
- In Chapter 15, Warda attacked Tharok, who retaliated by striking her for 29 damage [ch 015 / 015.01; ch 015 / moment].
- Tharok attempted to recruit Zephyr, claiming Zephyr was "demon spawn," but Zephyr rejected him with "I picked this side" [ch 015 / 015.01; ch 015 / moment].
- Tharok ranted about his capabilities, prompting Sequoia to mock him as "incredibly constipated" [ch 015 / 015.01; ch 015 / moment].
- Calmer cast *Guiding Bolt* on Tharok, granting Zinnia advantage [ch 015 / 015.01].
- Zinnia delivered the finishing blows, killing Tharok [ch 015 / 015.01].
- Brother Eldin later confirmed Tharok's death and asked if the Abbey was safe [ch 015 / 015.02].
- The party taunted Lareth by boasting of killing Tharok [ch 016 / 016.06].

## Last Observed State
Tharok was slain by Zinnia during the battle in the ritual cavern in Chapter 15 [ch 015 / 015.01; ch 015 / entry]. His death was confirmed to Brother Eldin [ch 015 / 015.02] and mentioned to Lareth [ch 016 / 016.06].
