## Identity
Rufus is a strong, experienced, tough-as-nails fighter who serves as the locally appointed military magistrate and shares command of the guard tower in Hommlet [ch 002 / entry]. He is also identified as the captain of the guard/militia and a member of the village council [ch 005 / entry; ch 008 / entry; ch 016 / entry]. He reports to Verbobonc and works alongside the magician Burne [ch 008 / entry; ch 016 / entry]. In some instances, he is depicted wearing flowing magical robes that expose a conspicuous amount of chest hair [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Rufus is deeply concerned with his reputation and authority, fearing that the resurfacing of the Temple of Elemental Evil would make him look incompetent [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.03]. He prioritizes controlling the flow of information, demanding that evidence be brought to him and "nobody else" to avoid accusations of incompetence behind his back [ch 002 / 002.03; ch 002 / moment]. He is skeptical of claims regarding the temple's return but quickly deputizes the party to investigate rather than investigating himself [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.03]. Rufus appears to view the defense of the region and the construction of the new castle as secondary to maintaining a comfortable life, though he acknowledges the need to accelerate construction when the threat becomes undeniable [ch 002 / 002.03; ch 017 / 017.06]. He suspects sabotage regarding the keep's blueprints and engages the party in covert investigations [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.02].

- The party visits the guard tower, where Rufus questions their claim that the Temple has reappeared and notes it would make him look foolish [ch 002 / 002.03; ch 002 / moment].
- Rufus deputizes the party to investigate the Moathouse and orders them to report proof directly to him alone [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.03; ch 002 / moment].
- Rufus asks the party to keep Jaroo out of the investigation, prompting the party to falsely deny knowing the druid [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.03; ch 002 / moment].
- Rufus dismisses the party so he and Burne can discuss "important material to discuss and activities to pursue" [ch 002 / 002.03; ch 002 / moment].
- High Cleric Terjon notes that Rufus and Burne have failed to do what is necessary to protect Hommlet and suspects them of shady activities since Canoness Y’dey’s disappearance [ch 002 / 002.05].
- After the party returns from the Moathouse, Rufus and Burne meet them at the Inn of the Welcome Wench [ch 004 / 004.01; ch 004 / 004.02].
- Rufus reveals that the keep’s blueprints were copied by someone, leading him to suspect a saboteur in the construction encampment [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.02; ch 004 / moment].
- Rufus asks the party to identify the spy and stresses that the eye rock alone does not prove cult activity [ch 004 / 004.02].
- At a meeting with elders, Rufus states there is no conclusive evidence of the Temple's re-emergence despite the party's findings [ch 005 / 005.04].
- The party decides not to tell Burne and Rufus who stole the maps (the Duke), suspecting they might be involved in the conspiracy [ch 006 / 006.02].

- Rufus and Burne question whether the Moathouse inhabitants were genuine cultists or "posers" [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.03].
- The party trusts Rufus and Burne enough to disclose that they met Lareth, who applauded their deception of the brigands [ch 008 / 008.03].
- The party discusses factions interested in the Chest of Rao, noting Rufus's interest [ch 015 / 015.03].
- Rufus questions the party about the Chest of Rao; they maintain a cover story that merchants took it, which matches Elmo's report, causing Rufus to visibly relax [ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / 016.04].
- Rufus notes that Elmo has given the party the urgent task of dealing with the Moathouse [ch 016 / 016.04].
- In a secret council meeting, Rufus calls the confirmed return of the Temple "very disturbing information" [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.06].

## Last Observed State
Rufus attended a secret meeting with the village council in Chapter 17, where he expressed deep concern over the confirmed resurgence of the Temple of Elemental Evil [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.06]. He was urged by the council to ensure the castle is built quickly to prepare for potential conflict [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.06]. Status not established in the summaries after this point.

## Relationships
- **Burne**: Rufus shares command of the guard tower with Burne and they are partners in both leadership and life (and romantic partners) [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.03]. They are described as exchanging meaningful looks and acting as a unified front [ch 004 / 004.02; ch 008 / 008.03].
- **Jaroo Ashstaff**: Rufus views Jaroo as a "busybody" and asks the party to exclude him from matters concerning the Temple [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.03].
- **Terjon**: High Cleric Terjon views Rufus and Burne with suspicion, believing they have failed their duties and wishing to see them fired [ch 002 / 002.05]. Terjon demands evidence be brought to him before Rufus, creating competing orders [ch 002 / 002.05].
- **Elmo**: Elmo is revealed to be a spy for Verbobonc, the entity that employs Rufus and Burne; his reports to Rufus influence how Rufus receives the party's cover stories [ch 016 / 016.02; ch 016 / 016.04].
- **Village Council**: Rufus is a member of the ruling council of Hommlet, alongside the Village Elder, Jaroo, Terjon, and others [ch 004 / 004.04].
