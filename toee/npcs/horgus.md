## Identity
Horgus is a mercenary, formerly a farm boy, who served as part of the household guard for the Glimmerfields [ch 025 / entry]. He was one of four men-at-arms employed by the Glimmerfields; the others were Kalen, Brask, and Davine [ch 025 / 025.05].

## Personality and Motivations
The evidence describes Horgus and his peers as "cheap mercenaries" and "not elite soldiers," noting they were farm boys who turned to sword-for-hire work to support the Glimmerfields' protection [ch 025 / 025.05]. No specific personality traits or personal motivations beyond this professional role are recorded.

## History with the Party
- Horgus was captured and imprisoned in a dungeon along with the Glimmerfields' crew [ch 025 / entry].
- The party unlocked the prisoners' chains, freeing Horgus and the rest of the captives [ch 025 / 025.05].
- Horgus did not know how to exit the location; the party guided him and the other freed prisoners to an escape route via a secret passageway found by Zephyr [ch 025 / 025.05].
- Upon exiting, the prisoners, including Horgus, did not alert the guards outside; when Zephyr checked back, no one had come out yet, indicating a successful, quiet escape [ch 025 / 025.05].

## Last Observed State
Status not established in the summaries. Horgus was seen escaping the temple dungeon with the rest of the group in Chapter 025, but his whereabouts or condition after that moment are not recorded [ch 025 / 025.05].
