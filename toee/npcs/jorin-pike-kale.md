## Identity
**Name**: Jorin Pike Kale
**Role**: River sailor [ch 025 / entry]
**Affiliation**: Independent (formerly associated with the river trade) [ch 025 / entry]
**Location of First Appearance**: Nulb, Prison Chamber [ch 025 / 025.03]

## Personality and Motivations
**Temperament**: Confrontational and suspicious [ch 025 / entry]. When first encountered, he demanded to know the party's intentions rather than expressing gratitude or fear [ch 025 / 025.03].
**Motivation**: Survival and avoidance of further trouble. He intends to disappear as far away as he possibly can [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.05].
**Knowledge Base**: Has studied the river closely and possesses detailed knowledge of local shipping schedules, specifically Captain Tolub’s movements [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.05].

## History with the Party
- The party discovered a prison chamber containing Jorin Pike Kale, who had been imprisoned by Dick Rentsch and his men as punishment for making a pass at Dala [ch 025 / 025.03; ch 025 / entry].
- Jorin was confrontational upon seeing the party, asking: "What are you doing here? And why are you talking to us?" [ch 025 / 025.03; ch 025 / entry].
- The party freed Jorin from his chains along with other prisoners, including Master Aldous Glimmerfield, Mistress Lenora Glimmerfield, and several mercenaries [ch 025 / 025.05].
- Jorin thanked the party profusely for his liberation [ch 025 / 025.05].
- In gratitude, Jorin provided the party with information regarding Captain Tolub's pirate ship schedule at the docks, having spent time studying the river [ch 025 / 025.05; ch 025 / moment].
- This information mechanically established the party's knowledge of when Tolub's boat would arrive or leave Nulb [ch 025 / 025.05].
- Jorin expressed his intent to disappear and go as far away as possible [ch 025 / 025.05; ch 025 / entry].

## Last Observed State
**Status**: Escaped the temple and free. [ch 025 / 025.05].
**Location**: Departed with the group of freed prisoners, heading away from Nulb to "disappear" [ch 025 / 025.05; ch 025 / entry].
**Current Condition**: Unharmed physically following escape, though previously described as impoverished and in pain while imprisoned [ch 025 / 025.03; ch 025 / 025.05].
