## Identity
Mari Clee is the cheerful gnome proprietor and barkeeper of the Honey Haven Inn in Sheernobb. She is described as a small figure who hums and dances while working, carrying a large ring of keys jingling at her belt [ch 008 / 008.05]. She serves the inn's exclusive local brew, known as Honey Haven mead, for which the establishment is famous [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Mari is described as cheerful but holds firm, inflexible views on business and manners. She insists on immediate payment, stating plainly, “There is no tab. It's cash up front,” and refuses to extend credit for food [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.05; ch 008 / moment]. She is socially observant and quick to correct rude behavior, such as when she chides Zephyr for ordering without asking for a menu [ch 008 / moment]. She acknowledges the utility of leadership within a group, remarking that “it never hurts to have a leader in charge” when Zephyr declared himself so [ch 008 / 008.05].

## History with the Party
- The party first arrived at the Honey Haven Inn to secure lodging and gather information; Mari greeted them, pulled a hedgehog from her mug, and offered them common-room bunks for five copper or private rooms for eight silver [ch 008 / 008.05; ch 008 / moment].
- Mari offered bathing and laundry services, noting that the party looked as though it could use a bath [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.05].
- When Zephyr condescendingly ordered for the group by claiming to be the leader and ordering "porridge, something poor" for his "peasants," Mari corrected him for failing to ask the proprietor for a menu [ch 008 / 008.05; ch 008 / moment].
- Mari refused to put the party's food on a tab, enforcing the "cash up front" policy until Zephyr paid for everyone [ch 008 / 008.05; ch 008 / moment].
- Zinnia ordered Honey Haven mead, which Mari described as an exclusive local brew [ch 008 / 008.05].
- The party incurred an expensive bill at the inn; the evidence describes Zephyr's pocket as having been "cleanly picked" by Mari Clee as he left to explore the village [ch 009 / 009.01].
