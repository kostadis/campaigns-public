## Identity
Jerry is a soldier from Verbobonc who was captured by earth cultists while traveling through the mountains [ch 014 / entry]. He is portrayed by the DM as an imperialist officer who believes in a bigger cause, contrasting with the local nationalism of other prisoners [ch 014 / 014.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Jerry initially prioritizes self-preservation and official protocol, wishing to flee to notify authorities rather than engage the cultists directly [ch 014 / 014.04]. He is suspicious of unknown parties and questions their allegiances immediately upon liberation [ch 014 / 014.04; ch 014 / entry]. He firmly denies that Verbobonc's expansion is imperialism, framing it instead as the creation of "a but appropriate buffer state" [ch 014 / entry]. His motivation shifts toward active combat participation after being appealed to by Zephyr and Calmer, invoking his duty to St. Cuthbert and Verbobonc [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.04].

## History with the Party
- Jerry was discovered in a prison cell alongside Warda, a gnome nationalist, after Sequoia unlocked the door [ch 014 / 014.04].
- Upon meeting the party, Jerry immediately questioned their allegiance, asking, "But who do you work for?" [ch 014 / 014.04; ch 014 / entry].
- He engaged in a heated political argument with Warda regarding Verbobonc's territorial policies, dismissing her concerns as "petty politics" while under threat of torture [ch 014 / 014.04].
- Jerry initially advocated for fleeing the dungeon to report the cult threat to Verbobonc authorities, rather than fighting [ch 014 / 014.04].
- He was persuaded to assist the party after Calmer argued that they needed to fight for "the greater good" rather than just for Verbobonc, leading Jerry to roll a 23 on a persuasion check [ch 014 / 014.04].
- Jerry revealed the existence of a secret passageway to the deeper temple of Beory, knowledge he possessed from his grandmother, a former believer in Beory [ch 014 / 014.04; ch 014 / entry].
- The party armed Jerry with scimitars taken from dead cultists to prepare him for the upcoming battle [ch 014 / 014.04].
- During the battle with High Cultist Tharok, Jerry charged forward and was killed by three attacks dealing a total of 44 damage [ch 014 / 014.05; ch 014 / entry].

## Last Observed State
Jerry is dead. He died during the battle with High Cultist Tharok after being struck by three devastating attacks dealing 44 damage [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.05].

## Relationships
- **Warda**: A gnome nationalist also held prisoner with Jerry. They share a relationship characterized by mutual disdain and political opposition, though they agreed to work together for survival. Warda described them as "allies of convenience" [ch 014 / 014.04].
- **Zephyr**: A member of the party who directly asked Jerry why they were imprisoned and later used the appeal to duty to persuade him to stay and fight [ch 014 / 014.04].
- **Calmer**: The party member whose speech regarding the "greater good" successfully convinced Jerry to abandon his desire to flee and join the fight [ch 014 / 014.04].
- **Sequoia**: The party member who unlocked the prison cells and later opened the secret door revealed by Jerry [ch 014 / 014.04].
