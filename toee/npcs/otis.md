## Identity
Otis is a tall, powerful blacksmith in Nulb. While he fits the physical description of a craftsman, Zinnia’s Insight check identified him as looking not 'Just the Smith' and capable in a fight [ch 039 / 039.08]. He is secretly an agent of the forces of good who has been spying on the Temple of Elemental Evil for years [ch 039 / entry]. He serves as a subordinate to Mother Screng, whom he identifies as his superior ("She is in charge") [ch 039 / 039.08].

## Personality and Motivations
Otis is discreet and cautious, initially asking only "who's asking?" when approached about the scarab, leaving it unclear if he was testing their identity or their allegiance [ch 039 / 039.08]. He expresses deep gratitude toward the party, noting they have accomplished more in a short tenure than he has managed in years [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.08]. He is skeptical of certain intelligence, specifically doubting that Prince Thrommel could have remained imprisoned for a decade after his guard failed, but he remains pragmatic and willing to investigate evidence provided by his superior [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.01]. He is dedicated to the cause against the temple, willing to provide resources like safe houses and transport to support the party’s efforts [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.01].

## History with the Party
*   Mother Screng directed the party to visit Otis the blacksmith for information about the mysterious scarab they had acquired [ch 039 / 039.08].
*   The party revealed their true intention to root out the Temple of Elemental Evil, prompting Otis to reveal his identity as a long-time spy for the forces of good [ch 039 / 039.08].
*   Otis identified the party's scarab as a Greater Temple Command Key, though he did not know its specific function or target, and advised them to hold onto it [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.08].
*   Otis provided the party with a note to present to Mother Screng, linking them to the local resistance leadership [ch 039 / 039.08].
*   Otis expressed skepticism regarding the theory that Prince Thrommel had been held prisoner for ten years, though he agreed to help investigate if the party could bring the prince to Nulb [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.01].
*   The party agreed to bring Thrommel to Otis, who promised to provide a safe house and discreet transport once the prince was in Nulb [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.01].

## Last Observed State
Otis was active in Nulb in Chapter 040, coordinating with Mother Screng and the party regarding the rescue of Prince Thrommel [ch 040 / 040.01]. He had established himself as a logistical support point, offering a safe house and transport for the rescued prince [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.01]. Status not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
*   **Mother Screng:** Otis identifies her as his superior and works alongside her in the resistance against the temple [ch 039 / 039.08; ch 040 / entry].
*   **The Party:** Viewed as effective allies who have achieved more than he could in years; he trusts them enough to share his secret and entrust them with the Prince rescue operation [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.08; ch 040 / 040.01].
*   **Prince Thrommel:** Skeptical of his capture and long-term imprisonment, but acknowledges the evidence warrants investigation [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / moment].
