## Identity

Commander Joradin is an Earth Temple commander recruited by Dick Rentsch. By chapter 043, he is a lieutenant reporting to Dren [ch 022 / entry; ch 043 / 043.02].

## Personality and Motivations

Joradin strongly asserts his authority over his assigned territory, refusing the party access despite their claim to freedom of movement. Romag describes him as a recent recruit who does not yet understand temple practices [ch 022 / 022.09; ch 022 / 022.10].

## History with the Party
*   The party encountered guards who identified themselves as Earth Temple recruits; Commander Joradin arrived and asked for their identities [ch 022 / 022.09].
*   Joradin acknowledged the party by name ("Sequoia, Zephyr, Zinnia") but asserted his command, denying them permission to explore the area and ordering them to return to their quarters [ch 022 / 022.09].
*   The party questioned their right to free roam, but Joradin reiterated that while they have free roam generally, this specific area is his to guard and they are not permitted to enter [ch 022 / 022.09].
*   Zephyr stated he would ask Romag about Joradin and his protected area before leading the party away [ch 022 / 022.09].
*   The party reported Joradin's actions to Romag; Romag dismissed Joradin's authority over the party, describing him as a new follower who does not understand all temple protocols [ch 022 / entry; ch 022 / 022.10].
- Joradin arrives with Dren and the Earth Guards to bolster the party’s forces, joining the force securing the doorway while the party visits the library [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.02].

## Last Observed State

Joradin is reporting to Dren and helping secure the doorway during the party’s library visit [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.02].

## Relationships

- **Dren:** Joradin reports to him as a lieutenant and accompanies the troops he brings to secure the doorway [ch 043 / 043.02].
- **Dick Rentsch:** Recruited Joradin during a recruitment drive [ch 022 / 022.10].
- **Romag:** Dismisses Joradin’s restrictions on the party and grants them written permission to explore freely [ch 022 / entry; ch 022 / 022.10].
- **The party:** Initially restricts their exploration; later joins the force securing their doorway [ch 022 / 022.09; ch 043 / entry].
