## Identity
Senshock is a Lord Wizard of the Greater Temple. [ch 031 / entry] He holds a position within the temple hierarchy beneath Barkinar. [ch 031 / entry; ch 032 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Barkinar describes Senshock as ambitious and paranoid. [ch 031 / entry; ch 032 / entry] Barkinar also describes him as "useful" and "paranoid, but useful." [ch 031 / entry; ch 032 / entry]

## History with the Party
*   The party was summoned to the Greater Temple following the collapse of the elemental prophets and was warned by Barkinar to be civil toward Senshock and let him underestimate them. [ch 031 / 031.06]
*   Barkinar explicitly warned the party that Senshock is "ambitious and paranoid" and "useful," instructing them to manage him carefully. [ch 031 / 031.06; ch 032 / entry]
*   During a recap of the events leading to their promotion, Barkinar again named Senshock in the context of administrative warnings to the new leadership. [ch 032 / 032.01]
*   Falrinth warned the party that Senshock is one of the "holy gentlemen who would like me dead" and possesses the Orb of Golden Death, which he refuses to surrender to Senshock. [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / 038.05]
*   Falrinth warned Calmer that Senshock would recognize Calmer's scarab (bearing the letters T-Z-G-Y) on sight and could use it against him. [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / 038.05]
