## Identity
Lenora Glimmerfield (also referred to as Mistress Lenora Glimmerfield) is a textile merchant from Dyvers [ch 025 / entry]. She was one of the captives held in the prison chamber of the Otyughs' Maw [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Evidence regarding her personality or specific personal motivations beyond survival and payment is not established in the summaries. She is associated with the Glimmerfield household, which included protection staff [ch 025 / 025.05].

## History with the Party
- The party freed Lenora from her chains in the prison chamber [ch 025 / 025.05].
- Lenora was freed alongside Master Aldous Glimmerfield, Jorin Pike Kale, and their household staff/mercenaries [ch 025 / 025.05].
- Lenora and Master Aldous Glimmerfield promised the party a reward of 100 silver pieces for their assistance [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.05].
