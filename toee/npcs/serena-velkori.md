## Identity
Serena Velkori is the daughter of Eryk Velkori, a leader of the Rhennee [ch 018 / 018.02; ch 019 / entry]. She is a member of the Rhennee clan who was held captive at the Iron Lash brothel as payment for a debt incurred by her people [ch 018 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Serena is described as defiant despite her captivity [ch 018 / entry]. She possesses a sharp tongue, directing threats toward her captors [ch 018 / 018.06]. She is proud, insisting she could have escaped without the party's financial assistance [ch 018 / 018.07]. She is vigilant, keeping watch on her surroundings and providing intelligence to the party regarding local dangers [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.03].

## History with the Party
- The party accepted a plea from her father, Eryk, to find her after she vanished from the Boatman's Tavern [ch 018 / 018.02].
- Vethra, the Rhennee matron, revealed through divination that Serena had been taken, drugged, bound, and traded [ch 018 / 018.04].
- Captain Tolub informed the party that Serena was being held at the Iron Lash brothel as payment for a debt [ch 018 / 018.05].
- The party paid 500 gold pieces to the brothel proprietor, Elvanna, to secure her release [ch 018 / 018.06].
- Serena was brought out of the brothel's pits to the party, appearing dazed but uncuffed [ch 018 / 018.06].
- The party escorted Serena back to the Rhennee camp, where her return sparked a celebration [ch 018 / 018.07].
- Serena explained to the party that she had been used as leverage for a debt she did not know about, forcing her father into ambushes [ch 018 / 018.07].
- Serena asserted that she did not need the party's money to escape and would have found a way out herself [ch 018 / 018.07].
- In Chapter 39, Serena arrived at the camp to report that the Rhennee caravan had been ordered to report to the mayor's house at dusk [ch 039 / 039.06].
- Serena confirmed to the party that the upper floor of the Boatman's Tavern remains dangerous [ch 039 / 039.03].

## Last Observed State
In Chapter 39, Serena was with the Rhennee caravan, delivering news that it had been commandeered by Madame Selentis for transport to the mayor's house [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.06]. The text notes she was present when Madame Selentis took the caravan [ch 039 / 039.06]. Status not established in the summaries beyond her presence with the caravan.

## Relationships
- **Eryk Velkori:** Her father and clan leader; he described her as his "firelight" and paid a ransom for her [ch 018 / 018.02; ch 018 / 018.06; ch 018 / 018.07].
- **Vethra Velkori:** Her grandmother (implied by "my granddaughter" in Vethra's dialogue); Vethra performed a divination specifically to locate her [ch 018 / 018.04].
- **Elvanna:** The proprietor of the Iron Lash who held Serena as "compensation for damages" and demanded 500 gold pieces [ch 018 / 018.06].
- **Madame Selentis:** An antagonist who coerced the Rhennee caravan into a transport job and spoke with Serena during the takeover [ch 039 / 039.06].
