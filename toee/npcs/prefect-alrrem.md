## Identity
Prefect Alrrem served as the Prophet of Fire and leader of the Fire Temple within the Temple of Elemental Evil complex [ch 027 / entry; ch 032 / entry]. He was one of the four regional leaders of the temple, alongside Romag, Hartsch, and Belsornig [ch 031 / moment; ch 032 / 032.01]. His tenure as prefect was defined by the trauma of the Battle of Emridy Meadows a decade prior, during which he led the temples to defeat [ch 027 / 027.05]. He was recognized by the sentient sword Frostbrand, which had been imprisoned since that battle, indicating Alrrem’s significance to the history of the site [ch 033 / 033.03].

## Personality and Motivations
Alrrem’s rule was haunted by the defeat at the Battle of Emridy Meadows [ch 027 / 027.05]. He displayed financial recklessness, likely bankrupting himself to fund high-cost assets such as a chimera used in an assassination attempt against the party [ch 027 / 027.05; ch 027 / 027.03]. He engaged in mercenary tactics, hiring werewolves to attack the party and attempting to buy the loyalty of the troll Oohlgrist during the temple war [ch 021 / 021.04; ch 036 / 036.02]. These overtures failed; the trolls betrayed him after accepting offers of power, choosing instead to scavenge the battlefield [ch 036 / 036.02; ch 036 / moment].

He was susceptible to manipulation through closed-door negotiations, reacting with immediate rage to news of betrayal by ordering his troops to attack the Water Temple [ch 028 / 028.07]. He displayed skepticism toward new allies, demanding proof of loyalty during negotiations behind a sealed door [ch 028 / 028.07]. His strategic judgment was questionable; he failed to act on a warning from Captain Varek Solain regarding a necromancer threat, a lapse Solain described with dismissive resignation [ch 038 / 038.02]. He attempted desperate, high-risk magical solutions to maintain power, culminating in a failed attempt to summon a fire elemental [ch 032 / entry].

## Last Observed State
Alrrem is deceased. He died alongside his only remaining apprentice while attempting to summon a fire elemental [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.01]. The summoned elemental killed them both [ch 032 / 032.01]. His death occurred during a confrontation with the party, who chose to let him die on his pyre rather than intervene [ch 031 / 031.02]. Barkinar subsequently listed Alrrem among the "problems gone by sundown" attributed to the party’s actions [ch 031 / moment; ch 032 / moment]. His death created a power vacuum in the Fire Temple, granting the party a one-week window to plunder the location before the Greater Temple installed new leadership [ch 032 / 032.01; ch 033 / entry].

## Relationships
**Varek Solain (Captain of the Fire Guard):** Solain served under Alrrem and warned him about a necromancer threat, which Alrrem failed to address [ch 038 / 038.02]. Solain noted that Alrrem’s attempt to recruit the troll Oohlgrist was a failure, as the trolls betrayed him after taking his resources [ch 036 / 036.02].

**Maeris Dorn (Fire Cleric):** Dorn expressed disdain for her former master, stating upon her surrender to the party that she "never really liked Alrrem" [ch 033 / 033.05; ch 033 / moment].

**The Party:** The party instigated a three-way war by revealing Belsornig’s betrayal to Alrrem, causing him to attack the Water Temple [ch 028 / moment]. They also weakened his forces by destroying his werewolves and the chimera he financed [ch 027 / 027.04]. The former Fire Temple commander identified the party as the "Obsidian Edge," accusing them of killing Alrrem and destroying his temple [ch 033 / 033.04]. Barkinar credited the party with Alrrem’s elimination [ch 031 / 031.06].

**Frostbrand:** The sentient sword recognized Alrrem, asking for him upon waking from its imprisonment, indicating a prior acquaintance or association from the era of the Battle of Emridy Meadows [ch 033 / 033.03].

**Belsornig (Water Temple Leader):** Alrrem was viewed as a rival by the Water Temple; guards spoke of killing Alrrem to seize his position [ch 020 / 020.09].
