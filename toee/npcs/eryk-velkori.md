## Identity
Eryk Velkori is the leader (captain) of a Rhennee river folk caravan in Nulb. He is characterized as a man of deep emotion, often described in terms of his grief, fury, and protective instinct toward his family. His presence is associated with the Rhennee encampment, which becomes notably somber when his daughter is missing [ch 018 / 018.02; ch 039 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
*   **Protective Patriarch:** Eryk is consumed by grief when his daughter Serena disappears, calling her his "firelight" [ch 018 / 018.02]. Vethra describes her son Eryk as burning with fury [ch 018 / 018.04].
*   **Guarded yet Generous:** He initially withholds detailed information about Nulb's criminal underworld until his daughter's safety is secured, telling the party, "What more I will tell you when you have gotten my daughter?" However, once aid is rendered, he offers the loyalty and protection of his clan [ch 018 / 018.03; ch 018 / 018.07].
*   **Cautious Networker:** Eryk says outsiders underestimate how much the Rhennee notice. He values friendship while warning the party that they should not trust all of their friends [ch 018 / 018.02; ch 018 / 018.07].
*   **Pragmatic Survivor:** By Chapter 39, he is focused on the survival of the clan, accepting coercion by Madame Selentis to protect his people and their wagons, noting that refusal would cost "wheels, animals, or people" [ch 039 / 039.03].

## History with the Party
*   **Meeting the Party:** The party encountered Eryk at the Rhennee encampment in Nulb, where he was grieving his missing daughter, Serena, and drinking rum [ch 018 / 018.02].
*   **The Deal:** Eryk offered the party the loyalty and brotherhood of the Rhennee if they helped find Serena, providing initial leads on criminal factions like the Crimson Reapers and Madame Selentis [ch 018 / 018.02; ch 018 / 018.03].
*   **Divination Context:** After the party engaged Eryk's mother, Vethra, for a divination, Eryk warned them that they had found "the map to hell" and were now in it [ch 018 / 018.04].
*   **Rescue of Serena:** The party successfully rescued Serena from the Iron Lash brothel and returned her to the encampment [ch 018 / 018.07].
*   **Formal Alliance:** Following the rescue, Eryk declared the party "family" and members of the Rhennee clan, granting them protection in Nulb and rewarding them with 500 gold pieces [ch 018 / 018.07].
*   **Information on the Scarlet Moon:** Before the party departed for the Hall of the Scarlet Moon, they consulted Eryk. He confirmed rumors of Elazar teaching a "rite of the wicker giant" and noted that other Rhennee had gone there to sell trinkets, and explicitly said he did not know whether the rite was connected to the Temple of Elemental Evil [ch 018 / 018.09].
*   **Reunification in Chapter 39:** The party reunited with Eryk at the Rhennee camp. He welcomed them warmly, providing food and shelter, and informed them that Madame Selentis had coerced the Rhennee into a mysterious caravan run [ch 039 / 039.03].
*   **Caravan Coercion:** Eryk initially did not know where the coerced caravan was going. Serena reported that it was to go to the mayor's house, and the party hid inside the caravan to reach Madame Selentis [ch 039 / 039.06].
*   **Gratitude:** After the party secured supply arrangements with Selentis, Eryk credited them with saving his daughter and, more importantly to him, his home [ch 039 / 039.07].

## Last Observed State
Eryk was last seen alive at the Rhennee camp in Nulb in Chapter 39, celebrating the party's return after they addressed the coerced caravan run and negotiated supply arrangements with Madame Selentis [ch 039 / 039.07].

## Relationships
*   **Serena (Daughter):** The primary motivation for Eryk's actions in Chapter 18. He loves her deeply, calling her his "firelight," and was coerced by criminals using her as leverage [ch 018 / 018.02; ch 018 / 018.07].
*   **Vethra (Mother):** The Rhennee family matron and truth-teller. She is protective of her family and authoritative over Eryk, silencing him during divinations [ch 018 / 018.04].
*   **Madame Selentis (Antagonist/Coercer):** Selentis coerces Eryk and the Rhennee into labor by threatening their wagons, animals, and people. Eryk views her as a threat to his home [ch 039 / 039.03; ch 039 / 039.06].
*   **Captain Tolub (Criminal Rival):** Tolub is described by Skole as "seriously bad business" and has threatened the party, whom Eryk considers family. Eryk's protection of the party is a counter-force to Tolub's influence in Nulb [ch 018 / 018.07; ch 019 / 019.06].
*   **The Party:** Considered "family" and members of the Rhennee clan. Eryk offers them protection, food, and information, though he remains cautious about their full intentions [ch 018 / 018.07; ch 018 / 018.09; ch 039 / 039.03].
