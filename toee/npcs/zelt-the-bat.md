Zelt the Bat is identified as the boss of the Crimson Reapers [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.03]. The Crimson Reapers are described as a criminal group seeking to dominate Nulb's underworld and funnel supplies through the town [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.03].

## History with the Party
- Zelt the Bat has not yet interacted directly with the party. They were named by Eryk Velkori during the party's investigation into Serena's disappearance [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.02; ch 018 / 018.03].
- Eryk Velkori told the party that Zelt is the boss of the Crimson Reapers, a group aiming to dominate the underworld and funnel supplies through Nulb [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.03].
- Eryk suggested that if his daughter Serena was taken by a criminal element, the Crimson Reapers, under Zelt's command, would likely know of it [ch 018 / 018.03].
- Eryk referenced Zelt's rats, the Reapers, and Nysera's Talons as potential captors of Serena, believing they might have taken her to the Iron Lash [ch 018 / 018.02].
