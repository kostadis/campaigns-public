## Identity
Brother Eelrich Vane (also referred to as Brother Eelrich) [ch 034 / entry]. He is described as a "soft, damp man" wearing cowled green robes with plate mail underneath. His robes feature a gold circle stitched on the front and back, and he carries a "deep stench of water." Water beads are perpetually present at his hairline and cuffs [ch 034 / entry]. He is the last remaining cleric of the Water Temple [ch 034 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Vane is characterized as a "sycophantic opportunist" who engages in shameless flattery to secure his own position [ch 034 / entry]. He is motivated by self-preservation and ambition, specifically angling to secure the top job of Chief Water Cleric [ch 034 / entry]. He is nervous and stumbling, often over-explaining or pitching information as currency to avoid violence [ch 034 / moment]. He displays a pragmatic willingness to defect and betray former allies if it ensures his survival or gain [ch 034 / entry; ch 034 / moment]. He is compliant when threatened but prone to whining about the loss of personal comfort or religious materials when he feels they are owed to him [ch 035 / entry]. He may possess hidden depths of knowledge that he conceals, as ritual texts suggest he knew the true, sentient nature of the temple's water elemental long before the party discovered it [ch 036 / moment; ch 037 / entry].

## History with the Party
*   Vane was discovered by the party when they opened a door to find him standing there, where he began to ingratiate himself immediately [ch 034 / moment].
*   He defected to the party shortly after his discovery, offering information and treasure to prove his worth and ensure his survival [ch 034 / entry; ch 034 / moment].
*   He claimed that Belsornig was dead and stated he "wept the regulation amount" for his former master [ch 034 / moment].
*   He offered the party access to Belsornig's secret treasury (the Fourth Basin) and an unsigned troll contract to secure his employment [ch 034 / entry; ch 034 / moment].
*   He navigated hidden traps in the Fourth Basin to retrieve a chest worth three thousand gold pieces, using it as proof of his loyalty [ch 034 / moment].
*   Vane was made to clear basin rubble personally as a condition for provisional robes and to check for traps; the party plans to keep an eye on him [ch 034 / entry].
*   During the exploration of the Water Temple pool, he noted the area was dangerous but deferred to Brother Calmer for orders [ch 035 / 035.01].
*   He protested the plundering of his bedroom chambers but was overruled by Sequoia [ch 035 / 035.02; ch 035 / moment].
*   Calmer forced Vane to address him as "Lord Master Calmer," establishing a hierarchy where Vane submits to Calmer's authority [ch 035 / 035.02; ch 035 / moment].
*   The party raided his private chambers, acquiring his personal ritual texts, a ceremonial bronze mace, a gold ring with a pearl, and potions of water breathing [ch 035 / 035.02].
*   Vane whined about the texts being his religious materials, but the party noted these texts revealed he knew more about the living water elemental than he admitted [ch 035 / entry; ch 036 / moment].
*   The party explicitly warned Vane that they see through his game, that he is on a "tight leash," and that the next surprise would be meant for him; Vane became frantically compliant [ch 035 / 035.02; ch 035 / moment].
*   The party looted the temple quickly "before Eelrich decides that it's time for him to stop the looting" [ch 036 / entry].
*   Vane was installed at the head of the Water Temple by the party after Belsornig's death [ch 039 / entry].
*   Tolub, a former Water Temple operative, was noted to now report to Vane at best [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.05].
*   Dren questioned Vane regarding a necromancer, resulting in Vane receiving a bruised eye [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.05].
*   Dren offered to kill Vane, but Calmer advised against it to avoid drawing the suspicion of Barkinar [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.05].

*   **Calmer (Brother Calmer / Lord Master Calmer)**: The party leader to whom Vane submits. Calmer asserted dominance over Vane, demanding specific honorifics and managing Vane's compliance [ch 035 / 035.02; ch 035 / moment; ch 036 / entry].
