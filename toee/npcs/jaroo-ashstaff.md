## Identity
Jaroo Ashstaff is an elderly druid of the Old Faith who maintains the grove in Hommlet and sits on the town’s ruling council [ch 002 / entry; ch 004 / entry; ch 017 / 017.06]. He was sent by the druids of the Gnarley Forest to monitor the region following the defeat of the Temple of Elemental Evil [ch 002 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Jaroo is warm and helpful to those he views as part of the "natural order," specifically showing a strong predisposition toward halflings [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.02]. Conversely, he holds an aversion to tieflings, viewing them as unnatural, and disapproves of conversions to the Church of St. Cuthbert [ch 002 / entry; ch 008 / 008.03]. He acts as an experienced intelligence handler, prioritizing compartmentalized information and honesty over embellishment [ch 002 / 002.02; ch 004 / entry]. His worldview favors the isolation of the elven kingdom Celene to protect its forests from "encroaching civilization," while he holds a deep disdain for the imperialist and religious expansionism of the Viscounty of Verbobonc [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.03]. In later interactions, he defines true druidism by calmness, expressing discomfort with the showy and ecstatic practices of the Circle of the Scarlet Moon [ch 018 / 018.09].

## History with the Party
*   In Chapter 002, Jaroo received the party’s report of bandit financing by the Temple of Elemental Evil, offered to send an agent to investigate, and directed the party toward the Moathouse while instructing them to alert Burne and Rufus [ch 002 / 002.02].
*   He reacted negatively to Zephyr’s donation of 100 copper pieces (equivalent to 1 gold), interpreting the denomination as an insult, while praising Sequoia’s two gold donation [ch 002 / 002.02; ch 002 / moment].
*   In Chapter 004, Jaroo confirmed evil was present at the Moathouse but demanded "real evidence" from deeper within the structure rather than accepting the stone eye as proof [ch 004 / 004.03].
*   After the party honestly disclosed whom they had consulted, Jaroo praised their integrity, invoked Beory’s blessing, and gave them a potion of healing [ch 004 / 004.03].
*   In Chapter 005, he advised the party to present their findings to the elders and later retold their evidence at a meeting, framing the threat as brigands masquerading as the temple [ch 005 / entry; ch 005 / 005.04].
*   In Chapter 008, Jaroo provided history on the stolen art chest intended for the elven kingdom of Celene, criticizing Verbobonc’s expansionism and suggesting the party give the chest to the elves on behalf of the druids to bypass the Viscount [ch 008 / 008.03].
*   In Chapter 011, Jaroo reiterated the importance of securing the chest, noting that non-followers of St. Cuthbert might be denied healing magic by Terjon [ch 011 / 011.06].
*   In Chapter 017, during a secret council meeting, Jaroo tasked the party with traveling to Nulb to investigate its connection to the Temple and then infiltrating the temple to disrupt its operations [ch 017 / 017.06].
*   In Chapter 018, Jaroo warned the party about the Circle of the Scarlet Moon, expressing suspicion of their practices as too ecstatic for true druids and hinting at a potentially sinister religious agenda [ch 018 / 018.09].

## Last Observed State
Jaroo was seen consulting with the party in Hommlet regarding the Circle of the Scarlet Moon in Chapter 018 [ch 018 / 018.09]. Status not established in the summaries beyond this point.

## Relationships
*   **Sequoia**: Jaroo is warmly disposed toward Sequoia due to his halfling nature, which Jaroo considers part of the natural order [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.02].
*   **Zephyr**: Jaroo is visibly averse to Zephyr, considering tieflings unnatural, and disliked his donation technique [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.02].
*   **Zinnia**: Jaroo disapproves of Zinnia’s conversion to the Church of St. Cuthbert [ch 008 / 008.03].
*   **Burne and Rufus**: The local officials view Jaroo as a "busybody" [ch 002 / 002.03].
*   **Ostler Gundigoot**: Ostler recognizes Jaroo’s authority, advising the party to consult him to gain his approval and directing them to him for information on druidic matters [ch 004 / 004.02; ch 016 / 016.02].
*   **The Village Elder**: Jaroo is a member of the ruling council and aligns with the Elder’s household, which follows the Old Faith [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 017 / 017.06].
*   **Circle of the Scarlet Moon**: Jaroo expresses discomfort and suspicion regarding this group, believing them to be "too showy, too flashy, too ecstatic" [ch 018 / 018.09].
