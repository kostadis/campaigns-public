## Identity
Jarran is a gnome spy sent by the merchants of Lortmil to investigate brigands [ch 005 / entry]. He is identified as one of the prisoners held captive in an ogre's nest [ch 005 / 005.02].

## Personality and Motivations
His motivation is to investigate brigand activity on behalf of the merchants of Lortmil [ch 005 / entry]. He displays a pragmatic or desperate demeanor by attempting to join the adventuring party upon his rescue [ch 005 / entry].

## History with the Party
The party discovered Jarran trapped behind a barred south door in an ogre's nest alongside other merchants [ch 005 / 005.02]. Jarran was freed from captivity by the party [ch 005 / entry; ch 005 / 005.02]. He offered to join the party, but Zinnia refused his offer [ch 005 / entry; ch 005 / 005.02]. Jarran gave the party a ring before they took everyone outside to the Duke [ch 005 / entry; ch 005 / 005.02].

## Last Observed State
Jarran was freed and taken outside to the Duke [ch 005 / entry; ch 005 / 005.02]. Status not established in the summaries beyond this handover.

## Relationships
He is employed by the merchants of Lortmil [ch 005 / entry]. He was held captive alongside Derrick and Florian, merchants from Narwell [ch 005 / 005.02]. He was rejected by Zinnia [ch 005 / entry; ch 005 / 005.02].
