## Identity
Ostler Gundigoot is the proprietor of the Inn of the Welcome Wench in Hommlet and a member of the village ruling council [ch 002 / entry; ch 004 / entry; ch 017 / entry]. He serves as a primary contact for the party, providing lodging, food, and local intelligence [ch 002 / entry; ch 015 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Ostler is described as cheerful, friendly, and happy [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.01; ch 015 / entry]. He is commercially pragmatic, enforcing payment policies such as requiring three days' payment in advance for adventurers or charging by the fortnight [ch 004 / 004.02; ch 008 / entry]. He is shrewd regarding profit, noting that local authorities like Burne and Rufus would pay for information about the Chest of Rao, and suggesting that extra payment could secure rooms during high occupancy [ch 008 / 008.03; ch 016 / 016.02]. He values discretion and community stability, initially reluctant to convene emergency meetings and insisting that news of the Temple’s return be kept secret to prevent panic [ch 016 / entry; ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.06].

## History with the Party
*   **Initial Lodging and Rumors:** The party first encountered Ostler upon arriving in Hommlet [ch 001 / 001.02]. He provided rooms to Zephyr (the suite) and Sequoia and Zinnia (private rooms), while the party gathered rumors about the Gnarley Forest and Black Jay [ch 002 / 002.01].
*   **Exposing Furnok:** Ostler enlisted the party to prove that the rogue Furnok was cheating at games [ch 004 / 004.05]. After Zinnia produced loaded dice as proof, Ostler publicly confronted Furnok and expelled him [ch 004 / 004.05]. As a reward, he granted the party three free nights of lodging, a food discount, and his confidence [ch 004 / 004.05].
*   **Council Context:** Ostler informed the party that adventurer lodging required three days' payment in advance, with abandoned goods sold to the highest bidder if the party failed to return [ch 004 / 004.02]. He is listed as a member of the village council by the Village Elder [ch 004 / 004.04].
*   **Post-Moathouse Updates:** Ostler expressed pleasure that the party had visited Jaroo before speaking with him [ch 005 / 005.04]. He greeted the party at breakfast, unaware of their secret nighttime activities, while facilitating their choice of companion (Calmer) [ch 006 / 006.03].
*   **Room Management:** Ostler reminded the party that rooms were rented by the fortnight and warned them that vacancies might not be available upon their return unless they paid extra [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.03].
*   **Intelligence on Trade:** Ostler provided the party with Honey Haven mead and reported that trade roads had reopened, crediting the party’s previous efforts against bandits for this improvement [ch 015 / 015.05]. He noted he had heard good reports of the party from innkeepers Glennon and Pasmarie Clee [ch 015 / 015.05].
*   **The Chest and Rannos/Gremag:** Ostler was told by the party that merchants Rannos and Gremag left town with a magical chest [ch 016 / 016.02]. Ostler revealed his own distrust of the merchants and suggested the party inform Jaroo about the chest, noting that Burne and Rufus would also be interested [ch 016 / 016.02].
*   **Council Meeting:** The party requested an urgent council meeting from Ostler, who initially suggested waiting until the following week [ch 017 / 017.06]. He hosted the secret meeting in his inn's back room [ch 017 / 017.06]. Ostler expressed shock that the Temple of Elemental Evil had returned, stating he believed it had been destroyed years ago, and insisted the news remain secret [ch 017 / 017.06].
*   **Scarlet Moon Inquiry:** Ostler directed the party to Jaroo for information regarding the "Scarlet Moon" rumor [ch 018 / 018.09].

## Last Observed State
Ostler was present at the Inn of the Welcome Wench in Chapter 018, engaging the party in conversation about the Hall of the Scarlet Moon [ch 018 / 018.09]. Status not established in the summaries beyond his presence in Chapter 018.

## Relationships
*   **The Party:** Ostler maintains a friendly but professional relationship with the party, offering them lodging, discounts, and intelligence [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.05; ch 015 / entry].
*   **Furnok:** Ostler viewed Furnok as a dishonest rogue who was cheating his patrons and expelled him from the inn after the party provided proof [ch 004 / 004.05].
*   **Rannos and Gremag:** Ostler stated he never trusted these merchants [ch 016 / 016.02].
*   **Burne, Rufus, and Jaroo:** As a council member, Ostler coordinates with these figures on village security and council meetings [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 005 / 005.04; ch 017 / 017.06]. He suggested the party leverage their relationship with Burne and Rufus regarding the Chest of Rao [ch 016 / 016.02] and direct them to Jaroo for druidic inquiries [ch 018 / 018.09].
*   **Glennon and Pasmarie Clee:** Ostler knows these innkeepers at Honey Haven, who provided him with positive references about the party [ch 015 / 015.05].
