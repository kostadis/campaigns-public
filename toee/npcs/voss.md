## Identity
Voss is a low-ranking bandit captured by the party [ch 003 / entry]. He describes himself as a desperate man who fell into robbery after losing his property and money to gambling and drink, eventually being accepted by a gang when he had nowhere else to go [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.05]. He claims no affiliation with the Temple of Elemental Evil, stating only that he is "just a bandit" [ch 003 / 003.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Voss presents himself as a simple, desperate individual rather than a conspirator. He argues that his criminal actions were necessary for survival, stating that "a guy's got to eat and live" [ch 003 / 003.05]. He exhibits genuine fear of the organization above him, specifically reacting with panic to the symbol of the "big boss" despite claiming ignorance of its meaning [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.05]. Zephyr judged his reactions as sincere, concluding he lacks useful knowledge of the larger organization due to his low rank [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.05].

## History with the Party
- The party captured Voss alive during an ambush at the Moathouse and chose to interrogate him using persuasion rather than intimidation [ch 003 / 003.05].
- Voss broke down in tears when questioned by Sequoia, Zephyr, and Calmer [ch 003 / 003.05].
- He identified himself as Voss and pleaded his case, explaining his history of financial ruin and addiction that led him to the gang [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.05].
- He denied serving the Temple of Elemental Evil, claiming ignorance of their existence until Sequoia suggested the gang served them indirectly [ch 003 / 003.05].
- Zephyr confirmed through magical or perceptual examination that Voss was not lying and genuinely believed the Moathouse was an abandoned ruin [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.05; ch 003 / moment].
- Voss panicked when shown a river rock inscribed with an eye and a tear, initially refusing to explain it due to fear [ch 003 / 003.05].
- After Calmer’s reassurances failed to calm him and Sequoia succeeded, Voss revealed the stone was a symbol provided by the bandits’ "big boss," though he did not understand its significance [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.05; ch 003 / moment].
- In Chapter 004, Voss was mentioned in a DM aside as someone who might be able to help the party investigate a suspected spy in a construction encampment [ch 004 / 004.02].

## Relationships
- **The Party:** Voss is the prisoner of Sequoia, Zephyr, and Calmer. He reacted with fear to Zephyr’s presence but was ultimately calmed by Sequoia [ch 003 / 003.05].
- **The Bandit Gang:** Voss considers himself a member of the gang that occupied the Moathouse, viewing them as his only refuge after losing his property [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.05].
- **The Big Boss:** Voss fears the unidentified leader of the bandits, referring to them only as "the big boss" who supplied the symbol [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.05; ch 003 / moment].
- **Temple of Elemental Evil:** Voss claims to have no knowledge of or affiliation with the Temple, a claim Zephyr deemed sincere [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.05].
