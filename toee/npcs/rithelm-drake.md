## Identity
Human soldier [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.08]. Physical descriptions provided by party members differ: Sequoia describes him as a massive man of average height with a square face and rough, pink skin, while Zephyr describes him as a burly man with a square face and green eyes [ch 011 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
He initially demanded a drink before softening upon learning the party were adventurers, buying them drinks in return [ch 011 / 011.08]. He expressed that he might have been an adventurer previously, but family life took precedence [ch 011 / entry]. Zephyr's account specifies he had a wife and three kids [ch 011 / entry].

## History with the Party
- Rithelm Drake is seen in a tavern in Ostverk, where he demanded a drink [ch 011 / 011.08].
- Upon discovering the party were adventurers, he bought them a drink and recalled the location of an abandoned monastery near Gallows Corner [ch 011 / 011.08; ch 011 / entry].
- The party hinted at the monastery and a chest, withholding specifics, prompting Rithelm to share the location [ch 011 / 011.08].
