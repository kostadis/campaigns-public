## Identity
Lieutenant Krell was an officer of the Earth Temple who acted as a double agent for the Fire Temple under Alrrem [ch 021 / entry; ch 022 / entry]. He wielded a scimitar and a peculiar hand crossbow contraption recognized as a pistol [ch 022 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Krell was tactically aware, directing his forces to break enemy concentration and avoid magical hazards [ch 022 / entry; ch 022 / 022.05]. He believed the Earth Temple wasted troops as "cannon fodder" and that the Fire Temple valued power and glory more highly [ch 022 / entry]. He claimed to have been tricked into betraying the Earth Temple moments before his death [ch 022 / entry; ch 022 / 022.07].

## History with the Party
- In the early stages of the campaign, Krell assigned the party a mission to raid Fire Temple gnolls, providing maps and intelligence on their positions [ch 021 / 021.05].
- Krell encouraged the party by noting the Earth Temple had long attempted to remove these gnolls, pointing to recent dead gnolls as evidence of conflict [ch 021 / 021.05].
- The party later realized Krell had set them up to attack Earth Temple allies, revealing him as a traitor within their midst [ch 021 / moment].
- Romag briefed the party on apprehending Krell, who had holed up with gnolls and bugbears to plot rebellion [ch 022 / 022.01].
- Krell was heard encouraging gnolls with promises of food from Earth Temple cadavers, declaring the Fire Temple protects its own [ch 022 / 022.04; ch 022 / moment].
- During the confrontation, Krell directed his archers to target the party's cleric to break concentration on a Spirit Guardians spell [ch 022 / 022.05; ch 022 / moment].
- Krell fired his pistol at the cleric, knocking the character unconscious with critical damage [ch 022 / 022.05; ch 022 / moment].
- Upon surrender, Krell attempted to negotiate, offering an alliance with Alrrem and arguing that the Fire Temple treats allies as heroes rather than expendable troops [ch 022 / 022.05; ch 022 / entry].
- The party refused his offer, attacked him non-lethally, and subdued him [ch 022 / 022.05].
- The party delivered Krell to Romag, who oversaw a ritual sacrifice where earth elementals tore Krell apart [ch 022 / 022.07; ch 022 / moment].

## Last Observed State
Lieutenant Krell was killed during a ritual sacrifice in Chapter 022, where earth elementals tore his body apart after Romag shoved dirt into his mouth and chanted to the elder elemental earth god [ch 022 / 022.07]. His former quarters were subsequently granted to the party as their base [ch 023 / 023.01].

## Relationships
- **Romag**: Romag commanded Krell initially, then sought his capture after identifying him as a traitor, and finally oversaw his execution [ch 021 / 021.05; ch 022 / 022.01; ch 022 / 022.07]. Romag claimed to have suspected Krell and kept him under watch [ch 021 / entry].
- **Alrrem**: Krell sought to introduce the party to Alrrem, claiming the Fire Temple would treat them as heroes [ch 022 / 022.05].
- **Calmer**: Krell specifically targeted Calmer with pistol fire to break his concentration on a spell, knocking him unconscious [ch 022 / 022.05].
- **Earth Temple Guards**: Krell betrayed them. After his death, guards viewed his passing as an "opportunity for promotion" [ch 022 / moment].
