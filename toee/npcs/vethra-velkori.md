## Identity
Vethra Velkori is an elder Rhennee woman and the mother of Eryk Velkori [ch 018 / entry]. She is a truth-teller and diviner who performs rituals using smoke, bones, and cards [ch 018 / entry]. She is described as the oldest among the Rhennee [ch 039 / 039.07].

## Personality and Motivations
Vethra is fiercely protective of her family [ch 018 / entry]. During divinations, she is authoritative and dismissive of interference, commanding silence from her son when he expresses concern for her health [ch 018 / 018.04]. She possesses a blunt, maternal demeanor, physically checking the party’s health by poking their cheeks and demanding they eat [ch 039 / 039.03].

## History with the Party
The party met with Vethra at the behest of Tala Velkori to seek guidance on Serena's fate [ch 018 / 018.04]. Vethra performed three distinct divinations for the party:
1.  **Smoke**: Burned braided black hair to reveal a crooked house, The Waterside tavern, and a man with a red sash who "deals in whispers and iron" [ch 018 / 018.04].
2.  **Bone**: Tossed wolf knuckles and pigeon vertebrae to show that Serena was not broken yet, and that while three paths existed, only one held her while the others would "bleed for trying" [ch 018 / 018.04].
3.  **Tarokka**: Despite Eryk's protests, she drew The Chain, The Betrayer, and The Flooded Gate, revealing a crimson-painted barge on the Velverdyva carrying Serena, who had been drugged, bound, and traded [ch 018 / 018.04].

Vethra collapsed from exhaustion after these readings [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.04].

Later, when the party reunited with the Rhennee, Vethra fussed over their physical condition, insisting they eat due to weight loss [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.03]. When the party asked her about a mysterious scarab inscribed with "TZGY," Vethra did not provide an answer but directed them to consult Mother Screng, noting that Screng "seems to know more than she lets on" [ch 039 / 039.07].

## Last Observed State
Vethra was last seen alive among the Rhennee in Nulb in Chapter 39. She fussed over the party's health during their reunion and later directed them to Mother Screng for information about the TZGY scarab [ch 039 / 039.03; ch 039 / 039.07].

## Relationships
*   **Eryk Velkori**: Her son. She overrides his protests during divinations and shares his protective drive regarding Serena [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.04].
*   **Serena**: Her granddaughter. Vethra is deeply concerned with her safety and fate [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.04].
*   **Tala Velkori**: Her relative (referred to as family). Tala approached the party on Vethra's behalf [ch 018 / 018.04].
*   **Mother Screng**: A local herb shop owner whom Vethra recommends to the party as a source of information, implying Screng has knowledge Vethra either lacks or chooses not to share [ch 039 / 039.07].
