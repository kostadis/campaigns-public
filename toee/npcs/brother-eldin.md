## Identity
Brother Eldin is a monk of the Abbey of Beory [ch 015 / entry] and a survivor of the abbey's collapse [ch 015 / 015.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Eldin acts as a guide through the "Path of Righteousness," a series of moral and physical dilemmas used to test the worth of travelers [ch 012 / 012.05]. He is characterized by his religious devotion to Rao, providing extensive lore on the deity, the Archdiocese, and the significance of the Chest of Rao [ch 015 / 015.03]. He possesses a sense of caution regarding powerful artifacts, warning that the Chest of Rao is dangerous and will draw the attention of both those who wish to use it for ill and those seeking to reclaim it for good [ch 015 / moment]. He demonstrates a certain levity, laughing off suggestions of using the chest's power as if it were a "parlor trick" [ch 015 / 015.03; ch 015 / moment].

## History with the Party
* Appeared in the library severely wounded after the ritual cavern collapsed [ch 015 / 015.02].
* Rewarded the party with copper, silver, gold coins, a magical scroll of *web*, and a potion of greater healing for their bravery in stopping the elemental cult [ch 015 / 015.02].

## Last Observed State
Eldin was last observed in the library, severely wounded following the ritual cavern collapse [ch 015 / 015.02]. While in the library, he provided the party with various rewards, including coins, a magical scroll of *web*, and a potion of greater healing [ch 015 / 015.02].

## Relationships
