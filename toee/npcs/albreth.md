## Identity
Albreth is a human man in robes and a druid of the Circle of the Scarlet Moon [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.10].

## Personality and Motivations
Albreth is identified as a druid of the Circle of the Scarlet Moon [ch 018 / 018.10]. He expresses a desire to teach, stating, "We are here to teach" [ch 018 / 018.10]. He becomes hostile when questioned regarding the Temple of Elemental Evil [ch 018 / entry] and reveals an allegiance to elemental fire [ch 018 / entry].

## History with the Party
* Albreth is a human man in robes located at Camp Number Two at the Hall of the Scarlet Moon [ch 018 / entry].
* He identifies himself as a 'druid of the Circle of the Scarlet Moon' [ch 018 / entry].
* Albreth and Ingulf introduced themselves as 'the Circle of the Scarlet Moon' and 'druids of the Circle of the Scarlet Moon' [ch 018 / 018.10].
* Albreth and Ingulf stated their allegiance as 'fire, water, earth, or air', confirming a connection to elemental evil [ch 018 / 018.10].
* He became hostile when questioned about the Temple of Elemental Evil [ch 018 / entry].
* He cast a fireball during the party's escape [ch 018 / entry].

## Last Observed State
