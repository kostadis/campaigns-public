# Chapter 43: The Bronze Door

Date: 2026-11-08

## Summary
The battered party regrouped outside the heavy bronze door after a short rest. Calmer, recently restored from near-death, observed that they were leaders with minions and ought to use them. Dren arrived after hearing the screaming, took one look at Calmer, and remarked, “Boss, you look like the last time I carried you out.”

Dren supported bringing in troops but warned against spending the Earth Temple’s entire force on the assault: if its soldiers died, the other temples would exploit the weakness. He dispatched his black-clad runner, Torlin, to the Air and Fire Temples. The Water Temple was deliberately excluded because its representative had already tried to kill the party after claiming to be an ally. Zephyr also proposed entering as a supposed turncloak to gather information. Dren considered the tiefling a plausible choice because the temple’s leaders had a history of killing one another, but made clear that he was a general rather than an actor.

While reinforcements were being gathered, the party heard crypts opening beyond the bronze door. Dren explained that the area had been the earth cult’s tomb. Sequoia’s recollection suggested mummies, something worse than the ghasts and wights already encountered, but nobody could recall useful details about them. Calmer suggested the library. Before the party left, Dren fetched the Broken Blades, Earth Guards, and Commander Joradin to hold the doorway. He positioned his own soldiers behind the Earth Guards, explaining, “Because, boss, I trust my guys.”

In the library, a conspicuously dust-free book fell open at a well-used page describing the raising of mummies. The party learned that the creatures spread rotting disease and were highly susceptible to fire. They returned to find an army assembling. Kelno had accepted Torlin’s request immediately and brought all his air goblins—twelve in total—with a bugbear leader. Addressing Calmer as the prophet, he declared the Air Temple’s support and wanted Barkinar to hear about it. Maeris Dorn arrived from the Fire Temple with Vurakhal, the party’s contracted fire salamander. Zephyr had handed over the creature’s token to establish Dren’s authority to summon it.

The proposed turncloak mission gave way to Dren’s preferred plan: send the Air Temple troops through first, observe what happened, and then act. The Air Temple was the remaining army that threatened his position, while the Fire Temple had brought no comparable force. Kelno accepted the honor of supplying the vanguard, delivered an impassioned speech, and then conspicuously moved as far from the danger as possible. Maeris stayed behind to provide healing and support, casting Bless and Aid; the Fire Temple also supplied twenty fire arrows. Calmer spent a third-level spell to dispel the door’s warding glyph before the troops advanced.

The door opened onto a chamber lit by heatless green candles, with unexplained sand on the floor and gray-wrapped figures spaced across the room, all facing the entrance. A man in stonescale vestments stood at the back. He laughed at the goblins and unleashed a necrotic emanation that devastated their leading ranks. Although prepared for an assault, he had expected the party or Fire Temple forces rather than air goblins, and the unexpected vanguard consumed his opening attack.

Zephyr used a shortbow supplied by Dren to wound a mummy with a fire arrow. Zinnia rushed in, finished it with a strike that reduced it to dust, attacked again, and withdrew using Step of the Wind. Sequoia persuaded the frightened bugbear to keep fighting, and the surviving goblins swarmed another defender. The mummies retaliated with attacks and paralyzing dread, freezing several goblins and the bugbear.

Calmer then broke the first defensive line. His initial Turn Undead affected all four targets, and Searing Undead dealt 28 damage to each. His fourth-level Spirit Guardians inflicted further damage while his Spiritual Weapon joined the battle. Delighted by his success, he declared, “The temple of earth does not need undead, the temple of earth needs dirt.” Sequoia interrupted his ensuing speech with, “Shut up, Calmer.” Calmer remained elated: “My crazy ass plan worked.”

The necromancer, identified by the DM as Muskin although the party had not learned his name, fired three Grave Bursts at Calmer. One hit for 21 damage, but Calmer maintained concentration. Muskin retreated deeper into the hallway. Zephyr killed another mummy, Sequoia attacked a weakened acolyte near a chained prisoner, and Zinnia struck a newly arrived mummy priest before withdrawing. Vurakhal used his tail to constrict and burn a priest, dragging the enemy out of the passage.

The deeper defenses included additional mummy acolytes, a priest, and the Exalted Hierophant. Calmer’s second Turn Undead affected only one target; the priest and Hierophant resisted. The Hierophant cast Contagion at Calmer, who rolled a natural 20 on his Constitution save, with no disease effect narrated. A remaining priest paralyzed more goblins, and the Hierophant used Create Undead to turn three fallen air goblins into ghouls behind the allied line. The grappled priest endured further salamander heat, escaped, and then died to Calmer’s magic. The session ended with the outer defenses largely broken and the more dangerous enemies still ahead. As Zephyr put it, “Alright, now the real fight begins.”

A chamber beneath the temple illuminated by heatless green candles, with sand-covered floors and niches in the walls. It serves as a tomb where mummies are raised and where the ongoing battle with Muskin takes place. The previously explored laboratory is a separate room outside the bronze door.

- Dren identifies it as the former tomb of the earth cult.
- The opening description emphasizes a room too small for its occupants: wrapped figures stand a step apart across the chamber, already facing the entrance.

A heavy, locked door inscribed with bronze glyphs that leads to the necromancer's final chamber. Calmer dispelled a glyph of warding protecting it before the assault.

- The party hears crypts opening behind it while waiting for reinforcements.
- The enemy has had time to hear the party’s previous fighting and prepare.
- Calmer spends a third-level spell to remove the magical ward before the goblins approach.

- Calmer proposes the visit after the party fails to recall useful mummy lore.
- Zinnia locates the relevant book, and Zephyr identifies the repeatedly consulted passage.
- The research also reveals the danger of rotting disease.

A cleric of St. Cuthbert and member of the party who recently recovered from near-death. He advocates for using minions as a tactical advantage and proves devastatingly effective against the undead with his divine powers.

- Suggests the library when recollection fails.
- Removes the door’s ward, prepares Spirit Guardians and Spiritual Weapon, and uses Turn Undead twice.
- His first turning affects four undead and deals 28 damage to each through Searing Undead.
- Maintains concentration after taking 21 damage from Muskin.
- Celebrates his success with a speech that Sequoia interrupts.
- Rolls a natural 20 against the Hierophant’s Contagion.

### Dren
A pragmatic fighter and general who serves as an operator for the party. He is protective of his own troops, advises on military strategy and temple politics, and coordinates the arrival of reinforcements.

- Arrives after hearing screams and recognizes Calmer’s battered condition.
- Warns that rival temples will exploit an Earth Temple army weakened by casualties.
- Excludes the Water Temple because of its previous attempted betrayal.
- Uses Torlin to recruit allies and fetches guards before allowing the party to leave the doorway.
- Recommends using Air Temple soldiers as the first wave because their army threatens his position.
- Supplies Zephyr with a shortbow.

### Torlin
A black-clad runner who serves Dren, tasked with delivering urgent messages to the other elemental temples to recruit reinforcements.

- Appears when Dren whistles.
- Reports Kelno’s unusually eager response and personal attendance.
- Makes further trips as the allied force assembles.

### Kelno
A representative of the Air Temple who eagerly joins the fight, bringing a swarm of air goblins and a bugbear to serve as the vanguard. He enthusiastically sends his troops in first and positions himself as far from the danger as possible.

- Brings all twelve air goblins.
- Addresses Calmer as the prophet and wants Barkinar informed of his support.
- Suggests a dead Earth Guard as a gift to support Zephyr’s proposed deception.
- Frames the vanguard assignment as an opportunity for Air Temple honor and glory.

### Maeris Dorn
A cleric of the Fire Temple who provides magical support before the assault and stands ready at the rear to offer healing, casting Bless and Aid on the party.

- Explains that supporting and healing others is her intended role.
- Blesses Vurakhal as well as the party.
- Does not extend her blessing to the air goblins.
- Brings fire arrows suited to the enemy’s newly discovered weakness.

### Vurakhal
A fire salamander bound by contract who arrives with the Fire Temple contingent to assist in the battle, using its tail to constrict and burn enemies.

- Its token establishes that Dren is acting under the party’s authority.
- Receives Bless from Maeris.
- Uses its tail to seize a mummy priest and pull it from the passage.
- Its heat continues damaging nearby enemies after the initial constriction.

### Commander Joradin
One of the lieutenants reporting to Dren who arrives with the Earth Guards to bolster the party's forces.

- Joins the force securing the doorway while the party visits the library.

### Muskin
The evil cleric in charge of the crypt, dressed in stonescale vestments. He is a necromancer who was surprised by the party's use of Air Temple troops rather than fire-based forces, unleashes necrotic energy on the goblins, and retreats deeper into the hallway when his defenses begin to fail.

- His name is supplied by the DM; the party does not learn it during the library search.
- Has prepared additional undead deeper in the complex as part of an intended takeover from Calmer.
- Uses an unnamed necrotic ritual against the goblin vanguard and Grave Burst against Calmer.
- His opening plan falters because the party has secured allied troops.

### The Bugbear
A leader of the air goblins who initially considers abandoning the fight due to the terrifying nature of the undead but is persuaded back into service by Sequoia.

- Pushes the reluctant goblins into the chamber.
- Expresses loyalty to Kelno and the Air Temple.
- Returns to the attack after Sequoia’s successful persuasion.
- Deals 11 damage with an attack and subsequently suffers paralysis.
- Later remains behind troops who have filled the passage.

### The Exalted Hierophant
A powerful undead priest of the earth cult who attempts to infect Calmer with a magical disease and transforms fallen air goblins into ghouls.

- Resists Calmer’s second Turn Undead.
- Uses a legendary action for Contagion before casting Create Undead on its own turn.
- Creates three ghouls behind the allied force.
