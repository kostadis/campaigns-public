# Chapter 10 The Black Earth Cult?

Date: 2025-04-13

## Summary

The session picked up in the Gnarley Forest. Between sessions the party had brought Quinna Poul her boar and found Glennon Clee's missing barrel with a heartbroken dryad. Back at the Honey Haven, Glennon greeted the recovered beer with cries of “Yes, yes, yes, you found my beer.” He rewarded the party with free lodging at the inn for a week and a small keg of enchanted honeymead that granted an advantage in social dealings. The party took the night off, shared a fine meal with Glennddarc at the Moot Hall, and looked forward to Quinna Poul's feast the following morning.

The party's morning took an unexpected turn when Quinna Poul burst in with alarming news: the enchanted ingredients she had purchased for the feast had animated and escaped into the village, and she feared someone would get hurt. Outside, the party saw potatoes, a pepper or carrot, and broccoli bouncing in different directions; what first appeared to be a single potato proved to be a group of five. The adventurers split up to chase them down. Zephyr found himself facing the furious animated potatoes. After he caught one, the others stopped as though glaring at him, and one launched itself at his head like a projectile. Zephyr stabbed the potato in his hand, horrifying the others into immobility, seized two more, and shot the terrified final fugitive with his shortbow. Zinnia pursued a laughing animated carrot toward the Tabac Shop, leapt onto it, endured its frantic shaking, and knocked it unconscious; its leafy top flopped down as its magic subsided. Sequoia tracked an animated broccoli into Djinibac's Apothecary, where his moment of indecision allowed it to slip inside before he dispatched it with a well-placed arrow. Quinna thanked the party, decided the ingredients had been “a little bit too excited in the magic,” and promised, “Don't you worry, no more quests from me.” She then sliced, diced, and cooked the defeated ingredients into a magnificent honey-glazed forest stew that blessed the adventurers and left them feeling stronger.

With full bellies and renewed strength from Quinna Poul's enchanted feast, the party set out toward the Kron Hills to find the bandit camp. Pasmarie explained that the attacked caravan had taken a secret shortcut rather than the normal road from Hommlet, remarking, “Shouldn't have done that if you asked me. That's the danger with taking shortcuts.” At the ambush site, the party found overturned wagons, broken barrels, scattered honey jars, and churned ground suggesting heavy impacts. Broken crates bore Pasmarie's seal, while pieces of stone were embedded in the wagon wood as though weapons had been carved from rock. Faintly glowing runes etched into nearby stones offered further evidence of magical interference. Zephyr successfully picked up the trail of large footprints leading away from the road and into the hills, and the party followed.

The terrain of the Kron Hills was rough and unforgiving, dotted with abandoned mines and hidden paths. Without warning, a rockslide came crashing down the hillside toward them, sending the party scrambling for cover. Zephyr found shelter behind a boulder while companions who tried to flee were battered by tumbling stones. Shaken but undeterred, they pressed on and eventually discovered the Raiders' camp—a shallow cave system built into a rocky outcrop, surrounded by crude stone walls roughly eight feet tall. Four Black Earth Raiders patrolled the perimeter, and a cave entrance led deeper into what Calmer grimly identified as a temple. These were no ordinary bandits, he warned: “This is some kind of evil cult here.” The discovery could also serve as proof that the Temple was becoming resurgent.

The party devised a plan: Calmer would approach the front gate and pretend to be lost, drawing the guards' attention while Zephyr, Zinnia, and Sequoia scaled the walls and positioned themselves for a surprise attack. Calmer adopted the bravest face he could manage and announced, “I appear to be lost.” The moment a guard spotted the holy symbol of St. Cuthbert hanging openly around his neck, however, the ruse collapsed: “You're a cleric of St. Cuthbert. What are you doing here?” A druid named Thalsor emerged from the temple, his gravelly voice rumbling like a slow landslide as he denounced Calmer as “a meddling fool who dares tread upon the sacred ground of the Earth Mother.” He gripped a stone staff adorned with jagged crystals, and the runes carved into the camp walls flared with ominous light. Calmer later reflected, “Perhaps next time when I'm trying to bluff an evil cult, I should not so obviously wear my symbol of St. Cuthbert.”

Fortunately, the other three had already made it over the walls undetected, even though Zephyr cheerfully suggested they “Let Calmer get a little roughed up.” Seizing the moment of chaos at the gate, Zephyr, Zinnia, and Sequoia launched a coordinated assault from within the camp, catching the cultists completely off guard. Sequoia struck one guard three times for a combined twenty-seven points of damage, only to discover that these cultists were tougher than ordinary bandits. Zephyr finished that guard and wounded another, while Zinnia unleashed a flurry of blows that left a second cultist barely standing. Thalsor summoned two mud mephits to bolster his defenses, but the tide was already turning against him. One of the mephits was destroyed in the fighting, releasing a burst of mud that briefly restrained Zephyr, while the cultist guards fell one by one.

When the party demanded the stolen honey, Thalsor declared, “That honey belongs to the Earth Temple now. A tribute to her enduring might.” He retreated toward the temple entrance, cried “Defend!” and awakened a stone golem—a massive, slow-moving guardian of living rock. The creature advanced with thunderous steps, slamming the ground and sending a localized shockwave through the earth around Zinnia. A surviving cultist struck Sequoia twice for twelve points of damage, while Calmer hit the golem with a powerful Guiding Bolt.

Seeing one guard wounded, the golem damaged, and the adventurers still standing, Thalsor reconsidered his bluster. “Now, you know, I think we've gotten off to a terrible start. Do you think we could negotiate?” he offered. The party demanded both the honey and the chest sought by Lareth the Beautiful. Thalsor refused to surrender the honey without receiving something valuable in return, extolled its religious significance and power as an aphrodisiac, and proved unmoved by Calmer's attempt to intimidate him. “I shall live even if I am reincarnated into the rocks and the dirt,” he declared. When the party chose violence instead, Thalsor conjured a hail of sharp stones across the camp. Sequoia and Zephyr nevertheless cut down the last bandit guards. The session ended with Sequoia at only three hit points, Thalsor reduced to four, the stone golem still advancing, and the chest still somewhere within the temple.

## Locations

### Sheernobb

A small gnome village centered around a large hill with a dominant building called the Moot Hall. The main road ends at the hall and divides into northern and southern branches that circle it before rejoining. The circular road is lined with businesses including Djinibac's Apothecary, a Tabac Shop, Fiver Woodcarver, Peony's Mill, Henker's Shop, Naglo Crystal Divine Shop, and Grindin Books. A pond is also visible along the route.

### The Moot Hall

The largest building in Sheernobb, situated on the village's largest hill. Wide stone steps lead to grand doors carved with runes. The inscription “Ni te pharkalu, quo lun verra lu” is translated into Common as, “If you see it, it's not the right it,” warning visitors about gnomish illusions. Inside, the central chamber is surrounded by three tiers of broad steps and benches beneath finely carved beams and supports. A magical statue of Keenleene, the village founder, stands outside and performs with pipes for visitors.

### Gnarley Forest

A sprawling ancient woodland straddling the borders of Verbobonc, Dyvers, Greyhawk, the Wild Coast, Celene, and the Kron Hills. The honey caravan was ambushed on the trade road near its edge.

### Honey Haven Inn

A local tavern and inn on the southern branch of Sheernobb's circular road. Glennon Clee rewarded the party with one week of free lodging there after they recovered his stolen beer barrel. Quinna Poul uses its kitchen or dining facilities to prepare her enchanted feast.

### Kron Hills

A rugged, rocky region filled with abandoned mines, concealed paths, and dangerous slopes. The Black Earth Raiders established a fortified camp in the area. While following their trail, the party was caught in a rockslide that injured members who failed to reach shelter.

### Earth Cult Camp

A fortified shallow cave system on a rocky outcrop in the Kron Hills, protected by crude stone walls approximately eight feet tall. Four Black Earth Raiders initially guarded the camp. Its entrance opens toward a temple dedicated to the Earth Mother, with magical runes set into the surrounding walls and a stone statue capable of animating as a golem. The stolen honey and Lareth the Beautiful's chest were believed to be within the site.

## NPCs

### Glennddarc

The wizened head gnome of Sheernobb. In the evening he is happy to see the party at the Moot Hall, speaks warmly about them, and shares a fine meal with them.

### Pasmarie Clee

A petite gnome beekeeper with a kind, weathered face, unruly curls, oversized boots, strong work-worn hands, and an apron smeared with honeycomb wax and pollen. Before this session she asked the party to recover her stolen honey shipment. As they set out, she marks on the map where the caravan was attacked and explains that it had taken a secret shortcut.

### Gnonsbim

The captain of the guard in Sheernobb, who previously tasked the party with investigating the bandits and has promised them a reward for killing the bandits.

### Quinna Poul

A local half-orcish cook preparing a grand feast for the party, who brought her a wild boar before this session. On the morning of the feast her enchanted ingredients escaped and caused chaos throughout the village, but after the party recovered them, she prepared honey-glazed forest stew and promised there would be no more quests from her.

### Glennon Clee

The Honey Haven innkeeper whose stolen beer the party recovered before this session. He welcomed its return with enthusiastic cries of “Yes, yes, yes, you found my beer.” He rewarded the party with free lodging for a week and a small keg of enchanted honeymead.

### Thalsor

A gravelly-voiced druid and leader of the Black Earth Raiders who wields a stone staff adorned with jagged crystals. His voice is compared to a landslide in slow motion. He is fanatically devoted to the Earth Mother, views surface-dwellers as insects, and commands mud mephits and a stone golem. He initially delivered a grandiose threat, then attempted to negotiate after his defenders began falling. When intimidation failed, he declared that he would survive through reincarnation into rock and dirt and attacked with a hail of sharp stones. He ended the session with only four hit points remaining.

### Black Earth Raiders

Cultist guards stationed at the Earth Temple camp who use stone-based weaponry and are considerably more resilient than ordinary bandits. Four were initially visible. The party surprised them by climbing over the camp's wall while Calmer distracted them, then killed the guards during the battle.

### Stone Golem

A massive, slow-moving guardian made of living rock that protects the Earth Temple. It began as a stone statue and was animated when Thalsor touched it and shouted, “Defend!” It is highly resistant to non-magical attacks, lacks ranged attacks, and can pound the ground to create a localized tremor. Calmer wounded it with Guiding Bolt, but it remained active at the end of the session.

### Mud Mephit

Small elemental creatures composed of earth and water summoned by Thalsor to assist the cultists in combat. Two appeared beside Calmer. One missed Calmer with its fists; another was destroyed and released a burst of mud that briefly restrained Zephyr.
