# Chapter 23 A Ghoul Time Was Had By All

Date: 2026-01-10

## Summary

Before play began, the DM added a note on the previous session's ending. When Sequoia revived Calmer, Calmer's thanks were meant as "Thank you, Sequoia, I knew I could trust you." In other words, Calmer now trusts Sequoia.

The party awoke in their newly granted quarters within the Earth Temple, the former chambers of the traitor Lieutenant Krell. The room is marked as room 23 on the map. It was still bloodied and messy from the recent battles with gnolls, who, as the DM put it, "are not exactly the cleanest eaters." It now served as their base of operations. With a secret door and strategic access points, it was a foothold in the heart of enemy territory.

The DM warned the party that if they did nothing, someone would come and try to draw them into a conspiracy. The party weighed three options:

- exploring the dungeon complex as agents of Romag;
- returning to the Fire Temple agents in Nulb to report Krell's death;
- consolidating their position.

Sequoia regretted not grabbing a Fire Temple badge off a body for later use. The DM agreed that would have been very useful. The DM recommended exploring, and the party decided to explore the dungeon to better understand the labyrinthine complex they had infiltrated. Since Zephyr and Zinnia usually lead, the DM asked Sequoia to choose the direction this time.

Their exploration began cautiously as they ventured west from their quarters. Opening a door, they encountered two ghasts, undead creatures with hollow eyes and an unsettling hunger. When the party claimed to be Earth Temple, the ghasts complained that the Earth Temple never brings food. They demanded to know whether the party had brought food, and then shouted "ARE YOU FOOD?" Sequoia quickly stepped out and closed the door on the threatening creatures.

Sequoia rolled a natural 1, rerolled with Lucky, and got a 3. He came away convinced he had heard that the ghasts were wonderful people worth sitting down to play with, "rainbows and unicorns," before realising that was not what he heard. Zinnia, observing the interaction, deduced that the Earth Temple forces and the ghasts were not allied. This was a valuable piece of intelligence about the fractured nature of the temple's inhabitants.

Continuing their exploration northward, the party discovered an abandoned kitchen, a chamber twenty feet by thirty feet. It had three doors in its southern portion. A massive fireplace dominated the north wall, large enough to roast an entire ox, with numerous side ovens built into its structure. The room was in ruins, littered with:

- broken containers and dishes;
- pieces of wooden tables and countertops;
- unidentifiable trash.

Skeletons lay scattered across the floor, including the massive bones of an ogre, testament to some past violence. Zephyr asked who the room belonged to. The answer was "Currently, nobody."

The party investigated the adjacent 10-foot storage rooms, which had held crockery and food. They found only spoiled food and shattered crockery. Calmer stood as far away as possible as Zephyr approached the fireplace to examine it more closely. A large giant snake suddenly erupted from within, hissing with hunger and fury, "very hungry and very cranky."

The battle with the snake was swift but intense. The DM warned that the snake carried a lot of poison and would do serious damage if it landed a bite.

- Zephyr struck first with his short sword and wounded the creature, which lunged at him with venomous fangs but missed.
- Zinnia joined the fray, his attacks landing with devastating precision.
- Calmer began holding forth on snakes as fascinating creatures until the table urged him to just kill it. He then cast Toll the Dead against the serpent, though it initially resisted his spell.
- Sequoia fought through an uncooperative dice roller and a string of natural 1s, but still managed to wound the creature further.
- The snake turned on Sequoia for attacking it "so ruthlessly and viciously," but failed to connect. The DM rolled a 1, a 2, and a 4 across its attacks.
- Calmer's second invocation proved more effective.
- Finally, Zinnia delivered a 21-point killing blow that left the snake lifeless on the kitchen floor.

Searching the area, the party discovered a short sword and twenty gold pieces among the debris. The DM noted that the kitchen was now cleared. If the party also dealt with the ghasts, the kitchen could become part of their territory.

Pressing onward, the party entered a vast hall that stretched over a hundred feet in length and thirty feet in width. The chamber told a story of past grandeur and recent violence. Broken tables, trestles, benches, chairs, and stools lay scattered and heaped throughout the space, evidence of great revelries that had once been conducted in this evil place. Tattered tapestries and drapes still clung to the walls. Bones, both individual and complete skeletons, littered the floor beneath the broken furniture. It was clear that a major battle had been fought here, a skirmish in the greater war for control of the temple. Various weapons lay scattered throughout, remnants of that violent confrontation.

Following a passageway to the south, Sequoia opened a door and immediately encountered several ghouls. They were mindless undead creatures with no allegiance to any temple, only to their insatiable hunger, and they had decided the party was "the tasty snack they've been promised for some time." Zephyr asked whether they had any temple allegiance. Calmer confirmed what the party suspected: these creatures served no master but their own bellies. Zephyr called it "an easy fight." The DM summed up the party's ambition: clear out the ghouls, the ghasts and the snake, expand the territory, and "Romag thinks you're heroes."

The ghouls attacked without hesitation, and the party responded in kind.

- Zinnia, who had rolled a 24 for initiative, struck the first ghoul with brutal efficiency.
- Sequoia killed one creature outright with 19 damage and wounded another, attacking with advantage from Vex.
- Zephyr, using his assassin's training against a ghoul that had not yet acted, struck it for twenty damage and finished it with his next attack.
- One ghoul, sensing the tide turning against it, disengaged and fled into an adjacent chamber.

The remaining ghouls pressed their attack. One charged Zephyr but missed twice. Another struck Sequoia with claws and teeth. A third lunged at Zinnia but failed to connect. Calmer invoked powerful magic, summoning spectral guardians that tore through the undead, and all three nearby ghouls failed to resist it. Zephyr taunted the ghouls: "You guys can't even hurt us, man." Sequoia then cut down one of the wounded survivors, and Zephyr's short sword finished the last.

The party pursued the fleeing ghoul into the adjacent chamber, but Zephyr's boldness led him into a trap. As he opened the door, seven more ghouls awaited within, their dead eyes gleaming with hunger. Zephyr wished aloud for an area attack and urged Calmer to walk into the middle of the pack. Calmer's Spirit Guardians, a yellow aura around him, was already up.

Zephyr killed the wounded ghoul that had fled, which had not yet attacked him, for 20 damage. He then chose to stay in the room. The DM's verdict was immediate: "Well, that was not the right move… You are about to get really beat up." His aggressive positioning left him vulnerable. The ghouls swarmed him in a frenzy of claws and teeth, overwhelming his defenses and dragging him down into unconsciousness.

More ghouls attacked Zinnia and Sequoia, tearing into them with savage fury. Another attempted to reach Calmer but failed to penetrate his defenses. Calmer's spectral guardians continued their work, devastating the ghouls that came within their reach. He then turned his attention to the fallen Zephyr, channeling second-level healing magic that brought the rogue back from the brink of death.

The battle raged on with renewed intensity.

- Zinnia cut down multiple ghouls with his martial prowess, his attacks flowing from one target to the next with deadly efficiency.
- Sequoia struggled initially but eventually found his mark.
- Zephyr, revived and vengeful, struck down another ghoul for 19 damage.
- One of the remaining ghouls wounded Zephyr again, prompting him to plead "Don't let him kill me again." It then retreated from Calmer's spectral guardians, provoking a retaliatory strike that left it at one hit point.
- Another ghoul, unable to escape the guardians, attacked Calmer directly. Its claws and teeth found purchase for 22 damage, and the second hit broke the cleric's concentration, causing his spectral guardians to dissipate.

The loss of the powerful spell was a setback, but the party had already thinned the ghoul numbers significantly.

Calmer, wounded but determined, cast a second-level Cure Wounds on himself, restoring his strength fully. Zinnia and Sequoia finished off the remaining ghouls in the chamber, their weapons finding their marks with lethal precision. As the last ghoul fell, Zinnia's keen senses detected more undead approaching from the north. His passive perception was 16, above Calmer's 14.

The party, bloodied but unbowed, made a crucial decision. Zephyr declared, "we want to conquer this place, so that takes a bit of blood, sweat, and tears." He chose to keep fighting to conquer this territory for the greater glory of the Earth Temple, maintaining their cover while simultaneously clearing out the dungeon's threats. Calmer, chastened by the last fight, suggested casting his spell before entering the next room so they wouldn't "have to do this all over again." Zinnia noted he could run away, but with Zephyr "very determined," he chose to help.

The next wave of six ghouls met a prepared and determined party.

- Zinnia struck first, hitting a ghoul for 18 before it could fully enter the fray.
- Sequoia followed suit, his attacks becoming more confident and deadly as he cut down multiple undead. He greeted the wave with "Holy ghoul!"
- The ghouls that managed to close with the party found their attacks largely ineffective. One did score a critical bite on Sequoia for 16, and two others tore into Zephyr.
- Zephyr's daggers flashed in the dim light, dropping ghouls with precise strikes.
- Calmer, having learned from the previous encounter, invoked his spectral guardians once more with his second 3rd-level spell, the divine magic tearing through the undead ranks.
- Zinnia and Sequoia worked in concert, eliminating the remaining ghouls with ruthless efficiency until the chamber fell silent once more.

The DM observed that when the party rolls well on initiative they deal an amazing amount of damage, and that "It's when you don't roll well on your initiatives that things get bad."

With the immediate threat neutralized, the party turned their attention to the two ghasts they had encountered earlier. Calmer noted that this was his last Spirit Guardians spell and suggested a tactical approach: pre-cast it, open the door, and blast them before the others engaged. He opened the door to the ghast chamber, and the undead threatened the party, declaring they would be nothing but food. Calmer, emboldened by their recent victories over what Zephyr counted as twenty ghouls, retorted that the last ghouls had said the same thing.

He invoked his spectral guardians, and the divine magic immediately began tearing into the ghasts. One of the creatures emitted a poisonous stench that overcame Calmer, leaving him with the poisoned condition, but the party pressed their advantage. The DM paused to explain the difference between poison damage and the poisoned condition to Sequoia's player: "Not every poison causes the poisoned condition."

Zinnia entered the chamber and struck one of the ghasts. The DM briefly ruled him poisoned, then corrected the ruling so that the stench applies only to a creature that starts its turn within it. Zinnia then tactically withdrew from the poisonous aura, though the movement provoked a retaliatory claw that wounded him for 10. He resisted the creature's paralyzing touch through sheer fortitude, rolling a 19 on the Constitution save.

Sequoia charged the other ghast, using his dagger's bonus attack. His attacks found their mark repeatedly until the creature fell. He then disengaged, avoiding the remaining ghast's stench. The surviving ghast clawed at Calmer but missed, and Calmer's spectral guardians continued their relentless assault. Finally, Zephyr drew his pistol and fired, with Sneak Attack thanks to Calmer standing beside the ghast. The shot struck for 19, echoing through the chamber and ending the ghast's unnatural existence.

The battle was over. The party stood victorious amid the corpses of a giant snake, about twenty ghouls, and two ghasts. The DM declared: "You have now conquered this area for the greater glory of the Earth Temple." The party had successfully cleared an entire section of the dungeon, claiming new territory for the Earth Temple, or so it would appear to any observers.

Zephyr asked whether the area was now theirs. The DM explained that the party would need to negotiate with Romag to officially claim it, and that simply holding ground was not enough. They would need to recruit monsters to populate and defend their new domain, a task that might mean being sent on a recruiting mission. The session ended at 12:30. For now, the party had established themselves as formidable agents within the temple, positioning themselves perfectly to continue their dangerous mission of espionage and sabotage from within the heart of the enemy stronghold.
