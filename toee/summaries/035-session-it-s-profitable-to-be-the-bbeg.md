# Chapter 35 It's Profitable to be the BBEG

Date: 2026-06-28

## Summary

When a triton's signal cut across the water — a desperate plea, and with it the warning that a kraken would be loosed if no rescue came — Zephyr didn't stop to question it. He took the bait whole and threw himself into the pool. The water answered with a will of its own: an elemental intelligence seized him, locked him in place, and would not let go. Acrid smoke began to curl off the surface, and the truth became clear too late — the water was acid, and Zephyr was caught in it.
Sequoia took command, rallying the party and lashing a rope around his waist to haul Zephyr free. But the pool ran four feet deep and Sequoia stood only three feet tall — a problem Calmer was quick to point out, and one Sequoia did not appreciate hearing. In the end it was Zinnia who took up the rope, tied off, and leapt in. The water made no grab for him. He dragged Zephyr out — alive, but coated head to foot in acid.

Shaken, the group turned to leave. Brother Eelrich named the place unwelcoming and said as much, though he agreed to follow Brother Calmer's lead; Calmer looked to Zephyr, who told him plainly to get out. As Brother Eelrich and Calmer hurried for the exit, certain it was over, Sequoia jumped in a second time. The acid burned him before he understood his mistake, and he scrambled clear. Only then did Calmer piece it together: the thing in the water could plant suggestions in their minds. The party fell back behind the curtain just outside the Hall of Verdigris to catch their breath. Calmer weighed going back in — but their thoughts drifted instead to the chambers Brother Eelrich had emerged from, and the treasure waiting there, now claimed in the name of the Prophet of Earth.

The party wasted no time claiming what was rightfully theirs. In the priest's chambers they were shown three drugged fruits, which they took but declined to sample. Zephyr searched the desk and uncovered a collection of powerful spell scrolls — Resist Poison, True Seeing, and Resist Energy (Fire) — and learned, to his disappointment, that a scroll can be cast by anyone but not learned by a non-wizard. When Brother Eelrich protested that the party was now plundering his very bedroom, Sequoia answered with a single "Yes." Zephyr and Sequoia, as the party's rogues, took the south door into the bedroom and Sequoia worked the locked chests open with sleight of hand, revealing clothing, ritual texts on elemental water, a ceremonial bronze mace, a gold ring set with a pearl, and two potions of water breathing. Calmer confronted Brother Eelrich directly, demanding the man address him as "Lord Master Calmer" and reminding him in no uncertain terms that the temple had fallen and a new order had risen in its place. Eelrich whined that the texts were religious materials he deserved; asked directly whether they mattered to him, he said yes.

As the party leafed through the ritual texts, an unsettling suspicion began to take root — the writings hinted that Brother Eelrich may have known far more about the living, sentient nature of the water elemental than he had ever let on. The party realised the potions of water breathing would be little help: the water was not merely water but an elemental intelligence, "the water itself is living." Zephyr's verdict was pragmatic — they could not trust Eelrich, but they needed him for now. Before leaving, the party made their position crystal clear: the next surprise found in these halls would be meant for him. The intimidation roll came up only a 9 even with Calmer's help, but Eelrich acknowledged them all the same.

Returning to their lair on the first level to rest and recover, the party tallied their haul — 828 gold pieces in all, cut to 745 after the 10% owed to Dren, who "expects to get paid" — and rose to 9th level for having defeated and plundered the Water Temple. Sequoia gained Magical Ambush, Zephyr gained Master of Mimicry and the ability to imitate another person's speech or handwriting, Zinnia learned to walk on water, and Calmer, after weighing Commune, Scrying, and Summon Celestial, settled on Greater Restoration.

After their rest, a knock came at the door. Dren, their ever-watchful subordinate, reported that a soft-looking, gray man who smiled far too much had arrived at the entrance, claiming to have been sent by Barkinar. The man had laughed when Hessa Claymoor, one of Dren's Broken Blades, patted him down for weapons, which Dren found deeply suspicious, though he admitted the visitor appeared harmless enough. The party allowed him in, and the man introduced himself as Lucius Graeme — a facilitator, a middleman, a carrier of things from clever people to clever people. By his own account, the new management was the cleverest thing to happen to the temple in a year. Sequoia wanted to know what the last clever thing had been; Lucius left that question for Barkinar. The party also needed a reminder of who Barkinar was: the man who had appeared after the war with four problems in the morning, all of them gone. He was their link to the next layer of management, and the one who had warned them about Senshock.

Lucius arrived bearing gifts. He clicked his fingers and had Dren fetch a cask of genuinely excellent wine. Dren nervously asked whether he should really bring it in, having heard of casks exploding. When Zephyr gave the order, Dren bristled that he had been talking to the boss.

Calmer formally assigned roles to each of his companions, naming Zinnia as the head of all intelligence operations, Zephyr as chief operating officer responsible for the day-to-day running of the temple, and Sequoia as the one charged with ensuring that valuables found their way into the right hands and stayed there. Calmer himself declared that his concerns lay with the spiritual matters of the earth temple gods, and that petty operational details were beneath him — a declaration he delivered with the full gravity of a man who had just forced a priest to call him "Lord Master." He then sat down and went back to his reading.

Zephyr poured a cup for Lucius. Lucius then presented each member of the household with a potion of superior healing as a free gift. He explained that his role was to serve as the conduit between Nulb and the temple's leadership, ensuring that whatever the new heads required, they would receive. When Zephyr asked to be taken to buy magical items, Lucius insisted such errands were beneath them and that he would procure the goods himself. He also made clear that money still had to change hands. Dren brought in Lucius's chest of goods. Dren nervously warned that some chests had a habit of turning into mimics. The chest held an assortment of enchanted items available for purchase.

The negotiations that followed were equal parts exciting and humbling. Lucius was clear that nothing beyond the healing potions was free: he had people to pay, and Lord Hedrack had made it plain that the temple's expenses must be covered. Sequoia openly wondered what the maintenance cost on an earth elemental could possibly be. Sequoia and Zephyr each walked away with finely enchanted blades, procured by Lucius with a merchant's practiced enthusiasm, and Zinnia invested in a Ring of Protection. Other goods in the chest drew curiosity but no purchase: boots of levitation, a hat of disguise, a ring of feather falling, a potion of speed, a spell scroll of teleport, and a 21,000-gold Staff of Striking. Lucius warned that his stock might not last the day and that anything else would take two to three days to procure.

The items that truly captured the party's imagination carried price tags that dwarfed everything the party had accumulated. One was a ring that rendered its wearer invisible. The other made the wearer immune to all magical restraint and to difficult terrain. Lucius, ever the professional, offered to hold both items in reserve for a deposit, ensuring no other buyer could claim them. The party declined to pay the deposits for now. As the meeting drew to a close, the party found themselves staring at the vast gulf between their ambitions and their coin purses. They were newly and powerfully motivated to plunder every last secret the temple had left to offer.

## Locations

A 10-foot-wide, 30-foot-long room covered in green decor, reached through the south door of Brother Eelrich's chambers. It contains a pair of beds separated by a folding screen, and each bed has a small stand, a bronze brazier, a low stool, and a locked chest. The chests held clothing, ritual texts, and treasure.

### Brother Eelrich's Chambers
The priest's quarters off the Water Temple, where the party found the drugged fruits and a desk holding three spell scrolls. Its south door leads to the bedroom.

### The Party's Lair
Located on the first level of the temple, this serves as the party's base of operations where they rest, receive visitors, and conduct business as the new heads of the temple. Dren and his people guard the entrance and search visitors before admitting them.

## NPCs
### Brother Eelrich Vane
A defeated priest of the Water Temple who whines about the loss of his religious materials and personal belongings. He ultimately submits to Calmer's authority after being reminded that the temple was defeated and the party is now in charge. He protested the plundering of his bedroom and told the party the ritual texts were important to him. The texts suggest he knew more about the living water elemental than he admitted. After Zephyr's threat he became frantically compliant ("yes, yes, of course, of course").

### Dren
A subordinate who manages security for the party's lair, reporting on visitors with suspicion and checking them for weapons. He bristles when Zephyr gives him orders, noting that Zephyr and he are peers. He takes a 10% cut of the party's treasure. He is comically wary of booby-trapped gifts, worrying that casks might explode and chests might turn into mimics, and offers to wait "just outside" if the chest transforms.

### Lucius Graeme
A soft-looking, gray man who smiles frequently. He acts as a conduit between Nulb and the temple leadership, sent by Barkinar to ensure the new management has everything it needs. He provides luxury goods and magical items for purchase, and offers to hold rare items for a deposit. He laughed off the pat-down and flatters the party as "the cleverest thing to happen here in a year." He insists that shopping is beneath the leadership and that he will procure what they need. He is firm that only the healing potions are free, citing his own suppliers and Lord Hedrack. He keeps a limited stock on hand and fills special orders in 2–3 days.

### Barkinar
A high-ranking figure who sent Lucius to support the party. Referenced as the man who had four problems in the morning that were all gone after the party's victory, and who warned the party about Senshock. Described as "the guy between you and the next layer of management" and as the former head of the Earth Temple before his promotion. According to Lucius, he "never smiles."

### Lord Hedrack
A high-ranking authority within the temple hierarchy, referenced by Lucius as having made it clear that magical items are not to be given away for free.

## Items

Three drugged fruits found in Brother Eelrich's chambers and offered with "just a bite!" The party declined to eat them and took all three.

### Spell Scroll of Resist Poison

### Spell Scroll of True Seeing

### Spell Scroll of Resist Energy (Fire)
A defensive spell scroll discovered by the party while plundering the Water Temple desk.

Four books detailing the elemental evil of water, found in the locked chests in Brother Eelrich's bedroom. Each is worth 250 gold pieces. Reading them raised suspicions about how much Brother Eelrich knew about the living water elemental.

### Ceremonial Bronze Mace
A decorative ceremonial weapon found among the spoils in the Water Temple bedroom.

### Gold Ring with a Pearl
A piece of valuable jewelry recovered from a locked chest in the priest's quarters.

### Potions of Water Breathing
Two magical elixirs found in the temple that allow the consumer to breathe underwater. The party noted they may not be straightforwardly useful against a living water elemental. Sequoia had hoped the potion would "just give you air," but the DM explained that the water itself is a living elemental intelligence.

### Cask of Excellent Wine
A gift of genuinely good wine brought by Lucius Graeme for the household to celebrate the party's new positions of power. It was held outside until Dren was told to bring it in, and Zephyr poured the first cup for Lucius.

### Potion of Superior Healing
Potent restorative tonics gifted to each member of the household by Lucius Graeme as a gesture of goodwill. One each, "for the household," explicitly a free gift.

### Shortsword +1
Magically enhanced blades purchased for one thousand gold pieces each, procured by Lucius Graeme to improve the wielders' accuracy and effectiveness in combat. Chosen instead of a Dagger of Venom, which the party already owns.

### Ring of Protection
A powerful magical ring available from Lucius that enhances the wearer's armor class and saving throws. Discussed as a strong option particularly for the monk who cannot wear armor; Zinnia bought one, priced at 3,500 gold pieces.

### Ring of Invisibility
A very expensive ring priced at 10,000 gold pieces. Lucius offered to reserve it for Sequoia for a deposit of 4,000 gold pieces.

### Ring of Free Action
A very expensive ring priced at 20,000 gold pieces that allows the wearer to move normally through difficult terrain and grants immunity to magical effects that reduce speed, paralyze, or restrain. Lucius offered to hold it for Zephyr for a deposit of 4,000 gold pieces. Zephyr refused the deposit.

### Staff of Striking
A very rare item in Lucius's stock, priced at 21,000 gold pieces and far beyond the party's means. It grants +3 to attack and damage rolls and has 10 charges. On a melee hit the wielder can expend up to 3 charges, each dealing an extra 1d6 force damage.

### Other Wares in Lucius's Chest
Boots of Levitation (suggested for Zinnia), a Hat of Disguise, a Ring of Feather Falling (Zephyr considered it and waited), a Potion of Speed, and a Spell Scroll of Teleport. None were purchased this session.

## Spells

A protective spell found in scroll form on the desk in the Water Temple, added to the party's inventory for future use.

### Resist Energy (Fire)

### True Seeing
A spell scroll found in the Water Temple that allows the caster to perceive the true nature of creatures and detect hidden things such as traps. It can only be cast once from the scroll.

### Greater Restoration
A powerful healing spell chosen by Calmer upon reaching level nine, intended to protect the party from debilitating conditions in future encounters. It was picked over Commune, Scrying (favoured by Zephyr for spying on "the other bosses"), and Summon Celestial.
