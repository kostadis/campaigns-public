# Chapter 30 Attrition

Date: 2026-05-10

## Summary
Things were not going well for the party as they made their way through the temple. The session opened with word from one of Hartsch's soldiers that "Hartsch needs you badly." Hartsch was the man the party had promoted to lead the Earth Temple by killing Romag. Zephyr shrugged that they didn't really need him anymore, "but… I guess we can go soon," and the group set off through the Air Temple.

Along the way they stepped over looted bodies while goblins scrambled past them toward Kelno's Air Temple, arms full of plunder, too desperate to care about the adventurers in their midst. The door to the Water Temple bore burn marks, evidence that the Fire Temple had tried to attack it.

Once inside the Air Temple, the party learned they had never been through the Greater Temple and decided to see it. They pressed on toward the Greater Temple entrance, only to find the massive door barricaded from the other side, as if someone were holding it shut. The Wraith watched them with gleaming amusement. It was a malevolent undead creature feasting on the carnage of the ongoing war, and it was too busy with the dead to bother with them yet. "Your turn will come! Don't worry, my little pets! Your turn will come," it taunted. Sequoia answered dryly, "Lucky me." Calmer, meanwhile, insisted he could "so totally kill him!"

When Calmer began hammering at the sealed door, the Wraith only laughed harder. Zephyr asked what was happening and backed it with a persuasive 23. The Wraith then thanked the party for all the bodies they had made. Seven specters materialized from the shadows, raised mostly from fallen Earth Temple soldiers, and the Wraith reveled in his growing power.

Then a cold, disembodied voice from behind the door declared: "The Greater Temple is sealed until the battle is over. No one gets in, no one gets out." The Wraith laughed harder than ever. This, it explained, was simply how things worked: every so often the lesser temples tore each other apart, and the Greater Temple locked its doors and waited, leaving everyone else trapped inside. The Greater Temple, it said, was "the deep temple… the people who are really in charge." When Zephyr said he'd like to meet them, the voice from behind the door offered only a chilling reply: survive the fight, and maybe they would get their chance.

Zephyr briefly floated camping out next to the door. The DM warned that the Wraith, now with seven specters, would be tempted to treat them as food. With no way forward, the party made the wise decision to retreat to the first level before they became the next course in the Wraith's feast. The Wraith called after them: "I can't wait till you come back here! I'll be waiting!"

The Earth Temple guards' first reaction was to start shooting arrows, but they recognized the group at once, and their leader called a halt. "STOP! Stop! Don't fight!" he shouted, then demanded, "Where the hell have you been?" The guards were haggard and shaken. After the party had opened the door for them, he said, they had disappeared, and things had not gone well. Hartsch needed the party immediately.

They found Hartsch at the temple, exhausted, bloodied, and barely holding himself together, surrounded by far fewer soldiers than before. The muffled boom of distant battle faded into the ragged breaths of the survivors. Hartsch looked to the party with desperate eyes and asked what they should do next.

The party steadied him, urging patience and strategy over panic. Zephyr first proposed waiting out the war. Hartsch balked: "But that… but that is Romag's passive strategy!" Zephyr then suggested Hartsch lead half the troops at the vanguard while the party held the territory. That drew funny looks. The bloodied Earth Temple survivors associated the party with the faction that had started the war, and they needed a reason to keep believing in them. Zephyr reversed course: the party would be at the vanguard and make a final stand.

Zinnia asked which temple was strongest. The Water Temple, came the answer; the Air Temple was the weakest, with the Fire Temple next. A plan took shape: strike the Air Temple first, take its treasure, and then see about the Water Temple. A relieved Hartsch agreed to hold the Earth Temple through the night, as it was the most heavily defended part of their territory, and attack in the morning.

The party retired to Romag's old room alongside Hartsch, confident in the plan. When Zephyr suggested a meeting with the senior officers, Sequoia wondered whether any were still alive. The exhausted Hartsch put the discussion off until morning.

But when morning came, Hartsch was nowhere to be found. Only a note remained in his office: "I couldn't wait! I sent the Broken Blades to attack!" At the temple there were fewer people than before. Hartsch admitted that there had been another attack in the night and that he had sent the mercenaries to raid the Water Temple without hearing back. "You have to do something!" he pleaded. Zephyr slapped him.

Zephyr's first plan was for Hartsch to take ten troops to find the Blades while the party hit the Water Temple from the other side. An Insight check then let Zinnia see the worse truth: Hartsch had told the Blades the orders came from the party. Realizing the danger, the party dropped the two-pronged attack and went straight to Captain Dren and the mercenaries, while Hartsch would advance from the east.

The party descended back to the second level to find their mercenaries, passing the Wraith once more. Zephyr told it he didn't have time for this. The Wraith wanted something in exchange: live bodies, or word of where the next attack was coming so it could feast on the dead. The party told it the next attack would fall on the Water Temple. The Wraith was unimpressed, saying it had "feasted on them enough" and had hoped for Earth Temple blood. Still, calculating and hungry, it smiled and let them pass.

What they found on the other side was grim. Captain Dren Halveth, the leader of the Broken Blades, grabbed the party by the arm with desperation in his eyes. Behind him, the surviving mercenaries stared blankly at the carnage around them, hands slick with gore, the stench of flesh thick in the air. Zephyr told Dren plainly that Hartsch, not the party, had sent them. Dren raged that they were "being fed to a meat grinder."

Zinnia stepped forward and rallied them with fierce conviction, gaining advantage for having shown up and rolling a 27. The battered mercenaries pledged to fight on: "whatever your plans are, you are with us. We are with you." Zephyr offered them rest in the Earth Temple, or a place at the party's side if they were true warriors.

To return to the Earth Temple, the Broken Blades had to get past the Wraith, which held the strategic choke point and was "causing us pain" with its seven specters. The party decided it was time to deal with the Wraith. The Blades offered to help, but Calmer declined, saying he needed no help to deal with the undead. He also hinted that it would be better if they did not see him call on St. Cuthbert's power. So the Blades held back while the party handled the undead.

"Oh, you're back!" the Wraith greeted them. Calmer had pre-buffed before walking in, and he strode into the chamber first, his divine power already stirring around him in a radiant aura that scorched the undead simply by proximity. Sequoia won initiative with a 25, struck fast and hard, and cut down a specter with 23 damage before it could react. Zephyr, an assassin striking a foe that had not yet acted, followed with a devastating 33-point blow that felled another, then nicked a third for 6.

The Wraith, furious, flew 60 feet into Calmer's aura. It failed its save, was slowed, and took 12 radiant damage. It then struck Calmer with a critical hit of necrotic energy so severe that the cleric was left with 3 hit points. That necrotic damage could not be healed, leaving his body unable to mend itself past the brink of death.

Yet even in that dire state, Calmer refused to fall. When a specter tried to slip out of his aura, he lashed it with the Gift of the Gem Dragon, a burst of force that dealt 15 damage, hurled it backward, and left it barely holding together. The specters' attacks on Sequoia and Zephyr all missed, one with a natural 1, and the aura burned them again.

Then, with the battle hanging on a knife's edge, Calmer called upon every ounce of his faith and invoked the full power of St. Cuthbert against the undead. A blinding wave of divine energy swept through the chamber. Only one specter made its Wisdom save; the Wraith did not. The Wraith and nearly all of the specters were obliterated in an instant.

It was the third time Calmer had attempted this feat, and the first time it had worked, and he made absolutely sure everyone knew it. "It worked!" he cried. Then: "Who's the man?" he shouted into the smoldering silence. "Who is the man?" "That's you," Zephyr answered. Zinnia finished off the last surviving specter with a series of precise strikes, and the passage was finally, gloriously clear.

Calmer, stuck at 3 hit points, would be out of action for a while and would have to stay with Hartsch. But the Earth Temple had won a huge strategic victory. There were now two ways down, and one of them was no longer guarded by an undead creature that killed enemies and allies alike and turned them into specters.

The party told the Broken Blades the coast was clear. As they regrouped and prepared to return to the first level, a panicked bugbear came stumbling into view. He clutched a sack of coins and babbled about Kelno's promise of safety and gold. He was one of the Earth Temple's own, part of their defensive perimeter, and Kelno was making a play for him.

Zephyr reminded him who the most powerful temple was. Calmer, getting far too into his Earth Temple cleric persona, bellowed, "Don't mess with me, man! I just killed the Wraith!" and proclaimed that this was how the Earth Temple ruled, "with power and force!" The party was not inclined toward mercy. Sequoia ran the bugbear down and cut him apart before he could escape, including a critical hit. Standing over the body, Sequoia delivered a dry declaration: "One does not simply take bribes to defect." The coins recovered from the bugbear, 20 gold pieces, were stamped with Air Temple insignia; the gnolls had been the ones doing the bribing.

Those very gnolls emerged moments later from a secret door. They were furious that their investment had been killed: "We paid good money for that! Now we will get our money, and get food!" Sequoia quipped, "Didn't know you guys liked eating while dead, but okay." Dren offered to take Calmer upstairs and protect him. Calmer roared, "I WILL STAY AND FIGHT!" Sequoia's answer was blunt: "Somebody knock her out and take her upstairs." Dren and his two mercenaries physically wrestled the protesting cleric away from the battle despite his outraged cries of "Unhand me, you creatures!"

The three gnolls lunged at the remaining party members with savage hunger. One of them eyed Zephyr with particular enthusiasm and snarled about how long it had been since it had tasted tiefling flesh. Zephyr shot back an answer the DM heard as "Come and get it." The gnoll managed only an 8-point wound on Zephyr.

The fight was swift and brutal, before the gnolls could do any real damage. Zephyr and Zinnia dropped the first gnoll between them. Sequoia, after missing both opening swings, killed the second. Zephyr finished the third with a 30-damage strike.

With the gnolls dead and the secret door to the Air Temple now exposed, the party stood at a crossroads. The passage was open, but Calmer was gone, dragged to safety by Dren's mercenaries, and without a healer, pushing forward felt reckless with only three of them. Zephyr was ready to go on without Calmer and explore the Air Temple; Sequoia wanted to wait for their healer. The DM reminded them of their ridiculous stealth, Zephyr called it a recon mission, and the DM agreed it could be a pure one; there the matter rested.

The strategic picture, at least, had shifted in their favor. The Wraith was destroyed, the passage to the second level was open, and the Earth Temple now had two routes to press its advantage. The war was far from over, but for the first time, the odds felt like they might be turning.

The party's cleric of St. Cuthbert, operating under an Earth Temple cleric persona.

- He keeps the Broken Blades away from the fight so they won't see him call on St. Cuthbert's power.
- He is reduced to 3 hit points by unhealable necrotic damage, then turns undead successfully on his third try, destroying the Wraith.
- Afterward he leans hard into the Earth Temple persona, and he has to be dragged off the field by Dren's mercenaries.

### Hartsch
The leader of the Earth Temple, promoted by the party after Romag's death. He is overwhelmed, panicked, and struggling to maintain control of his dwindling forces. He shows poor judgment by secretly sending the Broken Blades on a raid, falsely attributing the orders to the party.

- He summons the party with an urgent message through one of his soldiers.
- He rejects waiting out the war as "Romag's passive strategy" and doubts the wisdom of leading the vanguard himself, crying, "It's all gone to hell!"
- After agreeing to an Air Temple assault, he vanishes overnight and leaves the note. He then begs the party, "You have to do something!" and is slapped by Zephyr.

### Captain Dren Halveth
The captain of the Broken Blades mercenaries. He is found battered and desperate after his men were decimated in a poorly planned raid ordered by Hartsch. He later assists the party by forcibly escorting the severely wounded Calmer to safety during the gnoll ambush.

- He grabs the party by the arm, accuses them of not warning him it would be a slaughterhouse, and calls Hartsch "a madman."
- He removes Calmer with the help of two of his mercenaries.

### The Broken Blades
A group of mercenaries recruited by the party who were nearly wiped out after being sent on a suicide mission by Hartsch. Their morale is restored by Zinnia, and they pledge to fight alongside the party.

- They pledge, "whatever your plans are, you are with us. We are with you."
- They identify the Wraith's choke point as the obstacle to their retreat and offer to help fight it ("Do you need our help, boss?"), but Calmer turns them down.

### Earth Temple Guards
Haggard first-level guards whose first reaction is to start shooting arrows at the party, until they recognize them and their leader calls a halt. Their leader demands to know where the party has been and sends them to Hartsch.

### The Defecting Bugbear
A member of the Earth Temple's defensive perimeter, bribed by the Air Temple with gold and promises of safety. He is killed by Sequoia.

### Air Temple Gnolls
Three gnolls who emerge from the secret door to reclaim their bribe and eat the party. One of them singles out Zephyr for his tiefling flesh. All three are killed.

### Kelno
The Air Temple's prefect, named as offering bribes of gold and safety to Earth Temple forces in order to lure them into defecting. The DM notes he has been "busily recruiting." Looting goblins are seen racing toward his Air Temple.

### The Greater Temple Voice
An unseen voice from behind the barricaded Greater Temple door. It announces that the Greater Temple is sealed until the battle is over, and tells the party to survive the fight if they want to meet those in charge.

## Locations
### Air Temple
Kelno's temple on the second level, currently filled with looted bodies and roaming goblins. It contains the entrance to the Greater Temple and was haunted by a powerful Wraith until the party destroyed it this chapter. A secret door, where the Air Temple gnolls emerged, leads into it.

- The DM pointed out its secret door and a dark hole that serves as the exit up to the first level.
- Goblins laden with loot were seen streaming toward it.

### Greater Temple Entrance
A barricaded doorway that has been sealed shut from the inside to isolate the Greater Temple from the ongoing conflict between the lesser elemental factions. A mysterious voice from within told the party to survive the fight if they wished to meet those in charge.

- The door is being physically held shut from the other side.
- The Wraith describes what lies beyond as "the deep temple," the people "really in charge."

### Earth Temple
The headquarters for Hartsch's forces on the first level. It is the most defensible location in the area, currently a grim scene of ragged survivors and muffled sounds of distant battle, and serves as the party's base of operations.

- It suffered a further attack on the night the party rested there, leaving fewer defenders by morning.
- Its perimeter includes bugbears whom Kelno is trying to bribe away.

### Romag's Room
The party's headquarters within the Earth Temple, used for resting and private meetings. The party slept there alongside Hartsch "because we're, like, apostles."

### Water Temple
The target of Hartsch's ill-fated raid and considered the most powerful of the elemental factions. Its door bears burn marks from an attempted Fire Temple attack.

### Second Level Passage
A strategic choke point previously guarded by the Wraith and its specters, now cleared by the party, opening a second route down for the Earth Temple forces.

## Items
### Hartsch's Note
A message left in Hartsch's office: “I couldn't wait! I sent the Broken Blades to attack!” It names no target. Hartsch named the Water Temple in person, and Zinnia worked out from an Insight check that he had told the Blades the orders came from the party.

### Bribery Money
Twenty gold pieces stamped with Air Temple insignia, found on the body of the defecting bugbear. The coins were payment from Air Temple gnolls to convince the bugbear to abandon his post and defect. Sequoia took the coins. The gnolls emerged soon after, demanding their money back.

## Spells
### Spiritual Weapon
A divine manifestation of Calmer's faith, summoned before entering combat for the confrontation with the Wraith and its specters.

### Spirit Guardians
A protective aura of radiant spirits summoned by Calmer that slowed the Wraith and dealt consistent radiant damage to undead enemies who entered its golden light throughout the battle.

- The Wraith failed its save on entering the aura, was slowed, and took 12 radiant damage.
- Specters took 6 damage on a successful save and 12 on a failure.
- Zephyr was positioned to stay inside the aura's protection while attacking.
- The spell had ended by the time the party confronted the bugbear.

### Turn Undead
A powerful divine invocation used by Calmer that successfully destroyed the Wraith and several of its spectral servants in a single use, after having failed in previous attempts during earlier sessions.

- Every undead in range made a Wisdom save. Only one specter succeeded; the Wraith failed.
- The DM then added Calmer's searing ability (Sear Undead on his sheet: each undead that fails the save takes 4d8 radiant damage). The Wraith and the specters that failed died.

### Gift of the Gem Dragon
A reactive burst of force energy used by Calmer against a specter moving away from his aura. It forced the creature to make a saving throw, and on its failure, dealt significant force damage and pushed the specter away.

- The DM read the trigger as taking damage from a creature within 10 feet, using a reaction.
- The save is Strength. On a failure the target takes 2d8 force damage and is pushed up to 10 feet.
- In this use the specter took 15 damage and was left at 3 hit points.
