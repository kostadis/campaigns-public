# Chapter 29 Opening Battles

Date: 2026-03-29

## Summary
The party emerged onto the second level of the temple to find a battlefield already drenched in chaos. Scalding steam billowed through the corridors, and the bodies of fallen warriors from both the Fire and Water temples littered the ground. The steam could serve as cover, but anyone standing mostly inside it would be burned. Through the haze, Fire Temple bugbears and an evil cultist held a barricade against a stalled Earth Temple advance, and the Obsidian Edge saw their opportunity. Zephyr had struck first, closing in on the evil cultist with a dagger. Sequoia loosed an arrow from outside the steam for 19 damage, and Zinnia followed close behind, cutting down the evil cultist at the front of the line before the bugbears even knew what had hit them.

The bugbears retaliated with savage fury. One landed a critical hit on Zinnia for 25 damage that left him reeling, and the others struck at Zephyr and swung at Sequoia, though the swing at Sequoia missed. But Calmer turned the tide in a single, thunderous moment. Unable to reach the barricaded double doors on foot, he channeled divine light into a Guiding Bolt that smashed them wide open, allowing the Earth Temple bugbears to pour through. With the barricade broken and the mission accomplished, the party pressed their advantage. Zephyr cut down another bugbear with a flurry of precise strikes, and Sequoia delivered a crushing 32-damage sneak attack that finished off another, leaving the remaining enemies caught between two warring factions. A sharp read of the battlefield showed them that the scalding pools were magical, the product of elemental spells. Recognizing that the battlefield was far too dangerous to linger in, the party disengaged and retreated before the Earth Temple charge hit.

Their escape route led them through the Air Temple, where their least favorite resident was waiting: a malevolent wraith that haunted the corridors with cold menace. Calmer, driven by his deep religious hatred of the undead, tried to repel the creature with the power of his faith, but the wraith resisted. Zephyr managed to wound it with a pistol shot, though its resistance to piercing halved the damage to 5. He then dashed toward a secret passage, and the rest of the party followed in a desperate sprint. Calmer tried once more to turn it before his own furious retreat, and it resisted again; as he ran past, the wraith struck him hard for 16 damage, and its mocking laughter echoed behind them as they scrambled upstairs to safety. On the way, Zinnia noticed a door to the east opening and closing, as if someone were watching. Upstairs, the temple seemed to be at war with itself, and a stray fire bolt scorched Calmer for 8 more damage.

The party retreated to their own territory to regroup and wait out the war, but the respite was short-lived. The acrid stench of decay crept through the corridors from the south, and four ghouls lurched into view. Their sunken faces were eerily familiar: they resembled recently dead members of the Earth Temple, clearly raised by dark necrotic magic. Sequoia set a trap at the door to slow their advance; it sprang, and the ghouls shambled forward. Zephyr moved with lethal precision, using his assassin training to drive a dagger into the nearest ghoul for 27 damage before it could react, dropping it instantly. Then Calmer, his holy bloodlust fully ignited, channeled the full force of his divine wrath and sent the remaining ghouls fleeing in terror, cursing them with furious religious fervor, while Sequoia finished off a wounded ghoul with a melee strike. A religion check reminded the party that priests of St. Cuthbert are priests of war: this frenzy was Calmer's natural element. The party then weighed their next move. They could tip the battle toward one faction or let the temples bleed each other dry. They chose instead to use the chaos as cover to seek the Deep Temple.

After a brief rest to tend their wounds, the party descended once more into the temple's dimly lit corridors, pushing deeper while the sounds of battle raged around them. They passed the wraith yet again on the way down. The stone floors were scattered with signs of hurried looting: broken bottles, discarded armor, and blood trails that led nowhere. Through the haze, they glimpsed goblins and ghouls dragging weapons and bags of coin through hidden passages, and watched goblins escape through a secret door. The party realized with grim clarity that Kelno, the seemingly meek Air Temple cleric, had been quietly sending his goblins to scavenge the dead for his own purposes. All four temples were now entangled in the conflict, and the war had grown far more complicated than it first appeared.

Pressing further north, the party stumbled upon a massive, echoing hall that had once been the scene of great and terrible celebrations. The floor was strewn with bones and the wreckage of furniture, while the walls were hung with tapestries depicting scenes of raiding and violence. Grim trophies decorated the stone: mummified heads, torn battle standards, and a broken broadsword. Broad steps of dull black stone descended from the north wall into shadow. The party ignored a steam-shrouded fight to their right and headed north. There they witnessed two fire snakes and a wounded salamander locked in brutal combat with four water gargoyles amidst eruptions of scalding steam. Zephyr briefly considered snatching one combatant to question about the Deep Temple. But no one spoke Primordial, and the elemental creatures were not intelligent enough to bargain with, so the party wisely chose to slip away rather than wade into another fight.

Moving northeast through the corridors, the party discovered something unexpected: a chamber full of chained human prisoners huddled against the wall, trembling in their shackles and pleading not to be abandoned. The prisoners appeared to have been gathered as sacrifices or subjects for dark rituals, and they had now seen the party sneaking around. One among them spoke up with a desperate bargain: he knew the way to the Deep Temple, but he would only talk if they freed him first. Zinnia watched him carefully and determined he was telling the truth. Sequoia made short work of the iron shackles, while the other four prisoners, still chained, screamed to be freed too. Asked how to reach the Deep Temple, the freed man laughed, because nobody *wants* to go there. The freed man, a former member of the Air Temple, then revealed that Kelno himself had known of a secret door hidden within the Air Temple that led to the lower levels. Zephyr's intimidation looked set to fall flat, but backed by Calmer's imposing presence it convinced the man that pushing his luck further would be unwise, and the prisoner pointed out the exact location of the passage to the Greater Temple. With the secret of the Deep Temple finally in hand, the Obsidian Edge prepared to descend into the darkness below. Before they could go down, a runner from the Earth Temple found them. They had to get back to Hartsch. Things were not going well.

### 029.01 The Scalding Battlefield
#### The party arrives on the second level of the temple, finding themselves in the middle of a chaotic clash between Fire and Earth temple forces amidst scalding steam.
- The party observes Fire Temple bugbears holding a barricade against an Earth Temple advance. The defenders are one evil cultist and four bugbears.

- Zephyr initiates the assault: last session he closed in on the evil cultist (AC 13) with a dagger for 8 damage. The DM corrected the read-aloud recap: "Actually, it was Zephyr who attacked first."
- Sequoia fires his bow from outside the steam and hits for 19 damage. He chose to "be here and just attack" rather than step into the steam.
- Zinnia's Roll20 screen went white, so the DM shared the map over Zoom.
- Zinnia rushes into the mist and kills the evil cultist with a series of strikes. The cultist had AC 13, and Zinnia used both attacks on it.
- Zinnia asks about pulling back. The DM rules that disengaging as a bonus action costs a chi point. Zinnia chooses to hold position.
- The DM confirms the party is 6th level.
- Bugbears retaliate, severely wounding Zinnia and also striking Zephyr.
  - The first bugbear lands a critical hit on Zinnia for 25 damage.
  - A second bugbear also hits Zinnia but deals only 4.
  - A third runs to attack Sequoia with its morning star and "misses completely."
  - The fourth hits Zephyr for 9.
- The DM puts Calmer's choice to the players: heal someone, or try to open the barricaded double doors now that two bugbears have left them. The players choose: "Calmer to open."
- Calmer uses a bolt of guiding light to smash open a set of double doors, allowing Earth Temple forces to charge the barricade. He can't reach the doors on foot, so he blasts them open from range with Guiding Bolt.
- The Earth Temple bugbears are added to initiative and act alongside the other bugbears.
- Zephyr asks whether the trapped Fire Temple bugbears will surrender. The DM says they will probably try to run, but evil cultists don't suddenly convert. He adds that the Earth Temple allies are watching.
- Zephyr kills a bugbear with a series of precise attacks.
  - The bugbear has AC 16, and Zephyr gets sneak attack because Zinnia is adjacent.
  - His first attack deals 20 damage, his second misses, and his third deals 9 to kill it.
- Sequoia delivers a lethal blow to another bugbear, clearing the immediate threat.
  - The DM moves Calmer adjacent to grant Sequoia sneak attack.
  - The strike totals 32 damage after some Roll20 sneak-attack dice confusion. The DM explains that damage can be clicked from the Roll20 sheet after the attack roll.
- The DM reminds the party: "you guys did your mission, so you don't literally have to fight here anymore… Your goal was to free up the barricade, you've done that."
- Realizing the environmental hazards are magical and the battle is escalating, the party decides to disengage and retreat from the crossfire.
  - An insight/investigation roll (Zinnia rolls 24) shows that the scalding pools come from the temples' elemental spellcasting. "The last place you want to be is here, when the Earth Temple sort of charges in."
  - Zinnia takes the Disengage action to avoid an attack of opportunity.
- The Earth Temple bugbears charge in screaming. Calmer doesn't want to see the results, and the party slams the door behind them as they enter the Air Temple.

### Party Territory
A relatively secure area where the party attempts to rest, though it is eventually breached by scavenging ghouls that have come to collect dead bodies. It has two doors, which Zephyr suggested locking. The ghouls came from the south.

### Looted Second-Level Corridors
Chill, dimly lit passages strewn with signs of hurried looting: broken bottles, discarded armor, and "blood that leads nowhere." Goblins move through hidden passages here, including a secret door they used to escape.

### The Hall of Trophies
A massive, echoing chamber filled with the wreckage of furniture and decorated with tapestries depicting scenes of raiding and violence, as well as gruesome trophies like mummified heads, torn battle standards, and a broken broadsword. Broad steps of dull black stone in the north wall lead to a depressing descending stair. The floor is littered with skeletons, skulls, and bones, and dozens of empty sconces line the walls. Passages lead north, east, and west.

### Prisoners' Chamber
A grim room where human prisoners are kept in shackles, awaiting their fate as sacrifices or subjects for necrotic spells. The party discovers and frees one prisoner here who reveals the location of the secret door to the Deep Temple. The chamber lies off an eastern passage reached while heading north. It is probably not where the prisoners were originally held; someone brought them here.
