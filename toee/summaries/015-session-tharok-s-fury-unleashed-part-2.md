# Chapter 15 Tharok's Fury Unleashed, Part 2

Date: 2025-09-21

## Summary
The battle against High Cultist Tharok reached its thunderous conclusion deep within the collapsing ritual cavern. Warda bellowed "DIE, YOU CREEPY CREATURE!" and hacked at Tharok with her blades. Her longsword missed twice before her shortsword finally drew blood. Tharok raged and taunted the party, singling out Zephyr as "one of the demon spawn" who should have stood at his side. Zephyr answered that he had picked his side and darted in with a precise dagger strike, his sneak attack lifting the hit from 6 to 12 damage. Tharok tore into Warda for a crushing 29 points of damage, but his follow-up blows went wide. Calmer called down a searing Guiding Bolt that left the high cultist reeling and vulnerable. Zinnia seized the opening with a rapid and relentless series of unarmed strikes, rolling three sevens in a row along the way, and finally brought Tharok crashing to the ground. As the dark magic dissipated, the ritual cavern began to groan and crumble around them. After a brief hesitation over whether the artifact might be evil, the party snatched up the Chest of Rao, which Sequoia had knocked from the altar, and fled into the tunnels beyond.

The rest of the abbey remained eerily untouched as the party emerged from the collapsing passages. They briefly considered exploring further in hopes of treasure, until Warda reminded them that "the only safe place is the exit," and Calmer concurred. They made their way back to the library. There, they found Sister Alina dead and Brother Eldin barely standing, his wounds deep and his face pale. When Eldin learned that Tharok had fallen, a wave of relief washed over him, and he declared that the Abbey of Beory was safe once more. Apologizing that he could not give more, he pressed a generous reward into the party's hands: 400 copper, 400 silver, and 70 gold pieces each, a magical scroll capable of conjuring binding webs, and a potion of greater healing.

Brother Eldin's eyes fell upon the Chest of Rao, and his expression grew grave. He sat the party down and explained the weight of what they carried. Rao was the patron of peace, reason, serenity, truth, and harmony, and the chest had been crafted by his high priests and imbued with tremendous holy power. The Kingdom of Veluna was a theocracy led by the High Priest of Rao, the Canon of Veluna. Beneath him stood a proud hierarchy of seven dioceses, each jointly governed by a bishop of Rao and a noble secular family. When Zephyr asked about using the chest's magical energy, Eldin laughed. He explained that this was no artifact of parlor tricks but a tool for Rao's priests to commune with their god. Eldin warned the party plainly that possessing the artifact would draw danger from every direction, both from those who wished to corrupt it and from those who wished to reclaim it. He urged them to return it to whoever wanted it back as swiftly as possible.

Before they set out, the party turned over the tangle of factions all hungry for the chest. They knew that Lareth the Beautiful, the Temple of Elemental Evil, and the Archclericy of Veluna all coveted it (the chest had been meant as a gift for the Kingdom of Celene before it went missing). They also knew that two treacherous merchants in town, Rannos Davl and Gremag, were secret operatives of the temple who had already tried to have them killed. Zephyr floated the idea of holding "one big bid" and inviting everyone. Sequoia instead proposed using the chest as bait to walk into each enemy's office and kill them. They settled on starting with the two merchants. After a day and a half of travel, the party arrived back in the village of Hommlet and devised a gambit: they sent a child with a note proposing a meeting at the crossroads behind the carpenter's house, dangling the chest as bait. Rannos claimed to have "no idea" what they were talking about, declined the open-air meeting, and instead insisted they come to his establishment after dark. The party immediately recognized the response as the setup for an ambush.

Rather than walk into a trap, the party stopped first at the Inn of the Welcome Wench. There, the warm and cheerful Ostler Gundigoot welcomed them back with a beer for Zephyr, soup for Zinnia, and, after some coaxing, a one-gold-piece Honey Haven mead for Sequoia. He mentioned, with genuine gratitude, that the trade roads had reopened and that the mead had returned to his shelves. He credited this to some very kind travelers who had cleared out the bandits, as he had heard from Glennon and Pasmarie Clee. Mindful that temple agents might be listening, the party played it cool, neither confirming nor denying anything, and quietly laid their plans over their cups. They decided to leave the Chest of Rao somewhere safe. They would then lure Thorne, known as the Duke, into the stables of the merchant establishment, where they would be waiting. The Duke was the third operative working alongside Rannos and Gremag.

As night fell, the party slipped into position in the stables, hidden and ready. They had poured oil to light if needed and debated whether to use the web scroll. Overhearing him, they caught the Duke muttering bitterly that "those damn bastards not only are alive, but they've also got the chest." The Duke came to investigate a noise and walked straight into the ambush. The party fell upon him in a coordinated surprise round, striking hard and fast before he could raise an alarm. Zephyr and Zinnia tore into him and left him at just 10 of his 58 hit points. An intimidation attempt fell flat, but Sequoia delivered a final blow that left the Duke crumpled and unconscious on the stable floor. Calmer quickly tended to his wounds to keep him alive, because they had questions that needed answering.

When the Duke came to, he groaned, "I knew this was a bad idea," and asked why he was still alive. He found himself surrounded and very much at the party's mercy. He wasted little time in dropping any pretense of loyalty, admitting freely that he was no true believer, just a man of deeply questionable ethics willing to work for whoever paid. He had been told to follow the party and help set up the earlier ambush at the tavern. He revealed that Rannos and Gremag were in a panic now that the ambush had failed, desperate to recover the chest before their superiors in the temple turned on them. When Sequoia suggested they might decapitate the temple's leadership, the Duke laughed and told them they were dealing with "lowly operatives." He encouraged the party to go kill the pair. With grim humor, he even asked whether they might knock him out again, reasoning that if the cultists found him alive afterward he would be a dead man anyway.

The party obliged his last request, after a fashion. They made their entrance into Rannos and Gremag's private office by hurling the Duke's unconscious body through the door ahead of them. Rannos looked down at the crumpled Duke with cold indifference and remarked that he had outlived his usefulness anyway. He added that the party "were supposed to have died earlier this week." Gremag said nothing, his eyes moving over the party with the calculating look of a man deciding how best to kill them. Rannos opened negotiations by offering the party their lives. Sequoia retorted that they alone knew where the chest was, and Rannos conceded that extracting the information from the dead "would be costly." The adventurers then demanded that the temple abandon Hommlet and the surrounding region entirely. Rannos drily observed that both sides had put on the table "the things that they're not going to get," and the parley collapsed almost before it had begun.

When Sequoia turned to walk out, Gremag snarled "I've had enough of this conversation" and lunged into violence with terrifying force. Zinnia met him head-on with two hard unarmed strikes for 17 damage. Gremag shrugged off the assault, laughed "Do you really think you will leave?", and retaliated with a brutal counterattack that left the monk crumpled and unconscious on the floor. The rest of the party pressed in from all sides. Zephyr and Sequoia cut at Gremag with their blades while Calmer summoned a spectral weapon to harry him from a distance. Rannos conjured a spectral weapon of his own and struck Calmer with it, but the cleric held his ground. When the moment came, it was Calmer who found his mark and brought the fearsome cultist down for good.

With Gremag dead, the party turned as one toward Rannos Davl, the quintessential cult fanatic. Sequoia wanted no more talk: "I'm just killing them, to be honest." Zephyr shifted position to grant him a sneak attack, and Sequoia moved in with swift and punishing strikes, landing a devastating 26-point blow that left the cult operative badly wounded and reeling. The session drew to a close with Rannos cornered, bleeding, and running out of options. The party stood over the wreckage of the temple's local operation, battered but very much alive, and one step closer to deciding the fate of the Chest of Rao.

## Scenes
### 015.01 The Fall of Tharok
#### The party concludes their brutal battle against High Cultist Tharok in the elemental ritual cavern.
- Warda shouts "DIE, YOU CREEPY CREATURE!" and attacks Tharok. Her longsword misses twice, and then her shortsword lands a blow for six points of damage.
- Tharok taunts Zephyr, claiming he should be on Tharok's side ("You are one of the demon spawn!"). Zephyr replies, "I picked this side," and Sequoia jeers that Tharok, being human, should be on theirs.
- Zephyr moves in with a dagger and delivers a precise strike against the high cultist (AC 16). He gets sneak attack because an ally is adjacent, for 12 damage total.
- Tharok rants, "You fools don't know what you're doing! You don't know what I am capable of!" Sequoia mocks him as "incredibly constipated."
- Tharok answers with a powerful strike on Warda for 29 points of damage. His second and third attacks miss.
- Calmer invokes a bolt of guiding light (Guiding Bolt, 9 damage) that strikes Tharok, leaving him vulnerable and granting Zinnia advantage on the next attack.
- Zinnia delivers a series of rapid strikes, ultimately landing the finishing blow that kills Tharok:
  - The first unarmed strike misses, and so does the advantage reroll. The second strike hits for 5 ("three sevens in a row").
  - A bonus attack follows, for 5 + 9 = 14 damage.
  - The DM rules that only the first attack benefits from advantage.
- The ritual cavern begins to crumble and collapse as the dark magic dissipates. The DM offers the choice to "Search, take your chances, or run," and Zinnia runs.
- The DM asks whether to take the chest or "leave it here to be buried once and for all, and no longer anybody's problem." Sequoia worries it might be "some evil thing," and the DM clarifies it is "nominally a good thing."
- The party decides to seize the Chest of Rao before fleeing the collapsing cavern.

- As a reward for their bravery, Brother Eldin grants the party copper, silver, and gold coins, a magical scroll of web, and a potion of greater healing.
  - The coins total 1,600 cp, 1,600 sp, and 280 gp, or 400 cp, 400 sp, and 70 gp each. Eldin apologizes for being unable to give more.
  - The scroll and potion are placed in D&D Beyond's new shared party inventory.

### 015.03 The Lore of the Chest of Rao
#### Brother Eldin explains the historical and religious significance of the Chest of Rao to the party.
- Brother Eldin warns the party that the Chest of Rao is a dangerous artifact that should not be kept for long: "This particular magic item is very dangerous. You do not want to possess it for very long."
- Zephyr asks him to "tell us all the lore."
- The party learns that Rao is the patron of peace, reason, serenity, truth, and harmony, able to inspire calm in the most belligerent of creatures. Veluna is a theocracy led by the High Priest of Rao, the Canon of Veluna, pastor of the faithful.
- Eldin describes the Archdiocese:
  - It is divided into seven dioceses, each jointly headed by a bishop of Rao and a member of one of seven noble secular families.
  - The Canon appoints an archbishop to administer it. The post is fiercely coveted as a stepping stone to succeeding the Canon.
- Temples of Rao are majestic public structures with towers at each quarter and one in the center, connected by walls and supported by flying buttresses: "a proud and powerful religion."
- Brother Eldin explains that the chest was built by the high priests of Rao and imbued with powerful magic and religious significance. In evil hands it could become a battery to summon "whatever Tharok was trying to summon."
- He warns that possessing the chest will draw danger from those who wish to use it for ill and those who wish to reclaim it for good.
- When Zephyr suggests using its magical energy, Eldin laughs. He explains that it is no parlor trick: Rao's priests use it to summon, control, and commune with their gods.
- The DM shows the regional map, including the Archclericy of Veluna. One option is to travel to Verbobonc to find priests of Rao.
- The party discusses various factions interested in the chest, including the Temple of Elemental Evil, Lareth the Beautiful, and the Archclericy of Veluna. The chest was meant as a gift for the Kingdom of Celene before it went missing.
- Other factions also want the chest:
  - The Earth Temple.
  - Burne and Rufus.
  - Those who were looking for it in Ostverk.
- Zephyr jokes about holding "one big bid" and inviting everyone. He rules out selling to Burne and Rufus because they "are already poor."
- Zephyr suggests returning it to Celene. The DM notes they could go to Ostverk and seek out the magic user who once offered them a quest (presumably Gonnathrad Kevanarial, the would-be "quest giver" of Chapter 10), or find somebody there willing to pay for the chest. Zephyr notes that "selling to anyone in the temple might be risky."
- The DM reminds the party that Lareth sent them after the chest, which gives them a pretext to walk into his office and kill him. Zephyr is reluctant ("He hasn't been any wrong to me"), but the DM points out that Lareth set them up "to go be his patsy."
- Sequoia proposes to "get rid of as many people as possible by saying, we have the chest, and then walking into their office and killing them." The DM notes that they could still hand the chest to Burne and Rufus afterward.
- Zephyr settles it: "We could start by the two merchants."

### 015.06 Ambush at the Stables
#### The party sets a trap in the merchants' stables, successfully capturing him after a brief, violent struggle.
- The party scouts the merchant establishment, noting that Rannos, Gremag, and the Duke are inside arguing. The DM marks out the layout: the main store, the stables, and the private office.
- Zephyr's plan is to make a sound from the stables, hide, and surprise whoever comes out. Since there is no door into the main area, the investigator would come through the other side.
- They hide in the stables and prepare an ambush, considering using oil and fire to trap their targets:
  - Zephyr pours oil to light later and suggests a ring of fire to trap the target.
  - The DM notes that the web scroll is likelier to immobilize, but that fire would burn through the web.
  - Both Zephyr and Calmer have Thaumaturgy.
  - The party has healed up beforehand.
- Sequoia asks whether a single arrow could kill the target. The DM warns: "No, he's… he's powerful… they are dangerous."
- The DM notes that the Duke is a hired hand rather than a true believer, unlike the other two, and could be pressured once wounded. The party settles on immobilizing rather than killing.
- On Insight (Zephyr rolls 13), Calmer relays the Duke's anxious muttering: "that idiot ran out, screwed everything up, and now those damn bastards not only are alive, but they've also got the chest."
- The party confirms the chest is not on them. Zephyr says they left it "with a… Tavern guy," and Sequoia says "somewhere."
- The Duke enters the stables to investigate and is immediately ambushed by the party in a surprise round. Initiative is Zephyr 20, Sequoia 17, Zinnia 6.
- Zephyr strikes the Duke with a series of rapid attacks, utilizing the element of surprise for heavy damage. His first hit deals 18 with sneak attack, his second also hits, and his third misses against AC 17.
- Sequoia's first attack misses amid Roll 20 dice trouble. Zinnia joins the assault, landing blows that leave the Duke severely wounded: both unarmed strikes hit, and a ki-fueled strike adds 8 more, for 23 total.
- Calmer swings his mace rather than casting a spell that might kill the Duke, and misses. The Duke is down to 10 of 58 hit points.
- With the Duke badly wounded, Zephyr (helped by Calmer) tries to intimidate him into yielding, but the attempt falls flat: Zephyr rolls 12, then 5 with Calmer's help.
- Sequoia delivers a final blow against AC 17 to knock the Duke unconscious rather than killing him.
- Calmer provides medical aid to the Duke to ensure he survives for questioning. Zephyr argues that waking him will tell them what's going on inside, and that his absence may panic the other two.

### 015.07 The Duke Talks
#### The party wakes the captured Duke and extracts information about Rannos Davl and Gremag.
- The Duke awakens and, realizing he is still alive, asks why he was spared: "Ugh, I knew this was a bad idea. Hey, wh-what… what do you want? Why am I still alive?"
- Zephyr: "Well, you're gonna help us." He suggests the Duke would "love to help us in killing" Gremag and Rannos. The Duke says "Yeah," and the DM notes he is "saying whatever he thinks is gonna keep him alive."
- The Duke explains his role: "I just work for Rannos and Gremag… I was just told to follow you, and then they asked me to help set up an ambush, so I set up an ambush." That was the earlier ambush at the tavern.
- The Duke reveals that Rannos and Gremag are paranoid and desperate to recover the Chest of Rao to appease their bosses: "The ambush failed. Nobody knows where the damn chest is, their bosses are freaking out." The party's note looked like their salvation.
- Sequoia suggests they hold "the key to basically just… kill half the leadership of the… Temple of Elemental Evil." The Duke laughs: "you obviously have no idea what you're dealing with… these are lowly operatives here." He allows it would be "one way to get rid of the local cultists."
- He admits he is merely a hired hand and not a true believer, and encourages the party to eliminate Rannos and Gremag: "I don't believe in all of this culty, we're gonna, you know, corrupt the universe. Those two lunatics believe in it."
- The Duke suggests the party knock him out again so he has plausible deniability if the cult comes looking. "If they find me alive after… you guys try to kill them, I'll be a dead man anyway. And quite honestly, I'd rather be a dead man with, with you guys, then, whatever the cult's planning to do with me afterwards."
- Zephyr asks whether the pair would ransom the Duke. "No… The only thing they want is the chest."
- Sequoia proposes turning the chest into a bomb ("If you connect two ends of a battery, it explodes"). The DM rules that the party is not powerful enough to convert its magic, but that rigging it as a trap for the temple might be possible later. The DM also notes they could give it to Lareth.
- Zephyr opts to negotiate: "I think we have the more power."

### 015.08 Confrontation at the Merchant's Office
#### The party enters the office of Rannos Davl and Gremag, leading to a tense negotiation that devolves into a bloody battle.
- The party enters the office and tosses the Duke's unconscious body onto the floor to shock the cultists.
- Rannos: "Well, it would appear that the Duke has finally outlived his usefulness." Gremag sizes the party up for an attack.
- Rannos dismisses the Duke's failure and demands the Chest of Rao, offering the party their lives in exchange:
  - Rannos: "You were supposed to have died earlier this week. But, you're here. And I need the chest."
  - Zephyr: "It's somewhere… What are you willing to offer?"
  - Rannos: "I'm willing to offer your lives."
  - Zephyr: "Don't make silly jokes."
- Sequoia notes that only the party knows where the chest is. Rannos concedes this is "awkward," since getting the information from the dead "would be costly," and asks, "What do you want?" Zinnia answers, "A lot," and Zephyr, "the world."
- The party attempts to negotiate for the cult to leave Hommlet and the surrounding area, which Rannos mocks. Sequoia: "We want the Temple of Elemental Evil to leave Hommlet… and the greater area around it." Rannos calls both offers "the things that they're not going to get."
- Sequoia says "Alright guys, pack it up, let's go" and turns to walk away.
- Gremag loses patience and initiates combat, laughing at the party's defiance. "I've had enough of this conversation," and later, "Do you really think you will leave?"
- Initiative is Zinnia 19, Zephyr 18, Sequoia 9. The DM notes that Gremag's initiative was misrolled.
- Zinnia strikes Gremag with two unarmed strikes, but Gremag retaliates with a brutal counterattack that knocks Zinnia unconscious:
  - Zinnia's unarmed strikes both hit AC 16 for 17 damage, and Zinnia saves the bonus action.
  - Gremag's first hit drops Zinnia from 31 to 10 hit points, and his second knocks Zinnia out.
  - Gremag then misses Zephyr (23 HP) completely.
- Zinnia's death save sheet still carries two stale successes because no long rest had reset them. Zinnia rolls a 14 and a 1, and the DM rules one failure marked while keeping the two successes.
- Zephyr and Sequoia press the attack against Gremag with their blades:
  - Zephyr circles behind for sneak attack and deals 19.
  - Sequoia's short sword hits twice for 25.
  - Zephyr later adds another 20.
- Rannos summons a spiritual weapon of his own and strikes Calmer with it for 8 damage.
- Calmer, whose spiritual weapon missed Gremag when first summoned, eventually lands the killing blow on Gremag.
- The DM frames the choice for Rannos: he is "a cult fanatic, so he's probably not the kind of guy that you can convert, but you might be able to get information from." Sequoia: "I'm just killing them, to be honest."
- Zephyr moves to grant Sequoia sneak attack advantage.
- The party turns their full attention to Rannos Davl, with Sequoia landing a devastating blow (26 damage total) as the session ends.

## NPCs
### Tharok
The High Cultist of the Earth Temple who attempted to use the Chest of Rao as a magical battery in a dark ritual. He was a formidable foe who commanded the earth itself and taunted the party before being slain. He called Zephyr "one of the demon spawn" who belonged on his side and dealt Warda 29 damage in a single blow before Zinnia finished him.

### Warda
A fiercely anti-Verbobonc gnome rescued from the cell block who fought alongside the party against the cultists, sustaining heavy injuries from Tharok during the final confrontation. Her battle cry was "DIE, YOU CREEPY CREATURE!" She was also the voice of caution afterward, insisting that "the only safe place is the exit."

### Sister Alina
A member of the Abbey of Beory who was found dead near the library following the cultist occupation.

### Brother Eldin
A wounded survivor of the Abbey of Beory who rewards the party for stopping the elemental cult and provides crucial lore about the Chest of Rao and the deity Rao. He apologized that he could not offer more treasure. He laughed off Zephyr's idea of tapping the chest's power, calling it no artifact "used as parlor tricks."

### Ostler Gundigoot
The friendly tavern keeper in Hommlet who serves the party Honey Haven mead and shares news that the local trade roads have reopened, crediting the party's earlier efforts. He offers fruity and hoppy ales, gnomish beer, and mead (1 gp). He coaxes the reluctant Sequoia into ordering, and heard of the party's good deeds from Glennon and Pasmarie Clee.

### Thorne (the Duke)
A hired operative working alongside Rannos Davl who was lured into the stables and knocked unconscious by the party. He claims to be amoral rather than a true believer and provided information about his superiors, encouraging the party to eliminate Rannos and Gremag. He had been ordered to follow the party and set up the earlier tavern ambush. He had 58 hit points and was reduced to 10 before being knocked out. By his own account he would rather die at the party's hands than face what the cult would do to him.

### Rannos Davl
A merchant in Hommlet and a secret operative for the Temple of Elemental Evil who is desperate to acquire the Chest of Rao to appease his bosses. He is cold and dismissive, mocking the party's demands before Gremag initiated combat. Described by the DM as a "general-purpose cult fanatic," he wields spiritual weapon. He was left badly wounded at session's end.

### Gremag
A dangerous and powerful operative for the Temple of Elemental Evil, and Rannos Davl's henchman and bodyguard. He initiated combat with the party, nearly killing Zinnia, before being slain by Calmer. The DM called him "the real beefy, dangerous guy," a true believer of the cult.

## Locations
### Ritual Cavern
A vast chamber pulsing with elemental power where High Cultist Tharok attempted a dark ritual over the Chest of Rao. The cavern collapsed following his defeat, though the rest of the abbey remained unaffected. The evil magic appeared confined to that cavern alone.

### Abbey of Beory Library
An extra-dimensional library within the monastery where the party reunites with the wounded Brother Eldin and discovers Sister Alina dead. Between the tunnels and the library lay an unexplored chamber with doors leading east and north.

### Hommlet
A small village where the party returns to confront the treacherous merchants working for the Temple of Elemental Evil. It lies a day and a half's journey from the abbey.

### The Crossroads
A location behind the carpenter's house and the wainwright's shop where the party initially proposed to meet the merchants, at the intersection of two paths along a hedgerow.

### Inn of the Welcome Wench
A welcoming establishment in Hommlet run by Ostler Gundigoot where the party enjoys mead and soup while planning their next move against the cult operatives.

### The Merchants' Stables
A fenced area attached to the merchants' store and office where the party successfully ambushed and captured the Duke. The party poured oil there in case they needed fire.

### Rannos and Gremag's Office
A private office within the merchant establishment in Hommlet where the final confrontation with the cult operatives took place, ending with Gremag's death and Rannos Davl badly wounded.

## Items
### Chest of Rao
A powerful holy artifact built by the high priests of Rao and imbued with great magical and religious significance. It can serve as a magical battery or energy source and was coveted by multiple factions including the Temple of Elemental Evil, Lareth the Beautiful, and the Archclericy of Veluna. It was originally intended as a gift for the Kingdom of Celene before going missing. Sequoia had knocked it off the altar to break the ritual, and the party took it from the ritual cavern as it collapsed and used its existence as leverage during negotiations with the cult operatives.
- Per Brother Eldin, it is a tool for Rao's priests to summon, control, and commune with their gods, not a power source for parlor tricks.
- Turning its magic into power requires a complex ritual beyond the party's abilities.
- The party left it somewhere safe in Hommlet before the stable ambush. Zephyr suggested it was left with "a… Tavern guy."

### Scroll of Web
A magical scroll gifted by Brother Eldin as a reward for clearing the Abbey of Beory. It allows the user to conjure sticky webs to immobilize enemies and was added to the party's shared inventory. It was considered for trapping the Duke, but the DM noted that the party's oil fire would burn through the web.

### Potion of Greater Healing
A restorative potion provided by Brother Eldin as part of the party's reward for their efforts at the abbey. It was added to the party's shared inventory.

### Coin Reward
- Brother Eldin's reward totalled 1,600 cp, 1,600 sp, and 280 gp, split as 400 cp, 400 sp, and 70 gp per party member.

## Spells
### Guiding Bolt
A flash of radiant light cast by Calmer that struck Tharok during the final moments of the ritual cavern battle, dealing 9 damage and granting Zinnia advantage on the next attack against the high cultist.

### Spiritual Weapon
A spectral weapon summoned during the office battle. Both Calmer and Rannos conjured one: Rannos used his to strike at Calmer for 8 damage, while Calmer's missed Gremag on its first strike. Calmer later landed the killing blow.
