# Chapter 14 Tharok's Fury Unleashed, Part 1

Date: 2025-09-07

## Summary

The session opened with the DM teasing the table: "When we last left our noble adventures, are you guys noble adventurers or just suckers?" Zephyr answered, "We're adventures, but noble." The DM then gave crucial context about the political landscape and ongoing conflicts. The party learned about the tensions between Verbobonc and the gnomes of the Kron Hills, where territorial disputes over valuable gem mines had created a powder keg of conflict. Verbobonc's construction of a strategic castle in Hommlet served as force projection, much like a medieval aircraft carrier, allowing them to control the valley roads and threaten gnomish territory. Because the hills are hard to cross, the roads run through the valley, and the DM pointed out "what a convenient place to put a castle right where you could block the valley." The gnomes viewed this expansion as imperialism, while Verbobonc claimed it was necessary protection against the Temple of Elemental Evil. The DM compared the gnomes' suspicion to the war in Ukraine and its "simple border adjustments," and noted that from the gnomish side there are spies everywhere.

The party discovered that the various elemental temple factions operated like competing mob families, each controlling different territories and fighting for dominance. The DM invoked The Godfather and drug dealers fighting over street corners. Lareth controlled the fire temple territory and had been using the party to eliminate earth temple gangs, expanding his own power base. However, all these factions ultimately answered to the Temple of Elemental Evil, the overarching criminal organization pulling the strings. In the DM's terms, Lareth is a capo who may believe he "deserves a seat at the table." The Chest of Rao served as the prize everyone sought: a powerful magical battery capable of fueling devastating rituals according to the laws of magical thermodynamics. The DM explained that the chest had been sent to the abandoned monastery, a local earth-temple outpost ("the cathedral is the temple of elemental evil, and this is a local church"). Its high cleric now held the chest and was preparing a ritual in the basement. The DM laid out the party's options. Returning the chest to Verbobonc would make them heroes. Giving it to the Temple of Elemental Evil could earn them an entree into the cult. Holding it would get them a meeting with Lareth, and with it a chance to kill him and throw the whole network into chaos.

Continuing their exploration of the abandoned monastery, the party encountered a stone guardian in an ancient library. The guardian presented them with a riddle about the eternal cycles of nature, testing their understanding of Beory's old faith. Sequoia rolled a natural 20 on investigation. The DM hinted that they should look at the first and last lines and think of how Beory sees everything as a cycle. Zephyr then successfully identified the answer as the water cycle, demonstrating wisdom that honored the old ways ("My science class was useful."). The guardian declared their worthiness and became inanimate, opening the passage deeper into the monastery complex.

Moving stealthily through the stone corridors, the party overheard cultists excitedly discussing Tharok's impending ritual and wishing they could witness the momentous event. Calmer stayed behind while the three scouts crept forward. Zephyr and Sequoia rushed the two guards, and Sequoia killed both, finding a key among their possessions. The cultists wore brown robes marked with triangle symbols, indicating their allegiance to the earth temple faction.

The key unlocked a prison chamber where two captives were being held for torture. Jerry, a soldier of Verbobonc, and Warda, a gnome nationalist, were engaged in heated political arguments even while imprisoned. Zephyr silenced them by threatening to put both of them on the torture devices. Despite their ideological differences, with Jerry representing Verbobonc's interests and Warda opposing what she saw as imperial expansion, both agreed to help fight the cultists after the party explained the dire threat. Jerry at first wanted to leave and alert Verbobonc. He changed his mind after Calmer's appeal to "fight for the greater good" and Zephyr's persuasion. Jerry revealed crucial information about a secret passageway to the deeper temple, knowledge passed down from his grandmother who had once worshipped Beory in these halls.

Armed with scimitars taken from the fallen cultists, the group followed the secret passage to the heart of the monastery. They emerged into a vast ritual chamber that took their breath away. It was carved from ancient stone with intricate elemental motifs and towering pillars like colossal trees, and a massive cracked altar stood at the center. The Chest of Rao rested upon the altar, radiating both protective magic and creeping corruption, while arcane symbols floated above it in eerie greenish-gold light.

High Cultist Tharok stood before the altar, his imposing form marked by skin mottled like cracked stone and eyes burning with fervent intensity. Three robed cultists knelt around him in reverence, their whispered invocations weaving into the elemental hum that filled the air. The floor bore concentric rings of golden runes representing elemental power, and the very air vibrated with raw magical energy that made the chamber feel alive.

When Tharok spotted the intruders, he declared them defilers of his sacred ritual and threatened that the earth itself would swallow them whole. Battle erupted as the party moved to stop the ritual before it could be completed. The DM warned the table that Tharok was "kind of a deadly guy." Zinnia, Sequoia, and Zephyr quickly dispatched the three cultist guards with swift, decisive strikes, while Calmer blessed the party with divine magic to aid their efforts.

Jerry charged bravely toward the high cultist but was struck down by Tharok's devastating attacks, his body crumpling under the elemental fury. All three of Tharok's attacks hit, for 44 damage. Tharok bellowed, "You dare to attack me! You shall pay the price!" His earth magic caused the entire chamber to quake violently, dust and stones cascading from the ceiling as tremors knocked several party members to the ground. The earthquake disrupted Calmer's blessing as he struggled to maintain his footing on the shifting stone.

Sequoia recognized that disrupting the ritual itself might weaken their enemy and rushed to the altar, knocking the Chest of Rao from its resting place. The magical energies swirling through the chamber immediately began to dissipate as the ritual circle was broken. The DM later revealed that this prevented "a pretty nasty effect that was going to happen." Zephyr struck with deadly precision, landing a critical blow against the high cultist, while Warda, openly pleased that the imperialist Jerry was dead, kept fighting for the free gnomes. Tharok dismissed her blows as "but a scratch."

The battle raged on as Calmer called upon divine power and Zinnia pressed the attack with relentless strikes. Tharok retaliated with earth-shaking force, hurling Zephyr backward with a devastating blow while his elemental magic continued to make the chamber tremble. Luck held for the party, as three of Tharok's four attacks that round missed. He still found breath to taunt them: "You fight like trembling saplings in a storm." Though bloodied and battered, the high cultist remained standing as the session concluded, the outcome of this crucial confrontation still hanging in the balance. The DM closed by reminding the table that "lots of things are in motion."

### Narwell

The starting location where the party began their adventures before discovering the various elemental temple territories.

### Monastery Corridors

Stone passageways where the party overheard cultists discussing Tharok's ritual with excitement. Contains guard posts where cultists were stationed and passages leading south and east through the complex.

### Prison Chamber

A dungeon area containing locked cells where Jerry and Warda were imprisoned, along with a torture rack. Connected to the main monastery through a doorway requiring a key to access.

### The Ritual Chamber

A vast, awe-inspiring chamber carved from ancient stone that pulses with the heartbeat of the earth itself. Features towering pillars etched with swirling elemental motifs, a massive cracked stone altar at the center, and concentric rings of golden runes inscribed on the floor representing elemental power. The air vibrates with raw magical energy and tremors ripple through as the earth responds to the ritual. Flickering torchlight casts writhing shadows and the scent of damp stone and incense hangs heavy.

- The motifs depict "granite roots entwined with veins of glowing crystal, flickering flames, rushing water, and whispering woods."
- The energy makes "the hairs on your neck stand on end."
- The altar is "cracked yet resilient."

### Secret Passageway

A hidden passage revealed by Jerry that leads from the prison area to the deeper temple of Beory, known to him through stories from his grandmother who used to worship there.

- According to his grandmother, it lies "past the library." Sequoia found and opened the secret door, which leads down a passage into the big cave of the ritual chamber.

## Items

### Chest of Rao

A powerful magical artifact that radiates a complex aura of pure protective magic mixed with creeping, corruptive earth energy. Functions as a magical battery capable of powering powerful rituals according to the three laws of thermodynamic magic. Central to Tharok's ritual with arcane symbols floating above it, glowing with eerie greenish-gold light that pulsates with the chanting. When knocked off the altar by Sequoia, it disrupts the ritual and reduces the elemental effects in the chamber. Sought after by multiple factions including Verbobonc, the Temple of Elemental Evil, and Lareth.

- Its aura is "corruptive earth energy that twists light into shadow."
- The druid the party killed had stolen it and sent it to the monastery.
- The Earth Temple also wants it.
- Knocking it off the altar prevented "a pretty nasty effect that was going to happen."
- The DM on cults: "That's why they're always obsessed with finding powerful magic items because it's a battery they can use."

### Prison Key

A key found on the bodies of dead earth cultists that unlocks the prison cells where Jerry and Warda were being held captive by the cult.

- The DM: "Fortunately, you had the key to open the door."

### Cultist Scimitars

Weapons taken from defeated earth cultists and given to Jerry and Warda to arm them for the fight against Tharok and his followers. The cultists were easily dispatched despite being armed with these weapons.

- Jerry asked, "Do you have any weapons?" Zinnia answered, "Yeah, we have some scimitars."

## Spells

### Bless

A first-level spell cast by Calmer that allows party members to add 1d4 to attack rolls and saving throws, providing divine assistance in combat. The spell's effects were lost when Calmer was knocked prone by Tharok's earth magic and failed his concentration saving throw during the earthquake effect.

- The DM, announcing it: "All right, okay, you are blessed."

### Earth Magic

Powerful elemental magic wielded by High Cultist Tharok that causes the ritual chamber to quake violently. Creates tremors that knock party members prone and causes dust and stones to cascade from the ceiling as the earth rebels against intruders, requiring DC 14 dexterity saving throws to avoid being knocked prone.

- "You struggle to keep your footing as the earth rebels against your presence."
- Zephyr and Zinnia saved. Sequoia and Calmer were knocked prone.

### Guiding Bolt

A spell cast by Calmer against High Cultist Tharok that deals radiant damage to enemies. Despite rolling poorly for damage (8 points from 4d6), it still contributed to the ongoing battle against the high cultist.

- The DM on the roll: "That's like embarrassing."
