# Chapter 05 Repast for an Ogre

Date: Planting 18, 579 to Planting 19, 579

## Summary

Calmer resolves to lead the party into the lower level of the moathouse, after a brief rest, and insists that he must lead. At the bottom of the stairwell a blob of green slime drops on him and eats through his armor; Zephyr scrapes it off with a piece of wood, and tells him "Grow up". The storage room beyond holds only junk, so Sequoia takes them east, through a door with greased hinges. A bang from Calmer alerts an ogre, who announces "I am Lubash!" and attacks, calling the party food. Zinnia, Zephyr and Sequoia kill it, though Calmer nearly dies again.

In the ogre's lived-in room Zinnia finds a magical cloak of elvenkind in the nest, and a heavily barred door to the south. Behind it are the ogre's prisoners: two merchants, Derrick (in a strange purple robe) and Florian, from Narwell, and a gnome named Jarran, a spy sent by the merchants of Lortmil. Derrick explains that brigands captured six travellers on the road to Hommlet and that the ogre took one each day; he names Martin, Damien and Eltograph the Elf before breaking down. The merchants promise a reward if brought back to Hommlet. Jarran offers to stick around as another sword; Zinnia distrusts other spies and declines. The prisoners are taken outside, where the Duke volunteers to guard them.

Sequoia then leads the party to a hallway to the south that they had ignored before: fifty feet long, with three pillars and five rotten doors on the western wall. Zombies appear and Calmer turns them, but the rotten doors are not keeping the zombies inside the cells: more come out of one cell, and then more, until someone (Sequoia cannot tell whether Zephyr or Zinnia) shouts that the other three doors also have zombies. Sequoia calls the retreat and Zephyr runs for the stairs. Upstairs the Duke is handing out food to the merchants and the gnome gives the party a ring.

The road back to Hommlet is quiet. Sequoia insists they go to the Druid first, because "we can only trust the Druid", over Calmer's objection that they have an obligation to Terjon and his order. The Druid, Jaroo, finds that they have proof of brigands masquerading as temple members, but no proof of more, and says they must go to the elders. Sequoia, afraid of saying the wrong thing, asks the Druid to present; he agrees and seems flattered, and sets the meeting at the Village Elder's for the next day. Calmer goes to speak with the cleric, while Zinnia returns to the Inn of the Welcome Wench, where Spugnoir asks about the tower and Ostler says he is pleased they went to Jaroo before the "interloping outsiders".

On Planting 19 the party meets at the Village Elder's house with Burne, Rufus, Jaroo and Ostler. Calmer tries to insist that the Prefect attend such meetings, but Sequoia deflects him. Jaroo repeats the party's account, pushing the narrative that brigands are masquerading as agents of the temple; Zinnia lets it slide. Rufus says there is no conclusive evidence of the Temple's re-emergence and that the matter is not worth disrupting the arch-cleric of Verbobonc; Burne commends the party for their information. The party flees the offer to help with the end-of-month festival. The party takes Spugnoir along, to Calmer's disappointment, and on the way to Emridy Meadows Spugnoir tells them of the battle there and the Circle of Eight, who appeared and cast powerful spells to bind some evil presence in the Temple of Elemental Evil.

At the tower, which is more a single building, Zinnia finds a summoning circle and a dust and smoke mephit. Zephyr's song enrages it and the mephits are killed. Beyond another door are a laboratory and two cultists, who die; Spugnoir identifies two potions of gaseous form, a potion of healing and a magical dagger, which goes to Zinnia. Zephyr finds a secret door and goes through to a fountain room, then to a smaller room with the cultists' leader, whom he kills. From a spider idol, Zephyr concludes the man must be a human worshipper of Lolth. The leader's room is a battered bedroom with gnome-scale furniture and three bedrolls, where Spugnoir is thrilled to find scrolls and a spell book.

Back in Hommlet in the afternoon of Planting 19, Zephyr approaches the workers' encampment near the fortress to find who copied the map. A thirty-ish man with a construction worker's build is spooked; he is the man the party catches that evening.

## Locations

### the moathouse
The ruin whose lower levels the party explores: the stairwell arch with the slime, the storage room, the ogre's room, the pantry, the cell hallway, the torture room, the secret shaft and the crypt.

### Hommlet
The village to which the prisoners are to be returned. The party visits the Inn of the Welcome Wench, the Village Elder's house and the Druid's grove; after arriving in Hommlet, Zephyr goes to the workers' encampment near the fortress.

### the Inn of the Welcome Wench
The inn where Spugnoir writes at a table and Ostler tells Zinnia that the room is paid until the 19th.

### Emridy Meadows
The site of a great battle some years ago, according to Spugnoir, near which the tower stands.

### the tower
A single building with a summoning circle, a laboratory, a forge, a fountain room and a gnome-scale bedroom, where the party kills mephits, two cultists and the cultists' leader.

## NPCs

### Lubash
The ogre. He announces his name, says the party are "not members of the temple!", and swings his club. He kept six prisoners as food.

### Derrick
A merchant in a strange purple robe from Narwell. Tells the party of the six captives and names the dead.

### Florian
A merchant from Narwell, captured with Derrick.

### Jarran
A gnome spy sent by the merchants of Lortmil to investigate the brigands. Offers to join the party; Zinnia refuses. He gives the party a ring.

### the Duke (Thorne)
Mocks Calmer as he stretches. Guards the party's possessions and the freed prisoners, finding the rescued prisoners "very interesting" in his own section.

### Jaroo
The Druid, met in his grove. Hears the party's evidence, agrees to present it to the elders, retells it at the meeting as brigands masquerading as the temple, and asks the party to help with decorating and the location committee.

### the Village Elder (Elder Kenter)
Hosts the meeting in his house, calls it to order after an hour of eating, and closes the topic. He is distinct from Jaroo ("We will meet at the Village Elder," says Jaroo).

### Spugnoir
The magic user at the inn who wants the party to visit a tower. Likes to be flattered (Zinnia's read, per Zephyr), tells the story of the Circle of Eight, and is overjoyed at the spell book.

### Ostler
Tells Zinnia the room has been paid for until the 19th and is pleased they went to Jaroo first.

### Burne
Attends the meeting and commends the party's information.

### Rufus
The captain of the militia. Dismissive of the Temple evidence.
