# Chapter 39

Date: 2026-09-06

## Summary
The session opened with the DM reading Calmer's quest log aloud: "Gentlemen, we did not survive four prophets, a temple war, a wraith, trolls… a living pool, and several administrative meetings to throw away the first position of genuine authority any of us held. We will restore the supply line, secure the asset, learn what the deeper powers are hiding, and then, once the inventory is complete, make a properly informed decision about what, if anything, must be brought down." The DM said this captured Calmer's current state of mind.

Before play resumed on the map, the DM reminded the table of the region's geography. Verbobonc controls the borderland that includes Hommlet and is essentially a sub-territory of the Archclericy of Veluna, ruled by the clerical order of St. Cuthbert, with the Kingdom of Furyondy to the north and the City of Greyhawk to the west. The party had arrived back in Nulb. The DM reminded the players that Captain Tolub was the man who had sent them to be killed by the three clerics with the fireballs.

The party chose to visit the Rhennee first, both to see what the village thought of them and because the Rhennee could be allies. On the way, the townspeople recognized them but pretended not to. One man pointed and was about to blurt something out when someone hit him. Zinnia's spycraft identified the pattern: a gangster forcing everyone to repeat his lie, so that saying the lie becomes a demonstration of loyalty. Sequoia's Investigation of 22 confirmed that people were trying "REALLY hard not to recognize" them. The party made its way to the Rhennee camp, a collection of brightly painted wagons just inside the tree line of the Gnarley Forest. Eryk greeted them — "My friends, you still live" — and insisted that they "do not talk about business before pleasure." When Zephyr asked for beer, Eryk clapped his hands and his people brought out food and drink. It was the best the Rhennee could offer, and the first food in days that smelled good after Romag's rations. Vethra the fortune teller poked at their cheeks and scolded, "you have lost too much weight! Eat! Eat!" One of the Rhennee took Frostbrand and cleaned the mud off it with such devotion that the sword declared, "yes, this is the way it should be. Cleaned!" A child tugged at Sequoia demanding "A story! A story! Tell me a story!" Sequoia passed the job to Calmer, and the child chose "Adventures, adventures, adventures!" Calmer recounted his heroic destruction of the wraith, casting himself as the avenging leader, before he remembered he was a cleric of St. Cuthbert and credited the saint with their survival. Zephyr added, "All glory to St. Cuthbert." The Rhennee confirmed that "everybody knows you're not dead, but nobody dares tell anybody." Eryk eventually revealed his problem: "Madame Selentis… Man wearing her black and white cord has ordered one of my caravans to report at dusk for a short haul." He didn't know what would be carried, only that refusing would cost him "wheels, animals, or people." Eryk offered the party safe beds, food, and disguises, and pointed Calmer to Mother Screng as the best source of "medicine" in town, meaning potions.

With the caravan situation in mind, the party first paid a visit to Captain Tolub at the Boatman's Tavern. On the way, Zinnia remembered that Madame Selentis had run the temple's supply chain through Nulb, which might offer another way to solve the Rhennee's problem. Inside the tavern, Tolub sat in the back corner holding court with his gangsters. He was in the middle of embellishing the story of how he had supposedly had the party killed, each detail less credible than the last: "they survived the fire, they took Halveth, then I had all four put under honest dirt. Ask anyone here, carefully." Skole, the bartender, set down four cups without a flicker of surprise: "Dead people… dead people drink free. Tolub says you're dead, so send him the bill."

Zephyr had the server Ketta deliver a beer to Tolub with the message that it was from Zephyr. Ketta warned the party that they were "playing a dangerous game" and asked them to keep the bloodshed to a minimum. Tolub demanded to know what she meant: "Zephyr's dead!" Sequoia, knife out, shot back, "oh, really then? How did Sequoia die?" Sequoia sat down next to Tolub and Zephyr slid in between Tolub and his second-in-command Grud, who made space for him. Zinnia stayed by the bar to watch, and Calmer stood there trying to project the aura of the overall leader of the Upper Temple. Tolub met them with "good likeness! But the four who took Halveth are dead, and everyone in this room knows how fond I am of being… Of being doubted." Zephyr's Intimidation of 20 landed. Tolub turned to Zephyr and offered a way out: "We don't have to make this a messy event. How about I just give you a free supply run? Call it even, and you guys get to stay dead." Sequoia wanted to take the run and teach Tolub a lesson later, while Zephyr reasoned that his boasting didn't affect them. The party accepted. Tolub declared them "a bunch of fakes, dressing up like Zephyr" and gave Zephyr a friendly punch on the shoulder. Zephyr laughed along, and so did the room. Tolub told them that anything beyond the one trip had to go through Selentis, and confirmed, "and we agree, you're still dead, right?" Skole looked very disappointed and Grud ground his teeth. Zinnia knew Tolub had just secured his position and that the party had missed a chance to get rid of him. Tolub called for beers "for these clowns," summoned Zinnia to sit with them, and launched into a version of the story in which the party were cowards and wimps. The party let it go: "We'll deal with him another day." As the group prepared to leave, Sequoia quietly lifted a leather dispatch case from Tolub's chair. Tolub was somewhat drunk, and Sequoia's Sleight of Hand of 23 beat his Perception.

Outside, they opened the case and found three things. First, Tolub had been tied to the Water Temple, reporting to Belsornig, and had sent the party to the Fire Temple cleric as a distraction. With Belsornig dead, Tolub at best now reported to Brother Eelrich, the man the party had installed at the head of the Water Temple. Second, Selentis's manor controlled the onward route, so all food passing through Nulb went through her. Third, a shipment of meat, lamp oil, and coin could move that night, and Tolub could arrange that one shipment but no later ones. The DM pointed out that because the party had shown overwhelming power over their subordinates and had been installed by the Deep Temple, they were about as untouchable as it gets, as long as they weren't wounded or isolated. Kelno, however, was already expanding his domain; he had occupied a Water Temple room and commanded the only other surviving army. Zephyr said, "I trust Kelno." Sequoia said, "I don't." The DM also mused that "it's always very interesting to me to see, like, when players will start a fight, and when they won't," and Zephyr answered, "This was not worth it."

The party weighed going straight to Selentis but chose to stand by Eryk's caravan. The DM had challenged them: "you're gonna let Eryk, your family, be abandoned?" He also reminded Zinnia that a spy sometimes lets people take something and follows them. Eryk had no idea where the caravan was going. Then Serena came out with word that the caravan was to go to the mayor's house, which was exactly where Selentis operated. The party hid inside the caravan and rode it straight to her door, where they overheard Selentis smoothly reassure Serena: "don't worry, don't worry, this is just a one-time deal. I know how precious these little caravans of yours are." Zephyr decided, "let's not spy on Selentis if you want to do business with her," and the party stepped out. Selentis was startled — "my, my, my! Now, this is a very big surprise." — and turned the question back on them: "do you know who I am? Is a better question." When Zephyr answered, "You are… our supply chain," she studied them with the careful eyes of someone who had survived by reading people correctly. She had blonde hair, dark brown eyes, and the bearing of an important person fallen on hard times. She picked out Calmer as the new head prophet. Calmer replied that he preferred "the supreme prophet of the Lower Temple," and she cut through the title bluntly: he needed meat, lamp oil, and coin carried to a hole full of soldiers, and she owned the road between those nouns. She asked whether they wanted to pretend the meeting concerned anything else, and they did not.

Asked who now signed orders in the upper temple, Calmer named Sequoia for all matters of supply ("Yo, how's it going?"). Selentis told him to make sure the signature "isn't too trivial to copy." Sequoia's Sleight of Hand of 31 produced a signature she called "extraordinarily hard to copy," and she remarked on its flourishes. She asked which of the old agents — Kelno, Belsornig, and Alrrem — should still receive supplies. Zephyr explained that "There has been change in management," and the party told her to keep supplying Kelno and the Fire and Water Temples under their new leadership. Asked whether they wanted Tolub "useful, frightened, or gone," they answered gone; Zephyr said, "He has no use," and noted, "I don't think there's any Tolub likers here." Selentis agreed to re-establish the supply chain. She told Calmer she hadn't expected Kelno to be the last surviving member of the old order, or Calmer to be in charge, and stared at him oddly, as if trying to work out who he really was. Assuming Calmer now ran the Earth Temple, she asked whether he would need supplies for the ritual. He said yes, and she promised them by the end of the tenday. Outside, Calmer explained what the ritual was: "They bring us a prisoner, they bring us someone that we have the earth elementals tear apart in front of everyone." He made clear he had no intention of performing it — "I'm not an evil cleric! We… this… we're going in too deep!" Zephyr pointed out that Calmer didn't have to do the killing himself. The question of what to do instead was left unresolved.

The party spent the night with the Rhennee, where there was drinking and celebration. Eryk thanked them: "you have saved my daughter, but more importantly, you have saved my home," pointing to the caravan. The following morning, the party went looking for answers about Calmer's mysterious scarab. The Rhennee diviner Vethra, the oldest among the Rhennee, examined it and directed them to Mother Screng, the local herb shop owner, saying "She seems to know more than she lets on." On the way, Zinnia spotted a clumsy tail following them through the village streets. The party kept walking while Zinnia hid, then jumped him. He wore the same cord as the man who had given the Rhennee their orders. Zinnia's first Intimidation attempt rolled a 2; with Sequoia's help, the reroll of 13 was enough. "I'm just doing my job," the man insisted. "Madame Selentis paid me, who do you think?" He claimed he was only making sure nobody caused them trouble, since in Nulb that could mean "Your enemies, past and present." He was telling the truth, though not the whole truth. Zephyr judged that he was not a threat and was content to be watched, and the party sent him on his way. At Mother Screng's shop, the old woman examined the scarab and redirected them again: "oh, yes, this is, this is interesting. But I think you should go to Otis. He knows more about these things than I do."

Otis the blacksmith did not come across as an ordinary craftsman. He was tall, powerfully built, and looked like someone who could carry himself in a fight; Zinnia's Insight of 14 read him as giving off anything but "I'm Just the Smith" vibes. When shown the scarab, he asked a single question: "who's asking?" Zephyr's Insight of 16 couldn't settle whether Otis meant who they were or who they were pretending to be. Zephyr chose to take a chance: "We're determined to… Root out the temple of elemental evil." Otis smiled and said he had been waiting for them for some time. "I'm Otis. But the better question is, what am I?" He was an agent of the forces of good who had been spying on the temple for years. "you have done more in your short tenure here than I have done in years. And for that, I am grateful." He identified the scarab as a Greater Temple Command Key, not jewelry. It opens or commands something below, though he did not know what, which door, or which creature. He urged them to hold onto it: "You don't know when you will need it." He then handed them a note and sent them back to Mother Screng: "She is in charge."

When the party returned with Otis's note, they could clearly see that Mother Screng's old-woman appearance was a disguise. She was no ordinary herb seller but the leader of the local resistance against the temple. She looked at Calmer with an unsettling intensity, as though she could sense that something in him had shifted. "I see that you have become… you have risen in power in the Earth Temple… Are you still calmer?" Calmer insisted, "yes, I am. I've always been Calmer. Why do you ask?" Zephyr's Insight of 27 caught the lie, and Sequoia needed no roll to know it. She knew Calmer was anything but Calmer, yet the party could see that something of Calmer was still there. After a long, thoughtful pause, she said she would tell them "the deepest and most guarded secret of the entire temple. Prince Thrommel lives." Thrommel's marriage was meant to unite the Archclericy of Veluna and the Kingdom of Furyondy. He had vanished during the Battle of Emridy Meadows, and everyone had assumed he was dead. He was alive, and he was in the temple. The session ended there, with the party taking in what the revelation meant and what it would cost to act on it.

### Captain Tolub
A boastful local captain and gangster who previously tried to have the party killed and has been spreading rumors in Nulb that they are dead. He is revealed to have been secretly aligned with the Water Temple. When confronted, he backs down and offers a single free supply run to save face, but continues boasting after the party leaves. His dispatch case shows he reported to Belsornig and now, at best, to Brother Eelrich. Without temple backing he is in a weaker position, which may explain why he forces the town to repeat his lie. He calls the party "a bunch of fakes" and "clowns" and retells their deaths as cowardice. The party later tells Selentis they want him "gone."

### Brother Eelrich
The man the party placed at the head of the Water Temple. With Belsornig dead, Tolub at best now reports to him. The DM reminds the table that Eelrich sent them into the pool knowing it was possessed by an evil creature, so whether he is "on your side" is "a complicated phrase."

### Eryk
A leader among the Rhennee river folk who views the party as family. He welcomes them warmly with food and drink, and reveals that Madame Selentis has coerced the Rhennee into performing a mysterious caravan run at dusk under threat of losing wagons, animals, or people. He offers safe beds, food, and disguises, recommends Mother Screng for "medicine," and later thanks the party for saving his daughter "but more importantly… my home."

### Vethra
An elderly Rhennee woman and fortune teller who fusses over the party's health, encourages them to eat, and directs them to Mother Screng when shown the mysterious scarab. She is the oldest among the Rhennee and pokes at the party's cheeks, saying "you have lost too much weight! Eat! Eat!"

### Madame Selentis
A blonde woman with dark brown eyes and an aura of fallen nobility who controls the roads and supply lines around Nulb. She is pragmatic and quickly aligns herself with the party's new authority, agreeing to re-establish the supply chain; asked whether Captain Tolub should be useful, frightened, or gone, the party answered gone. She also offers Calmer supplies for the Earth Temple's ritual by the end of the tenday. Her agents wear a black and white cord. She studies Calmer oddly, as if trying to work out who he really is, and afterward has the party followed.

### Serena
A member of the Rhennee whose caravan was commandeered by Madame Selentis for a transport job to the mayor's house. She also confirms that the Boatman's upper floor remains dangerous.

### Skole
The bartender at the Boatman's Tavern who quietly assists the party by offering them free drinks at Tolub's expense, and who looks visibly disappointed when the party lets Tolub off the hook. He sent the note telling the party Tolub was still claiming they were dead. The DM also notes that he does not like Tolub.

### Grud
Captain Tolub's second-in-command, who stands by his captain during the tense confrontation at the tavern. He makes space for Zephyr at the table, and grinds his teeth while smiling and laughing once Tolub secures the deal.

### Ketta
A serving wench at the Boatman's Tavern who warns the party they are playing a dangerous game and anxiously asks them to keep the bloodshed to a minimum. She delivers Zephyr's beer to Tolub with the message that it is from Zephyr.

### Selentis's Agent
A clumsy tail sent by Madame Selentis to follow the party through Nulb. He claims he was only ensuring their safety, and Zinnia's insight confirms he is telling the truth but not the whole truth. He wears Selentis's cord and protests, "I'm just doing my job."

### Mother Screng
An agent of good disguised as an old hag who runs an herb shop in Nulb. She is revealed to be the superior of Otis and the leader of the local resistance. She questions Calmer's identity, sensing something has changed in him, before revealing the temple's most guarded secret. She first deflects the party to Otis.

### Otis
A tall, powerful blacksmith in Nulb who does not give off ordinary craftsman vibes and looks capable of carrying himself in a fight. He is secretly an agent of the forces of good who has been spying on the temple for years. He identifies the party's scarab as a Greater Temple Command Key and directs them to Mother Screng as his superior. He had "been waiting for you for some time" and credits the party with doing more in their short tenure than he has in years.

### Prince Thrommel
The lost prince whose disappearance during the Battle of Emridy Meadows left a political union between two kingdoms unfinished. Mother Screng reveals he is alive and being held prisoner within the temple. The two kingdoms were the Archclericy of Veluna and the Kingdom of Furyondy, which his marriage was meant to unite.

### Kelno
The Air Temple priest and one of the few survivors of the old temple order. The party chooses to keep him supplied, and Selentis is instructed to continue delivering to him. He commands the only other surviving army and has already moved to expand his domain by occupying a Water Temple room, while promising complete loyalty. Zephyr trusts him; Sequoia does not.

## Locations
### Nulb
A muddy, dilapidated village near the temple, characterized by rotting piers, smoke over the river, and a tense atmosphere where rumors and lies are enforced by local power players like Captain Tolub.

### Rhennee Estate
A camp of brightly painted wagons resting just inside the tree line of the Gnarley Forest near Nulb, home to the Rhennee river folk who view the party as family. The party feasted and spent the night here.

### Boatman's Tavern
A rough waterfront establishment in Nulb where Captain Tolub and his crew gather to drink, boast, and enforce their influence over the locals. The party confronted Tolub here. Tolub holds court in the back corner, and the upper floor is still said to be dangerous.

### Waterside Hostel
A Nulb lodging offered as one of the party's options for the night, alongside the Boatman's Tavern and the Rhennee camp. The party chose the Rhennee.

### The Mayor's House
A noble-looking estate in Nulb currently occupied by Madame Selentis, serving as the hub for the town's logistics and supply lines. The party infiltrated it via the Rhennee caravan. Serena called it "the old mayor's manor."

### Mother Screng's Herb Shop
A small shop in Nulb run by an old woman who serves as a front for a high-ranking agent of good working against the temple. Eryk recommended it as the best source of potions in town.

### Otis's Smithy
A blacksmith shop in Nulb run by Otis, who secretly monitors temple activity as an agent of the forces of good.

## Items
### Frostbrand
Sequoia's magical sword. One of the Rhennee cleaned the mud from its blade with great care, and the sword expressed considerable satisfaction at the attention. At the Rhennee camp it declared, "yes, this is the way it should be. Cleaned!"

### Scarab inscribed TZGY
A mysterious artifact carried by Calmer. Otis identifies it as a Greater Temple Command Key that opens or commands something below, though he does not know what. The party found it long ago and had forgotten about it until Falrinth raised it. Otis urged them to hold onto it because "You don't know when you will need it."

### Leather Dispatch Case
A leather case stolen by Sequoia from Captain Tolub's chair as the party left the Boatman's Tavern. It contained intelligence revealing Tolub's secret allegiance to the Water Temple and details about Madame Selentis's control over the local supply routes. It also revealed Tolub's former reporting line to Belsornig and a shipment of meat, lamp oil, and coin that could move that night.

### Supply Orders
Official documents signed by Sequoia with an intricate, hard-to-copy flourish to authorize the transport of meat, lamp oil, and coin to the temple. Madame Selentis was visibly impressed by the complexity of the signature. The signature came from a Sleight of Hand roll of 31.

### Otis's Note
A written message given by Otis to the party to verify their alliance to Mother Screng, allowing her to speak freely with them.
