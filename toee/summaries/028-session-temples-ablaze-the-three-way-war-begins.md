# Chapter 28 Temples Ablaze: The Three-Way War Begins

Date: 2026-03-14

## Summary
The night had barely settled before the party found themselves deep in the aftermath of a brutal battle. A Fire Temple warlock had unleashed a monstrous Chimera upon them, a horrifying amalgamation of lion, goat, and dragon, and proclaimed it the champion of Alrrem, the all-consuming. The beast ravaged the party, battering Sequoia and Zephyr into unconsciousness before Calmer's divine gifts pulled them back from the brink of death. In the end, Calmer's necrotic resonance, ringing out like a funeral bell, dropped the Chimera dead upon the cold stone floor.

In the quiet aftermath, the party searched the warlock's charred remains and uncovered a trove of damning evidence. A wax-sealed ledger bore the crest of a crimson ship. It was a bill of sale from Captain Vesrek of the Crimson Trader, detailing the purchase of the Chimera by the Fire Temple. More revealing still, the name Vesrek appeared in the document as an ally of Belsornig, the Water Temple's prophet. Belsornig had sold Alrrem the Chimera in a win-win scheme that would either kill the party or bankrupt the Fire Temple. The warlock also carried:
- a heavy gold amulet bearing the eight-pointed symbol of the Fire Temple
- a potion of fire resistance he had never managed to reach for
- a masterwork whip of alchemically treated red leather
- heavy iron chains stamped with the marks of Nulb's river pirates

The party brought their findings to Romag, the High Priest of the Earth Temple. He received the news with thunderous fury and ordered them to strike the weakened Fire Temple. A private meeting with Hartsch, Romag's sub-general, proved far more illuminating. Hartsch laid out the political landscape in stark detail:
- Alrrem's power had been waning since a catastrophic defeat a decade prior.
- The Air Temple was a powerless remnant.
- The Water Temple remained a formidable rival.

When Zinnia pressed Hartsch on his feelings about Romag's passive, erosion-like philosophy of leadership, Hartsch made no effort to hide his ambitions. If a position of leadership were to suddenly open itself, he would absolutely consider himself worthy of it.

The party recognized the signal for what it was. Hartsch wanted Romag removed, and he wanted them to do it. A bold and dangerous plan crystallized: assassinate Romag, install Hartsch as the Earth Temple's new leader, and engineer a three-way war between the Earth, Fire, and Water Temples to destroy all three factions from within. They quietly agreed that the time for subtlety was nearly over.

After a long rest that left everyone fully healed, the DM laid out the two objectives in plain terms: replace Romag with Hartsch, and trigger Alrrem into attacking Belsornig. Zinnia drew on his background as a spy and master of subterfuge and rolled a 27. He worked out the lever. Alrrem had gambled everything on the Chimera, lost his werewolves, and spent all his money, so his natural instinct would be to do nothing. The ledger, however, proved that the Water Temple's agent had sold him the monster at an inflated price. That would show Alrrem that Belsornig had taken him for a fool, and it would push him into attacking the Water Temple while the Earth Temple attacked him. The party chose to deal with Romag first.

Rather than alerting Hartsch to their intentions, the party chose to execute a surgical coup without his foreknowledge, planning to present him with power as a fait accompli. Zephyr drew on his abyssal past (Insight 17) and recognized a danger in that plan. When somebody kills the boss, everyone assumes the killer is the new boss. The party would have to pledge loyalty to Hartsch at once, or he would assume they wanted power for themselves.

They enlisted Dren, the leader of the Broken Blades, to arrange a morning meeting with Romag, ensuring the high priest would be present and off-guard. Dren is chaotic evil, so the party told him only about the meeting and nothing of the larger plan. They also chided him for sleeping through the Chimera fight and played up their own victory. Dren was visibly impressed and began to suspect he had picked the winning side. He agreed without hesitation and returned with confirmation: "the meeting's set for tomorrow morning, Romag will be there, he's looking forward to it."

The party slipped through a secret passage into the Earth Temple's inner sanctum. Sequoia opened the secret door with a Stealth roll of 25. They emerged into Romag's sleeping chamber, a room with:
- earthen motifs on the walls
- a dirt-covered floor
- a small altar in the corner
- a desk scattered with personal notes

Calmer, deciding that sneaky work called for sneaky spells, cast Guidance on the party. Sequoia rolled a 1, then a 2, then a clutch 18, and crept behind a curtain separating the chamber from the adjoining room. From there he heard Romag lecturing Hartsch on the slow, erosive power of dust. Hartsch barely concealed his disdain behind carefully chosen words of agreement, and Sequoia could practically hear him rolling his eyes. Sequoia also noticed, with some urgency, that the door to the south stood open. If Romag cried out, guards would come flooding in and the entire operation would unravel.

The party moved swiftly. Calmer had to be won over first. Zephyr had been needling him for some time, but he made the case that their target was "an evil man" (Persuasion 23), and Calmer agreed to hold by the door and charge in after the first strike. The party's group Stealth earned them a surprise round.

Zephyr invoked a subtle but powerful magic that slammed the doors of Romag's chamber shut with a resonant bang, sealing the room from the outside world. Hartsch understood at once what was happening. He placed his weapons on the ground and stepped aside. Romag turned, his face shifting from confusion to horror, as Zinnia rushed him with a flurry of precise, devastating strikes for 23 damage. Sequoia followed, landing blow after blow on the stunned high priest with Sneak Attack. Calmer nearly invoked the name of his holy patron, catching himself at the most spectacularly ill-timed moment imaginable. He instead channeled a wave of necrotic power, and Romag withered and collapsed dead upon the cold stone floor.

Hartsch did not hesitate. He plunged his hands into Romag's body and covered them in blood, sprinkling some on the party as well. Then he marched into the main chamber of the Earth Temple, holding Romag's sacred vestments aloft. He declared himself Supreme Prophet, announcing that the era of passive resignation was over and that the destructive power of earth would now be unleashed upon their enemies.

The party needed a name. "Sequoia and friends" and "Agents of Chaos" were floated, and the party settled on the Obsidian Edge. Zephyr fed Hartsch's ego, assuring him the other temples would kneel now that he was in charge. Hartsch, now clearly a megalomaniac and completely unhinged, ordered the Edge to stage a secret attack to disrupt Alrrem. The gathered cultists erupted in cheers. Hartsch introduced the party to his troops as the Obsidian Edge, personally loyal to him: his vanguard, who had brought them power and glory.

With Hartsch rallying his forces, the party slipped away to set the second part of their plan in motion. The other ways of reaching Alrrem, such as working through an agent or a contact in Nulb, would take time they did not have. They encountered Dren preparing his men for battle and doubting whether the party knew what they were doing. Zinnia weighed the angles. If Dren died, that solved a problem, but if he stayed out of the fight, it might raise suspicion. After a brief exchange, they sent him and his Broken Blades into the fray.

Then they descended to the second level of the dungeon. They passed through a magically lit corridor pulsing with gray, green, and red light, its massive brass doors covered in leering faces and horrible scenes. At the entrance to the Fire Temple, Sequoia slipped a note under the door claiming to have information about a betrayal. When a voice, apparently Alrrem's, finally came through the door, the party took a careful approach. Their collective Insight told them these paranoid cultists respect double-crosses and raw power, so they played up their admiration for the power Alrrem had unleashed. Then they revealed the truth: Belsornig of the Water Temple had orchestrated the Chimera incident, using the Fire Temple as a pawn to eliminate the party or drain Alrrem's resources. The silence that followed was brief. Then Alrrem's enraged voice screamed Belsornig's name and ordered his troops to attack the Water Temple immediately.

The party vanished before the Fire Temple doors could open and made their way back to Hartsch. He was deep in ritual and speechifying, raising earth elementals from the ground and whipping his cultists into a frenzy. The party was welcomed back as heroes and publicly hailed as the Obsidian Edge before the assembled army. They swaggered up through the crowd trading fist bumps.

At the dais, Hartsch looked worried. Usually this is the moment when the people who installed a leader slaughter him and take over. The party made clear he was still their man, and he visibly relaxed. When the party told him that the Fire Temple was now fully distracted by its assault on the Water Temple, Hartsch erupted with triumphant fury and ordered a full-scale charge. The war had begun.

As Hartsch's forces surged forward, the party followed just long enough to maintain appearances before peeling away at the stairs. The DM explained that the war would now play out as a series of skirmishes, events, and random encounters, and that the party's actions would determine the final outcome. The advance quickly stalled. A lieutenant reported that Fire Temple bugbears had dragged heavy wooden pews and stone rubble across the stairway to the second level, creating a makeshift barricade that was bottlenecking the entire Earth Temple assault. Hartsch turned to the Obsidian Edge and asked them to do what his army could not.

The party chose to bypass the barricade entirely by cutting through the Air Temple, a section of the dungeon that was eerily, unsettlingly quiet. They knew a wraith haunted its halls. Zinnia's and Calmer's Stealth rolls gave the party away, and sure enough, the spectral guardian began drifting toward them through the dim corridors. Rather than engage it, the party ran. The wraith, seemingly satisfied once they had fled its domain, turned back into the darkness. Calmer caught his breath and, after a Religion check, noted with grim amusement that the Air Temple's leader Kelno was so ignorant he apparently believed the wraith to be an air elemental. It was a fitting symbol of the faction's irrelevance.

Emerging onto the second level, the party found a chaotic battlefield flooded with water and choked with zones of scalding steam, where Fire and Water Temple forces were already tearing into each other. Dead bodies from both factions littered the ground, and ogres were present in the area. Through the haze, the party could see the Fire Temple bugbears holding the barricade against the Earth Temple's stalled advance. Sequoia wondered whether they could simply wait the war out. The DM made clear that if the Earth Temple's troops were all cut down at the barricade, the war would not go well. To let Hartsch's army charge through, the four bugbears had to be killed or badly wounded.

The party prepared to strike, coordinating their assault to hit the bugbears from behind and break the stalemate. Zephyr moved first, rushing the lead Fire Temple warrior through the mist with a pair of precise dagger strikes, and the battle for the second level began in earnest.

Before the combat could continue, the DM paused the session. For killing one of the four temple leaders, every party member advanced to level 6. The players spent the remaining time choosing new expertise and abilities.

## NPCs
### Alrrem
The leader of the Fire Temple, described as "the all-consuming." He sent the Chimera to destroy the party. When the party revealed that Belsornig of the Water Temple had orchestrated the Chimera incident to bankrupt him, he flew into a rage and immediately ordered his troops to attack the Water Temple.
- He is desperate and nearly broken. He gambled everything on the Chimera, spent all his money, and lost his werewolves to the party.
- A voice through the door, apparently his, demanded proof of loyalty: "Have you come to swear loyalty to Alrrem and the Fire Temple and the Burning Fires?… and what proof, what proof do I have…"
- He was holed up in his temple, the Fire Temple's most defensible position.

### Hartsch
Romag's sub-general in the Earth Temple. He is ambitious and believes in the destructive power of earth over Romag's passive philosophy. He subtly encouraged the party to remove Romag. After the assassination he declared himself Supreme Prophet, marked his hands with Romag's blood, and launched a full-scale war against the Fire Temple. He is a megalomaniac who views the party as his elite vanguard, the "Obsidian Edge."
- While Sequoia eavesdropped, Hartsch flattered Romag while visibly rolling his eyes.
- He laid down his weapons the instant the doors slammed shut.
- He is easily flattered: "Yes, yes, I am powerful! Yes, I am!"
- He is not a tactical genius. He ordered the Edge to "stage a secret attack" and sent his army down a path the Fire Temple could barricade.
- He feared the party would kill him in front of his troops, as kingmakers often do. He was visibly relieved when they didn't, and became convinced the party sees his genius.

### Romag
The former High Priest of the Earth Temple, who advocated for a slow, erosive approach to power and believed dust to be the ultimate force. He was assassinated by the party in his own chambers after being isolated from his guards.
- He had AC 17.
- He invoked the teachings of a former chief, who believed "we must erode, not strike with earthen violence."
- He was too surprised to call the guards. He took 23 damage from Zinnia and Sneak Attack damage from Sequoia, then failed his save against Calmer's Toll the Dead.

### Belsornig
The prophet of the Water Temple, whose name appeared in the warlock's ledger as the orchestrator of the Chimera purchase. It was a calculated move to either eliminate the party or bankrupt the Fire Temple.
- The party used his betrayal to turn Alrrem's forces against the Water Temple.

### Captain Vesrek
Captain of the Crimson Trader, mentioned in the wax-sealed ledger as the one who sold the Chimera to the Fire Temple on behalf of the Water Temple.

### Kelno
The prophet of the Air Temple, whose faction is dismissed as a powerless remnant "reduced to a handful of rooms and no meaningful power." Calmer noted that Kelno is so ignorant he mistakes the wraith haunting his temple for an air elemental.

### Dren
Leader of the Broken Blades faction and an ally to the party. He was enlisted to set up a morning meeting with Romag and later rallied his men to join the temple war with promises of plunder and blood.
- He is chaotic evil, so the party kept the full plan from him.
- The Chimera kill impressed him, and he concluded he "picked the winning side."
- He openly doubted the war: "Do you guys know what the hell you're doing?"
- Zinnia weighed whether his death would be a convenience or a loss before the party sent him into the fight.

### Calmer
A cleric of St. Cuthbert and member of the party. He healed fallen allies during the Chimera battle, delivered the killing blow to both the Chimera and Romag using necrotic power, and assisted in the infiltration despite being naturally noisy.
- He learned new spells suited to sneaky work and cast Guidance on the party.
- He was reluctant after Zephyr's recent needling, but was persuaded that Romag was "an evil man."
- He nearly shouted "For Saint Cuthbert" mid-assassination.
- He failed his Stealth in the Air Temple, then identified the wraith with a Religion check.

### Hartsch's Lieutenant
A commander in the Earth Temple army. He reported to the party that the Fire Temple bugbears had blocked the stairs to the second level with a makeshift barricade, and asked for the Obsidian Edge's help to break the stalemate: "The Obsidian Edge, your prophet must help us!"

### Air Temple Wraith
A spectral undead guardian haunting the Air Temple. It briefly pursued the party before they fled its domain, then turned back. Kelno mistakes it for an air elemental.

## Locations
### Earth Temple
The base of operations for the Earth Temple faction. Here the party survived the Chimera attack and plotted and executed the coup against Romag, and Hartsch rallied his troops by summoning earth elementals and delivering speeches about a new era of destructive power.
- A secret door to the south on the first-level map gave the party a back way into the high priest's quarters.
- Romag's private room adjoins the sleeping chamber through a curtain, and its door to the south opens onto the guards.

### High Priest's Sleeping Chamber
A room decorated with earthen motifs, with a dirt-covered floor, a small local altar, and a desk with personal notes. The party infiltrated this chamber via a secret passage and used it as a staging point for the assassination of Romag.
- A curtain on its south side separates it from the room where Romag and Hartsch met.

### Second Level Corridor
A twenty-foot-wide passageway lit by magical means:
- disgusting gray luminosity in the center and the short northern extension
- green radiance to the west
- bands of dull red light floating in the air to the east

It features heavy brass double doors covered in horrible scenes and leering faces, and to the south a massive solid bronze portcullis raised and lowered by a windlass on its east side.

### Fire Temple Entrance
A defensible position on the second level, behind the corridor's eastern door. The party slipped a note under the door and negotiated with Alrrem through it, revealing the Water Temple's betrayal and triggering the Fire Temple's attack on the Water Temple.

### The Air Temple
An eerily quiet section of the dungeon haunted by a wraith that the Air Temple's leader, Kelno, mistakes for an air elemental. The party passed through it quickly to bypass the barricade blocking the Earth Temple's advance. Their route led them through its southern door.

### The Second Level War Zone
A chaotic battlefield flooded with water and filled with zones of scalding steam where Fire and Water Temple forces clashed. Dead bodies from both temples were visible, and Fire Temple bugbears held a makeshift barricade of pews and rubble against the Earth Temple's forces.
- Ogres were present on the battlefield.
- Earth Temple bugbears were stuck in front of the barricade and debris, trying to take it.
- The haze grants partial cover against attacks from range.

Cast by Zephyr to make the unlocked doors of Romag's chamber slam shut, sealing the room and preventing guards from intervening during the assassination. In Zephyr's words, it "can cause an unlock… an unlocked door to slap."

Considered by Calmer against Romag, then set aside for Toll the Dead because positioning would have required him to move.
