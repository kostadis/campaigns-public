# Chapter 32 A Blazing New Beginning

Date: 2026-05-31

## Summary
The session opened with housekeeping. The characters were levelled up to 8th level, a step skipped the previous week; some had been sitting at 6th. Sequoia discovered Reliable Talent ("I don't fail stealth checks anymore, by the way"). The DM restated his house rule that a natural 1 always fails, and that a character with a bonus over +20 must roll double ones to fail. Calmer gained 4th-level spells, including Fire Shield, Freedom of Movement, Death Ward and Aura of Life, which the DM said Calmer would keep permanently so he "will never be killed by a Wraith again." He now has Wisdom +5, a spell save DC of 16, and he now does 3d8+5 damage thanks to his newly acquired 7th-level cleric ability. The DM then read the recap of Chapter 27, "Dungeon Boss," before play resumed on the second level.

The dust had barely settled on the fall of the elemental prophets when Barkinar, a cold and composed leader of the Greater Temple, summoned the party into a vast, heavily guarded chamber flanked by ogre bodyguards and a hill giant. He looked upon them with something approaching genuine admiration, remarking that Romag, Hartsch, Alrrem, and Belsornig had all been problems on his list the previous morning, and that four problems were gone by sundown. Without waiting for a response, he declared the Upper Temple theirs, tasked them with stabilizing the leaderless elemental factions, recovering the Chest of Rao, and warned them to remain civil toward the ambitious and paranoid Lord Wizard Senshock — and to let him underestimate them. Then he turned and walked back into the dark, leaving the party standing at the heart of the very organization they had come to destroy, now its rulers.

The DM posted the formal task list in the Discord chat:
- pacify the Fire Temple
- resolve the troll contract
- audit and secure the Water Temple treasury
- formalize Dren's command
- recover the Chest of Rao
- deal with Kelno
- "figure out who this damn necromancer is, if he's still alive"

The weight of their new responsibilities settled quickly. Dren Halveth, the mercenary captain who had fought loyally at their side, approached and looked to Calmer as the new boss, seeking clarity on his own standing. After some deliberation, the party agreed to promote Dren to lead the Earth Temple's forces, with the understanding that he would report directly to Calmer. Calmer, visibly pleased with himself, wasted no time relocating the party's headquarters to the far more comfortable chambers once belonging to Hartsch. He proposed a ceremony of sorts and a meeting of all the troops later that day to announce "changes in the management." Zinnia's insight left him without a "warm fuzzy" about Calmer's infatuation with his new role. Sequoia's insight confirmed the uncomfortable truth: the party now had bosses.

Zephyr, realizing that the temples' leadership needed to be addressed, suggested elections. Dren burst out laughing. Sequoia reminded him that they were in an evil temple and that they had not exactly come to power by peaceful means. Dren, after he stopped laughing, explained with barely concealed amusement that Alrrem and his only remaining apprentice had died in a failed attempt to summon a fire elemental. By the rules as Dren understood them, only clerics can rule the temples, and the Fire Temple now had none. The Greater Temple wanted the mess cleaned up before it installed a new Fire Temple cleric, which meant the party now had a narrow window of roughly one week to plunder the Fire Temple before order was expected to be restored there.

The party descended into the Fire Temple without hesitation, walking in through the front entrance now that they were in charge. Sequoia announced the new plan to take down the temple: "We're gonna bankrupt it." The great doors of beaten brass swung open to reveal a massive hall of red granite, lit by scores of torches and flaming crescents, the air thick with the smell of heated metal and sulfur. Tapestries depicting scenes of fire and evil adorned the walls, and a large brass tube hung suspended from the ceiling by chains. Copper cauldrons filled with a glowing amber liquid flanked the central area, and at the far southern end stood a translucent golden altar surrounded by skulls inlaid with hammered gold. At the heart of it all burned a diamond-shaped fire pit, its coals glowing hot, with tiny forms cavorting within the flames — unmistakable signs of elemental life lurking just beneath the surface.

It was Calmer who suggested dousing the coals with water. An insight check had already told Zephyr that the pit was magical and evil, and the likeliest hiding place for the treasure. Zephyr gave the go-ahead. The moment the flask was emptied into the pit, a great eruption of steam and vapor filled the chamber — and the tiny forms within began to grow. Three fire salamanders, serpentine and blazing, hauled themselves out of the pit and sniffed the air with predatory calm. The party erupted into a heated argument over whose idea it had been. Calmer insisted "Zephyr told me to do it!" and sheepishly admitted he was "just trying to sound pompous and arrogant" when he had claimed to have read about the situation moments before. With the creatures now fully emerged and hunting, the party scrambled behind the great stone pillars lining the hall, and Calmer wrapped himself in a protective aura of biting frost to ward off the heat.

The salamanders moved with terrifying purpose. Two of them zeroed in on Calmer as the most obvious target, attempting to coil around him, though he managed to twist free of their grasps each time. Zephyr, hidden in the shadows, seized the moment and burst from concealment to unleash a devastating ambush on one of the creatures, striking with lethal precision and dealing a staggering amount of damage before the salamander even knew he was there. Zinnia charged across the battlefield in a blur of motion, landing a series of powerful blows against another foe before wisely retreating out of reach. Sequoia, meanwhile, moved through the chaos with quiet efficiency, cutting down one of the injured salamanders before the creature could recover.

The tide of battle shifted when one of the salamanders drove its flame spear into Calmer, the searing heat washing over him — but the frost aura surrounding the cleric retaliated instantly, blasting the creature with a burst of intense cold that sent it reeling backward and fleeing across the chamber. Calmer swung his mace at the retreating beast as it passed, but the blow sailed wide, missing entirely. Sequoia, badly wounded from a previous exchange, disengaged from the front lines and called out for healing, which Calmer provided with a surge of divine energy before turning his attention back to the remaining salamanders. With the creatures bloodied and the party regrouping behind the pillars, the Fire Temple's fearsome guardians were on the back foot — and the plundering of the temple was only just beginning.

The session ended mid-combat. Zephyr called it an "easy fight," and the table agreed that the real problem was a boss promoted above his qualifications.

- **The hall:** 40 feet wide and 90 feet long under a vaulted ceiling 30 feet high. Its swirling blue-and-green malachite is enchanted to glow, so the chamber feels as though it lies at the bottom of the sea.
- **Altar:** A great bronze altar sculpted with marine creatures. Its concave top forms a basin holding water, coins, gems, and seashells.
- **Idol:** A horrific serpentine idol, 8 feet high, depicting a writhing mass of fish, eels, and octopi.
- **Gargoyles:** Four bronze gargoyle statues, coated in verdigris, stand on the corner plinths.
- **Plunder:** The DM notes it is now completely unoccupied, with gold and treasure likely there for the taking.

### Hartsch's Chambers
The former headquarters of the Prophet of Earth — Romag's chambers, which Hartsch took over as head of the Earth Temple — now claimed by Calmer as the party's new base of operations due to its superior comfort.
- They contain Hartsch's books, which Calmer pores over to "read up on some of the rituals."

### The Earth Temple
The area where the party has recently taken control and where they plan to hold a ceremony to announce changes in management.
- Calmer ordered Dren to gather all the troops there "later today."

### The Fire Temple
A grand hall of red granite adorned with massive beaten brass doors and tapestries depicting scenes of fire and evil. It features a large brass tube suspended from the ceiling, copper cauldrons filled with glowing amber liquid, and a central diamond-shaped fire pit filled with magical coals where fire salamanders were awakened.
- **Access:** Reached via the second level's front entrance.
- **Lighting and walls:** Scores of flambeaux light the hall. Blue streaks and mica flakes dance in the granite.
- **Brass tube:** 20 feet long and 2 feet in diameter. A shallow fire pit lies to its north, with brass stands of charcoal blocks on either side.
- **Cauldrons:** East and west, beyond lines of pillars. Before each stands an octagonal brass table with 16 bowls.
- **Altar:** A translucent golden block, 3 feet wide and high and 9 feet long, set with gold-inlaid skulls and topped with an eight-pointed fire symbol in wrought gold.
- **Fire pit:** 10 feet by 8 feet and several feet deep. Its fire is magical and evil in nature, and the party believes the treasure is hidden there.
- **Pillars:** Line the hall and gave the party cover during the fight.
