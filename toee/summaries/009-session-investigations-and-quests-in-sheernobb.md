# Chapter 09 Investigations, and quests in Sheernobb.

Date: Planting 21, 579 (early afternoon, then late afternoon) to Planting 22, 579

## Summary

The chapter opens on Zephyr, stung by an expensive bill (“Well, that was expensive!”) that felt as if Mari Clee had cleanly picked his pocket, and looking for an opportunity to get out. He proposes exploring the village of Sheernobb to learn anything he can; Calmer finishes his mead and answers, in as disrespectful a tone as he can muster, “As you wish, boss!” Sheernobb is a small town built around the Moot Hall, with a road that splits into a south and a north branch around it and rejoins beyond it. Most stores sit on the circle those branches form, and the Tavern is on the south branch. Zephyr recalls a bookstore on the northern branch, but chooses to walk the southern branch first, naming the shops as he passes: “Djinibac’s Apothecary, a Tabac Shop, Fiver Woodcarver, Peony’s Mill, and would you look at that a pond, Henker’s Shop, Naglo Crystal Divine Shop, and there is Grindin Books.”

Grindin Books is a cluttered, dimly-lit cottage shop built to its owner's specifications for reading, with a reclining chair, a stool and green-tinted candle bulbs behind the counter. Its owner is the gnome Grindin Well-Read. The chapter then switches to Zinnia's point of view. Zinnia, a spy, reasons that bookstore owners have either a lot of information or none, and watches how Grindin answers Zephyr's question about bandits. Grindin's concern is not merchandise but his stolen conference invitation: “My invitation to the conference was stolen! I didn’t have the opportunity to reject the invitation!” The bookkeeper suggests enchanted wards and protective charms for caravans, and Zinnia sighs, “Another ignorant fool.” Just as Zinnia is about to leave in irritation, Grindin confirms that Pasmarie Clee has information on the bandits. Zephyr finds a map of the local area, and Zinnia notes a book titled Map of the Elemental Nexus that could be purchased later.

In Sequoia's section the halfling leaves the bookstore and urges the group to the Moot Hall, since “The mayor may have more information and a bigger reward.” Before the entrance stands a statue with a sign reading Keenleene, Founder of Sheernobb. The stone figure comes to life, laughs, picks up his pipes and plays a lively tune; Sequoia shrugs it off: “Gnomes are master illusionists. And they love to put on shows like this.” Runes over the stone doors read “Ni te pharkalu, quo lun verra lu,” with the common translation “If you see it, it’s not the right it.” Inside, an elder gnome is asleep in an oak chair until a weasel jumps into his lap. He introduces himself as Glennddarc, Headgnome of the village. Told by Sequoia that the party seeks information on bandits, he is annoyed (“Bandits? We have problems with giants, and you are interested in Bandits?”). Sequoia explains that Gnonsbim, the Captain of the Guard, asked them to investigate; Glennddarc retorts that Gnonsbim is running for re-election and wants to show he's a man of action, and snickers that Gnonsbim has gone sweet on Pasmarie. He directs them to Pasmarie's beehives and says that for helping the town he needs help with some giants, for greater rewards. He falls asleep again before Sequoia can protest.

In the late afternoon, a quarter mile southeast of Sheernobb, the party reaches Pasmarie's Beehives. Pasmarie Clee, a petite gnome woman, says a shipment of her honey bound for the merchants of Verbobonc was stolen when a caravan was ambushed on the trade road just north of the village. The surviving guards said the attackers weren't bandits but spoke of the ‘Black Earth’ and carried weapons of stone; they vanished into the Kron Hills. She asks the party to follow the trail, find the raiders and bring the honey back, offering gratitude and a share of her finest enchanted honey. Zephyr thinks of it as a reward added to the other reward, and that they get to find the chest. She says the last raid was a few miles to the north east, and marks Zephyr's purchased map.

On Planting 22, after a good night's rest and breakfast, the half-orc cook Quinna Poul points to a spot on Zephyr's map where he might find a boar, “a tricky little bugger.” The boar is in the Gnarley Forest, and Zephyr reads notes on his map describing the forest. In a valley he meets an old woman who calls him “My dear!” and offers sweets. Zephyr is unconvinced, but Zinnia wants to go; the old woman introduces herself as Aunt Emma and says her house is made of gingerbread, and they follow her to it. Zinnia identifies her: “She’s a hag.” Zinnia draws his sword; Aunt Emma screams and her illusion drops, revealing a green hag. She threatens to eat them all, then pleads for her life in exchange for her treasure, but Zephyr kills her.

Zinnia's section says he had long wanted to kill a hag and feels relief at the vengeance. He finds a hat of Wizardry, which he thinks can be sold or shared with Spugnoir, and a book titled “The Hag's Guide to 101 Recipes Of Children.” He sniffs the pie and realizes it is poisonous, but decides the frosting and gingerbread must be genuine, breaks off a piece, chews, and smiles. Zephyr finds the boar's trail to a glade of bronze wood trees. Calmer is told to stay put: “You are not required for this.” Zephyr's illusion fails to trick the boar but Zinnia's succeeds, and hours later Zinnia stands with the boar next to Quinna, who is delighted, will throw the most fantastic feast on the 30th of this month, and promises the party a feast of their own: “Tomorrow, you will dine the likes of which you have never dined!”

Glennon Clee then asks Zinnia whether he found his barrels. Zinnia says no, but yes he will. Glennon shows him where the barrels were stolen, and Zinnia calls it an “Easy trail.” An hour or so later, in another glade with a solitary tree, they find a humanoid with branches extruding from its arms and leaves growing on its head, crying over a barrel. When Zephyr approaches, it explains that it is a dryad whose beloved doesn't think they are their beloved. It asks Zephyr “You’re a tiefling?”, is told “Yes.”, and asks whether they have come from the abyss to torment it; Zephyr, gritting his teeth, answers “No, but you really should not steal.” The dryad says it was told that when humans are dumped, they drink. Zephyr explains that the barrel wasn't the dryad's for the taking and that stealing is wrong. The dryad contemplates this and declares, “Well, I didn’t want to steal. I was just so upset.” The chapter ends there.

## Locations

### Sheernobb
A small town whose dominant building is the Moot Hall. The road ends at the Moot Hall and splits into a south and a north branch that converge on the road out of town; most stores sit on the circle they form, with the Tavern on the south branch. Shops named: Djinibac’s Apothecary, a Tabac Shop, Fiver Woodcarver, Peony’s Mill, a pond, Henker’s Shop, Naglo Crystal Divine Shop, and Grindin Books.

### Grindin Books
A cottage bookshop with a studded oak door, books, maps and scrolls on solid oak shelves, and a reading nook for the owner, Grindin Well-Read.

### The Moot Hall
On the largest hill in the center of Sheernobb, with wide stone steps and grand stone doors carved with runes. The doors open into the Feasting Hall, the village's largest building, used for special gatherings. A statue of Keenleene stands before the entrance.

### Pasmarie’s Beehives
A quarter mile southeast of Sheernobb, a farm surrounded by fields and wildflowers, with neatly arranged hives and a small building where Pasmarie Clee stands.

### Gnarley Forest
Where the boar lives. Zephyr's map carries notes on it. Its interior features fertile glades and valleys, where the party meets Aunt Emma and later the dryad.

## NPCs

### Grindin Well-Read
A gnome bookseller seated on a cushion-covered chair. Zinnia reads him as annoyed at the party's intrusion; he is concerned about his stolen conference invitation, suggests enchanted wards for caravans, and confirms that Pasmarie Clee has information on the bandits.

### Glennddarc
Elder gnome and Headgnome of Sheernobb, with brown wrinkled skin and long white wild hair, who dozes with a weasel in his lap. He is grouchy, dismissive of Gnonsbim, who he says is running for re-election and has gone sweet on Pasmarie, and wants the party to help with giants instead.

### Keenleene
Founder of Sheernobb, shown as a statue that comes to life to laugh and play pipes. Sequoia attributes it to gnomish illusion.

### Pasmarie Clee
A petite gnome woman who keeps Sheernobb's beehives. She asks the party to recover her stolen honey shipment and offers gratitude and enchanted honey.

### Quinna Poul

### Aunt Emma
A green hag disguised as an old woman with a gingerbread house. She tried to lure the party with sweets, pleaded for her life when exposed, and was killed by Zephyr.

### Glennon Clee
