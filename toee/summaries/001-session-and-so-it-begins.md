# Chapter 01 And so it begins

Date: Planting 1 through 15, 579

## Summary

The heroes arrived in Hommlet, a small village at the edge of Verbobonc’s territory near the Gnarley Forest. Before arriving they had protected a caravan from Narwell that went through the forest (the Gnarley Forest). The journey through the forest led them to discover brigands, and one of those brigands worked for Canon Belsornig, a leader of the Temple of Water.

Upon arrival, they visited the Inn of the Welcome Wench, met Spugnoir Balkof and Furnok, the fabulous rogue, and encountered the innkeeper Ostler Gundigoot. The group also visited the church of St. Cuthbert, where the cleric was invited to join the group. They visited the traders Rannos and Gremag, who convinced them to hire men-at-arms for protection. They also visited the leatherworker, who would sell all his goods through the traders. At the chapter's close the group is at the Inn of the Welcome Wench.
