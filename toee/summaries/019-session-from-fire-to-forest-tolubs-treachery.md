# Chapter 19 From Fire to Forest: Tolub’s Treachery

Date: 2025-11-15

## Summary

The party found themselves catching their breath in the wilderness after a harrowing escape from the Hall of the Scarlet Moon. The session picked up where the last one had left them, fleeing the two fire priests after their fireballs, still invisible, into the darkness, far away from anyone. Now, hidden in the hills with their hearts still racing, they debated their next move. Zephyr summed up the mood: "Well, we almost just died, so I think we should go back to… I think we should go back to the town. So as to not die."

Zinnia's expertise proved invaluable as he found them a well-hidden cave to rest in and carefully obscured their tracks, muttering about his companions' inability to hide. During the night, patrols passed nearby but failed to discover their hiding spot. By morning Calmer, recovered from his brush with death, insisted they needed to change their approach. He declared he was done being the face of the party, tired of looking like a fool and nearly getting killed. The party's strength, he argued, lay in infiltration and stealth, not direct confrontation. After some discussion, they agreed on a plan: return to Nulb and confront Tolub, proving they had survived whatever test he had thrown at them, and demand an audience with the temple leadership. Calmer made clear that the other three would do the talking. Zinnia's concern for Calmer's wellbeing—though he claimed it was merely because reviving him was annoying—brought a moment of levity to their tense situation, as did Zinnia's refusal to explain the origin of the moniker "The Masked One."

The party chose to travel stealthily through the forest rather than risk being seen in towns. The journey back to Nulb took about two days at a careful pace. As they made their way, they encountered a suspicious patch of soft ground that collapsed when poked, revealing a nest of angry poisonous snakes that they carefully avoided.

Later, while moving quietly through the woods, they spotted a group of gnolls dressed in fire temple garb. The party successfully hid as the creatures marched past, clearly searching for something or someone, sniffing the air and looking around. Zinnia noticed the gnolls seemed to be on patrol, possibly looking for the party themselves or dealing with the various bandit factions that plagued the region. Zephyr suggested kidnapping some of them, but none of the party spoke Gnoll, and the party chose not to fight. The encounter reinforced how dangerous the area had become and how carefully they needed to move.

As they drew closer to Nulb, the party observed a bandit chief leading another group of gnolls through the forest. Sequoia's keen hearing picked up the chief muttering complaints about recruiting gnolls from the local gnoll king for the fire temple. This revelation was troubling—the Temple of Elemental Evil wasn't just surviving, it was actively growing and recruiting forces. The party realized that stopping individual gnoll groups would be futile; they needed to eliminate whoever was doing the recruiting. They also learned more about the temple's structure: four cults led by prophets—Romag of Earth, Belsornig of Water, and two unnamed leaders of Fire and Air—all ruled by a mysterious deep temple. They recalled that Rannos Davl of Hommlet had worked for the deep temple, and that Lareth had "worked" for the fire temple, while the deep temple disliked Lareth and tried to subvert him.

Upon reaching Nulb, the party made their way to the Boatman's Tavern. Skole, the bartender, was visibly shocked to see them alive. He revealed that Tolub had been telling everyone he had disposed of the party, claiming they were meddlers who didn't belong. Taking them into his back room, Skole confided that he despised both Tolub and Lodriss, the tavern's actual owner, and wished to be free of them both—he had come to Nulb "for some peace and quiet" and wanted his bar back. More importantly, he told them that if they wanted to contact the Earth Temple, they needed to speak with Dick Rentsch at the Waterside Hostel, not Tolub, who worked for the fire prophet.

The party walked openly through Nulb toward the Waterside Hostel, passing Gallows Table where they saw a dead man hanging from the gallows—Elfar Twell, executed on the orders of the powerful Madame Selentis for the crime of crossing her. They encountered various merchant stalls, including Magda's Trinket Trove with its strange magical curiosities like cursed ink, and Garrick's armory selling rusted weapons. These encounters painted a picture of Nulb as a desperate, dangerous place where power was everything.

At the Waterside Hostel, a dilapidated building with sagging timbers and lopsided walls, they met Dick Rentsch. The gruff proprietor sized them up with suspicion and warned them he ran a tight operation that didn't tolerate trouble. Zephyr introduced himself by name and dismissed his companions as his "assistants." When the party mentioned they had something for the prophet, he left Wat to mind the bar, and they noticed the bandit chief from the forest was also present in the tavern, suggesting this was neutral ground where various factions coexisted uneasily. In his back office, Dick Rentsch grew visibly excited when they hinted at an item the Fire Temple had been eager to get, but refused to bring them to the prophet without first knowing he could trust them.

While waiting for Dick Rentsch to return that evening, the party was approached by Wat, the bartender, who attempted to extract information about their business. The party deflected his questions, claiming it was a personal matter. Wat asked whether their business was about Dala, seemed relieved when it was not, and warned them not to drink the beer and serving them gin instead. When Dick Rentsch finally returned, he explained that to earn his trust and the prophet's favor, they needed to complete a mission: ambush and kill a group of gnolls and their handlers who were being recruited for the fire temple that very night.

The party returned to the forest and positioned themselves for the ambush. When the bandit chief appeared with his gnoll recruits, he seemed paranoid, muttering that Dick Rentsch might be up to something. The party struck swiftly and decisively, catching the group in a surprise round. Sequoia and Zinnia focused their attacks on the bandit chief, cutting him down before he could react. Calmer created a terrifying booming voice that froze the gnolls in place, while Zephyr cut down two of them.

The battle was brutal but efficient. Sequoia delivered a devastating strike that literally cut one bandit in half, while Zinnia moved with deadly precision through the chaos. The remaining gnolls, driven by bloodlust despite their fear, fought desperately but were overwhelmed. The last bandit, realizing his leader was dead and contemplating his poor life choices, made a futile attack before Zinnia knocked him unconscious. When the dust settled, the party stood victorious over the bodies of their enemies, having proven themselves capable of the ruthless work the Earth Temple required. They had successfully completed Dick Rentsch's test and earned their entry into the Temple of Elemental Evil. The session ended with the unconscious bandit still available to be questioned next time.

## NPCs

Daughter of Eryk Velkori, leader of the Rhennee Caravan. In Chapter 14 she had been taken to the Iron Lash Brothel as payment for damages caused by the Rhennee and was rescued by the party for 500 gold pieces.

### Eryk Velkori

Leader of the Rhennee Caravan whose daughter Serena was rescued by the party in Chapter 14. He had declared them family and credited them the 500 gold pieces they paid for her release. He was not at the Boatman's Tavern when the party returned to Nulb.

### Vethra

Eryk Velkori's mother, who in Chapter 14 performed a harrowing divination to locate Serena when she was taken.

A contact who betrayed the party by directing them to the fire temple instead of the Earth Temple, intending for them to be killed. Skole and the party believe he works for the prophet of fire. He spread rumors that the party was dead. Skole says he dominates all the criminal elements in Nulb.

### Skole

The bartender of the Boatman's Tavern in Nulb, which Lodriss actually owns. He was surprised to see the party alive after Tolub claimed to have disposed of them. He dislikes both Tolub and Lodriss and wants to be free of them. He directed the party to Dick Rentsch at the Waterside Hostel as the contact for the Earth Temple.

- His stated reason for being in Nulb: "I just came to Nulb for some peace and quiet. And I got mixed up with some very sketchy people."

### Lodriss

The actual owner of the Boatman's Tavern in Nulb, mentioned by Skole as someone he wishes to be rid of. Skole described her as the one "who's in charge of this, who actually owns this place."

### Prophet Romag

The leader of the Earth Temple, one of the four cults of the Temple of Elemental Evil. The party seeks to contact him through Dick Rentsch. He wants the gnoll recruits and their handlers working for the fire temple dead.

### Prophet Belsornig

The leader of the Water Temple, one of the four cults of the Temple of Elemental Evil.

### Rannos Davl

A figure from Hommlet whom the party recalls worked for the deep temple, which rules over the four cults.

### Lareth

A figure the party recalls "worked" (the DM's air quotes) for the fire temple. The deep temple did not like Lareth and was trying to subvert him.

### Magda

An aging merchant who wears a patchwork cloak of mismatched fabrics and runs Magda's Trinket Trove, a stall selling strange odds and ends, charms, baubles, cursed ink, and other curiosities. She does not sell weapons and directed Zephyr to Garrick.

### Garrick

A grizzled ex-mercenary with a missing ear and a perpetual scowl who runs an armory stall in Nulb, selling rusted weapons that have 'seen action'. He hailed the party loudly ("You there, adventurer!"). He told them who Elfar Twell was and why he was hanged.

### Dick Rentsch

The gruff and unwelcoming proprietor of the Waterside Hostel in Nulb. He is the local contact for Prophet Romag of the Earth Temple. He is cautious but becomes excited when the party mentions having something valuable. He tasks the party with ambushing and killing gnoll recruits and their handlers for the fire temple to earn his trust.

- He has a broad chest and crosses his arms when sizing people up. He insisted on knowing the name of anyone who buys him a drink.
- He runs "a straight, tight ship" and warns troublemakers "you'll find yourself out on your arses quicker than you can draw a blade."
- He refused to take the party straight to the prophet: "that's not how it works. I need to know that you're somebody I can trust."
- The bandit chief knew Rentsch had been "talking to somebody today" and suspected he was up to something.

### Wat

A wiry man of medium height with deep-set, observant eyes and a complexion darkened by years of working in dimly lit environments. He works as a bartender at the Waterside Hostel and attempts to pump the party for information about their business with Dick Rentsch. He asked whether the party's business was about Dala, a serving wench, and looked visibly relieved when it was not. He may not fully work for Dick Rentsch despite being employed there.

- He has short, unkempt hair.

### Dala

A serving wench at the Waterside Hostel. Wat asked whether the party's business was about her.

### Bandit Chief

A leader of brigands who was observed recruiting gnolls from a local gnoll king for the fire temple. ### Gnolls

Fierce, hyena-like humanoids encountered in patrols within the forest. Some are affiliated with the Fire Temple, serving as recruits. Calmer describes them as a disgusting species that breeds by depositing larvae in living creatures, which then explode out of them, and says they either eat their captives or turn them into breeding creatures. Gnoll kings are glorified tribal leaders.

- On patrol, they sniffed the air and looked around as if searching for something.

## Locations

### Nulb

A town with a criminal underworld controlled by Madame Selentis from the shadows. The party previously investigated Serena's disappearance here and returns after fleeing the fire priests. Notable locations include the Boatman's Tavern, Waterside Hostel, and Gallows Table.

- On the way in the party passed a bakery and a cottage shop in ruins.
- Tolub's pirates hang out on the docks.
- The DM summed up its politics: "everybody is in with the temple. The question is with which part of the temple."

### Hall of the Scarlet Moon

An encampment in the Kron Hills featuring a square tower, a massive bonfire shaped like a fiery giant, and seven smaller camps. In Chapter 14 the party was ambushed here by fire priests Albreth and Ingulf after being betrayed by Captain Tolub. The DM likened the bonfire area to "Burning Man… the burning festival." Two fireballs exploded here during the party's escape, drawing everyone's attention.

### Kron Hills

The location of the Hall of the Scarlet Moon, where Captain Tolub had directed the party under false pretenses in Chapter 14.

### Hommlet

A town where the druid Jaroo resides, who in Chapter 14 warned the party about the suspicious nature of the Hall of the Scarlet Moon. Calmer suggested telling "some of our allies in Hommlet" about the gnoll patrols. The Waterside Hostel is the first building you reach when entering Nulb from Hommlet.

### Sheernobb

A location the party considers passing through but decides to avoid for stealth purposes on their journey back to Nulb.

### Wilderness Cave

A cave in the hills near the Hall of the Scarlet Moon where Zinnia found refuge for the party to hide and rest after fleeing the fire priests.

### Forest

A dense woodland area between the Kron Hills and Nulb, used by the party for stealthy travel. They encountered poisonous snakes, gnoll patrols, and later ambushed fire temple recruits here. A careful pace through it took about two days to reach Nulb.

### Boatman's Tavern

A tavern in Nulb owned by Lodriss but run by Skole as bartender. The party previously met many local figures here, and the Iron Lash brothel operates upstairs. The party returns here seeking information about Tolub. Skole keeps a private back room that serves as his office.

### Gallows Table

A public execution site in Nulb where notices from the constable are posted. Elfar Twell, said to be a brigand, was found hanging here, executed on Madame Selentis's orders. The notices were shared with the players as a "Notices from the Constable" handout, one of which concerned the hanging. The stalls of the armory, Hilda's Rotten Delights and Magda's Trinket Trove are nearby.

### Magda's Trinket Trove

A merchant stall in Nulb run by an aging merchant named Magda, overflowing with strange odds and ends, charms, baubles, and magical curiosities like cursed ink and glowing mushrooms.

### Garrick's Armory Stall

A merchant stall in Nulb run by Garrick, a grizzled ex-mercenary with a missing ear, selling rusted weapons that have 'seen action'.

### Waterside Hostel

A dilapidated building perched beside the crumbling bridge where the high road crosses the Imeryds Run, characterized by its sagging timber roof, lopsided walls, and a crooked sign. It serves as a hub of activity in Nulb and is run by Dick Rentsch, who is the local contact for the Earth Temple.

- It is the first building one encounters upon entering Nulb from Hommlet.
- The DM described its exterior as "disguised as a hub of scum and villainly."
- Inside it is a dimly lit tavern with clinking mugs and low murmurs, and Dick Rentsch has a back office.
- Drink prices: small beer 2 cp, river ale 3 cp, black pitch stout 6 cp, bog grog 8 cp.
- Rival factions drink side by side here; the fire temple's bandit chief was present.
