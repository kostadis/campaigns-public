# Chapter 17 Decimating the Moathouse and the Call to Nulb

Date: 2025-10-11

## Summary
The session resumed mid-fight in Lareth the Beautiful's halls, with initiative already rolled. The DM opened with "Big climactic battle, hopefully it doesn't end in, you know, TPK."

The climactic battle for the Moathouse erupted in a storm of steel and sorcery. Zephyr vanished into thin air using a pinch of magical dust, slipping silently behind Lareth the Beautiful while Zinnia and Sequoia cut down the surrounding guards with ruthless efficiency. But Lareth was no ordinary foe. He taunted the party with a sneer, revealing that he had conjured a perfect illusory double of himself, leaving the adventurers scrambling to determine which Lareth was real and which was a phantom. Zephyr asked whether any check could tell them apart, and the DM ruled none could. The DM numbered the two figures 1 and 2. Zephyr struck figure 1 only to find his blade passing through empty air, his invisibility dissolving in the same instant.

The battle grew desperate as Lareth unleashed a torrent of dark necrotic energy directly at Calmer. The force of it was so overwhelming that the cleric failed his saving throw against 4d10 damage and was left barely standing at 2 hit points, clinging to life by the thinnest of threads. Calmer, refusing to fall, poured every ounce of his divine power into healing himself and pressing on, even as the Sarge and the guards kept targeting him. Lareth conjured fresh illusory copies of himself with Mirror Image. Zephyr then cut Lareth down to a single hit point.

With a cold and calculated gesture, Lareth then froze both Zephyr and Zinnia in place with a paralyzing enchantment, leaving Sequoia and the battered Calmer to face him alone. With the rest of the party immobilized, Sequoia shook off a natural 1 and drove his blades home, and Lareth the Beautiful crumpled to the ground, dead at last. The paralysis lifted the moment Lareth died. The remaining cult fanatic sergeant screamed wild prophecies about the temple's vengeance and the purging fire of his goddess. He dropped Calmer to 4 hit points one last time, then he too was cut down, ending the battle. As the DM put it: "You now have incontrovertible proof of the presence of the Temple of Elemental Evil's resurgence that you can take to Burne and Rufus. And you have the chest of Rao."

The party swept through Lareth's lavishly appointed private chambers, a room dripping with thick rugs, fragrant incense, fine wines, and dishes of sweetmeats, a stark contrast to the blood-soaked halls outside. From his body and his quarters, they recovered:
- a fearsome enchanted mace
- a suit of magical plate armor
- a ring that would free its wearer from any force that sought to bind them
- a stunning fire opal necklace studded with diamond chips
- a gleaming silver service set
- a collection of nuggets
- a pile of gold

The haul was staggering, and the adventurers divided the wealth among themselves before turning their attention to the rest of the dungeon. The plate mail, it turned out, was useless to them: "we don't have any fighters." The party also noted from the guards' quarters that they had killed six of Lareth's goons and six more were unaccounted for. Everyone gained a level. They also quietly contacted the Duke, their shadowy fixer, arranging for him to come and make the evidence of the slaughter disappear for a modest fee of fifty gold pieces. Zephyr made the call using his "magical phone."

Deeper in the dungeon, the party stumbled upon a chamber full of gnolls, dim-witted mercenaries who were very much still alive and very much armed. Sequoia, now leading because Calmer "doesn't do the front anymore" after his near-death, almost blew it by greeting them as "Bunch of adventurers." Rather than draw more blood, the party recovered and spun a convincing tale, presenting themselves as allies of Lareth. They assured the gnolls that their employer was planning a generous pay raise. The gnolls, grumbling about unpaid wages and insufficient food, were delighted by the news. In the process they revealed that Lareth had a deal with a gnoll "king" who supplied fighters. A final bluff that Lareth was too busy for visitors fell flat, and the gnolls sent a delegation marching off toward Lareth's chambers, blissfully unaware that they were heading toward a corpse. The party slipped away before the gnolls could discover the truth.

Elsewhere in the dungeon's winding passages, the adventurers found a hidden room containing a mysterious mechanical lever. Pulling it sent a heavy iron grate crashing down with a "BOOM!" to seal off a corridor, and no amount of effort could budge it back into place. Nearby, a hallway of three doors revealed two empty rooms and one false door, which opened onto a blank wall and was rigged with hidden cables. This was a trap designed to drop the very same gate and seal off intruders. The party pieced together the mechanism and moved on, leaving the trap behind.

The final chamber of the dungeon held six bugbears, hairy, brutish goblinoids who were equally dissatisfied with their arrangement under Lareth. One of them stepped forward and demanded to know who the party was. He banged his mace on the ground and bellowed about broken promises of plunder and food. An offer to lead them to plunder the Temple of Elemental Evil instead nearly unmasked the party as Lareth's enemies. Zephyr seized the moment, revealing that Lareth was dead and that the entire dungeon was now ripe for the taking, and that the gnolls were already on their way to grab everything first. He rolled a 25 on Persuasion, revealing to his teammates' dismay that he had a +9 all along. The bugbears erupted in gleeful chaos and charged off cheering to claim their prize. This set the two remaining factions against each other and cleared the dungeon without the party having to lift a single blade.

With the Moathouse fully cleared, the party returned to the village of Hommlet, choosing to keep everything "on the low." They requested an urgent secret meeting with the town's leadership, overriding Ostler's suggestion that it "could wait till next week." In the back room of the Inn of the Welcome Wench, they laid out their findings before Ostler Gundigoot, Captain Rufus, Burne, Jaroo, and the Village Elder, presenting undeniable proof that the Temple of Elemental Evil had risen again. The council was shaken, with Jaroo gravely noting that there was only one course of action left. The party must travel north to the shadowy village of Nulb, uncover its ties to the temple, and then infiltrate the temple itself to disrupt whatever dark machinations were unfolding within. The party was praised for a great service: Lareth's defeat would weaken the bandits but would not end the temple's threat. The council agreed to:
- keep the information secret, because "the temple is still very powerful"
- accelerate the construction of Hommlet's new castle
- prepare the militia

Meanwhile, the adventurers quietly kept the Chest of Rao to themselves, letting the council go on believing it had been taken, as they prepared for the road ahead. The DM warned: "Eventually, somebody's gonna come looking for the chest." Next session begins the Temple of Elemental Evil phase of the adventure.

## NPCs
### Lareth the Beautiful
A paranoid and dangerous leader within the Moathouse who used illusions and dark magic to defend himself. He created illusory duplicates of himself to confuse attackers, cast paralyzing enchantments on Zephyr and Zinnia, and nearly killed Calmer with a devastating necrotic spell before being slain by Sequoia.
- He had AC 19.
- The DM ruled his "perfect simulation" double gave him advantage on attacks.
- He ordered a guard to hunt the invisible Zephyr.
- He resisted Calmer's Toll the Dead at one hit point.
- He refused to surrender even at one hit point.
- His body carried a mace of smiting, magical plate mail, a ring of free action, and a large amount of gold.

### Sarge
A cult fanatic sergeant who led the guard defense of the Moathouse, focusing his attacks on Calmer. He fought to the death, screaming about the revenge of the temple and the purging fire of his goddess before being cut down.
- He had AC 11.
- He made repeated multi-attack strikes on Calmer, hitting for 6, 9, and 12 among others.
- Per the DM, he deliberately targeted Calmer, "realizing that killing Calmer will significantly weaken you."
- He was finished off by Zephyr and Sequoia.

### Thorne (the Duke)
A shadowy fixer who provides cleanup services for the party, removing bodies and evidence. He operates on a monthly retainer plus a per-incident fee, and agreed to come clean up the Moathouse for 50 gold pieces.
- This job cost 50 GP. The DM restated his terms as 100 GP per month plus 50 per incident.
- He refuses to come until the site is clear of threats.

### Gnoll Guards
A group of eight dim-witted mercenaries allied with Lareth, primarily motivated by pay and food. They were deceived by the party into believing Lareth was planning a pay raise, and sent a delegation to find him unaware he was dead.
- They speak broken Common.
- They serve a gnoll "king" allied with Lareth, who is supposed to pay them.
- They threatened that the king would "not provide any more gnolls to fight" without a raise.

### Bugbears
Hairy, opportunistic goblinoid raiders occupying a chamber in the Moathouse dungeon. They were dissatisfied with Lareth over lack of food and plunder, and were easily convinced to abandon their post and pillage the dungeon once told Lareth was dead.
- They are intelligent and speak Common.
- Their spokesman, marked number one on the map, banged his mace on the ground while airing grievances.
- He briefly saw through the party's "friends of Lareth" claim.

### Ostler Gundigoot
The proprietor of the Inn of the Welcome Wench and a member of the town council who facilitated the secret emergency meeting with Hommlet's leadership.
- He was initially inclined to wait a week before meeting.
- He hosted the meeting in his back room.
- He was shocked the temple had returned ("I thought we destroyed it").

## Spells
### Inflict Wounds
A devastating necrotic spell cast by Lareth the Beautiful directly against Calmer, dealing massive damage and nearly killing the cleric outright, leaving him with only a sliver of health.

### Mirror Image
Cast by Lareth the Beautiful to create illusory duplicates of himself, making it extremely difficult for the party to land a solid hit. He cast it once, after his first illusory double had been exposed; the party had to destroy each duplicate before they could reliably strike the real Lareth.

A necrotic cantrip used by Calmer in an attempt to finish off the gravely wounded Lareth the Beautiful. Lareth managed to resist its effects, surviving the attempt. Calmer chose it "because that does not require a hit," since "It's not an attack roll."

### Cure Light Wounds
Healing magic employed by Calmer multiple times throughout the battle to keep the party standing, including restoring Sequoia's health mid-combat and healing himself after Lareth's devastating necrotic assault.
