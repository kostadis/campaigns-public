# Chapter 13 The mysteries of the temple

Date: not stated in chapter

## Summary

The chapter opens with a combat trial: the party fought the monastery's martial monks in a grueling, non-lethal bout meant to test their strength and weaknesses. During the exchange of longswords and short swords, Sequoia was knocked unconscious, and Calmer's healing magic swiftly revived him.

After the bout, Zephyr and Zinnia interrogated a captive monk. Zephyr used intimidation successfully and shattered the monastery's peaceful facade. The captive confessed that the monastery was not devoted to the nature goddess Beory but to the Earth Cult. The captive further revealed that a ritual to summon a Titan was already underway, orchestrated by the High Templar Tharok Stonevein.

Learning that they needed access to the restricted library to stop the ritual, the party formed a tactical alliance with the defector Brother Eldin. They split their forces. Zinnia and Calmer would confront the supposed cult leader Sister Alina, with Brother Eldin providing a distraction, while Zephyr and Sequoia infiltrated the library.

Inside, Zephyr and Sequoia navigated a magical library of floating books, glowing runes and earth magic. After struggling with initial investigation checks, they deciphered a clue about four stones and four passages, and revealed a hidden door by knocking three times on a specific bookshelf. Beyond it was a puzzle requiring four specific books. Searching the shelves for the missing fourth tome triggered the library's magical defenses and surrounded them with a swarm of animated books. Zephyr and Sequoia shredded the swarms, the remaining books vanished, and the path forward was revealed.

The session concluded with the party deciphering a cryptic poem about the four elements and deducing that the overarching answer to the riddle was the Temple of Elemental Evil itself.
