# Chapter 07

Date: Planting 20, 579 to Planting 21, 579

## Summary

Zephyr finds a room with new locks and oiled hinges holding fifty shields, twelve suits of leather armor and barrels of salted meat, and a room to the north with more weapons and black robes that symbolise the fire temple. Calmer mutters, "Either the Temple is alive and well, or someone is doing a fantastic job of faking it." Zinnia declares they will wear the robes. Zinnia follows a trail of blood in the torture room to a pillar and finds a secret door in it that opens on a shaft with rungs leading down to a passage and another secret door. Beyond it Sequoia finds a crypt with four ghouls; Calmer turns them, the rest are killed, and the party rests.

On Planting 21, Zephyr finds a giant pool and sends Calmer to fetch the coin, a pin with a ruby and a small tube; a giant crayfish attacks Calmer, and Sequoia secures the treasure during the distraction. Sequoia explores and finds a passage out; Zinnia says they will go through as members of the cult of fire. Zinnia recalls that Erin Darkmoon told them Lareth the Beautiful runs this place, and that the dead Snej worked for the water temple. Posing as recruits, the party bluffs the guards; Zephyr bluffs the sergeant and then tells Lareth that the water temple recruited them but they now join him. Asked what they have done for him, the party says they eliminated Snej. Lareth is persuaded, and then sends them instead to recover a stolen chest from bandits pretending to be earth elemental cultists in the Kron Hills near Sheernobb, promising them the abbey in exchange.
