# Chapter 42: The Necromancer's Laboratory of Lost Souls

Date: 2026-10-25

## Summary
The air in the Necromancer's Laboratory storeroom was thick with dread as shuffling footsteps approached from the meditation room.

Sequoia and Zephyr positioned themselves for attacks from concealment, and Zinnia also attempted to hide rather than race ahead alone. Calmer advanced with theatrical confidence, declaring himself the Prophet of Earth and winking back at his allies to signal the ruse. His existing Spirit Guardians immediately began damaging the approaching undead. Several ghasts dashed to close the distance, leaving only one initially able to attack him. Its bite dealt 15 damage, but Calmer passed his Constitution save and maintained the spell.

The ghasts soon surrounded Calmer: three ordinary creatures and two much stronger ferocious ghasts. Their assault combined claws, bites, and a debilitating stench. Zephyr landed a 35-damage sneak attack against a wounded ferocious ghast, but both he and Sequoia subsequently lost turns to paralysis. Zinnia's opening attacks were less successful, though he continued battering the weakened enemies. His elven immunity to their paralysis was explicitly recognized later in the fight. Calmer was eventually overwhelmed and knocked unconscious, ending Spirit Guardians and leaving the surviving undead unhindered by its protective spirits.

With Calmer down, the battle became a struggle to survive and protect his body. Zinnia took 24 damage and later used Uncanny Metabolism to recover, reporting that he was back to 22 hit points before resuming his attacks. Sequoia suffered 24 damage while paralyzed. Zephyr finally shook off the stench's effects, killed a ghast reduced to one hit point, and dealt 28 damage to another enemy. On a later turn, he moved to Sequoia's side and finished the remaining ferocious ghast with a second weapon, freeing Sequoia from paralysis. Immunity to the stench did not protect Zephyr from paralysis caused by attacks, but he passed those later saves despite taking another 23 damage.

Calmer made two death saves while the others fought through the remaining undead. Sequoia returned to the battle with a 29-damage kill, Zinnia finished another weakened ghast, and the last creature rolled double ones while attacking Zephyr. Sequoia then dealt the final 27 damage, ending the fight. The party resuscitated Calmer at one hit point, and the cleric greeted his survival with characteristic understatement: “well, that was awkward.”

The group took a short rest and used Calmer's healing magic to recover. Zephyr had been down to six hit points before resting and later reported 35; Calmer restored himself to near maximum health. Greater Potions of Healing were available if anyone needed more. Calmer also reminded the party that they had troops and suggested sending some into the next room to absorb damage after he dealt with the door's defenses. The party chose to explore the laboratory before confronting the necromancer.

On the bench furthest from the door, partly beneath a sheet, they found a former prisoner they had freed, brutally carved up and still wearing the clothes he had worn when he left them. Zinnia had watched this man tell the truth, and Sequoia had picked his shackles. Three recognizable victims have now been found: this freed man, and two of the four prisoners the party left chained (one dismembered, one turned into a ghoul). Two of those four remain unaccounted for.

Calmer also uncovered notes from the necromancer, who called himself the true prophet, treating Romag, Hartsch, and now Calmer as false prophets. The notes rejected their approach to Earth Temple rituals: a victim alone was insufficient; the victim had to be willing. The writer expressed frustration that this prisoner had refused to cooperate. Sequoia suggested using the necromancer himself for an Earth Temple sacrifice. Calmer entertained a brutal tradition provided it was not a religious ritual to the Earth Elemental God, but Religion checks clarified that asking him to perform a ritual sacrifice would cost him St. Cuthbert's favour. Sequoia immediately abandoned the proposal.

Zinnia's natural 20 on Investigation uncovered the laboratory's full cache of valuable components: phosphorus, lion's blood, pure sulfur, saffron, three amber rods, quicksilver, ground tourmaline, and powdered ruby. The haul was allocated at approximately 250 gold pieces each. The necromancer and the glyph-covered bronze door remained ahead.

## NPCs
### Ghasts
Undead creatures that ambushed the party, including three ordinary ghasts and two particularly powerful ferocious variants. They possess vicious bite and claw attacks and nearly kill Calmer after surrounding him.

The ferocious ghasts are distinguished by their paralyzing stench. The fight establishes a distinction between becoming immune to that stench and still needing saves against paralysis from attacks. Their AC is 15. Bodies obstruct some of their movement during the battle, and the final survivor misses Zephyr with double ones before Sequoia kills it.

### Calmer
A cleric of St. Cuthbert and party member who acts as a front-line defender. He claims to be the Prophet of Earth to deceive approaching enemies, winking to assure his companions that the declaration is a performance. His existing Spirit Guardians damages and slows the ghasts until they surround him and knock him unconscious.

Calmer survives two death saves and is resuscitated at one hit point. Afterward, he heals himself to near maximum health, offers healing to the others, and suggests employing the party's available troops in the next confrontation.

He discovers the necromancer's notes claiming the title of true prophet. Calmer initially entertains killing the necromancer according to a brutal Earth Temple tradition provided it is not a religious ritual to the Earth Elemental God, before Religion checks establish that ritual sacrifice would cost him St. Cuthbert's favour.

## Locations
### Necromancer's Laboratory Storeroom
A room beneath the temple filled with the remains of destroyed creatures and a prisoner lying in pieces on a bench. The storeroom and adjoining hallway become the site of the ghasts' attack.

The corridor offers little obvious cover for hiding. During the battle, fallen bodies obstruct some of the remaining undead.

### Meditation Room
A nearby chamber from which the powerful ghasts emerge to attack the party. Earlier scouting by Zephyr and Sequoia had identified a powerful occupant and two lesser ones here.

### Necromancer's Laboratory
A grim workspace beneath the temple containing benches, sheets, and the remains of failed experiments and prisoners tortured in an attempt to make them willing ritual participants. The recognized former captive lies on the bench furthest from the door, partly beneath a sheet.

The laboratory contains the necromancer's theological notes and a cache of valuable alchemical components. A bronze door marked with glyphs separates the party from the confrontation still ahead.

## Items

### Greater Potion of Healing
A potent restorative tonic available to each party member during the short rest. Calmer reminds the group that it restores 4d4 + 4 hit points. No potion is explicitly recorded as consumed.

### Earth Temple Notes
Notes written by the necromancer, who refers to himself as the true prophet and rejects the rituals practiced by false prophets Romag, Hartsch, and Calmer.

The notes insist that a willing victim is required, rather than merely an available captive, and express frustration that the dead prisoner refused to become one. Their discovery prompts the proposed sacrifice of the necromancer and the Religion checks that establish the limits imposed by Calmer's allegiance to St. Cuthbert.

Calmer maintains the spell after an early bite deals 15 damage, but it ends when he is knocked unconscious.
