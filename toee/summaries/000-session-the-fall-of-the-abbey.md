# Chapter 00 The Fall of the Abbey

Date: not stated in chapter

## Summary

Hired by the merchants of Narwell, the party escorted a small merchant caravan into the Gnarley Forest. The wagons were bait, meant to draw out the highly organized brigands plaguing the region.

Deep in the forest, a massive ogre blocked the caravan's path and demanded payment for safe passage. The chapter gives the ogre 59 hit points. The party chose not to fight a draining melee and instead bypassed the threat with copper bribes and intimidation.

The party then crossed paths with an eccentric, wizened gnome tinkerer named Jijohn (or "Gee John"). The gnome offered cryptic warnings about the organized forces backing the bandits and recounted the crushing of the Temple of Elemental Evil ten years prior. Jijohn advised the party to seek out Burne and Rufus on reaching Hommlet.

The party left the caravan to strike at the root of the threat and located the bandit stronghold, the ruined Abbey of Allitur. The rogues (Zephyr and Sequoia) and the masked monk (Zinnia) made a stealth-based assault on the lower levels. The chapter calls it flawless: they killed the unsuspecting bandits with sneak attacks before the alarm could be raised.

In the kitchen the party cornered the orc cook, Urot. Terrified by the party's ruthless efficiency, she confessed that the bandit leader, Snej, was no mere outlaw, but an operative receiving resources from a "big shot" within the Temple of Elemental Evil: Canon Belsornig.

In the ruined chapel, amid defaced frescos of the god Allitur, the party found a captive baby silver dragon named Airgid. She was chained to a statue and ravenous. She explained that her mother was in the Lortmil Mountains. The party agreed to escort her to Hommlet to plan a reunion.

The party killed a giant spider lurking in the collapsed roof of a guest room in mere seconds and secured the area for a brief rest. Finally they raided the bandits' hidden cache and took a substantial hoard of treasure: 123 gold pieces, *dust of disappearance*, and a *bag of holding*.
