## Identity
Fheminor Scrivenbark is a dowdy female halfling who holds the title of Great Reader at Candlekeep [ch 059 / entry]. She is widely expected to succeed the late Keeper Janussi as Keeper of Tomes [ch 071 / 071.04; ch 062 / 062.02]. Other scholars and entities have described her variously as the "short-legged one" [ch 058 / 058.06], the "unfriendly girl gnome" [ch 060 / 060.08], and having a "steely nature" [ch 059 / 059.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Fheminor is intensely focused on the preservation of knowledge and the physical integrity of the library. Her first concern during a crisis is the count of burned books [ch 071 / 071.04]. She values practical wisdom and judgment, viewing Gyrgum’s decision to keep combat out of the stacks as proof of worthiness for the rank of Reader [ch 071 / 071.04]. Daral described Fheminor as seething over the dissolution of her interests, with a "steely nature" [ch 059 / 059.05].

She is politically ambitious, with Daral Yashenti noting she would do anything to obtain Candlekeep’s highest office [ch 060 / 060.04]. Conversely, she displays scholarly enthusiasm, eagerly offering academic collaboration and authorship opportunities to those who uncover mysteries [ch 071 / 071.07]. She is pragmatic about security threats, agreeing to let Daz take the Vecna-linked Tome of the Stilled Tongue so that Vecna's attention falls on the party rather than on Candlekeep's leadership [ch 071 / 071.08].
