## Identity
Neheedra Duskryn is a Drow with white hair composed of pale, hissing snakes [ch 041 / entry; ch 042 / entry]. She possesses a petrifying gaze that forces a Constitution save within a 30-foot range or cone, depending on the instance [ch 041 / entry; ch 042 / entry]. In combat, she attacks with claws and venomous snake bites [ch 041 / entry]. Her lair contained five drow spider-silk dresses [ch 042 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Neheedra was driven mad by her time in the chamber, causing her to immediately charge the party upon being seen [ch 041 / entry]. Despite her madness, she displayed vanity, screaming at her reflection, "My hair. My hair's a mess," when faced with a mirror [ch 041 / entry]. During combat, she actively cursed and taunted the party, specifically targeting Zalthir with insults regarding his draconic heritage and the circumstances of his birth [ch 042 / entry].

## History with the Party
- Neheedra was discovered sitting on a zurkhwood throne with her eyes closed and arms crossed, appearing to meditate, before she charged the party immediately upon seeing them, having been driven mad by her time in the chamber [ch 041 / entry].
- She engaged the party in combat in Chapter 041, sustaining damage from Daz's Fireball (15 points), Zalthir (21 points), and Thorin's darts (20 points); she saved against Daz's Fireball and her own reflected gaze, and was knocked prone by Thorin's Hill Strike [ch 041 / entry].
- In Chapter 042, she cursed and taunted the party, specifically targeting Zalthir with insults regarding his draconic heritage and birth; she was defeated when her own petrifying gaze was reflected back at her via a mirror, which restrained her, and she ultimately fell to Daz's Magic Missile after dropping Zalthir to unconsciousness [ch 042 / entry].
- Her lair contained five drow spider-silk dresses [ch 042 / entry].

## Last Observed State
