## Identity
- Tadric is a 23-year-old Watcher of Candlekeep with a bowl-cut haircut and dark brown hair. [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.06]
- He is a lieutenant to Kalan Strongbranch. [ch 065 / entry; ch 067 / entry]
- He first appears guarding the door to Janussi's chambers when the party arrives. [ch 057 / 057.06]
- His job there was crowd control outside the room. [ch 057 / 057.06]
- Kalan Strongbranch later gave him the genuine high tower key. [ch 063 / 063.03; ch 063 / 063.04]
- He is the one who held the real High Tower key until he handed it to the party. [ch 065 / entry; ch 064 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
- **Self-critical.** When asked whether he had done a good job guarding the scene, he reflected that he had not. [ch 057 / entry]
- **Squeamish.** He admitted he does not really like blood and murder scenes. [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.06]
- **Loyal to Kalan.** He defended Kalan Strongbranch as a great, honest, stand-up guy and seemed troubled by Kalan's removal. [ch 057 / 057.06; ch 057 / entry] Kalan himself called Tadric "a loyal" guard who "does not know what he carries." [ch 063 / moment]
- **Nervous and suggestible.** After Thorin asked about shaking or taking the statue's candle, he fixated on it for the rest of the session. [ch 058 / 058.02; ch 058 / moment]
- **Dutiful and wants to be part of the outcome.** He handed over the real key but refused to retire. He insisted on accompanying the party because he had faced Moziqodo. [ch 064 / entry; ch 064 / 064.01]
  - He said he had held the key all night without knowing what it was. Now that he knew, he would not give it up and go to bed. [ch 064 / 064.01]
- **Conscious of obligations.** He raised the matter of telling Sylvira her son was dead. [ch 064 / 064.01] He raised it again in ch 072. [ch 072 / 072.06]
- **Receptive to scholarly flattery and appeal.** He recognized the party's deputized authority and their record of service. [ch 067 / entry] He suggested a monograph when Gyrgum cited his own new philosophy, then signed off on the bargain. [ch 067 / 067.02]

## History with the Party
- Tadric, a 23-year-old watcher with a bowl-cut haircut and dark brown hair, guarded the door to Janussi's chambers when the party arrived. Bookwyrm told him Kalan was relieved and the party was taking over. [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.06]
- He gave the party a timeline of visitors to the room. A'lai Aivenmore and Miss Hollypocket found the body around 7:30. The Gate Warden arrived with Tadric around 8, and Kazryn Nyantani came shortly after. Fheminor Scrivenbark was turned away at 8:30, Bookwyrm arrived at 9, and the Gate Warden returned at 11 for a full inspection. [ch 057 / 057.06]
- He admitted he was not watching closely enough to know what the Gate Warden did inside the room, saying his job was crowd control outside. He also said he does not really like blood and murder scenes. [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.06]
- He defended Kalan Strongbranch as a great, honest, stand-up guy and seemed troubled by Kalan's removal. He noted Kalan looked more determined after his inspection. [ch 057 / 057.06; ch 057 / entry]
- He confirmed Miss Hollypocket would never have let the Keeper spend the night with candles burning down, which supported the party's deduction that Janussi died many hours before the body was found. [ch 057 / 057.07]
- He did not know whether anyone had attempted *Speak with Dead* in the room. [ch 057 / 057.06; ch 057 / 057.07]
- When asked whether he had done a good job, he reflected that he had not. [ch 057 / entry]
- He explained that the fallen three-foot marble statue was likely knocked over by A'lai Aivenmore, who discovered the body. [ch 058 / 058.02]
- After Thorin asked about shaking or taking the statue's candle, he became visibly distressed and fixated on it, staring at it and muttering for the rest of the session. [ch 058 / 058.02; ch 058 / entry; ch 058 / moment]
- Kalan revealed he had given Tadric the genuine high tower key. He posted Tadric alone in the North Galleries, away from the barracks, without telling him what he carried. [ch 063 / 063.03; ch 063 / 063.04; ch 063 / entry]
- Thorin ordered Fembris not to reveal that Tadric held the real key. [ch 063 / 063.03; ch 063 / moment]
- The party reached Tadric as Moziqodo, Sylvira's abyssal spawn son, had him grappled and was tearing him apart. The party killed Moziqodo before it could act. [ch 063 / 063.04; ch 063 / 063.05; ch 063 / entry]
- Gyrgum healed Tadric's wounds with Cure Wounds. Tadric clutched the key, identified his attacker as Sylvira's son and asked what had happened. He sat in shock after the party explained the conspiracy, then asked whether Kalan was alive. [ch 063 / 063.05; ch 063 / 063.06]
- Status at the end of ch 063: the summary states "Tadric alive." [ch 063 / 063.06]
- Tadric handed the genuine High Tower key to the party, but refused to retire. He insisted on accompanying them, saying he had faced Moziqodo. Gyrgum agreed and noted he now had resurrection magic. [ch 064 / entry; ch 064 / 064.01]
- When the chant of prophecies in Candlekeep fell silent, Tadric was stunned. He then reminded the party that someone needs to tell Sylvira her son is dead. The party deferred this as a later problem. [ch 064 / 064.01; ch 064 / entry]
- He knew the way to the High Tower and led the party there. [ch 064 / 064.01]
