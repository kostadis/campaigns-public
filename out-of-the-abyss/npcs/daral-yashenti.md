## Identity
Daral Yashenti is a scholar attached to Candlekeep, serving as a member of the council [ch 060 / entry]. He is regarded by Sylvira Savikas as a "bitter drunk" lacking the organizational capacity for complex conspiracies [ch 061 / entry]. He possesses access to private collections and magical items, including a scroll of *Detect Magic* and a poisoned book from his own library [ch 060 / entry; ch 063 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Daral is defined by extreme paranoia and self-preservation instincts. Following the death of Janussi, he identified Thorin and Daz as the only individuals in Candlekeep who did not actively wish for his death, causing him to cling to the party for protection while fearing they might allow his poisoning to progress for investigative purposes [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.01; ch 061 / moment; ch 063 / entry]. He is acutely aware of his vulnerability, fearing that the party may reveal drunken, disparaging comments he previously made about Dragonborn to Zalthir [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.01].

His primary motivation is survival, often achieved through desperate flattery and academic pandering. Convinced he would die at midnight, he aggressively flattered Thorin, Dawnbringer, and Gyrgum to secure healing, with Dawnbringer’s cure eliciting an immediate shift to scholarly enthusiasm regarding sentient magical swords [ch 061 / entry; ch 061 / 061.01; ch 061 / moment; ch 062 / entry; ch 062 / moment]. He is skeptical of mundane security measures, insisting that true keys are "immensely magical artifacts" rather than standard locksmith items, and he reacted to the invasion of Deneir’s Sanctum with academic disbelief rather than combat readiness, insisting the creatures "can't be here" [ch 063 / entry; ch 063 / 063.01; ch 062 / entry; ch 062 / 062.06].

## Last Observed State
Daral was present with the party during the confrontation at the High Tower, where he was identified by A'lai Aivenmore as one of the victims of the conspiracy who had been "nearly killed" [ch 064 / 064.04]. Prior to this, he had accompanied the party from Deneir’s Sanctum to Bookwyrm’s office and then to the Sea Warden’s Tower, where he deduced Kalan’s location among the Watchers [ch 063 / entry; ch 063 / 063.02; ch 063 / 063.03]. During this time, he used a scroll of *Detect Magic* to prove that the key held by the party was a mundane decoy, noting that while the party's gear glowed, the key remained completely dark [ch 063 / entry; ch 063 / 063.01]. He explained that Candlekeep’s anti-teleportation ward suppresses ordinary combustion and that the ward’s source lies within the high tower room protected by the two keys [ch 063 / entry; ch 063 / 063.02]. Status regarding his specific actions during the final combat round of the session is not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
**Thorin, Dawnbringer, Daz, and Gyrgum:** Daral views these four as his only protectors, stating they are the only people who do not want him dead [ch 060 / entry; ch 063 / entry]. He maintains close physical proximity to Thorin and Dawnbringer for safety [ch 060 / 060.03; ch 060 / 060.05; ch 063 / entry]. He sought to curry favor with them by offering a "warm intro" to an apothecary and by promising extensive new research into Dawnbringer’s sentience and semiotics after being healed [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.02; ch 062 / entry; ch 062 / 062.05].

**A’lai Aivenmore:** Daral identified A'lai as the individual most likely framing him, reasoning that A'lai viewed Daral’s presence on the council as the sole obstacle to becoming Keeper of Tomes [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.03; ch 060 / 060.04]. In the final confrontation, A'lai acknowledged Daral as a victim of his scheme [ch 064 / 064.04].

**Janussi:** Daral was Janussi’s "loving rival," a characterization supported by a gift tag reading "you old snob" [ch 058 / moment]. He gifted Janussi a book from his private collection, which Janussi died after receiving; Daral later reclaimed this book, potentially exposing himself to poison while handling it [ch 060 / entry; ch 061 / entry; ch 061 / 061.01].

**Sylvira Savikas:** Sylvira considers Daral a bitter drunk and initially suspected him of framing her, though she later dismissed his capacity for the larger frame-up [ch 061 / entry; ch 061 / 061.05].

**Zalthir:** Daral fears Zalthir will expose Daral’s prior drunken remarks about Dragonborn [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.01].
