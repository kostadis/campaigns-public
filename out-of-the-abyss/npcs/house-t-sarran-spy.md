## Identity
An unnamed drow agent of House T'sarran, sent to track Daz and identify whoever paid for his protection. She refused to reveal her name and offered her piwafwi as a trophy of her failure [ch 054 / entry]. The party later identified her as a noblewoman valuable in negotiations with the drow [ch 076 / 076.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Stealthy and defiant, she disclosed her mission only under sustained pressure. She proposed mutual information-sharing and requested transfer to Candlekeep's guards [ch 054 / entry]. While imprisoned under Glabbagool's supervision, she repeatedly attempted escape, attacked him, and demanded restoration to normal size and formal imprisonment [ch 056 / entry].

## History with the Party
- An agent of House T'sarran was sent to the surface to track Daz and identify his mysterious protector [ch 054 / entry].
- When confronted by the party, the spy refused to give her name, offering her piwafwi as a trophy of her failure [ch 054 / entry].
- Under pressure from Zalthir's Stunning Strike and grapple, Thorin's intimidation, Gyrgum's disarming friendliness, prayer beads, and Dawnbringer's light, the spy revealed her mission was to observe and report on whoever paid for Daz's protection [ch 054 / entry].
- The spy proposed a mutual information-sharing deal and suggested being handed to Candlekeep's guards [ch 054 / entry].
- She was subdued and reduced to tiny size using a shrinking mushroom, which made her voice "very squeaky and indignant" [ch 054 / entry].
- The party nicknamed her "Polly Pocket," stored her in a bag of holding, and assigned her to Glabbagool's custody [ch 054 / entry].
- The spy was kept as a prisoner in the bag of holding under Glabbagool's watch and subjected to Glabbagool's meditation lessons [ch 056 / entry].
- She frequently attempted to kill Glabbagool and tried to run away whenever possible [ch 056 / entry].
- She demanded to be restored to her normal size and formally imprisoned by Candlekeep's guards rather than remaining in Glabbagool's custody [ch 056 / entry].
- The party decided to leave her with the dwarves of Gauntlgrym as a political bargaining chip. Thorin asked whether she would receive fair treatment; the DM explained that a prisoner exchange was more likely than summary execution because she was a noblewoman [ch 076 / 076.02].
- A later discussion confirmed that she had been left with the dwarves of Gauntlgrym for future negotiations or a prisoner exchange [ch 076 / 076.03].

## Last Observed State
Reported left in the custody of the dwarves of Gauntlgrym as a captive bargaining piece. This was retrospective discussion, with no direct action or speech from her recorded in these scenes [ch 076 / 076.02; ch 076 / 076.03].

She was previously shrunken and imprisoned in the party's bag of holding; the later account does not establish whether she had been restored to normal size [ch 056 / entry; ch 076 / 076.03].
