## Identity
A drow agent of House T'sarran sent to the surface to track Daz and identify his mysterious protector [ch 054 / entry]. She refused to reveal her personal name upon capture, instead offering her piwafwi as a trophy of her failure and identifying herself solely by her house affiliation [ch 054 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
The spy is characterized by stealth and defiance, refusing to give her name, and revealing her mission only under sustained pressure [ch 054 / entry]. Her primary motivation is to observe and report on whoever paid for Daz's protection [ch 054 / entry]. She possesses a pragmatic streak, having proposed a mutual information-sharing deal and suggesting she be handed over to Candlekeep's guards once captured [ch 054 / entry]. In captivity, she displays profound indignity and fury, describing her situation as "outrageous and unacceptable" [ch 056 / entry]. She is actively hostile to her captors, frequently attempting to kill Glabbagool and trying to escape whenever possible [ch 056 / entry].
