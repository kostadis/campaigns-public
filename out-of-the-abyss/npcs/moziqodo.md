## Identity
Moziqodo was a massive, powerful demon spawn revealed to be the abyssal spawn son of Sylvira [ch 063 / entry; ch 063 / 063.04; ch 067 / 067.03]. He normally resided within a magical sanctuary provided by his mother, though he had been outside it for some time prior to his encounter with the party [ch 063 / entry]. He was apparently the creature that tore out Bookwyrm's throat and took her High Tower key [ch 063 / entry; ch 065 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Moziqodo acted as a lethal operative in a conspiracy involving the High Tower keys. He was apparently expected to deliver Tadric’s High Tower key to A'lai Aivenmore at the High Tower [ch 063 / entry; ch 064 / entry; ch 064 / 064.03]. To achieve this, he violently assaulted Tadric in the North Gallery to seize the second key [ch 063 / entry; ch 063 / 063.04]. Despite being a massive and powerful demon spawn, he was easily dominated in combat; Thorin frightened him, and Zalthir’s stun left him completely helpless until he died [ch 063 / entry; ch 063 / 063.05; ch 063 / moment]. He never recovered from the stun and took no actions during his final encounter [ch 063 / entry; ch 063 / 063.05].
