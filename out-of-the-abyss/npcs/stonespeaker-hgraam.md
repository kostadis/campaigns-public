## Identity
Stonespeaker Hgraam is the clan leader of Clan Cairngorm and the revered Stonespeaker for Gracklstugh’s stone giants [ch 013 / 013.03; ch 015 / entry; ch 026 / entry]. He serves as the mentor and master to the stone giants Rihuud and Dorhun [ch 013 / 013.03; ch 015 / 015.02]. He is a native of the Underdark and has never visited the surface world, referring to it exclusively as "the Dreamlands" [ch 015 / 015.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Hgraam embodies the patience of his kind, refusing to speak first and waiting for visitors to break the silence, a trait the DM notes he will maintain "more or less forever" if not prompted [ch 015 / 015.01]. He is described as thoughtful, stony, and prone to delivering warnings in the form of omens and rants about how quickly others act, though he was noted to be "uncharacteristically talkative" in later interactions [ch 015 / entry; ch 026 / entry]. He acts as a counselor to the Deepking, advising him to be patient and to "watch the stones", though his advice has been ignored since the arrival of the advisor Shal [ch 015 / 015.02]. He possesses the ability to commune with the stones to detect magical corruption and demonic presences, using these insights to guide his clan’s response to threats [ch 015 / moment; ch 026 / entry]. He places high value on the well-being of his kin, seeking to cure those afflicted by the two-headed curse while maintaining a cautious, observational stance regarding political conflicts [ch 026 / 026.04].

## History with the Party
* Stonespeaker Hgraam is the clan leader of Clan Cairngorm and mentor to Dorhun and the mad giant Rihuud [ch 013 / 013.03; ch 015 / entry].
* The party met Hgraam at the entrance of Cairngorm Cavern, where he waited with infinite patience for them to break the silence first [ch 015 / 015.01].
* Hgraam grieved the madness of his apprentice Rihuud and thanked the party for their attempt to spare the giant's life [ch 015 / 015.02].
* He warned the party of an ancient evil stirring in the Underdark and identified the surface world as "the Dreamlands," a place he had never visited [ch 015 / 015.02; ch 015 / moment].
* Hgraam revealed that the Deepking had become paranoid and isolated after taking the advisor Shal, and he advised the king toward patience [ch 015 / 015.02].
* He gifted the party a polished crystal capable of communing with animals, plants, and the dead, before ending their audience with a complaint about everyone being in a rush [ch 015 / entry; ch 015 / 015.02; ch 015 / 015.03].
* The party discovered a derro cult creating two-headed stone giant statues, and Gyrgum deduced that bringing such a statue to Hgraam might allow him to reverse the curse [ch 023 / 023.01].
* Upon returning, the party presented Hgraam with a recovered two-headed statue; he accepted it as a key to curing his afflicted kin [ch 026 / 026.04].
* Hgraam rewarded the party with five hundred gold pieces and advised them to leave the cavern while he consulted the stones, noting that the Stone Guard had become agitated [ch 026 / 026.04].
* The DM noted that Hgraam was the one faction leader the party had not betrayed, and the Stone Giants remained friendly with them [ch 027 / 027.04].

## Last Observed State
Hgraam was last present and acting in chapter 026 at Cairngorm Cavern [ch 026 / 026.04]. At that time, he was actively contending with another of his brothers who had succumbed to the two-headed curse [ch 026 / 026.04]. Upon receiving a recovered ritual statue from the party, Hgraam accepted it gravely as a key to beginning the cure for his afflicted kin [ch 026 / 026.04]. He rewarded the party with five hundred gold pieces and warned them that the Stone Guard had become agitated, advising them to leave the cavern so he could consult the stones in silence [ch 026 / 026.04]. He closed the cavern to outsiders at that time [ch 026 / 026.04]. In chapter 027, he was not present but was mentioned by the DM, who confirmed that Hgraam and the Stone Giants remained friendly with the party and that he was the only faction leader the party had not betrayed [ch 027 / 027.04]. Status established as alive and functioning as clan leader in ch 026.

## Relationships
**Dorhun and Rihuud:** Hgraam is the master of these stone giants [ch 013 / 013.03]. He expressed grief over the madness of his apprentice Rihuud, who was driven mad by the stones, and thanked the party for their attempt to spare the giant's life [ch 015 / 015.02]. Dorhun is a fellow apprentice who invited the party to visit Cairngorm Cavern [ch 013 / 013.03].
**The Party:** Hgraam initially viewed the party with cautious interest, waiting for them to speak first [ch 015 / 015.01]. He gifted them a polished crystal capable of communing with animals, plants, and the dead [ch 015 / 015.03]. He later rewarded them with 500 gold pieces for delivering the ritual statue necessary to cure his kin [ch 026 / 026.04]. The party considers him and his faction the "most level-headed" of the Gracklstugh factions [ch 026 / 026.04].
**The Deepking:** Hgraam is a former close advisor to the ruler of Gracklstugh [ch 015 / 015.02]. He noted that the Deepking has become paranoid and isolated, particularly since taking on the advisor Shal, and that the king no longer discusses Gracklstugh's problems with him [ch 015 / 015.02]. Hgraam advised the king toward patience regarding the unrest in the Underdark [ch 015 / 015.02].
**Shal:** Hgraam has never met this advisor to the Deepking and views Shal's arrival as the catalyst for the king's erratic behavior [ch 015 / 015.02].
**The Stone Guard:** Hgraam warned the party about this faction, noting in chapter 026 that they had become "more agitated recently" [ch 026 / 026.04].
