## Identity
- Pizwog is an ancient tortle librarian at Candlekeep, the resident librarian of the Immortal Chambers, the theology and religion library in the West Inner Ward [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.01].
- He is extraordinarily slow-moving, shuffling through the theology library at about one mile per hour [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.01].
- He writes his own academic papers. One is the tract *On the Necessary Absence of the Gods Who Are Present*, a dense syncretic analysis of religions across the Sword Coast [ch 057 / 057.01].
- Chapter 072 calls him a slow-moving tortle scholar who authored a long, tedious, syncretic paper [ch 072 / entry; ch 072 / 072.03].
- No speech from Pizwog is recorded in the summaries. He appears only in passing, in chapters 057 and 072 [ch 057 / 057.01; ch 072 / 072.03].

## Personality and Motivations
- **Eager to share his scholarship.** He distributes what look like religious tracts but are his own academic papers, and he pressed one into Gyrgum's hands [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.01].
- **Wants recognition.** The chapter 072 entry says he "watches for Gyrgum to acknowledge his work" [ch 072 / entry]. In 072.03 he stared at Gyrgum's satchel as he shuffled past the doorway [ch 072 / 072.03]. The summaries do not state his thoughts. This reading rests on the entry's wording and his behaviour.
- **Outcome of his interest.** Vareth promised to find his research a place in the Pantheon, and the recognition is expected to make Pizwog's next century [ch 072 / entry; ch 072 / 072.03].
- Beyond this, the evidence is silent on his temperament, beliefs, or goals.
