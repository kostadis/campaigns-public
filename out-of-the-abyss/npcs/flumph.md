## Identity
Flumph is a sentient, floating telepathic creature native to the Underdark [ch 017 / entry; ch 019 / entry]. It functions as a psionic filter feeder, collecting energy via a distinctive tendril rather than by harming its sources [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.04]. Flumph utilizes telepathy as a survival trait to avoid attracting predators through sound, communicating directly into minds via a "party line" [ch 017 / 017.04]. Its physical appearance includes a tendril and a body that changes color to reflect its literal emotional state [ch 018 / entry; ch 017 / 017.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Flumph is characterized by cheerfulness and a desire to feed on psionic energy, specifically from "evil" entities like mind flayers, aboleths, and githyanki, and it calls a nearby mind flayer "a tasty snack" [ch 017 / 017.04; ch 019 / entry]. Its primary motivational framework revolves around "trauma dumping": it copes with its own distress by psychically sharing unpleasant memories and visions with others, thereby offloading its pain [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.04]. It adopted the term "trauma dump" from the party and plans to teach the technique to other flumphs [ch 017 / 017.04; ch 018 / entry].

Flumph seeks novelty and "trauma" for entertainment and sustenance, growing bored when standard trauma dumping becomes unsatisfying [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / moment]. It expresses dark amusement regarding violence, specifically noting disappointment when the party avoids death or brain-devouring [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.04]. It is also deeply distressed by the corruption of its home due to the flickering of the *faerzress*, which has made the local population "crankier and crueler" [ch 017 / 017.04].

## History with the Party
*   The Flumph appeared to the party in the Whorlstone Caverns, introducing itself telepathically and explaining that it uses psionic energy as a survival trait to avoid attracting predators. [ch 017 / 017.04]
*   It provided the party with crucial intelligence regarding the Gray Ghost lair, the obelisk of doom, the Assassin's Guild location, and the Council of Savants. [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.04]
*   The creature revealed that a derro savant named Plinki was torturing a stolen red dragon egg in an attempt to create a two-headed mount for Demogorgon. [ch 017 / 017.04]
*   Despite the party's hesitation, the Flumph decided to accompany them on their mission. [ch 017 / 017.04; ch 018 / entry]
*   In Gracklstugh, the Flumph served as living evidence by projecting the vision of the egg into Gartokkar’s mind. [ch 017 / entry]
*   It explained its coping mechanism to the party, adopting the term "trauma dump" after they coined it, and stated plans to share this technique with other flumphs. [ch 017 / 017.04; ch 018 / entry]
*   During the negotiation with the Empty-Scabbard Killers, the Flumph stayed outside the lair because of the presence of a mind flayer, preferring to wait and absorb the traumatic vibes of the situation. [ch 019 / 019.02]
*   The Flumph acted as a guide when the party bypassed the Council of Savants, though it admitted it only knew the north route because it was the only place with "psychic energy to eat." [ch 019 / 019.04]
*   It expressed disappointment that no one’s brain was devoured after the assassins' lair, noting that "There's still time." [ch 019 / 019.04]

## Last Observed State
As of the events in Chapter 019, Flumph was accompanying the party in the Whorlstone Caverns [ch 019 / 019.01]. During the negotiation with the Empty-Scabbard Killers, Flumph remained outside the lair, specifically to avoid the mind flayer within, though it attempted to absorb the "traumatic vibes" of the interaction [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.02].

When the party moved to bypass the Council of Savants to reach the obelisk chamber, Flumph served as a guide. It admitted it only knew the north route because it was the only path with "psychic energy to eat," and was unfamiliar with the northwest passage or how to open the main double doors [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.04]. Having probed the party's minds after the assassins' lair, Flumph reported finding "no trauma," expressing disappointment that no brains had been devoured during the encounter, noting, "There's still time" [ch 019 / 019.04]. Status not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
*   **The Party:** Flumph joined the party uninvited, despite their hesitation, seeking new trauma to absorb and assistance in dealing with the corruption of its home [ch 017 / 017.04; ch 018 / entry]. It acts as their intelligence source, guide, and living evidence (having projected visions into Gartokkar's mind in Gracklstugh) [ch 017 / entry]. It views their lack of reaction to its telepathy as rude [ch 017 / moment].
*   **Empty-Scabbard Killers:** Flumph avoids direct contact with this faction due to the presence of a mind flayer in their lair, preferring to feed on secondary psionic effects [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.02].
*   **Council of Savants:** Flumph has fed on their psionic energy "quite energetically," using them as a major food source [ch 017 / 017.04; ch 019 / 019.04].
*   **Demogorgon Cultists/Derro:** Flumph despises them for corrupting its home and is motivated to help the party disrupt their plans regarding the red dragon egg [ch 017 / 017.04].
