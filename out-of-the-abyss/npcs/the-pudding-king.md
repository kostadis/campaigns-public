## Identity
The Pudding King was a sentient, telepathic ooze and the primary antagonist of the ooze threat in Blingdenstone [ch 038 / 038.04; ch 047 / entry]. Formerly a gnome tunnel worker, he transformed after embracing a connection to Juiblex, the demon lord of oozes, becoming a manifestation of ooze power [ch 039 / 039.06; ch 047 / 047.01]. He commanded an army of oozes and possessed abilities including slime waves, green slime attacks, and the ability to project his voice [ch 038 / 038.04; ch 047 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
His transformation and subsequent aggression were fueled by vengeance, wounded pride, resentment, and madness, which were amplified by demonic influence [ch 039 / 039.06; ch 039 / entry]. He viewed calm, rational speech as a weakness reserved for those who made deals with devils, insisting instead on telepathic screaming as appropriate for an ally of demons [ch 047 / moment]. His primary motivation was to convert the biomass of Blingdenstone and gnome kind into ooze biomass, promising to turn the city into "Blingdenpuddle" [ch 046 / entry; ch 046 / 046.09; ch 047 / 047.01]. He expressed a grandiose desire for supreme power, crying, "I am the pudding king. The manifestation of ooze power." [ch 047 / 047.01].

## Relationships
**Juiblex:** The Pudding King was a proxy for Juiblex, the demon lord of oozes [ch 038 / entry; ch 046 / 046.08]. The DM noted he had not just embraced a relationship with Juiblex but had become one of Juiblex’s minions [ch 047 / 047.01].
**Zuggtmoy:** The demon queen Zuggtmoy addressed the Pudding King as "my little king" and dismissed his forces as "crude" in a psychic message [ch 044 / entry; ch 044 / 044.03]. The Pudding King referred to her as "the queen of rot" [ch 046 / 046.09].
**Glabbagool:** Glabbagool, a sentient gelatinous cube, considered the Pudding King a "mindless pudding master" and offered to fight him [ch 046 / 046.08]. During the final battle, the Pudding King and Glabbagool swapped bodies, causing confusion [ch 047 / 047.06]. Glabbagool ultimately inhabited the Pudding King's ooze form after the King's dissolution [ch 047 / 047.08; ch 047 / 047.10].
**Chief Chipgrin:** The Pudding King was a former gnome tunnel worker whose fall, per Chief Chipgrin’s account, appeared rooted in "vengeance, pride, madness, resentment at being ignored" [ch 039 / 039.06].
**Gnome Leadership (Dorbo, Senni, etc.):** The Pudding King was the central threat that united the gnome factions in Blingdenstone, prompting Chief Dorbo and Senni Diggermattock to form coalitions and plan military distractions to remove him [ch 038 / 038.06; ch 045 / 045.02; ch 046 / 046.01].
