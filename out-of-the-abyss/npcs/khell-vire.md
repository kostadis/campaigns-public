## Identity
- Brother Khell-Vire (also called "the avowed Khell-Vire" and "Khell-Vire") is a scholar-monk at Candlekeep [ch 054 / entry; ch 056 / entry].
- He is a 200-year-old half-elf and a former monk of the Garden of Shadow. He left the Garden 80 years ago after refusing an order [ch 056 / 056.05; ch 056 / entry].
- He wears a faded shadow monk under-robe beneath the avowed gray of Candlekeep [ch 056 / 056.05].
- His office is in the lower gallery of the Echoing Hall in the Atheneum, where the Shadow Discipline Lineage Records are kept [ch 056 / 056.05].
- Waterdeep contacts called him a shadow monk master and "the best of the best" [ch 055 / 055.07; ch 055 / entry].
- In chapter 072 he performs a deity-identification ritual in the Echoing Hall [ch 072 / 072.06; ch 072 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
- **Scholarly interest in Gyrgum.** The First Reader said he "wishes to learn about Gyrgum in return" for Zalthir's questions about Jimjar [ch 054 / 054.06]. The evidence does not show whether he pursued this.
- **Cryptic, monk-style manner.** He opened with "What were you sent toward?" and Zalthir found this annoying. When asked, he rephrased it gently, but he would not explain the note on his desk [ch 056 / 056.05].
- **Hands-off teaching.** He sent Zalthir to the Trial of the Broken Mirror and said he would not be there: "This is your work, not mine." [ch 056 / 056.05]
- **Candid about what he does not know.** Neither he nor Zalthir could explain the note "Zalthir, the Underdark, until the Bone King sings" [ch 056 / 056.05; ch 056 / entry].
- **Warm toward the party later.** In chapter 072 he welcomed them and praised their defense of Candlekeep for several minutes [ch 072 / 072.06].
- **Interpreter of divine signs.** He explained that gods leave "when they are seen." He read the gambler's bone as a god's marker and a debt Callarduran means to pay [ch 072 / 072.06; ch 072 / moment].
- **Reason for leaving the Garden of Shadow.** The evidence says only that he refused an order. His reasons are not stated.

## History with the Party
- Candlekeep's First Reader directed Zalthir to learn about Jimjar and noted that the avowed Khell-Vire "wishes to learn about Gyrgum in return". Khell-Vire is described here as a scholar with a specific interest in Gyrgum. He was spoken of, not present. [ch 054 / 054.06; ch 054 / entry]
- In Waterdeep, Zalthir looking for shadow monk training was pointed to Candlekeep and a master named Khell-Vire, "the best of the best". Khell-Vire was spoken of, not present. [ch 055 / 055.07; ch 055 / entry]
- A local Waterdeep monastery offered a letter of introduction to Khell-Vire and cited a leaky roof. The price settled at 140 gp after Zalthir rolled 4 against its 18. Daz mended the roof with Mending instead, and the monks gave the letter. [ch 055 / 055.07]
- On reaching Daggerford, the party still had Candlekeep and "its master Khell-Vire" ahead of them. [ch 055 / 055.09]
- At Candlekeep, Bookwyrm (the First Reader) assigned Zalthir to Brother Khell-Vire. In the same assignment, Gyrgum went to Vareth. [ch 056 / 056.04]
- Zalthir met Brother Khell-Vire in his lower gallery office in the Echoing Hall of the Atheneum, where the Shadow Discipline Lineage Records are kept. [ch 056 / 056.05]
- Khell-Vire is a 200-year-old half-elf. He left the Garden of Shadow 80 years ago after refusing an order. He wears a faded shadow monk under-robe beneath the avowed gray of Candlekeep. [ch 056 / 056.05; ch 056 / entry]
- A note on Khell-Vire's desk read "Zalthir, the Underdark, until the Bone King sings". Neither Zalthir nor Khell-Vire could explain it. [ch 056 / 056.05; ch 056 / entry]
- Khell-Vire opened with a cryptic question, "What were you sent toward?", which Zalthir found annoying. He rephrased it when asked, but gave no answer about the note. Zalthir had no lived experience that resonated with the phrase. [ch 056 / 056.05]
- Khell-Vire sent Zalthir to the Trial of the Broken Mirror and said he would not be there. [ch 056 / 056.05]
- The sparring trial that followed was run by a blind half-elf monk, and Master Kenshi taught Zalthir a technique. The evidence does not show Khell-Vire present at the trial. [ch 056 / 056.05]
- In the Chapter House, a council member said Brother Khell-Vire had told them Zalthir possessed a unique skill. Zalthir was evasive, and Gyrgum redirected the room to the death under investigation. Khell-Vire was spoken of, not present. [ch 057 / 057.05]
- During the celebration after the vault, Brother Khell-Vire was named among the scholars with unfinished business with the party. His specific role is not established in that exchange. [ch 071 / 071.09; ch 071 / entry]
- Zalthir remembered that Khell-Vire claimed to have an answer about Jimjar. He gathered the party and went to Khell-Vire in the Echoing Hall. Khell-Vire welcomed them warmly, praised their defense of Candlekeep, and led a deity-identification ritual. [ch 072 / 072.06; ch 072 / entry]
- The ritual revealed Jimjar to be Callarduran Smoothhands, the Deep Brother and god of the svirfneblin. Khell-Vire explained that Callarduran had been in the Abyss in a quarrel with Yeenoghu, Demogorgon and Zuggtmoy. He was dragged to the Material Plane with the demons and disguised himself among them. [ch 072 / 072.06]
- Khell-Vire explained that Jimjar vanished because someone recognized his divinity and that his work is unfinished. [ch 072 / 072.06; ch 072 / moment]
- Khell-Vire identified the gambler's knucklebone as a god's marker and a debt Callarduran means to pay for the party's help saving the deep gnomes. He read the signs to mean Jimjar will return one more time. Timing and length of stay are unknown. [ch 072 / 072.06]
- Daz wondered whether Callarduran was helping him because the god was hostile to whoever wants Daz dead ("the enemy of his enemy"). [ch 072 / 072.06]
