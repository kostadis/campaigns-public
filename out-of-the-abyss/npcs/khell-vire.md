## Identity
Khell-Vire is a 200-year-old half-elf scholar and former monk of the Garden of Shadow, currently serving as an avowed scholar at Candlekeep [ch 056 / entry]. He left the Garden of Shadow eighty years ago after refusing an order [ch 056 / entry]. He wears a faded shadow monk under-robe beneath the standard avowed gray of Candlekeep [ch 056 / entry; ch 056 / 056.05]. Waterdeep contacts have described him as "the best of the best" for shadow monk training [ch 055 / entry; ch 055 / 055.07]. His office is located in the lower gallery of the Echoing Hall within the Atheneum, where the Shadow Discipline Lineage Records are kept [ch 056 / 056.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Khell-Vire specifically expressed a desire to study Gyrgum, the orc cleric of Bahamut, when the party arrived at Candlekeep [ch 054 / 054.06; ch 054 / entry]. In his first meeting with Zalthir he opened with “What were you sent toward?” rather than “Why were you sent here?” [ch 056 / 056.05]. He maintains a distinct professional boundary, refusing to personally intervene in a student's trials, stating, "I will not be there. This is your work, not mine" [ch 056 / entry; ch 056 / 056.05]. A council member cited him as having said Zalthir possessed a unique skill [ch 057 / 057.05].
