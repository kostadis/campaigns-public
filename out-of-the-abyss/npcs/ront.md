## Identity
Ront was an orc prisoner held at the drow outpost of Velkynvelve [ch 002 / entry]. He was physically imposing, described as a "hulking" figure, and possessed considerable physical strength which made him a natural choice for labor details [ch 002 / entry; ch 003 / entry]. During the prison break, he utilized a short sword taken from the armory [ch 003 / 003.02]. In the final moments of the escape, he wielded a great axe [ch 004 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Ront’s primary motivation was the restoration of his personal honor. He was deeply humiliated by his captivity in Velkynvelve and became determined to prove that he remained a powerful warrior [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.03]. This drive for pride led him to make fatal decisions; rather than fleeing with the rest of the party, he chose to engage a wounded vrock demon to demonstrate his worth [ch 004 / moment]. During the initial guard tower skirmish, he acted decisively to eliminate threats, killing a drow guard once space allowed him to reach the enemy [ch 003 / 003.02].

## History with the Party
- Ront was an orc prisoner assigned to a water-hauling labor detail alongside Thorin and Prince Derendil near the waterfall at Velkynvelve [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.01; ch 002 / 002.03].
- During the escape attempt, Ront entered the lower guard room, squeezed past combatants, and killed the second drow guard with a short sword [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.02].
- While fleeing Velkynvelve, Ront attacked a wounded vrock demon with his great axe to prove his warrior status after the humiliation of captivity [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.03; ch 004 / moment].
- Ront was killed by the vrock's talons in retaliation for his attack [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.03; ch 004 / moment].

## Last Observed State
Ront is dead. During the escape from Velkynvelve, he charged a wounded vrock demon with his great axe, dealing nine points of damage [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.03]. The demon retaliated with its talons, striking Ront down and killing him [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.03; ch 004 / moment]. His body was left behind as the rest of the survivors fled into the tunnels of the Underdark [ch 004 / 004.03].

## Relationships
*   **Thorin and Prince Derendil:** Ront worked alongside Thorin and Prince Derendil, a quaggoth who believes he is an elven prince, during his forced labor detail, hauling water barrels near the waterfall [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.01; ch 002 / 002.03].
*   **The Party (Prisoners):** Ront cooperated with the broader group of prisoners, including Thorin, Zalthir, Daz, Gyrgum, and others. He assisted in the guard tower skirmish by killing the second ordinary drow guard, allowing the party to focus on the Elite Warrior Imbros [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.02].
*   **Shuushar:** During the escape, Shuushar refused to follow Ront into the fight against the vrock demon, leaving Ront to face the beast alone [ch 004 / moment].
