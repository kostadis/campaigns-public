## Personality and Motivations
*   Exhibits the detached enthusiasm of a scholar, initially greeting Dawnbringer as "a remarkable specimen" until Thorin corrected him that she was his friend. [ch 056 / entry; ch 056 / 056.06]
*   Shifts readily from clinical assessment to gentle, precise emotional reframing, identifying therapeutic needs over mechanical ones. [ch 056 / entry; ch 056 / 056.06]
*   Values successful outcomes in "interpersonal conflict issues" of sentient artifacts and tracks the strength of bonds between artifacts and wielders. [ch 054 / entry; ch 056 / 056.06]
*   Was eager to hold one more therapy session with Thorin once he had become one of Candlekeep's celebrated heroes. [ch 071 / entry; ch 071 / 071.09]

## History with the Party
*   Philemon is an Avowed monk at Candlekeep specializing in the psychological preservation and historical cataloging of ancient sentient artifacts. He was assigned by the First Reader to work with Thorin and Dawnbringer. [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / 054.06]
*   Philemon greeted Thorin and Dawnbringer, initially referring to Dawnbringer as "a remarkable specimen," which Thorin corrected, asserting she was his friend. Philemon identified Dawnbringer as a late Netherese-era sun-worship artifact suffering arcane stress from millennia of darkness. [ch 056 / 056.06; ch 056 / moment]
*   Philemon reframed the interaction from an artifact assessment to grief counseling, outlining a two-phase recovery process for Dawnbringer: the work of light, followed by the work of mourning. He noted mid-sentence that Dawnbringer could not be polished. [ch 056 / entry; ch 056 / 056.06; ch 056 / moment]
*   After the party returned to the upper levels of Candlekeep following the vault incident, Philemon approached Thorin to request one more day of therapy, eager to treat him now that Thorin was celebrated as a defender of the keep. [ch 071 / entry; ch 071 / 071.09]
*   Thorin agreed to the final session, noting he owed it to Dawnbringer, who had heard herself referenced in prophecy during the vault events. [ch 071 / 071.09]
