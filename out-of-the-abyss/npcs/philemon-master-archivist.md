## Identity
- Philemon, also styled Master Archivist Philemon, is an Avowed monk at Candlekeep. He specializes in "psychological preservation and historical cataloging of ancient sentient artifacts" and has a "tremendous track record of success" [ch 054 / entry].
- He is a clinical specialist in sentient artifacts [ch 056 / entry]. In ch 071 he is described as an Avowed monk and sentient-artifact specialist who acts as Thorin's therapist [ch 071 / entry].
- He was assigned to Thorin and Dawnbringer by the First Reader, Bookwyrm, on the party's arrival. In those scenes he was spoken of and not shown present [ch 054 / 054.06; ch 056 / 056.04].
- He first appears in person in ch 056, in the therapy session with Thorin and Dawnbringer [ch 056 / 056.06].
- He hosts sessions in his reading salon [ch 072 / 072.02].

## Personality and Motivations
- **Clinical first, then corrected.** He opens by calling Dawnbringer "a remarkable specimen," and Thorin corrects him: "This is my friend Dawnbringer." He adjusts at once [ch 056 / 056.06]. The ch 056 entry describes him as "gentle and precise" [ch 056 / entry].
- **Self-correcting and reframing.** He catches himself mid-sentence ("She can't be polished, actually") and recasts the assessment as grief counseling [ch 056 / 056.06; ch 056 / moment].
- **Methodical about process.** He set out a two-phase plan, the work of light first and the work of mourning second [ch 056 / moment]. In ch 072 he moves the ritual "to its next layer" [ch 072 / 072.02].
- **Scholarly appetite.** He took notes for a future paper once Thorin understood Brysis had been Dawnbringer's captor [ch 072 / 072.02]. He asks the spelling of "Dynaphyse" and connects the name to a line in the Netherese temple scrolls [ch 072 / 072.02]. The ch 072 entry says he "eagerly documents every breakthrough for future scholarship" [ch 072 / entry].
- **Warm toward the pair.** He noted that the bond between Thorin and the blade was stronger than he had feared [ch 056 / 056.06]. He told Thorin the relationship had improved "even in the last 48 hours" [ch 072 / moment]. He handed Thorin a note about Lathander as the god of second chances [ch 072 / 072.02].
- **Eager to continue.** In ch 071 he asked for one more therapy session, now eager to work with one of Candlekeep's celebrated heroes [ch 071 / 071.09].
- **Motive for the Dynaphyse inquiry.** He said he had never known what Khaem had taken [ch 072 / 072.02]. The summaries do not state any deeper personal motive.

## History with the Party
- Philemon is an Avowed monk at Candlekeep who specializes in "psychological preservation and historical cataloging of ancient sentient artifacts" and has a "tremendous track record of success". He was assigned to work with Thorin and Dawnbringer. [ch 054 / entry]
- On the party's arrival, the First Reader directed Thorin and Dawnbringer to Philemon. In that scene Philemon was spoken of and not shown present. Thorin responded enthusiastically to the pairing. [ch 054 / 054.06]
- In ch 056, Bookwyrm, the First Reader, assigned Thorin to Master Archivist Philemon. Philemon was only named in that scene. [ch 056 / 056.04]
- In their first session, Philemon greeted Dawnbringer as "a remarkable specimen — late Netherese-era recovery, sun-worship origins, sustained darkness exposure of approximately 3,000 to 4,000 years". Thorin corrected him: "This is my friend Dawnbringer." [ch 056 / 056.06]
- Philemon then noted with some surprise that the bond between Thorin and the blade was stronger than he had feared. [ch 056 / 056.06]
- Philemon asked Dawnbringer whether the corpse she was found with was her wielder. She faltered and went silent. [ch 056 / 056.06; ch 056 / entry]
- Philemon corrected himself mid-sentence that the sword "can't be polished" and reframed her need as remembering daylight without fighting, then grieving. He set out a two-phase process, the work of light first and the work of mourning second. This effectively signed Thorin and Dawnbringer up for therapy. [ch 056 / 056.06; ch 056 / moment]
- In ch 071, after the party returned from the vault, Philemon (described there as Thorin's therapist) approached Thorin at the Candlekeep celebration and asked for one more therapy session. Thorin agreed, saying he owed it to Dawnbringer, who had herself heard the prophecy in which she appeared. [ch 071 / 071.09; ch 071 / entry]
- In ch 072, Philemon hosted the session in his reading salon and had built a stand for Dawnbringer's scabbard beside Thorin's seat. He explained that the ritual had to move to its next layer: Dawnbringer must name her true wielder. [ch 072 / 072.02]
- Dawnbringer erupted in anger and cursed Brysis. Thorin realized Brysis had been her captor and not her wielder. Philemon began taking notes and told Thorin, "You may have a gift here." [ch 072 / 072.02; ch 072 / moment]
- When Philemon asked again, Dawnbringer named "Dynaphyse", a servant of Lathander and her first and true wielder. She said Brysis of Khaem killed Dynaphyse and buried Dawnbringer with her own corpse. [ch 072 / 072.02]
- Philemon handed Thorin a note about Lathander as the god of second chances. After this breakthrough her fear of the dark was gone, and she bound the blessing Lathander's Dawn to Thorin. [ch 072 / 072.02]
- Philemon asked whether "Dynaphyse" is spelled with an "S" or a "Z" (Dawnbringer said "S"). He tied the name to a line in the Netherese temple scrolls about a Lathanderite woman of the Dawn, struck from the record and lost with Khaem. He said he had never known what Khaem had taken. [ch 072 / 072.02]
- Philemon wished Thorin and Dawnbringer well and suggested Thorin consider therapy as a second career. Thorin promised to consider it. [ch 072 / 072.02; ch 072 / entry]
- The summaries show Philemon last in his reading salon in ch 072. Status not established in the summaries beyond his appearances in these chapters. [ch 072 / 072.02]

## Relationships
- **Thorin:** Philemon is his therapist and the party member he works with most [ch 071 / 071.09]. Thorin was enthusiastic about the pairing: "I'm all up for couples counseling with Dawnbringer" [ch 054 / entry]. Thorin corrected Philemon's "specimen" framing [ch 056 / 056.06]. Philemon later praised his insight and empathy and suggested therapy as a second career [ch 072 / 072.02].
- **Dawnbringer:** Philemon's patient. She went silent when he asked about the corpse she was found with [ch 056 / 056.06]. She named Dynaphyse in ch 072 [ch 072 / 072.02]. In the same session she bound Lathander's Dawn to Thorin [ch 072 / 072.02].
- **Bookwyrm (the First Reader):** She assigned Thorin and Dawnbringer to him [ch 054 / 054.06; ch 056 / 056.04].
- **Brysis of Khaem:** Dawnbringer says Brysis killed Dynaphyse and buried her with her own corpse. Philemon uses Brysis's name only in the session, and the summaries show no other link between them [ch 072 / 072.02].
- **Dynaphyse:** Dawnbringer's first wielder, a servant of Lathander. Philemon links her to the Netherese temple scrolls [ch 072 / 072.02].
- **Other Candlekeep scholars:** Sister Yvenne, Brother Khell-Vire and Brother Vareth were assigned to other party members. They appear alongside Philemon in the ch 071 celebration but are not shown interacting with him [ch 056 / 056.04; ch 071 / 071.09].
