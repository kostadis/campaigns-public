## Identity
Yestabrod is a larval creature serving as a leader in the Garden of Welcome [ch 031 / entry]. Vestigial fungal growths suggest it was once an ordinary myconid, but it possesses unnatural resilience to sunlight, unlike adult myconids [ch 031 / entry; ch 031 / 031.04]. It speaks both aloud in a gurgling voice and telepathically into the minds of others [ch 031 / entry; ch 031 / 031.03]. During its life, it commanded drow spore servants [ch 031 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Yestabrod was obsessively focused on facilitating a wedding rehearsal for the Demon Queen of Fungi, Zuggtmoy [ch 031 / entry; ch 031 / 031.03]. It viewed the party’s presence merely in terms of their role in this ceremony, asking if they were friends of the bride or the intended, though it concluded the distinction did not matter as all would be incorporated into the "coworkers of pain" [ch 031 / 031.03]. It was dismissive of social manipulations, explicitly rejecting the party’s attempt to perform an "audit" with the statement, "There will be no audit" [ch 031 / 031.03]. In combat, it demonstrated a willingness to utilize grotesque tactics, including detonating corpses to heal itself and attacking with spores that induce madness [ch 031 / 031.04].

## History with the Party
- Yestabrod is a larval creature and leader in the Garden of Welcome, who appears to have been an ordinary myconid but is not debilitated by sunlight [ch 031 / entry].
- Yestabrod confronted the party in the Garden of Welcome, welcoming them to a wedding rehearsal for Zuggtmoy and refusing the party’s "audit" ploy [ch 031 / 031.03].
- During the battle, Yestabrod was struck by Gyrgum's necrotic energy and healed itself by exploding nearby corpses to absorb their remains [ch 031 / 031.04].
- Yestabrod released spores that afflicted Daz and Zalthir with madness, while also inflicting gore and acid wounds through corpse detonations [ch 031 / 031.04; ch 031 / moment].
- Zalthir killed Yestabrod with a flurry of strikes, preventing the creature from detonating any further corpses [ch 031 / 031.04].
- After death, Yestabrod’s corpse was animated by three spore servants during a mock wedding ceremony to play the role of the priest [ch 032 / 032.02].
- Spores released by Yestabrod during the initial battle continued to affect Daz and Zalthir in the subsequent session, causing Zalthir to exhibit hoarding behavior and Daz to experience hallucinations [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.02].

## Last Observed State
Yestabrod was killed by Zalthir with a flurry of strikes during the battle in the Garden of Welcome in Chapter 031 [ch 031 / entry; ch 031 / 031.04]. This action prevented the creature from detonating any further corpses [ch 031 / 031.04]. In Chapter 032, its corpse was animated by three spore servants and manipulated like a puppet to play the role of the priest during a mock wedding ceremony [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.02].
