## Identity
Senni Diggermattock is a gnome leader in Blingdenstone who serves as Chief Dorbo Diggermattock’s assistant, fellow authority, and wife [ch 036 / entry; ch 048 / entry]. She holds the title of Quartermaster and sits at a stone desk in Diggermattock Hall [ch 035 / 035.05; ch 036 / entry]. She acts as the representative and advocate for the non-mining population, ordinary residents, and the mining workers who do the physical labor, contrasting with Chief Dorbo’s focus on profit and corporate interests [ch 037 / entry; ch 039 / entry; ch 045 / entry]. She is credited with forming a "coalition of the willing" to combat the Pudding King [ch 045 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Senni is characterized by her advocacy for quality-of-life concerns, civilian safety, and community stability, whereas Chief Dorbo prioritizes profit margins and mining interests [ch 039 / entry; ch 046 / entry]. She is pragmatic and diplomatic, often pushing for cooperation with the Goldwhisker Clan when others are hostile, and seeking common purpose among Blingdenstone's factions [ch 037 / entry; ch 038 / 038.02; ch 039 / entry]. She displays frustration with bureaucratic arguing and leadership incompetence, pleading "Can we like not argue?" when faced with the Zuggtmoy threat [ch 043 / 043.05]. Senni opposes the exploitation of summoned creatures, famously chanting "Gnome Labor, not Summoned Labor!" to rally workers against elemental slavery [ch 045 / moment]. She is protective of the city's reforms and civilian areas, fearing they will collapse if the party fails against the oozes [ch 046 / entry].
