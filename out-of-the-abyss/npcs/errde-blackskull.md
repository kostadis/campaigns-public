## Identity
Errde Blackskull serves as the Captain of the Stone Guard in Gracklstugh [ch 013 / 013.06; ch 019 / entry; ch 022 / entry]. She operates as the city’s primary enforcement figure, stating clearly, "I am the law" [ch 013 / 013.06]. She oversees investigations into civic instability, specifically targeting derro activities and mysterious assassinations within duergar society [ch 013 / 013.06; ch 014 / 014.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Errde is pragmatic, volatile, and driven by a desire to protect her clan and enforce order through overwhelming force. She projects confidence, noting she would have brought a "whole platoon" to arrest the party if she intended to, rather than just four guards [ch 013 / 013.06]. Despite publicly dismissing the existence of the Assassin’s Guild, she privately seeks proof of their existence because several of her relatives were killed [ch 014 / 014.02; ch 017 / 017.04]. She is sensitive about her personal involvement in these deaths, snapping at Gyrgum when he inquired who found one of the bodies [ch 014 / 014.02].

Her primary goals are to obtain documented proof of conspiracy and to identify the assassins targeting local figures [ch 019 / entry; ch 022 / entry]. She strongly prefers physical evidence, such as books or documents, over manufactured proofs [ch 022 / entry]. Errde is prone to extreme, violent reactions if provided with specific intelligence:
*   Proof of derro demonic dealings would cause her to attempt to kill all derro [ch 019 / entry; ch 021 / entry].
*   Proof of the Deepking’s ties to the Assassin’s Guild would likely send her "all homicidal on the Deepking" [ch 019 / entry; ch 021 / 021.02].
*   News that the Assassin’s Guild was supported by the king would lead her to attempt to kill the king [ch 021 / entry].

*   In Chapter 018, Errde received the party at the Stone Guard headquarters, attempting charm while barely containing seething rage [ch 018 / 018.01].
*   She demanded documented proof of conspiracies by the Gray Ghosts and Council of Savants, secretly seeking evidence of the Assassin’s Guild targeting her clan, and aimed to use findings to justify a violent "pacification mission" against the Derro [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.01].
