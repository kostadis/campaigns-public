## Identity
Dorbo Diggermattock is the Chief of Blingdenstone and the leader of Clan Diggermattock [ch 035 / entry; ch 036 / entry]. He serves as one of the two primary authorities presiding over Diggermattock Hall, alongside Quartermaster Senni Diggermattock [ch 036 / entry; ch 036 / 036.04]. He leads a mining coalition focused on extracting resources and expanding housing for the returning community [ch 037 / entry; ch 037 / 037.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Dorbo is characterized by a strong focus on financial metrics, specifically "gross margins," and mining profits [ch 045 / entry; ch 046 / entry]. He is resistant to cooperating with Clan Goldwhisker, suspecting them of being wererats and noting territorial disputes [ch 037 / entry; ch 037 / 037.01]. He attempts to maintain plausible deniability in risky operations, backing away from direct ownership of plans to blame adventurers if they fail or claim credit if they succeed [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.01]. He is protective of his privacy and personal assets, intercepting the party when they attempted to enter his private quarters to clarify that he possessed no acid-resistant armor there [ch 039 / 039.02].

Party members theorized that his unusual greed and aggression were amplified by demonic influence from the Pudding King [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.06]. Despite this, he displays anxiety when facing threats but shifts to relief and confidence when the party is present [ch 045 / entry]. He is reluctant to pay for military protection and dislikes paying a "hero's wage" to the working class [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.06]. He fears losing his political standing if the party, particularly Daz, becomes more popular than he is, prompting him to send them away quickly to proclaim the city "open for business" [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.06].
