## Identity
Fembris Lancer is an acolyte adjutant associated with the Avowed of Candlekeep [ch 063 / entry]. He serves as a liaison and messenger, notably delivering Bookwyrm’s summons to the party [ch 062 / entry; ch 062 / 062.03]. During the events of Chapter 62, he was subjected to what appeared to be a disciplinary confinement, ordered to remain with the party while they were held in Deneir's Sanctum [ch 062 / entry; ch 062 / 062.05]. The party utilized this proximity to use him as an unwitting food taster, having him eat their delivered meals first due to fears of poisoning [ch 062 / entry; ch 062 / 062.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Fembris is characterized by a notable lack of discretion, which Thorin summed up with “Loose lips sink ships, dumbass.” [ch 063 / moment]. He reported the transfer of a mysterious key to Bookwyrm in the presence of A'lai Aivenmore, inadvertently leaking sensitive intelligence [ch 063 / entry; ch 063 / 063.03]. Despite this breach of security, Fembris appears to lack agency or deep understanding of the political machinations surrounding him; he acted as a messenger for Bookwyrm and followed orders without apparent strategic intent [ch 062 / 062.03; ch 063 / 063.03]. Under extreme pressure, such as when confronted by the party regarding the leak, he became "paralyzed with fear" but ultimately confessed the truth [ch 063 / entry; ch 063 / 063.03]. He is compliant when directly warned; after Thorin’s explicit threat regarding Tadric’s location, Fembris heeded the instruction to remain silent on that specific point [ch 063 / entry; ch 063 / 063.03].

## History with the Party
- Fembris Lancer is identified as the party's adjutant who steps away during private deliberations and returns with Bookwyrm’s summons [ch 062 / entry].
- He returned from a discreet distance to report that Bookwyrm wished to see the party [ch 062 / 062.03].
- The party considered feeding false information to Bookwyrm through Fembris but noted he was not present for Alkrist's confession [ch 062 / 062.03].
- The party retained information about A'lai because neither Bookwyrm nor Fembris knew that Alkrist had named him [ch 062 / 062.03].
- Fembris remained with the party in Deneir's Sanctum, apparently as a punishment, when Bookwyrm ordered them confined [ch 062 / entry; ch 062 / 062.05].
- The party, distrusting the food served, allowed Fembris to eat the delivered meal first, effectively making him an unwitting food taster, a practice they jokingly adopted [ch 062 / entry; ch 062 / 062.05].
- Fembris was present when Gyrgum handed the mysterious key to Daz, though the exchange was not performed with particular secrecy [ch 062 / entry; ch 062 / 062.06].
- During the helmed horror attack, the party considered handing the key to Fembris to test the constructs' tracking but rejected the idea due to a lack of trust [ch 062 / 062.06].
- After the attack, the party identified Fembris as the only person who had witnessed the key changing hands, making him a person of interest for potentially leaking the location [ch 062 / entry; ch 063 / 063.01].
- Fembris raced with the party and Daral to Bookwyrm’s office, where he stood paralyzed with fear [ch 063 / 063.02].
- Under pressure, Fembris admitted that A'lai Aivenmore was present when he reported the key transfer to Bookwyrm [ch 063 / entry; ch 063 / 063.03].
- Thorin warned Fembris not to disclose Tadric’s location [ch 063 / entry; ch 063 / 063.03].

## Last Observed State
As of Chapter 63, Fembris was present at the Sea Warden's Tower when the party confronted Kalan [ch 063 / 063.03]. He stood paralyzed with fear following the discovery of Bookwyrm’s murder and the subsequent realization that the constructs had tracked the party to Deneir's Sanctum [ch 063 / entry; ch 063 / 063.02]. His last confirmed action was admitting under pressure that A'lai Aivenmore was present when he informed Bookwyrm of the key handoff from Gyrgum to Daz [ch 063 / entry; ch 063 / 063.03]. He then received a direct warning from Thorin not to disclose Tadric's location [ch 063 / entry; ch 063 / 063.03]. There is no evidence in the final chapters indicating that Fembris departed, was harmed, or was detained after this confrontation; he remains in the general vicinity of the party and the leadership of Candlekeep. Status not established in the summaries beyond his presence at the tower.
