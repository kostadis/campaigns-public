## Identity
Pelek is the ghost of a deep gnome traveler from Blingdenstone [ch 016 / entry]. He was named after an actor by his mother, though his relationship with his father and childhood was described by Pelek himself as "complicated and sordid" [ch 016 / 016.01]. Physically, his spirit manifested with a gruesome cleaved head wound, resulting from his murder and dismemberment by his former companion, a derro named Buppido [ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / moment]. He identified himself as a gem cutter and described his state as being "very well-adjusted" despite his untimely death [ch 016 / moment; ch 032 / moment].

## Personality and Motivations
Pelek was primarily motivated by the desire for proper burial and spiritual peace, having been trapped near his murderer's remains for approximately one year [ch 016 / entry]. His humor was characterized by dry wit and self-deprecation regarding his undead status [ch 016 / moment; ch 023 / moment]. He demonstrated a strong awareness of Blingdenstone’s cultural taboos, specifically warning against numbers divisible by eight or associations with spiders due to their connection to the drow and Lolth [ch 016 / 016.01; ch 016 / moment]. He possessed a cautious nature, requesting to be physically restrained with silk rope when possessing a body to prevent the host’s consciousness from resurfacing, citing the "infinitesimal possibility" of failure [ch 023 / 023.02; ch 023 / moment]. He explicitly stated that he was not qualified to provide medical diagnoses, noting, "I am a ghost. I am a traitor. I am a gem cutter. I am not a doctor" [ch 032 / moment].

## History with the Party
*   The ghost of a deep gnome traveler from Blingdenstone with a cleaved head wound, murdered and dismembered by his former companion Buppido, who lured him with promises of business deals; he has been trapped in the Whorlstone Tunnels for roughly a year and has no loyalty to any Gracklstugh faction. [ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / 016.01; ch 016 / moment]
*   He agreed to travel with the party after they collected his specific bones to return to Blingdenstone for burial, binding his spirit to their location so he could scout short distances through stone cracks but not leave his bones far. [ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / 016.01; ch 016 / 016.02]
*   During the initial encounter, he warned the party that deep gnomes of Blingdenstone are hostile to signs of Lolth, advising them to declare the party’s drow member is from the "Overbright" and not Menzoberranzan, and to carry no items associated with the number eight or spiders. [ch 016 / 016.01]
*   He revealed that the strange magic in the tunnels had animated his severed hand, which scuttled away wearing his obsidian ring, and he tried to possess Buppido and failed. [ch 016 / 016.01; ch 016 / moment]
*   As a companion, he provided advice on navigating cultural taboos in Blingdenstone and confirmed that Droki passes through the tunnels approximately once a day. [ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / 016.02]
*   In the fungi forest, he reassured the party that the whispering sounds were harmless wind, noting they were preferable to the sound of Buppido chewing, and he remained hovering near Gyrgum’s bones during combat. [ch 016 / 016.04; ch 016 / moment]
*   During negotiations with Werz, he provided a menacing presence, delivering a "boo" to intimidate the NPC, and insisted that any gold earned on his behalf be deposited in his grave. [ch 018 / entry]
*   He stated that he could not haunt physical objects to be carried around more easily, saying "Trust me, I tried," and clarified that being trapped within 30 feet of his murderer was the source of his boredom. [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / moment]
*   During a battle against cultists, he possessed the body of a derro assassin to engage enemies with a withering touch, acting on Gyrgum's turn and surviving a sacrifice threat with a "scary" voice, while the party retained his bones. [ch 020 / entry; ch 021 / entry; ch 021 / 021.01]
*   After taking over the assassin’s body, he cooperatively requested to be tied up with silk rope before sleeping to prevent the host’s consciousness from resurfacing, remarking that it was his first sleep since being dead. [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.02; ch 023 / moment]
*   He noted that the party had "completed a full circle" in the caverns and, after being securely tied up through the night, Gyrgum won a wager that Pelek would not escape. [ch 023 / 023.02; ch 023 / moment]
*   When asked to diagnose Zalthir’s behavior, he firmly stated the limits of his expertise: "I am a ghost. I am a traitor. I am a gem cutter. I am not a doctor." [ch 032 / moment]
*   The party took his remaining bones (described as "Catholic relic-sized") to the cemetery beside the Temple of the Ruby in the Rough in Blingdenstone to fulfill his wish for interment. [ch 037 / entry; ch 037 / 037.02; ch 037 / 037.03]
*   He materialized to speak with the priest, Glyphic Shroomlight, who agreed to make an exception for his burial to avoid being haunted; Pelek expressed relief at ending his journey and stated he planned to go "wherever I'm supposed to go next." [ch 037 / 037.03]
*   Gyrgum performed a dragon-themed ritual and planted a gold coin with the bones, after which Pelek’s spirit vanished, leaving the party with a sense of closure. [ch 037 / entry; ch 037 / 037.03]
*   His interment was later counted by the ghost Burrow Warden Jadger as the first "put to rest" task in a bargain where the party would earn answers to questions for every ghost they laid to rest. [ch 037 / 037.04]


## Last Observed State
Pelek’s spirit departed this plane after being interred in the catacombs beneath the Temple of the Ruby in the Rough in Blingdenstone [ch 037 / 037.03]. Gyrgum buried the "Catholic relic-sized" collection of his remaining bones in a quiet corner and performed a dragon-themed ritual, planting a gold coin with the remains [ch 037 / 037.03]. Upon completing the rites, Pelek’s spirit vanished, leaving the party with a sense of closure [ch 037 / 037.03]. He confirmed to the party that he planned to go "wherever I'm supposed to go next" [ch 037 / 037.03]. Status: Departed/Passing on. [ch 037 / 037.03]

## Relationships
*   **Buppido:** A derro who was Pelek’s former companion. Buppido lured Pelek with promises of business deals before murdering and dismembering him. Pelek described him as "really an annoying person" and noted that watching Buppido chew had been unbearable [ch 016 / moment; ch 037 / 037.03]. Pelek once attempted to possess Buppido but failed [ch 016 / moment].
*   **Gyrgum:** The party member who carried Pelek’s bones in a bag. Pelek relied on Gyrgum to transport his remains and requested silk rope from him for restraint purposes [ch 016 / 016.01; ch 023 / 023.02]. Pelek’s spirit hovered near Gyrgum’s bones during combat to keep them safe [ch 016 / 016.04].
*   **Glyphic Shroomlight:** A priest of Segojan Earthcaller at the Temple of the Ruby in the Rough. Glyphic agreed to inter Pelek’s remains after realizing the alternative was being haunted by the ghost [ch 037 / 037.03].
*   **Burrow Warden Jadger:** A ghost who counted Pelek’s burial as the first task in a bargain to answer the party's questions [ch 037 / 037.04].

*   **Daz:** A party member who noted the stability of Pelek’s possession but withheld that information to keep Pelek restrained for safety reasons [ch 023 / 023.02; ch 023 / moment].
