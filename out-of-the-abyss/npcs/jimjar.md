## Identity
Jimjar is a deep gnome who travels with the party, presenting himself as a compulsive gambler and bookkeeper [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / moment]. He maintains a personal ledger of all debts and victories, prioritizing the act of betting itself over monetary gain [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / moment]. While initially appearing to be a prisoner and later a guide, his nature is revealed to be far more complex; Thorin’s *Know Your Enemy* check identified him as possessing divine-level immunities (poison, psychic, charm, fear) and resistances (radiant, non-magical physical), a profile resembling that of an angel or deity [ch 048 / 048.07]. This identification coincided with his ability to vanish from existence [ch 048 / 048.07].

## Personality and Motivations
Jimjar is defined by an obsessive need to wager on outcomes, often betting against himself or on scenarios that do not directly benefit him [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / moment]. His companions increasingly suspect that his wagers are a disguised means of revealing information or directing their actions — as when he predicted the party would need to equip an army with acid-resistant gear [ch 035 / entry]. For example, he wagered that the "circle of joy" would be a "circle of unwilling vows," effectively warning the party before entering Neverlight Grove [ch 027 / 027.05].

His motivation appears to be a combination of gambling instinct and a detached, philosophical view of probability, which he terms "world math" [ch 048 / 048.05]. He is willing to risk significant sums (such as ten platinum pieces) if the alternative is total party death, noting that the downside of betting is negligible in such scenarios [ch 048 / 048.05]. He often exhibits a lack of conventional gnome behavior, displaying knowledge far exceeding his apparent station and receiving unusual deference from locals [ch 035 / 035.04; ch 048 / 048.07]. He also displays a playful, teasing demeanor, particularly toward Thorin, whom he bet would do something embarrassing [ch 026 / 026.03] and could not clear a gap with his pack on [ch 046 / 046.05].

## Last Observed State
Jimjar was last seen in Chapter 048, departing Blingdenstone [ch 048 / 048.07]. After assisting in the defeat of the ooze army by using a bone die, said by the Duergar he won it from to have been carved from a saint's knucklebone, to pacify them via Glabbagool [ch 048 / 048.05], he warned the party of a new fungal threat, Zuggtmoy, moving in silently [ch 048 / 048.07].

When Thorin used *Know Your Enemy* to scrutinize him, Jimjar realized he had been exposed. He winked at the party and vanished from existence, leaving behind the sound of platinum coins [ch 048 / 048.07]. The party retained his true nature as an unresolved mystery, listing it as a primary question for their research at Candlekeep [ch 053 / 053.05; ch 053 / 053.06]. In Chapter 054, the First Reader at Candlekeep assigned Zalthir specifically to study Jimjar’s identity, indicating the library holds relevant information [ch 054 / 054.06]. Jimjar has not physically appeared since Chapter 048; his current location and status are not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
**The Party:** Jimjar is a long-time companion, traveling with them from the drow slave pen (Chapter 2) through the Underdark [ch 002 / entry; ch 048 / 048.07]. He acts as an informal guide, though his navigational skills are prone to error, often reframing getting lost as finding shortcuts [ch 034 / 034.01; ch 034 / 034.08].

**Thorin:** Jimjar frequently wagers with Thorin, particularly on physical challenges or embarrassing outcomes, such as Thorin’s pants falling down [ch 026 / 026.03] or his ability to cross a gap [ch 046 / 046.05]. Thorin is the one who uncovers Jimjar’s true nature via a skill check [ch 048 / 048.07].

**Gyrgum:** Jimjar maintains a running debt ledger with Gyrgum, owing him five gold from the tunnel-blockage bet [ch 006 / 006.03]. He proposed a ten-platinum bet to Gyrgum that led to the pacification of the ooze army, and admitted he kept the bet small so Gyrgum would not get suspicious [ch 048 / 048.05].

**Glabbagool:** Jimjar used Glabbagool as a conduit to control the ooze army, tossing a bone die onto the sentient ooze to trigger a telepathic link that calmed the creatures [ch 048 / 048.05]. Glabbagool complained that the die "doesn't seem to be responding to the acid" until it popped out [ch 048 / 048.05].

**Buppido:** Jimjar referred to Buppido as "the crazy derro" and dismissed the idea that Buppido could summon Demogorgon, offering to take bets on what could [ch 015 / 015.04; ch 012 / 012.03].

**Zalthir:** Jimjar was "curiously happy" with Zalthir during preparations for the Pudding King battle [ch 046 / 046.03]. He pressed Zalthir to take a bet in Chapter 6 [ch 006 / 006.03] and later offered a bet about Zalthir's next emotion as a distraction from having gotten the party lost [ch 032 / 032.06].

**Local Gnomes:** Gnomes in Blingdenstone treat Jimjar with unusual deference, with one guard finding him familiar but unable to place him, leaving the party with the impression that he is "some sort of VIP something" [ch 035 / 035.04].
