## Identity
- Jimjar is a deep gnome who was a prisoner in the drow outpost with the party and a compulsive gambler. He offered to keep the books and extend credit to the other prisoners. [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.02]
- He acts as a guide and companion on the party's Underdark route to Blingdenstone. He also fights alongside them, using hand and short crossbows. [ch 003 / 003.02; ch 020 / entry; ch 032 / 032.04]
- At Blingdenstone the gnomes treated him with peculiar deference, and a guard said he looked familiar but could not place him. The party suspected he was "some sort of VIP something." [ch 035 / 035.04]
- He is revealed in Chapter 072 to be Callarduran Smoothhands, the Deep Brother and god of the svirfneblin. The deity-identification ritual performed by Brother Khell-Vire showed this. [ch 072 / 072.06; ch 072 / moment]
- Before the planar intrusion, Callarduran was in the Abyss in a long quarrel with Yeenoghu, Demogorgon, and Zuggtmoy over their depredations against the deep gnomes. The intrusion dragged him to the Material Plane with the demons, and he disguised himself so they would not recognize him. [ch 072 / 072.06; ch 072 / entry]
- The Know Your Enemy result in Chapter 048 showed immunity to poison and psychic damage, resistance to radiant damage and non-magical physical attacks, and immunity to being charmed or frightened. Zalthir noted it was "suspiciously similar to the stat block for angels." [ch 048 / 048.07]
- Mechanical details in the evidence: survival +4, Deception +5, Persuasion +5. [ch 005 / entry; ch 006 / 006.03; ch 007 / 007.03]

## Personality and Motivations
- **Gambling as a habit and a method.** He bets on almost everything: lift passengers, survivor counts, Thorin's embarrassments, whether the party would find crystals useful. He keeps an almost freakish memory of every debt and victory. [ch 002 / 002.02; ch 002 / moment; ch 008 / 008.01; ch 026 / 026.03; ch 028 / 028.04]
- **He bets for the thrill, not to win.** The party concluded this in Chapter 006. He refused to act without a wager, bet against himself, and lost on purpose. [ch 006 / 006.03]
- **Small stakes, loose accounting.** The DM ruled his small wagers are not tracked unless they reach "hundreds", because he is "more into the betting experience than the gotcha." [ch 026 / 026.03] He also misremembered a bet and claimed to have no cash before paying Gyrgum. [ch 032 / 032.04]
- **Bets as steering.** The party came to suspect his bets were a way of telling them what to do. [ch 035 / 035.04] Daz put it as "If you make enough bets with enough people, they start telling you things." [ch 035 / entry] In Chapter 048 he said he kept the ten-platinum bet small so Gyrgum would not get suspicious. [ch 048 / 048.05]
- **Evasive about his knowledge.** He seemed to know "way more about things than anybody should." [ch 011 / entry] He denied any link to demon lords, saying he "collects information and makes bets". [ch 035 / 035.04] Chapter 015 notes he "may have had other agendas." [ch 015 / 015.02]
- **Callous streak early on.** He remarked there was "always the possibility to having fewer people left behind" when the boats could not hold everyone. [ch 008 / 008.03]
- **Reactive loyalty.** He saved Gyrgum with a rope [ch 027 / 027.03], carried the incapacitated Daz to the boat [ch 009 / 009.02], and vouched for the party at Blingdenstone's gate. [ch 035 / 035.03; ch 035 / 035.05]
- **Face-saving under pressure.** When he got the party lost, he first deflected with a weak bet, then admitted he was covering. Afterwards he reframed the detour as a shortcut. [ch 032 / 032.06; ch 034 / 034.08]
- **Motivation, as far as the evidence shows.** He appears to push the party toward helping Blingdenstone and the deep gnomes. He welcomed Grinta Ironhead's trade terms as "very advantageous to the folks in Blingdenstone." [ch 018 / 018.07] Khell-Vire reads his unfinished work and his debt to the party as tied to the party's role in saving the deep gnomes. [ch 072 / 072.06]
