## Identity
Demogorgon is a towering demon lord, described as thirty feet tall or greater, possessing two baboon-like heads, wickedly curved tusks, oily tentacles, and red eyes [ch 008 / entry; ch 009 / entry]. He is explicitly known as the Prince of Demons [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / moment]. While the entity that physically manifested in the Underdark near Sloobludop was identified as an avatar rather than the whole being—summoning a demon lord of his power being beyond ordinary capabilities—he is treated by the party and NPCs as the source of the local demonic presence [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.02; ch 009 / moment]. Jimjar characterizes his nature as representing "relentless damage and carnage," distinguishing him from other demon lords like Zuggtmoy (decay) or Juiblex (shapelessness) [ch 035 / 035.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Demogorgon’s primary motivation in the observed events was the destruction of his former worshippers and the pursuit of feeding opportunities. Upon rising from the Darklake, he waded toward Sloobludop with the specific intent of slaughtering the Kuo-toa, distracted by the "opportunity for feasting" [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.01]. His arrival came just after the civil war's final blow and brought chaos and destruction to the Kuo-toa [ch 008 / 008.04; ch 009 / entry].

He is associated with madness and instability. Witnesses are subjected to a DC 13 Charisma saving throw against madness upon seeing his rise, with failure resulting in screaming, laughing, or weeping [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.01]. Cultists serving him describe him as "always hungry" and "the prince of chaos, always unseen," attributing their own insanity and lack of restraint to his influence [ch 022 / 022.04; ch 023 / moment].
