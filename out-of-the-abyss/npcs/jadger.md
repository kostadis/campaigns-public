## Identity
Jadger is a three-foot-tall ghost and a former protector of Blingdenstone who originally defended the city against drow, oozes, and other Underdark threats [ch 037 / 037.04; ch 037 / entry]. Most of his predecessors in the Burrow Wardens died during the drow invasion or the subsequent flight from the city, and Jadger has taken it upon himself to re-establish the order [ch 037 / 037.04]. He secretly trains promising young gnomes, believing one of them will soon lead the renewed Burrow Wardens [ch 037 / entry; ch 037 / 037.04]. Jadger uses the pronouns he/him [ch 037 / 037.04].

The name "Uth-Jadger" also appears as an alias for this NPC in later chapters, referring to the same ghostly entity [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.07].

## Personality and Motivations
Jadger is proud, long-winded, and deeply invested in Blingdenstone's survival [ch 037 / entry]. He reacts with indignation when compared to a trickster-genie, insisting, “I'm not some trivial genie. I am Burrow Warden Jadger” [ch 037 / entry; ch 037 / 037.04]. He views himself as an expert on the Underdark, claiming to know “things about the Underdark that other people have forgotten were things” [ch 037 / entry; ch 037 / 037.04].

Jadger offers knowledge as a boon: one truthful answer about the Underdark for every tormented ghost the party puts to rest [ch 037 / entry; ch 037 / 037.04; ch 043 / 043.03]. He frames unity among the gnome clans as essential to victory [ch 039 / 039.07]. He is willing to provide tactical advice, such as analyzing the unnatural massing of oozes and advising that they are likely directed by a proxy empowered by a demon rather than controlled directly by Juiblex [ch 037 / entry; ch 037 / 037.04].

The GM noted that Jadger seemed unaware he was tormenting the party with his "sanctimonious preaching" [ch 043 / 043.03].

## Relationships
Jadger has a mentor-like relationship with young gnome gnomes whom he secretly trains to become the new Burrow Wardens [ch 037 / entry; ch 037 / 037.04]. He specifically identified the Dragonborn monk Zalthir as a potentially excellent asset because Zalthir carried no weapons, and Jadger attempted to learn a monk technique from him to teach the trainees [ch 037 / 037.04].

Jadger interacts with the party primarily through his bargain of knowledge-for-ghosts, with the GM noting his "sanctimonious preaching" [ch 037 / 037.04; ch 043 / 043.03]. He said other Burrow Warden spirits would join the fight if the party completed a quest on their behalf [ch 039 / 039.09].

He is described as a spectral leader of the Burrow Warden ghosts, who dipped their banners in salute at the party's departure [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.07].
