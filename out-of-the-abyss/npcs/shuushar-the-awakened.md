## Identity
Shuushar is a kuo-toa who served as a translator for the drow while imprisoned in Velkynvelve [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.02]. He is distinct among his people for his pursuit of "enlightenment" and spiritual balance, a state he attempts to maintain by filtering out "colorful adjectives" from the insults he translates [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.02]. During the campaign, he became a companion to the party, acting as a guide through the Underdark with knowledge of routes toward Darklake and his home near Sloobludop [ch 002 / 002.05; ch 004 / 004.03].

## Personality and Motivations
Shuushar is characterized by a calm, pacifist demeanor and a deep commitment to mindfulness and spiritual clarity. He actively practices meditation and breathing exercises, often imposing them on his companions, such as Buppido [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.06]. His motivation is rooted in achieving enlightenment and understanding the universe, which he initially believed was hindered by the summoning of his people's god, Leemooggoogoon [ch 009 / moment].

He possesses a pragmatic survival instinct, evidenced when he refused to follow the companion Ront into a suicidal attack against a vrock demon, a decision that preserved his life [ch 004 / 004.03; ch 004 / moment]. While generally non-violent, he has fought defensively when necessary, using a hand crossbow to aid in the defeat of the Drow Elite Warrior Imbros and a carrion crawler [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.02; ch 005 / entry; ch 005 / 005.02]. He is visibly distressed by the presence of Faerzress in the Underdark [ch 005 / entry; ch 005 / 005.06]. Following the destruction of Sloobludop, his primary motivation shifted to leading his people toward a new society "awakened from its madness" [ch 009 / moment].

## Last Observed State
Shuushar departed from the party during their journey across the Darklake. After witnessing the eruption of Demogorgon at Sloobludop, Shuushar was struck by sudden madness and had to be carried to their escape boat [ch 009 / 009.01; ch 009 / 009.02]. Upon recovering, he initially insisted that his people had summoned Leemooggoogoon, but was slapped by a fellow escapee who corrected him that the entity was Demogorgon [ch 009 / 009.02; ch 009 / moment].

He traveled with the party across the Darklake, enduring the cramped conditions and environmental hazards [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.03]. When the party encountered a boat of kuo-toa refugees fleeing the destruction of their homes, Shuushar recognized them and realized his calling was to lead them [ch 009 / 009.06; ch 009 / moment]. He left the party to join these refugees, declaring his intent to build a new, enlightened kuo-toa society [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.06]. Before departing, he left the party an imperfect map of the Darklake [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.01].

## Relationships
**The Party:** Shuushar travelled with the party, knew a route toward the Darklake, and fired a hand crossbow in the tower fight and the carrion crawler fight [ch 002 / entry; ch 003 / 003.02; ch 005 / 005.02]. His departure was accepted by the party as he was not their only guide; the GM noted the other guide was "drow" [ch 009 / 009.06].

**Buppido:** Shuushar frequently instructed Buppido in mindfulness practices, which eventually drove Buppido to the brink of insanity [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.06]. Upon departing, Shuushar pointedly reminded Buppido to "remember to always practice your mindfulness" [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / moment].

**Gyrgum:** Shuushar was impressed by Gyrgum’s cooking, stating it penetrated his calm demeanor and might lead his people to imagine a god resembling Gyrgum [ch 002 / 002.03; ch 002 / moment]. In response to Shuushar’s final lecture, Gyrgum simply spat [ch 009 / moment; ch 009 / 009.06].

**Ront:** Shuushar’s relationship with Ront ended in divergence during a fight with a vrock demon; Shuushar refused to follow Ront into a suicidal attack, while Ront was killed [ch 004 / 004.03; ch 004 / moment].

**Stool:** Stool pulled Shuushar (along with Buppido) to safety during a rockfall escape, saving him from potential death [ch 005 / entry; ch 005 / 005.01].

**Sarith:** Sarith warned the party that the map left behind by Shuushar was imperfect [ch 010 / 010.01].
