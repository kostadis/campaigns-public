## Identity
Manshoon is a legendary wizard and the founder and long-time leader of the Zhentarim, whose early power was intertwined with Bane’s clergy [ch 064 / entry; ch 071 / entry]. He is historically associated with the Moonsea and Zhentil Keep and is known to have died more than once only to return [ch 064 / entry; ch 064 / 064.04]. His true self has reportedly been dead for 170 years, meaning the entity interacting with the party was a simulacrum [ch 069 / 069.04]. Manshoon does not meet servants in person, preferring to manifest mystically, sometimes killing people during these appearances [ch 071 / entry; ch 071 / 071.07].

## Personality and Motivations
*   **Methodical and Detached:** Manshoon operated from the shadows for eleven years, using an anonymous courier to direct book thefts from Candlekeep rather than acting personally, which A'lai took to mean he "wasn't worth the face" [ch 067 / 067.01; ch 067 / 067.02]. Edvaldo said Manshoon had considered the party beneath his notice until they ruined his plan [ch 071 / 071.07].
*   **Pragmatic and Polite:** The second simulacrum displayed a habit of politeness developed over six hours of waiting, preferring to negotiate a trade rather than fight, though he threatened violence if necessary [ch 069 / 069.05]. He agreed to relocate a duel to protect library books, valuing their preservation over convenience [ch 068 / 068.05; ch 068 / moment].
*   **Grand Ambition:** Manshoon sought the *Book of Vile Darkness* not for personal power enhancement, but to seize control of the Demon Lords that had recently appeared in the Underdark, intending to use them as an army [ch 071 / entry; ch 071 / 071.07]. He spent a decade preparing for this move, exhausting significant resources like a staff of power and enchanted iron gloves to breach Candlekeep [ch 071 / entry; ch 071 / 071.07].
*   **Permanent enemy:** Edvaldo said the party had acquired Manshoon as a permanent enemy after thwarting his plan [ch 071 / entry; ch 071 / 071.07]. Edvaldo also claimed Manshoon would have honored a deal had the party surrendered the book and stayed out of his way [ch 071 / 071.07].
