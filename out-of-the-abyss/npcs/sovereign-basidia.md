## Identity
Sovereign Basidia is the co-sovereign of Neverlight Grove, sharing authority with Sovereign Phylo [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.05]. She serves as a voice of reason and a stabilizing force within the myconid colony, maintaining traditional communal values while Phylo drives disruptive changes [ch 029 / entry; ch 031 / entry]. Basidia possesses the sovereign ability to identify nearly every spore produced in the Underdark, a skill she uses to diagnose corruption in other myconids [ch 029 / 029.06; ch 029 / moment]. She is a skilled alchemist, capable of brewing potions from the grove’s molds, ranging from common to rare varieties depending on preparation time [ch 029 / 029.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Basidia is deeply suspicious of Phylo’s recent behavior, specifically his restructuring of the grove’s circles, which she believes breaks their communal harmony and is contrary to the myconid way of life [ch 029 / 029.05; ch 029 / 029.06]. She is motivated by a desire to preserve communal harmony and opposes Phylo’s policy of segregating myconids into specialized circles [ch 029 / 029.06; ch 031 / entry]. Although she tells the party "Phylo is not well," she privately fears he has been infected by an unknown spore and acts to counter his influence by vouching for the party and directing their investigation [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.06].

Basidia displays visible discomfort when Phylo acts strangely and intervenes to lead the tour herself, leaving Phylo behind [ch 029 / 029.05]. She is blunt and lacks discretion when investigating corruption, openly prompting allies to question Phylo’s supporters and revealing infections without subtlety [ch 031 / 031.02; ch 031 / entry]. Despite her caution, she possesses a fierce loyalty to her people; upon learning of Phylo’s alliance with a demonic entity, she abandons caution to confront him publicly, preferring to fight for her colony even against hopeless odds [ch 032 / 032.03]. Her decision to flee is driven not by cowardice, but by the realization that the individuals she cares about—such as Stool, Rumpadump, and Rasharoo—are leaving, and staying would mean dying alone [ch 032 / 032.03].

## Last Observed State
Basidia fled Neverlight Grove after the grove’s trees animated to attack her and Zalthir physically restrained her from attacking Phylo [ch 032 / 032.03; ch 032 / moment]. She departed with Stool, Rumpadump, and the surviving myconids to remain among their own kind, leaving the player characters behind [ch 032 / 032.04]. Before departing, she apologized for failing to complete the reanimation of the grick carcass provided by the party [ch 032 / 032.03]. As a final reward for saving the members of her circle who could be spared, she provided the party with potions of greater healing and a box of treasure totaling 975 gold pieces [ch 032 / 032.03]. Status not established in the summaries regarding her specific location or condition after the departure, other than that she left the grove with the survivors.

## Relationships
**Sovereign Phylo**: Basidia’s co-sovereign and former close friend, whom she described as being "such good friends" before his corruption [ch 029 / entry]. She publicly accused him of betrayal and allying with corruption during the grove's final moments, attempting to fight him before being restrained [ch 032 / 032.03]. Phylo called after her as she fled, lamenting her departure and insisting the grove would be "better than ever" [ch 032 / 032.03; ch 032 / moment].

**Stool and Rumpadump**: Basidia acts as a protector and authority figure for these two myconids. She vouched for them and the party throughout the grove, ensuring their safety under her authority [ch 029 / entry; ch 031 / 031.02]. Rumpadump trusts Basidia implicitly, relying on her judgment to validate the party’s actions and having prepared escape routes in case she chose to abandon the grove [ch 031 / 031.02]. Stool and Rumpadump ultimately departed with Basidia rather than staying with the party [ch 032 / 032.04].

**Loobamub**: Leader of the Circle of Hunters and a staunch ally of Basidia. Loobamub shares her unease with Phylo’s new policies and confirmed that "Phylo's new ways are not good" when introduced to the party by Basidia [ch 029 / 029.07].

**Hebopbe**: Leader of the Circle of Growers. Basidia revealed to the party that Hebopbe had already been infected by the strange evil spores, explaining his indifference to the political upheaval [ch 031 / 031.02].

**Rasharoo**: A myconid who advised Basidia to flee rather than confront Phylo. When Basidia refused initially, Rasharoo committed to evacuation by shouting, "If you want to live, come with me," leading the survivors to the escape route [ch 032 / 032.03; ch 032 / moment].

**Zalthir**: A party member who physically grabbed and dragged Basidia away from a suicidal charge against Phylo. Basidia accepted this intervention, which saved her life [ch 032 / 032.03; ch 032 / moment].

**Gasbide**: Leader of the Circle of Builders and a supporter of Phylo. Basidia rolled her eyes while Gasbide questioned the party about surface buildings [ch 030 / 030.05].
