## Identity
Nym Duskryn is a professional drow mercenary and fighter [ch 053 / entry]. He operates in partnership with Kaelira Duskryn, with whom he maintains a professional and possibly familial or deeply linked bond, as they are referred to together as partners or a pair [ch 051 / 051.06; ch 053 / 053.01]. They are established specialists hired by wealthy entities in Menzoberranzan, specifically to protect clients from targeted threats, such as Ilvara of House Mizzrym and agents of House T'sarran [ch 053 / entry; ch 053 / 053.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Nym is characterized as blunt, arrogant, and highly conscious of professional standards [ch 053 / entry]. He views his work strictly through the lens of contractual obligations, refusing to expand his services beyond the specific threats named in his contract, such as dismissing Asha Vandree as a trivial threat outside the scope of his duties [ch 053 / entry; ch 053 / 053.01]. He displays a dry, sardonic wit, particularly when discussing his brother Jorlan, whom he calls "the dumber one" before giving Kaelira permission to strike him [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.06].

He expresses disdain for what he perceives as incompetence in others, such as Thorin’s decision to grapple rather than kill a cleric of Lolth, stating that such enemies should be decapitated [ch 053 / 053.02; ch 053 / moment]. His primary motivation is financial and reputational; he and Kaelira initially attempted to leave upon fulfilling their contract, agreeing to extend their escort to the surface solely to protect the legitimacy of their fee collection, not out of altruism [ch 053 / entry; ch 053 / 053.01]. He is baffled by the extraordinary expense paid for the protection of Daz, a drow of seemingly no noble standing, and Kaelira compares Daz’s speculation about hidden importance to every orphan imagining a secret noble heritage [ch 053 / 053.04].
