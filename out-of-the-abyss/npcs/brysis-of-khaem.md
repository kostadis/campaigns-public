## Identity
- Brysis of Khaem is female [GM]. Fargas described her as a half-elf sorcerer from the height of Faerûn's great empires of magic, interred in a floating tomb [ch 007 / 007.02].
- The ch 028 entry calls her a Netherese noble and sorcerer, and says the tomb was built in her honor. A stone diorama of her in her prime, surrounded by attendants and slaves, stands at the tomb's entrance [ch 028 / entry; ch 028 / 028.04].
- Her tomb plunged into a crevasse after the fall of the Empire of Netheril and its flying cities, and has stayed in the Underdark for centuries [ch 007 / 007.02].
- The tomb is the Lost Tomb of Khaem, on the northeastern edge of the Darklake [ch 007 / 007.02; ch 028 / 028.04].
- In ch 029 she is a cursed undead creature, imprisoned in a stone sarcophagus for a millennium. She is resistant to acid and has AC 13 [ch 029 / entry].
- The ch 028 wraith is not named as Brysis in that summary. Ch 029 links the sarcophagus fight to her by saying Zalthir opened it "so that Brysis could no longer hide inside" [ch 028 / 028.06; ch 029 / 029.01].
- Her treasure is the secret tomb's contents, per Fargas [ch 007 / 007.02]. The party took a thin gold covering from her sarcophagus worth 250 gp and an invisible chest of coin and magic items [ch 029 / 029.02].
- Fargas said the obvious sarcophagus is a decoy and that a secret passage leads to the real tomb [ch 007 / 007.02].

## Personality and Motivations
- **Defiant and vengeful.** The ch 029 entry describes her this way [ch 029 / entry]. She raged that she would not be defeated after a millennium of imprisonment [ch 029 / 029.01; ch 029 / moment].
- **Wants release from her prison and curse.** Her own words are "I have been waiting this millennium to be freed of this curse" [ch 029 / moment]. In ch 028 a wraith cackled that the party's deaths would free her from her prison and that her victims would serve her in death [ch 028 / 028.06]. Ch 028 does not name that wraith.
- **Theatrical and threatening.** The ch 028 wraith cackled with theatrical menace, hid in the sarcophagus when hurt, and screamed about being defeated once before by dwarves and drow [ch 028 / 028.06].
- **Possessive of Dawnbringer.** Her last words to the sword were "not done, Dawnbringer" [ch 029 / entry].
- **Captor, not wielder.** She killed Dynaphyse, took Dawnbringer, and was buried with the sword atop her own corpse [GM]. Dawnbringer's anger at Brysis in ch 072 is consistent with this [ch 072 / 072.02].
- **The telepathic voice.** In ch 028 a faint feminine voice begged to be freed from the dark, and later said "in the sarcophagus, I can help you" [ch 028 / 028.04; ch 028 / 028.06]. The summaries do not say whose voice it was. UNSUPPORTED: that it was Brysis.
- **Netherese morality.** The GM warned that Netherese notions of good and evil may not match the party's [ch 028 / 028.04]. The evidence does not apply this to Brysis herself.

## History with the Party
- Fargas, the halfling cut from a spider cocoon, told the party the history of Brysis of Khaem. She was a half-elf sorcerer from the height of Faerûn's great empires of magic, interred in a floating tomb. [ch 007 / 007.02]
- The tomb plunged into a crevasse after the fall of the Empire of Netheril and its flying cities, and has stayed in the Underdark for centuries. [ch 007 / 007.02]
- Fargas said the obvious sarcophagus is a decoy and that a secret passage from the servant's tomb leads to the real tomb holding Brysis's magic and treasure. [ch 007 / 007.02]
- Fargas did not know the tomb's precise location, only that it lies somewhere in the passages on the northeastern edge of the Darklake. Sarith drew a rough map and estimated three or four possible places. [ch 007 / 007.02]
- Thorin misheard the name as "Brys". Fargas corrected the pronunciation to "Brysis". [ch 007 / 007.02; ch 007 / moment]
- At the end of the ch 028 journey the party reached the Lost Tomb of Khaem. At its entrance is a stone diorama of Brysis in her prime, surrounded by attendants and slaves. [ch 028 / 028.04; ch 028 / entry]
- The summary calls her a Netherese noble and sorcerer in whose honor the tomb was built. [ch 028 / entry]
- A 24 on Arcana confirmed this was Brysis's Netherese tomb and that powerful magic lay within. [ch 028 / 028.04]
- A faint, feminine telepathic voice begged to be freed from the dark. The ch 028 summary does not say whose voice it was. [ch 028 / 028.04]
- In a lower chamber a wraith appeared, cackling that the party's deaths would free her from her prison. She slipped into a gilded sarcophagus to hide. The ch 028 summary does not name her as Brysis. Ch 029 says Zalthir opened the sarcophagus "so that Brysis could no longer hide inside." [ch 028 / 028.06; ch 029 / 029.01]
- In ch 029 the party fought Brysis in magical darkness. Gyrgum's Tasha's Caustic Brew coated her in acid after she failed the DC 15 Dexterity save. Daz's three Scorching Ray attacks did 23 damage in total. [ch 029 / 029.01]
- Brysis raged that she would not be defeated after a millennium of imprisonment. [ch 029 / 029.01; ch 029 / moment]
- Brysis was destroyed in ch 029 by the lingering acid, which finished her at three hit points. [ch 029 / 029.01; ch 029 / entry]
- The ch 029 entry describes her as a cursed undead creature imprisoned in a stone sarcophagus for a millennium. [ch 029 / entry]
- Mid-fight, Thorin took the sentient sword Dawnbringer from the sarcophagus instead of attacking. Brysis's last words to the sword were "not done, Dawnbringer." [ch 029 / 029.01; ch 029 / entry]
- After Brysis fell, the party searched the tomb. They took a thin gold covering from her sarcophagus worth 250 gp and opened an invisible chest holding coin, zircons, a necklace of fireballs, a philter of love and a potion of greater healing. [ch 029 / 029.02]
- Status: Brysis was destroyed in ch 029. In ch 072 she is spoken of only as a past figure. [ch 029 / 029.01; ch 029 / entry; ch 072 / 072.02]
- In ch 072 Dawnbringer, asked by Philemon to name her true wielder, erupted in anger and cursed Brysis, on whose body the party had found her. [ch 072 / 072.02]
- Thorin had believed Brysis was Dawnbringer's former wielder. He realized she was Dawnbringer's captor. [ch 072 / 072.02; ch 072 / entry]
- Philemon connected Dynaphyse to a line in the Netherese temple scrolls about a Lathanderite woman of the Dawn, struck from the record and "lost with Khaem". He said he had never known what Khaem had taken. [ch 072 / 072.02]
- The GM's edit sets Brysis as female. [GM]
