## Identity
Spiderbait is a small goblin who acts as a guide and companion to the party [ch 006 / 006.05; ch 054 / entry]. He is skilled in navigating the Silken Paths and other hazardous Underdark terrains, having demonstrated an ability to traverse webs with speed and style [ch 006 / 006.05; ch 006 / 006.06]. Prior to the events of Chapter 54, he had never been to the surface world [ch 054 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Spiderbait is primarily motivated by self-preservation and a desire to avoid death or traumatic encounters [ch 008 / 008.03; ch 010 / 010.04]. He expresses fear openly during life-threatening situations, stating, "I don't want to die" [ch 008 / moment], and has previously sought to leave the party after suffering personal loss and trauma [ch 010 / 010.04].

He displays a casual, colloquial demeanor, often using slang and informal speech [ch 006 / 006.05; ch 032 / 032.06]. When Jimjar tried to deflect with a cryptic bet after the cave-in, he told him "That’s weak, man. That’s like really weak." [ch 032 / 032.06]. He also demonstrates a keen, if sometimes alarmed, awareness of threats, such as identifying a spectator as being "damn scary" despite its resemblance to a beholder [ch 007 / moment].

Upon reaching the surface, Spiderbait displayed an initial, naive optimism regarding social integration, believing goblins were "totally integrated into the society" [ch 054 / 054.01]. This indicates a lack of awareness of surface-world prejudices, which is quickly corrected by the party [ch 054 / 054.01].

## History with the Party
*   Introduced himself as a goblin guide in the Silken Paths, offering services to the party for two gold pieces per day [ch 006 / 006.05].
*   Participated in a wager with Jimjar, where he and Yuk Yuk demonstrated their ability to traverse the webs, winning the bet and securing a deal to guide the party in exchange for a share of future gold [ch 006 / 006.05].
*   Acted as a guide for the party across the Silken Paths, providing advice on navigation and warning that the webs could not be burned down due to the spiders' rapid rebuilding [ch 006 / 006.06].
*   Mocked the party’s frantic escape from giant spiders during the first wave of the crossing, describing the party’s maneuvers as less stylish than their own [ch 007 / 007.01].
*   Demanded his share of the gold from Jimjar at the end of the Silken Paths, but agreed to continue traveling with the party under the promise of future payment [ch 007 / 007.05].
*   Accompanied the party through the Underdark, including an encounter where he and Yuk Yuk warned the group about a spectator that looked like a beholder [ch 007 / moment].
*   Was among the NPCs escaping with the party during a Kuo-toa attack, expressing his fear of dying [ch 008 / 008.03].
*   Was grappled and nearly kidnapped by duergar slavers on the Darklake; the party intervened, killing or capturing the duergar to rescue him [ch 010 / 010.03].
*   Expressed a strong desire to leave the party after the rescue, citing the death of his best friend and the trauma of being attacked by a demon lord [ch 010 / 010.04].
*   Errde Blackskull agreed to arm him with medium armor, short swords, shortbows, and shields during negotiations in Gracklstugh [ch 018 / 018.03].
*   Traveled with the party into the Whorlstone Caverns to confront duergar assassins and locate a dragon egg [ch 019 / 019.01].
*   Was nearly fed to zombies by a derro cultist named Skiit, who requested permission from Gyrgum to feed Spiderbait to them [ch 022 / 022.02].
*   Dismissed one of Jimjar’s bets during a cave-in as "weak" and "really weak," prompting Jimjar to admit his attempt to distract the party was a failure [ch 032 / 032.06].
*   Climbed the staircase to Blingdenstone with Jimjar, finding the stairs comfortable for his size, while larger party members struggled [ch 035 / 035.02].
*   Joined Eldeth and Jimjar in taking on the ghost problem [ch 040 / 040.01].
*   Spiderbait is a small goblin who emerged from the Underdark with the party and had never been on the surface before [ch 054 / entry].
*   Upon reaching the surface, Spiderbait initially believed goblins were fully integrated into surface society and expressed excitement about exploring freely [ch 054 / 054.01].
*   The party, specifically Gyrgum, informed Spiderbait that goblin-surface relations are currently poor and that he might face hostility for being a goblin [ch 054 / 054.01].
*   Following this correction, Spiderbait asked to travel with the party to Candlekeep to find safe, innocuous work, such as cooking [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / 054.01].

## Last Observed State
Spiderbait is currently accompanying the party on the surface, having recently emerged from the Underdark with them [ch 054 / entry]. He had initially intended to explore the surface freely, believing he would face no hostility [ch 054 / 054.01]. However, after being informed by Gyrgum that goblin-surface relations are "rough at the moment," Spiderbait revised his plans [ch 054 / 054.01].

He requested to travel with the party to Candlekeep to find "safe, innocuous work," specifically expressing interest in cooking [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / 054.01]. Gyrgum endorsed this plan, though it is noted by the GM that Spiderbait is actually terrible at making food [ch 054 / 054.01].

## Relationships
*   **Jimjar:** Spiderbait has a working relationship with Jimjar, engaging in wagers and joint actions, such as traversing webs to win a bet or navigating terrain [ch 006 / 006.05; ch 032 / 032.06; ch 035 / 035.02]. Spiderbait is willing to dismiss Jimjar's actions as "weak" when they fail [ch 032 / 032.06].
*   **Yuk Yuk:** Yuk Yuk, the other goblin guide, had been murdered by this point [ch 010 / entry]; Spiderbait cites the death of his best friend as a reason to leave the party [ch 010 / 010.04].
*   **The Party:** Spiderbait is a guide and companion to the group, though his tenure has been transactional and conditional on payment and safety [ch 006 / 006.05; ch 007 / 007.05]. He receives equipment from Errde Blackskull during negotiations in Gracklstugh [ch 018 / 018.03]. He is currently traveling with the party to Candlekeep [ch 054 / 054.01].
*   **Gyrgum:** Gyrgum acts as an informational source regarding social realities, correcting Spiderbait’s assumptions about surface-world acceptance of goblins [ch 054 / 054.01].
*   **Skiit:** A derro cultist who attempted to feed Spiderbait to zombies [ch 022 / 022.02].
*   **Duergar Slavers:** Grappled Spiderbait on the Darklake, leading to a rescue by the party [ch 010 / 010.03].
