## Identity
Nomi Pathshutter is a deep gnome wizard and an aide-de-camp working with Gurnik Tapfinger [ch 039 / entry; ch 043 / entry]. She served in the leadership circle of Blingdenstone, participating in the moot convened to finalize plans against the Pudding King [ch 039 / 039.07]. Her primary role involves the arcane preparation for the battle, specifically attempting to summon Earth Elementals by locating Entémoch’s Boon [ch 039 / 039.08; ch 043 / 043.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Nomi is characterized by high intelligence paired with sharp-tongued, abrasive, and prejudiced behavior. She initially mistook Gyrgum for an ogre, insulting his intellect, and openly glared at Daz for being a drow, reflecting what the evidence describes as "obvious racism" [ch 043 / 043.04; ch 043 / moment]. She is sharp-tongued ("Clean your ears") and, once impressed, asked, "Why didn't anybody tell me this beforehand?" after learning the party had already dealt with insane elementals [ch 043 / 043.04].

Her motivations are centered on the defense of Blingdenstone and the successful execution of magical strategies. She is dedicated to her work, having stayed awake for 48 hours attempting to open the path to Entémoch’s Boon [ch 043 / entry]. She is pragmatic about alliances, affirming that the party’s companion Glabbagool was "welcome anywhere" when Thorin asked whether they could bring him [ch 043 / 043.04]. She became impressed when learning the party had dealt with four insane earth elementals and a Medusa, shifting from antagonism to reliance on their expertise [ch 043 / entry].

## Last Observed State
As of Chapter 045, Nomi Pathshutter stated her intent to prepare spells to summon an earth elemental to assist in the fight against the Pudding King [ch 045 / entry]. She had previously reported that hallowing Ogrémoch’s altar failed to open the path to Entémoch’s boon, noting that the earth was "frozen" and the tunnels to the stone prince’s throne had "shifted and sealed themselves" [ch 043 / 043.04]. At that time, she directed the party to investigate strange vibrations in the tunnels reported by miners in Pickshine and Whiteshell, as the rock seemed to be "trying to speak" [ch 043 / 043.04]. Status not established in the summaries beyond her preparation of spells.

## Relationships
**Gurnik Tapfinger:** Nomi works closely with Gurnik, delivering "transparently rehearsed" exchanges during political gatherings to justify strategic plans, such as explaining the necessity of cleansing the Steadfast Stone before summoning elementals [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.08].

**The Party (Daz, Gyrgum, Thorin):** Nomi’s relationship with the party began with hostility. She insulted Gyrgum as an ogre and glared at Daz for being a drow, causing a tense moment that required Thorin to demand respect [ch 043 / 043.04]. She later apologized ("Okay, this is not going to work") and shifted to a cooperative stance after the party demonstrated their power by dealing with insane elementals [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.04].

**Glabbagool:** Nomi explicitly welcomed Glabbagool to their meetings, affirming he was "welcome anywhere" [ch 043 / 043.04].
