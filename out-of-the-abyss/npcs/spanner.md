## Identity
- Spanner is a gnome librarian in Candlekeep who oversees the House of Mechanus, a library of construction, engineering and technology. He directs thirteen modrons in ordered routines [ch 067 / 067.05; ch 067 / entry].
- The chapter 072 entry calls him the administrator of the House of Mechanus and supervisor of its Modron librarians [ch 072 / entry]. The building is a large rectangular structure with a blue slate roof and a clock tower [ch 067 / 067.05].
- He appears in chapters 067 and 072, and was mentioned in chapter 068 [ch 067 / entry; ch 072 / entry; ch 068 / 068.02].

## Personality and Motivations
- **Methodical and fond of long explanations.** The chapter 067 entry describes him this way. Gyrgum's "asking politely" interruption left him momentarily stunned before he resumed his structured explanation [ch 067 / entry; ch 067 / 067.05].
- **Protective of his modrons.** He called killing one of them to obtain Dust of Mechanus absolutely unacceptable, and offered tool loans as the alternative [ch 067 / 067.05].
- **Cautious, but open to persuasion.** He first asked for collateral on the tools, then relented when the party argued they only needed to borrow them [ch 067 / 067.05].
- **Driven by research interest.** His price for the loan was a study of Zalthir's and Glabbagool's mechanical symmetry [ch 067 / 067.05; ch 067 / entry].
- **Anxious about his place in events.** He was deeply distraught when the Avowed told him his "tools" were not the "dust" in Alaundo's riddle. He believed this excluded him from the foretold events [ch 072 / 072.04; ch 072 / entry].
- **Emotionally swift and ceremonial.** Reassurance moved him from despair to delight at once, and he treated the returned tool as a blessed artifact [ch 072 / 072.04].
- **Impatient with his own procedure.** After about three steps of the 169-step survey he proposed doing the remaining 166 by correspondence. He promised the extra baseline would take thirty seconds [ch 072 / 072.04].
- **Observant and exact.** He noted "The subject does not announce the move" [ch 072 / moment]. He caught the film from the Modron's faceplate on a clipboard and identified it at once as Dust of Mechanus [ch 072 / 072.04].

## History with the Party
- Spanner is a gnome librarian who oversees the House of Mechanus in Candlekeep and directs thirteen modrons in ordered routines. [ch 067 / 067.05; ch 067 / entry]
- The party needed Dust of Mechanus for the dormant gears in the decoded instructions. They learned Candlekeep had its own House of Mechanus and went there. [ch 067 / 067.05]
- Spanner confirmed that Dust of Mechanus is "the most perfect of all lubricants." [ch 067 / 067.05]
- Gyrgum interrupted his explanation by guessing the method was "asking politely." Spanner was momentarily stunned and then continued. [ch 067 / 067.05; ch 067 / entry]
- Spanner said the only direct way to get the dust was to kill one of his modrons, which he called absolutely unacceptable. [ch 067 / 067.05]
- Thorin objected to killing any modron: "we don't kill librarians. They're our friends." [ch 067 / 067.05; ch 067 / moment]
- Spanner offered to lend tools made by the modrons. He first asked for collateral, then agreed after Thorin pointed out that the party only needed to borrow them. [ch 067 / 067.05]
- Spanner's condition for the loan was that Zalthir and Glabbagool submit to a study of their mechanical symmetry. Zalthir consulted Glabbagool and accepted, provided Spanner gave "constructive feedback." [ch 067 / 067.05; ch 067 / moment]
- Spanner deferred the interview until after the expedition. He then spoke to the modrons in a mechanistic language, and they responded with a synchronized dance. [ch 067 / 067.05]
- In the lava chamber, the party used "the tools borrowed from Spanner" to extend the bridge toward the tower. Spanner himself was only mentioned in this scene. [ch 068 / 068.02]
- Spanner was found "deeply distraught" when Zalthir and Glabbagool visited the House of Mechanus. The Avowed had told him his "tools" were not the "dust" in Alaundo's riddle, so he believed he had not taken part in the foretold events. [ch 072 / 072.04; ch 072 / entry]
- Zalthir reassured him that the tools worked and that his insights had made the Zalthir–Glabbagool partnership possible. Spanner went from despair to delight, and thirteen Modrons celebrated in sync. [ch 072 / 072.04; ch 072 / moment]
- The Modrons reverently disassembled the returned tool piece by piece as a blessed artifact. [ch 072 / 072.04]
- Spanner ran a 169-step survey with thirteen observers. After about three steps he proposed doing the remaining 166 by correspondence and handed Zalthir a form. [ch 072 / 072.04]
- When Zalthir shadow-stepped unannounced, Spanner noted: "The subject does not announce the move." [ch 072 / 072.04; ch 072 / moment]
- The Modrons found that "subject B" (Glabbagool) had no axis of symmetry. After Glabbagool compressed himself into a square, all thirteen declared "Symmetrical within Tolerance." [ch 072 / 072.04]
- During Glabbagool's restrained grapple of a volunteer Modron, a film lifted from its faceplate. Spanner stopped the survey, caught it on a clipboard, and identified it as the first Dust of Mechanus taken without a Modron dying. [ch 072 / 072.04; ch 072 / moment]
- Spanner gave the party one pinch of Dust of Mechanus. [ch 072 / 072.04]
- Spanner asked the rest of the party to join an asymmetric baseline of about thirty seconds. Zalthir objected that everyone was busy ("One of them's in therapy"), and Gyrgum agreed after hearing he would get author credit. [ch 072 / 072.04]
