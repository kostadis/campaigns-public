## Identity
Prince Derendil was a Quaggoth prisoner held in Velkynvelve who claimed to be an elven prince transformed by a curse [ch 002 / entry; ch 003 / entry]. Stool the Myconid distinguished Derendil from other Quaggoths, describing him as "very civilized" in contrast to the brutish nature of his species [ch 018 / 018.06]. The first of Alaundo's recovered prophecies referenced a "prince who is not a prince," whom the party recognized as Derendil [ch 071 / 071.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Derendil maintained a strong sense of dignity and social station, demanding treatment appropriate to his claimed title even while performing menial labor such as hauling water barrels [ch 002 / 002.03; ch 002 / moment]. He possessed physical strength but lacked stealth capabilities, openly admitting this limitation while offering his muscles for the prison breakout [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.01]. He was willing to accept physical harm to protect the group’s interests, remaining in the lower guard room to hold enemies in place while others armed themselves, despite the risk [ch 003 / 003.02; ch 003 / 003.03].

## History with the Party
- Prince Derendil was a Quaggoth prisoner in Velkynvelve who claimed to be a cursed elven prince [ch 002 / entry].
- He was assigned to labor alongside Thorin and Ront, hauling water barrels from the waterfall to the upper levels of the outpost [ch 002 / 002.01; ch 002 / 002.03].
- While delivering water to Mistress Ilvara, Derendil demanded dignified treatment, prompting the high priestess to draw a knife and say “if he wants to be dealt with, he can be dealt with”; Derendil backed down [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.03; ch 002 / moment].
- During the breakout from Velkynvelve, Derendil offered his physical strength to the party but admitted he was not suited for stealth [ch 003 / entry].
- He remained in the lower guard room to fight the drow captors while other prisoners escaped to the armory above, taking a short-sword wound to hold the enemy in place [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.02].
- The Drow Elite Warrior Imbros focused his attacks on Derendil, killing him with his poisoned blade during the skirmish [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.03; ch 003 / moment].
- In Chapter 018, Stool the Myconid referenced Derendil, contrasting the "very civilized" prince with brutish and savage quaggoths who are "bred to be violent" [ch 018 / 018.06].
- In Chapter 071, the first prophecy referenced a "prince who is not a prince," whom the party recognized as Derendil, the Quaggoth who died during their escape [ch 071 / 071.05].

## Last Observed State
Prince Derendil was killed by Imbros, the Drow Elite Warrior, during the skirmish in the guard tower of Velkynvelve [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / moment]. Imbros focused his attacks on Derendil, using a poisoned blade to inflict lethal wounds while Derendil attempted to hold the doorway [ch 003 / 003.03]. The death was confirmed by the Dungeon Master immediately following the event [ch 003 / moment]. A later reference in Chapter 071 noted his death during the escape [ch 071 / 071.05].
