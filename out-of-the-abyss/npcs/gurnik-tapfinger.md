## Identity
Gurnik Tapfinger is the head priest of Callarduran Smoothhands in the Stoneheart Enclave. [ch 039 / entry] He acts as a representative of Blingdenstone's leadership, participating in high-level strategic planning alongside other clan leaders. [ch 039 / 039.07] He was responsible for providing the party with a red spell gem containing the *Hallow* spell to cleanse the Temple of the Steadfast Stone. [ch 039 / 039.08; ch 042 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Gurnik has a strong desire to see the Temple of the Steadfast Stone and the surrounding Rockblight cleansed. [ch 039 / entry] His motivation appears partly tied to utilizing Earth Elementals in the upcoming war against the Pudding King, as he argues that elementals would be driven mad unless the Steadfast Stone is cleansed to allow the Stoneheart Enclave to protect them. [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.08]

During the moot, he and Nomi Pathshutter delivered an obviously prepared presentation advocating for their strategic preferences. [ch 039 / entry] When assigning the hallowing quest, Gurnik initially described the task simply as placing the gem in the temple's menhir, omitting the dangerous details about defending it from Ogrémoch's Bane's servants until Daz used Insight to confirm the omission. [ch 039 / 039.08] He assured the party that "nothing bad would happen." [ch 042 / entry]

Gurnik shows visible disappointment and annoyance when his agenda is thwarted. He became visibly disappointed when the party exposed the leaders' ulterior motives and proposed attacking from the north, bypassing his cleansing mission. [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.08] However, upon the party's successful completion of the quest, he was very happy to see them return and provided a significant reward. [ch 043 / entry]
