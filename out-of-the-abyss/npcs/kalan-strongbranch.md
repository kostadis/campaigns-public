## Identity
Kalan Strongbranch serves as the Gatewarden of Candlekeep and Head of the Avowed. He is an academic investigator who employs the "Threefold Proof" methodology, asserting that magical evidence is adversarial and requires corroboration via multiple independent facts to be meaningful. His investigative focus includes forensic analysis, the management of Candlekeep’s security protocols (including the anti-magic cells and vault defenses), and the maintenance of institutional order during crises.

## Personality and Motivations
*   **Methodical and Skeptical:** Kalan insists on rigorous proof, stating, "The confession is one spell, the weakest channel by the standard I wrote." He declined to ambush Bookwyrm with a Zone of Truth, though he had done so to Alkrist, preferring open confrontation or political pressure, and believes, "Sometimes the clever play is to be patient." [ch 062 / moment; ch 062 / 062.02]
*   **Institutional Pride:** He is deeply committed to Candlekeep’s protocols, initially dismissing outsiders ("You don't matter. You're outsiders") before accepting their utility when they align with his methods. He values the integrity of the library, as evidenced by his concern for the proper sealing of the vault. [ch 059 / 059.03; ch 071 / 071.07]
*   **Adaptable under Pressure:** Though initially arrogant and quick to condemn, Kalan concedes error when presented with superior reasoning, admitting, "Perhaps I was a little bit too hasty." He is willing to deputize outsiders and share resources when institutional security fails. [ch 059 / 059.03; ch 063 / 063.03]

## Last Observed State
During the aftermath of the vault breach in Chapter 071, Kalan arrived with high suspicion of all present identities following the exposure of a doppelganger. He demanded verifiable proof of identity from the party and other scholars, suggesting consultation with the truth-bound dragon on the bridge. He warned Daz that non-evil creatures touching the *Book of Vile Darkness* would suffer catastrophic necrotic damage, and he approved when Daz used a *mage hand* to close the tome without physical contact, saying, "Well done. Well done, Daz." [ch 071 / 071.04; ch 071 / 071.06; ch 071 / entry]

Kalan promised that the Avowed would properly reseal the vault, eliminating its exploitable entrances and escape routes. He also cautioned the party regarding the prisoner Edvaldo, noting that Candlekeep’s prison is not intended for indefinite confinement and that Edvaldo might eventually be released if the scholars tire of holding him. Fheminor Scrivenbark noted that Kalan had previously refused to allow the *Book of Vile Darkness* to leave Candlekeep, though she permitted the *Tome of the Stilled Tongue* to be given to the party. [ch 071 / 071.07; ch 071 / 071.08]
