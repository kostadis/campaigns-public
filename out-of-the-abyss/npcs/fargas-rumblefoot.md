## Identity
Fargas Rumblefoot is a halfling adventurer identified as the last surviving member of his own adventuring group [ch 028 / entry; ch 028 / 028.04]. The ch 007 entry describes him as a halfling adventurer who "made a bunch of poor life choices" [ch 007 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Fargas is characterized by desperation and a transactional approach to survival. When discovered cocooned as a spider snack, he bargained for his release by leveraging knowledge of a lost tomb and its treasure [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.07; ch 006 / moment]. Gyrgum assessed Fargas as "desperate rather than boastful," though the party remained cautious due to his earlier inflated claims [ch 007 / entry]. He displayed defensiveness regarding his physical condition, insisting he was not infected by gnoll spawn and reacting with offense when Daz examined him for such infection [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.07; ch 007 / entry]. As the sole survivor of his group, his reliability as a witness is subject to doubt, with Gyrgum quipping, "Real adventure is all of our companions who died along the way." [ch 028 / entry; ch 028 / 028.04; ch 028 / moment].

## History with the Party
- Fargas Rumblefoot was found cocooned in the Silken Paths, stored as a snack by giant spiders, and was freed by Thorin [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.07; ch 006 / moment].
- He claimed his adventuring band had been seeking a long-lost tomb when they were attacked by mad gnolls, and he bargained for his rescue by offering to lead the party to the tomb's treasure [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.07; ch 006 / moment].
- He described gnolls as demon-possessed creatures that implant spawn in living bodies and insisted he was not infected [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.07].
- Thorin cut through the cocoon with a short sword, successfully freeing Fargas just as spiders arrived [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.07; ch 006 / moment].
- Fargas provided information about the Lost Tomb of Khaem and its hidden treasure vault, claiming his team found evidence of a secret passage from the servant's tomb [ch 007 / entry].
- He admitted he did not know the tomb's precise location, only that it lay on the northeastern edge of the Darklake [ch 007 / entry].
- Daz examined Fargas and confirmed he was free of gnoll spawn, a process Fargas found offensive [ch 007 / entry].
- The party referenced Fargas Rumblefoot when debating whether to seek the Lost Tomb of Khaem versus returning to the Neverlight Grove [ch 027 / 027.05; ch 027 / 027.06].
- Fargas is described as the last survivor of his own adventuring group [ch 028 / entry].
- He provided the crucial tip that the Lost Tomb of Khaem contains a secret or false tomb, information that "other adventurers who perished here apparently lacked" [ch 028 / entry; ch 028 / 028.04].
- He warned the party that the southern chamber of the tomb was a decoy [ch 029 / 029.03; ch 029 / moment].

## Relationships
**The Party**: Fargas was freed by Thorin, who cut through his cocoon just as giant spiders approached [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.07; ch 006 / moment]. Gyrgum questioned Fargas's morality based on his name and assessed his emotional state during the rescue [ch 006 / moment; ch 007 / entry]. Daz examined Fargas for gnoll spawn, a process Fargas found offensive and told Daz to stop [ch 007 / entry]. Thorin expressed skepticism regarding Fargas's claim of being infection-free, comparing it to zombie apocalypse rhetoric [ch 006 / 006.07; ch 006 / moment].

**Former Adventuring Band**: Fargas claims to be the last survivor of his group [ch 028 / entry; ch 028 / 028.04]. He provided the party with specific knowledge that "other adventurers who perished here apparently lacked", such as the existence of a false tomb within the Lost Tomb of Khaem [ch 007 / entry; ch 028 / entry; ch 028 / 028.04]. Gyrgum implies Fargas's survival may cast doubt on the circumstances of his companions' deaths [ch 028 / 028.04; ch 028 / moment].
