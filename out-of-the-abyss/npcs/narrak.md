## Identity
Narrak is a derro cultist and a member of the Council of Savants [ch 023 / 023.01; ch 024 / entry]. He serves as the lead spellcaster for a crew conducting dark experiments in a brimstone cavern, specifically working to graft second heads onto stone giant statues to destroy Gracklstugh [ch 023 / 023.01; ch 023 / 023.03]. He is described as having a notably squeaky voice and approximately 70 hit points [ch 023 / entry]. His actions are justified by his declaration that his experiments serve the elevation of the derro, the triumph of Demogorgon, and the destruction of the duergar [ch 022 / 022.06; ch 022 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Narrak is arrogant, dramatic, and zealous, frequently losing patience with his followers and berating them for their lack of "vigor" during rituals [ch 023 / 023.03; ch 023 / moment]. He maintains a peculiar self-image as a prim, profanity-averse individual; he explicitly refuses to curse, claiming that invoking demonic powers does not obligate him to swear, preferring exclamations like "by golly" and "Gosh darn it" [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / moment]. He delivers threats and ritual chants in rhyming couplets [ch 023 / entry]. His motivations are rooted in the worship of Demogorgon and the desire to cause mass casualty events in Gracklstugh through the corruption of stone giants [ch 022 / 022.06; ch 023 / 023.03]. He is also fiercely protective of his knowledge, ordering followers to stand back from the trillimac cap books because they cannot read them [ch 022 / 022.06; ch 022 / moment].

## History with the Party
- The party first observed Narrak from the shadows in a brimstone cavern, where he declared his experiments served the elevation of the derro, the triumph of Demogorgon, and the destruction of the duergar [ch 022 / 022.06; ch 022 / entry].
- The party identified the trillimac cap books Narrak was reading as physical evidence of a demonic conspiracy within the Council of Savants, which Errde Blackskull of the Stone Guard sought [ch 022 / 022.06].
- Upon the party's return to the ritual cavern in Chapter 023, they found Narrak had successfully corrupted one statue and was preparing the third, intending to destroy Gracklstugh [ch 023 / 023.03].
- Narrak, a member of the Council of Savants, led a ritual to graft second heads onto stone giant statues [ch 023 / 023.01; ch 024 / entry].
- The party launched a surprise attack on the ritual site; Gyrgum rolled a 20 on stealth, allowing the party to bypass Narrak's passive perception of 7 [ch 023 / 023.04].
- Narrak countered the ambush by casting Fear, forcing Thorin, Gyrgum, and Zalthir to flee, though Daz resisted the effect [ch 023 / 023.04].
- Zalthir repositioned his sphere of magical darkness to block Narrak’s line of sight, allowing the party to shake off the Fear effect [ch 023 / 023.04].
- Daz cast *Earthen Grasp* (described as a hand of compacted stone) to restrain Narrak, breaking his concentration and ending the Fear spell [ch 023 / 023.04; ch 024 / entry].
- During the subsequent combat, Narrak cast *Silence* to create a zone of dead air that blocked the party's spellcasting [ch 024 / 024.02].
- Narrak remained restrained by Daz’s magic and Zalthir’s eldritch tendrils, unable to break free despite his efforts [ch 024 / 024.02; ch 024 / 024.03].
- Thorin delivered the killing blow to Narrak, ending his life and his spell effects [ch 024 / 024.03; ch 024 / entry].
- In his final moments, Narrak claimed the ritual could not be undone and that someone had been "sacrificed" [ch 024 / 024.03; ch 024 / moment].
- Gyrgum retrieved a key from around Narrak's neck, which Thorin used to open an iron chest containing gold, silver, and Keoghtom's Ointment [ch 024 / 024.04].

## Last Observed State
Narrak was slain by Thorin during combat in Chapter 024 [ch 024 / 024.03; ch 024 / entry]. Prior to his death, he was restrained by Daz’s *Earthen Grasp* and Zalthir’s eldritch tendrils, unable to break free despite attempting to do so while straining against the magic [ch 024 / 024.02; ch 024 / moment]. In his final moments, he gasped that the ritual could not be undone and that someone had been "sacrificed," though the specific name was not heard clearly by the party [ch 024 / 024.03; ch 024 / moment]. Following his death, Gyrgum retrieved a key from around Narrak's neck, which Thorin used to open an iron chest containing gold, silver, and Keoghtom's Ointment [ch 024 / 024.04].
