## Identity
The Flying Iron Owlbear is a tiny, winged iron statue that functions as a sentinel and guardian within the first chamber of the Obsidian Tower [ch 068 / entry; ch 069 / entry]. Upon activation, it exhibits mechanical behavior: its eyes glow white, its wings unfold, and it hovers approximately twelve feet above the floor [ch 068 / entry]. It serves as a checkpoint that demands specific knowledge, including the naming of the Hearth (the divine triumvirate of Oghma, Deneir, and Milil) and the height of the High Tower of Exaltation, before permitting passage [ch 068 / entry]. A previous, similar iron owlbear was destroyed during Manshoon’s earlier entry into the tower, after which the current Flying Iron Owlbear assumed its post [ch 068 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
The Owlbear’s primary motivation is to enforce the security protocols of the Obsidian Tower by testing petitioners’ knowledge [ch 068 / entry]. Regarding failure, the sentinel claims to have spent a significant amount of time imagining how it would tear failed petitioners to pieces, though its actual enforcement of this threat is not detailed in the evidence [ch 068 / entry]. Beyond its duty, the Owlbear displays a sense of judgment and humor, offering commentary on the quality of interactions it witnesses [ch 069 / entry]. It remarked that the Manshoon duplicate the party had just destroyed "didn't even stop to say hello" [ch 069 / entry; ch 069 / moment].
