## Identity
Gartokkar Xundorn is a high-ranking member of the Keepers of the Flame in Gracklstugh [ch 026 / entry]. He presents himself as a representative of this order, claiming they tend the red dragon Themberchaud to keep the city’s forges burning and are treated as the Deepking’s most prized advisors [ch 013 / 013.04]. He possesses a badge used to disperse onlookers and exert legal influence [ch 013 / 013.04]. He revealed to the party the Keepers' secret hold over Themberchaud via psionic influence and their plan to groom a replacement using a stolen red dragon egg [ch 014 / 014.07]. He views his position and reputation in the kingdom as directly dependent on securing this dragon egg [ch 026 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Gartokkar is pragmatic, condescending, and deeply invested in securing a dragon egg to maintain control over the city's power source [ch 026 / entry]. He threatened the party with invented charges if they refused his task, and delivered a cold, furious threat when payment changed hands [ch 014 / 014.07; ch 017 / 017.05]. He displays a capacity for "depraved mad insanity" when cornered, though he quickly regains composure [ch 017 / 017.05]. He is dismissive of those he deems intellectually inferior or foolish, particularly those who play coy or fail to communicate clearly [ch 026 / 026.06; ch 026 / moment].

His primary motivation is securing the red dragon egg to replace Themberchaud before the dragon realizes he is not truly in control of Gracklstugh; he believes such a realization would lead to catastrophic violence from the beast [ch 026 / 026.06]. He prioritizes secrecy above all, negotiating substantial fees for confidentiality and reminding the party that their arrangement is to remain between them alone [ch 017 / 017.05; ch 026 / 026.06]. He is financially shrewd, negotiating terms of 30% upfront and 70% on delivery, while adding penalties for potential leaks regarding the egg's survival [ch 017 / 017.05]. He is observant of city rumors and political shifts, using information about the Deepking’s agents and the Stone Guard to contextualize the party's survival [ch 026 / 026.06].

## History with the Party
- Gartokkar Xundorn first approached the party in Gracklstugh after making himself invisible, revealing himself as a representative of the Keepers of the Flame and flashing a badge to disperse onlookers [ch 013 / 013.04].
- He explained that his order tends the red dragon Themberchaud to keep the city's forges burning, and claimed the Deepking treats them as his most prized advisors [ch 013 / 013.04].
- He invited the party to his quarters the following day to discuss a task, offering city passes and limited legal protection in exchange for their help [ch 013 / 013.04].
- When the party met Themberchaud, the dragon summoned Gartokkar to act as their liaison and declared them his personal agents [ch 014 / 014.06].
- Gartokkar revealed to the party that the Keepers secretly control Themberchaud through psionic influence and are grooming a replacement using a stolen red dragon egg, which was taken by the Gray Ghosts [ch 014 / 014.07].
- He insisted that the old dragon would not be killed but "set free," and attributed a recent psionic disturbance in the Underdark to the Gray Ghosts [ch 014 / 014.07].
- He tasked the party with tracking the derro courier Droki and recovering the magic that caused the disturbance, threatening to turn them over to the Stone Guards on fabricated charges if they refused [ch 014 / 014.07].
- He believed the Gray Ghosts were behind the psionic disturbance, though the party's checks failed to find further details [ch 014 / 014.07].
- After the party located the egg but did not retrieve it, Gartokkar negotiated payment, initially offering 2,000 gold pieces but rejecting Gyrgum's eager acceptance of it as a full advance [ch 017 / 017.05].
- He insisted on terms of 30% up front and 70% on delivery, eventually conceding an extra 1,000 gold pieces due to the risk of a leak regarding the egg's survival [ch 017 / 017.05].
- He agreed to keep the egg's existence secret from Themberchaud after a moment of "depraved mad insanity," muttering that what the dragon doesn't know doesn't hurt him [ch 017 / 017.05].
- Upon handing over the 1,100 gold pieces upfront and badges for companions, he delivered a fierce threat enumerating every geological formation in the Underdark where the party might hide if they failed to deliver the egg [ch 017 / 017.05].
- The Keepers of the Flame formally threatened permanent hostility if betrayed, with Gartokkar's leadership noted in these deliberations [ch 018 / entry].
- Gartokkar is described as frantic about the missing egg because his power and position depend on managing Themberchaud, and losing the dragon could lead the Deepking to deem the Keepers expendable [ch 018 / 018.02].
- The party planned to return the egg to Gartokkar to secure safe passage and trade rights, deciding to withhold incriminating evidence about the Deepking to avoid chaos [ch 021 / 021.02].
- The party intended to tell Gartokkar they fought cultists, recovered the egg, and found "no evidence" of who was behind the theft [ch 021 / 021.03].
- As of Chapter 025, the original plan remains to hand the egg to Gartokkar, head of the Keepers of the Flame, to resolve their contract, though the party debated holding onto evidence for future leverage [ch 025 / 025.01].

## Relationships
**The Party (Ember Vanguard):** Gartokkar hired the party to retrieve the dragon egg and manage the psionic disturbance, threatening them with imprisonment or violence to ensure compliance [ch 013 / 013.04; ch 014 / 014.07; ch 017 / 017.05]. He paid them in full upon delivery of the egg, treating the transaction as a closed business deal that required absolute secrecy [ch 026 / 026.06]. He is dismissive of Daz, remembering him only as a third-level drow wizard and calling him a fool for feigning ignorance of the egg [ch 026 / 026.06]. He asked the party how they survived the rumored "Ember Vanguard" assassins, to which Thorin replied that they appear "small and insignificant," a response Gartokkar deemed wise [ch 026 / moment].

**Themberchaud:** Gartokkar serves as the liaison between the Keepers of the Flame and Themberchaud [ch 014 / 014.06]. He claims Themberchaud believes the Keepers work for him, while in reality, the Keepers control the dragon [ch 014 / moment]. Gartokkar views the dragon as a volatile asset that must be replaced if he discovers the lack of control [ch 026 / 026.06].

**The Keepers of the Flame:** As a high-ranking member, Gartokkar’s leadership is central to their operations and their formal threats regarding betrayal [ch 018 / entry]. His power and position depend on the order's successful management of Themberchaud; losing the dragon could cause the Deepking to deem the Keepers expendable [ch 018 / 018.02].
