## Identity
Miirym is known as "the Sentinel Wyrm," a guardian dragon of Candlekeep [ch 067 / entry]. Her true name, **Vydykyq**, is the answer to the first riddle guarding the Vault of Secrets, provided by Sylvira Savikas [ch 067 / entry; ch 067 / 067.03]. Sylvira learned this name from Miirym herself during a discussion about demonic possession and the power of true names [ch 067 / entry; ch 067 / 067.03]. When manifesting, she appears as a translucent, spectral silver dragon; in the lava chamber, molten rock flowed directly through her insubstantial form [ch 068 / entry; ch 068 / 068.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Miirym is described as grouchy and imperious [ch 068 / entry; ch 068 / 068.04]. She functions as a verifier of knowledge and truth, strictly bound to act as a "witness, not weapon" [ch 068 / moment]. Consequently, she refused the party's request to join them in battle against Manshoon, stating, "If I could have stopped him, I would have stopped him at dawn" [ch 068 / moment]. She values deep learning and theological nuance, expressing that "wisdom, wisdom truly flows from Bahamut" into Gyrgum after he explained his philosophy on fixed points and personal paths [ch 068 / moment]. She approves of strategic decisions that maximize tactical advantage, repeatedly noting "Well chosen" when the party selected the anti-magic field boon [ch 068 / moment].

## History with the Party
*   Sylvira Savikas provided the true name of Miirym, **Vydykyq**, to the party as the answer to the first riddle guarding the Vault of Secrets [ch 067 / entry; ch 067 / 067.03].
*   Sylvira explained she learned Miirym's true name during a conversation about demonic possession and the power conferred by knowing such names [ch 067 / entry; ch 067 / 067.03].
*   Miirym manifested as a translucent ghost-dragon in the lava chamber, with molten rock passing through her spectral form [ch 068 / entry; ch 068 / 068.04].
*   Miirym revealed that Manshoon had entered her keep and remained inside, though she did not know his exact location [ch 068 / entry; ch 068 / 068.04].
*   Miirym demanded the party identify the Reader; Gyrgum identified himself and proved his knowledge of Candlekeep by describing its books and recent lore, satisfying the verification condition of the riddles [ch 068 / entry; ch 068 / 068.04].
*   Miirym was particularly impressed by Gyrgum's devotion to Bahamut and his theological answer regarding fixed points and personal paths, stating that wisdom flowed from Bahamut into him [ch 068 / entry; ch 068 / 068.04; ch 068 / moment].
*   Miirym refused to join the party in battle, stating she was bound to act as a witness rather than a weapon [ch 068 / entry; ch 068 / 068.04; ch 068 / moment].
*   Miirym offered several possible boons, including teleportation denial, information retrieval, identity verification, or widening the anti-magic field [ch 068 / entry; ch 068 / 068.04].
*   The party chose to have Miirym widen the anti-magic field to thirty feet for ten minutes; Miirym agreed to hold this boon in reserve for a surprise activation at Zalthir’s request, approving the decision [ch 068 / entry; ch 068 / 068.04; ch 068 / moment].

## Last Observed State
Miirym manifested in the lava chamber outside the guardian chamber in Chapter 068 [ch 068 / 068.04]. She confirmed that Manshoon had entered Candlekeep and remained inside the keep, though she did not know his exact location [ch 068 / entry; ch 068 / 068.04]. She verified Gyrgum as the Reader and offered a boon, which the party chose to be the widening of the anti-magic field surrounding the bridge to thirty feet for ten minutes [ch 068 / entry; ch 068 / 068.04]. At Zalthir’s request, Miirym agreed to hold this effect in reserve for surprise activation rather than deploying it immediately [ch 068 / entry; ch 068 / 068.04]. She approved this delay with "Well chosen. Well chosen. Well chosen." [ch 068 / moment]. Status not established in the summaries after the party returned to the tower.
