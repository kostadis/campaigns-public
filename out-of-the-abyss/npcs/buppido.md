## Identity
Buppido is a derro who served as an uncomfortable, short-term companion to the party. He claimed to be on a "divine mission" and believed himself to be the avatar of a major deity, a delusion that manifested in erratic behavior and violent outbursts [ch 010 / 010.02; ch 015 / 015.06]. He corrected the party early on regarding his species, stating, "Derro, not a duergar. Can't you tell the difference?" when they confused him with his kin [ch 010 / 010.03]. He was formerly a companion to the deep gnome Pelek, whom he murdered and dismembered with an axe in the Whorlstone Tunnels after descending into madness [ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / 016.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Buppido was characterized by a blend of grandiosity, selfishness, and dangerous incompetence. He invoked a vague "divine plan" to justify inaction, such as lying prone on a deck during a battle while pretending to be invisible, and, asked why his claimed invisibility hadn't helped them escape, answering "Because the divine plan said I shouldn't do it." [ch 010 / 010.02; ch 010 / moment]. He displayed moral flexibility and cruelty, enthusiastically supporting the enslavement of the prisoner Brannum Redmarch and collaborating with Hemeth on the logistics [ch 011 / 011.01; ch 011 / moment]. When forced to cooperate, he revealed resentment toward his own people's leadership, calling the Council of Savants corrupt and claiming they had always failed the derro, and claimed a divine mission to free his people [ch 015 / 015.04].

His mental state deteriorated into full paranoia and narcissism. After building a shrine of bones in the tunnels, he demanded worship from the party, declaring, "I sense you are ready to receive my truth into your hearts" and "you will finally give me the worship I deserve" before attacking them with a hooked spear [ch 015 / 015.06; ch 015 / moment]. Under a Tongue of Madness mushroom he aired every grievance he had accumulated against the party, in a tirade of about 20 minutes [ch 012 / 012.03; ch 012 / moment].

## History with the Party
- Buppido is a derro companion who flopped onto the deck during a duergar ambush, falsely believing he had turned invisible and insisting his inaction was part of a divine plan [ch 010 / 010.02; ch 010 / 010.03; ch 010 / entry].
- The GM corrected the narrative to clarify that Buppido is a derro, not a duergar, and thus cannot turn invisible; he was lying about his ability [ch 010 / 010.02; ch 010 / 010.03].
- Buppido corrected the party regarding his race, stating, "Derro, not a duergar. Can't you tell the difference?" [ch 010 / 010.03].
- After the duel on the Darklake, Buppido cheerfully offered his services as an expert interrogator for the captured duergar, boasting, "I have many techniques" [ch 010 / 010.04; ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / moment].
- When Thorin floated the idea of selling their prisoner Brannum Redmarch into slavery, Buppido enthusiastically supported the plan, calling it an "excellent idea" [ch 011 / 011.01; ch 011 / entry].
- Buppido and Hemeth collaborated on the logistics of selling Brannum, with Hemeth remarking that he "knew a guy" [ch 011 / 011.01; ch 011 / moment].
- Upon arriving in Gracklstugh, Buppido explained the city's advantages, noting that the duergar have no love for the drow and that their draconian laws offer safety from drow pursuit [ch 011 / 011.07].
- Buppido became anxious to leave the party in Gracklstugh, claiming his divine mission required him to visit a personal shrine in the nearby caverns [ch 012 / 012.03; ch 012 / entry].
- The party considered Buppido's fate, noting he was a murderer who had killed their goblin guide, Yuk Yuk, and that he knew the location of the tomb they were seeking, posing a security risk [ch 012 / 012.03; ch 012 / entry; ch 015 / 015.04].
- To determine his true intentions, the party fed Buppido a Tongue of Madness mushroom, causing him to unleash a long tirade of insults against every party member, criticizing their breath, muscles, and hygiene [ch 012 / 012.03; ch 012 / moment].
- Buppido screamed at Jimjar to stop making bets during the tirade, to which Jimjar remarked he should have bet on that happening [ch 012 / 012.03; ch 012 / moment].
- The party debated marooning Buppido in the Underdark to prevent him from leading drow pursuers to them, but Zalthir concluded they could simply cut him loose once they were done with the city [ch 012 / 012.03].
- While the party was away, Buppido remained cranky but healthy, staying in the party's lodging [ch 014 / 014.03].
- The party returned to their lodgings to consult Buppido about the Derro population and the Whorlstone Tunnels, finding him imprisoned and "forlorn" [ch 015 / 015.04].
- Buppido revealed that the entrance to the Whorlstone Tunnels was hidden within the Derro slums, which explained why the Deepking could not easily crush the factions there [ch 015 / 015.04; ch 015 / moment].
- Buppido delivered a manic rant about the Council of Savants, claiming they were corrupt and had always failed the derro, and asserted he had a divine mission to free his people [ch 015 / 015.04; ch 015 / moment].
- The party agreed to provide Buppido with a spare Stone Guard pass so he could accompany them and guide them into the caverns, with Thorin warning him that a double-cross would result in his death [ch 015 / 015.04].
- In the West Cleft District, Buppido gleefully showed his pass at the gate and bolted after the courier Droki when Droki vanished into a fissure, leading the party into the Whorlstone Tunnels [ch 015 / 015.05; ch 015 / entry].
- The party, following Droki into the tunnels, found Buppido at his makeshift shrine of bones and skulls, where he declared himself their divine leader and demanded worship [ch 015 / 015.06; ch 015 / moment].
- Buppido attacked the party with a hooked spear and skeletal servants, declaring, "Yes, I sense you are ready to receive my truth into your hearts" and "you will finally give me the worship I deserve" [ch 015 / 015.06; ch 015 / moment].
- Daz cast Magic Missile, dealing 15 damage against Buppido's 13 hit points, killing him and ending his delusions of grandeur [ch 015 / 015.07; ch 015 / moment].
- The DM confirmed Buppido was dead, stating his "long quest of greatness and his belief in his divine mission has now ended" [ch 015 / 015.07; ch 015 / moment].
- Buppido was a former companion of the deep gnome Pelek who murdered and dismembered him with an axe in the Whorlstone Tunnels, scattering his remains. `[ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / 016.01]`
- Pelek described Buppido as suddenly "convinced that he was the second coming of some major deity and an avatar and that everybody needed to bow to him"; Pelek denied that Buppido was possessed and said he himself had tried to possess Buppido and failed. `[ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / 016.01; ch 016 / moment]`
- The party killed Buppido in a fight that ended just before Chapter 016, and his body lay among Pelek's bones in an area marked on the map as "Buppido's resting place." `[ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / 016.01]`
- Pelek recounted that pre-asylum Buppido resented the Council of Savants for their perceived subservience to the Duergar, viewing them as a mechanism of control, which fueled Buppido's later delusion that he was "the force of history." `[ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.02]`
- During the voyage across the Darklake, the party reorganized their roster on the map, explicitly listing Buppido as dead. `[ch 027 / 027.05]`
- Pelek referred to Buppido as "really an annoying person" during his own interment ceremony, noting that Buppido was the cause of his death and subsequent torment. `[ch 037 / 037.03]`

## Last Observed State
Status: Deceased. Buppido was killed by the party in the Whorlstone Tunnels after he attacked them at his shrine. Daz cast *Magic Missile*, dealing 15 damage against Buppido's 13 hit points, ending his life [ch 015 / 015.07; ch 015 / moment]. The DM confirmed his death, stating, "Buppido's long quest of greatness and his belief in his divine mission has now ended. He's dead" [ch 015 / moment]. His body lay among the bones of his victim, Pelek, in an area marked on the map as "Buppido's resting place" [ch 016 / 016.01; ch 016 / moment].

## Relationships
**The Party:** Buppido viewed the party primarily as tools for his "divine plan" or obstacles to his freedom. He offered his services as an expert interrogator, and offered to show the party the Whorlstone entrance "if you let me go" [ch 010 / moment; ch 015 / 015.04]. The party ultimately concluded he was a security risk due to his knowledge and violent tendencies, deciding to cut him loose or kill him if he double-crossed them [ch 012 / 012.03; ch 015 / 015.04]. His final interaction with them was a betrayal that resulted in his death [ch 015 / 015.06; ch 015 / 015.07].

**Pelek:** A deep gnome ghost who was formerly Buppido's companion. Pelek reveals that Buppido lured him into the Whorlstone Tunnels with promises of business deals before murdering and dismembering him with an axe [ch 016 / 016.01; ch 037 / 037.03]. Pelek, a trapped ghost, tried in desperation to possess Buppido and failed [ch 016 / 016.01; ch 016 / moment]. Pelek describes Buppido as having gone "stark raving mad," becoming convinced he was an avatar of a deity, and refers to him retrospectively as "really an annoying person" [ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / 016.01; ch 037 / 037.03].

**Hemeth:** A traveling companion who, like Buppido, took up Thorin's idea of selling Brannum Redmarch into slavery, engaging in a cryptic exchange in which Hemeth "knows a guy" [ch 011 / 011.01; ch 011 / moment].

**Jimjar:** A traveling companion Buppido screamed at during a madness-induced tirade to stop making bets [ch 012 / 012.03; ch 012 / moment].
