## Identity
Yuk Yuk is a goblin who lived in the Underdark and served as a guide for the Silken Paths alongside his companion Spiderbait [ch 006 / 006.05; ch 006 / entry]. He was highly skilled at sliding and surfing across giant spider webs, utilizing special equipment on his feet to navigate the shifting strands while dodging predators [ch 006 / 006.05; ch 006 / entry]. He styled himself as one of "the best guides" and claimed knowledge of secret paths through the webs that others, such as the drow, lacked [ch 007 / entry; ch 007 / 007.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Yuk Yuk exhibited a laid-back, surfer-like attitude, often speaking with casual slang and describing his maneuvers as "hyper cool" or "super cool" [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / moment; ch 007 / 007.01]. He was boastful and competitive, keeping an informal score with Spiderbait of who performed the coolest actions, such as a "twist and flip" while dodging spiders [ch 006 / moment; ch 006 / 006.06]. He found the party’s frantic struggles against spiders entertaining and mocked their lack of style compared to his own claimed abilities [ch 007 / 007.01; ch 007 / entry].

Motivationally, Yuk Yuk was driven by payment and survival. Initially, he sought a direct fee of two gold pieces per day plus a twenty-gold wager won against Jimjar [ch 006 / 006.05; ch 006 / 006.06]. When Jimjar proved destitute, Yuk Yuk and Spiderbait agreed to continue guiding in exchange for a share of future gold to cover their debts, reasoning that they needed to follow the party to get paid [ch 006 / 006.05; ch 006 / 006.06]. At the end of the Silken Paths, he and Spiderbait again demanded their twenty gold from Jimjar, only accepting continued employment when promised future payment and access to mushrooms [ch 007 / 007.05]. When spiders approached the trapped halfling, he cut the deliberation short, saying "dudes, I have a really bad feeling about this, but I think there are some spiders that are coming here" [ch 006 / 006.07; ch 006 / moment].

## History with the Party
- Yuk Yuk, along with his companion Spiderbait, introduced themselves as guides for the Silken Paths at the edge of the chasm, offering their services for two gold pieces per day [ch 006 / 006.05].
- Yuk Yuk accepted a wager from Jimjar, betting 20 gold pieces that he could cross the webs; he won the bet and was very annoyed to learn Jimjar had no money to pay [ch 006 / 006.05; ch 006 / entry].
- After winning the bet, Yuk Yuk and Spiderbait agreed to guide the party in exchange for a share of future gold to cover their fee plus the debt, stating they needed to follow the party to get paid [ch 006 / 006.05; ch 006 / 006.06].
- During the crossing, Yuk Yuk warned the party that spiders were approaching, prompting them to quickly free a trapped halfling named Fargas Rumblefoot from a cocoon [ch 006 / 006.07].
- Yuk Yuk mocked the party’s frantic escape from the spiders, calling it "hyper cool" while describing a more stylish maneuver he claimed he would have performed [ch 007 / 007.01].
- Yuk Yuk claimed knowledge of secret paths through the webs that the drow pursuers lacked, asserting himself as one of the best guides [ch 007 / entry; ch 007 / 007.02].
- When the party approached a suspicious chest near the end of the Silken Paths, Yuk Yuk cheerfully assured them that spiders rarely attacked at night, an assurance that failed to convince Zalthir or the others to stay [ch 007 / 007.04].
- At the end of the Silken Paths, Yuk Yuk and Spiderbait demanded payment from Jimjar; Jimjar negotiated to defer the debt, convincing them to continue traveling with the party in exchange for future payment and access to mushrooms [ch 007 / 007.05].
- Yuk Yuk was killed by Buppido, the derro companion, during the party's travels in the Underdark; Thorin noted this loss after the party defeated Buppido [ch 015 / 015.07].
- Status: Deceased, having been killed by Buppido [ch 015 / 015.07].

## Last Observed State
Yuk Yuk is deceased. He was killed by Buppido, the derro companion who had traveled with the party after the Silken Paths, during their travels in the Underdark [ch 015 / 015.07]. His death was noted by Thorin after the party defeated Buppido in the Whorlstone Tunnels [ch 015 / 015.07].

## Relationships
*   **Spiderbait:** A goblin companion who traveled with Yuk Yuk. They worked together as guides through the Silken Paths, kept score of each other's "coolest" maneuvers, and shared in the payment demands and negotiations with the party [ch 006 / 006.05; ch 006 / 006.06; ch 007 / 007.05].
*   **Jimjar:** The companion with whom Yuk Yuk struck a bet. Yuk Yuk was "very annoyed" to learn Jimjar could not pay the twenty gold piece wager immediately, leading to the arrangement for future payment [ch 006 / 006.05; ch 006 / 006.06; ch 007 / 007.05].
*   **Buppido:** The derro who killed Yuk Yuk during the party's subsequent travels in the Underdark [ch 015 / 015.07].
*   **The Party:** Yuk Yuk and Spiderbait guided the party across the Silken Paths. The GM described their vibe as positive, akin to "two goblin surfer dudes," though they mocked the party's less stylish escape from spiders [ch 006 / 006.05; ch 006 / 006.06; ch 007 / 007.01].
