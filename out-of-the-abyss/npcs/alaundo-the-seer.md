## Identity
Alaundo the Seer is a legendary figure from Candlekeep, recognized as a seer whose predictions are considered absolutely true by the institution's scholars [ch 071 / 071.05]. He is known for embedding prophecies in prismatic gemstones and crystal vessels that can be activated by spoken words to repeat his visions [ch 069 / 069.05; ch 071 / 071.05]. Alaundo died at the age of 97; his grave is located in the grove where Candlekeep’s keepers of tomes are buried [ch 067 / 067.05]. He is remembered for his fondness for animal similes and specific prophetic imagery [ch 071 / 071.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Alaundo held a philosophical objection to conventional locks, viewing them as "a promise that a thing can be stolen," and thus designed his most protected spaces, such as the Obsidian Door, to be opened by questions and acts of freely given intent rather than physical keys [ch 069 / moment; ch 070 / 070.01]. His prophecies often contain specific, verifiable details about individuals and locations, such as references to a "crow" whom the scholars identified as Daz and a "sword afraid of the dark" [ch 071 / 071.05]. He described his prophecies as statements of fact rather than curses, explicitly stating regarding a burning city: "This is not a curse. This is the whole of my prophecy" [ch 071 / 071.05].

## History with the Party
*   Alaundo’s prophecies are recited continuously in Candlekeep’s high gallery, with fulfilled prophecies removed, leaving only unfulfilled ones [ch 057 / 057.02].
*   A specific prophecy heard by the party—"On the longest night of winter's deepest cold, the keeper falls, the heart is sold"—took on chilling significance when the Keeper of Tomes was murdered the following morning [ch 057 / moment].
*   Daral Yashenti disparaged the party by comparing their anonymity unfavorably to the fame of Alaundo [ch 057 / 057.05].
*   The party deciphered a cryptogram instructing them to "Feed the quill of Alaundo the seer" and "Tread as many steps as he lived in years" to access the Vault of Secrets [ch 067 / 067.03].
*   The party visited the House of Alaundo in Founders Court, where they poured fresh ink into the empty ink pot of Alaundo’s brass statue to reveal a hidden staircase [ch 067 / 067.04].
*   The statue’s mechanism had remained undiscovered for centuries because scholars considered defacing the monument, such as putting ink in the pot, unthinkable [ch 067 / 067.04].
*   The party learned that Alaundo died at the age of 97 by consulting his grave in the grove where Candlekeep’s keepers of tomes are buried [ch 067 / 067.05].
*   The party descended the hidden staircase beneath the statue, toward the 97th step, where Gyrgum planned to recite the original prophecy; a section of the stairs then hinged open and Thorin fell into a vast circular well [ch 067 / 067.06].
*   Alaundo left ten prismatic gemstones containing prophecies in a velvet tray near the Obsidian Door, which Manshoon threatened to destroy [ch 069 / 069.05].
*   Alaundo’s voice in the Obsidian Door chamber was recognized by Daz as artificial or conjured due to a lack of breathing and vocal fluctuation [ch 069 / entry].
*   One of Alaundo’s gemstones foretold Eldeth Feldrun’s return to a hall of stone bearing names of demons, and the summoning of Thorin, Zalthir, Daz, and Dawnbringer to an ancient forge [ch 070 / entry].
*   Alaundo designed the Obsidian Door’s protection to substitute a question and an act of freely given intent for a conventional lock [ch 070 / entry].
*   The party solved Alaundo’s riddle ("What is consumed to make light?") by speaking "A candle" in darkness with genuine intent, opening the door [ch 070 / 070.03].
*   Edvaldo smashed two of Alaundo’s prophecy crystals in a panic, threatening to destroy the rest, but Gyrgum secured the four surviving crystals afterward [ch 070 / 070.03].
*   Alaundo is the legendary seer of Candlekeep whose prophecies are stored in crystal vessels; Gyrgum identified the recovered crystals as his works [ch 071 / 071.03].
*   Alaundo's voice is recorded within the prophecy crystals and can be activated by spoken words to repeat his visions [ch 071 / 071.05].
*   Inda, a scholar of Candlekeep, asserts that Alaundo's prophecies are always true, a fact that defines their scholarly value and reliability [ch 071 / 071.05].
*   Alaundo’s prophecies reference specific figures, including Prince Derendil (a Quaggoth who believed himself royalty), a sword afraid of the dark, and a "crow" whom the scholars identified as Daz [ch 071 / 071.05].
*   The scholars interpret Alaundo's use of animal similes to identify Daz, due to his Drow heritage, as the "crow" in the prophecy who must choose to wear, refuse, or burn a name offered to him [ch 071 / 071.05; ch 071 / moment].
*   Alaundo’s prophecy explicitly states that the city will burn because its clock, lungs, and heart can be made to burn, clarifying that this outcome is not a curse but the entirety of the prophecy [ch 071 / 071.05].
*   The House of Mechanus scholars expressed delight that their borrowed tools helped the party reach Alaundo's prophecies [ch 071 / 071.09].
*   The arrival of Eldeth’s note summoning the party to Gauntlgrym was recognized by Thorin as the apparent fulfillment of another of Alaundo's prophecies [ch 071 / 071.10].

## Last Observed State
Alaundo himself is deceased and does not appear in the recent events; his presence is mediated through recorded artifacts [ch 067 / 067.05]. In Chapter 071, his voice was activated from four surviving crystal vessels after Inda instructed the party on the mechanism [ch 071 / 071.05]. The crystals contained prophecies concerning Prince Derendil, a choice regarding the Book of Vile Darkness ("what to keep"), and a warning to Daz as the "crow" [ch 071 / 071.05]. Two of the six original crystals had been smashed by Edvaldo prior to this activation [ch 070 / 070.03; ch 071 / 071.05]. The activation of these crystals and the subsequent arrival of Eldeth’s note were recognized as the fulfillment of prophecies, confirming Alaundo's continued influence on the campaign's direction [ch 071 / 071.10]. Status not established in the summaries regarding his spiritual state or afterlife; he is treated as a historical source.

## Relationships
**Candlekeep Scholars:** Alaundo is the central subject of their academic work, with prophecies being cataloged and studied rigorously [ch 057 / 057.02; ch 071 / 071.05]. Inda, a scholar, asserts that his prophecies are always true and was distressed by the uncertainty caused by the destroyed crystals [ch 071 / 071.05].
**The Party:** Alaundo’s prophecies directly involve the party’s actions and fate. A prophecy recited at dinner took on chilling significance after the Keeper of Tomes was murdered [ch 057 / moment]. His crystals reference Daz as a "crow" who must choose to wear, refuse, or burn a name [ch 071 / 071.05]. Thorin identified the party's summons to Gauntlgrym as a fulfillment of Alaundo’s work [ch 071 / 071.10].
**Daz:** Alaundo’s prophecy specifically identifies Daz via animal imagery (the "crow") and predicts his role in the fate of a city [ch 071 / 071.05].
