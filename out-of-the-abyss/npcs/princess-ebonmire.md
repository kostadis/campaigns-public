## Identity
Princess Ebonmire is a black pudding, one of the Pudding King's two ooze henchmen, whom the King refers to as a "pet" [ch 046 / entry; ch 046 / 046.09]. She serves as a combatant for the Pudding King, possessing no ranged attacks beyond throwing acidic blobs [ch 047 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
The evidence is silent on Ebonmire's internal motivations or personality traits beyond her role as one of the Pudding King’s ooze henchmen. The DM said she would "scramble to come help her Prince in Shining Armor" [ch 047 / 047.07]. She and Prince Livid "fought on" after the Pudding King was defeated [ch 047 / 047.09].

## History with the Party
*   Princess Ebonmire is identified as one of the Pudding King's two ooze henchmen, specifically described as a black pudding he refers to as a pet [ch 046 / entry; ch 046 / 046.09].
*   During the assault on the throne room, the Pudding King monologues to Princess Ebonmire and Prince Livid, promising that Blingdenstone will become "Blingdenpuddle" [ch 046 / 046.09].
*   In the combat phase, Princess Ebonmire attacked the party by throwing blobs of acidic ooze; one blob missed Zalthir [ch 047 / 047.03].
*   Zalthir performed an Arcana check to determine if Princess Ebonmire possessed psychic abilities, though the outcome of the check is not explicitly stated in the summary text other than the action itself [ch 047 / 047.03].
*   During the coordinated assault, Princess Ebonmire charged toward the party to aid Prince Livid [ch 047 / 047.07].
*   When the Pudding King was defeated, Princess Ebonmire continued to fight despite the loss of their leader [ch 047 / 047.09].
*   Princess Ebonmire took full damage from Daz's Fireball, which dealt twenty-nine points of damage to both remaining oozes [ch 047 / 047.09].
*   Princess Ebonmire charged Thorin, using her action to dash [ch 047 / 047.09].
*   Gyrgum cast Inflict Wounds on Princess Ebonmire, dealing eighteen points of damage, and likened her name to "black mud" [ch 047 / entry; ch 047 / 047.09].
*   Zalthir used Shadow Step to teleport to Princess Ebonmire, grappled her, and attacked her with advantage [ch 047 / 047.09].
*   Thorin delivered the killing blow to Princess Ebonmire [ch 047 / 047.09].
*   Two spellbooks containing numerous magical formulas were discovered in Princess Ebonmire's stomach after her death [ch 047 / entry; ch 047 / 047.10].
*   The party later donated "Princess Ebonmire's spellbook" to the library at Candlekeep as part of their entry fee [ch 054 / 054.05].

## Last Observed State
Princess Ebonmire was killed during the assault on the Pudding King’s throne room. She took twenty-nine points of fire damage from Daz’s *Fireball*, which dealt full damage to her [ch 047 / 047.09]. She then charged Thorin but was struck by Gyrgum’s *Inflict Wounds*, which dealt eighteen points of necrotic damage [ch 047 / 047.09]. Zalthir then used *Shadow Step* to teleport to her, grappling her and attacking with advantage, dealing further damage [ch 047 / 047.09]. Thorin delivered the final killing blow, ending her existence [ch 047 / 047.09]. Two spellbooks containing numerous magical formulas were later discovered inside her remains [ch 047 / entry; ch 047 / 047.10].

*   **Prince Livid:** She is paired with Prince Livid, another of the Pudding King’s ooze henchmen. During the battle, she charged toward the party specifically to aid him [ch 047 / 047.07].
