## Identity
Imbros was a drow commander and Drow Elite Warrior stationed in the guard tower at Velkynvelve. [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.02; ch 004 / entry] He was armed with a poisoned short sword and possessed significantly greater endurance than the ordinary guards. [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.02] His body yielded a vial of poison and three poisoned crossbow bolts upon death. [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.01]

## Personality and Motivations
Imbros operated with the intent to keep prisoners alive, as they were considered valuable assets; consequently, he initially attempted to subdue rather than kill Thorin, using nonlethal force despite having a lethal weapon. [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.02] As the situation devolved into a full revolt, he cursed the slaves in Undercommon. [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.03] He concentrated his attacks on Prince Derendil, whom he slew before being overwhelmed. [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.03]

## History with the Party
- Imbros was a drow commander stationed in the guard tower, armed with a poisoned short sword and possessing greater endurance than ordinary guards. [ch 003 / entry]
- When the party attempted an escape, Imbros initially tried to subdue Thorin rather than kill him because the prisoners were valuable. [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.02]
- Thorin attempted an unarmed strike and shove against Imbros, missing the attack; Imbros struck Thorin with his short sword, attempting subdual. [ch 003 / 003.02]
- Imbros survived multiple attacks from the party, including unarmed strikes from Zalthir, short sword blows from Sarith, crossbow volleys from above, and repeated Magic Missiles from Daz. [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.03]
- Imbros cursed the revolting slaves in Undercommon and shifted his focus to Prince Derendil, ultimately slaying the Quaggoth with his poisoned blade. [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.03]
- Sarith delivered the killing blow to Imbros after being steadied by Gyrgum. [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.03]
- Following his death, Imbros’s corpse yielded a vial of poison and three poisoned crossbow bolts, which Gyrgum claimed. [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.01]

## Last Observed State
Imbros was killed in the lower floor of the guard tower during the prisoner revolt. [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.03] He survived numerous attacks, including unarmed strikes from Zalthir, short sword blows from Sarith, crossbow volleys, and Magic Missiles from Daz, only falling after Sarith delivered the killing blow. [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.03; ch 003 / 003.02] Following his death in Chapter 003, his corpse was looted in Chapter 004, where Gyrgum claimed the poisoned bolts and the vial of poison. [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.01] Status: Deceased. [ch 003 / entry; ch 004 / 004.01]
