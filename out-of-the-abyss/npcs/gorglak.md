## Identity
Gorglak is a Duergar captain, approximately four feet tall, with ashen gray skin, scars, and tattoos. He wears a heavy iron chain with a clenched fist pendant, a symbol Zalthir identifies via history knowledge as indicating loyalty to the Deepking [ch 026 / 026.01; ch 026 / entry]. In Chapter 25, he was referred to as "the keeper here" [ch 025 / entry]. In Chapter 26, he holds the rank of captain and stands alone in the center of the West Cleft Gate passage, supported by five invisible Duergar soldiers positioned throughout the hallway [ch 026 / 026.01; ch 026 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Gorglak is suspicious, authoritative, and openly corrupt. While initially rigid in enforcing the lockdown, declaring that "passes mean little here" and demanding strict compliance or surrender [ch 026 / moment; ch 026 / entry], he quickly shifts from ideological enforcement to financial extortion once the party's identity is verified. He displays a "thin, unsettling smile" while maneuvering for bribes [ch 026 / entry]. His primary motivation is personal gain within the constraints of his duty; he leverages the threat of a "full inspection" (which could yield coin) to demand twenty gold pieces for a quick pass [ch 026 / 026.02; ch 026 / moment]. Despite his corruption, he remains aware of the broader political tensions, noting the Deepking's fear of Drow raids and the derro's suspicion of the Deepking [ch 026 / 026.02].

## History with the Party
*   Gorglak intercepted the party at the sealed gates of Gracklstugh after they emerged from the Whorlstone Caverns, demanding to inspect their possessions for contraband. He stated they were not allowed in the city and should not be there. [ch 025 / 025.08; ch 025 / entry]
*   At the West Cleft Gate, Gorglak, identified as a scarred Duergar captain loyal to the Deepking, questioned the party against rumors of the "Ember Vanguard." He noted discrepancies between their actual appearances and the rumored forms (red dragonborn, wizened drow). [ch 026 / 026.01; ch 026 / entry]
*   Gorglak declared that passes meant little in the lockdown area, stating no one from that district would enter. He demanded proof the party were not the Ember Vanguard, or for them to confess and surrender. [ch 026 / 026.01; ch 026 / moment]
*   He required the party to lay down weapons and magic items for inspection and speak the Stone Guard’s secret salute. He maintained a posture of standing alone, while secretly supported by five invisible Duergar soldiers positioned in the hallway. [ch 026 / 026.01; ch 026 / entry]
*   After Gyrgum performed the salute convincingly, Gorglak conceded they were working for the Stone Guard and were not the Ember Vanguard. However, he still demanded a bribe of twenty gold pieces (five from each member) to allow them through without a full inspection. [ch 026 / 026.02; ch 026 / entry]
*   Following the payment, Gorglak warned the party to conduct their business quickly and leave. He revealed that the Deepking feared a Drow raiding party, while the derro believed the Deepking was behind the unrest. [ch 026 / 026.02]

## Last Observed State
In the final scenes of Chapter 26, Gorglak successfully extorted twenty gold pieces from the party (five from each member) after they performed the Stone Guard secret salute [ch 026 / 026.02]. He then opened the back gate, allowing the party into the city proper. His final recorded action was warning the party to conduct their business quickly and leave, citing the threat of a Drow raiding party and internal derro unrest [ch 026 / 026.02]. Status not established in the summaries regarding his fate after the party entered the city.

## Relationships
*   **The Deepking:** Gorglak wears a symbol of loyalty to the Deepking [ch 026 / 026.01]. He expresses knowledge of the Deepking's fears regarding Drow raids [ch 026 / 026.02].
*   **The Party (Gorglak):** In Chapter 25, he attempted to block and inspect the party at the sealed gates, telling them they "should not be in here" [ch 025 / 025.08]. In Chapter 26, he interrogated them at the West Cleft Gate, suspecting they were the "Ember Vanguard" due to exaggerated city rumors [ch 026 / 026.01]. He was ultimately convinced by Gyrgum's performance of the Stone Guard salute that they were legitimate agents of that faction [ch 026 / 026.02].
*   **Stone Guard:** Gorglak recognized the party as working for the Stone Guard, noting the salute as proof [ch 026 / 026.02]. He used their status to justify waiving a full inspection in exchange for a bribe [ch 026 / 026.02].
