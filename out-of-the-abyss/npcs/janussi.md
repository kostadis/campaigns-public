## Identity
Janussi was a human male, approximately seventy years old, who served as the Keeper of Tomes for the Avowed of Candlekeep [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.07]. He was a murder victim; the party was appointed by Bookwyrm to investigate his death, which involved post-mortem removal of his heart to prevent resurrection [ch 057 / 057.05; ch 057 / 057.07]. He was known for wearing a locket containing one of the two keys required to access the High Tower Library [ch 058 / 058.02; ch 063 / 063.01].

## Personality and Motivations
The party judged that Janussi's Intelligence far exceeded his Wisdom, having alienated powerful colleagues on annual-review day while surrounded by lethal magic [ch 061 / entry]. He was described as an "old snob" by his rival, Daral Yashenti, in a poisoned gift tag [ch 058 / moment]. His professional conduct was rigid and dismissive; on the day of his death, he blocked Kalan Strongbranch’s methodology, terminated Alkrist’s research, denied Bookwyrm succession, and gave A'lai Aivenmore ten days before taking his chair, an ultimatum A'lai said Janussi thought was kind [ch 061 / 061.04; ch 064 / 064.04]. He maintained a volatile romantic relationship with Sylvira Savikas and engaged in frequent confrontations with Great Reader Daral Yashenti [ch 058 / 058.05; ch 059 / 059.05]. Despite suffering from lung cancer, he filled his chambers with cherry-scented tobacco smoke, a habit that potentially masked the scent of the poison used to kill him [ch 058 / 058.03]. Fheminor Scrivenbark later remarked that Janussi might have benefited from reading the *Tome of Leadership and Influence* to improve his abilities as a Reader [ch 071 / 071.08].

## Last Observed State
Janussi was dead. His body was discovered in his chambers with a fractured nose, bruising, and his rib cage caved in by axe blows, with his heart removed post-mortem [ch 057 / entry; ch 058 / 058.01]. The cause of death was poisoning via "Midnight Tears" applied to a book gift from Daral Yashenti, which he ingested while reading [ch 059 / 059.04; ch 061 / 061.07]. His locket, containing the second High Tower key, was stolen after his murder [ch 059 / 059.03], and his heart was found hidden in Milil’s chalice [ch 060 / 060.05], which the party suspected Bookwyrm of arranging [ch 062 / 062.02; ch 063 / 063.01]. Status not established in the summaries beyond confirmation of death and body disposal.

## Relationships
*   **Alkrist:** Janussi’s killer. Alkrist admitted to stealing Midnight Tears, applying them to a book Janussi received from Daral, and re-wrapping it [ch 061 / 061.07; ch 063 / entry]. Alkrist noted they were "discussing how to deal with our mutual problem of Janussi" [ch 061 / moment].
*   **Daral Yashenti:** Janussi’s primary professional rival and the source of the poisoned gift. Daral admitted to physically striking Janussi ("caught him lightly on the nose") during a confrontation over the book earlier that evening [ch 058 / 058.04; ch 059 / 059.05].
*   **Bookwyrm:** A colleague who Janussi denied succession to. Bookwyrm later entered Janussi’s room after his death to remove his heart and secure the key, apparently to protect her nephew, Alkrist [ch 061 / 061.08; ch 063 / 063.02]. Bookwyrm told the party she could not believe there would be accomplices in the death of a man "beloved by all," a conspicuously theatrical display of outrage [ch 062 / moment].
*   **A'lai Aivenmore:** A Great Reader to whom Janussi gave ten days before taking his academic chair, leading to the conspiracy that ultimately killed him [ch 064 / 064.04].
*   **Sylvira Savikas:** Janussi’s romantic partner. Sylvira stated she had an argument with him regarding a private matter and was likely framed via magical impersonation for any role in his death [ch 058 / 058.05; ch 061 / 061.05].
*   **Kalan Strongbranch:** The Gatewarden. Bookwyrm said Janussi had asked Kalan to tender his resignation the morning before the murder [ch 057 / 057.05]. Kalan later said he feared being targeted for the key [ch 059 / 059.03].
*   **Hollypocket:** Janussi’s gnome housekeeper, who testified to the party regarding his personal habits and relationships [ch 058 / 058.05].
