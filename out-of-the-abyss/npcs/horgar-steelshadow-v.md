## Identity
Horgar Steelshadow V is the ruler of the Duergar city of Gracklstugh, holding the title of Deepking [ch 013 / entry; ch 018 / entry; ch 021 / entry]. His own clan is Clan Steelshadow [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.02]. The party possesses a document bearing his signature and the royal seal of Gracklstugh, which serves as proof of his secret alliance with the Empty-Scabbard Killers assassin guild [ch 019 / 019.03; ch 019 / moment; ch 021 / entry; ch 025 / 025.01]. He is widely regarded as a paranoid and unstable figure who has granted exclusive weapons contracts to his own clan [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.02; ch 018 / 018.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Horgar Steelshadow V is described as having descended into a form of madness characterized not by rampaging violence, but by the isolation and disconnection seen in a person in power who has lost touch with society [ch 013 / 013.05; ch 015 / 015.02]. His behavior is increasingly erratic; he imposes arbitrary laws, creates crackdowns that drive factions underground, and is always in a rush [ch 013 / 013.05; ch 015 / 015.02]. This instability appears to have coincided with the rise of a favored advisor named Shal, who wormed his way into Horgar's confidence [ch 015 / entry; ch 015 / 015.02]. The party speculates that an "evil vizier" is influencing his actions [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.02].

Horgar has isolated himself from his oldest allies, no longer discussing Gracklstugh's problems with Hgraam, the Stone Giant leader [ch 015 / 015.02]. Gartokkar claims the Deepking treats the Keepers of the Flame as his most prized advisors, and says their power derives in part from "the leadership of the Deepking" and their influence over Themberchaud [ch 013 / 013.04; ch 014 / 014.07]. His attempts to enforce control have reportedly caused problems to escape the city and seize control from without [ch 015 / 015.02]. He hates and fears the Ember Vanguard, which in his mind is a drow raiding party because of Daz [ch 026 / 026.02; ch 027 / 027.04]. Additionally, he is withholding materials desired by the gnome kingdoms, acting as an obstacle to the party's allies [ch 021 / entry; ch 021 / 021.02].
