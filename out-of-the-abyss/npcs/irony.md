## Identity
Irony is a junior Avowed member of Candlekeep who works as a waitress. She has a compulsive need to tell the truth, which made her a useful witness [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.06]. She serves food at major events, including the Deadwinter supper [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Irony dislikes predictable jokes about her name [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.06]. She has been actively resisting unwanted advances from Great Reader Teles Ahvoste [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.07]. Her compulsion to speak the truth made her a useful witness, describing events such as the menu and guest interactions during the feast, though she may be unaware of errors in her own memory (such as the location of discarded wrapping paper) which are corrected by external evidence [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.06].

## History with the Party
*   Irony is a junior Avowed member and waitress who serves food at Candlekeep events, such as the Deadwinter supper [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.06].
*   She possesses a compulsive need to tell the truth, which made her a useful witness for the party's investigations, though she dislikes predictable jokes about her name [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.06].
*   She has been resisting unwanted advances from Great Reader Teles Ahvoste [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.07].
*   During the Deadwinter feast, she helped serve the meal alongside Orrin Glass and Sprig Summerfoot [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.06].
*   She provided the party with details about the feast’s twelve dishes, the tense mood of the guests, and the fact that no one who ate the leftovers became ill [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.06].
*   She initially stated that Janussi’s discarded wrapping was in a wicker basket near the door, but this was corrected by the GM/the party, who had already found the wrapping paper in Janussi’s office [ch 060 / 060.06].
*   She reported seeing a figure resembling Sylvira Savikas in the kitchens at approximately 1 a.m., who pointed to her throat instead of speaking and asked for honey; the party suspects this figure was an impostor using magical disguise [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.06].
*   Sprig Summerfoot, not Irony, directed the party to Orrin Glass, who was noted to be highly observant despite being deaf in one ear [ch 060 / 060.07].
*   While preparing the supper table near the Deadwinter Tree, she arrived with Orrin Glass and interrupted Alkrist as he attempted to burn evidence; she carried a basin of water that allowed Alkrist to wash his hands of potential poison traces [ch 061 / entry; ch 061 / 061.07].

## Last Observed State
Irony was last seen in Chapter 061, arriving with Orrin Glass at the Deadwinter Tree to prepare the supper table. She carried a basin of water, which Alkrist used to wash his hands after he had coated a gift with Midnight Tears [ch 061 / entry; ch 061 / 061.07]. Status not established in the summaries regarding her current location or safety after this event.
