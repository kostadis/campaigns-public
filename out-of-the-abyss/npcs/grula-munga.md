## Identity
Grula-Munga was a massive female ettin who served as physical muscle for the Derro cultists led by Narrak [ch 023 / entry; ch 024 / entry]. She possessed two heads and wielded both a battle axe and a morning star in combat [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.04]. She began her involvement in the conflict with 85 hit points [ch 023 / 023.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Grula-Munga acted primarily as a guard for the cult's ritual site, initially observing from a hidden fungal alcove before joining the fight [ch 023 / 023.01]. She displayed exasperation when ambushed, shouting "Oh, brother!" upon pointing out the intruders [ch 023 / 023.04]. Her motivation appears tied to her role as muscle for the cultists performing the ritual to create two-headed stone giants [ch 023 / entry].

## History with the Party
- Grula-Munga is a massive female ettin who serves as muscle for Derro cultists performing a ritual to create two-headed stone giants [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.01].
- She was initially spotted lurking silently in a hidden fungal alcove, observing the ritual site before the ambush began [ch 023 / 023.01].
- During the ambush, after the first magical darkness was cast, she pointed out the intruders and joined the combat [ch 023 / 023.04].
- In the first session's combat, she waded into the fray, striking Thorin for 14 damage with her morning star while her battle axe missed, and she ended the session at 54 hit points after taking damage from Daz, Gyrgum, and Thorin [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.04].
- In the subsequent session, she engaged in heavy melee combat, hitting Thorin for 13 damage with her battle axe during a sequence where she was outside the zone of darkness [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.02].
- Zalthir used his eldritch tentacles to grapple and pummel her within a zone of magical darkness, reducing her hit points significantly while she remained blinded [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.02].
- Zalthir's tentacle strikes finally slew the ettin after Thorin's longsword attack, ending her participation in the battle [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.03].

## Last Observed State
Grula-Munga was slain in combat during the second session of the encounter. She spent the final phase of the battle blinded inside Zalthir’s zone of magical darkness, where she was grappled by eldritch tentacles and pummeled until her hit points were depleted [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.02]. Zalthir's tentacle strikes finally ended her life after Thorin's longsword attack [ch 024 / 024.03].
