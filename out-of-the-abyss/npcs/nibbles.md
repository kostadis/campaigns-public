## Identity
Nibbles is a friendly squirrel assistant to Leuwin [ch 060 / entry]. The squirrel is capable of fetching tools and implements for the apothecary [ch 060 / 060.08; ch 060 / entry]. Nibbles serves as a witness to events overlooked by humanoids [ch 060 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Nibbles trades information strictly for nuts [ch 060 / entry]. The squirrel prioritizes acquiring food over revealing information, requiring increasingly generous bribery to disclose details [ch 060 / 060.08]. Nibbles explicitly states this motivation: “I know more if you have more nuts.” [ch 060 / moment] When presented with nuts, the squirrel’s focus shifts immediately to the food source: “Oh, you’ve got nuts.” [ch 060 / moment] Nibbles cannot provide distinctive markings for suspects, limiting the utility of testimony beyond presence and action [ch 060 / 060.08].

## History with the Party
*   Nibbles is Leuwin’s friendly squirrel assistant, capable of fetching tools and witnessing crimes overlooked by humanoids. [ch 060 / entry]
*   Nibbles trades information strictly for nuts. [ch 060 / entry]
*   Nibbles identified Fheminor, A’lai, and a Dragonborn visitor while Leuwin was occupied. [ch 060 / entry]

*   Daz used *Speak with Animals* and bribed Nibbles with nuts to obtain the testimony; Nibbles initially identified Fheminor, then A’lai, and finally the Dragonborn only after receiving more generous bribery. [ch 060 / 060.08]

*   Nibbles’s testimony helped connect Alkrist to the spilled peppermint and stolen poison. [ch 061 / entry]

## Last Observed State
Nibbles was last seen at the Oak Tree Apothecary, where the squirrel provided testimony regarding a theft [ch 060 / 060.08]. During an interview conducted by Daz using *Speak with Animals*, Nibbles consumed five additional nuts provided as a reward for crucial information, leaving the squirrel with its mouth comically full and eager for further questions [ch 060 / 060.08]. In subsequent events, Nibbles’s testimony was used to corroborate physical evidence and connect the Dragonborn Alkrist to the scene of the theft [ch 061 / entry]. Status after chapter 061 is not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
**Leuwin:** Nibbles acts as Leuwin’s assistant, fetching tools and witnessing crimes in the apothecary [ch 060 / entry]. Leuwin refers to Nibbles when investigating discrepancies in his shop [ch 060 / 060.08].

**Daz:** Daz established a transactional relationship with Nibbles, using *Speak with Animals* and a supply of nuts to extract testimony [ch 060 / 060.08]. Daz successfully bribed Nibbles to identify Fheminor, A’lai, and a Dragonborn visitor [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.08].

**Alkrist:** Nibbles witnessed Alkrist (described as a "huge lizard skin") entering the apothecary, moving items, causing a loud noise, and breaking a vial while Leuwin was distracted [ch 060 / 060.08; ch 061 / entry]. This testimony helped connect Alkrist to the stolen poison and spilled peppermint [ch 061 / entry].

**Fheminor Scrivenbark:** Nibbles identified Fheminor as an "unfriendly girl gnome" who visited the apothecary [ch 060 / 060.08].

**A’lai:** Nibbles identified A’lai as a "big, clumsy, dark-skinned elf" who arrived after Fheminor and subsequently distracted Leuwin by taking him to the greenhouse [ch 060 / 060.08].
