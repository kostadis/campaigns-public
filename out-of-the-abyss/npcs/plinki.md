## Identity
Plinki was a female derro savant and a fanatical leader within the Cult of Demogorgon [ch 019 / entry]. She served as a mad scientist specializing in the infusion of demonic energy into biological specimens, specifically a stolen red dragon egg [ch 017 / entry; ch 019 / 019.05]. She was identified by the flumph as the savant responsible for working on a massive black obelisk in the Underdark [ch 017 / 017.04]. Plinki commanded a group of approximately ten derro guards and minions, some of whom were possibly hidden or invisible [ch 017 / 017.04; ch 019 / 019.05]. Her Armor Class was 13 [ch 019 / entry]. She possessed a journal containing demonic lore, which the party later donated to Candlekeep [ch 054 / 054.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Plinki was driven by a desire to curry favor with the demon lord Demogorgon by creating a two-headed dragon mount through dark rituals [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.04]. She exhibited fanatical pride in her work, displaying inscriptions on the dragon egg with "Kung Fu Panda-style fan enthusiasm" and vibrating with excitement [ch 019 / 019.06]. Her demeanor was easily swayed by flattery and false authority; she enthusiastically accepted Gyrgum’s bluff as a cleric of Bahamut, interpreting it as Demogorgon sending a holy man to acknowledge her achievement [ch 019 / 019.05; ch 019 / moment]. However, her madness prevailed when confronted with obstacles, leading her to order attacks and reveal that her ritual required the beating heart of a cleric of Bahamut [ch 019 / 019.06; ch 019 / moment]. She was authoritative enough to promise followers like Skiit "a better job" and inspire loyalty through promises of derro greatness [ch 022 / 022.02; ch 022 / moment].

## History with the Party
- The flumph telepathically revealed to the party that Plinki, a derro savant, was torturing a stolen red dragon egg with dark rituals to create a two-headed mount for Demogorgon [ch 017 / 017.04; ch 017 / moment].
- During the psychic revelation, the flumph identified Plinki as the derro working on the obelisk and noted she had approximately ten derro guards [ch 017 / 017.04].
- Plinki stood beside the 50-foot black obelisk in the chamber, stroking its surface with reverent intensity while scribbling in a notebook, initially ignoring the party [ch 019 / 019.05].
- Gyrgum approached Plinki bluffing as a cleric of Bahamut, claiming to come worship the egg; Plinki enthusiastically believed him, declaring Demogorgon had sent him to acknowledge her achievement [ch 019 / 019.05; ch 019 / moment].
- Plinki led Gyrgum to the egg, showing off the incantations she had inscribed on the surface with fanatical pride and explaining the egg was stolen from the Keepers of the Flame and infused with demonic energy [ch 019 / 019.06].
- The diplomatic encounter collapsed when Plinki revealed that the final ingredient for her ritual was the beating heart of a cleric of Bahamut, causing her to command her followers to attack [ch 019 / 019.06; ch 019 / moment].
- Zalthir utilized magical darkness to ambush Plinki, grappling her and striking her with advantage, dealing 11 damage after a focus point expenditure [ch 019 / 019.07].
- Gyrgum blasted Plinki with a 2nd-level *Inflict Wounds*, which she partially resisted via a Constitution save, taking 12 damage and leaving her severely wounded while held by Zalthir [ch 019 / 019.07; ch 020 / 020.01].
- The derro minions, influenced by Plinki’s leadership, focused on killing Gyrgum to drop his heart over the egg [ch 020 / 020.01].
- Plinki attempted to attack Gyrgum but failed due to the disadvantage caused by the surrounding magical darkness [ch 020 / 020.01].
- Zalthir unleashed a breath attack in a cone, which failed to inflict significant damage but successfully killed Plinki outright [ch 020 / 020.01; ch 020 / moment].
- The GM noted the kill had a "salutary effect" and blinded other enemies, though the damage roll was low [ch 020 / moment].
- After her death, the derro Master Thief proved a tenacious foe, and the GM noted his boss was dead [ch 020 / 020.02].
- Skiit, a derro cultist encountered later, claimed Plinki had promised him a better job and preached that her followers would make the derro "great again" [ch 022 / 022.02; ch 022 / moment].
- Ulnara, a derro savant unaware of Plinki’s death, asked the party to convey praise to Plinki for Ulnara’s assistance, while the GM reminded players that Plinki was the owner of the egg [ch 022 / 022.02; ch 022 / entry].
- The GM explicitly ruled out the theory that Plinki’s obelisk experiments caused the faerzress blink, stating that mystery was solved much later in the campaign [ch 028 / 028.01].
- Plinki’s Journal was one of five rare texts donated by the party to Candlekeep to gain access to the Avowed scholars [ch 054 / 054.05].

## Last Observed State
Plinki was killed in Chapter 020 [ch 020 / entry]. During the final moments of her life, she was grappled by Zalthir in a chamber filled with magical darkness [ch 019 / 019.07; ch 020 / 020.01]. She attempted to attack Gyrgum but failed to land a blow due to the disadvantage imposed by the darkness [ch 020 / 020.01]. She was subsequently slain outright by Zalthir’s breath attack, which rolled low damage but was sufficient to kill her, while also blinding other enemies in the area [ch 020 / 020.01; ch 020 / moment]. With Plinki dead, the derro Master Thief proved a tenacious foe [ch 020 / 020.02]. Although her journal remained in the party's possession until it was donated to Candlekeep in Chapter 054, Plinki herself has been dead since Chapter 020 [ch 054 / 054.05].
