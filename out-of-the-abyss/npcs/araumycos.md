## Identity
Araumycos is a massive, sentient fungal growth located in the Underdark, potentially existing for centuries or millennia [ch 032 / 032.01; ch 032 / entry]. It is distinct from the myconid habitat in Neverlight Grove; it is a separate entity elsewhere in the Underdark [ch 032 / entry]. In the context of the recent events, Araumycos is identified as the intended "groom" in a demonic wedding ritual orchestrated by the Demon Queen Zuggtmoy [ch 031 / entry; ch 032 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Status not established in the summaries. The evidence indicates Araumycos is a sentient entity [ch 032 / entry], but no direct interactions, dialogue, or internal motivations from Araumycos are recorded. It is the subject of Zuggtmoy's intent to ritually mate with or possess [ch 032 / 032.01]. Gasbide, a myconid architect, mentioned it in relation to a "great awakening" and a celebration promised by Sovereign Phylo, though he lacked understanding of what Araumycos actually was [ch 030 / 030.05].

## History with the Party
*   In Neverlight Grove, Gasbide stated his desire to build a fungal tower reaching the surface, "possibly with the aid of Araumycos," though he lacked understanding of what Araumycos actually was [ch 030 / 030.05].
*   Gasbide mentioned a coming "Great Awakening" and celebration involving Araumycos, promised by Sovereign Phylo [ch 030 / moment].
*   The party heard a telepathic wedding hymn declaring that Araumycos and Zuggtmoy would be joined “heart to heart,” becoming one until death parts them [ch 031 / 031.06; ch 031 / entry].
*   Daz, Thorin, and Gyrgum identified Araumycos through pooled lore as a massive, sentient fungal growth in the Underdark, potentially existing for centuries or millennia [ch 032 / 032.01; ch 032 / entry].
*   The party deduced that Zuggtmoy intends to ritually mate with or possess Araumycos, and that the "groom" is currently absent, preventing the true ceremony from beginning [ch 032 / 032.01].
*   In a mock wedding ceremony, a chamberlain and bridesmaid stood in for the absent Araumycos and Zuggtmoy [ch 032 / 032.02].
*   The party identified Araumycos as a separate entity from the myconid habitat in Neverlight Grove; Zuggtmoy appears to require extensive ritual preparation before confronting or possessing it [ch 032 / entry].
