## Identity
*   Ilvara Mizzrym was the high priestess of Lolth and commander of the drow outpost Velkynvelve [ch 002 / entry].
*   She was of House Mizzrym [ch 053 / entry; ch 053 / 053.03].
*   Her belongings included a prayer book to Lolth, a letter from Neverlight Grove, the payroll for Velkynvelve’s guards, confiscated prisoner items, and a corrupted five-tailed scourge with living serpent heads [ch 053 / 053.03].
*   She carried a magical mushroom artifact that served as a conduit for Zuggtmoy’s control [ch 050 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
*   Ilvara displayed contempt for prisoners, threatening Prince Derendil with violence when he demanded dignity [ch 002 / 002.03].
*   She was characterized as smug, comfortable, and exhibiting disturbing instability [ch 002 / moment].
*   She reserved the privilege of killing the party fugitives for herself [ch 031 / 031.03].
*   Her pursuit of the party was driven by the humiliation of their escape, which ruined promises she had made to powerful individuals in Menzoberranzan [ch 049 / 049.03; ch 049 / moment].
*   She descended into madness, falsely communing with a fungal mass she believed was Lolth but was actually Zuggtmoy, becoming fanatically devoted to an entity called "the bride" who demanded the party’s death [ch 049 / 049.03; ch 049 / entry].
*   She was seduced by the heretical "bride" cult of Zuggtmoy and turned most of her followers into mindless fungal servants [ch 050 / 050.02].
*   House Mizzrym’s orders to her became increasingly frantic as she stopped responding, indicating she was obeying house instructions only while Zuggtmoy’s influence initially overtook her [ch 053 / 053.03; ch 053 / entry].

## History with the Party
*   Ilvara Mizzrym is the cruel drow high priestess and commander of the outpost Velkynvelve, where she presided over prisoners from private quarters beneath a spider shrine. [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.03]
*   She treated the prisoners with contempt, including threatening Prince Derendil with a knife when he demanded dignity, and was observed by Thorin to be smug, comfortable, and exhibiting disturbing instability. [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.03; ch 002 / moment]
*   The party concluded that their confiscated possessions were likely stored in a treasure chest in Ilvara’s private quarters, as drow custom concentrates captured wealth with the highest-ranking leader. [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.05]
*   Jorlan Duskryn, formerly Ilvara’s favored lieutenant and lover, offered the party an escape route; he harbored resentment toward her after she replaced him with Shoor Vandree following his disfigurement by a black pudding. [ch 002 / 002.05; ch 002 / moment]
*   The party’s escape from the prison was set up by Jorlan, who offered to leave the slave-pen gate unlocked, driven by a desire to shame Ilvara and Shoor through the loss of valuable prisoners. [ch 002 / 002.05; ch 002 / moment]
*   During the planning stages of the escape, the party debated whether to raid the armory or risk entering Ilvara’s quarters to retrieve their gear, ultimately prioritizing weapons first. [ch 003 / 003.01]
*   Xinaya, a drow sent by Ilvara to search for and capture the party near Neverlight Grove, revealed that Ilvara had reserved the privilege of killing the fugitives for herself; Xinaya was already consumed by mold and fungi when the party found her. [ch 031 / 031.03; ch 031 / moment]
*   The party speculates that Ilvara continues to pursue them across the Underdark, driven by obligation to her house, the humiliation of their escape, and the madness spreading through the region. [ch 032 / entry]
*   Rasharoo warned the party that Ilvara and her drow hunters remained at large and might begin catching up, prompting the party to discuss misleading the drow into believing the party died at the Circle of Welcome. [ch 032 / 032.04]
*   Valen, a scout from House T’sarran, revealed that Ilvara had made promises to two powerful people in Menzoberranzan that the party’s escape ruined, causing her to hunt them relentlessly with a force reduced from twenty to eight members. [ch 049 / entry; ch 049 / 049.03; ch 049 / moment]
*   Ilvara has descended into madness, communing with a giant pulsing fungal mass she falsely believes is the goddess Lolth; she is now fanatically devoted to an entity called "the bride" (Zuggtmoy) who demands the party’s death. [ch 049 / entry; ch 049 / 049.03; ch 049 / moment]
*   Ilvara commands a massive heart fungus creature in her camp and can track the party through the pervasive fungi of the Underdark; "wherever there's fungi, Ilvara seems to have eyes and ears." [ch 049 / entry; ch 049 / 049.03; ch 049 / 049.04; ch 049 / 049.05]
*   The party plans a preemptive strike on Ilvara’s camp at the Fungal Altar, intending to exploit the fracture between Ilvara, the dissident junior priestess Asha Vandree, and the resentful warrior Jorlan Duskryn. [ch 049 / 049.04]
*   Status not established in the summaries, though the party currently faces her forces and plans to engage her camp. [ch 049 / 049.03; ch 049 / 049.05]
* Ilvara Mizzrym is a former high priestess of Lolth who has been corrupted by Zuggtmoy's influence after visiting the Neverlight Grove, appearing visibly fungal and carrying a cat-o'-nine-tails scourge as well as a magical mushroom artifact that serves as a conduit for Zuggtmoy's control [ch 050 / entry].
* The party learned from Asha Vandree that Ilvara had been seduced by the "bride" cult of Zuggtmoy and had turned most of her followers into mindless fungal servants and commanding a massive Heart Fungus, prompting Asha to task the party with killing Ilvara and destroying the mushroom artifact [ch 050 / 050.02].
* The party agreed to the mission, with Daz noting they could loot Ilvara's treasures and that they "can always stab Asha in the back later," and they prepared by having Gyrgum inscribe a Glyph of Warding on a bridge in case of reinforcements [ch 050 / 050.02; ch 050 / 050.03].
* During the initial assault, Zalthir used Shadow Step to strike Ilvara for 31 damage and grappled her, but Ilvara used a legendary action to dissolve into a fungal patch and reappear elsewhere, screaming in fury [ch 050 / 050.04].
* Daz cast a 4th-level fireball that engulfed Ilvara and her mushroom icon; Ilvara saved and took 12 damage, while the mushroom also took 12 damage [ch 050 / 050.04].
* In the ensuing aerial struggle, Zalthir grappled Ilvara, dragged her forty feet into the air, and delivered two critical strikes before releasing her, causing her to fall forty feet and take 17 damage [ch 051 / 051.01; ch 051 / moment].
* Upon landing, Ilvara used a legendary action to force Zalthir to make a Wisdom saving throw, which he failed, leaving him frightened and unable to approach her; she then commanded a drone to attack and moved toward cover [ch 051 / 051.01].
* Ilvara conjured an Insect Plague at the cave entrance, which killed her own lesser minions and injured Glabbagool, and she used a legendary action to create a fungal bloom in Jorlan Duskryn's path to prevent him from reaching her [ch 051 / 051.02; ch 051 / 051.05].
* Ilvara invoked Zuggtmoy, declaring that "the bride" would come to spread chaos, and cast Flamestrike on Daz and Nym Duskryn, though both took only 13 damage due to successful saving throws [ch 051 / 051.05].
* Thorin destroyed the bridge beneath Ilvara, causing it to collapse; Ilvara and Jorlan leapt clear and landed on the cavern floor without suffering significant falling damage [ch 051 / 051.06].
* Gyrgum cast Guiding Bolt at Ilvara, striking true and exploiting her vulnerability to radiant damage; Ilvara detonated, erupting into a twenty-foot cloud of poisonous spores that caught Zalthir and Thorin [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.06; ch 051 / moment].
* The party later rested near the Fungal Altar where Ilvara had held her ritual, marking the location of the confrontation that led to their advancement to eighth level [ch 052 / 052.01].
*   Ilvara’s prayer book contained Zuggtmoy-related marginalia showing a timeline of corruption, while sealed orders from House Mizzrym confirmed she was obeying house instructions when Zuggtmoy’s influence began to overtake her [ch 053 / 053.03; ch 053 / entry].
