## Identity
Prince Livid is a gray ooze who served as one of the Pudding King's two ooze henchmen. The Pudding King referred to Prince Livid (and Princess Ebonmire) as his "pets" or "ooze henchmen" [ch 046 / entry; ch 046 / 046.09]. Prince Livid possessed fire resistance [ch 047 / 047.09; ch 047 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Prince Livid acted as a combatant and ally to the Pudding King, attacking intruders with psychic blasts [ch 047 / 047.03; ch 047 / 047.09]. He expressed confidence in his abilities against oozes, stating: "This works so well against oozes." [ch 047 / moment].

## History with the Party
- Prince Livid is a gray ooze who serves as one of the Pudding King's two ooze henchmen, referred to by the King as a "pet." [ch 046 / entry; ch 046 / 046.09]
- During the initial assault on the Pudding King's throne room, Prince Livid was mentioned by the King to Princess Ebonmire. [ch 046 / 046.09]
- In the ensuing combat, Prince Livid attacked Zalthir with a psychic blast, forcing a DC 10 Intelligence saving throw; Zalthir succeeded, halving the damage from 14 to 7. [ch 047 / 047.03; ch 047 / entry]
- Prince Livid and Princess Ebonmire charged toward the party later in the fight. [ch 047 / 047.07]
- Daz cast a Fireball engulfing both Prince Livid and Princess Ebonmire. Due to his fire resistance, Prince Livid took 14 damage (reduced from 29), leaving him with 8 hit points. [ch 047 / 047.09; ch 047 / entry]
- Prince Livid used another psychic attack on Daz, dealing 5 points of damage. [ch 047 / 047.09]
- Prince Livid was killed by Thorin's second attack, which hit despite being made at disadvantage. [ch 047 / 047.09; ch 047 / entry]

## Last Observed State
Prince Livid was killed during the combat in the Pudding King's throne room. After Daz cast a Fireball that reduced Prince Livid to 8 hit points due to his fire resistance, Prince Livid used a psychic attack on Daz [ch 047 / 047.09; ch 047 / entry]. Prince Livid was subsequently killed by Thorin's second attack, which hit despite being made at disadvantage [ch 047 / 047.09; ch 047 / entry].
