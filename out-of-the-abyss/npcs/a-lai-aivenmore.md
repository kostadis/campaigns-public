## Identity
A'lai Aivenmore is a drow wizard appearing as a tall human man, who was a Great Reader and had lived at Candlekeep for thirty-one years [ch 064 / 064.04; ch 065 / entry]. He held an academic chair but possessed no house, family, or country, defining his entire existence through his institutional position at the library [ch 064 / 064.04; ch 065 / entry]. Unbeknownst to the wider institution, he was a long-term traitor who removed specific books from the library on behalf of Manshoon for eleven years, receiving orders via an anonymous courier wearing a glove [ch 064 / 064.04; ch 065 / entry; ch 067 / 067.01]. Alkrist called him "the real brains of the operation" behind Janussi's murder, and the party believed he coordinated the attacks on the apparent key holders, including Bookwyrm and Daz [ch 061 / 061.07; ch 063 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
A'lai’s primary motivation is the preservation of his academic position and connection to Candlekeep; he stated he would do "a great deal" to keep his chair, as he has no other identity [ch 064 / 064.04]. He operates with calculated ruthlessness and strategic opportunism, attempting to redirect violence and redefine surrender as a tactical "position" rather than a defeat [ch 065 / 065.01; ch 065 / 065.02]. He possesses a photographic memory, which the party utilized to transcribe cryptogram questions [ch 067 / 067.03]. He demonstrates significant academic pride, particularly in his ability to deduce patterns through "patience" rather than direct financial tracing, such as identifying House Baenre as the patron of Daz’s support network [ch 067 / 067.02; ch 067 / moment]. However, he is ultimately insecure and disposable in the eyes of his masters; the realization that Manshoon never deemed him worth meeting face-to-face broke his resolve, leading him to trade secrets for safety [ch 067 / 067.01; ch 067 / entry].

## Last Observed State
As of the conclusion of Chapter 071, A'lai Aivenmore is alive and confined within Candlekeep’s null magic prison, a status arranged through a bargain struck with the Watcher Tadric in Chapter 067 [ch 067 / 067.02]. He accepted this imprisonment in exchange for providing information regarding the Vault of Secrets, two cryptogram riddle answers (“Fustilugs” and “Bow”), and his deduction that House Baenre was funding the operations around Daz [ch 067 / entry; ch 067 / 067.02]. In Chapter 071, the doppelganger Edvaldo confirmed that Manshoon had planned to use A'lai as part of his vault breach scheme and requested the same anti-magic confinement for himself, citing A'lai’s current placement [ch 071 / 071.01; ch 071 / 071.07]. A'lai is currently out of sight, secured in the prison, with his value now derived from his remaining knowledge and the threat Manshoon poses [ch 065 / 065.03; ch 067 / 067.02].

## Relationships
**The Party (Daz, Gyrgum, Thorin, Zalthir):** Alkrist called A'lai the "real brains of the operation" regarding Janussi’s murder, and the party concluded he conspired in it [ch 061 / 061.07]. They view him with contempt, debating punitive measures such as letting the ooze Glabbagool consume him, before ultimately securing his arrest for information [ch 065 / 065.02; ch 065 / moment]. They utilized his photographic memory to solve vault riddles and his intelligence to identify external threats [ch 067 / 067.03].

**Manshoon:** A'lai served Manshoon for eleven years as a mole within Candlekeep, removing books on request [ch 064 / 064.04; ch 065 / entry]. A'lai believed Manshoon’s anonymity was discretion, but with Manshoon’s simulacrum already through the inner sanctum’s door, he realized Manshoon had never come for him and concluded he was merely a pawn [ch 067 / entry; ch 067 / 067.01]. He said he "wasn't worth the face" [ch 067 / moment].

Alkrist acted as the physical applicator of the poison while A'lai provided the intelligence and distractions [ch 060 / entry; ch 061 / 061.08].

**Daral Yashenti:** Daral identified A'lai as a political rival who viewed Daral’s advocacy for poetry as an obstacle to A'lai’s ambition to become Keeper of Tomes [ch 059 / 059.05; ch 060 / 060.04].

**Tadric:** The Watcher Tadric oversaw A'lai’s transfer into the null magic prison, honoring the bargain facilitated by the party [ch 067 / 067.02].

**Edvaldo:** The doppelganger Edvaldo acknowledged that Manshoon kept A'lai ready for the vault breach and requested to be imprisoned adjacent to A'lai in the null magic cells to avoid Manshoon’s reach [ch 071 / 071.01; ch 071 / 071.07].
