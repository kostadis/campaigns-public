## Identity
Eldeth is a female dwarf warrior and tracker who travels with the party as an ally. She is skilled in survival, consistently using this skill to mask the party’s tracks against drow pursuers [ch 005 / 005.06; ch 006 / entry; ch 027 / entry; ch 028 / entry; ch 029 / entry]. Her combat proficiency includes the use of hand crossbows, short swords, and shortbows [ch 003 / 003.02; ch 018 / entry]. A prophecy in the tower — "A dwarf will return to the Hall of Stone with the names of demons in her mouth." — appeared to the party to refer to her [ch 069 / 069.04; ch 069 / moment].

## Personality and Motivations
Eldeth demonstrates practical caution and a strong sense of responsibility toward the party’s survival. She warns against dangerous shortcuts, such as warning Thorin that a gray ooze would destroy his armor [ch 004 / 004.03] and cautioning against stealing a boat after their own nearly sank [ch 009 / 009.05]. She acknowledges the risk of injury in combat, choosing to hang back when severely wounded to avoid death [ch 020 / 020.03].

She holds strong racial prejudices which she is actively working to refine. She initially views duergar as corrupt abominations deserving damnation [ch 010 / entry] and gnomes as less formidable warriors despite their magical skill [ch 035 / entry]. However, her time with the party, particularly the drow Daz, has caused her to reconsider her upbringing’s mantra that "The only good drow is a dead drow," leading her to distinguish between the cult of Lolth and redeemable individuals [ch 035 / entry; ch 035 / 035.05]. She applies practical geological knowledge, soberly noting that stories of escaping flooding caves leave out the people who drowned: "I didn’t hear about all the people who did that and drowned anymore." [ch 034 / entry; ch 034 / 034.07].

Her primary motivation in later chapters shifts to diplomatic duty. She feels responsible for warning her clan and the lords of Gauntlgrym and Mithral Hall about the threats in the Underdark [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / 054.01]. She also swears on her honor to secure Thorin’s acceptance by her clan [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / 054.01].

## Relationships
**Thorin (Dwarf/Giant-raised):** Eldeth has a close, brotherly relationship with Thorin. She values their shared history, stating, "Giant friend, we are like brothers. We have fought together. We have bled together. We have faced the abyssal creatures together." [ch 054 / moment]. Thorin has expressed romantic interest in her ("kind of got a thing for Eldeth") [ch 010 / 010.03], though in Buppido’s Tongue of Madness tirade he insulted Thorin with "Eldeth is never going to go for him" [ch 012 / moment]. Regardless, she sincerely intends to advocate for his acceptance by her clan [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / 054.01].

**Gyrgum (Orc):** Eldeth expresses solidarity with Gyrgum, ironically applying the phrase "dwarfs should stick together" to their alliance despite his race [ch 008 / 008.02]. Gyrgum used Channel Divinity to give her 6 hit points, raising her from 14 to about 20 [ch 022 / entry; ch 022 / 022.01].

**Jimjar (Gnome):** Eldeth has a collaborative relationship with Jimjar, particularly regarding stealth and ghost-handling missions in Blingdenstone [ch 002 / 002.02; ch 031 / 031.04; ch 040 / 040.01; ch 043 / entry].

**Daz (Drow):** Eldeth’s time with Daz has directly influenced her willingness to revise her deep-seated prejudices against drow [ch 035 / entry; ch 035 / 035.05]. On arrival at Neverlight Grove she judged Thorin “crazy” for following the sprouts and stood next to Daz instead [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.05].

**Sarith (Drow/Fungal Zombie):** Eldeth was forced to fight alongside Jimjar to strike down the spore-corrupted remains of Sarith [ch 031 / entry; ch 031 / 031.04].

**Uth-Jadger and Burrow Warden Ghosts:** Eldeth assisted Uth-Jadger and the ghosts in dealing with the ghost Vazuk, a task she undertook alongside Jimjar and Spiderbait [ch 039 / entry; ch 040 / 040.01; ch 043 / entry].
