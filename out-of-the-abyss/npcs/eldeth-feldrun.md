## Identity
- Eldeth Feldrun is a dwarf warrior and scout. The ch 003 entry calls her a "shield dwarf scout" [ch 003 / entry]. She is first seen among the prisoners at Velkynvelve, taking part in the lift labor and in Jimjar's wagers [ch 002 / 002.02; ch 002 / entry].
- She has the party's best Survival: +5 in ch 005 and +6 in ch 028. She is the party's tracker and trail-coverer [ch 005 / 005.06; ch 028 / 028.03].
- She comes from a clan with ties to Mithral Hall and Gauntlgrym [ch 054 / 054.01; ch 072 / 072.01]. One of Gyrgum's religion checks suggested she belongs to the dwarven ruling class [ch 010 / moment]. That is the check's suggestion, not a stated fact.
- She is a long-term companion who has travelled with the party since Velkynvelve [ch 027 / entry]. She parts from them on the surface in ch 054 [ch 054 / 054.01].
- Her ch 003 weapon was a hand crossbow taken from the armory [ch 003 / 003.02]. In ch 018 the GM said she would arm herself, and Errde Blackskull agreed to equip her with medium armor, a short sword, a shortbow and a shield [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.03].

## Personality and Motivations
- **Practical and self-preserving.** She hung back from an ambush press with "I don't want to get killed" [ch 020 / 020.03]. She worried that harvesting crystals would leave evidence for Drow trackers [ch 028 / 028.02]. She called Thorin "crazy" for following the sprouts and stayed beside Daz, apart from the myconids [ch 029 / 029.05].
- **Competent and relied upon.** She led the effort to cover the party's tracks, and Zalthir and Sarith insisted she be protected and helped [ch 006 / 006.02; ch 009 / 009.02]. She also read the flooding temple's cracked wall as an underground river breaking through, and she raised the survivor-bias counterpoint to the swimming-to-safety stories [ch 034 / 034.07].
- **Hatred of the duergar.** She holds a deep, vocal hatred of them [ch 010 / moment]. She called them followers of an evil god [ch 011 / 011.01].
- **Prejudice that has softened.** She was raised to believe "The only good drow is a dead drow" and her captivity reinforced that. Travelling with Daz made her reconsider, and she now blames the cult of Lolth rather than every drow [ch 035 / 035.05].
- **Dwarven solidarity.** She said "we dwarfs should stick together" to Gyrgum, who pointed out he is an orc [ch 008 / 008.02]. The same phrase was used about her by Thorin [ch 005 / moment].
- **Sincere and promise-keeping.** She swore on her honor to Thorin that her clan would embrace him [ch 054 / 054.01]. She later signed her summons "Do not make me a liar" [ch 072 / 072.01].
- **Politically aware.** She wanted to brief the lords before Thorin became the topic. The GM noted a knowing look she gave Zalthir about this [ch 054 / 054.01].
- **Other reactions on record:**
  - She reacted with disgust to Gyrgum's manure comparison for raising myconid children [ch 031 / entry].
  - She was relieved not to be among the "walking zombies" [ch 049 / 049.04].
  - She considers gnomes clever with magic and gems but not formidable fighters [ch 035 / 035.05].

## History with the Party
- Eldeth was among the prisoners at Velkynvelve, where the work groups were assigned. She periodically accepted Jimjar's wagers during the lift labor. [ch 002 / 002.02; ch 002 / entry]
- During that labor Eldeth found a comb, noted as potentially valuable in the filthy slave pen. [ch 002 / 002.02; ch 002 / entry]
- In the guard-tower brawl, Eldeth slipped away from the melee with other prisoners and climbed to the armory. She armed herself with a hand crossbow and fired from the opening between floors. Her first shot missed. [ch 003 / 003.02; ch 003 / entry]
- The ch 003 entry calls her a shield dwarf scout. [ch 003 / entry]
- During the escape from Velkynvelve, Eldeth warned Thorin that the gray ooze's body could eat his newly acquired chain shirt. Thorin then chose to flee rather than fight it. [ch 004 / 004.03; ch 004 / entry]
- In the rockfall aftermath, Eldeth and Jimjar were still trapped after most others were freed. [ch 005 / 005.01]

- On the next day Eldeth took charge of covering the group's tracks, with the party helping her. She rolled a 21 and the pursuit level dropped from four to three. [ch 006 / 006.02; ch 006 / entry]
- Zalthir insisted on helping her: "She's the best. We're helping her." [ch 006 / 006.02]
- During the Kuo-toan civil war, Eldeth moved up beside Gyrgum saying "we dwarfs should stick together." Gyrgum pointed out that he is an orc. [ch 008 / 008.02; ch 008 / entry]
- Eldeth was listed among the NPC prisoners who began frantic escapes toward the boats. [ch 008 / 008.03]

- Sarith insisted the party not abandon Eldeth, saying "She's the only one keeping us hidden." The party gathered her up with Shuushar to carry her to the boat. [ch 009 / 009.02; ch 009 / entry]
