## Identity
Hemeth is a Duergar (gray dwarf) who operates as a professional arms dealer. [ch 008 / entry; ch 009 / entry; ch 012 / entry] He identifies as a "neutral arms dealer" and claims to be interested only in closing deals with factions, rather than engaging in their conflicts. [ch 008 / moment] He is established within the Underdark’s mercantile networks, specifically claiming to know people in Gracklstugh. [ch 009 / 009.03] His trade goods are sourced from suppliers he considers superior to those of other merchants, and he is capable of acquiring +1 weapons. [ch 012 / 012.02]

## Personality and Motivations
Hemeth is primarily motivated by self-preservation and profit. [ch 008 / entry; ch 009 / entry] He exhibits a cynical, survivalist worldview, famously stating that "It's an Underdark-citizen-eat-Underdark-citizen world," and advises avoiding unnecessary conflict or diplomacy with hostile factions like the Kuo-toa because the last time he talked to them he was "wrapped up in ropes and about to be hanged". [ch 009 / 009.05; ch 009 / moment] Despite his professed neutrality, he is easily offended by competition, particularly when the party considers purchasing goods from rivals, and insists on offering his own wares at a discount after being passed over. [ch 012 / 012.02; ch 012 / entry] He maintains a pragmatic relationship with the party, offering a "2% discount" on his high-quality weapons in gratitude for saving his life, while simultaneously employing dark humor regarding the lethality of his products and their users. [ch 012 / 012.02; ch 012 / moment] He possesses specific knowledge of Duergar capabilities, such as their invisibility durations, and uses this expertise to warn the party of ambushes. [ch 010 / 010.02; ch 010 / entry]

## History with the Party
*   Hemeth was held captive by the Kuo-toa and prepared for sacrifice at the Altar of the Deep Father in Sloobludop. [ch 008 / 008.01; ch 008 / entry]
*   While awaiting sacrifice, Hemeth whispered to Gyrgum to tell him when to act and instructed the party to look him up in Gracklstugh if they survived the chaos. [ch 008 / 008.01; ch 008 / moment]
*   During the journey across the Darklake, Hemeth traveled with the party and insisted they head to Gracklstugh, claiming he knew people there. [ch 009 / 009.03; ch 009 / entry]
*   Hemeth advised against negotiating with Kuo-toa refugees, citing a past experience where he was captured and nearly hanged, and noted their boat was a potential target if the party's vessel sank. [ch 009 / 009.05; ch 009 / moment]
*   When the party encountered an abandoned duergar keelboat, Hemeth identified it and demonstrated the duergar ability to turn invisible to warn of potential ambushes. [ch 010 / 010.02; ch 010 / moment]
*   Hemeth informed Thorin that duergar invisibility could last between thirty minutes and an hour. [ch 010 / 010.02; ch 010 / entry]
*   When Thorin floated selling their duergar prisoner, Brannum Redmarch, Hemeth announced he "knew a guy" who could facilitate the transaction. [ch 011 / 011.01; ch 011 / moment]
*   Hemeth joined the combat against a beholder zombie, rowing toward the fight with the companion crew and firing crossbow volleys alongside them. [ch 011 / 011.03]
*   Upon arriving at Gracklstugh, Hemeth advised the party to enter through the docks rather than the guarded main gates, warning that "this is not the time or place for heroics." [ch 011 / 011.07; ch 011 / moment]
*   In Gracklstugh, Hemeth explained the city's strict laws restricting non-duergar to the Darklake district and the lethal penalties for theft. [ch 012 / 012.01]
*   Hemeth expressed offense when the party negotiated with Brannum for magical items, offering his own high-quality weapons instead and boasting about their craftsmanship. [ch 012 / 012.02; ch 012 / entry]
*   As a token of gratitude for saving his life, Hemeth offered the party a "2% discount" on his goods. [ch 012 / 012.02; ch 012 / moment]
*   Hemeth left on an errand with Brannum Redmarch as strange behaviors among the duergar merchants escalated in the Blade Bazaar. [ch 012 / 012.04]

## Relationships
*   **The Party:** Hemeth views them as saviors and customers. He instructed them to "look me up in Gracklstugh" upon their rescue from the Kuo-toa. [ch 008 / 008.01; ch 008 / moment] He offered them advice on navigating Gracklstugh’s laws and entered the city through the docks with them. [ch 011 / 011.07; ch 011 / moment] He fought alongside them against a beholder zombie. [ch 011 / 011.03] He is currently providing them with a discount and goods, though he remains competitive regarding the party's alternative acquisition of magical items through Brannum. [ch 012 / 012.02]
*   **Brannum Redmarch:** A captive Duergar turned ally for procurement tasks. Hemeth facilitated the party's initial idea to sell Brannum into slavery by noting he "knows a guy." [ch 011 / 011.01; ch 011 / moment] Hemeth was offended at being passed over for Brannum, and the two later left together on an errand. [ch 012 / 012.02; ch 012 / 012.04]
*   **Buppido:** Hemeth shares a rapport with Buppido, notably exchanging the cryptic "I know a guy" / "the other guy" exchange regarding Brannum's sale, and jointly advising the party on Gracklstugh’s advantages. [ch 011 / moment; ch 011 / 011.07]
*   **Kuo-toa:** Hemeth harbors a deep distrust of the Kuo-toa, citing a past experience where he was captured and nearly hanged, and advising against any negotiation with their refugees. [ch 009 / 009.05; ch 009 / moment]
