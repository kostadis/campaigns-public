## Identity
Kazook Pickshine is a gnome alchemist and representative of the Miners' Guild residing in Blingdenstone [ch 018 / 018.06; ch 039 / entry]. He is known for his expertise in creating spell gems, a process he guards closely [ch 043 / 043.06; ch 043 / entry]. Kazook possesses knowledge of chemical formulas to combat acidic threats, specifically those posed by oozes [ch 039 / 039.09; ch 043 / 043.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Kazook exhibits a demeanor described as that of a "snake-oil salesman" [ch 043 / 043.06; ch 043 / entry]. He is notably secretive and defensive regarding his professional work; when party members attempted to observe his spell gem creation, he physically blocked their view [ch 043 / 043.06]. Despite this professional guardedness, he was upset when the party mocked Bimble [ch 043 / 043.06]. When the party mocked his infected friend Bimble, Kazook rebuked them, asking for compassion and clarifying the depth of their relationship [ch 043 / 043.06].

His primary motivation during the recent crisis was the preservation of the gnome army's equipment. He sought to prevent ooze acid from destroying weapons and armor, initially proposing the shipment of salt to Gracklstugh as an alternative to gathering rare ingredients [ch 039 / 039.09; ch 039 / entry]. He later pursued a localized solution, utilizing Ghost Hold Lichen found beneath the city rather than in Neverlight Grove, to brew protective coatings [ch 043 / 043.05; ch 045 / 045.03].

## History with the Party
*   The party was advised by Jimjar to sell five empty spell gems, which were identified as banal by others but valuable to gnomish defenses, specifically to Kazook Pickshine [ch 018 / 018.06].
*   Kazook attended the moot convened by Daz to discuss the Pudding King threat, sitting alongside other leaders like Chief Dorbo and Nomi Pathshutter [ch 039 / 039.07].
*   Representing the Miners' Guild, Kazook warned that ooze acid would destroy the gnome army's weapons and proposed two solutions: shipping salt to Gracklstugh for better weapons or gathering ingredients from Neverlight Grove to brew a protective treatment [ch 039 / 039.09; ch 039 / entry].
*   The party chose the Neverlight Grove option because Gracklstugh's status was uncertain and previous relations were poor [ch 039 / entry].
*   Kazook claimed to have a simpler solution to the acid problem than traveling to Neverlight Grove and was waiting on his scout, Bimble, for results [ch 043 / 043.03].
*   Kazook arrived at the meeting with news that Bimble had found Ghost Hold Lichen below the city instead of in the Grove, but Bimble had returned infected by the "Lady of Rot" (Zuggtmoy) [ch 043 / 043.05; ch 043 / entry].
*   Kazook exhibited a "snake-oil salesman" demeanor and physically blocked the party’s view of his spell gem creation process when they visited his chambers [ch 043 / 043.06; ch 043 / entry].
*   When the party mocked the infected Bimble, Kazook asked for compassion, clarifying that Bimble was a dear friend rather than just an assistant [ch 043 / 043.06].
*   Daz convinced Kazook to allow the use of beakers for investigation, and the party successfully pinpointed the location of the lichen using a natural 20 [ch 043 / 043.06].
*   The lichen harvested by the party serves as the ingredient for Kazook to brew an acid-resistant weapon coating for the battle [ch 044 / 044.02].
*   Kazook prepared potions from the lichen, stating he had built enough potion to cover the weapons for the upcoming battle [ch 045 / 045.03; ch 045 / entry].

## Last Observed State
Kazook was last seen preparing for the battle against the Pudding King in Blingdenstone. Following the party's successful harvest of Ghost Hold Lichen, Kazook prepared potions from the lichen to serve as an acid-resistant coating for the army's weapons [ch 044 / 044.02; ch 045 / 045.03]. He stated, "I have now built enough of the potion... we will cover the weapons," indicating the task was complete [ch 045 / 045.03]. His status is not established in the summaries following this preparation phase.

## Relationships
**Bimble:** Kazook’s scout and "gopher." Kazook considers Bimble a dear friend, not merely an assistant, a distinction he made forcefully to the party [ch 043 / 043.03; ch 043 / 043.06]. Bimble was infected by the "Lady of Rot" (Zuggtmoy) while gathering lichen, a condition Kazook described as returning "wrong" [ch 043 / 043.05; ch 043 / moment].

**The Party:** Werz gave the party empty spell gems as a gesture of good faith, and the party acted on Jimjar’s advice that Kazook would pay handsomely for them [ch 018 / 018.06]. Kazook collaborated with the party to solve the acid-weapon problem, initially resisting their intrusion into his privacy but eventually allowing Daz to use his beakers for investigation [ch 043 / 043.06].

**Blingdenstone Leadership:** Kazook attended the moot convened by Daz alongside leaders such as Chief Dorbo and Nomi Pathshutter [ch 039 / 039.07]. He presented himself to the council as a problem-solver for the military’s equipment concerns [ch 039 / 039.09].
