## Identity
Turvy is a deep gnome and the twin brother of Topsy [ch 002 / entry; ch 004 / entry]. He was initially held as a prisoner alongside other captives [ch 002 / entry; ch 003 / 003.02]. He possesses the ability to transform into a rat, a trait shared with his twin [ch 004 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Turvy is pragmatic and willing to assist with illicit activities, such as theft, creating distractions, and scavenging food to aid the prisoners [ch 002 / entry]. He displays a reluctance to return to Blingdenstone, a city where he and his twin are unwelcome due to their past activities [ch 004 / entry]. During conflicts, he follows the lead of stronger companions, such as Thorin, executing tasks like digging out trapped allies when directed [ch 005 / 005.02]. He is susceptible to overwhelming magical or divine madness, losing his grip on sanity when witnessing high-level threats like the Prince of Demons [ch 009 / 009.01; ch 009 / 009.02].

## History with the Party
- Turvy is identified as a deep gnome prisoner and twin to Topsy, assisting with theft, distractions, and scavenging food. He helped secure two kitchen knives for the prisoners after Gyrgum delegated the theft attempt to the twins. [ch 002 / entry]
- During the skirmish in the guard tower, Turvy was part of the group that disengaged from the melee, scrambled upstairs, and armed themselves with short swords and hand crossbows. [ch 003 / 003.02]
- Turvy fired a hand crossbow alongside Topsy during the guard tower battle; one of the twin's bolts struck the remaining ordinary guard for five damage. [ch 003 / 003.02]
- Turvy was mentioned as having also transformed into a rat to descend the outpost heights, sharing his twin's reluctance to return to Blingdenstone. [ch 004 / entry]
- Turvy participated in the group's defense against a carrion crawler, firing a volley of crossbow bolts that included critical hits and dealt 20 damage total from the companions. [ch 005 / entry; ch 005 / 005.02]
- When Eldeth and Jimjar became trapped or incapacitated during the carrion crawler fight, Thorin sent Topsy and Turvy to dig out Eldeth ("dwarves stick together") and rescue Jimjar. [ch 005 / 005.02]
- Turvy was overcome by madness when witnessing the Prince of Demons, Demogorgon, erupt from the waters at Sloobludop. [ch 009 / 009.01; ch 009 / 009.02]
- During the escape from Sloobludop, Thorin carried the small and light twins Topsy and Turvy because they were incapacitated by madness. [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.02]
- In Gracklstugh, the Topsy-Turvy twins quietly slipped away from the group during the night while the party took a long rest in a shared room. [ch 014 / 014.03]

## Last Observed State
The last confirmed observation of Turvy occurred in Gracklstugh, where he was part of a group staying in a shared lodging room [ch 014 / 014.03]. During the party's long rest, Turvy and his twin Topsy "quietly slipped away" [ch 014 / 014.03]. The party noticed their disappearance in the morning [ch 014 / 014.03]. Zalthir asked whether they were the wererats (they are), and the GM notes that they "haven't" necessarily left for good, though their current physical location or status beyond their departure from the lodging is not established in the summaries [ch 014 / 014.03].

## Relationships
**Topsy**: His twin brother. They act as a unit, often sharing thoughts, actions, and fates, such as being carried by Thorin during a rescue [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.02]. They share a mutual aversion to Blingdenstone [ch 004 / entry].
**Thorin**: A dwarf companion. Thorin ordered Turvy and Topsy to dig out trapped companions during a battle with a carrion crawler [ch 005 / 005.02]. Thorin also physically carried Turvy when he was incapacitated by madness during the escape from Sloobludop [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.02].
**Gyrgum**: A cleric who delegated theft attempts to Turvy and his twin early in their imprisonment [ch 002 / entry].
**Drow Guards**: Former captors whom Turvy fought against during a prison breakout, using armed resistance including hand crossbows [ch 003 / 003.02].
