## Identity
Droki is a derro agent and courier for the Gray Ghosts Thieves Guild who transports messages and supplies for multiple factions [ch 013 / 013.06; ch 014 / entry; ch 015 / entry]. He is characterized by a pale face, a floppy hat adorned with two tentacle-like protrusions, and a demeanor that moves with unnatural, magical speed while muttering about being late [ch 015 / 015.05; ch 015 / moment]. Droki follows an obsessive, repetitive route through the Whorlstone Tunnels, leaving a messy trail of food droppings behind him [ch 016 / entry]. The GM describes him as a "half-crazed… drug-addled mule" whom factions use because they view him as utterly innocuous and predictable [ch 016 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Droki’s primary motivation appears to be a compulsive adherence to schedules and routes, driven by a fear of angering his employers [ch 015 / moment]. He refers to himself in the third person and exhibits paranoid or obsessive behaviors, such as passing specific landmarks like Pelek’s resting place roughly once a day [ch 015 / entry; ch 016 / entry]. He fails to notice when he is being followed; the GM calls him "a creature of ridiculous habits" [ch 015 / entry; ch 017 / 017.02]. He is not described as having independent political goals; rather, he serves as a neutral logistical node that both the Stone Guard and the Keepers of the Flame can manipulate to lead the party into the Whorlstone Caverns [ch 018 / 018.01].

## History with the Party
*   **Mission Assignment**: Captain Errde Blackskull of the Stone Guard tasked the party with tracking, seizing, or killing Droki, reporting that the derro was evading patrols as if he knew their positions in advance and lived in the volatile West Cleft District [ch 013 / 013.06].
*   **Behavioral Profile**: Droki is described as a derro agent for the Gray Ghosts who carries messages, is a courier for multiple factions, and moves with magical speed while muttering about being late [ch 013 / 013.06; ch 014 / entry; ch 015 / entry].
*   **Competing Orders**: Gartokkar of the Keepers of the Flame also tasked the party with tracking Droki, identifying him as a messenger for the Gray Ghosts Thieves Guild who carried messages and supplies [ch 014 / 014.07].
*   **First Sighting**: The party spotted Droki in the West Cleft District; he was a pale-faced derro in a floppy hat with tentacle-like protrusions, muttering to himself and moving with unnatural speed before vanishing down a narrow fissure [ch 015 / 015.05].
*   **Pursuit Entry**: The party followed Droki into the Whorlstone Tunnels, reasoning that tracking him might lead them to every faction he served [ch 015 / 015.05].
*   **Tunnel Behavior**: Droki moved through the tunnels with magical speed, muttering about being late, and failed to notice he was being followed [ch 015 / entry].
*   **Route and Habits**: Droki follows an obsessive, repetitive route through the Whorlstone Tunnels, passing Pelek’s resting place about once a day and leaving a trail of food droppings [ch 016 / entry].
*   **Tracking Methods**: The party tracked Droki through the tunnels by following his messy trail of food scraps and his predictable circuit [ch 016 / 016.02; ch 017 / 017.02].
*   **Information Broker**: Both the Keepers of the Flame and the Stone Guard knew Droki would lead the party to specific information within the Whorlstone Caverns, a fact they revealed after the party met Droki [ch 018 / 018.01].

## Last Observed State
Status not established in the summaries. The party last actively tracked Droki in the Whorlstone Tunnels, following his food-dropping trail to a solid rock wall riddled with cracks too small for the party to pass through [ch 016 / 016.02].
