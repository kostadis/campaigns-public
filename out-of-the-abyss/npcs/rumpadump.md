## Identity
Rumpadump is a myconid sprout and a nervous, energetic scout who leads the Circle of Explorers within Neverlight Grove [ch 018 / entry; ch 031 / entry]. He was originally from Neverlight Grove but was teleported to Whorlstone Caverns by spores released by a myconid named Voosbur [ch 025 / 025.04]. He returned to the grove with the party, where he resumed his role as a pathfinder and advocate for nomadic myconid traditions [ch 029 / 029.05; ch 031 / entry]. He is distinct from other myconids in his avoidance of the corruption affecting the colony, having refused to consume "silly mushrooms" during his initial teleportation [ch 025 / 025.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Rumpadump is characterized by deep anxiety regarding the changes in his colony; he expresses fear that the infection is spreading and that the colony changes for the worse after every expedition [ch 025 / 025.04; ch 031 / 031.02]. He is pragmatic and forward-thinking, having prepared escape routes and hidden nutrient caches in the exit tunnels in anticipation of the colony's collapse [ch 031 / 031.02]. He holds strong ideological convictions, advocating for a nomadic lifestyle and dismissing plans to colonize the Overbright as "completely insane and perverse," insisting that myconids belong in the darkness of the Underdark [ch 031 / 031.02]. He distrusts the sovereign Phylo [ch 031 / 031.02]. Conversely, he is deeply loyal to Stool and trusts Basidia, accepting the party solely on her recommendation [ch 018 / 018.06; ch 031 / 031.02]. Gyrgum convinced him that Neverlight Grove was no longer what it had been and that he needed to leave immediately [ch 032 / 032.03].

## History with the Party
*   Rumpadump was identified by Stool as his friend and a myconid sprout who appeared depressed and distant near a group of strangely dancing myconids [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.06].
*   Stool frequently mentioned Rumpadump to the party, urging them to rescue him or check on him while the party focused on other objectives such as dealing with assassins, resting, or looting [ch 018 / 018.06; ch 023 / 023.02; ch 024 / 024.04; ch 024 / moment].
*   Stool insisted that Rumpadump was not a monster and would not harm the party, claiming that myconids do not attack unnecessarily and that Rumpadump’s concern was "very worrisome" [ch 018 / 018.06; ch 023 / 023.02].
*   The party eventually agreed to accompany Stool to find Rumpadump, prioritizing this detour due to loyalty to Stool despite concerns about infected creatures and spores [ch 025 / 025.02].
*   The party found Rumpadump in a natural cave among frantic, dancing myconids and mold-covered ape-creatures; Rumpadump was calm and studious, standing apart from the chaos [ch 025 / 025.03].
*   Rumpadump explained that his group had been teleported from Neverlight Grove to Whorlstone Caverns by strange spores released by a myconid named Voosbur [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.04].
*   Rumpadump stated that he avoided the madness affecting his kin by not consuming the suspicious mushrooms ("silly mushrooms") that the others ate after their teleportation [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.04].
*   Rumpadump expressed fear that the infection had spread back to the rest of his kin in Neverlight Grove and urged the party to help him return there [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.04].
*   Rumpadump indicated that the Myconid Sovereign in the Neverlight Grove might know a way out of the Underdark, making the Grove a potential destination [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.04].
*   Rumpadump was unaffected by a stirge swarm ambush on the Darklake because he, like Stool, lacks blood [ch 027 / entry; ch 027 / 027.05].
*   Upon arriving at Neverlight Grove, Rumpadump cried "We're home! We're home! We're home!" and ran ahead with Stool to greet the sovereigns [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.05].
*   Rumpadump traveled with the party and had returned home to Neverlight Grove, though he had originally been teleported there by a crazed myconid [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.05].
*   When Gyrgum offered Rumpadump and Stool the chance to continue adventuring with the party, Rumpadump preferred to stay home but replied, "That's very sweet of you" [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.05].

## Last Observed State
Rumpadump fled Neverlight Grove with Basidia, Stool, and other surviving myconids, having been persuaded by Gyrgum that the grove was no longer safe [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.03; ch 032 / 032.04].
