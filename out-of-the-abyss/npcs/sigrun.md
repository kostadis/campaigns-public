## Identity

- Sigrun is a veteran ranger turned archivist who accompanies expeditions into the Underdark to gather information. She joined the party to map its journey and carries a survey gadget. [ch 076 / entry]
- Lord Eravien Haund arranged her participation; she claims no faction allegiance. [ch 076 / 076.01]

## Personality and Motivations

- Pragmatic and fond of philosophical sayings, she favors fieldwork and frequently recalls her younger days ranging across the North. [ch 076 / entry]
- She believes strong state power protects innocent people. [ch 076 / 076.01]
- She explains that a map captures a place at a particular moment, reflecting her awareness that locations change. [ch 076 / 076.08]
- She enthusiastically swims in freezing underground water and mentions her granddaughter during the encounter. [ch 076 / 076.06]

## History with the Party

- Joined the expedition, demonstrated her survey gadget, and gladly accepted responsibility for mapping. Shared cartography interests immediately connected her with Thorin. [ch 076 / 076.01]
- By the second day of the surface journey, the expedition had adjusted to her pace. [ch 076 / 076.02]
- At the Underdark entrance, recalled traveling underground without lizard mounts and compared her former daily walking distance with the guides’ pace. [ch 076 / 076.03]
- Around the seventh day underground, swam enthusiastically in a freezing stream and asked that her granddaughter not be told. [ch 076 / 076.06]
- On the nineteenth day underground, discussed maps and changing places with Thorin and began to volunteer to scout a fungal cavern. The party was reminded that protecting her was part of its Lords’ Alliance obligation. [ch 076 / 076.08]

## Last Observed State

Sigrun was with the expedition at the fungal cavern, beginning to volunteer as a scout. Daz identified ten duergar spore servants among the fungus; the party remained in discussion, with no crossing or combat begun before the session ended. [ch 076 / 076.08]

## Relationships

- **Thorin:** Their shared interest in cartography developed into friendship over nineteen days. Their recurring philosophical sayings began to irritate others. [ch 076 / 076.08]
- **Lord Eravien Haund:** Arranged her participation in the expedition. [ch 076 / 076.01]
- **Lords’ Alliance:** Keeping her alive is part of the party’s obligation to the Alliance, despite her stated lack of faction allegiance. [ch 076 / 076.01; ch 076 / 076.08]
- **Granddaughter:** Sigrun says her granddaughter would want to join the underground swim if told about it. [ch 076 / 076.06]
