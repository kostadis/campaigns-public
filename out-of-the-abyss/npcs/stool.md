## Identity
Stool is a young Myconid Sprout [ch 002 / 002.01; ch 004 / entry]. He possesses rapport spores that allow everyone within fifteen feet to communicate telepathically as a shared "party line," though this function is limited by range [ch 002 / entry]. Stool was originally kidnapped and held as a prisoner in Velkynvelve, assigned to cleaning duties alongside other captives [ch 002 / 002.01; ch 030 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Stool displays the demeanor and emotional maturity of an eight-year-old child [ch 004 / entry]. He chose Gyrgum as his protector, initially bonding with him as his "bestest friend" and relying on the orc for safety [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.04]. Stool prioritizes the safety of his own kind, frequently urging the party to rescue his friend Rumpadump, insisting that "Myconids don't attack people unnecessarily" and that his friend’s presence ensures safety [ch 018 / 018.06]. When his requests are denied, he exhibits childish behavior, such as falling asleep to be carried, throwing tantrums, or threatening to go alone [ch 023 / 023.02; ch 025 / 025.02]. When Zalthir refused to go, he declared Thorin "much nicer than Zalthir" and attempting to switch friendships, while labeling Zalthir a "scaredy cat" [ch 025 / 025.02]. Stool also holds specific views on biological determinism, arguing that quaggoths are "bred to be violent" [ch 018 / moment].

## Last Observed State
Stool departed Neverlight Grove with Basidia and the surviving myconids to be with his own kind [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.04]. Prior to leaving, he was persuaded by Gyrgum to abandon the grove, recognizing that the conflict between Sovereign Phylo and Basidia marked the end of the grove as he knew it, and expressing sadness at the changes to his home [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.03]. ## Relationships
**Gyrgum:** Stool views Gyrgum as his protector and best friend, hoping the orc will keep him safe [ch 004 / entry]. Gyrgum eventually persuades Stool to flee Neverlight Grove when the situation becomes untenable [ch 032 / 032.03].
**Thorin:** Thorin carries Stool when the myconid is tired or slow [ch 005 / 005.05; ch 029 / 029.05]. Stool favors Thorin’s kindness, stating he is "much nicer than Zalthir" [ch 025 / 025.02].
**Zalthir:** Stool frequently clashes with Zalthir, calling him a "scaredy cat" and questioning his willingness to help rescue myconids [ch 025 / 025.02]. Zalthir worries about Stool’s potential exposure to Phylo’s infection prior to the final rescue [ch 032 / 032.03].
**Rumpadump:** Stool is deeply worried about his friend Rumpadump, repeatedly pleading for his rescue and trusting his judgment [ch 018 / 018.06]. The two return to Neverlight Grove together, with Stool crediting the party for their safety [ch 029 / 029.05].
**Basidia:** Stool departs with Basidia and the surviving myconids after the grove falls [ch 032 / 032.04].
