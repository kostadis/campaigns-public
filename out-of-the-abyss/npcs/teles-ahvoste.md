## Identity
Teles Ahvoste is a Great Reader of Candlekeep, an elderly figure described as an "older Great Reader" [ch 059 / entry; ch 061 / entry]. He is generally regarded as more skilled in magic than Janussi; Sylvira believes he has been hostile toward Janussi since being passed over for the position of Keeper of Tomes [ch 061 / entry; ch 061 / 061.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Sylvira believes Teles has been hostile toward Janussi for nearly twenty years after being passed over for Keeper of Tomes, and Daral claims Teles thought he was first in line last time around [ch 059 / entry; ch 059 / 059.05; ch 061 / 061.05]. His behavior is described by Daral Yashenti as that of a "deluded old git" who believes he is first in line for power despite the political reality [ch 059 / 059.05]. Teles is willing to engage in public disruption of authority, evidenced by his screaming match with Janussi where he shouted "hypocrite" before storming out [ch 058 / 058.04; ch 058 / 058.05]. At the Deadwinter feast he mocked Alkrist’s military-history proposal by comparing it to Fheminor Scrivenbark’s program [ch 060 / 060.06]. Additionally, Sprig Summerfoot revealed to the party that Irony, a junior Avowed member, had been trying to thwart unwanted advances from Teles [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.07].
