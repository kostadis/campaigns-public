## Identity
Alkrist is a Dragonborn Great Reader within Candlekeep who holds the title of Reader. [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.06] He was an active member of the institution, attending social functions like the Deadwinter feast and participating in council meetings. [ch 059 / 059.02; ch 060 / 060.06] He proposed a work titled *Tales of War by the Generals That Waged Them* at the Deadwinter feast, and kept *1001 Tashalian Nights* in his chambers. [ch 060 / 060.06; ch 060 / 060.09]

## Personality and Motivations
Alkrist displayed conspicuous cheerfulness at the Deadwinter feast, though this behavior appeared to mask underlying deceit. [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.07] He harbored resentment toward Janussi, specifically regarding Janussi’s denial of Alkrist's leadership potential and the command to abandon germ-warfare research. [ch 061 / entry; ch 061 / 061.07] He conspired with A'lai to poison Janussi; asked what they discussed, he said "We were discussing how to deal with our mutual problem of Janussi." [ch 061 / entry; ch 061 / 061.07]

His methodology involved experimentation; evidence found in his chambers included jars of dead rats with blistered skin, suggesting testing of diseases or poisons. [ch 060 / 060.09; ch 060 / moment] He was capable of premeditated, indirect murder, coating a gift for Janussi with Midnight Tears stolen from the Oak Tree Apothecary. [ch 061 / 061.07] He tried to minimise his guilt, stating, “I didn’t actually kill him. I just, you know, put some poison on a book.” [ch 061 / moment] He attempted to obscure his involvement by burning evidence, such as a scrap of paper and the poison label, though he was observed destroying a note during the feast. [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.07; ch 061 / 061.07] Upon being subjected to a *Zone of Truth* cast by Kalan, he confessed to the conspiracy but claimed the confession was extracted by entrapment or mind control. [ch 061 / entry; ch 061 / moment] He expressed genuine shock that Janussi’s heart had been removed, indicating his intent was limited to poisoning rather than the subsequent mutilation. [ch 061 / 061.07]

## Last Observed State
Alkrist was confined securely in Candlekeep’s prison by Kalan’s order. [ch 062 / entry; ch 062 / 062.04] His confinement status was described as "neutralized" by ch 065, contributing to the collapse of Manshoon’s defensive planning. [ch 065 / 065.03] Manshoon later stated that the party’s disruption of plans involving Alkrist cost him staggering amounts of magic and a Staff of Power. [ch 070 / 070.01]

During his confinement, Zalthir used the creature Glabbagool to secretly steal a potion of flying from Alkrist’s safe. [ch 062 / 062.04; ch 062 / moment] The safe also contained gold, two potions, and a gold coin engraved with a red herring. [ch 063 / entry] Alkrist did not participate in the events surrounding the High Tower keys or the final confrontation with Manshoon; his room’s contents were examined, and he was last described as confined by Kalan's order in Chapter 062 [ch 062 / entry]. [ch 063 / entry; ch 063 / 063.01] The evidence does not establish his status (alive, dead, or escaped) beyond the fact of his neutralization by ch 065. [ch 065 / 065.03]
