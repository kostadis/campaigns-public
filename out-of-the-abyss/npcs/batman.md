## Identity
Batman is a bat familiar summoned by Daz [ch 038 / entry]. Chosen specifically for echolocation and the ability to navigate dark caverns [ch 038 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
The evidence describes Batman's function rather than distinct personality traits. He acts as a scout, relaying information telepathically to Daz within a hundred-foot radius [ch 038 / entry]. He follows Daz’s commands to conduct breadth-first reconnaissance of dangerous areas [ch 038 / 038.04].

## History with the Party
*   Batman is a bat familiar summoned by Daz to scout dangerous areas, chosen for its echolocation and ability to navigate dark caverns. [ch 038 / entry]
*   During the exploration of the ooze caverns, Batman conducted a breadth-first reconnaissance of surrounding passages, discovering abandoned gnomish districts, ooze-filled passages, a destroyed fungal barrier, and the Pudding King’s throne room. [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / 038.04]
*   Batman’s scouting allowed the party to locate and observe the Pudding King without entering the slime-covered chambers or attracting the Pudding King’s attention, earning Daz inspiration for the successful reconnaissance. [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / 038.04]
*   Batman was later sent by Daz into the Drow Statue Chamber to test the animation mechanism. [ch 041 / entry]
*   In the Drow Statue Chamber, the animated statues chased Batman through the cavern, occupying all six animation threads of the malevolent entity. [ch 041 / entry]

## Last Observed State
