## Identity
Sarith Kzekarit was a drow who served as a guide for the party through the Underdark [ch 004 / entry; ch 031 / entry]. He claimed to have been one of Lolth’s own guards [ch 010 / 010.04]. He was infected with unknown spores, likely originating from Zuggtmoy, which affected his behavior long before he joined the party [ch 025 / 025.04; ch 029 / 029.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Sarith displayed erratic and manic behavior, often laughing madly or making strange associations, such as describing revenge and the "Tongue of Madness" fungus as "tasty" [ch 004 / moment; ch 004 / 004.05]. Despite his instability, he served as navigator, appearing truthful about his knowledge of the tunnels, and explained the Darklake and the blinding quartz crystals [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 028 / 028.01]. He was pragmatic but prone to cruelty, advocating for torture during interrogations and expressing disappointment when it was not employed [ch 011 / 011.01; ch 011 / moment]. He also showed moments of surprising insight, urging the party to keep the madwoman Eldeth with them during an escape because she was their only cover [ch 009 / 009.02]. As his infection progressed, he became agitated by dancing myconids and spoke of a "Dark Lady" calling to him [ch 025 / 025.03; ch 025 / 025.04].

## History with the Party
- Sarith was mentioned in the slave pen, where he rambled through insane tangents while the prisoners pooled observations and listened to Buppido’s escape plan [ch 002 / 002.04].
- During the skirmish in the guard tower, Sarith climbed to the armory, armed himself, and attacked a drow guard, killing him while hurling bitter insults [ch 003 / 003.02; ch 003 / entry].
- In the final confrontation against the Elite Warrior Imbros, Sarith was tempted to shout and alert the outpost, but was calmed by Gyrgum’s spiritual appeal and subsequently delivered the killing blow to Imbros [ch 003 / 003.03; ch 003 / moment].
- Following the battle, Sarith scavenged studded leather armor from a fallen drow warrior [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.01].
- Sarith advised the party against sabotaging Velkynvelve, arguing for a quick escape to avoid greater retaliation, though his advice was accompanied by a mad cackle about the tastiness of revenge [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.01; ch 004 / 004.02; ch 004 / moment].
- Sarith volunteered as the party’s navigator through the Underdark, appearing truthful about his familiarity with the tunnels and recommending they head toward the Darklake [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.04].
- While foraging, Sarith identified the Tongue of Madness fungus as “the tastiest thing in the world” [ch 004 / 004.05].
- Sarith was not trapped by the rockfall that endangered the party; he was noted as having not been buried under the rocks [ch 005 / 005.01; ch 005 / entry].
- During the carrion crawler ambush, Sarith contributed to a volley of hand crossbow bolts that dealt significant damage, while a tentacle aimed at him missed spectacularly [ch 005 / 005.02].
- While leading the group through Faerzress-filled tunnels at a fast pace, Sarith nearly led the party astray due to pride in his navigational skills, though he begrudgingly accepted Gyrgum’s correction to stay on course [ch 005 / 005.06; ch 005 / moment].
- Sarith successfully guided the party to the Silken Paths, a vast chasm of spider webs, correctly confirming the party’s heading and noting that the paths are always changing [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.04].
*   After Fargas revealed the history of the tomb of Brysis of Khaem, Sarith narrowed its possible location to "three or four places" and drew a rough map of the region, including the Darklake and the drow outpost of Velkynvelve, while clarifying that the Darklake is a three-dimensional web of tunnels rather than a single body of water [ch 007 / entry; ch 007 / 007.02].
*   During the chaotic escape from Sloobludop following Demogorgon's manifestation, Sarith urged the party not to abandon the madwoman Eldeth, noting that she was the only one keeping them hidden [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.02].
*   While navigating the Darklake, Sarith warned the party that the map left by their departed companion Shuushar was imperfect [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.01].
*   Sarith expressed surprise at Thorin's competent navigation, asking if Thorin knew where he was going, to which Thorin replied he was "following the vibes" [ch 010 / 010.01; ch 010 / moment].
*   When the party captured a duergar prisoner, Sarith offered his own services as an expert interrogator, citing his background as one of Lolth's own guards, though he conceded that Daz's techniques had been impressive recently [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.04].
*   During the interrogation of the duergar Brannum Redmarch, Sarith was impressed by Thorin's direct intimidation tactics but expressed disappointment that torture had not been employed [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.01; ch 011 / moment].
*   While traveling near Rumpadump and the infected Myconids, Sarith became agitated upon seeing the dancing fungi and attempted to join them, requiring Zalthir to grapple and pull him back [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.03].
*   Sarith began speaking of a "Dark Lady" calling to him, which led the party to realize he had been infected by Zuggtmoy's influence, likely before the party met him in prison, and noted that he had been "a little bit crazy over the last few weeks" [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.04; ch 025 / moment].
*   During the Darklake voyage toward the tomb, Sarith felt a compulsion to visit the Neverlight Grove [ch 027 / entry; ch 027 / 027.05].
*   Upon reaching the hidden shore near the tomb, Sarith led the party away from the boat into the Underdark [ch 027 / entry; ch 027 / 027.06].
*   When deciding how to dispose of their boat, Sarith rejected selling it as there was no one to buy it, and proposed sinking it so it could be repaired later with the *Mending* spell [ch 027 / 027.06; ch 027 / moment].
*   In the Faerzress-suffused tunnels, Sarith identified glowing quartz crystals, describing them as "excellent," "powerful," and "very useful," and explained their blinding flash mechanics [ch 028 / entry; ch 028 / 028.01].
*   Sarith guided the party through the Faerzress and announced their arrival at the Lost Tomb of Khaem with "Aha, we have arrived" [ch 028 / 028.03; ch 028 / 028.04].
- Sarith arrived at Neverlight Grove with the party, displaying a quiet, peaceful expression that was new to his companions [ch 029 / 029.05].
- He advised the party to stay on the sovereign's good side, sensing that the myconids take their leaders seriously [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.05].
- Sarith was accompanied by the party to the meeting with Sovereign Phylo and Sovereign Basidia [ch 029 / 029.05].
- During Basidia's tour of the grove, Sarith was mentioned as being part of the group receiving the warning about Phylo's strange behavior and the missing travelers [ch 029 / 029.06].
- Sovereign Basidia identified that Sarith was infected with unknown spores she did not recognize, noting this was remarkable because myconid sovereigns can typically identify nearly all spores in the Underdark [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.06; ch 029 / moment].
- Sarith traveled with the party for the entire journey through the Underdark prior to the events in Neverlight Grove [ch 031 / entry; ch 031 / 031.04].
- During the battle in the Garden of Welcome, Sarith's head exploded, transforming him into a mindless drow spore servant [ch 031 / entry; ch 031 / 031.04; ch 031 / moment].
- Eldeth and Jimjar were forced to cut down the transformed Sarith immediately after his transformation [ch 031 / entry; ch 031 / 031.04; ch 031 / moment].
- Following his death, Sarith's remains were discovered in the Garden of Welcome, confirming the ultimate progression of the corruption that had afflicted him [ch 031 / 031.05].
- With Sarith dead, the party lost its original guide and subsequently turned toward Blingdenstone, relying on myconid scouts provided by Rasharoo for the first part of the journey [ch 032 / 032.04].

## Last Observed State
Sarith is dead. During the battle in the Garden of Welcome, his head exploded, transforming him into a mindless drow spore servant [ch 031 / 031.04; ch 031 / moment]. He was immediately cut down by his former companions Eldeth and Jimjar [ch 031 / 031.04]. His remains were discovered in the garden after the battle, confirming the ultimate progression of his corruption [ch 031 / 031.05].
