## Identity
Chef Goromach is the named chef of a high-end orc-dwarven fusion restaurant in Gauntlgrym [ch 073 / entry; ch 073 / 073.04]. He is not identified as the unnamed waiter who speaks to the party.

## Personality and Motivations
His menu combines unfamiliar ingredients and elaborate preparations, including warhammer-tenderized basilisk and a pomegranate adaptation of traditional Red Wizard blood glaze. The menu advertises a non-lethal culinary duel to the death for dissatisfied guests and encourages surviving challengers to tip thirty-five percent; the chapter does not settle the contradictory wording or stage a duel [ch 073 / entry; ch 073 / 073.04].

## History with the Party
- Gyrgum and Daz ate at his restaurant. The menu named him as the potential culinary opponent, but no direct speech or personal conversation with him is established [ch 073 / 073.04; ch 073 / entry].
- The unnamed waiter supplied gossip and declined the invitation to join the expedition. Those actions are not assigned to Goromach [ch 073 / 073.04].
- Bruenor later ordered his chamberlain to arrange food from the restaurant within the next few weeks; no completed royal meal is recorded [ch 073 / 073.05].

## Last Observed State
Known as the restaurant’s chef in Gauntlgrym. No direct personal appearance, departure, expedition agreement or culinary duel is established [ch 073 / entry; ch 073 / 073.04].

## Relationships
Works at the restaurant whose staff discuss Eldeth and the summit. The restaurant lost its catering bid; staff report that the winner had an extended family connection to Bruenor. This is attributed gossip, not Goromach’s own proven relationship to the king [ch 073 / 073.04].
