## Identity
Miss Hollypocket is an elderly gnome and the ancient housekeeper responsible for the daily needs of Janussi, the Keeper of Candlekeep [ch 057 / entry; ch 058 / entry]. Her duties included bringing morning tea and emptying chamber pots [ch 058 / entry]. She resided in a modest apartment located below the Keeper's chamber [ch 058 / 058.04; ch 058 / 058.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Hollypocket provided the party with herbal tea and a "flood of carefully observed gossip" during their interview [ch 058 / 058.04]. She was observant enough to note the demeanor of Janussi's visitors, such as Kalan Strongbranch leaving with a "face like thunder" [ch 058 / 058.05]. She adhered strictly to Janussi’s domestic rules; she did not investigate a loud thud heard after midnight because Janussi disliked being disturbed after eleven o'clock [ch 058 / 058.05]. Conversely, Tadric confirmed she would never have permitted the candles to burn down to nothing, a fact the party used to time the murder [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.07]. She described Janussi and Sylvira Savikas's relationship as volatile, noting that "the hookups were explosive, the breakups were explosive" [ch 058 / 058.05].

## History with the Party
*   Miss Hollypocket is Janussi's ancient gnome housekeeper and was one of the first two people to discover Janussi's body alongside A'lai Aivenmore at approximately 7:30 a.m. [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.06]
*   Tadric confirmed that Miss Hollypocket would never have permitted the Keeper to let his candles burn down to nothing, which the party used to deduce that the murder had taken place many hours before the body was found. [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.07]
*   The party visited Miss Hollypocket in her modest apartment below the Keeper's chamber, where she offered them herbal tea and provided testimony regarding Janussi's daily routine and the missing locket. [ch 058 / 058.04; ch 058 / 058.05]
*   Miss Hollypocket recounted a day of explosive one-on-one meetings, including Kalan Strongbranch leaving with a face like thunder, Teles Ahvoste screaming "hypocrite," and Daral Yashenti shouting insults while hungover. [ch 058 / 058.04; ch 058 / 058.05]
*   She described a violent confrontation between Janussi and Sylvira Savikas, whom she referred to as the "devil woman," noting that Sylvira screamed Janussi was a traitor before storming out. [ch 058 / 058.04; ch 058 / 058.05]
*   Miss Hollypocket reported hearing a loud thud from the chamber above shortly after the midnight bells, but did not investigate because Janussi did not like to be disturbed after eleven. [ch 058 / 058.05]
*   She claimed to have seen a vision of Sylvira Savikas's face glowing red in the candlelight moving downward around two or three in the morning, an observation the party noted was odd because Sylvira is a tiefling with darkvision and would not need a lantern. [ch 058 / 058.04; ch 058 / 058.05]
*   Miss Hollypocket confirmed that Janussi always wore a locket on his person, that it was never removed, and that it was intimate, supporting the party's theory that the locket contained one of the two keys to the High Tower Library. [ch 058 / 058.05; ch 058 / 058.06]
