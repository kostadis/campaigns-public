## Identity
Whistler is a Kenku whom the party connects to the "Ardragon" rumors [ch 055 / entry; ch 055 / 055.04]. He is identified as "Kenku of the First Faction" on a memorial sign in Waterdeep [ch 055 / entry; ch 055 / moment]. He is a figure from the War of the Dragons, depicted in Milo Goodbarrel’s historical accounts and paintings as hooded in black, hovering approximately one foot off the ground, and using mimicry to speak [ch 055 / 055.06]. Rumors of the Ardragon describe a former thief for the Black Network (the Ardragon) who underwent a transformation to become a silent ranger who stops banditry and takes nothing [ch 055 / 055.04; ch 055 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Whistler’s motivations are interpreted through conflicting public narratives and party recollections. Gyrgum recalls that Whistler is prone to theft or resourcefulness; specifically, Gyrgum noted that Whistler would have "stolen the roof tiles" to fix a monastery leak [ch 055 / 055.07]. However, Gyrgum also connects Whistler to a philosophy of constrained theft, noting that when given access to Tiamat's horde, Whistler took only what was needed for "legitimate expenses, operating goals, day-to-day funds, per diems" [ch 056 / 056.03]. Daz recalls that Whistler "would always search all bodies" [ch 026 / 026.07]. Current legends describe a figure who "takes nothing" and leaves coins untouched [ch 055 / 055.04]. Public debate focuses on whether his post-lycanthropy morality represents a redemption or merely a framing of "operational expenses" [ch 055 / entry; ch 055 / 055.06].

## History with the Party
*   In Gracklstugh, Daz whistled while searching bodies, a nod to a character from a past campaign ("Whistler would always search all bodies") [ch 026 / 026.07].
*   Whistler was a Kenku hero from the War of the Dragons, whose current status is debated between factions declaring him fallen and those who still speak of him; his "redemption arc" is a subject of intense public debate regarding the authenticity of his post-lycanthropy morality [ch 055 / entry].
*   In Triboar, the party encountered legends of the "Ardragon," a black-hooded Kenku who stopped banditry, took nothing, and mimicked a hawk; the party recognized the connection between this figure and Whistler, though the public narrative separates the literary debates from these caravan rumors [ch 055 / entry; ch 055 / 055.04].
*   In Waterdeep, the party discovered a memorial sign at Rishaal's Pageturners declaring Whistler "fallen at Glazhael's lair," while fans debated whether Milo Goodbarrel’s account of Whistler’s "morally confused post-lycanthropy stage" was too generous or a philosophical breakthrough [ch 055 / 055.06; ch 055 / moment].
*   Gyrgum noted that if Whistler were present, he would have stolen the roof tiles to fix the monastery's leak, implying Whistler’s known propensity for theft or resourcefulness [ch 055 / 055.07].
*   In Beregost, a bard sang a song about a Kenku thief who "learned to steal no more," which Gyrgum associated with Whistler’s alleged restraint in stealing Tiamat's horde, only taking what was needed for "legitimate expenses, operating goals, day-to-day funds, per diems" [ch 056 / 056.03].

## Last Observed State
Whistler has not appeared physically in the campaign; all mentions are retrospective, legendary, or debated by third parties. A memorial sign in Waterdeep declares him "fallen at Glazhael's lair, the year of the Alliance, redeemed in life and death" [ch 055 / 055.06; ch 055 / moment]. However, this status is contested by factions and rumors of the Ardragon still active on caravan trails north of Yartar [ch 055 / 055.04; ch 055 / 055.06]. The evidence does not confirm whether Whistler is alive, dead, or if the Ardragon and Whistler are the same person in the present day, only that the party recognizes the connection between the legends [ch 055 / entry; ch 055 / 055.04]. Status not established in the summaries.
