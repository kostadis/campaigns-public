## Identity
Topsy and Turvy are a pair of twin deep gnome companions who possess the nature of wererats [ch 018 / entry]. They were formerly prisoners in Velkynvelve alongside the party, working in the drow dining hall [ch 002 / 002.02]. As wererats, they are described as being hard to hurt [ch 018 / 018.03]. They were not known to belong to Clan Goldwhisker in Blingdenstone, though the party drew parallels to that clan’s wererat inhabitants [ch 038 / 038.02].

## Personality and Motivations
The twins display opportunistic and thieving tendencies, actively seeking ways to steal food during their first shift in the Velkynvelve kitchens [ch 002 / 002.02]. Topsy advised Gyrgum to steal “carefully, quietly, and not make a big production of it,” specifically gesturing at his size [ch 002 / 002.02]. When Gyrgum asked them to handle the theft themselves, with six or seven drow watching him cook, they successfully stole two knives [ch 002 / 002.04].

They prioritize loot over survival, a trait highlighted during the civil war in Sloobludop when they transformed into rats and scurried toward a treasure lair amidst the chaos [ch 008 / 008.02; ch 008 / moment]. Daz noticed them whispering to one another during a rest, though their motives remained unclear [ch 006 / 006.03]. They are reluctant to visit Blingdenstone directly due to prior disputes concerning their “profession” and reputation as thieves [ch 004 / 004.04].
