## Identity
Jorlan Duskryn is a drow warrior and the former favored lieutenant and lover of Ilvara Mizzrym [ch 002 / entry; ch 049 / entry]. He is the brother of Nym Duskryn [ch 051 / 051.06]. His identity is defined by a black pudding attack that melted and scarred his face and twisted his sword hand, leaving him missing two fingers [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.05]. These injuries caused Ilvara to replace him with Shoor Vandree, a demotion that defines his current status and demeanor [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Jorlan is driven primarily by bitterness, resentment, and a desire for revenge against Ilvara and her new favorite, Shoor Vandree [ch 002 / entry; ch 049 / entry]. His motivations are self-centered rather than altruistic; when aiding the prisoners in Velkynvelve, he explicitly stated he did not care if they survived, only that their escape (or failed attempt) would humiliate his rivals [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.05]. Valen of House T'sarran describes him as harboring deep resentment and saying he "might even sabotage anything out of spite" [ch 049 / 049.03]. Jorlan appears impulsive and easily manipulated; he was taunted into charging into a trap during combat [ch 050 / 050.04] and abandoned a potential tactical advantage to charge blindly toward Ilvara when she was vulnerable [ch 051 / 051.02]. His brother Nym characterizes him as "the dumber one," a sentiment reflected in his inability to navigate the complex political landscape of the Underdark factions [ch 051 / 051.06].

## History with the Party
- Jorlan Duskryn was once Ilvara's favored lieutenant and lover until a black pudding attack melted and scarred his face and left his sword hand twisted and missing two fingers [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.05].
- Due to his injuries, Ilvara replaced him with Shoor Vandree, leaving Jorlan bitter and eager for revenge [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.05].
- During the party's imprisonment at Velkynvelve, Jorlan offered to leave the slave-pen gate unlocked and create a distraction to humiliate Ilvara and Shoor, stating he was indifferent to the prisoners' survival [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.05].
- He identified the armory location as the chamber above the guard post and suggested the prisoners jump from the outpost into the pool below [ch 002 / 002.05; ch 002 / moment].
- By the time the party pursued Ilvara into the Underdark, Valen of House T'sarran identified Jorlan as a resentful dissenter who might sabotage Ilvara's mission out of spite [ch 049 / entry; ch 049 / 049.03].
- The party recognized a schism in Ilvara's camp involving Asha Vandree and Jorlan, deciding they could only approach one without tipping off the other [ch 049 / 049.03; ch 049 / 049.05].
- The party chose to ally with Asha Vandree rather than Jorlan, placing a Glyph of Warding on a bridge keyed to trigger against anyone approaching from Jorlan's side [ch 050 / 050.03].
- During the battle at the Fungal Altar, Thorin taunted Jorlan into charging directly into Gyrgum's Glyph of Warding trap [ch 050 / 050.04; ch 050 / moment].
- The trap detonated, dealing 21 damage to Jorlan and destroying a sporewalker [ch 050 / 050.04; ch 050 / moment].
- Thorin subsequently struck Jorlan with Dawnbringer for 14 damage, applying a sap effect that gave Jorlan disadvantage on his next attack [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.04].
- When Zalthir engaged Ilvara in the subsequent rounds, Jorlan broke away from the party and charged toward Ilvara, crying her name [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.02].
- Thorin allowed Jorlan to pass without making an opportunity attack, allowing the enemies to fight one another [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.02].
- Ilvara used a legendary action to create a fungal bloom in Jorlan's path, turning the ground into difficult terrain and preventing him from reaching her [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.05].
- Gyrgum maneuvered to follow Jorlan's direction but kept enough distance to avoid being involved in the former couple's dispute [ch 051 / 051.02; ch 051 / 051.03].
- Thorin destroyed the bridge supporting Ilvara and Jorlan; Jorlan made his save and landed on the cavern floor without falling damage but was left furious [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.06].
- Kaelira, a drow warrior, asked permission to strike Jorlan, to which Nym Duskryn agreed, noting Jorlan was "the dumber one" [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.06].
- Kaelira struck Jorlan with a Lightning Bolt; he made his saving throw and took 14 damage [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.06].
- Status not established in the summaries regarding his condition after the final session, only that he survived the lightning bolt [ch 051 / entry].

## Last Observed State
Jorlan was last observed in Chapter 051 during the final battle against Ilvara. After Ilvara blocked his pursuit with a fungal bloom, Thorin destroyed the bridge Jorlan was standing on; Jorlan made his saving throw and landed on the cavern floor without falling damage but was left furious [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.06]. During the aftermath of the bridge collapse, Nym Duskryn gave permission for Kaelira to attack Jorlan. Kaelira struck him with a Lightning Bolt, and Jorlan made his saving throw, taking 14 damage [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.06]. The summaries confirm he survived this attack but do not establish his condition or status after this moment [ch 051 / entry].
