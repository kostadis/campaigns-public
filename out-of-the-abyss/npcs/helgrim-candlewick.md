## Identity
Helgrim is the smith associated with replacing the Great Forge’s damaged coils. The chapter names him only as Helgrim; Candlewick is the canonical registry identity, not a surname newly learned on screen [ch 075 / 075.04].

## Personality and Motivations
His technical remit is established: replacing the coils takes two days, while waking the old mechanism requires a means he cannot supply [ch 075 / 075.04].

## History with the Party
- In ch 075 Bruenor explained that Helgrim could run new coils in two days but lacked the means to restart the old housing. The party supplied its Dust of Mechanus and completed the Great Forge restart [ch 075 / 075.04; ch 075 / 075.05].

## Last Observed State
Associated with the Great Forge’s completed repair and restart in Gauntlgrym. No additional commission is recorded as finished yet [ch 075 / 075.04; ch 075 / 075.05].

## Relationships
- **Bruenor Battlehammer and the party:** the king reports his repair estimate; the party supplies the missing means of restart [ch 075 / 075.04; ch 075 / 075.05].
