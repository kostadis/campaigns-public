## Identity
Dawnbringer is a sentient magical sword that communicates directly with her wielder, Thorin, producing sounds in his mind [ch 062 / entry]. She is explicitly identified as female [ch 064 / entry]. Mechanically, she is a +2 weapon that deals radiant damage rather than slashing damage, inflicts an extra 1d8 damage against undead, and grants the ability to cast *lesser restoration* once per day [ch 029 / entry; ch 031 / entry; ch 052 / entry]. When Thorin attuned to her, she revealed she emits actual sunlight in a 15-foot radius [ch 031 / entry]. Her blade is formed of light rather than metal, rendering her immune to corrosive effects such as those of gray oozes [ch 034 / entry]. Thorin’s attack bonus when wielding her is +10 [ch 064 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Dawnbringer exhibits a strong personality characterized by a deep-seated fear of darkness, and a desire for aesthetic recognition. Upon discovery, she was upset to be found by a monk rather than a fighter, stating, "I've been waiting for somebody to save me and I get a monk" [ch 029 / moment]. She harbors a persistent fear of the dark, admitting, "I don't trust the dark. I've been in the dark for a very long time," and refuses to be deactivated at night due to this anxiety [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / moment]. Consequently, she dislikes magical darkness and has threatened to impose her will on Thorin via a Charisma saving throw if he enters it [ch 041 / entry]. She strongly prefers to emit light even when it makes nearby myconids uncomfortable [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.03].

She maintains a pragmatic and sometimes abrasive relationship with combat tactics, challenging Thorin’s caution with questions like, "Is that the best you can do?" [ch 037 / entry]. She takes offense at mischaracterizations of her nature; when Thorin forgot her power and called her a flaming sword, she reminded him that her damage was radiant and took offense [ch 063 / entry; ch 063 / 063.05]; her presumed objection to being thrown as a weapon was "I'm a sword, not a spear" [ch 069 / moment]. Dawnbringer desires a fancy scabbard, initially wanting "the fanciest, schmanciest... most over-the-top scabbard," and later muttering about adding bling to the Vaultmaster locking scabbard she received [ch 045 / entry; ch 045 / moment]. She engages with academic discourse, eagerly absorbing discussions on the semiotics of magical swords after Daral promised to make sentient swords a subject of research [ch 062 / entry; ch 062 / 062.05]. While she denies being afraid of the dark, insisting "I'm not afraid of the dark, I just don't like the dark," she engages in therapeutic sessions with Master Archivist Philemon to process her trauma and grief regarding her imprisonment [ch 053 / moment; ch 056 / moment].
