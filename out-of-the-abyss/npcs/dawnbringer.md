## Identity

- Dawnbringer is a sentient magical sunblade with a gilded longsword hilt and a blade of pure radiance. She speaks, reads, and understands Common, identifies as female, and speaks with a kind feminine voice. [ch 029 / entry; ch 031 / entry; ch 032 / entry]
- Thorin claimed her from Brysis's sarcophagus. Philemon identified her as a sun-sword of late Netherese origin. [ch 029 / entry; ch 056 / entry]
- Her first and true wielder was Dynaphyse, a servant of Lathander. Brysis of Khaem killed Dynaphyse and imprisoned Dawnbringer with Brysis's own corpse. [ch 072 / entry]
- She is a +2 weapon dealing radiant damage, with an additional 1d8 against undead, and grants lesser restoration, recorded as once per day. Her sunlight radius is recorded as fifteen feet in some entries and twenty feet in another. [ch 031 / entry; ch 042 / entry; ch 047 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations

- She values her quality as a weapon, enjoys becoming more deadly, and objects to being treated as a ritual implement. [ch 053 / entry]
- She enjoys attention and elaborate accessories, but also takes pleasure in healing orphans and the needy. [ch 045 / entry; ch 055 / entry]
- Millennia of darkness caused profound distress and fear of abandonment. Therapy with Philemon progressed from work with light to mourning; naming Dynaphyse allowed her to grieve and overcome her fear of the dark. [ch 031 / entry; ch 056 / entry; ch 072 / entry]
- She is protective of her reputation: after grieving, she ordered Thorin never to mention that she cried. [ch 072 / entry]
- She graciously accepted Clan Feldrun’s gifts, including an impractical sharpening tool, and asked whether she belonged to the clan. [ch 076 / 076.02]

## History with the Party
- Found in Brysis's sarcophagus, atop her mummified corpse, as a sentient, talking magic sword. She speaks, reads and understands Common, and manifests a blade of pure radiance when the hilt is grasped and activated. [ch 029 / entry]
- Thorin claimed her. [ch 029 / entry]
- In her first appearance she showed a strong personality. She expressed disdain at being left with a monk rather than a fighter, and she refuses to be deactivated because of a deep fear of the dark. She met Gyrgum's surprise at her literacy with an exasperated "seriously?" [ch 029 / entry]
- She grants lesser restoration. [ch 029 / entry; ch 031 / entry]
- Gyrgum, a cleric proficient only with simple weapons, declined to borrow her. Thorin used her to strike the Grick Alpha for radiant damage. [ch 030 / entry]
- She is described as a legendary sunblade with a gilded longsword hilt. It emits sunlight in a 15-foot radius and speaks in a kind feminine voice. [ch 031 / entry]
- She may panic and try to take control of Thorin if he enters magical darkness. [ch 031 / entry]
- Zalthir's magical darkness suppresses her sunlight. [ch 031 / entry]
- She already appeared fond of Thorin. [ch 031 / entry]
- Thorin struck the rocktopus twice with her and channeled Lesser Restoration through her to cure Zalthir of his spore-induced madness. She strongly prefers emitting light even when it discomforts nearby myconids. [ch 032 / entry]
- Her blade is made of light rather than metal, so the party concluded gray oozes' corrosion of nonmagical metal weapons would not affect her. [ch 034 / entry]
- On entering the poorly lit Maze, a party member remarked that his sword was "getting very upset" in darkness. [ch 035 / entry]
- Dawnbringer challenged Thorin's cautious use of darts. Thorin refused to risk submersion in acid to impress her and said their relationship was "purely platonic." [ch 037 / entry]
- She can impose her will on her wielder through charisma saving throws, but can be persuaded with promises of better treatment. [ch 041 / entry]
- She proposed the "dart in and out" compromise, and Thorin swung her into the darkness and back out. [ch 041 / entry]
- After the scabbard deal she said "I can live with this." [ch 041 / entry]
- Because her blade is light, the promised scabbard is a craftsmanship problem. [ch 041 / entry]
- Thorin used her in combat against the Earth Elementals and the Medusa, and against the shambling mound. Her light radius was recorded as 15 feet. [ch 042 / entry; ch 043 / entry]
- The table treated her as genderless at that point; the party later settled on "she." She was pleased to receive the Vaultmaster locking scabbard while muttering about adding bling. Locals, particularly children, were impressed by her. [ch 045 / entry]
- She argued against jumping into the acid pit, which helped convince Thorin to take the bridge. [ch 046 / entry]
- Thorin attacked the Pudding King with her after the party realized he had swapped positions with Glabbagool. Her light gave Zalthir the shadows he needed for Shadow Step. [ch 047 / entry]
- Thorin used her to cast lesser restoration on Valen, halting his fungal infection and shrinking his growths. Thorin believes she can do this once per long rest. [ch 049 / entry]
- Thorin struck Jorlan with her two-handed for 14 damage, applying sap. [ch 050 / entry]
- Her radiant damage type influenced tactical discussion during the level-up session. [ch 052 / entry]
- She reluctantly helped purify Ilvara's corrupted scourge after being reminded of her Vaultmaster Locking Scabbard. She insists she merely dislikes darkness, rejects the idea that she needs therapy, and is enthusiastic about becoming more deadly. [ch 053 / entry]
- Her light was held close to the drow spy's face during interrogation. [ch 054 / entry]
- The party hopes to find Philemon at Candlekeep to help her. [ch 054 / entry]
- Thorin called this "couples counseling with Dawnbringer." [ch 054 / entry]
- In Waterdeep she vocally backed Thorin's argument in the ooze rights confrontation and offered lesser restoration to the bewildered guard. She was later used to heal orphans and the needy, which made her very happy. [ch 055 / entry]
- Master Archivist Philemon identified her as a late-Netherese sun-sword with profound arcane stress from millennia of darkness, and noted she cannot be polished. A two-phase therapeutic recovery was begun: the work of light first, then the work of mourning. [ch 056 / entry]
- At the council meeting she spoke up herself. She was cited as proof of the party's discovery credentials when Daral challenged them, and she argued they must be the greatest discoverers of all, having found her after two thousand years. [ch 057 / entry]
- She remains invested in the Candlekeep therapy and felt the first session established genuine progress. She cured Daral of his suspected poisoning, and his promise of academic research into sentient swords greatly pleased her. Thorin used a sapping strike with her against the helmed horrors. [ch 062 / entry]
- She took offense when Thorin momentarily forgot her power was radiant, not fire. He corrected himself and the matter was set aside for a future therapy session. In the rotunda battle she struck the final critical hit that killed Moziqodo. [ch 063 / entry]
- Thorin's attack bonus with her is +10. [ch 064 / entry]
- Thorin suggested using her hilt or pommel to smash the sapphire, saying she wouldn't mind. [ch 064 / entry]
- Thorin used her against a Zhentarim thug and in his attack sequence on the Zhentarim assassin, including a critical radiant strike that left the assassin at five hit points. [ch 064 / entry; ch 065 / entry]
- Thorin connected her name to a high-school girlfriend named Dawn. Gyrgum linked it to the "rosy-fingered Dawn" of the *Iliad*. [ch 065 / entry]
- As Thorin plunged into another dark shaft, she reacted with dismay. [ch 068 / entry]
- In the tower she was the primary light source and twitched at the numerous *Glyphs of Warding*. Thorin considered throwing her while immobilized, but the sword's presumed response and a silent look persuaded him to use a dart instead. [ch 069 / entry]
- Alaundo's prophecy refers to her as the "bright blade." Thorin used a Menacing Attack with her to frighten Edvaldo. She was described as displeased with the chaotic events. [ch 070 / entry]
- With Philemon, Thorin learned that Brysis was her captor, not her wielder. Her first and true wielder was Dynaphyse, a servant of Lathander whom Brysis of Khaem killed. Naming Dynaphyse let her grieve, overcome her fear of the dark, and grant Thorin Lathander's Dawn. She ordered Thorin never to mention that she cried. [ch 072 / entry]
- Status is not in question in these summaries. She is shown in Thorin's possession through ch 072, and no later change of custody is stated in this chunk. [ch 072 / entry]

- In ch 073 the therapy Dawnbringer and Thorin had undergone encouraged him to be honest and take responsibility in his conversation with Sylvira. Eldeth explicitly welcomed sentient swords to the otherwise dwarves-only clan gathering, allowing Dawnbringer to accompany Thorin [ch 073 / 073.01; ch 073 / 073.02].

- In ch 075 Thorin declined another sentient weapon and chose the Helm of the Citadel. He described himself and Dawnbringer as a package deal, credited their relationship’s strength to their counseling, and included her in his Clan Feldrun association [ch 075 / 075.05; ch 075 / entry].
- The discussion joked about jealousy and competing demands between two sentient weapons. These remarks do not establish a new power, a new therapy session, or a new statement of Dawnbringer’s own intentions [ch 075 / 075.05].
- At Clan Feldrun’s farewell, Dawnbringer received straps, polish, leather conditioner for the Fancy Scabbard 2000, and a sharpening tool she could not use. She accepted the gifts graciously and asked whether she was a clan member; Thorin deferred resolving her formal membership until their next visit. [ch 076 / 076.02]

## Last Observed State

Dawnbringer actively participated in Clan Feldrun’s farewell, accepting gifts and asking about membership. Thorin postponed settling her formal clan status until their next visit. The party then set out toward the Surbrin Hills. [ch 076 / 076.02]

## Relationships

- **Thorin:** Her wielder and counseling partner. He describes them as a package deal and chose the Helm of the Citadel rather than acquire another sentient weapon. [ch 054 / entry; ch 075 / entry; ch 075 / 075.05]
- **Clan Feldrun and Eldeth:** Eldeth explicitly welcomed sentient swords to the dwarves-only gathering. Thorin includes Dawnbringer in his clan association, but her formal membership remains unresolved; the clan nevertheless gave her farewell gifts, which she accepted graciously. [ch 073 / 073.02; ch 075 / entry; ch 076 / 076.02]
- **Philemon:** Identified her arcane stress and began her therapeutic recovery. During their later session, the truth about Dynaphyse emerged, enabling her grief and recovery from fear of darkness. [ch 056 / entry; ch 072 / entry]
- **Dynaphyse and Brysis of Khaem:** Dynaphyse was her first and true wielder; Brysis killed Dynaphyse and became Dawnbringer’s captor. [ch 072 / entry]
- **Daral:** She cured his suspected poisoning; his promise to research sentient magical swords greatly pleased her. [ch 062 / entry]
- **Zalthir and Valen:** Her restorative magic cured Zalthir’s spore-induced madness and halted Valen’s fungal infection. [ch 032 / entry; ch 049 / entry]
