## Identity
Inda is a scholar at Candlekeep and a recognized leader among its senior scholars, alongside figures such as Fheminor Scrivenbark and Kalan Strongbranch [ch 071 / 071.04; ch 071 / entry]. She is deeply committed to the study and cataloging of the prophecies of Alaundo, the legendary seer of Candlekeep [ch 071 / entry]. Her role involves interpreting these prophecies and guiding others in the use of the prophecy crystals [ch 071 / 071.05; ch 071 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Inda exhibits a rigid devotion to the absolute truth of Alaundo's prophecies. When Gyrgum remarked that he trusted the prophecies more because the first one came true, Inda immediately corrected him, insisting that the prophecies are always true regardless of perceived fulfillment [ch 071 / 071.05; ch 071 / entry]. She emphasizes the extraordinary academic privilege and weight of hearing Alaundo's voice, treating the experience with profound seriousness [ch 071 / 071.05; ch 071 / entry].

She is visibly distraught and deeply distressed by uncertainty in the prophetic sequence, particularly the gaps caused by Edvaldo destroying two of the six crystals [ch 071 / 071.05; ch 071 / entry]. This distress stems from the fact that cataloging Alaundo's prophecies is her life's academic work, and the missing stones compromise the chronological integrity of the record [ch 071 / 071.05]. Inda also engages actively in academic debate, specifically regarding the interpretation of prophecies involving animal similes, such as identifying Daz as the "crow" based on Alaundo's known use of such imagery [ch 071 / 071.05; ch 071 / entry]. She is persistent in her duties, repeatedly attempting to return discussions to the crystals even when interrupted by other scholars' investigations [ch 071 / 071.04; ch 071 / entry].

## Last Observed State
Inda was present at the vault in Chapter 71, arriving with other Candlekeep leaders after the confrontation with Edvaldo [ch 071 / 071.04]. She waited for Kalan Strongbranch to complete identity verifications before beginning her explanation of the crystals [ch 071 / 071.04]. Once permitted, she instructed the party on how to make the prophecy crystals speak, with Gyrgum delivering the activating words [ch 071 / 071.05]. She remained present during the activation of the four intact crystals, scribbling notes as Alaundo's voice repeated the prophecies [ch 071 / 071.05]. She expressed distress over the missing stones and participated in the debate interpreting the "crow" prophecy [ch 071 / 071.05]. Finally, upon the arrival of Eldeth's note summoning the party to Gauntlgrym, Inda insisted that this event confirmed the truth of Alaundo's prophecies [ch 071 / 071.10]. Status not established in the summaries beyond her presence in the vault during these events.

## Relationships
**Kalan Strongbranch**: Inda's ability to explain the crystals was significantly delayed by Kalan's extreme paranoia and demand for proof of identity [ch 071 / 071.04]. She attempted multiple times to return the discussion to the crystals during his investigation but was sidelined by his insistence on verification protocols [ch 071 / 071.04].

**Gyrgum**: Inda guided Gyrgum on the mechanism to activate the prophecy crystals [ch 071 / 071.05]. She corrected his skeptical view of the prophecies, asserting their inherent truth [ch 071 / 071.05]. Gyrgum's remark about his dramatic reading ("it's decorative gourd season, motherfuckers") was his way of reassuring Inda that he was qualified to give the prophecies the delivery they deserved [ch 071 / moment].

**Daz**: Inda identified Daz as the "crow" referenced in Alaundo's prophecy, citing Alaundo's tendency to use animal similes to describe individuals with dark Drow complexions [ch 071 / 071.05; ch 071 / entry].

**Thorin**: When Thorin noted that while Alaundo's prophecies come true, no one knows *when*, Inda reiterated the absolute truth of the prophecies [ch 071 / 071.10].
