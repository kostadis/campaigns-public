## Identity
Kaelira Duskryn is a Drow wizard and mercenary partnered with Nym Duskryn [ch 051 / entry; ch 053 / entry]. She and Nym are established professionals within the circles of Menzoberranzan capable of countering high-level threats from rival noble houses [ch 053 / 053.01]. They operate under strict contractual terms, accepting employment from unknown patrons to protect specific targets from specific factions [ch 051 / entry; ch 053 / 053.01]. Kaelira is identified as a specialist from House Duskryn, though she displays no familial sentiment toward Jorlan Duskryn [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Kaelira exhibits a sardonic, professionally detached demeanor, prioritizing payment and contractual precision above personal loyalty or moral considerations [ch 051 / entry; ch 053 / 053.01]. She responds to Daz’s refusal to hide during combat not with concern for his safety, but by seeking tactical clearance to eliminate a threat (Jorlan), asking Nym for permission to attack him [ch 051 / 051.06; ch 051 / moment]. She is dismissive of risks outside her contract scope, stating that Daz’s employer “deserves to lose his money” if Daz cannot handle a low-level cultist, and refusing to protect against “evil demon gods” as it was not in the terms of the job [ch 053 / 053.01; ch 053 / moment].

She views violence with professional approval, applauding Zalthir’s killing of Asha Vandree and noting that Daz was “not completely defenseless” [ch 053 / 053.02; ch 053 / moment]. Kaelira mocks speculative narratives regarding Daz’s heritage, comparing his suspicion of a secret noble lineage to the fantasies of orphans, and asks if he has “daddy issues” or “mommy issues” [ch 053 / 053.04; ch 053 / moment]. She and Nym explicitly state they were not paid to analyze the motives behind their contract, preferring to leave complex political mysteries to their employer [ch 053 / 053.04].

## History with the Party
- Kaelira emerged from hiding to protect Daz during the battle with Ilvara, pulling him northeast through an insect plague and suffering thirteen damage in the process [ch 051 / 051.03; ch 051 / entry].
- She clarified that she would take Daz with her regardless of his willingness to survive, stating that cooperation simply made the task easier [ch 051 / 051.03; ch 051 / entry].
- When Daz refused to stay out of combat, Kaelira asked her partner Nym for permission to attack Jorlan Duskryn; after Nym agreed, she struck Jorlan with a lightning bolt [ch 051 / 051.06; ch 051 / moment].
- Following the battle, Kaelira and Nym attempted to leave, explaining they had been hired in Menzoberranzan by an unknown patron to protect Daz specifically from Ilvara of House Mizzrym and agents of House T'sarran [ch 053 / 053.01; ch 053 / entry].
- She dismissed threats outside their contract scope, such as Asha Vandree or evil demon gods, stating that if Daz could not handle a low-level cult initiate, his employer deserved to lose their money [ch 053 / 053.01; ch 053 / moment].
- Kaelira applauded Zalthir for killing Asha Vandree, noting to Nym that Daz was not completely defenseless [ch 053 / 053.02; ch 053 / moment].
- Upon examining the burned extraction orders, Kaelira and Nym confirmed that House T'sarran had been paid to abduct Daz alive, finding the massive expense bizarre for someone with no known noble house [ch 053 / 053.04].
- She mocked Daz’s speculation about his own hidden importance, comparing him to an orphan imagining a secret noble heritage, and maintained that they had not been paid to analyze the reasons behind the contract [ch 053 / 053.04; ch 053 / moment].
- Kaelira guided the party through the remaining miles of the Underdark to the surface, leading them into the light of the Overbright [ch 053 / 053.05; ch 053 / 053.06].
