## Identity
Morista Malkin is a dwarven representative of the Emerald Enclave who attended Bruenor’s feast [ch 075 / entry; ch 074 / 074.05].

## Personality and Motivations
She is deeply concerned about the spreading fungal outbreak and seeks reports on water, growth, settlements, and sickness. She asks the party to respect the Underdark’s ecosystem and spare those who did not choose the fight. Her allegiance within the Enclave’s interventionist–naturalist dispute remains uncertain [ch 075 / entry; ch 075 / 075.02].

## History with the Party
- In ch 074, when the runner arrived with news of the corrupted wells, she immediately moved toward him [ch 074 / 074.05].

- In ch 075 she came from questioning the runner and joined the party between Zalthir and Daz. She offered protection for one named fungal crossing, requiring one day of preparation and lasting long enough to cross; it does not stop attacks, cure an existing infection, or protect them from the source if it is present [ch 075 / 075.02; ch 075 / entry].
- She offered enough giant lizard mounts for the whole party for one leg of its journey, in return for looking for her missing scout Sladis Vadir rather than guaranteeing his recovery [ch 075 / 075.02; ch 075 / entry].
- She requested reports about water, growth, settlements and sickness, and asked the party to spare those who had not chosen the conflict. The party accepted both offers; her own position in the Enclave’s internal dispute was not established [ch 075 / 075.02].
- Before departure, Morista explained that the giant lizards awaited the party at the Underdark entrance and were better suited to underground travel [ch 076 / 076.01].
- The party found saddled giant lizards waiting inside the cave entrance. Rainaldus identified the equipment as Morista’s and said Davra had mentioned a favor being called in [ch 076 / 076.03].

## Last Observed State
Morista last directly speaks during the party’s preparations to leave Gauntlgrym, explaining where its mounts await. At the cave entrance, Rainaldus mentions her equipment; this does not establish her presence there [ch 076 / 076.01; ch 076 / 076.03].

Status not established in the summaries. The agreement requires searching for Sladis Vadir and providing scouting reports; the supplied evidence does not establish completion [ch 075 / 075.02; ch 076 / 076.03].

## Relationships
- **Emerald Enclave:** She represents the organization, but her personal position in its internal dispute is not established [ch 075 / entry; ch 075 / 075.02].
- **Sladis Vadir:** Her missing scout; she asks the party to search for him without requiring a guarantee of recovery [ch 075 / 075.02].

- **Davra:** Rainaldus associates Morista’s equipment with a favor Davra mentioned; the favor’s terms are not supplied [ch 076 / 076.03].
