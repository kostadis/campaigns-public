## Identity
Sir Lanniver Strayl represents the Order of the Gauntlet at Bruenor’s feast, accompanied by his squire, Rhiele Vannis [ch 074 / 074.04; ch 074 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Eager to recruit the party and deeply concerned with the moral implications of adventuring in the Underdark [ch 074 / entry]. He accepts force against actual enemies, but objects to atrocities against innocents and needless destruction carried out under cover of a mission [ch 074 / 074.04; ch 074 / entry].

## History with the Party
- In ch 074, when Bruenor made his offer, Lanniver spoke to an aide, who wrote on a slate [ch 074 / 074.01].
- He offered a team of four veterans led by Aldric for one agreed, plausible tactical task per operation or subquest, such as holding a breach, covering an extraction or executing a flank. Their help is not continuous battlefield control [ch 074 / 074.04].
- The offer depends on the party protecting innocents and avoiding gratuitous slaughter, genocide and unnecessary destruction, including needless despoiling of tombs. Support can be lost if the party violates the agreement, and the veterans can die if sent into a fatal assignment [ch 074 / 074.04].
- When Daz pressed him, Lanniver warned against treating the Underdark as a pillaging ground, then generalized about the destructiveness of drow and orcs while standing in front of Daz and Gyrgum. He stumbled into a clarification that he meant a general warning rather than an accusation [ch 074 / 074.04].
- The DM clarified that the Order does not automatically observe every action; witnesses and reports matter [ch 074 / 074.04].
- When the runner arrived, Lanniver looked to his veterans, whose singing and cheering stopped [ch 074 / 074.05].

- In ch 075 the party discussed his moral conditions and the possibility that he would oppose them if they caused massive civilian deaths. Daz’s historical reading suggested that Lanniver’s personal concern for who lives or dies was distinctive within the Order; this is the party’s interpretation, not a newly stated secret motive [ch 075 / 075.01; ch 075 / 075.03; ch 075 / entry].
- The party publicly accepted every faction offer, including the Order’s existing offer. No new deployment or completed assignment for Aldric’s team was recorded [ch 075 / 075.04; ch 074 / 074.04].

## Last Observed State
His Order of the Gauntlet offer has been accepted as part of the party’s public acceptance of all faction offers. Its previously stated moral conditions still govern the support; no new assignment for the veterans is established [ch 075 / 075.04; ch 074 / 074.04].

## Relationships
- **Rhiele Vannis:** his squire [ch 074 / 074.04].
- **Aldric:** leads the veteran team he offers [ch 074 / 074.04].
- **Daz and Gyrgum:** the subjects of his awkward generalization about drow and orcs [ch 074 / 074.04].
