## Identity
Werz Saltbaron is a Duergar merchant operating in Gracklstugh who was the target of a deliberate assassination attempt by the Empty-Scabbard Killers at the Darklake Docks [ch 014 / 014.04; ch 014 / moment]. He is described as smooth-talking and business-oriented, with a suspiciously deep knowledge of assassin operations [ch 018 / entry]. Werz claims to have no idea why he was targeted, and his clan affiliation is unknown to the party [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.04]. He possesses empty spell gems linked to the defenses of Blingdenstone, the origin of which remains a mystery to the party [ch 018 / 018.05; ch 018 / moment].

## Personality and Motivations
Werz presents himself as a pragmatic merchant who avoids direct confrontation and physical heroism, stating, "I'm not that kind of heroic individual" [ch 018 / 018.04]. He utilizes economic logic to frame political and social interactions, such as citing "comparative advantage" to justify hiring the party [ch 018 / 018.04]. While he claims ignorance regarding why he is targeted, he admitted he might have helped clans sell goods "on the down low," and Gyrgum remarked that this sounded like "reason enough" [ch 018 / 018.04; ch 018 / entry]. Werz is susceptible to haggling tactics; he paid a 1,100 gp fee for a Non-Disclosure Agreement after balking at the party's discounted 1,000 gp offer ("highway robbery"), at which point the price was raised [ch 018 / 018.05; ch 018 / moment]. His primary motivation is self-preservation, seeking to have the assassins removed from his back in exchange for trade favors in Blingdenstone [ch 018 / 018.04; ch 019 / entry].

## History with the Party
*   Werz Saltbaron is a Duergar merchant who was viciously attacked by psionic assassins at the Darklake Docks; he was saved by the party, healed by Gyrgum, and invited them to the Shattered Spire for a reward [ch 014 / 014.04].
*   The party confirmed Werz was a deliberate target when they looted one of the fallen assassins and found a fish-skin parchment bearing his charcoal likeness [ch 014 / 014.05; ch 014 / moment].
*   Werz claims to have no idea why he was targeted, and his clan affiliation is unknown to the party [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.04].
*   When the party visited Werz to collect their reward, he gave each member an obsidian gem worth 10 gp and covered their lodging expenses [ch 018 / 018.04].
*   Werz revealed he was on the Empty-Scabbard Killers' target list and admitted to helping "some clans sell some goods on the down low," and Gyrgum remarked that this sounded like "reason enough" [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.04].
*   Werz provided intelligence that the Deepking has grown paranoid due to his friend Shal, competitors are disappearing, and Stone Guards have begun accepting bribes [ch 018 / 018.04].
*   Werz hired the party to ensure the assassins stop targeting him, offering trade favors in Blingdenstone as compensation [ch 018 / 018.04].
*   Gyrgum forced Werz into a Non-Disclosure Agreement (which Werz misunderstood as a "Non-Demonic Agreement") by intimidating him with the party's ghost; Werz paid 1,100 gp after attempting to haggle [ch 018 / 018.05].
*   Werz gave the party five empty spell gems, claiming they were of interest to gnomes; Jimjar identified them as vital to Blingdenstone's defenses, raising questions about their origin [ch 018 / 018.05; ch 018 / moment].
*   Werz is currently seeking to have the assassins removed from his back and offers better trade deals with gnomes as an incentive [ch 019 / entry].
*   The objective regarding Werz is considered complete if the party deals with the Demogorgon cultists, as Eldgrim agreed to wipe Werz's name from the assassins' list in exchange [ch 019 / 019.02; ch 019 / 019.03].

## Last Observed State
Werz was last observed in chapter 018 during negotiations with the party [ch 018 / entry]. In chapter 019, he was not physically present but was the subject of a negotiation between the party and Eldgrim, the leader of the Empty-Scabbard Killers [ch 019 / 019.02]. Eldgrim agreed to "wipe the slate for Mr. Werz" and remove him from the assassins' list free of charge if the party eliminated the Demogorgon cultists [ch 019 / 019.02; ch 019 / 019.03]. The DM declared the objective regarding Werz complete upon this agreement, assuming the cultists are dealt with [ch 019 / 019.03]. Status not established in the summaries after chapter 018.

## Relationships
*   **The Party:** Werz owes them for saving his life in chapter 014 and subsequently hired them to remove the assassins targeting him, providing obsidian gems, lodging coverage, and a non-disclosure agreement fee [ch 014 / 014.04; ch 018 / 018.04; ch 018 / 018.05].
*   **Daz:** Daz perceived that Werz "knows way too much" about working with assassins, suggesting he is less innocent than he claims [ch 018 / 018.04; ch 018 / moment].
*   **Eldgrim:** The leader of the Empty-Scabbard Killers agreed to remove Werz from the hit list in exchange for the party dealing with Demogorgon cultists, though no direct relationship between Werz and Eldgrim was shown [ch 019 / 019.02; ch 019 / 019.03].
*   **Blingdenstone Gnomes:** Werz offered trade favors in Blingdenstone as compensation and handed over empty spell gems significant to gnomish defenses, leading Jimjar to identify him as likely honest but connected to gnomish affairs [ch 018 / 018.04; ch 018 / 018.06].
