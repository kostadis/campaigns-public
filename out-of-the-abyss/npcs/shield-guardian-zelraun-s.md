## Identity
An animated construct originally serving Lord Zelraun Roaringhorn as his chess proxy, now accompanying the party through the Harpers. It is described as sentient and more aware than such constructs usually are; continuous practice has made it a chess master [ch 074 / entry; ch 076 / entry; ch 076 / 076.05].

## Personality and Motivations
It takes chess personally: Gyrgum’s unaided play disappointed it, and Daz’s victories prompted demands for rematches. It recognizes deliberately poor play and insists on genuine effort, clearing and resetting the board when dissatisfied [ch 074 / entry; ch 076 / 076.05].

It interprets commands literally: Daz’s instruction to reveal itself prompted it to begin removing its armor until he stopped it [ch 076 / 076.05].

## History with the Party
- In ch 074 it beat Gyrgum in two moves, lost to Gyrgum with Daz assisting, then sought a rematch against Daz, who won decisively [ch 074 / 074.03; ch 074 / entry].
- The party recognized Shield Guardians as resembling constructs previously encountered in a tower; Gyrgum mentioned having a recipe for making them [ch 074 / 074.03].
- Zelraun offered to send it with the party after Daz’s win; the party has not yet accepted [ch 074 / 074.03; ch 074 / entry].

- In ch 075 the party accepted every faction offer, including Zelraun’s offer of its companionship. The chapter records no separate handover of the guardian or its control amulet and no new action by the construct [ch 075 / 075.04; ch 074 / 074.03].
- Before the stream crossing, it presented Daz with its control amulet, granting him authority over it. His experimental command to reveal itself caused it to begin removing its armor before he ordered it to stop [ch 076 / 076.05].
- It produced a chessboard from its bag and accepted Daz’s proposal for speed chess. When he deliberately played to lose, it cleared the board and demanded a genuine match; Daz then played seriously and won [ch 076 / 076.05].
- It began challenging Daz every long rest, refusing less than his best effort. Their sustained practice prompted Daz to ask about developing chess proficiency, which the DM agreed to [ch 076 / 076.05].

## Last Observed State
Traveling with the party, with its control amulet entrusted to Daz. It carries its chessboard and challenges him every long rest, resetting games if he plays below his ability [ch 076 / 076.05].

## Relationships
- **Lord Zelraun Roaringhorn:** Its original master, who used it to test the party at chess and offered its assistance [ch 074 / entry; ch 074 / 074.03].
- **Daz:** Holds its control amulet and is its recurring chess opponent. It remembers his earlier victory and demands honest competition from him [ch 076 / 076.05].
- **Gyrgum:** Defeated him unaided in two moves, then lost when Daz assisted him; it found his unaided play disappointing [ch 074 / 074.03].
- **Octavia:** Recognized its amulet and proposed using it to test traps; no such use is recorded in the scene [ch 076 / 076.05].
