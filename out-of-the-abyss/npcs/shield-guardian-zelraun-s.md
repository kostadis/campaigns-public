## Identity
An animated construct serving Lord Zelraun Roaringhorn, which has become a master of chess through continuous practice [ch 074 / 074.03; ch 074 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
It found Gyrgum’s unaided play disappointing and wanted revenge against Daz, which gave the contest a personal quality [ch 074 / 074.03; ch 074 / entry].

## History with the Party
- In ch 074 it beat Gyrgum in two moves, lost to Gyrgum with Daz assisting, then sought a rematch against Daz, who won decisively [ch 074 / 074.03; ch 074 / entry].
- The party recognized Shield Guardians as resembling constructs previously encountered in a tower; Gyrgum mentioned having a recipe for making them [ch 074 / 074.03].
- Zelraun offered to send it with the party after Daz’s win; the party has not yet accepted [ch 074 / 074.03; ch 074 / entry].

- In ch 075 the party accepted every faction offer, including Zelraun’s offer of its companionship. The chapter records no separate handover of the guardian or its control amulet and no new action by the construct [ch 075 / 075.04; ch 074 / 074.03].

## Last Observed State
Last directly shown with Zelraun at the feast [ch 074 / 074.03]. Its offered companionship is now accepted as part of the public acceptance of all faction proposals, but a separate physical handover or control-amulet transfer is not established [ch 075 / 075.04; ch 074 / 074.03].

## Relationships
- **Lord Zelraun Roaringhorn:** serves him as his chess proxy [ch 074 / 074.03].
