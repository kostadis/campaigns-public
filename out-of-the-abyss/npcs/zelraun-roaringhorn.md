## Identity
Lord Zelraun Roaringhorn is a human wizard representing the Harpers at Bruenor’s feast in Gauntlgrym. He wears a Harper pin and is accompanied by a Shield Guardian [ch 074 / 074.03; ch 074 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Described as lawful neutral, self-assured, fickle and noticeably careless with money. He keeps three small spice boxes beside his wand and smells a spice before choosing a dish, and he pours a rare vintage for all four adventurers [ch 074 / 074.03; ch 074 / entry]. He uses his chess-master Shield Guardian to test the wits of potential allies [ch 074 / entry].

## History with the Party
- In ch 074 he appeared to reassess his own offer when Bruenor made his [ch 074 / 074.01].
- A chess piece placed before Gyrgum, with the note “His Lordship’s compliments. White moves first.”, drew the party to his table [ch 074 / 074.02].
- He set Gyrgum against his Shield Guardian. Gyrgum lost the first game in two moves and won the second with Daz’s help; Daz then won the deciding game. Zelraun applauded and added the Guardian’s companionship to his offer [ch 074 / 074.03; ch 074 / entry].
- He stated the Harpers’ interest: what caused the blink, why the demons are here, and who did it [ch 074 / 074.03; ch 074 / moment].
- His offer is access to the Harpers’ intelligence network already operating throughout the Underdark, through the Whisper Chain: across the campaign, two questions invoked before an operation, one answered with complete truth and one whose answer may contain misinformation or uncertainty, with the party knowing which kind it is invoking. An initial description involving seven agents was corrected [ch 074 / 074.03].
- Daz thanked him but explicitly withheld acceptance until every faction had been heard [ch 074 / 074.03].
- When the runner arrived with news of the corrupted wells, Zelraun closed his spice boxes [ch 074 / 074.05].

- In ch 075 the party clarified that deliberately withholding discoveries would break its agreement with the Harpers, then publicly accepted every faction offer, including Zelraun’s proposal [ch 075 / 075.01; ch 075 / 075.04; ch 074 / 074.03].
- This accepts the previously offered Whisper Chain access and the Shield Guardian’s companionship; the new chapter does not separately record transfer of the guardian or its control amulet [ch 075 / 075.04; ch 074 / 074.03].

## Last Observed State
His Harper proposal has been accepted. The chapter establishes acceptance of the existing support offer, not a separately observed guardian handover or a newly exercised Whisper Chain question [ch 075 / 075.04; ch 074 / 074.03].

## Relationships

- **Daz:** applauds his decisive chess victory [ch 074 / 074.03].
- **Gyrgum:** his chess opponent, who raises compensation and co-authorship during the negotiation [ch 074 / 074.03].
