## Identity
Dasco Pickshine is the owner and overseer of the Pickshine Mine, described by the DM as a "sales VP" who cares about quotas and business numbers [ch 044 / entry; ch 044 / 044.04; ch 046 / 046.01]. He is a gnome of approximately three feet in height [ch 046 / 046.02]. He is one of the owners of the mine, distinct from other figures like Chief Dorbo or Senni Diggermattock [ch 044 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Pickshine is driven primarily by profit, brand visibility, and operational efficiency, often at the expense of ethical considerations or worker welfare.
*   **Transactional and Evasive:** He attempts to avoid payment for services unless threatened. When the party resolved a Galeb Duhr obstruction, he initially stated, "well, I don't think you're expecting any payment as a result of this," only offering two ruby gems worth 500 gold pieces after Daz threatened to reverse the fix [ch 044 / 044.03; ch 044 / 044.04].
*   **Cost-Effective Exploitation:** He eagerly pursued using Earth Elementals for labor because "they don't cost a lot other than the summoning spell," prioritizing low overhead over ethical concerns [ch 045 / entry; ch 045 / 045.02].
*   **Branding Obsessed:** He views heroic deeds as marketing opportunities to "increase gross margins" [ch 046 / 046.01; ch 046 / entry]. He wore all-clothing with his logo, sewed a patch onto Zalthir’s clothes personally, and offered "branded tea" that he bought and packaged rather than mined, explaining, "I don't mind tea" [ch 046 / 046.02; ch 046 / moment]. When asked what a sticker was, he replied, "stickers? What is a sticker?" [ch 046 / 046.02].
*   **Pragmatic Negotiator:** While initially resistant to labor demands, he eventually agreed to a wage increase and sustainable mining practices at the Council of Blingdenstone to secure peace and continued operations, shaking hands with the labor union but deferring details to be discussed "underground" [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.06].

## History with the Party
- Dasco Pickshine, owner and overseer of Pickshine Mine, blocked the mine entrance with Galeb Duhr sentinels and attempted to force them aside with his pickaxe and threats invoking the Stoneheart Enclave contract; he initially refused payment to the party for resolving the issue, offering two ruby gems worth 500 gold pieces only after Daz threatened to undo the solution [ch 044 / 044.03; ch 044 / 044.04; ch 044 / entry].
- Pickshine expressed delight at the prospect of hiring Earth Elementals for mining labor, noting they "don't cost a lot other than the summoning spell," before the party convinced Blingdenstone’s leadership to restrict elemental usage to defense and warfare only [ch 045 / 045.02; ch 045 / entry].
- During the assault on the Pudding King, Pickshine led the Pickshine Miners as one of three proposed allied forces, motivated by a desire to "increase gross margins" through heroic branding; he offered "branded tea" (bought and packaged, not mined) and personally sewed a logo patch onto Zalthir’s clothes [ch 046 / 046.01; ch 046 / 046.02; ch 046 / entry].
- Pickshine warned the party about a black pudding pit, describing it as "a dissolved trap," and offered to bridge it with mithral braces and salt neutralizers; this required his miners to stay behind to maintain the supports, removing them from the final battle, though the party accepted the bridge in exchange for safe passage [ch 046 / 046.04; ch 046 / entry].
- At the Council of Blingdenstone, Pickshine argued for immediate strip-mining of salt to fund repairs, clashing with working-class demands for higher wages; he eventually agreed to a sustainable mining compromise and a wage increase, shaking hands with the labor union while deferring details to be discussed "underground" [ch 048 / 048.06; ch 048 / entry].
- After the conflict, an officer from Dasco Pickshine's army led an honor guard to escort the party toward the Overbright; Jimjar later credited the party with getting Pickshine "to pay a fair wage for once" [ch 048 / 048.07; ch 048 / moment].

## Last Observed State
Dasco Pickshine was last seen in Chapter 048. During the Council of Blingdenstone, he argued for immediate strip-mining to fund repairs but shook hands with the labor union over a wage bump and agreed to a sustainable mining compromise brokered by the party [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.06]. Following the council, an officer from "Dasco Pickshine's army" led an honor guard to escort the party toward the Overbright as they departed for the surface [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.07]. Jimjar noted that the party had gotten Pickshine "to pay a fair wage for once" [ch 048 / moment].

## Relationships
*   **The Party (The Ember Vanguard):** Pickshine interacted with the party primarily as a service provider or obstacle. He attempted to avoid paying Daz, Zalthir, Thorin, and Gyrgum for resolving the Galeb Duhr issue [ch 044 / 044.04]. He attempted to recruit them (specifically Daz, whom he asked to call "general") for his branded campaign, sewing a logo patch onto Zalthir [ch 046 / 046.02; ch 046 / moment]. He engaged in political maneuvering with the party at the Council, where they brokered a wage agreement between his guild and the workers [ch 048 / 048.06].
*   **Pickshine Miners:** He is their overseer and employer. He offered to bridge the pit with them on the stated condition that they stay behind to maintain the anchors, leaving the choice to Daz with "Your call" [ch 046 / entry; ch 046 / 046.04]. The miners were proud to add value without risking their lives in the final assault [ch 046 / 046.04].
*   **Blingdenstone Leadership:** He is a counterweight to Chief Dorbo Diggermattock and Senni Diggermattock. While Dorbo focused on immediate defense costs and Senni on community welfare, Pickshine focused on mining logistics and cost-cutting via elemental labor [ch 045 / 045.02; ch 046 / 046.01]. He clashed with working-class demands for higher wages at the Council [ch 048 / 048.06].
*   **Daz:** Pickshine asked to call Daz "general," but Daz rejected the title, instructing him to "call me Daz" [ch 046 / 046.04; ch 046 / moment].
*   **Jimjar:** Though not directly interacting in the provided scenes, Jimjar referenced Pickshine in a comment to the party, crediting them with forcing Pickshine to pay fair wages [ch 048 / moment].
