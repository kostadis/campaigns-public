## Identity
Bookwyrm, also known as the First Reader, was a green dragonborn and the real name of Skoda Vanaster [ch 062 / entry]. She served as the current acting head of Candlekeep following the death of Janussi [ch 062 / entry]. She was the aunt of Alkrist [ch 062 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Bookwyrm presented herself with regal composure and acted decisively to maintain order, such as restoring calm to the Council of Twelve with the command "FOCUS, PEOPLE!" [ch 057 / 057.04; ch 057 / 057.05]. She displayed a strong sense of institutional pride, noting that "The Library of Candlekeep is not in the habit of giving out books," and negotiated rewards involving library access rather than gold [ch 057 / moment; ch 057 / 057.05].

Privately, Bookwyrm was motivated by ambition and a belief that the leadership of Candlekeep was rightfully hers; she felt betrayed when Janussi indicated she would not become Keeper of Tomes [ch 061 / 061.07]. She engaged in political maneuvering, pushing hardest against Kalan Strongbranch’s proposed investigative reforms and urging Janussi to reject them [ch 061 / 061.05]. During the investigation, she adopted a theatrical demeanor, performing heartbreak over allegations against her nephew and referring to Janussi as "beloved" despite her private resentment [ch 062 / entry; ch 062 / 062.03; ch 061 / 061.07]. Observers noted her relief rather than grief when evidence against Alkrist emerged, and suspicion regarding her impartiality due to her removal of Kalan from the investigation [ch 059 / entry; ch 062 / entry; ch 062 / 062.03].

## History with the Party
- Bookwyrm received the party upon their arrival at Candlekeep, accepting donated texts with enthusiasm, particularly the Velkynvelve Prisoner Journal, which she called "a masterpiece of interesting value" [ch 054 / 054.06; ch 054 / entry].
- She efficiently directed the party to specialized scholars: Zalthir to learn about Jimjar, Thorin and Dawnbringer to Philemon, and noted Avowed Khell-Vire’s interest in Gyrgum [ch 054 / 054.06].
- The party presented a shrunken drow spy from House T'sarran; Gatewarden Kalan Strongbranch, summoned in the same scene, left the decision on her fate to the party [ch 054 / 054.06; ch 056 / 056.04].
- Following the murder of the Keeper of Tomes, Bookwyrm summoned the party through her adjutant Fembris Lancer before the rest of the Avowed awoke [ch 057 / 057.03].
- She restored order to the chaotic Council of Twelve with a sharp tap and the command "FOCUS, PEOPLE!" [ch 057 / 057.04; ch 057 / 057.05].
- Bookwyrm proposed delegating the investigation to the party to ensure impartiality and required them to sign confidentiality papers [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.05].
- She publicly stripped Gatewarden Kalan Strongbranch of the investigation, citing his troubled relationship with the victim and the need for impartiality [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.05].
- Bookwyrm negotiated the reward for the party: upon successful resolution, each member would receive a copy of any book in the library, made at the House of the Binder [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.05].
- She escorted the party to the crime scene, stepping carefully around a mysterious gray cat on the stairs [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.06].
- Bookwyrm deflected questions about Kalan’s specific negligence, stating she did not want to prejudice the investigation, but affirmed she had nothing to do with the murder [ch 057 / 057.06].
- She assigned the party Deneir's Sanctum as their base of operations, providing Fembris Lancer and Vooshadi Moonriver as support [ch 059 / entry; ch 059 / 059.01].
- During the debrief, Bookwyrm probed for specific suspects and displayed a marked, though unstated, preference for the party to speak with Sylvira Savikas [ch 059 / entry; ch 059 / 059.01].
- The party noted Bookwyrm was a suspect of interest due to the suspicious circumstances of Kalan's removal, though they declined to share specific suspects with her [ch 059 / entry; ch 059 / 059.02].
- Bookwyrm was expected to attend an emergency academic disputation at the Oval Theatre alongside other senior figures like Teles and Fheminor, where her presence was viewed as a political statement rather than just an intellectual one [ch 060 / 060.01].
- During the investigation, the party identified Bookwyrm as one of the high-ranking officials whose chambers were targeted for a stealth search by Zalthir and Glabbagool [ch 060 / 060.09].
- The search of Bookwyrm’s spacious, orderly chambers on the top floor of the Readers’ Tower yielded no significant findings; Glabbagool detected no trace of poison in the room [ch 060 / 060.09].
- Bookwyrm instructed the junior Avowed to take leftovers from the Deadwinter feast back to their dormitories rather than spending the night cleaning; none of those who ate the leftovers became ill [ch 060 / 060.06].
- Sylvira Savikas identified Bookwyrm as the person who pushed hardest against Kalan Strongbranch’s proposed investigative reforms and urged Janussi to reject them [ch 061 / 061.05].
- The party identified Bookwyrm as one of three promising avenues for further investigation, alongside Alkrist and Sylvira, when they split their leads [ch 061 / 061.03].
- Alkrist, Bookwyrm’s nephew, revealed under the Zone of Truth that he visited Bookwyrm shortly after midnight, announcing that Janussi had been poisoned; Bookwyrm was appalled, argued with him, and stormed out between midnight and 12:30 a.m. [ch 061 / 061.07; ch 061 / 061.08].
- The party concluded that Bookwyrm concealed the critical information that she knew about the poisoning when she placed the investigation in the party’s hands [ch 061 / 061.08].
- Bookwyrm believed the leadership of Candlekeep was rightfully hers and felt betrayed when Janussi told her she would not become Keeper of Tomes [ch 061 / 061.07].
- Because Bookwyrm’s knowledge of the heart’s removal was not established by Alkrist’s testimony, suspicion regarding the mutilation shifted toward her and others who might understand the chalice’s properties [ch 061 / 061.08].
- Status not established in the summaries regarding Bookwyrm’s current location or freedom, though she was last seen storming out of her own chambers after arguing with Alkrist there, sometime between midnight and roughly 12:30 a.m. [ch 061 / 061.07].

## Last Observed State
Bookwyrm is dead [ch 065 / entry]. She was found dead in her own office with her throat torn apart by Moziqodo, Sylvira Savikas’ abyss-born son [ch 063 / entry; ch 065 / entry]. Her heart remained untouched, distinguishing her killing from the surgical murder of Janussi [ch 063 / entry]. A half-finished note was discovered on her desk reading "*He is using the beast to—*," indicating she recognized the immediate threat before she was killed [ch 063 / moment; ch 063 / 063.02]. Moziqodo took her High Tower key and delivered it to A'lai Aivenmore [ch 065 / entry]. Her death, along with the neutralization of other senior Avowed, critically weakened Candlekeep’s intended final line of defense, allowing Manshoon’s simulacrum to breach the keep’s inner sanctum [ch 065 / entry; ch 065 / 065.03]. Some Avowed members are currently seeking vengeance for her death [ch 065 / 065.03].
