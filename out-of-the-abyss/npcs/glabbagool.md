## Identity
Glabbagool is a sentient grey ooze who formerly identified as a gelatinous cube. He possesses the ability to speak and think, having transitioned from a mindless creature to a sentient being through unknown means [ch 034 / 034.03; ch 035 / entry]. He distinguishes himself from hostile oozes, insisting on taxonomic accuracy; he was a gelatinous cube for most of his career but currently inhabits a grey ooze body after swapping bodies with the Pudding King [ch 036 / 036.04; ch 047 / 047.06]. Glabbagool serves as an eighth-level sidekick bonded to Zalthir, possessing abilities such as *Shadow Step*, a flurry of blows, and a *Corrosive Embrace* when Zalthir grapples an enemy [ch 059 / 059.06].

Physically, Glabbagool is 10x10x10 feet (when in cube form) and moves slowly, often arriving late to combat [ch 034 / 034.04; ch 041 / 041.04]. He does not breathe, needs no mask against spores, and can absorb matter through his body [ch 034 / 034.08; ch 043 / 043.08]. He often manifests large, googly eyes to appear friendly, though he is capable of manifesting many eyes simultaneously to intimidate [ch 054 / 054.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Glabbagool is characterized by earnest, hero-worshipping enthusiasm and a naive desire for social belonging [ch 069 / entry]. Before joining the party he asked whether they would be upset if they all died and he ate them, and later decided he wanted friends more than payment [ch 034 / 034.03; ch 038 / 038.01]. He insists he is a gelatinous cube and not an ooze, correcting those who confuse him with mindless oozes, whom he refers to as his "fat friends" [ch 036 / 036.02; ch 036 / 036.04]; Daz later called the black pudding pit Glabbagool's "evil cousin" [ch 046 / 046.04].

Glabbagool acts as the party’s "living bulldozer," "cleaning crew," and garbage disposal, often absorbing debris, spilled alcohol, or hazardous materials [ch 038 / 038.02; ch 040 / 040.02; ch 043 / 043.04]. He is prone to curiosity and distraction, often asking questions about new concepts (such as birds, dairy, or generalship) and struggling to remain still during stealth checks [ch 040 / 040.06; ch 046 / 046.08; ch 055 / 055.04]. Despite his loyalty, he admits to vulnerability regarding external mental influences, having previously heard a voice calling him from a pool of slimes, but he promises to fight any attempts by entities like the Pudding King to harm his friends [ch 046 / 046.08].

## Last Observed State
Glabbagool is currently accompanied by the party in the upper levels of Candlekeep following the defeat of Manshoon’s simulacrum and the capture of the doppelganger Edvaldo [ch 069 / 069.04; ch 071 / 071.01]. ## Relationships
*   **Zalthir:** Glabbagool’s closest bond is with Zalthir, whom he views as a "kindred spirit" [ch 038 / 038.02]. Zalthir had offered, in some sense, to teach Glabbagool the ways of a monk, but Glabbagool learned by watching — at his trial in the Whispering Dome he answered, "The body that mimics nothing is already shaped by what it watches." — and bonded with him as a sidekick [ch 054 / 054.01; ch 059 / 059.06; ch 060 / 060.04]. Zalthir relies on Glabbagool for tactical support, such as using him to steal items or attack enemies via *Corrosive Embrace* [ch 062 / 062.04; ch 062 / 062.06]. Glabbagool often interprets Zalthir’s actions through a lens of heroic awe, though he once earnestly asked why Zalthir had not simply thrown Manshoon into the lava, and Zalthir answered that killing him before he cast a spell would not have been "sporty" [ch 069 / 069.03].
*   **The Party:** Glabbagool considers the entire party his friends and protects them, specifically shielding Zalthir when he was knocked unconscious in the High Tower [ch 064 / 064.04]. Gyrgum refers to him as the party’s friend, bulldozer, and cleaning crew [ch 043 / 043.04].
*   **A'lai Aivenmore:** Glabbagool wondered aloud whether an Avowed wizard dissolves like other people, which frightened A'lai [ch 065 / 065.02].
*   **Edvaldo Sedanur:** Glabbagool was suggested as a guard for Edvaldo but remained with the party [ch 069 / 069.04]. He watched Edvaldo’s transformation abilities with unusual interest while the party speculated about whether a sentient ooze might someday learn similar transformations [ch 071 / 071.01].
