## Identity
- Dynaphyse was a servant of Lathander, the Morning Lord, and the first and true wielder of the sentient blade Dawnbringer. [ch 072 / entry; ch 072 / 072.02]
- Brysis of Khaem killed her. [ch 072 / entry; ch 072 / 072.02]
- The party has never met her. She appears only as a name and a history, spoken by Dawnbringer in Philemon's reading salon. [ch 072 / 072.02]
- Dawnbringer confirmed the spelling is with an "S," not a "Z." [ch 072 / 072.02]
- An obscure Netherese temple record appears to preserve part of her story. It refers to "a Lathanderite woman of the Dawn," struck from the record and lost with Khaem. [ch 072 / entry; ch 072 / 072.02]
- Age, appearance, origin, and rank within Lathander's service are not established in the summaries.

## Personality and Motivations
- Her personality and motives are not established in the summaries. She is never shown acting or speaking. [ch 072 / 072.02]
- The only descriptors are her service to Lathander and her role as Dawnbringer's first and true wielder. [ch 072 / entry]
- Dawnbringer's grief at speaking her name suggests a close bond. The summary says Dawnbringer wept in Thorin's mind as she said it. [ch 072 / 072.02]
- Philemon's note says Lathander is "the god of second chances" and that Dawnbringer "has just had hers." This refers to Dawnbringer, not to Dynaphyse's own motives. [ch 072 / 072.02]

## History with the Party
- Dynaphyse was not present in this chapter; she was named and spoken of, with no sign of her acting or speaking. [ch 072 / 072.02]
- Dawnbringer spoke the name "Dynaphyse" as her true wielder in Philemon's reading salon. This followed Philemon's prompt that the ritual had to move to its next layer. Dawnbringer wept in Thorin's mind as she said it. [ch 072 / 072.02; ch 072 / moment]
- Dynaphyse was a servant of Lathander, the Morning Lord, and Dawnbringer's first and true wielder. [ch 072 / entry; ch 072 / 072.02]
- Brysis of Khaem killed Dynaphyse. Dawnbringer says Brysis "buried me with her own corpse." [ch 072 / 072.02; ch 072 / moment]
- Her death is established in the evidence only as a killing by Brysis of Khaem. The summary frames Dawnbringer as stolen and buried by her captor, not lost. [ch 072 / entry; ch 072 / moment]
- Thorin had believed Brysis was Dawnbringer's former wielder. His insight led him to understand that Brysis was her captor, never her wielder. This is what opened the way to Dynaphyse's name. [ch 072 / 072.02]
- Dawnbringer confirmed the name is spelled with an "S," not a "Z," when Philemon asked. [ch 072 / 072.02]
- Philemon connected Dynaphyse to a line in the Netherese temple scrolls about "a Lathanderite woman of the Dawn," struck from the record and lost with Khaem. He had never known what Khaem had taken. The entry says the record "appears to preserve part of her story." [ch 072 / 072.02; ch 072 / entry]
- Dawnbringer said her story survived as an obscure fragment of Netherese history, a civilization from four or five thousand years ago that was lost after attempting to break the Weave. [ch 072 / 072.02]
- Naming Dynaphyse was followed by Dawnbringer losing her fear of the dark. She then bound the blessing Lathander's Dawn to Thorin. Once between long rests, when he would fall, she flares with the light of dawn and leaves him standing with a single breath of life. [ch 072 / 072.02]
