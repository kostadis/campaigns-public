## Identity
- Sylvira Savikas is a tiefling scholar of demons and devils, described as Candlekeep's demonologist [ch 058 / entry; ch 071 / 071.07]. She was also spoken of as a Great Reader who curates the Infernal Fortress, Candlekeep's library of the Abyss and the Nine Hells [ch 057 / entry].
- Appearance: copper-red skin, magnetic golden eyes and blue-black hair [ch 061 / entry].
- She is the mother of Moziqodo, an abyss-born son who was kept in a magical sanctuary [ch 063 / 063.02; ch 063 / 063.04] [GM].
- She had an on-and-off, volatile romantic relationship with Janussi [ch 058 / entry; ch 058 / 058.05].
- In ch 061 Daz interviewed her in her sickroom [ch 061 / 061.05].

## Personality and Motivations
- **Proud and quick to anger.** She initially took the warning that she was being framed as an accusation, then calmed once Daz persuaded her of his sincerity [ch 061 / 061.05]. Earlier she had screamed at Janussi that he was a traitor and stormed out [ch 058 / 058.04]. She also stormed out of the Deadwinter supper [ch 060 / 060.06].
- **Practical and cooperative once convinced.** She named suspects (Daral Yashenti, then Kazryn Nyantani, and Teles Ahvoste as a longtime resenter of Janussi) [ch 061 / 061.05; ch 061 / entry]. By ch 067 she was grateful and eager to help Candlekeep, supplying the true name of Miirym, the Sentinel Wyrm, and a warning about three strangers in the Orrery [ch 067 / entry; ch 067 / 067.03].
- **Motivation, stated:** "We must focus on the problem at hand, capturing the person who murdered my love and who is impersonating me." [ch 061 / 061.05]
- **Sense of being a target.** She said prejudiced people in the Overbright see a tiefling and assume "abyssal spawn", and that her infernal-plane research deepens that [ch 061 / entry; ch 061 / 061.05].
- **Professional judgment.** She declined to intercede for Kalan with Janussi because she thought his research "third rate at best" [ch 061 / 061.05].
- **Anger at the party (ch 072).** She is angry that the party never told her what happened to her son [ch 072 / entry; ch 072 / 072.06] [GM].
