## Identity
Sylvira Savikas is a tiefling, identified by her glowing red face in candlelight and darkvision [ch 058 / 058.05]. She holds the title of local demonologist at Candlekeep [ch 071 / entry; ch 071 / 071.07]. She possesses an abyss-born son named Moziqodo [ch 063 / 063.02; ch 063 / 063.04]. During the investigation into Janussi’s murder, she was subject to scrutiny regarding her relationship with the victim and the magical disguise of her likeness [ch 058 / 058.05; ch 060 / 060.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Sylvira has a volatile and explosive temperament, particularly in her romantic relationships [ch 058 / 058.05]. Queenie reported that Sylvira blasted an armchair with magic missiles during a lover’s quarrel with Janussi [ch 058 / 058.06]. Daral Yashenti called her "his horned tart", one of several racially tinged remarks that drew pushback from the party [ch 059 / 059.05]. She is deeply knowledgeable in demonic lore and cosmology, having provided the true name of Miirym (Vydykyq) and identified the Eastern Light of Mystra's Mantle (Limniz) [ch 067 / 067.03; ch 067 / entry]. She is cooperative with those she views as allies, offering assistance with riddles and thanking Thorin for his actions [ch 067 / 067.03]. When approached regarding her alleged involvement in crimes, she became distraught and defensive, asking, "What do you mean I’ve been framed? … This is outrageous. Are you here to accuse me of a crime?" [ch 061 / moment]. She denied being the person seen with honey: "Honey? I would never drink honey. What kind of nonsense is this?" [ch 061 / moment].
