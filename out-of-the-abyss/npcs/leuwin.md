## Identity
Leuwin is an elderly, absent-minded satyr druid who operates the Oak Tree Apothecary, a wooden treehouse constructed among the boughs of a huge evergreen oak [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.02]. He spends his days distilling tinctures, crushing seeds, and macerating herbs in his shop, which is stocked with ointments, balms, and medicines [ch 060 / 060.02; ch 060 / 060.08]. He employs a squirrel assistant named Nibbles [ch 060 / 060.08]. Daral identified Leuwin as the person capable of definitively identifying the poison found on Janussi’s book [ch 060 / 060.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Leuwin is characterized by his absent-mindedness, which resulted in him leaving his "dangerous toxins" shelf accessible while he attended to a customer in the greenhouse [ch 060 / entry]. He is dedicated to his trade, working among shelves of medicines and maintaining a year-round herb garden using magic [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.08]. When he found the broken vial he called for his squirrel assistant, Nibbles, hoping it had witnessed what he missed [ch 060 / 060.08].

## History with the Party
*   Leuwin is the elderly, absent-minded proprietor of the Oak Tree Apothecary, a wooden treehouse situated among the boughs of a large evergreen oak [ch 060 / 060.02; ch 060 / 060.08].
*   Daral identified Leuwin as the person who could definitively identify the poison found on Janussi’s book, and Thorin used Daral to facilitate an introduction to him [ch 060 / 060.02].
*   Daz visited Leuwin at his apothecary, where Leuwin was working among shelves of medicines, distilling tinctures, crushing seeds, and macerating herbs [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.08].
*   Leuwin recalled that Fheminor had visited at approximately 2:30 p.m. for cold medicine, and he provided her with tincture of horehound and licorice [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.08].
*   Leuwin also recalled that A’lai arrived around 3 p.m. seeking fresh fennel, after which Leuwin accompanied A’lai to the greenhouse below via the rear exit [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.08].
*   Leuwin was shocked when Daz discovered broken glass and a peppermint-covered rag near a shelf labeled "dangerous toxins," and he could not explain how the vial had broken [ch 060 / 060.08].
*   Leuwin supplied nuts to Daz to facilitate the use of *Speak with Animals* on Leuwin’s squirrel assistant, Nibbles, who witnessed a Dragonborn intruder entering while Leuwin was in the greenhouse [ch 060 / 060.08].
*   It was later confirmed under the Zone of Truth during Alkrist’s interrogation that A’lai distracted Leuwin in the garden specifically to allow Alkrist to steal the Midnight Tears [ch 061 / entry; ch 061 / 061.07].
*   Alkrist admitted that while A’lai distracted Leuwin, he stole a vial from a high shelf and spilled ground peppermint across the floor, actions Leuwin had witnessed the physical aftermath of but not the theft itself [ch 061 / 061.07].

## Last Observed State
Leuwin was last seen in Chapter 060, reacting with shock when Daz discovered broken glass and a peppermint-covered rag near his "dangerous toxins" shelf, an area Leuwin could not explain how the vial had broken from [ch 060 / 060.08]. In Chapter 061, Leuwin was mentioned in Alkrist’s confession, which confirmed that A’lai had distracted him in the garden to allow Alkrist to steal the Midnight Tears [ch 061 / entry; ch 061 / 061.07].
