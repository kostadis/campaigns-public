## Identity
Edvaldo Sedanur is a doppelganger who infiltrated Candlekeep by assuming the identity of a scholar. While posing as a researcher, he presented himself as an Avowed and provided historical context on Candlekeep’s architecture [ch 068 / entry]. His true nature was revealed when he escaped bonds by shapeshifting into a smaller form and subsequently into a halfling to flee [ch 070 / entry; ch 071 / 071.01]. He possesses telepathic abilities, demonstrated when he assumed Thorin’s appearance and then shifted his face into a visage from Thorin’s childhood, proving he can read surface thoughts [ch 071 / 071.01]. His physical transformation is limited to humanoid forms ranging from halfling to Goliath size; he explicitly denied the ability to transform into objects like pizza slices [ch 071 / 071.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Edvaldo is motivated primarily by self-preservation and the preservation of his utility to his captors. He frames his infiltration and betrayal as a professional disagreement rather than a personal vendetta [ch 071 / 071.01]. During his interrogation, he argued, "Kill me, and I go gray and quiet, and you've got a robe and a story," and claimed his long service to Manshoon made him valuable [ch 071 / 071.01]. He is desperate to avoid Manshoon’s retribution, citing the fear of his former master’s remote manifestations and lethal punishments as his primary reason for requesting confinement in a magic-proof cell [ch 071 / 071.06]. He maintains a facade of academic professionalism, attempting to negotiate second authorship on papers to ensure his involvement in party activities [ch 069 / 069.04].

*   He presented himself as an Avowed and recognized the central tower’s architectural style, lecturing on its history and the loss of similar examples to renovation. [ch 068 / entry; ch 068 / 068.02]

*   The party debated using a boon to verify identities, which would have exposed him, but chose the anti-magic curtain instead, leaving him unexposed when combat with Manshoon began. [ch 068 / entry]
*   In the battle against Manshoon, Edvaldo was caught by a Ward Eater effect and immobilized; he survived damage that calculation suggested should have killed him, raising the party's suspicions. [ch 069 / entry; ch 069 / 069.01]
*   Manshoon positioned himself behind Edvaldo, using the researcher as a body shield, which Edvaldo disliked. [ch 069 / 069.01]
*   After the first Manshoon encounter, Zalthir grappled Edvaldo and dragged him toward the lava; Edvaldo protested his innocence and spoke fluently about Candlekeep lore to prove his identity. [ch 069 / entry; ch 069 / 069.03]
*   Daz’s investigation found Edvaldo’s account convincing, though the party remained suspicious due to his impossible survival and earlier vague knowledge of the tower’s height. [ch 069 / entry; ch 069 / 069.03]
*   Gyrgum suggested leaving Glabbagool to watch Edvaldo; Edvaldo negotiated to remain involved if he received second authorship on the eventual paper about the combat. [ch 069 / entry; ch 069 / 069.04]
*   Edvaldo was thoroughly bound and left in the anti-magic zone while the party ascended to confront the second Manshoon. [ch 069 / entry; ch 069 / 069.04]
*   The party questioned Edvaldo about the Obsidian Door riddle; he first answered “Books!” then “Candles,” failing to demonstrate knowledge sufficient to open the door. [ch 069 / entry; ch 069 / 069.06]
*   Edvaldo escaped his bonds by changing into a smaller, thinner shape and appeared at the top of the stairs revealed as a pale, featureless doppelganger serving Manshoon. [ch 070 / entry; ch 070 / 070.02]
*   Edvaldo warned the party that killing the Manshoon simulacrum would trigger an explosion, which could cause a catastrophic chain reaction among the chamber’s warded books. [ch 070 / entry; ch 070 / 070.02]
*   With Manshoon thrown into the lava, Edvaldo panicked and smashed two prophecy crystals using debris from a dead owlbear, threatening to destroy the rest unless the party opened the door. [ch 070 / entry; ch 070 / 070.03]
*   After Gyrgum opened the door, Edvaldo believed he could still seize the book; he was deceived by Daz’s illusion that the book had turned to ash. [ch 070 / entry; ch 070 / 070.03; ch 070 / 070.04]
*   Thorin frightened Edvaldo with a Menacing Attack, and Zalthir grappled him, dragging him toward the stairs. [ch 070 / entry; ch 070 / 070.03]

## Last Observed State
Edvaldo was captured by Zalthir on a bridge spanning a lava pit and dragged back to the tower in an active grapple to prevent his escape via shapeshifting [ch 071 / 071.01]. He was subsequently held while Fheminor Scrivenbark cast *true sight*, which revealed his true doppelganger form to the assembled Candlekeep leaders [ch 071 / 071.04]. Following an interrogation regarding Manshoon’s plan to control Demon Lords using rites from the Book of Vile Darkness, the party debated his fate [ch 071 / 071.07]. Thorin opposed execution, Gyrgum honored his promise of confinement, Daz saw potential future utility, and Zalthir accepted the majority judgment [ch 071 / 071.08]. Edvaldo was escorted to a magic-proof cell within Candlekeep to protect him from Manshoon [ch 071 / 071.08]. As a final gesture of gratitude to Zalthir, he transformed into a Dragonborn that Zalthir had previously found attractive [ch 071 / 071.08]. Status is confined/alive [ch 071 / 071.08].

## Relationships
*   **Manshoon:** Edvaldo served Manshoon, posing as an Avowed researcher [ch 070 / entry]. He expressed resentment over the failure of Manshoon’s ten-year preparation, stating, "We spent 10 years setting up today, 10 years" [ch 071 / 071.07]. He fears Manshoon’s retribution and views the magic-proof cell as necessary protection [ch 071 / 071.06].
*   **Zalthir:** Zalthir physically apprehended Edvaldo and maintained custody of him via an active grapple. Edvaldo recognized Zalthir as the individual responsible for disposing of Manshoon’s duplicate in the lava, saying, "I watched where you put the last one. I would very much like not to go there" [ch 071 / 071.01]. Edvaldo’s final act of gratitude was transforming into a specific Dragonborn form Zalthir found attractive [ch 071 / 071.08].
*   **Thorin:** Edvaldo attempted to prove his value by reading Thorin’s mind, assuming Thorin’s form and then a face from Thorin’s childhood. The summary calls the demonstration a chilling display that did little to make Edvaldo appear trustworthy [ch 071 / 071.01].
*   **The Party (Daz and Gyrgum):** Gyrgum promised Edvaldo a cell, and Daz advocated for his utility despite noting Edvaldo added "entropy than clarity" to their situation [ch 071 / 071.08]. Edvaldo attempted to engage the party’s academic interests by requesting second authorship on research papers [ch 069 / 069.04].
