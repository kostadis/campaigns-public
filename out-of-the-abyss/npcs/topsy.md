## Identity
Topsy is a deep gnome and the twin of Turvy. She was initially introduced as a prisoner in Velkynvelve [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.02]. She possesses a secret ability to transform into a rat, a skill she uses to facilitate escape [ch 004 / entry]. Topsy and Turvy are wererats; when the twins disappeared, Zalthir asked "Were they the wererats?" [ch 004 / entry; ch 014 / 014.03].

## Personality and Motivations
Topsy is an experienced thief who prioritizes discretion, advising that theft be done "carefully, quietly, and not make a big production of it" while noting the conspicuous nature of her companions [ch 002 / 002.02]. She is hesitant to return to Blingdenstone due to a past disagreement regarding the twins' profession and their reputation for thievery, preferring instead to follow the party elsewhere [ch 004 / entry]. She is capable of decisive action in combat and rescue, assisting in freeing trapped companions during a carrion crawler ambush [ch 005 / entry; ch 005 / 005.02].

## History with the Party
