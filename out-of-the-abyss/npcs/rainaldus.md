## Identity

Rainaldus is a senior Zhentarim guide employed by Davra, tasked with delivering the party safely to Mantol-Derith alongside his trainee, Octavia. The guides introduce themselves as Davra’s employees without openly advertising their Zhentarim affiliation. [ch 076 / 076.02]

## Personality and Motivations

- Experienced, somewhat cynical, and protective of Octavia, though often exasperated by her mistakes. [ch 076 / entry]
- Reads at every halt and initially prefers walking to riding a giant lizard, eventually agreeing to ride with the expedition. [ch 076 / 076.03]
- Emphasizes preparation, discretion, and adapting when conditions contradict written accounts. Davra’s instruction to deliver the party safely supplies his stated assignment. [ch 076 / entry; ch 076 / 076.02]

## History with the Party

- Joined the expedition with Octavia as its guides to Mantol-Derith; read during halts throughout the eight-day surface journey. [ch 076 / 076.02]
- Presented Davra’s authorization at the hidden cave checkpoint and recorded the expedition’s headcount. Identified the mount equipment as Morista’s, explaining that Davra had mentioned calling in a favor. [ch 076 / 076.03]
- Was visibly unsettled by the slaughtered underground camp and determined that its ashes had been cold for several days. [ch 076 / 076.04]
- Explained the Shield Guardian’s control amulet to Daz and suggested that the construct could carry him. [ch 076 / 076.05]
- Reprimanded Octavia for leaving a testing tool at the cave, reminding her to make lists. [ch 076 / 076.06]
- Attempted to quiet Octavia’s disclosure about Khalessa Draga and distinguished the merchant’s reputation from confirmed whereabouts. Estimated an underground journey of about twenty-five days. [ch 076 / 076.07]
- At the fungal cavern, reminded Octavia that actual conditions could differ from her written route. [ch 076 / 076.08]

## Last Observed State

At the fungal cavern, Rainaldus spoke to Octavia about the limits of written guidance. The party was still considering how to cross; no crossing or combat had begun when the session ended. [ch 076 / 076.08]

## Relationships

- **Octavia:** His trainee; he is protective and exasperated, correcting her forgotten equipment, indiscreet conversation, and reliance on written descriptions. [ch 076 / entry]
- **Davra:** His employer, whose papers secure checkpoint passage and whose instructions require the party’s safe delivery. [ch 076 / 076.02; ch 076 / 076.03]
- **Zhentarim:** An experienced agent who handles the organization’s checkpoint procedures for the expedition. [ch 076 / 076.02; ch 076 / 076.03]
