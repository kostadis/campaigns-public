## Identity
Sovereign Phylo is one of the two co-leaders of Neverlight Grove, sharing sovereignty with Basidia. [ch 029 / entry] He is a myconid sovereign who has been fully corrupted by the influence of the demon Zuggtmoy (also referred to as the "Great Seeder," "great cedar," or "great Zuggtmoy"). [ch 029 / 029.05; ch 032 / entry] While myconids are naturally genderless, Phylo consistently uses feminine pronouns when referring to the entity he serves, a behavior the party identified as unnatural and indicative of corruption. [ch 029 / entry; ch 031 / 031.02]

## Personality and Motivations
Phylo presents a facade of warm hospitality, addressing visitors as "friendly softers" and eagerly inviting them to a "great celebration." [ch 029 / 029.05; ch 030 / 030.05] His core motivation is the fulfillment of a "great awakening" orchestrated by Zuggtmoy, which he believes will bring happiness and perfection to the Grove. [ch 030 / 030.05; ch 032 / 032.03] He views this corruption as a beneficial restructuring, having segregated the myconid circles into specialized groups and created a secretive "Circle of Masters" to hoard resources and send followers out in secret. [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.06] Phylo remains cheerfully deluded even in the face of confrontation, insisting that the "Great Cedar" will make everyone happy and lamenting those who reject his invitation to the "Garden of Welcome," which functions as a trap for conversion or sacrifice. [ch 031 / 031.03; ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.03]

## History with the Party
- Sovereign Phylo is one of the two co-leaders of Neverlight Grove, alongside Sovereign Basidia. [ch 029 / entry]
- Phylo warmly welcomed the party upon their arrival, addressing them as "friendly softers" and promising to show them the Circle of Masters and a "great celebration" the following day. [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.05]
- During the initial welcome, Phylo spoke ominously of a coming "day of joy" and described a "Great Seeder" (also referred to as the "great cedar" and "great Zuggtmoy") using feminine pronouns, a behavior noted by the party as deeply unnatural for genderless myconids. [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.05; ch 031 / 031.02]
- Phylo acknowledged Basidia as his "sovereign and co-equal in all things" when she intervened to lead the tour away from him. [ch 029 / 029.05]
- Phylo had implemented controversial policies segregating the myconid circles and creating a secretive "Circle of Masters," which Basidia and other loyal myconids feared indicated corruption. [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.06]
- Phylo planned to guide the party to the "Garden of Welcome" (referred to as the "Circle of Welcome" in later dialogue), which Basidia warned was a trap where previous visitors had disappeared. [ch 029 / 029.06; ch 032 / 032.03]
- Myconids such as Gasbide and Hebopbe cited Phylo’s promises of a "great awakening" and "better day" to justify their support for his new organizational structure. [ch 030 / 030.05; ch 031 / 031.02]
- The party discovered that a drow patrol sent by Ilvara, including Xinaya, had been captured and transformed into fungal thralls after accepting his invitation to the Garden of Welcome. [ch 031 / 031.03]
- Upon the party’s return to Basidia after investigating the Garden, Phylo attempted to maintain a facade of normalcy, cheerfully asking if the guests had enjoyed the "changes" to the grove. [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.03]
- When Basidia accused Phylo of betrayal and allying with corruption, Phylo turned hostile; the grove’s vegetation began moving to attack Basidia. [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.03]
- As the party fled, Phylo remained cheerfully deluded, calling after them and promising that the "Great Cedar" would make everyone happy and that the Grove would be "better than ever." [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.03]

## Last Observed State
The party returned to the Grove after discovering the horrors of the Garden of Welcome and attempted to maintain a facade of normalcy to avoid immediate conflict. [ch 032 / 032.03] Phylo greeted them cheerfully, asking if they had enjoyed the "changes" to the grove. [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.03] When Basidia accused him of betrayal and corruption, Phylo turned hostile, and the grove's vegetation began to move and attack her. [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.03] As the party and loyalists fled, Phylo remained behind, calling out promises that the Grove would be "better than ever" and that the Great Cedar would bring happiness. [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.03] The evidence indicates that Zuggtmoy’s influence fully consumed the Grove after their departure, but Phylo's physical status after this event is not established in the summaries. [ch 032 / 032.03]

## Relationships
*   **Sovereign Basidia:** Phylo’s co-equal sovereign. He publicly acknowledged her as his "sovereign and co-equal in all things" while he and his allies ignored her objections to his policy changes. [ch 029 / 029.05; ch 029 / 029.06] Basidia feared Phylo was corrupted by unknown spores and feared for his mental state. [ch 029 / 029.06] Phylo turned violent against her when she accused him of betraying the circle. [ch 032 / 032.03]
*   **Zuggtmoy (The Great Seeder/Great Cedar):** The entity Phylo serves and worships. He speaks of her with reverent, feminine pronouns and describes her as all-knowing. [ch 029 / 029.05; ch 031 / 031.02] Yrberop’s lullaby praised her coming wedding to a “great body”. [ch 031 / 031.02]
*   **The Party:** Phylo greeted them warmly as guests invited to his celebration, unaware they had already uncovered the trap. [ch 029 / 029.05; ch 032 / 032.03] He expressed sadness that they rejected his "hospitality" (the transformation into fungal thralls) and fled. [ch 032 / 032.03]
*   **Circle Leaders (Gasbide, Hebopbe, Yrberop):** Phylo’s policies and promises of a "better day" secured their loyalty or induced a corrupted enthusiasm. [ch 030 / 030.05; ch 031 / 031.02] Gasbide cited Phylo’s promises of a "great awakening," while Hebopbe viewed Phylo’s restructuring as pragmatic. [ch 030 / 030.05; ch 031 / 031.02]
