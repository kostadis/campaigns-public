## Identity

Eravien Haund is a lord, gourmet, and representative of the Lords’ Alliance [ch 074 / entry; ch 076 / 076.01].

## Personality and Motivations

Charming, sly, and worldly, he uses fine food and special deals to influence the party. He personally helped prepare the feast’s dishes and carries an apron folded into his formal cloak [ch 074 / 074.06; ch 076 / entry].

He seeks elimination of the Underdark threat or actionable intelligence sufficient for a military response, promising a great reward for either outcome [ch 074 / 074.06].

## History with the Party
- In ch 074, when Bruenor made his offer, Eravien stopped moving, recognizing the comparison his own proposal would face [ch 074 / 074.01].
- He opened with amuse-bouche pastries for each adventurer and cheese on a plate for Glabbagool [ch 074 / 074.02].
- When the runner arrived, he set down his spoon and asked which outpost was affected [ch 074 / 074.05].
- He recovered and asked about his food. Glabbagool’s chemically precise critique surprised him; he admitted he had not expected an ooze to talk [ch 074 / 074.06; ch 074 / moment].
- The Lords’ Alliance wants the Underdark threat eliminated, or actionable intelligence sufficient to send an army against it, and promises a great reward on the party’s return for either result [ch 074 / 074.06].
- He offered access to the Alliance’s information network: one answer with certainty and one that may be incomplete, labelled as such, with a full day needed for an answer. Continued access requires escorting an archivist on a future non-combat observation errand whose details remain to be determined [ch 074 / 074.06].
- The party deferred its decision. The DM warned that a serious betrayal of the Lords’ Alliance could make the party a target [ch 074 / 074.06].

- In ch 075 the party publicly accepted every faction offer, including the Lords’ Alliance proposal previously presented by Eravien. The chapter does not establish completion of the promised future archivist escort [ch 075 / 075.04; ch 074 / 074.06].
- The party received the Alliance Brief: historical and political background on Menzoberranzan, plus a substantial tome allowing one question before each questline. Answers distinguish certain knowledge from partial information [ch 076 / 076.01].
- Eravien arranged for Sigrun, an experienced ranger and archivist, to accompany the expedition for mapping and information gathering. She claims no faction membership [ch 076 / 076.01].
- An additional note accompanying the Alliance material offered further rewards for further services [ch 076 / entry].

## Last Observed State

His faction’s offer has been accepted, and the party has received its Alliance information material. Sigrun joins the expedition through his arrangement; the summaries do not establish completion of the promised archivist escort [ch 075 / 075.04; ch 076 / 076.01].

Chapter 076 records his arrangements and further offers without depicting him speaking or acting in person. Status not established in the summaries [ch 076 / entry; ch 076 / 076.01].
