## Identity
Kazryn Nyantani is a Great Reader at Candlekeep [ch 057 / entry; ch 059 / 059.02]. She served as the chief cleric who organized the distribution of Deadwinter gifts during the feast in question [ch 060 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Kazryn is portrayed as emotionally volatile regarding Janussi’s death, arriving at the crime scene distraught and weeping [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.06]. In a political context, she prioritized investigative justice over administrative maneuvering, insisting that the murderer be found before other issues were addressed during a council meeting [ch 059 / 059.02].

Her primary motivation is romantic resentment. Sylvira Savikas identifies Kazryn as Janussi’s former lover who "never forgave Janussi for discarding her" and remained "deeply resentful" of being replaced by Sylvira [ch 061 / entry; ch 061 / 061.05]. Sylvira characterizes Kazryn’s disposition as "bitter" and, in tears, called her a "bitter old hag" [ch 061 / 061.05].

## Last Observed State
