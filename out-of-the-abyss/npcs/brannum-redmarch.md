## Identity
Brannum Redmarch is a duergar [ch 011 / entry]. He initially identified himself by distancing his crew from the kuo-toa, stating, "We are not kuo-toa with our boops and beeps and baba boots. They are mad and crazy." [ch 011 / moment]. He possesses very low Charisma, which allows observers to easily detect when he is lying [ch 011 / 011.01; ch 011 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Brannum is opportunistic and self-preserving. His primary motivation is survival, demonstrated by his immediate cooperation under the threat of death and his eagerness to bargain for his freedom [ch 011 / 011.01; ch 012 / 012.02]. He attempts to maintain a facade of dignity and legitimacy, insisting that his arrangement with the party be a "free exchange" and "not under duress," despite being coerced [ch 012 / 012.02]. He displays a sense of entitlement regarding his lost assets, attempting to claim a share of treasure recovered from his own missing crew's wrecks [ch 011 / 011.05]. He is fearful of duergar justice, fearing being turned over to his own people if he fails to comply [ch 012 / 012.02].

## History with the Party
*   Brannum Redmarch is a duergar who attempted to enslave the party after spotting their boat, viewing them as "easy pickings" [ch 011 / entry].
*   After his capture, Brannum introduced himself with an attempt at dignity, distancing himself from the kuo-toa, though his bravado crumbled under the threat of thirteen people he had just tried to enslave [ch 011 / 011.01].
*   Thorin intimidated Brannum into serving as a reluctant navigator to Gracklstugh, making it clear that his survival depended on faithful guidance; Gyrgum warned that if he tried to cheat them, he would be the first to die [ch 011 / 011.01; ch 011 / entry].
*   Brannum acted as a hostage in the party's boat, providing directions across the Darklake; his low Charisma made it easy to verify he was telling the truth about the route [ch 011 / 011.01; ch 011 / entry].
*   Upon encountering a bone pyramid islet on the fifteenth day of travel, Brannum panicked, admitting the route was a mistake and warning the party to change course [ch 011 / 011.02].
*   Brannum confessed that the shortcut was dangerous, having lost two boats and their crews to what he initially assumed was abandonment, but which he now feared involved zombies and an "evil altar" [ch 011 / 011.04].
*   The party discovered the sunken wrecks of Brannum's lost boats on the lakebed, guarded by zombies [ch 011 / 011.04].
*   When the party retrieved a treasure chest from the sunken boats, Brannum claimed a share of the loot on the grounds that it belonged to his crew, but was rebuffed and sprayed with water by Daz [ch 011 / 011.05; ch 011 / entry].
*   Upon arrival at Gracklstugh, the party discussed ransoming Brannum, noting that they had never promised him freedom and that he had attempted to sell them into slavery first [ch 011 / 011.07].
*   In the Blade Bazaar of Gracklstugh, the party bargained with Brannum, offering his freedom in exchange for using his underworld connections to procure uncommon magical items from restricted areas of the city [ch 012 / 012.02].
*   Brannum eagerly agreed to this arrangement, insisting the deals be done as a "free exchange" and not under duress, despite the threat of being turned over to duergar justice [ch 012 / 012.02; ch 012 / entry].
*   Brannum said he might know a contact who could procure an Eldritch Claw tattoo for Zalthir and asked for two days [ch 012 / 012.02].
*   Hemeth, the party's companion, protested being bypassed for magical item acquisition, causing Brannum to protest that "we had a deal" [ch 012 / 012.02; ch 012 / entry].

## Last Observed State
Brannum was located in the Blade Bazaar of Gracklstugh [ch 012 / 012.02]. He agreed to act as a middleman to procure uncommon magical items for the party in exchange for his freedom, and asked whether helping with "this one thing" would clear the debt [ch 012 / 012.02; ch 012 / entry]. Specifically, he said he might know a contact who could procure an Eldritch Claw tattoo for Zalthir and asked for two days [ch 012 / 012.02]. Status not established in the summaries [ch 011 / entry; ch 012 / 012.02].

## Relationships
*   **The Party**: Brannum is a captive who attempted to enslave them prior to his capture [ch 011 / entry]. He is currently coerced into cooperation by Thorin and Gyrgum, who have made clear that his survival depends on faithful navigation and assistance [ch 011 / 011.01]. The party considers ransoming him or turning him over to duergar justice as leverage [ch 011 / 011.07; ch 012 / 012.02].
*   **Thorin**: Thorin intimidated Brannum into servitude, stating that Brannum’s "position is not well understood quite yet" when Brannum questioned the nature of their agreement [ch 011 / 011.01; ch 012 / 012.02].
*   **Gyrgum**: Gyrgum warned Brannum that he would be the first to die if he attempted to cheat the party, and later added, "The quid is pro" in response to Brannum's claim of a free exchange [ch 011 / 011.01; ch 012 / 012.02].
*   **Hemeth**: Hemeth, a party companion, protested being bypassed for magical item acquisition. Brannum protested Hemeth's competing offer, stating, "I thought we had a deal," and arguing that he could procure items Hemeth could not [ch 012 / 012.02].
*   **Kuo-toa**: Brannum explicitly distinguishes himself and his kind from the kuo-toa, whom he describes as "mad and crazy" [ch 011 / moment].
