## Identity
Manshoon (Simulacrum) is a magical duplicate of the legendary and feared wizard Manshoon, associated with the Zhentarim’s transformation into an evil cult [ch 065 / entry; ch 067 / entry; ch 069 / entry]. The entity is explicitly a simulacrum, not the original Manshoon, who reportedly died 170 years ago [ch 069 / entry; ch 070 / entry]. These duplicates are created from ice and snow, possess half the original’s maximum vitality, cannot learn new spells or regain expended slots, and revert to snow upon destruction [ch 069 / entry]. The original Manshoon has historically returned from apparent death "without ever becoming a better person," described as "the ultimate bad penny" [ch 065 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
The simulacra exhibit an academic, theatrical, and unnervingly patient demeanor, characterized by "evil-overlord exposition" [ch 070 / entry]. They display a surprising level of civilized behavior, even in the midst of breach or combat; for instance, the first simulacrum agreed to relocate a duel to protect valuable books and framed the fight as a formal magical duel [ch 068 / entry]. The second simulacrum spent six hours maintaining patience before proposing a bargain, emphasizing that his ultimatum was "not a threat" while simultaneously leveraging the party's desires [ch 069 / entry; ch 069 / moment].

The primary motivation for the simulacra’s presence in Candlekeep is access to the Book of Vile Darkness behind the last door, not Alaundo’s prophecies [ch 070 / entry]. The breach of Candlekeep was achieved through the expenditure of massive magical resources and artifacts, including a Staff of Power, leaving the entity weakened [ch 065 / entry; ch 067 / entry; ch 070 / entry]. The simulacra are fixed at the power and knowledge they possessed at their creation and cannot grow stronger [ch 070 / entry].

## History with the Party
- Manshoon (Simulacrum) breached Candlekeep using massive magical resources and artifacts, arriving alone and weakened, and used a *Wall of Force* to destroy the High Tower’s magical door. [ch 065 / entry; ch 067 / entry]
- The simulacrum displayed disregard for A'lai Aivenmore, a scholar who had served him for eleven years, exposing her insignificance. [ch 065 / entry; ch 067 / entry]
- A first simulacrum (dressed in standard robes) engaged the party in a formal magical duel in the lower guardian chamber, utilizing *Mirror Image*, *Shield*, and *Ward Eater*. [ch 068 / entry; ch 069 / entry]
- The first simulacrum declined to use *Counterspell* on Daz’s *Phantasmal Killer*, taking 13 psychic damage but ending the spell without disadvantage; it was subsequently destroyed by Zalthir, reverting to snow. [ch 068 / entry; ch 069 / entry]
- A second simulacrum was later found waiting in the upper tower chamber wearing shabbier/gray robes. [ch 068 / entry; ch 069 / entry]
- The second simulacrum spent six hours attempting to access the Obsidian Door and warded books, proposing a bargain: Gyrgum opens the door using a prophecy gem, the party receives the prophecies, and Manshoon takes a specific book. [ch 069 / entry; ch 069 / moment; ch 070 / entry]
- Status not established in the summaries regarding the ultimate fate of the original Manshoon, though the party learned that the original was reportedly killed 170 years ago. [ch 069 / entry; ch 070 / entry]
- The second simulacrum’s true objective was the Book of Vile Darkness behind the last door, not the prophecies, and he could not open the door by force because it required a willing Reader. [ch 070 / entry]
- Zalthir interrupted the second simulacrum’s negotiation, grappled him, and threw him into a lava pit within an anti-magic field, despite the simulacrum’s warning that his destruction would invoke the real Manshoon's vengeance. [ch 070 / entry]

## Last Observed State
The second simulacrum, dressed in shabbier/gray robes, was found in the upper tower chamber after the first simulacrum was destroyed by Zalthir [ch 068 / entry; ch 069 / entry]. This second duplicate had spent six hours attempting to access the Obsidian Door, which required a willing Reader and could not be opened by force [ch 069 / entry; ch 070 / entry]. It proposed a bargain where Gyrgum would open the door, the party would receive prophetic gemstones, and Manshoon would take a specific book [ch 069 / moment; ch 070 / entry].

Zalthir interrupted this negotiation, grappled the simulacrum, and threw it into a lava pit within an anti-magic field [ch 070 / entry]. Before his destruction, the simulacrum warned that killing him would invoke the real Manshoon's vengeance [ch 070 / entry].

Status not established in the summaries regarding the ultimate fate of the original Manshoon [ch 069 / entry; ch 070 / entry].

The simulacra are magical duplicates of the original Manshoon.
