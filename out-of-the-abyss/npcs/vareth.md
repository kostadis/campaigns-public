## Identity
Vareth is an elderly, frantic, and officious half-elf scholar and member of the Avowed [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / 054.06]. He is known as Brother Vareth [ch 057 / entry]. He has held his position in the Theology Library (specifically the Immortal Chambers in the West Inner Ward) for forty-one years [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.01]. His specialty is the conversion of half-orcs to the worship of Bahamut, a field that was previously obscure but has become highly significant due to recent events [ch 054 / entry; ch 057 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Vareth is characterized by extreme academic enthusiasm and a tendency toward frantic energy, particularly regarding his niche specialty [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / 054.06]. He greeted Gyrgum as "a real living orc cleric of Bahamut" and a "living data point" [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / 054.06]. He is highly reactive to new theories, becoming "electrified" or "ecstatic" when presented with novel philosophical frameworks that support or expand his work [ch 057 / entry; ch 071 / entry]. In Chapter 060, his proposal of a fourth option to the Gyrgumite triangle set off an emergency debate at the Oval Theatre that drew most of the Avowed despite an active murder investigation [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.01]. Upon hearing a promising concept, he rushed off to begin his own research [ch 057 / entry].

## History with the Party
*   Vareth is an elderly, frantic half-elf scholar and member of the Avowed who specializes in orc conversions to the worship of Bahamut, a previously obscure field that gained significant attention following the events of Stroud and Protanther [ch 054 / entry].
*   Upon learning of the arrival of Gyrgum, a cleric of Bahamut, Vareth rushed to the First Reader, furious that he had not been summoned immediately, and dragged Gyrgum to his office, describing him as a "living data point" [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / 054.06].
*   Vareth had secured a record of the legendary chess game known as "Stroud's Gambit" and excitedly shared this with Gyrgum, anticipating that Gyrgum's interview would benefit the field of Bahamut orc conversions [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / moment].
*   The GM explains that Vareth's research relevance exploded due to the controversy surrounding Stroud's actions, with Vareth now being invited to the right conferences and receiving funding requests [ch 054 / 054.06].
*   Gyrgum was assigned to Vareth as a mentor [ch 056 / 056.04].
*   Vareth has kept his corner of the Theology Library for forty-one years and specializes in the puzzle of why a half-orc would convert to Bahamut given traditional orc-dragon enmity [ch 057 / entry].
*   Vareth led Gyrgum to the Immortal Chambers, Candlekeep's theology library, to discuss the three major schools of thought regarding the Question of the Age: Stroudite (mortal choice), Bahamutian (divine providence), and Draconic (ancient agency) [ch 057 / 057.01].
*   Vareth explained to Gyrgum that the theological debates had caused three duels at Candlekeep and that the courts of two cities had requested written opinions [ch 057 / 057.01].
*   Vareth explained that Alaundo's prophecies are recited continuously, with fulfilled prophecies being removed from circulation, though the outcome of them running out is unknown [ch 057 / 057.02].
*   When Gyrgum proposed a fourth philosophical position involving "fixed points" around which choices become constrained, Vareth was electrified and labeled Gyrgum a "weak Stroudite and strong Bahamutian around fixed points" [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.01].
*   Vareth declared this new concept the "strong-weak hypothesis" and a "philosophical quadrangle," pressing Stroudite notes into Gyrgum's hands before rushing off to begin his own research on the concept [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.01].
*   Vareth's unveiling of a fourth option to the Gyrgumite triangle transformed the established model into a quadrangle, causing an intellectual sensation that drew most of the Avowed to an emergency debate at the Oval Theatre despite an active murder investigation [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.01].
*   Following the party's success in reaching Alaundo's prophecies, Vareth was ecstatic, interpreting the prophecies' fixed points as supporting evidence for the emerging "Gyrgumite school" of thought [ch 071 / 071.09].
*   Vareth expressed a desire to discuss future collaborations and research with Gyrgum, who promised to place Vareth high on his list of potential collaborators [ch 071 / 071.09].
