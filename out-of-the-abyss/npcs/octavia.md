## Identity

Octavia is a young gnome and Zhentarim trainee serving as a guide to Mantol-Derith. She and Rainaldus introduce themselves as Davra’s employees; Davra has instructed them to deliver the party safely. [ch 076 / entry; ch 076 / 076.02]

## Personality and Motivations

- Pleasant and charming, she nevertheless gives the impression that approaching her carelessly could be dangerous. [ch 076 / entry; ch 076 / 076.02]
- Fine craftsmanship excites her: she admires the giant lizards’ saddle stitching and wants to know who made the saddles. [ch 076 / 076.03]
- She habitually leaves things behind, losing a pouch temporarily and later forgetting her water-testing tool at the cave. [ch 076 / entry]
- She expects the Black Road to be safe and its conditions to match her records. The slaughtered camp and fungal cavern unsettle those expectations. [ch 076 / 076.07; ch 076 / 076.08]
- She recognizes the Shield Guardian’s control amulet and enthusiastically suggests using the construct to test traps. [ch 076 / 076.05]

## History with the Party

- Joined the expedition with Rainaldus as one of Davra’s guides to Mantol-Derith. During the overland journey, she lost a pouch on the first morning and recovered it on the third. [ch 076 / 076.02]
- At the hidden cave entrance, she asked Rainaldus about his written record of the crossing and admired the waiting giant lizards’ saddles. [ch 076 / 076.03]
- Was visibly unsettled by the slaughtered underground camp, alongside Rainaldus. [ch 076 / 076.04]
- Recognized the Shield Guardian’s amulet and proposed employing the construct as a trap-tester. [ch 076 / 076.05]
- At the underground stream, realized she had left her testing tool at the cave; Rainaldus reprimanded her about making lists. [ch 076 / 076.06]
- While stirring the camp pot on the ninth night underground, introduced Khalessa Draga’s name as a merchant associated with Mantol-Derith’s Zhentarim trading circles. Rainaldus tried to quiet her and qualified whether Khalessa’s reputation established her whereabouts. [ch 076 / 076.07]
- On the nineteenth day underground, expressed confusion and alarm when a fungal cavern contradicted her records. Rainaldus reminded her that conditions do not always follow the book. [ch 076 / 076.08]

## Last Observed State

Octavia was with the expedition at the fungal cavern, alarmed by its departure from her maps. The party was still considering how to cross; no crossing or combat began before the session ended.  [ch 076 / 076.08]

## Relationships

- **Rainaldus:** Her senior guide and trainer, both protective of and exasperated by her. He corrects her forgetfulness and her reliance on recorded expectations. [ch 076 / 076.02; ch 076 / 076.06; ch 076 / 076.08]
- **Davra:** Her employer, who charged both guides with delivering the party safely. [ch 076 / 076.02]
- **The party:** Their guide to Mantol-Derith. Gyrgum challenges her repeated insistence that the road should be safe after the expedition has found evidence of slaughter. [ch 076 / 076.02; ch 076 / 076.07]
- **Khalessa Draga:** Octavia supplies her name and merchant affiliation; the evidence does not establish a personal relationship between them. [ch 076 / 076.07]
