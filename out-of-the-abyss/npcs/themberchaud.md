## Identity
Themberchaud, also known as the Wyrmsmith, the Father of Flame, and Themberchaud the 28th [ch 014 / 014.06; ch 025 / 025.01]. He is a red dragon, physically described as massive, overweight, and arrogant, with lava-colored scales and glowing yellow eyes [ch 014 / 014.06]. Despite his reputation as a terrifying beast, the DM describes him as "too fat and lazy," "out-of-shape," and a "glorified lighter" for the city's forges [ch 021 / entry; ch 026 / 026.06; ch 027 / entry]. He serves as a tool for the duergar of Gracklstugh, though he believes himself to be the independent power behind the city's industry [ch 014 / moment].

## Personality and Motivations
Themberchaud is defined by profound vanity and egotism, styling himself "father of the flame" and "bringer of the flame" [ch 027 / moment]. He has been manipulated by his handlers into believing he is the true power in the city; he believes he works for himself, while his handlers know he works for the duergar [ch 014 / moment; ch 018 / entry]. He desires complete information on local events to maintain his illusion of control, yet when asked where to investigate with his "vast network" he had no idea [ch 021 / entry; ch 027 / 027.03]. He is disdainful of those he deems inferior, such as brass dragonborn or clerics of other faiths, while valuing secrecy and lack of ties in his agents [ch 014 / 014.06]. Recently, he has begun to "grow up" and notice inconsistencies in the political reality presented to him by his handlers [ch 018 / 018.02]. His primary motivation is draconic arrogance, and his laziness means he is easily deceived into believing others are acting on his behalf; as the DM put it, he is "too fat and lazy" to notice if the party slipped away [ch 021 / entry; ch 027 / 027.03].

*   Themberchaud made his physical presence known to the party by flying over the city, prompting the halfling to explain the Keepers' practice of replacing aging dragons [ch 013 / 013.05].
*   Themberchaud revealed himself to the party with the line, "You didn't say I was having surfacers for dinner tonight," and introduced himself as the Wyrmsmith of Gracklstugh and Father of Flame [ch 014 / 014.06; ch 014 / moment].
*   Themberchaud is described as massive, overweight, and arrogant, with lava-colored scales and glowing yellow eyes [ch 014 / 014.06; ch 014 / entry].
*   Themberchaud dismissed the priests of the Keepers of the Flame to speak privately with the party, creating an atmosphere of tense secrecy [ch 014 / 014.06].
*   Inside his lair, Themberchaud breathed fire in all directions and thrashed his wings to ensure no invisible spies were present, warning that "Anyone who's invisible will be turned to ash" [ch 014 / 014.06; ch 014 / moment].
*   Themberchaud interrogated Gyrgum and dismissed his devotion to Bahamut with a curt "Next! You!", showing disdain for the cleric's divine allegiance [ch 014 / 014.06; ch 014 / moment].
*   Themberchaud expressed mild disdain for Zalthir’s brass dragonborn heritage, calling him "an inferior Dragonborn" [ch 014 / 014.06; ch 014 / entry].
*   Themberchaud showed approval for Daz’s Drow origins from Menzoberranzan, valuing his capacity for "conspiracies and secrecy" [ch 014 / 014.06; ch 014 / entry].
*   Themberchaud was delighted by Thorin’s lack of family or associations, stating, "Nobody who can black, nobody to be blackmailed. That's wonderful" [ch 014 / 014.06; ch 014 / moment].
*   Without waiting for formal acceptance, Themberchaud declared the party to be his personal agents, granting them his protection and presenting them with golden badges bearing his profile [ch 014 / 014.06].
*   Themberchaud summoned Gartokkar Xundorn to act as the party's liaison, instructing them to do whatever the Keepers ask but to report back to the dragon first [ch 014 / 014.06].

*   Gartokkar revealed that Themberchaud believes he works for himself, while Gartokkar noted, "What he doesn't realize is he works for the duergar" [ch 014 / moment].

*   Gartokkar agreed to the party's confidentiality rider, muttering, "What Themberchaud doesn't know doesn't hurt Themberchaud" [ch 017 / moment].
*   The party learned that Themberchaud has been manipulated by his handlers into believing he is the true power in the city, though he has recently "started to grow up" and notice inconsistencies [ch 018 / 018.02; ch 018 / entry].
*   The DM advised the party that they could destroy the corrupted egg and report this to Themberchaud, potentially altering their standing with him [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.05].
*   The party identified Themberchaud as a potential faction leader who desires complete information on local events, but they determined he is dangerous to inform about the corrupted dragon egg because it could send him on a destructive rampage against the Keepers of the Flame and the city [ch 021 / entry; ch 021 / 021.01; ch 021 / 021.02].
*   The DM described Themberchaud as "too fat and lazy," noting that if the party simply slipped away without him, it would take him years to realize they weren't coming back [ch 021 / entry; ch 021 / 021.02].
*   The party possessed a dragon baby rattle made by Clan Thrazgad, which they considered using as a sweetener or leverage for Themberchaud, though they ultimately decided to leave the city quietly rather than engage him directly [ch 021 / entry; ch 021 / 021.03].
*   The party uncovered proof that Themberchaud is actually "Themberchaud the 28th," which served as significant political leverage alongside other secrets held in the city [ch 025 / 025.01].

*   The DM reminded the party that they had separately agreed to act as Themberchaud’s agents, a status that was safe only as long as the dragon did not notice them [ch 026 / 026.06].
*   Themberchaud descended upon the party in the industrial heart of Gracklstugh, mistaking them for his loyal agents and expressing frustration with the inefficiency of the Keepers of the Flame [ch 027 / 027.01; ch 027 / 027.02].
*   Themberchaud offered the party "express service" to their next destination, inviting them to climb aboard his back; the party accepted to bypass the city gates [ch 027 / 027.02].
*   During the flight, Themberchaud scoffed that his agents had better things to do than visit the docks, but was persuaded by Zalthir’s argument that recent attacks there offered fresh intelligence [ch 027 / 027.03].
*   Themberchaud’s lack of fitness caused a chaotic, lurching turn during the flight, requiring the party to make Athletics or Acrobatics checks to avoid falling off; Gyrgum was saved by a rope thrown by Jimjar [ch 027 / 027.03].
*   Themberchaud made a proud detour to the Forge of Thrazgad, delivering a speech about his own greatness and breathing fire to light the forge, while the party tied themselves to his back for safety [ch 027 / 027.03].
*   Citizens of Gracklstugh witnessed the party riding Themberchaud and mistook them for the legendary Ember Vanguard, causing the entire city to fall into panic; the DM noted that in rumor, the party was associated with Daz as an 18th-level mage [ch 027 / 027.03; ch 027 / 027.04].
*   Themberchaud deposited the party at the Darklake docks with an ungainly landing, using his frightful presence to terrify the locals before flapping off toward his lair, convinced his agents were on a vital mission [ch 027 / 027.03; ch 027 / 027.04].
*   After the party left Gracklstugh, local gnomes told exaggerated tales of the Ember Vanguard, claiming the group had slain "an ancient red dragon of titanic power" rather than facing Themberchaud, whom the legends described as "fat and tired" [ch 048 / 048.07].

## Last Observed State
Themberchaud was last physically observed in Chapter 027 [ch 027 / 027.04]. He flew the party over Gracklstugh, landing ungainly at the Darklake docks. He used his frightful presence to terrify the local populace before departing for his lair, convinced that the party was on a vital mission for him [ch 027 / 027.04]. In Chapter 048, he was mentioned in exaggerated local legends as having been slain by the party[ch 048 / 048.07]. Status is established as alive and in his lair based on his departure in Chapter 027 [ch 027 / 027.04].

## Relationships
*   **The Party:** Themberchaud considers the party to be his personal agents, a self-appointed relationship established in Chapter 014 [ch 014 / 014.06]. He provided them with golden badges and protection, believing them to be acting in his interest [ch 014 / 014.06]. The DM noted that their status as his agents is safe only as long as he does not notice them [ch 026 / 026.06].
*   **Keepers of the Flame:** The dragon views them as inefficient handlers who fail to report to him properly, expressing frustration with their methods [ch 027 / 027.02]. Unbeknownst to him, they are grooming a replacement for him using a corrupted dragon egg [ch 014 / 014.07; ch 026 / 026.06].
*   **Gartokkar Xundorn:** Acts as the liaison between Themberchaud and the party. Gartokkar knows Themberchaud is manipulated but maintains the charade [ch 014 / moment].
*   **Duergar City Guards:** A duergar guard at the docks countered that Themberchaud is a servant of the Deepking, which contradicts the dragon's self-image [ch 027 / 027.04].
