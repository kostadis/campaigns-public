## Identity
Asha Vandree was a drow cleric of Lolth who served in Ilvara’s camp [ch 049 / entry; ch 053 / entry]. She held the status of a junior priestess or under-priestess [ch 049 / entry]. The mercenaries Nym and Kaelira Duskryn dismissed her as a "low-level cult initiate" [ch 053 / 053.01; ch 053 / moment].

## Personality and Motivations
Asha was obsessively clean, maintaining a ritualistic regime of scraping fungus from her clothes, staying beyond 20 feet of Ilvara, and consuming only her own sealed food to avoid infection [ch 049 / entry; ch 050 / 050.02; ch 050 / entry]. She viewed Ilvara’s embrace of fungal corruption as heresy, explicitly stating that Lolth "does not share her web with fungal filth" [ch 049 / moment; ch 049 / entry]. Her sanity was fragile; she suffered from paranoia, seeing shadows everywhere, and expressed frustration at being stuck between the broken romantic affair of Ilvara and Jorlan Duskryn [ch 049 / entry; ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / moment].

She was fiercely loyal to Lolth and rejected the idea of the Spider Queen as a bride, asserting that being a priestess meant one did not get married [ch 049 / 049.05]. Asha was suspicious and tested allies, initially questioning if Daz was a charlatan or a trick of Ilvara before accepting his deception as a divine sign [ch 050 / moment; ch 050 / 050.02]. She interpreted events through a lens of divine election, believing her survival of the camp’s destruction confirmed she alone had kept the faith [ch 053 / entry; ch 053 / 053.02]. This delusion led her to treat non-drow and drow males with contempt and attempt to command the party [ch 053 / 053.02].

## Last Observed State
Asha Vandree is dead [ch 053 / entry; ch 053 / 053.02].

Following the death of Ilvara, Asha emerged from the chaos convinced she had been divinely touched by Lolth [ch 053 / entry; ch 053 / 053.02]. She declared, “Excellent! Now that I am in charge!” and marched forward, treating the party members with condescension [ch 053 / 053.02; ch 053 / moment]. The party had previously planned to allow her to help briefly before killing her [ch 053 / 053.02]. Gyrgum attempted to strike her with a guiding bolt, which missed; Asha cried, “I am blessed by Lolth!” [ch 053 / 053.02; ch 053 / moment]. Thorin grappled and gagged her to prevent speech, but Nym criticized the approach as insufficient for a cleric of Lolth [ch 053 / 053.02; ch 053 / moment]. Zalthir concluded she was an evil cleric whose removal would benefit the Underdark and killed her with a barrage of strikes and tentacle attacks [ch 053 / entry; ch 053 / 053.02].

Her body was looted, yielding a holy symbol of Lolth, scale mail with spider-fretted trim, obsidian-and-bone prayer beads, coins, and a sealed theological text [ch 053 / entry; ch 053 / 053.03]. The text, a post-corruption argument against Zuggtmoy’s claims upon Lolth, was later donated by the party for entry into Candlekeep [ch 053 / 053.03; ch 054 / 054.05].

## Relationships
**Daz:** Asha believed Daz was a powerful mage communing with Lolth, initially suspecting spore infection or deception before accepting him as a "confused vessel" for divine prayer [ch 050 / 050.01; ch 050 / moment]. She accepted his explanation that new drow arrivals were friends of Lolth, preventing conflict with House T'sarran forces [ch 051 / 051.04]. She remained unaware that her alliance was based on Daz's deception [ch 053 / 053.02].

**Ilvara:** Asha was a junior priestess under Ilvara but accused her of heresy for embracing fungal corruption [ch 049 / entry; ch 049 / 049.03]. The DM summarized her continued belief in Daz’s devotion to Lolth as an “enemy of my enemy situation” [ch 051 / 051.02]. Asha offered Ilvara’s treasures in exchange for her death and the destruction of the Heart Fungus [ch 050 / 050.02].

**Jorlan Duskryn:** Asha expressed exasperation at being trapped between Ilvara and Jorlan Duskryn, noting their failed affair and mutual bitterness [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / moment].

**Nym and Kaelira Duskryn:** The mercenaries dismissed Asha as beneath their concern, stating she was a low-level initiate whom Daz should be able to handle [ch 053 / 053.01; ch 053 / moment]. Nym later criticized Thorin’s grappling of Asha, advocating for decapitation instead [ch 053 / 053.02; ch 053 / moment].

**The Party:** Asha allied with the party to exploit the schism in the drow camp, viewing them as allies against Ilvara [ch 049 / 049.04; ch 050 / 050.03]. She tested their tactical judgment and accepted their caution [ch 050 / 050.03]. The party ultimately executed her as part of a pre-planned betrayal [ch 053 / 053.02].
