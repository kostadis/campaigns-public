# Chapter 029

Date: 2025-09-15

Attendance: Thorin's player was absent, so the GM ran Thorin.

## Summary
The party stood before the stone sarcophagus of Brysis, its heavy lid finally pried open to reveal the withered, mummified remains within. Zalthir had spent his action opening it so that Brysis could no longer hide inside. Resting atop the ancient corpse was a sword that immediately made its presence known, pleading desperately to be rescued from its long imprisonment: "You can't leave me here. Please." Gyrgum groaned at yet another talking magic sword and wished she had been a less squeaky talking mace. Zalthir, a monk with no use for bladed weapons, said she could at least have been a glove. The sword was equally unimpressed. After her long wait, she had been found by someone who couldn't even wield her, and she demanded, "wait, you're a monk. Get the fighter over here."

The battle against Brysis was fierce and chaotic, fought largely in a shroud of magical darkness that blinded friend and foe alike. Zalthir moved his orb of darkness over Thorin and spent focus points to strike twice with advantage from inside it. Gyrgum weighed turning the undead. He held back on the GM's advice, saving his last use in case the party needed a quick getaway. He also passed on Guiding Bolt, which would have fired blind at disadvantage. Instead he threaded a five-foot stream of Tasha's Caustic Brew past Zalthir. Brysis blew the saving throw, and acid clung to her and kept eating away at her. Daz, unable to see his target, fired three scorching rays into the darkness anyway. Two hit for 10 each and one for 3, a total of 23, which earned him "the award for pissing off the demonic creature." Brysis raged that she would not be defeated after a millennium of imprisonment. Daz retreated up the tunnel so the creature could not turn him into a specter out of revenge. The lingering acid proved merciless all the same. Brysis was resistant to acid, but she had only three hit points left, and she was destroyed before she could make good on his threat.

In the midst of the chaos, Thorin made a decision that surprised everyone. Rather than pressing the attack, he reached into the sarcophagus and pulled free the sentient sword. The moment he grasped the hilt, a blade of pure radiance blazed to life. The GM described it as essentially a lightsaber. The sword announced herself with "Dawnbringer here! Let me show you what I can do!" and "Finally, light!" She is a +2 sword that deals radiant damage instead of slashing, adds 1d8 against undead, and grants lesser restoration. Brysis managed only "not done, Dawnbringer." The sword was powerful, she was legendary, and she was, the party would soon discover, absolutely impossible to shut up. She could even read, which prompted Gyrgum to marvel, "Wow, a sword that can read." The sword's response was a pointed "seriously?"

With Brysis defeated, the party searched the tomb. They found a thin gold sheath covering the sarcophagus worth 250 gold pieces, as well as a chest that had been rendered completely invisible by magic. Daz's Faerie Fire stripped away the enchantment. Thorin had the worst sleight of hand in the group, so Zalthir "took one for the team" and opened the chest. Its contents were themselves invisible and had to be dumped onto the floor. They held 4,000 silver pieces, 1,200 gold pieces, eleven zircons worth 50 gold each, a necklace of fireballs, a philter of love, and a potion of greater healing, all claimed by the group. The GM ruled that consumables belong to the whole party. Daz moved the money into D&D Beyond's new party inventory tab.

Gyrgum, a self-described completionist, wanted to see the unexplored southern chamber, and the party went in with Daz hanging back ready to throw fire. Fargas Rumblefoot had once warned them that this chamber was a decoy. True to his word, it held nothing but canopic jars of desiccated organs and a heavy sarcophagus decorated with mosaics of great floating cities. There was no danger whatsoever, because its only curse had already been destroyed along with the wraith.

The party rested and then pressed onward through the winding Underdark caverns. After several days of careful travel, Eldeth brought welcome news: the drow pursuit had finally been broken. Each successful Wisdom check she made had obscured their trail, and the party's pursuit rating dropped to zero. The GM warned that the hunters would eventually pick up the trail again. Daz was hopeful: "Maybe we'll be doing something so incredible they won't be able to find us." Before long, the narrow tunnels gave way to something breathtaking.

Neverlight Grove opened before them like a dream. It was a vast cavern where bioluminescent mushrooms covered every surface, floor to ceiling, with a lake at its heart. Rumpadump cried "We're home! We're home! We're home!" and sprinted ahead with Stool. Sarith walked with a quiet, peaceful expression that none of the party had seen on him before. Zalthir, ever cautious, wrapped himself in a shroud of darkness, explaining, "I don't trust any of these things."

The two sovereigns of the grove, Phylo and Basidia, received them warmly, but something was immediately, unsettlingly wrong with Phylo. He spoke of a coming "day of joy" with eerie enthusiasm. He praised a figure called the "Great Seeder," who could answer any question, and kept calling the entity "she" and "her." Daz recalled that this was deeply strange, because myconids have no gender. An insight check revealed that Basidia was staring at Phylo in visible discomfort. She arranged for Phylo to stay behind and led the tour herself. As she guided the group through the grove, she spoke in hushed, urgent tones. A previous group of "softers" had accepted Phylo's hospitality and been taken to the Garden of Welcome. She had not seen them since, and Phylo had never mentioned them again.

Basidia's concerns ran deeper still. She had noticed that Sarith was infected with spores she did not recognize. That was alarming, since myconid sovereigns could identify nearly every spore produced anywhere in the Underdark. She feared that Phylo had been similarly infected. He had restructured the grove's seven circles so that members melded only within their own circle and only leaders melded across circles. He had also created a new inner circle and a Circle of Masters. The Circle of Masters was taking most of the zurkh carcasses the hunters brought in and sending groups of myconids outside the grove without telling anyone. Basidia gave the party a moldy scroll case holding a scroll of protection against fiends. She also offered potions brewed from the grove's molds, plus treasure, if they would investigate the Garden of Welcome on her behalf. She could not go herself for fear of falling to the same corruption that had claimed her close friend and co-sovereign.

The tour brought them to the Circle of Hunters. There they met Loobamub, a circle leader and staunch ally of Basidia who shared her unease about Phylo's new ways. He explained that myconid hunters did not kill prey. They tracked creatures that had already died near the grove and brought the remains back for reanimation. He asked the party to kill a grick alpha lurking in the nearby fungal wilds and bring back its carcass intact. Zalthir asked why this errand mattered, and then answered his own question: a reanimated grick could fight alongside them against whatever demonic force was in the Garden of Welcome. Basidia called it "an excellent idea." Through it all, Dawnbringer blazed at Thorin's side and refused to be dimmed, a constant, chattering reminder that she had spent far too long in the dark and had absolutely no intention of ever going back.

## NPCs
### Brysis
A cursed undead creature imprisoned within a stone sarcophagus for a millennium. Resistant to acid, she was defiant and vengeful, vowing not to be defeated before ultimately succumbing to the party's assault. She had AC 13. She survived 23 damage from Scorching Ray and was finished off at three hit points by Tasha's Caustic Brew. Her last words to the sword were "not done, Dawnbringer."

### Dawnbringer
A sentient, talking magic sword found within Brysis's sarcophagus. She can speak, read, and understand Common, and manifests a blade of pure radiance. She has a strong personality, expressing disdain for being left with a monk rather than a fighter, and a deep, persistent fear of the dark that causes her to refuse to be deactivated. She announced herself with "Dawnbringer here! Let me show you what I can do!" She met Gyrgum's surprise at her literacy with an exasperated "seriously?"

### Eldeth
A companion traveling with the party who used her knowledge of the terrain to obscure their trail and successfully break the drow pursuit. She remains cautious and skeptical, choosing to stand apart from the myconids rather than follow the group deeper into the grove. She considered Thorin "crazy" for following the sprouts and stayed beside Daz.

### Stool
A young myconid sprout who was rescued by the party and joyfully returned to his home in Neverlight Grove, excitedly crediting the party to the sovereigns. He asked where Zalthir was and said that Thorin carried him when he was tired.

### Rumpadump
A myconid sprout and companion of Stool who was overjoyed to return to the safety of the grove. He shouted "We're home! We're home! We're home!" on arrival. He is not a sovereign, so he cannot identify foreign spores.

### Sarith
A drow companion of the party who felt a sense of peace and relief upon arriving at the grove. Basidia identified him as being infected by unknown spores she did not recognize, potentially connected to the same corruption affecting Sovereign Phylo. He advised the party to stay on the sovereign's good side, sensing that the myconids take their leaders seriously.

### Jimjar
A deep gnome companion who was present as the party arrived at Neverlight Grove. He walked over to the sovereigns alongside Sarith.

### Sovereign Phylo
One of the two co-leaders of Neverlight Grove. He welcomed the party warmly but spoke ominously of a coming 'day of joy' and referred to a mysterious 'Great Seeder' using gendered pronouns, which is unusual for myconids. He has implemented a controversial policy of segregating the myconid circles and established the secretive Circle of Masters.
- He calls the party "friendly softers" and promised to take them to the Circle of Masters and the "great celebration" tomorrow.
- He calls Basidia "my sovereign and co-equal in all things."
- He ignored the party once the tour began.

### Sovereign Basidia
The co-sovereign of Neverlight Grove and a voice of reason within the colony. She is deeply suspicious of Phylo's recent behavior and the new Circle of Masters, and fears the grove is being corrupted by unknown spores. She tasked the party with investigating the Garden of Welcome, provided them a scroll of protection against fiends, and offered to craft potions as a reward.
- She says she and Phylo "were such good friends."
- She promised to vouch for the party throughout the grove, everywhere except the Garden of Welcome.
- She endorsed the plan to reanimate the grick as an ally.

### Loobamub
The leader of the Circle of Hunters in Neverlight Grove and an ally of Basidia who opposes Phylo's new methods. He explained that myconid hunters track deceased creatures for reanimation rather than hunting live prey, and tasked the party with retrieving the carcass of a grick alpha lurking in the nearby fungal wilds. The carcass is to go to Basidia for reanimation.

### Dawnbringer
A sentient, talking magic sword found within Brysis's sarcophagus atop her mummified corpse. When the hilt is grasped and activated, she manifests a blade of pure radiance. She can speak, read, and understand Common, possesses a strong personality with a deep fear of the dark, and is particularly effective against undead. She was claimed by Thorin.
- She is a +2 weapon.
- Igniting the blade takes a bonus action.
- She deals radiant damage instead of slashing, plus an extra 1d8 against undead.
- She also grants lesser restoration.


## Locations
### Lost Tomb of Khaem
An ancient burial site in the Underdark containing a stone sarcophagus where the mummified remains of Brysis were interred alongside a sentient magical sword. A second, southern chamber served as a decoy tomb filled with canopic jars and a heavy sarcophagus decorated with mosaics of floating cities. The decoy sarcophagus rests on a black marble bier. Its canopic jars sit in niches cut into stone blocks along the western and eastern walls.

### Underdark Caverns
The winding, dangerous tunnels where the party traveled and rested while evading drow pursuers after leaving the tomb.

### Neverlight Grove
A hidden, enchanted cavern in the Underdark filled with a brilliantly colorful bioluminescent mushroom forest covering every surface including the ceiling. A majestic mushroom tower can be glimpsed through rising mists across a ravine. It is the communal home of the myconids and is currently experiencing a schism between its two sovereigns due to a mysterious fungal infection.
- Most passages into the grove are narrow fissures. Only one tunnel, lit by glowing lichen, is fit for easy travel, and it opens above the grove floor.
- The cavern contains a lake.
- The grove has seven circles, each gathered around a circle mound.

### Garden of Welcome
A restricted area within Neverlight Grove where Sovereign Phylo takes guests. A previous group of travelers disappeared there and were never seen again. Basidia suspects it is the source of the corrupting spores and fears entering it herself. Phylo intends to bring the party there tomorrow.

### Circle of Masters
A new circle created at Phylo's insistence, alongside a new inner circle, operating apart from the rest of the grove's communal structure. It has been hoarding zurkh carcasses and sending myconids outside the grove without explanation.

### Circle of Hunters
A specialized area within Neverlight Grove centered around a crater-like mound where myconid hunters gather. Led by Loobamub, an ally of Basidia, the circle tracks and retrieves creature carcasses for reanimation rather than hunting live prey. The grick alpha lurks in the fungal wilds nearby.

## Items
### Gold Covering from Brysis's Sarcophagus
A thin gold sheath covering the lid of Brysis's sarcophagus, pried loose by the party after the battle and found to be worth 250 gold pieces.

### Invisible Chest
A magically hidden container found in the tomb that remained completely unseen until revealed by Faerie Fire. Its contents were also invisible and had to be dumped out to be examined.

### Necklace of Fireballs
A magical necklace found in the invisible chest, bearing four beads that can each be detached and thrown to create explosive bursts of fire. All four beads can also be thrown simultaneously for a devastating combined blast. The GM described the combined throw as a seventh-level fireball; by the item's rules, four beads make a 6th-level fireball. Gyrgum claimed it, though the GM treats consumables as party property.

A potent restorative tonic found among the treasures in the invisible chest.

### Philter of Love
A love potion found among the treasures in the invisible chest.

### Zircons
Eleven valuable gemstones found within the tomb's hidden invisible chest, each worth 50 gold pieces.

### Scroll of Protection against Fiends
A moldy scroll case given to the party by Sovereign Basidia. It contains a magical ward that creates a protective cylinder blocking demons and other fiendish entities from entering or affecting those within it, though its protection lasts only a short time. The ward lasts five minutes.

## Spells
### Darkness
An orb of magical darkness conjured by Zalthir and used during the fight with Brysis to obscure the battlefield. It was later dispelled to allow the party to search the tomb. Zalthir also maintained a new orb of darkness around himself upon entering Neverlight Grove as a precaution against potential demonic threats. In the fight, it was moved to cover Thorin and gave Zalthir advantage on his attacks, while forcing blind ranged attacks at disadvantage.

### Tasha's Caustic Brew
A five-foot-wide stream of caustic acid cast by Gyrgum against Brysis. Brysis failed her saving throw and was coated in the acid, suffering ongoing damage at the start of each of her turns. Despite Brysis having resistance to acid, the lingering damage ultimately contributed to her defeat. The save was DC 15 Dexterity, and the ongoing damage was 4d4. It was aimed to avoid hitting Zalthir.

### Scorching Ray
Cast by Daz at 2nd level with his last slot, firing three separate rays of scorching fire into the magical darkness at Brysis. Despite the disadvantage from not being able to see the target, multiple rays connected, dealing significant damage and earning Daz the distinction of most angering the creature. Each ray was a separate attack roll against AC 13, and the hits totaled 23 damage (10, 10, and 3).

### Faerie Fire
Cast by Daz to outline the invisible chest in dim light within a 20-foot cube, stripping it of its invisible condition and making it visible to the party so they could retrieve its contents.
