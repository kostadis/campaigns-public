# Chapter 75:Loss

Date: 2026-11-02

## Summary
The great hall of Gauntlgrym buzzed with difficult decisions as the Ember Vanguard gathered around a table strewn with cups and plates, the intelligent grey ooze Glabbagool resting at its center. The party reviewed the assembled factions’ offers, distinguishing the official handouts from Glabbagool’s more informal notes, and debated what it meant to “break faith.” Withholding discoveries from the Harpers would violate their agreement; failing to solve Bruenor’s problem would not, provided the party made an honest effort. Sir Lanniver Strayl expected moral conduct and might oppose them if their plans involved massive civilian suffering. The Lords’ Alliance valued useful allies and would object if information or assistance strengthened its rivals.

None of the offers were mutually exclusive yet. The party recognized, however, that organizations’ principles and individual representatives’ priorities could pull in different directions. Daz suggested that accepting everything would create problems for the future Ember Vanguard: they could still make the choices they thought right and deal with disappointed allies afterward. The group resolved to hear the remaining offers before making its commitments public.

Morista of the Emerald Enclave joined them, settling between Zalthir and Daz after questioning a runner whose news had disrupted the feast. Water at one of the king’s outposts had turned into fungal spores and dried out. The report had changed the expedition’s reception: what some had treated as Bruenor’s vanity project suddenly seemed urgent. Morista offered protection for one named fungal crossing, prepared with a day’s work, sufficient to keep contaminated air and water from harming them while they passed. It would not stop attacks, cure an existing infection, or protect them from the being responsible if it stood in their path.

She also offered enough giant lizard mounts for everyone for one leg of the road. Their price was an honest search for her missing scout, Sladis Vadir—not a guarantee of finding him. Morista wanted reports on water, growth, settlements, and sickness, and asked them to spare those who had not chosen this fight. The Underdark was an ecosystem whose inhabitants and living systems deserved consideration.

Daz’s historical knowledge, informed by Milo Goodbarrel’s account of the War of the Dragons, supplied the political complication. The decentralized Emerald Enclave was divided between interventionists, who considered demons and devils outside the natural order, and naturalists, who regarded even those forces as part of it. Malfire’s success in bringing the Enclave into the war’s final battle had intensified that division. A victory over the demons purchased by destroying a major ecosystem might deepen the split. Morista’s own allegiance within that dispute remained uncertain. The party accepted both the mounts and the fungal protection; ingenious uses of the latter would be considered individually rather than guaranteed in advance.

After Bruenor asked for an answer before the lights came up, Daz met privately with Davra Jassur of the Zhentarim. She continued writing without acknowledging him until he tapped his toe and turned to leave. Invited to sit, he declined her tea by observing that other hosts had already welcomed and refreshed him. Davra smiled approvingly.

Davra presented the Underdark as a place of freedom for Zhentarim trade and warned that, whatever faction prevailed, the drow stood to lose most. Her maps reached Mantol-Derith where the crown’s maps stopped short, and she supplied the name Ghazrim DuLoc. She offered known information, without promising safety. In return, she wanted whatever Daz discovered about “the blink,” without exclusivity or priority, and explicitly did not want the names of his people. Further services would carry separately stated prices. Daz judged the maps and contact valuable, while recognizing that Davra’s claim to have little agenda might conceal a less obvious one.

The party then publicly accepted every faction’s offer. Bruenor celebrated Clan Feldrun and recalled his history with Drizzt Do’Urden freeing Gauntlgrym from the orcs, quickly moving past that subject when Gyrgum stared back at him. Daz received the speech with restrained, sage nodding. Bruenor explained their route: reach Mantol-Derith, find Ghazrim DuLoc, and obtain a map to Gravenhollow, a legendary repository built by stone giants where answers about the Underdark might be found.

Before they departed, Bruenor explained what prevented him from equipping them for the depths. The Great Forge had been cold since Maegera’s awakening tore its coils. Helgrim could replace them in two days but could not restart the old housing. The party’s single pinch of Dust of Mechanus—a magical lubricant capable of freeing stuck machinery and disrupting shapechangers’ forms—provided the missing means. Restoring the forge would let Bruenor release one vault item to each hero and begin the promised commissions for their return. The party restarted the Great Forge with the dust during this session, accepting a two-day delay despite the warning that conditions below would continue to worsen.

Thorin chose the Helm of the Citadel rather than complicate his relationship with Dawnbringer by acquiring another sentient weapon, particularly one whose hatred of giants clashed with his own history and Giantfriend name. Daz selected the Stone Staff, Gyrgum the Fiendwracker Staff, and Zalthir the Salamander Flame Gauntlets. The group also argued that Glabbagool deserved a reward. No suitable item had been prepared for him; the discussion turned toward something he could digest to gain a useful benefit, perhaps an apparently decorative treasure never intended to be eaten.

During this interval, Daz discovered a half-drawn spider in the margin beside his Clan Feldrun notes: three legs and the beginning of a body, in his own handwriting, with no memory of drawing it. A fleeting vision brought a room without light, someone very close, and something cold against his brow. An Arcana result of 25 did not explain the sigil, but religious investigation uncovered an obscure account of a Sharran Rite of Forgetting. The rite did not destroy a memory: a priest took it and kept it. It was neither casual nor cheap. Someone had paid dearly for Daz’s loss, though whether he had requested it himself remained unknown.

## Scenes

### Deliberations at Gauntlgrym Hall
#### The party gathers in the hall to review various faction offers and discuss the implications of their potential alliances.
- The party examines a map of Gauntlgrym Hall and reviews faction tables provided by the Dungeon Master. Glabbagool rests on their table, oozing among the cups and plates.
- The DM distinguishes the official faction handouts from Glabbagool’s informal summary. The faction table provides a condensed comparison of the offers.
- The group discusses “breaking faith” as a concrete obligation rather than simply failing a mission:
  - The Harpers would consider deliberately withheld discoveries a breach.
  - Bruenor expects an honest attempt to find and stop the plague’s source; unsuccessful effort is still effort.
  - Sir Lanniver Strayl may decide the party itself needs stopping if its plans cross too many moral lines, such as causing massive civilian deaths.
  - The Lords’ Alliance may regard assistance that advantages its rivals as hostile.
- The Lords’ Alliance is described as a coalition of city-states seeking greater control and influence in the Underdark. Doing nothing would make the party unhelpful, rather than automatically make them enemies.
- Daz’s background as a drow from Menzoberranzan might give him reason to distrust surface powers acquiring control below, though his missing knowledge complicates what prejudices he actually understands.
- The players note that the offers currently fit together, while later discoveries and choices may produce conflicts.
- The DM emphasizes that individual representatives have priorities distinct from their organizations’ general principles.
- Thorin worries that accepting the Order’s support could bind the party’s hands during a future moral dilemma.
- Daz argues that accepting several offers need not predetermine those choices: they can act as they otherwise would and face the reactions afterward.
- Zalthir invokes Glabbagool’s perspective: “Glabbagool says you can stretch in different directions. We're done, right? Decision made.”
- The party decides to hear out the remaining representatives before committing to any alliances.

### Daz's Missing Memories
#### Daz experiences strange visions and discovers evidence of magical memory manipulation tied to the goddess Shar.
- While reviewing his evening notes, Daz finds a mysterious spider sigil beside his entry about Clan Feldrun: three legs and the start of a body, drawn in his own handwriting and stopped.
- He has no memory of making it and wonders whether he blacked out while drawing.
- On the second night, Daz experiences a sudden vision: the hall vanishes, replaced by a pitch-black room, someone very close, a cold sensation pressed to his brow, and then nothing.
- The DM also connects the fleeting experience to the period after Daz’s conversation with Davra and clarifies that it is a mental flash rather than an established physical teleportation.
- Daz seeks an explanation through his knowledge of magic and history. An Arcana result of 25 does not explain the sigil.
- Gyrgum contributes religious knowledge. His first attempt is a natural 1; he spends inspiration for another attempt, and the investigation reaches a Religion result of 24.
- The relevant recollection comes through Bahamutian texts and an obscure Candlekeep heresiology concerning the Lady of Loss.
- The source describes darkness functioning as a vessel, a cold kiss on the brow, and a person unable to grieve a loss because they no longer know it occurred.
- The group identifies a “Rite of Forgetting” associated with Shar, goddess of loss and secrets.
- The rite preserves what it removes: the priest takes the memory and keeps it.
- The practice is neither casual nor cheap. Someone requested it and paid dearly.
- Whether Daz requested the rite himself or someone else arranged it remains unresolved.
- Daz suggests that the removal might prevent him from remembering something harmful while allowing him to rediscover matters for himself. The DM treats this as a possibility rather than a confirmed motive.

## Locations

### Gauntlgrym Hall
A grand dwarven hall where a feast is held and various factions have gathered to present offers to the party. The atmosphere is thick with political tension, featuring tables scattered with cups, plates, and a chessboard. An apron left by Lord Eravien Haund is visible in the corner.

Glabbagool occupies the center of the party’s negotiating table. Morista sits between Zalthir and Daz, and Bruenor waits nearby for an opportunity to speak. The apron prompts joking speculation about a “dwarf bikini” before the DM explains that Eravien had been cooking for the guests. By the time the Vanguard announces its decision, the representatives are watching for the answer.

### Mantol-Derith
A secret Underdark trading post where the Zhentarim hold influence. Mentioned by both Davra Jassur and King Bruenor as the party’s first major destination, where they are to meet the agent Ghazrim DuLoc.

The crown’s maps stop short of it; Davra’s directions reach it. Neither her information nor her contact comes with a promise of safety.

### Gravenhollow
A legendary place built by Stone Giants, said to contain all the knowledge of the depths of the Underdark. King Bruenor identifies it as the likely source of answers about what has happened in the region.

The party must first reach Ghazrim DuLoc at Mantol-Derith to obtain the map that leads there.

### The Great Forge of Gauntlgrym
An ancient dwarven forge whose massive coils had been cold and stuck since the primordial Maegera awoke and tore them. The party restarted it during this session using the Dust of Mechanus.

Helgrim distinguishes torn coils from cut ones and explains that furnaces with undamaged coils remained operational. Replacing the damaged coils requires two days; freeing the old housing requires the party’s extraordinary lubricant. Restoration also permits the promised commissions to begin.

### The Vaults of Gauntlgrym
A secure area containing the ancient treasures and magical artifacts of the dwarven kingdom, opened to equip the heroes for the depths after the party restarted the Great Forge.

Bruenor offers each hero one item of their choice. The question of what Glabbagool can receive remains open, prompting suggestions ranging from valuable armor exchanged for cheese to a consumable object that grants him a useful benefit.

## NPCs

### Morista
A dwarven representative of the Emerald Enclave who is deeply concerned about the fungal outbreak spreading through the Underdark outposts. She offers the party spore protection and giant lizard mounts in exchange for scouting reports and a search for her missing scout, Sladis Vadir. She urges the party to spare those who did not choose the fight and to respect the Underdark’s ecosystem.

She comes directly from questioning the runner about the corrupted water. Her request requires looking for Sladis rather than guaranteeing his recovery. Although the Enclave’s internal divisions make the expedition politically sensitive, the party does not establish which faction she personally supports. She thanks them after they accept.

### Sladis Vadir
A missing scout belonging to the Emerald Enclave whom the party has been tasked to look for in the Underdark. Morista provides a description and notes that the scout answers to the name Sladis.

The transcript supplies no further details of that description or a confirmed location. Morista’s bargain requires a search.

### Davra Jassur
A cold and calculating representative of the Zhentarim who values economic freedom and the open nature of the Underdark. She initially ignores Daz upon his arrival, then offers secret maps to Mantol-Derith and a contact named Ghazrim DuLoc in exchange for intelligence on whatever the party discovers about “the blink.” She is pleased when Daz declines her tea with suspicion.

Daz’s move to leave prompts her to acknowledge him. She frames her appeal around the drow’s vulnerability to the other factions’ ambitions and the Zhentarim’s freedom to trade. She wants neither exclusive information nor the names of Daz’s people. Further bargains will have their prices disclosed separately.

### Ghazrim DuLoc
A Zhentarim agent stationed at Mantol-Derith who reportedly possesses a map to Gravenhollow. Mentioned by both Davra Jassur and King Bruenor as a key contact for the party’s journey.

The party has not met him during this session, and Daz explicitly recognizes that the usefulness of having a contact does not settle whether that contact can be trusted.

### Bruenor Battlehammer
The dwarven King of Gauntlgrym who sponsored the party’s expedition. He celebrates the party’s acceptance of all faction offers, references his history with Drizzt Do’Urden, and explains that the cold Great Forge has forced him to hoard items he wants to give the heroes. The party restarts it using the Dust of Mechanus, enabling him to equip them from his royal vaults.

He expects honest effort rather than guaranteed success against the plague. He asks for a decision before the lights come up, celebrates Clan Feldrun when it arrives, and lays out the route through Mantol-Derith to Gravenhollow. Restoring the forge also allows him to resume the commissions promised for the party’s return.

### Sir Lanniver Strayl
A representative, rather than a principal, of the Order of the Gauntlet who cares about who lives or dies and emphasizes moral integrity and warns the party that the Order may turn against them if they cross too many ethical lines, such as causing massive civilian casualties.

Daz’s reading suggests Lanniver’s personal concern is distinctive within an organization otherwise eager to destroy evil creatures in the Underdark. His role illustrates why the party must consider individual emissaries’ priorities alongside faction doctrine.

### Glabbagool
An intelligent grey ooze and full party member who rests on the table during the discussions, oozing as is his nature. The party debates whether he is entitled to a vault item, speculating he might consume something decorative of no magical value.

His informal summary is distinguished from the official offers. Zalthir uses his ability to stretch in different directions as an argument for accepting multiple alliances. The party advocates for a reward he can consume to gain a useful ability; no specific item is finalized.

### Dawnbringer
Thorin’s sentient magical sword, whose ongoing relationship with its wielder is a factor in Thorin’s decision not to take a second sentient weapon from the vault.

Thorin says their relationship has benefited from couples counseling and insists they are a package deal. He also includes Dawnbringer in Clan Feldrun. Suggestions of a second sentient weapon produce jokes about jealousy, competing demands, and the weapons deciding Thorin is not good enough for either of them.

## Items

### Spore Pass
A specialized protection offered by Morista of the Emerald Enclave. Given a day’s notice, she can prepare protection for one named passage through a fungal hazard, keeping spores and bad air out of the party’s lungs long enough to get through. It will not stop physical attacks, cure existing ailments, or help if the source of the corruption is present in the passage.

Morista’s explanation includes contaminated water as well as air. The protection must be prepared for the specified crossing. Its use against a fungal hazard is established; a clever tactical application would require a ruling on the actual plan.

### Giant Lizards
Large reptilian mounts offered by Morista of the Emerald Enclave to assist the party in traversing one leg of their journey through the difficult terrain of the Underdark, given in exchange for the party searching for her missing scout Sladis Vadir.

Morista offers enough mounts for everyone. Gyrgum is particularly enthusiastic about acquiring a giant lizard.

### Zhentarim Maps
Secret navigational charts provided by Davra Jassur that detail routes into the Underdark extending beyond the maps provided by the dwarven crown, pointing toward Mantol-Derith.

They accompany directions and the contact name Ghazrim DuLoc. Davra expressly offers information she knows, rather than guaranteeing a safe route.

### Dust of Mechanus
A magical dry lubricant of extraordinary potency. It is described as the perfect frictionless substance, capable of restarting ancient stuck machinery. It also has the property of preventing shapechangers from retaining their forms. The party used its only pinch to restart the Great Forge during this session.

The DM compares it to magical WD-40 and describes possible applications to stuck objects or tight spaces. Zalthir investigates using it to make his feet temporarily frictionless; a one-minute duration is suggested provisionally. Using the pinch on the forge forgoes those other possible uses and allows Bruenor to release equipment for the heroes.

### Salamander Flame Gauntlets
Dark metal gauntlets available in King Bruenor’s vault. They allow the wearer to use a bonus action to conjure a blade of fire, with the wearer’s choice of spellcasting ability, strength, or dexterity governing the effect. For Zalthir’s ranged use, the table ruled that the attack uses Dexterity.

Chosen by Zalthir. Their source is identified as *The Griffon’s Saddlebag: Book Two*. The discussion focuses particularly on the ability governing the ranged spell attack; Dexterity is the table’s ruling.

### Helm of the Citadel
A sturdy and imposing piece of headwear available in King Bruenor’s vault, ultimately chosen by Thorin as his reward for helping restart the Great Forge.

Thorin prefers it to another sentient weapon, avoiding both tension with Dawnbringer and a giant-hating spirit at odds with his own background.

### Stone Staff
A magical staff available in King Bruenor’s vault, chosen by Daz as his reward.

Daz records the choice in his character notes. *Vault of Magic* for fifth edition is mentioned as his item’s source during the discussion.

### Fiendwracker
A rare staff requiring attunement by a spellcaster, available in King Bruenor’s vault and chosen by Gyrgum during the vault review.

Gyrgum announces his selection while the group is also investigating Daz’s experiences. The DM asks that its text be copied into the character sheet because the available rewards are not standard entries in their tools.

## Spells

### Flame Blade
A spell that can be channeled through the Salamander Flame Gauntlets as a bonus action, creating a blade of fire in the wearer’s hand. The wearer may use their spellcasting ability, strength, or dexterity as the governing ability for the spell.

The spell is discussed while Zalthir reviews his chosen reward; it is not cast during the session. The associated ranged-attack question receives a Dexterity ruling at the table.
