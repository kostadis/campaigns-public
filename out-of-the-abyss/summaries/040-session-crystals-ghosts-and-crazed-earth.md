# Chapter 40 Crystals, Ghosts, and Crazed Earth

Date: 2025-12-08

## Summary

Eldeth, Jimjar, and Spiderbait took on the ghost problem. Glabbagool initially volunteered too, but the plan changed and he remained with the main party; before that change, he wondered aloud whether he would become the first gelatinous cube ghost if he died.

That evening, the party rested at the Foaming Mug Tavern, a staggeringly large cave converted into an inn specifically built to house visitors to Blingdenstone. Its rooms had beds large enough for dwarves, elves, dragonborn, ogres, and other “normal-sized” guests rather than only deep gnomes. The tavern served Darklake Stout imported through Mantol-Derith. Its proprietor, Tappy Foamstrap, was a bored deep gnome desperate for new stories after hearing every local’s troubles dozens of times.

The tavern’s patrons supplied plenty of news. The ghost problem was worsening, with spirits now haunting Blingdenstone’s catacombs. Scouts had reportedly seen a crazy gnome from the first days of the reclamation skulking through unrecovered portions of the settlement, whom the party suspected was the Pudding King. The Stoneheart Enclave was said to be “closing in on a solution” to Ogrémoch’s Bane—a claim that amused the party, since the solution was evidently to send them to deal with it. Debate also continued over the wererats and whether they should be considered gnomes at all.

A merchant recently returned from Gracklstugh described the duergar city as outwardly calm but deeply on edge. The authorities had imposed controls while blaming the Ember Vanguard for recent chaos, and the merchant carried a wanted poster reading “wanted dead or alive,” with “alive” misspelled. He noticed the party’s superficial resemblance to the wanted fugitives, but reassured himself that the poster depicted a red rather than gold dragonborn, as well as an older and more fearsome drow, orc, and dwarf. He also mentioned rumors that the Assassin’s Guild had returned, an influx of unexplained surface coins, and summary executions, decapitations, and derro heads displayed on pikes. His companion remarked that the duergar had been emphatic about the derro needing to “learn their place.”

The merchant also described Gracklstugh’s peculiarly portly red dragon, used as an extravagant matchlighter and distinguished by a double chin and two bellies. Despite the city’s murders, assassinations, political struggles, and controlled chaos, it had not collapsed into open war. The party was relieved that its goal of leaving Gracklstugh without causing the city to burn down had been achieved.

A deep gnome returning from the Whiteshell Mine offered another strange report: he had encountered a parade of dancing myconids communicating through Rapport Spores and announcing that they were on their way to a wedding celebration. After briefly calling it a “sweating celebration,” he corrected himself and emphasized how peculiar this was. Myconids do not normally marry, and no one he knew had ever heard of them holding celebrations. The party understood the likely cause: Zuggtmoy’s intended wedding. The dancing myconids might mean Neverlight Grove was emptying—or that its inhabitants had gone completely insane.

As the evening wore on, Glabbagool cleaned the tavern floor by absorbing spilled drinks and became increasingly intoxicated. The gelatinous cube grew loopier than usual and began singing traditional gelatinous cube sea shanties. This accidental discovery inspired the party to consider using alcohol against hostile oozes and puddings.

The next morning, the party asked Tappy for barrels of old or spoiled booze. He was willing to provide it only if no one attributed its questionable quality to the Foaming Mug and damaged the tavern’s reputation. The party countered that he might instead advertise himself as the hero whose alcohol saved the city. Tappy agreed if Blingdenstone’s leaders would provide an official notice he could display behind the bar. The party also discussed using Shape Water, a seltzer bottle, mugs, or some other improvised delivery mechanism to spray the alcohol.

The party then traveled to the Cultivation Cave, a vast complex where the deep gnomes grew useful fungi for cooking and crafting. A member of the Stoneheart Enclave welcomed them and supplied the freshest fungi from the garden for their expedition. Glabbagool enthusiastically ate mushrooms and plowed through the dirt and gravel blocking the alternate entrance to the Rockblight, spraying debris aside like a gigantic transparent snowblower. After several hours of excavation, the party reached the caverns.

The Rockblight was dark, damp, and chill, with rubble, shallow streams, and pools of difficult terrain. In one chamber, bioluminescent fungi shone through semi-transparent crystal formations, casting a kaleidoscope of colors across the walls. A stone statue of a deep gnome cowered beside one outgrowth, holding a broken crystal toward its face as though trying to peer through it. Thorin’s natural 20, combined with Daz’s arcane expertise, revealed that looking through the crystals protected against creatures capable of harming or petrifying victims with their gaze—effectively making the crystals magical sunglasses against a Medusa. The party collected portable crystals in several colors. Although the colors did not alter this particular protective property, Daz and Thorin nearly launched into a much longer discussion about crystal growth, color, and interactions with different spells.

Exploring farther, the party entered abandoned residential caverns devoid of warmth or joy and marked by carnage from the old drow assault. A furious deep gnome ghost appeared and attacked. Zalthir struck it repeatedly, prompting the spirit to cry, “They’re coming! Everyone must hide!” The party realized it had become frightened. Gyrgum recalled that frightened ghosts sometimes retreat to their lairs—and possibly their treasure—so the party tried not to destroy it. After Gyrgum struck it once more, the ghost disengaged, turned ethereal, and fled into a nearby burrow.

The party followed and found skeletal remains beside a stone bed. Just before vanishing, the ghost had appeared to reach beneath that bed, leading the adventurers to uncover a hidden compartment containing six gems worth six hundred gold pieces each and a potion of invisibility. The bones bore no signs of violent death, leaving the exact cause of death unknown. The remains belonged to Udhask, one of the spirits Burrow Warden Jadger wanted rescued. The party collected the bones so they could return them to the temple and restore the ghost.

Continuing south past a waterfall, the party reached a shallow, swiftly flowing stream. Crystalline formations rose on both sides, while rough-hewn stairs climbed thirty feet to a barren ledge occupied by two winged statues. Suspecting gargoyles, the party invited Glabbagool to clean them. When the cube approached, both statues animated. Their customary plan of remaining motionless until prey drew near was disrupted by the baffling sight of a friendly gelatinous cube with googly eyes.

Zalthir rushed the first gargoyle and battered it with repeated punches. The wounded creature disengaged and flew away in search of a target it could see. Daz cast Toll the Dead, but the gargoyle resisted the cantrip and took no damage. Thorin moved closer and hurled darts at it, finishing it with his second dart despite its resistance to nonmagical piercing attacks. Gyrgum shot the second gargoyle with his crossbow, likewise dealing reduced damage. The creature retaliated by flying directly at him and striking with both bite and claws for ten points of damage, prompting jokes about gargoyle venom and rabies.

Before the party could finish the remaining gargoyle, a crazed earth elemental erupted from the ground near the water and prepared to attack. Its appearance confirmed that the warnings about elementals being driven mad in the Rockblight were true. As the session ended, the party wondered what would happen if Maximilian’s Earthen Grasp were cast upon an earth elemental.

### Discovery of the Crystal Caverns

#### The party enters a dark cavern illuminated by bioluminescent fungi and crystal formations, discovering their protective properties.

- The Rockblight’s connecting tunnels resembled those of Inner Blingdenstone but were empty, damp, chill, and strewn with rubble.

- Shallow pools and streams crossed the area, and the pools constituted difficult terrain.

- The party entered a cavern where light from bioluminescent fungi refracted through large semi-transparent crystal formations, casting a kaleidoscope of colors across the walls.

- Images seen through the crystals appeared slightly distorted.

- They discovered a stone statue of a deep gnome cowering beside a crystal outgrowth and holding a broken piece toward his face as though trying to look through it.

- Examining the crystal from both directions revealed a distorting or protective effect.

- Thorin’s natural 20 Arcana check, combined with Daz’s expertise, revealed that the crystals provide protection against creatures with harmful gaze attacks when viewed through them, like magical sunglasses against a Medusa.

- The protection worked by allowing the bearer to look at the threatening creature through the crystal, interfering with the creature’s gaze.

- The petrified gnome had probably been trying to use the crystal but failed to position it correctly before being turned to stone.

- The party warned Glabbagool not to eat the crystals.

- They collected portable crystals of several colors, including a teal one, so they could raise them to their faces if confronted by a gaze-wielding creature.

- The crystals’ colors did not change this particular protective property. Thorin recalled that a crystal’s magic depended more on where and how it grew, while Daz noted that different colors could still produce different effects when combined with particular spells.

- The discussion threatened to become so technical and prolonged that everyone else might begin praying for a Medusa to arrive.

- The party knew the temple lay to the northeast or east but chose to explore first, reasoning that the crystal chamber had already provided something potentially useful against whatever guarded their objective.

## NPCs

### Burrow Warden Jadger

An individual pleased by the decision to address Blingdenstone’s ghost problem. He wished the volunteers well and noted that even if they died, they could still fight in the coming battle as ghosts. His explanation prompted Glabbagool to ask whether he would become the first gelatinous cube ghost. Jadger had previously asked the party to recover specific lost spirits, including Udhask, by finding and returning their remains.

### Tappy Foamstrap

The bored deep gnome proprietor of the Foaming Mug Tavern. Because there were few current visitors and he had heard every local’s story repeatedly, he was thrilled to meet newcomers with fresh news. He served Darklake Stout imported from Mantol-Derith.

Tappy agreed to provide old or spoiled booze for use against the Pudding King’s ooze forces, but insisted that its source never be used to tarnish the Foaming Mug’s reputation. He warmed to the arrangement after the party suggested he could advertise himself as a hero who helped save Blingdenstone, provided the city’s leaders signed an official notice for him to display.

### Gracklstugh Merchant

A trader encountered at the Foaming Mug Tavern who had visited Gracklstugh more recently than the party. He described the city as intact and conducting business, but increasingly tense and held together by controlled chaos.

He shared news of restrictions connected to the Ember Vanguard, rumors that a foreign adventuring group had made deals throughout the city and vanished, and claims that the Assassin’s Guild had returned. He carried an Ember Vanguard wanted poster with “alive” misspelled and noticed the party’s resemblance to its subjects, though discrepancies in color, age, and apparent ferocity reassured him.

He also reported an unexplained influx of surface-world coins, murders, assassinations, decapitations, and derro heads placed on pikes. His preferred survival strategy was to stay in foreigner-approved areas and avoid all deep duergar political conspiracies. He described Gracklstugh’s portly red dragon matchlighter as having a double chin and two bellies.

### Deep Gnome Scout

A deep gnome returning from the Whiteshell Mine who encountered a parade of dancing myconids. The myconids communicated through Rapport Spores and said they were on their way to a wedding celebration. After briefly calling it a “sweating celebration,” the scout drank again and corrected himself.

He considered the report bizarre because myconids neither marry nor commonly hold celebrations. He admitted that he only traded with them and was not an authority on their culture, but maintained that no one he knew had ever seen a myconid celebration.

### Stoneheart Enclave Member

A deep gnome responsible for managing the Cultivation Cave and its useful fungi. Having heard that the party intended to cleanse the temple, he greeted them warmly and provided the best and freshest mushrooms from the garden. He reluctantly accepted the damage when Glabbagool began eating his way through the fungi while clearing the blocked route.

### Udhask

A deep gnome ghost encountered in the abandoned residential caverns of the Rockblight. Initially aggressive and furious, he attacked the party but became frightened after Zalthir repeatedly punched him. He warned, “They’re coming! Everyone must hide!” before fleeing to his burrow.

Udhask vanished beside his skeletal remains and appeared to reach beneath his stone bed just before disappearing, leading the party to his hidden treasure. His bones showed no evidence of violent death, leaving his cause of death undetermined. He is one of the ghosts Burrow Warden Jadger asked the party to rescue.

### Gargoyle

Two winged stone statues perched on a thirty-foot ledge above a swiftly flowing stream. They animated when Glabbagool approached within approximately twenty to thirty feet. They attack with bites and claws and resist nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage unless the weapon is adamantine.

With an intelligence of six, their principal strategy was to remain motionless until prey approached. The arrival of a friendly gelatinous cube with googly eyes confused them enough that they failed to surprise him. One was destroyed by Zalthir’s punches and Thorin’s darts; the other remained engaged with Gyrgum when the earth elemental appeared.

### Crazed Earth Elemental

An earth elemental that erupted from the ground near the stream during the gargoyle battle. It immediately appeared hostile toward the party. Its crazed state confirmed warnings that the malign influence within the Rockblight could cause summoned earth elementals to turn on everyone nearby.

## Locations

### Diggermattock Hall

A central meeting place in Blingdenstone where the settlement’s leaders and factions discuss major threats and assign quests. The party endured a long meeting there concerning the Pudding King, Ogrémoch’s Bane, Entémoch’s Boon, the Temple of the Steadfast Stone, and the city’s ghost problem.

### The Foaming Mug Tavern

A staggeringly large tavern carved out of a cave in Blingdenstone. It was originally built as an inn for outside visitors and has beds sized for dwarves, elves, dragonborn, ogres, and other guests larger than deep gnomes.

The tavern serves Darklake Stout imported through Mantol-Derith and functions as a center for gossip and outside news. Tappy Foamstrap owns and operates it. Its well-spilled floor also proved capable of getting a cleaning gelatinous cube drunk.

### Cultivation Cave

A massive cavern complex managed by the Stoneheart Enclave and devoted to growing useful fungi for deep gnome cooking and crafting. A blocked route through its dirt and gravel provided an alternative entrance to the Rockblight. Glabbagool excavated the passage by plowing through debris and eating mushrooms along the way.

### Rockblight Caverns

A network of dark, damp, and chill caverns and connecting tunnels built in the same style as Inner Blingdenstone. The passages are empty, strewn with rubble, and crossed by shallow pools and streams that can impede movement.

Some chambers contain bioluminescent fungi and great semi-transparent crystals that scatter light into kaleidoscopic colors. Other sections mirror Blingdenstone’s old residential districts but contain only darkness and signs of the drow slaughter. The region is influenced by Ogrémoch’s Bane and can drive earth elementals mad.

### Udhask’s Burrow

A small chamber within the abandoned residential section of the Rockblight where Udhask’s frightened ghost retreated. His skeletal remains lay beside a stone bed and showed no sign of violent death. Beneath the bed was a hidden compartment containing six valuable gems and a potion of invisibility.

### Stream Cavern

A cave divided by a shallow, swiftly flowing stream. Crystalline formations rise from the floor on both sides, while rough-hewn stairs ascend thirty feet to a barren stone ledge. Two gargoyles waited atop that ledge in the form of winged statues. A crazed earth elemental erupted near the water during the ensuing battle.

### Gracklstugh

A duergar city the party previously explored, where they became entangled in murders, factional power struggles, conspiracies, and the activities attributed to the Ember Vanguard. Their goal was to leave without pushing the city into open war or burning it to the ground.

According to a later merchant, Gracklstugh remained intact and operational after their departure, although it was tense and barely held together. Authorities blamed the Ember Vanguard for much of the disruption, the Assassin’s Guild was rumored to have returned, derro were subjected to brutal reprisals, and unexplained surface-world coins had begun appearing. The party therefore achieved its goal of leaving the city in controlled chaos rather than outright collapse.

## Items

### Stone of Controlling Earth Elementals

A magical stone offered as a reward for cleansing the Temple of the Steadfast Stone and dealing with Ogrémoch’s Bane. It would allow its wielder to summon an earth elemental as an ally. The party has not yet received it, and finding Entémoch’s Boon remains a separate objective.

The item would be particularly valuable against the Pudding King because earth elementals are highly resistant or invulnerable to oozes and puddings.

### Fungi Supplies

The best and freshest fungi from the Cultivation Cave’s gardens, given to the party by a member of the Stoneheart Enclave. They did not possess the growth- or shrinking-related properties of previously encountered magical mushrooms. Glabbagool enthusiastically consumed fungi along the excavated route while clearing the blocked passage.

### Protective Crystals

Semi-transparent crystals found in the Rockblight Caverns that distort images and refract bioluminescent light into a kaleidoscope of colors. Looking at a creature through one of these crystals provides protection against harmful gaze attacks, functioning like magical sunglasses against a Medusa.

The party collected several portable pieces in different colors, including teal. Color does not alter this specific protective effect, though color, growth conditions, and interaction with different spells may influence other magical properties.

### Udhask’s Gems

Six valuable gems, each worth 600 gold pieces, found in a hidden compartment beneath the stone bed in Udhask’s burrow. Udhask appeared to reach toward the compartment immediately before his ghost vanished.

### Potion of Invisibility

A magical potion found alongside Udhask’s six gems in the hidden compartment beneath his stone bed.

### Udhask’s Bones

The skeletal remains of the deep gnome Udhask, found beside a stone bed in his burrow. Examination showed that he had not died violently, although his exact cause of death remained unknown. ### Spoiled Booze

Barrels of old or spoiled alcohol obtained from Tappy Foamstrap after the party discovered that alcohol could intoxicate Glabbagool. They intend to use it against oozes and puddings.

Tappy required that the poor-quality alcohol not be publicly associated with the Foaming Mug unless he received official recognition for helping save the city. The party discussed transporting barrels in the Bag of Holding and delivering the alcohol with mugs, a spray bottle, or Shape Water.

## Spells

### Toll the Dead

A necromantic cantrip used by Daz against the badly wounded flying gargoyle. The spell required a Wisdom saving throw and would have inflicted additional damage because the target was already injured. The gargoyle succeeded on its save, so the cantrip dealt no damage.

### Shape Water

A cantrip discussed as a way to direct or spray alcohol against hostile oozes. It can alter the flow of water, move it, form it into simple animated shapes, or change its color for up to an hour.

The party debated whether alcohol or impure water qualified as “water,” noting that naturally occurring water is never perfectly distilled. They also raised—and immediately rejected—the game-breaking possibility of manipulating the water inside a living creature, with Mystra invoked as the ultimate reason such abuse would not work.

### Lesser Restoration

A restorative spell mentioned after a gargoyle bit and clawed Gyrgum. The party joked that he might need treatment for rabies or “gargoyle venom,” and Lesser Restoration was suggested as a possible cure.

### Maximilian's Earthen Grasp

An earth-manipulating spell considered immediately after the crazed earth elemental erupted into the gargoyle battle. The session ended with the unresolved question, “I wonder what happens if I use Maximilian’s Earthen Grasp on an Earth Elemental.”
