# Chapter 030

Date: 2025-09-22

Attendance: Thorin's player was absent, so the GM ran Thorin. Daz's player joined from a hotel in San Jose. He had moved his flight so he could make the session.

## Summary
The party ventured deeper into the vast caverns surrounding Neverlight Grove. The underground landscape was filled with towering, psychedelically colored mushrooms that glowed with strange, inviting light. The GM introduced a new, larger map to convey the true scale of the area, which was far bigger than the previous map had implied.

Their quarry was a Grick Alpha, a surly predator lurking in the rocky, mossy terrain to the south. As the party set out, Loobamub warned them that a Shambling Mound also lurked nearby: "if you do find that, kill it. We don't reanimate shambling mounds." A Shambling Mound is a massive creature of living swamp matter. It does not walk around but flops down as if it were part of a mossy swamp.

Daz devised an unusual strategy. Advancing slowly within Shape Water's 30-foot range, he froze small sections of the mossy ground in a checkerboard pattern, spacing his lines so that no 10-by-10 gap could hide the Large creature. The spell only freezes water that has no creature in it, so any patch that failed to freeze would reveal the beast. The party advanced cautiously, with Thorin in front, "because that's what he does." Dawnbringer glowed fiercely in the dark, since the magical blade refuses to tolerate the absence of light.

It was the Grick Alpha that revealed itself first. The party's freezing of its surroundings disturbed it, and it woke, bared its teeth and claws, and lunged toward them with surly, cranky fury. The creature is a grotesque thing resembling a great snake with a hooked beak and writhing, talon-tipped tentacles. Zalthir immediately shrouded it in a sphere of impenetrable magical darkness and waded into the blackness. He landed a relentless series of unarmed strikes for 29 damage, though the Grick shook off his Stunning Strike. Daz could not see the creature through the darkness, so he trusted his memory of where it stood and angled a fireball to miss his allies. The Grick made its save and took only 13 damage, prompting Daz's lament: "That could have been 26."

Thorin charged in after, Dawnbringer blazing with radiant light as he hacked at the creature. Gyrgum could not use Guiding Bolt without line of sight, so he tried to drench it in Tasha's Caustic Brew, which the Grick Alpha dodged entirely. Battered and confused, the creature could not land a single blow on Thorin or Zalthir through the magical darkness. It "ponders its life for a moment," then made a desperate decision to flee. It did not get far: Zalthir's attack of opportunity hit for 13, and Thorin's blow finished it as it ran. The party harvested the creature's tough, camouflaged hide, which could be fashioned in six days into a cloak granting advantage when hiding in rocky terrain.

With the Grick Alpha dealt with, the party turned to the Shambling Mound. Strictly speaking they had only committed to the Grick. However, an Arcana check revealed that although Shambling Mounds are "notorious for not having any treasure," their vines are valued for crafting enchanted ropes. The party might trade them in Blingdenstone, where Jimjar's crew was headed, and that was motivation enough. Daz resumed his methodical freezing across the lake and eventually hit the creature lurking by a pool of water. Zalthir closed in along the shore after being warned that the mound likes to grab people and shove them under. He opened with a critical hit and stunned the massive creature with a well-placed strike. Daz followed with three scorching rays, all of which hit, though the creature's resistance halved the damage. Gyrgum then invoked Spirit Guardians, summoning spectral platinum dragons that swirled around him in a 15-foot radius. He spared Thorin and Zalthir from their effects.

Zalthir, now with "a reason to grapple for once," seized the stunned creature and locked it in place within the burning aura. Daz detonated another enormous fireball, having first checked that nobody would take steam damage from him "melting the lake." That blast brought the mound down to 9 hit points. The Shambling Mound thrashed with electrified tendrils and struck Zalthir, but he deflected the bludgeoning force and took only 2 lightning damage. It then engulfed Thorin within its rotting mass. Thorin, blinded and muttering that he had "smelt better cologne," fought his way free and shouted, "damn you, Gyrgum, do your thing." The spectral dragons scorched the creature once more at the end of its turn, and it collapsed into a heap of lifeless vines. A 2d6 roll yielded enough vine material for six ropes, worth 300 gold pieces.

The party returned to Neverlight Grove and presented the remains of the Grick Alpha to Basidia. She was mildly disappointed at the state of the hide but mused, "hmm, this will be an interesting transformation. I can't wait." The party checked in on their old Myconid companions, Stool and Rumpadump. Gyrgum told them they were welcome to come adventuring, but the pair preferred to stay home. Daz wondered whether they should head on to Blingdenstone, but Basidia reminded them that she had promised magic and treasure if they explored the Circle of Masters. During a brief rest she held forth about the circles. She then led them to the Circle of Builders, a vast cavern of terraced irrigation systems and thousands of glowing fungi that filled the air with a thick, sweet smell.

There they met Gasbide, a Myconid of intense and obsessive curiosity and a supporter of Sovereign Phylo. He immediately began interrogating the party about surface-world architecture: "Describe to me your buildings." Gyrgum offered that "we have some that are tall and some that are big," which did not satisfy him. Gasbide demanded shapes, materials, and counts of windows and doors, and was captivated by the unfamiliar word "window." Zalthir conjured an illusion of a small surface building, and Gasbide was utterly transfixed. He wondered whether it was built for small people, then decided that its creator must be a member of the circle of builders in their own community. Meanwhile, Basidia rolled her eyes.

Gasbide revealed that he dreamed of building a fungal tower even greater than Yggmorgus, the colossal mushroom of Neverlight Grove. He hoped it would break through the Underdark to the surface world, "possibly with the aid of Araumycos." He asked for a construction that could shield him from the sun, and Gyrgum helpfully suggested "shade." Daz happily nerded out with him over zurkhwood and construction minutiae. Yet Gasbide had no real understanding of what Araumycos was, only that it was part of a great celebration to come. In his words: "Phylo said that there would be a great awakening, that everything will be better after the great celebration." The party noted the unsettling vagueness of his words and the blind faith he placed in Phylo's promises. Something was being planned in Neverlight Grove, and it was far larger than a simple celebration.

## NPCs
### Grick Alpha
A surly, cranky predator resembling a snake with a beak and multiple tentacle-like appendages. The party's magical interference disturbed it from hiding, and it attacked before attempting to flee. It is resistant to non-magical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing, but not to fire. It attacks with its beak and tentacles. Gricks are known to accumulate treasure. It was a designated reanimation target for the Myconids.

### Shambling Mound
A Large creature made of moss and swamp matter. It camouflages itself in wet areas by flopping down as if it were part of a mossy swamp. It is resistant to fire and cold, and not immune to bludgeoning. It can electrify its tendril attacks, dealing bludgeoning and lightning damage, and can attempt to engulf creatures, blinding them. It has good wisdom but low intelligence. It carries no treasure, but its vines are valuable for crafting. The Myconids do not reanimate Shambling Mounds.

### Basidia
A Myconid Sovereign who would reanimate the Grick Alpha that Loobamub of the Circle of Hunters had asked the party to hunt (chapter 29). She expressed mild disappointment at the state of the hide but accepted the remains, musing that it "will be an interesting transformation." She reminded the party of her promised magic and treasure for exploring the Circle of Masters, lectured on the circles during their rest, and guided them toward the Circle of Builders.

### Gasbide
Leader of the Circle of Builders and a supporter of Sovereign Phylo. He is obsessively curious about surface-world architecture and grilled the party on materials, windows, doors, and scale. He mistook the builder of the illusion for a member of a surface "circle of builders." He dreams of building a fungal tower to break through to the surface, and wants a construction to protect him from the sun. He speaks of an upcoming "Great Awakening" promised by Phylo, involving something called Araumycos, though he does not know what Araumycos is.

### Sovereign Phylo
Co-sovereign of Neverlight Grove with Basidia. Gasbide says Phylo foretold a "great awakening" and a coming celebration that will supposedly make everything better.

### Stool
A young Myconid sprout and former companion to the party who has returned home to Neverlight Grove. Stool had originally been kidnapped. Stool was happy and cheering on seeing the party, and declined Gyrgum's invitation to adventure again.

### Rumpadump
A Myconid companion who traveled with the party and has returned home to Neverlight Grove. Rumpadump had been teleported by a crazed Myconid using a power Thorin refused. Rumpadump prefers to stay home but was touched by Gyrgum's offer ("That's very sweet of you").

### Dawnbringer
A sentient magical sword wielded by Thorin. She emits radiant light and hates being dark, glowing brightly even in the depths of the Underdark. Thorin used her to strike the Grick Alpha for radiant damage. Gyrgum, a cleric proficient only with simple weapons, declined to borrow her.

## Items
### Grick Alpha Hide
The tough, camouflaged skin harvested from the defeated Grick Alpha. It can be crafted into a cloak or cape that grants advantage on dexterity checks when hiding in rocky terrain. Such a cloak takes six days of work.

### Shambling Mound Vines
Vines harvested from the remains of the defeated Shambling Mound. They are not magical in themselves, but are highly valued in certain communities for crafting enchanted ropes. The party recovered enough for six such ropes, worth approximately 300 gold pieces. The GM suggested they might be traded in Blingdenstone.

### Zurkhwood Mushroom
A type of fungal material found in the Underdark, discussed by Gasbide and Daz as a potential construction material equivalent to surface wood, with Gyrgum's rule of thumb of about three zurkhwood mushrooms to the square foot.

## Spells
### Shape Water
Daz repeatedly cast this cantrip, with a range of 30 feet, to freeze five-foot sections of water and moisture in the mossy ground in a checkerboard pattern. He was trying to detect and flush out the camouflaged Shambling Mound. The spell does not freeze water occupied by a creature, and the GM ruled out using it to freeze a creature's insides.

### Darkness
Zalthir cast this spell to create a zone of magical, ink-black shadow over the Grick Alpha. It imposed disadvantage on the Grick's attacks and forced the party to rely on area-of-effect abilities and knowledge of the creature's general location. It also blocked Gyrgum's Guiding Bolt and Daz's Magic Missile. Zalthir can only move the darkness at the start of his turn.

### Fireball
Daz cast this spell twice during the session: once to blast the Grick Alpha through the magical darkness, dealing 13 on a successful save, and again to hit the Shambling Mound, dealing 12 after resistance on a failed save. The 20-foot radius explosion dealt significant damage, though the Shambling Mound's resistance to fire reduced the effect. Daz angled both blasts to avoid his allies and had to double-check the radius first.

### Tasha's Caustic Brew
Gyrgum attempted to spray a five-foot-wide stream of acid at the Grick Alpha, but the creature succeeded on its DC 15 Dexterity saving throw and took no damage. The table joked about the spell's visual as the orc vomiting acid, and Gyrgum invented a toothbrush as its "material component."

### Scorching Ray
Daz fired three beams of intense heat at the stunned Shambling Mound, with advantage, and all three rays hit for 6, 10, and 7. The creature's resistance to fire halved the 23 total to 11 damage, but it was still a meaningful contribution to the fight.

### Spirit Guardians
Gyrgum summoned spectral spirits resembling platinum dragons that swirled around him in a 15-foot radius, exempting Thorin and Zalthir. This third-level concentration spell dealt radiant damage to the Shambling Mound whenever it entered or remained within the aura, on a failed DC 15 Wisdom save, and halved its speed. Combined with Zalthir's grapple, it trapped the creature and damaged it repeatedly (13, then 15) until it was destroyed. The GM ruled that it is not an infinite-damage hack, since a creature saves only once per turn.

### Minor Illusion
Zalthir used this cantrip to conjure a small, detailed scale model of a surface building to demonstrate surface-world architecture to the fascinated Myconid Gasbide in the Circle of Builders.
