# Chapter 44 From Zuggtmoy to Entémoch's Boon

Date: 2026-01-19

## Summary
The party found themselves locked in fierce combat with a Shambling Mound in the Festering Fissure, while charmed Violet Fungus stood by in confusion. The DM reminded Daz that as an evoker he could sculpt spells. Daz unleashed a carefully controlled explosion of fire, sculpting the flames to avoid both Zalthir and the precious lichen they had come to harvest. A natural-20 Arcana check told Daz that the 75-hit-point Mound resisted cold and fire and was immune to lightning. The party switched to radiant and force damage. The Shambling Mound proved resilient, shrugging off magical assaults with uncanny luck: it rolled natural 20s on back-to-back saves against Maximilian's Earthen Grasp and Tasha's Caustic Brew. Zalthir's relentless strikes and Thorin's devastating blows eventually brought the creature down. Its end came when Zalthir deflected its slam and Thorin landed an 8-point opportunity attack.

As they finished harvesting the rare lichen needed for an acid-resistant weapon coating, a chilling voice echoed in their minds. It was Zuggtmoy, the Lady of Rot, speaking to the Pudding King about her sinister plans to use oozes to "soften the shell" so her garden could "take root in the flesh beneath." She ended with "Digest them all. I will claim this outpost." The DM then reminded the party that pursuing Entémoch's boon was optional. It would help summon Earth Elementals for the coming battle. Thorin summed up the case for going: "I mean, we could get some mad loot, just saying."

The party arrived at Pickshine Mine, where four massive Galeb Duhr blocked the path. They were vibrating rhythmically like stones singing the first half of a forgotten duet. Dasco Pickshine, one of the mine's owners, had tried everything to move them, even striking them with his pickaxe, but the ancient sentinels refused to budge. Gyrgum sensed they were waiting for divine authority. Daz perceived their vibrations as a linguistic pattern, a call waiting for an echo that never came. The DM framed it as a broken network handshake: the stones were sending packets and never getting an acknowledgement. After discovering resonant geodes and arranging them while humming, Daz completed the ancient handshake protocol, and the Galeb Duhr finally relaxed and opened the way. Dasco first suggested the party shouldn't expect payment. Daz answered with a veiled threat to put everything back the way they found it. Dasco then grudgingly offered, through a crowd of miners, two ruby gems worth five hundred gold pieces as thanks.

The geode, now acting as a beacon locator, led them deeper into the earth to the Whiteshell Mine, Blingdenstone's largest salt mine. The mine was ominously quiet. Glabbagool stayed back because of the salt. There, Perigrog Scrapedust explained that a tunnel had become impassable. It was impossibly dry, with blinding crystallite walls and absolute, suffocating silence, and even the lizards refused to go down it. Shadows cast by torches appeared painted on the walls rather than flickering, as if time itself had stopped. Zalthir recalled a metaphysical puzzle from his training. Magical pressure had caused time to snag at this point, and breaking it directly would unleash a catastrophic explosion that would crush this tunnel and all the tunnels around it. The solution required creating a hole for the backlog of reality to pass through safely, which only a Shadow Monk could accomplish.

The gnomes eagerly presented their specialized explosives, and Zalthir meditated on the challenge, channeling all his frustration into the void. With Gyrgum's blessing to aid him, and a +2 and advantage from the DM for the roleplay, Zalthir positioned himself as the explosives detonated. He executed a perfect maneuver (Acrobatics 23) to redirect the magical blast through shadow space. The wall shattered without collapsing the tunnels. The gnomes celebrated their success, largely ignoring the possibility that Zalthir had been blown to smithereens, while the party caught their breath with a short rest.

Beyond the breached wall, they discovered a large cavern containing a summoning circle inscribed with runes in giant script. Two basilisks guarded the circle, moving with mechanical precision rather than natural animal behavior, as if they viewed the party as flaws in perfect stone. The party worked through the petrification rules:
- Looking at the basilisk is what causes petrification (DC 12 Constitution, 30-foot cone), not the basilisk seeing you.
- Averting your eyes means fighting as if blinded.
- Magical darkness blocks the gaze.
- Greater Restoration would undo petrification (the DM called Stone to Flesh an older-edition rule), and Gyrgum only has the lesser version.

Zalthir engaged one basilisk in close combat inside his darkness while averting his eyes. Daz successfully enchanted the other, freezing it in place with an incapacitating charm.

While his companions held the basilisks at bay, Thorin rushed to the summoning circle and deciphered the giant runes. To free Entémoch and claim his boon, three sacrifices were required: wealth, a weapon, and blood. Thorin placed a dart onto the runes as the weapon sacrifice, and the stone instantly swallowed it, rusting it to dust in seconds. The runes transformed from yellow to blinding orange, the air grew hot, and the uncharmed basilisk became frantic as the ritual began. The party now stood on the threshold of completing their quest, with only two sacrifices remaining to unlock Entémoch's power. The DM warned that finishing would end the fight but would "take a few rounds."

## Locations
### Festering Fissure
A location in the Underdark where the party fought a Shambling Mound and charmed Violet Fungus, and successfully harvested rare lichen from a giant's corpse. Zuggtmoy has established a garden of nightmare mushrooms shaped like children here. The DM noted of the mushroom children: "these are not real children."

### Pickshine Mine
A rich gem mine co-owned by Dasco Pickshine where the party encountered four Galeb Duhr blocking the path. The air is thick with the smell of ozone and crushed quartz, and a low rhythmic thrum vibrates through the ground from the Galeb Duhr. It is one of the two mines (with Whiteshell) whose miners reported the rock "trying to speak."

### Whiteshell Mine
Blingdenstone's largest salt mine, an interconnected network of caverns producing salt for Blingdenstone and Mantol-Derith. Run by Perigrog Scrapedust, it houses giant cave lizards used as beasts of burden. The party encountered a mysterious time-snagged wall here that was impossibly dry with blinding crystallite walls and absolute silence, where shadows appeared painted rather than flickering.
- The caverns are dug into vast salt veins.
- The DM compared the mines to 19th-century mines, one leading into the next through caves.
- The name isn't about shells or whites. Gyrgum: "Is it full of whites?" DM: "God, no."

### Summoning Circle Cavern
A large cavern within the Whiteshell Mine containing a summoning circle with runes written in giant script. Two mechanical-looking basilisks guard this location, which is the site of Entémoch's boon. The runes require sacrifices of wealth, a weapon, and blood to free Entémoch. The runes glow yellow at rest and turned blinding orange, with the air growing hot, after the weapon sacrifice.

## Items
### Lichen
Rare and pure lichen successfully harvested by the party from a giant's corpse in the Festering Fissure. It is needed by Kazook Pickshine to brew an acid-resistant coating for weapons in preparation for an upcoming battle. The party completed the harvest with careful chanting by Gyrgum and arcane identification by Daz. The checks were Arcana 22, Nature 26, and a failed Sleight of Hand, for two of three successes.

### Resonant Geodes
Initially useless-looking rocks discovered by Daz in the Pickshine Mine. They can be arranged to mimic sounds and were used to complete the 'handshake protocol' with the Galeb Duhr. After the Galeb Duhr opened the path, one geode began to hum violently and pull magnetically toward the Whiteshell Mine, acting as a signal tracer pointing to Entémoch's location. They emit a "hollow, resonant, humming, faint, high-pitched note."

### Ruby Gems
Two ruby gems collectively worth 500 gold pieces, given to the party by Dasco Pickshine and the deep gnome miners as a token of appreciation for resolving the Galeb Duhr situation at Pickshine Mine. At the summoning circle the runes call for a ruby as the wealth sacrifice; the party still holds both gems when the session ends. Daz logged the value as 500 gold in the party inventory.

### Dart
A small, easily disposable weapon used by Thorin as the weapon sacrifice on the runes in the summoning circle. Upon placement, the stone instantly swallowed it, rusting it to dust in seconds, causing the runes to transform from yellow to blinding orange.

### Gnome Explosives
Specialized explosive devices containing 'rude powder', a substance the deep gnomes are specialists in creating. These explosives were used to breach the time-snagged wall at Whiteshell Mine, with the gnomes shaping the charge to direct the blast while Zalthir redirected the magical explosion through Shadow Step. Rival gnomes each championed their own device.

## Spells
### Fireball
A powerful area-of-effect spell cast by Daz against the Violet Fungus in the Festering Fissure. As an evoker, Daz carefully sculpted the spell to avoid hitting Zalthir and the valuable lichen, demonstrating precise magical control. The DM noted Daz could sculpt out "up to, I think, everybody in your party" and warned the lichen was at risk.

### Magic Missile
A spell cast by Daz that creates unerring streaks of magical force. It dealt only 6 points of damage to the Shambling Mound, barely registering against the tough creature.
- Daz chose it "because that always works."
- It was later ruled out for breaching the wall because it must target "a creature of your choice that you see within range."

### Guiding Bolt
A radiant damage spell cast by Gyrgum that struck the Shambling Mound for 13 points of damage and granted advantage to the next attack against it, which proved crucial in the battle. The spell attack rolled 17 against AC 15, and Zalthir's punches benefited from the advantage.

### Maximilian's Earthen Grasp
A spell attempted by Daz to restrain the Shambling Mound with a DC 15 strength save, but the creature rolled a natural 20 and completely resisted its effects.

### Tasha's Caustic Brew
A corrosive spell attempted by Gyrgum that creates caustic liquid. The Shambling Mound miraculously rolled a natural 20 on its dexterity save, avoiding the spell's effects entirely, much to Gyrgum's frustration. It was cast at 2nd level against a Mound with 8 hit points left. Gyrgum: "it's not called caustic for no reason."

### Shadow Step
A monk ability used by Zalthir to teleport through shadows and create a momentary void. He used this ability to safely redirect the magical explosion from the time-snagged wall, preventing a catastrophic collapse of the tunnels while allowing the wall to be breached. The DM: "whenever you use a shadow step, you literally are creating a momentary space for things to flow through."

### Hypnotic Pattern
A spell successfully cast by Daz on one of the basilisks in the summoning circle, incapacitating it and reducing its speed to zero. The spell ends if the creature takes damage or someone uses an action to shake it out of its stupor.
- It was a DC 15 Wisdom save against the basilisk's -4.
- It is the same spell that earlier caught Zalthir.

### Bless
A spell Gyrgum offered to cast to aid Zalthir before his Shadow Step attempt at the time-snagged wall. After that offer and Zalthir's meditation roleplay, the DM granted +2 and advantage on the check.

### Deflect Attacks
A monk ability used by Zalthir to completely negate damage from the Shambling Mound's bludgeoning slam.
