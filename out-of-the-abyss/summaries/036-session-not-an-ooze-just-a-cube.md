# Chapter 36 Not an Ooze, Just a Cube

Date: 2025-11-12

## Summary

The party arrived at a gate deep within the caverns of Blingdenstone, where the deep gnome guards who had previously admitted them came huffing and puffing after them. One squeaky-voiced guard asked, “So you escaped from Velkynvelve from the Drow. Is this correct?” He then disclosed a “small semantical problem”: the visibly embarrassed sergeant had not read the latest report all the way through. Standing orders required any escapees from the Drow to be escorted immediately to Chief Dorbo of Clan Diggermattock.

The guards explained that Clan Diggermattock had taken this territory from the Drow and considered any information about their long-standing enemies immediately and critically important. Chief Dorbo wanted to question the party so the deep gnomes could better prepare their defenses. The officer presented the situation as an embarrassing procedural oversight and apologized for not informing the party earlier.

The party remained wary of being ordered to accompany the guards. They discussed whether the guard’s timid manner was genuine or manipulative and observed that their practical alternatives were to leave Blingdenstone or attempt to fight the guards. An insight check established that the request was sincere and contained no malevolence: the deep gnomes routinely sought intelligence from anyone who had escaped the Drow. Reassured, the party agreed that meeting Dorbo might place them in the clan’s good graces and could also provide information about a route to the surface. One party member joked that this was preferable to “trying to kill all the guards, which was the other thing I was considering.”

Before proceeding, the party consulted Jimjar, reasoning that he had repeatedly demonstrated that he knew more than he openly revealed. Asked whether meeting Dorbo was a good idea, Jimjar replied, “I’ll bet you five empty spell gems that meeting with them is the right thing to do.” He added, “But you’re going to have to figure out how to get rid of the oozes.” This prompted some confusion over whether he meant Glabbagool or the broader demonic ooze threat. Glabbagool corrected the terminology: “It’s not an ooze, it’s a cube.” Jimjar clarified that Blingdenstone was suffering from an extensive ooze infestation. The party carefully confirmed the terms of the wager and accepted, recognizing that Jimjar was effectively predicting that the clan would send them on a quest.

The guards escorted the party through Blingdenstone’s reception hall, where a cart was jokingly described as a “medieval pickup truck,” and onward through the Traders’ Grotto. Glabbagool followed along. Although the party had explained his friendly nature, less adventurous deep gnomes reacted nervously when they saw a sentient gelatinous cube moving through a settlement already troubled by oozes.

The party was eventually brought to Diggermattock Hall. Roughly two dozen Svirfneblin occupied the well-lit cavern. Some hurried about with messages and papers in hand, while others gathered around stone tables covered with maps and documents, speaking in hushed, serious tones. At the back stood a rock-carved dais bearing two stone desks. A male and female Svirfneblin sat there conferring with one another and their advisors.

The male leader introduced himself: “My name is Dorbo. And over here is my assistant, Senni. And we are in charge here. Who are you?” The party initially blurted out “the Emerald Vanguard,” then became tangled in a discussion of the Emerald Vanguard, Ember Vanguard, and Emerald Enclave. They remembered that one of those names was associated with a faction blamed for destroying a capital city and decided against using it. Instead, they introduced themselves simply as adventurers trying to find their way home and reach the Overbright.

Dorbo and Quartermaster Senni Diggermattock responded with muttering, finger-pointing, and an apparently heated private argument. Anticipating Jimjar’s warning, the party openly asked whether the clan had a side quest involving oozes and volunteered their interest. Senni seized upon the offer: “We’d normally be delighted to help you go to the Overbright, but unfortunately, our community is suffering from an ooze problem. And since you so graciously volunteered to go look at it, we won’t feel at all encumbered or guilty about asking you to do that before we help you.”

Glabbagool immediately defended the distinction between himself and the creatures troubling Blingdenstone: “Well, I’m not an ooze. I’m a gelatinous cube. A totally different species.” He added, “And I don’t know why my fat friends are causing you pain, but I’m here. I am the friendly type.” The party supported his argument by noting that gelatinous cubes have much higher viscosity than oozes and can maintain a square form: “Have you ever seen a square ooze? I don’t think so.” Delighted by the reasoning, Glabbagool declared, “I knew there was a reason I was just better than them.”

The discussion wandered to shambling mounds. Because Glabbagool had never heard of one, a party member described the creature from both study and the group’s previous encounter. Glabbagool excitedly asked, “So does it have a lot of stuff inside of it when it blew up?” The party explained that shambling mounds did not sweep up and retain objects the way gelatinous cubes did. Glabbagool knew ordinary oozes from personal experience but characterized them as “very not talkative.” When asked whether he ate them, he answered, “No, no, I don’t eat oozes. I mean, I do punch them sometimes, but no, I don’t eat oozes.” He then extended a pseudopod to demonstrate that he could strike things as well as absorb them.

## Scenes

### Arrival at Blingdenstone Gate

#### The party arrives at a gate in the caverns of Blingdenstone, where they are met by deep gnome guards who question them about their escape from the Drow.

- The party arrives at a gate within the caverns of Blingdenstone.

- The deep gnome guards who had previously admitted the party through another gate catch up to them, huffing and puffing from exertion.

- A squeaky-voiced guard confirms the party’s history: “So you escaped from Velkynvelve from the Drow. Is this correct?”

- The officer explains that there has been a “small semantical problem.” He points to a flummoxed and embarrassed sergeant who failed to read the latest report carefully from beginning to end.

- The officer publicly admonishes the sergeant: “Shame on you,” then stresses, “It’s very important in any military operation that people read the orders thoroughly.”

- The sergeant looks as though he wishes a hole would open and swallow him while the officer appears increasingly pleased with his own handling of the situation.

- According to standing orders, any escapees from the Drow must be escorted immediately to Chief Dorbo of Clan Diggermattock.

- When asked what Dorbo wants, the guards explain that he wishes to speak with the party rather than reward or imprison them.

- Prompted by “the other memo,” the guards add that Clan Diggermattock took the area from the Drow. Any intelligence the party can provide about the Drow is therefore of immediate and critical importance.

- The party answers that they may possess “some limited intelligence on the Drow.”

- The officer explains that Dorbo hopes to use this information to better prepare Blingdenstone’s defenses, then apologizes for failing to inform the party sooner.

- The party remains suspicious because the meeting is framed simultaneously as an order and a request for help. They wonder whether the guard’s embarrassment and timidity are genuine or manipulative.

- Insight reveals that the request is sincere and contains no malevolence. The Svirfneblin routinely view Drow escapees as valuable sources of information about a long-term enemy.

- The party contrasts Blingdenstone’s straightforward concern with their previous experiences: “Not everything is a deep-seated conspiracy. That’s the Drow. The Duergar.”

- Recalling the intrigues they previously encountered, one party member remarks, “There were so many conspiracies. I needed a friggin’ spreadsheet to keep track of the conspiracies.”

- Reassured, a party member says, “Well, I’m in favor of going then, and instead of trying to kill all the guards, which was the other thing I was considering.”

- The party considers its practical options: cooperate, turn around and leave, or try to fight the guards. They also reason that Dorbo may know a way out of the Underdark.

- The party agrees to accompany the guards to Diggermattock Hall.

## NPCs

### Deep Gnome Guards

A group of Svirfneblin guards who previously admitted the party to Blingdenstone and then had to hurry after them. They arrive huffing and puffing. One officer speaks in a squeaky voice and enjoys emphasizing proper military procedure, while the sergeant responsible for missing the relevant memo appears flummoxed and deeply embarrassed. The officer publicly tells him, “Shame on you,” and stresses the importance of reading orders thoroughly.

Their standing orders require all Drow escapees to be escorted to Chief Dorbo Diggermattock for questioning. Their request is genuine and free of malevolence: because Clan Diggermattock took this territory from the Drow, the guards regard intelligence about their long-term enemies as critically important to Blingdenstone’s defenses.

### Jimjar

A knowledgeable deep gnome companion of the party who is prone to conveying information through wagers. The party recognizes that he “tends to know a lot of stuff” and discusses using more bets to coax information from him reliably.

When asked whether meeting Dorbo is wise, Jimjar bets five empty spell gems that it is the right decision. He also predicts that the party will have to deal with Blingdenstone’s oozes. After the party carefully clarifies the wager’s terms, he confirms that they will receive the gems if his prediction leads them into the anticipated quest.

### Glabbagool

A friendly, googly-eyed, sentient gelatinous cube who travels with the party. His presence unsettles some non-adventuring Svirfneblin, especially because Blingdenstone is already suffering from an ooze infestation.

Glabbagool insists upon the taxonomic distinction between himself and ordinary oozes: “Well, I’m not an ooze. I’m a gelatinous cube. A totally different species.” He describes the hostile oozes as his “fat friends” but assures the gnomes that he is “the friendly type.” The party supports his claim by pointing to a cube’s greater viscosity and ability to maintain a square shape, prompting Glabbagool to conclude, “I knew there was a reason I was just better than them.”

He is intensely curious about shambling mounds and wants to know whether they contain objects or treasure when destroyed. He has encountered ordinary oozes before and describes them as “very not talkative.” He does not eat oozes, although he sometimes punches them. He demonstrates this capability by extending a pseudopod and confirms that he can both strike and absorb things.

### Chief Dorbo Diggermattock

A male Svirfneblin leader of Clan Diggermattock and one of the two authorities presiding over Diggermattock Hall. He sits behind a stone desk on a rock-carved dais, conferring with advisors and Quartermaster Senni Diggermattock.

He introduces himself plainly: “My name is Dorbo. And over here is my assistant, Senni. And we are in charge here. Who are you?” His clan considers intelligence about the Drow essential because the Diggermattocks took this part of Blingdenstone from them. After learning that the visitors are adventurers seeking the Overbright, Dorbo engages Senni in an animated private discussion involving muttering and finger-pointing.

### Quartermaster Senni Diggermattock

Chief Dorbo Diggermattock’s female assistant and fellow authority in Diggermattock Hall. She sits at the second stone desk on the dais and confers closely—and at times heatedly—with Dorbo.

Senni directly links the party’s desired assistance to Blingdenstone’s immediate crisis. She says the clan would ordinarily be delighted to help them reach the Overbright, but its community is suffering from an ooze problem. When the party volunteers interest in an ooze-related quest, she deftly treats that offer as permission to send them: “Since you so graciously volunteered to go look at it, we won’t feel at all encumbered or guilty about asking you to do that before we help you.”

## Locations

### Blingdenstone

A deep gnome city in the Underdark, built throughout a network of caverns and stone structures. Its name is explicitly treated as a pun on “bling in the stone,” referring to gems in the rock.

Clan Diggermattock took at least part of this territory from the Drow, making intelligence about Drow activity essential to the settlement’s defenses. Blingdenstone is currently suffering from a widespread ooze infestation. The party enters through its guarded approaches and is escorted through the reception hall and Traders’ Grotto to Diggermattock Hall. Glabbagool’s presence draws uneasy looks from residents who are not accustomed to friendly gelatinous cubes.

### Velkynvelve

A Drow outpost from which the party previously escaped. Their escape from Velkynvelve triggers Blingdenstone’s standing order that all Drow escapees be brought before Chief Dorbo Diggermattock. The party’s firsthand knowledge of the outpost and its Drow inhabitants may be valuable to the deep gnomes’ defensive planning.

### Reception Hall

An area within Blingdenstone through which the guards escort the party. It contains a cart, which the party jokingly mistakes for or compares to a pickup truck before calling it a “medieval pickup truck.”

### Traders' Grotto

A trading area within Blingdenstone through which the party passes on its way to Diggermattock Hall. Deep gnomes conduct trade and daily activities there. Residents notice Glabbagool accompanying the party, and some less adventurous Svirfneblin react nervously despite the party’s explanation that he is friendly.

### Diggermattock Hall

A well-lit cavern serving as Clan Diggermattock’s administrative and command center. Approximately two dozen Svirfneblin occupy the chamber. Some hurry about with messages and papers, while others gather around stone tables covered with maps and documents and converse in hushed, serious tones.

At the back of the hall stands a dais carved from the cavern rock. Two stone desks face one another atop it, occupied by Chief Dorbo Diggermattock and Quartermaster Senni Diggermattock as they consult with their advisors and each other. Here the party identifies itself as a group of adventurers seeking the Overbright and is asked to address Blingdenstone’s ooze infestation before receiving help.
