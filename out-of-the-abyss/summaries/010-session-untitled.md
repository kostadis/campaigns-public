# Chapter 010

Date: 2025-03-03

## Summary
The party continued their perilous journey across the Darklake. They had only an imperfect map left behind by their departed kuo-toa companion, Shuushar, who had gone off to pursue his fate. Sarith had warned them of the map's flaws, so the group had to rely on their own survival instincts. With no rangers or rogues in the party, the GM called for survival checks. The GM ruled that with so many companions aboard, the group would eventually find the food and water they needed. He added the slightly ominous warning that this could change if people started dying off, since the crew was effectively the party's dice pool.

Their progress was briefly interrupted when the boat lurched to a sudden halt, grounded on a submerged sandbar hidden beneath the black surface of the lake. The boat carried no oars or poles, and Thorin quoted *Apocalypse Now*: "Never get out of the boat." Daz had long been teased by his companions for carrying a water-shaping cantrip of seemingly little use. He finally had his moment, conjuring a surging wave from behind the boat that shoved it clean over the sandbar and back into open water. The whole crew cheered. Jimjar wryly admitted he should have placed a bet on whether Daz would ever use the trick for something practical. Back in open water, the GM called for the survival checks. Thorin proved himself a surprisingly capable navigator, guiding the vessel with an almost uncanny confidence through the dark waters. His roll was good enough to spare the party the next day's check as well. When Sarith asked whether he knew where he was going, Thorin claimed he was simply following the vibes.

The celebration was short-lived. As the boat drifted onward toward a field of rocks threaded with narrow navigable passages, the party spotted something unsettling. About fifty feet away sat a duergar keelboat, seemingly abandoned, still laden with cargo crates and fitted with oars. Buppido claimed never to have seen such a boat. Hemeth, the gray dwarf traveling with the group, identified it as a duergar keelboat and remarked that it was very odd that it was there with its cargo still inside. When Thorin asked whether the crates were big enough to hide people, Hemeth grimly reminded everyone that duergar can turn invisible, and promptly did so himself. He whispered that the invisibility could last half an hour or an hour.

At Zalthir's suggestion, Daz used the cantrip again to shower the area around the keelboat. Sure enough, the water revealed an invisible figure, who immediately plunged deeper into the lake to avoid detection. Buppido flopped flat onto the deck with a thud, believing himself invisible, and insisted that his inaction was all part of the divine plan. The GM later corrected his own narration: Buppido is a derro, not a duergar, so he had never turned invisible at all.

Combat erupted across the dark water as gray dwarf slavers materialized from the shadows. Jimjar explained their goal: the duergar made their living kidnapping travelers and selling them as slaves in the city of Gracklstugh. Thorin spent a superiority die on his initiative. Zalthir conjured a sphere of magical darkness and shifted it across the battlefield at will. Inside the void his own attacks canceled out the invisibility penalty, and he struck down enemies with flurries of blows.

Daz learned the hard way that Magic Missile needs a target he can see. He fell back on Scorching Ray aimed at a spot in the water, landing one of three rays for seven fire damage. Thorin waded into the water to engage in brutal melee, using feints and Hill Strikes to batter the gray dwarves.

The duergar grappled members of the crew, one of whom turned out to be the goblin Spiderbait, and began dragging them away. The party pressed hard enough that the slavers eventually abandoned their prisoners to save themselves. Gyrgum repeatedly coated the slavers in Tasha's Caustic Brew. He finished off one wounded duergar with it and left the fleeing ones burning with ongoing acid damage.

As the last duergar, enlarged and invisible, struggled to flee through the water, the party made a calculated decision. They needed a guide to Gracklstugh, and a living prisoner was worth more than a dead one. Thorin delivered a twelve-point feinting strike, and the GM ruled the enlarged slaver knocked unconscious rather than killed. As the magic faded, the duergar shrank back to his normal small size.

With the battle over, Buppido cheerfully offered his services as an expert interrogator with "many techniques." Sarith, not to be outdone, declared that as one of Lolth's own guards he too was an expert in questioning techniques. He then paused to allow that Daz's techniques had been most impressive of late. Spiderbait, rescued but deeply shaken, made it clear he would leave at the next opportunity. He quietly noted that his best friend was dead and he had been attacked by a demon lord.

The party turned to the spoils. The duergar keelboat was captured, giving the group a small armada, and the chest aboard yielded 1,100 gold pieces along with a remarkable magical alchemy jug. The jug holds a gallon, weighs 12 pounds full or empty, and can be set to produce a chosen liquid until dawn. The adventurers celebrated solving their water supply problem. They then turned to the merits of producing mayonnaise instead of the more tactical acid or poison, reasoning that it would make their weapons "more delicious." Daz was enthusiastic about fish with mayonnaise and a "fungus cake, mayonnaise, fungus cake" sandwich. The group also secured two casks of harvested fungi, ensuring they would not go hungry in the days ahead.

With a prisoner to interrogate, a new boat, and an inexhaustible supply of condiments, the party set their sights on Gracklstugh. The GM reminded them that the captive was, like many folks, more interested in profit than in being a hero. Whether they would arrive as visitors or as merchandise remained, for the moment, an open question.

Thorin admits he has "kind of got a thing for Eldeth."

### Duergar Slavers
Gray dwarf raiders capable of turning invisible and enlarging their physical size using innate magic. They ambushed the party on the Darklake, grappling crew members to sell as slaves in the city of Gracklstugh. They have AC 16 and disengaged to flee once the fight turned, abandoning their captives. One was captured alive for interrogation.

## Locations
### Darklake
A vast, dark subterranean waterway in the Underdark where the party is navigating by their kuo-toa boat and the captured keelboat. The area features treacherous submerged sandbars, rocky outcroppings threaded with narrow navigable passages, and shallow sections where combatants can wade. Wading through these sections counts as difficult terrain, limiting Thorin to 15 feet of movement. The party was ambushed here by duergar slavers.

## Items
### Duergar Keelboat
A sturdy subterranean vessel belonging to the gray dwarf slavers, found floating near rocks with its cargo still aboard. It served as the staging point for the ambush and was captured by the party after the skirmish, effectively becoming part of their small armada. It also contained oars, which the party noted would be useful, since their own boat had no oars or poles.

### Alchemy Jug
A heavy ceramic jug found in a chest aboard the captured duergar boat. It appears to hold a gallon of liquid and weighs 12 pounds whether full or empty. It has the magical ability to produce various liquids on command once per day, including fresh water, wine, oil, vinegar, honey, beer, mayonnaise, acid, and poison. The chosen liquid can be changed each day at dawn. The party celebrated solving their water supply problem and immediately debated producing mayonnaise for their mushroom rations. The GM ruled that mayonnaise would stay stable for two weeks.

### Zurkhwood Casks
Two casks made from zurkhwood, a mushroom whose wood is also used for construction. They were found on the duergar boat, filled with harvested fungi, and provide the party with a supply of food.

## Spells
### Shape Water
Daz employed this cantrip twice during the session.
- First, he created a wave from the back of the boat that dislodged the party's boat from a submerged sandbar.
- Later, at Zalthir's suggestion, he sprayed water up around the abandoned duergar keelboat. This revealed an invisible lurker, who dove deeper into the lake to avoid detection.

### Darkness
Zalthir created a 15-foot radius sphere of magical shadow that he could move at will up to 60 feet away at the start of each turn. He used it to blind duergar enemies while seeing freely within it himself, controlling the battlefield throughout the combat. The advantage it granted him canceled his disadvantage against invisible foes. The GM noted it could not be cast directly on a creature. The drawback was that Daz could not target enemies inside it with spells.

### Magic Missile
Daz launched glowing darts of magical force. Early in the fight the GM ruled he could not aim them at the invisible slavers, since the spell needs a target he can see. Once the duergar revealed themselves, his darts tore into one and later finished off another for 6 damage; that duergar shrank back to normal size as it fell into the water.

### Scorching Ray
Daz hurled three rays of fire at a duergar near his allies in the water, aiming at the square where he suspected it was. Two rays missed, but the third connected for 7 fire damage, badly burning the slaver and leaving it at 1 hit point.

### Tasha's Caustic Brew
Gyrgum repeatedly sprayed a stream of acid in a straight line, coating duergar enemies and causing ongoing corrosive damage (2d4) at the start of their turns. He used it multiple times throughout the combat, including to finish off a wounded duergar and to slow the escape of the last fleeing slaver. That slaver was left "doubly acidified" and took 8 acid damage.

### Toll the Dead
Gyrgum cast this against one of the duergar, who resisted it.

### Enlarge
An innate ability used by the duergar to double their size and increase their physical strength during the fight, making them more formidable opponents as they attempted to flee or continue fighting. It does not grant extra hit points. When the effect ended, a slain duergar shrank back to small size.

### Invisibility
The duergar used this innate ability to remain unseen while attempting to ambush the party and kidnap their companions. Attacking ends it. The last surviving duergar remained invisible while enlarged, making him a particularly difficult target until he was knocked unconscious. Hemeth also demonstrated the ability aboard the party's boat.
