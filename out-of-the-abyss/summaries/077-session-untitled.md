# Chapter 77

Date: 2026-11-16

## Summary
The party resumed planning at the entrance of a vast, dripping cavern choked with moss and fungal growth, its floor carpeted in spores. Immobile duergar stood throughout the cave, their bodies overtaken by the fungus that blanketed the surrounding terrain. The GM reminded the party that burning everything would anger both factions of their Emerald Enclave allies, while indiscriminately killing the spore servants could offend the naturalists. Gyrgum offered a simpler proposal: “Maybe we should just hire an exterminator.”

The crossing was only sixty feet, but difficult terrain and the party’s horse-sized lizard mounts complicated matters. Darkness might conceal them from sight, but the party doubted the spore servants relied primarily on vision. Rainaldus recommended a tight formation and a rush for the far side, with fireballs as an alternative. Before proceeding, Daz named his Shield Guardian Rook, after their nightly chess games. The construct tilted its head in apparent acknowledgment and hung a rook piece around its neck.

Daz then realized his attuned Stonespeaker Crystal could let him speak with the cavern’s plants. The GM confirmed that fungi counted for this encounter. Daz proposed asking them to clear the difficult terrain, withhold their spores, and keep the duergar away from the party. Zalthir offered to fly him into the center, where a thirty-foot radius could cover most of the crossing, though the group ultimately advanced in stages instead.

The plants revealed that previous travelers had passed through and remained: “Well, they came, and they died, and they stayed.” Investigation showed that the duergar had suffered bludgeoning and cutting injuries rather than dying from spores. Their killers were creatures the plants called the Keepers, described as having long claws and beaks. Rainaldus identified them as Hooked Horrors, and the plants indicated where they lurked.

Further examination revealed that the vegetation and duergar belonged to one enormous organism. Daz could not simply address the duergar as plants; instead, he spoke to the spores coating them and directed those spores to move their hosts. He established a clear path, suppressed spore releases, and arranged the affected servants into a defensive line. Additional crystal charges extended the safe area as the party advanced.

Zalthir flew overhead to provide reconnaissance and placed their allies in magical darkness. His wingbeats stirred spores, however, and he noticed the danger before crossing beyond the controlled area. Gyrgum considered using consecutive dashes to reach the exit, but stayed with the group rather than risk disturbing the uncontrolled growth. Thorin moved toward the front, positioning himself to cover the others as they passed.

The noise of mounts, rustling plants, and shuffling duergar alerted two Hooked Horrors. They dashed into the cleared terrain, benefiting from the same easy footing the party had created, but the servants blocked a direct approach. Zalthir struck one Horror three times and grappled it. Thorin attacked the same creature with Dawnbringer, then knocked it prone and frightened it. The commanded duergar attacked with war picks, mostly missing, though one blow dealt damage.

Gyrgum finished the wounded Horror with a third-level Guiding Bolt dealing thirty damage. Its body fell within the controlled zone without stirring a spore cloud. Daz advanced the protective area again, taking care to keep Rook inside it. The remaining Horror disengaged and fled. Although Thorin raised the possibility of pursuing it for treasure, the party chose to leave. They crossed without needlessly slaughtering the cavern’s inhabitants or destroying the fungal growth.

Afterward, the GM clarified that faction reactions depended on witnesses and how the party explained its actions, rather than a hidden numerical reputation score. Sigrun celebrated the crossing with memories of her adventuring days. Following a discussion about her portrayal, the GM clarified that she remained mentally sharp, capable of casting spells, and excited about the expedition, particularly the prospect of visiting Gravenhollow, despite her physical frailty.

At the first camp, Gyrgum joined Sigrun to survey tunnels and discuss maps. She praised their lizards as an improvement over walking, especially for carrying supplies, and produced rope when he asked about maintaining scale. Her notes openly included observations for Lord Eravien and the Alliance. She showed Gyrgum what she had written and invited him to contribute something memorable. His account of the day’s combat earned an accurate but unflattering assessment: little memorable conversation, an excellent description of the battle.

At the following camp, Octavia interrupted Zalthir’s meditation to inspect a gauntlet. She promised care while admitting that care was not her usual habit. A close look suggested an intense fascination with gadgets rather than an intention to damage it. Zalthir handed one over, and she spent an hour examining its construction and naming its rivets while Glabbagool watched with four eye stalks. Zalthir claimed Glabbagool had been naming the rivets already, prompting the ooze’s alarm at this previously unknown responsibility. Octavia returned the glove, wandered away, and later searched her pack convinced she had put it there.

On the next night, Rook set up their chessboard, using the rook piece worn around its neck. Octavia noticed Daz’s control amulet and asked what it controlled, whether it had a manual, and whether she could examine it. Daz allowed this while keeping half an eye on her and continuing the game. Rook won, bringing their recorded score to one game each. Octavia set the amulet down and forgot it, but Daz recovered it before she later asked whether he still had it.

The party continued through the Underdark and reached a rope bridge. The session ended before they attempted the crossing.

## Locations
### Spore-Infested Cavern
A large cavern with a thirty-foot ceiling, heavily infested with fungal spores and moss, creating difficult terrain. It is inhabited by immobile duergar spore servants and predatory Hooked Horrors. The duergar and the surrounding plant life are part of a single massive organism.

The party needs to cross approximately sixty feet. Daz establishes successive thirty-foot-radius areas of cleared terrain and suppressed spores, using the servants as a defensive screen. The cleared ground also benefits approaching predators. Wingbeats and falling bodies are potential sources of spore disturbance outside the controlled area.

### Underdark Camp
Successive camps within the Underdark tunnels. On the night after the fungal cavern, Gyrgum maps with Sigrun; the next night Octavia examines Zalthir’s gauntlet; on the following night she examines Daz’s amulet while he plays chess with Rook.

Travel between camps is quiet and marked by dripping tunnels and the companions’ recurring note-taking. These stops provide opportunities for conversation and inspiration awards. The party reaches the rope bridge after traveling on again.

### Rope Bridge
A precarious crossing reached by the party at the end of their latest day of travel, presenting a new challenge for the next session. The party has not yet attempted to cross it.

An ally traveling with the party who suggests a tight formation to cross the cavern or using fire to clear the path. He recognizes the description of the Keepers as Hooked Horrors and provides tactical information about their blindsight.

An elderly former adventurer traveling with the party. She meticulously records observations for the Alliance and Lord Eravien, openly showing Gyrgum her notes and inviting a contribution. Her transparency leaves room for the party to debate whether this is spying or diligent reporting.

She recognizes Speak with Plants, recalls a similar use from her past, and offers advice during Daz’s preparations. She enjoys cartography and is excited to visit the great library of Gravenhollow. Although physically frail and unable to participate in combat as she once did, she remains mentally sharp and capable of casting spells.

She welcomes improvements in adventuring equipment: she likes the lizards and wishes she could have afforded them during her earlier travels.

### Octavia
A gnome who appears deceptively young but is noted to be dangerous if provoked. She has an intense, almost manic fascination with gadgets and magical devices.

She spends an hour examining Zalthir’s gauntlet, naming rivets and studying its construction. Zalthir’s careful assessment supports curiosity about the device. She returns it, then later becomes convinced she put it in her pack.

Her interest shifts to Daz’s control amulet, including whether it has a manual. She sets it down, forgets it, and later worries she has lost it. Her fascination is paired with easy distraction and forgetfulness.

### Duergar Spore Servants
Duergar killed by Hooked Horrors and subsequently overtaken by fungal spores. Their bodies bear bludgeoning and cutting injuries. They form part of one massive organism with the local flora.

Daz directs the spores covering the servants rather than commanding the duergar directly. Those within his effective area form a defensive line and attack the Horrors. They move slowly, understand simple instructions, and use war picks, but lack their former magical abilities and cannot enlarge themselves. Most attacks miss, though one deals six damage. Unengaged servants continue ignoring the party.

### Hooked Horrors
Large, vicious subterranean predators with long claws and beaks, referred to by the fungal life as the Keepers. They possess blindsight and killed the duergar in the cave. They are not undead.

Two approach in response to the noise of the crossing. One is grappled by Zalthir, struck and disabled by Thorin, and slain by Gyrgum’s Guiding Bolt. The other disengages and flees after being surrounded. The party leaves its lair unexplored.

### Glabbagool
An intelligent gelatinous ooze traveling with the party. It raises four eye stalks to watch Octavia inspect Zalthir’s gauntlet.

Zalthir claims it has been naming the gauntlet’s rivets. Glabbagool is horrified to discover this supposed assignment and protests that it was never told to do so.

Cast by Daz through his Stonespeaker Crystal. The GM confirms that fungi qualify for this encounter.
