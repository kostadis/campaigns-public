# Chapter 46 Zalthir's Daring Grapple

Date: 2026-02-02

Thorin's player was absent; the GM ran Thorin.

## Summary
Chief Dorbo Diggermattock stood before the assembled forces and gave an impassioned speech: "Today is the day. Today we will triumph. Today is victory." He pointed to Daz as the one who would lead them to victory. Then he pulled out a map of the whole area. He had apparently owned it for a long time and never shown it to the party.

The chief laid out a battle plan. The Galeb Duhr's area had been cleared, so the party could break through the wall there and enter the Pudding King's court from the south. Meanwhile, Dorbo's forces and the clans would attack from another direction to draw off the pudding court. He offered three groups of allies:
- an elemental imbued with Entémoch's power
- wererats led by Chipgrin
- the Pickshine Miners under their enthusiastic overseer, Dasco Pickshine

The GM described Dasco as the mines' "VP of sales," who had decided to be heroic because he saw a chance to "increase gross margins." The GM also explained the mechanics out of character. The party could fight, weasel, or talk their way through each encounter between the hall and the court, or spend an ally to get past it. They would reach the final battle either with allies and weakened, without allies and fresh, or somewhere in between.

The party questioned whether troops really needed to go into the ooze-infested purple area (room 28). Zalthir pointed out that the old plan was to snatch the Pudding King. Once he was gone, the slimes should revert to ordinary wandering oozes. Senni Diggermattock raised two concerns:
- If the oozes weren't distracted, they could pour south and swamp the party with "an almost infinite supply."
- If the party failed, the town might only find out when a mass of oozes burst through.

Dorbo was profit-motivated and cared about the mines outside town. Senni cared about the people and the community they had built. She also worried that her reforms would collapse if the party died. Reactions to the idea of a big battle were mixed:
- Some higher-ranking officers were disappointed.
- The ghosts had hoped for a big historic battle.
- The more gung-ho militia were bummed.
- Many others were quietly relieved.

When Zalthir joked about sending the ghosts because "they're not going to die," a ghost replied: "There will be no picking on the unalived." The party settled on a minimal distraction, a probing feint, instead of a full assault. Daz told the chief: "we have a plan that requires us to be discreet and careful."

Dasco Pickshine took every chance to promote his brand. He promised "branded tea." It turned out he doesn't mine tea; he buys it and packages it, because "we believe in making sure that we create a holistic brand around our mining operation." Gyrgum summed it up: "It's a branding exercise. It's not a digging exercise." Zalthir asked for stickers. Dasco didn't know what a sticker was, but he produced a patch. He took out needle and thread, and Zalthir helped him sew it on while bending down to the three-foot-tall gnome. Everything Dasco wore carried the Pickshine logo: Pickshine pants, Pickshine shoes, nothing but swag.

The party set terms with their allies and chose a reconnaissance-first approach. Gyrgum asked whether any ally had good stealth. The wererats were offended that anyone might think them noisy: "we are wererats." They assumed the party meant the Earth Elemental and the miners. Gyrgum quipped that they were "probably quieter than werebears."
- The allies said they were there to help, not to lead. The party would scout ahead and fetch them if needed. Otherwise they would "sit back here and enjoy a cup of tea," with the miners supplying snacks.
- The allies would wait at area 22. Dorbo's forces took position next to the Traders' Grotto, and the wererats in the pudding warrens.
- The distraction would start only on the party's signal, carried either by a wererat runner or a pass from Daz's bat.
- Jimjar seemed "curiously happy" with Zalthir.

A wererat scout slipped through the rubble and returned with bad news. There was enough space to get through, which meant the oozes could come through the other way too. Ahead, a 20-foot stretch of passage had been hollowed into a pit that looked like still dark water but rippled. It was "a massive, semi-sentient basin of black pudding waiting like a stomach for anything to fall in," under a slick ceiling dripping acid. Daz noted it was like Glabbagool's evil cousin. The pit ran wall to wall, so there was no way around.

Dasco warned that it was "a dissolved trap if I ever saw one." He then offered to bridge it with mithral braces and salt neutralizers. The catch was that his miners would have to stay behind to mine the anchors and keep the acid off the supports. Daz accepted. When Dasco asked to call him "general," Daz answered, "You can call me Daz." Dasco cried, "boys, let's show them what the Pickshine miners can do." The gnomes built the span in about twenty minutes, singing hi-ho songs under their anti-acid canopies.

While the bridge went up, Jimjar bet five gold that Thorin couldn't clear the gap with his pack on. Gyrgum's persuasion roll was an 11. His insight check came to 20, and it showed him that Jimjar never said how Thorin had to cross. Gyrgum told Thorin: "well, look how easy it is. There's nothing to it at all." Thorin's wisdom and sense of self-preservation were low, but Dawnbringer thought ending up in a pool of acid was a terrible idea. After a short argument, Thorin agreed that walking across was just as fun.

Once the bridge was done, Gyrgum told the wererat scout to keep scouting, then caught himself: Daz was meant to give the orders. Chipgrin insisted on a clear chain of command, and Daz named Gyrgum and Zalthir (and Thorin) as lieutenants who could also give orders.

The wererat scout went further and didn't come back. After about ten minutes, while Daz was sending his bat ahead, Chipgrin went after his own people without waiting for permission. The scout was frozen in terror. The tunnel's polished, translucent salt walls had softened into sheets of gelatinous slime that worked like dark, weeping mirrors, and wet, rhythmic clicking echoed from the ceiling. Chipgrin reported: "There's some big brain slime up there singing a song that turns a man's mind into puddle."

He offered a trade. His pack would flood the rafters and howl at a frequency to drown out the "psychic filth." That would draw every ooze in the vents onto the wererats and leave the party with "no eyes in the dark and no one to watch your backs." The party took the offer. Chipgrin then bet ten gold that Daz's reflection was better looking than the real thing, daring him to look at his psychic reflection. Daz refused: "I'm not playing parlor games." Chipgrin replied that this "explains why this man should be the general."

Daz's bat found a grand vaulted corridor blocked by a colossal ochre jelly. It filled the hall from floor to ceiling and pulsed in time with the Pudding King's heartbeat. The bat dodged pseudopods "thick as trees." The throne room passage was open behind it and visible through the jelly.

The party debated options:
- Glabbagool said "you're not going to like any of them," then offered to carry them inside himself. They would take acid burns and oxygen deprivation, but the jelly wouldn't bother attacking him.
- Gyrgum asked about blasting the jelly with fireball. The GM said it was their choice whether the Earth Elemental helped with the fight or with the passage.
- The Earth Elemental tapped Daz. It was immune to the acid and strong enough to smash straight through.
- Zalthir planned to teleport past.
- The GM warned that the party didn't know all of the Pudding King's abilities, or Glabbagool's, and that the module held surprises.

Green slime patches on the throne-room ceiling would drop on anyone who stopped beneath them.

Asked whether the Pudding King could control him, Glabbagool promised "I would never hurt my friends." He admitted he had once heard a voice calling him at the big pool of slimes. He also promised to fight any attempt by the Pudding King to hurt them, calling the King "a mindless pudding master." The GM made clear that asking Glabbagool to attack the King directly was untested. The community's view was that the Pudding King had allied with a demon to kill everyone and convert their biomass into ooze biomass. Zalthir said he'd be "kind of interested" if the King could actually turn them into oozes. Glabbagool called Daz "the general." Daz replied, "I like just being Daz," and Glabbagool asked, "What's a general?"

The Pudding King, standing and furious, ignored the party. He monologued to his pets, Princess Ebonmire (the black pudding) and Prince Livid (the gray one), and promised that Blingdenstone would become "Blingdenpuddle." Gyrgum's answer: "Do not gloat. We still plot." The signal went out and the distraction force attacked. The King cheered as he described members of the Blingdenstone army being consumed by oozes, and coached his troops: "Ooze them slowly."

The GM checked the rules for the throw. The Earth Elemental could throw Zalthir up to 30 feet; landing called for a DC 15 Dexterity save or 1d6 bludgeoning per 10 feet. Thorin declared there would be no dwarf tossing. Zalthir activated his tentacles, and the Earth Elemental broke through and hurled him into the throne room. Zalthir's first attack roll was very bad. His next attack hit, and he grappled the King with his tentacles from reach.

The Pudding King turned into ooze form. Zalthir found online that oozes can be grappled rules-as-written, and the GM agreed. The GM ruled that the cost was the ooze automatically hitting its grappler. Zalthir dragged the King 15 feet south, away from his allies, and the GM awarded him inspiration for the tactic. The session ended there. Gyrgum had rolled a 21 for initiative but had a connection problem and never got into the order; he will act before the Pudding King next time.

He produces a map of the whole area that he had apparently held all along.

### Senni Diggermattock
A people-focused gnomish leader deeply concerned about the safety of Blingdenstone's civilian areas and the community she has worked to build. She worries that without a diversion, the party's failure could result in the town being overrun, and that her reforms might fall apart if the party dies. She cares about the party members. She also warns that undistracted oozes could swamp the party with "an almost infinite supply."

### Chipgrin
The leader of the wererats who offers his clan's assistance in scouting and creating diversions. He is protective of his people, takes offense at suggestions that wererats would be noisy, and shows initiative by investigating when one of his scouts goes missing. He offers the party a choice: his pack howls them through the psychic slime corridor (“the quiet path”), or the wererats stay with them for the door. He insists on "a clear chain of command," dares Daz to look at his psychic reflection, and approves when Daz refuses.

### Dasco Pickshine
The overseer of the Pickshine Mines is obsessed with branding and seeing heroic opportunities to increase gross margins. He offers branded tea and personally sews a Dasco Pickshine patch onto Zalthir's clothes. All of his clothing and equipment bears the Dasco Pickshine logo. His miners bridge the black pudding pit using mithral braces and salt neutralizers, but must stay behind to maintain it. He does not know what a sticker is and doesn't mine tea; he buys and packages it. He asks to call Daz "general" and is told to call him Daz.

### Jimjar
A friendly NPC who makes lighthearted bets, including one about Thorin's ability to cross the black pudding pit ("I'll bet five gold Thorin can't clear that gap with his pack on. Any takers?"). He seems "curiously happy" with Zalthir.

### Glabbagool
An intelligent gelatinous cube who expresses strong loyalty to the party and promises to never hurt his friends. He recalls a past instance of hearing a voice calling him from a large pool of slimes, suggesting potential vulnerability to the Pudding King's influence. He offers to help the party pass through the ochre jelly by having them get inside him, though this would cause acid burns and oxygen deprivation. He asks what a general is and sounds increasingly like Mickey Mouse. He shapes his slime into a sad face and dismisses the King as "a mindless pudding master."

### Pudding King
A gnomish figure possessed by the demon lord Juiblex, who seeks to convert Blingdenstone's biomass into ooze biomass. He monologues to his two ooze henchmen about the impending defeat of Blingdenstone and his conflict with Zuggtmoy, whom he calls “the queen of rot,” over the kingdom. He turned into an ooze form when Zalthir grappled him; the GM ruled that in that form he automatically hits anyone grappling him. He pulses with a rhythmic heartbeat that the ochre jelly barricade matches. He promises to turn Blingdenstone into "Blingdenpuddle" and instructs his oozes to "Ooze them slowly."

### Princess Ebonmire
A black pudding that serves as one of the Pudding King's two ooze henchmen, whom he refers to as a pet.

### Prince Livid
A gray ooze that serves as one of the Pudding King's two ooze henchmen, whom he refers to as a pet.

### Thorin
A dwarf party member who initially considers jumping across the black pudding pit but is persuaded by Gyrgum and his sentient sword Dawnbringer to simply walk across the bridge instead. He declares there will be no dwarf tossing when the party discusses throwing strategies. Daz names him among those who can give orders.

### Dawnbringer
Thorin's sentient sword who argues against jumping into the acid pit, helping to convince Thorin to take the safer route across the bridge.

### Blingdenstone ghosts
Undead among the town's forces. They were looking forward to a big historic battle and are let down by the minimal-distraction plan. One objects to Zalthir's joke: "There will be no picking on the unalived."

### Wererat scout
One of Chipgrin's clan. He slips through the rubble and describes the black pudding pit, then goes further ahead and is caught, terrified and frozen, in the psychic slime corridor.

### Earth Elemental
The elemental imbued with Entémoch, offered by Dorbo. It taps Daz to propose smashing through the ochre jelly, is immune to its acid, and throws Zalthir into the throne room.

## Locations
### Diggermattock Hall
The location where the party receives Chief Dorbo Diggermattock's battle plan briefing before the assault on the Pudding King.

### Blingdenstone
The gnomish settlement under threat from the Pudding King's forces, with civilian areas that Senni Diggermattock is concerned about protecting.

### Area 22 (Steadfast Stone)
The staging area where the party's allies (wererats, Earth Elemental, and Pickshine Miners) wait in reserve, accessible through rubble that provides passage for both the party and potentially the oozes.

### Area 28 (Purple Area)
A heavily ooze-infested area where the bulk of the Pudding King's forces are concentrated, seething out of a purple gooey mass. The target location for the main distraction force. The GM notes that if the party goes through the middle, all the oozes will attack.

### Black Pudding Pit Passageway
A 20-foot stretch of passageway hollowed out into a massive, semi-sentient basin of black pudding, with a slick, acid-dripping ceiling. The Pickshine Miners bridge this obstacle with mithral braces and salt neutralizers. The pit spans the passage completely, so there is no way around.

### House of Horrors Corridor
A tunnel where polished salt walls have softened into sheets of gelatinous slime acting as dark, weeping mirrors with psychic effects. A 'brain slime' in the ceiling emits a psychic song that affects the minds of those who pass through. Wet, rhythmic clicking echoes from above.

### Area 30 (Pudding King's Court)
A grand vaulted corridor of ancient stone leading to the Pudding King's throne room. The entrance is blocked by a colossal ochre jelly that pulses with the Pudding King's heartbeat. The ceiling is covered with patches of green slime that can drop on intruders. The Pudding King holds court here with his two ooze henchmen, Princess Ebonmire and Prince Livid. The passage behind the jelly is open, and the King sits far back from the entrance.

### Traders' Grotto
The location where Chief Diggermattock's forces are stationed, ready to create a diversion as part of the battle plan.
