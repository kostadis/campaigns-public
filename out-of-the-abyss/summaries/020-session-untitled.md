# Chapter 020

Date: 2025-06-02

## Summary
Deep within the Underdark, the party found themselves locked in a desperate struggle to protect a mysterious and coveted egg hidden within a dark cavern. The Derro cultists, their minds twisted beyond reason by the manipulations of sinister forces, swarmed the chamber with fanatical purpose. From the shadows, hidden spies loosed crossbow bolts at the party's allies, one bolt striking Eldeth with brutal force and dropping her to the edge of death. As if the ambush were not enough, a beholder-kin creature known as a spectator materialized from the darkness, its many eyes gleaming with malice as it joined the assault.

Chaos reigned across the battlefield as the magical darkness that blanketed the chamber turned the fight into a fumbling, frantic melee. Daz conjured a massive hand of compacted earth that surged up from the ground and seized the spectator, pinning it in place and buying the party precious moments. Zalthir, gifted with the ability to perceive through the supernatural darkness, moved with deadly precision, striking at enemies that could not even see him coming. Meanwhile, Gyrgum called upon his ghostly ally to defend the egg, and Zalthir unleashed a terrifying breath that swept across the chamber in a wide cone, killing the fanatical Derro leader Plinki outright and leaving the survivors blinded and reeling.

With Plinki dead, the battle shifted, but the danger was far from over. A skilled derro Master Thief proved to be a tenacious foe, shrugging off blow after blow as Zalthir repeatedly grappled and wrestled him across the battlefield. Thorin planted himself like an immovable wall, and when the Master Thief attempted to slip away, Thorin's sentinel instincts snapped into action, landing a punishing strike that rooted the thief in place. Daz, ever calculating, had already used a burst of teleportation magic to vanish from the fray, secretly concealing the egg beneath his robes as the Derro cultists scrambled blindly in the darkness, screaming about their missing prize.

The crazed Derro grew more frantic with every passing moment, shrieking that whoever held the egg held absolute power over them all. One cultist howled for the sacrifice of Gyrgum, the cleric of Bahamut, demanding his heart be cut out and fed to some dark entity. The ghost responded to this outburst with a voice that chilled the air, and the cultists faltered. Gyrgum unleashed streams of corrosive acid that coated the fumbling Derro and ate away at them with each passing moment; knocked to the ground more than once by the swarming attackers, he finally pulled back from the press and used restorative magic to mend his wounds.

As the battle neared its end, Zalthir delivered the killing blow to the Master Thief, finally ending the rogue's stubborn resistance, then struck a Derro warrior and pushed him off a ten-foot ledge, sending him crashing to his death below in a moment that felt ripped from legend. When Daz cried out for help as the last remaining spy cornered him, the ghost ally swept across the cavern and plunged into the spy's body, possessing him entirely and causing his eyes to glow with an eerie inner light. Thorin cut down the last Derro, who never realised the egg had long since been spirited away beneath Daz's robes, and the party stood victorious, the stolen red dragon egg safely in their possession.

Out of game: Daz's player was absent this session, so the GM ran Daz — weighing, in the GM's words, whether "should he let them die? That's what the drow in him says he should do." The session ended the moment the last Derro fell ("You have now are in possession of the egg, and we are out of time"), with play paused until after the 13th while the GM travels to Greece.

## NPCs
### Plinki
A Derro combatant who was determined to kill Gyrgum and claim the egg. She attacked Gyrgum with disadvantage due to the surrounding darkness and was ultimately slain by Zalthir's breath attack. At the session's start she was held in Zalthir's grapple, and the derro under her had fixed on killing Gyrgum and dropping his heart over the egg.

### Derro Spies
Hidden attackers who used crossbows to ambush the party from the shadows. One severely wounded Eldeth with a crossbow bolt, while the other missed Daz. One spy, freaked out by the ghost, decided to run away. A spy (the tape does not say whether it was the same one) was later restrained by Daz's earthen hand, took 8 damage, broke free, moved to attack Daz, and was possessed by the party's ghost ally; the possessed spy is a derro with the spy's abilities.

### Spectator
A beholder-kin creature that appeared suddenly to join the fight against the party. It was restrained by Daz's earthen magic but eventually broke free. It took 16 from the earthen hand and 10 from a helper, escaped on a lucky Strength roll of 17, and was left at one hit point by the party helpers. Its fate after that is not stated on the tape.

### Eldeth
A companion of the party who was badly wounded by a Derro spy's crossbow bolt during the initial ambush, dropping to four hit points. She hung back during the most intense moments to avoid being overwhelmed ("I don't want to get killed").

### Uskvil
A skilled derro rogue (Uskvil, per the GM's ruling; the name is not said at the table) who fought fiercely against the party in the darkness. He was repeatedly grappled by Zalthir, locked down by Thorin's sentinel ability, and ultimately slain by Zalthir. The GM framed him as roughly a fifth-level rogue; he was twice dropped off the ledge, still had about 30 hit points mid-fight, and repeatedly attacked the ghost.

### Pelek
Pelek's ghost, a spectral ally bound to the party via his bones. Its first attempt to possess the Master Thief failed on his saving throw; it attacked enemies with a withering touch, was resistant to non-magical attacks, and ultimately flew across the battlefield to possess the body of the enemy spy. It acts on Gyrgum's turn, was blinded by the darkness like everyone else, dropped to 15 hit points, and answered the cultists' sacrifice threat in a voice Gyrgum's player called "scary."

### Jimjar
One of the party's companions who assisted in the fight by firing a short crossbow at enemies, though with limited effectiveness.

### Derro Cultists
Mad, fanatical underdark dwellers obsessed with the egg, believing that whoever possesses it holds absolute authority. They screamed about sacrificing the cleric of Bahamut and attacked the party with hooked spears and crossbows, though their coordination was hampered by the darkness. Per the GM, derro were once a slave race to the Mind Flayers, are predisposed to madness, and cooperate only "out of necessity and when riled up by a charismatic leader." Their hits could knock targets prone (DC 9 Strength save).

## Locations
### Obelisk Chamber
A cavern in the Whorlstone Tunnels, plunged into Zalthir's magical darkness, where the party fought to protect and secure a mysterious egg amidst a Derro ambush. The chamber features elevated ledges with steep drops that were used tactically during the skirmish. The egg sat at the top of a flight of stairs; a ten-foot ledge above a lower level let Zalthir drop enemies for falling damage, and enemies below could not reach characters ten feet above them.

## Items
### Missing Red Dragon Egg
A mysterious and highly coveted object that the Derro cultists believed granted leadership to whoever held it, screaming 'he who has the egg rules.' Gyrgum had been protecting it, Daz secretly snatched it up and concealed it beneath his robes using Misty Step to escape. The party knows it as the red dragon egg stolen from the Keepers of the Flame; the derro call it the egg of Demogorgon. Earlier, Thorin had asked, "does it have two yolks?" The cultists never learned it was gone; the session ended with the party in possession of it.

## Spells
### Maximilian's Earthen Grasp
Cast by Daz to summon a large hand of compacted soil that reached out to restrain the spectator, pinning it in place and dealing crushing damage. Daz used the spell again later in the session to attempt to restrain the spy, though the spy eventually broke free. First cast: 16 damage and restraint (the spectator later escaped on a Strength check). A second attempt failed. The final use restrained the spy for 8 damage before he broke out.

### Breath Weapon
Zalthir exhaled a 15-foot cone of energy that struck multiple enemies and delivered the killing blow to Plinki, also inflicting a blinded condition on surviving targets who failed their saving throws. DC 11 Dexterity save; the damage roll was low (others took one or two points), but it was enough to kill Plinki.

### Tasha's Caustic Brew
Cast by Gyrgum as a stream of acid projected in a line, coating multiple Derro and causing ongoing corrosive damage at the start of each affected creature's turn. Gyrgum used this spell on multiple occasions throughout the battle. It is 5 feet wide, which prompted a beam-vs-line rules discussion. First cast: DC 14 Dex save, one of three failed and took 7. Second cast: both targets failed and took 5 each. A target can spend its action to scrape off the acid.

### Magic Missile
Cast by Daz at a higher level, releasing multiple unerring darts of magical force that killed one Derro and wounded another nearby enemy. At second level it fired four darts; the wounded target took 9.

### Bless
A divine enchantment active on several party members, granting them an additional die to add to attack rolls and saving throws throughout the fight. The die is a d4 and applies to every attack roll, not just once. Gyrgum himself was not among those blessed.

### Darkness
Zalthir's magical shroud, which blinded most combatants in the egg chamber, leading to a confused and fumbling skirmish as the Derro searched for the egg. Zalthir could see through his own darkness, and Thorin fought inside it by blindsight. The GM ruled that neither those inside nor those outside can see into it. It blocked spells that require seeing a target, but not area-targeted spells, and left even the ghost attacking with disadvantage.

### Misty Step
Cast by Daz to instantly teleport himself to a safer position while concealing the egg beneath his robes, disappearing with a whoosh that left the Derro scrambling. GM: "it's not like he ran away. They hear a whoo."

### Healing Word
Cast by Gyrgum as a bonus action to mend some of his own wounds amidst the chaotic combat after being knocked prone. It healed 2d4; the GM confirmed it works on a creature you can see, which includes yourself.

### Possession
The party's ghost ally used its supernatural essence to inhabit and take control of the spy's body after flying across the battlefield in response to Daz's cry for help, incapacitating the spy and effectively adding him to the party's side. DC 13 Charisma save. An earlier attempt against the Master Thief failed, and the ability had to recharge. The possession lasts until the host dies or the ghost leaves, and the possessed spy's eyes periodically glow.
