# Chapter 008

Date: 2025-02-03

## Summary
The party was escorted to the Altar of the Deep Father near the docks of the Darklake, where the Archpriest Ploopploopeen presented them as offerings to the rival Deep Father faction. The Deep Father's followers were already preparing to sacrifice a bruised and bedraggled Duergar arms dealer named Hemeth. Bloppblippodd, archpriest of the Deep Father and Ploopploopeen's own daughter, accepted the offerings in the name of her god: "May their blood nourish and strengthen him." A burbling cheer rose from the surrounding Kuo-toa, fists raised in the air.

Hemeth begged the party to do something. When Gyrgum said they had been told they wouldn't have to do anything, Hemeth was horrified: "You're just going to show up here and die?" Gyrgum whispered that there was more going on than met the eye. Hemeth then asked to be told when to act, and promised that if they survived they should look him up in Gracklstugh. He explained that he was a neutral arms dealer who had been trying to close a deal with one of the two factions, and had simply picked the wrong one.

The ritual chanting grew louder as the Kuo-toa circled the altar, splashing through the shallows of the Darklake. Ploopploopeen's son Gloog, one of the whips, moved toward the altar. Bloppblippodd called for the sacrifices to be brought forward, and one Kuo-toan was assigned to prod each prisoner. The party was herded into a stone depression 20 feet from the altar, stained with the blood of innumerable sacrifices, with the lapping of the lake audible through a large grate.

Nobody had taken the party's weapons. Gyrgum's religion check (20) told him this was a conventional throat-slitting ritual, and that the Kuo-toa had not thought it through. It also told him that if Ploopploopeen chose to turn on them, the two archpriests could cast enough to make this "a very short adventure."

Just as Bloppblippodd cried "Bring me the knife!", Ploopploopeen struck his own daughter with his scepters. The settlement erupted into chaos. Kuo-toa loyal to the Sea Mother surged forward to attack. The guards escorting the party stood frozen in shock, surprised and unable to act. Father and daughter screamed curses at one another, trading spells and sword strikes before the profane altar as their followers tore into each other. Still armed and momentarily forgotten, the party seized the chance to disengage from the center of the conflict.

The GM marked the factions on the map. Purple dots were Ploopploopeen's side. Pink dots were Bloppblippodd's side. A second dot marked a leader, and Bloppblippodd carried three. The GM also ruled that the melee Kuo-toa would ignore anyone who did not engage them.

Gyrgum dashed behind the friendly Kuo-toan lines, planning to heal their allies from a safe distance. Thorin warned that he would have nobody tanking for him there. Daz summed up the plan as letting everybody else fight, comparing it to Samwise finding the orcs had killed each other in *The Lord of the Rings*. Zalthir, run by the GM, conjured magical darkness. Thorin, "itching to swing a sword" and carrying no ranged weapon, moved behind the friendly lines to cover Daz and Zalthir.

The other prisoners followed the party's lead. Jimjar remarked mid-melee that he was surprised they were all still alive, but that it was still too soon to tell. Topsy and Turvy turned into rats and scurried frantically toward Bloppblippodd's lair in search of treasure. The Kuo-toan monitors and whips made it plain that the fighters could fight, but nobody was touching the boats.

The party weighed its options: steal a boat, or loot the enemy's lair for provisions first. They settled on the most isolated boat, moored off the northeastern jetty, which was guarded by only about four Kuo-toa while everyone else was caught up in the melee.

On the way, Gyrgum ducked into Ploopploopeen's hut. He objected to the word "stealing," since "he promised us stuff for helping, and we've helped." Inside he found:
- 500 copper
- 2,000 silver
- 150 gold
- 27 platinum
- a strand of matched pearls worth 1,000 gp
- two potions of healing
- a potion of water breathing
- a scroll of light

Daz led the party in a confident walk toward the northeastern boat, "like the reservoir dogs," with Zalthir and Thorin following, trying not to draw attention. Thorin glanced back and saw pools of blood blooming across the water where Kuo-toa had been standing moments before, the water churning around each crimson stain. Some of the victims had not even been part of the melee. Something beneath the surface was dragging the fish-folk down with terrifying speed. Gyrgum concluded that going into the water might not be the best idea, and then dashed 60 feet toward the boats.

Then Bloppblippodd cried out "Leemooggoogoon!" as her father struck a final fatal blow, and she dropped before the profane altar. For a moment it seemed the civil war was over. Then the dark surface of the lake bubbled and foamed, and thick oily tentacles burst forth. Two monstrous heads broke the water, resembling hideous, angry baboons with wickedly curved tusks, both joined to a single torso. The creature rose to 30 feet or more, water cascading from its shoulders, its red eyes burning with bloodlust and madness.

Demogorgon threw back its heads and roared, then came straight at the gathered Kuo-toa. The GM advised that "the most useful thing to be is anywhere but here." The party confirmed they were already running, and the session ended on the cliffhanger.

## NPCs
### Ploopploopeen
The Archpriest of the Sea Mother who orchestrated the scheme to present the party as offerings to the rival Deep Father faction. During the ritual he betrayed his own daughter Bloppblippodd, clubbing her with his scepters, and fought her with spells and blades until he struck her down. His hut held a sizable stash, which Gyrgum looted as payment owed. His son Gloog (Glooglugogg) serves as one of the whips. Moments after his victory Demogorgon rose from the lake, and his fate is unresolved at session end.

### Bloppblippodd
The archpriest of the Deep Father and daughter of Ploopploopeen. She accepted the party as sacrificial offerings ("May their blood nourish and strengthen him"), called for the sacrifices and the knife, and led the rival faction in the civil war. Mid-battle she ordered her followers to "keep the sacrifice." Her father ultimately struck her down before the altar. Her dying cry invoked Leemooggoogoon. Her lair lies near the altar, and Topsy and Turvy headed there in search of treasure.

### Hemeth
A bruised and bedraggled Duergar arms dealer held captive by the Kuo-toa and prepared for sacrifice. He was aghast that the party seemed willing to "show up here and die." He whispered to Gyrgum to tell him when to act and to look him up in Gracklstugh if they survived. He explained that he had tried to close a deal with one of the two factions and picked the wrong one, protesting that he was "just a neutral arms dealer."

### Jimjar
A deep gnome prisoner who kept wagering on the party's survival even in the face of ritual sacrifice, taking the pessimistic side of Daz's bet. Mid-melee he remarked that it was "still too soon to tell." He urged the group to head for the boats. He also noted the boats couldn't hold everyone, adding that there's "always the possibility to having fewer people left behind."

### Topsy and Turvy
Twin deep gnome prisoners who transformed into rats during the chaos and scurried toward Bloppblippodd's lair in search of treasure.

### Eldeth
A fellow prisoner who fled toward the boats alongside the party during the Kuo-toan civil war. Eldeth is the dwarf who moved up beside Gyrgum saying "we dwarfs should stick together."

### Demogorgon
A towering demon lord, thirty feet tall or more, with two baboon-like heads, wickedly curved tusks, oily tentacles, and red eyes burning with bloodlust and madness. It rose from the Darklake immediately after Bloppblippodd's death and came straight at the Kuo-toa, bringing chaos and destruction to their settlement.

## Locations
### Altar of the Deep Father
A ritual site near the docks of the Darklake. It features a stone depression 20 feet from the altar, stained with the blood of innumerable sacrifices, and a large grate through which the lapping of the lake can be heard. The Kuo-toa circle it in procession while chanting. It became the epicenter of a violent civil war between the Kuo-toan factions.

### Ploopploopeen's Hut
A simple dwelling belonging to the Kuo-toa archpriest, one of a cluster of huts on the way to the docks. During the chaos of the ritual the party found a stash of coins, pearls, potions, and a scroll here.

### Sloobludop Docks
A series of jetties and stone piers on the edge of the Darklake, with rafts and more seaworthy boats moored along them. Kuo-toan monitors and whips guard them closely, and made clear that nobody touches the boats. The most isolated boat, off the northeastern jetty, had only about four guards. The party made their desperate escape attempt here as Demogorgon rose from the water.

### Darklake
A vast underground body of water with no waves, whose shallows the Kuo-toans use for religious processions and rituals. During the session, sudden pools of blood and churning water, some where Kuo-toa had been swimming, revealed that something monstrous lurked beneath the surface.
