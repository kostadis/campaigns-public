# Chapter 43 Harvesting Horror in the Underdark

Date: 2026-01-12

## Summary
After defeating the deranged earth elementals, the party witnessed a white light shine from the ancient menhir, creating a powerful hallowing effect that cleansed the temple. The three Galeb Duhr, their rocky forms pulsing with satisfaction, acknowledged the party's success with a grave "well done." The first Galeb Duhr recognized Gyrgum as a cleric of Bahamut, and Gyrgum proclaimed this "the Realm of the Dragon." The Galeb Duhrs quickly corrected him that this was actually "the Realm of the Rock." The creatures explained that the party had successfully hallowed the temple from the corrupting power of Ogrémoch, the evil elemental earth god who had infested these rocks for centuries. They argued first about whether to explain who Ogrémoch was, because telling the party might be "insulting." When Daz admitted, "We could use a hint on who Ogremoch is," one of them said, "see, I told you that you should have told them."

Thorin could not resist making a pun: "I mean, we're in here. It's a cavern. Of course, it's hollow."

The Galeb Duhr found great amusement in his joke about the temple being both "hollow" and "hallowed," laughing that this ancient dwarf humor they hadn't heard in these caverns for ages. They questioned Thorin about his unusual choice to wear giant clothes, and he explained his complicated relationship with giants, having been raised by them despite being a dwarf. He called himself "a study in contrasts." However, the Galeb Duhr revealed that the party's work was not yet complete. They still needed to obtain Entémoch's boon, the blessing of the good elemental prince who had once aided the deep gnomes of Blingdenstone. The Galeb Duhr gave a long, rambling account of Entémoch's boons, which the GM compared to a "grandpa Simpson kind of 20-minute speech." From it the party gathered that the boon might help in the coming fight against the Pudding King. The creatures argued amongst themselves about where Entémoch might be found before finally agreeing that the gnomes would surely know, having visited him regularly in ages past. Having done a century's worth of talking, they sat down and stared at the menhir.

The party returned to Diggermattock Hall, where they were greeted warmly by the community. Gurnik Tapfinger, the cleric who had sent them on this quest, rewarded them with a precious empty fourth-level spell gem that could be imbued with powerful magic. The party discussed who should attune to this valuable item. Daz could only cast third-level spells so far, so he suggested Gyrgum take it for now and revisit the choice once Daz reached fourth level. The GM ruled that the gem can be loaded with a ritual cast, and Gyrgum loaded it with Mass Healing Word. Jimjar and Eldeth arrived with dramatic news of their own success. They had destroyed Vazuk, a deep gnome ghost mad with grief who had been threatening the residents. Jimjar proclaimed the battle epic and spectacular, the kind of combat that would inspire songs for centuries to come. Daz dryly suggested someone might write it down and sell it as a book, "perhaps even a bestseller."

Burrow Warden Jadger materialized as a friendly ghost, thanking the party for putting two tormented spirits to rest in the catacombs. As a reward for their service to Blingdenstone, he offered them two boons. They could ask him any two questions they wished, and he would answer truthfully. Thorin worried aloud that "we're going to make the wrong choices." Jadger explained that he would be waiting at the Ruby in the Rough temple, training the next generation of Burrow Wardens, whenever they were ready to claim their reward. Chief Dorbo Diggermattock expressed concern about the ongoing threat of acid attacks from the puddings, warning that if these creatures destroyed their weapons, their troops would die instantly. He proposed they reconvene the next day. The GM laid out the remaining paths:
- Find Entémoch's boon to gain an earth elemental ally. The temple cleansing was already done.
- Fetch ingredients from Neverlight Grove, though the alchemist Kazook Pickshine believed he had a simpler solution and was waiting on his "gopher," meaning his errand boy.

The next morning, an exhausted Nomi Pathshutter appeared in the crowded meeting hall, having been awake for forty-eight hours attempting to summon earth elementals. The hall had swelled to about 60 people now that Daz had been put in charge of everything and everyone wanted to be in the room. As Thorin put it, "I don't want to be in the loop, but I want to be aware of the loop." She spread a map on the table and explained with frustration that hallowing Ogrémoch's altar had not opened the path to Entémoch's boon as expected. The tunnels to the stone prince's throne had shifted and sealed themselves, and the earth seemed frozen, unable to heal. Miners were reporting strange vibrations, as if the rock itself was trying to speak but couldn't find the words. In an awkward moment, Nomi called Gyrgum an ogre and then glared at Daz for being a drow, leading to a tense exchange about respect. The community rallied to defend their allies, making it clear that outsiders could not insult members of their group.

When the party revealed they had dealt with four insane earth elementals and a Medusa, Nomi's attitude shifted to one of genuine respect and admiration. She even blamed everyone but herself for not telling her sooner. She tasked them with investigating the strange vibrations and finding the lost path to Entémoch's boon. Before they could depart, Kazook Pickshine, the local alchemist, arrived with troubling news. He gave off the vibe of a snake-oil salesman. He had recalled a legend about a garden of stone deep in the mines and had sent his scout Bimble to investigate. Bimble had found the rare Ghost Hold Lichen they needed for their alchemical preparations, which normally grows only in the Grove. Bimble had found it right here below the city. But he had returned wrong, infected and raving about a garden that sang to him and the Lady of Rot feeding the soil.

Gyrgum immediately recognized the reference to the Lady of Rot as Zuggtmoy, the demoness who had destroyed Neverlight Grove and nearly possessed Thorin in a previous encounter. Thorin backed up the religion check with advantage plus a Lucky reroll. The revelation sent waves of alarm through the assembled leaders of Blingdenstone. They now faced not only Juiblex and the Pudding King but also the corrupting influence of Zuggtmoy herself. Arguments broke out about priorities and past decisions until the party intervened, insisting that they focus on the tasks at hand. They decided to investigate the Festering Fissure where Bimble had found the lichen, choosing their destination based on the alliterative appeal of the name.

In Kazook's chambers, surrounded by bubbling beakers and mysterious potions, they found Bimble strapped to a table, coughing up spores that formed tiny laughing faces before dissolving into the air. The party cracked a joke, mocking the situation. Kazook was upset. He asked for compassion, explaining that Bimble was a dear friend, not just an assistant. Daz rolled a natural 20 on Investigation and talked Kazook into letting him use some beakers. He probed Bimble with questions about stone, depth, and water, and pieced through Bimble's ramblings, the smells clinging to him, and the textures of rock under his fingers. The party successfully pinpointed the exact location of the cave. They avoided random encounters and headed directly into the Underdark once more.

The Festering Fissure proved to be unlike anything else in Blingdenstone, humid, hot, and reeking of sweet vegetation rot. The party fashioned makeshift bandanas to protect themselves from spores before Daz summoned a bat familiar to scout ahead. The bat flew into a living nightmare where walls appeared to breathe, covered in pulsing white mold. Fungi had grown into disturbing shapes resembling deep gnome children, and these mushroom figures seemed to turn and stare as the bat passed. The bat passed a Constitution save on another natural 20. Deeper in the chamber, it discovered a shambling mound tending to a bizarre garden, violet fungi hybrids, and most importantly, the corpse of a giant from which Ghost Hold Lichen grew.

Thorin's knowledge of giants revealed that the lichen had to be cut carefully without bursting the poisonous sacs growing beside it. Gyrgum understood that a warding prayer would be needed to keep demonic spirits at bay during the harvest. The party formulated a plan where Zalthir would teleport in to cut the lichen while Gyrgum chanted the protective prayer. However, when Zalthir made his attempt, his cut was not perfectly clean. He ruptured one of the poisonous sacs, taking significant damage and alerting the shambling mound to their presence.

Just as Zalthir made his cut, he remembered his training as a monk in the gardens. The ancient monk, The Garden Shadow, had explained exactly how to cut lichen and tend the trees, and told him that this training would come in useful. But Zalthir had no intention to spend his time in contemplation of the wild; he would be out there, changing the world. As the poison took hold, he thought to himself: who would have thought?

Daz quickly cast a hypnotic pattern of shimmering colors that incapacitated the violet fungi and even caught Zalthir in its mesmerizing effect. The shambling mound barely resisted the enchantment. Gyrgum rushed forward and punched Zalthir in the face to break him from the trance. His first blow was so weak it dealt no damage at all, forcing him to draw a dagger and stab his companion to properly wake him. He then retreated, "a little afraid of Zalthir at this point." The shambling mound attacked Zalthir with crushing blows and wrapped him in a powerful grapple. Zalthir responded by grappling the creature in return, creating a deadlock that prevented the mound from engulfing him entirely.

Thorin charged into battle with Dawnbringer, his enchanted weapon striking true against the shambling mound multiple times. His attacks weakened the creature, causing it to fight at a disadvantage. Zalthir pummeled the grappled mound with a flurry of strikes while locked in their mutual embrace. As the combat continued in the eerie fungal garden, surrounded by mushroom children that seemed to watch with hollow eyes, the party fought to complete their mission and obtain the Ghost Hold Lichen they desperately needed. All the while, the corrupting influence of Zuggtmoy, the Lady of Rot, lingered in the very air around them. The session ended mid-combat, just after Thorin's Hill Strike.
