# Chapter 022

Date: 2025-06-30
Attendance: Zalthir's player was absent, so the GM ran Zalthir.

## Summary
The Ember Vanguard stopped in the dim, oppressive tunnels beneath Gracklstugh to tend their wounds before moving on. Gyrgum used Channel Divinity to heal himself by 14 hit points. He gave the remaining 6 to their badly wounded dwarf companion, Eldeth Feldrun, who had been down to 14 hit points, bringing them both to about 20. In Roll20, the GM relabelled the party as "the Ember Vanguard and Groupies." The group moved carefully through the huge cavern system beneath the Deepking's city and slowed at every turn in the winding passages.

Their path led to a vast chamber that reeked of death and decomposed flesh. The glow of faerzress drifted through it like fog. A lone derro cultist named Skiit stood on a ledge above a deep pit full of zombies. He muttered to himself and tossed body parts into the darkness below, complaining that he always got "the low end of the stick." Daz rolled a 20 on a religion check and recognized the symbols of the Cult of Demogorgon on him. Skiit was a low-ranking true believer, the kind who needs someone else to think for him. He was also insane, as many derro are, from the mind flayers' experiments and the demonic influence in the area.

Gyrgum stepped forward in the role of a visiting supervisor. He asked coolly about the "big project" and the work of Ulnara, and pressed Skiit to keep his zombies "on plan." For a while the ruse worked: Skiit was completely taken in and even asked permission to feed the party's goblin companion to the zombies.

The bluff collapsed when Gyrgum rolled a natural one. Skiit puffed himself up, boasted of Demogorgon and Plinki, and accused the party of spying for the duergar or the mind flayers. Before it could get worse, Gyrgum shoved the ranting cultist off the ledge into the pit. At the same moment, Daz used minor illusion to dim the area and fill the air with floating icons of Demogorgon, giving the party an aura of dark authority.

Skiit's hysterical screaming from the pit drew a derro savant named Ulnara out of the back of the chamber. A hulking zombie walked beside her. She demanded to know who these strangers were, and the party answered with baffling confidence. They invoked the name "Ember Vanguard" and offered to look up their employers in a "database," which led to a debate about inner and outer joins. The borderline-insane cultist was left momentarily speechless.

The GM hinted that a follower of Demogorgon would respond to gratuitous violence, not words. Daz took the hint. He cast Maximilian's Earthen Grasp on Skiit at the bottom of the pit and crushed him for 8 damage. That was the language Ulnara understood. She changed tone, called Skiit a disappointment to "your lordship," and promised to raise him as a zombie so he could serve the cult forever. When Skiit broke free and ran, Daz finished him with Toll the Dead. She thanked the party for the "excellent supplies" and asked them to tell Plinki what a wonderful ally she had been. Convinced they were powerful allies, she let them pass. The GM noted that if the party had entered from the other side, past a marked line on the map, the zombies would have attacked on sight.

The party took a short rest, which also let Zalthir reset his focus points. They then pushed through a set of large double doors, which they had first listened at and found cool to the touch, and came to a branching passage. To the east they looked into a chamber heavy with animal musk. Three enormous cave bears rested in cages of scrap iron. A spiral path marked by small stone pylons wound toward a tent and a large cauldron at the center. Two insane derro stood at the cauldron, each talking while ignoring the other.

- One grumbled about endless contributions for a "prince" who was "always hungry."
- The other recited a cryptic, almost poetic speech about a scale "smooth as polished obsidian" that "came from the deep, deep, deep," from a place where a voice sang of two heads. He then stopped, corrected himself, and started again.

The treasure in the tent was unknown, and the bears were a real risk, so the Ember Vanguard quietly withdrew and took the western path.

About 100 feet down that path, a derro patrol spotted them. The party tried a casual, confident stroll past, helped by the derros' wretched wisdom and a deception roll of 21. It ended when one of the guards asked whether they worshipped the dual-headed master. Gyrgum declared his devotion to Bahamut, and the derro attacked at once. The fight was fierce but decisive:

- Thorin won initiative with a 19 and hit hard with his longsword; his Sap mastery left his target at disadvantage.
- Zalthir moved his magical darkness over two of the enemies and scored a critical unarmed strike from inside it, leaving one at a single hit point.
- Daz split magic missiles across the patrol.
- Gyrgum hit them with Tasha's Caustic Brew.
- Thorin took up a Guardian stance and cut down a derro that tried to go after Zalthir.

Gyrgum parried a hooked spear while calmly debating theology with his attacker. He then cast True Strike and killed the last standing derro with a bone-crushing blow of his mace. Standing over the fallen patrol, he remarked with quiet arrogance that one head was indeed better than two.

Pressing on, the party reached another intersection and chose to explore a large chamber that smelled of brimstone and chemicals. Gyrgum, with a passive perception of 14, spotted a pit trap near the entrance just in time and warned the others. The group crept inside. The cavern was lit by the hellish red glow of faerzress swirling in spiral patterns on the walls. Dripping water made no echo, and thick fungi covered the floors and walls. A death dog lurked near a group of derro who had not noticed the intruders.

At the center of the chamber stood a five-foot natural platform. On it were a glowing ritual circle and several books with pages made from trillimac mushroom caps, laid out almost but not quite in a line and filled with mad scribblings. These were exactly the kind of physical evidence Errde Blackskull of the Stone Guard wanted in order to expose a demonic conspiracy within the Council of Savants.

The cult leader Narrak picked up one of the books. When a follower asked what they were doing, he declared that their experiments served the elevation of the derro, the triumph of Demogorgon, and the destruction of the duergar. He ordered his crowding followers to stand back, since none of them could read the book, and announced he had found "the spell we need." The Ember Vanguard watched from the shadows, knowing that the most dangerous part of their mission was only just beginning.

## Spells
### Channel Divinity
Gyrgum used his divine power to heal himself and a badly wounded dwarf companion so the party could continue in better condition. He restored 14 hit points to himself and put the remaining 6 into the dwarf, bringing both to about 20. Gyrgum called the feature "actually pretty useful."

Cast by Daz to conjure a hand of compacted earth that restrained and crushed the fallen derro Skiit at the bottom of the pit. He used it to show Ulnara the party's power.

### Toll the Dead
Cast by Daz as a cantrip, rather than a magic missile, to finish off the fleeing Skiit with necrotic damage after he broke free of the earthen grasp. Ulnara, already deferential, thanked the party.

Used by Zalthir to cover two derro in a zone of magical, absolute shadow during the patrol skirmish. It blinded and disoriented them and gave him cover for his attacks. It also stopped Daz from targeting those derro with magic missiles and meant attacks into it were made at disadvantage.

### Magic Missile
Cast several times by Daz at first level to fire unerring darts of magical force, striking and finishing off several derro in the patrol skirmish.
- **First casting:** he split three darts, killing a derro at 1 hit point and sending the rest into another.
- **Later casting:** he split two darts to one derro and one to another, dropping the second to 3 hit points.

### Tasha's Caustic Brew
Cast by Gyrgum to spray a line of corrosive acid across the battlefield during the derro patrol skirmish, coating several targets and burning them over time. Targets make a dexterity save. One derro saved and was unaffected; derro number four failed and took 2d4 acid at the start of each turn until it scraped the acid off. The GM ruled that area spells don't require seeing the target, so the line could reach into Zalthir's darkness.

### True Strike
Cast by Gyrgum to magically guide his mace so the strike would land against the last derro in the patrol skirmish. The attack hit with a 13, and the blow broke the derro's neck.
