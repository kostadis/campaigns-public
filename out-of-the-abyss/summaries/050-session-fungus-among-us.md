# Chapter 50 Fungus Among Us

Date: 2026-03-23

## Summary
Deep in the fungal cavern of the Underdark, Daz orchestrated a breathtaking act of deception. He commanded his spider familiar to scurry back toward him while weaving a dazzling display of magical light, making it appear as though a radiant form of the Spider Queen Lolth herself shrank into the spider and was absorbed into his body. At the same moment he dismissed the familiar, so the spider seemed to vanish into him. The rest of the party held their breath in the shadows. Glabbagool, the sentient grey ooze who was formerly a gelatinous cube, made a valiant but deeply unconvincing attempt to disguise himself as an inanimate rock. He kept starting to talk and had to be reminded that "Invisible Rock doesn't talk." His blinking eyes darted left and right and betrayed him entirely. Asha Vandree, the paranoid and fervent drow priestess, watched the spectacle with wide, crazed eyes and searched for signs of trickery.

Daz pressed the illusion further. He adopted the persona of a vessel possessed by Lolth's will, placed a hand at his throat and addressed the suspicious priestess as "Dear servant." Asha was not fully convinced of his divinity. She had seen too many charlatan mages claim divine possession, and the Deception rolls kept coming up short, including a string of fives that even a spent inspiration could not fix. Still, she was intrigued enough to listen. At one point she wondered aloud whether Daz had been infected by Zuggtmoy's spores or whether this was "another trick of Ilvara's." A sharp Insight read (20) told Daz that the claim of divinity was landing "like a fat lead balloon," but that Asha believed *he* believed he was communing with Lolth, and might see him as a useful ally.

Sensing the shifting tide, Daz wisely abandoned the claim of embodying Lolth outright. He presented himself instead as a powerful, perhaps spore-touched mage who had communed with the Spider Queen and now led a band of capable allies. When he beckoned the rest of the party forward, Asha's suspicion softened into something resembling relief: "perhaps my prayers have been answered through this… confused vessel." Later, while the party planned, Thorin suggested passing off the hapless Glabbagool as Daz's familiar. Asha was even more impressed with Daz for securing the services of an ooze familiar.

Asha wasted little time in laying out the grim situation. Ilvara, once a high priestess of Lolth, had been seduced by the heretical "bride" cult of Zuggtmoy after visiting the Neverlight Grove. She now carried a corrupted mushroom artifact that served as a conduit for Zuggtmoy's influence. She had turned most of her followers into mindless fungal servants and commanded a massive, pulsing Heart Fungus at the center of the cavern that could lash out with tendrils and belch clouds of poisonous spores. Asha herself had avoided infection by staying clean of fungal dust, never coming within 20 feet of Ilvara, and eating only her own food. That was also why she needed someone else to do the killing.

Asha made her terms clear. Kill Ilvara, destroy the mushroom, and the party could claim whatever treasures and magical items Ilvara had accumulated, even if they technically belonged to Asha's house. Her first offer was only "the gratitude of Lolth," which Daz waved off because he "already" felt it. The party slipped away to confer, weighing the risks of fungal infection against the promise of loot and survival. Daz noted they "can always stab Asha in the back later." They ultimately agreed that Ilvara had to die.

The group spent the next hour preparing carefully. Gyrgum inscribed a Glyph of Warding (explosive runes, cast at 3rd level to save his single 4th-level slot) on one of the bridges leading into the cavern. It was set to detonate against anyone approaching from the side held by Jorlan, a bitter, lovelorn drow warrior who was one of the few not yet consumed by the spores. The GM had pointed out that in the Underdark the party had no idea who else might be following them. Asha tested the party's tactical sense. When Daz and Zalthir pressed her on the spores' side effects, she assured them the Heart Fungus's spores caused only poisoning and pain, not the "bride" infection ("I believe"). She then promised to rush in and defend the party from any spore servants Ilvara might summon, and retreated to wait for the signal.

When combat finally erupted, Zalthir used Shadow Step to reach Ilvara and unleashed a furious flurry of blows for 31 damage, grappling her. He then attempted to haul her over the Heart Fungus and drop her into the area of Gyrgum's impending magical assault. Ilvara, however, used a legendary action to dissolve into a fungal patch and reappear elsewhere in the cavern, screaming in fury. The Heart Fungus retaliated by releasing a choking cloud of poisonous spores (DC 15 Constitution), which poisoned Zalthir. He flew 10 feet upward out of the haze, but stayed poisoned until his next turn.

The battle erupted in earnest across the cavern. Thorin taunted the bitter Jorlan into charging forward, directly into Gyrgum's waiting trap. The resulting explosion tore through Jorlan, several of the shambling sporewalkers Ilvara had summoned from the earth, and the Heart Fungus itself. It destroyed one sporewalker outright and left another at 3 hit points. Thorin then waded into melee with Jorlan and struck him with the blazing sun-blade Dawnbringer on a 20 for 14 damage, sapping him so his next attack would have disadvantage. A sporewalker climbed up and struck back at Thorin for 5 damage.

Gyrgum unleashed a devastating sphere of force energy, Dust of Suleiman, centered on the Heart Fungus and shook the massive creature to its roots. Daz then hurled a carefully shaped 4th-level fireball that engulfed both Ilvara and her precious Zuggtmoy mushroom. Ilvara partially shielded herself from the worst of the flames by saving with a 19 and took 12 damage. The mushroom smoldered after also taking 12, and the tide of the battle had unmistakably turned against the corrupted priestess. The session ended there, mid-combat.

## NPCs
### Asha Vandree
A drow priestess who has remained loyal to Lolth and opposes Ilvara's fungal 'bride' heresy. She is paranoid and somewhat unstable, "seeing paranoid shadows everywhere," but willing to use the party as tools to see Ilvara killed.

She has diligently avoided the fungal spores. She keeps off the dust, stays beyond 20 feet of Ilvara, and eats her own food. She provides tactical information about the heart fungus and Ilvara's spore servants, and tested the party's tactical judgment ("this was just a test").

She offered Ilvara's treasures, though they belonged to her house, in exchange for Ilvara's death and the mushroom's destruction. She is also exasperated by being stuck between Ilvara and the bitter Jorlan.

She holds drow-male mages in contempt but was impressed by Daz's apparent "servants" and ooze familiar. She accepted "boss," Zalthir's word for Daz, and concluded it must be a Dragonborn term.

### Ilvara
A former high priestess of Lolth who has been corrupted by Zuggtmoy's influence after visiting the Neverlight Grove. She now appears visibly fungal and still carries her cat-o'-nine-tails scourge. She carries a small magical mushroom artifact that serves as a conduit for Zuggtmoy's control, kept on the ground beside her.

She possesses the ability to meld into fungal patches and reappear elsewhere, and can command the heart fungus and summon reanimated spore servants. She uses legendary actions and has a high Dexterity. She took 31 damage from Zalthir and 12 from Daz's fireball this session.

### Jorlan
A drow warrior and former lover of Ilvara who harbors deep bitterness toward her following their breakup. He is one of the few drow in the cavern not yet turned into a spore servant.

He is lured into Gyrgum's magical trap during the battle and takes 21 damage. Thorin then hits him with Dawnbringer for 14 damage, which saps him.

### Glabbagool
A sentient grey ooze, formerly a gelatinous cube, who accompanies the party. He attempts to disguise himself as an inanimate rock but struggles to remain motionless and keep his blinking eyes still. He keeps wanting to talk and ask questions.

The party successfully passes him off as Daz's familiar to Asha Vandree, who is impressed by the notion of an ooze familiar.

### Sporewalkers
Reanimated drow guards, including some the party recognized as their former prison guards, converted into mindless fungal automatons by Ilvara. They are fungal, not undead, and "not very buff."

They move with a disturbing mix of zombie-like slowness and sudden drow grace, and are summoned from the ground by Ilvara during the battle. They attack with short swords at +4. One was destroyed by the glyph.

### Heart Fungus
A massive, supernatural fungal entity in the center of the cavern that serves as a weapon for Ilvara. It is capable of lashing out with tendrils and releasing toxic spore clouds that poison those within range (DC 15 Constitution).

It has a low Dexterity. Asha claims its spores do not carry the "bride" infection. It took 21 damage from the glyph and further force damage from Dust of Suleiman.

### Dawnbringer
A sentient sun-blade wielded by Thorin, used two-handed to strike Jorlan during the battle for 14 damage, applying sap.


## Locations
### The Fungal Altar
A dark, oppressive chamber in the Underdark dominated by a massive 'Heart Fungus' and filled with poisonous spores, fungal patches, shallow pools, and bridges. It serves as Ilvara's base of operations and is the site of the party's climactic assault.

- The pools are difficult terrain, 3 to 4 feet deep.
- The drop to the Heart Fungus floor is about 20 feet. It can be climbed safely, but being thrown off causes damage.
- Randomly erupting vents release blinding clouds on a 5 or 6 on a d6.
- Exits lead onward into the Underdark, where unknown pursuers may follow.

## Items
### Spider Familiar
Daz's familiar, used to facilitate a magical ruse by appearing to carry the blessing or essence of Lolth, scurrying back to Daz's hand as part of the illusion. It was dismissed at the climax so it seemed to vanish into Daz.

### Ilvara's Mushroom
A small, corrupted mushroom carried by Ilvara since her visit to the Neverlight Grove. It serves as a conduit for Zuggtmoy's control and must be destroyed to break Zuggtmoy's influence over Ilvara. If it is left intact, someone else could pick it up and restore that influence.

It is located near Ilvara and is targeted by Daz's fireball at the end of the session, taking 12 damage. Thorin and Gyrgum were warned to keep away from it.

### Light Crossbow
A ranged weapon Gyrgum lends to Thorin to provide him with better combat options during the ranged engagement against Ilvara. Gyrgum also has a limited supply of poisoned crossbow bolts.

## Spells
### Dancing Lights
Used by Daz to create a dazzling, shrinking light effect that mimicked the form of Lolth being absorbed into his spider familiar and then into his body, as part of the ruse to convince Asha Vandree.

### Glyph of Warding
A magical trap inscribed by Gyrgum on a bridge, set to trigger if anyone approached from Jorlan's side of the cavern. It was cast at 3rd level with the explosive-runes option.

It explodes in a 20-foot radius, and targets make a DEX save against DC 15, taking 21 damage or 10 on a success. It must be inscribed in person and takes an hour.

When Jorlan was lured into it, it detonated in a large explosion, dealing area-of-effect damage to Jorlan and several nearby sporewalkers.

### Dust of Suleiman
A powerful spell cast by Gyrgum that creates a twenty-foot sphere of damaging force energy. It has a 120-foot range, deals 20 damage per turn with no save, and lasts up to 10 minutes with concentration. It was centered on the big heart fungus, dealing significant damage to it.

### Fireball
A destructive evocation cast by Daz at a higher level (4th), carefully shaped to strike Ilvara and her fungal mushroom icon while sparing allies. The GM put the shaping at up to three chosen targets; by Daz's sheet, Sculpt Spells covers 1 + the spell's level (five at 4th level). It dealt 24 damage against a DEX DC 15 save. Ilvara partially resisted the blast, taking reduced damage (12).

### Phantasmal Killer
A fourth-level illusion spell in Gyrgum's arsenal, discussed during planning as an option to frighten and deal psychic damage to Ilvara, though ultimately not used this session. It has a 120-foot range, a Wisdom save at DC 15, and deals 4d10 psychic damage per failed save for up to a minute.

### Shadow Step
Zalthir's Warrior of Shadow feature, not a spell. Used to teleport and instantly close the distance to Ilvara at the start of the engagement, granting him an advantage on his initial strike. His darkness was placed behind the party during preparation so Ilvara would not notice it.

### Melt into Fungal Patch
A legendary action used by Ilvara to escape Zalthir's grapple by dissolving into a fungal patch and reforming in a different location, screaming in fury at herself.
