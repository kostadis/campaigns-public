# Chapter 47 The Fall of the Pudding King

Date: 2026-03-02

## Summary
The fight in the throne room resumed with Zalthir still grappling the Pudding King. In his ooze form the Pudding King was a sentient ooze who could still communicate through telepathy. To the party's horror, he had not just embraced his relationship to Juiblex, the demon lord of oozes, but had become one of Juiblex's minions. The DM noted that other parties might be freaked out by this, but given the party's friendship with Glabbagool, a talking ooze was "old hat." The Pudding King's telepathic voice screamed through their minds, declaring his intention to reduce all of gnome kind into nothing but ooze. The party quickly asked him to lower his mental volume. Gyrgum told him to "Turn it down just a notch," and the Pudding King complained that there was no point in allying with a demonic power if he couldn't scream at people. Calm and rational speech, he said, was for those who made deals with devils.

Despite the Pudding King's new form, Zalthir maintained his grapple, wrestling with the acidic creature as it oozed around him. The DM restated his earlier ruling that grappling an ooze meant being covered in it, "essentially swimming in the ooze," and that the ooze could still strike the creature grappling it. The party discussed using their Bag of Holding to contain the ooze. They realized that while they could temporarily shove him inside to deprive him of an action, a sentient ooze could simply ooze back out through the opening. While inside, he could use none of his magical abilities and give no commands. The throne room itself proved treacherous, with green slime hanging from the ceiling ready to drop on anyone who passed beneath it, and the Pudding King's lair actions giving him additional power within his domain.

The battle began in earnest as Daz launched magical missiles at the Pudding King. One die rolled a 1, and the volley did only six damage, taking the Pudding King from 85 to 79 hit points. Prince Livid, one of the ooze king's allies, retaliated with psychic blasts. Thorin carefully navigated around the green slime hazards, and Princess Ebonmire hurled blobs of acidic ooze at the party. The Pudding King then unleashed a devastating slime wave at Zalthir, who took acid damage but halved it with Stone Skin, and used his lair to drop green slime on Gyrgum from across the room, though Gyrgum brushed it off.

Recognizing that the Pudding King's lair was giving him too much advantage, Zalthir made a tactical decision that would prove crucial. He first checked with the party, and Thorin urged him to "bring the fight to us." Using his monk abilities to dash at incredible speed while maintaining his grapple, Zalthir dragged the struggling ooze out of the throne room entirely. In a bizarre moment, he pulled the Pudding King directly through Glabbagool, their gelatinous cube companion, which Thorin joked must tickle. The DM then reminded Zalthir that Glabbagool had been affected by demonic power the last time. "Maybe nothing happens. Maybe something does." Zalthir took damage from being inside Glabbagool's acidic body, but his magical protections helped him resist the worst of it.

As Zalthir passed through the body, he thought that Glabbagool should have the mental discipline to control his body and how the acidity affected anyone inside. He took a mental note to suggest that Glabbagool begin the training to become a monk.

As the battle continued, something strange occurred that threw the party into confusion. When Thorin struck what he thought was the Pudding King with his darts, a familiar voice cried out asking why they were hurting him. The party quickly realized that somehow, impossibly, Glabbagool and the Pudding King had swapped positions or bodies. The DM left open whether it might be "the Pudding King trying to pull a fast one." Thorin tested this by asking the gelatinous cube near him where they first met. In a voice that did not quite sound like the cheerful Glabbagool, it answered that they had met "in a cake," and Thorin concluded the two had swapped. The real Pudding King was now in Glabbagool's gelatinous-cube body, and Glabbagool was in the Pudding King's ooze body.

Once the confusion was sorted out, Thorin struck the real Pudding King with his radiant sword Dawnbringer, her holy light searing through the corrupted ooze, and the party coordinated a devastating assault. Daz channeled powerful magical energy into missiles that unerringly struck the Pudding King, while Gyrgum cast Inflict Wounds, dealing twenty-three points of necrotic damage. The Pudding King fought back desperately, unleashing more slime waves and trying to use his lair's green slime against them, but he had been separated from his throne room, and his power was waning. When he tried to slither under the hanging slime, Thorin's opportunity attack stopped him dead in his tracks. Meanwhile, Zalthir released the real Glabbagool, who sprouted two googly eyes on his new gray ooze body and oozed well away from the fighting.

Zalthir delivered the final assault, teleporting through shadows to close the distance and unleashing a flurry of strikes enhanced by eldritch energy channeling through his tentacles. Each blow caused the Pudding King's form to destabilize further, his stolen gelatinous-cube body unable to maintain cohesion under the relentless assault. With a final telepathic scream of "Behold the true feast, the soul's final blend!" the Pudding King dissolved completely, his consciousness dissipating as his physical form melted into inert puddles. The DM praised the tactic, saying "Nobody thinks to grapple the ooze."

With their leader defeated, Prince Livid and Princess Ebonmire fought on, but they stood no chance against the party's combined might. With the Pudding King gone, the green slime would now fall only on those who stood directly beneath it. The two oozes, being acidic themselves, were unharmed by the acid pools. Daz called down a massive explosion of fire that engulfed both ooze creatures, though Prince Livid's resistance to flames allowed him to survive with grievous injuries. Prince Livid tried his psychic blast on Daz, only for the wizard to shrug most of it off. The party quickly finished off both minions, with Princess Ebonmire falling to a combination of necrotic magic and physical strikes, while Thorin cut down Prince Livid with his second attack.

As the last enemy fell, the party felt a palpable shift in the atmosphere around them. The toxic, oppressive presence of Juiblex's demonic power began to recede from the stones and air, seeping away like water draining from a basin. However, they noticed that the fungi growing throughout the cavern were becoming more twisted and malevolent in appearance. This suggested that Zuggtmoy, the demon queen of fungi, was already moving to fill the power vacuum left by the Pudding King's defeat. A demonic voice echoed through the chamber, possibly Juiblex himself, declaring that he would be reborn and consume the banquet of the queen of fungi to grow even stronger.

Glabbagool, now an Ooze, seemed remarkably cheerful about the entire experience. He explained that being a sentient gelatinous cube had always been somewhat inconvenient: he moved slowly and people had to wait for him constantly. But now, having experienced being a smaller, more mobile ooze, he saw new possibilities. He suggested enthusiastically that the party could carry him in a jar or even in their Bag of Holding, allowing him to travel with them and see the world while serving as the ultimate garbage disposal. He assured them that, as a sentient ooze who could control his viscosity, his jar would not even need a lid. Gyrgum offered his Revivify spell, but Glabbagool, who was not dead, declined. The party discussed the logistics of this arrangement and even mentioned beginning his training as a monk, teaching him to control his form with precision. Glabbagool promptly demonstrated by sinking his eyes into his body and going perfectly still.

Searching the aftermath of the battle, the party discovered treasure that had been consumed by Princess Ebonmire: two spellbooks containing numerous magical formulas that would greatly expand their arcane capabilities. More importantly, they had achieved their primary objective, securing a way out of the Underdark. Though the demonic forces of the Abyss still churned in the depths below them, and though Zuggtmoy waited to claim what Juiblex had lost, the party had won a significant victory and earned their ticket to escape the endless darkness beneath the world. They also leveled up.

### The Bag of Holding Gambit
#### Zalthir considers using a Bag of Holding to contain the ooze form of the Pudding King, leading to a discussion of the item's mechanics.
- Zalthir proposes shoving the Pudding King into a Bag of Holding.
- The pitch: "We've got this guy who's now an ooze and therefore can be kind of squeezed and compressed and shoved into things."
- The party discusses the rules about living creatures in Bags of Holding, including suffocation and size constraints.
- The DM reads the rule: an action can force a creature in if it fits through the two-foot-wide mouth, the bag can be opened from the inside, and the creature suffocates after a limited time.
- The DM rules that while a normal creature could be sealed in the bag, an ooze could 'ooze out' of it.
- The DM allows the bag to be used to temporarily deprive the Pudding King of an action, but not to contain him permanently.
- While inside, the Pudding King cannot use his magical abilities or give commands, but his two allies remain active.
- Zalthir notes they could "keep stuffing him back in, which would really annoy him."
- The DM rules that Zalthir has already spent his action jumping and grappling, so the bag would have to wait until next round.
- Zalthir instead tries to push the Pudding King as part of his unarmed strike.
- A rules check distinguishes shove from push: "Shove is shoving allows you to knock it prone or push it away from you. Push only allows you to move it away from you."
- Gyrgum comments: "If it was simple, they wouldn't need so many rulebooks."

## NPCs
### Pudding King
A former humanoid who transformed into a sentient, telepathic ooze after embracing his connection to Juiblex, the demon lord of oozes. He commanded an army of oozes and possessed powerful abilities including slime waves and green slime attacks. The slime wave was a cone that knocked targets prone, and the green slime could be hurled anywhere within his lair. He was immune to being knocked prone. He screamed telepathically and resented being asked to quiet down, insisting that screaming was the point of serving a demon. He started the fight at 85 hit points. He was grappled and dragged out of his lair by Zalthir, and at one point swapped bodies with Glabbagool. He was ultimately defeated and dissolved, screaming 'Behold the true feast, the soul's final blend!' as he died.

### Glabbagool
An intelligent gelatinous cube who was previously affected by demonic power. His acidic body damages anyone inside him, and he cannot control this. During the battle, he somehow swapped positions with the Pudding King, causing confusion for the party. His first sign was crying "why are you hurting me?" from the Pudding King's ooze body. After the Pudding King's defeat, Glabbagool remained in the Pudding King's ooze body, now a gray ooze with googly eyes. He expressed excitement about potentially being carried in a jar or Bag of Holding, viewing his new mobility as an opportunity to experience the world differently. He says he can control his viscosity and can go "totally motionless" to be unrecognized. He sees himself as a potential garbage disposal and is interested in monk training.

### Prince Livid
An ooze ally of the Pudding King who attacked with psychic blasts. These were DC 10 Intelligence saves, and he taunted "This works so well against oozes." He was resistant to fire damage, which allowed him to survive Daz's initial Fireball with only eight hit points remaining. He was ultimately killed by Thorin.

### Princess Ebonmire
An ooze ally of the Pudding King who attacked by throwing blobs and charging at the party. She had no ranged attacks beyond the blob, and Gyrgum likened her name to "black mud." She was killed by the party's combined efforts, and after her death, two spellbooks containing many spells were found in her stomach.

### Juiblex
The demon lord of oozes, whose power was channeled through the Pudding King. After the Pudding King's defeat, his infernal influence receded from the area, but a demonic voice claimed he would be reborn by consuming the banquet of the queen of the fungi.

### Zuggtmoy
The Demon Queen of Fungi. After the Pudding King's defeat, the fungi in the area became more twisted, suggesting she was waiting to seize power in the vacuum left by the Pudding King's death.

### Dawnbringer
A magical weapon wielded by Thorin that emits light in a twenty-foot radius and deals radiant damage. She was used to attack the Pudding King after the party realized he had swapped positions with Glabbagool. Her light also gave Zalthir the shadows he needed for Shadow Step.


## Locations
### Throne Room
The Pudding King's throne room, accessed through a hole created by an Earth Elemental. It is the only opening, and the rest of the wall remains blocked. The room contains green slime hazards on the ceiling that can drop on those beneath them. Those directly under a slime are hit automatically; elsewhere the slime must roll to hit, with a DC 10 Dexterity save to avoid being covered. A covered creature must spend an action to clear it or take five damage. The room also contains acidic green pools that do not harm the oozes and in fact feed them. It served as the Pudding King's lair, granting him special lair actions until he was dragged out of it. The lair's boundary is marked by a line on the map. After his death, the slime falls only on those directly beneath it.

## Items
### Bag of Holding
A magical bag capable of storing vast amounts of items and potentially creatures. The party discussed using it to contain the Pudding King, but the DM ruled that while it could temporarily deprive an ooze of an action, a sentient ooze could 'ooze out' of it and escape. By the rules read at the table, a creature can be forced in with an action if it fits the two-foot-wide mouth, can open the bag from inside, and eventually suffocates. While inside, the Pudding King could not use magical abilities or give commands. Glabbagool later expressed interest in being carried in it.

### Spellbook
Two spellbooks containing many spells, found in Princess Ebonmire's stomach after her death. They significantly expanded the party's magical repertoire. The DM directed Daz to add the spells to his own book.

## Spells
### Magic Missile
A reliable force damage spell cast by Daz multiple times during the battle. It does not require a roll to hit and has a range of 120 feet. Daz used it at both first and third level. The first-level cast did six damage after a rolled 1, and the third-level version dealt twenty points of damage to the Pudding King.

### Guiding Bolt
A spell cast by Gyrgum early in the battle that missed its target. Gyrgum rolled a 10 from just inside its 120-foot range.

### Stone Skin
A temporary magical effect that grants resistance to a chosen damage type for one round, usable once per long rest. It is declared when used. Multiple party members used it to resist acid damage from the Pudding King's slime wave attacks. Zalthir's acid resistance also halved the damage from passing through Glabbagool.

### Inflict Wounds
A necrotic damage spell cast by Gyrgum that requires the target to make a constitution saving throw (DC 15). It was used successfully against the Pudding King for twenty-three points of damage and against Princess Ebonmire for eighteen points of damage.

### Fireball
A powerful area-of-effect spell cast by Daz that deals fire damage in a large area. It dealt twenty-nine points of damage to both Prince Livid and Princess Ebonmire, though Prince Livid's fire resistance reduced his damage to fourteen points. Daz had been saving it and has one more left.

### Shadow Step
A monk ability used by Zalthir to teleport short distances, allowing him to quickly close the gap to enemies. He used it to reach the Pudding King and Princess Ebonmire during combat.

### Second Wind
An ability used by Thorin to heal himself during the battle.

### Eldritch Claw
An ability used by Zalthir that adds force damage to his melee attacks, manifesting as damage from his tentacles. Each hit adds 1d6 force damage.

### Revivify
A resurrection spell mentioned by Gyrgum as a potential option to help Glabbagool, though it was not cast as Glabbagool was not dead.
