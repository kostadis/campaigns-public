# Chapter 16

Date: 2025-04-28

## Summary

The skeleton fight in the Whorlstone Tunnels was over, and before play resumed everyone advanced to 4th level.

Among the scattered bones and cadavers of the recent battle, something unexpected happened. A ghost with a gruesome cleaved head emerged from the ground. He introduced himself with surprising cheerfulness as Pelek, a deep gnome traveler from Blingdenstone. His mother had named him after an actor; his father never liked it, and the rest was "complicated and sordid", which he declined to get into. He explained that he had once been friends with a derro named Buppido, who had lured him in with promises of business deals before slaughtering and dismembering him with an axe. Pelek described how Buppido had changed overnight, going from merely eccentric to utterly convinced he was the avatar of a major deity, raving and ranting while the gnome tried desperately to reason with him. The ghost had been trapped in the caverns ever since, which he reckoned was about a year, unable to move on. In his desperation he had even tried to possess Buppido, and the effort failed entirely.

Pelek made a simple but heartfelt request. He asked the party to collect his specific bones and carry them back to Blingdenstone, where they could be buried in his ancestral home and his spirit could finally find peace. He pointed out each bone carefully, and Gyrgum wrapped them up and tucked them into his pack.

Thorin pressed the ghost on what the party got out of the deal and fumbled an intimidation attempt badly. Pelek then offered what wisdom he could. When they arrived at the gates of Blingdenstone, they must make absolutely clear that the drow among them had no ties to Menzoberranzan and no devotion to the spider goddess Lolth. They must carry nothing marked with the number eight, or divisible by eight: eight for a spider's eight legs, the sign of Lolth. And no spiders. He added that the deep gnomes of Blingdenstone know ways to reach the "Overbright." He also revealed one last unsettling detail: the strange magic pervading the tunnels had animated his severed hand, which had scuttled away through the narrow crevices of the cavern wall, still wearing his obsidian ring. With his bones now in the party's care, Pelek's spirit drifted alongside them as a companion, bound to wherever his remains traveled.

Pelek told them that Droki passed through once a day. Following the messy trail of food scraps Droki left behind, the party pushed southeast until they reached a massive cavern filled with a deep, black pool of water stretching nearly a hundred feet across. Turning away from the water, they traced Droki's path to a solid rock wall riddled with narrow cracks. The cracks were far too small for any of them to squeeze through, even after Gyrgum ate one of the magical shrinking mushrooms growing nearby. Smashing through the wall was ruled out, since the caverns were now held by people plotting against the Deepking and noise would draw attention.

Pelek slipped effortlessly through the stone as only a ghost could, scouting ahead and confirming that a tunnel continued on the other side, clear of any immediate danger. With the cracks ruled out as a passage, the party turned back to the dark pool. Daz stepped to the water's edge and began freezing successive sections of the surface, conjuring a makeshift ice bridge that stretched all the way across. The whole group walked safely over the black water to the far shore.

On the other side, the party followed Droki's trail into a dense forest of giant mushrooms, some standing nearly five feet tall, their stalks filling the cavern with an eerie whispering sound. Pelek reassured the group that the noise was harmless, just the wind of the Underdark breathing through the fungi. The party relaxed enough to begin foraging, gathering an assortment of useful mushrooms from the forest floor.

The calm did not last. Without warning, swarms of centipedes erupted from the surrounding fungi, skittering across the ground in relentless waves. One mass of insects crawled directly onto Zalthir, covering him in writhing, biting legs, though his clothes and scales kept the worst of it at bay. Gyrgum unleashed a stream of caustic acid at the nearest swarm, but the agile insects twisted away from the spray. Daz's attempt to drain the life from them with necrotic energy was similarly shrugged off.

The party fought back with everything they had. Thorin struck hard at a wounded swarm, nearly dispersing it entirely. Just as the party began to gain the upper hand, two more swarms emerged from the depths of the fungi forest, surrounding the group on all sides. Zalthir tore the centipedes from his body and crushed them before moving to engage another cluster. Gyrgum hacked at the insects swarming around him with his hand axe, landing a light blow before his mace swing went wide, and Thorin's last swing missed even with a superiority die. With the battle still raging and the mushroom forest alive with the sound of countless skittering legs, the party braced themselves for what was still to come.

## Scenes

### A Ghostly Encounter in the Whorlstone Tunnels

#### After finishing their previous combat, the party encounters the spirit of a deep gnome named Pelek who reveals the dark fate he suffered at the hands of Buppido.

- Before play resumes, everyone levels up to 4th level. Daz's Intelligence reaches 19; Gyrgum bumps a score to 19 "at least temporarily" and defers browsing feats.
- The party has not taken a long rest since the fight.
- A ghost with a gruesome cleaved head emerges from the ground near a pile of cadavers and bones. The spot is marked on the map as "Buppido's resting place." Gyrgum tries not to look straight at it.
- The ghost introduces himself as Pelek ("P-E-L-E-K") and expresses relief that the party killed the derro nearby: "He is dead, yes?"
- Asked what his name means, Pelek says his mother named him after an actor who showed up in Blingdenstone. He then trails off into "the jealousy and the screaming and the terrible childhood" before declining to go further.
  - Thorin (out of character): "Stop torturing the DM. He'll get back at us."
- Pelek recounts his tragic history: he was a traveler from Blingdenstone who fell in with Buppido and was betrayed and murdered by him.
- The ghost describes how Buppido lured him in with business deals only to slaughter and dismember him. Pelek points at an axe: "he cut my head with that axe."
- Gyrgum briefly mistakes Pelek's request for a tip and reaches for his pocket.
- Pelek reveals that a strange magic pervading the tunnels animated one of his severed hands. It scuttled away through a narrow tunnel wearing an obsidian ring.
  - Thorin asks whether Pelek still needs the ring. Pelek: "Well, I'm dead. I don't need anything."
  - Pelek hints that he "could say it does many powerful things" if that would encourage the party.
- Pelek has no alignment with any Gracklstugh faction. He estimates he has been trapped for "a year or so."
- Pelek knows of Droki only as the mad derro who comes in and out. He has never met him and cannot leave his bones to follow.
- Pelek describes Buppido's sudden descent into madness: going from eccentric to "convinced that he was the second coming of some major deity and an avatar and that everybody needed to bow to him."
  - Pelek denies that Buppido was possessed, and confirms he grew no extra body parts.
- Pelek requests that the party collect his specific bones and return them to his ancestral home in Blingdenstone so he can find peace. He flits over to point out exactly which bones; "You don't have to bury all of them."
- Thorin questions what the party actually gains from the deal. When pressed, Pelek offers, "I could try to possess one of you."
- The DM calls for an intimidation roll. Thorin and then Daz try to intimidate the ghost. Thorin rolls a 1. Pelek laughs both off ("You do realize I'm a ghost, right?"), and Thorin concedes that the dead have nothing to lose.
- Pelek offers crucial advice for the journey to Blingdenstone. At the main gates, explain that the drow (Daz) "is not allied with any of the agents of Menzoberranzan, but actually comes from the Overbright."
  - Avoid any association with Lolth.
  - "No eights, no eight of anything. Don't have 208 coins. 207." Nothing divisible by eight.
  - The reason, delivered to a confused Thorin: "Spiders."
- Pelek adds that the svirfneblin of Blingdenstone "know all these wonderful ways to get to the Overbright." He also notes that Jimjar and many others could guide the party there.
- The party agrees to help. Gyrgum wraps the bones and stores them in his pack, binding Pelek's spirit to travel alongside them as a companion.
  - The DM confirms Pelek does not gain levels but is now effectively a companion whose thoughts the party hears whether they like it or not.
  - Daz predicts "two sessions of Gyrgum and Pelek just like chatting."

### Choosing a Path and the Dark Pool

#### The party follows Droki's messy trail southeast through the tunnels, encountering a massive underground pool and a dead end with narrow cracks.

- The party decides to head southeast, following the DM's red line marking Droki's route and the messy trail of food droppings he leaves behind.
  - The DM describes Droki as "a litter bug."
  - Gyrgum suggests Droki may just be obsessive-compulsive. The DM replies, "that's only a piece of his madness."
- Pelek mentions that Droki passes through roughly once a day, so the party could wait for him or even take a long rest.
- They discover a large cavern filled with a deep, black pool. "The opposite shore is nearly 100 feet away, but nothing can be seen beneath the surface of the black water."
  - Daz sees it as his chance to shine with Shape Water.
  - Zalthir suggests continuing on Droki's path first and circling back if needed.
- Following Droki's footsteps away from the water, they arrive at a solid rock wall filled with tiny cracks leading to an adjacent tunnel.
  - Investigation rolls: Daz 11, Gyrgum 10. There are no secret doors.
  - Through the cracks the party can see a tunnel running about 30 feet.
- Thorin proposes growing large and "Hulk smash[ing]" through. Gyrgum's contrarian streak wants to cast mending to seal the cracks instead.
  - The DM warns that the caverns, once "a hippie place," are now held by groups committed to overthrowing the Deepking, so drawing attention would be unwise.
- Pelek slips through the narrow stone cracks to scout ahead ("I knew I would be of use to you, fellows"). He reports that the tunnel continues for some distance with nobody there. Tied to his bones, he cannot go far.
- The party debates using magical shrinking mushrooms to bypass the barrier.

### Crossing the Dark Pool

#### Unable to fit through the narrow cracks in the wall, the party uses ice magic to cross the deep, dark pool instead.

- Gyrgum eats Pygmywort to shrink himself through the narrow rock cracks, but cannot get small enough to pass.
  - The mushroom works like enlarge/reduce, taking a Medium creature only to Small, while the cracks require Tiny.
  - Eating more does not shrink him further, "but you do get the distinct impression you might be smaller for a longer period of time."
- Before trying Pygmywort, Thorin wonders whether the mushrooms behave like Photoshop zoom ("it's hard to get back to just 100%"). Daz compares them to adding a picture to a Word document.
- Turning to the alternate route, the party returns to the large, dark pool.
- Daz uses magic to freeze successive sections of the water, creating a temporary ice bridge for the party to walk across safely.
  - Daz can freeze a five-foot cube and have no more than two non-instantaneous effects active at once.
  - Zalthir suggests layering five-foot chunks into a bridge.
  - As each new slab forms, the old one melts behind them. Thorin has him making "Spider-Man motions."
  - Gyrgum: "I like the iceberg spell."
- Once across the pool, Gyrgum learns that staying small weakens his Strength attacks, so he takes a Bigwig ("the antidote") to restore his size. More Pygmywort and Bigwig grow on the far shore, and he makes sure to stock "an ample supply."

### Ambush in the Fungi Forest

#### The party follows Droki's trail into a dense fungi forest and is ambushed by multiple swarms of centipedes while foraging for mushrooms.

- At an intersection, the party follows Droki's crumbs east. Thorin moves to the back of the group: "I'm like clink clank every time I step."
- The party enters a dense forest of giant mushrooms that whispers with the sound of wind through the stalks: "A dense fungi forest blocks your way. Its tallest specimens growing some five feet high… a hissing sound starts to rise, like uncounted tiny voices whispering in tongues you don't understand."
  - Thorin considers covering mouths against possible spores.
  - The forest is difficult terrain.
  - Gyrgum climbs a tall mushroom and spots a tunnel heading southwest.
- Pelek reassures the party that the eerie sounds are benign. They begin foraging for various Underdark mushrooms, including barrelstalks and bluecaps.
  - The DM also mentions Nilhogg's Nose.
  - Quantities are set to dice averages (3 per d6). Thorin tracks them in a separate inventory column.
- While the party harvests the fungi, two swarms of centipedes ambush them from the surrounding mushrooms. Pelek hovers wherever Gyrgum is to keep his bones safe.
- The DM explains the swarm rules:
  - Swarms can't be grappled or restrained. Daz asks about Maximilian's Earthen Grasp and is told no.
  - Swarms can move through other creatures' spaces.
  - Single-target spells hit only the swarm, but area spells will also hit an ally the swarm covers.
- Zalthir acts first. Finding "none of my useful powers will work here," he punches at the swarm and hits twice, the second for 6 damage.
- Daz casts Toll the Dead against the creatures, but the swarm makes its Wisdom save and resists the effect.
- A swarm crawls onto Zalthir, covering him in biting insects. Its bite misses his AC 16 as the centipedes "stumble on your clothes, your scales, on each other."
- Gyrgum unleashes a stream of caustic acid at the swarms, but the insects roll a 19 on the Dexterity save and dodge the brunt of the spray.
- Thorin strikes a swarm for 10 damage, leaving it at 1 hit point, then uses Action Surge. The players also discover they can draw spell-effect animations on the map ("It was in the wrong place, but it looked really cool").
- A second swarm moves to cover Gyrgum and bites, but its roll of 13 falls just short of his AC 14 ("I hate getting bit").
- Two additional swarms emerge from the surrounding fungi, one of them initially hidden under Pelek's token, further surrounding the party.
  - A nature check establishes, through Daz, that the swarms are not infinite.
  - Pelek starts to tell a ghost story about a group of adventurers, then stops mid-sentence.
- Zalthir finishes off the swarm covering him, then moves at half speed through the difficult terrain to engage another cluster, punching for 10 damage.
- Daz uses Toll the Dead on a wounded swarm for 6 damage.
- Gyrgum fights the centipedes on his person with two-weapon fighting. His hand axe hits (15) for 4 damage and his mace swing misses entirely (7).
  - The DM notes that a five-foot space is big, "so it's more like you hitting all these bugs."
- Thorin attempts a tactical strike, adding a superiority die to a missed attack, but still fails to land the blow.
- The session ends mid-combat with the party still surrounded by centipede swarms. The DM: "I was having so much fun that time passed by."

## NPCs

### Droki

A derro messenger who obsessively follows the same route through the Whorlstone Tunnels each day, passing Pelek's resting place about once a day. He leaves a trail of food droppings and makes no effort to hide his path. The DM pictures him as "this half-crazed… drug-addled mule" whom others use because they see him as utterly innocuous. He is suspected of being linked to the corruption plaguing Gracklstugh and is the party's current quarry. He somehow passed through the narrow cracks in the cavern wall.

### Pelek

The ghost of a deep gnome traveler from Blingdenstone, bearing a gruesome cleaved head wound. He was murdered and dismembered by his former companion Buppido after being lured in with promises of business deals. He was named after an actor, and hints at a troubled childhood he would rather not discuss. He has been trapped for roughly a year, has no loyalty to any Gracklstugh faction, and calls himself "a very well-adjusted ghost."

Remarkably well-adjusted despite his fate, he agrees to travel with the party after they collect his bones. He is bound to his bones, so he can scout only a short distance: he passed through the stone cracks and reported the way clear. He offers advice about navigating Blingdenstone's cultural taboos and claims the deep gnomes there know ways to the Overbright.

### Buppido

A former companion of Pelek who suddenly went stark raving mad, becoming convinced he was the avatar of a major deity. By Pelek's account he ranted and went naked. He murdered and dismembered Pelek with an axe, scattering his remains in the Whorlstone Tunnels. The party killed him in the fight that ended just before this session; his body lies among Pelek's bones.

### Jimjar

The party's friend. Pelek names him as someone who could guide them to Blingdenstone.

## Locations

### Gracklstugh

Also known as the City of Blades, this duergar stronghold is currently gripped by political tension, factional infighting, and a growing sense of supernatural insanity.

### Whorlstone Tunnels

A complex cave system beneath Gracklstugh, currently serving as a hiding place for those working against the ruling establishment and protected by the unpredictable derro. Per the DM, it was once "a hippie place." The party tracked Droki through its tunnels, encountering:

- a dark pool;
- a cracked stone wall whose openings lead to a tunnel beyond;
- an intersection;
- a dense fungi forest.

### Blingdenstone

The ancestral home of the deep gnomes, mentioned by Pelek as the place where his bones must be returned for a proper burial. Pelek warns the party about cultural taboos there, including avoiding the number eight and any association with Menzoberranzan or Lolth. The drow must declare himself from the Overbright. Pelek says the danger lies not in finding it but in "not getting killed on the way there."

### The Dark Pool

A large subterranean cavern filled with deep, black water spanning nearly a hundred feet across, with nothing visible beneath the surface. The party crossed it using a magical ice bridge created by Daz. Pygmywort and Bigwig grow on its far shore.

### The Fungi Forest

A dense subterranean grove within the Whorlstone Tunnels filled with giant mushrooms up to five feet tall; it counts as difficult terrain. The wind creates an eerie whispering sound through the stalks, "like uncounted tiny voices whispering in tongues you don't understand." It holds "every kind of mushroom you could possibly want," and a tunnel leads southwest from it. The party foraged here before being ambushed by centipede swarms.

## Items

### Pelek's Bones

The dry, dismembered remains of the deep gnome Pelek, specifically identified by his ghost and collected by the party to be returned to his ancestral home in Blingdenstone so his spirit can find peace. Gyrgum wrapped them and carries them in his pack. Pelek's spirit is bound to them.

### Buppido's Axe

The weapon Pelek points to as the instrument used to cleave his head and dismember his body, left among the remains in the cavern.

### Obsidian Ring

A ring worn by Pelek in life, last seen on his severed hand, which was animated by the strange magic pervading the tunnels and scuttled away into the narrow crevices of the cavern wall. Pelek hints that bringing it to Blingdenstone along with his bones would earn the party help there. He also dangles the suggestion that it "does many powerful things," but never confirms it.

### Pygmywort

A small mushroom with a one-inch stem and a stubbly blue cap with white dots, known for its magical ability to shrink those who consume it. Its effect works like the reduce option of enlarge/reduce; eating more lengthens the effect rather than shrinking further. Gyrgum ate some in an attempt to fit through the narrow cracks in the cavern wall, and later stocked more.

### Bigwig

A four-inch tall mushroom with a wide purple cap, known for its magical ability to cause growth in those who eat it. Gyrgum ate one to restore his size after shrinking, and later stocked more.

### Barrelstalk

A type of edible fungi found in the Underdark, gathered by the party during their trek through the fungi forest.

### Bluecap

A common Underdark mushroom collected by the party for its utility or nutritional value while foraging in the fungi forest.

### Nilhogg's Nose

A mushroom the DM lists among the forest's finds, which "allows you to smell very much."

### Hand Axe

A light chopping weapon Gyrgum used to hack at the centipedes swarming around him during the ambush in the fungi forest (a hit for 4 damage).

### Mace

A bludgeoning weapon Gyrgum swung in a failed attempt to crush the insects swarming his space during the centipede ambush.

## Spells

### Shape Water

Daz used this cantrip creatively to freeze successive sections of the dark underground pool, creating a temporary ice bridge that let the entire party walk safely across the hundred-foot expanse of black water. The spell affects a five-foot cube and allows no more than two non-instantaneous effects at once, so each slab melted as the next formed. The DM called it a "new creative use of the freeze, the transform water."

### Toll the Dead

Daz cast this necrotic cantrip against the centipede swarms twice during the ambush in the fungi forest. The first cast called for a DC 14 Wisdom save, against 9 necrotic damage on the already-wounded swarm; the swarm saved and took no damage. The DM summarized cantrips as "save or suck." The second cast hit a wounded swarm for 6 necrotic damage.

### Tasha's Caustic Brew

Gyrgum unleashed a line of caustic acid aimed at the centipede swarms in the fungi forest (DC 14 Dexterity save). The swarm rolled a 19, dodged the initial stream, and avoided being coated in acid that would have dealt damage at the start of each of its turns. The DM ruled that an area effect would also have hit Zalthir if aimed at the swarm covering him.
