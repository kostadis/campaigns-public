# Chapter 73 Thorin of Clan Feldrun

Date: 2026-10-19

## Summary
Before departing for Gauntlgrym, Thorin followed Tadric to Sylvira Savikas's office while Daz continued studying books to improve his knowledge of Arcana and magic. Bolstered by the therapy he and Dawnbringer had undergone, Thorin wanted to take responsibility for Moziqodo's death. He apologized for how events had unfolded, wished things had been different, and offered to answer her questions.

Despite Thorin's poor persuasion attempt, Sylvira's clenched jaw relaxed. She acknowledged his remorse, then revealed that Moziqodo's illness had not been natural. It was connected to the demonic corruption spreading through the Underdark, and it had been killing her as well. With Moziqodo dead, her own symptoms were receding. Thorin let her speak before describing what the party had witnessed below and explaining that their work against the threat was unfinished.

Recognizing them as fellow victims, Sylvira handed Thorin a magical research scroll connecting them to the Infernal Fortress. She had previously intended to withhold it regardless of what they could pay. Now their first question would be free, with subsequent questions costing a thousand gold each. Known answers could come immediately; questions requiring research would receive an answer after she investigated. The scroll offered scholarly knowledge about demons, their abilities and weaknesses, conspiracies, and unfamiliar artifacts, rather than intelligence about current events or the location of an active portal. A casual reference to the Cult of Claudien nearly derailed the reconciliation: Sylvira reacted with horror, warning against surrendering judgment to an alien god and forbidding the name within the fortress.

The party departed fully rested and arrived at Gauntlgrym's front gates through the one-use teleportation arranged by King Bruenor Battlehammer. The subterranean city had once been the capital of the Delzoun Empire, its enormous halls enriched by iron, silver, gold, and mithral. Its true treasure was the Great Forge, heated by the bound primordial Maegera. The account of the city's successive occupations prompted Gyrgum to question the dwarven version of history and suggest that such a complicated past might at least produce excellent fusion restaurants.

Eldeth greeted the party with hugs and delight. Her warnings about the Underdark had struggled to gain credibility during their absence, and their arrival gave her the witnesses she needed. Before the evening festival and royal audience, she wanted Thorin inducted into Clan Feldrun. The gathering was reserved for dwarves, although sentient swords were explicitly welcome, allowing Dawnbringer to accompany him. Eldeth also acknowledged that her travels with Daz had challenged her old prejudices, though her declaration that not all drow were alike remained awkward.

At the clan gathering, Thorin emphasized Eldeth's courage and importance to their survival. Her kin were proud of her return; some had believed her dead. Now that Thorin was family, they explained that she had been captured while on Clan Feldrun business amid the long conflict over Underdark resources. Thorin received a clan symbol and the right to call himself Thorin of Clan Feldrun.

Meanwhile, Gyrgum and Daz discovered an orc-dwarven fusion restaurant. Daz proposed trying a little of everything, and the menu rewarded the ambition: Myconid mushroom carpaccio, Kraken Mary with Red Wizard glaze, honeyed wolf pie, and a Basilisk Smash Burger tenderized with a warhammer. The traditional blood-based glaze had been adapted into a sweet-and-sour pomegranate sauce imported from Thay. Zalthir requested Rothé cheese for Glabbagool. Dessert options included gelatinous cube compote and a chocolate-and-molasses cake glazed with spiced fireberries. The menu also offered dissatisfied guests a non-lethal culinary duel to the death against Chef Goromach, with thirty-five-percent tips appreciated from survivors.

The restaurant staff described the scouting disaster that had apparently killed Eldeth's companions. Violent claw wounds suggested a demon attack, but the waiter dismissed Eldeth's subsequent warnings as the product of drow torture and mind-altering substances. Daz, initially more interested in the food than the conversation, responded after Gyrgum nudged him. He invited the skeptic to accompany them into the Underdark; Gyrgum added the prospect of extraordinary ingredients and a position as expedition chef. The waiter declined. They also learned that Bruenor had called an important gathering and that the restaurant's catering bid had lost to someone reportedly connected to the king's extended family.

A flattering royal summons brought the party to Bruenor's hall. Gyrgum's account of the restaurant immediately built rapport, and the king instructed his chamberlain to arrange food from it within the coming weeks. The party then shared their experiences, including what they had learned about Manshoon and the prophecy involving Menzoberranzan. Thorin emphasized that the drow were also victims of the demonic crisis. Daz withheld his possible noble connections, concerned that they could make him useful as a hostage or bargaining piece. His keen observation also revealed that Bruenor welcomed the strategic implications of Menzoberranzan's possible destruction.

Bruenor asked the party to return below, investigate the plague's source, and find a way to stop it. He had gathered the North's major factions to offer assistance, but explicitly recognized that the adventurers had become an independent power. They could decide what to disclose and which promises to make. Thorin felt committed through his new clan ties, while the others pressed for reasons and rewards sufficient to justify returning to the place they had only recently escaped. Gyrgum considered a powerful clerical item; Daz wanted time to consult the party about useful support. No specific reward was settled.

At the evening feast, representatives of the Harpers, Order of the Gauntlet, Emerald Enclave, and Lords' Alliance watched discreetly from around the hall. Before negotiations could begin, Eldeth arrived with dwarven veterans wearing Ember badges. Inspired by her stories, they had renamed their unit the Ember Vanguards. Gyrgum offered valiant adventuring as their ideal, and a veteran praised the strength of dwarves, orcs, drow, dragonborn, and sentient oozes working together. Beer and cheering followed, alongside talk of a crest and merchandising rights. One admirer admitted that he had expected Daz to be older. Eldeth eventually made room for the waiting faction representatives, whose approaches would begin next session.
