# Chapter 51 A Spore-Filled Finale

Date: Apr 4th, 2026

## Summary

High above the massive heart fungus that dominated the cavern floor, Zalthir launched himself through the air toward Ilvara, the corrupted drow priestess at the center of it all. Using his eldritch tendrils to strike from fifteen feet away, he seized her in a crushing grip and dragged her through the air. He then rained blow after devastating blow upon her, using Flurry of Blows and landing two critical strikes before releasing his hold entirely — sending her plummeting forty feet to the stone below. The fall dealt another brutal blow, and though Ilvara struck the ground hard, she was far from finished. In retaliation, she reached into the darkest corners of her power and flooded Zalthir's mind with supernatural terror. He failed to resist and was left frightened, unable to advance on her no matter how fiercely he willed himself forward. Ilvara then used a legendary action to order one of her drones to attack before retreating toward cover.

While Zalthir wrestled with his fear, the enormous heart fungus lashed out at him with a thunderous critical strike, its massive limbs reaching all the way to the cavern ceiling. The blow inflicted both bludgeoning and poison damage, though Zalthir used his martial training to deflect nearly all the physical force. Ilvara, meanwhile, conjured a swirling sphere of biting insects at the entrance of the cave, targeting Gyrgum, Daz, and Glabbagool. The swarm filled the air with a deafening, chittering roar and transformed the passage into difficult, lightly obscured terrain. Ilvara's lesser fungal minions were caught in her own spell and destroyed outright, while Glabbagool suffered badly but remained alive. Jorlan Duskryn, the scarred drow warrior who had once been Ilvara's lover before she cast him aside, saw his opportunity in the chaos and broke away from the party entirely. Crying out, “Ilvara!” he charged toward her with fury burning in his eyes. Thorin considered taking the opening to strike Jorlan but ultimately decided to “let the enemies… let the girls fight.”

Thorin, growing increasingly frustrated by his inability to find a target in a battle where enemies kept their distance, descended from the elevated walkways and plunged into the difficult terrain below, making straight for the heart fungus. His strikes with Dawnbringer, his sentient sun-blade, crackled with radiant energy. The fungus proved “doubly not resistant” to radiant damage and recoiled under the holy light before wilting entirely, collapsing in on itself and dying. The DM had clearly prepared something elaborate for the encounter, while Gyrgum ruefully admitted, “Boy, I had so much prepared for the fungus. Oh well.” With the primary fungal threat neutralized, Gyrgum dropped concentration on his thirty-foot-radius sphere and the party turned its attention to Ilvara and the growing chaos around her.

From the shadows at the edge of the cavern, five drow warriors emerged. Their arrival was initially confused by Zalthir's magical darkness, Ilvara's insect plague, and the layered hazards filling the entrance. The thick insect bloom blocked their line of sight, preventing them from clearly seeing Daz or safely advancing further into the cavern. They declared themselves devoted to drow glory, but not devoted enough to walk blindly into a lethal cloud of insects. Their initial crossbow volley against Daz was therefore retaken under the proper obscured conditions, and all three bolts missed.

Before Daz could fully recover, a drow elite warrior stepped forward from hiding and grabbed him by the arm, pulling him northeast through the stinging insect swarm and away from the worst of the fighting. The warrior's appeal amounted to, “Come with me if you want to live, you idiot,” followed by the clarification that Daz would be taken along even if he did not particularly want to live. The rescuer suffered thirteen points of damage while leading him through the plague. Asha Vandree, watching from nearby, immediately grew suspicious and denounced the newcomers as “Traitors to Lolth!” Daz quickly assured her, “No, these are friends of Lolth, and they're here to help.” His persuasion narrowly succeeded, preserving the unstable alliance.

A drow mage lurking in the magical darkness fired a bolt of lightning blindly into the cavern, the crackling energy streaking past everyone without finding a target. The remaining House T'sarran combatants argued over whether to charge through the insects, remain in the tunnel, or risk being caught together by a fireball. Daz resolved that debate himself by unleashing a fourth-level fireball into the darkness. The explosion lit the tunnel in a brilliant orange flash, dealing thirty-seven points of damage, incinerating the drow guards outright, and leaving the mage barely standing. Zalthir, highly annoyed that his magically induced fear prevented him from rushing Ilvara to deliver his monk strikes, realized that while he could not get closer to her, he could advance in a different direction. He teleported across the battlefield, away from Ilvara, and took his frustration out on the drow of House T'sarran, finishing the wounded mage off with a single decisive punch before missing the surviving elite warrior.

Ilvara, increasingly desperate, reshaped the battlefield around her. Using a legendary action, she conjured a bloom of fungal growth in Jorlan Duskryn's path, turning the ground into difficult terrain and preventing him from reaching her. She then dropped concentration on the insect plague, opening the passage again, and muttered invocations to Zuggtmoy, declaring that the bride — Zuggtmoy — would come to spread chaos and mayhem. After considering several possible spells, she called down a column of divine fire upon Daz and Nym Duskryn. Both managed to blunt the impact and suffered only thirteen points of damage, leading the DM to pronounce Flamestrike “the lame alternative” available to clerics.

Thorin, thinking tactically, turned his attention to the bridge beneath Ilvara's feet. At Zalthir's suggestion, he attacked the structure rather than Ilvara herself, striking it twice and dealing enough damage to send it crashing down. Ilvara and Jorlan Duskryn both leapt clear of the collapse, landing on the cavern floor below without suffering the worst of the fall. Jorlan was left furious amid the ruined terrain, though he did not know whom to blame.

Daz's drow protectors urged him to remain out of the fighting, explaining, “We only get paid if you live.” Daz flatly refused, declaring that he would not allow everyone else to fight for him while he stood aside and that their best chance of getting paid was to help prevent the entire group from dying. Kaelira responded by asking her partner, “Can I kill your brother?” Nym Duskryn dryly observed that Jorlan had always been the dumber one and was now covered in fungus, then gave her permission. A lightning bolt struck Jorlan, though he twisted aside and took only half its force.

Gyrgum paused long enough to ask whether anyone needed serious healing. Daz, reduced to eighteen of his forty-four hit points, and Zalthir both believed they could survive one more round, so Gyrgum committed to another attack. He channeled a concentrated beam of radiant holy light directly at Ilvara. This time the Guiding Bolt struck true, and the effect was immediate and catastrophic. Ilvara, deeply vulnerable to radiant energy, did not simply fall — she detonated, her body erupting in a massive cloud of poisonous spores that billowed twenty feet outward through the cavern. Zalthir and Thorin were caught in the toxic bloom and staggered, but both endured, with Thorin's resistance reducing the harm.

The remaining drow elite warrior of House T'sarran surveyed the wreckage and decided the battle was lost. He offered a cold promise — “We will meet again. Enjoy your victory for the moment” — before disengaging and vanishing into the tunnels. Asha Vandree, standing amid the settling spores and the silence that followed, began loudly denouncing “the evil” and “the undead,” then dropped to her knees and murmured prayers and beatitudes to Lolth. The final battle had ended amid fireballs, lightning bolts, darkness, broken bridges, fungal hazards, and the poisonous remains of Ilvara herself.

## NPCs

### Ilvara

The primary antagonist and a high-ranking drow priestess, now partially corrupted with myconid-like hybrid parts. She remains mechanically a humanoid despite the fungal corruption, preventing effects keyed specifically to fungal creatures from harming only her hybrid growths.

She is a formidable opponent who uses legendary actions to teleport, command drones, create fungal blooms, and magically frighten her enemies. After Zalthir dropped her forty feet, she frightened him so that he could no longer approach her, then commanded a drone to attack and moved toward cover. She also reshaped the terrain to prevent Jorlan from reaching her.

Ilvara wields powerful clerical magic including Insect Plague and Flamestrike. Her Insect Plague killed her own lesser minions and badly injured Glabbagool, while also creating an obstacle that disrupted the arrival of House T'sarran. Her Flamestrike targeted Daz and Nym but failed to incapacitate either of them.

She invoked Zuggtmoy and spoke of the bride — Zuggtmoy herself — bringing chaos and mayhem. Obsessed with vengeance, Ilvara continued howling threats even as the battlefield collapsed around her. She is particularly vulnerable to radiant damage, which ultimately leads to her destruction — Gyrgum's Guiding Bolt causes her to explode into a twenty-foot cloud of poisonous spores.

### Glabbagool

A sentient grey ooze, formerly a gelatinous cube, and an ally to the party. He was positioned near Gyrgum and Daz when Ilvara cast Insect Plague and took twenty-six points of damage from the biting swarm.

Glabbagool narrowly survived and remained in the fight. His reduced, jar-like form briefly caused confusion over where his token should be positioned, but the DM explained that he had been sitting nearby, “kind of chillaxing with his eyes,” and trying to participate in the conversation. Daz later shaped his Fireball to avoid striking him.

### Jorlan Duskryn

A scarred drow warrior and former lover of Ilvara. Ilvara discarded him after he was scarred, and he remains driven by humiliation and spite.

Jorlan initially fought the party because he did not believe they could defeat Ilvara. Once he saw that she had been wounded, however, he disengaged and charged toward her, shouting, “Ilvara!” Thorin deliberately allowed him to leave rather than taking an opportunity attack.

Ilvara blocked Jorlan's pursuit with a fungal bloom that turned the path into difficult terrain. Thorin later destroyed the bridge supporting Ilvara and Jorlan, but Jorlan avoided injury in the collapse. Nym subsequently identified him as his brother, agreed that he had always been the dumber one, and allowed Kaelira to strike him with Lightning Bolt. He survived after taking fourteen damage.

### Asha Vandree

A drow combatant who has been tricked into believing Daz is a follower of Lolth. She regards him as an insane mage who has somehow converted to Lolthism, but the important fact is that she believes his story.

When the House T'sarran drow appeared to drag Daz away, Asha assumed they were attacking or abducting him and denounced them as “Traitors to Lolth!” Daz reassured her that they were friends of Lolth who had come to help. His successful persuasion prevented her from turning on the newcomers.

Following Ilvara's death, Asha cried out about “the evil” and “the undead,” then began chanting prayers and beatitudes to Lolth amid the settling spores.

### Kaelira Duskryn

Kaelira Duskryn is a specialized drow combatant of House Duskryn, contracted with Nym to protect Daz, who emerges from hiding to pull him clear and claims they only get paid if he lives.

She tells Daz, “Come with me if you want to live, you idiot,” then adds that she will still take Daz if he does not want to live because cooperation would make the task easier. She leads Daz northeast through the Insect Plague, succeeds on her saving throw, and still takes thirteen damage in the process.

Kaelira and her allies later urge Daz to remain out of combat, but Daz refuses and tells them their best hope of getting paid is to help everyone survive. After Ilvara's death, the surviving House T'sarran elite warrior disengages and retreats, vowing, “We will meet again. Enjoy your victory for the moment.”

### The House T'sarran Mage

A drow spellcaster who blindly fires a Lightning Bolt into the fray from the darkness, frustrated by the lack of easy kills and the presence of magical hazards. The bolt misses “everybody and their mother.”

He is criticized by the House T'sarran elite warrior for failing to bring Dispel Magic. The warrior threatens that the matron mother of House T'sarran will hear about how badly the supposedly easy assassination has gone.

Daz's Fireball badly wounds him — the fourth-level blast inflicts thirty-seven points of damage and kills the lesser guards — and Zalthir delivers the killing blow.

### Nym Duskryn

A drow warrior who works with Kaelira to protect Daz and assists the party. He survives Ilvara's Flamestrike after taking thirteen damage.

When Daz refuses to withdraw from combat, Kaelira asks him, “Can I kill your brother?” Nym responds that his brother Jorlan was always the dumber one and confirms that he is now covered in fungus before granting permission.

He shows little mercy for Jorlan Duskryn, granting Kaelira permission to strike him with a Lightning Bolt. Jorlan partially avoids the blast but still takes fourteen damage. Nym then mutters about the stupidity of those around him and dashes to a safer position as the battlefield continues to shift.

## Locations

### The Fungal Altar

A high-ceilinged underground chamber approximately forty feet tall, dominated by a massive heart fungus and elevated walkways. The cavern features pools of difficult terrain, elevated mushroom pods, zones of magical darkness, blinding yellow circles, and a thirty-foot-radius protective sphere.

Ilvara's twenty-foot-radius Insect Plague filled the height of its passage from floor to ceiling. The plague created both difficult terrain and light obscuration, effectively sealing the route against fragile reinforcements.

The battlefield grew progressively more deformed as spells and attacks reshaped it. Ilvara created an additional fungal bloom to slow Jorlan, Thorin destroyed one of the bridges, and Fireball ignited unattended flammable material inside the darkened tunnel.

A mysterious icon linked to Zuggtmoy stood near Ilvara's position. The party avoided approaching it because of its apparent ability to commune with the Demon Queen and the danger it posed to those already touched by her influence.

By the battle's end, the heart fungus had wilted, a bridge had collapsed, the tunnels had been scorched by Fireball and Lightning Bolt, and Ilvara's poisonous spores hung in the air alongside the scent of ozone.

### The Icon

A mysterious object present in the cavern used by the Demon Queen Zuggtmoy to communicate. The party is warned to stay away from it, as proximity to it is considered dangerous — particularly for those who have already had a deep personal encounter with its power.

When Thorin considered advancing toward Ilvara, the DM reminded him that getting too close to the icon could be especially dangerous because of his prior contact with Zuggtmoy. This warning helped prompt the decision to attack Ilvara's bridge from a distance.

## Spells

### Insect Plague

Cast by Ilvara, this spell filled a twenty-foot-radius sphere at the cave entrance with biting insects, targeting Gyrgum, Daz, and Glabbagool. It required Constitution saving throws and dealt piercing damage.

The swarm killed Ilvara's own thirteen-hit-point minions outright and inflicted twenty-six damage on Glabbagool, although he survived. It also created difficult, lightly obscured terrain extending from the floor to the ceiling of the forty-foot passage.

The plague became a tactical barrier that blocked the arriving group of five drow from seeing Daz and advancing. One rescuer crossed it to pull Daz to safety, taking thirteen damage despite making the save. Ilvara later dropped concentration on the spell to free her focus for another assault.

### Shield

A reactive barrier of magical force cast by Daz as a reaction when the first series of hand-crossbow attacks appeared to hit him. It would have raised his armor class for the remainder of the volley.

The spell and its damage were later rewound after the table remembered that Zalthir's Darkness imposed disadvantage on the drow attacks. Daz recovered both the hit points and the expended spell slot. He later considered using Shield against Flamestrike, but it could not help because that spell required a Dexterity save rather than an attack against armor class.

### Lightning Bolt

Used by both the House T'sarran mage — who fired it blindly into the darkness and missed everyone — and by Kaelira Duskryn, who directed it at Jorlan Duskryn during the final stages of the battle.

The first bolt streaked harmlessly through the cavern. The second struck Jorlan after Nym authorized an attack against his brother; Jorlan made his saving throw and suffered fourteen damage.

### Fireball

Cast by Daz at fourth level, it engulfed the area of magical darkness in flames. Daz positioned the blast to encompass the hostile House T'sarran combatants while sparing Glabbagool.

The spell dealt thirty-seven fire damage. It killed the three lesser Drow guards outright, heavily damaged the Drow Mage, and inflicted half damage on the elite warrior who successfully saved. Any unattended flammable objects inside the area also caught fire. Zalthir subsequently finished the wounded mage with a single punch.

### Guiding Bolt

A flash of radiant light cast by Gyrgum. His first attempt against Ilvara missed.

Later, after checking whether Daz and Zalthir required immediate healing, Gyrgum cast Guiding Bolt again at third level. The second attack struck Ilvara directly and rolled substantial radiant damage. Her special vulnerability amplified the spell enough to kill her outright, causing her body to erupt into a twenty-foot cloud of poisonous spores.

### Flamestrike

A clerical spell used by Ilvara to call down a vertical column of divine fire upon Daz and Nym Duskryn. It required Dexterity saving throws rather than attack rolls, so Daz's Shield spell offered no protection.

Daz rolled a twenty on his save, and both targets suffered thirteen damage. The result was sufficiently unimpressive that the DM described Flamestrike as “the lame alternative” available to clerics.
