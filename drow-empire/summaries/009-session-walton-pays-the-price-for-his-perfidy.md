# Chapter 9 – Walton pays the price for his perfidy

Date: 14 Flocktime 595 to 15 Flocktime 595 CY (595-05-14 to 595-05-15; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
The party is in the city of Fax, and the army is outside readying to leave. After a few days the party goes shopping, and realises they have only 650 GP to their collective names. That is too little, so they sell "the instrument of the bards," a device Reepicheep "would have found useful, but he is no more." They find Xenos, the magic middleman, and sell it.

With the item sold, Mr. Corrin, via Mr. Rimardo, remarks that there is unfinished business: the killing of Walton. Rimardo is quick to point out that Mr. Corrin will do the murder. The party approaches the upper gate to the fort and finds it heavily guarded. Corrin and Rimardo plan to sneak across the walls in front of the Bishop's residence, scale down to the river, climb the bluffs in the dark, find Walton in the fort, kill him and escape. KP asks, "So, what spells would you like me to cast on you before you go off?" Rimardo: "You were betrayed as well!" KP: "You want ME to SNEAK?" Corrin and Rimardo realise that Rimardo cannot sneak as well, and that Corrin alone would be too far separated. Since they also do not know where Walton is, they regroup.

They decide to ask the guards, pretending to have something to give Walton. Bayard refuses this dishonest act, which would leave him no longer beyond reproach, so Rimardo steps up: "So, we have some potions for Walton, and do not know where he is." The guard says, "Walton, Walton is joining the army." Asked whether the guards are joining up, an annoyed guard says someone has to protect the city from miscreants and, staring viciously at Rimardo, "losers." The irritated guards ask whether he "wouldn’t have happened to have an extra potion for us as a token of appreciation." Rimardo hands them one. "There are four of us," they say. Rimardo backs off, explaining he has "the one potion for Walton and the one for you guys," and leaves quickly.

The party heads for the meadow where the army is staging, going through the lower gate in the hope of recovering their weapons. They realise that presenting themselves to claim the weapons would link them to the people Walton wanted dead, so they abandon the weapons. Rimardo is not at all happy. Rather than sneak around a military camp in the dark, they realise the army is open to anyone after the rallying cry for troops, so armed men walking in would not cause a stir. As they enter, Rimardo shouts, "We are here to perform our civic duty and answer the call."

The encampment holds "Several dozen tents of various styles" across The Meadows, with "Over a hundred men" tending arms and armor, playing games, and camping in clusters grouped by livery. "A clutch of dark elves is moving among the groups conferring with individual leaders." A soldier points them to Walton's cluster of tents, held by 20 armed men. Drums beat and flags wave, and the troops assemble. A Drow, "or is a Dark Elf," announces in a sneering voice: "Grand Master Tolub has assigned me the enormous task of leading this army in our conquest of the Wild Coast to free it from external influences." The crowd answers, "HUZZAH!" The speaker continues: "Before we can free the people of the Wild Coast, we must teach them the price of resisting." Then: "We shall march to Vallaki, and we shall sack Vallaki. All soldiers are free to loot and pillage to their heart’s content." One soldier whispers that "Vallaki is poorly defended with only two dozen soldiers. It will be easy pickings." Rimardo shouts, "When do we march?" and the Drow General answers, "TOMORROW!"

At nightfall the party scouts Walton's encampment. Walton's tent holds himself and several guards. KP wonders how to avoid chaos; Rimardo proposes casting silence, and the party agrees. Corrin will enter the tent, Rimardo will block the exit, and KP will hide in the nearby trees. Bayard, choosing to remain beyond reproach, declares, "I will not participate in this evil enterprise, but I will be at the ready to help you if something were to go wrong." Rimardo: "Okay."

Corrin sneaks in and does double critical damage on the sleeping Walton, 46 HP in total, but Walton is wounded, not dead. Walton reacts faster than Corrin. Bayard will not attack yet because, though a strong advocate for justice, "this reeks of revenge." Walton disengages, wakes the three guards in the tent, and hides behind them. Bayard mutters, "With this mess, I am forced to engage." Just as he is about to enter the battle, KP taps him on the shoulder: "That will not be necessary." KP casts fireball for 30 HP of damage, "smiling at the smell of burnt human flesh and destruction." Rimardo screams, "RUN!" KP whines, "I have more fireballs!!" but runs when he sees everyone else running. The party escapes and rushes toward Vallaki to warn it.

During the long hike through the forest KP finally figures out how ritual spells work, and is excited about taking an hour every morning to prepare for battle. As they head west, a giant log swings at the party; Rimardo, KP and Bayard fail to dodge and take 11 HP. They have stumbled on a picket of the army: a quaggoth, an acolyte with an owl familiar, three Drow, and three Drow initiates with homunculi familiars. At first they see only the quaggoth and three Drow in partial cover.

Rimardo's hold person catches the quaggoth but not Quavyl. Corrin, with advantage "as an assassin," hits Quavyl with both rapier and shortsword "and drops the Drow." An owl familiar swings from the trees and cures Quavyl for 8 hP, and Quavyl attacks, ineffective "in the mid-day light." A homunculus fires 3 magic missiles at KP for 10 Hp; Ilzith misses Rimardo twice with his hand crossbow; another homunculus's magic missile does 7 HP to Rimardo and breaks his concentration. Tazznar casts faerie fire on the party; only KP is affected. KP's lightning bolt does 26 HP of damage, "killing Ilzith and Tazznar and the homunculi." Bayard smites Quavyl for 28 HP with his first attack and kills him. One of the initiates casts dimness. Rimardo kills the owl. The acolyte reveals himself, and Corrin, given advantage, kills the quaggoth. Bayard: "Well, that was easy."

The acolyte's sacred flame misses Rimardo, who deftly dodges. Initiates 2 and 3 hit KP with magic missiles for 12 and 10 HP, and the initiates misty step with their bonus actions, though the party overhears where they go. Bayard smites and kills the acolyte, and smiles. Vuzlyl's faerie fire fails against Rimardo and Bayard. Rimardo heals KP for 16 HP. Corrin hits Nilonim for 18 Hp; an initiate misty steps away and hits KP for 11 HP, and Nilonim's magic missile does 9 more. KP answers with a fire bolt cantrip for 27 hp; "The party is astonished at the power of this lowly spell, but KP just looks at them and shrugs." He then moves into the forest, unwittingly next to an initiate. Bayard unleashes both attacks on Vuzlyl for 38 HP total, and smiles again. Rimardo runs to KP and heals him "with 2nd level cure-wounds and a healing word for 31 HP." The chapter ends as Ulvir "casts 10 Hp of damage and then misty steps away and runs off into the forest, and the party can no longer find him."
