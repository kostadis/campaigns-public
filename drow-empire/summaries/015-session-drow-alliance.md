# Chapter 15 – Drow Alliance

Date: 23 Wealsun 595 to 25 Wealsun 595 CY (595-06-23 to 595-06-25; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
After securing the farm, the party waits for the return of the Dark Elf and his gang. Another bloody battle follows, and the party overcomes the enemy. On the Drow leader, Nalven, they find treasure. Examining the haul, Rimardo and Corrin feel once again that their decision to work for Bayard pleased Istus: the weaving of their threads with Bayard's was something Istus was rewarding. Why she was so pleased is a mystery to the halfling and the cleric. In the past Istus had rewarded them with treasure; now she seemed to pay them in more abstract things, "like a sense of accomplishment or heroism, and that was odd. It was almost as if they were pawns in a game she was playing."

On the Dark Elf they also find a note revealing that his name is Nalven and that he is a bounty hunter. Xhalh, "the local commander of the Drow Empire," had ordered him to disrupt the alliance and, if he could, kill the independent contractors. With the note in hand, Bayard strides triumphantly into Fhuu to Sir Ryton and Primus Yagrat, declaring that the honour of Juste-Havre is preserved. "These scum – were scum." Sir Ryton is delighted and the Primus relieved: the last thing he needed was a war against the humans with the Drow at his doorstep.

Over the next few days the party decompresses, relaxes, and takes part in the grand debates over strategy. The operational plan rests on a reading of the enemy. "The people of Fax were notorious for being brigands, not soldiers." They plundered to fill a gap in their personal treasury, had no ideology and no interest in power over people, and their effete ruling class could not conceive of an empire; the individualistic nature of the Wild Coast did not suit the self-sacrifice an empire needs. The Drow, for their part, "were creatures of the under-dark," uninterested in ruling overland: "Theirs was a smash-and-grab on a large scale." The elves, who know the Drow, understand that they are bullies, dominant in the under-dark where they can hide, paralyze and terrorize, but weak on the surface. Typically it was some house from the under-dark hoping to seize surface resources for an internal Drow strife. "The Drow were push-overs," unwilling to sacrifice much because that would undermine their position below.

The war council plans two thrusts from Fhuu to Fax to free the Wild Coast of the Drow, with Sir Ryton leading the non-orc forces and the Primus leading the Orcs. Sir Ryton asks Bayard to go with the Orcs, "to make sure that the Orcs remembered that this was not another raid." As the armies head towards Fax, they liberate the towns the Drow had occupied, and the Drow forces flee rather than fight.

All goes well until the Orcs reach the gnomish town of Crossroads. Burgher Teeble, standing beside his "pathetic militia of twenty-five," tells the Orcs they must go around his village. This is a severe insult to the Orcs, who want to massacre everyone in the town, and if that happened the Anti-Drow Alliance would collapse. The Primus restrains his troops, but no solution seems possible. Bayard remembers the gnome Seth, and that Burgher Teeble is related to him, and steps forward. Just then a gnome named Seth jumps out from the militia: "That is Bayard! He was the one who saved me from the Quaggoth. When his partner pointed out that they could flee, he attacked at great personal risk. If he vouches for the Orcs, then I say the Orcs are on a mission that will benefit us all." Bayard is pleased to see his fame so widespread. The Burgher grumbles, "So this is the Great Bayard who is without fear and without reproach?" After thinking it over, he tells Seth that Crossroads will join the alliance, with Seth leading a small contingent of troops.

Thanks to Bayard's heroism the Orcs make rapid progress, and Seth's presence assures other villages that this is not another Orc raiding party, as the Drow "and their patsies the WLF" seem to be suggesting. The Primus then reports that the bulk of the Drow army has taken the field outside Vallaki near the western gate. Since Vallaki sits on the main road to Fax, this is an excellent chance to destroy them. The two forces meet three miles from Vallaki: the Orc army of 200 and the Alliance army of 400. Scouts report that the Drow army has just entered Vallaki and is small, "approximately 200'ish," which, given the Drow's magic and powers, makes the combined force just enough. But the scouts are not sure whether this is the whole Drow army or only part of it. The council wants to attack at once, until Rimardo reminds them of the Drow's obvious advantages in a night battle, and they agree to attack in the morning.

The Primus and Sir Ryton plan to march through Vallaki and attack the Drow through the western gate. Rimardo points out that the full army would be bottlenecked in the town roads, and that it would be better to converge on Vallaki from the north and the south. Sir Ryton agrees, but notes that this lets the Drow enter the town and use it as a fortress: "Someone will have to seal the western gate!" KP notes that he can do it, to Rimardo's horror, since KP should have demanded PAYMENT first. Bayard is delighted at his gnomish ally's courage; KP, of course, was only observing that he can do it, not that he wants to. The decision is made: the independent contractors will enter the city at night and use magic to keep the Drow from using the western gate.

The stealthy infiltration goes badly, and they make too much noise. Before the fight begins, seeing KP rather excited about using fireball, Bayard points at him: "No fireballs!" The battle is quickly over, the independent contractors overwhelming the Drow guards, and they reach the western gate unobserved. Their plan is to wait near the entrance for the Anti-Drow Alliance armies to advance, and to block the Drow when they try to use the western gate to enter the city. Seth and his merry band of gnomes are with them.
