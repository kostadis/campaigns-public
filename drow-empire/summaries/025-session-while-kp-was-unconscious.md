# Chapter 25 – While KP was unconscious

Date: 17 Goodmonth 595 CY (595-08-17; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
While KP was unconscious, he dreamed he was back home in the Kron Hills, in the library, in charge of 11 Gnome apprentices. In the dream he sat in a meeting with other gnome managers discussing bonuses, in a protracted debate over whether his apprentices "met or exceeded the bar"; KP, being somewhat literal, kept looking for a measurement stick to measure them. Just as the dream reached its climax, the screams of his party members woke him.

Meanwhile Gruneel decides that protecting the exits does not fit his aspiration to be a hero of great renown — "Better, he thinks, to be a man of action than to be the dude who guards the door" — and, seeing Bayard surrounded, charges in just as another Githyanki appears. KP casts thunder wave; each foe takes 18 points of damage, and one suffers attacks of opportunity from both Corrin and Bayard. Bayard kills one Githyanki, then, down to 16 hit points, lays hands on himself. Corrin kills another with his sneak attack and disengages to face another foe, and Gruneel's bursting arrow does 19 HP of damage.

With only three Githyanki left, one says, "parley?" Bayard wonders whether this is a creature "with honor or without," and seeing its sword concludes that the Githyanki, though not good, understand honor. KP asks, "are you Githyanki or Githzerai?" The Githyanki is offended: the Githzerai "are outcasts, liars, and the only thing worse than mind flayers." They explain that they rob the Drow when the Drow Empire uses the portal to plunder from worlds, and are "interested in treasure, not death." KP calls it "a great plan," and Rimardo at last finds a race he can relate to. Corrin nudges Rimardo about changing teams; Rimardo decides Bayard is the surest path to vengeance, though working with the Githyanki is an exciting option if things go sideways.

The Githyanki proposes to disengage, and KP adds, "and mutually prey on the Drow?" When KP excitedly offers a contract, the Githyanki are offended — "We do not sign contracts like Devils. We say, and it is so." KP's quip, "I am sure some of the mind flayers survived!", accidentally pleases them, since the Githyanki believe the mind flayers remain a threat. But KP does not stop while he is ahead: "I would so love to study them!" is a mortal insult, and they hurl their worst abuse, "Are you a thrall?" KP obliviously asks how they kill mind-flayers, which miraculously puts them back in a good mood, and the creature describes in brutal detail how thralls, mind flayers and their hive brain are slaughtered. It dismisses KP's question about mind-flayers in the Drow Empire: "The Drow will do business with anyone and anything, including mind-flayers. The Drow and the mind-flayers exchange one currency, slaves!"

Bayard asks about the portal and the tactical situation. The Githyanki asks what he has to offer, and Bayard, shifting his sword forward, says, "Your lives! That is why we were in parley?" The Githyanki scoffs that they can escape at will into the Ethereal plane. They want the treasure the party took from the room — "that was ours, not yours?" KP: "Finders, Keepers!" The Githyanki suggests "Perhaps 50-50?", and Bayard agrees at once: "That’s fine for me, that’s not what I am fighting for!" Before Corrin can kill Bayard, Gruneel calls a huddle, Rimardo insists it comes "Out of your share, not mine!", and Bayard charms the Githyanki into a 20-80 split; since the 20% comes from Bayard, Rimardo is satisfied.

The Githyanki reveals a wealth of information. The Drow Empire is headquartered in the Abyss and is not the only Drow Empire — Lolth likes to have many — but it is her favorite, and has controlled a swathe of the Abyss because it has controlled this portal for millennia. Asked in one voice, "Where is the portal?", the Githyanki laughs: this entire area is the portal, an entrance "like a wagon that materializes and dematerializes in different planes." How it is controlled remains a mystery; only who goes in and out can be controlled. The Drow just use it to plunder. Gruneel asks whether anyone knows how to close it, which baffles the Githyanki — "No, and why would you want to?" — and then whether it can be stopped from coming to their plane. The Githyanki adds that the Drow are not liked in the Abyss, being viewed as an abomination, and that the folks in the Abyss do not trust Lolth — "Trust is a rare commodity in the Abyss." Bayard's thoughtful question about returning home earns the answer that "the portal completes a circuit," which changes every so often for unknown reasons. The Drow send slaves out to explore, promising them freedom; if the slaves do not die, the Drow come out. The Githyanki attack the Drow when they are weak and hide when they are healthy. "The portal, in short, is a source of infinite plunder, and if the Drow control it, they are invulnerable." All seems bleak until the Githyanki reveals that the Drow Empire has factions: "They are Drow. You may be able to get some faction of the Drow to help you if you give them power or some demonic power that wants the Drow out."

The Githyanki salutes the paladin's honour in the parley and fades away: "When you see the Red Dragons at the walls of Maenerius, call for us." Gruneel blurts, "Dragons, What? What did you say?" KP notices they never said how to contact them — and one Githyanki reappears, apologising that the "Dramatic fade to Ethereal plane" was ruined because he forgot to drop the coin. "You hold it and call out for us! Let me try this again." He fades once more, repeating the line as a coin clinks onto the ground. Gruneel still has not gotten over it: "Did you hear him say – Dragons? Like many Dragons?"

As the party explores the room waiting for the portal to move, Gruneel speaks in a peculiar voice: "I’m Selzyt." The possessed Gruneel calls them "morons who cannot pronounce my name" and wants a deal. Selzyt is "the guardian of this portal, the failsafe. I am the overseer." He lays out his alternative plainly: possess and get each of them killed in turn, the last at the hands of the Drow guards in the Drow city — "So, we can do that, or can we make a deal?" The queen of House Zollazo trapped him in a gem; he can use a magic jar to possess creatures, and he has watched everything come and go: "Drow come in, Drow come out. Slaves come in; demonic creatures come out."

KP, recalling the dream's lessons on career growth and regularly scheduled "1x1’s," realises Selzyt is rage quitting. "So what you want is to escape?" Selzyt is overjoyed — "Yes, but first I need my body!" He notices Rimardo holds his gem and warns against destroying it: they would kill Gruneel and he would keep this body, "Not an incredibly good one." People talk freely around a gem, so he hears everything. His body is "in House Zollazo’s treasure room," but they are not ready yet: they must first acquire power, and if House Zollazo thinks he is helping them, all will be lost. When KP asks for the less arduous process, Selzyt pitches himself as "your gem behind the scenes. Your DEEP THROAT!" KP offers, "Your abyssal tour guide?" — "You’re in!" Selzyt, astonished and touched, tries to give very awkward hugs, until Corrin points his rapiers at him: "no hugs! Got it!"

With the Paladin agreeing to the deal, Selzyt vows revenge on House Zollazo and all the Drow, who never once asked, "Hello Mr. Overseer, how goes it?" and never gave him a new book. "You are literally the first group of adventurers to make it to this point. Most of the time, the Dragons kill them!" Asked about the Dragons, he explains that the Drow have run "a multi-century program to breed Abyssal Dragons" — which is why white dragons use fire to heal. KP: "I used a fire bolt!" Selzyt: "Didn’t work, did it?" KP pulls out a pen to write about the breeding program; Selzyt groans, "I am the overseer, not the head of breeding!" and tells Bayard, "Bayard, you need better help."

Rimardo wants assurance that the party keeps the gem once Selzyt has his body, or Mr. Bayard covers their losses. Selzyt agrees: his body and revenge, their gem, in exchange for accurate information about the Drow, the Drow Empire and their cities, "Because I can be trusted and am a Drow of integrity. The fact that I am betraying my queen should not cause anyone to question my integrity, right?" He offers the best bar in Plagueridge Rampart but nothing on breeding — "No references, no books, and no intros." — and refuses to tell everything at once lest they betray him: "I will give information when you need it and not a minute before." KP tests him: "What is the hair of House Zollazo’s matron?" "she is a Drow, all women Drow have white hair." "Oh, right, I knew that." This goes on for hours, until Selzyt gives up first: "I am done with your pestering gnome, I will go into my gem and hide from your endless questions!" Gruneel recovers: "I am so glad I missed this conversation."
