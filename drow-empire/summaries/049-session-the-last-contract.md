# Chapter 49 – The Last Contract

Date: The Abyss, Day 151, then Lolth's demi-plane, Day 1 (??? on Oerth; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
With Vhaeraun's weapons and boons in hand, all that remains is how to reach the hidden fortress of Lolth and confront the Queen of the Spiders. Lolth's demi-plane is warded against planar travel, so no one can simply plane shift into her fortress. The only way in is through the slave pits: powerful clerics of Lolth can plane-shift prisoners there, unconscious and unarmed. The chapter reproduces the text of the spell Leomund’s Secret Chest (twice), which hides a chest and its contents on the Ethereal Plane, recallable by touching a Tiny replica.

Brorna proposes, and the group agrees, that she pretend to have captured the heroes and urge G’elde, the ranking Lolth cleric, to take them, now, to Lolth as prisoners. That gets them into the demi-plane, stripped and unconscious. For their weapons, Brorna once again has a solution: anticipating the victory over Talaryn, she has prepared a Leomund’s Secret Chest and a replica for the group. G’elde is unlikely to let them carry the replica, but Vhaeraun and Brorna have an inside Drow in the slave pits, a Drider who will hand the miniature chest to the party. "A simple plan, really."

The first part goes without a hitch. Brorna gives the party a sleeping draught, and while they sleep she composes herself and screams: "I have the killers of Mistress Talaryn, here in my chambers." When a guard comes down, she screams again for G’elde, threatening that "House Zollazo will no longer have my support to be the first house of Abyssal Drow Empire." G’elde, "an old and beautiful, and evil, Drow cleric," arrives: "The Empress is dead. And here are her killers". Then, with a sigh: "Why are they still alive? Kill them now." Brorna grabs her forcefully: "Do you want to deprive Lolth of the privilege to feast on the souls and body of those who destroyed her favorite?" G’elde relents ("Very well said, Brorna") and proposes to question them first; Brorna rolls her eyes and argues that Lolth, handed broken men, would assume they knew things they do not, so "It is better if we just hand them over to her as quickly as possible." G’elde agrees: "Once again, Brorna, you are wise," and hopes that "maybe Lolth will not punish House Zollazo for Talaryn’s faults.”" She plane-shifts the group to the slave pits and leaves at once, thinking, "Let Lolth and her minions deal with these monsters."

The party wakes naked in a slave pit, with six drider guards patrolling. A small box drops into the pit; KP picks it up, tries to put it in his pockets, and everyone watches it fall to the ground. "Oops." Their Drider contact Sabdra calls out, "If you are going to do anything, now is the time." Rimardo shouts, "All right guys, let’s suit up." Equipped, they climb out of the pit. As their heads reach the top, Sabdra screams, "in the name of Vhaeraun!" and fires at one of the surprised driders for 12 points of damage. Corrin hums, "a bit underwhelming there, Sabdra," then charges another drider and strikes it for 91 points. KP's lightning bolt kills the drider Corrin injured.

The driders hide in darkness, since the situation memo said the silent assassin could not strike with advantage in the dark. D’ror, the drider that survived the lightning, hits KP with a poisoned sword and a poison spray. Ka’va fails to paralyze Bayard but hits him with her bow. Tal’ra's rope of entanglement does not affect Bayard, and Dal’ty's rope misses Corrin. Rimardo's wand of polymorph is absorbed by Ka’va's ioun stone. Bayard kills Tal’ra with 41 HP of damage. Corrin dashes away from Dal’ty, dodges the attack of opportunity, and hits D’ror for 48 HP; KP's fireball scorches D’ror and wounds Ka’va. Ka’va climbs 20’ up the wall, fires three arrows that miss KP, and casts silence. Dal’ty paralyzes Bayard and then misses him; D’ror fails to paralyze Corrin, whose foresight protects him, and climbs the wall. Rimardo's lesser restoration frees Bayard. Corrin kills Dal’ty with his bow, leaving two driders on the walls.

Sabdra climbs up to attack Ka’va and misses. Ka’va paralyzes KP and hits him twice with a longbow for 22 HP; Rimardo restores him and shrugs. Bayard will not fight with a bow, so he stands next to KP and Rimardo. With Sabdra beside Ka’va, Corrin hits her for 41 HP, and KP's fire bolt for 39 HP drops Ka’va dead off the wall. D’ror's three arrows hit KP for 22 HP; Rimardo's flamestrike does 15 HP, and "Istus is not impressed with Mr. Rimardo’s decision to not use the door." Bayard taunts ("YOUR MOTHER WAS A HAMSTER AND YOUR FATHER SMELT OF ELDERBERRIES"), but even taking a dead adversary's bow a few feet away is beneath "the incredible Bayard." KP's fire bolt does 32 HP. Sabdra climbs next to D’ror, who moves away. Rimardo casts daylight. Bayard taunts again ("I fart in your general direction!"). KP fires another fire bolt, Sabdra grapples D’ror, D’ror fails to paralyze Corrin, and Rimardo waves his hand. Bayard taunts one last time, and Corrin kills the last Drider.

After healing, the group asks Sabdra where Lolth is. Sabdra opens the door of the slave pit and points to a massive ziggurat on what appears to be a floating rock inside the astral plane; Lolth has chosen not to be in her own plane of existence. The streets of her stronghold wind through buildings housing the deity's support system, the ziggurat at the center is clearly the seat of power, and a dim red sun gives ample light without counting as direct sunlight. Rimardo shouts, "Eureka! Banishment for the win!" Corrin glares. Rimardo takes it back: "If we banish Lolth, she will come back. The only solution is the magic mirror." Corrin thinks, "no, you are not going to deprive me of the biggest score of my professional life." "The strain between the healer and the killer has begun to show."

KP proposes, and the group agrees, to use the wand of polymorph to disguise themselves as female Drow and walk to the ziggurat. It works: patrols stare and move on, and drow warriors mutter about some revolt. KP tries to recall a back entrance and remembers a book, "Being a Dark Lord for Dummies or When I am the Dark Lord, I will.", a compendium of what a Dark Lord should do to avoid defeat, which includes no secret backdoor entrances. Some drow clerics may have borrowed it from the Great Gnome Library of the Kron Hills; whoever did, Lolth seems to know the guideline.

At the entrance of the ziggurat stand several Drow Clerics. One begins to cast a dispel magic, and Rimardo blocks it with his rod of absorption. Rimardo, posing as a cleric of Lolth, demands: "Who do you think you are? You see me, a cleric of Lolth, and you cast spells? I am on urgent business to see her." Asked "Why did you block our spell?", Rimardo fakes irritation: "Are you the kind that assumes that anyone who is casting a spell that targets you, does it out of kindness?" The cleric of Istus tries to deceive the clerics of Lolth into letting the party pass, and fails. "Have no fear. We will kill you. And then we will bring you back to life so you can give your message to Lolth."
