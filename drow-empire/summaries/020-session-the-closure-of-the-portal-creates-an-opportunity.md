# Chapter 20 – The closure of the portal creates an opportunity

Date: 3 Reaping 595 to 20 Reaping 595 CY (595-07-03 to 595-07-20; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
Five bearded devils emerge from the lava. The party asks KP whether he has a spell to communicate with them, and he answers, "yes, yes! It will only take 10 minutes to cast the ritual!" The devils are not inclined to wait. One declares, "you have entered the mistress' chamber! You have found the portal! You shall die!" Rimardo, delighted that the portal has been found, replies, "thank you, that was very helpful!" Another devil snaps at the first, "you fool, you will tell him things that he should not know!", and the first shrugs: "It doesn't matter," since "They will all be dead before they can do anything with this information." Rimardo sighs, "yes, yes, we will all be dead."

Corrin attacks first for 41 HP of damage; "Istus stares at the thread." The new guy finishes the injured devil with one of his arcane arrows, and it turns into "a pile of goo, or is it dust or a puddle." KP's snowball swarm does 11 and 5 HP to two devils. When Bayard's sword lands he is hit with a blast of cold, and KP realises the outer planar creatures are protected by armor of agathys; Bayard's strikes do 31 and 13 HP. One devil tries to grapple Rimardo, who escapes by strength; it then knocks Bayard prone while Rimardo shakes his head. Rimardo banishes one devil. Then the devils start throwing people into the lava: Bayard, then Mr. Corrin, who takes 18 HP and is near death, then Gruneel, who takes 18 HP, comes out singed for another 18, and kills a devil with two arrows. Two devils remain.

KP surveys the situation, runs to the lava, jumps in, grabs Corrin, and dimension doors to safety, taking 18 HP. Everyone is in shock; Bayard is delighted to see his mercenary allies so willing to sacrifice their lives for each other: "Not for him, but for each other!" Bayard fails to climb out of the lava pit, taking 18 HP of lava damage, dealing 21, and taking 15 HP of cold damage. Rimardo channels his divinity, healing Mr. Corrin for 36 HP with the rest going to KP, and mutters to Bayard, "I am sorry, Bayard, but I am all out of healing spells." The devil swings at Bayard for 6 HP, and "Rimardo is getting excited about the idea of being the new leader." Gruneel is thrown into the fire pit again for 18 HP. Mr. Corrin dashes in, flanks a devil for 25 HP and takes 15. Gruneel takes 18 HP, climbs out, and hits the devil that keeps pulling him into the lava twice for 21 HP, blinding it, and KP kills it with magic missiles for 10 HP.

Bayard is down to one hit point. Take off his armor and flee, or strike? "There is no question, the knight who is beyond reproach can do nothing but attack." He scores two critical hits and kills the last devil. Rimardo notes that he is too far away to make it in time and does not move; Gruneel, close enough, pulls Bayard out. Rimardo says, "you are alive!" and "The disappointment is etched into his face."

After a well-deserved rest, the party explores. To their horror there is no mechanism to close the portal from this side: the eastern wall disappears and the portal opens, letting the Drow enter this plane. Upstairs in the Drow quarters they search for a key, a magic item, anything, and find nothing but treasure, which pleases Rimardo and Corrin. Leaving the tower, they find Gussh. As in all civil wars there are the dead in battle and the dead in vengeance, and here "the number of revenge killings was more significant."

Bayard declares, "I defeated the enemy with my last breath!" Gussh, delighted, explains that with the Drow dead they can never enter this plane, because the Lizardfolk will guard the portal. The party is not delighted. Gussh explains that when the Drow Empire enters their realm "they send a small force," and "We shall overwhelm that force." Rimardo asks, "but didn't the Drow takeover last time?" Gussh answers that "the lizard King chose to work with the Drow," which is why they secured a place on this plane, and that "All those who support the Drow are dead." He suddenly looks left and shouts, "yes, kill that one too," then remarks smugly, "all who support the Drow are now dead."

Gussh explains the portal: at random intervals, but no sooner than once a month, it opens, using an artifact in the Abyss. It stays open only briefly; the lizardfolk will wait for the Drow to enter and, when the entrance closes, kill them, so the Drow will never secure this plane again. Corrin and KP are not pleased with any plan relying on lizardfolk for the safety of the realms, so they help close the portal room: KP uses move earth to enlarge the lava field and then drop dirt to fill the cavern, and guards are posted outside. To stay in contact, Gussh is given a sending stone.

After a few days, the demolition complete, the party returns to Captain Flipper, who exclaims, "you are alive! I know that Primus would've believed me if I said you were dead." Everyone realises that Primus would not, and Flipper knows it, and he starts repeating nervously, "I know he would have! I know!" The voyage back to the Wild Coast is uneventful. At the beach where the trip began, an agent of Primus takes the heroes to Fhuu, now "a disaster area": the Drow Empire has not assaulted the city, but the number of maimed and injured orcs there is vast.

Sir Ryton is delighted to see Bayard: "you have saved the honor of Juste-Havre. You are a man with butter on his brioche." Bayard can't help answering "burned brioche!" and explains his curious adventures with Lava. Then Halete, the local representative of the WLF, walks in: "I have a message." Bayard steps forward as leader, and Halete, self-importantly, says, "Our leader tells us we have a unique opportunity." KP asks, "Business?" Irritated, she says no: "with the Drow cut off, we can attack Fax and destroy their leadership."

Primus explains the situation: with the alliance defeated, the Drow have re-established control around Fax, and a frontal assault on the Drow armies would be impossible. Sir Ryton proposes a bold plan. The citizens of Fax are not willing to die for any ruler; they fight only because the Drow make them. Primus and Sir Ryton propose that the orcs and the alliance attack Fax, and while the bulk of Fax's forces defend the city, a small group of adventurers enters the town and crosses onto the island where the tower is, where the leader of the Drow lives. The alliance forces are too small to defeat Fax's defenders, but if the Drow leader dies, the soldiers of Fax would rebel. The army is small because of the losses at Vallaki and, more importantly, the disintegration of the coalition: after the display of Drow power, the gnomes, the elves, the halflings and many of the humans chose to avoid confrontation. Sir Ryton's 600-person army is a third of that size, and raids and attacks have halved Primus's fighting strength.

Sir Ryton asks, "what do you think of our plan? Will you do your part?" Rimardo, doubting the value of the Alliance Army, argues that they are not needed, but relents when Primus and Sir. Ryton explain that the tower guards would call for help during the assault and overwhelm the heroes. Mr. Corrin thinks he could handle it without much fuss, and Rimardo agrees, "but everyone else just sees him sharpen his sword." After much back-and-forth it is agreed: the alliance will bring the army south to Fax, bypassing the Drow forces, and during the attack the heroes will assault the fortress. If the heroes fail, the alliance will be destroyed and the area will become "a permanent part of the inter-dimensional Drow Empire." The only question is how to sneak into the city, cross the bridge and make the assault, and the party stares at the map the Alliance provides. Gruneel re-enters, showing off the shiny new sword he bought from the WLF operative, who gave him a discount because he was working for the WLF: "Instead of marking up the sword at 2x regular prices, she sold it at the list price."
