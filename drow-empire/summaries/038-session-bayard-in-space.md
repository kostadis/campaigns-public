# Chapter 38 – Bayard in Space

Date: The giants' plane, Day 1 (??? on Oerth; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
As the party sits in the control room, Rimardo declares that he has memorized a remove curse spell. The rest of the group stares at him, thinking of a story about sheep that have fled a barn and the shepherd closing the door afterward.

The portal chamber shudders and there is a sense of motion for a few minutes, then a tremor as it stops. Selzyt goes, "Oh, dear, not again." The temperature spikes, the scent of sulfur becomes disruptive, and the volcano belches poisonous gas that affects the heroes. KP starts searching his photographic memory for an explanation until Selzyt sighs, "KP, my gnomish friend, this portal opens in a volcano," and the simpler answer is "some poisonous gas that is endemic to volcanoes." KP ignores the gem and keeps searching. Bayard smiles, remarks that the first skill a Paladin learns is how to cure poison, and heals his friends.

Just as the last member of the party is healed, four earth elementals burrow into the portal chamber. Selzyt mumbles, "well, that’s awkward." KP's 3rd level lightning bolt catches two of them for only 15 points of damage. An elemental slams KP and frightens him, then slams Bayard; as it moves away, KP strikes with his dagger, and "The air is struck." Corrin is slammed but minimizes the damage, then critically hits an elemental for 84 points of damage and adds 1 with his second attack. Another elemental slams Corrin prone, and yet another burrows underground and pops up beside him for 30. Bayard kills the injured elemental with a slash of his greatsword.

Rimardo approaches the elementals surrounding Corrin, and "the halfling assassin gives him the okay" to use Rimardo's horn of blasting. The elementals, susceptible to thunder damage, take 30 points each; Corrin takes seven; the horn does not break. An elemental hits Corrin and fails to knock Bayard prone. KP's second lightning bolt deals 35 points of electrical damage. Then the elementals slam "Mr. Corrin," knock Bayard prone and hit him twice. Bayard sees three elementals, stands, "grimly stares at the beasts," and heals himself.

Rimardo analyzes the tactical situation and uses his horn of blasting on all the elementals and on his leader Bayard. It does not go as he would have liked: "the horn explodes in his hands for 40 points of damage," one elemental partially avoids the blast, and two are hit head-on. "No matter, he is a healer after all," and he uses mass-healing word on his allies. An elemental stares at Rimardo, then turns to Bayard and slams the Paladin twice "like a cheap toy in a toy store." KP maximizes a cone of cold for 69 points of damage, killing two elementals, and smiles "the smile of the accomplished." Rimardo, annoyed at KP's arrogance, points out Corrin's 84, his own exploded horn, and how Bayard sat there "acting like a pincushion" — and on reflection the party agrees that perhaps KP's bravado is justified. Selzyt says, "I want to be with a magic-user." Corrin and Bayard finish off the last monster.

The temperature rises and the sulfur fills the room again, and the party is damaged. Rimardo suggests a short rest in the portal room. Selzyt is horrified: "Are you mad? Have you lost any kind of common sense? Did you have any, to begin with?" Rimardo asks Bayard's opinion, and the reply comes that the time spent arguing is more than it would have taken to cast the spells: "It doesn’t take a gem to see that the more we argue, the more danger we expose ourselves to!" Bayard stares at the chamber, stares at the gem, and lets Rimardo heal the group.

The group exits through tunnels in the ground to the base of a 40-foot cliff. It is a dark cloudy day, and in the middle of the clearing four Drow guard cases and a tent, at attention after feeling the portal's arrival; they have not noticed the heroes. KP stares at them, trying to decide whether they are loyal to Lolth or "the masked one." Selzyt works out what he is doing: "of course, they are loyal to Lolth! They are members of the House Illal! That is the house that provides the military strength to the Drow Empire." Meanwhile Rimardo heals Bayard and Corrin with his channel divinity.

Corrin sneaks into the encampment. The Drow he targets does not see him, but another across the way does; Corrin's target dies regardless. The remaining three hit Corrin hard — 34 plus nine from a fire shield, then a second Drow casts darkness, flanks him and does 34 plus another nine. Bayard walks into the darkness, finds a Drow by smell, sound and touch, and hits him with his greatsword for 88 points of damage; only death ward keeps it alive, and the fire shield burns Bayard. The Drow nearest to Corrin drops him. KP's Sunburst dispels the darkness, blinds one Drow and burns all three. Rimardo heals Corrin. Both Drow attack KP for 84 points of damage, "and he falls." Bayard's first attack on a Drow is parried; his second smashes through the armor and kills the death-warded Drow. Rimardo's toll the dead kills another, and he uses a bonus action to heal KP for 12 hit points.

Bayard tries to jump on a barrel behind the last Drow, fails, but pushes the barrel out of position and flanks — only for "all that gymnastics" to unbalance him so he misses twice. "The Drow laughs." The Drow drops KP again with one attack and misses Bayard with the other while using his quickened darkness spell. Rimardo dispels the darkness and heals KP. The assassin sees an opening despite the Drow's powerful armor and charges in for 42 HP of damage, and Bayard kills the Drow.

Looking through the goods the Drow were protecting, the heroes find much treasure, including a map. Selzyt goes, "Hm, this might be a problem."
