# Chapter 18 – Assault on Alaneth

Date: 1 Reaping 595 to 3 Reaping 595 CY (595-07-01 to 595-07-03; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
The party heads to the village of Sylanth to meet the village elder the Drow installed, or as their ally had put it, "the village idiot." On entering, they see to their horror "what happens when lizardfolk has no females": the town is "a bachelor pad gone wrong." These are the male lizardfolk the Drow kicked out because killing them was beneath the Drow, "Not exactly the top of the food chain." On reflection KP realises that the lizardfolk do have gender roles: in this village the females kept everything clean and neat while the men hunted. The place "stinks to all high heaven," there are blood and guts everywhere, and anyone without a robust constitution would be puking. As the group enters the village, they meet Gruneel, who is skilled with the bow.

The as-yet-unnamed lizardfolk guiding them says, "We must go speak to Traz!" Traz sees the adventurers and asks, "who are these people? Are they food?" The party laughs, and Rimardo remarks under his breath, "we've been food before." Traz goes on to wonder why they are armed and whether he is expected to kill them, reckoning that with 50 of them against five, "we could kill them." Confused, Bayard asks, "are you asking him or us?", and offers food: "if he wants food, we can give them food, we have food." Traz smiles: "perhaps you do not understand, for lizardfolk humans are food." He demands to know why the party is still alive, and when Bayard offers to get rid of the Drow infestation, Traz pouts: "in Alaneth I was the village idiot, the Drow made me the village elder, I like the Drow. I will do as the Drow say. I will have you killed." He screams to the lizardfolk who have surrounded the party, "Guards! GUARDS! Kill them!"

None of them come to his aid. Bayard appeals to the lizardfolk: "given the evolving situation, perhaps you want to reconsider your alliance to the Drow." After conferring in their own language, the one who met the party in the jungle says what Bayard says makes sense, "but we still have a village leader." Rimardo tells Bayard that Mr. Corrin will be glad to deal with that problem, and Bayard says, "he could be food." Rimardo, confused, asks, "Is that Corrin's cue?" Mr. Corrin, excited to finally do what he trained for, attacks Traz, who is dead before he can respond. Corrin remarks to Rimardo, "finally, Bayard tells me to do what I'm paid to do and lets me kill someone"; everyone but Rimardo sees only "a very creepy smile on the halfling covered in blood." Bayard, acting surprised, says, "I didn't expect you to take me literally." The chronicle observes: "Plausible deniability is especially vital to paladins when they order assassinations." Bayard concludes stoically, "what is done, is done."

The guiding lizardfolk looks at his dead leader and asks, "So, what's your plan for getting rid of the Drow?" The party had not thought that far. After much staring, Rimardo and Bayard convince the lizardfolk to join an assault on Alaneth. Rimardo worries what will happen when the Drow learn their stooge Traz is dead; the lizardfolk tell him, "oh well, just tell him he died, and we ate him," because "Drow are foolish and think lizardfolk eat lizardfolk." Overnight the lizardfolk build canoes amid woodcutting, high-fiving, music and smiling, "the 1980s training montages" come to life.

In the morning the question is where the forces should meet. The party intends to go to Telsn but can only bring half the new army by canoe. Once the lizardfolk confirm that the only predator they fear is the Drow, Bayard declares that the lizardfolk should walk through the jungle; the new archer is pleased, since that will let them prepare with the army in Telsn. Rimardo asks, "when will you leave Capt. Flipper?" The captain answers, a little too sincere and anxious, that he will stay until the mission is done or the heroes are dead, because "The Primus and Sir Ryton of the Juste-Havre" asked him to. Rimardo thinks, "that is more loyalty than I have to Bayard." KP, watching intently, reads it differently: the captain is not loyal to the party, Primus or Ryton; "he is terrified of the orc leader of Fhuu."

At Telsn the party meets the shaman who fled Alaneth. The reunion goes badly at first: Gussh is not pleased to see people who betrayed the true God of the lizardfolk return to his village. Bayard mediates and convinces them to stage a raid on the Drow. When the new guy asks for maps, Gussh provides a rough sketch of Alaneth. Sneaking in alone looks too dangerous in a town of 500 lizardfolk and an unknown number of Drow, so the plan becomes a diversion by the lizardfolk of Sylanth and Telsn. Gussh makes clear that if the party kills S'kazs, the lizardfolk will quickly turn on the Drow, or at least stop fighting.

Next morning the party heads for Alaneth. Mr. Corrin creeps through the deep grass to scout and finds the villagers of Alaneth, having heard the 250 lizardfolk approach, arrayed for battle. He sneaks back and says, "maybe you guys should attack," and Rimardo conveys the suggestion; the rebels of Telsn and Sylanth "packed." As the battle rages, the party slips around the fighting towards the Drow tower and is blocked by a small number of lizardfolk and some "buff lizardfolk." Rimardo holds them and KP uses a fireball "to barbeque the rest." The new guy, the arcane archer, finds to his horror that his bow does not work: the lizardfolk's armor is immune to piercing. Asked if he has a sword, he says, "yeah, a dagger." Daggers also do piercing damage. No matter: Bayard kills with The Great Sword Tueur, Mr. Corrin kills with abandon with a magical rapier, Rimardo keeps concentration through the lizardfolk's hellish rebukes, "perhaps the sternest form of rebuke," and the new guy proves useful after all with arcane arrows that do mystical damage. The party wins.

But "a fireball on a battlefield is distinctive": it signals a magic-user, and that some other player is on the board. Some of the lizardfolk fighting Gussh's forces in Alaneth head towards the conflagration. Before rushing for the keep to avoid being overwhelmed, Mr. Corrin searches the dead bodies, and the new guy gets a club.
