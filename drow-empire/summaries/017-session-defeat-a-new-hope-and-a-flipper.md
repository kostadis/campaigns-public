# Chapter 17 – Defeat, a new hope, and a flipper

Date: 25 Wealsun 595 to 1 Reaping 595 CY (595-06-25 to 595-07-01; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
With the Drow general Namyn dead and the spy now an ally, the party surveyed the battlefield. The Drow, having received the false instructions, began an orderly retreat, and the alliance forces used it to make good their escape. But the Drow had more surprises: to the north a dragon attacked the stragglers of Sir Ryton, and to the south a shadow demon attacked the orcs. Rimardo and Mr. Corrin, though badly injured and short on spells, wanted a measure of revenge on the Drow Army and convinced Bayard to attack the shadow demon. Bayard agreed; after all, he had promised "on his honor as a man with butter on his brioche" that he would defend the orcs.

The party charged the shadow demon as it killed the last stragglers still on the field. Mr. Corrin tried to sneak up on it but, like the General, the demon was not surprised: "You thought you could surprise the Great Xazlozuhn!" Mr. Corrin, impressed, still hit it for 21 HP with one hand and 1 HP with the other. Xazlozuhn conjured an incorporeal creature beside Mr. Corrin that let the demon strike the distracted Mr. Corrin for 19 HP; Rimardo healed him, Bayard charged, and Xazlozuhn healed himself. KP recalled from his days of study in the vast library of the City of Greyhawk that this kind of demon is immune to anything other than magic missiles. Mr. Corrin struck for 23 HP; the demon summoned its creature again and hit Mr. Corrin for 19, leaving him at 1 HP, smiling. Rimardo used a channel divinity to heal him, and Xazlozuhn growled.

Bayard, finally in range, smiled: "Let the big boys in!" Mr. Corrin mimed "USE SMITE!" and Bayard shouted as he swung, "I have none left!", yet did 27 HP of damage. The demon healed itself to 29 HP. KP's thunderwave pushed Xazlozuhn away as Bayard hit him, dropping him to 10 HP, and Mr. Corrin, without advantage, did 9 more, leaving the beast at 1 HP. "The creature, unlike Mr. Corrin, does not smile." It summoned its creature once more and attacked Mr. Corrin, who used his reaction on the damage, which let the demon leave without an attack of opportunity. Rimardo's Toll the Dead did not affect it, and Xazlozuhn healed himself again. Then Bayard closed the distance and scored a critical hit, killing the Shadow Demon, which screamed, "I will get my revenge!" As it vanished in a puff of smoke, Xazlozuhn mumbled something about contracts; KP worked out that the demon had been under contract and was angry about the terms, perhaps that this was not supposed to be a dangerous assignment.

Before leaving, Mr. Corrin checked the pile of dust for treasure and found none; Rimardo silently agreed that Istus is not happy. The party ran through the woods, killing a few pickets on the way, and reached the war council of the Anti-Drow Alliance at the agreed rallying point. No one was happy, and everyone was furious at the elf who had promised the Drow would be pushovers. When Bayard asked, "So, how did the battle go?", the generals answered in a cacophony that it went very badly. As the Army approached the battlefield the weather had turned cold, and the Drow Army was equipped for cold while they were not; the armies stood and fought until exhaustion set in, then fled. Primus Yagrat said, "Only sheer force of will of the commanders prevented a rout," along with "the very curious decision of the Drow general to order a retreat"; without that withdrawal the armies of the alliance "would have been vaporized."

Sir Ryton asked Bayard whether he knew anything, and Bayard revealed all: the outer planes, the portal, and the secret alliance. Sir Ryton exploded: "This is insane!" The people of Juste-Havre had signed up to fight the Drow of the Underdark, not an interplanar force that summons demons and dragons and changes the weather, and "Unless someone does something about the portal, we will not fight." The halflings shouted that peaceful co-existence with marauding forces of evil is possible "if you are willing to shrink yourself into a sufficiently small target." Every member of the alliance began shouting: some wanted to hide, some to flee and let other states deal with the Drow, others to resist.

Bayard asked, "What if we shut down the portal?" Stunned silence followed, and someone asked whether he would be willing to do that. Bayard smiled: "With appropriate help." Rimardo clarified: "Magical armor, weapons, the usual." Sir Ryton, somber, asked in a deep voice, "Bayard, for the honor of Juste-Havre, you will take on the quest to destroy the portal?" Bayard nodded yes, then added, "If there is some appropriate equipment." Rimardo chimed in, "It's not for us, it's for the success of the mission," while Mr. Corrin, sharpening his knives, asked Rimardo whether they had found the only uncharismatic paladin in Oerth. Sir Ryton and the Primus agreed to the plan. Primus grunted that they would find someone in Fax who knows where the Drow are coming from, since "Many boats come and go in these waters." Sir Ryton promised, "We shall go and get you some equipment, Bayard!", Bayard beamed "There you go, guys!", and Rimardo insisted, "Not just for Bayard!" Sir Ryton offered Rimardo the tools he needed if he promised on his honor to defeat the Drow; Rimardo said yes, and "Poor Sir Ryton does not realize that Rimardo's definition of honor and his are not … the same." Primus and the alliance members equipped the party with magic weapons, and Bayard was given the Great Sword Tueur, "a legendary blade in these parts."

A few days later the party reconvened at the Primus's castle. The alliance leadership, shaken by the defeat at the Ruins of Vallaki, had regrouped. They knew an assault would provoke an overwhelming Drow response they lacked the resources to meet, so they would use guerilla warfare to keep the Drow focused on maintaining order rather than expanding. Alliance intelligence had identified the island where the Drow send their slaves and treasure, and interrogations of captured Drow revealed an extra-planar portal there connecting to the Drow reserves. A robust, covert expedition might cut the Drow off from their resources, and a ship had been procured.

The party was led to a small boat off the Wild Coast and introduced to Captain Flipper, a long-time smuggler with a long relationship with the lizardfolk on "the Wasted Isle." He and the lizardfolk King S'kazs used to be partners in a slave-trading business, though according to Capt. Flipper the Lizard King has not been responding to business outreaches. Rather than head straight for the main village of Alaneth, the party surveyed the island and found, to Capt. Flipper's surprise, three villages; last time there had only been Alaneth. Asked whether he would stick around, Capt. Flipper looked sheepish and said the Drow had encroached on his operations and the Orcs had made him a serious offer. The party recalled that the last time they tried to free someone, it was a slave trader not working for the Drow: "The Drow Empire wants to be the only slave operation."

The group chose to explore Sylanth first. Landing on a beach out of sight, they sneaked towards the village and found signs of lizards, broken twigs, scat, dead carcasses, and a 6’7” lizardfolk about 20' ahead of them, injured. KP and Mr. Rimardo argue over what to do: "KP – we should help him!" against "Mr. Rimardo– No, we should kill him!", then "We are trying to find allies!" against "We are trying to find the portal!", ending with Rimardo's "You would say that!" Bayard was asked to intervene: "I will not kill someone who has done me no harm." KP suggested they try kindness, inspected the lizardfolk, who was carrying a carcass over his shoulder, and found him injured, and Bayard volunteered to heal him: "It is a good show of goodness!"

The lizardfolk asked who they were ("I am here to learn!" said KP) and whether the Drow had sent them. KP noticed the lizardfolk feared the Drow and said they were investigating the Drow. Reassured, the lizardfolk told all. The lizardfolk of Sylanth are exiles from the main town of Alaneth, banished for crimes the Drow overlords did not think worth killing them for; Sylanth is in fact a prison isle for the Drow with 50 or so lizardfolk, kept docile because everyone there has relatives in Alaneth. The other village on the main island, Telsn, has a different story. The Drow emerged from the ground after a violent earthquake, and S'kazs quickly swore fealty to them, seeing a chance to expand lizardfolk dominion. The village shaman, Gussh, warned by his deity, disagreed and escaped with 200 or so supporters before he could be killed. The Drow do not know about Telsn, because S'kazs, uncomfortable killing a shaman of the lizardfolk gods, lied that the losers were eaten after their defeat; the Drow were not convinced, but S'kazs insisted it was the lizardfolk way.

The lizardfolk said they must talk to the village leader Traz, whom the Drow installed. When Rimardo asked why, the answer was one word: "Because he is stupid." The Drow want stupid lizardfolk to rule; "Dull lizardfolk work for Drow, not like Gussh." Over the map, the party debated sneaking up on Alaneth or forging a grand alliance of lizardfolk against S'kazs, and after much debate decided to try to form an army out of the alienated lizardfolk.
