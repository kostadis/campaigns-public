# Chapter 12 – The Fall of Vallaki

Date: 16 Flocktime 595 to 19 Wealsun 595 CY (595-05-16 to 595-06-19; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
Bayard, Corrin, Rimardo and KP defeat the marauding gangs in the city that had meant to distract the guards at the gate. Hearing warnings that the gate is in danger of falling, they rush forward, defeating a group of Dark Elves with Quaggoths and then a group of hobgoblins. The cost is high: both Corrin and KP end up on death's door. "But it was all for naught." The defenders of Vallaki cannot stop the combination of Drow magic and the sheer numbers directed at them. The western gate falls, and the forces of the Free City of Fax enter the town of Vallaki and begin their rampage. "The pillage was great. The houses burned. Death and mayhem. There was nothing to be done."

Only Rimardo and Bayard are still alive at this point, carrying the bodies of their fallen comrades: Rimardo carries Corrin, and it is up to Bayard to take KP. Rimardo would have been okay with leaving KP behind, as he likes to point out: "Bayard, you're the boss, you want a new magic-user, I am cool with that." Caught up in the chaos of the fleeing victims, Bayard knows there is only one decisive action: reach Fhuu and get the Primus to join an alliance against the Drow. Surely the Orc leader knows that if he does not wage war on the Drow, his Orc city is next.

They head towards the north gate while the looters of Vallaki methodically pillage the town. As they pass the gate carrying their comrades, a group of townsfolk recognise them and take pity on them. Their leader is Seth, "an average build, copper-haired, blue-eyed gnome, who by the looks of it looked like a blacksmith." He offers his cart and oxen to carry the wounded and dying far from the burning town. When the villagers realise the Drow army will encircle the city and slaughter them as well, they scatter from the main roads, and Seth leads them along secret forest paths.

A Quaggoth finds them. Rimardo is cool with taking Corrin, letting Bayard take KP if he wants, and running; after all, the Quaggoth would probably slaughter Seth and his family first, giving them plenty of time to escape. But Bayard, with "vengeance in his eyes, justice burning in his soul," grabs his sword and attacks, though he has no healing spells and no allies. "His brioche would have butter." The battle is fierce. At the very end the Quaggoth is left with 3 HP and Bayard with 7 HP, and everything depends on who hits first. Bayard kills the monster.

Whether Mr. Rimardo would have simply taken Mr. Corrin and run is "left as an unanswered mystery." Perhaps he would have consulted Istus, and Istus would have directed him to pull on the thread he was still following, "and not to abandon or cut it – quite yet. Or not." Either way, Mr. Rimardo is not happy. Vallaki has fallen, Mr. Bayard did not see that as an opportunity to plunder the town, and the failure to acquire wealth in defending Vallaki made the entire endeavour worthless in Rimardo's eyes.

The adventurers flee far enough into the forests. Mr. Seth leaves them to their own devices and heads to a town called Crossroads, where his brother, Burgher Teeble, is in charge. Standing in the forest gloom, recovered and healed, Bayard says they should go to the Orc City of Fhuu. KP, having discovered that Bayard has the magical ability to increase magic resistance for anyone within 10 feet, now never leaves Bayard's side.

They do not know how to get to Fhuu or what to do once there, when Captain Amos, Humph's henchman and a defender of the gate, stumbles through the woods. "It was a slaughter," he tells them. "The Drow did the heavy lifting and then sent the Faxian troops to slaughter innocent women and children. The butchery was horrific. The town was in flames. I escaped only because they thought I was dead." He is disgusted by the betrayal of the WLF, who had promised help to free the coast and are now allied with the Faxian armies. He describes the leader of the WLF troops, a woman who sounds disturbingly like Halete, and spits out: "They betrayed us!"

Asked what to do next, he says: "There is only one path for us, to get Primus Yagrat, the ruler of the city of the orcs of Fhuu to lead an alliance against the City of Fax." Bayard almost chokes. Orcs leading an alliance? Orcs doing anything but pillage and murder, when the Wild Coast has already suffered so much bloodshed from Orc armies? But Amos insists this is the only force that can stop the madness. Bayard agrees, and asks Amos to lead them to the City of Fhuu.

As they approach, the weather changes and storm clouds block the sun; Rimardo knows this cannot be good. On the road, KP detects a trap in the dirt some 20' ahead. The party convinces him to let them first kill the forces trying to trap them and then examine the mechanical trap. KP is disappointed but agrees. Corrin creeps up and attacks the Drow forces, "7 Drow and 3 Quaggoth." The battle is vicious and, in the end, victorious.

As things settle down and the party begins recovering, the storm breaks in flashes of lightning, and at the fringe of their vision there is the glint of steel. A voice announces: "Filzyr's the name, and bounty hunting is my game, and Xhalh has put a massive bounty on the fools that killed Walton and dared to try and stop his conquests." The battle is joined.
