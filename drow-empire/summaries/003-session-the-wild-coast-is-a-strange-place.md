# Chapter 3 – The Wild Coast is a strange place.

Date: 5 Flocktime 595 to 7 Flocktime 595 CY (595-05-05 to 595-05-07; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
The chapter opens on the Wild Coast: "A bastion of independent spirits, heroes, scoundrels, and villains." After the Greyhawk Wars, the north swore fealty to the City of Greyhawk and the south was absorbed by the Orc Empire of the Pomarj, but the homage of a ruler and order are two different things. Adventuring groups are just as fluid: "A triton can be saving your ass one day and going for a swim and never coming back the next day." Fortunately, Farjvad is the kind of place that attracts paladins.

None is more famous than Bayard, whose legendary exploits killing a few orcs earned him the moniker "Without fear and without reproach." At the tavern in his hometown, where his kinsmen drink, he is known instead as "Without butter and without brioche." Brioche is a bread common in the Wild Coast, one of the area's few delicacies, and a meal without butter and without brioche is a tasteless one. Looking to grow his fame, Bayard hears about the appearance of a Drow.

The chapter then tells the story of the Drow, a legendary evil force in Oerth that parents use to frighten their children: a branch of the elves that went evil eons ago. The Elves disagreed over how to deal with the younger races — one group believed Elves should rule, the other wanted to withdraw — and might still be arguing but for Araushnee, consort of Corellon Larethian, better known as Lolth. Araushnee, goddess of destiny and artisans, became obsessed with her fate and feared the new gods the younger races would force into existence. When Corellon was silent because he did not know the right answer, she created her alter ego, Lolth, and offered the Elves who wanted to rule the power they needed if they would forswear Corellon. They did, and those who worshipped the new cult became known as the Drow. Araushnee told Corellon all was well while she undermined him, but when the Drow moved to kill Corellon's priests, he realised what had been done and banished her. The clerics of Lolth lost their power, and Corellon's priests led the Elves in a war that expelled the Drow from the sunlit lands. Lolth fled with her son Vhaeraun; the two plotted revenge, but her rage, hatred, bitterness and paranoia were without end. She blamed all men for her loss of power and made the Drow a matriarchal monotheistic society devoted to her, in which men existed to breed and fight and only women could rule. Her hatred of other sources of power made magic-using Drow rare, and magic users tended to be men. There was no room for Vhaeraun, another god and a man; Lolth tried to kill him and failed, and he escaped.

These stories are centuries old. In modern times the Drow are a rumour, discussed by sages in the vast library of Greyhawk. They may exist deep in the Underdark, but no Drow walk the surface lands: the sun hurts them, and the Elves would work hard to destroy them. So a story of Drow surfacing so near the Elvish kingdom of Celene is something Bayard has to check out. His childhood plan had been to investigate the ruins of the Temple of Elemental Evil, but stories from Hommlet said it had been destroyed 14 years ago, and Farjvad was much closer to home.

Bayard is frustrated to find that a group of adventurers has just defeated a Drow necromancer in Farjvad — but this Drow was not alone; he led a group of Drow hiding in an outpost nearby. Sitting at a table in the Inn at the River, Bayard overhears the adventurers talking about more Drow to be defeated. Their Paladin has left "for wetter climes," so Bayard offers his services and the group accepts him.

Following the necromancer's map, they reach a large pool of clear water in a dense forest. Bayard jumps in, finds a tunnel, swims through and sees a sizeable cavernous opening, but fails to surface quietly, gasping and thrashing. He sees 8 tribal warriors get out of their beds shouting to prepare to attack, judges 8 a considerable number, and swims back as two javelins fall harmlessly behind him. The caves are natural, hollowed out to be more livable. Reepicheep looks for another entrance, finds none, and says he has no interest in going into a room full of prepared warriors, so the group spends the night outside watching; no one comes out.

In the morning they explode out of the water with "Shock-And-Awe tactics." This time the warriors are surprised in their beds — "They are not morning people." Boradoon's Thunder Wave hits three warriors; Bayard kills the one that made its saving throw, Reepicheep punctures an artery on another, and Dimitrov kills a third. One warrior aims a spear at Reepicheep screaming "Die Rat! I hate rats!", and Reepicheep insists he is a mouse. The warriors shout as they fight: "you will never defeat the Drow Empire!!!", "The demoness will protect us! The demoness wants your blood!", and "if we don’t kill you, the empress will punish us!!!" Reepicheep smirks and asks, "Some?", and a flummoxed warrior answers "No, ALL OF IT!!!!!" before Reepicheep kills him. Dimitrov shatters a warrior's skull before he is knocked out, and Bayard and Reepicheep kill the last two. Among the bodies the group finds coins and a horrifying truth: these are local men from the surrounding area who have taken to worshipping the Drow.

In the hallway to the south, a green slime falls on Bayard, who dashes back into the pool screaming "It burns, it burns!" while Reepicheep chirps "I can’t believe it’s not butter!" Bayard then trips a rope that fires a crossbow bolt at him, dodges it at the last moment, and keeps marching in front because "it’s my job."

In the next room, piles of bones animate into skeletons. Everyone expects the cleric to turn the undead; Dimitrov instead uses guiding bolt, twice, while everyone stares. Reepicheep is surrounded and repeatedly misses, Bayard flanks and cleaves, and Boradoon uses witch bolt and fire bolt. Dimitrov finally turns the remaining undead, and Reepicheep flanks and kills the last skeleton. The party takes a short rest. At the only exit, Boradoon investigates the door, finds nothing, and insists the paladin open it — "Dude, I am a magic user, Paladins open the door." Bayard opens it.

Inside are 6 creatures: three Drow and three humans. The party's minds race: it is almost unheard of to see three Drow, bizarre to hear Drow talk about the Abyss, and the legends say the Drow never had allies. The battle begins 4v6. Boradoon charges into the middle and casts thunder wave — "Let’s see if we can get rid of these mooks." — but stays in the centre of the room and is surrounded by three human guards, while the party mumbles about "Clerics who cannot turn and Magic-Users who tank." Reepicheep kills a guard ("Aargh, the mistress will not be pleased I was killed by a rat"), and Bayard kills another wounded guard. A Drow announces "I am a Drow" and uses faerie fire on the party. The Drow Initiates hammer the party with magic missile: one drops Reepicheep unconscious, one counterspells Boradoon, one misty steps behind Bayard. Dimitrov kills an initiate with his mace. The other initiate kills Boradoon the bladesinger with magic missile. Reepicheep bleeds out, Boradoon dies of internal bleeding, and the Drow Initiate kills Dimitrov. Bayard kills the Drow and then the initiate, and is left alone against the last guard.

"Now it is 1v1." Bayard trades blows, weighs whether to heal or attack, and finally lays hands on himself; the guard's next hit proves the decision "without reproach," and Bayard kills the guard with 11 HP of damage. With the room empty of hostiles, he heals Dimitrov. The party finds much plunder. Bayard and Dimitrov carry their two fallen comrades back toward Farjvad, and in a clearing Dimitrov performs "the last rites for Boradoon and Reepicheep," consecrating them to his Gods.

At the River Drink in the late evening, Alexia Boulderdown greets them with delight: "Where is the rat? And the fish?" When Bayard tells their sad tale, the whole bar raises a toast. But when he talks about Drow, Alexia sneers that "The Drow are tales your momma used to scare you," warns that talk of another power is dangerous because this part of the Wild Coast is free only because it lies between the city of Greyhawk, the Elves, and the Orcs, and says of the Drow-worshippers: "They were fools." She sends them to sleep. After 16 hours of sleep, Dimitrov tells Bayard, "I’m done," and leaves. As Bayard sits alone, Alexia tells him that some adventurers have heard of a Paladin who fought a bunch of Drow to a standstill, and offers to introduce him.

## NPCs
### Bayard
A paladin known as "Without fear and without reproach" for killing a few orcs, and as "Without butter and without brioche" among his kinsmen. Looking to grow his fame, he joins the party, swims into the cave alone, leads from the front through slime and crossbow trap, and ends as the last one standing, killing the final guard alone and then healing Dimitrov.

### Boradoon
The party's magic-user, a bladesinger. He opens with Thunder Wave in both fights, uses witch bolt and fire bolt, refuses to open the door ("Paladins open the door"), and ends up surrounded in the centre of the room. He is killed by an initiate's magic missile and dies of internal bleeding.

### Reepicheep
A mouse who objects to being called a rat. He finds no second entrance to the cave, chirps "I can’t believe it’s not butter!" at Bayard's slime, and fights with "style and valor and panache" while missing often. He is knocked unconscious by magic missile and bleeds out.

### Dimitrov
The cleric, who uses guiding bolt and sacred flame instead of turning the undead until late in the fight. He kills an initiate with his mace, runs out of spells, and is killed by the Drow Initiate before Bayard heals him. He performs the last rites for his fallen comrades, then after 16 hours of sleep says, "I’m done," and leaves.

### The tribal warriors
8 local men from the surrounding area who have taken to worshipping the Drow. They invoke the Drow Empire, "the demoness" and "the empress" as they fight.

### The Drow and the Drow Initiates
Three Drow in the inner room. One announces "I am a Drow" and uses faerie fire; the initiates use magic missile, counterspell and misty step, and one speaks of "my mistress." They kill Boradoon and Dimitrov and leave Reepicheep to bleed out before Bayard kills them.

### The human guards
Three guards fighting with the Drow. A guard hurled by Boradoon's thunder wave nearly lands on one of his allies, who screams "Damn you, you crazy old coot."; another dies lamenting that "the mistress will not be pleased I was killed by a rat." The last fights Bayard one-on-one and is killed.

### Alexia Boulderdown
At the River Drink, delighted to see the survivors until Bayard talks about Drow. She dismisses the Drow as tales, fears that talk of another power will bring war, and calls the Drow-worshippers fools. She offers to introduce Bayard to new adventurers.
