# Chapter 6 – Journey to Fax

Date: 9 Flocktime 595 to 12 Flocktime 595 CY (595-05-09 to 595-05-12; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
The party returns to the village of Farjvad. Before entering it they interrogate Lyless, "the Drow leader of the blockade." They learn very quickly that she is from a different plane, and that her home plane is the Abyss. They learn that "there is an artifact that the Drow use to travel between the planes," that Lyless does not know where it is, and that "the only person who does is Xhalh, the leader of the Drow." The last detail she reveals is that "the grandmaster of Fax is working with the Drow because of the hostage situation."

When the party enters Farjvad, they learn that no one believes in the Drow.

After the Drow have been defeated, Branigan Cartwright approaches the party. Branigan calls it "a great victory for the free people of the Wild Coast," claiming that "We have managed to prevent the Orc armies from invading deeper into our lands and averted war." The victory, Branigan says, is tenuous, and the party must go to Fax and warn their allies. In Branigan's telling the threat is the elves: "The elvish kingdom sees our weakness and found some dark-skinned elves they chose to call," with the name Drow put in quotation marks, and they are "planning to invade and seize this land." At Fax, Branigan says, there is "another smithy called Halete" whom they must find, who "can lead you deeper into the resistance against the would-be rulers of the Wild Coast." Branigan closes with a plea, "please stop talking about the Drow," and with travel advice: "The road is safest."

Carc Mistsplitter, the Major and ruler of Farjvad, finds the party next and addresses Bayard: "You have won a great victory for the Orc Empire, Bayard! You are without fear and without reproach." He commands Bayard to go to Fax and "warn the Grand Master of what you have seen," because "The Elves will attack Fax as it is a free city caught between Safeton and the Orcish Empire." His travel advice matches Branigan's: "The road is the safest way to Fax."

Alexia Boulderdown is described as "the only person who sees the threat for what it is." She has lived her life in the Wild Coast and just wants to survive, and the chapter notes that "This isn’t the first time some legendary evil power has shown up out of nowhere trying to carve out a kingdom in the Wild Coast." She does not care about that; what worries her is how the Drow ended up here and what their plans are, and she knows that asking those questions could be dangerous. She tells the party that "Dimitrov told us about Xhalh, another Drow who is in charge. And we didn’t find him." She names Fax as an obvious target, "a free city used by all the local powers to advance their agendas," listing "The Elves, the free city of Greyhawk, the Orcish Empire, freebooters, pirates, criminals, and liberation movements." Her advice contradicts the other two: "The river is the safest. Every criminal element uses the road."

The party also learns a few things about Fax. It is run by "a guy named Tolub." There is a liberation group in the Wild Coast "dedicated to freeing the land from the City of Greyhawk and the control of the Orcs," led by a woman close to Tolub, "a lawful good cleric of Heironeous." Fax "used to be this crazy messy place with no rules, where the basic revenue strategy was to pillage some town nearby," but over the last year a militia and "the beginnings of an army" have been put in place, along with more taxation, rules and controls. Tolub has gone missing from the city, and only pronouncements and notices in his name are published. Crimes once punished with fines now bring confinement in a prison whose location is unknown, and "Asking for information about the prisoner can lead to death." Carc tells of a contact in the city militia called Walton, and Cynthia Branigan tells of a contact who is also a smithy.

Following Alexia's advice that the safest travel is by the rivers, the party arrives in Fax after an uneventful journey. Approaching the city walls they stumble on a riot. They stay out of the way, do not get involved, and do not get arrested, but their equipment is taken from them. Fortunately they know a smithy, and they buy all the equipment they need from Halete. From Halete they learn that the cleric leading the resistance "used to work for Tolub in a village in Nulb as his sheriff."

After buying their equipment the party goes for a walk and is jumped by a set of thugs. And owls. As the chapter puts it, "Healing owls is the thing of the hour."
