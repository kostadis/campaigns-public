# Chapter 16 – Victory and Defeat

Date: 25 Wealsun 595 CY (595-06-25; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
The armies of the Alliance make their way through the Wild Coast, travelling from Fhuu through the villages in the mountains leading to Vallaki, which sits on the main road to Fax. The final battle begins. Primus and Sir Ryton had agreed with the Independent Contractors to wait until morning and split their forces to surround the Drow encampment.

The Alliance forces advanced confident of victory, because according to Lord Virmaer of the Elves the Drow were "push-overs easily crushed," and until now events had unfolded exactly as he had foretold: the Drow forces melted before the Alliance. Then the battle with the main forces outside the destroyed Vallaki began, and "the pushovers turned out to be a lot more deadly." The Drow had found a cleric who could cast control weather. The weather turned freezing, the Alliance forces were unprepared, and, facing exhaustion and death, they broke and ran. Primus to the north and Sir Ryton to the south tried to prevent a rout.

The adventurers were caught behind the front lines at the western gate of Vallaki. After defeating an advance guard of hobgoblins that tried to enter Vallaki through the west gate, they saw the calamity facing the Alliance armies, and while scanning the battlefield they noticed a Drow commander they later learned was Jaezton of House Illal. Jaezton's task was to protect the center and ensure no Alliance forces used the city to escape: his troops would seal the western gate and fan out hunting any human or orc troops trying to hide from the slaughter. The party, "wise to his tricks, or maybe just lucky," thwarted his plans by crushing his advance guard.

Jaezton was some 600 yards away. The party first thought to advance quietly; instead, Bayard charged. This startled Jaezton, who wondered what "the adamantine-clad fighter" was doing charging straight at him, and whether it was one of his own guards he had forgotten. As Bayard charged, the party loosed a volley of arrows, and Corrin unleashed volleys of his own. Jaezton used darkness to thwart them, but "Sheer dumb luck allowed the arrows to hit the Drow hiding in the dark," so by the time Bayard arrived the Drow were badly weakened. KP isolated the commander from his troops with Otiluke's resistant sphere, and trapped inside it Jaezton "helplessly watched his soldiers die."

The group turned to Jaezton thinking they had found the general in charge of the whole Drow assault, but "Jaezton was but a minion." Threatened, he "spilled a small number of beans": the general in charge was Namyn of House Illal, not Nimh, who had led the initial assault on Vallaki; the full power of the Drow Empire is still hidden; and he gave up Namyn's current location. His warnings of superweapons at first sounded like idle boasts, but as the party sneaked towards Namyn they saw white dragons approaching the battlefield from a distance.

This time there was no heroic charge across an open field; the party sneaked on Namyn's position. Five Drow Initiates guarded Namyn, who seemed curiously relaxed and was using an invisible homunculus to communicate with his armies. The party decided not to kill the creature, which "turned out to be a fortuitous decision." Corrin tried to sneak in to attack and KP prepared a fireball, but Corrin tripped an alarm spell the Initiates had prepared. KP's fireball nonetheless devastated the Drow. In the battle the Initiates targeted Corrin, "downing him," and Bayard, bursting in to kill an Initiate, "too was killed from a fusillade of magic missiles." Rimardo had to heal both; after recovering Bayard he made it clear that this was a courtesy and a necessary step to ensure Corrin's survival. Namyn then knocked Corrin unconscious and used darkness to hide.

KP, "the expert tactician," exploited the dome shape of the darkness and cast spells from behind it; the Initiates could not see through the darkness, but he could, thanks to his gem of true seeing. He was cheeky enough to hide in the zone of darkness, daring Namyn to kill him, and hiding there neutralised the Initiates' counter-spell capability. Frustrated that his darkness had become a tactical advantage for the Independent Contractors, Namyn dispelled it, which let Bayard see him. Bayard "took no chances, killing him with his smiting ability." The remaining Initiates were not difficult to kill, though the last one misty-stepped away from Corrin and tried to finish him with a Fire bolt, but missed.

Then a Drow emerged from the shadows offering parley. His name was Valnozz, and he demanded to speak to the leader and smartest member of the party; Rimardo was about to defer to Bayard but stepped forward, while Bayard restrained himself from killing Valnozz, though sorely tempted. Valnozz explained that "The Drow on this plane is the weak variety," trapped in the Underdark and fantasising about revenge on the sun, whereas "the Drow Empire of my mistress is much grander": her base is on the Abyss, and this plane is "simply a place we harvest resources for our real wars with the demonic forces on the Abyss." The Great Mistress, he said, makes plans with other Deities to secure her power. He shared this because they are both her enemy: "I am a follower of Vhaeraun," whose only ambition is to overthrow Lolth's clerics. Rimardo jumped in to explain who Vhaeraun is: the son of Araushnee and Corellon Larethian, who held the unique view among Drow that males and females were equally valuable and was prayed to mostly by Drow males who sought a better life than slavery under Lolth's matriarchy. Araushnee is, of course, Lolth. KP then jumped in: "We must learn more!" Valnozz snorted and continued.

Xhalh oversees this planar invasion, and while the portal is intact she has access to all the resources of the Great Mistress. "The portal is like a caravan," an artifact able to take 50-100 people along a circuit around the planes; it always starts in Freedahl in the Abyss, and control of the city and the portal is key to the Drow Empire's power. The portal can be broken, and once broken, "Xhalh will have to go back to the Island to fix it with the key." The party would find the portal on the island in the Nyr Dyv. The dragons they see, he added, will soon go back to the Abyss as part of the Drow Empire's forces. When Rimardo tried to convince Valnozz to lead them to Xhalh, he laughed: "Do you think I can penetrate her spells and her paranoia? You poor fools." Instead he would signal the retreat of the Drow forces, claiming Namyn had achieved his objectives. The chapter remarks that "killing the homunculus was a good idea," since Valnozz can send the message to the armies and no one will question the source, although it earlier says the party decided not to kill the creature. He would also explain to Xhalh how "once again, the accursed Independent Contractors, thwarted us," so that the party's armies could fight another day. His parting instruction: "You must close the portal before you attempt to take Fax by force. And you must get the portal key from Xhalh." Then he disappeared into the trees.

As the party stood in shock, a terrible sight greeted them to the south. At the fringes of the retreating army, the shadows near an isolated soldier coalesced into a figure that struck him down, then another soldier a few yards away, and another. The Drow had summoned a Shadow Demon to aid them. The party was too weak to fight such monsters, but from their vantage point they could see that the bulk of the Alliance forces had escaped.
