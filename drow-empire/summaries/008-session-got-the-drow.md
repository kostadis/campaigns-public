# Chapter 8 – Got the Drow!

Date: 12 Flocktime 595 to 13 Flocktime 595 CY (595-05-12 to 595-05-13; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
The chapter opens with the party standing in a basement, having rescued slaves "who turned out to be slavers." Mr. Corrin attempts to sneak upstairs and fails. Two Drow were in the kitchen for a mid-day snack and "are not surprised," and combat erupts. The first Drow, Hadnyl Illal, hits Mr. Corrin with his hand-held crossbow for 5 HP; Corrin is poisoned "despite his unique mastery of poisons" and is rendered unconscious.

The party is stunned. Bayard and KP discuss Bayard's next move; at first they think he should try to save Corrin and retreat, but KP points out that the Drow can go and get reinforcements. Bayard, "a man of no reproach," will not retreat. He charges up. And misses. The second Drow, Pharius Illal, uses his handheld crossbow and knocks Bayard out for 5 HP. Rimardo says they should grab their guys and flee. KP decides otherwise, and acts: he charges up and casts thunderwave on Hadnyl, who is standing along the north wall, and Hadnyl "is blown out through the window and dies from the effect taking max damage 16 HP." Rimardo casts hold person on Pharius, then uses a healing potion on Mr. Corrin, who charges and kills Pharius. Rimardo then heals Bayard with a healing potion. Bayard gets up, looks at Rimardo, and says, "I expected no less."

Corrin stealthily explores the rest of the floor and finds it empty, and the party slowly and quietly climbs to the second floor. Corrin enters the southwestern room, but a Drow there has cast alarm. Corrin tries to take advantage of the Drow's befuddled state, but the Drow is too perceptive: "Mr. Corrin gets a 25 on stealth, and the Drow rolls a natural 20." Corrin dashes in and attacks with advantage, dealing 18 HP and bringing the Drow down to 15. The Drow screams, "My brothers come to me," and casts magic missile, doing 14 HP of damage to Mr. Corrin. Rimardo moves in to flank. KP breaks the window on the eastern wall of the hallway and Bayard breaks the one along the western corridor. Corrin attacks again with advantage for 20 HP and kills the Drow, then looks around for an owl.

KP stares at the northern door. Rather than break through the windows, he remembers the original plan and waits. Rimardo heals Corrin for 15 HP and tells the party there is 1 spell left. Bayard stands next to KP in front of the northern door. Three initiates misty step into the southwestern room, where Rimardo and Corrin are still standing. Bayard charges in and hits Zekinid Zollazo for 24 HP. Corrin attacks Zekinid, dodging around Xunzmyr Zollazo, and with Bayard "surprisingly" positioned for a flanking attack, Corrin kills him with his sneak attack. Rimardo casts a spell on Xunzmyr, who is unaffected. Xunzmyr and Rissorin Zollazo misty step out of the room. Corrin figures out that they are in the northern room, and Rimardo screams, "They are in the north room."

Bayard charges in and uses his smiting ability for 23 HP of damage on Xunzmyr. Corrin dashes in, and KP charges in too; while they charge, KP and Rimardo discuss "a game they played as children involving puzzles and monsters and a board that changes all the time." The two Drow initiates attack Bayard for 17 HP. Rimardo tells Bayard to position himself for a flanking attack, and Bayard "uses his aggression and kills Xunzmyr with 11 HP of damage." There is one Drow left. Corrin hits it for 23 HP. KP casts "a 2nd level thunderwave," but the Drow Initiate uses counterspell and blocks it, then hits Corrin with a magic missile for 11 HP. Bayard charges the remaining Drow, "muttering about having to once again save the day," which the chapter calls "a desperate attempt at getting praise." He ponders whether to use smiting, does, and kills the initiate.

The group searches for treasure and finds coins, gems, two spell scrolls, a Bag of Holding and potions. They find "no map to any portal." Rimardo recommends heading back to Akia to get access to Lundbeams.

The group finds Akia, who introduces them to her boss, Alice. Alice tells them, "You guys are not bad for independent contractors," promises to see about getting them into Lundbeams tomorrow, and says, "We did well. We stopped the slave trade." Rimardo asks, "Who is this we?" Alice answers, "The WLF," and Rimardo adds, "and their independent contractors." Bayard asks, "Do we get dinner as well?" and Alice offers, "Brioche with butter?" Alice takes them to Halete, where they spend the night.

Alice walks in frantically: "we have a problem." Rimardo replies that they have not met Lundbeams. Alice: "we have a bigger problem. We have a huge problem." Armed camps have bivouacked outside Fax, with "a bunch of Drow, who are martially trained," commanding the field. Akia marches into the smithy and whispers to Alice, who says it is worse: "Tolub has made an announcement that Fax must create its own empire as a buffer zone against the expansionist Orc and Greyhawk empires." Alice whispers back to Akia, and Akia is shocked: "Lundbeams says the WLF must help the Army of Fax and not resist it." The party is paralysed by choices: the army, Lundbeams, or Walton.

KP asks, "Is there a library around here?" Everyone stares. He explains, "I am looking for a lightning bolt spell to inscribe in my book." A relieved Halete tells him he wants a spell caster, not a library: "That is easy, you want Garett." KP is taken to Garett, who has a charming young elf at his side. KP asks, "Do you have knowledge? I want to buy knowledge." Garett replies, "Yes, I have the knowledge, and I have expensive tastes," and tells the elf she can have the coat once he sells the spell. Garett sells the spell for 270 GP, and KP copies it into his book. Garett asks whether his name is KP; KP answers, "No, it is Knazreponnium," and when asked if his friends call him KP, says, "No." Back with the party KP wants to buy more spells; Rimardo reminds him the goal is to accumulate wealth. KP: "Consider it an investment." Rimardo: "Is that money or knowledge?" As they leave the tower, Garett and his young lady are pleased "to see they found another pigeon to sell spells."

Outside, people are staring at a proclamation from "Tolub, Grand Master of Fax, First of His Name, Erstwhile Lord Mayor of Nulb, Pirate King of Nyr Dyv," who declares "that the era of patience is over." It calls for resisting the expansionist forces to the south and north, says "All strong men of Fax must sign up," and promises "conquest of an empire of free peoples under our rule." Tolub has called up his feudal levies, and they are assembling outside the city walls. KP asks what the Wild Coast's liberation front wants to do. Halete says, "Something is wrong. This is against everything she ever believed in." Rimardo wants to go and see her; Halete says they have more knowledge now: "We know the target, it is Vallaki." KP asks, "Do they have a library?" Rimardo: "Let us first go to Lundbeams and then the library at Vallaki." KP looks annoyed.

The party finally reaches "a much older Lundbeams." Rimardo introduces them as independent contractors; KP corrects, "TEMPORARY independent contractors." Lundbeams knows them as "the ones who worked for Alice and Halete," calls that "a mistake," and insists, "The Drow are friends and our allies." She stumbles through her own line: Tolub "has wisely submitted to, sorry aligned himself with the Drow," and the plan is to "create an empire, that is the only way we will serve – no save the Wild Coast." Corrin sizes her up and realises she is not telling the whole story: "She looks like she is forced." KP flatters her and asks for more. Lundbeams says the Drow will lead the soldiers and "defeat the forces at Vallaki," that "If the Drow were to be frustrated at Vallaki, it would be a disaster for the Drow," and that full WLF members must support the action. The party realises they are not members of the WLF and that Lundbeams does not want them to win, and Lundbeams figures out that they figured it out. Bayard sees the lying but realises it is for the greater good.

KP asks what to do after defeating the forces at Vallaki. Lundbeams answers, "Why it would be a disaster if an army were to form outside the city of Fax to strike the forces of the Drow." Asked where to find such an army, she names "the free city of Fhuu. The last free city of orcs." Asked if there is someone to kill there, she says she does not know, but "we must ensure that the Drow are not defeated at Vallaki." Bayard is intrigued and perplexed: he knows Fhuu, which is close to where he is from, and it "was never one to get involved"; the gnomes and others around it meant an orcish army from there could never reach Fax without allying with races that hate orcs. Rimardo asks about Tolub. Lundbeams: "he has most wisely allied himself with the Drow." He will not lead the army; "He will be here in the city, protecting it from harm. He is not a general." He will be in the castle, and she sees him "Every day." Rimardo: "But the people do not." Lundbeams: "That is what I do, I reassure them of his safety." She looks at the sun, says it is past time and that all WLF members must support the army of Fax and its victory.

KP hands her a feather.
