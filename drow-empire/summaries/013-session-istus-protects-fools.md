# Chapter 13 – Istus Protects Fools

Date: 19 Wealsun 595 CY (595-06-19; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
The adventurers defeat the bounty hunter of the Drow in a gruesome fight. Afterwards Corrin and Rimardo are eager to seize the treasure "as payment for services rendered." They find a lot of treasure and a note: "By order of Xhalh, trusted of House Zollazo of the city Maenerius, the seat of power of the Drow Empire, Filzyr is given bounty and all help to kill the independent contractors and enemies of the Drow Empire." The city of Maenerius is unknown to the party, though it may be another Drow city from the Underdark; because the Drow are legendary, their homelands are poorly known. With the note is a scroll describing the party, with pictures. Rimardo takes Filzyr's clothing and the order from Xhalh, thinking there may be a chance to impersonate Filzyr at some point.

Fhuu is only a few hours away, and Bayard wonders whether they can rest before finishing the journey. Before they get any rest, a group of Orc warriors shows up. Rimardo wants to hide, but is reminded that so close to Fhuu this is not surprising. Bayard declares, "These are our new friends!" Rimardo defers to him to negotiate, wishes the injured Bayard well, and offers no healing. Bayard approaches the vanguard "without fear and without reproach," while Rimardo and Corrin, convinced this will end badly, hide. The orc leader asks, in a thick orcish accent, "Who are you?" Bayard answers, "I bring warning of the Drow Empire!" Asked how they know he is not Drow, Bayard shows the corpse and a notebook. The orc holds the notebook upside down and stares at the corpse, is convinced, and agrees to take them to Fhuu, where the Primus can decide whether to talk to them. "Sounds good to me," says Bayard. Rimardo and Corrin are surprised he is still alive, and follow. The followers of Istus have a saying: "Istus protects the foolish because they amuse her."

Passing through the slums of Fhuu's outskirts, the party sees some wounded adventurers near the gates. Rimardo heals two of the wounded warriors: "He cures strangers and not Bayard." The two perk up, see Rimardo, and run to him: "Rimardo, good friend!" Rimardo mutters, "Hello friends, I did not know I had." It is Halete, the blacksmith from Fax. Amos looks at her and says, "You traitorous, backstabbing, creature! She promised us help and allied herself with the Drow." He grabs his sword and charges. Bayard tries a command spell to make him stand down, but it fails, and Amos screams, "You dare stop my rightful revenge?" Bayard, seeing the situation is hopeless, charges after Amos; Corrin and Rimardo think about Istus and fools and stand aside; and KP, desperate to stay within 10 feet of Bayard, chases after him. The orc leaders remark, "this is highly irregular."

Bayard grapples Amos, who shrieks, "Let me go! This horrible woman has allied herself with the horrible Drow! She is a traitor – nothing but scum." One of the orcs in the slums mutters, "She is a Drow lover, we will kill her." Halete, freaked out, protests: "The Drow betrayed us! Lundbeams betrayed us! We thought we were going to help Vallaki! We lost most of our members in a massacre! You must believe us!" Rimardo asks, "Not a great story. Are you buying that one, Bayard!" Bayard does not, but insists, "we cannot just kill her, we need a proper trial." Corrin and Rimardo laugh hard and appreciate Istus even more. Bayard tells Amos to calm down and leads everyone into the city to see the Primus.

In the narrow streets an imposing Orc with an air of authority approaches. The soldiers need to rejoin their legion, he says, because of a rumoured incursion from the north: "I am Centurion Guttja; I can tend to you from here. I shall take you to Primus." The party shrugs and follows. Halete and Amos curse each other, and Amos reveals his extreme prejudice against Humanoids: "She allied herself with humanoids to fight against her own kind! She is the worst kind of scum." Guttja answers in a deep, threatening voice: "This is a city of humanoids, and you have come for our help? You humans never trust the orcs. If you had listened to the free orcs, we would have defeated the Orc Empire. But you never listen!" Amos almost launches into a colourful declaration of racial hatred, but Rimardo calms everyone: "We're all going to Primus."

Guttja leads them past markets full of craft orcs towards the castle, then turns into an alleyway. The party does not follow. Guttja announces that they will never get to Primus, and they will die. Bayard, who is always surprised by betrayal, asks, "Why?" Rimardo, who is not, says, "Why do you think?" Bayard remarks, "I am surprised by such deviousness," and then, "You cannot even trust Orcs! What is the world coming to? But I – I believed you." Rimardo is convinced Bayard will stand and fight, but Bayard announces, "There is no dishonor to flee when you are that wounded, so let us high tail out of there!!!!" The party flees deeper into the city and escapes, except for Amos, who has done little to endear himself to the orcs and is not rescued. During the pursuit Corrin and Rimardo end up covered in manure.

When the Centurion gives up the chase, the party finds itself in a craftsman area. Rimardo asks Halete whether she knows anyone who could help. She names "the Dwarves of Darnagal, they are great blacksmiths." Rimardo hears "Duergar" and wonders why anyone would work with evil Dwarves. Now really convinced Halete is not on the up-and-up, he tells Bayard: "if you want me to kill Halete, let me know. In the back, anytime. Corrin will take care of it." Halete overhears and corrects him, Darnagal, and Rimardo responds that they sound too much the same.

The party heads for an Inn to rest, "with visions of pillows dancing in their heads," and a tavern brawl spills out. "Bayard – No. NO! NO!!!!" As they hide and watch, Orc guards appear behind them: "Primus says you must be brought to him! We will take you!" Bayard agrees at once. Rimardo asks, "Really, really, we are going to trust these guys?" and Bayard says yes: "The other one was a bad apple. You must trust in the essential goodness of Orc Kind." Corrin and Rimardo wonder what kind of sick sense of humour Istus has, and Corrin wonders whether it is time to leave the party with Rimardo and his share, "but 1300 GP is not enough."

On the main drag into the castle, a young man steps in front of the Orcs: short, husky, amber-eyed, with a previously broken nose, golden blond hair, and a tattoo of a raccoon skull on his right arm. "Michel: Bayard!" He is a man from Fairhaven, Bayard's old village, known in the local dialect as "Juste-Havre." Michel asks, "Are you still a brioche without butter?" Bayard replies, "Yes, my dear friend, whom I cannot recall at all." In fact Bayard does recognise him: it was Bayard's fist that broke Michel's nose years ago, when Michel questioned Bayard's honour over whether Bayard had stolen an apple from Jean-Luc's farm. Michel says the Primus has asked Monseigneur Ryton for help against the Drow, and asks whether the Primus is being a fool. Bayard remembers Sir Ryton as the pompous mayor of Juste-Havre who claimed Bayard's brioche would never have butter. The Mayor, Michel says, is coming to Fhuu: "Apparently, there is an alliance being set up. Do you think this is wise? Is it really the Drow or Elves?" Rimardo and Bayard assure him the Drow are evil and real, and Michel resolves to advise Ryton to listen to Primus. As Bayard walks away, Michel says, "Bayard, if this is true, you have earned your butter!" Offended, Bayard asks, "Do you doubt my word?" Michel answers, "Bayard, you have said much but delivered little. Sir Ryton will trust my word! Do not lead me astray."

The Orc guards finally bring the party to an audience with the Primus. His great hall is decorated with trophies of the hunt and war, large mammals and what must be captured enemy banners, and an oversized rustic wooden throne decorated with hides commands the room. The Primus is "grizzled and scarred, but his eyes are sharp," and speaks with authority in a gravelly voice. He asks, "Who are you?" Rimardo answers, "We carry a warning from the WLF of the Drow and urge you to create an alliance against the elves of the Underdark." The bemused Primus questions the sanity of an alliance with humans, gnomes and elves, and when Rimardo suggests an alliance of all good peoples, he laughs: "Your kind never thought of us as good, they would rather sit and watch the Drow burn us to the ground…" Then: "Why should I trust you? How do I know you are not spying for the Drow?" Rimardo replies, "We are not spying, but this one," pointing casually at Halete, "we do not know."

Halete takes this as her cue to rant: "I am a representative of the Wild Coast Liberation Front. We are dedicated to the freedom of all peoples!" A unified Wild Coast, she says, could have prevented "the Evil Orc Empire and the annexation by the free city of Greyhawk. Lundbeams has betrayed us all." At the name Lundbeams the Primus stops her: "Lundbeams has promised very much and delivered little. And her toy Tolub has become a bigger disgrace! And now he has allied himself with the Drow! Why should I listen to you?" Rimardo says, "We will both lose if we do not ally ourselves," and the Primus tells him, "You are wise!" Rimardo rolls his eyes.

Guttja marches in: "Primus, it is good I came here. You must not listen to these men. The Drow Empire is our friend." Bayard cannot hold it in: "The DROW EMPIRE is OUR Friend?" Guttja explains that "The Drow have promised if we do not join this fight, we will get a piece of their Empire, Primus." Bayard asks, "Have you been talking to the Drow? Are you a traitor?" A very worried Guttja bows to the Primus and says he had "conversations – to explore all options for you, Primus!" The Primus answers, "You have been a good friend and a good ally, but you have spoken to the ancient enemy. Guards take him away." Guttja screams as he is taken away: "You are making a mistake! The Drow are our friends!!"

The Primus says he does not know whether he should trust these humans. KP cannot resist: "Not a human! Very gnome." The Primus weighs what is in front of him: a party that got rid of the human who hated humanoids, brought him an agent of the WLF, exposed a traitor in his midst, and bore evidence of the Drow. He declares, "We will call an alliance of the seven villages and see if we can create an army. There is much distrust among the towns of the Wild Coast, and no one wants to be a patsy."
