# Chapter 4 – Bayard objects to Paladin stereotypes

Date: 7 Flocktime 595 to 8 Flocktime 595 CY (595-05-07 to 595-05-08; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
Bayard sits in The River Drink wondering how to continue his pursuit of the Drow alone when Alexia Boulderdown arrives with a man, a halfling, and a gnome who are looking for someone to adventure with. Rimardo introduces himself on behalf of the pair as followers of Istus: "Corrin is the dealer of death. And Rimardo, the dealer of health." Their conviction that money is the only truly objective way to determine the value of any enterprise unites them in their faith; the path of most considerable wealth is the path Istus wants them to follow. The gnome, grabbing his beard, introduces himself as Knazreponnium Ketternoppapux — "It Is pronounced with a silent K before the N" — and, as KP, as Rimardo eventually calls him, he is concerned with knowledge and the accumulation of power. Rimardo offers his services as long as Bayard promises treasure, because "That is what matters to us." KP offers his if there is knowledge to be accumulated. But although Bayard now has a group to fight the Drow, there is no Drow to be found.

Serious news then circulates through town. A second supply shipment, an overland cargo wagon from the south, failed to arrive that morning, and the riverboat that hauled grain from Farjvad downriver to the east has not returned when it should have the previous evening. Riverboat crews sometimes relax a day or two in St. Aurelius, but with two shipments missing this is now viewed with trepidation. The Major sent militia to scout the road south with orders to return before dusk; dusk has arrived without them, and Farjvad is in "a condition more severe than concern, but not quite panic." The party overhears snide remarks about people who do not know how to leave things alone, and that an occasional zombie raid that caught the odd tourist is preferable to being cut off by some unknown force.

KP asks where the zombies come from, and Bayard suggests asking the bar until KP reminds him of Alexia. She explains that the zombies would occasionally kidnap outsiders crossing the river and take them to the Temple of Mort, where a Drow necromancer experimented on them. "The Drow necromancer was kind-a cool," she says: he never attacked locals, and the Major made her and all the boat people agree not to talk about it. KP perks up when he learns the necromancer is dead: "More importantly, where are his spell books?"

The Major walks into the Inn, furious. He is an Orc, Carc Mistsplitter, set up as the local leader of this border town of the Orc empire. When KP asks if he is the mayor, Carc growls that he is the Major, and that Bayard has caused the Orc Empire big problems. Rimardo observes that this may interfere with the accumulation of wealth. Bayard, confused, explains that he only joined Dimitrov, Boradoon, and Reepicheep after their Paladin, a drunk Triton, went swimming instead of fighting, but the Major blames him by association for the crimes of the previous four. KP congratulates the Paladin on "assuming so much personal accountability for everything," and Rimardo wonders if he signed up with the right guy, before declaring, "where you go, I go. The strand of our fates is interlinked for now". Bayard turns to Alexia for advice, and she sighs and talks to him as if he were a child: "For crying out loud dude, we are now surrounded by monsters who are going to starve us, yes? You are without fear and without reproach? Yes? Figure it out on your own." Bayard tells the group they attack at dawn, and after some discussion they decide to head south in the morning to re-establish the caravan route.

Rimardo casts Aid on the party before they set out, giving a 5 HP benefit. On the road south they find the remains of the scouts, who appear to have been dragged off — probably dead — by Dragonborn for food; acid burns on the scouts' wagons suggest black Dragonborn, in other words, evil. They follow the trail to the lair. Watching from a hidden vantage point, Rimardo asks Bayard whether he would mind Corrin sneaking in to kill someone with surprise, and Bayard erupts: "If Orcus were to show up for breakfast and all I had was a spoon, I would attack! This stereotyping of Paladins that has gone on for centuries has got to stop!!!!" In vicious combat the Dragonborns are killed at the cost of much magic and health. Corrin almost kills the Dragonborn leader outright with surprise but fails, giving the leader just enough time to shout into a stone, "We are being attacked!"

Some forty minutes into their short rest, Lizardfolk emerge from the edges of the clearing. Bayard attacks the lead Lizardfolk, Riporus, who screams into a similar stone: "Lyless we are being attacked by a Paladin. You promised us militia!" Having talked at length about how Paladins do not blindly charge into combat, Bayard does exactly that and is quickly surrounded by three more Lizardfolk. A fifth attacks Rimardo, and Corrin chooses to help Rimardo, leaving Bayard alone against four. Bayard chooses to stand and fight, and KP helps with carefully sculpted spells with large areas of effect, but Bayard is eventually killed — losing consciousness knowing his stand was without fear and without reproach, because he spared his comrades and gave KP a target. KP's thunderwaves give Rimardo and Corrin attacks of opportunity, which they miss, but the Lizardfolk are killed. Before Bayard joins his maker, Rimardo saves him with "a well-timed use of a “Spare the Dying spell”" and heals him, channeling Istus.

KP finds the sending stones and speaks into one: "Hello, hello, tell me your secrets!" A voice answers, asking if Riporus is there and whether the heroes are dead. Rimardo whispers, "say yes, say yes," but KP fumbles it into claiming they "killed the Dragonborn heroes," to Rimardo's despair. The voice — Lyless — asks who this is, and KP answers, "Knazreponnium Ketternoppapux at your service!" Lyless replies, "You have interfered with our plans for the last time," and refuses to say more: "My hobgoblins will be there soon and slaughter you." KP offers to strike a deal and asks if Lyless needs a researcher, while Rimardo pleads with Bayard to retreat. Bayard grabs the stone: "Listen you steaming filthy Drow, I will retreat today and kill you tomorrow."

On the outskirts, militia question the group, and Bayard explains that they are the adventurers saddled with cleaning up the mess the previous set made. In town the Major tells them they must deal with the rest of the blockade before the leaders find more troops. At the River Drink, prices have gone up: the innkeeper says it is unclear whether they are heroes or villains, and having plundered monsters, they must have gold. Alexia, the only friendly face, warns them that the Major is under pressure, and that "if the orc empire sends an army here, this place will be destroyed. You need to fix this now."

The party reflects on what is going on. Farjvad has been relatively immune to the Orc Empire's depredations because it lies at the edge of the Empire and near the Elven kingdom, so the orcs cannot station a large force there. If the orcs felt their power threatened, the armies would come, and if they heard it was Drow they would come in overwhelming force — in their minds it would be the Elves of Celene invading. Gruumsh hates Lolth and the Drow as much as he hates the Elves, because he allied himself with Araushnee and suffered when she failed to overthrow the Elvish gods. As for the Major, calling for help would cost him a very profitable post with some power, the post might not survive the armies' arrival, and he might be viewed as a failure — "and the Orc Empire is not big on failure." So he is very invested in the problem being solved quietly and by outsiders.
