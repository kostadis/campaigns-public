# Chapter 51 – 12 Seconds

Date: Lolth's demi-plane, Day 1 (??? on Oerth; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
The question is how. Bayard’s heroes know their secret weapon is Corrin’s anti-magic shield: Lolth has no reason to expect it, and "The spell itself is an impossibility." The problem is getting him close to her. The door to Lolth’s inner sanctum is closed, and the heroes prepare to advance one last time. Rimardo lays out the plan: Corrin enters first and advances on Lolth, and once he is close she will not be able to use her magic spells, and none of her ranged attacks will work. KP sees the opportunity this creates: he will cast time stop, dimension door Bayard to her, and trap her with a wall of force.

Twelve seconds to the end. Corrin and the zombies enter. Lolth screams, "finally, you will be destroyed! I have waited long to feast on your body!" Corrin advances 10 feet, considers an arrow, and does not fire. Bayard waits near the door, out of sight. Rimardo enters and banishes two of the shield maidens. Lolth screams, "Watch how I destroy those who dare to enter my sanctum! Watch, my pretties, the defeat of Istus' champions!" Her disintegrate at Corrin has no effect, and for a moment she is concerned: "I do not know what magic you have. No matter. I will enjoy tearing you apart limb from limb!" She casts mirror image on herself and charges Corrin. KP casts time stop, "and Istus smiles." Inside the stopped time he dimension doors Bayard next to Lolth, casts Mordenkainen's sword, surrounds her with a wall of force, and with his last action, just as the time stop ends, casts a sunburst. Lolth takes 27 points of damage, turns on KP, and deals 29 points of damage and 45 of poison, breaking his concentration and bringing down the wall of force.

Six seconds. Corrin misses twice despite flanking, but uses Vhaeraun's boon to turn a miss into a hit for 43 points of damage. Bayard, once mocked as "a brioche without butter, no more," does not miss, inflicting 129 HP of damage; Lolth is shocked, and doubt enters her mind for the first time. Rimardo casts a seventh level harm spell for 21 HP. "The Zombies hold the mirror, but it does not trap her yet." Lolth's bite and stinger kill Bayard, but he holds at 1 HP because of death ward, and her last attack on him misses; encircled, she cannot flee. Her last attack on Corrin deals 63 HP of damage, but his anti-magic shield confounds her and does not go away, and she is perplexed.

KP smiles. He was never playing 13th-dimensional chess, KP was playing 20th-dimensional chess. He breaks the staff of power, and the explosion rips through the room. Lolth takes 160 HP of damage, and Bayard is killed. Rimardo and Corrin, in shock, realize they are now in charge, free of "the meddling morality of the paladin." Lolth smiles, thinking "The cleric and the assassin will be easy pickings."

Zero seconds. Corrin smiles back and shows her the mirror, and she is trapped. "He has killed a god": Corrin has completed the most daring and audacious assassination of all time. Rimardo smiles too, since "sometimes glory is more important than money," and Bayard is particularly pleased, since the plan's tactical brilliance will be studied for millennia, he was in charge, and so the credit must go to him. The chapter explains the victory: Lolth's power rested on her spells, her poison and her mobility, and the plan neutralized all three. The anti-magic shield blocked her spells, the hero's feast made her poison less deadly, and once she was surrounded her recklessness took over; away from her home plane, she thought she was invulnerable and would simply rematerialize later. "But she was wrong."

With Lolth defeated, Lolth’s Pocket Plane begins to shudder. Rimardo casts mass heal, raising Bayard, and the group runs for the slave pits, the one place they can teleport out of. KP is waiting for them there: the blast had sent him to a random plane, and he used Arcane Gate to come back. Rimardo sees his friend and smiles. The group teleports from Lolth’s Pocket Plane to the Prime Material Plane outside of Fax. "The group has one more thing to take care of, Lundbeams, and this time it is personal."
