# Chapter 11 – The Rescue of Humph

Date: 16 Flocktime 595 CY (595-05-16; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
The battle still rages in Vallaki. Groups of marauding troops have scaled the walls and entered the town, and the heroes defeat them one after another, "Sometimes with bold frontal assaults, and sometimes with an assassin who stealthily walks into a house and slaughters everyone."

The rescue of Humph the Champion in his mansion is, the chronicle says, telling. Corrin kills the scattered guards by moving stealthily between each group: he surprises one guard and kills him, then kills the other before that one can strike. "All that anyone could hear was the soft, gentle thud of their bodies," a sound lost in the chaos of the battle. When Corrin finally enters the room with Humph, the party surrounds Lord Patricio, a minor merchant lord of Fax who had been promised Vallaki as his personal fiefdom if he brought Humph's head on a platter. The party cuts him down, "and the ground was stained wine-dark with his blood."

Once Humph is free, the party kills another group, led by another Faxian noble and his champion. This is Lord Nerpotel, "an erstwhile bandit-king, who settled in Fax and lost everything." The plunder of Vallaki was meant to restore his prestige and wealth, but Nerpotel wanted power as well as gold and fame. While Tolub waited in the city of Fax, Nerpotel imagined he could weasel himself into the good graces of the Drow and seize Fax for himself. "His ambitions were vast, his dreams of glory and power unimaginably large, his life too brief, and his visions died with him."

What interests the party is a pendant that the Drow all wear: "A black leather patch with a golden slash across it." They have not seen it before.

When the chronicle last left the group, they were being surrounded by yet another force. This one is led by Lord Patricio's nephew, Alejandro, who had been given troops to help his uncle secure Humph. The chapter says that when he discovered Lord Patricio was dead, he set out after the party, determined to kill them to please the Drow and perhaps win the prize that Lord Patricio had been promised.

The fighting is now grisly. The party's spells are at a minimum, and their ability to heal and survive is marginal. But so is the enemy's: the defeats of the roving troops have depleted the Faxian army. The critical battle will be at the gates. The party must protect the city a little longer and stop the troops inside the town from encircling the guard's soldiers. The assault on the gate cannot last much longer: either the gate defenders will be destroyed and the armies will enter, or Vallaki will be saved. Everyone knows that "these are the last few titanic moments of the Battle of Vallaki."
