# Chapter 42 – Wherein Ziamar’s Betrayal Becomes Clear

Date: The Abyss, Days 98–99 (??? on Oerth; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
The chapter opens as "Istus, on death's door, smiles at Corrin." The narration pauses on a theological dispute within the cult of Istus about her role at death's door: one group believes death has already been decided; another that Istus chooses at that moment to let chance make the final decision — "It is almost as if the one who sees everything wishes to not understand something"; a third that Istus has already made up her mind and that only our unwillingness to accept her fate makes us think she has left it to chance. "The goddess has refused to express an opinion."

The fight with the vrocks continues. "Lord Ziamar does not approve of your presence," screeches a vrock. "Lord Ziamar has other plans for the city, and you are not in them." A vrock tosses a bead of force at Bayard, and a monster tries to rend and tear KP apart but only manages to cause air to fly around the gnome. Rimardo heals Corrin, invoking Istus's power. The narration plays with fate: in an alternate reality KP dispels the bead of force, and in another existence he remembers from the book "Demons and Spells" that demons are resistant to cold and casts dispel magic — but "In this reality, KP did not," and he casts a cone of cold instead. A vrock casts hellish rebuke on KP for 45 points of damage, and another hits Rimardo, who answers, "'tis but a flesh wound." Corrin kills a vrock, and Rimardo dispels the bead. KP uses Sunbeam on two of the vrocks, blinding one. Bayard hits a vrock and dodges its hellish rebuke, and Corrin disengages to strike a vrock menacing KP for 35 points of damage. KP is severely wounded — "The vrock can taste his blood" — but the vrock fails to kill him or break his concentration. Rimardo heals KP, KP kills a vrock with his Sunbeam and blinds another, Bayard attacks twice, and Corrin kills the last vrock.

A halfling who had remained behind jumps out and says desperately, "We never intended this to happen." Rimardo stares: "You mean to tell me the portable hole just happened to make itself all by its lonesome into the bag of holding?" The halfling insists, "Lord Ziamar said that if we were to do this, it would be a great reward for you and for us," and that they were "controlled" and "charmed." KP notices that the halflings do not appear to be the suicidal kind, and that their tattered simple clothing means they could not have bought such powerful items; he concludes they are not lying. KP then remembers the majordomo's note and opens it: "Do not trust Lord Ziamar."

Corrin is excited. Rimardo asks, "so, Bayard, will we go kill this, Lord?" Bayard reasons it through: "Under normal circumstances, I would be opposed to the disruption of a lawful order. But, Lord Ziamar is a demon. And he is evil. Therefore, killing him is okay even though it disrupts the lawful ordered state of affairs." KP is pleased with this rationalization.

The party returns to the Tavern of the Gilded Dryad, where the Hobgoblin owner who betrayed them to the Drow is delighted to see them and announces that he has secured high-quality meat: "cow! No more gnome meat." The party rests and recovers over an excellent burger, then marches on Ziamar's tower, because, as KP announces, "we have a zero-tolerance policy towards backstabbing."

Walking through the city, the group is surrounded by four Drow assassins. Corrin smiles and tries to talk to them, asking whether they belong to the local guild, telling Rimardo that these are his people, and suggesting they are brethren who should all drink to each other's health. The assassins are stunned into silence, and KP's job offer gets no reaction. Rimardo is confused — Ziamar tried to kill them with the halflings and the vrocks, so why send assassins? Corrin reminds him that the assumption that the killers were hired by Ziamar is only an assumption, and Rimardo concedes.

Corrin hits twice, but the Drow dodges most of the damage and smiles. "Corrin is unamused. Corrin is never amused." One Drow levitates and hits KP with a handheld crossbow, though KP avoids the worst of the poison. Bayard is trapped in a rope of entanglement. KP, recalling that the Drow have access to counterspell, casts a fire bolt to test the theory, and the Drow can indeed counter. Bayard breaks out of the rope. An assassin hits Corrin for 20 points of damage and another casts darkness. Rimardo dispels the darkness, to KP's groan and Bayard's rolled eyes: Bayard has spent many a month explaining that they must always be communicating on the field rather than staying silent and hoping everybody knows everybody else's plans. KP is hit for 25 HP of damage and Corrin for 34 HP. KP's spell is countered and he glares at Rimardo. Bayard invokes his divine smite for 41 points of damage, two Drow hit Corrin for 48 points of damage, and Rimardo casts toll the dead — "the foolish Drow takes 28 HP of damage and dies."
