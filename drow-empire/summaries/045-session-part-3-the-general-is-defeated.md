# Chapter 45 – Part 3 The General is Defeated

Date: The Abyss, Day 99 (??? on Oerth; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
The city is besieged. Trebuchets placed around it launch poison gas projectiles, and an increasingly significant number of the civilian population is dying. To stop the siege the party must kill the Drow general, who directs the enemy forces beyond the Valley of the banshees. They can take the main road, risking an encounter, or an alternative route; as Valnozz made clear, taking the trebuchet near the general would let them bypass the Valley entirely. But the party's magic is low: "The Paladin can no longer smite. KP is down to his last few spells." Rimardo has a few spells, but nothing like what is needed to defeat the general.

The first plan is to cast control weather and rely on the local population to take out the Drow general. Freedahl overhears: "need I remind you that our agreement was the following: you protect my city from the Drow, and I promise not to invade your plane for 100 years. If you choose not to defend my city, I may decide to change my part of the bargain today." The narration notes that no one notices he already did, and that in demonic law that represents a mutually agreed upon contract change. Rimardo asks Bayard whether they should just kill Freedahl. The chapter lays out the problem: the Drow Empire will not commit the bulk of its forces while the demons are in the city; if Freedahl dies, the demons leave, and without them the non-demonic population cannot hold off the hordes of the abyss the Drow have assembled; as more non-demons die, the demons are more likely to abandon the city and let the Drow recapture the portal.

The majordomo pipes up: "if you were to kill the general, or so said the spy Valnozz, the Drow forces would collapse." Bayard concludes that they must kill the general. While they rest, Rimardo negotiates with Freedahl for a teleportation spell to get near the general; the demon lord agrees "for a modest fee," though KP and Rimardo do not agree that the payment of all the treasure they find in the hands of the Drow general is modest. The party dithers between destroying all the trebuchets, going to the trebuchet with a device that would teleport them to the general past the banshees, or walking from the city entrance through the Valley to the general's HQ — "Their tactical brilliance that has shown through, unfortunately, is almost undone with their strategic dithering." The majordomo picks up a timepiece: "tick-tock." With the screams of people dying from the gas in the background, Bayard decides they will march through the Valley and defeat the general.

They march through the hills, Bayard singing a song from his village, KP muttering aloud, Rimardo trying to be quiet. Corrin notices an ambush point, sneaks ahead over a hill and sees four demons: mezzoloths. As KP will explain later, demons hire out their services to other demons to fight wars on planes not their own — "a critical component of the intra-abyssal plane exchange" — and on a plane that is not their home they risk pain-and-suffering but not death. The mercenaries shimmer — "They are wearing cloaks of displacement," Corrin sings in his head — so he must hit once to strip the shimmer before his foresight lets him land the killing blow. Surprising the demons, who are watching the noisy party, Corrin hits, the shimmer goes away, and he inflicts 162 points of damage, killing one. He then hits another for 42 points of damage. The mezzoloth M creates a stinking cloud around Corrin (24 points of damage), and N creates another around the rest of the party. Bayard charges L, whom Corrin had wounded, for 22 points of damage, and M hits Bayard for 18. KP kills L with a magic missile. Rimardo breathes the poison for 22 points of damage, then casts banishment, and M and N disappear. "good call on coming this way," Rimardo remarks. Among the treasure the party finds 450 points of platinum "clearly marked with the symbol of House Zollazo. This was the payment promised."

Continuing toward the drow HQ, they see a raised mound of dirt holding a drow encampment. The general is sending messages to his troops with dancing lights, surrounded by four shield guardians, with eight banshees in the field in front guarding the way. Corrin proposes to sneak in and assassinate the general, and the group agrees. He approaches unnoticed — "The general's overconfidence in his defenses will be his undoing." His first strike wounds the general, and the shield guardians' ability to absorb damage does not work: "Is this the hand of Istus?" With the initiative still his, Corrin strikes the general down where he stands, and the general disappears, "probably being summoned to explain his failures." The Drow Imperial Army is defeated, but the banshees and shield guardians remain.

Corrin only needs to flee and rejoin his comrades, but "The lure of the treasure that Freedahl seemed so keen to want draws Rimardo and the rest in." Rimardo advances on the banshees and destroys three with his clerical powers, and a shield guardian blasts him with a flame strike. Bayard is torn between defending his ally from the shield guardians and facing the undead; KP reassures him that he can kill all the banshees and urges him toward the shield guardians, and Bayard agrees. KP's fireball burns three banshees to the North for 31 points of damage, but none die. A shield guardian uses a quickened 9th level magic missile on Corrin for 33 Hp of damage and a flame strike on Rimardo. The wounded banshees flee, leaving two, who wail: Bayard takes 21 HP of necrotic damage but survives, "Rimardo, however, is not as fortunate and dies." Corrin sees it and screams silently in horror.

Corrin, "caught between despair and vengeance," attacks the nearest shield guardian. Bayard charges and turns two of the remaining banshees. KP notices three shield guardians lined up and casts "a maximize fifth level lightning bolt" from one of the scrolls he picked up in Plagueridge Rampart, for 65 HP of damage. Corrin's 47 points of damage destroy the shield guardian between him and his friend, and, wounded by the magic missiles, he drinks a potion of supreme healing as he runs toward Rimardo. A shield guardian pounds KP for 22 points of damage.

Bayard, seeing his fallen friend, "selflessly and without reproach, heals Rimardo," using all his healing to bring back the healer. The rest of the party is not sure Bayard understands the concept of "saved him," given how often Rimardo has seen a wounded Bayard on death's door and said, "oh, he's fine." But Bayard has seen the once selfish cleric weep when the innocent die, rush to save the slaves, and rush to save the family killed in the house where KP was hiding; "The Paladin can see into the hearts of men and knows that Rimardo has changed." The act cements his reputation as a man without fear and without reproach — "a man whose brioche is very well buttered." What Corrin thinks of it is unclear.

KP casts another lightning bolt on three lined-up shield guardians. One shield guardian dashes to Corrin, who reflects that it is easier when the monsters come to him and strikes twice for 36 points of damage. Touched by Bayard's act, Rimardo uses his most powerful spell to heal the Paladin, just in time: two shield guardians hit Bayard three times for a total of 44 points of damage. Bayard damages a construct and KP finishes it with a magic missile, then takes 22 points of damage from the last construct. The monsters decide KP is a worthy target and hurt him again; Bayard slashes the one hurting KP for 29 points of damage, KP steps away and burns it with a fire bolt for 40 points of damage, and Corrin kills the monster he was facing. The battlefield goes quiet: "all the enemies of Plagueridge Rampart, and of the demon lord Freedahl, are dead." The heroes head back to the Drow general's encampment to collect their treasure — "A lucre they were unwilling to part with and risked life and limb for."
