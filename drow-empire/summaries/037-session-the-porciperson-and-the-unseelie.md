# Chapter 37 – The Porciperson and the Unseelie

Date: The Feywild, Days 1–2 (??? on Oerth; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
The chapter rejoins the party in the middle of combat with the Drow guard near the portal. They have learned that Tathdax, the governor-general of the Drow Empire, is not as loyal as other Drow, but "the Drow force that was desperately trying to kill our heroes did not share Tathdax’s questionable loyalty."

Corrin maneuvers to an uninjured Drow, critically hits it with his first attack and kills him. Rimardo moves into the room and casts toll the dead on the blind Drow for 8 HP of damage. A Drow casts faerie fire on the party, who are all unaffected; the Drow then casts fire-shield on himself and hits Corrin twice for 25 HP. "Corrin is not concerned, Rimardo is right next to him, and all will be well." Bayard advances and misses. The griffon attacks a Drow from a flanking position and "discovers to his horror that the Drow are wearing armor that makes them immune to piercing attacks"; they are not resistant to slashing, though, and the griffon inflicts 11 points of damage. The blind Drow swings wildly at the griffon for 34. KP uses his sunbeam again: one Drow dodges the full blast and takes 13, the blind Drow takes 27. A Drow flanks Corrin for 27.

Corrin has a quandary: he carries a magical rapier and a standard rapier, and he wonders whether the Drow are immune to magical blades. He takes a chance, disengages, flanks another Drow and hits with the magical rapier for 44 HP of damage; the Drow, expecting the weapon to be ineffective, did not parry. It would have died "except for death ward," and is reduced to one hit point. Rimardo walks right up to the bunched, weakened Drow and casts word of radiance, but they are unaffected. With KP out of range, the Drow hit Corrin for 25. Corrin is confused — he is sure Istus should have nothing against him — but on reflection decides that given the mayhem and destruction he is a worthy target. "And there is that creepy beard he has. After all, the elves hate the dwarves."

Bayard flanks one of the two remaining Drow and misses twice, taking 6 from the fire shield. The griffon avoids the Drow in piercing-proof armor and slashes the other, misses, takes 6 from the fire shield, then turns on "the Drow that is vulnerable and kills it." KP casts sunbeam on the two remaining Drow; one dies and the other takes 17 points of radiant damage. Corrin hits the last for 46, Rimardo's toll the dead deals 12, and the Drow, now on one hit point, misses once and hits Corrin for 8. With the Drow dead, Rimardo and Corrin look for treasure and find none.

The party turns to Selzyt. Rimardo, underwhelmed by his contributions, asks, "so, would you mind giving us some information, o great and powerful failsafe that failed to do anything." Selzyt is offended — "I am feeling insulted. I am feeling disrespected" — and threatens that he could have helped the Drow. Rimardo scoffs, "You are just a piece of glass, right?" Selzyt panics: "no, no! I am a precious gem. Unbelievably valuable." Rimardo muses about destroying the gem and mentions he thinks he has a banishment spell; KP excitedly asks, "what would a dispel magic do?" Selzyt screams, "BAYARD!" Bayard stares at the gem and urges it to answer all of Rimardo's questions before he reconsiders their bargain: he is "a leader of freemen," and at some point must choose between his men and "my promise to a gem." He pauses for dramatic effect: "housing an evil Drow."

Selzyt talks. "Tathdax has a tower 10 miles into the Feywild." The Feywild has magical effects that can affect humans, and the Governor-General "has disrupted the careful balance between the Unseelie and Seelies as he attempts to carve out an empire of his own." War has broken out; "What is unclear is what power he has acquired that has made this possible." With the tower several hours away, the group uses the beds in the room to rest.

They climb the spiral staircase into the Feywild. It is as surreal and beautiful as KP promised — deeper blue sky, brighter green grass, bark saturated with red, flowers in stark fluorescent colors. But the very air is toxic: the heroes are poisoned, lose three points of constitution and are immediately weakened. KP recalls that his mentor, the great sage Ronbar Dorhik, wrote that a mortal entering the Feywild would be cursed. The mage and the cleric stare at each other, each waiting for the other's remove curse. KP: "I didn’t memorize it. You were supposed to be the one with all the buff spells." Rimardo sighs, "I suppose I shouldn’t have memorized bestow curse."

Bayard leads them along the road Selzyt pointed out. A few miles in, KP notices a porcupine with a human face in a tree. It climbs down, stands erect and shouts in a high voice, "you are not welcome here! Go away!" KP has never read about such a creature in the great sage's books and speculates that it may not be indigenous. He tries to negotiate anyway, and his reward is a volley of spikes that hits each member of the party; KP is caught by two and takes 40 points of damage. Corrin hits it with his rapier for 10. Rimardo casts harm, and the chapter records 58 HP of damage. "We do not wish to hurt you," Bayard says apologetically as he advances to kill it. KP's fire bolt deals 12 fire damage; the creature fires four spikes at KP for another 33, cries "My master will hear about this!" and blinks away.

KP uses his gem of true seeing and sees it standing there. Then two Unseelie emerge from the underbrush and scream, "Where is the creature?" KP points to where it would be if it were not in the ethereal plane, and casts a wall of force in a sphere, trapping the beast. Another two Unseelie emerge from the underbrush, and the chapter ends.
