# Chapter 27 – After the unfortunate incident at the Tavern of the Gilded Dryad

Date: The Abyss, Days 1–2 (??? on Oerth; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
After the unfortunate incident at the Tavern of the Gilded Dryad, the party runs into the city of Plagueridge Rampart, hearing the hobgoblin scream in the distance as they flee into the warrens. Taking stock, they realize they cannot escape: the city is "a tiny little eco-bubble inside the abyss that barely sustained non-abyssal life." Their only hope is to take control of the town, which means taking the citadel; they cannot take the stronghold unless the garrison is busy, and the only force available to overwhelm the soldiers is the slaves. They meander toward the slave pens. Bayard is conflicted: "On the one hand, killing as many demons before I die would be glorious, but it would not be smart, and smart and glorious are not synonymous."

They see a Drow patrol asking questions. KP uses his helm to understand what is being said and relays that the patrol is looking for intruders, and that "Anyone who fails to surrender the intruders will not get their share of goodberries and join the slaves in the slave houses." Rimardo mutters, "They are so nice, the Drow." Bayard replies, "They are evil. Very evil." Rimardo rolls his eyes.

A rat follows them through the city, and in an unoccupied space it transforms into a small demon, a quasit. It stares at the Paladin and announces, "Hello! My name is Siuch!" Rimardo keeps walking. Siuch has "a proposition to make from my master," and threatens in a squeak to tell the Drow they are here. Rimardo sneers, "The Drow know we are here," and when Siuch grasps at "I could tell them you are here!", Rimardo laughs, "We could be somewhere else!" KP, always interested in propositions, would rather learn about the economy, "all of the above."

Siuch can only tell them about this city. It exists to protect and manage the portal; everyone who lives there is not from the Abyss or is Drow, a local population kept as "a meat shield" around the portal to slow a demonic attack. The walls protect the locals from the effects of the abyss, and the Drow keep the peace by promising that good inhabitants can someday be set free through the portal. "Of course, the Drow kill them all." At a festival called Caroussel, the inhabitants gather around the portal entrance and are taken down the portable hole, to be slaughtered, enslaved on the other side, or die as the first people into a newly opened location. Everyone depends on the Drow Rangers who bring back the goodberries, so the Drow have complete control: "It’s quite beautiful, actually." When Rimardo asks why others can't escape, Siuch says coldly that the Drow guard the walls, the environment would kill them, and the residents of the abyss would kill whatever the environment didn't: "This is the safe place of the Abyss." KP asks whether the Abyss is then safe for demons, and Siuch reflects, "well yes, especially if they are loyal to my master Freedahl!"

Siuch's proposal is that "my master Freedahl would like to make a deal with you!" Freedahl would help if the party removed the Drow from power, and when KP asks how Freedahl will remove them, Siuch laughs: "oh no, you will remove them!" Rimardo asks, shocked, "We are planning to make a deal with a demon?" Siuch is thrilled: "YES! Not just a demon, but a demon lord, my master Freedahl!" KP says the obvious: Freedahl wants control of the portal, which would elevate him in Graz’zt’s eyes. Rimardo thinks Freedahl is making the smart play. Siuch explains that "My master cannot be seen attacking the Drow!", since the Drow would attack him and Graz’zt would have to intervene; Freedahl will come after the party has taken the city and take possession of it. When KP asks whether Freedahl would like to be lord mayor, Siuch is aghast: "Freedahl is not elected! His power flows from his greatness!", though the locals could choose "Freedahl’s elect or suffer an eternity of pain and suffering in my dungeons!"

Rimardo asks, "so what’s in it for us?" Siuch opens: Freedahl will not use the portal to invade their place "for 1 whole day!" Seeing stone faces and a Bayard losing interest, he panics, "Perhaps more?" Rimardo counters, "How about 1000?" Siuch offers 100; KP, who obviously learned to negotiate in the gnome markets, says "10000?"; Siuch tries "100 with an option to renew?" KP recalls what he read in the gnome library about the fundamental distinction between Devils and Demons: Devils keep to the letter of their contracts, Demons do not follow any ever, and, as Rimardo reminds him, Demons only respect power. Looking for a power to frighten Freedahl, KP says, "Do you know of Lundbeams who is the protector of the realms? We are her emissaries." The quasit is impressed, and KP adds, "It would be a shame if you were to come through the portal and have Lundbeams destroy you!" Siuch agrees that the thrice-blessed Lundbeams must be avoided, and spirals into calculation about what would be bad for Freedahl and whether it would be bad for Siuch, until paranoia takes him: "Spies are everywhere. Perhaps he has a spy here?" KP reinforces it: "we could send him a message." Resigned, his visions of new power dissipating, Siuch concedes: "very well, my master will accept 1000 years!"

The group weighs it. A thousand years is long after they will all be dead; Rimardo notes Lundbeams will be gone in 20 years from old age, "and thus, this is only a deal for 20 years"; the portal may jump from their plane anyway; and KP, always the optimist, wonders, "perhaps we will discover how to control the portal?" KP then asks whether Freedahl is the most powerful demon around, and Siuch reiterates Freedahl's commitment to Graz’zt and that Freedahl is tasked with monitoring the Drow. When KP asks about Demogorgon, Siuch is offended: "Demogorgon, blessed be his name, has no representative here!" KP shrugs, "just asking." Siuch's eyes bulge, then he leans in and asks, "you know of Demogorgon’s allies? Here?" KP lands him hook, line and sinker: "You may not have been the first offer we got!" Terrified of Freedahl's eternal dungeons, the little demon stamps, spins, jumps up and down, and says, "I offer 10,000 years! Not a day more. Demogorgon would never be so generous!"

Bayard is pleased with KP's negotiation, but Siuch tries to add an addendum about the long-term ownership of the group's soul. KP rejects the clauses; Siuch claims they are boilerplate and that KP is not authorized to review such contracts; KP counters that they are under Drow jurisdiction, outside the Abyssal legal structure, so Siuch is not legally allowed to produce opinions on the deal. Siuch jumps up and down like a small child. Bayard demands the contract be signed quickly, and it is: Bayard, the great Paladin of Juste-Havre, has signed an agreement with the demon Freedahl to support his conquest of the city of the Drow, in exchange for Freedahl not invading Bayard's plane of existence for 10,000 years. KP and Rimardo are delighted that it cost nothing in gold. Siuch gives them a small whistle to signal Freedahl after the tower is captured, turns into a rat, and disappears.

Selzyt wakes up: "Did you just sign a contract with Freedahl?" Rimardo is done with conversations and recommends discarding the gem, but Bayard, a man of honor and beyond reproach, takes Selzyt's phylactery because of his deal. Selzyt explains the slave houses: the Drow cull several hundred slaves from the planes they occupy and keep them in buildings near the citadel across from the garrison. The slaves do not escape, because the occupants of Plagueridge Rampart would kill them on sight, and they die of hunger because they have no permit to get goodberries, so they tend to go back to the Drow. KP wonders whether the demons would be worse. Selzyt says, "The demons are not interested in live souls, only dead ones," and that the residents "are not good people"; the system pushes them to perform evil acts so they end up in the abyss eventually. "the most important thing to realize about Freedahl is that he really needs this city," the gem says, because his relationship with Graz’zt is complicated: his former boss was Demogorgon, he was supposed to conquer the city a while ago, and he is on a short leash. The slave pens, the gem drones on, are "large windowless buildings next to cobblestone streets with Hobgoblin guards at the doors with saber-tooth tigers."

Selzyt reminds them that the objective is to free the slaves and arm them to distract the garrison while the party takes the Citadel, and directs them for weapons to the garrison HQ, on the other side of the citadel across from the slave pens. The plan: KP and Corrin sneak into the slave pens, Corrin frees the slaves from their chains, and KP creates a portal into the garrison, where the slaves will arm themselves. They choose a building at random. KP and Corrin sneak in while Bayard and Rimardo wait by the garrison.

Inside, Corrin gestures for the invisible KP to speak; KP points back at Corrin, who cannot see the finger. KP realizes the plan is flawed: Corrin cannot speak, and when KP does, "it is like a screaming child in an airplane with broken toilets." He opens with a joke, "woo, it’s haunted!", and a third of the slaves become catatonic with fear, because the last thing that said that sucked the blood from many slaves, after which the furious Drow whipped the hobgoblin guards, who beat the slaves. As Corrin frees more slaves, they ask him questions, and the silent one remains silent. KP goes flamboyant: "Shazaam!" he says as he becomes visible, then, "Knazreponnium’s traveling adventure! Are you willing to go on an adventure?" The slaves are convinced KP is insane, but not dangerous.

At the garrison, Gruneel, Rimardo and Bayard realize it is not empty. Bayard thinks to go in and kill the soldiers until Rimardo points out that with Corrin missing he can do nothing. Bayard agrees: "bravery when you can do nothing is simply foolishness! We wait! And if our friends die, then we will go hand in hand to kill all of the demons of the abyss until we are dead!" Rimardo nods.

In the slave pens, KP announces, "We are going to get out of here!" A dwarf asks how, and KP, spreading his arms like an explosion "with the grace of a cart with one broken wheel," says, "MAGIC!" The dwarf asks whether they are the ones who escaped from the portal and whether they control it; KP says, "yes, we occupied it! We have a contract." Asked whether he controls the citadel, KP says, "No, because we need your help. And we heard that there was a stout dwarf who could help us!" The flattered dwarf says, "The Drow took me from my kingdom and replaced me with a false thane. I am truly that dwarf that you need," and rouses the slaves: "We have talked about our freedom, about our vengeance, it is now or never! Let us follow the crazy gnome!"

As the hobgoblins try to enter the pens, KP casts his spell and the slaves jump through the portal into the garrison. KP and Corrin charge first. KP's cone-of-cold hits the surprised Drow for 40 HP; of the original 4 knights and 8 squires, only 3 squires and one uninjured knight remain, with three more knights severely wounded. Rimardo's attempt to hold a person on the four knights fails, because the knights have a boon that protects them from spells once. Corrin dashes to a knight, thrusts his rapier into the soft, inviting flesh of the Drow, and hits a cushion.

## Locations
### Plagueridge Rampart
A city that exists to protect and manage the portal, "a tiny little eco-bubble inside the abyss." Its walls protect the locals from the abyss; its people, not from the Abyss or Drow, are trapped and fed goodberries brought back by the Drow Rangers.

### The citadel
The stronghold the party must take to control the town. The slave houses lie near it, across from the garrison.

### The slave pens (slave houses)
Large windowless buildings next to cobblestone streets, with Hobgoblin guards and saber-tooth tigers at the doors. KP and Corrin enter one chosen at random and free the slaves.

### The garrison HQ
On the other side of the citadel across from the slave pens; the source of weapons. It is not empty: 4 knights and 8 squires are inside when the slaves come through KP's portal.

## NPCs
### Siuch
A quasit who first appears as a rat, servant of Freedahl. He speaks in a squeak, flatters his master endlessly, is easily panicked and paranoid about spies, and is outmaneuvered by KP into offering 10,000 years. He tries to slip in an addendum on the group's soul, gives them a whistle to signal Freedahl, and leaves as a rat.

### Freedahl
Does not appear. Siuch's master, a demon lord who wants the portal and needs the city; Selzyt says his former boss was Demogorgon and he is on a short leash. Under the signed contract he will support the party's conquest and not invade Bayard's plane for 10,000 years.

### Selzyt
The gem. He wakes in time to ask about the contract, explains the slave houses and why Freedahl needs the city, and steers the plan toward freeing and arming the slaves.

### The dwarf
An unnamed slave who says the Drow took him from his kingdom and replaced him with a false thane. Flattered by KP, he rallies the slaves: "Let us follow the crazy gnome!"

### The Drow patrol
Hunting the intruders and threatening to withhold goodberries from anyone who shelters them.
