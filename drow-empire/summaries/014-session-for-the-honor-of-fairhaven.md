# Chapter 14 – For the Honor of Fairhaven!

Date: 19 Wealsun 595 to 23 Wealsun 595 CY (595-06-19 to 595-06-23; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
After convincing Primus Yagrat to start creating a coalition to wage war on the Drow, the party takes a few days of downtime to question Halete. Using a zone of truth spell, they ask all sorts of questions, and the answers are surprising. Halete's definition of freedom for the Wild Coast "is not quite the kind of freedom that everyone in the Wild Coast aspires to." It looks more like the freedom of the Wild Coast Liberation Front to impose "its doctrinaire form of government through a totalitarian regime that would free everyone from the need to think." Halete believes such a scheme is necessary to organise the people of the Wild Coast and guarantee their freedom from outsiders; in her mind, "freedom of outsiders demanded subservience to the WLF." Which makes one wonder what exactly Lundbeams is thinking.

About Lundbeams, the party learns two things of importance. First, Lundbeams is being blackmailed: "Apparently, the Drow held her daughter hostage." Second, the nature of the betrayal. According to Halete, Lundbeams was supposed to send the party directly to Fhuu, not to stop at Vallaki and try to halt the Drow advance. But Lundbeams was a little more devious, and thought her independent contractors could stop the advancing Drow army. Then the party hit the Drow General, Nimh, with a fireball. That fireball told the Drow general that someone had sent help to Vallaki's defenders, and "the only group that would be stupid enough to use a fireball against a Drow were members of the Wild Coast Liberation Front or some independent contractors." Nimh was intelligent enough to work out that Lundbeams was satisfying the letter of her orders, not the spirit.

Incensed and wanting revenge on the WLF and Lundbeams, Nimh demanded that Halete's troops, a full detachment of WLF fighters, have "the honor to attack first." He withheld support during the attack, judging it more efficient to have the WLF killed softening up Vallaki's defenders than to kill them himself. The WLF fighters were massacred. "Only Halete and three other members survived," by pretending to die during the slaughter. Afterwards they slinked off to Fhuu, hoping to find the independent contractors and get them to do their job: get the Orcs to attack Fax with a local alliance.

The party is in shock. The WLF's agenda sounds less like "the hippy-dippy save Wild Coast" and more like "a scary cultish group with a secret plan to subjugate the region." Suddenly, being an independent contractor does not seem such a bad idea.

A few days later, better equipped with more armour from another member of the WLF ("what is it with women smiths that are members of the WLF"), the party is invited to the opening of the conference. Present are the elves of Istivin (Lord Virmaer) and Juli (Lord Daevalur); the humans of Fairhaven (Sir Ryton), Noldrun (Earl Berit) and Suzal (Bishop Arne); the gnomes of Fort Dolor and Antira, both represented by Burgher Farkur; the dwarves of Darnagal (Thane Balgron); and the halflings of Yonderdell (First Fellow Zenzu).

At the opening, an Orcish Constable Jugag marches in with members of the local community and accuses members of the alliance of stealing from the local merchants. The party is incensed: "this bozo was about to blow everything up." Sir Ryton of Juste-Havre (Fairhaven) reaches out to Bayard and asks him to do anything. The party, fed up, pulls out the head of the dead Drow, drops it on the ground, and asks if anyone has any questions. "The Drow head silences the crowd." The danger is evident, the townspeople admit they do not recognise any of the people in the meeting, and the conference begins.

Primus Yagrat and Sir Ryton call for a sidebar. Sir Ryton, who never respected Bayard in the past, is now very obsequious. Constable Jugag has brought bad news: "someone dressed in the livery of Juste-Havre attacked a farm on the outskirts of Fhuu." The Primus agrees with Sir Ryton that this would be a grave breach of honour, but admits there are many like his own second in command, Centurion Guttja: someone from Juste-Havre may be allying with the Drow to blow up the meeting. The truth must be uncovered, and the Primus lets the adventurers investigate. Their role as independent contractors for the WLF marks them as someone Lundbeams trusts, and Bayard's character, "without reproach," guarantees the right outcome. Sir Ryton is delighted and reminds Bayard of his obligations to the honour of Juste-Havre. Bayard, practically driven out of the town in the past, is surprised by how many people now want to be his friend "and offer to butter his brioche."

On the way to the farm the party is assaulted by a set of brigands wearing the livery of Juste-Havre. Once they are defeated and unmasked, the party knows the brigands are not from Juste-Havre, and Rimardo declares the mission complete. But Bayard remembers the mission Sir Ryton gave him, to protect the honour of Juste-Havre and to protect the farmers, and insists they move on. Rimardo is annoyed, and more so when he finds only "a pittance of treasure" on the brigands.

At the farmhouse, more brigands dressed as members of the Juste-Havre military have taken over the farm. They tell the party to leave or they will kill the Orcish farmers. Bayard, incensed at the treachery, declares they will not move. The brigand leader takes his hostages into the homestead and sends his thugs to kill the party. A mass of bad guys is coming, and the hostages are inside a house with armed guards ready to kill them if the party gets too close.

"KP is not phased but instead phases." He grabs Corrin by the hand and dimension doors the two of them into the house with the brigands and hostages, as the chapter's map marks with a red arrow. The brigands in the homestead are startled to be attacked from the inside. Corrin exploits their surprise to attack, and KP moves to protect the orcs. Outside, 6 thugs surround Bayard. Rimardo uses hold person to hold 2, and Bayard kills the remaining four, using his power to smite and staying alive thanks to Rimardo's heal spells. Bayard appreciates Rimardo's healing but realises he must not get too used to it. Inside, "KP demonstrates his tactical military brilliance," and he and Corrin outflank and kill the brigands.

With the brigands dead, the group tries to question the farmers, who are in shock. The Orcish matron demands that the party take the dead bodies outside and clean her house. The Orcish male thanks Rimardo and Bayard for their help and asks whether they killed "the black elf leader." The party realises more bad guys are coming. They hide the dead thugs' bodies and wait quietly for the Drow leader to arrive.
