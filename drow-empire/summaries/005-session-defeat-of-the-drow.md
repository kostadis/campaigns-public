# Chapter 5 – Defeat of the Drow

Date: 9 Flocktime 595 CY (595-05-09; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
Morning arrives, and everyone wants the party out of the village. A woman screams at them over the death of her son on the scout expedition; another says the Drow promised to let them be if the party went north. An old man walks up to them. Bayard tries to explain that, strictly speaking, he was not responsible, but things are going south.

A woman who owns the local smithy appears and invites them to her smithy to talk safely. There she introduces herself — "Branigan Cartwright" — as a follower of St. Cuthbert, and says their presence has been foretold. When Rimardo asks, "Really?", she admits that it was not; she just thought it would make them feel better. Bayard is crushed, and KP is sad that no occult science will be discovered.

Branigan sees the Drow presence as an opportunity: the Orc Empire does not have the manpower to hold the entire Wild Coast, and "We could leverage the Drow to get rid of the Orcs!" When KP asks what is wrong with the Orcs, she answers that as a gnome he knows — "They rape, and they pillage, and they murder." KP adds gnome tossing, and Branigan says these orcs cut the heads off first and toss the heads. Rimardo's contribution is that the orcs have treasure they do not deserve. But Branigan also finds something odd about the Drow being here: "It is well known that there is no path from the Underdark to here." She claims her god told her to talk to them, and when KP asks, "he did?", she answers, "No, just thought it would make you feel more important." Bayard is crestfallen.

Bayard tells the group that "We must venture forth," that there are Drow spies in town and they are being given contradictory information, and that "in general, we should kill the Drow." Rimardo hopes for lower prices; Bayard predicts the innkeeper will demand higher ones. The spies will know which way they go, so KP proposes a trap, and they agree on a plan in which Bayard stands "without fear and without reproach alone" while the party attacks from behind. Bayard asks Rimardo whether, if they are all attacking him, "perhaps this time you will join the fight instead of leaving me alone?" To keep the Drow from wondering where his allies are, Bayard suggests they pretend to be angry with one another. Rimardo answers, "Ok, boss," and has to be reminded he is supposed to be upset, whereupon he declares, "Right! I hate you! The townspeople hate me, and I have gotten no treasure! I will never fight with you again! Come, Mr. Corrin!" KP refuses to fight with Bayard and follows Rimardo — "I will follow and learn your mercantile ways" — and he and Rimardo very publicly vow never to work with "the knight without butter and without brioche!" Bayard hopes they really do follow him, or "this will be the inglorious end of the Knight without Fear and without Reproach!"

The party goes north, flanking Bayard, and allies of the Drow attack: Hobgoblins burst out of the trail. Their leader reports to Lyless that they have found the party and will kill them, and Lyless answers, "Yes, do it!" Corrin misses both attacks on a Hobgoblin, and everyone stares. Bayard flanks Lud and lands a critical hit for 27 HP of damage, and Corrin kills it. Corrin's smugness fades when the dead Hobgoblins rise again: invisible goblins are passing healing potions to the fallen. The party eventually defeats them and takes a short rest on the battlefield, with Rimardo holding the sending stone so KP does not start any conversations with Lyless. But the Drow soldiers know where they are — "A curious side effect of the sending stone."

Hiding in the trees, the party sees two Drow Initiates, three Drow warriors, an acolyte and an owl. Corrin's attempt to sneak fails, and the first Drow warrior uses faerie fire on Rimardo and KP. Corrin's assassin ability does 18 HP of damage to a Drow. Lyless casts magic missile at Corrin. KP's ice storm hits the two initiates. Bayard kills the Drow Corrin injured, but the owl swoops down and casts cure light wounds, reviving it to 4 HP, and KP realises the owl is a familiar. Kelyrd taunts Bayard, "You did not think it would be this easy?", and Bayard replies, "No matter, the forces of goodwill always win." Rimardo smirks: "You would say that".

The Drow focus Bayard with magic missiles. "Bayard says – have no fear, I can heal myself," and Rimardo retorts, "You say that every time we go into battle." KP's magic missiles kill the owl and Teboyn, and Lyless deflects another with the shield. Bayard orders Corrin to "Attack the Initiate," and Corrin, unwilling to be surrounded, flanks Kelyrd for 16 HP instead. Lyless's magic missile drops the Paladin to 1 HP — only the Aid spell Rimardo cast that morning keeps him alive — and Rimardo channels Istus's divinity to give Bayard 16 HP. Bayard screams, "See! I am indestructible!!" Another magic missile leaves him at 2 HP, again saved by Aid.

KP's thunderwave kills Kelyrd and exposes the Initiate to attacks from Bayard and Rimardo. Corrin kills the Initiate despite her Shield, and turns his offhand on Lyless. Lyless misty steps away and casts fire bolt at KP, then counter-spells KP's Melf’s acid arrow. Corrin kills the acolyte after she heals herself, and strikes Lyless again. With Lyless all alone on the battlefield, Bayard tells her, "Surrender!" She agrees "only if you swear to not kill me!", and Bayard, uncertain, answers "It depends, and it’s more than you will get if you stick around here" — and persuades her to surrender.
