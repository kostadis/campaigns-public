# Chapter 32 – Rimardo, the hero.

Date: The Abyss, Day 3, then Brungril Forge, Day 1 (??? on Oerth; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
After the capture of Plagueridge Rampart, the freeing of the slaves and the arrival of Freedahl, the party heads toward the portal along with all the slaves it has just released. From the sources at their disposal they have learned that the portal moves among several realms, some but not all colonized by the Drow. Three hold Drow Empire forces who could mount a surprise counterattack through the portal if they are not dealt with: Giuht Caverns, The Ailmar Wilds, and Mount Flagurc.

The party's position is grim. Defending Plagueridge Rampart against both the Drow Empire's inevitable attempt to retake it and an invasion through the trans-dimensional portal would be impossible. Freedahl is unwilling to defend the city against a Drow assault through the portal, because the Drow would then point at him as "the real brains and brawn behind the conquest of Plagueridge Rampart." Waiting is not an option either: the city depends on goodberries, and with the Drow clerics dead the food will run out in about five days unless the city secures a supply line from another plane. Most of the freed slaves hope to go home through the portal, but cannot until the other landing points are safe. So the only option is to go through the portal and eliminate the Drow invaders — to clear the way for the refugees, let native populations rebel against the Drow Empire, and secure Plagueridge Rampart. The city itself, torn between Drow, slaves, slave masters, demons and permanent residents, is paralyzed; the only camp able to defeat the Drow while preserving the balance of power is the party. Or, as KP says, "this is a job for some independent contractors."

More bad news arrives as they prepare: the portal stays in each location for only about 24 hours, so each situation must be resolved quickly. A few hours after they enter the control room, the shudder and groan of the portal announces its activation, and with a blink and another tremor they are somewhere else. The cleanup detail meant to dispose of the white dragons never got to do its job, and "The combination of rotting Dragon and 150 unbathed humanoids has made for a very ripe and rancid smell."

As the party is about to leave the control room, Selzyt screams, "be careful! They are coming! We are on the prime material plane, just on another planet." KP perks up: "another planet? What is another planet?" — "Now? You’re asking me about planets now?" But the gnome's eager face and his interest in interplanetary trade win out, and in a few short minutes KP learns of the phlogiston, the circle of spheres, the turtles that hold up the planets, and the legend of the spelljammer, along with how many planets there are and how the stars are "sometimes balls of fire and sometimes lanterns and sometimes flies." Selzyt keeps interrupting himself: "you are the first 17th creature to ever ask me about this. All these years, the Drow never cared to learn what the true nature of the prime material plane was." He is so excited that he describes the entire planet and the location of the caverns on it. Bayard rolls his eyes.

Before anyone can say anything wittier or meaner, four quaggoths emerge from the ground, and poisonous gas pours into the room with them, harming everyone. The heroes survive; the slaves fall unconscious, except for a half-giant and Marco. The fight is brutal. A quaggoth hits Bayard for 38 points of damage, another slashes Corrin for 38, and Corrin pierces it for 31; Bayard's great sword answers for 28. Rimardo channels his divinity and heals 65 of the unconscious slaves, who now have 1 HP each. KP casts Mordenkainen’s Sword and misses. The quaggoth fighting Corrin turns on the mob and kills 19 of the slaves; the slaves strike back for 22 points of damage, and the quaggoth casts hellish rebuke and kills them all. Rimardo is upset. Corrin is frustrated that his friend thinks "being a goody-goody two shoes is a good plan."

Corrin kills the quaggoth he was fighting, but it had a death ward spell cast on it. Rimardo takes 19 points of slashing damage; Bayard is hit with a critical for 38 and slashes back for 28. Rimardo prays to Istus for divine intervention, asking her to create a gate that will let the slaves escape from this deathtrap. Istus ponders his request and ultimately denies it. KP's magic missile finishes the quaggoth Corrin had brought to death's door and hits the one facing Bayard for five points of damage, and his sword slashes it for 18. Corrin kills that monster with 11 points of damage, and when it recovers using its death ward, he stabs it dead again. KP takes 19 points of damage; the slaves hit the quaggoth for 22, and "The pet of the Drow responds with hellish rebuke killing 27 slaves." Bayard's greatsword lands for 16, and a hellish rebuke he is too slow to dodge costs him 30.

With Istus ignoring his prayers, Rimardo channels her divinity to heal 65 of the slaves, bringing them back from death's door, and tells them to stay out of combat. His mass healing word heals Bayard, Corrin, KP and another eight slaves. KP's sword misses and he takes 19 points of damage, but his cantrips inflict 51. Corrin attacks the same quaggoth twice and kills it. The last quaggoth lacerates Bayard's arm and he loses control of his sword arm; he heals himself of 60 points of damage. Rimardo deals 17 points of necrotic damage, KP slashes for 11 and misses with his fire bolts, and Corrin sneaks up for 38, seeing the hellish rebuke "from a mile away" and moving out of range. The enraged quaggoth slashes Bayard for 52 points of damage, and Bayard kills the wounded monster.

The chamber is full of dead slaves, dead dragons and dead quaggoths, the floors slick with blood and guts. Rimardo feels the weight of his failure to save everyone. Corrin is confused. Bayard is happy to see the spark of heroism in his companion, while empathizing with the cleric's grief. Rimardo asks if anyone has spare resurrection scrolls or raise dead spells; KP puts his head in his hands and shakes it vigorously, wondering what has happened to his cynical friend. Rimardo is not even looking for treasure — but KP is, and surprisingly finds a silver mask inlaid with gems and a comb inlaid with Jade. Istus did not answer Rimardo's prayers, but "she must have been pleased with his choices for treasure was found." Corrin, thinking it over, realizes that Istus is about balance: while he kills, Rimardo heals, and in healing brings balance. "Healing is not just so Corrin can snuff more lives, sometimes it is just an act of mercy."
