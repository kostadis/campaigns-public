# Chapter 1 – The Inn at the River

Date: 3 Flocktime 595 CY (595-05-03; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
The chapter opens on an idea that "seemed like a good one at the time": four strange and proud adventurers heading into the Wild Coast to hunt for gold and adventure. Their friend Boradoon, who was connected to people in Greyhawk, told them that Farjvad was a great place to go looking for evildoers, because something was apparently going on there. The team seemed perfect — "The proud Triton Paladin, and the sly bard, and cleric who could cast spells..." — except that it "went to shit very quickly." The Triton drank like a fish.

The party arrives late at night knowing little about Farjvad beyond an old journal entry handed to Boradoon by his contacts, and Boradoon promises that those contacts will get them a meeting with the Major. A few drinks at the Inn at the River, followed by a quick meeting with the current Major Carc Mistsplitter, seems like a good idea at the time. Instead the Triton gets seriously drunk and starts to curse, and Walter Caskbow, a half-orc, finds the behaviour objectionable and demands that they leave and head across the river. Boradoon is stunned to be kicked out for boorish behaviour by a half-orc.

At 17:00 the heroes are thrown out of the Inn at the River by an irate Walter Caskbow, who mutters that in the famous orc metropolis of Fhuu this would never have happened. Triton, Dimitrov, Reepicheep, and Boradoon end up on the docks of the Tlerowth River. There they meet Alexia Boulderdown, a woman with a long silver mohawk, black eyes, rough pink skin, an overweight build and a sharp, attractive face, who squints whenever she talks as if the sun on the river has hurt her eyes. Seeing the triton stagger out, she knows she has an easy mark: the only place to stay is the River Drink, a seedy tavern and hotel, and she is the only person working the late shift. Being a straight shooter, she cannot resist pointing out that they have to spend the night there.

As they board the boat, Reepicheep tries to be helpful by asking for an oar, and Alexia is deeply offended. She blames the patriarchy and the Major, who is from Fhuu, where, she says, "Orc women are only expected to give birth and take care of the house." Boradoon tries to point out that Reepicheep is a mouse, but Alexia is on a roll.

The ride is uneventful until four aquatic zombies try to board the little boat. There is grappling, jostling and jumping, and the zombies keep refusing to die. Everyone notices that the zombies ignore Alexia. Boradoon is grappled and crushed by a zombie, and then Reepicheep is grappled too; Reepicheep at first escapes, but Boradoon is not as lucky. The Triton forks the zombies and Dimitrov casts spells "like it was going out of style," searing them with guiding bolts while the trident chews them up. The party slaughters the zombies before they take anyone. Zombie monsters have apparently been catching unwary travelers for a while, and Alexia is furious that the Major refuses to do anything about it and insists that no boatman speak of it, because it would be bad for tourism.

At the docks on the south end they meet Boris Boulderdown, a 60-year-old peddler with cropped wavy grey hair, green eyes, sunburned pink skin, a banal oval face and a piercing in his left ear, who stands 6’3” tall. He is Alexia's uncle, though he does not introduce himself that way at first. Having seen the massacre of the aquatic zombies, Boris decides to nudge the travelers in the right general direction, and Alexia, seeing that her uncle will not shut up, spills the Major's secret: many travelers have been kidnapped over the last little while.

Boradoon realises this is why his contacts sent him here. Recovering from the haze of his terror, he sees that the zombies were far more organised than mindless beasts should be: someone had trained them, someone had targeted the party, and that somebody had to be a necromancer. The Triton jumps into the water to search for a hidden lair and finds nothing — no fish, and no sign of dead travelers of wealth. Boradoon remembers the journal and its reference to the temple of Mort, and Boris volunteers that the temple has plenty of dead bodies. Although it is late and the adventurers want to rest, they rest only a little and set out.

At 8pm, a few hundred yards from the Temple of Mort, a log comes hurtling through the air and grazes the Triton. Out of the trees come four cultists, humans devoted to protecting the temple. Boris, it seems, was willing to talk about the Major's omissions but unwilling to share the details he knew.
