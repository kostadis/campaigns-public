# Chapter 44 – More Betrayals are Revealed

Date: The Abyss, Day 99 (??? on Oerth; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
The fight with the white dragons continues. Corrin, armed with foresight, kills the closest dragon, then walks across the area and lightly injures another. KP peers out of the house he was hiding in and casts magic missile at a dragon, to no effect, "as the dragon shimmers blue." The dragon sees where KP was hiding, realises it is a perfect place for its breath weapon, swoops down, sticks its head into the one-room house and breathes on KP and the family hiding inside. "The man, his wife, and two children die." KP is injured and in shock. Rimardo is determined to heal them: the Drow and their monsters have killed too many innocents.

Rimardo's toll the dead fails as the dragon shimmers blue. Another dragon attacks Corrin, but "the silent assassin foresees all the attacks and is uninjured," and Corrin strikes it for 14 HP of damage as it tries to fly away. A dragon breathes on Bayard from across an alley; the paladin dodges and takes only 22 HP of frost damage. "Our hero, who is without fear and without reproach, jumps across the alley onto the roof with the dragon," and hits it, invoking the divine powers he worships, for 42 HP of damage. Corrin kills one of the dragons near Bayard "with a precise blow to the head." KP's magic missile is again absorbed as the dragon shimmers blue. Seeing only two dragons left, Rimardo decides the assassin and the paladin can handle it, leans back, and watches. A dragon scores a critical hit on Bayard for 33 points of damage, and another breathes on him, "but all it does is melt the butter of his brioche." Left with one hit point, Bayard attacks and kills the dragon that tore at him. Only one dragon is left, and Corrin finishes the battle.

Rimardo channels Istus' divinity and heals the innocent victims of the white dragons and Bayard. The adults, breathing again, start screaming at KP, accusing him of putting them and their children in danger, and KP leaves apologizing profusely; his lack of tact does not help. Rimardo wonders whether the dead dragons carry treasure, and Corrin sighs on realising there is none.

Someone is screaming, "the Drow has pierced the walls of the city. Someone must do something." A militia member in a commander's armor — pale skin, blue eyes, dark brown hair cropped short — grabs Bayard: "you, you must go and do something." Rimardo wonders aloud, "Will our patron demon lord, quasi god, ruler do anything to help us?" The majordomo appears: "You called?" He has not come to fight. If the city were captured, Freedahl would leave and let the Drow take possession of the portal, and "he suspects that the revenge of the Drow on your plane would be terrible." KP speaks icily: "Freedahl is now resorting to blackmail?" The majordomo restates the terms: "If you deal with the Drow empire, then Freedahl will ensure the portal will not be used to invade your plane," and Freedahl will defend the city while they are alive and have not fled, "But he will not defend the city to his death." Rimardo's counteroffer — "why don't you join us?" — is refused, and the majordomo adds that his master "considered swapping your location in the city for continued ownership of Flaka. The Drow have refused this generous offer, so far." If the Drow killed the party and Freedahl kept the city, the deal would be off. The party realises the deal they made has been changed, and heads for the city walls.

At the walls they find dead soldiers everywhere and hobgoblins, "the vanguard of the Drow imperial army," walking through the demolished wall. Corrin and Rimardo achieve surprise; "KP and Bayard wake up the dead." Corrin kills one hobgoblin with one strike and wounds another, and Rimardo piles on with toll the dead. Hobgoblins hit Corrin for 28 points of damage and 47 more. "Corrin smiles, for it is his turn." KP drops a fireball, Rimardo kills the wounded hobgoblin with a cantrip, Bayard cruelly wounds another with his divine smite, and Corrin, "who can see every attack," inflicts 36 points of damage on the one that dared try to hit him. Bayard kills the last one. The cowering soldiers cry, "huzzah!" and rush to rebuild the breach and man the walls.

An officer of the guard warns that "a Drow assassination team ran into the city to kill Freedahl. They are headed towards the tower." Rimardo shrugs — "surely a demon has nothing to fear from a collection of Drow?" — and the officer smiles at him as if he were a child: "if the Drow assassination team gets close, Freedahl will flee, and the demons who guard the city will also flee." Rimardo is excited: the tower he did not destroy with an earthquake spell is now a legitimate target, "and more importantly, the treasure has been plundered." The officer is concerned that Rimardo is more interested in casting earthquake than in saving Freedahl. Rimardo brushes it off and summons the majordomo — "You called?" — who says they know of the attempt, but "The Drow assassins have magic that makes it impossible to use divination or scrying to find them," and hoped the party would find them. Rimardo has "a better plan": Freedahl waits in the castle, the party creates a portal for him to escape when the assassins come in, "and then we will cast an earthquake and destroy the tower. They will die inside." The majordomo blinks out and back in: "my master Freedahl agrees with your plan." When KP says he has no portal spells left, the majordomo snorts, "Lord Freedahl does not need your puny mortal magic to move in his own domain."

After a leisurely perusal of the hobgoblins' treasures, while the captain of the militia looks at them frantically, the party heads to the citadel and, within range of the spell, notifies the majordomo. Freedahl appears: "This is an excellent plan. However, I must caution you that this is the Abyss. And the crust separating the Abyss and the city is quite thin. It's unclear what will happen." Rimardo casts the spell. Three injured assassins escape the rubble while one is still trapped inside; Corrin dashes at the staggered Drow and misses, and everyone is in shock. "But the outcome does not change." There are tense moments: the assassins parry all the group's attacks, teleport out of combat, seem to have a natural form of cloaks of displacement, and carry swords of wounding. Regardless, the battle ends with the Drow dead.

Then Valnozz shows up — the spy who saved the alliance's armies at the battle of Vallaki and revealed the complexity of Lolth's domains and her plots, and who "always seems to appear at the very oddest of moments." "As you know, I work against Lolth. I am a follower of Vhaeraun," he says, and the Drow Empire of Maenerius "is in dire straits." House Zollazo must defeat the party now or risk losing everything; "Even if they lose the city, killing you would reestablish their supremacy." The alliance with House Illal is shattered, Illal having lost so many of its house on the planes, and House Zollazo is forced to rely on demonic mercenaries and monsters. If the Drow Empire fails to capture Plagueridge Rampart, many noble houses will want to replace "her," though "her power is immense and open rebellion dangerous."

Valnozz draws on a map: a road leads to the valley of the banshees, where "The Drow general has a patch of hallowed ground and has placed his HQ." He relies on his own powers and the banshees to keep all away; "Find him and kill him, and the mercenaries will abandon this battle." Valnozz does not know exactly where the general is, but the mercenaries who guard the trebuchets have a communication device that can find him. They must hurry: when enough non-demons have died from the poison being lobbed, Freedahl's demons will flee — "They will not die FOR this city." Afterwards they should come to the Gilded Dryad, where he will tell them how to get into Maenerius and reach the noble houses, each of which will try to sway them and "will all ask you to destroy the matron. If you are successful, the ones who made the deal will rule over the Drow. If you fail, all the houses will insist that there was no betrayal." Before they can ask more, he disappears.
