# Chapter 28 – The Garrison HQ is Under Assault

Date: The Abyss, Day 2 (??? on Oerth; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
The war for control of the portal continues. Having freed some 17 slaves led by Marco the dwarf, the party attacks the garrison HQ. The plan: seize weapons, arm the slaves, use that distraction to attack the citadel, hand over Plagueridge Rampart for 10,000 years to a demon, "make a small stop at a temple of Heironeous near Fax to kill an annoying cleric," and ride off into the sunset. But first they must defeat the forces in the garrison.

KP's portal from the Slave Pens surprises the lounging garrison, and his cone of cold kills many. Corrin is surprisingly ineffective. Bayard bursts in from the door, the slaves swarm the heavily armed Drow troops, and Gruneel stops at the door to guard it, "Because that is what he does, he defends the entrance."

The Drow Knight (7) Kalanolg of House Illal, down to 12 HP, declares himself for the cause of Lolth and hits Corrin twice for 15 HP. KP targets a squire and singes the carpet. Bayard kills the other Drow Knight (4), Welvatlab of House Illal, then misses Kalanolg. Rimardo, overcoming his feelings of inadequacy and Corrin's mean-spirited comments, hits Gelerd of House Illal with a cantrip, and Gelerd is unaffected; some unique ability of the Drow of the abyss seems to make the spell ineffective. Corrin is not surprised. Rimardo is crushed, and then Gelerd uses a reaction to cast Hellish Rebuke on him for 17 HP, and hits him once more for 10. Corrin kills Kalanolg, then misses Gelerd.

Corrin's third miss shocks everyone but the destinist faction of the cult of Istus, who believe fate controls everything. To them, the chapter says, Corrin does not miss because "some magical 20-sided die is rolled," but because she chooses to have him miss; and "Corrin acts as if he is in control of his fate, instead of her, and that is heresy." Istus punishes those who question why things happen. Rimardo is aware of this somewhere in his mind and worries that Corrin's tempting of Istus will get them all killed.

The three squires surrounding Quaphyn the Knight enter the combat. The first gives Quaphyn a bonus to attack and then crits Corrin with a hand crossbow for 10 HP; Corrin is poisoned and falls unconscious. The destinists smile: "Istus does not mess around when she punishes her people," and "Corrin’s string looks alarmingly short right now." The second squire runs up and hits Corrin for 10 HP, and Corrin realizes he is not unconscious, "just freaked out"; Istus has apparently decided to stop punishing him. The third squire's crossbow knocks Bayard unconscious. The fourth Knight, Imdar of House Illal, shouts encouragement to his squires. The freed slaves run up to Bayard and slap him silly, waking him. KP's cantrip at the second squire is blocked by counterspell, and KP, annoyed, hides behind a chair. Bayard hits the first squire for 11 HP. Rimardo's Toll the Dead on Gelerd fails. Gelerd hits Corrin twice for 15 HP, and Corrin kills Gelerd, then charges squire 3 for 4 HP and is down to 27 HP. The squires hit Bayard for 10 and miss with a crossbow; Imdar hits Bayard for 10. The slaves swarm Imdar to give Bayard advantage; KP's fire bolt kills the squire Corrin was fighting; Bayard hits Imdar for 23 HP; Rimardo's cantrip meets a Knight who is immune. Corrin moves to take advantage of Bayard's positioning, and Bayard, disgusted that the "Knight-to-Knight battle" is sullied, accepts the help in the name of practicality. A single squire is left; he could surrender, "but Lolth would get her revenge," and he hits Corrin for 2 HP. KP hits him for 18 HP, and Bayard kills him.

The slaves jump on the bodies to despoil them, and Rimardo, horrified, screams, "Halt! First, I must take my share. I need local currency." Bayard asks the slaves to arm themselves. Marco says, "17 slaves are not enough, you must free the rest!" Rimardo wants to hole up and take a short rest, and the dwarf is horrified: "A! Short! Rest! Are. You. People. Insane?!!" KP says, "yes." The dwarf looks over the halfling who doesn't speak, the cleric concerned with gold, the magic-user "with the charisma of a piece of chalk on a blackboard being scratched the wrong way," and the hero guarding the door, and concludes, "Yes, you are insane!"

A garrison patrols the citadel and has miraculously not noticed them, and the slaves cannot free the other slaves alone. Bayard wants to free the slaves. Rimardo wants to attack the leaders in the citadel: the guards may kill the slaves, or once the party rules the city they can order the hobgoblins to free them, so the right play is to leave the slaves where they are. The dwarf, rather than listen to the bickering, declares, "We shall cause enough of a distraction! And we shall die gloriously!" Rimardo is delighted: "That’s fantastic! You gotta love dwarves!" The dwarf warns that once the riot starts, the Drow garrisoning the city will visit the Garrison HQ, find the dead troops and rush to the citadel, leaving maybe 10 -15 minutes: "There can be no leisurely naps." He tries one last appeal: "One downside of us all being dead, you won’t have anyone to garrison the city! The Drow will overwhelm you!" Bayard, shamed, is about to go after the slaves when he remembers that Freedahl promised muscle to guard the city if the party secured it. Rimardo adds that freeing the slaves would end the distraction.

Bayard thinks aloud, pacing: "To free the slaves or not to free the slaves, that is the question." He cries out to the ceiling, weighing outcomes, and the dwarf is excited that his hero the Paladin will do the right thing. Instead Bayard says, "Our mission is to destroy the Drow menace, to secure Juste-Havre, to protect the artifact. Freeing the slaves cannot get between us and the grander mission. We attack the citadel." The dwarf is crestfallen. Rimardo is delighted.

KP realizes the citadel must be like the fort at the portal, with one level above ground and further levels built underground. The dwarf proposes a plan: "I will run out, drop my pants, and show them my arse, and then run into the city." KP reminds him of the need to taunt twice, and the appalled dwarf says, "Two taunts are dishonorable!" He declares, "We will do our part. And we shall die for the freedom of our realms!" The slaves look at him as if he is insane, but when he raises his ax they answer, "yah?" The dwarf commands the party, "You will die trying to save us!" Bayard shouts, "yes!" while KP, Rimardo and Corrin look at each other sideways, thinking about KP's escape spells and whether they could sell out Bayard if worse comes to worst. Marco runs out shouting "FREEDOM!!!!" and the Drow guarding the citadel follow him.

The party charges into the citadel. Corrin sneaks in, then the rest, except Gruneel, who guards the entrance. Four helmed horrors attack. When Corrin wonders why the Drow is not attacked, Gruneel shouts, "Because they are constructs that the Drow control." KP's fireball on the second reveals they are immune to fire. A horror hits Rimardo for 16 HP; Corrin hits the first for 38 HP; the fourth hits Bayard for 8. Bayard hits the fourth for 38 HP and takes 18 HP from its hellish rebuke, then 24 HP from the third. Rimardo thinks, ponders, and casts word of radiance, 13 HP to the second, third and fourth constructs. KP, from his multi-dimensional vantage point, uses a wall of force to split the room, separating the injured first and fourth horrors from the uninjured second and third. Corrin kills the first horror, missing a few times without realizing Istus must have smiled on him; the Horror forgets its Hellish Rebuke, but Istus wants Corrin to know not all is forgiven, so it hits him for 24 HP. Bayard kills the fourth horror. The two constructs behind the wall of force pound their fists helplessly against it.

Rimardo considers a word of radiance but realizes the wall of force would make it ineffective, and thinks Toll the Dead would meet an immune construct. Bayard's body bleeds while Rimardo, indifferent, looks for a way to help Corrin; seeing no other options, he heals Bayard for 10 HP. "Rimardo is very generous." The stairs to the basement and the lair of the Drow ruler beckon.
