# Chapter 22 – Endings and Beginnings

Date: 1 Goodmonth 595 to 15 Goodmonth 595 CY (595-08-01 to 595-08-15; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
With the defeat of Xhalh, the party finds many chests. Opening them becomes a running contest of method. Rimardo casts Dispel Magic, and Corrin lets him know that sometimes you can search for a trap. Corrin disarms his first trap "under the employ of Mr. Bayard," and the haul — coins and eight gems — has Rimardo crying, "THE TREASURE WE ARE LOOKING FOR!" Rimardo dispels the next trap with Dispel Magic again and glares at Corrin, who shrugs; that chest holds more coin and gems along with a Helm of Comprehending Languages, a Bag of Holding, Dust of Sneezing and Choking, and a Potion of Fire Breath. A fourth level Dispel Magic goes on the next chest, but Corrin finds a tripwire, triggers it while trying to disable it, and is struck by falling rocks. Once satisfied that Corrin is alive, Rimardo declares that Istus has blessed this moment and that the treasure — art objects, two first-level spell scrolls (Sanctuary and Illusory Script), and potions of healing — is still there. Corrin notices a pressure plate under the fifth chest, disables it, and finds twelve gems and three Potions of Healing.

Rimardo is ready to go: "Mr. Bayard, this has been a great partnership. It has taken a long time to get our reward. Nonetheless, you have delivered. We will take our share and move on. Good luck." Mr. Corrin points out that there are five more chests. When Rimardo tries to return, Bayard needles him — "I am sorry you have left. The rest of the treasure does not belong to you." — and Rimardo replies, "I was practicing for after the 10th chest is opened!" Corrin misses the magical trap on the sixth chest, takes 22 HP of damage, and dies; Rimardo has two first-level healing spells left and cures the rogue. The treasure, "however, is okay," including Pipes of Haunting. KP, who had been standing by with his finger raised to cast a dispel magic, investigates the next chest, finds a pit beneath it, and takes careful notes on how to build pit-traps while Rimardo and Corrin glare; inside are a Potion of Poison, a Robe of Useful Items, a potion of flying, and a scroll of Hunger of Hadar. "The expert investigator, KP," finds nothing suspicious about the next chest, opens it, and takes 12 HP of fire damage: "But there is no trap!" Corrin mutters to himself that the mage needs to understand that sometimes theory and practice are in conflict, and reality matters more. Rimardo happily opens it to find coin, gems, a Scroll of Protection - Aberrations, a Potion of Clairvoyance, a Flame Strike scroll, and a Potion of Superior Healing. The last two chests give up art objects, a +2 magical arrow, and a Dominate Beast scroll.

Rimardo and Corrin, spoils in their bags, tell Bayard, "Goodbye!" Bayard wonders if he will now get more trustworthy and reliable allies than these two mercenaries. But Corrin reminds Rimardo that Istus smiled on them and that it would be foolish to leave just yet, and greed overcomes Rimardo: perhaps one last drink with Bayard to tell him what a great leader he is.

With the Drow treasure in tow, the party leaves the abandoned tower for a Fax "now drenched in blood and in flames." They piece it together: Tolub declared Xhalh dead, his troops killed the Drow commanders, and the residents celebrated — but with the Drow dead the local police force dissolved, "because, well, this is Fax." The militia only existed because the Drow insisted on it, the freebooters reverted to pillage and slaughter, and everyone else settled old debts. "In retrospect, the Drow were a force for order in this crazy old town." Rimardo asks, "We are out of here, right?" Bayard is stunned: he thought liberation would bring a better world, and wonders whether his defeat of the Drow was a good or bad thing. Rimardo reminds him they were hired to protect Fhuu, not save Fax, and it is to Fhuu they must return.

Crossing the burning city, they are approached by a member of the WLF asking for a sitdown with Lundbeams. Rimardo slaps his forehead — "Oh yes, the daughter, we forgot about the daughter! Oops, we’ll have to go back!" — but the representative says, "There is no need, Lundbeams learned all she needed to learn." Bayard, in his "all-high-and-mighty-paladin voice," insists they hear her out. Rimardo demands a party — "We defeated the Drow Empire, we freed the city of Fax, we saved the town of Juste-Havre." — and names The Molly Waggler as the place, where Lundbeams may join them for a drink she can pay for. The Molly Waggler, known for its foreign bouncers, is a three-story brick building with a horrible reputation, home that night to a man in bright clothing talking to no one, a tabaxi with shaggy fur selling strange, annoyed livestock, a man who looks eerily familiar, and locals shouting "FREE FAX!"

Later, grim WLF agents walk in and the tavern empties, the barkeep included. Lundbeams sits, throws a coin on the table, pours herself a drink, and begins: "You have saved my daughter. You have saved my husband. And, you may have saved the realms." She says she has spent many years searching for heroes, that the WLF was a tool for finding them, and that she had set the party up "as the weapon that would defeat the invasion, secure the portal, and drive out the Drow." She had hoped closing the portal would dissuade Lolth, but Lolth is determined; her forces have begun their invasion again, and "This time she will try and infiltrate quietly to secure her prize, an artifact!"

Rimardo asks, "Why don’t we just gate in Lolth and kill her?" The cleric of Heironeous regards the cleric of Istus with contempt; the chapter explains that the two faiths never get along — Istus's clerics see Heironeous's as arrogant fools, Heironeous's see Istus's as passive stooges of evil. Lundbeams explains that gods cannot be killed: "If you killed one on this plane, all you would have done is destroy their avatar, and secured an enemy with almost infinite powers." Rimardo answers, "Well, okay, but we are already her enemy," and Lundbeams wonders if Heironeous was mistaken in sending them to her. When Rimardo suggests bringing Istus to fight Lolth and KP wonders about disguises, she shouts, "ASK ABOUT THE ARTIFACT! ASK ABOUT THE ARTIFACT!" KP obliges: "Uh, what about the artifact?" Rimardo whispers to him that they are rich and done.

Lundbeams tells the history. Zuggtomy built her temple, seduced by promised access to the artifact's power, and was defeated at the battle of Emiry Meadows and imprisoned; while she was trapped, her knowledge of the artifact was hidden from her allies, but when "some damn fool adventurers killed her, the secret of the artifact was revealed to Iuz and Lolth." After the temple was destroyed under Tolub's leadership, Heironeous gave Lundbeams her new role: to create a force so that anyone who came looking for the device would be stopped. Iuz, after his humiliation at the hands of St. Cuthbert, massacred the leaders of the Horned Society thinking they knew where it was, then tried to invade, "triggering the wars known as the Greyhawk wars that destroyed the council of 8," and was thwarted. Lundbeams created the precursor to the WLF to find and kill the misguided fools who came seeking it, aided by misinformation about its location. When Greyhawk captured the north of the Wild Coast and the Pomarj Orc Empire conquered the south, she transformed it into the WLF, never intending to free the Wild Coast as its adherents believed: "Their purpose was to be my eyes and ears." Tolub became Captain of Fax because "I needed a base. He needed a job. My daughter required safety. And Tolub was safe if I lived." When the Drow invaded she repurposed the WLF to find heroes, modelled on Tolub's decision to hire independent contractors against the temple of elemental evil. Rimardo and Bayard whisper their unease. Asked which artifact, she says even she does not know: Heironeous has said only that "if Lolth were to secure it, all good people would be destroyed across all planes." The party must go back to the island, enter the portal, defeat the Drow, and ensure Lolth's minions can never threaten this place again.

Gruneel points out that the portal is closed. Bayard chokes — "Defeat the Drow Empire by myself?" — and when Lundbeams shrieks, "You are heroes? I spent a lot of time looking for you!", the Cleric of Istus smirks, "We are not heroes, we are rich!" She switches to greed: the Drow Empire "has been collecting treasure across the multi-verse for millennia." KP asks, "Have they been amassing knowledge?" and Rimardo screams that KP is "likely to find Lolth, have the means to end her existence, and decide to befriend her to find out about the economics of intra-deity trade." KP asks, "Does she know about the 737th plane of Boeing?" "No." Rimardo concludes that her credibility "is not very good." Then he has a brainwave: through the portal they can gain enough power and magic to kill Lundbeams and take the artifact. Corrin agrees, and the two followers of Istus say, "we are in." Bayard reaches the same conclusion and finds destroying the WLF and its criminal leader appealing; KP realises she is lying about not knowing the artifact and is excited by what a God might know.

Rimardo is sure she is messing with them: they closed the portal, the lizardfolk assured them it could not be re-opened, and KP says, "we buried it in lava." Lundbeams replies with contempt, "By lizardfolk," and reports that scrying shows the village of Alaneth is gone. When the party remains unmoved, she threatens to tell the world that Bayard "WAS without reproach and certainly has no butter on his brioche." Rimardo dismisses her — "GO. We need to discuss privately." — and she leaves saying there is no need to decide before the end of the week. Bayard grumbles that the lizardfolk's assurance makes no sense; Rimardo's look of contempt makes him realise he is asking why lizardfolk don't understand an interplanar device, and KP reminds him the lizardfolk did ask them to destroy the portal. They recall that at the battle of Vallaki the Drow had casters of 9th level spells, several white dragons, and summoned demons. Rimardo has one question, "So after we do all of this, we kill Lundbeams?" Bayard: "I would say she is not a good person." The cleric: "I’m in if we kill Lundbeams." Bayard: "There must be some justice." The plan: check the portal, go through if open, defeat the forces on the other side, return, and hold Lundbeams to account.

In the week before departure the party shops in a Fax glutted with magic from dead Drow and unfortunate citizens. Garett, the purveyor of magic, is delighted to see his old buddy KP — he needs cash, since one of his dead ex-girlfriends has come back as a vampire, a detail he wisely keeps from the Paladin. Gruneel picks up a longbow of viciousness until Garett names 11,000 GP; Rimardo's jaw drops, Corrin is not happy, and Gruneel puts it back and finds a longbow of warning instead. Garett insists on telling its story over his wife's protests: the person who had it was surprised, because "There are loopholes to this weapon" and he did not read the instructions carefully, and "The previous assassin who killed the elf who owned it had a lot of patience." His advice: "Just always remember to hold onto the bow." KP brings some scrolls; asked for something interesting, he offers a robe of useful items, and Garett says contemptuously, "Information, KP. Information." Rimardo offers a copy of the treatise on interplanar economics between the Drow Empire and the prime material plane (KP has already memorized it), and Garett trades a sixth level scroll for it: "This will go great next to volume two!"

On the way to the boat for the Wasted Isle the party meets Sir Ryton, leader of the alliance army — "Bayard! The plan worked! We defeated Xhalh!" — who asks for the sword back, since "the sword we gave you is to defeat Xhalh." Bayard tells him, "The Drow have NOT been defeated," fills him in, and Sir Ryton, in awe, insists: "For the honor of Juste-Havre, I insist you keep the blade!" Over the next days the party enjoys "The usual cacophony of Fax." Primus Yagrat tells Bayard, "You have done well by the orcs of Fhuu!" and in a deep, menacing voice warns that if "that buffoon Sir Ryton" comes near Fhuu, the orcs will slaughter "every human, demi-human, and non-orc." Bayard agrees that "That would be an appropriate response to an evil act." KP buys more spells, and Garett offers to introduce him to a gnomish woman looking for a mate; KP's deal-breaker question is, "Does she have a big, huge… LIBRARY?" She does not.

Back at the Molly Waggler, Lundbeams clears the bar again — the group thinks good people do not need to clear a bar before they walk in — and, knowing from her spies about the purchases and the talk with Sir Ryton, points Bayard to Captain Flipper at the port. She offers the finest adamantine armor from the WLF's armorers "For a nominal fee of 3000 GP, of course," delaying their departure once more. At the docks she warns that the Drow may have re-opened the portal; when Bayard says she has resources, she glares: "Do you recall the battle of Vallaki? Where my forces were sacrificed because you decided to use a fireball? My troops are depleted." Bayard boards. As they leave she calls, "May the blessings of Heironeous be with you!" and Rimardo cannot resist adding, "And Istus!" — a grave insult implying heroism is pre-ordained rather than courageous.

After several days at sea Flipper asks, "So, eh, Tolub’s back in charge, eh?" and says Tolub "was always good for business," though "his wife was one scary woman." From the boat the party sees only hints of a former village, ruins beyond the beach, a campfire, a makeshift watchtower with a couple of lizardfolk on lookout, bedrolls, and a large tent. A lizardfolk approaches Bayard: "You are the ones! YOU ARE THE ONES!" They convinced Gussh to attack S’kazs, defeat the Drow, and secure the portal — and now the village is destroyed, friends and family slaughtered, and "the Drow back from their damned portal!" The Drow came out of the portal area with demons and dragons. "Our bad. We’ll fix it," says Bayard, and when the lizardfolk sneers about being left "to die guarding your shoddy workmanship," the Paladin squares his shoulders: "No, this time we will fix it for good." The lizardfolk says, "I never saw you," and pointedly ignores them.

## Locations
### The abandoned tower
Where the party opens Xhalh's chests before heading out into Fax.

### Fax
Liberated and in flames. The chapter describes it as a city that "had open-air slave markets," raided neighbouring towns, and had "the rule of the blade." After the Drow fall, its militia dissolves and it descends into "an orgy of violence and mayhem." In the following days it is glutted with magic items.

### The Molly Waggler
A three-story brick building with a horrible reputation, known for its foreign bouncers. The party meets Lundbeams there twice, and both times her arrival empties the tavern.

### Garett's shop
Where Garett sells magical scrolls and spells. Gruneel finds his longbow of warning here, and KP and Rimardo trade for scrolls.

### Wasted Isle
Reached on Captain Flipper's boat. Only hints of a village remain, with ruins beyond the beach and a small lizardfolk camp. The Drow have come back out of the portal area.

### Alaneth
The lizardfolk village. Lundbeams's scrying shows it is gone, and the lizardfolk confirms it was destroyed.

## NPCs
### Lundbeams
Tolub's wife, cleric of Heironeous, and the leader behind the WLF. She thanks the party, reveals that she built the WLF as her "eyes and ears" against anyone seeking a hidden artifact, and presses the party to return through the portal. She mixes appeals to heroism, wealth and knowledge with threats against Bayard's reputation. She claims not to know which artifact it is; Rimardo doubts her credibility and KP concludes she is lying.

### Garett
"the purveyor of magic" in Fax and KP's old buddy. He insists on telling the longbow of warning's story, values information over items, and offers to set KP up with a gnomish woman.

### Garett's wife
Shouts, "Can you just sell them the weapon?" and is rebuked.

### Sir Ryton
"The leader of the alliance army," an old general. He asks Bayard to return the sword given to defeat Xhalh, then insists Bayard keep it once he hears the Drow are not defeated.

### Primus Yagrat
Thanks Bayard on behalf of the orcs of Fhuu and threatens slaughter if Sir Ryton comes near their lands.

### Flipper
Captain Flipper, who takes the party to the Wasted Isle. He remembers Tolub as good for business and his wife as "one scary woman."

### The lizardfolk
An unnamed lizardfolk at the camp on the Wasted Isle. It blames the party for convincing Gussh to attack S’kazs, reports the Drow's return with demons and dragons, and then pointedly ignores them.

### The WLF representative
Asks for the sitdown and tells Rimardo there is no need to return for the daughter.

### Tolub
Does not appear. The party learns he announced Xhalh's death, which set off the slaughter of the Drow commanders.
