# Chapter 10 – The Battle of Vallaki

Date: 15 Flocktime 595 to 16 Flocktime 595 CY (595-05-15 to 595-05-16; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
After being waylaid on the way to Vallaki, the party takes a short rest. KP asks repeatedly whether there is "knowledge to preserve in Vallaki?" and the rest of the party ignores him. At Vallaki they find a small Drow army besieging the town, with each exit blocked by 50 or so Drow and their allies. The drow cannot guard every part of the wall, but they can keep the town starved, and the party realises that a small group of heroes should be able to scale the walls, especially at night.

KP, however, wants to bluff his way through the Drow stockade with a story about local farmers attacking and the death of a picket a few miles away. The others stare at him in confusion; KP gets cold feet, but the party encourages him. Rimardo: "I want to see how this turns out." And it works: "the party member with the worst charisma walks up to the Drow and successfully bluffs them." KP claims, "We are the supporting forces from the army." The Drow tells them to set up their tents "so we can begin the slaughter tomorrow." Rimardo cannot keep quiet and asks, "Why wait?" The Drow explains, "Nimh wants the army of Fax to do the assault. We are here to make sure the enemy is wounded." Asked where Nimh is, the Drow says, "With the army of Fax," and asks why Rimardo keeps asking questions. Rimardo: "Because I want to get started!" KP: "I am a student of knowledge." The drow declares that "the day after tomorrow, the city of Vallaki will be in ruins, and the plane will be ours." Rimardo: "Huzzah!" Drow: "Why are you happy that you will be our slaves?" Rimardo: "I will be your slaves? I am fighting with you." Drow: "Oh! That’s what Nimh told you! Oh!" The party realises the Drow will not let them through.

While they wait for darkness, KP suggests a spell to dig a hole under the walls. Rimardo does the math and sees that it would take a lot of time and pile up a large amount of dirt. The party thanks KP for his initiative and repeats "the team mantra that any idea from anyone is welcome," but Rimardo concludes, "Thank you KP, we appreciate your efforts, but we’re climbing the wall." They climb the wall and arrive in Vallaki in the middle of the night.

Near every entrance are armed encampments manned not by battle-hardened veterans but by "villagers pressed into combat duty," who are starving. KP notices there are no chickens anywhere, a sign that people have eaten them to stay alive, and that the defenders are haggard and tired. Rimardo warns the guards that the army of Fax is coming and claims, "We are from the wild coast liberation front." Matfey: "Those maniacs, they keep telling us that they will bring freedom." Afanasy: "Did they bring aid? Is there any aid?" Bayard, bombastic and self-important: "We are it!" Lev wonders if they are Drow spies; Rimardo: "Well, we are not elves." Bayard, offended: "We are NOT spies." Sergej sends them to Humph, the Champion, in the Lord Mayor's office. Rimardo asks who the mayor is, and the confused guard says, "Humph, the champion!" Rimardo: "Oh, he is the mayor, I thought he was the champion."

On the way, they see tired, hungry, haggard town dwellers, and have no food to give. At the office KP asks Bayard to leverage his "without reproach and without fear." Bayard is silent and just stares at the guards. When asked who he is, he says, "We are helping. We have come to relieve you of your miserable situation." The party wonders if this is his idea of leadership and charisma; Bayard thinks his presence alone should tell people "that their hero and salvation has arrived." Rimardo groans. Bayard: "I am not a talker but a doer." Surprisingly, it works.

Humph is a 5’8”, muscular man with greying hair, about 45 years old, studying a map. Rimardo, no longer trusting Bayard to speak, says in a bored monotone, "We are from the WLF, and we have come to warn you of an army from the city of Fax that will attack you tomorrow." Humph asks where their army is; Rimardo: "This is it." Bayard: "We are ELITE!" Rimardo: "You don’t go to war with the army you wish to, but with the army you have." Humph sighs. The Drow have besieged the town for weeks to soften it up "so that Tolub can send his army into here and claim a comfortable victory and grow his army and make an empire. He’s just a stooge of the Drow." It is too late to improve the defences, but Humph will use the party "as a swing team" against forces that break through the walls. "The objective here isn’t to win but to make the victory so bloody that Nimh is forced to pull back," since Nimh's army was promised "a comfortable victory with easy plunder." Humph asks, "Does this make any sense?" KP: "No." Rimardo: "Not really". Bayard: "Nope." Killing someone important will not help: "They will attack even if Nimh’s dead." KP: "I can use fireball." Humph stares at him and at the Paladin.

The party starts to see that the Army of Fax will abandon its attack only if sufficiently bloodied, and that KP's "Fireball!" may have merit. Rimardo and Corrin think the plan is sound and ask Bayard whether he is okay with killing "all these victims of the Drow plans." Bayard: "They chose the wrong army to support. It is war." Rimardo presses his luck: "Will you be okay if we loot the bodies?" Bayard cringes: "I will look the other way." KP: "I am delighted we have a moral compass."

Rimardo wants to sleep, but KP wants to go to the walls and throw fireballs: "Why not? It’s going to be fun." Bayard worries that provoking the Drow, who "seem remarkably uninterested in attacking," might change their minds, "because you dropped a fireball on top of their heads." The party reminds KP about the Drow Initiates, who can cast magic missile; KP says he will fire, then hide. Rimardo: "Seriously? Your ability to persuade and stealth are your core strengths?" Mr. Corrin cannot forget that KP objected to sneaking when it was Mr. Corrin's plan. KP looks at Rimardo with contempt and balances on one foot. From the guards KP learns why no one fires arrows from the battlements: the last time they did, "the Drow levitated and used a magic missile to kill anyone they could see." KP muses, "well, those were arrows…" But the party wants to sleep, and asks KP to give up his plan. KP casts Leomund's Magical hut, and the party sleeps inside it.

In the morning the party goes to the western gate, where Humph stands, and sees Nimh approaching. KP: "Now fireball time? Now?" Humph groans. Nimh accuses Humph of treason against "The Empire of Fax" and demands that he surrender to "face justice at the court of Xhalh in St. Aurelius," while "The drow present will take control of Vallaki." In a possibly sarcastic tone, or maybe merely elven detachment, he finishes, "And we can all start learning to live harmoniously together as free people of the Faxian Empire led by the great grandmaster Tolub." KP suggests a lightning bolt; Humph says, "No, let me negotiate." KP: "okay." Humph: "Nimh, I am willing to surrender." Nimh is very irritated at this surprising turn. Humph asks that everyone be treated fairly. Nimh: "The only way to fairly treat you Humph, a traitor, is to torture you and the ruling body of Vallaki and everyone you love and then kill you all." Humph: "But we didn’t." Nimh, condescending: "You did, it is not essential that you knew."

Rimardo looks at KP: "Now. It was a boring conversation anyway." Seeing an initiate beside Nimh who could counterspell, KP hides in the battlements, and Nimh is unaware. KP casts fireball on Nimh and his retinue, who did not expect "death-dealing magic from the town’s defenders." The acolyte is killed, one of the drow guards dies, the initiate is wounded, and Nimh survives. Nimh says, "So the creature that killed Walton is here. I will enjoy plucking its head off," and screams, "Attack!!!!"

The battle is joined. Humph asks the party to go down and protect against intruders who make it over the walls, and runs to the center of the city to make a last stand in the mayor's office. Soon "a group of human troops with a Drow liaison" attack: a local member of the Faxian ruling class, "several troops (8)," and a strong veteran soldier. Rimardo is outraged that these are trained fighters rather than farmers; the narration observes that had he investigated the army instead of pursuing revenge against Walton, he might have known. "Istus holds his strand between the scissors. Bayard was right, nothing good comes from vengeance."

The guards surround Bayard: "I will just finish off everybody. No worries." KP's snowball swarm kills the Nobleman and the guards Mather and Parr and leaves the Drow at 1 HP. The Drow steps back 5’ to use its crossbow and Rimardo's attack of opportunity misses; the Drow misses too. Corrin kills the Drow, then kills Tanner with his rapier and assassin ability. Herbert and Barnett attack Bayard, one hitting for 4 HP: "But a scratch," says Bayard. Fuller hits KP for 4 HP, Wright hits Corrin "in a soft spot" for 8 HP, and the veteran misses Rimardo. Bayard: "My turn." He kills Herbert and does 6 HP to Barnett. Rimardo's "toll the dead" on Barnett fails; KP's fire bolt at Fuller fizzes. Corrin hits Wright for 17 HP. Bayard flanks and hits Fuller for 12 HP, then, "with the sword still drenched with wine-dark blood," misses Barnett. KP's fire bolt kills Kent with 15 Hp. Corrin kills Barnett, then crits the Veteran for 33 HP. The Veteran drops his crossbow and hits Corrin with longsword and shortsword for 20 HP. Bayard is about to smite when KP suggests this is not the time; Bayard does 18 HP total, Rimardo's toll the dead is ineffective again, and KP's fire bolt kills the Veteran with 8 HP. Over the course of this fight the chapter also says the party faces "a group of soldiers who made it into the city right by the gates," and defeats them. Then a runner arrives: "A group of soldiers is attacking the mayor’s office, please come!"

The party heals and moves stealthily to the town center, where soldiers surround a building and another member of Fax's ruling class, with a Drow, smiles with glee as they prepare to burn it. KP is delighted to see "everyone in a nice 20’ radius" and his Fireball does 27 HP of damage: the guards, the Nobleman and the Drow all die. Only the Veteran survives. Bayard charges; the Veteran grabs a villager, holds a knife to his throat, and says, "Put your weapons down, or I will kill the hostage." The villager screams at them to listen to his captor. Rimardo's toll the dead finally works for 7 HP, and Corrin raises an eyebrow, "surprised perhaps that Istus was kind to Rimardo?" Corrin's poisoned dagger lands in the dirt. Bayard: "Hold on, we will save you." KP misses with a fire bolt. Bayard uses command on the Veteran: "DROP!" The Veteran drops the villager, who flees. Rimardo does 4 HP, and Corrin closes in from a flanking position and kills the Veteran.
