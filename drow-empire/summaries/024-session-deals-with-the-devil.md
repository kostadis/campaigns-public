# Chapter 24 – Deals with the Devil

Date: 15 Goodmonth 595 to 17 Goodmonth 595 CY (595-08-15 to 595-08-17; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
Gruneel, on further reflection, decides that solving the contract is too perilous, and stands at the door, away from any negotiations with the Bearded Devils. KP, "whose knowledge of contracts is honed from significant research on the topic," takes the lead.

The chapter pauses to explain KP's background. He has spent significant time in the gnomish libraries of the Kron Hills, famous for their collections and ease of access: a patron asks a question of a magic mirror, which records it and sends it to a gnome librarian who finds the right book or passage. The recorded queries help find books and guide purchases, but they leak privacy. A wizard looking up the location of an evil artifact "like the sword of Kas" would have that interest known to the librarians, and outsiders could force the gnomes to reveal it. Some outsiders see this privacy as a peril to the kingdoms of good; the librarians answer that a good wizard looking up how to reverse a curse could likewise become a target. Library management also found the queries useful for targeted solicitations for donations, which outraged patrons who did not want letters from gnomes showing up at their secret lairs — "There was the whole incident involving Patroncholis, the Lich, and the creeping plague." KP has worked with the library for many years on an opt-in system that lets the librarians optimize search, acquisitions and solicitations while preserving patron privacy. Many folks think this work underpins his almost obsessive need for more information; KP has chosen not to say whether this is true.

Meanwhile, Bayard stands ready to kill these evil extra-planar creatures, but KP has other plans. The bearded devil, in a somewhat blasé tone, declares, "Per our contract, and the terms of the contract we are required to either kill you or remove you from the area in front of the threshold." KP asks for a copy of the contract, is told it is under NDA, and asks for the NDA instead. The devil, not really interested in fighting, will give it "for something of equal value" — but "as gold from this plane is of marginal value to us on ours, we require either magic or innocent souls." Rimardo and Corrin warn KP that any treasure or magic comes out of his share. KP offers a +2 magical item; the devil wants a potion as well, KP refuses, and after conferring with his team the devil says, "you and the paladin do not represent enough of a tactical threat for us to accept such a deal." Bayard is offended — "Not enough of a tactical threat? I will show you a TACTICAL THREAT!" — but KP suggests he persuade them instead. Bayard rolls a natural 20, and the devils hand over the contract and take the arrow.

The contract was signed by the seneschal of House Zollazo, "of the pre-eminent house," on behalf of his mistress. The Bearded Devils agreed to guard the portal and kill any entities crossing the threshold on behalf of the portal's owner, without saying which side. Since the Drow are all dead and the party holds the area, KP declares, "I am so pleased with your excellent efforts in protecting my portal." The devil concedes, "hmm, we will need to write better contracts," but still will not let KP pass: KP has only read the NDA, and the actual contract says the devils must prevent anyone crossing the lava who is not a member of the Drow elite or their named allies. The devils will hand the real contract only to the party's leader, so KP has Bayard fetch it — "by all means, you are the brains." KP's first loophole fails: he cuts a Drow head off in the other room, puts it on a pole, and says, "this Drow leader said we can pass!" (he rolled a 12). "No."

Corrin has a brainwave: "wait, all we have to do is stop the lava!" KP uses a cone of cold to freeze the lava into stone — "I don’t see any more lava here, so I think I’ll just walk across." — which meets the letter of the contract. On the other side, the devils note that their contract "was silent on whether we had to do anything if an intruder crossed to the other side and did not cross the lava," and will not attack. Bayard walks purposefully into the portal and takes 2 HP of damage from the solid stone wall. The devils have no idea how the portal operates, only that when it does, the Drow come and go through the wall. Bayard tries his suave charm — "yes, but remind me!" — and is refused: "If you do not recall how your own portal works, it is not our job to remind you."

KP proposes an information contract. He asks for 24 hours of truthful information; the devil counters, "24 hours? No. 1 question, 1 truthful answer, 1 magic item." KP persuades them to trade truthful answers for magical items of significant value. The devil refuses Rimardo's scroll of Hunger of Hadar on its own and rants about bags of holding — "every party has a bag of holding" — but finally agrees to the bag and two scrolls; Rimardo's attempt to offer sanctuary does not work. The final trade is a bag of holding, Hunger of Hadar, and protection from aberrations.

KP asks about the Drow's trade patterns and economic systems, and the bearded devil talks non-stop for 30 minutes while KP writes frantically. The chapter explains why: Asmodeus is known across the multi-verse as the Master of Commerce and the art of the deal. Early bargains with the Devil were easily broken, but he played the long game, building "a deep legal department" until every deal favoured the Devil; legends of outwitted devils persist because up-and-coming fiends with their own legal departments lacked the archfiend's polish. "Regardless, every devil was a deep expert in markets." From the notes the group learns the portal opens every 2-8 days. Prodded to ask more, KP learns that the devils do not know how the portal works — "The Drow closely guard the secret of the portal. And it is not controlled from this site." KP recalls from earlier encounters that it is controlled from Freedahl's city, Plagueridge Rampart. The portal was last open five days ago, so Rimardo reckons it should open in the next 2 or 3 days. The seneschal hired devils because the relationship with the demons was fraught with peril — the Drow and the Demons have an uneasy peace — and "they preferred to work with entities that would follow contracts very precisely." Asked how the Drow got in touch, the devil explains as if to a child: "rituals were cast, planes were traveled, slaves were exchanged, innocent souls were sacrificed."

Rimardo is relieved that the abyss runs like the rest of the multi-verse, and notes, "Innocent souls are cheaper than magic items!" The devil agrees, explains it didn't offer that route because Paladins tend not to want such deals, and sees an opening: "However, Mr. Rimardo if you were to offer the Paladin’s soul in exchange…" Rimardo says, "I’ll think about it." "it would solve many of your problems." Corrin sharpens the knives and confers with Rimardo; they decide that revenge on Lundbeams is the priority, and that gaining the power to defeat her means working with Bayard. Bayard is unamused.

The party plans to make a hole with pass-wall and cover it with an illusion when the portal opens. It opens with great fanfare: lava spikes into the air, the ground shakes, stones grate, sulfurous odors fill the chamber — "ah, the sweet smell of home," say the devils — and the eastern wall of the lava chamber warps as if about to melt before stabilizing. The party hides. A collection of Drow and Orcs enters, looks around, and leaves. Bayard charges the eastern stone wall and takes 3 HP; the holes the Drow and Orcs came through are gone. A wave of necrotic damage comes from the wall, but KP warns the party in time and they take only 11 HP.

Then, 12 seconds later, two white dragons with red demonic eyes burst from the dirt. Corrin opens with a critical hit for 67 HP; Rimardo's toll-the-dead does 23. KP's fire bolt heals the dragon, and he is outraged; Rimardo has to scream, "they are not resistant, they are absorbing!" KP pouts, "well, that’s not a very useful skill." The second dragon grabs KP and burrows into the dirt. Bayard runs down the tunnel after him — "my hero!" — and does 79 HP of damage with his smiting attacks, while Rimardo wonders if he should go get Gruneel. The other dragon moves into the lava, taking attacks of opportunity, and hits Corrin for 26 HP from the lava pit; Corrin disengages and hits the dragon holding KP for 36. That dragon counterspells Rimardo's cantrip, holds KP in its grapple, breathes on Rimardo, Corrin and Bayard for 22 HP, and glides into the dirt. KP rolls a 1, Corrin hits for 7, Bayard rolls a 2, and the dragon, down to 15 HP, escapes. Bayard cannot reach the first dragon; asked if he has a bow, he says, "A bow is a coward’s weapon." The dragon leaves the lava pit and bites and claws Rimardo for 28 HP. Corrin hits it for 37, and Rimardo casts harm for 29, then turns to ask KP for a magic missile. KP is asleep. The stress of negotiating with Devils, being dragged into the dirt by a dragon, and fire bolts that did not work got to him, "So, he went to sleep to recharge his emotional energy."
