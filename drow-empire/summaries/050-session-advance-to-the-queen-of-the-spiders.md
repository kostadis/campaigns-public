# Chapter 50 – Advance to the Queen of the Spiders

Date: Lolth's demi-plane, Day 1 (??? on Oerth; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
Before heading out to face Lolth, the heroes cast a hero’s feast, because they remember KP's warnings about her. In a quick flashback: before leaving the gnomish library in the Kron Hills, KP had pursued an extensive study of demon lords, which the other librarians took for another of the eccentric gnome's bizarre obsessions; as a structuralist in economic theory, KP felt the inter-planar financial system could not be understood without in-depth knowledge of the demon lords and their powers. His information on Lolth was not as precise as he, or Bayard, would have liked. He recalls her armor class and abilities, and Rimardo realizes that with the demoness’ strength and constitution some spells are off-limits. Then KP's memory fails him (those were the days he was "out amongst the stars looking to the future"), and he tells Bayard "that she can perceive nothing." Corrin sighs. Fortunately he remembers her spells: she has legendary immunity and "is immune to spells under the 6**th** level," and her deadly disintegration attack and all her attacks inflict poisonous damage. When KP presented this just before they accepted their final contract, Bayard was convinced it would cement his glory, and KP that it would be his end.

In the present, the heroes face Lolth’s priestesses guarding the entrance to the Ziggurat. Corrin strikes first for 48 HP of damage on the second cleric, who summons a demon and moves away; KP's fire bolt kills her. Bayard dashes across a 60’ distance. Rimardo's attempt to silence the clerics is countered, and the fourth cleric's insect plague deals 17 damage to Rimardo, 8 to Corrin and 17 to Bayard, until Corrin's attack with foresight ("a measly 41 HP of damage") breaks her concentration. The third cleric conjures two giant spiders; the summoned Yochlol demon tries to dominate Corrin, whose iron will blocks it. KP climbs onto a roof and casts a fireball for 38 HP of damage, killing the giant spiders and deeply wounding several clerics. Bayard "kills" the third cleric, but she has death ward, and Rimardo kills her. The fourth cleric casts web; Corrin kills the first cleric; KP and Bayard bring the last to 1 HP, and she resists Rimardo's cantrip. Attacking Bayard, she reflects on her life choices: betraying Lolth is a lousy plan, and fighting to the death is a dangerous one. The Yochlol misses the paladin twice, Corrin finishes off the conflicted cleric, and the Yochlol blinks out of existence declaring, "I never wanted to fight for Lolth anyways." Rimardo uses animate dead to turn three of the dead clerics into Zombies, and the griffon is summoned.

As the heroes are about to enter the Ziggurat, Istus invites Rimardo into a conference. To the others he seems lost in a trace; to Rimardo, he is in a small room with an old woman spinning wool. He launches straight in ("What I want is."), and Istus cuts him off: "Always with what you want, what you need. Don’t you want to chat?" Blushing, Rimardo asks, "How have you been?" "All good. Everything hangs in the balance." She cannot intervene in the battle, because "I would upset the balance." "Can you intervene now?" "Perhaps." "Could you cast an anti-magic shield on Corrin?" "Yes." Her decision to give Corrin an anti-magic field is, the chapter notes, still argued over among the followers of the Lady of the Fate: was it an intervention, and what did Lolth do to Istus and the Fates? As Rimardo prepares to leave, Istus stops him: "You asked two and a boon. You have one more!" He asks, "what advice would you give us for defeating Lolth?" "Kill her shield maidens." KP, disappointed but not surprised, learns that he cannot ask questions himself, and that Rimardo did not ask about the Pareto coefficient.

Rimardo casts death ward on everyone but Bayard. When Bayard is about to open the temple doors, Rimardo stops him: "No, that’s why we have the zombies." A zombie enters and can see nothing; the party follows into a dark hallway heading east and west, its floors and ceiling covered in non-magical webs that slow them. Corrin ignites the web, but it is slow going. Through his gem of true seeing, KP sees centuries of webs, a giant monstrous spider and a Drow who must be a shield maiden of Lolth: "Hey guys, we are surrounded." KP's sunbeam blinds the first spider and Drow. Bayard goes east, and Corrin sighs, "If you only had a bow." Bayard glares.

Rimardo's toll the dying grievously wounds one of the Drow maidens. A spider hits Corrin for what would have been a staggering amount of damage, but he and his friends are immune to poison, and though he loses concentration the anti-magic spell holds, because "Istus’ boon depends on Istus’ will." Istus smiles. Corrin then blunders: he forgets that the anti-magic shield neutralizes KP's foresight, loses his expected advantage, and hits a spider for a marginal amount. Bayard nearly kills a Drow, who survives on death ward and answers with an almost ineffective hellish rebuke. Rimardo is confused that the Drow have countered none of their spells: "Are they waiting for us to cast some powerful spell?" The zombies move into flanking positions; the griffon survives a hellish rebuke to strike again, a death ward again saving the Drow maiden. Corrin runs under the giant spider's legs and kills it, leaving everyone staring, then brings another to 1 HP; Bayard brings another to 1 HP; one zombie scores a critical hit; Rimardo finishes off the last spider. The shield maidens flee.

Bayard sends the Zombies west and the party follows as the hallway turns north, still covered in the webs of the pets of Lolth. KP's gem of true seeing reveals a doorway some 100 feet to the north, and the group arrives at the door to Lolth’s chamber. "The time has come to face their enemy and fulfill the terms of their last contract."

A demon summoned by a cleric. It fails to dominate Corrin, curses the heroes in demonic, misses repeatedly, and blinks out declaring, "I never wanted to fight for Lolth anyways."

Rimardo's goddess, appearing as an old woman spinning wool. She wants to chat, says she cannot intervene without upsetting the balance, then grants an anti-magic shield on Corrin and the advice, "Kill her shield maidens."

Drow guardians of Lolth in the Ziggurat, protected by death ward and casting hellish rebuke. They never counter the party's spells, and flee.

Summoned before the party enters the Ziggurat; fights the shield maidens and survives a hellish rebuke.

Three dead clerics animated by Rimardo; sent in first, used for flanking, one landing a critical hit.
