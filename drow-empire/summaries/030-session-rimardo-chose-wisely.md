## Summary
"Rimardo chose wisely." He heals the Paladin, who is then able to fight and avoid dying. Selzyt proves surprisingly useful, using his magic jar ability to control one of the Drow knights and "giving the party an unfair advantage." Although the battle is close, victory is never in doubt.

Once the Drow soldiers who entered from the door are defeated, Selzyt frantically asks whether anyone has a dispel magic spell. KP volunteers, rushes downstairs, and casts it, succeeding despite "the powerful spell that had been cast to create the portal to the pocket dimension." With that, the Drow are defeated, and "The citadel belonged to our great adventurers."

Rimardo and Corrin insist on looking for treasure, Bayard agrees, and much treasure is found. As the adventurers count it, the spy Valnozz, who had helped them at Vallaki, enters the room and says, "now that you have made it this far, it is important that I tell you the true nature of the Drow Empire."
