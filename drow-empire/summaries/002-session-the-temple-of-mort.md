# Chapter 2 – The Temple of Mort

Date: 3 Flocktime 595 CY (595-05-03; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
The chapter picks up with the adventurers on their way to the Temple of Mort, a temple down a long road heading west that starts at the southern edge of the town. A hundred yards from the temple they are trapped, but Boradoon notices the log, allowing the group to escape it.

The cultists who set the trap are harder to escape. Armed with scimitars and apparently resistant to piercing attacks, they launch a furious assault. Luck turns for the heroes with "A timely casting of thunder wave" and "some bad luck on the part of the evil cultists." Wounded, the group rests a little before continuing to the temple.

The temple is a ruin: "All that remains are the roofless shell and some adjoining structures - which are still intact." Piles of bones stacked around the ruins testify to the sinister nature of the place, and it is easy to see why it has an evil reputation among the residents. In the courtyard, a powerful skeleton emerges from the piles of bones and is quickly dispatched. Boradoon once again detects a trap, and a powerful glyph of warding is averted.

This must be where the necromancer is hidden, and he is: Ralnor, a Drow Necromancer, is hiding in the temple's inner sanctum. At first he hides in the dark, but the spell casters dispel his magic. Reepicheep then uses Tasha’s Laughter, and while caught between laughs Ralnor gives up some useful tidbits: that he works for Realm leader Xhalh, that the Drow Empire is just after slaves, that his role is to capture slaves for the empire, and that the Drow would crush them all.

Dimitrov is surprised, because Realm Leader is not a title that the Drow of Oerth use. Boradoon is confused, because if there were such a thing as a Drow Empire in Oerth, the powers of Greyhawk would have done much to crush it. "The story of Ralnor made no sense."

Before they can ask him more, Ralnor recovers from the laughs and combat resumes. Both Boradoon and Dimitrov are surprised to have their spells countered by Ralnor. He looks likely to be sent quickly "to whatever Drow hell, the Drow go to," yet he survives: he casts Faerie fire, drinks potions of healing, and tries to ram through Dimitrov, who guards the door. It is for naught. The Drow's last desperate attempt to escape fails, and the Triton puts him on a fork.

The group finds the treasure hoard Ralnor collected from his victims, casually discarded — the Drow Empire appears to have no use for gold. In his personal effects they find a map showing a trail that leads out of Farjvad to someplace in the forested hills. The adventurers must heal up and continue deeper into the plot, left with three questions: "What is the Drow Empire? Who is Xhalh? What do these Drow want?"
