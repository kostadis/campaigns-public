# Chapter 19 – Assault on the Drow Lair

Date: 3 Reaping 595 CY (595-07-03; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
The party stares at the tower: a two-story building whose windowless first floor has a large door, and whose second-floor windows have their shades drawn against the sunlight, which the party recognises as a sign the Drow live there. Behind them, the hordes of lizardfolk who saw the fireball begin to advance on their position. Corrin asks Bayard, "so, boss, do we charge in, or do I sneak in and do my assassin thing?" Bayard thinks, then nods to Corrin to move in and attack.

A glyph just outside the door goes unnoticed. The door explodes open and the thunder wave hits Corrin, who fails his saving throw, takes 22 HP of damage, and glares. The New Guy, given his club, is told to guard the entrance and will not go into battle. Corrin gets up and sneaks into the room past the blasted doors, where he sees S'kazs; fortunately the Lizard King does not notice him. S'kazs says in bad Elvish, "something came in, but I can't see it," and Bayard, who speaks Elvish, translates. From deeper in the room comes a reply in archaic Elvish: "we paid you to protect us, do your job." Mr. Rimardo and Mr. Corrin wonder "why is it that the Drow seem to acquire more and more powerful allies with more and more treasure, and we cannot seem to find any of it?"

Corrin hits S'kazs for 59 HP on a critical attack, and S'kazs misses with both of his attacks. Rimardo's flame strike does 24 HP. S'kazs casts hellish rebuke on Rimardo (the chapter records 10 HP of damage) and then blinds him with his spittle. KP's lightning bolt hits for 41 HP. S'kazs knocks Corrin out with a head butt; Bayard enters and smites for 45 HP; S'kazs hits the incapacitated Corrin for 31 HP. Rimardo, though blind, finds Corrin and heals him for 21 HP, then takes 19 HP from another hellish rebuke. KP's second lightning bolt, sculpted to avoid Bayard, does 32 HP. S'kazs fails to head-butt Corrin again, and Mr. Corrin manoeuvres into a flanking position, does 30 HP, and disengages, while the rest of the party stares, confused: "the Lizard King is dead, and Mr. Corrin did not notice."

The new guy shouts, "a large number of lizardfolk coming." Bayard remembers Gussh's word that killing S'kazs would make the other lizardfolk stand down, so he dumps the body onto the steps leading into the tower; in Bayard's own telling, he gently placed S'kazs on the ground so the lizardfolk could perform whatever burial rites they felt appropriate. "Istus smiled on this adventure because Rimardo and Mr. Corrin found some treasure."

While the party debates its next moves, the Drow come from below, wrapped in a dark spell. Corrandus of House Illal surprises the party with a bead of force: Corrin dodges, but Rimardo and KP are trapped, taking 6 HP each. Corrandus misses Mr. Corrin twice; KP dispels the force field; Corrin hits Corrandus twice "for a staggering total of 9 HP." The chronicle notes that Istus, seeing how smug Rimardo and Corrin were against the Lizard King, "has decided to show them the dangers of hubris." The second Drow, Ruanden of House Illal, throws another bead of force, trapping KP for 16 HP while Rimardo escapes at the last minute. Bayard charges, pauses, and dispels the darkness Ruanden cast, but Ruanden is still inside Corrandus' darkness. The new guy asks if he can help; the party answers, "no, no, it is too perilous."

Rimardo imagines a hold person that would leave Corrandus unaffected and catch Ruanden, but because he cannot see either Drow, "this remains a fantasy"; his cantrip does 12 HP to Corrandus instead. Corrandus hits Mr. Corrin for 17 HP. KP pushes his bead of force against Corrandus, pinning him to the wall, and Mr. Corrin takes an attack of opportunity for 7 HP before KP dispels the field. Ruanden, suddenly out of the darkness, takes a 37 HP sneak attack from Mr. Corrin, hits back for 17 HP, and is then smitten by Bayard for 32 HP, leaving him on death's door. Bayard misses, Rimardo's cantrip does nothing, and "Ruanden smiles." Corrandus hits KP for 34 HP. "Mr. Corrin smiles back at Ruanden and kills him."

Corrin dashes into the darkness to attack the remaining Drow, and Bayard misses Corrandus twice. Mr. Corrin reflects that he, "a small helpless halfling," has been attacked repeatedly while Bayard, in plate mail with a large sword, is uninjured; could it be that he sneaks into battle looking tiny and helpless in light armor? He and Mr. Rimardo conclude, no, "it is some judgment from Istus," and the lack of treasure reaffirms the theory. Rimardo heals him. KP takes 17 HP and drops to 0 HP, "but death has not taken him over." Corrin does only 24 HP, Bayard misses twice, Rimardo's cantrip fails, and Corrandus puts Mr. Corrin on death's door with 34 HP of damage. Before anything worse can happen, Bayard kills Corrandus, and Rimardo heals the wounded with channel divinity.

The party finds "a suit of magical armor that looks like leaves," then descends the stairs to where they assume the portal is. Mr. Corrin sneaks ahead, falls into a pit trap he did not see for 22 HP, and is hauled out with the party's 50' of rope.
