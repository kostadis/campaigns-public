# Chapter 35 – Corrin Learns to Appreciate the Beauty of Dwarven Opera

Date: Brungril Forge, Day 1 (??? on Oerth; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
After the Dwarven knight warns the party of the danger to the North, Corrin, who "was just turned into a pincushion," wonders whether they can lick their wounds before they attack. They cannot: the Drow in charge, Antyln, is not going to wait. He enters the room wielding a short sword and a hand crossbow. Fonouc enters too and calls on the knights: "loyal knights of Brungril Forge, it is time you did your duty and served your rightful Thane. Defeat these outsiders! Earn your honor back!" The Dwarven knights stand.

Antyln has been preparing for a while and will not be surprised when his allies turn on him. He casts darkness and fires his hand crossbow at KP, but the arrow flies past the gnome. Corrin drinks a healing potion and cures 26 points of damage. The Knights of Brungril Forge prove loyal first to their Thane, and one attacks Antyln, but the Drow strikes first with his short sword, dodges the dwarf's first attack and parries the second. Rimardo dispels the darkness and casts "a mass healing word spell," healing "his erstwhile enemies and current allies." Bayard is pleased: "well done, Rimardo!" The moment passes when an unseen servant throws a bead of force and Rimardo is trapped. The other Dwarven knight's heavy crossbow hits the wall.

KP wonders, "is this Drow immune to fire? Can he counterspell any spell?" and decides that a 5th level lightning bolt will split the difference. Antyln fails to counter it and fails to dodge it, and is electrocuted for 40 points of damage; one of his unseen servants is destroyed. An unseen servant pours a potion of supreme healing down his master's throat, healing him for 28 points. Antyln hits Bayard for 17 before the Paladin can raise his sword; Bayard slashes back for 19. An unseen servant heals Antyln of another 28, Antyln creates a new unseen servant, and his short sword pierces Bayard's armor for 17. The dwarves who had served under Damrith and were caught next to Antyln try to flee, and Antyln kills them all.

A Dwarven knight kills two of the unseen servants despite their invisibility, and Corrin charges in and kills another. The last remaining unseen servant throws a bead of force that traps the Dwarven knight near Corrin; Corrin, as expected, dodges. Rimardo dispels the bead of force and stays put. The untrapped Dwarven knight marches into the darkness surrounding Antyln and kills the last unseen servant. KP dispels the darkness; Antyln fails to counter it and screams, "damn you!"

Bayard smiles, but Antyln parries his first attack; the second hits for 18. Antyln then "unleashes his full rage on Bayard, dropping him." Bayard manages to whisper just before he goes unconscious, "be seeing you." Antyln creates another unseen servant. Corrin closes and is hit for 17, but his uncanny dodge cuts it to eight. His rapier punctures Antyln for 43 points of damage, and the unseen servant heals the Drow for 28. The dwarf trapped in the bead of force pushes on its wall and forces Antyln to move, exposing him to an attack of opportunity from Corrin for seven.

Rimardo casts harm; Antyln fails to counter it and fails to resist it, and is brought to one hit point. Rimardo, being a healer, does not try to kill Antyln but casts a mass healing word that heals the party for 20 points each. The free dwarf tries to kill Antyln, but the Drow has cast the death ward spell on himself. KP, behind Antyln, casts a fourth level magic missile and kills him. Afterward the spell casters compare their remaining slots — KP has three first, two seconds and one fourth; Rimardo has three first. KP believes he is more efficient; Rimardo believes he "had to heal a lot of meat sacks" and should not be judged the way a magic-user is judged.

With the defeat of Damrith, Fonouc declares the party entitled to a standard discount as heroes of Brungril Forge. Bayard refuses to buy a bow, and at first refuses the +1 arrows of Cthulup, a Dwarven merchant — until Fonouc points out that they once belonged to the great archer Ethenelp, a Dwarven hero of high repute, and Bayard realizes that refusing would be a grave insult. Fonouc then offers treasure from the Dwarven treasury: for Corrin, a small terra-cotta cat packed with 2666 GP worth of gems; for Bayard, a gold music box lined with a silk border, 2500 gold pieces, that plays Dwarven Opera. Bayard is disappointed but has the grace to smile politely. Corrin, under the influence of a girdle of dwarvenkind and a bit of a singer himself, is delighted, and soon starts to hum the tunes and learn them — though were he ever to lose the girdle, he would notice that this Dwarven Opera "is indistinguishable from chalk on a blackboard." KP is given "five gems of high value and a gem of true seeing," and Rimardo is handed two +2 great axes and a horn of blasting.

Fonouc declares all debts settled. Marco thanks the party: they saved the dwarves from the Drow, rescued the myconids and their hero Pelynumph, defeated the duergar slavers, "And finally, you have let me hear the beautiful, wonderful, and sonorous musical voice of Corrin." He offers an honor guard to the portal room, since Brungril Forge "will not be safe until the Drow Empire is defeated." Fonouc says that had they not been victims of a Drow incursion he would offer Dwarven armies: "But I cannot. And I am ashamed." Rimardo rolls his eyes and thinks to himself that this was unexpected, not! Fonouc does offer food and supplies, good news since feeding the slaves was a source of anxiety.

Rimardo asks Selzyt whether to keep the slaves with them. Selzyt is excited: "finally, someone is asking the failsafe. Every portal room is protected by some monster whose only purpose in life is to slaughter any incursion." He would keep them as a meat shield, "But you are, well, goody two shoes. Net-net, if you care for their health, don’t take them." Fonouc at first resists letting the freed slaves stay, but Marco argues that the dwarves are free because of these heroes and decency requires that the request be honored. Fonouc agrees to let them stay near the portal room until the portal re-materializes; then they must leave, for he intends to bring the entire area down and seal the portal. Ominously, he declares that if the portal is full of Drow the next time it comes through, "that will not be a problem of the dwarves of Brungril Forge. The pathway to our kingdom will have been sealed with spells, magic, and dirt." The party agrees, the freed slaves are evacuated from the portal room, and the party waits. The portal begins to shake.
