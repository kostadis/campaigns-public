# Chapter 29 – Will We Survive?

Date: The Abyss, Day 2 (??? on Oerth; from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-13)

## Summary
With two of the metal constructs defeated, the party wonders what to do next. KP suggests they first kill the trapped helmed horrors. Rimardo is in a rush and urges speed, and relents to let Bayard decide only when the gnomish magic-user reminds the cleric of Istus what happens should he no longer be able to concentrate on his spell. Bayard, resting in a chair, is raised from his reveries: "Given there is no way to hold them here indefinitely, we will have to dispatch them. Otherwise, it’s like the battle of Thornwick." Everyone stares. He explains that when the orcs began their invasion, a small village stood in their way, the Orc Army ignored it, and later that village harassed the Orc Army supply lines until the Orcs retreated into the Pomarj: "They buttered their brioche!" Still everyone stares. "You know, a small group of soldiers left behind, then surround and kill you!" With that rousing speech, the battle begins.

KP drops the wall of force and misses with a ray of frost, and the construct's hellish rebuke does 30 points of damage to him. The construct hits Bayard twice for 23 HP. Corrin hurts the second construct for 22 HP, and at the last minute the halfling turns so its hellish rebuke misses him completely. Bayard swings at the damaged second construct; after a moment of silence it flies apart, smashing the furniture, "like Bayard spread the monster like butter on a brioche." Bayard grins at the remaining helmed horror, which is impassive and not at all impressed, and does 21 HP to it. Rimardo casts a spell to no effect. KP's second ray of frost hits for 19 frost damage. The construct hits Bayard for 8 HP. Corrin, unwilling to let Bayard have the last word, picks the horror apart; the parts of its metal armor are like rings on his two rapiers.

The party heads down and finds four Drow cabalists, one in each of the four corners of the room, and a curious device. The Drow scream eerily in unison, "you have come to the citadel, and here you will die. The Drow Empire shall not let you pass!" The third member of this cabal casts hold person on Bayard, who is unaffected. The second cabalist's lightning bolt hits Bayard for 17 HP; the first moves to catch Bayard and Corrin, and Bayard takes 18 Hp "from a second lightning bolt." Corrin does 27 hp to the Drow that has not yet attacked. Rimardo casts a 5th level hold-person and paralyzes two members of the cabal; the lightning generator hits him in the chest for 7 HP, but his concentration holds. Bayard kills one paralyzed Drow and hits the other for 32 hit points, but the dead Drow comes back to life as a horrible monster, so horrifying the party must react to avoid being frightened. KP kills the horror.

One of the two unparalyzed cabalists casts dispel magic, freeing their brethren. The first Drow casts a seventh level lightning bolt on Bayard and KP for 40 hit points. Seeing Bayard seriously wounded, Rimardo thinks to himself that he could come to an arrangement with these Drow if worst came to worst. Corrin dashes and hits the third cabalist for 58 points, and it too comes back as a horror. The lightning bolt generator does 3 HP to Rimardo. Rimardo ponders killing the horror, healing Bayard, or another hold person; he kills the creature, then heals Bayard and the party for 22 HP. Bayard kills a Drow with 26 and 28 hit points of damage, it returns as a horror, and KP kills it.

A Drow lightning bolt hits Corrin, Bayard and KP; Corrin uses his uncanny dodge, while Bayard and KP take 17 hit points, leaving Bayard alive with seven hit points. Corrin misses. Rimardo's toll the dead is met by a counterspell from the last Drow. Bayard avoids the generator's bolt and does 49 hit points to the Drow. KP's magic missile is counter spelled. The Drow smiles at Bayard, knowing the Paladin is doomed, and a last 5th level lightning bolt drops him unconscious near death. Corrin kills the Drow with his first attack and the horror with his second. Bayard is healed for 12 hit points.

Rimardo suggests resting in the room for a short while, but "the Drow Empire has other plans": a group of Drow emerges from the door to the South. The party notices in time, climbs the stairs, and secures a defensive position around the door. Corrin's first attack does 41-point damage to a charging Drow, and he takes 10 points of heat damage from a fire shield and another 8. Ghirax tries to sneak by Mr. Corrin and is hit for 14 points, and Mr. Corrin is struck by the fire-shield and the armor of agathys. The battle is grim, and the chapter closes on the hard decisions facing Mr. Rimardo: "Does Rimardo channel Istus to heal the party? Does he make an offer to the Drow? Does he cast a spell? And what about the failsafe, and Mr. Gruneel? And isn’t there some demon that wants a deal?"
