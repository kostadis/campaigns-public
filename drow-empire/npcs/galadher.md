## Identity
Galadher Eleneth is a 245-year-old male Drow who oversees the Rangers collecting the good berries. He wears scale armor, has a bald head and green eyes, and wears matching lip and right-eyebrow piercings; the lip piercing is poorly done. [ch 031 / entry; ch 031 / 031.06]

## Personality and Motivations
Practical and skeptical of assurances, Galadher prioritizes payment for his band. He judges the new leadership’s credibility through its power and resources, but is willing to reconsider when offered a persuasive argument. [ch 031 / 031.06]

## History with the Party
- Galadher Eleneth approached the party in the morning and asked whether Bayard was the new boss. [ch 031 / 031.06]
- He oversees the Rangers who collect the good berries and warned that they would run out of berries in a few days. [ch 031 / 031.06]
- He asked who would pay his Rangers and questioned what power the party had that warranted his trust. [ch 031 / 031.06]
- KP persuaded him that his Rangers would be paid; Galadher promised to relay that assurance to his band. [ch 031 / entry; ch 031 / 031.06]

## Last Observed State
At the end of his morning exchange with the party, Galadher accepted KP’s assurance of payment and committed to passing it to his Rangers. Actual payment and a resolution to the impending berry shortage are not established in that scene. [ch 031 / 031.06]

## Relationships
- **His Rangers:** Galadher oversees the berry collectors and represents their concerns about pay to the party. [ch 031 / 031.06]
- **KP:** Persuades Galadher that the new leadership can pay his band. Galadher accepts the argument sufficiently to relay the assurance. [ch 031 / entry; ch 031 / 031.06]
- **Bayard:** Galadher approaches him as the apparent new authority and directs his payment question to him as mayor. [ch 031 / 031.06]
- **Rimardo:** Bayard directs Galadher’s payment inquiry to Rimardo, calling him the Treasurer. [ch 031 / 031.06]
