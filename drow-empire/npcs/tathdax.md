## Identity
Tathdax is the Drow Governor-General in these parts, identified with the Drow Empire. Selzyt reports that he has a tower 10 miles into the Feywild. [ch 036 / entry; ch 037 / 037.01; ch 037 / 037.02]

## Personality and Motivations
Selzyt portrays Tathdax as weakly loyal to the Empire and pursuing an empire of his own. This supports personal territorial ambition; his personality is not directly demonstrated, since he does not appear. [ch 036 / 036.05; ch 037 / entry]

## History with the Party
- Tathdax does not appear in either chapter; he is mentioned as the Drow Governor-General. [ch 036 / entry; ch 037 / entry]
- Selzyt described Tathdax as not particularly loyal to the Empire and speculated that he would not mind staying behind when the portal moves. [ch 036 / 036.05]
- Selzyt told the party that Tathdax has a tower 10 miles into the Feywild. [ch 037 / 037.02]
- According to Selzyt, Tathdax’s attempt to carve out his own empire disrupted the balance between the Unseelie and Seelies, and war has broken out. [ch 037 / 037.02]
- The power enabling Tathdax’s expansion was explicitly unclear. [ch 037 / 037.02]

## Last Observed State
Tathdax was mentioned, not encountered. The power enabling that expansion remains unclear. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 037 / entry; ch 037 / 037.02]
