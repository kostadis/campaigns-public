## Identity
Damrith held the title of Thane of Brungril Forge. Marco and Fonouc described him as a usurper who had deposed Fonouc in a coup. [ch 033 / 033.02; ch 034 / 034.05]

## Personality and Motivations
The Drow note portrays Damrith as a useful pawn who accepted trade benefits and military support against the Duergar. Selzyt interpreted the arrangement as mutual exploitation. These are outside assessments of his interests; Damrith’s own explanation is not recorded. [ch 033 / 033.02]

When Fonouc challenged his authority, Damrith responded frantically and called for guards, showing his immediate reliance on armed protection. [ch 034 / 034.05]

## History with the Party
- Damrith was mentioned but did not appear in chapter 033. A Drow note called him a useful idiot who benefited from Drow trade and military support against the Duergar. [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.02]
- Marco accused Damrith of usurping Fonouc through a coup, allying with Antyln, and sending the defeated dwarves to the Drow empire to die. [ch 033 / 033.02]
- Marco promised Rimardo a discount if the party killed Damrith and put Fonouc on the throne. [ch 033 / 033.05]
- After the freed slaves disrupted the marketplace, Marco warned that surprising Damrith was unlikely. [ch 034 / 034.02]
- Fonouc confronted Damrith in his hall, accused him of allying with the Drow and partnering with the duergar, and declared his rule ended. Damrith called for guards as Fonouc withdrew and the party entered. [ch 034 / 034.05]
- KP killed Damrith with a sculpted cone of cold, freezing him into a solid block of ice while sparing the civilians. [ch 034 / 034.05; ch 034 / moment]
- Antyln subsequently killed the dwarves who had served under Damrith and tried to flee from beside him. [ch 035 / 035.02]
- Following Damrith's defeat, Fonouc granted the party a standard discount as heroes of Brungril Forge. [ch 035 / 035.04]

## Last Observed State
Dead: KP’s sculpted cone of cold explicitly killed Damrith in his hall, freezing him into a solid block of ice. The spell spared the civilians. [ch 034 / entry; ch 034 / moment]

The subsequent account mentions Damrith’s former servants and his defeat; it does not depict another appearance by him. Fonouc grants the party rewards following that defeat. [ch 035 / 035.02; ch 035 / 035.04]

## Relationships
- **Fonouc:** Deposed rival and claimant to the title of Thane. Fonouc personally confronted Damrith and declared his rule ended. [ch 033 / 033.02; ch 034 / 034.05]
- **Marco:** Open enemy who accused Damrith of betrayal and promised Rimardo a discount for killing him and restoring Fonouc. [ch 033 / 033.02; ch 033 / 033.05]
- **Antyln and the Drow:** Marco identified Antyln as Damrith’s ally. The Drow note documented trade and military support while disparaging Damrith’s usefulness to them. [ch 033 / 033.02]
- **Duergar:** The Drow note described military support against them; Fonouc nevertheless accused Damrith of partnering with them. The supplied accounts do not reconcile these descriptions. [ch 033 / 033.02; ch 034 / 034.05]
- **Dwarven knights and officials:** Knights guarded Damrith’s hall, while unarmed bureaucrats implemented his wishes and benefited from his regime. [ch 034 / 034.05]
- **KP:** Member of the party opposing his rule and the caster who killed him. [ch 034 / 034.05]
