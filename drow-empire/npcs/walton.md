## Identity
Walton is Carc’s named contact in the Fax city militia, subsequently reported to be joining the army. His rank is not specified. [ch 006 / 006.03; ch 009 / 009.02]

## Personality and Motivations
Akia’s account portrays Walton as using intermediaries and engineered confrontations to eliminate the party on Carc’s behalf. A WLF member identifies payment from Carc as the incentive; Walton’s own explanation is not recorded. [ch 007 / 007.05; ch 007 / 007.06]

His response to a direct attack prioritizes self-preservation: wounded in his tent, he disengages and shelters behind guards he has awakened. [ch 009 / 009.04]

## History with the Party
- Carc named Walton as his contact in the Fax city militia. [ch 006 / 006.03]
- While the party sought Walton at the barracks, guards arrested them; the Sergeant said Walton had ordered it. [ch 007 / 007.04]
- A WLF member told the party that Carc had paid Walton to kill them; the party accepted the WLF’s rescue. [ch 007 / 007.05]
- Akia attributed four attempts against the party to Walton: provoking an arrest through a gate riot, confiscating their weapons to provoke resistance, sending slavers, and finally sending guards to arrest them. [ch 007 / 007.06]
- Rimardo expressed betrayal despite acknowledging that the party had never met Walton. [ch 007 / 007.06]
- Pursuing revenge, the party learned Walton was joining the army and located his cluster of tents among 20 armed men at The Meadows. [ch 009 / 009.02; ch 009 / 009.03]
- The party abandoned its confiscated weapons rather than identify themselves as the people Walton wanted dead. [ch 009 / 009.03]
- Rimardo cast silence on Walton’s tent, and Corrin attacked Walton in his sleep for 46 HP; Walton survived that attack, woke three guards, and hid behind them. [ch 009 / 009.04]
- KP caught Walton in a fireball, after which the party fled toward Vallaki; chapter 009 does not establish whether Walton died. [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.04]
- Nimh subsequently spoke of Walton as killed, identifying the attacker after surviving KP’s fireball at Vallaki. [ch 010 / 010.06]
- Filzyr later announced that Xhalh had placed a massive bounty on the party for killing Walton and trying to stop his conquests. [ch 012 / moment]

## Last Observed State
Walton’s last directly depicted location is his tent at The Meadows. He survives Corrin’s initial attack and is subsequently caught in KP’s fireball; that chapter leaves his survival unresolved. [ch 009 / 009.03; ch 009 / 009.04; ch 009 / entry]

Later reports identify him as dead: Nimh attributes his killing to KP, and Filzyr subsequently names Walton’s killing among the reasons for Xhalh’s bounty on the party. These are later statements about Walton, rather than further appearances. [ch 010 / 010.06; ch 012 / 012.05]

## Relationships
- **Carc:** Names Walton as his militia contact; according to the WLF, pays him to kill the party. [ch 006 / 006.03; ch 007 / 007.05]
- **The party:** Initially treats Walton as a prospective ally through Carc, then regards him as a betrayer and pursues revenge. Rimardo explicitly acknowledges that they had never met him before that pursuit. [ch 007 / 007.05; ch 007 / 007.06; ch 009 / 009.02]
- **Guards:** The Sergeant attributes the party’s arrest order to Walton. Three guards in Walton’s tent provide immediate protection when he is attacked. [ch 007 / 007.04; ch 009 / 009.04]
- **Nimh:** Recognizes KP as Walton’s killer and threatens retaliation; their personal relationship is not specified. [ch 010 / 010.06]
- **Xhalh and Filzyr:** Filzyr invokes Walton’s killing when announcing Xhalh’s bounty on the party. The statement does not establish Walton’s precise relationship with either. [ch 012 / moment]
