## Identity
Boradoon was the party’s magic-user, a bladesinger, and a friend whose contacts in Greyhawk supplied the journal entry that brought them to Farjvad. [ch 003 / entry; ch 001 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Observant and analytical, Boradoon detected traps and reasoned that the aquatic zombies’ organisation indicated training and deliberate targeting by a necromancer. He questioned Ralnor’s account against his understanding of Greyhawk’s powers. [ch 002 / 002.01; ch 002 / 002.02; ch 001 / 001.04; ch 002 / 002.03]

He expected the paladin to handle opening doors, but could act aggressively in battle, charging into a room to cast thunder wave and remaining surrounded by guards. [ch 003 / 003.07; ch 003 / 003.08]

## Last Observed State
Boradoon is dead: an initiate’s magic missile killed him, with internal bleeding explicitly recorded. His companions carried his body toward Farjvad, and Dimitrov performed last rites in a clearing. [ch 003 / 003.08; ch 003 / 003.09]

His latest mention was in Bayard’s explanation to Carc Mistsplitter of how he had joined the earlier party; Boradoon was spoken of, not present. [ch 004 / 004.04]
