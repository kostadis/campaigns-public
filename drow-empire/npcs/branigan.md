## Identity
Branigan Cartwright is a woman who owns the local smithy and follows St. Cuthbert. [ch 005 / entry] Chapter 006 also uses the name Cynthia Branigan when reporting a smithy contact; the naming discrepancy is unresolved. [ch 006 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Her humor includes invented claims of divine guidance, which she readily retracts when questioned; Bayard finds these admissions disappointing. [ch 005 / 005.02]

She seeks to drive out the Orcs and resist would-be rulers of the Wild Coast. Her proposals show a willingness to exploit competing powers, including using the Drow against the Orcs. [ch 005 / 005.02; ch 006 / 006.02] Her later request to stop discussing the Drow establishes a desire to suppress that discussion, but her reason is not stated. [ch 006 / 006.02]

## History with the Party
- Branigan Cartwright owns the local smithy, follows St. Cuthbert, and invites the party there to talk safely. [ch 005 / 005.02]
- She twice claims divine sanction for meeting the party and admits both claims were jokes, disappointing Bayard. [ch 005 / entry; ch 005 / 005.02]
- She proposes using the Drow to drive out the Orcs, arguing that the Orc Empire lacks the manpower to hold the entire Wild Coast. [ch 005 / 005.02]
- She finds the Drow's presence odd because, according to her, there is no path from the Underdark to the area. [ch 005 / 005.02]
- After the Drow's defeat, she approaches the party and credits their victory with preventing further Orc invasion and averting war. [ch 006 / 006.02]
- She claims the elvish kingdom intends to invade and has designated some dark-skinned elves as Drow. [ch 006 / 006.02]
- She urges the party to warn allies in Fax and find Halete, whom she describes as another smithy able to lead them deeper into the resistance. [ch 006 / 006.02]
- She asks the party to stop talking about the Drow and recommends traveling by road. [ch 006 / 006.02]
- The party follows Alexia Boulderdown's advice to take the river, contrary to Branigan's and Carc's road recommendations. [ch 006 / moment]

## Last Observed State
Her last directly described interaction with the party occurs in Farjvad after the Drow’s defeat. She considers the victory tenuous and directs the party toward allies and a resistance contact in Fax. [ch 006 / 006.02] The subsequent intelligence summary attributes information about a smithy contact to Cynthia Branigan without establishing a separate encounter. [ch 006 / 006.03]

The party follows Alexia Boulderdown’s river recommendation instead of Branigan’s road advice; Branigan’s subsequent movements are not reported. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 006 / moment]

## Relationships
- **The party:** She offers a safe place to talk and later supplies warnings, contacts, and travel advice. [ch 005 / 005.02; ch 006 / 006.02]
- **Bayard:** Her false claims of divine sanction leave him crestfallen; no deeper personal relationship is established. [ch 005 / 005.02]
- **Halete:** She identifies Halete as a smithy in Fax who can connect the party more deeply with the resistance. The extent of their personal relationship is unspecified. [ch 006 / 006.02]
- **Carc Mistsplitter:** They both recommend the road to Fax, but describe the victory in different political terms. No coordination between them is established. [ch 006 / 006.02]
- **Alexia Boulderdown:** Her river advice contradicts Branigan’s road recommendation; no direct interaction between the two is described. [ch 006 / 006.02]
