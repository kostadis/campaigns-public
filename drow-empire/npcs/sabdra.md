## Identity
Sabdra is a drider and the party’s contact in the slave pits. The chapter uses both “He” and “She” for Sabdra; pronouns are unresolved. [ch 049 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Sabdra shows initiative and willingness to fight alongside the party, urging action and joining the attack on the guards. Sabdra invokes Vhaeraun in combat; the evidence does not establish a broader religious role or motive. [ch 049 / 049.03]

## History with the Party
