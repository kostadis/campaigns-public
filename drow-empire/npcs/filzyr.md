## Identity
Filzyr is a bounty hunter working for the Drow, with orders from Xhalh authorizing bounty and assistance to kill enemies of the Drow Empire. His species and origins are not established. [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.01]

## Personality and Motivations
His theatrical introduction suggests a boastful manner. He presents bounty hunting as his trade and Xhalh's reward as his reason for targeting the party; any deeper allegiance is unestablished. [ch 012 / moment]

## History with the Party
- Filzyr appeared out of the storm as the party recovered from a Drow ambush, announced Xhalh's massive bounty on them, and joined battle. [ch 012 / 012.05]
- The party defeated Filzyr in a gruesome fight. [ch 013 / entry]
- The party found orders authorizing Filzyr to receive bounty and assistance to kill the independent contractors and enemies of the Drow Empire, alongside a scroll describing the party with pictures. [ch 013 / 013.01]
- Rimardo took Filzyr's clothing and Xhalh's order, considering a future opportunity to impersonate Filzyr. [ch 013 / 013.01]

## Last Observed State
Filzyr was last observed defeated after a gruesome fight. The summaries establish defeat but do not specify whether he survived: Status not established in the summaries. [ch 013 / entry]

Rimardo holds his clothing and Xhalh's order, creating a potential means of impersonating him. [ch 013 / 013.01]
