## Identity
Boris Boulderdown is a 60-year-old peddler, 6’3” tall, with cropped wavy grey hair, green eyes, sunburned pink skin, a banal oval face, and a piercing in his left ear. [ch 001 / 001.04]

## Personality and Motivations
Boris offers selective guidance: witnessing the aquatic zombie massacre prompts him to steer the travelers toward the temple of Mort, but he withholds details he knows about it. His broader motives are not established. [ch 001 / 001.04; ch 001 / entry]

## History with the Party
- The party met Boris Boulderdown, a 60-year-old peddler and Alexia's uncle, at the south docks. [ch 001 / 001.04]
- Boris had witnessed the massacre of the aquatic zombies and decided to guide the travelers toward the temple of Mort. [ch 001 / 001.04]
- Boris volunteered that the temple had plenty of dead bodies but withheld other details he knew about it. [ch 001 / 001.04; ch 001 / entry]

## Last Observed State
Boris was last observed speaking with the party at the south docks, providing information about the temple of Mort before the adventurers rested briefly and set out. His subsequent location and condition are not given. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 001 / 001.04]

## Relationships
