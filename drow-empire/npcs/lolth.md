## Identity
Lolth is a deity worshipped by Drow. Their origin legend identifies her as Araushnee’s alter ego and links her rule to a matriarchal society devoted to her. [ch 003 / 003.01]

Her worship has an institutional presence through clerics, shrines, and noble-house allegiance. [ch 033 / 033.03; ch 047 / 047.03; ch 050 / 050.02]

## Personality and Motivations
Valnozz’s account portrays Lolth as maintaining control through exclusive worship, restrictions on magic, and internal conflict that prevents rivals from consolidating power. This is the account of an opponent of her rule. [ch 031 / 031.02]

In direct confrontation, she displays contempt for intruders and pleasure at the prospect of physically destroying them. When her magic fails against Corrin, she turns to defensive magic and a direct attack. [ch 051 / 051.02; ch 051 / moment]

Her pursuit of the artifact persisted after the portal’s closure, shifting toward quieter infiltration. Tolub identifies her intended use of the dagger as killing Corellon. [ch 022 / 022.05; ch 052 / 052.02]

## Last Observed State
The latest reported status is **imprisoned using a magical trinket**, according to Lundbeams. Tolub speaks of Lolth as dead, but Lundbeams explicitly distinguishes imprisonment from killing. Lolth is only mentioned in these scenes; she does not appear or act. [ch 052 / 052.02; ch 052 / 052.04]

Rimardo claims the artifact is safe from her for all time. This is his assurance, rather than a demonstrated guarantee of permanent containment. [ch 052 / 052.04]

## Relationships
- **Corellon:** The origin legend names him as responsible for her banishment; Tolub later identifies him as her intended target for the dagger. [ch 003 / 003.01; ch 052 / 052.02]
- **Vhaeraun and his followers:** Opponents of her rule. Valnozz explicitly works against her, Brorna covertly thwarted her plans, and Vhaeraun commissioned her imprisonment. [ch 044 / 044.06; ch 048 / 048.07; ch 048 / 048.08]
- **Drow houses and servants:** Allegiance is divided. House Do’ty’s representatives invoke her authority against toleration of Vhaeraunites, while Valnozz describes House Illal’s matron as opposing her acquisition of the artifact’s power. [ch 047 / 047.03; ch 031 / 031.03]
- **The party:** Direct adversaries who ultimately trapped her in the mirror, with Corrin presenting it to her. Istus supported their confrontation through protective magic and tactical advice. [ch 051 / 051.04; ch 050 / 050.03]
- **Graz’zt:** Selzyt reports that he displaced her from the 66th layer of the Abyss. [ch 026 / 026.02]
- **Gruumsh:** His hatred of Lolth and the Drow informed the party’s assessment of the Orc Empire’s likely response to invasion. [ch 004 / 004.08]
