## Identity
Vhaeraun is the masked one, a self-described evil god. Rimardo identifies him as the son of Araushnee and Corellon Larethian; Araushnee is Lolth. [ch 048 / 048.09; ch 048 / moment; ch 016 / 016.04]

Selzyt is revealed as Vhaeraun when the green glow from Selzyt’s gemstone flows into Brorna in the dungeon. The scene does not explain the precise nature of this embodiment. [ch 048 / 048.07]

## Personality and Motivations
Vhaeraun is patient and intent on revenge against Lolth, claiming to have waited a thousand years for suitable adventurers. His objective is lasting imprisonment: he explains that killing her would merely allow her to rematerialize. [ch 048 / 048.07; ch 048 / 048.08]

He uses concealed agents, negotiated rewards, and indirect intervention. His response to Rimardo’s suggestion of shared responsibility for a double-cross distances him from the party’s liability. He also takes explicit pleasure in House Do’ty’s anticipated downfall. [ch 048 / 048.07; ch 048 / 048.09; ch 048 / 048.10]

Rimardo describes his creed as valuing Drow males and females equally. Valnozz’s account also attributes to him opposition to Lolth’s hostility toward magic, while acknowledging some ambition to rule in her place. These are attributed accounts of his principles and ambitions. [ch 016 / 016.04; ch 031 / 031.02]

## History with the Party
- Vhaeraun was first mentioned in the Drow origin story: Lolth fled with him, later tried to kill him, and he escaped. [ch 003 / 003.01]
- Valnozz identified himself as Vhaeraun’s follower during his parley with the party. Rimardo described Vhaeraun as the son of Araushnee and Corellon Larethian, who valued Drow males and females equally. [ch 016 / 016.04]
- Valnozz revealed that he trained Selzyt and that both belonged to Vhaeraun’s cult. [ch 031 / 031.01]
- In Valnozz’s account, Vhaeraun suggested the Lolth persona, later opposed Lolth’s conflict between the genders and hostility to magic, and gained followers among Drow men after she cast him out. [ch 031 / 031.02]
- Finaeli Talraidal spoke for worshippers of the masked one; KP recognised his allegiance to Vhaeraun, and Bayard appointed him the party’s steward. [ch 031 / 031.07]
- Valnozz reaffirmed his allegiance to Vhaeraun while advising the party how to defeat the Drow general and approach Maenerius. [ch 044 / 044.06]
- Valnozz warned that House Do’ty wished to purge Vhaeraun’s followers. [ch 046 / 046.02]
- In Maenerius, the party heard a cleric of Lolth denounce Vhaeraun’s faith and blame it for the Empire’s defeats. [ch 047 / 047.05]
- In the dungeon, Brorna emerged and the green glow from Selzyt’s gemstone flowed into her; Vhaeraun then identified himself as the masked one. [ch 048 / 048.07]
- Vhaeraun claimed to have waited a thousand years and credited Brorna with protecting his cult, thwarting Lolth, and directing the Drow Empire from behind the scenes. [ch 048 / 048.07]
- Vhaeraun hired the party to weaken Lolth and imprison her in a magic mirror, explaining that killing her would only make her rematerialize. [ch 048 / 048.08]
- Vhaeraun supplied equipment and boons to Corrin, KP, Rimardo, and Bayard; he explicitly identified himself to Bayard as an evil God. [ch 048 / 048.09]
- Vhaeraun repeatedly stabbed Talaryn’s body to stage her death and anticipated House Do’ty losing power when Lolth was trapped. [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.10]
- Vhaeraun and Brorna’s infiltration plan used feigned capture, transport through Lolth’s slave pits, and a Drider contact to return the party’s equipment. [ch 049 / 049.01]
- Their contact Sabdra invoked Vhaeraun while attacking a drider guard during the party’s escape from the slave pit. [ch 049 / 049.03]
- Corrin used Vhaeraun’s boon to turn a missed attack against Lolth into a hit. [ch 051 / 051.03]
- Lundbeams later said she knew about the party’s deals with Vhaeraun and accused them of disrupting the balance of divine power by imprisoning Lolth. [ch 052 / 052.04]

## Last Observed State
His latest direct actions in the supplied summaries occur during the dungeon negotiations and the staging of Talaryn’s death. The subsequent infiltration plan refers to Vhaeraun and Brorna’s contact in the slave pits without establishing Vhaeraun’s presence there. [ch 048 / 048.10; ch 049 / 049.01]

Later evidence shows Corrin using his boon against Lolth. Lundbeams subsequently speaks of the party’s dealings with Vhaeraun and confirms Lolth’s imprisonment; Vhaeraun is mentioned rather than present in that encounter. His location and condition afterward are not given. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 051 / 051.03; ch 052 / 052.04]

## Relationships
- **Lolth:** His mother and principal enemy. He seeks revenge through her imprisonment; Valnozz describes their competing cults as enemies throughout the Drow realms. [ch 048 / 048.08; ch 031 / 031.02]
- **Brorna:** His agent and collaborator. He credits her with protecting his cult and secretly directing the Drow Empire; she arranges the party’s infiltration of Lolth’s domain. [ch 048 / 048.07; ch 049 / 049.01]
- **Valnozz:** An avowed follower whose timely assistance Vhaeraun presents as deliberate coordination. [ch 044 / 044.06; ch 048 / 048.07]
- **Finaeli Talraidal and Sabdra:** Finaeli speaks for worshippers of the masked one; Sabdra serves as the infiltration contact and invokes Vhaeraun while aiding the escape. [ch 031 / 031.07; ch 049 / 049.03]
- **The party:** Contracted agents rewarded with equipment and divine boons. Bayard voices moral objections to accepting gifts from him, while the group accepts the mission. [ch 048 / 048.08; ch 048 / 048.09]
- **House Do’ty:** Persecutors of his cult whose expected loss of power he welcomes. [ch 046 / 046.02; ch 048 / 048.10]
