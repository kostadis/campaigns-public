## Identity
Triton is a paladin who wielded a trident and adventured with Dimitrov, Reepicheep, and Boradoon. [ch 001 / 001.01; ch 001 / 001.03]

## Personality and Motivations
Described as proud, Triton drinks heavily; his drunken cursing prompted Walter Caskbow to expel the party from the Inn at the River. [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.01] He joined the expedition seeking gold and adventure, with Boradoon directing the group toward evildoers in Farjvad. [ch 001 / 001.01]

## History with the Party
- Triton, a paladin, became seriously drunk and cursed at the Inn at the River; Walter Caskbow expelled the party. [ch 001 / 001.01]
- Triton fought the aquatic zombies with his trident during the river crossing; the party slaughtered them before they took anyone. [ch 001 / 001.03]
- Triton searched the water for a hidden lair and found nothing. [ch 001 / 001.04]
- A hurtling log grazed Triton during an ambush near the Temple of Mort. [ch 001 / 001.05]
- Triton put Ralnor on a fork after Ralnor’s final escape attempt failed. [ch 002 / 002.03]
- Alexia Boulderdown greeted the party by asking after “the rat” and “the fish”; the memorable moment states that Triton was gone. [ch 003 / moment]
- Bayard later explained that he joined Dimitrov, Boradoon, and Reepicheep after their paladin, a drunk Triton, went swimming instead of fighting. Triton was spoken of in this scene. [ch 004 / 004.04]

## Last Observed State
Triton was reported gone in chapter 003. [ch 003 / moment] In chapter 004, he was spoken of by Bayard, who said that Triton had gone swimming instead of fighting before Bayard joined the party. This is a retrospective account, not a new appearance; Triton’s current location and condition are not established in the summaries. [ch 004 / 004.04]

## Relationships

- **Walter Caskbow:** Expelled Triton and his companions because of Triton’s drunken behaviour. [ch 001 / 001.01]
