## Identity
Selzyt is the gemstone-speaking portal guardian, overseer and failsafe, ultimately revealed as Vhaeraun, the masked one. He can possess others, including through magic jar. [ch 025 / 025.08; ch 030 / 030.01; ch 048 / 048.07]

His account of imprisonment by the queen of House Zollazo and a body awaiting recovery was the basis of his bargain with the party; it should be treated as his account in light of the later revelation. [ch 025 / 025.08; ch 025 / 025.09; ch 048 / 048.07]

## Personality and Motivations
Selzyt is sarcastic, sensitive to disrespect and protective of his own survival. He responds to threats against his gem with panic and admits withholding assistance to preserve his failsafe role if the party died. [ch 037 / 037.02; ch 033 / 033.02]

He uses information as bargaining power, controlling when he answers questions and withholding the library’s location until his body is recovered. His stated goals include recovering that body and obtaining revenge; his later bargain specifically demands Talaryn’s death and protection for anyone found in the dungeon. [ch 025 / 025.09; ch 040 / 040.01; ch 048 / 048.02]

He treats betrayal as ordinary Drow behavior, while showing enthusiasm for effective magic and sustained discussion of inter-planar trade. [ch 031 / 031.07; ch 038 / 038.03; ch 040 / 040.01]

## Last Observed State
In the palace dungeon, Selzyt’s gemstone emits a green glow that flows into Brorna. He reveals himself as Vhaeraun, the masked one, and says he has waited a thousand years for someone to reach him. [ch 048 / 048.07]

The scene establishes the revelation and transfer of the glow, but does not explicitly establish recovery of the body he sought or his subsequent condition. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 048 / 048.07]

## Relationships
- **Bayard:** His principal bargaining partner and a source of protection for the gem. Selzyt relies on Bayard’s commitment to their agreement, though Bayard also pressures him to cooperate. He insists on negotiating the dungeon bargain directly with Bayard and accepts its qualifications. [ch 027 / 027.05; ch 037 / 037.02; ch 048 / 048.02]
- **KP:** An inquisitive interlocutor whose questions can exhaust him, but also a valued rescuer and partner in discussing trade theory. Selzyt expresses gratitude while continuing to attach conditions to further information. [ch 025 / 025.09; ch 040 / 040.01]
- **Rimardo:** Their exchanges are antagonistic: Rimardo proposes discarding the gem and later threatens it, provoking Selzyt’s defensive outbursts. [ch 027 / 027.05; ch 037 / 037.02]
- **Valnozz:** Valnozz claims to have trained Selzyt and identifies them both as members of the cult of Vhaeraun. At the revelation, Vhaeraun implies that Valnozz’s timely appearances were arranged. [ch 031 / 031.01; ch 048 / 048.07]
- **Brorna:** Revealed as his agent and recipient of the gemstone’s green glow. Vhaeraun credits her with protecting his cult and quietly thwarting Lolth’s plans. [ch 048 / 048.07]
- **House Zollazo and Talaryn:** Selzyt distrusts House Zollazo and makes Talaryn’s death a condition of his dungeon bargain. [ch 047 / 047.04; ch 048 / 048.02]
- **Do’ty:** Selzyt warns that Do’ty will consolidate power quickly; Bayard chooses to support house Do’ty despite that warning. [ch 047 / 047.08]
