## Identity
Pelynumph is an adult myconid whose spores enable shared communication, including allowing the party to hear Corrin. [ch 033 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Pelynumph seeks allies to protect his colony from the Drow Empire’s harvesting of myconids for food. His assigned purpose is to find heroes capable of stopping it. [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.06]

He speaks candidly about the dwarves’ complicity and the enslavement of dissenters. He asks whether the group wishes to maintain the spore connection. [ch 033 / 033.06]

## History with the Party
- Pelynumph, an adult myconid and old friend of Marco, approached the party seeking allies to stop the Drow Empire’s slaughter of myconids for their flesh. [ch 033 / 033.06]
- Pelynumph spread spores over the group, connecting them and allowing everyone to hear Corrin speak. [ch 033 / 033.06]
- Pelynumph explained that the dwarves willingly allied with the Drow and tolerated the Duergar’s role as slavers; those who objected ended up in slave pens. [ch 033 / 033.06]
- Pelynumph reported that Fonouc lived but was unwilling to take direct action. [ch 033 / 033.06]
- Pelynumph offered to keep the group connected through his spores; Corrin reluctantly consented despite concerns about exposing his thoughts and voice. [ch 033 / 033.06]
- Marco later credited the party with rescuing the myconids and their hero Pelynumph; Pelynumph was mentioned in this farewell scene, without an established presence. [ch 035 / 035.05]

## Last Observed State
Marco’s farewell credits the party with rescuing the myconids and Pelynumph, whom he calls their hero. Pelynumph is mentioned in that scene; his presence, location, and subsequent activities are not established. [ch 035 / 035.05]

## Relationships
- **Marco:** An old friend who readily promises help when Pelynumph appeals to him. [ch 033 / 033.06]
- **The myconids:** His endangered community, for whom he seeks outside assistance. Marco later describes him as their hero. [ch 033 / entry; ch 035 / 035.05]
- **The party:** Prospective allies whom he connects through spores to facilitate communication. [ch 033 / 033.06]
- **Corrin:** The spore connection exposes his voice to the group. Corrin reluctantly agrees to remain connected despite concerns about access to his private thoughts. [ch 033 / 033.06]
- **The Drow Empire:** The power slaughtering his kind and the target of his appeal for intervention. [ch 033 / 033.06]
