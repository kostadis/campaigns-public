## Identity
Nadal is a negotiator for House Noquarth with knowledge of inter-planar economics and KP's published work. [ch 047 / entry; ch 047 / 047.07]

## Personality and Motivations
Nadal is confrontational about economic theory, challenging KP's credentials, methods, and originality. He nevertheless shows willingness to reconsider an opponent's arguments after sustained debate. [ch 047 / 047.07]

He pursues House Noquarth's interests through an assassination offer against Talaryn. [ch 047 / entry]

## History with the Party
- Nadal, a House Noquarth negotiator, offered 30,000 GP each for Talaryn's death. [ch 047 / entry]
- Nadal challenged KP's expertise in inter-planar economics and called him a hack. [ch 047 / 047.07]
- After several hours of debate, Nadal conceded that KP's theories had merit and said they would consider them carefully. [ch 047 / 047.07]
- The debate led to Drow societies called the "Nadal-KP Equilibrium," proposing a model to address the limits of the plunder economy; their long-term impact is unclear. [ch 047 / 047.07]

## Last Observed State
At the close of his debate with KP, Nadal acknowledged merit in KP's theories and said they would receive careful consideration. The resulting economic model's long-term impact remains unclear. [ch 047 / 047.07]

Status not established in the summaries. [ch 047 / entry; ch 047 / 047.07]

## Relationships
