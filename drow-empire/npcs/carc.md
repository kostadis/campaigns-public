## Identity
Carc Mistsplitter is an orc from Fhuu and the Major and ruler of Farjvad, a border town of the Orc Empire. [ch 001 / entry; ch 004 / entry; ch 006 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Carc is forceful and protective of his title, angrily correcting KP when addressed as mayor. He frames the party's actions in terms of the Orc Empire's interests, assigning blame and offering praise accordingly. [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 006 / 006.02]

The party interprets his blockade orders as a desire for outsiders to resolve the problem quietly. Alexia's account of his suppression of discussion about zombie attacks also suggests concern with controlling information. These are attributed assessments of his motives. [ch 004 / entry; ch 001 / entry]

## History with the Party
- Carc Mistsplitter is identified as the Major of Farjvad, an orc from Fhuu; he does not appear in person in chapter 001. [ch 001 / entry]
- Alexia says Carc refuses to act on the zombie attacks and insists that no boatman speak of them. [ch 001 / entry]
- Boradoon's contacts were expected to arrange a meeting between the party and the Major. [ch 001 / 001.01]
- Carc enters the Inn furious and corrects KP's use of “mayor,” insisting that he is the Major. [ch 004 / 004.04]
- Carc blames Bayard by association for the previous four adventurers' crimes and the Orc Empire's problems. [ch 004 / 004.04]
- Carc orders the party to break the blockade; the party concludes that he wants outsiders to solve the problem quietly. [ch 004 / entry]
- After the Drow's defeat, Carc praises Bayard for a victory for the Orc Empire. [ch 006 / 006.02]
- Carc commands Bayard to warn the Grand Master in Fax, saying that the Elves will attack the city. [ch 006 / 006.02]
- Carc recommends the road as the safest route to Fax. [ch 006 / 006.02]
- Carc names Walton as a contact in Fax's city militia. [ch 006 / 006.03]
- A WLF member tells the party that Walton was paid by Carc to kill them; the party connects this allegation with Carc's road recommendation and referral to Walton. [ch 007 / 007.05]
- Akia explains that Carc told Walton to take care of his problem, and describes Walton's successive attempts to have the party arrested or killed. [ch 007 / 007.06]

## Last Observed State
Carc's last direct interaction places him in Farjvad, sending Bayard toward Fax with a warning for the Grand Master and recommending Walton as a militia contact. [ch 006 / 006.02; ch 006 / 006.03]

In chapter 007, Carc is spoken of rather than present: a WLF member alleges that he paid Walton to kill the party, and Akia attributes Walton's campaign against them to Carc's instruction. His subsequent whereabouts are unreported. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 007 / 007.05; ch 007 / 007.06]

## Relationships
- **Bayard and the party:** Carc treats them as people he can command in the Empire's interests. They come to regard his assistance as betrayal after the WLF's allegations. [ch 004 / entry; ch 006 / 006.02; ch 007 / 007.05]
- **Walton:** Carc's named contact in Fax's city militia; according to the WLF member and Akia, Walton also acts against the party at Carc's direction. [ch 006 / 006.03; ch 007 / 007.05; ch 007 / 007.06]
- **Alexia Boulderdown:** She criticizes his handling of the zombie attacks and gives travel advice that directly contradicts his recommendation. [ch 001 / entry; ch 006 / 006.02]
- **Grand Master:** The intended recipient of the warning Carc orders Bayard to deliver; their broader relationship is not established. [ch 006 / 006.02]
