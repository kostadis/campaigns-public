## Identity
Dimitrov is a cleric whose abilities include guiding bolt, sacred flame, and turning undead; he also fights with a mace. His deity or deities are not named. [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.09]

He has knowledge of Drow customs sufficient to recognize that Realm Leader is not a title used by the Drow of Oerth. [ch 002 / 002.03]

## Personality and Motivations
Dimitrov initially wanted adventure, but the deaths he witnessed led him to reject that life. His stated reason for quitting was that he had misunderstood what adventure meant and had seen enough death. [ch 003 / moment]

He expresses alarm and frustration openly when combat deteriorates. His care for fallen companions extends to carrying their bodies and performing their last rites. [ch 003 / 003.08; ch 003 / 003.09; ch 003 / 003.10]

## History with the Party
- Dimitrov was among the adventurers left on the Tlerowth River docks after Walter Caskbow expelled the group from the Inn at the River. [ch 001 / 001.01]
- He seared the aquatic zombies attacking their boat with guiding bolts. [ch 001 / 001.03]
- He recognized that Realm Leader was not a title used by the Drow of Oerth. [ch 002 / 002.03]
- Ralnor countered his spells and tried unsuccessfully to escape through the doorway Dimitrov guarded. [ch 002 / 002.03]
- Dimitrov killed warriors during the barracks assault and was knocked out. [ch 003 / 003.04]
- Against animated skeletons, he cast guiding bolt twice before finally turning the remaining undead. [ch 003 / 003.06]
- He killed a Drow initiate with his mace. [ch 003 / 003.08]
- He reported running out of spells during the fight against the Drow and their human allies. [ch 003 / 003.08]
- The battle summary says the Drow Initiate killed Dimitrov; the following scene explicitly records Bayard healing him. [ch 003 / 003.08; ch 003 / 003.09]
- Bayard and Dimitrov carried their fallen comrades back toward Farjvad. [ch 003 / 003.09]
- Dimitrov performed the last rites for Boradoon and Reepicheep in a clearing. [ch 003 / 003.10]
- After sixteen hours of sleep, he quit adventuring and left. [ch 003 / 003.10]
- Bayard later mentioned Dimitrov while explaining how he had joined the previous party. [ch 004 / 004.04]
- Alexia Boulderdown later recalled Dimitrov telling them about Xhalh, another Drow in charge who had not been found. [ch 006 / 006.02]

## Last Observed State
Dimitrov’s last direct appearance ends with him quitting adventuring and leaving after sixteen hours of sleep at the River Drink. [ch 003 / 003.10]

The battle summary earlier describes him as killed, but the following scene explicitly records Bayard healing him, followed by Dimitrov acting and departing. [ch 003 / 003.08; ch 003 / 003.09; ch 003 / 003.10]

He is subsequently mentioned by Bayard and Alexia Boulderdown, without appearing in those scenes. His whereabouts and status after departure are not established in the summaries. [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 006 / 006.02]
