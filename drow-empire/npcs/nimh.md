## Identity
Nimh is a Drow general who led the army of Fax against Vallaki, demanding surrender in the name of “The Empire of Fax.” [ch 010 / entry; ch 014 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
- **Cruel and condescending:** He treated Humph's alleged treason as grounds to torture and kill both Humph and those close to him, dismissing ignorance as irrelevant to guilt. [ch 010 / 010.06]
- **Perceptive and vindictive:** Halete's account credits him with detecting Lundbeams' indirect defiance and using an unsupported assault to punish her WLF troops while weakening Vallaki's defenders. [ch 014 / 014.01]
- **Personally hostile toward KP:** Recognizing KP as Walton's killer, he expressed eagerness to kill him. [ch 010 / 010.06]

## History with the Party
- At the stockade, Nimh was mentioned by a Drow who said he wanted the army of Fax to conduct the assault on Vallaki. [ch 010 / 010.01]
- Humph told the party that inflicting sufficient casualties could force Nimh to withdraw, but killing Nimh would not stop the attack. [ch 010 / 010.04]
- Nimh approached the western gate and demanded Humph surrender to face justice at the court of Xhalh in St. Aurelius, with the Drow taking control of Vallaki. [ch 010 / 010.06]
- When Humph offered surrender and requested fair treatment, Nimh threatened to torture and kill Humph, Vallaki's ruling body, and everyone Humph loved. [ch 010 / 010.06]
- KP struck Nimh and his retinue with a fireball from hiding; Nimh survived, identified KP as Walton's killer, threatened him, and ordered the attack. [ch 010 / 010.06]
- During Halete's questioning under a zone of truth spell, the party learned that Nimh had interpreted the fireball as evidence of Lundbeams' double game. [ch 014 / 014.01]
- The same account revealed that Nimh retaliated by sending Halete's WLF detachment to attack first and withholding support; only Halete and three others survived the massacre by pretending to die. [ch 014 / 014.01]
- Jaezton later told the party that Namyn of House Illal commanded the Drow assault then underway; Nimh had led the initial assault on Vallaki. [ch 016 / 016.02]

## Last Observed State
Nimh's last directly depicted action was ordering the attack after surviving KP's fireball at Vallaki's western gate. [ch 010 / 010.06]

The latest mention comes from Jaezton, who identified Namyn of House Illal as commander of the assault then underway and Nimh as leader of the initial assault. This report does not establish Nimh's subsequent whereabouts or condition. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 016 / 016.02]

## Relationships
- **Humph:** An enemy whom Nimh accused of treason; Nimh threatened Humph, Vallaki's ruling body, and everyone Humph loved. [ch 010 / 010.06]
- **KP and Walton:** Nimh identified KP as Walton's killer and threatened revenge against KP. His relationship with Walton is otherwise unspecified. [ch 010 / 010.06]
- **Lundbeams:** According to Halete, Nimh recognized her circumvention of orders and sought revenge against her and the WLF. [ch 014 / 014.01]
- **Halete and the WLF:** Nimh exercised authority over Halete's detachment, deliberately withholding support when he sent it into battle as punishment. [ch 014 / 014.01]
- **Tolub and the Drow:** Nimh presented Tolub as leader of the Faxian Empire while demanding that the Drow take control of Vallaki. [ch 010 / 010.06]
- **Namyn of House Illal:** Jaezton distinguished their commands: Nimh led the initial assault; Namyn commanded the later assault. Their direct relationship is unspecified. [ch 016 / 016.02]
