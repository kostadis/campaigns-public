## Identity
Akia is a WLF leader and the party’s WLF contact, subordinate to her boss, Alice. [ch 007 / entry; ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.05]

## Personality and Motivations
Akia prioritizes shielding the WLF from reprisals, using independent operators against the Drow to conceal the organization’s involvement. She invokes the party’s debt for their rescue when seeking their cooperation. [ch 007 / 007.06]

She is contemptuous of Tolub’s political position and impatient with Rimardo’s interruptions. Her stated justification for identifying the enemy as Drow rests on the WLF’s communion with Heironeous. [ch 007 / 007.06]

## History with the Party
- Akia, identified as the WLF leader, refused to take the party to Lundbeams. [ch 007 / 007.06]
- Akia dismissed Tolub as “a stooge and a member of the ruling class and a toy of the empire” and said nobody knew why he had not been seen in months. [ch 007 / 007.06]
- Akia recruited the party as independent operators against the Drow, explaining that their apparent independence would shield the WLF from reprisals. [ch 007 / 007.06]
- Akia said the WLF had confirmed the Drow’s identity through communion with Heironeous and knew where slaves were stockpiled and shipped by boat, alongside the Drow’s gold. [ch 007 / 007.06]
- Akia explained that Walton, acting on Carc’s instructions, had tried to provoke the party at the gate, confiscated their weapons, sent slavers against them, and finally sent guards to arrest them. [ch 007 / 007.06]
- After searching the house, Rimardo recommended returning to Akia to obtain access to Lundbeams. [ch 008 / 008.04]
- Akia introduced the party to her boss, Alice, who promised to see about getting them into Lundbeams the next day. [ch 008 / 008.05]
- Akia entered the smithy and whispered news to Alice; Alice then reported Tolub’s announcement that Fax must establish its own empire as a buffer against the Orc and Greyhawk empires. [ch 008 / 008.06]
- After Alice whispered back, Akia reacted with shock and reported that Lundbeams wanted the WLF to help the Army of Fax and refrain from resisting it. [ch 008 / 008.06]

## Last Observed State
Akia was last observed at Halete’s smithy, exchanging whispered information with Alice. She reacted with shock to news that Lundbeams wanted the WLF to support the Army of Fax and refrain from resisting it; her own response to that directive beyond shock is not established. [ch 008 / 008.06]

## Relationships
- **Alice:** Akia’s boss within the WLF; they exchange sensitive news privately. [ch 008 / 008.05; ch 008 / 008.06]
- **The party:** Independent operators whom Akia recruits for WLF objectives and connects with Alice. Her exchanges with Rimardo show friction over payment, obligation, and the risks of their arrangement. [ch 007 / 007.06; ch 008 / 008.05]
- **Lundbeams:** Akia initially refuses the party access, then later relays his directive concerning the Army of Fax with shock. [ch 007 / 007.06; ch 008 / 008.06]
- **Tolub:** Akia regards him as serving the ruling class and the empire. [ch 007 / 007.06]
- **Heironeous:** Akia identifies him as the WLF’s god and cites communion with him as confirmation of the Drow’s identity. [ch 007 / 007.06]
- **The Drow:** Targets of the operation Akia recruits the party to undertake; she attributes slave trafficking to them. [ch 007 / 007.06]
