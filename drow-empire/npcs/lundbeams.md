## Identity
Lundbeams is Tolub’s wife and a powerful cleric of Heironeous. Earlier accounts identify her as Archbishop and Bishop of Fax; she later relinquishes guardianship of the dagger and returns to work as a sheriff. [ch 007 / 007.05; ch 008 / 008.09; ch 052 / 052.02; ch 052 / 052.05; ch 052 / 052.06]

## Personality and Motivations
Lundbeams treats protecting the dagger as justification for manipulation, secrecy, and lethal force. Her admission that the WLF existed as her intelligence network shows the gap between her priorities and her followers’ expectations of liberation. [ch 022 / 022.06; ch 052 / 052.04]

She presents herself as indispensable to preserving peace. Tolub interprets this as an obsession with deciding who may use the dagger’s power; that is his assessment of her motives. [ch 052 / 052.02; ch 052 / 052.04]

Her ruthlessness coexists with attachment to her family: her daughter’s captivity enabled Drow blackmail, and concern for Tolub remains central when she finally gives up guardianship. She can retreat from an extreme threat and accept a successor, despite initially responding to exposure with threats of mass killing. [ch 014 / 014.01; ch 052 / 052.04; ch 052 / 052.05]

## History with the Party
- During the WLF rescue, Rimardo mentioned Lundbeams; the Sergeant identified her as the Archbishop. Akia subsequently refused to take the party to her. [ch 007 / 007.05; ch 007 / 007.06]
- After the raid on the Drow, Alice promised to seek an audience with Lundbeams for the party. Akia later reported that Lundbeams had ordered the WLF to support the Army of Fax. [ch 008 / 008.05; ch 008 / 008.06]
- The party met an older-looking Lundbeams, identified in the proclamation as the Bishop of Fax. She publicly endorsed the Drow alliance, but stumbled over her words; Corrin judged that she appeared forced. [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.09]
- During that audience, Lundbeams described setbacks at Vallaki and an army attacking the Drow outside Fax as disasters for the Drow, and named Fhuu as a source of such an army. The party understood that she wanted the Drow to lose. [ch 008 / 008.09]
- Lundbeams claimed to see Tolub every day in the castle and to reassure the public of his safety. KP handed her a feather before the audience ended. [ch 008 / 008.09]
- In Fhuu, Halete accused Lundbeams of betraying the WLF. The Primus said Lundbeams had promised much and delivered little. Lundbeams did not appear in that chapter. [ch 013 / 013.03; ch 013 / 013.08; ch 013 / entry]
- Questioning Halete under a zone of truth, the party learned that the Drow held Lundbeams’s daughter hostage and were blackmailing her. [ch 014 / 014.01]
- According to Halete, Lundbeams had been ordered to send the party directly to Fhuu, but sent them to Vallaki in hopes they could stop the Drow advance. Nimh recognised her evasion and retaliated by sending the WLF detachment into an unsupported attack that killed most of its members. [ch 014 / 014.01]
- Tolub later told the party that Xhalh held his daughter and that his wife Lundbeams had demanded they serve the Drow and take no action. He described her as an immensely powerful cleric. [ch 021 / 021.05]
- At the Molly Waggler, Lundbeams thanked the party for saving her daughter and husband, and said she had arranged for them to defeat the invasion, secure the portal, and drive out the Drow. [ch 022 / 022.05]
- Lundbeams revealed that she had created the WLF’s precursor to stop seekers of a hidden artifact. She said the WLF’s purpose was to serve as her eyes and ears, rather than deliver the liberation its adherents expected. [ch 022 / 022.06]
- Lundbeams directed the party to return through the portal and stop Lolth’s minions. She claimed Heironeous had not disclosed the artifact’s identity; Rimardo doubted her credibility, and KP concluded she was lying. [ch 022 / 022.06; ch 022 / 022.07]
- Lundbeams appealed to heroism, treasure, and knowledge, then threatened Bayard’s reputation. The party planned to investigate the portal, defeat the forces beyond it, return, and hold her to account; Rimardo explicitly proposed killing her. [ch 022 / 022.06; ch 022 / 022.08]
- Before their departure, Lundbeams directed Bayard to Captain Flipper, offered adamantine armor for 3000 GP, warned that the portal might have reopened, and blamed the party’s fireball for the sacrifice of her troops at Vallaki. She blessed them as they left. [ch 022 / 022.11]
- In the caverns, Rimardo reaffirmed that he wanted enough power to kill Lundbeams. Later, he and Corrin agreed that revenge against her required continuing to work with Bayard. [ch 023 / 023.01; ch 024 / 024.07]
- While bargaining with Siuch, KP invoked Lundbeams as protector of the realms and claimed the party were her emissaries. Siuch was impressed and agreed that she must be avoided. Lundbeams was spoken of during this negotiation. [ch 027 / 027.04]
- Lundbeams was mentioned as protecting an artifact that would give Lolth enough power to become the supreme power. [ch 031 / 031.03]
- Selzyt described Lundbeams as a cleric and a mayor's wife who allied with the Drow; Rimardo said she claimed to protect an artifact and play both sides, but judged her evil. She did not appear. [ch 036 / entry; ch 036 / 036.05]
- Corrin reflected on Lundbeams during the party's debate over questionable alliances. [ch 039 / 039.03]
- The party weighed the consequences of defeating the Drow against the risk to the multi-verse attributed to Lundbeams's warning. [ch 039 / 039.06]
- Inspecting the party's line of credit, the Ranger asked whether Lundbeams held their money; Rimardo nodded, hoping the Rangers could deal with her. [ch 041 / 041.04]
- After escaping Lolth’s Pocket Plane and returning outside Fax, the party identified Lundbeams as its remaining unfinished business, now personal. [ch 051 / 051.05]
- Tolub identified his wife Lundbeams as responsible for the party's contract and described her as a powerful cleric of Heironeous guarding a reality destroying artifact. [ch 052 / 052.02]
- Tolub said Lundbeams protected the dagger and sent people to kill those seeking it; he attributed her obsession to the idea of controlling its power. [ch 052 / 052.02]
- A former member of the WLF accused Lundbeams of betrayal and setting the party up as patsies, and urged them to kill her. [ch 052 / 052.03]
- Lundbeams received the party in the temple with guards, powerful clerics, and a mage, and stopped Rimardo from approaching her. [ch 052 / 052.04]
- Lundbeams knew of the party's deals with Vhaeraun and corrected KP's claim of god slaying, saying they had imprisoned Lolth with a magical trinket. [ch 052 / 052.04]
- Lundbeams authorized payment from the cult's gold, which she said existed to protect the dagger. [ch 052 / 052.04]
- After learning that the dagger's location was widely known, Lundbeams threatened to kill everyone in town, beginning with the party; she stopped when KP told her to calm down. [ch 052 / 052.04]
- Lundbeams withdrew her threat to kill everyone and spoke of loving Tolub and their troubled marriage. [ch 052 / 052.05]
- Lundbeams gave Bayard the dagger and her organization, transferring responsibility for defending it against demonic and angelic powers. [ch 052 / 052.05]
- The temple disappeared when the pact between Heironeous and Lundbeams ended. [ch 052 / 052.05]
- A few years later, Lundbeams and Tolub had resolved their issues; she was a sheriff again and continued making amends. [ch 052 / 052.06]

## Last Observed State
In the epilogue a few years later, Lundbeams is a sheriff again, has resolved her marital difficulties with Tolub, and continues making amends. Fax has become a model of order and safety on the Wild Coast. [ch 052 / 052.06]

She has transferred the dagger and her organization to Bayard. Her pact with Heironeous ended, causing the temple to disappear; the summaries do not establish her remaining clerical powers. [ch 052 / 052.05]

## Relationships
- **Tolub:** Husband and enduring emotional attachment. Their marriage suffered under her guardianship and his feelings of inadequacy; the epilogue establishes reconciliation and his sobriety. [ch 052 / 052.05; ch 052 / 052.06]
- **Their daughter:** The Drow used her captivity to blackmail Lundbeams. Lundbeams credits the party with saving her. [ch 014 / 014.01; ch 022 / 022.05]
- **The WLF:** An organization she used for intelligence, leaving followers who regarded her as a betrayer. [ch 022 / 022.06; ch 052 / 052.03]
- **Rimardo and Corrin:** Their cooperation with her developed into a shared desire for revenge; Tolub’s account later prompted Rimardo to view her with sympathy. [ch 024 / 024.07; ch 052 / 052.02]
- **KP:** Challenges her account and authority; his intervention interrupts her threat against the town. [ch 022 / 022.07; ch 052 / 052.04]
- **Bayard:** Her chosen successor, selected for strength of character and willingness to make morally ambiguous decisions. [ch 052 / 052.05]
- **Heironeous:** Her clerical patron and the other party to the pact sustaining the temple until she relinquished guardianship. [ch 052 / 052.02; ch 052 / 052.05]
