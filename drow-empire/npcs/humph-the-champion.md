## Identity
Humph the Champion is the Lord Mayor of Vallaki, a muscular man about 45 years old, standing 5’8” with greying hair. [ch 010 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Humph shows pragmatic judgment under siege: he aims to make the attack costly enough to force Nimh’s withdrawal and assigns the party to respond to breaches. [ch 010 / 010.04]

He is willing to negotiate surrender and asks for fair treatment for everyone; Nimh responds with threats against him, Vallaki’s ruling body, and his loved ones. [ch 010 / 010.06]

## History with the Party
- Humph is the Lord Mayor of Vallaki. Sergej directed the party to his office. [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.03]
- Humph told the party that the Drow siege served Tolub's expansion and called Tolub a stooge of the Drow. [ch 010 / 010.04]
- Humph assigned the party to act as a swing team against breaches, aiming to inflict enough casualties to force Nimh to withdraw. [ch 010 / 010.04]
- At the western gate, Humph offered to surrender and asked for fair treatment. Nimh threatened to torture and kill Humph, Vallaki's ruling body, and everyone Humph loved. [ch 010 / 010.06]
- When battle began, Humph ordered the party to intercept intruders and ran to make a last stand in the mayor's office. [ch 010 / 010.07]
- The party freed Humph from his mansion and killed Lord Patricio, who had been promised Vallaki in exchange for Humph's head. [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.02]
- Alejandro, Lord Patricio's nephew, had troops assigned to help secure Humph; after discovering his uncle's death, he pursued the party. [ch 011 / 011.04]
- Humph was mentioned when his henchman Captain Amos encountered the party in the forest. Humph's status after the fall of Vallaki is not established in the summaries. [ch 012 / 012.04]

## Last Observed State
Humph’s last confirmed physical appearance is in his mansion during the battle, where the party frees him and kills Lord Patricio. [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.02]

In the forest afterward, Humph is mentioned through his connection to Captain Amos; Humph himself is not shown there. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 012 / 012.04]

## Relationships
- **The party:** Humph entrusts them with responding to breaches; they subsequently rescue him from his mansion. [ch 010 / 010.04; ch 011 / entry]
- **Captain Amos:** Identified as Humph’s henchman and a defender of the gate. [ch 012 / 012.04]
- **Nimh:** Opposing commander who accuses Humph of treason and threatens to torture and kill him, Vallaki’s ruling body, and everyone he loves. [ch 010 / 010.06]
- **Tolub and the Drow:** Humph regards Tolub as a Drow stooge and attributes the siege to their support for Tolub’s expansion. [ch 010 / 010.04]
- **Lord Patricio:** Promised Vallaki in exchange for Humph’s head; killed by the party during Humph’s rescue. [ch 011 / 011.02]
- **Alejandro:** Lord Patricio’s nephew, assigned troops to help secure Humph; pursues the party after discovering his uncle’s death. [ch 011 / 011.04]
