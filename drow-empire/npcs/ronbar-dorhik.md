## Identity
Ronbar Dorhik is a sage and author famous for travels across the planes. His writing examines inter-planar trade as a possible explanation for the emergence of godlike powers. [ch 036 / 036.01]

## Personality and Motivations
His writings establish scholarly interests in planar travel and inter-planar trade; his personal temperament and motivations are not established. [ch 036 / 036.01]

## History with the Party
- Ronbar Dorhik does not appear in either chapter; he is mentioned through KP's recollections of his writing. [ch 036 / entry; ch 037 / entry]
- A sage famous for travels across the planes, Ronbar Dorhik wrote a book KP read while working in the great library. [ch 036 / 036.01]
- His work on inter-planar trade inspired KP's obsession with the nature of trade in the Drow Empire. [ch 036 / 036.01]
- When the party learns its next destination is the Feywild, KP recalls Ronbar Dorhik's book and lectures the party about that realm. [ch 036 / 036.01]
- After entering the Feywild, KP recalls that his mentor, Ronbar Dorhik, wrote that a mortal entering it would be cursed. [ch 037 / 037.03]

## Last Observed State
Ronbar Dorhik is only mentioned in the latest account: KP recalls his written warning that a mortal entering the Feywild would be cursed. He does not appear. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 037 / 037.03; ch 037 / entry]

## Relationships
KP identifies Ronbar Dorhik as his mentor. His writing influenced KP's interest in trade within the Drow Empire, but the evidence does not establish whether their mentorship involved direct personal contact. [ch 037 / entry; ch 036 / 036.01]
