## Identity
Traz was the Drow-installed lizardfolk elder of Sylanth, formerly regarded as Alaneth’s village idiot. [ch 018 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Traz valued the Drow’s patronage because it elevated his standing, and he explicitly tied his loyalty to that promotion. He treated humans as food and responded to an offer to remove the Drow with threats against the party. [ch 018 / 018.02]

His confidence in his authority exceeded his actual support: he ordered the surrounding lizardfolk to kill the adventurers, but none obeyed. [ch 018 / 018.02]

## History with the Party
- Before meeting Traz, the party heard that the Drow had installed him as Sylanth's village leader. [ch 017 / 017.04]
- Traz asked whether the adventurers were food and told them that humans were food for lizardfolk. [ch 018 / 018.02]
- When Bayard offered to remove the Drow, Traz declared that he liked them because they had made him village elder after he had been Alaneth's village idiot. [ch 018 / 018.02]
- Traz ordered the guards to kill the party; none came to his aid. [ch 018 / 018.02]
- After Bayard said “he could be food,” Mr. Corrin attacked and killed Traz before he could respond. [ch 018 / 018.02]
- Following Traz's death, Rimardo and Bayard convinced the lizardfolk to join an assault on Alaneth. [ch 018 / 018.02]

## Last Observed State
Dead in Sylanth, killed by Mr. Corrin before he could respond to the attack. The villagers subsequently proposed telling the Drow that Traz had died and been eaten; the summary establishes this as a proposed explanation, not an observed disposal of his body. [ch 018 / 018.02]

## Relationships
- **The Drow:** His patrons and the source of his position; Traz professed affection and obedience toward them. [ch 018 / 018.02]
- **Sylanth’s lizardfolk:** His nominal subjects, who refused his command to attack the party and joined the proposed assault on Alaneth after his death. [ch 018 / 018.02]
- **Bayard:** Offered to remove the Drow, provoking Traz’s declaration of loyalty. Bayard’s subsequent suggestion that Traz could be food preceded Mr. Corrin’s lethal attack. [ch 018 / 018.02]
- **Rimardo and Mr. Corrin:** Rimardo suggested Mr. Corrin could deal with Traz as an obstacle to the villagers’ cooperation; Mr. Corrin killed him. [ch 018 / 018.02]
