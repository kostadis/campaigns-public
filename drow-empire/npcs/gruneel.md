## Identity
Gruneel is a native of Fax and a former bookkeeper for his uncle’s merchant’s firm. He is skilled with a bow and uses arcane arrows; he also worked for the WLF. [ch 023 / 023.06; ch 018 / 018.01; ch 018 / entry; ch 020 / 020.04]

## Personality and Motivations
Gruneel wants renown, but his appetite for adventure competes with his fear of danger. He resents being confined to guarding exits, yet repeatedly chooses that role when combat or negotiation seems too perilous. [ch 025 / 025.02; ch 024 / 024.01; ch 026 / 026.01]

Concern for his companions can overcome his caution: he argued against waiting when friends might need help and charged when Bayard was surrounded. [ch 021 / 021.03; ch 025 / 025.02]

He also contributes practical questions and observations, including how to prevent the portal from reaching their plane, the danger posed by flying dragons, and the usefulness of a city map. [ch 025 / 025.06; ch 026 / entry; ch 026 / 026.02]

## Last Observed State
The epilogue places Gruneel in Alaneth as a member of the lizardfolk tribe. After the party forgot him guarding a door in Plagueridge Rampart, he secured a magic user’s help to plane shift to the Wasted Isle and returned to Alaneth. Adoption into a particular local clan remains a possibility rather than a confirmed outcome. [ch 052 / 052.06]

- **The lizardfolk of Alaneth:** Gruneel found common ground with them and became a member of their tribe. [ch 052 / 052.06]
