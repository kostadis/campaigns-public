## Identity
Asmodeus is described as the Master of Commerce, an archfiend renowned across the multi-verse for commerce and dealmaking. [ch 024 / 024.06]

## Personality and Motivations
The account portrays him as patient and methodical: he built a deep legal department over time to make bargains favour the Devil. This supports an emphasis on lasting contractual advantage; no direct speech from him is supplied. [ch 024 / 024.06]

## History with the Party
- Asmodeus does not appear in chapter 024; he is described as the Master of Commerce, whose legal department made every devil a deep expert in markets. [ch 024 / entry]
- Ziamar's majordomo mentions Asmodeus while explaining how captains receive territory, presenting territorial gains in the endless blood war as the official account. [ch 041 / 041.03]

## Last Observed State
Asmodeus was mentioned by Ziamar's majordomo in an official explanation of how captains receive territory seized in the endless blood war; he was not shown acting or speaking. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 041 / 041.03]
