## Identity
Siuch is a quasit and servant of Freedahl. He can take rat form and speaks in a squeak. He acts as Freedahl’s intermediary in negotiations with the party. [ch 027 / entry; ch 027 / 027.04]

## Personality and Motivations
Siuch flatters Freedahl endlessly but is easily panicked and paranoid about spies. Fear of Freedahl’s eternal dungeons makes him vulnerable to bargaining pressure. Frustration produces stamping, spinning, and childlike jumping. [ch 027 / entry; ch 027 / 027.04]

He seeks to advance Freedahl’s control of the city while keeping his master from openly attacking the Drow. His admiration for the Drow’s control through food dependency shows approval of coercion; his attempted soul clauses show a willingness to exploit negotiations. [ch 027 / 027.03; ch 027 / 027.04]

## History with the Party
- Siuch, a quasit serving Freedahl, followed the party as a rat, then transformed and introduced himself to the Paladin. [ch 027 / 027.03]
- Siuch threatened to tell the Drow the party was there; Rimardo dismissed the threat because the Drow already knew. [ch 027 / 027.03]
- Siuch described the city's inhabitants as a protective meat shield around the portal and said the Drow controlled them through goodberries and false promises of freedom. [ch 027 / 027.03]
- Siuch proposed that the party remove the Drow from power so Freedahl could take possession of the city without openly attacking them himself. [ch 027 / 027.04]
- Siuch initially offered one day without an invasion through the portal; KP and Rimardo bargained him up to 10,000 years, invoking Lundbeams and suggesting a competing offer associated with Demogorgon. [ch 027 / 027.04]
- Siuch attempted to add clauses concerning ownership of the group's soul; KP rejected them and challenged Siuch's legal authority under Drow jurisdiction. [ch 027 / 027.04]
- Bayard signed an agreement supporting Freedahl's conquest of the Drow city in exchange for Freedahl not invading Bayard's plane for 10,000 years. [ch 027 / 027.04]
- Siuch gave the party a small whistle to signal Freedahl after the tower was captured, transformed into a rat, and disappeared. [ch 027 / 027.04]

## Last Observed State
Siuch’s last observed action was to give the party a whistle for signalling Freedahl after the tower’s capture, transform into a rat, and disappear. His subsequent whereabouts are not established in the summaries. [ch 027 / 027.04]

## Relationships
- **Freedahl:** Siuch serves and praises him, bargains on his behalf, and fears punishment in his dungeons. [ch 027 / entry; ch 027 / 027.04]
- **KP:** A negotiating opponent who exploits Siuch’s fears of Lundbeams and a competing offer associated with Demogorgon, then rejects his soul clauses and challenges his legal authority. [ch 027 / 027.04]
- **Rimardo:** Dismisses Siuch’s threat to inform the Drow and presses him for better terms. [ch 027 / 027.03; ch 027 / 027.04]
- **Bayard:** Signs the agreement with Freedahl that Siuch negotiates; the evidence establishes a transactional connection. [ch 027 / 027.04]
- **Lundbeams and Demogorgon:** Invoked during bargaining as threats to Freedahl’s interests. Siuch reacts with alarm; no direct relationship with either is established. [ch 027 / 027.04]
