## Identity
Amos, also called Captain Amos, is Humph's henchman and a defender of the gate. [ch 012 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Amos is driven by outrage over the slaughter of civilians and the WLF's perceived betrayal. He seeks an alliance led by Primus Yagrat against the City of Fax. [ch 012 / 012.04]

He treats revenge against Halete as a right and reacts angrily to restraint. His extreme prejudice against Humanoids conflicts with his insistence that the party seek help from Fhuu's orcs. [ch 013 / 013.03; ch 013 / 013.04; ch 012 / 012.04]

## History with the Party
- Captain Amos is Humph's henchman and a defender of the gate; he survived the slaughter because the enemy thought him dead. [ch 012 / entry]
- Amos encountered the party in the woods and reported that the Drow and Faxian troops had slaughtered civilians; he condemned the WLF's alliance with the Faxian armies as betrayal. [ch 012 / 012.04]
- Amos urged an alliance against the City of Fax led by Primus Yagrat and led the party towards Fhuu. [ch 012 / entry; ch 012 / 012.04]
- Near Fhuu's gates, Amos accused Halete of betrayal and charged her. Bayard's command spell failed to stop him, so Bayard grappled him and insisted on a trial. [ch 013 / 013.03]
- Amos expressed extreme prejudice against Humanoids while arguing with Halete, provoking Guttja's rebuke; Rimardo calmed the confrontation. [ch 013 / 013.04]
- When Guttja threatened the party and they fled, Amos was left behind and was not rescued. His subsequent status is not established in the summaries. [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.04]

## Last Observed State
Amos was last observed in Fhuu during the confrontation with Centurion Guttja. When Guttja threatened the party and they fled, Amos was left behind and was not rescued. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 013 / 013.04; ch 013 / entry]

## Relationships
- **Humph:** Amos serves as his henchman. [ch 012 / entry]
- **Halete:** Amos regards her as a traitor and seeks revenge. Halete disputes his accusation, saying the Drow and Lundbeams betrayed her group. [ch 013 / 013.03]
- **Bayard:** Accepts Amos as a guide to Fhuu, but restrains his attack on Halete and insists on a trial. [ch 012 / 012.04; ch 013 / 013.03]
- **Primus Yagrat:** Amos considers him the necessary leader of an alliance against the City of Fax. [ch 012 / 012.04]
- **Centurion Guttja:** Rebukes Amos's prejudice against Humanoids before threatening the party. [ch 013 / 013.04]
- **Rimardo:** Calms the confrontation when Amos nearly escalates his exchange with Guttja. [ch 013 / 013.04]
