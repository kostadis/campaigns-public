## Identity
Reepicheep is a mouse and an adventuring companion of Triton, Dimitrov, and Boradoon, later joined by Bayard. He uses Tasha’s Laughter and fights in close combat. [ch 003 / entry; ch 001 / 001.01; ch 004 / 004.04; ch 002 / 002.03; ch 003 / 003.04]

## Personality and Motivations
He firmly objects to being called a rat. His humor includes taunting enemies and joking at Bayard’s expense during danger. [ch 003 / 003.04; ch 003 / 003.05]

He shows practical caution: he searches for another cave entrance and objects to attacking warriors who are already prepared. His offer to help row Alexia Boulderdown’s boat also shows a willingness to assist. [ch 003 / 003.03; ch 001 / 001.02]

## History with the Party
- After the party is expelled from the Inn at the River, Reepicheep accompanies Triton, Dimitrov, and Boradoon to the Tlerowth River docks. [ch 001 / 001.01]
- Reepicheep asks for an oar aboard Alexia Boulderdown’s boat; she takes offense at his offer. [ch 001 / 001.02]
- During the aquatic zombie attack, Reepicheep is grappled and escapes. [ch 001 / 001.03]
- Reepicheep casts Tasha’s Laughter on Ralnor, who reveals that he works for Realm leader Xhalh and captures slaves for the Drow Empire. [ch 002 / 002.03]
- Reepicheep is a mouse who objects to being called a rat. [ch 003 / entry]
- Reepicheep finds no alternative entrance to the cave and objects to entering a room of prepared warriors; the party watches outside overnight. [ch 003 / 003.03]
- Reepicheep kills warriors during the morning assault and insists he is a mouse when called a rat. [ch 003 / 003.04]
- Reepicheep jokes as Bayard washes off green slime in the pool. [ch 003 / 003.05]
- Surrounded by skeletons, Reepicheep repeatedly misses before flanking and killing the last skeleton. [ch 003 / 003.06]
- A Drow Initiate’s magic missile knocks Reepicheep unconscious; he subsequently bleeds out. [ch 003 / 003.08]
- Bayard and Dimitrov carry their fallen comrades toward Farjvad; Dimitrov performs last rites for Boradoon and Reepicheep in a clearing. [ch 003 / 003.09]
- Reepicheep is mentioned when Bayard explains to Carc Mistsplitter how he joined the earlier party. [ch 004 / 004.04]
- In Fax, the party sells “the instrument of the bards” to Xenos; the summary says Reepicheep “would have found useful, but he is no more.” [ch 009 / 009.01]

## Last Observed State
Dead: a Drow Initiate’s magic missile knocks him unconscious, and he subsequently bleeds out. Dimitrov performs his last rites in a clearing on the return toward Farjvad. [ch 003 / 003.08; ch 003 / 003.09]

His latest mention occurs during the sale of the instrument of the bards in Fax; the summary explicitly describes him as absent and “no more.” This is a remembrance, not a physical appearance. [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.01]
