## Identity
Molril was a Drow leader served by ravens and falcons, with a keep whose tower housed ogres. [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.07]

## Personality and Motivations
Molril judged others in terms of honor, condemning KP for helping the Giants against the gnomes. He treated the party’s unexpected allegiance as a possible sign of developments in the city. [ch 039 / 039.07]

## History with the Party
- Molril was the Drow leader; ravens and falcons served him. [ch 039 / entry]
- A lead gnome told the party that Molril had said they attacked the outpost and killed the Drow commander; KP confirmed the accusation. [ch 039 / 039.05]
- Molril's gnome allies attacked the party while mounted on dogs. [ch 039 / 039.05]
- Rimardo cast earthquake at Molril's keep; Molril emerged with ogres and surrounding falcons as the tower collapsed. [ch 039 / 039.07]
- Molril expressed surprise that the party helped the Giants against the gnomes and judged KP “without honor like the Drow.” KP was silenced. [ch 039 / 039.07]
- The gnomes reported that Molril periodically took their power-enabling gem into the abyss and returned with it glowing. [ch 040 / 040.03]
- Molril died in the fight at his tower in Chapter 040. [GM]

## Last Observed State
Molril died in the fight at his tower in Chapter 040. [GM] His tower was destroyed. [ch 040 / 040.01]

Afterward, the gnomes spoke of his former role in replenishing their power-enabling gem: he periodically took it into the abyss and returned with it glowing. This was a report about Molril, not a subsequent appearance. [ch 040 / 040.03]

## Relationships
- **The gnomes:** His allies depended on a gem he periodically brought back glowing from the abyss; they credited that gem with enabling their powers. [ch 039 / 039.05; ch 040 / 040.03]
- **KP:** Molril condemned KP’s honor over his support for the Giants against the gnomes; KP was silenced by the rebuke. [ch 039 / 039.07]
- **Rimardo and the party:** Molril confronted the party after Rimardo’s earthquake collapsed his tower. [ch 039 / 039.07]
- **Animal servants and ogres:** Ravens and falcons served him; ogres emerged alongside him from the collapsing tower. [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.07]

## Notable Quotes
> "how curious you decided to help the Giants against the gnomes. This is an unexpected outcome. Something must be going on in the city. As for you, gnome, it would appear you are without honor like the Drow." — Molril [ch 039 / 039.07]
