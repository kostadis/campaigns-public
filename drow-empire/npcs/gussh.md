## Identity
Gussh is a lizardfolk shaman formerly of Alaneth, associated with the refuge at Telsn. An injured lizardfolk identified him as a religious opponent of S'kazs's allegiance to the Drow, acting on a warning from his deity. [ch 017 / 017.04]

## Personality and Motivations
Religious loyalty shapes Gussh's judgments: he resented the returning exiles for betraying the true God of the lizardfolk, though Bayard's mediation secured his cooperation. [ch 018 / 018.04]

He is vengeful toward Drow supporters and confident in the lizardfolk's ability to resist further incursions. His delight at victory accompanied revenge killings, including an execution he ordered during conversation with the party. [ch 020 / 020.02]

## History with the Party
- An injured lizardfolk told the party that Gussh, the village shaman, had opposed S'kazs's allegiance to the Drow after a warning from his deity and escaped with about 200 supporters. [ch 017 / 017.04]
- At Telsn, Gussh resented the returning exiles for betraying the true God of the lizardfolk; Bayard mediated and persuaded them to raid the Drow. [ch 018 / 018.04]
- Gussh provided a rough sketch of Alaneth to help plan the attack. [ch 018 / 018.04]
- Gussh advised that killing S'kazs would make the lizardfolk turn on the Drow or stop fighting. [ch 018 / 018.04]
- Gussh's forces fought in Alaneth while the party advanced toward the Drow tower. [ch 018 / 018.05]
- After S'kazs was killed, Bayard remembered Gussh's advice and placed the body on the tower steps as lizardfolk approached. [ch 019 / 019.02]
- The party met Gussh outside the tower after defeating the Drow; he was delighted and presided over revenge killings, ordering another killing during their conversation. [ch 020 / entry; ch 020 / 020.02]
- Gussh proposed that the lizardfolk guard the portal and overwhelm incoming Drow forces after it closed behind them. [ch 020 / 020.02]
- Gussh explained that an artifact in the Abyss opened the portal at random intervals, no sooner than once a month, and that it remained open only briefly. [ch 020 / 020.02]
- Gussh received a sending stone so the party could stay in contact. [ch 020 / 020.02]
- On the party's return, a lizardfolk blamed them for persuading Gussh to attack S’kazs and secure the portal, reporting that Drow had returned with demons and dragons and destroyed the village. Gussh was mentioned in this encounter; his presence was not established. [ch 022 / 022.12]
- Gussh's status after the reported destruction: Status not established in the summaries. [ch 022 / 022.12]

## Last Observed State
Gussh was last directly encountered outside the Drow tower after the party's victory. He proposed guarding the portal and received a sending stone for contact with the party. [ch 020 / 020.02]

In the later encounter at the ruined village, a lizardfolk mentioned Gussh while reporting that Drow had returned with demons and dragons and destroyed the village. Gussh's presence was not established. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 022 / 022.12]

## Relationships
- **Bayard and the party:** Allies against the Drow. Bayard mediated Gussh's dispute with the exiles; Gussh supplied local knowledge and tactical advice. Corrin and KP distrusted his proposed portal defense. [ch 018 / 018.04; ch 020 / 020.02]
- **Telsn's supporters and the Sylanth exiles:** Gussh fled with about 200 supporters. His initial resentment toward the returning exiles gave way to cooperation in the joint raid. [ch 017 / 017.04; ch 018 / 018.04]
- **S'kazs:** Opposed over allegiance to the Drow. Gussh identified S'kazs's death as the means to break the opposing lizardfolk's resistance. [ch 017 / 017.04; ch 018 / 018.04]
- **The Drow and their supporters:** Enemies whom Gussh sought to exclude from the realm; he endorsed lethal reprisals against their supporters. [ch 020 / 020.02]
- **His deity:** The reported source of the warning behind his opposition to Drow allegiance; the deity's name is not supplied. [ch 017 / 017.04]
