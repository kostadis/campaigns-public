## Identity
S’kazs is the lizardfolk ruler called “the Lizard King.” He speaks bad Elvish. [ch 017 / 017.03; ch 019 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
An injured lizardfolk described his allegiance to the Drow as an opportunity to expand lizardfolk dominion. The same account identifies a religious restraint: he was uncomfortable killing a shaman of the lizardfolk gods and concealed Gussh’s escape from the Drow. [ch 017 / 017.04]

In combat, he used hellish rebuke, blinding spittle and head butts, and attacked Corrin while Corrin was incapacitated. [ch 019 / 019.02]

## History with the Party
- Captain Flipper described King S'kazs as his former slave-trading partner and said the king had stopped responding to business outreaches. [ch 017 / 017.03]
- An injured lizardfolk told the party that S'kazs swore fealty to the Drow after they emerged following an earthquake, seeing an opportunity to expand lizardfolk dominion. [ch 017 / 017.04]
- The same lizardfolk said S'kazs concealed Gussh's escape with about 200 supporters by telling the Drow that the defeated followers had been eaten; he was uncomfortable killing a shaman of the lizardfolk gods. [ch 017 / 017.04]
- The party decided to raise an army from the alienated lizardfolk after discussing an alliance against S'kazs. [ch 017 / 017.04]
- Gussh told the party that killing S'kazs would make the lizardfolk turn on the Drow or stop fighting. [ch 018 / 018.04]
- Corrin entered S'kazs's room unnoticed. S'kazs spoke in bad Elvish about sensing an unseen intruder, and a voice from deeper in the room reminded him he had been paid to protect its speakers. [ch 019 / 019.02]
- S'kazs fought the party with hellish rebuke, blinding spittle, and head butts; he blinded Rimardo, knocked Corrin unconscious, and struck Corrin while he was incapacitated. [ch 019 / 019.02]
- Corrin, Rimardo, KP and Bayard killed S'kazs. His death is explicitly established in chapter 019. [ch 019 / 019.02]
- With lizardfolk approaching, Bayard placed S'kazs's body on the tower steps, recalling Gussh's prediction; Bayard described the placement as allowing burial rites. [ch 019 / 019.02]
- On the party's return, a lizardfolk blamed them for convincing Gussh to attack S’kazs and reported a destroyed village, slaughtered friends and family, and Drow returning through the portal with demons and dragons. [ch 022 / 022.12]

## Last Observed State
Explicitly dead in chapter 019, killed by Corrin, Rimardo, KP and Bayard. His body was last observed on the tower steps, where Bayard placed it as lizardfolk approached; Bayard described this as allowing burial rites. [ch 019 / 019.02]

In chapter 022, S’kazs was mentioned by a lizardfolk blaming the party for encouraging Gussh’s attack and reporting the subsequent destruction of the village and the Drow’s return. This was a retrospective mention, not a new appearance. [ch 022 / 022.12]

## Relationships
- **Drow:** His overlords by sworn fealty, according to the injured lizardfolk. During the confrontation, a voice deeper in his room said he had been paid to protect its speakers. [ch 017 / 017.04; ch 019 / 019.02]
- **Gussh:** A shaman who opposed his allegiance to the Drow. S’kazs concealed Gussh’s escape; Gussh later advocated killing him to break lizardfolk participation in the Drow’s defense. [ch 017 / 017.04; ch 018 / 018.04]
- **Captain Flipper:** A former slave-trading partner who reported that S’kazs had stopped responding to business outreaches. [ch 017 / 017.03]
- **The party:** Armed opponents who targeted him as a means of disrupting Drow control and killed him in his room. [ch 018 / 018.04; ch 019 / 019.02]
