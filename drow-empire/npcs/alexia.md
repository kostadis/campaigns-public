## Identity
Alexia Boulderdown is a boatwoman working the late shift for the River Drink in Farjvad. She has a long silver mohawk and habitually squints when speaking. [ch 001 / entry; ch 006 / 006.02]

## Personality and Motivations
Alexia is blunt and protective of her competence, interpreting an offer to help row as a patriarchal slight. She readily blames the Major for local problems. [ch 001 / 001.02]

Her chief motivation is survival. She fears that outside intervention will destroy the town and later focuses on how the Drow arrived, what they intend, and the danger of asking those questions. [ch 004 / 004.08; ch 006 / 006.02]

Her concern for outsiders is uneven: she exposes the kidnappings but also speaks approvingly of the necromancer because he spared locals. She helps Bayard find companions while showing impatience when he asks her to solve the town’s predicament. [ch 001 / 001.04; ch 004 / 004.03; ch 004 / 004.01; ch 004 / 004.04]

## History with the Party
- Alexia Boulderdown met the party on the Tlerowth River docks and ferried them toward the River Drink, where she worked the late shift. [ch 001 / 001.01; ch 001 / 001.02]
- She took offence when Reepicheep offered to take an oar, blaming the patriarchy and the Major. [ch 001 / 001.02]
- The four aquatic zombies attacking the boat ignored Alexia; the party destroyed them. [ch 001 / 001.03]
- At the south docks, Alexia revealed that many travelers had been kidnapped, exposing a secret the Major wanted kept quiet. [ch 001 / 001.04]
- Alexia greeted the survivors at the River Drink, dismissed the Drow as frightening tales, and warned that talk of another power could bring war. [ch 003 / 003.10]
- After Dimitrov left, she offered to introduce Bayard to other adventurers. [ch 003 / 003.10]
- She introduced Bayard to Rimardo, Corrin, and Knazreponnium Ketternoppapux. [ch 004 / 004.01]
- Alexia explained that zombies kidnapped outsiders for a Drow necromancer’s experiments at the Temple of Mort, and that the Major had made the boat people agree to silence. [ch 004 / 004.03]
- When Bayard sought advice about the town’s predicament, Alexia told him to figure it out on his own. [ch 004 / 004.04]
- She later warned the party that an Orc Empire army would destroy the town and urged them to resolve the crisis immediately. [ch 004 / 004.08]
- Alexia reminded the party that Dimitrov had identified Xhalh as another Drow in charge whom they had not found, and identified Fax as an obvious target. [ch 006 / 006.02]
- She recommended river travel to Fax; the party followed her advice and completed the journey uneventfully. [ch 006 / 006.02; ch 006 / 006.04]
- During the WLF intervention, Alexia’s warning about the dangerous overland route was recalled as the party recognized Carc’s betrayal. [ch 007 / 007.05]

## Last Observed State
Her last direct appearance is in Farjvad, advising the party about the unresolved threat from Xhalh and recommending river travel to Fax. [ch 006 / 006.02]

In the later WLF intervention, Alexia is only spoken of: the party recalls her warning about the dangerous road while recognizing Carc’s betrayal. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 007 / 007.05]

## Relationships
- **Boris Boulderdown:** Her uncle; his persistence prompts her disclosure of the kidnappings. [ch 001 / 001.04]
- **Bayard and the party:** A local contact who provides introductions, information, and travel advice, though she expects Bayard to exercise his own judgment. [ch 004 / 004.01; ch 004 / 004.04; ch 006 / 006.02]
- **Carc Mistsplitter:** She resents his handling of the kidnappings and breaks the silence he imposed on the boat people. Her later travel advice directly contradicts his. [ch 001 / 001.03; ch 004 / 004.03; ch 006 / 006.02]
- **Dimitrov:** A source of information about Xhalh, whose unresolved role she brings back to the party’s attention. [ch 006 / 006.02]
