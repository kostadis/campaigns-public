## Identity
Freedahl is a demon lord who took control of Plagueridge Rampart and renamed it Flaka. [ch 045 / entry; ch 031 / 031.04; ch 043 / 043.01] Selzyt identified Graz’zt as his liege lord and Demogorgon as his former master. [ch 026 / 026.02; ch 027 / 027.05]

## Personality and Motivations
Freedahl asserts authority through strength and reacts angrily when challenged. He accepts conquest as grounds for ownership. [ch 031 / 031.04; ch 043 / moment]

His priorities are possession of the city, protection from Drow retaliation, and his own survival. He initially sought to conceal his role in the conquest and later made clear that he would abandon the city rather than die defending it. [ch 027 / 027.04; ch 032 / 032.01; ch 044 / 044.03]

He uses bargains coercively: threatening to withdraw protection from the party’s plane, offering their location to the Drow, and demanding the general’s entire treasure as payment for teleportation. [ch 044 / 044.03; ch 045 / 045.01]

## Relationships
- **The party:** Contractual allies whom Freedahl pressures to secure his city against the Drow. Bayard signed the original agreement, while KP resisted clauses concerning their souls. The original protection term was 10,000 years; Freedahl later asserted a term of 100 years. [ch 027 / 027.04; ch 045 / 045.01]
- **Graz’zt and Demogorgon:** According to Selzyt, Freedahl served Demogorgon previously and was under urgent pressure from Graz’zt to obtain the city. [ch 027 / 027.05]
- **Siuch and the majordomo:** Siuch negotiated for Freedahl; the later majordomo conveyed his conditions and coordinated assistance with the party. [ch 027 / 027.04; ch 044 / 044.03; ch 044 / 044.05]
- **Ziamar:** His son and a subsequent overseer of the city. Freedahl denounced him as traitorous after the party killed him and declined to execute the party for it. [ch 040 / 040.06; ch 043 / 043.01]
- **Finaeli Talraidal:** A steward whom Freedahl privately regarded as a useful scapegoat. Their arrangement ended with Finaeli dead and his head displayed on a pike. [ch 031 / 031.07; ch 040 / 040.06]
- **The Drow:** Military enemies and potential negotiating partners. Freedahl sought an accommodation preserving his ownership of Flaka, even at the party’s expense. [ch 044 / 044.03]
- **Valnozz:** Warned the party against trusting Freedahl and against expecting his demons to sacrifice themselves for the city. [ch 031 / 031.03; ch 044 / 044.06]
