## Identity
Tolub is the Lord Mayor and Captain of Fax, a former river pirate, Lundbeams’ husband, and the father of their daughter. [ch 052 / entry; ch 021 / 021.05]

## Personality and Motivations
Protecting his daughter motivates his resistance to Xhalh: he seeks help against her captor but opposes measures that could destroy clues to her location. [ch 021 / 021.05; ch 021 / 021.06]

Tolub describes himself as intimidated by Lundbeams while remaining attached to her. His distress over the dagger centers on her obsession with controlling its power and his desire to recover their earlier partnership in enforcing order. [ch 052 / 052.02] Lundbeams attributes his feelings of inadequacy to her religious advancement and his inability to protect their daughter; this is her account of his emotional state. [ch 052 / 052.05]

His position on raiding is disputed within the summaries: his guards describe raids planned under his authority, while Tolub says he has tried to discourage the locals’ reliance on them. [ch 052 / 052.01; ch 052 / 052.02]

## Last Observed State
The epilogue reports that, a few years later, Tolub is sober, has resolved his issues with Lundbeams, and advocates for the rights of women. Lundbeams is again a sheriff, and Fax has become a model of order and safety on the Wild Coast. This is a reported later outcome rather than a scene showing Tolub acting. [ch 052 / 052.06]

## Relationships
- **Lundbeams:** His wife and former partner in bringing order to towns. Despite his fear of her and their conflict over the dagger, she expresses love for him and determination to protect him; their later reconciliation is explicitly reported. [ch 052 / 052.02; ch 052 / 052.04; ch 052 / 052.06]
- **Their daughter:** Her captivity gives Xhalh leverage over him. The party later identifies itself to his guards as the contractors who saved her. [ch 021 / 021.05; ch 052 / 052.01]
- **The party:** Tolub treats them as potential rescuers and intermediaries with Lundbeams, but denies responsibility for her contract. Rimardo agrees to speak with her; KP later calls Tolub a criminal and raises dealing with him. [ch 021 / 021.05; ch 052 / 052.02; ch 052 / 052.05]
- **Xhalh and the Drow:** Tolub describes coerced service through his daughter’s captivity, then seeks Xhalh’s defeat and mobilizes his troops against the Drow commanders. [ch 021 / 021.05; ch 022 / 022.03]
- **Flipper:** Speaks favourably of their past work together and regards Tolub as good for business. [ch 022 / 022.11]
