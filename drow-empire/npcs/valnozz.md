## Identity
Valnozz is a Drow spy and follower of Vhaeraun who works against Lolth and her clerics. He styles himself the master spy, also known as the big finger. [ch 016 / entry; ch 044 / 044.06; ch 046 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Dry, contemptuous of naive questions, and resistant to familiarity, Valnozz treats cooperation as a convergence of interests rather than friendship. He explicitly distinguishes his long-term plans from the party’s. [ch 046 / entry; ch 031 / 031.01]

His stated aims include undermining Lolth’s clerics and securing the Empress’s death. He allows the party considerable latitude over methods and political alliances, but specifically opposes trusting House Do’ty because it seeks to purge Vhaeraun’s followers. [ch 016 / entry; ch 046 / 046.02]

## History with the Party
- Valnozz approached the party near Vallaki as a follower of Vhaeraun opposed to Lolth's clerics and the Great Mistress. [ch 016 / 016.04]
- He described the Drow Empire's Abyssal base, identified Xhalh as the invasion's overseer, and explained the planar portal's circuit through Plagueridge Rampart. [ch 016 / 016.04]
- He refused to lead the party to Xhalh and instructed them to close the portal before attacking Fax and obtain the portal key from her. [ch 016 / 016.04]
- He returned while the party counted treasure, promising to explain the Drow Empire's true nature. [ch 030 / 030.03]
- He ordered the Drow army to retreat at the second battle of Vallaki, allowing the forces of Primus and Sir Ryton to withdraw safely, as recalled by Rimardo. [ch 031 / 031.01]
- He rejected Rimardo's description of him as a friend, stressing that their intentions and long-term plans differed. [ch 031 / 031.01]
- He congratulated the party on securing the failsafe and said he had trained Selzyt, identifying both of them as members of Vhaeraun's cult. [ch 031 / 031.01]
- He presented the followers of Vhaeraun's account of Drow history, Lolth's control of Drow society, and the conflict between their cults. [ch 031 / 031.02]
- He warned the party against trusting Freedahl and said they would be overrun unless they secured the other sides of the portal. [ch 031 / 031.03]
- He urged them to secure Plagueridge Rampart and replace the ruling house with a noble house, presenting House Illal as a possible replacement. [ch 031 / 031.03]
- Before disappearing, he warned of a slave revolt that would be crushed and an ally in Marco who would die; these were warnings, not confirmed outcomes in that scene. [ch 031 / 031.03]
- Bayard later recalled Valnozz's explanation when considering an alliance with Finaeli Talraidal and the followers of Vhaeraun. [ch 031 / 031.07]
- Valnozz returned during the siege, reporting that House Zollazo needed to kill the party and that its alliance with House Illal had shattered. [ch 044 / 044.06]
- He mapped the route toward the Drow general's headquarters, identified a communication device held by the trebuchet mercenaries, and advised killing the general to make the mercenaries abandon the battle. [ch 044 / 044.06]
- He warned that Freedahl's demons would abandon the city as non-demon casualties mounted and arranged a subsequent meeting at the Gilded Dryad to explain access to Maenerius and its noble houses. [ch 044 / 044.06]
- During the party's siege deliberations, the majordomo repeated Valnozz's advice that killing the general would collapse the Drow forces; Valnozz was spoken of in this scene. [ch 045 / 045.01]
- Valnozz subsequently supplied a map toward Maenerius and an entry permit signed by the Empress, explaining that the city could only be entered through its gates. [ch 046 / 046.02]
- He said his desired outcome was the Empress's death and warned that the noble houses viewed the party as both a threat and a weapon. [ch 046 / 046.02]
- He specifically warned against House Do’ty, saying it wished to purge Vhaeraun's followers; Rimardo remembered Valnozz's allegiance when questioning that house's representatives. [ch 046 / 046.02; ch 047 / 047.03]
- In the dungeon revelation, Vhaeraun invoked Valnozz's timely appearances by asking whether the party thought they were coincidental; Valnozz was mentioned rather than shown acting there. [ch 048 / 048.07]

## Last Observed State
His last direct appearance is the briefing before the party’s journey into Maenerius, where he supplies access documents and advice on the noble houses. His location afterward is not established. [ch 046 / 046.02]

He is subsequently mentioned during the House Do’ty negotiations and Vhaeraun’s dungeon revelation; neither scene shows him present. Vhaeraun invokes the timing of his appearances as evidence that they were no coincidence. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 047 / 047.03; ch 048 / 048.07]

## Relationships
- **The party:** Useful collaborators with differing long-term aims. Rimardo’s attempt to call him a friend meets explicit rejection. [ch 031 / 031.01]
- **Vhaeraun:** His professed deity and factional allegiance. Vhaeraun later links Valnozz’s timely interventions to his own explanation of events. [ch 044 / 044.06; ch 048 / 048.07]
- **Selzyt:** Valnozz claims to have trained him and identifies them both as cult members; the later revelation identifies Selzyt as Vhaeraun. [ch 031 / 031.01; ch 048 / 048.07]
- **Freedahl:** An object of explicit distrust; Valnozz warns the party against relying on him or his demons’ commitment to defending the city. [ch 031 / 031.03; ch 044 / 044.06]
- **The noble houses:** Potential instruments for replacing the ruling house. Valnozz initially proposes House Illal, while identifying House Do’ty as hostile to his faith. [ch 031 / 031.03; ch 046 / 046.02]
