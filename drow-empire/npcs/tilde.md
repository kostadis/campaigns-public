## Identity
Tilde is a merchant and independent slaver operating in Fax. [ch 007 / entry; ch 007 / 007.08]

## Personality and Motivations
Tilde hesitates before admitting his trade, but responds indignantly when challenged about slavery, invoking Fax’s large slave market as justification. His complaint about restrictions on independent operators shows concern for his ability to conduct business. [ch 007 / 007.08]

He attributes his captivity to commercial rivals, describing his captors as dark-skinned Elves pretending to be Drow and blaming the kingdom of Celene. These are his accusations. [ch 007 / 007.08]

## History with the Party
- Corrin killed the cellar’s Drow guard and freed Tilde, a merchant, and his two guards. [ch 007 / 007.08]
- Tilde said he had been captured in the city. [ch 007 / 007.08]
- Tilde described his captors as dark-skinned Elves pretending to be Drow and blamed the kingdom of Celene. [ch 007 / 007.08]
- When KP asked about his wares, Tilde admitted selling slaves and said independent slave operators were being barred from business. [ch 007 / 007.08]
- After Tilde’s bodyguards left, Rimardo told him, in disgust, “You can go too.” [ch 007 / 007.08]

## Last Observed State
Freed from the cellar, Tilde is last shown being told by Rimardo that he can go, after his two bodyguards leave. His own departure and subsequent whereabouts are not established in the summaries. [ch 007 / 007.08]

## Relationships
