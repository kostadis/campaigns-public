## Identity
KP introduces himself as Knazreponnium Ketternoppapux and explicitly identifies as a gnome. He is a spellcaster whose repertoire includes destructive magic, protective barriers and planar travel. [ch 004 / 004.01; ch 013 / 013.08; ch 051 / 051.02; ch 051 / 051.05]

A scholar of inter-planar economics, KP worked for years with the gnomish libraries of the Kron Hills on a system preserving patron privacy. He also writes books and develops theories connecting magic, trade and political power. [ch 024 / 024.02; ch 033 / 033.01; ch 047 / 047.07]

## Personality and Motivations
Knowledge is KP’s recurring priority: he purchases spells as investments, exchanges valuable magic for information and pursues questions until his sources lose patience. Libraries, research and publication repeatedly shape his decisions. [ch 008 / 008.07; ch 024 / 024.06; ch 025 / 025.09; ch 039 / 039.03; ch 052 / 052.05]

He approaches problems through experimentation, contractual loopholes and economic incentives. His proposals range from exploiting the precise terms of a devil contract to replacing slave labor and monopoly rents with free workers, trade and an artisanal class. [ch 024 / 024.05; ch 047 / 047.06]

His enthusiasm can become recklessness: companions restrain his appetite for fireballs, and his curiosity about mind flayers offends potential allies. His scholarship is extensive but fallible, including confused book entries and imperfect recollections. [ch 010 / 010.05; ch 015 / 015.05; ch 025 / 025.04; ch 046 / 046.09; ch 050 / 050.01]

His concern for others is uneven but consequential. He risks himself to rescue Corrin and deliberately protects civilians, yet also entertains abandoning Bayard and accepts morally troubling arrangements when pursuing larger objectives. He expresses remorse after his sheltering in a family’s house exposes them to a dragon attack. [ch 020 / 020.01; ch 028 / 028.05; ch 034 / 034.05; ch 039 / 039.03; ch 044 / 044.01; ch 044 / 044.02]
