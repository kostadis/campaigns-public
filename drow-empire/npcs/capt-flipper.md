## Identity
Capt. Flipper, also called Captain Flipper and Flipper, is a long-time smuggler and former slave-trading partner of King S'kazs, with established contacts among the lizardfolk of the Wasted Isle. [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.03; ch 020 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Business interests shape his outlook: he describes Drow encroachment on his operations, acknowledges a serious offer from the Orcs, and remembers working with Tolub as profitable. [ch 017 / 017.03; ch 022 / 022.11]

He appears sheepish when asked to remain and anxious when promising to wait. KP interprets that resolve as fear of the orc leader of Fhuu; this is KP's assessment of his motivation. His later nervous insistence about Primus also shows concern over how he would account for the party's fate. [ch 017 / 017.03; ch 018 / 018.03; ch 020 / 020.03]

## History with the Party
