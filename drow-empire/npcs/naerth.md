## Identity
Naerth is the chamberlain and consort of the matron of House Illal, authorized to negotiate on the house’s behalf. [ch 047 / entry; ch 047 / 047.05]

## Personality and Motivations
Described as a soldier’s soldier, Naerth favors conventional military strength and House Illal’s proposed return to the traditional martial ways of the Drow Empire. He regards Talaryn’s reliance on magical enhancements and abyssal magic as a failure. [ch 047 / entry; ch 047 / 047.05]

His diplomacy combines candor about military limitations with conditional pressure. He seeks a change in leadership and a temporary peace while retaining the expectation of future war. [ch 047 / 047.05; ch 047 / moment]

## History with the Party
- The party met Naerth in a barracks; he identified himself as chamberlain and consort of the matron of House Illal, authorized to negotiate with them. [ch 047 / entry; ch 047 / 047.05]
- Naerth blamed House Zollazo's failures on Talaryn's investment in magically enhanced troops and abyssal magic rather than conventional soldiers. [ch 047 / 047.05]
- Naerth asked the party to kill Talaryn so a new conclave could choose a leader and House Zollazo would have to bow without House Illal's military support. [ch 047 / 047.05]
- Naerth offered a truce and a peace treaty with Freedahl, admitting that House Illal could not take Plagueridge Rampart by force. [ch 047 / 047.05]
- Naerth offered to make House Illal's guards at the Drow House entrance “particularly weak” if the party allied with them, and the opposite if they refused; Rimardo responded, “Fair enough.” [ch 047 / 047.05]
- Naerth closed by anticipating battle with the party's children after the truce; Bayard understood this as about the closest a Drow Lord comes to a compliment. [ch 047 / 047.05; ch 047 / moment]
