## Identity
Garett is a spell caster and purveyor of magic in Fax, selling spells and magical equipment. [ch 008 / entry; ch 022 / entry; ch 022 / 022.09]

## Personality and Motivations
Garett combines expensive tastes with an opportunistic approach to customers; he and his young elf companion regarded KP as another easy spell buyer. [ch 008 / 008.07]

He prizes information, preferring an unusual scholarly treatise to the magical item KP offered. He also insists on explaining an item's limitations, continuing his account of the longbow of warning despite his wife's impatience. [ch 022 / 022.09]

His need for cash is tied to a dead ex-girlfriend's return as a vampire, a circumstance he deliberately conceals from the Paladin. [ch 022 / 022.09]

## History with the Party
- Halete directed KP to Garett to purchase a lightning bolt spell. Garett sold it for 270 GP, and KP copied it into his book. [ch 008 / 008.07]
- Garett had a charming young elf at his side and promised her a coat once he sold the spell. [ch 008 / 008.07]
- Garett asked whether KP was his name; KP answered that it was Knazreponnium and denied that his friends called him KP. [ch 008 / 008.07]
- In Fax, Garett welcomed KP as his old buddy and needed cash because a dead ex-girlfriend had returned as a vampire; he kept this detail from the Paladin. [ch 022 / 022.09]
- Garett priced a longbow of viciousness at 11,000 GP; Gruneel put it back and found a longbow of warning instead. [ch 022 / 022.09]
- Over his wife's protests, Garett explained that the longbow of warning's previous owner had been surprised and advised keeping hold of the bow. [ch 022 / 022.09]
- Garett sought information from KP and traded Rimardo a sixth level scroll for a copy of the treatise on interplanar economics between the Drow Empire and the prime material plane. [ch 022 / 022.09]
- When KP bought more spells, Garett offered to introduce him to a gnomish woman looking for a mate; she lacked the library KP wanted. [ch 022 / 022.10]
