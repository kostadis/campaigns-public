## Identity
Tharkun is an old, short gnome scribe at the Mystical Ink Scriptorium. He wears tattered green robes and an earring in one ear, and has a gash over his head. [ch 043 / 043.03]

## Personality and Motivations
Tharkun blames everyday misfortunes on unseen tiny dragons. KP interprets his shaking hands, sweating, dilating pupils and darting gaze as signs of abyssal madness; this is KP's assessment. [ch 043 / 043.03]

He fears for his scrolls and responds sharply to perceived insults during bargaining. His twinkling eyes when closing the sale briefly leave the party uncertain how to interpret his behavior. [ch 043 / 043.03]

## History with the Party
- The party met Tharkun, an old gnome scribe, at his Mystical Ink Scriptorium. [ch 043 / 043.03]
- KP examined Tharkun and concluded that he suffered from abyssal madness; Tharkun blamed unseen little dragons for everyday problems. [ch 043 / 043.03]
- After KP rejected the first batch of scrolls, Tharkun responded to the majordomo's discreet signal by revealing more powerful scrolls behind a secret door. [ch 043 / 043.03]
- KP frightened Tharkun by claiming that over 100 purple dragons would nest in his shop; Tharkun feared they would eat his scrolls and offered his mirror image scroll for a thousand gold pieces. [ch 043 / 043.03]
- Tharkun sold the entire first lot of scrolls for 3500 gold pieces after Rimardo accepted that price. [ch 043 / 043.03]
- After leaving, KP saw several spells vanish from the scrolls: Tharkun had written spells that did not exist in KP's time-space continuum, leaving KP with magic he could not use. [ch 043 / 043.03]
- Xyrona later told the party that Tharkun believed her water with purple coloring was a purple Dragon repellent. [ch 043 / 043.04]

## Last Observed State
Last directly observed conducting business at his scriptorium. He is subsequently spoken of at Xyrona's shop, where she describes his belief in her supposed dragon repellent; that mention does not establish his presence there. Status not established in the summaries beyond the shop encounter. [ch 043 / 043.03; ch 043 / 043.04]
