## Identity
Antyln is a Drow leader identified by Marco as Damrith's ally. He fights with a short sword, a hand crossbow, and magic, supported by unseen servants. [ch 033 / 033.02; ch 035 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Antyln shows tactical preparation: he anticipates his allies turning against him and uses darkness, healing servants, and death ward to sustain himself in combat. [ch 035 / 035.01; ch 035 / 035.02; ch 035 / 035.03]

He responds ruthlessly to desertion, killing the fleeing dwarves who had served under Damrith. His anger becomes explicit when KP dispels his darkness. These actions establish his conduct in battle; broader personal goals are not established. [ch 035 / 035.02]

## History with the Party
- Antyln does not appear in Chapter 033; Marco identifies him as the Drow with whom Damrith allied himself. [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.02]
- Antyln enters combat carrying a short sword and a hand crossbow; the Knights of Brungril Forge turn against him after Fonouc calls for their loyalty. [ch 035 / 035.01]
- Antyln casts darkness and fires at KP, missing; Rimardo dispels the darkness. [ch 035 / 035.01]
- Antyln's unseen servants heal him and throw beads of force that trap Rimardo and a Dwarven knight. [ch 035 / 035.01; ch 035 / 035.02]
- Antyln kills the fleeing dwarves who had served under Damrith. [ch 035 / 035.02]
- Antyln strikes Bayard unconscious during their fight. [ch 035 / 035.03]
- Corrin wounds Antyln, and Rimardo's harm brings him to one hit point; Rimardo then heals the party instead of trying to kill him. [ch 035 / 035.03]
- Antyln's death ward prevents a dwarf from killing him; KP subsequently kills Antyln with a fourth level magic missile. [ch 035 / 035.03]

## Last Observed State
Dead: KP kills Antyln with a fourth level magic missile after Rimardo's harm leaves him at one hit point and death ward prevents a Dwarven knight's killing attempt. [ch 035 / 035.03]

## Relationships
- **Damrith:** Marco identifies Antyln as Damrith's Drow ally; Antyln later kills dwarves who had served under Damrith when they try to flee. [ch 033 / 033.02; ch 035 / 035.02]
- **Fonouc and the Knights of Brungril Forge:** The knights turn against Antyln after Fonouc calls for their loyalty to their rightful Thane. [ch 035 / 035.01]

- **The party:** Antyln is a direct combat opponent. He strikes Bayard unconscious; Corrin and Rimardo wound him, and KP kills him. [ch 035 / 035.03]
