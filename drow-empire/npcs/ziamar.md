## Identity
Ziamar is the son of the demon lord Freedahl and the ruler who oversaw Plagueridge Rampart with demon knights and mercenaries from other planes. His majordomo represented him to the party; Ziamar did not appear in person during their return and initial briefing. [ch 040 / entry; ch 041 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
His policies emphasized inter-planar commerce and changed food production: he introduced a new economic system and, according to the former Rangers, replaced cultivation methods that depended on their services. [ch 041 / 041.01; ch 041 / 041.04]

He exercised authority through intermediaries and force, refusing the Rangers an audience and ordering the party’s arrest for disturbing the peace. His vrocks said his plans for the city excluded the party; those plans were not explained. [ch 041 / 041.04; ch 041 / 041.06; ch 042 / 042.02]

## History with the Party
- Ziamar, son of Freedahl, oversaw Plagueridge Rampart with demon knights and hired mercenaries from other planes; he did not appear in person during the party's return. [ch 040 / entry]
- His majordomo thanked the party for securing the city and portal for Freedahl, but warned that the undefeated Drow empire still threatened the portal. [ch 040 / 040.06]
- Ziamar introduced a new inter-planar economic system; his majordomo supplied the party with an analysis and relayed his reminder that defeating the Drow Empire was necessary for continued safety. [ch 041 / 041.01]
- His majordomo warned the party against killing demons and explained that the city garrison was loyal to Ziamar, who was loyal to Freedahl. [ch 041 / 041.03]
- The former Rangers reported that Ziamar's new cultivation technique had displaced their goodberry farming services and that he refused to meet them. [ch 041 / 041.04]
- Four vrocks arrived on Ziamar's orders to arrest the party for disturbing the peace and take them to his palace; combat followed. [ch 041 / 041.06]
- During the fight, a vrock declared that Ziamar had other plans for the city that excluded the party; the party killed the remaining vrocks. [ch 042 / 042.02]
- After resting at the Tavern of the Gilded Dryad, the party marched on Ziamar's tower. [ch 042 / 042.04]
- Rimardo suspected Ziamar had hired the Drow assassins who ambushed them, but conceded Corrin's reminder that this was only an assumption. [ch 042 / 042.05]
- Ziamar was dead by Freedahl's arrival over his body; Freedahl explicitly attributed his killing to the party and called him traitorous. [ch 043 / 043.01]
- Freedahl refused to appoint the majordomo as ruler and said he would remain to defend the city himself after the Drow subverted a demon Lord loyal to him. [ch 043 / 043.01]
- Freedahl required a few days to consolidate and review what Ziamar had done before discussing the party's next steps against the Drow Empire. [ch 043 / 043.02]

## Last Observed State
Ziamar was dead when Freedahl appeared over his body. Freedahl explicitly attributed his killing to the party and condemned him as traitorous. [ch 043 / 043.01]

Freedahl announced that he would defend the city himself and needed several days to consolidate control and review Ziamar’s actions. [ch 043 / 043.01; ch 043 / 043.02]

## Relationships
- **Freedahl:** His father and reported superior. The majordomo initially described Ziamar as loyal to Freedahl; Freedahl subsequently denounced him as a traitor. [ch 041 / 041.03; ch 043 / 043.01]
- **The majordomo:** Conveyed Ziamar’s policies and warnings, then claimed before Freedahl that he had warned the party about Ziamar’s threat to Freedahl’s rule. [ch 041 / 041.01; ch 041 / 041.03; ch 043 / 043.01]
- **The party:** Initially received hospitality and instructions through his administration; later became targets of his arrest order. Freedahl ultimately held them responsible for his death. [ch 040 / 040.06; ch 041 / 041.06; ch 043 / 043.01]
- **City garrison and vrocks:** The garrison was reported loyal to him, and four vrocks enforced his order against the party. [ch 041 / 041.03; ch 041 / 041.06]
- **Former Rangers:** Reported that his cultivation changes had displaced their livelihoods and that he refused to meet them. [ch 041 / 041.04]
- **Drow assassins:** Rimardo suspected Ziamar had hired them, but conceded Corrin’s reminder that this remained an assumption. [ch 042 / 042.05]
