## Identity
Xazlozuhn is a shadow demon who styles himself “the Great Xazlozuhn.” His demonstrated abilities include conjuring an incorporeal creature to support his attacks and repeatedly healing himself. [ch 017 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
His self-styling and taunt at Mr. Corrin suggest boastfulness; his final threat expresses a desire for revenge. [ch 017 / moment; ch 017 / 017.01]

He muttered about contracts as he vanished. KP interpreted this as anger over the terms of an assignment; the contract’s actual terms and employer are not established. [ch 017 / 017.01]

## History with the Party
- Xazlozuhn, a shadow demon calling himself “the Great Xazlozuhn,” attacked the orcs during the Drow retreat. [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.01]
- Rimardo and Mr. Corrin persuaded Bayard to attack Xazlozuhn; Bayard had promised to defend the orcs. [ch 017 / 017.01]
- Mr. Corrin tried to sneak up on Xazlozuhn, but the demon was not surprised. [ch 017 / 017.01]
- Xazlozuhn repeatedly conjured an incorporeal creature to help him attack Mr. Corrin and repeatedly healed himself during the fight. [ch 017 / 017.01]
- Bayard killed Xazlozuhn with a critical hit; Xazlozuhn swore revenge and vanished in a puff of smoke. [ch 017 / 017.01]
- Xazlozuhn muttered about contracts as he vanished; KP inferred that he had been under contract and was angry about its terms. [ch 017 / 017.01]

## Last Observed State
Explicitly reported killed by Bayard’s critical hit. He swore revenge and vanished in a puff of smoke while muttering about contracts; the threat does not establish survival or a return. [ch 017 / 017.01]
