## Identity
Xhalh is a Drow leader identified in recovered orders as the local commander of the Drow Empire and trusted of House Zollazo of the city Maenerius. Valnozz identifies Xhalh as overseer of the planar invasion, with access to the Great Mistress’s resources through the portal. [ch 013 / 013.01; ch 015 / 015.01; ch 016 / 016.04]

## Personality and Motivations
Valnozz describes Xhalh as paranoid and protected by spells. Orders and bounty reports indicate priorities of conquest, disrupting the alliance, and eliminating the independent contractors who opposed the invasion. These accounts establish methods and objectives through others’ reports rather than Xhalh’s own speech. [ch 016 / 016.04; ch 012 / 012.05; ch 015 / 015.01]

## History with the Party
- Ralnor told the party he worked for Realm leader Xhalh and captured slaves for the Drow Empire; Dimitrov questioned the title, and Boradoon questioned the supposed empire’s existence in Oerth. [ch 002 / 002.03]
- Lyless told the party that only Xhalh, the Drow leader, knew the location of the artifact used to travel between planes. [ch 006 / 006.01]
- Alexia Boulderdown recalled Dimitrov’s report that Xhalh was in charge and said they had not found Xhalh. [ch 006 / 006.02]
- Nimh demanded Humph surrender to face justice at Xhalh’s court in St. Aurelius. [ch 010 / 010.06]
- Filzyr announced that Xhalh had placed a massive bounty on those who killed Walton and opposed the conquests, then attacked the party. [ch 012 / 012.05]
- After defeating Filzyr, the party found an order naming Xhalh “trusted of House Zollazo of the city Maenerius” and authorizing assistance in killing the independent contractors; Rimardo took the order and Filzyr’s clothing for a possible impersonation. [ch 013 / 013.01]
- A note recovered from Nalven identified Xhalh as the local commander of the Drow Empire and ordered Nalven to disrupt the alliance and, if possible, kill the independent contractors. [ch 015 / 015.01]
- Valnozz identified Xhalh as overseer of the planar invasion, supported by the Great Mistress’s resources while the portal remained intact. [ch 016 / 016.04]
- Valnozz refused Rimardo’s request to lead the party to Xhalh, citing spells and paranoia; he instructed them to obtain the portal key from Xhalh and close the portal before taking Fax by force. [ch 016 / 016.04]
- Tolub told the party that Xhalh held his daughter hostage and that Lundbeams had demanded they serve the Drow. [ch 021 / 021.05]
- Bayard committed to Xhalh’s death; Tolub described metal constructs in the antechamber and spellcasting quaggoths in Xhalh’s chamber. [ch 021 / 021.05]
- Tolub opposed Rimardo’s proposed earthquake because destroying Xhalh’s chambers could destroy clues to his daughter’s location. [ch 021 / 021.06]
- Chapter 022 reports Xhalh’s defeat and the party’s subsequent looting of Xhalh’s trapped chests; the supplied evidence does not describe the confrontation itself. [ch 022 / 022.01]
- Tolub declared Xhalh dead, after which his troops killed the Drow commanders and Fax descended into violence. Xhalh’s death is reported through Tolub’s declaration. [ch 022 / 022.03]
- Sir Ryton credited the plan with defeating Xhalh and requested the loaned sword back; Bayard argued that the Drow remained undefeated, and Sir Ryton insisted he keep it. [ch 022 / 022.10]
- A later Drow commander boasted that, unlike Xhalh, he would not be defeated. [ch 023 / 023.02]
- On the party’s later return, the Captain’s tower was described as less polished than it had been under Xhalh. [ch 052 / 052.02]
