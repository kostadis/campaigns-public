## Identity
Finaeli Talraidal is a 112-year-old male Drow fighter with pasty white skin, grey eyes, and a bald head. He speaks for worshippers of the masked one, whom KP identifies as followers of Vhaeraun. [ch 031 / 031.07]

## Personality and Motivations
Finaeli favors negotiated governance: his proposed stewardship would represent the party’s interests through someone with the loyalty of the Drow forces and the merchant guild’s respect. He distrusts the party’s demon allies and explicitly anticipates betrayal. [ch 031 / 031.07]

## History with the Party
- Finaeli Talraidal approached the party, spoke for those who worship the masked one, and warned that their demon allies would betray them. [ch 031 / 031.07]
- When KP insisted that he invade the city, Finaeli proposed a Regent or steward to represent the party’s interests while they were off the plane. [ch 031 / 031.07]
- Bayard appointed Finaeli steward; Finaeli and Freedahl then entered the citadel to finalise their arrangement. [ch 031 / 031.07]
- On returning to Plagueridge Rampart, the party found Finaeli dead, his head on a pike; his deal with Freedahl had not been airtight, and his reign as Regent had been cut short. [ch 040 / 040.06]

## Last Observed State
Dead, with his head displayed on a pike when the party returns to Plagueridge Rampart. His arrangement with Freedahl failed to protect his regency; the summary does not identify his killer. [ch 040 / 040.06]

## Relationships
- **Followers of the masked one:** Finaeli acts as their spokesman and offers an alliance on their behalf. [ch 031 / 031.07]
- **Bayard:** His appointing authority, who accepts him as steward despite distrust and expresses belief that the Drow can be redeemed. [ch 031 / 031.07]
- **KP:** Presses Finaeli to seize the city; Finaeli counters with a proposal for stewardship. [ch 031 / 031.07]
- **Freedahl:** His negotiating counterpart, who privately regards him as a useful scapegoat and sees their alliance as something to exploit. [ch 031 / 031.07]
