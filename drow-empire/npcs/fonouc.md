## Identity
Fonouc is the rightful Thane of Brungril Forge, deposed by Damrith, and Marco’s brother. His claim commands the loyalty of the Knights of Brungril Forge. [ch 034 / entry; ch 035 / 035.01]

## Personality and Motivations
- **Values plain dealing:** Bayard’s refusal to give an expected heroic speech earns his trust. [ch 034 / 034.04]
- **Distinguishes coercion from guilt:** He forgives Marco’s compelled attack, treating him as another victim of the Drow. [ch 034 / 034.04]
- **Delegates violence:** He is willing to assert his authority publicly but explicitly requires the party to handle the fighting and killing. [ch 034 / 034.04]
- **Prioritizes the kingdom’s security:** His intended response to the portal threat is to collapse and seal the approach. He expresses shame that the Drow incursion prevents him from providing armies. [ch 035 / 035.05]

## History with the Party
- Marco identified Fonouc as the rightful Thane of Brungril Forge and said the dwarves could not rally without him. [ch 033 / 033.02]
- Pelynumph reported that Fonouc lived but was unwilling to take direct action; Fonouc did not appear in this chapter. [ch 033 / entry]
- Marco promised Rimardo a discount if the party killed Damrith and put Fonouc on the throne. [ch 033 / 033.05]
- Marco attacked Fonouc under a Drow compulsion intended to end the resistance by killing him; KP dispelled the magic. [ch 034 / 034.03]
- Fonouc identified Marco as his brother, friend, and ally. [ch 034 / 034.03]
- Fonouc forgave Marco as a victim of the Drow after Rimardo released Fonouc from paralysis. [ch 034 / entry; ch 034 / 034.04]
- Bayard’s refusal to deliver a heroic speech won Fonouc’s trust in him and his companions. [ch 034 / 034.04]
- Fonouc agreed to declare himself Thane in Damrith’s Hall, requiring the party to handle the fighting and killing. [ch 034 / 034.04]
- Fonouc confronted Damrith as a usurper, then dashed outside when Damrith called for guards; the party fought the knights, and KP killed Damrith with a cone of cold. [ch 034 / 034.05]
- Fonouc rallied the Knights of Brungril Forge to serve him as rightful Thane, and they turned against Antyln. [ch 035 / 035.01]
- Fonouc granted the party a standard discount as heroes of Brungril Forge. [ch 035 / 035.04]
- Fonouc offered treasury gifts: a terra-cotta cat containing gems for Corrin, a gold music box for Bayard, five valuable gems and a gem of true seeing for KP, and two +2 great axes and a horn of blasting for Rimardo. [ch 035 / 035.04]
- Fonouc declared all debts settled and offered food and supplies, expressing shame that the Drow incursion prevented him from offering armies. [ch 035 / 035.05]
- After Marco intervened, Fonouc agreed that the freed slaves could wait near the portal room until the portal re-materialized; he intended to collapse and seal the area afterward. [ch 035 / 035.05]

## Last Observed State
Fonouc is alive and exercising authority as Thane in Brungril Forge, settling obligations to the party and offering food and supplies. [ch 035 / entry; ch 035 / 035.05]

His final stated arrangement permits the freed slaves to wait near the portal room until the portal re-materializes, after which they must leave. He intends to collapse and seal the area; completion of that work is not established in the final scene. [ch 035 / 035.05]

## Relationships
- **Marco:** Brother, friend, and ally. Fonouc absolves him of responsibility for the magically compelled attack; Marco later persuades him to accommodate the freed slaves. [ch 034 / 034.03; ch 034 / 034.04; ch 035 / 035.05]
- **Bayard and the party:** Trusted allies whose service he recognizes with rewards. By their departure, he considers all debts settled. [ch 034 / 034.04; ch 035 / 035.04; ch 035 / 035.05]
- **Damrith:** The usurper who displaced him, whom Fonouc condemns for alliances with the Drow and duergar. [ch 034 / 034.05]
- **Knights of Brungril Forge:** Accept his authority as rightful Thane and answer his appeal to duty and restored honor by turning against Antyln. [ch 035 / 035.01]
- **Freed slaves:** Grants temporary refuge after Marco’s appeal, conditional on their departure when the portal returns. [ch 035 / 035.05]
