## Identity
Bayard is a paladin from Fairhaven, also called Juste-Havre, and the party’s leader; the summaries describe his upbringing as part of the Wild Coast’s anti-intellectual, libertarian tradition. [ch 013 / 013.07; ch 041 / entry] He is also a licensed marriage counselor who worked in Juste-Havre. [ch 052 / 052.05]

His abilities include divine smiting, healing, curing poison, and an aura of courage that protects nearby companions from fear. [ch 003 / 003.09; ch 036 / 036.04; ch 038 / 038.02] He ultimately takes guardianship of the dagger and establishes The Knights of the Dagger around it as their central relic. [ch 052 / 052.05; ch 052 / 052.06]

## Personality and Motivations
Bayard values personal glory, public recognition, and Juste-Havre’s honour. His early pursuit of fame remains visible in his expectation that confronting Lolth will cement his glory. [ch 003 / 003.01; ch 014 / 014.04; ch 050 / 050.01] He favours conspicuous courage: he rejects bows on principle and prefers entering an enemy castle openly to adopting a disguise. [ch 046 / 046.05; ch 046 / 046.07]

His protective convictions include risking himself for civilians, demanding a proper trial, and believing even the Drow can be redeemed. [ch 012 / 012.03; ch 013 / 013.03; ch 031 / 031.07] Nevertheless, he accepts severe compromises, including using slaves as a diversion despite warnings of slaughter and supporting Freedahl’s conquest in exchange for protecting his own plane. [ch 026 / 026.04; ch 027 / 027.04]

His honour is therefore flexible in practice: he sometimes prioritizes the larger mission over immediate rescue and explicitly qualifies promises when necessity might require breaking them. [ch 028 / 028.04; ch 048 / 048.02] He describes this flexibility as pragmatism when accepting the dagger’s guardianship. [ch 052 / 052.05]

He can reconsider his decisions: liberation’s violent aftermath troubles him, and he later treats helping the gnomes as an opportunity for redemption. [ch 022 / 022.03; ch 040 / 040.05]

## Last Observed State
Bayard is alive after Rimardo raises him following the fatal staff explosion, and he returns with the party to the Prime Material Plane outside Fax. [ch 051 / 051.03; ch 051 / 051.05] His final direct appearance places him in possession of the dagger and Lundbeams’s organization, responsible for arranging its defence against the powers seeking it. He withholds information about the relic as internal order business. [ch 052 / 052.05]

The later epilogue reports that his secret order continues guarding the dagger, perhaps near Juste-Havre. Its knights wear daggers on their wrists, kill people who ask too many questions, and eat butter with their brioche. Bayard himself is spoken of through the order; his personal status at that later point is not established in the summaries. [ch 052 / 052.06]

## Relationships
- **Rimardo:** A repeatedly lifesaving companion whose mercenary priorities strain their partnership. - **Selzyt / Vhaeraun:** A bargaining partner whose agreement Bayard initially preserves, then threatens to reconsider for his companions’ sake.
