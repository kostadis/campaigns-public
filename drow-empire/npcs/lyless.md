## Identity
Lyless is identified as the Drow leader of the blockade and a spellcaster. She says her home plane is the Abyss. [ch 005 / entry; ch 006 / 006.01]

## Personality and Motivations
Lyless responds to interference with threats and refuses to explain her plans. Her broader aims are not established. [ch 004 / 004.07]

When isolated in battle, she prioritizes survival: she seeks an oath against being killed and accepts surrender despite Bayard’s conditional answer. [ch 005 / 005.05]

## History with the Party
- Riporus appealed to Lyless through a sending stone during the Lizardfolk attack, claiming she had promised militia. [ch 004 / 004.06]
- Lyless answered KP through the captured sending stone, asked whether the heroes were dead, refused to explain her plans, and threatened to send hobgoblins to slaughter the party. [ch 004 / 004.07]
- When Gott reported finding the party, Lyless ordered the Hobgoblins to attack over the sending stone. [ch 005 / 005.04]
- Lyless fought the party alongside the Drow, using magic missile, shield, misty step, fire bolt and counter-spell. [ch 005 / entry]
- Left alone on the battlefield, Lyless demanded an oath that she would not be killed; Bayard gave a conditional answer and persuaded her to surrender. [ch 005 / 005.05]
- The party interrogated the captured Lyless before entering Farjvad; the summary identifies her as “the Drow leader of the blockade.” [ch 006 / 006.01]
- During interrogation, Lyless revealed that her home plane is the Abyss. [ch 006 / 006.01]
- Lyless revealed that the Drow use an artifact to travel between planes; she did not know its location, and said only Xhalh, the leader of the Drow, knew it. [ch 006 / 006.01]
- Lyless revealed that the grandmaster of Fax works with the Drow because of a hostage situation. [ch 006 / 006.01]

## Last Observed State
Lyless is last observed as the party’s captive, being interrogated outside Farjvad before they enter the village. She provides information about planar travel and the Drow’s cooperation with the grandmaster of Fax. Her disposition after this interrogation is not established in the summaries. [ch 006 / 006.01]

## Relationships
- **The party:** Adversaries who defeat and capture her, then question her. [ch 005 / 005.05; ch 006 / 006.01]
- **Bayard:** Secures her surrender after she requests protection against being killed. [ch 005 / 005.05]
- **KP:** Receives her threats through the sending stone and later exchanges spells with her in battle. [ch 004 / 004.07; ch 005 / 005.05]
- **Riporus:** Appeals to her for militia support he claims she promised. [ch 004 / 004.06]
- **Gott and the Hobgoblins:** Gott reports to her through the sending stone; she authorizes their attack on the party. [ch 005 / 005.04]
- **The Drow and Xhalh:** Lyless leads the blockade and identifies Xhalh as the Drow’s leader and sole knower of the planar artifact’s location. [ch 006 / 006.01]
- **The grandmaster of Fax:** Lyless reports that the grandmaster works with the Drow because of a hostage situation; no direct personal relationship with her is established. [ch 006 / 006.01]
