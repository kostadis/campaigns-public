## Identity
Seth is an average-build, copper-haired, blue-eyed gnome who leads a group of Vallaki townsfolk. He looks like a blacksmith, but his occupation is not confirmed. He later serves in the Crossroads militia and leads a small contingent for the Anti-Drow Alliance. [ch 012 / entry; ch 015 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Seth shows practical concern for others through his assistance to the wounded and his guidance of fleeing villagers. [ch 012 / 012.02] His trust in Bayard rests on Bayard’s willingness to risk his life against the Quaggoth; that trust extends to accepting the Orcs as allies on Bayard’s recommendation. [ch 015 / 015.03]

## History with the Party
- Seth led a group of Vallaki townsfolk and offered his cart and oxen to carry the wounded and dying away from the burning town. [ch 012 / 012.02]
- Seth led the fleeing villagers through secret forest paths to avoid the Drow army. [ch 012 / 012.02]
- Bayard killed a Quaggoth rather than abandon Seth's family to it. [ch 012 / moment]
- Seth left the party in the forest and headed for Crossroads, where his brother, Burgher Teeble, was in charge. [ch 012 / 012.04]
- At Crossroads, Seth stepped out of the militia, credited Bayard with saving him from the Quaggoth, and vouched for the Orcs on Bayard's word. [ch 015 / 015.03]
- Burgher Teeble agreed that Crossroads would join the alliance, with Seth leading a small contingent of troops. [ch 015 / 015.03]
- During the march on Vallaki, Seth's presence reassured other villages that the Orcs were not another raiding party. [ch 015 / 015.04]
- Seth and his band of gnomes accompanied the party into Vallaki and were with them at the western gate. [ch 015 / 015.05]

## Last Observed State
Seth is with the party and his band of gnomes at Vallaki’s western gate, where the party waits to block the Drow’s access as the Anti-Drow Alliance advances. The scene establishes his presence but describes no individual action by him there. [ch 015 / 015.05]

## Relationships
- **Bayard:** Seth regards him as his rescuer and publicly stakes his support for the Orcs on Bayard’s judgment. [ch 015 / 015.03]
- **Burgher Teeble:** Seth’s brother, who is in charge of Crossroads and assigns Seth to lead its allied contingent. [ch 012 / 012.04; ch 015 / 015.03]
- **Orcs and the allied villages:** Seth serves as a trusted intermediary; his presence reassures villages about the Orc force’s intentions. [ch 015 / 015.04]
