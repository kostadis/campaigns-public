## Identity
Yagrat, addressed as Primus, is the Orc leader of Fhuu and commander of its Orc forces within the Anti-Drow Alliance. He is grizzled and scarred, with sharp eyes and an authoritative, gravelly voice. [ch 013 / 013.08; ch 015 / 015.02]

## Personality and Motivations
Yagrat distrusts human willingness to defend orcs, but accepts cooperation when mutual survival demands it. He can reconsider suspicion in light of evidence and recognizes attempts to exploit tensions between allies. [ch 013 / 013.08; ch 014 / 014.03]

He exercises restraint when his troops threaten the alliance, yet also threatens indiscriminate slaughter if Sir Ryton approaches Fhuu. His commitment to cooperation therefore coexists with explicit hostility toward non-orcs when he perceives a threat to Fhuu. [ch 015 / 015.03; ch 022 / 022.10]

He supports practical responses to military setbacks, including equipping the party for the portal mission and committing his diminished forces to a diversion supporting their strike against the Drow leadership. [ch 017 / 017.02; ch 020 / 020.04]

## History with the Party
- At their first audience, the Primus questioned the party’s trustworthiness, ordered Guttja taken away after his admission of Drow contacts, and agreed to call an alliance of seven villages. [ch 013 / 013.08]
- Primus Yagrat authorized the party to investigate an attack by someone wearing Juste-Havre livery, recognizing that it might be an attempt to sabotage the alliance. [ch 014 / 014.03]
- Bayard brought Yagrat evidence of Nalven’s orders to disrupt the alliance; the Primus was relieved that conflict with the humans could be avoided. [ch 015 / 015.01]
- The war council assigned the Primus command of the Orc forces advancing toward Fax; Sir Ryton asked Bayard to accompany them. [ch 015 / 015.02]
- At Crossroads, the Primus restrained his troops when they wanted to massacre the town over its refusal to let them pass. Bayard’s intervention and Seth’s endorsement helped secure Crossroads’ participation in the alliance. [ch 015 / 015.03]
- The Primus agreed with Sir Ryton and the Independent Contractors on a morning encirclement of the Drow. During the freezing battle outside Vallaki, he held the north and tried to prevent a rout. [ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / 016.01]
- After the defeat, Yagrat credited the commanders’ resolve and the Drow retreat with preventing annihilation. He supported the party’s mission to destroy the portal and helped equip them with magic weapons. [ch 017 / 017.02]
- The party reconvened at the Primus’s castle, where alliance leadership planned guerrilla warfare and a covert expedition to the portal island. [ch 017 / 017.03]
- Capt. Flipper said the Primus and Sir Ryton had asked him to remain until the mission ended or the heroes died. KP interpreted his resolve as fear of Fhuu’s orc leader. [ch 018 / 018.03]
- After the party’s return, an agent of Primus escorted them from the beach to Fhuu. [ch 020 / 020.03]
- With his fighting strength halved, Primus co-proposed an attack on Fax to draw defenders away while the party struck the Drow leader’s tower. The party agreed to the plan. [ch 020 / 020.04]
- In Fax, Yagrat praised Bayard’s service to the orcs of Fhuu, then threatened to slaughter every human, demi-human, and non-orc if Sir Ryton approached Fhuu. Bayard endorsed the threatened response. [ch 022 / 022.10]
- Yagrat’s forces were later mentioned in Rimardo’s recollection that Valnozz had ordered the Drow retreat at Vallaki, allowing the forces of Primus and Sir Ryton to withdraw safely. [ch 031 / 031.01]

## Last Observed State
Yagrat’s last direct appearance is in Fax, praising Bayard’s service to Fhuu and warning of slaughter if Sir Ryton approaches his city. [ch 022 / 022.10]

He is later mentioned only in Rimardo’s recollection that Valnozz ordered the Drow retreat at Vallaki, allowing his and Sir Ryton’s forces to withdraw safely. This supplies no new observation of Yagrat’s whereabouts or condition. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 031 / 031.01]

## Relationships
- **Bayard and the party:** Trusted operational partners whom Yagrat supports with equipment and coordinated military action. He explicitly praises Bayard’s service to Fhuu; Bayard endorses his threatened response to Sir Ryton approaching the city. [ch 017 / 017.02; ch 020 / 020.04; ch 022 / 022.10]
- **Sir Ryton:** A military collaborator against the Drow, but subsequently the target of Yagrat’s contempt and conditional threat against non-orcs. [ch 016 / 016.01; ch 020 / 020.04; ch 022 / 022.10]
- **Guttja:** His second in command and formerly valued friend and ally; Yagrat orders him taken away after learning of his contacts with the Drow. [ch 013 / 013.08; ch 014 / 014.03]
- **Capt. Flipper:** Reports that Yagrat and Sir Ryton required him to remain until the mission ended or the heroes died. KP interprets his compliance as fear of Yagrat. [ch 018 / 018.03]
- **The Drow:** Yagrat regards them as an ancient enemy and commits forces and resources to opposing them. [ch 013 / 013.08; ch 017 / 017.02; ch 020 / 020.04]
