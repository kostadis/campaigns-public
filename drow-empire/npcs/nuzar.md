## Identity
Nuzar identifies himself as a noble of the True Gith. He speaks with a thick accent and initially addresses the party unseen from about 30 feet away. [ch 046 / 046.04]

## Personality and Motivations
Nuzar pursues the red dragons as prizes his people want to secure amid the Drow Empire’s fall. His bargaining combines a threat against the party’s lives with polite, appreciative language. [ch 046 / 046.04]

He shows embarrassment when the party’s generosity exceeds his people’s capacity to accept: he blushes and apologetically explains the limit of one dragon per Gith. [ch 046 / moment]

## History with the Party
- Nuzar introduced himself as a “noble of the True Gith,” speaking unseen with a thick accent from about 30 feet away. [ch 046 / 046.04]
- Nuzar offered to let the party live if they allowed his people to take the red dragons flying over the basalt plain. [ch 046 / 046.04]
- Nuzar explained that the dragons were keyed onto the party’s appearance and would attack only them, requiring the party to remain visible. [ch 046 / 046.04]
- Nuzar proposed taking four dragons; when the party offered all of them, he blushed and explained that they could take only one per Gith. [ch 046 / 046.04]
- As eight young red dragon wyrmlings approached, the party recognized Nuzar from their time in the portal on their home plane. [ch 046 / 046.04]
- Nuzar thanked the party for their generosity and welcomed doing business with them again. [ch 046 / 046.04]

## Last Observed State
Nuzar is alive and speaking during the bargain on the basalt plain. At the scene’s close, eight young red dragon wyrmlings approach; he raises his head and thanks the party. The evidence does not establish a completed dragon capture or his subsequent whereabouts. [ch 046 / 046.04]
