## Identity
Ralnor was a Drow Necromancer who claimed to serve Realm leader Xhalh as a slave-catcher for the Drow Empire. Dimitrov and Boradoon questioned whether his account fit their knowledge of the Drow. [ch 002 / 002.03]

## Personality and Motivations
Ralnor expressed confidence that the Drow would crush his opponents. His use of healing potions and desperate attempt to escape show an immediate drive to survive; his stated mission was capturing slaves for the Drow Empire. [ch 002 / 002.03]

## History with the Party
- The party found Ralnor, a Drow Necromancer, hiding in the temple's inner sanctum; the spell casters dispelled his magic. [ch 002 / 002.03]
- Under Reepicheep's Tasha’s Laughter, Ralnor said he worked for Realm leader Xhalh, captured slaves for the Drow Empire, and that the Drow would crush them all. [ch 002 / 002.03]
- Dimitrov questioned the title Realm Leader; Boradoon reasoned that the powers of Greyhawk would have crushed any Drow Empire in Oerth. [ch 002 / 002.03]
- Ralnor recovered and resumed combat, countering spells from Boradoon and Dimitrov, casting Faerie fire, and drinking potions of healing. [ch 002 / 002.03]
- Ralnor tried to ram through Dimitrov at the door; his escape failed, and the Triton killed him. [ch 002 / 002.03; ch 002 / entry]
- The party found a casually discarded treasure hoard Ralnor had collected from his victims. [ch 002 / 002.04]
- In Ralnor's personal effects, the party found a map showing a trail out of Farjvad into the forested hills. [ch 002 / 002.04]

## Last Observed State
Dead: the chapter entry explicitly states that the Triton killed Ralnor after his failed attempt to force past Dimitrov. [ch 002 / entry] His effects subsequently supplied the party with a map marking a trail from Farjvad into the forested hills. [ch 002 / 002.04]

## Relationships
- **Xhalh:** Ralnor named Realm leader Xhalh as his employer; this relationship rests on Ralnor's own account. [ch 002 / 002.03]
- **Drow Empire:** His professed allegiance and the intended recipient of the slaves he captured. [ch 002 / 002.03]
- **Reepicheep:** Used Tasha’s Laughter to draw information from him. [ch 002 / 002.03]
- **Dimitrov and Boradoon:** Opponents whose spells he countered and who challenged the plausibility of his claims. Dimitrov also guarded his escape route. [ch 002 / 002.03]
- **His victims:** The source of the treasure hoard attributed to him; no individual victims are named in the supplied account. [ch 002 / 002.04]
