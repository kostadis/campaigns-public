## Identity
Xyrona Jolin Qimrya Daphici is a 322-year-old female gnome potion merchant, 3’2” tall, with brown eyes, silky golden skin, and long wavy blond hair shaved on the left side. Her right hand is missing. She describes herself as a “middle gnome,” sourcing potions rather than making them. [ch 043 / 043.04]

## Personality and Motivations
Xyrona is a sharp, outspoken negotiator who resists assumed discounts but responds to shared intellectual interests. She studies inter-planar economics and regards the shifting portal’s traffic of demons and heroes as a source of demand for potions. Her decision to remain in the abyss is explicitly commercial. [ch 043 / 043.04]
