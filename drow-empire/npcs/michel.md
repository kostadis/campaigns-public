## Identity
Michel is a young man from Fairhaven, locally called Juste-Havre. He is short and husky, with amber eyes, golden blond hair, a previously broken nose, and a raccoon-skull tattoo on his right arm. [ch 013 / 013.07]

## Personality and Motivations
Michel is teasing and openly skeptical of Bayard’s credibility. He seeks reassurance about the Drow threat before supporting the proposed alliance, and changes his position after hearing Rimardo and Bayard’s assurances. His warning to Bayard shows concern that his own advice to Ryton rest on reliable information. [ch 013 / 013.07]

## History with the Party
- Michel is a young man from Fairhaven, Bayard's old village, locally called Juste-Havre. He is short and husky, with amber eyes, golden blond hair, a previously broken nose, and a raccoon-skull tattoo on his right arm. [ch 013 / 013.07]
- Years earlier, Bayard broke Michel's nose after Michel questioned Bayard's honour over whether he had stolen an apple from Jean-Luc's farm. [ch 013 / 013.07]
- Michel stopped the Orcs on the way to the castle and teased Bayard about being a “brioche without butter.” Bayard pretended not to remember him despite recognising him. [ch 013 / 013.07]
- Michel reported that the Primus had asked Monseigneur Ryton for help against the Drow and that an alliance was being arranged. He questioned its wisdom and whether the threat was Drow or Elves. [ch 013 / 013.07]
- After Rimardo and Bayard assured him that the Drow were evil and real, Michel resolved to advise Ryton to listen to Primus. [ch 013 / 013.07]
- Michel conditionally acknowledged that Bayard had earned his butter, then challenged his record of delivering on his words and warned him against misleading him. [ch 013 / 013.07]

## Last Observed State
Michel is present on the main drag into the castle, speaking with the Orcs. By the conversation’s end, he intends to advise Ryton to listen to Primus, while warning Bayard against misleading him. His intended advice is not shown being delivered. Status not established in the summaries beyond this encounter. [ch 013 / 013.07]
