## Identity
George is a thug hired to abduct visitors for the slave market; he identified his employers as dark-skinned elves. [ch 007 / entry; ch 007 / 007.02]

## Personality and Motivations
George presents his work as a paid transaction without personal animosity toward his targets. His attempted flight when the fight turns against the thugs suggests concern for his own survival. [ch 007 / 007.02; ch 007 / 007.01]

He insists that Drow are fictional, explaining his employers’ avoidance of sunlight as concern about other people’s superstitions. [ch 007 / 007.02]

## History with the Party
- George, the last remaining thug, tried to flee the street fight; Bayard grappled him and demanded an explanation. [ch 007 / 007.01]
- George said he had been paid to collect visitors for the slave market and that the party had been selected as unequipped, easy targets. [ch 007 / 007.02]
- George identified his employers as dark-skinned elves who avoided sunlight, but insisted that Drow were fiction. [ch 007 / 007.02]
- Rimardo offered George his life if he led the party to the elves. [ch 007 / 007.02]
- The city watch took George away. [ch 007 / 007.03]
- Dante predicted George would be back on the street in twenty minutes; the summary does not establish that this happened. [ch 007 / 007.03]

## Last Observed State
George was last observed being taken away by the city watch. Dante predicted a quick return to the street, but the summaries do not establish his release or subsequent fate. Status not established in the summaries beyond that departure in watch custody. [ch 007 / 007.03]

## Relationships
- **The party:** Intended abduction targets whom George described as selected for being unequipped and easy to seize. [ch 007 / 007.02]
- **Bayard:** Captured George during his attempted escape and demanded an explanation. [ch 007 / 007.01]
- **Rimardo:** Offered to spare George’s life if he led the party to his employers; George’s acceptance is not established. [ch 007 / 007.02]
- **Dark-skinned elves:** George’s reported employers for the slave-market abductions. Their identities are not supplied. [ch 007 / 007.02]
- **City watch:** Took George into custody after the party questioned him. [ch 007 / 007.03]
