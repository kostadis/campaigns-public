## Identity
Namyn was a Drow general of House Illal, commanding the whole Drow assault. [ch 016 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
He appeared curiously relaxed at his command post. His composure gave way to frustration when KP turned his darkness into an advantage for the Independent Contractors. [ch 016 / 016.03]

## History with the Party
- Under threat, Jaezton identified Namyn of House Illal as the general commanding the Drow assault and revealed his location. [ch 016 / 016.02]
- The party sneaked up on Namyn, who was guarded by five Drow Initiates and communicating with his armies through an invisible homunculus. [ch 016 / 016.03]
- Corrin triggered the Initiates' alarm spell; KP's fireball devastated the Drow. [ch 016 / 016.03]
- Namyn knocked Corrin unconscious and hid in darkness. [ch 016 / 016.03]
- KP used his gem of true seeing to fight from within the darkness and neutralise the Initiates' counter-spells. [ch 016 / 016.03]
- Namyn dispelled his darkness, allowing Bayard to see and kill him with his smiting ability. [ch 016 / 016.03]
- Valnozz offered to signal a Drow retreat by claiming Namyn had achieved his objectives. [ch 016 / 016.04]
- Chapter 017 explicitly describes Namyn as dead and reports that the Drow retreated after receiving false instructions. [ch 017 / 017.01]

## Last Observed State
Dead: chapter 017 explicitly confirms his death following Bayard’s fatal smite. He is mentioned in the battlefield aftermath, when the Drow retreat under false instructions. [ch 016 / 016.03; ch 017 / 017.01]

## Relationships
- **Jaezton:** A subordinate commander who revealed Namyn’s identity and location under threat. [ch 016 / 016.02]
- **Drow Initiates:** Five guarded his command post; an invisible homunculus provided communication with his armies. [ch 016 / 016.03]
- **Independent Contractors:** Battlefield enemies whose attack ended in his death. [ch 016 / 016.03]
- **Valnozz:** Offered to invoke Namyn’s supposed success as the justification for ordering a Drow retreat. [ch 016 / 016.04]
