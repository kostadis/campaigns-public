## Identity
Marco is a dwarf freed from Drow slavery and a leader among the freed slaves. He comes from the plane containing Brungril Forge and supports Fonouc as its rightful Thane. [ch 028 / entry; ch 033 / 033.01; ch 033 / 033.02]

His restored voice has the accent of a Dwarven Lord; the summaries do not establish a specific title for him. [ch 034 / 034.03]

## Personality and Motivations
- **Freedom and obligation:** Marco treats liberation as an obligation extending beyond those already rescued. He expects the party to honor its promises and regards the freed dwarves’ sacrifices as grounds for demanding help. [ch 028 / entry; ch 033 / 033.02]
- **Blunt and forceful:** He openly challenges the party’s judgment and invokes honor even when discussing a diversion against the Drow. [ch 028 / 028.03; ch 028 / 028.05]
- **Politically pragmatic:** He objects when KP publicly questions a rallying claim and ties a promised discount to the party achieving his political goals. [ch 034 / 034.02; ch 033 / 033.05]
- **Warmth and humor:** His teasing of Corrin accompanies praise for his beard, and his later gratitude includes delight in Corrin’s voice. [ch 033 / 033.01; ch 035 / 035.05]

## History with the Party
- Marco, a dwarf, led the 17 slaves freed by the party and demanded that they free the rest. [ch 028 / entry]
- Marco objected to taking a short rest and concluded that the party was insane. [ch 028 / 028.03]
- Marco led the slaves out shouting "FREEDOM!!!!", drawing the Drow guards away from the citadel. [ch 028 / 028.05]
- Valnozz warned that Marco would die in the slave revolt; this was a prediction. [ch 031 / 031.03]
- After the city's Drow resistance was overcome, Marco arrived and demanded that Bayard fulfill his promise to free the slaves. [ch 031 / 031.04]
- Marco explained that the portal connected four planes, each with a Drow incursion that could support an attempt to recapture the city. [ch 031 / 031.04]
- Marco survived the poisonous gas alongside a half-giant while the other slaves fell unconscious. [ch 032 / entry]
- Marco recognized his home plane from crafts made in Brungril Forge in a Drow storage chamber. [ch 033 / 033.01]
- Marco teased Corrin about his dwarven appearance and praised his beard. [ch 033 / 033.01]
- Marco said that a coup had displaced the rightful Thane, Fonouc, and that the defeated had been sent to the Drow empire to die. [ch 033 / 033.02]
- Marco pressed the party to help overthrow Damrith, invoking their debt to the freed dwarves and the Paladin's promise. [ch 033 / 033.02]
- Marco promised Rimardo a discount only if the party killed Damrith and put Fonouc on the throne. [ch 033 / 033.05]
- Marco pledged help to his old friend Pelynumph, who sought allies against the Drow slaughter of myconids. [ch 033 / 033.06]
- Marco drew a map of Brungril Forge and identified the Carls as noble houses whose support they needed. [ch 033 / 033.06]
- After the Duergar slaves were freed, KP and Rimardo noticed Marco's changed accent; Marco scolded KP for publicly questioning his claim that the Drow Empire had been defeated. [ch 034 / 034.02]
- Marco acknowledged that the freed slaves' commotion had cost the party surprise against Damrith. [ch 034 / 034.02]
- Marco attempted to kill Fonouc under a Drow compulsion; KP's dispel magic freed him and restored his voice with the accent of a Dwarven Lord. [ch 034 / 034.03]
- Marco apologized after the compulsion was removed; Fonouc declared that Marco was a victim of the Drow and had nothing to answer for. [ch 034 / 034.03; ch 034 / 034.04]
- Marco thanked the party for saving the dwarves, rescuing the myconids and Pelynumph, defeating the duergar slavers, and letting him hear Corrin's voice. [ch 035 / 035.05]
- Marco offered an honor guard to the portal room and persuaded Fonouc to let the freed slaves stay near it until the portal re-materialized. [ch 035 / 035.05]

## Last Observed State
Marco is alive and acting during the departure preparations at Brungril Forge. He thanks the party, offers an honor guard to the portal room, and successfully advocates for temporary shelter for the freed slaves nearby until the portal re-materializes. The offer does not establish that he accompanies the party. [ch 035 / 035.05]

The Drow compulsion was removed by KP, and Fonouc explicitly absolved Marco of responsibility for the compelled attack. [ch 034 / 034.03; ch 034 / 034.04]

## Relationships
- **Fonouc:** Marco champions his restoration. Fonouc calls Marco his brother, friend, and ally, and treats him as a victim of the Drow after the compulsion is removed. [ch 033 / 033.02; ch 034 / 034.03; ch 034 / 034.04]
- **Bayard:** Marco holds him personally accountable for the promise to free the slaves and invokes the Paladin’s promise when seeking help for Brungril Forge. [ch 031 / 031.04; ch 033 / 033.02]
- **KP:** Their exchanges include irritation over public disclosures; KP also frees Marco from the Drow spell. [ch 034 / 034.02; ch 034 / 034.03]
- **Corrin:** Marco expresses affectionate amusement at his dwarven appearance and admiration for his voice. [ch 033 / 033.01; ch 035 / 035.05]
- **Rimardo:** Marco uses a conditional discount to encourage his support against Damrith. [ch 033 / 033.05]
- **Pelynumph:** An old friend whose appeal for help receives Marco’s pledge of support. [ch 033 / 033.06]
- **Freed slaves:** Marco serves as their leader and advocate, pressing both the party and Fonouc to meet their needs. [ch 028 / entry; ch 035 / 035.05]
- **Damrith and the Drow:** Marco condemns Damrith as a usurper and seeks his overthrow. The Drow enslaved Marco and used magical compulsion to turn him against Fonouc. [ch 033 / 033.02; ch 034 / 034.03]
