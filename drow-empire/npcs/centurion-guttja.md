## Identity
Centurion Guttja is an imposing Orc whom the Primus identifies as his second in command. [ch 013 / 013.04; ch 014 / 014.03]

## Personality and Motivations
Guttja voices resentment of human distrust toward orcs and of humans’ failure to heed the free orcs. His response to Amos’s prejudice is openly threatening. [ch 013 / 013.04]

He advocates cooperation with the Drow Empire, citing promised territory in exchange for staying out of the fight. He presents his conversations with the Drow as service to the Primus and continues defending them when ordered taken away. [ch 013 / 013.08]

## History with the Party
- Centurion Guttja, an imposing Orc, offered to escort the party to Primus. [ch 013 / 013.04]
- Guttja rebuked Amos’s prejudice against humanoids and accused humans of ignoring the free orcs. [ch 013 / 013.04]
- Guttja turned into an alleyway and announced that the party would never reach Primus and would die; the party fled and escaped except for Amos. [ch 013 / 013.04]
- Guttja interrupted the audience with the Primus, urged him not to listen to the party, and claimed the Drow had promised territory in exchange for staying out of the fight. [ch 013 / 013.08]
- Guttja admitted to conversations with the Drow, presenting them as an exploration of options for the Primus. [ch 013 / 013.08]
- The Primus ordered guards to take Guttja away for speaking to the ancient enemy; Guttja protested as he was taken away. [ch 013 / 013.08]
- In chapter 014, the Primus spoke of Centurion Guttja as his own second in command while discussing possible collaboration with the Drow; Guttja’s presence in the sidebar is not established. [ch 014 / 014.03]

## Last Observed State
Guttja was last directly observed being taken away by guards on the Primus’s order for speaking to the ancient enemy. [ch 013 / 013.08]

In the later sidebar, the Primus mentions him as his own second in command while discussing possible collaboration with the Drow; Guttja’s presence is not established. His subsequent custody or release is not reported. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 014 / 014.03]

## Relationships
- **The Primus:** Superior and acknowledged friend and ally, though the Primus rejects Guttja’s dealings with the Drow and orders guards to take him away. [ch 013 / 013.08]
- **The Drow Empire:** Guttja’s declared friends and negotiating contacts. The promised territorial reward is his account of their offer. [ch 013 / 013.08]
- **The party:** Hostile beneath an initial offer of assistance; Guttja threatens their lives and later urges the Primus to disregard them. [ch 013 / 013.04; ch 013 / 013.08]
- **Amos:** Directly opposed over Amos’s prejudice against humanoids and human distrust of orcs. [ch 013 / 013.04]
- **Bayard:** Challenges Guttja’s Drow contacts and asks whether he is a traitor. [ch 013 / 013.08]
