## Identity
Jaezton is a Drow commander of House Illal, subordinate to the assault’s general, Namyn. His assignment was to protect the center and prevent Alliance forces from escaping through Vallaki’s western gate. [ch 016 / 016.02]

## Personality and Motivations
Jaezton’s response to threats shows a willingness to disclose military information under pressure: he revealed his general’s identity and location. His warnings emphasized the Drow Empire’s still-hidden power; the evidence does not establish whether these warnings reflected conviction or an attempt at intimidation. [ch 016 / 016.02]

## History with the Party
- Jaezton was a Drow commander of House Illal, tasked with protecting the center, sealing Vallaki's western gate, and hunting human and orc troops hiding from the slaughter. [ch 016 / 016.02]
- The party defeated Jaezton's advance guard of hobgoblins attempting to enter Vallaki through the western gate. [ch 016 / 016.02]
- Bayard charged across some 600 yards, startling Jaezton; volleys from the party and Corrin struck the Drow despite Jaezton's darkness. [ch 016 / 016.02]
- KP captured Jaezton in Otiluke's resistant sphere, isolating him from his troops and leaving him watching his soldiers die. [ch 016 / 016.02]
- Under threat, Jaezton identified Namyn of House Illal as the general commanding the assault, distinguished him from Nimh, and revealed Namyn's location. [ch 016 / 016.02]
- Jaezton warned that the Drow Empire's full power remained hidden; after his warnings of superweapons, the party saw white dragons approaching the battlefield while sneaking toward Namyn. [ch 016 / 016.02]

## Last Observed State
Last directly observed trapped in KP’s Otiluke's resistant sphere at Vallaki’s western gate, Jaezton was isolated from his troops and compelled to provide information. His subsequent disposition is not recorded; status beyond that captivity is not established in the summaries. [ch 016 / 016.02]

## Relationships
- **House Illal and Namyn:** Jaezton belongs to House Illal and identified Namyn of House Illal as the general commanding the assault. [ch 016 / 016.02]
- **His troops:** He commanded the force assigned to seal the western gate and hunt concealed human and orc troops; confinement left him helpless to intervene as his soldiers died. [ch 016 / 016.02]
- **The party:** Battlefield enemies who became his captors and interrogators. Bayard’s charge startled him, and KP’s sphere confined him. [ch 016 / 016.02]
- **Nimh:** Jaezton distinguished Namyn from Nimh, who had led the initial assault on Vallaki; no personal relationship with Nimh is established. [ch 016 / 016.02]
