## Identity
Brorna is an ancient drow wizard. Vhaeraun identifies her as his cult’s protector and the hidden power behind the drow Empire, selecting matrons and directing strategy for a thousand years. [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.07]

## Personality and Motivations
Brorna works through concealment, preparation, and persuasion. Her long opposition to Lolth and her advance preparations for the party’s infiltration show a sustained commitment to Vhaeraun’s cause. [ch 048 / 048.07; ch 049 / 049.01]

She can use forceful theatrics and appeals to religious authority to influence others, redirecting G’elde’s intentions while maintaining her cover. She expresses personal dislike for Talaryn’s obsession with power. [ch 049 / 049.02; ch 048 / 048.10]

## History with the Party
- Brorna stepped out of a shadowy doorway in the dungeon; the green glow from Selzyt's gemstone flowed into her. [ch 048 / 048.07]
- Vhaeraun described Brorna as his cult’s protector and the hidden power behind the drow Empire, picking matrons, defining strategy, and quietly thwarting Lolth’s plans for a thousand years. [ch 048 / 048.07]
- Brorna said she had never liked Talaryn and considered her obsessed with power. [ch 048 / 048.10]
- The party agreed to Brorna’s proposal that she pretend to have captured them and persuade G’elde to send them to Lolth as prisoners. [ch 049 / 049.01]
- Brorna prepared a Leomund’s Secret Chest and replica for the party’s equipment; she and Vhaeraun had a Drider contact in the slave pits who would deliver the miniature chest. [ch 049 / 049.01]
- Brorna put the party to sleep with a sleeping draught and summoned G’elde by claiming to have captured Talaryn’s killers. [ch 049 / 049.02]
- Brorna persuaded G’elde to spare the sleeping party and forgo questioning; G’elde then plane-shifted them to the slave pits. [ch 049 / 049.02]

## Last Observed State
Brorna was last directly observed in her chambers, successfully persuading G’elde to send the unconscious party to the slave pits without killing or questioning them. The transfer is confirmed; Brorna’s subsequent location and activity are not established. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 049 / 049.02]

## Relationships
- **Vhaeraun:** His agent and longtime protector of his cult, working with him against Lolth. [ch 048 / 048.07]
- **The party:** A covert ally and infiltration planner; they agreed to her staged capture and relied on her arrangements for recovering their equipment. [ch 049 / 049.01]
- **G’elde:** Someone Brorna can influence through apparent loyalty to Lolth; G’elde accepts her counsel as wise during the staged capture. [ch 049 / 049.02]
- **Lolth:** The target of Brorna’s concealed opposition over a thousand years. [ch 048 / 048.07]
- **Talaryn:** Personally disliked by Brorna for her fixation on power. [ch 048 / 048.10]
- **House Zollazo:** Brorna claims sufficient influence to threaten withdrawal of her support for its advancement. [ch 049 / 049.02]
- **Drider contact:** An inside contact shared with Vhaeraun, assigned to deliver the miniature chest in the slave pits. [ch 049 / 049.01]
