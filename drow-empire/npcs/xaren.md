## Identity
Xaren is a Drow general with new facial scarring following his recent assassination by Corrin. His house affiliation is undisclosed; he refused Rimardo’s request to identify it. [ch 047 / 047.01]

## Personality and Motivations
Revenge against Corrin drives Xaren’s hostility. He displays boastful confidence and threatens repeated killing and raising of the party, but becomes openly frustrated when his magic fails and his forces lose. [ch 047 / 047.01; ch 047 / 047.02]

He prepares extensive magical protection and healing support, uses concealment to evade attacks, and retreats when his mages are defeated. [ch 047 / 047.02]

## History with the Party
- Xaren, a Drow general whom Corrin recently assassinated, returned with new facial scarring and ambushed the party with Drow mages in the open sector. [ch 047 / 047.01]
- Xaren claimed House Illal had no presence in the open sector and refused to identify his own house, threatening to kill and repeatedly raise the party. [ch 047 / 047.01]
- Corrin triggered Xaren's death ward, and 12 owl familiars healed him. [ch 047 / 047.02]
- Xaren used a gem-shaped item to cast hold person on Corrin; Corrin was unaffected under foresight. [ch 047 / 047.02]
- Xaren shot Corrin and hid beyond KP's line of sight, thwarting the plan for KP to finish him with lightning. [ch 047 / 047.02]
- Xaren later struck Rimardo, and owls healed Xaren further. [ch 047 / 047.02]
- After his mages were defeated, Xaren escaped into a sewer, vowing revenge; Rimardo and Corrin were dissatisfied with his escape. [ch 047 / 047.02]

## Last Observed State
Xaren escaped alive into a sewer after his mages were defeated, vowing revenge. His destination beyond the sewer and subsequent condition are not established in the summaries. [ch 047 / 047.02]

## Relationships
- **Corrin:** His recent assassin and principal target of revenge; Xaren directs his mages against Corrin when his own disabling magic fails. [ch 047 / 047.01; ch 047 / 047.02]
- **Drow mages:** His subordinates in the ambush. They appear bored during his threats and are subsequently defeated by the party. [ch 047 / 047.01; ch 047 / 047.02]
- **Owl familiars:** Repeatedly provide Xaren with healing during combat; their ownership is not specified. [ch 047 / 047.02]
- **Rimardo and KP:** Combat opponents; Xaren strikes Rimardo and evades KP’s planned lightning attack by hiding outside his line of sight. [ch 047 / 047.02]
- **House Illal:** Xaren claims it has no presence in the open sector and dismisses its willingness to support the party. His own relationship with the house is not established. [ch 047 / 047.01]
