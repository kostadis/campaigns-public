## Identity
Tethir is the largest of four hobgoblins encountered at the slave pens, distinguished by a very straight back and a severe demeanor. [ch 031 / 031.05]

## Personality and Motivations
Tethir admires tactical competence and perceived ruthlessness. He treats slaves as expendable resources and interprets an order to move them as a military sacrifice. His readiness to reach for his blade when mocked suggests sensitivity to insults; the promise of mentorship eases his hostility. [ch 031 / 031.05]

## History with the Party
- The party encountered Tethir, the largest of four hobgoblins at the slave pens, standing with a very straight back and a severe demeanor. [ch 031 / 031.05]
- Tethir praised the conquest of the citadel, swore fealty to Bayard, and offered to slaughter the slaves. [ch 031 / 031.05]
- Tethir interpreted Bayard's order to lead the slaves to the portal as a plan to use them as a meat shield during an invasion of the Drow strongholds. [ch 031 / 031.05]
- Tethir reached for his blade after Bayard mocked him; Rimardo's explanation and Bayard's promise to mentor him eased the tension. [ch 031 / 031.05]
- Tethir disparaged the party's allies as too short and too small; KP and Corrin were offended but kept silent. [ch 031 / 031.05]
