## Identity
Alice is Akia's boss in the WLF and a contact for the party's work as independent contractors. [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.05]

## Personality and Motivations
Alice shows approval of the party's work and offers hospitality. She takes pride in the WLF's role in stopping the slave trade. [ch 008 / 008.05] Her frantic delivery of news about the army outside Fax shows immediate alarm at the military situation. [ch 008 / 008.06]

## History with the Party
- Akia introduced the party to Alice, her boss in the WLF. [ch 008 / 008.05]
- Alice praised the party as independent contractors and credited the WLF with stopping the slave trade; Rimardo added recognition for their independent contractors. [ch 008 / 008.05]
- Alice promised to see about getting the party into Lundbeams the next day. [ch 008 / 008.05]
- Alice offered brioche with butter and took the party to Halete, where they spent the night. [ch 008 / 008.05]
- Alice arrived frantically and reported armed camps outside Fax commanded by martially trained Drow. [ch 008 / 008.06]
- After Akia whispered to her, Alice reported Tolub's announcement that Fax must create an empire as a buffer against the Orc and Greyhawk empires. [ch 008 / 008.06]
- Alice whispered to Akia, who then reported that Lundbeams required the WLF to help the Army of Fax and not resist it. [ch 008 / 008.06]
- Alice was mentioned during the party's audience with Lundbeams, who knew them as having worked for Alice and Halete and called that a mistake. [ch 008 / 008.09]

## Last Observed State
Alice was last directly observed at Halete's smithy, urgently reporting armed camps outside Fax under Drow command and exchanging whispered information with Akia. Through Akia, she relayed Lundbeams's demand that the WLF assist the Army of Fax and offer no resistance. [ch 008 / 008.06]

She was subsequently mentioned by Lundbeams during the party's audience; this does not establish Alice's presence there. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 008 / 008.09]

## Relationships
- **Akia:** Alice is her boss in the WLF; they exchange urgent information privately. [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.06]
- **The party:** Alice treats them as independent contractors, approves of their work, and offers practical assistance and hospitality. [ch 008 / 008.05]
- **Halete:** Alice brings the party to Halete for the night; Lundbeams later associates both Alice and Halete with the party's previous work. [ch 008 / 008.05; ch 008 / 008.09]
- **Lundbeams:** Alice offers to seek access to her for the party and relays her directive through Akia. Lundbeams later calls the party's work for Alice and Halete a mistake. [ch 008 / 008.05; ch 008 / 008.06; ch 008 / 008.09]
