## Identity
Halete is a blacksmith in Fax, a resistance contact, and a representative of the Wild Coast Liberation Front (WLF). She also commanded a detachment of WLF fighters. [ch 006 / entry; ch 013 / entry; ch 014 / 014.01]

## Personality and Motivations
Halete believes that freedom from outside powers requires submission to the WLF and its imposed government. Her commitment to liberation therefore includes support for authoritarian control of the Wild Coast. [ch 014 / 014.01]

She seeks an alliance against Fax and the Drow. Her latest appearance shows a self-important manner and irritation when KP interprets her announcement of an opportunity as a business proposition. [ch 014 / 014.01; ch 020 / 020.04]

## History with the Party
- Branigan Cartwright named Halete, a smithy in Fax, as a contact who could lead the party deeper into the resistance. [ch 006 / 006.02]
- Halete sold the party replacement equipment after their gear was taken at Fax's walls. [ch 006 / 006.04]
- Halete told the party that the resistance's cleric had worked for Tolub as sheriff in a village in Nulb. [ch 006 / 006.04]
- Alice took the party to Halete, where they spent the night. [ch 008 / 008.05]
- Halete directed KP to Garett when he wanted a lightning bolt spell. [ch 008 / 008.07]
- Halete questioned Lundbeams' changed stance and identified Vallaki as the army's target. [ch 008 / 008.08]
- Halete did not appear in Chapter 012; Captain Amos described a WLF troop leader who sounded disturbingly like her, without confirming her identity. [ch 012 / entry; ch 012 / 012.04]
- Rimardo healed Halete outside Fhuu; Amos attacked her over her alleged alliance with the Drow, and Bayard restrained him and insisted on a proper trial. [ch 013 / 013.03]
- Halete claimed that the Drow and Lundbeams had betrayed the WLF and that most of its members had died in a massacre. [ch 013 / 013.03]
- Halete recommended the Dwarves of Darnagal as potential help; Rimardo misheard “Duergar,” offered to have her killed, and was corrected by Halete. [ch 013 / 013.05]
- Before the Primus, Halete identified herself as a Wild Coast Liberation Front representative and declared its dedication to the freedom of all peoples. [ch 013 / 013.08]
- Questioned under a zone of truth, Halete revealed that her vision of freedom required subservience to the WLF and its imposed government. [ch 014 / 014.01]
- Halete revealed that Lundbeams was being blackmailed because the Drow held her daughter hostage. [ch 014 / 014.01]
- Halete explained that Nimh sent her WLF detachment into battle first and withheld support as punishment for Lundbeams' interference at Vallaki. [ch 014 / 014.01]
- Halete and three other WLF members survived the massacre by pretending to die, then went to Fhuu seeking the independent contractors and an alliance against Fax. [ch 014 / 014.01]
- In Chapter 020, Halete appeared as the local WLF representative and delivered her leader's message urging an attack on Fax while the Drow were cut off. [ch 020 / 020.04]

## Last Observed State
Halete is alive and acting as the local WLF representative when she delivers her leader’s message proposing an attack on Fax while the Drow are cut off. She appears in person during the planning discussion with the party, Primus and Sir Ryton; her subsequent movements are not established in that scene. [ch 020 / 020.04]

## Relationships
- **The party:** Halete provides equipment, lodging and contacts, but their trust is strained by her alleged collaboration with the Drow and her account of the massacre. Their questioning reveals the coercive government she supports. [ch 006 / 006.04; ch 008 / 008.05; ch 008 / 008.07; ch 013 / 013.03; ch 014 / 014.01]
- **Lundbeams:** Halete initially regards her changed stance as inconsistent with her beliefs, later accuses her of betrayal, and subsequently reveals that the Drow held Lundbeams’ daughter hostage. [ch 008 / 008.08; ch 013 / 013.03; ch 014 / 014.01]
- **Captain Amos:** He accuses Halete of betraying promised aid and attacks her over her alleged alliance with the Drow. [ch 013 / 013.03]
- **Bayard:** He restrains Amos and insists that Halete receive a proper trial, despite doubting her explanation. [ch 013 / 013.03]
- **Rimardo:** He heals Halete but remains suspicious of her; after mishearing Darnagal as Duergar, he offers to have her killed. [ch 013 / 013.03; ch 013 / 013.05]
- **Nimh and the Drow:** Halete identifies them as betrayers. According to her account, Nimh deliberately withheld support from her detachment, causing the massacre she and three others survived. [ch 013 / 013.03; ch 014 / 014.01]
