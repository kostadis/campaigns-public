## Identity
Talaryn is the Empress of the Drow Empire, associated with House Zollazo, whose rivals sought her assassination and her house’s displacement. [ch 047 / entry; ch 047 / 047.03; ch 047 / 047.05]

## Personality and Motivations
Talaryn seeks to preserve her rule through force: she prepared a campaign of terror to purge threats from the safety of her palace. [ch 048 / 048.01]

Her theatrical entrance and taunting challenge to the attackers show confidence in her own defenses and those of the empire. Brorna describes her as obsessed with power; this is Brorna’s assessment. [ch 048 / 048.04; ch 048 / 048.10]

She distrusts Drow as personal protectors, according to the Chamberlain, and relies instead on a hobgoblin honor guard inside the palace. [ch 048 / 048.02]

## History with the Party
- Talaryn is identified as the Empress; she does not appear in chapter 047. [ch 047 / entry]
- House Do’ty accused Talaryn and House Zollazo of allowing Vhaeraunites to prosper and offered Sir Ryton in exchange for killing her and destroying her house. [ch 047 / 047.03]
- Naerth requested Talaryn’s death on behalf of House Illal, offering a truce with Freedahl and weakened palace entrance guards. [ch 047 / 047.05]
- House Noquarth offered 30,000 GP each to kill Talaryn and leave a note crediting the house; Rimardo rejected the price as insufficient. [ch 047 / 047.06; ch 047 / 047.07]
- Bayard refused to submit to Talaryn and chose House Do’ty’s offer, saving Sir Ryton. [ch 047 / 047.08]
- Talaryn was marshaling a response from the palace, preparing a campaign of terror against threats to her rule. [ch 048 / 048.01]
- Selzyt offered information about the palace dungeon in exchange for Bayard’s promise to kill Talaryn and spare whoever they found in the dungeon; Bayard agreed with qualifications. [ch 048 / 048.02]
- The party attacked a decoy, which Rimardo transformed into a frog; the real Talaryn then entered protected by mirror image. [ch 048 / 048.03; ch 048 / 048.04]
- Talaryn fought with finger of death, chromatic orb and glyphs, healed a guard, and killed Rimardo during the battle. [ch 048 / 048.04; ch 048 / 048.05]
- Talaryn healed herself completely after KP’s cone of cold nearly defeated her; after further attacks from the party, Bayard struck her and she died. [ch 048 / 048.06]
- A later scene complicates that death account: Rimardo said Talaryn was not dead, and Vhaeraun repeatedly stabbed her body. [ch 048 / 048.10]
- G’elde subsequently explicitly declared the Empress dead and identified the sleeping party as her killers. [ch 049 / 049.02]

## Last Observed State
Dead according to G’elde’s explicit declaration in chapter 049, where Talaryn is spoken of rather than shown acting. G’elde identifies the sleeping party as her killers. [ch 049 / 049.02]

The preceding accounts differ on the timing: Bayard’s blow is described as killing her, but Rimardo later says she is not dead, after which Vhaeraun repeatedly stabs her body. G’elde’s subsequent declaration establishes the latest reported status. [ch 048 / 048.06; ch 048 / 048.10; ch 049 / 049.02]

## Relationships
- **House Zollazo:** Her associated house and political base; House Do’ty demanded both her death and the house’s destruction. [ch 047 / 047.03]
- **House Do’ty, House Illal, and House Noquarth:** Rival houses seeking her assassination. Their stated grievances concern religious toleration, military policy, and disrupted trade respectively. [ch 047 / 047.03; ch 047 / 047.05; ch 047 / 047.07]
- **Bayard and the party:** Armed opponents. Bayard refused submission to her and backed House Do’ty; the party subsequently fought her in the palace. [ch 047 / 047.08; ch 048 / 048.04]
- **Rimardo:** A combat opponent whom she killed during the palace battle. [ch 048 / 048.05]
- **Selzyt and Vhaeraun:** Selzyt secured Bayard’s qualified promise to kill her; Vhaeraun later repeatedly stabbed her body after Rimardo disputed her death. [ch 048 / 048.02; ch 048 / 048.10]
- **Brorna:** Expressed personal dislike of Talaryn and characterized her as obsessed with power. [ch 048 / 048.10]
- **G’elde and Lolth:** G’elde feared that Lolth might punish House Zollazo for Talaryn’s faults. Brorna described Talaryn as Lolth’s favorite while persuading G’elde to deliver the party to Lolth. [ch 049 / 049.02]
