## Identity
Aeglossil is a vampire who manifests as a disoriented female elf when awakened from a statue-like stasis [ch 052 / 052.03]. She is identified by Vardis as a vampire immediately upon her transformation from a white stone statue [ch 052 / 052.03].

## Personality and Motivations
Aeglossil operates on predatory instinct, biting Orsik to feed for the first time in eons upon awakening [ch 052 / 052.03; ch 052 / entry]. She displays immediate fear and aggression toward divine symbols of protection, snarling when Vardis presents the symbol of Corellon [ch 052 / 052.03; ch 052 / entry]. Her demeanor shifts rapidly from disorientation to violent aggression.

## History with the Party
- Aeglossil appeared as a white stone statue of a sleeping elven woman on a raised platform in an excavated tomb decorated with elven nature bas-reliefs, alongside two duergar who were cleaning it [ch 052 / 052.03].
- Thistle approached the statue after feeling a "peculiar mind" reach out, and kissed it, causing the statue to transform into a disoriented female elf [ch 052 / 052.03].
- Vardis immediately identified the figure as a vampire upon her transformation [ch 052 / 052.03].
- Thistle introduced the vampire to Thuul as "my new friend, Aeglossil" [ch 052 / 052.03].
- Aeglossil grappled Orsik and bit him, feeding for the first time in eons [ch 052 / 052.03; ch 052 / entry].
- When Vardis presented the symbol of Corellon, Aeglossil snarled in fear at the power of The Protector [ch 052 / 052.03; ch 052 / entry].
- Unla Key cast a banishment spell against Aeglossil, twisting time and space, which resulted in a loud bang and her disappearance from the party's sight [ch 052 / 052.03; ch 052 / entry].
- The remaining Derro stayed in the tomb, waiting for their vampire mistress to return [ch 052 / 052.03].
- Status not established in the summaries; she was banished into another realm, and the party fled without confirming her final state [ch 052 / entry; ch 052 / 052.03].
