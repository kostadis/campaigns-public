## Identity
Tapouach is a market merchant located in the Central Market who specializes in selling apples, described by Lord Drylund as someone "who sells the best apples" [ch 079 / 079.03].

## Personality and Motivations
Tapouach prioritizes social standing and political alliances over material loss or immediate justice. After noticing his purse and apple stolen by Unla Key, he suppressed any negative reaction and smiled upon realizing the thief was a friend of Lord Drylund [ch 079 / 079.03].

## History with the Party
*   Tapouach is a market merchant introduced by Lord Drylund in the Central Market [ch 079 / 079.03].
*   The party member Unla Key stole Tapouach's purse, containing only 23 coppers, and an apple [ch 079 / 079.03].
*   Tapouach noticed the theft but smiled upon realizing Unla Key was a friend of Lord Drylund [ch 079 / 079.03].

## Last Observed State
Tapouach was present in the Central Market when Lord Drylund introduced him to the party. During the introduction, he was pickpocketed by Unla Key, who took a purse containing 23 coppers and an apple. Tapouach noticed the theft but reacted by smiling rather than pursuing the thief or alerting authorities, likely due to Unla Key’s connection to Drylund [ch 079 / 079.03].

## Relationships
*   **Lord Drylund**: Drylund serves as the connection that grants Tapouach political cover. Tapouach’s decision to smile at his own theft was directly contingent on the thief’s affiliation with Drylund [ch 079 / 079.03].
*   **Unla Key**: Unla Key stole from Tapouach, taking 23 coppers and an apple. Despite being the victim, Tapouach did not act against Unla Key [ch 079 / 079.03].
