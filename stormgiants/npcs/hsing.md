## Identity
Hsing is a golden-scaled pseudodragon with milky irises or milky-white eyes, described by Unla Key as blind [ch 007 / entry; ch 010 / entry]. He is the patron of Azam, though the ownership dynamic is explicitly reversed by Vardis and recalled by Wallace: "Azam belongs to the pseudo-dragon" [ch 009 / 009.01]. Hsing serves as the emissary of the SEER, the top spymaster of the Lords’ Alliance, a role he has held for some time prior to the current campaign events [ch 009 / 009.01; ch 010 / entry]. He appears to be ancient, referring to his own naps and age, and possesses the ability to teleport and conjure mental images [ch 010 / 010.08; ch 019 / 019.03].

## Personality and Motivations
Hsing speaks in a "quiet old voice" but can shout to command silence, as seen when he barked "AZAM!" to stop Azam's outburst, noting that the adventurers could not distinguish between a lizard and him [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.01]. He prioritizes the SEER's orders over individual initiative or personal gain, refusing to pay Orsik more than the SEER commands despite Orsik's demands [ch 013 / 013.06]. He displays moments of panic regarding magical threats, specifically fearing a Lich when discussing the Wizard of the Crag, before correcting his misunderstanding [ch 013 / 013.06].

While primarily a coercive agent of the Lords’ Alliance—willing to conjure images of death to intimidate allies into compliance [ch 019 / 019.03]—Hsing possesses a capacity for self-awareness regarding his employer's nature. During a dispute in Parnast, he conceded to the townspeople and Orsik that the SEER is a spymaster who "do lie" and should not be trusted, aligning momentarily with Orsik's skepticism [ch 023 / 023.05; ch 023 / moment]. He offers cryptic, riddle-laden guidance, such as instructing the party to find the drake Thuul by looking for "the yellow sky" [ch 007 / 007.07].

## Relationships
**Azam**: Azam is Hsing's host or servant, not his owner [ch 009 / 009.01]. Hsing frequently commands Azam, who displays extreme loyalty and deference, even when Hsing silences him [ch 014 / 014.01].
**The SEER**: Hsing is her emissary and executes her will, including collecting books and commissioning quests [ch 010 / 010.08]. He defends her authority and gender-neutral pronouns ("she, he or they") while acknowledging her dishonesty [ch 014 / 014.01; ch 023 / 023.05].
**Orsik**: Hsing has a fraught relationship with Orsik, marked by payment disputes and coercion. Orsik frequently threatens Hsing, and Hsing responds with intimidation or by siding with the town against Orsik [ch 013 / 013.06; ch 023 / 023.04]. Despite this, Hsing acknowledged Orsik's point about spymasters in chapter 23 [ch 023 / 023.05].
**The Party (Vardis, Wallace, Thistle, Unla Key)**: Hsing assigns them tasks via teleportation, often with little warning [ch 010 / 010.08]. Vardis provides context on the Hsing-Azam dynamic, and Wallace warns the party of the SEER's reach, influencing Hsing's interactions with them [ch 009 / 009.01; ch 013 / 013.08]. Thistle has openly criticized Hsing's "game" and rewards [ch 013 / 013.08].
