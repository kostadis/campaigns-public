## Identity
Kaelen Sarssir is a playwright and the keeper of the Talking Troll [ch 024 / entry]. He describes himself as a "method actor" [ch 024 / 024.05]. He is identified as a "young man" by Lord Zymorven [ch 064 / 064.08] and is the author of the script that inadvertently summoned Bob Saget [ch 055 / 055.07; ch 056 / 056.03]. He owns the Talking Troll property rather than renting it [ch 035 / 035.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Kaelen is driven by a desire for fame and artistic recognition, aiming to produce a hit play to fund a theater after being inspired by *The Inn of Shadows* [ch 026 / entry]. He claims to use his interactions with customers as "material for my off-High Road production" [ch 024 / 024.05]. Beneath his theatrical demeanor, he displays ruthlessness; he turns "ice cold" when money is discussed [ch 026 / 026.03] and blackmailed Darz Helgar into committing a theft [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.11; ch 025 / 025.07].

His motivations escalate to megalomania when he secretly plans the play as a summoning ritual intended to massacre theatergoers as revenge for their lack of admiration [ch 035 / entry; ch 035 / 035.07]. The evidence indicates his original plan was the "mass murder of the entire town of Triboar" [ch 064 / 064.08]. When the ritual resulted in a hit play instead of his intended destruction, he shouted that he would be famous [ch 035 / moment; ch 035 / 035.07]. He has also shown a willingness to borrow vast sums from dangerous entities like the Hand of Yartar and the Zhentarim to fund his projects [ch 034 / entry; ch 034 / 034.06].

## History with the Party
*   Kaelen Sarssir is the keeper of the Talking Troll and self-described "method actor" who inflated Uncle Joon's tab and sold sparkling wine of dubious provenance; Darz named him as the man who blackmailed him into the Lionshield theft [ch 024 / entry].
*   Kaelen greets the party at the Talking Troll, confirms he is an actor, and manages Uncle Joon's debt, claiming it is "material for my off-High Road production" while selling the party wine from a hidden dimension [ch 024 / 024.05].
*   Darz Helgar confesses to the theft of Lionshield Coster goods, identifying Kaelen as the one who threatened to reveal his past to the Lord Protector unless he committed the crime [ch 024 / 024.11; ch 025 / 025.07].
*   Orsik and the party visit the Talking Troll, where Kaelen asks them to review and act in his script; Orsik lures him into the cellar under the pretense of a performance [ch 026 / 026.02; ch 026 / moment].
*   In the cellar, Kaelen confesses that he stole the Lionshield key to fund a theater after seeing *The Inn of Shadows*, and turns "ice cold" when money is discussed; he surrenders the key and a bottle of Champagne du le Stomp to Orsik [ch 026 / entry; ch 026 / 026.03; ch 026 / moment].
*   Orsik establishes a "loan-sharking arrangement" with Kaelen, trading silence about the theft for a 70/30 split of the profits, while Kaelen dreams of a 5000 gold investment to fix the roof and hire magic users [ch 026 / 026.03].
*   Urgala brings Urgala her drink at the Talking Troll, and Cindell rejects opera admissions as payment because she is "Not a big fan of theater and Kaelen's work" [ch 027 / entry; ch 027 / 027.12].
*   Kaelen commits to a production date of the "5th day of the third tenday of Flamerule" and asks the party to bring Cindell to dinner to help ticket sales; Orsik refuses [ch 028 / entry; ch 028 / 028.02].
*   The party discovers Kaelen has borrowed 5000 gold pieces from the Hand of Yartar, the Zhentarim, and local criminals, claiming the party would cover the debt; Urgala warns that these lenders will pursue the party if Kaelen defaults [ch 034 / entry; ch 034 / 034.06].
*   Kaelen delivered the play script, which Urgala notes has messy dialogue and story, while the party learns Kaelen bought the Talking Troll property rather than renting it [ch 035 / 035.01].
*   Kaelen rewrites the script in an archaic, incomprehensible common tongue, requiring Unla Key to intervene with a "play within a play" structure to make it coherent [ch 035 / 035.02].
*   Kaelen secretly planned the play as a summoning ritual and a massacre of the theatergoers as revenge on the public for not admiring his genius, but the party's improvements turned it into a hit; he then shouts that he will be famous [ch 035 / entry; ch 035 / 035.07].
*   Kaelen served wine to Urlam Stockspool and his Zhentarim crew at the Talking Troll, with Orsik paying for the rounds [ch 037 / entry; ch 037 / 037.01; ch 037 / 037.02].
*   Bob Saget informs the party that Kaelen wrote the play that summoned him, but refuses them seats for a performance, stating their presence would be a distraction [ch 055 / 055.07].
*   Bob Saget identifies Kaelen as "The fool Kaelen" whose summoning spell brought him to the Prime Material [ch 056 / 056.03].
- Kaelen is a playwright whose originally planned mass murder of Triboar was thwarted by the 3HPP [ch 064 / entry; ch 064 / 064.08].
- His summoning ritual was partly thwarted and resulted in the appearance of Bob Saget rather than the intended destructive force [ch 064 / entry; ch 064 / 064.07].
- The party discusses his fate, with Lord Zymorven advocating for interrogation rather than execution, while Urgala argues he should have been killed immediately for the attempted genocide [ch 064 / 064.08].
- The party ultimately decides to leave Kaelen alone in his dusty house in Silverymoon to avoid alerting Bob Saget and losing the element of surprise [ch 064 / entry; ch 064 / 064.09].

## Last Observed State
Kaelen is currently located in a dusty house in Silverymoon [ch 064 / entry; ch 064 / 064.09]. Thistle reports that Kaelen is sitting inside, tapping his fingers, with the door unopened for some time; Thistle notes, "Something is very wrong with him" [ch 064 / 064.08]. The party decides to leave him alone in this state to avoid alerting Bob Saget and to maintain the element of surprise for their upcoming attack [ch 064 / entry; ch 064 / 064.09].
