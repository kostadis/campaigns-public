## Identity
Jacoby Hysstryn is the brother of Mirak Hysstryn, who has been missing for ten years [ch 024 / 024.06]. He has a brother named Persson Hysstryn [ch 024 / 024.06]. He is not of noble status, which he believes contributes to his family's lack of influence [ch 024 / 024.10].

## Personality and Motivations
Jacoby is deeply concerned with finding his missing brother and is frustrated by the lack of official action, particularly from the Lord Protector [ch 024 / 024.06; ch 024 / 024.10]. He is suspicious of those who might deceive him regarding the investigation, demanding tangible proof before offering information or resources [ch 024 / 024.06]. He possesses a defiant streak, openly mocking the Lord Protector’s dismissal of their plight by imitating her mannerisms and threats to the amusement of locals [ch 024 / 024.06]. He insists on sobriety in the face of accusations to the contrary [ch 024 / 024.06].

## History with the Party
- Jacoby Hysstryn walked into the tavern with his brother Persson, upset that no one was looking for their missing brother, Mirak Hysstryn [ch 024 / 024.06].
- Jacoby imitated the Lord Protector, Darathra, claiming Mirak "ran away with a lady friend" and threatening jail time, to the amusement of the clientele [ch 024 / 024.06].
- He offered a magical bow as a trade for information about his brother [ch 024 / 024.06].
- When Orsik suggested they had been drinking, Jacoby snarled that they were sober [ch 024 / 024.06].
- After Thistle offered help, Jacoby muttered that Urgala Meltimer has always denied her involvement with Mirak [ch 024 / 024.06].
- Seeing the party's interest in potentially deceiving them, Jacoby demanded proof [ch 024 / 024.06].
- Jacoby was present at the Tower when the party confronted the Lord Protector regarding Mirak's case [ch 024 / 024.10].
- Darathra Shendrel was furious with Jacoby and Persson for their "wild goose chase," leading the brothers to blame their family's lack of noble status for being ignored [ch 024 / 024.10].

## Last Observed State
Jacoby was present at the Tower when the party confronted the Lord Protector, Darathra Shendrel, regarding the murder investigation and Mirak’s case [ch 024 / 024.10]. He exploded at Darathra over a decade of being ignored, blaming their family's lack of noble status for the inaction [ch 024 / 024.10]. Following Darathra's dismissal of the crowd (except Tolmara) and her promise to have a cleric cast *speak with the dead* the next day, Jacoby was dismissed from the room [ch 024 / 024.10]. Status not established in the summaries beyond this scene.
