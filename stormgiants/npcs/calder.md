## Identity
Calder is a halfling shopkeeper operating in Parnast [ch 013 / entry; ch 021 / entry]. He is described as a "small halfling" and is known locally as "Calder the Halfling" [ch 013 / entry; ch 021 / entry]. His shop explicitly operates outside the Lionshield Coster network [ch 013 / 013.07].

## Personality and Motivations
Calder expresses open resentment toward the Lionshield Coster, stating they "are not welcome in Parnast" [ch 013 / 013.07]. He appears to be a struggling merchant, referred to as a "poor local merchant" forced to rely on Zhentarim physical transportation of goods due to the unavailability of transdimensional markets in Parnast [ch 021 / entry; ch 021 / 021.06]. His demeanor appears apologetic or resigned regarding his inventory limitations, telling Thistle, "Sorry about that!" regarding the lack of magic for sale [ch 021 / entry; ch 021 / 021.06].

## History with the Party
*   Calder is a Parnast shopkeeper described as "Calder the Halfling" and a "small halfling" [ch 013 / entry; ch 021 / entry].
*   Unla Key purchased a sword from Calder, who noted that his shop is unaffiliated with the Lionshield Coster [ch 013 / 013.07].
*   Calder was identified as the "poor local merchant" in Parnast, forced by circumstances to rely on Zhentarim physical transportation of goods rather than transdimensional markets [ch 021 / 021.06].
*   Calder has no magic available for sale to the party [ch 021 / 021.06].

## Last Observed State
In the most recent evidence, Calder was spoken of by Chandra as having "no magic to sell" because he is constrained to local physical trade rather than the transdimensional markets used by the Lionshield Coster [ch 021 / 021.06]. Chandra quoted Calder’s previous excuse of "Sorry about that!" regarding this lack of inventory [ch 021 / 021.06].
