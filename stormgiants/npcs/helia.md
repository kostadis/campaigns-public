## Identity
Helia is a Seelie Dryad responsible for guarding the easternmost tree of the Grandfather Tree. [ch 048 / 048.09; ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.04] He was assigned this duty as a punishment by Queen Titania after he and Halisa offended the Queen at a ball; specifically, Halisa spoke freely and Queen Titania demanded the presence of Helia, whom Helia claims he dumped immediately. [ch 048 / 048.09; ch 050 / 050.04] He is physically capable of flight, as he flew off from his tree upon being released. [ch 050 / 050.07]

## Personality and Motivations
Helia presents himself as indifferent and hostile toward Halisa, repeatedly insisting he was not her date and had dumped her on the spot. [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.04] He attempts to provoke others through insults, specifically targeting the disguised Orsik-Thornacious in hopes of triggering a reaction. [ch 050 / 050.04] His primary motivation during the encounter was to remain stationary unless compelled to move; he offered passage to the party only if they could force Halisa to apologize, effectively stalling his own departure or seeking a reason to break the stalemate. [ch 050 / 050.04] Despite his protests of indifference, his behavior reveals a complex attachment or rivalry with Halisa; he became upset when she left, and the other dryads noted that he and Halisa had "cursed each other" through their mutual belief in a false condition set by Titania. [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.07]

## History with the Party
- Helia is mentioned by Thistle as one of two Seelie Dryads (along with Halisa) who were punished by Queen Titania to protect the Grandfather Tree [ch 048 / 048.09].
- Helia is described as being "punished for protecting this tree for some indeterminately long period" [ch 048 / 048.09].
- Helia is encountered by the party at the easternmost tree, where he is delighted to see "Thornacious" (Orsik in disguise) and immediately unleashes insults [ch 050 / 050.04].
- Helia explains that he was Halisa’s plus-one at a ball, but when Halisa spoke freely, Queen Titania demanded the plus-one appear; Helia insists he was not her date and "dumped her on the spot," yet remains stuck guarding the tree [ch 050 / 050.04].
- Helia refuses to grant the party permission to pass unless they can "convince Halisa to apologize" [ch 050 / 050.04].
- The old dryad at the northern tree refuses permission unless Helia and Halisa stop "arguing" and "bickering" [ch 050 / 050.05].
- Sleth at the western tree expresses frustration that Helia and Halisa are not caring for their trees, noting Helia’s neglect leads to "terrible imbalance" [ch 050 / 050.06].
- The party realizes Queen Titania lied about the terms of exile; Helia and Halisa were told that if one left, the other would be safe, but the one leaving would die, yet they hate each other too much to sacrifice themselves [ch 050 / 050.07].
- Helia becomes upset when Halisa is freed, despite his previous insistence that he does not care for her [ch 050 / 050.07].
- Helia is told by the disguised Orsik-Thornacious that he is pardoned and can leave; Helia stares for a moment and then flies off [ch 050 / 050.07].
- The old dryad confirms that Helia and Halisa had "cursed each other" through their mutual belief in the lie, rather than being bound by a true curse from Titania [ch 050 / 050.07].
- Queen Titania, via a sprite emissary, commends the party’s "subterfuge and subtlety" in freeing Helia and Halisa to advance their own goals [ch 051 / 051.04].

## Last Observed State
Helia was at the easternmost tree of the Grandfather Tree when approached by the party, who were disguised or acting under false pretenses. [ch 050 / 050.04] He engaged with them, complaining about his eternal exile, and offered: "I would give you permission if you could convince Halisa to apologize!" [ch 050 / 050.04] When Orsik-Thornacious (disguised as Prince Thornacious) announced a royal pardon and cure, Halisa departed first; Helia appeared upset that she was leaving. [ch 050 / 050.07] He was then told he could also leave. Helia stared for a moment before flying off. [ch 050 / 050.07] Subsequent confirmation from the other dryads and Queen Titania indicates he is no longer bound to the tree. [ch 050 / 050.07; ch 051 / 051.04] His specific location after flying off is not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
**Halisa**: Helia’s primary relationship is with Halisa, a fellow dryad guarding another tree. They were punished together by Queen Titania after an incident at a ball. [ch 048 / 048.09] Helia claims he dumped her immediately and that they hate each other, yet he became visibly upset when she departed upon hearing of her pardon. [ch 050 / 050.04; ch 050 / 050.07] The old dryad noted they could not find the "love" to sacrifice themselves for one another due to their animosity. [ch 050 / 050.05]

**Queen Titania**: Titania imposed the punishment on Helia and Halisa. [ch 048 / 048.09] The narrative reveals Titania lied to them, stating that if one left, the other would be safe but the leaver would die, a condition that trapped them through their mutual stubbornness. [ch 050 / 050.05; ch 050 / 050.07] Titania later commended the party for freeing them via "subterfuge and subtlety." [ch 051 / 051.04]

**Sleth**: Sleth, the dryad of the western tree, expressed frustration with Helia and Halisa, blaming their neglect and argumentative behavior for creating a "terrible imbalance" in the trees. [ch 050 / 050.06]

**The Old Dryad**: The dryad of the northern tree was exhausted by Helia and Halisa’s "bickering" and refused passage to the party until it stopped. [ch 050 / 050.05]
