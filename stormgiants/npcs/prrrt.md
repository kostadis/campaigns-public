## Identity
Prrrt is a young Galeb Duhr serving as one of the stones of a "great pyramid" of rock. [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.01]

## Personality and Motivations
Prrrt is described by the elder Galeb Duhr, Grrrk, as "young and optimistic." [ch 002 / entry] Unlike his stoic elder, Prrrt is prone to complaining, specifically about the discomfort of his feet and an infestation of rock mites. [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.01] He displays a pragmatic honesty regarding loot, clarifying when the party thanked him that the treasure they found "Wasn't ours!" [ch 002 / 002.03; ch 002 / 002.04] He appears eager to help, sharing information about a dead giant’s testimony after a fairy assisted him with his mites. [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.01] His optimism regarding the party’s survival proved accurate when they ascended the pyramid without being "zapped" like the giant had been. [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.01]

## Relationships
**Grrrk:** An older Galeb Duhr who shares Prrrt’s post as a stone in the pyramid. Grrrk finds Prrrt irritating due to his fidgeting and complaints, contrasting him with the silence of his own generation. [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.01]
**The Fairy:** The fairy tickled Grrrk’s nose, but Prrrt interacted with the fairy to get help with his rock mites, subsequently revealing the dead giant’s words to the group. [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.01]
