## Identity
- Mirak Hysstryn: brother of Jacoby and Persson Hysstryn, missing for 10 years when the party first heard of him. [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.06]
- Husband of Tolmara, whose house is the Six Windows Inn. [ch 024 / 024.08; ch 025 / entry]
- He never appeared in person. Every scene only mentions him: his brothers, Urgala and the party speak of him, and his remains are found. [ch 024 / 024.06; ch 024 / 024.08; ch 024 / 024.09; ch 025 / 025.01; ch 025 / 025.02]
- The summaries use both "Mirak Hysstryn" and "Mirak". [ch 024 / entry; ch 025 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
- The summaries give almost nothing first-hand, because Mirak never acts or speaks on the page.
- He had an affair with Urgala Meltimer. Urgala says they "were involved for a while" after her wife disappeared, and that "Mirak had no other woman than me." [ch 024 / 024.08; ch 025 / entry]
- His wife claimed he "ran off with one of his lady friends." This is her claim, as the brothers report it. It is not established as fact. [ch 024 / 024.06]
- His motivations are not established in the summaries.

## History with the Party
- Mirak Hysstryn is the brother of Jacoby and Persson Hysstryn. He had been missing for 10 years when the party heard of him. [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.06]
- He did not appear in person in this chunk. He was spoken of by his brothers, by Urgala, and by the party. [ch 024 / 024.06; ch 024 / 024.08]
- Mirak's wife claimed he ran off with one of his lady friends. Rumor named Urgala Meltimer, an innkeeper in Triboar, as that lady friend. [ch 024 / 024.06]
- His brothers said Darathra, the Lord Protector, dismissed their concerns. They offered a magical bow in trade for information about him. [ch 024 / 024.06]
- Persson said a drunken priest of Helm had reported that Mirak "is still at home and never left." [ch 024 / 024.06; ch 025 / 025.01]
- Thistle offered to help the brothers. In chapter 025 the Hysstryn brothers are described as having convinced Thistle to look into Mirak's mysterious death. [ch 024 / 024.06; ch 025 / 025.01]
- Urgala admitted that she and Mirak "were involved for a while" after her wife disappeared. She denied any part in his disappearance and suggested his wife, Tolmara, had found out. [ch 024 / 024.08; ch 025 / entry]
- Urgala identified Mirak's wife's house as the Six Windows Inn. [ch 024 / 024.08]
- Thistle's botched break-in served as a distraction. Under its cover Orsik, Unla Key and Vardis entered the Six Windows Inn, and Orsik found bones in the attic. [ch 024 / 024.09; ch 025 / 025.02; ch 025 / moment]
- Unla Key judged that the bones fit Mirak's description. She noted they found no evidence of what killed him, and that speak with the dead would be needed. [ch 024 / 024.09]
- When touched, the skeleton animated, and Orsik struck it down with one blow. [ch 024 / 024.09]
- The summaries describe the attic remains as Mirak's. [ch 024 / entry; ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.02]
- The cause of death and the killer are not established in the summaries. [ch 024 / 024.09; ch 025 / entry]
- Mirak's death is called "mysterious." [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.01]
- Using the remains as evidence, the party convinced the Lord Protector to hold Tolmara in prison until a cleric able to cast speak with the dead could be found. [ch 025 / 025.02]
