## Identity
Herald is a dwarf sergeant of the guard in Mirabar [ch 039 / entry]. He serves within the Axe of Mirabar, the city's guard force known for its deep-red double-bladed axe crest on a black field [ch 039 / 039.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Herald is professional, decisive, and dismissive of criminal deceit. He reacts with delight and congratulation when his objectives are met, indicating a focus on results and institutional success [ch 039 / 039.04]. He is authoritative and intolerant of excuses, shutting down The Weevil’s denial immediately with a command for silence and a factual accusation [ch 039 / 039.04].

He exhibits a grim sense of irony, making a dark pun regarding The Weevil’s sentencing to the mines [ch 039 / 039.04]. Herald is also interested in intelligence gathering; upon learning The Weevil possessed code-cracking skills, he noted that his department has methods to extract such information, signaling a pragmatic approach to interrogation [ch 039 / 039.04].

## Last Observed State
In Chapter 039, Herald oversaw the handover of the prisoner The Weevil to the Axe of Mirabar. He confirmed the prisoner's identity by examining his braid, rejected the prisoner's claim of innocence, and sentenced him to work in the mines for life [ch 039 / 039.04; ch 039 / entry].

After the identification and sentencing, the 3HPP left with their reward of 5,000 gold pieces marked with the symbol of Mirabar, and agreed to meet again at the entrance in five days [ch 039 / 039.04]. This is the final recorded observation of the character in the provided evidence.
