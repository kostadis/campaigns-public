## Identity
Bob Saget is a demon from the Abyss, characterized by purple skin, jagged teeth, white gloves, and a purple top hat with a jacket featuring living tassels [ch 035 / 035.07]. He refers to himself as "the tormentor of the kings who killed their jesters" [ch 035 / 035.07]. His primary function, as described in Chapter 85, involves cracking bad jokes at souls requiring punishment, a role he insists is a "portfolio change" rather than a demotion [ch 085 / 085.06]. He identifies as male, stating his pronouns are "he, him" [ch 035 / 035.07].

## Personality and Motivations
Saget is theatrical, narcissistic, and obsessed with performance and status. He describes the Abyss as "so - boring" and expressed love for his "new job" of performing comedy on Toril [ch 035 / 035.07]. He views the 3HPP’s use of amateurs in his play as beneficial because "the authenticity of their terror" enhanced the performance [ch 056 / 056.03]. While he claims to cause "chaos and mayhem," he also frames his actions as solving societal problems or providing entertainment [ch 056 / 056.03].

He is highly sensitive to insults regarding his power or relevance, reacting with anger when questioned about his strength or status, demanding, "ARE YOU QUESTIONING BOB SAGET!!!!" [ch 085 / 085.07]. He is driven by the desire to remain the center of attention, stating, "this is my show. I share my stage light with no one" [ch 085 / 085.08]. Despite his demonic nature, he displays a degree of bureaucratic concern and fear regarding interplanar treaties, such as the "1489 Treaty on Export Regulation of Interplanar Storage Devices," fearing that Toril's violation invites invasion by three other empires [ch 085 / 085.05]. He is also prone to boredom, describing time in the Astral Plane as "the longest, most boring hour of my existence" [ch 085 / 085.05].

## Last Observed State
In the final chapters, Bob Saget was summoned to Ascore by the 3HPP via the *Planar Ally* spell to fight Iymrith and free prisoners [ch 085 / 085.05]. He arrived frightened of the legal implications of the party's previous storage device usage but eventually agreed to the deal [ch 085 / 085.05]. He accepted payment of two hybrid zombie purple worm/dragon hybrids in exchange for performing at Iymrith's reveal disguised as a dragon and freeing 200 prisoners [ch 085 / 085.07; ch 085 / 085.08].

Bob fulfilled his end of the bargain regarding the prisoners. He opened a magical door at the Royal Theater in Silverymoon at 9 am as promised, releasing 200 confused but happy guests [ch 085 / 085.08; ch 086 / 086.05]. Following this, he summoned several additional demons to assist with upcoming battles, though he insisted on remaining the sole focus of the main event [ch 085 / 085.08]. The party intends to polymorph him into a dragon for the performance against Iymrith, though they must first return to Silverymoon as they lack the necessary spell at that moment [ch 085 / 085.07].

## Relationships
*   **The 3HPP:** Bob views them as patrons and fellow performers, though he mocks their competence, noting the "fool Kaelen’s summoning spell brought me here" [ch 056 / 056.03]. Orsik considers him an "old friend" and admits he "kind of liked Bob" because he was funny and profitable [ch 073 / 073.01; ch 073 / moment]. Unla Key frequently needles Bob, asserting intellectual superiority and dismissing his fears, such as telling Bob the hours he spent in the Astral Plane felt like only one hour [ch 085 / 085.05]. Vardis uses Planar Ally to summon him and debates the ethical implications of his agency [ch 059 / 059.01; ch 085 / 085.05].
*   **Lord Methrammar Aerasumé:** The Lord blames Bob’s play for the disappearance of 200 notables and the destruction of the Royal Theater, viewing Bob as the cause of his current crisis [ch 086 / 086.02].
*   **Kaelen:** Bob holds him in contempt, identifying him as the playwright whose ritual summoned him, though the spell was "partly thwarted" [ch 056 / 056.03; ch 064 / 064.07].
*   **Iymrith:** Bob is excited by the prospect of humiliating Iymrith by exposing her fraud during her reveal, viewing the performance as a chance to prove he is not a "joke of a demon" [ch 085 / 085.07].
