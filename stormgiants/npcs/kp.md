## Identity
KP is a famous gnomish sage credited with the first analysis of interplanar trade and its impact across the entirety of existence [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.05]. KP is the namesake of "KP Trade Equilibrium Theory," a framework which considers all markets across all possible universes rather than a single local physical market [ch 021 / 021.06]. The theory involved a novel application of the Smalistin conjecture [ch 021 / 021.06].

## Relationships
KP is cited as the originator of economic theories that influenced the operations of the Lionshield Coster, an organization that was a "devotee" of KP Trade Equilibrium Theory [ch 021 / 021.06]. The Lionshield Coster’s interplanar supply chain leverages the trade routes KP analyzed [ch 025 / 025.05]. KP's equations are controversial because they ignore the impact of multi-planar economic optimization on local social cohesion, a factor cited as a cause of the social disruption and local resentment surrounding Lionshield Coster outposts [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.05].
