## Identity
Tolmara Hysstryn is Mirak's wife and the keeper of the Six Windows Inn [ch 024 / entry]. She is a resident of the building associated with the missing Mirak, whose home is identified as the Six Windows Inn [ch 024 / 024.08].

## Personality and Motivations
Tolmara displays an assertive and defensive demeanor regarding her property and rights. Upon perceiving an intruder, she screamed, "Thief! You were trying to sneak into my house to avoid paying rent! I will call the Lord Protector! I have rights," and physically confronted the intruder with a rolling pin [ch 024 / 024.09]. Her reactions are volatile; she initially attacked Thistle with potatoes, stating, "I never trusted a fairy," but instantly shifted to a nurturing attitude upon hearing his excuse about a giant bumblebee, offering, "My poor boy! A giant bumblebee! Let me get you a salve," before rushing back inside [ch 024 / 024.09; ch 024 / moment]. While Urgala suggested Tolmara might have discovered Urgala's affair with Mirak, Tolmara's specific internal motivations regarding the murder are not established in the evidence [ch 024 / 024.08].

## Last Observed State
Tolmara was taken into custody by the Lord Protector Darathra following the discovery of bones in her attic [ch 024 / entry; ch 025 / 025.02]. At the Tower, Darathra separated Tolmara from the party, telling her, "Not you," when dismissing the group [ch 024 / 024.10]. The party used the remains found in the attic to convince Darathra to imprison Tolmara until a cleric capable of casting *speak with the dead* could be found [ch 024 / entry; ch 025 / 025.02; ch 025 / 025.03]. While the evidence confirms she was put in prison, her status beyond this imprisonment pending the cleric's arrival is not established in the summaries [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.03].
