## Identity
Elquen is a wight residing in the Wizard of the Crag’s library [ch 012 / entry; ch 012 / 012.05]. He formerly served in the Wizard’s household before a wild magic surge transformed him into a wight [ch 012 / entry]. This event also trapped his former guards and manservants; when Elquen attempted to aid them, they turned into Skeletons [ch 012 / entry; ch 012 / 012.05]. He possesses the memories of the household prior to the Wizard's death, including the details of the duel in which Taskor killed the Wizard of the Crag [ch 012 / 012.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Elquen is motivated by a deep-seated fear of the consequences of magic, stemming from the incident that transformed him and his colleagues [ch 012 / 012.05]. He views his existence and location as a "curse and burden to protect the world" from further magical disasters, explicitly stating that there will be no removal of books or use of magic within the library [ch 012 / 012.05]. He harbors a strong aversion to magic, muttering "never again" upon returning to consciousness from his memories of the surge [ch 012 / 012.05]. He displays suspicion toward outsiders, expecting them to seek powerful artifacts that would require him to deny access, leading to further death [ch 012 / 012.05]. Once he recognized a master, his demeanor shifted to familiar informality; he commented on the master's historical inability to hold onto money while negotiating for back pay [ch 012 / 012.06].

## Relationships
**Unla Key:** Elquen initially reacted to Unla with the comment "Your arrogance" before Unla manipulated the fates [ch 012 / 012.06]. Immediately following this, Elquen expressed joy at seeing Unla alive and accepted him as his master [ch 012 / 012.06; ch 012 / moment]. He negotiated a transactional relationship for his release, asking for back pay, which Unla paid [ch 012 / 012.06].

**The Wizard of the Crag (Taskor's victim):** Elquen remembers the Wizard fondly or respectfully enough to seek confirmation of his life from Unla [ch 012 / 012.06]. He recalls the manner of the Wizard's death in a duel with Taskor, a fact he learned from a guard and his manservant [ch 012 / 012.05].

**Former Guards and Manservants:** These individuals were trapped alongside Elquen during the wild magic surge and subsequently transformed into Skeletons when Elquen tried to help them [ch 012 / 012.05]. Elquen remembers their interactions, including the manservant's dry explanation of the castle's financial state following the Wizard's death [ch 012 / 012.05].
