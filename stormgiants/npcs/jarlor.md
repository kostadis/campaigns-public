## Identity
Jarlor is identified as a spy and a conduit for secret communications in Parnast [ch 021 / 021.05; ch 022 / entry]. He is referred to as "the one-time spy" [ch 022 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Jarlor displays reluctance and self-preservation as his primary motivations, only acting when socially coerced by family obligations [ch 021 / 021.05]. He possesses a dry, observant wit, noting the absurdity of Orsik’s leadership style and threats but choosing silence to maintain the status quo ("never had kids and he’s the boss") [ch 021 / 021.05; ch 021 / moment].

## History with the Party
*   Jarlor was suggested by Thistle as a conduit to send a message to Kurglow via the spy channel; he was initially reluctant until his wife shamed him into compliance [ch 021 / 021.05; ch 021 / entry].
*   While listening to Orsik deliver a threat intended for Kurglow ("if you attack, we send the whelp to bed without dessert and will not read a bed-time story!"), Jarlor privately judged that Orsik "never had kids and he’s the boss," deciding not to correct the dwarf [ch 021 / 021.05; ch 021 / moment; ch 021 / entry].
*   Later, identified as "the one-time spy," Jarlor mumbled a dry remark regarding the ineffectiveness of Orsik's nursery-style threats when the party realized an orc chief was unafraid of them [ch 022 / 022.02; ch 022 / entry].

## Last Observed State
Jarlor was present in the vicinity of the confrontation with the orc chiefs. He mumbled a dry remark explaining the lack of fear shown by an orc chief, attributing it to the ineffectiveness of Orsik's threat to withhold dessert or bedtime stories [ch 022 / 022.02; ch 022 / entry]. Status not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
*   **Thistle**: Thistle suggested using Jarlor as a spy channel conduit to contact Kurglow [ch 021 / 021.05].
*   **Jarlor's Wife**: She shamed Jarlor into agreeing to carry the message, telling him to "grow a spine and do what you need to do to protect your family" [ch 021 / 021.05].
*   **Orsik**: Jarlor served as the recipient of Orsik's threat intended for Kurglow. Jarlor privately judged Orsik as a boss who had never had children, leading Jarlor to decide against correcting the dwarf's ineffective threats [ch 021 / 021.05; ch 021 / moment].
