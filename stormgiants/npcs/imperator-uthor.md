## Identity
Imperator Uthor is a middle-aged Storm Giant characterized by steel-gray hair and a trident crackling with latent electricity [ch 067 / 067.04]. He held the title of Imperator and sat beside Queen Serissa in the throne room of Maelstrom [ch 067 / 067.04]. He is referred to as "Brother" by King Hekaton, indicating a familial or deep hierarchical bond within the Storm Giant hierarchy [ch 082 / 082.04; ch 082 / moment]. His role involved managing court affairs and exerting influence over the young Queen, Serissa [ch 067 / 067.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Uthor displayed a demeanor of "perpetual condescension" toward Queen Serissa, favoring a monarchy he could manipulate rather than one led by a competent, independent ruler [ch 067 / 067.04; ch 067 / entry]. He actively suppressed Serissa's autonomy, physically restraining her when she attempted to rise and verbally rebuking Adimond Kroskas for suggesting a change in leadership [ch 067 / 067.04]. Uthor exhibited disdain for the party's presence, attempting to control the narrative surrounding the missing party members by pressuring Mirran and Nym to confirm false accusations of assassination [ch 067 / 067.04]. He dismissed efforts to find King Hekaton, claiming lack of progress despite the Queen's hopefulness, revealing a lack of genuine commitment to the King's safety [ch 067 / 067.04; ch 082 / 082.04]. Upon Hekaton's return, Uthor feigned enthusiasm but was exposed by the party for his prior inaction [ch 082 / 082.04].

## History with the Party
- Imperator Uthor is observed by the party (specifically Thistle) sitting beside Queen Serissa in Maelstrom’s throne room, acting with perpetual condescension toward her [ch 067 / 067.04].
- Barely hiding his irritation, Uthor asks Mirran and Nym to confirm their claim that the party were assassins who had been killed: "I wouldn't want this to be another misunderstanding" [ch 067 / 067.04].
- The party deduces that Uthor prefers a Queen he can manipulate, noting his physical restraint of Serissa when she attempted to rise and his rebuke of Adimond for suggesting a change in leadership [ch 067 / 067.04].
- Uthor dismisses efforts to find King Hekaton, stating "We have sent so many, your highness," which the party identifies as a lack of genuine support while Serissa remained hopeful [ch 067 / 067.04; ch 082 / 082.04].
- Upon King Hekaton’s return, Uthor claims enthusiastic support ("So wonderful to see you alive!"), but the party exposes his lack of support to the King, leading to his dismissal [ch 082 / 082.04].
- Hekaton removes Uthor from his position, stating "Brother, Uthor, you are dismissed," to which Uthor smiles and accepts the transfer of his insignia of office to Serissa before marching out [ch 082 / 082.04].

## Last Observed State
In chapter 082, Uthor attempted to deceive King Hekaton by claiming he had "left no stone unturned" in finding the King, but the party (3HPP) exposed his lack of support [ch 082 / 082.04]. Hekaton, seated on the Wyrmskull Throne, declared Serissa the new Imperator and dismissed Uthor with the words, "Brother, Uthor, you are dismissed" [ch 082 / 082.04; ch 082 / moment]. Uthor accepted this dismissal with a smile, stating, "Yes, my brother, it is time I took care of other business," handed his insignia of office to Serissa, and marched out of the throne room into stunned silence [ch 082 / 082.04]. Status beyond his departure from Maelstrom is not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
**Serissa:** Uthor served as her high steward but viewed her as a puppet to be manipulated, noting her temper and physically restraining her during court proceedings [ch 067 / 067.04]. He handed the insignia of office to her upon his dismissal [ch 082 / 082.04].
**King Hekaton:** Hekaton addressed him as "Brother," suggesting a close familial or clan bond [ch 082 / 082.04]. Hekaton held Uthor accountable for his failure to support the search for him, resulting in Uthor's dismissal from office [ch 082 / 082.04].
**Adimond Kroskas:** Uthor maintained a position of dominance over Adimond, silencing him when he suggested leadership changes and asserting that Adimond’s "privilege" did not grant the right to speak on such matters [ch 067 / 067.04].
**Mirran and Nym:** Uthor leveraged their presence to confirm false narratives about the party, though he later stood exposed alongside them when Hekaton learned of their betrayals and his own inaction [ch 067 / 067.04; ch 082 / 082.04].
