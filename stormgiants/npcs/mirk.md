## Identity
Mirk is a Goblin boss, identified as the other goblin leader alongside Tlega [ch 051 / entry; ch 052 / entry]. He is distinct from Tlega and operates with his own troops [ch 052 / 052.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Mirk places high value on traditional Goblin social structures, specifically arguing that the "Bullied/Bully relationship is the core of Goblin society" and that the distinction between a bully and an abuser is an "imaginary line" [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.09]. This viewpoint led him to initially oppose Tlega’s plan to attack Maergun [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.09].

In combat, Mirk exhibits a leadership style characterized by verbal encouragement rather than physical participation. During the assault on the Fomorian, he stayed out of the fight, shouting "Well done!" at his troops [ch 052 / entry; ch 052 / 052.01]. This inaction caused dissension among his troops until he threw a tantrum, personally hurled a javelin at the giant, and shouted "Satisfied!" [ch 052 / entry; ch 052 / 052.01]. He is susceptible to magical distraction; he agreed to attack Maergun after Unla Key cast "Jim's Glowing Coin" [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.09].

## Last Observed State
Mirk was present in Maergun’s throne room during the final battle against the Fomorian [ch 052 / 052.01]. After causing his troops to cheer by hitting the giant with a javelin, he was part of the group that witnessed the goblins breaking into a celebratory dance after the giant’s death [ch 052 / 052.01]. Status not established in the summaries after the goblins' celebration.

## Relationships
**Tlega:** Mirk is another goblin boss who initially opposes Tlega’s decision to attack Maergun, defending the traditional Bully/Bullied dynamic [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.09]. He eventually agrees with Tlega to dispatch Maergun after being distracted by a spell [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.09]. Both leaders joined forces to lead troops into the throne room for the assault [ch 052 / entry; ch 052 / 052.01].

**Maergun:** Mirk initially opposed taking action against Maergun, arguing for the preservation of their current relationship [ch 051 / 051.09]. He later agreed to "dispatch" him [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.09] and fought against him [ch 052 / 052.01].

**Goblin Troops:** Mirk leads troops that initially followed his passive command style, but which grew dissatisfied with his lack of participation in combat until he physically engaged the enemy [ch 052 / entry; ch 052 / 052.01].

**Unla Key:** Unla Key used a spell ("Jim's Glowing Coin") that distracted Mirk, leading him to agree to attack Maergun [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.09].
