## Identity
Halisa is a Seelie Dryad, one of a pair (along with Helia) punished by Queen Titania to protect the Grandfather Tree [ch 048 / 048.09]. She resides at the southernmost tree of the cardinal points. She was exiled from the Fey court for offering an honest opinion regarding Queen Titania’s dress [ch 048 / 048.09; ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.02]. Her specific punishment is referred to as "Tree Protection Schedule D" [ch 050 / 050.02; ch 050 / moment].

## Personality and Motivations
Halisa is characterized by her inability to filter her opinions, having offended the Queen directly by criticizing her attire [ch 048 / 048.09; ch 050 / 050.02]. She displays hostility toward dwarves, driving off Orsik solely because of his race, dismissing his attempts to explain his respect for nature [ch 050 / 050.02]. She is terrified of royalty, demanding the party leave immediately when the transformed Prince Thornacious appeared, mistaking Orsik for actual nobility [ch 050 / 050.03].

Her primary motivation shifts from a perceived tragic love to vindictive spite. While her exile was framed by Queen Titania as a mutual trap where neither she nor Helia loved the other enough to sacrifice themselves, Halisa’s reaction to freedom reveals a different sentiment. Upon learning Helia would remain trapped, she expressed surprise and regret at their past dynamic, stating, "We could have been happily in love instead of rotting away" [ch 050 / 050.07]. However, her stated intent upon departure was to return every 100 years specifically to "taunt Helia" [ch 050 / 050.07]. She is prone to bickering with Helia, which annoyed other dryads [ch 050 / 050.05].

## Last Observed State
Halisa was last seen departing from the southern tree area during a staged cure orchestrated by the party. The party used Orsik (possessed by Prince Thornacious) to declare a royal pardon, telling the dryads they could leave [ch 050 / 050.07]. Halisa expressed that the "knot dumped me just as Queen Titania was about to send me here" and declared she would return in 100 years to taunt Helia before flying away [ch 050 / 050.07].

Following her departure, the dryads Sleth and the old dryad revealed that Queen Titania’s original story about a mutual sacrifice curse was a lie; in reality, Halisa and Helia had simply cursed each other due to their inability to stop arguing [ch 050 / 050.07]. In a later chapter, Queen Titania’s sprite commended the party’s subterfuge in freeing Halisa and Helia, noting the Queen was pleased the interference advanced a personal goal [ch 051 / 051.04].

Status not established in the summaries following her departure.

## Relationships
**Helia:** A fellow Seelie Dryad stationed at the eastern tree. They were exiled together for the incident at the ball where Halisa insulted the Queen and Helia was her "plus-one" [ch 050 / 050.04]. While publicly bickering and apparently hating each other [ch 050 / 050.05], Halisa later claimed they could have been "happily in love" [ch 050 / 050.07]. Their dynamic was described by an old dryad as a cycle of bickering born from neither wanting to sacrifice themselves [ch 050 / 050.05].

**Queen Titania:** The ruler of the Seelie Court who punished Halisa for criticizing her dress [ch 048 / 048.09]. Titania lied about the nature of the punishment, claiming one could leave only if the other died, which kept Halisa and Helia trapped by their mutual stubbornness [ch 050 / 050.07]. Titania later commended the party for tricking Halisa into leaving [ch 051 / 051.04].

**Orsik:** Halisa is hostile to him due to his dwarf heritage. She drove him away despite his defense that he was a former scout who respected nature [ch 050 / 050.02]. She was terrified when he appeared disguised as Prince Thornacious [ch 050 / 050.03].

**Thistle:** Halisa interacted with Thistle at the southern tree, informing him that other Dryads might be able to help with their quest [ch 050 / 050.02].
