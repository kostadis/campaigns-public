## Identity
Kolbaz was an old Harper mage and a host to the party in Yartar [ch 030 / 030.02; ch 030 / entry]. He claimed involvement in research regarding the Spring’s First Blossom, though he noted it was not used in the ritual [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.03]. He served as a predecessor to Gauche the Young [ch 033 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Kolbaz expressed strong resentment toward Krowen’s use of contractors, stating, "Being a Harper meant something. It wasn’t just a job!" [ch 030 / 030.02]. He delivered a long denunciation of the Lords’ Alliance as a league of wealthy lords who "do not care for the little people" [ch 030 / 030.03]. His professional pride was easily hurt, particularly when his illusions were criticized or when he was questioned about his loyalty to the Cult of the Dragon, prompting him to straighten and declare, "I FOUGHT THE CULT AT THE WELL OF DRAGONS!" [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.03].

## History with the Party
- Kolbaz, an old Harper mage in Yartar, served as the party's host and greeted them at the guardian patio after introducing himself with the line, "I am Kolbaz and your host. Welcome to Yartar." [ch 030 / 030.02]
- Kolbaz expressed strong resentment toward Krowen’s use of contractors, stating, "Being a Harper meant something. It wasn’t just a job!" [ch 030 / 030.02]
- He denied any alliance with the Cult of the Dragon, declaring, "I FOUGHT THE CULT AT THE WELL OF DRAGONS!" [ch 030 / 030.03; ch 030 / moment]
- Kolbaz claimed involvement in the research regarding the Spring’s First Blossom, noting it was "not used in the ritual," though he admitted having never heard of Adimond Kroskas at that time. [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.03]
- He directed the party to the Wink and Kiss tavern to locate the Hand of Yartar, telling them, "The Wink and Kiss is their hangout. Go to the tavern and ask a server." [ch 030 / 030.03]
- Kolbaz was attacked and crumpled to the ground by a flash of lightning after muttering, "Oh gods, no. That’s not possible," followed by a small figure with a deep voice calling him a "meddling little fool." [ch 030 / 030.07; ch 030 / moment]
- His death was confirmed when his successor, Gauche the Young, stated, "Oh, he was killed by a lightning bolt," and noted that Kolbaz's room had been destroyed as if something substantial materialized near the teleportation circle. [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.08]
- Papers left on Kolbaz's desk revealed that his research into Adimond Kroskas was the proximate cause of his death. [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.09]
- In later chapters, the party recalled Kolbaz’s death as evidence pointing toward a Blue Dragon as the culprit, linking it to his research into Adimond Kroskas. [ch 055 / 055.05]

## Last Observed State
Kolbaz was killed. His death was confirmed by Gauche the Young, who stated, "Oh, he was killed by a lightning bolt," and noted that Kolbaz's room had been destroyed as if something substantial materialized near the teleportation circle [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.08]. Papers left on Kolbaz's desk revealed that his research into Adimond Kroskas was the proximate cause of his death [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.09]. In later chapters, the party recalled his death as evidence pointing toward a Blue Dragon as the culprit [ch 055 / 055.05].

## Relationships
**Krowen**: Kolbaz resented Krowen’s use of contractors, viewing it as a degradation of Harper ideals [ch 030 / 030.02].
