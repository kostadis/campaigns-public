## Identity
Nahala is identified as the Chief's "other wife". [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.06]

## Personality and Motivations
No evidence regarding Nahala's specific personality traits or independent motivations is present in the summaries. She is described only by her role in the Chief's household and her participation in communal rituals. [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.06]

## History with the Party
- Nahala is identified as the Chief's "other wife". [ch 060 / entry]
- Nahala participates in the Chief's cleansing ritual, forming a circle by holding Unla Key's right hand and Vardis' left hand to close the ring. [ch 060 / 060.06]
- During the ritual, Nahala is present when the Yakfolk minds attempt to take over the party's minds; the attempt fails after Orsik warns the party, triggering a melee. [ch 060 / 060.06]

## Last Observed State
Nahala participated in the Chief's cleansing ritual, where she closed the circle by holding Unla Key's right hand and Vardis' left hand. [ch 060 / 060.06] While she was physically present during the Chief's humming, the evidence specifies that the Yakfolk minds attempted to take over the party members (specifically Thistle, Vardis, Unla Key, and Imberu based on the hand-holding pairs) rather than explicitly stating Nahala was the active agent of the possession. The attempted possession failed after Orsik, who had stayed outside, warned the party, which triggered a free-for-all melee. [ch 060 / 060.06] Status not established in the summaries following the start of this melee.

## Relationships
**The Chief:** Nahala is the Chief's "other wife". [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.06] She acts in concert with him and his "first wife," Imberu, during communal rituals. [ch 060 / 060.06]

**Imberu:** Identified as the Chief's "first wife". [ch 060 / 060.06] They occupy adjacent positions in the ritual circle (Nahala holds Unla Key's right hand; Imberu holds Unla Key's left). [ch 060 / 060.06]
