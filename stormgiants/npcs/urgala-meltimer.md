## Identity
- Urgala Meltimer runs the Northshield House in Triboar as innkeeper. She is a retired adventurer. [ch 024 / entry; ch 025 / entry]
- She refers to her past herself: "I wasn't always an innkeeper," and "I was an adventurer for a long time." [ch 035 / 035.05; ch 026 / moment]
- She had a wife, who died "Over ten years ago." Urgala abandoned a mine at that time. The Oracle later said her wife "fell to her death in a well that lead to the Underdark." [ch 024 / 024.08; ch 047 / 047.01]
- She fights with sword and bow. She fought in the battle of Triboar and against Bob's zombie hordes in Silverymoon. [ch 027 / 027.01; ch 027 / 027.02; ch 065 / 065.01]

## Personality and Motivations
- **Blunt and practical.** She called the Lords' Alliance "pompous fools" and said she believes "in small local governments." [ch 024 / 024.08] She mocked the party's deal with an out-of-work actor. [ch 035 / 035.01] She said Kaelen should have been killed on the spot. [ch 064 / 064.08]
- **Community-minded.** After the battle of Triboar she said the losses "can be rebuilt, especially if I am in a strong community of friends," and she hugged Narth Tezrin and Othovir. [ch 027 / 027.05; ch 027 / 027.06]
- **Carries guilt.**
  - She confessed, "My book has some bad pages, and I have sinned." [ch 036 / 036.05]
  - She asked, "Is this my fault, too?" when the theatergoers turned out to be missing. [ch 065 / 065.02]
  - She cried briefly over the Sheriff's-office events. [ch 026 / 026.05]
- **Wry humour, shrewd about consequences.**
  - She laughed hardest at the two "self-righteous jerks" and said she had not had this much fun in years. [ch 065 / 065.05; ch 065 / entry]
  - She warned the party that "There are spells that could reveal the truth." [ch 065 / 065.06]
  - She does not take flattery at face value: "I have dealt with your kind before." [ch 026 / moment]
- **Grateful and loyal to the party.** The party counts her among those who owe them, for learning what happened to her wife. [ch 064 / 064.02] She offered them her help: "If you need anything from me, I'm here for you." [ch 065 / 065.05]
- **Resolve.** When she joined the party she said, "I suppose we could have one last adventure." [ch 064 / 064.04]

## History with the Party
- Urgala Meltimer is the innkeeper of the Northshield House in Triboar and a retired adventurer. [ch 024 / entry; ch 025 / entry]
- She was first mentioned by Jacoby and Persson Hysstryn. Persson repeated the rumour that she was the "lady friend" Mirak Hysstryn ran off with ten years earlier. Jacoby said she has always denied it. [ch 024 / 024.06]
- Thistle told Orsik, "Let's go chat with Urgala," after paying Loomy's tab and getting his second clue. [ch 024 / 024.07]
- She said she abandoned a mine when her wife "plunged into the Underdark", "Over ten years ago." [ch 024 / 024.08]
- She called the Lords' Alliance "pompous fools" and said she believes "in small local governments." [ch 024 / 024.08]
- She gave Thistle a raw map with notes on landmarks to the mine, and he copied it. [ch 024 / 024.08; ch 025 / 025.04]
- She admitted her affair with Mirak and denied any part in his disappearance. [ch 024 / 024.08]
- Vardis privately judged her "Guilty." [ch 024 / moment]
- After the Sheriff's-office events, she already knew what had happened and cried briefly. [ch 026 / 026.05]
- She fought in the battle of Triboar. [ch 027 / 027.01; ch 027 / 027.02]
- Thistle mocked her aim: "Urgala needs to attend my classes." [ch 027 / 027.03]
- In the market square after the battle she spoke with Narth Tezrin and Othovir about the losses, and they all hugged. [ch 027 / 027.05; ch 027 / 027.06]
- At the Talking Troll she chided the party for not helping against the magmins. [ch 027 / 027.12]
- Vardis added "Urgala's dungeon or cave complex or whatever it was in the Dessarin" to the party's options. She was mentioned, not present. [ch 028 / 028.01]
- She invited the party to tea at the Northshield House, which had been repaired after the magmin damage. [ch 034 / 034.06]
- She brought the party the bad news about the production and set out the stakes. She laughed off any delay: "No." [ch 035 / 035.01]
- She told Vardis that the Invitation lay with kobolds south of Yartar in the Dessarin Hills, adding, "I wasn’t always an innkeeper." [ch 035 / 035.05]
- On opening night she told Vardis, "WE HAVE A HIT!" [ch 035 / 035.07]
- Thistle told her of Cindell's contract to kill Hyuth Kolstaag. [ch 036 / 036.05]
- A party member asked the Oracle what happened to Urgala's wife. The answer was "She fell to her death in a well that lead to the Underdark." Urgala was spoken of, not present. [ch 047 / 047.01]
- Orsik listed "Urgala from Triboar" among the party's allies. She was mentioned, not present. [ch 063 / 063.12]
- The party counted Urgala among those who owe them, "for figuring out what happened to the love of her life." [ch 064 / 064.02; ch 064 / entry]
- On the 1st day of the 3rd tenday she greeted Lord Zymorven. [ch 064 / 064.04]
- At the castle she asked why they were confined to one part of it and why there were zombies. [ch 064 / 064.06]
- In the argument over Kaelen, she reminded everyone that his original plan was the mass murder of Triboar. She said he should have been killed on the spot. [ch 064 / 064.08; ch 064 / entry]
- Status not established in the summaries beyond her joining the party at the end of this chunk. [ch 064 / 064.04]
- Fought alongside the 3HPP and their allies against Bob's zombie hordes. "the sword and bow of Zymorven, Darathra, and Urgala" helped finish them off after Bob was gone. [ch 065 / 065.01]
- In the theater after the battle, asked where all the theatergoers were. The party admitted they did not know. [ch 065 / 065.02; ch 065 / entry]
- After Darathra's outburst over the missing theatergoers, asked whether it was her fault too. [ch 065 / 065.02; ch 065 / entry]
- Said there was "some promise" in Orsik's proposal to burn the theater. [ch 065 / 065.03]
- Had dated Lord Zymorven. During his speech she muttered that she had dumped him. [ch 065 / 065.03; ch 065 / entry]
- Traded jokes with Vardis about "the experimental years." She smirked when Orsik said he and Vardis were "just friends." [ch 065 / 065.03]
- Burst out laughing during the fire-setting and said she had not had this much fun in years. Her entry says she laughed hardest at the two "self-righteous jerks." [ch 065 / 065.05; ch 065 / entry]
- Offered the party her help. [ch 065 / 065.05; ch 065 / entry]
- Urged the party to flee rather than stay to be praised, warning that spells could reveal the truth. [ch 065 / 065.06; ch 065 / entry]
- Left before she lost her "game face." [ch 065 / 065.06]
