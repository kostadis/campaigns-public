## Identity
Lord Khaspere Drylund is the owner of the Grand Dame, a gambling boat moored in Yartar, and a candidate for the position of Waterbaron [ch 028 / 028.06; ch 078 / entry]. He is described as a fat, foppish man with a grinning portrait [ch 078 / entry]. He speaks with the "characteristic h's and gargled r's of the Calishites" [ch 079 / entry]. He is a member of the Kraken Society [ch 079 / moment].

## Personality and Motivations
Drylund is characterized as charismatic yet secretive, ambitious, and manipulative [ch 076 / entry]. His primary motivation is winning the Waterbaron election, which he views as a lifetime appointment [ch 078 / 078.01]. To secure votes, he employs "special persuasion operations," specifically kidnapping voters' children to coerce them into voting for him [ch 079 / 079.03]. He refuses to use assassination to remove rivals, citing the risk of retaliation from guilds and assassins [ch 078 / 078.07]. He is easily rattled by suspicion, lying poorly when confronted with evidence of his involvement in high-profile crimes [ch 079 / 079.01]. When trapped, he attempts to maintain plausible deniability before caving to intimidation [ch 079 / 079.04]. He views the party's arrival as a "godsend and a positive endorsement" of his candidacy [ch 078 / 078.07].

## History with the Party
- In Yartar, Imber identified Lord Khaspere Drylund as the owner of the Grand Dame and noted his ambition to become the new Waterbaron; Drylund was mentioned as a gambler who would be frequenting the boat [ch 028 / 028.06].
- Unla Key recognised a token found by Queen Serissa as a Golden Goose chit from the Grand Dame, recalling that Drylund ran the boat in Yartar and was attempting to become the Waterbaron [ch 069 / 069.09].
- The party identified the Grand Dame as a hub for intelligence regarding the storm giant king’s fate, noting Drylund’s connection to the Hekaton plot and the difficulty of extracting information from him [ch 076 / 076.04].
- The party planned to infiltrate the Grand Dame, with Orsik expecting Drylund to admit them because of their wealth; Thistle scouted the boat and noted Drylund was the owner [ch 077 / 077.07].
- Imber’s earlier intel was confirmed when the party arrived in Yartar, learning that Drylund owned the Grand Dame and was manipulating votes for the Waterbaron election [ch 078 / 078.01].
- When the party’s arrival alarmed the city guards, Drylund intervened to prevent a conflict, inviting them to the Grand Dame and promising comped food and drink despite not recognising the rare Champagne du le Stomp Orsik presented [ch 078 / 078.03].
- Drylund met the party in his gaudy office, saving the Champagne du le Stomp for his Waterbaron victory, and viewed the party’s arrival as an endorsement of his candidacy while discussing the recent kidnapping of Lord Zymorven’s son [ch 078 / 078.07].
- Drylund refused Orsik’s offer to kill his political rivals, citing potential assassin retaliation, but agreed to an alliance, pledging Yartar’s friendship if he became Waterbaron [ch 078 / 078.07].
- When Orsik confronted Drylund with the golden goose coin found on the bodies of Queen Neri’s assailants, Drylund lied about knowing its significance, displaying worry that he was suspected of the murder [ch 079 / 079.01].
- Drylund introduced the party to market merchants and revealed that Oosith, a sorcerer, was his "campaign manager" responsible for kidnapping voters' children to secure votes [ch 079 / 079.03].
- Drylund led the party into the sewers to the Kuo-toa guards of Oosith, where he attempted to maintain plausible deniability before caving to intimidation and entering [ch 079 / 079.04].
- Drylund confessed to being a member of the Kraken Society and revealed that Hekaton was imprisoned on the Morkoth, a ship undetectable on the northern islands [ch 079 / moment].
- Drylund was dead at the start of Chapter 080; Orsik attempted to ask Vardis to raise him, but Vardis stated it was impossible because Drylund’s brain was gone [ch 080 / 080.01; ch 080 / entry].
- Thistle looted Drylund’s body, finding a ring with keys and currency, and searched his room, finding no correspondence or incriminating paperwork, only treasure including a weapon with an octopus pommel [ch 080 / 080.03; ch 080 / 080.07].
- Thistle argued that Drylund lacked the resources to steal and hide a storm giant, identifying Iymrith as the likely mastermind behind the Kraken Society’s plot [ch 081 / 081.03].
- Unla Key later recalled Drylund’s sudden death, suspecting a Kraken Society hive-mind mechanism triggered the demise after the party attacked a society member in Yartar [ch 083 / 083.01].

## Last Observed State
Lord Khaspere Drylund is dead. His body was found in the sewers of Yartar at the start of Chapter 080 [ch 080 / 080.01]. Vardis determined that raising him was impossible because "His brain is gone" [ch 080 / 080.03]. The party looted his body, finding a ring with keys, 120 gold pieces, and three platinum pieces [ch 080 / 080.03]. His body was left in the sewer chamber [ch 080 / 080.03]. Thistle searched Drylund’s room on the Grand Dame and found no correspondence or incriminating paperwork, only treasure including a weapon with an octopus pommel [ch 080 / 080.07]. Unla Key suspects Drylund’s sudden death was triggered by a Kraken Society hive-mind mechanism after the party attacked a society member [ch 083 / 083.01].

## Relationships
**The Party (3HPP):** Drylund sought an alliance with them, pledging Yartar’s friendship in exchange for their support against cloud giants [ch 078 / moment]. Orsik offered to kill Drylund’s political rivals, but Drylund refused [ch 078 / 078.07]. Drylund lied to Orsik about his knowledge of a golden goose coin linked to Queen Neri’s murder [ch 079 / 079.01]. Under threat, Drylund confessed his Kraken Society membership and revealed Hekaton’s location before dying in Orsik’s hands [ch 079 / moment].

**Oosith:** Drylund identified Oosith as his "campaign manager in charge of special persuasion operations," acknowledging that Oosith kidnapped voters' children [ch 079 / 079.03]. Drylund led the party to Oosith’s Kuo-toa guards in the sewers, where he was mocked by the guards for his "ugly face" [ch 079 / 079.04].

**Lady Atalia:** Drylund blackmailed Lady Atalia into helping his operation [ch 079 / 079.02].

**Pow Ming:** Pow Ming states he is "in charge of everything so Lord Drylund can do his job" [ch 078 / 078.04].

**Imber:** Imber identified Drylund as the owner of the Grand Dame and noted his Waterbaron ambitions [ch 028 / 028.06].
