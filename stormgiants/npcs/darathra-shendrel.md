## Identity
Darathra Shendrel is the Lord Protector of Triboar [ch 024 / 024.04; ch 027 / entry]. She originates from Waterdeep and was installed in Triboar to bring Waterdhavian prestige and magical security to the town, which the locals viewed favorably at the time of her election [ch 024 / 024.04; ch 027 / 027.06]. Publicly, she is regarded as "fair and clearheaded and widely regarded as good" [ch 024 / 024.04]. Privately, she is an agent of the Harpers, a fact she initially conceals and eventually reveals to the party by providing a platinum badge and sending them to Everlund [ch 027 / 027.14; ch 036 / 036.02]. Her political opposition, Cindell Trueshot, identifies her as a Harper agent and a "nobody" who defeated her in an election [ch 036 / 036.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Darathra’s primary motivations are maintaining stability in Triboar, securing her political tenure, and advancing Harper interests. She initially displays bureaucratic indifference, dismissing complaints from the Hysstryn brothers about their missing brother Mirak for ten years and insisting "there is no murder" when presented with evidence [ch 024 / 024.10; ch 024 / 024.06]. However, she is responsive to undeniable proof; upon seeing Mirak’s remains, she swiftly imprisons the suspect Tolmara and orders a *speak with the dead* [ch 024 / 024.10; ch 025 / 025.02].

She is pragmatic and politically astute, often choosing caution over heroism. During the giant attack, she forbids attacking a second giant to prevent escalation, preferring to "monitor the situation and take copious notes" while sheltering in the tower [ch 027 / 027.03; ch 027 / 027.05]. She engages in political maneuvering, using intelligence provided by the party to discredit her rival Cindell, planning to "bury" Cindell with a signed assassination contract to win re-election "in a landslide" [ch 036 / 036.06; ch 036 / moment]. She views the party’s actions through a lens of political utility and debt, noting that their success against Cindell kept Triboar from changing allegiance to the Lords' Alliance [ch 064 / 064.05].

Darathra values discretion and plausible deniability. She is deeply frustrated by reckless actions that threaten Harper operations, such as the party’s betrayal of teleportation circles, which resulted in agent deaths and strained her credibility with Krowen Valharrow [ch 064 / 064.05]. She is also acutely aware of multiversal dangers, reacting with fury when threats are merely banished rather than destroyed, fearing they will return or leave loose ends such as missing persons lost in the Astral Plane [ch 065 / 065.02].

## Last Observed State
Darathra’s status is alive. Her last confirmed presence is in Chapter 065, where she participated in the battle against Bob Saget at Zymorven Hall, fighting alongside Lord Zymorven and Urgala to destroy zombie armies using a sword and bow [ch 065 / 065.01; ch 065 / 065.02].

Following the battle, she confronted the party in the theater regarding the 300 missing theatergoers, who were lost in the Astral Plane due to the party's decision to banish Bob rather than kill him. She screamed that the situation was a "DISASTER" and that the only person who knew the victims' locations was lost in that plane [ch 065 / 065.02; ch 065 / moment]. When Orsik remarked that her desire to deny involvement sounded "Harper-like," Darathra terminated her alliance with the party [ch 065 / 065.04; ch 065 / moment]. She declared, "I am out of here. We are done. Don’t ever come looking for me," forbidding future favors or special requests, and shouting "And no more plays" as she departed [ch 065 / 065.04; ch 065 / moment].

## Relationships
**The Party (3HPP):** Darathra’s relationship with the party evolved from mutual utility to hostile severance. She initially hired them as Harper contractors, providing them with spell scrolls and a platinum badge to report the giant incursion to Dral Thelev in Everlund [ch 027 / 027.14; ch 030 / 030.01]. She credited them with saving Triboar from Cindell’s influence and clearing her political debts, though she noted they had "betrayed the circles" [ch 064 / 064.05; ch 064 / 064.06]. After Orsik’s comment about her being "Harper-like" and the unresolved issue of the missing theatergoers, she ended all contact, allowing only casual greetings but refusing further assistance [ch 065 / 065.04].

**Cindell Trueshot:** Cindell is Darathra’s political rival who defeated her in the previous election context [ch 036 / 036.02]. Darathra refers to Cindell as a "carpetbagger," mirroring Cindell’s criticism of Darathra being from Waterdeep [ch 027 / 027.06; ch 027 / moment]. Darathra utilized the party’s acquisition of a contract signed by Cindell to order an assassination to undermine Cindell’s campaign, planning to use it to "bury" her rival and secure re-election [ch 036 / 036.06; ch 064 / 064.05].

**Krowen Valharrow:** Darathra vouched for the party to Krowen, a senior Harper, as contractors for investigating the sundered Ordning of Giants [ch 029 / 029.10; ch 030 / 030.01]. This relationship was strained when the party betrayed Harper teleportation circles, leading Krowen to accuse them of the act and causing Darathra to feel her credibility had blown up in her face [ch 064 / 064.05].

**Dral Thelev:** Darathra is known to Dral Thelev as a friend; Dral interpreted the presentation of her platinum badge as a sign that "Something must be amiss" with her [ch 029 / 029.09].

**Thistle:** Thistle personally revived Darathra after she was killed by a fire giant during the attack on the market square, which she acknowledged by noting she would attend his classes [ch 027 / 027.02; ch 027 / entry].

**Orsik:** Darathra recognized Orsik (initially confusing him for his son) and debated his family’s legacy regarding the betrayal of Harper agents [ch 064 / 064.05]. Orsik’s remark that her deniability was "Harper-like" directly caused her to walk out and sever ties with the group [ch 065 / 065.04].
