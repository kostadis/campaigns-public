## Identity
Thane Braxow is a stone giant [ch 066 / 066.05; ch 067 / 067.02]. He holds the title of Thane and is a giant lord [ch 066 / 066.05]. He is physically characterized by having gemstones embedded in his flesh [ch 067 / 067.02].

## Personality and Motivations
The evidence indicates Braxow was characterized as "the quiet one" during his introduction [ch 066 / 066.05]. He acted as a combatant defending the giants against the party, participating in the battle where his comrades declared they would kill the intruders [ch 066 / 066.05]. No other motivations or personality traits are recorded in the available summaries.

## History with the Party
*   Thane Braxow is identified by Duchess Tartha as "the quiet one" during the introduction of the giant lords [ch 066 / 066.05].
*   Orsik strikes the stone giant thane twice with his giant slayer blade, leaving the giant unaffected by the sword's magic beyond bleeding, then strikes twice more to knock him prone [ch 066 / 066.05].
*   Orsik kills Thane Braxow with one final strike [ch 066 / 066.05; ch 066 / entry].
*   Orsik later loots 15 gemstones embedded in Braxow's flesh, estimating their value at 12,000 gold pieces [ch 067 / 067.02].

## Last Observed State
Braxow is dead [ch 066 / 066.05; ch 066 / entry]. He was killed by Orsik, who struck him with a giant slayer blade, knocking him prone before delivering a fatal strike [ch 066 / 066.05; ch 066 / entry]. In a subsequent event, Orsik looted Braxow's corpse, extracting 15 gemstones embedded in his flesh [ch 067 / 067.02]. Braxow's body was then dragged by Orsik into a storeroom to hide it from Mirran [ch 067 / 067.02].
