## Identity
Cindell Trueshot is an elven cleric and a rival candidate for Lord Protector of Triboar [ch 027 / 027.06; ch 027 / 027.07]. She resides at Westpharl Ranch, which she describes as "Just six rooms," "a couple of acres," and "more of a pied-a-terre," though Vardis characterizes it as a bungalow [ch 027 / 027.06; ch 036 / 036.01]. Physically, she has ash blond hair, an exposed elven ear, and wears colourful leather armor dyed blue, red, and yellow, often topped with a large brim hat [ch 027 / 027.06; ch 027 / entry; ch 036 / entry]. She presents herself as a person of noble upbringing but is frequently characterized by others as a "carpetbagger" from Waterdeep, a label Darathra Shendrel also shares [ch 027 / 027.06; ch 036 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Cindell is sardonic, status-conscious, and politically ambitious, viewing local governance through the lens of property values and elite social circles [ch 027 / entry; ch 036 / 036.02]. She explicitly states, "I don't like to be defined by my profession," preferring to engage in commerce and politics where she prices her clerical services at 90 gold for *speak with dead* and 1250 gold for *raise dead* [ch 027 / 027.07; ch 027 / 027.08]. Her primary motivation is the removal of Darathra Shendrel from office and the realignment of Triboar toward the Lords' Alliance [ch 036 / 036.02]. She is willing to employ subterfuge and contract violence for political gain, as seen when she hired the 3HPP to eliminate Hyuth Kolstaag, justifying it by claiming his violence causes "a drop in the property values of Triboar" and that he is "a bad person" [ch 036 / 036.02]. Urgala characterizes her actions as "cold-blooded," noting Cindell’s lack of social graces compared to her own expectations [ch 036 / 036.05]. Cindell displays disdain for those she deems lacking in class or money, viewing the party’s ignorance of high society with sorrow rather than anger [ch 036 / 036.01].

## History with the Party
*   Arrives at Triboar during the aftermath of the fire giant attack, introducing herself as a cleric and mayoral candidate; Darathra calls her a "carpetbagger," though Orsik notes Darathra is also from Waterdeep [ch 027 / 027.06; ch 027 / 027.07].
*   Prices her clerical services (90 gold for *speaks with dead*, 1250 for *raise dead*) and raises Darz from the dead at a discount specifically to undermine Darathra’s political standing [ch 027 / 027.07; ch 027 / 027.10].
*   Hosts the party at Westpharl Ranch, where she hires the 3HPP to deal with Hyuth Kolstaag, hoping his elimination will highlight Darathra’s ineffectiveness and help her win the next election [ch 036 / 036.01; ch 036 / 036.02].
*   Attempts to negotiate the contract with Orsik, offering 500 gold (550 if elected), but is forced by Thistle to sign a detailed IOU after her attempts to offer political favors fail [ch 036 / 036.03].
*   Thistle reveals he has betrayed her by giving the contract to Darathra, who plans to use it to bury Cindell’s political career [ch 036 / 036.04; ch 036 / 036.06].
*   Darathra later credits the party’s information against Cindell with preventing Triboar from changing its allegiance to the Lords’ Alliance [ch 064 / 064.05].
*   Darathra states that saving the town from Cindell cleared the party's debts with her [ch 064 / 064.06].

## Last Observed State
Cindell was not present in the final chapters (028–064) where the party resolved the conflict with Hyuth Kolstaag and the subsequent political fallout [ch 028 / 028.02; ch 036 / 036.04; ch 064 / 064.05]. In Chapter 036, Thistle betrayed her by providing her signed contract to Darathra, which Darathra intended to use to "bury her with this" and secure her own re-election [ch 036 / 036.04; ch 036 / 036.06]. In Chapter 064, Darathra confirmed that the information provided against Cindell had successfully prevented Triboar from changing its allegiance to the Lords' Alliance and credited the party with "saving the town from Cindell" [ch 064 / 064.05; ch 064 / 064.06]. There is no evidence in the summaries regarding her whereabouts, political status, or well-being following this defeat; status not established in the summaries [ch 064 / 064.05].

## Relationships
*   **Darathra Shendrel**: Cindell’s primary political rival. Darathra refers to Cindell as a "carpetbagger" and, upon receiving evidence of Cindell’s plot against Hyuth, states she intends to "bury her" politically [ch 027 / 027.06; ch 036 / 036.06]. In Chapter 064, Darathra confirms Cindell’s removal from power helped her clear the party’s debts [ch 064 / 064.06].
*   **Thistle (Fae)**: Thistle betrayed Cindell. After forcing her to sign an IOU for the contract against Hyuth, Thistle turned the contract over to Darathra to ensure Cindell did not win the election, noting that "intrigue is what members of the court do for fun" and Cindell was poor at it [ch 036 / 036.03; ch 036 / 036.04].
*   **Hyuth Kolstaag**: A retired adventurer and target of Cindell’s contract. She views him as lacking class and a threat to Triboar’s social order and property values [ch 036 / 036.02].
*   **Urgala**: An ally of the party who views Cindell with caution, stating, "I'd expect anything from her," and expressing surprise at Cindell’s "cold-blooded" nature [ch 036 / 036.05].
*   **Vardis**: Vardis observes Cindell with amusement, noting she is "one of those clerics who doesn't want to be a cleric" and correcting her description of her home to "A bungalow" [ch 027 / 027.07; ch 027 / 027.06].
