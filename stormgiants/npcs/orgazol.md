## Identity
Orgazol is an individual associated with Lord Narbeck Horn of Xantharl’s Keep. Narbeck identifies Orgazol as someone who facilitates audiences with the lord by accepting bribes from those seeking an audience [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / 038.04].

## Personality and Motivations
The evidence indicates Orgazol operates via bribery. Narbeck notes that Orgazol has "once again" accepted a bribe, implying this is a recurring behavior or established pattern for facilitating access to the lord [ch 038 / 038.04].

## History with the Party
*   Orgazol is mentioned by Narbeck as having accepted a bribe from an adventurer who sought an audience [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / 038.04].

## Last Observed State
Orgazol was not directly present in the scene; he was mentioned by Narbeck Horn during a conversation with the party at Xantharl’s Keep [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / 038.04]. Orgazol serves as an intermediary or gatekeeper for Narbeck, though Narbeck claims his own job is "to set policy rather than handle details" [ch 038 / 038.04].
