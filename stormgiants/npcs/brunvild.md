## Identity
- Brunvild is a frost giant, described as "the local frost giant jarl" of the Miklos Glacier, where Blagothkus' castle crashed [ch 074 / 074.07].
- By Blagothkus' account, the Heroes of the War of the Dragon persuaded Brunvild to help them repair and steal the castle. Sandesyl came aboard as a stowaway, let Brunvild try to destroy the Cult of the Dragon, then turned on him [ch 074 / 074.07].
- Blagothkus said Sandesyl kept him alive "so she has something to snack on." Blagothkus also noted that "Esclarotta has a different version of the facts" [ch 074 / 074.07].
- When Thistle first met him, he was exhausted and emaciated [ch 074 / 074.08; ch 074 / entry].
- He had men under him and dwarves who trusted him. He says both groups are dead [ch 074 / 074.08].

## Personality and Motivations
- **Grievance against Blagothkus.** He blames Blagothkus for his imprisonment, the deaths of his men and the deaths of the dwarves who trusted him [ch 074 / 074.08; ch 074 / moment]. He later gave Esclarotta "the real version" of how Blagothkus got the castle back [ch 077 / 077.04; ch 077 / entry]. He then struck the final blow against Blagothkus with one of Blagothkus' old hammers [ch 077 / 077.04].
- **Concern for those in his charge.** He asked Thistle about the dwarves [ch 074 / 074.08].
- **Fatalism about Sandesyl.** He believed he could not escape while she lived [ch 074 / 074.08]. He agreed to one more escape attempt as a distraction after seeing his own prisoner's resolve mirrored in Thistle [ch 074 / 074.08; ch 074 / entry].
- **Wry, self-aware humour.** He remarked, "First, there was a Kenku, and now you" [ch 074 / moment]. On leaving the control room he said, "Big castle, after all!" [ch 077 / 077.05].
- **Opportunism, quick to back down.** He claimed the castle by salvage rights. When Orsik threatened violence he hedged ("It doesn't belong to me, I just want …") and withdrew [ch 077 / 077.05; ch 077 / entry].

## History with the Party
- Brunvild was spoken of before the party met him: Blagothkus described him as the local frost giant jarl whom the Heroes of the War of the Dragon convinced to help repair and steal the castle after it crashed on the Miklos Glacier. [ch 074 / 074.07]
- By Blagothkus' account, Sandesyl was a stowaway who let Brunvild try to destroy the Cult of the Dragon, then turned on him. She keeps him alive "so she has something to snack on." [ch 074 / 074.07]
- Blagothkus added that "Esclarotta has a different version of the facts." [ch 074 / 074.07]
- When Orsik asked about another ally, Blagothkus judged Brunvild too weak. Thistle thought of him as a possible distraction. [ch 074 / 074.07]
- Thistle flew to Brunvild, who was exhausted and emaciated, and told him he had come to free him at Harshnag's request. [ch 074 / 074.08; ch 074 / entry]
- Brunvild was bitter toward Blagothkus. He blamed him for his imprisonment, the deaths of his men and the deaths of the dwarves who trusted him. [ch 074 / 074.08; ch 074 / moment; ch 074 / entry]
- When Brunvild asked about the dwarves, Thistle, remembering the cadavers he had tossed over the Evermoors, said, "They aren't here. Maybe they escaped?" [ch 074 / 074.08]
- Brunvild said he cannot escape while Sandesyl lives. [ch 074 / 074.08]
- Thistle proposed that Brunvild escape and draw some of Sandesyl's allies out of her lair so the party could kill her. Brunvild agreed to try one more escape as a distraction. [ch 074 / 074.08; ch 074 / entry]
- Brunvild said he had met a Kenku. This stunned Thistle, because the flying Kenku who defeated the Cult of the Dragon was a story the prisoners told themselves. [ch 074 / 074.08; ch 074 / moment; ch 074 / entry]
- Guard drakes followed Brunvild as he lumbered out through the southern tunnel. [ch 074 / 074.08]
- In the control room, Brunvild was present with a furious Esclarotta and added "And a vampire!" to her accusation against Blagothkus. [ch 077 / 077.04]
- Brunvild had told Esclarotta the real version of how Blagothkus got his castle back. [ch 077 / 077.04; ch 077 / entry]
- After the party's attacks on Blagothkus, Brunvild finished him with one of Blagothkus' old hammers. [ch 077 / 077.04; ch 077 / entry]
- Brunvild then argued that the castle was his by salvage rights. The argument with Orsik edged toward blows until Esclarotta declared, "THIS IS MY CASTLE!" [ch 077 / 077.05; ch 077 / moment]
- Brunvild backed out of the room, muttering that he would find a place to sleep. [ch 077 / 077.05; ch 077 / entry]

## Relationships
- **Blagothkus:** hostile. Brunvild blamed him for his captivity and losses, and finished him in the control room [ch 074 / 074.08; ch 077 / 077.04].
- **Sandesyl:** his captor, who kept him alive as food, according to Blagothkus [ch 074 / 074.07]. Brunvild saw her survival as the barrier to his escape [ch 074 / 074.08].
- **Esclarotta:** he told her the real story of the castle's recovery and joined her accusation against Blagothkus ("And a vampire!") [ch 077 / 077.04]. She rejected his salvage claim [ch 077 / 077.05].
- **Thistle:** freed him, saying Harshnag had sent him, and recruited him as a distraction [ch 074 / 074.08]. Thistle withheld the fate of the dwarves, saying "Maybe they escaped?" [ch 074 / 074.08].
- **Orsik:** the salvage dispute between them edged toward blows. Orsik was "itching to kill another giant" [ch 077 / 077.05].
- **Harshnag:** Thistle named him as the one who sent the rescue. Brunvild asked whether Harshnag was there [ch 074 / 074.08]. The summaries do not show the nature of their prior connection.
- **A Kenku:** Brunvild says he met one. Among the prisoners, a flying Kenku who defeated the Cult of the Dragon was a legend [ch 074 / 074.08; ch 074 / moment].
- **Vardis, Unla Key:** fought Blagothkus alongside Brunvild in the control room [ch 077 / 077.04]. The summaries show no direct interaction with them.
