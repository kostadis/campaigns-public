## Identity
Fritz is a short, middle-aged goblin trader [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.08]. He is dressed in a red velvet shirt, blue pants, and green suspenders [ch 032 / 032.08]. He travels as part of a clan heading eastward [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.08].

## Personality and Motivations
Fritz is motivated by the survival of his clan, which is moving east due to threats from giants and nomads [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.07; ch 032 / 032.08]. He operates under the mantra that "Our goods are priced to move," framing his sales as a necessity rather than a choice [ch 032 / 032.07; ch 032 / 032.08]. When challenged on his low pricing, he cites his mother’s advice regarding profit margins and the value of items carried by giants [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.07; ch 032 / 032.08]. He introduces himself with the declaration, "My name is Fritz, and this is my store," asserting ownership over the transaction space [ch 032 / 032.07; ch 032 / 032.08].
