## Identity
Blasius is a Satyr and a member of the court of Prince Thornacious [ch 013 / 013.07; ch 015 / entry]. He served as the Prince's Chief of Staff, a role he assumed after drunkenly accusing the sprite Oblivillish of killing a stag [ch 015 / entry; ch 015 / 015.07]. He is typically seen drinking from a hip flask and is accompanied by a giant eagle wearing an ornamental leaf necklace [ch 015 / entry; ch 015 / 015.07]. As Chief of Staff, he possessed the privilege of opening Fey portals that bypassed trickster Fey puzzles [ch 016 / 016.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Blasius is characterized by a deep aversion to administrative duty and a primary motivation to return home [ch 015 / 015.07]. He describes his tenure as Chief of Staff as miserable, consisting of "meetings, discussions, action items, more meetings, decisions, recommendations, and follow-ups" with no drinking allowed [ch 015 / 015.07]. His internal monologue reveals that he considers being Chief of Staff worse than death: *"Better dead than a chief of staff"* [ch 015 / moment]. He is prone to drunkenness, which he exploits both socially and tactically, noting that his alcohol tolerance is a social lubricant for others [ch 015 / 015.07].

He is reluctant to engage in direct combat, preferring to fight from cover or remain inactive during battles, often blaming his involvement on the machinations of others [ch 022 / 022.07; ch 022 / 022.10]. Privately, he views the cause he fights for with disdain, thinking *"Certainly not for that buffoon"* when fighting for Prince Thornacious, and attributing his presence in battles to Oblivillish’s revenge for his past indiscretion [ch 022 / 022.10; ch 022 / 022.11]. Despite his reluctance, he possesses a degree of bravery, described internally as "the bravest of all Seelies" when forced into action [ch 022 / entry]. He is deeply fond of Unla Key, whom he refers to as his "true honored drinking friend" [ch 016 / 016.02].

## Last Observed State
The last direct observation of Blasius occurs in Chapter 023. After the battle against Bad Fruul, he emerges from his hideout offering wine and celebrating the victory, unaware of the severe casualties and damage to Parnast [ch 023 / 023.02]. The following morning, he is found drunk and singing with Unla Key when the SEER arrives to administer a headband of intellect to Orsik [ch 023 / 023.07]. In Chapter 036, Blasius is mentioned as a friend of Thistle due to Thistle’s status as a minor member of the Seelie Court, but he does not appear on screen [ch 036 / 036.04]. Status not established in the summaries beyond the aftermath of the Bad Fruul battle.

## Relationships
*   **Unla Key:** Blasius holds Unla Key in high regard, designating him as his "true honored drinking friend" and gifting him a ring of jumping [ch 016 / 016.02]. He ensures Unla Key is supplied with alcohol during missions [ch 015 / 015.08; ch 022 / 022.05]. The two frequently drink and sing together [ch 023 / 023.02; ch 023 / 023.07].
*   **Oblivillish:** Blasius originally caused Oblivillish's exile via a drunken false accusation [ch 015 / entry]. They reconcile after the party kills the Unseelie, with Blasius fabricating a story to restore Oblivillish’s position [ch 016 / 016.02]. Blasius privately blames Oblivillish for his current predicaments and involvement in battles [ch 022 / 022.10].
*   **Prince Thornacious:** Blasius serves the Prince but resents the administrative burden of the role. He manipulates the Prince with fabricated stories to avoid work or blame [ch 016 / 016.02]. Privately, he views the Prince as a "buffoon" [ch 022 / 022.11].
*   **Thistle:** Thistle is described as Blasius’s good friend due to Thistle’s ties to the Seelie Court [ch 013 / 013.07; ch 036 / 036.04]. Blasius bids Thistle farewell with "Well, my boy! I am off" before departing for battle [ch 020 / 020.01].
*   **Orsik:** Blasius hails Orsik as a hero after the battle with Bad Fruul, offering him fine wine [ch 023 / 023.02].
