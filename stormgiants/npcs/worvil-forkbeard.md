## Identity
Worvil Forkbeard is a dwarf known by the aliases "the Weevil" and "Larg" [ch 027 / 027.11; ch 038 / 038.05; ch 039 / entry]. He is identified by the distinctive braiding of his beard, a pattern recognized by shield dwarves, which serves as his primary identifier for authorities [ch 039 / 039.01; ch 039 / 039.04]. He is a wanted brigand with a 5,000 gold piece bounty placed by the Axe of Mirabar [ch 027 / 027.11; ch 038 / 038.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Worvil Forkbeard is deceptive and opportunistic, attempting to manipulate those who capture him to secure his freedom or profit. He feigns innocence, claiming to have been "rescued" by the party from bugbears to refute his status as a criminal [ch 038 / 038.06]. He is knowledgeable in illicit activities, specifically claiming to have deciphered a "secret pattern" that allows for the isolation and robbery of caravans from the mines of Mirabar, which he offers in exchange for his freedom [ch 039 / 039.02]. He is familiar with local adventurers, recognizing Orsik by name and noting that he is infamous ["Who doesn't"] [ch 039 / 039.02].

## History with the Party
*   A merchant from Mirabar identified a dwarf cleaning stables in Xantharl's Keep as Weevil, recognizing him from a wanted poster that values him at 5000 gold to the Axe of Mirabar [ch 027 / 027.11].
*   The party agreed to a quest involving the Eye of the All-Father but secured an agreement with Harshnag to claim the bounty on the Weevil, splitting the 5000 gold reward with the giant taking 100 gold [ch 037 / 037.04].
*   Harshnag explicitly agreed to the 100 gold piece share of the 5,000 gold piece reward for the capture of the brigand Worvil Forkbeard, also known as the Weevil [ch 038 / 038.02].
*   The party pursued the Weevil to the Falling Orc, identifying him as Larg, and fought bugbears who were attempting to secure the reward first; fireballs from the party wounded the Weevil [ch 038 / 038.05].
*   The Weevil attempted to claim he had been rescued by the party from the bugbears, but the fairy member of the party presented his wanted poster to refute his claim [ch 038 / 038.06].
*   An after-action report revealed that Kaltivar's attack on Xantharl's Keep was a ruse to distract the guard while bugbears attempted to secure Worvil Forkbeard; the party prevented this and later handed him over [ch 039 / 039.01].
*   While traveling on the Long Road to Mirabar, the party escorted the prisoner Worvil Forkbeard, who was gagged and bound [ch 039 / 039.02].
*   The party refused the Weevil’s offer of treasure and a caravan-robbing scheme based on a secret pattern in exchange for his freedom [ch 039 / 039.02].
*   The party handed Worvil Forkbeard over to Herald, a sergeant of the Axe of Mirabar, who confirmed his identity via his beard braid and sentenced him to work in the mines for the rest of his life [ch 039 / 039.04].
*   The party collected the 5,000 gold pieces reward marked with the symbol of Mirabar [ch 039 / 039.04].
*   Orsik mentioned at the Mines of Mirabar that the Weevil had apparently cracked some code, a detail Herald found intriguing [ch 039 / 039.04].

## Last Observed State
Worvil Forkbeard was handed over to Herald, a sergeant of the Axe of Mirabar, who confirmed his identity via his beard braid [ch 039 / 039.04]. Herald sentenced him to work in the mines for the rest of his life [ch 039 / 039.04]. Orsik had previously informed the party that Worvil had apparently cracked a code, an detail Herald expressed interest in extracting [ch 039 / 039.04]. Later, the Foreman of the Mines of Mirabar suspected that Worvil had revealed the details of the caravan code to the party, though the Axe of Mirabar confirmed via sending stones that they had extracted the information themselves [ch 040 / 040.05]. Status not established in the summaries.
