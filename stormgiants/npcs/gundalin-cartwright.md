## Identity
Gundalin Cartwright is a resident of Parnast and one of the town's most skilled craftsmen [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.02]. He is identified by Ragnar Redtooth as the suspected saboteur of the town's defenses and infrastructure [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.02]. Physical descriptions are limited to being a man; no further physical traits are recorded in the summaries.

## Personality and Motivations
Gundalin and his associates regularly question Ragnar's decisions, despite being essential to the town's defense efforts [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.02]. He is skeptical of drastic or desperate measures, labeling the plan to release the wyvern as "a desperate ploy of a madman!" [ch 022 / entry; ch 022 / 022.05]. Following the battle for Parnast, he is highly critical of the Lords' Alliance, arguing that their machinations caused the town's suffering and asking, "What good have they brought us?" [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.05]. He is engaged in debating the practical restoration of the town [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.02].

## History with the Party
*   Ragnar Redtooth named Gundalin Cartwright as the suspected saboteur, noting that he and his cronies regularly question Ragnar's decisions despite being the town's most skilled craftsmen [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.02].
*   Gundalin replaced the wheels and axle of the cart that hauls materials from Old Parnast, which subsequently broke its second axle in a tenday [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.03].
*   Footsteps from a trampled garden led directly to Gundalin Cartwright's front door [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.08].
*   Gundalin waited at the Golden Tankard with Ragnar and Chandra when the heroes returned to Parnast, appearing ready to fight the orcs [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.01].
*   When Orsik proposed releasing the wyvern to fight Bad Fruul, Gundalin screamed that it was "a desperate ploy of a madman!" [ch 022 / entry; ch 022 / 022.05].
*   After the battle for Parnast, Gundalin was about to agree with Ragnar’s shouted accusation regarding the damage to the town [ch 023 / 023.01].
*   Gundalin argued alongside Chandra and Ragnar about how to fix the devastated town [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.02].
*   Out of breath, Gundalin argued that the Lords' Alliance's machinations were responsible for the town's suffering, asking "What good have they brought us?" [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.05].

## Last Observed State
In the aftermath of the battle against Bad Fruul (Chapter 023), Gundalin was physically present in the devastated town [ch 023 / 023.01; ch 023 / 023.02]. He was initially poised to agree with Ragnar’s accusation regarding the damage caused by the heroes [ch 023 / 023.01]. He subsequently argued alongside Chandra and Ragnar regarding how to fix the town, appearing out of breath while blaming the Lords' Alliance for the destruction [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.05].

## Relationships
*   **Ragnar Redtooth:** Gundalin is a frequent critic of Ragnar's leadership, though Ragnar acknowledges Gundalin is necessary due to his craftsmanship skills [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.02]. Ragnar initially viewed Gundalin with suspicion, offering gold for information on him [ch 017 / 017.02]. Post-battle, Gundalin aligns with Ragnar in criticizing the outcome and the Alliance [ch 023 / 023.05].
*   **Chandra Stol:** Gundalin argues alongside Chandra about rebuilding efforts and shares her skepticism of the Lords' Alliance [ch 023 / 023.02; ch 023 / 023.05]. He waited with her at the Golden Tankard prior to the final battle [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.01].
*   **The Party (Thistle, Orsik, Vardis):** Thistle intended to stake out Gundalin's house based on evidence of sabotage [ch 017 / 017.04]. Footsteps from a trampled garden led directly to Gundalin's front door [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.08]. Gundalin waited with the party's allies at the Golden Tankard, ready to fight [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.01]. He opposed Orsik's plan to release the wyvern [ch 022 / 022.05].
