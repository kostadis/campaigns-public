## Identity
Worug is a gaunt ogre with a scar on his face [ch 059 / entry; ch 059 / 059.04]. He is one of two surviving ogres, alongside Tak, found alive behind a heavily barred door in a second tower within the central outer courtyard of the cloud castle [ch 059 / entry; ch 059 / 059.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Worug is driven primarily by hunger and self-preservation, having survived a vampire massacre by adhering to the rule that "if you don’t let a Vampire in, it can not enter your house" [ch 059 / entry; ch 059 / 059.04]. He is direct in his needs, asking for food immediately upon being freed [ch 059 / 059.04]. He is willing to trade information for survival, revealing the location of a dracolich and the status of Blagothkus in exchange for escape from the castle [ch 059 / 059.04; ch 059 / 059.05]. He appears affectionate or emotionally open toward Tak, seeking physical comfort (hugging Thistle) after eating and expressing disappointment that Tak did not previously reveal his feelings for him [ch 059 / entry; ch 059 / 059.04].

## History with the Party
- Worug is a gaunt ogre with a scar on his face, found alive behind a heavily barred door in a second tower within the central outer courtyard of the cloud castle. [ch 059 / entry; ch 059 / 059.04]
- When Orsik opened the barred tower door with knock, Worug screamed in terror, then identified himself and asked the party if they had food. [ch 059 / 059.04]
- Worug ate Goodberries provided by Thistle, which helped restore some of his strength. [ch 059 / 059.04]
- Worug attempted to hug Thistle after eating. [ch 059 / entry; ch 059 / 059.04]
- Worug explained that he and Tak survived because they did not let a vampire into their dwelling, contrasting this with the other ogres who let in the "purple lady" (the vampire), resulting in a massacre. [ch 059 / entry; ch 059 / 059.04]
- Worug reported that Blagothkus had been crying and stating that he had killed his son. [ch 059 / 059.04]
- Worug stated that Blagothkus has been holed up in the control room since arriving at Parnast. [ch 059 / 059.04]
- Worug revealed that a dracolich named Glazhael, formerly the steed of Talis the White, resides in the den inside the glacier around which the castle is built. [ch 059 / entry; ch 059 / 059.05]
- Worug explained that the First Faction killed Glazhael, who returned as a dracolich and attempted to capture the castle before being led away on a wild goose chase for an artifact. [ch 059 / 059.05]
- Worug revealed that when the return of Tiamat was thwarted, Blagothkus took control of the castle and Glazhael returned to the area. [ch 059 / 059.05]
- Worug was present alongside Tak when Tak declared that they would remain in the tower to rest and provide safety from the vampire. [ch 059 / 059.05]
- Worug asked Tak why he had not revealed a secret earlier, after Vardis suggested that one ogre had feelings for the other. [ch 059 / 059.04]
