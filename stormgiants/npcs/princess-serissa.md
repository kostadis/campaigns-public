## Identity
Serissa is a Storm Giant who serves as the acting head of the Storm Giant court and later the Imperator [ch 076 / 076.06; ch 082 / 082.04; ch 083 / entry]. She is the daughter of King Hekaton and is referred to as "the Queen" and "Queen Serissa" throughout most of her interactions with the party, a title she retained even while navigating the political vacuum left by her father’s disappearance [ch 067 / 067.03; ch 069 / 069.04; ch 069 / 069.09]. Following the return of her father, she is named Imperator by Hekaton, an act she accepts by taking Uthor’s insignia [ch 082 / 082.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Serissa presents herself as a pragmatic and authoritative leader who prioritizes stability and alliances over prestige. She insists on diplomatic terminology, correcting the party to use "non-giant" instead of "little people" and proposing common cause with dwarves, elves, fairies, and halflings [ch 069 / 069.05; ch 069 / 069.08]. She displays a dry wit and composure under pressure, noting that Thistle’s dragon eyes are "quite fetching" and maintaining a professional distance when dealing with threats, telling Adimond Kroskas, "That is of no concern to us. What is of concern is who the dragon is and who you are" [ch 070 / moment; ch 069 / 069.04].

Her primary motivation has been the restoration of order and the retrieval of her father, a goal she pursued through questionable means, including acknowledging that dragon-fairy hybrid experiments were a "stopgap" to retain power while he was missing [ch 069 / 069.06]. She is deeply frustrated by the incompetence and betrayal of those around her. This culminated in a violent emotional eruption at her father, accusing him of abandoning his post and leaving her in a "nest of vipers" [ch 085 / 085.02]. Her final motivation shown is the defense of honor, stating she will take the palace guard to fight "even if our king will sit here like a coward" [ch 085 / 085.03].

## Relationships
**King Hekaton**: Her father. Their relationship is strained; she viewed him as missing and undermined by his disappearance, and upon his return, she openly resents his absence and current inaction [ch 082 / 082.04; ch 085 / 085.02]. Despite her anger, she accepted the title of Imperator from him [ch 082 / 082.04].

**Imperator Uthor**: Initially viewed as an ally who supported her, though she later noted a lack of support from him during her father’s absence [ch 067 / 067.04; ch 082 / 082.04]. She accepted his insignia upon her promotion [ch 082 / 082.04].

**Mirran and Nym**: Her sisters. They undermined her rule, mocked her, and aligned with the blue dragon Adimond Kroskas [ch 067 / 067.03; ch 067 / 067.04; ch 069 / 069.04]. Serissa had them escorted out after being named Imperator [ch 082 / 082.04].

**Adimond Kroskas**: A blue dragon who manipulated her sisters and proposed the dragon-fairy hybrid program [ch 069 / 069.03; ch 069 / 069.06]. Serissa interrogated her sisters about this alliance [ch 069 / 069.04].

**The 3HPP (Party)**: Allies she views as useful but requiring management. She tasked them with finding her father and provided them with resources, including a token from her mother's murder scene and the golden armor, with a warning that failure would have consequences [ch 069 / 069.09; ch 076 / 076.06]. She attempted to teleport them to their castle but was blocked by a *forbiddance* spell [ch 083 / 083.01].

**Iymrith**: An enemy associated with the Kraken Society and the hiding of Hekaton [ch 083 / 083.01]. Serissa resolved to attack Iymrith's lair to defend storm giant honor [ch 085 / 085.03].
