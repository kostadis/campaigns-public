## Identity
Ghelryn Foehammer is a dwarven smith who operates Foehammer's Forge [ch 024 / entry]. He is the competitor of Harriet Uldinath, who runs the smithy Uldinath's Arms across the road [ch 024 / 024.11]. Ghelryn is described as too old to have been the burglar suspected in the Lionshield Coster theft [ch 024 / 024.11].

## Personality and Motivations
Ghelryn holds a specific philosophical view regarding discretion: he believes in privacy but is convinced that privacy ends at the limits of burglary [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.06]. This principle drove him to reveal the identity of his customer, Darz Helgar, after the party appealed to his dwarven pride by recounting the slaying of a hill giant [ch 024 / 024.11; ch 025 / moment; ch 025 / 025.06].

He displays a pragmatic, survival-oriented worldview, expressing the belief that it would have been safer to ignore the giants rather than fight them, while still honoring the party's refusal to back down as a tribute to their bravery [ch 028 / 028.01]. Ghelryn also demonstrates knowledge of unconventional smithing or alchemy, suggesting that a fire giant's heart could be turned into a living lamp [ch 027 / 027.08].

## History with the Party
- Harriet Uldinath suggested that a masterfully crafted grappling hook found at a burglary scene might be the work of her competitor, Ghelryn Foehammer [ch 024 / 024.11; ch 025 / 025.06].
- Ghelryn confirmed he made the grappling hook and, after being impressed by the party’s tale of slaying a hill giant, revealed that he had sold it to Darz Helgar [ch 024 / 024.11; ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.06; ch 025 / moment].
- Ghelryn was present when the party was looting the body of a fire giant and suggested that the giant's heart could be turned into a living lamp [ch 027 / entry; ch 027 / 027.08].
- Ghelryn visited the party at the Talking Troll to present a sealed letter of marque, recognizing their act of killing a fire giant despite his belief that it would have been safer to ignore the giants, and directed them to King Morinn or Queen Tithmel at Citadel Felbarr for reward [ch 028 / entry; ch 028 / 028.01].

## Last Observed State
Ghelryn was present at the Talking Troll in Chapter 028, where he coughed to interrupt the party and presented a sealed letter of marque recognizing their killing of a fire giant [ch 028 / 028.01]. He directed the party to take the letter to King Morinn or Queen Tithmel at Citadel Felbarr for their reward [ch 028 / 028.01]. Status not established in the summaries.
