## Identity
- **Name:** Queen Titania; also called simply Titania. Ruler of the Seelie Court [ch 023 / 023.07]. Described as "the Goddess of the Fey and ruler of the Fey court" [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.04].
- **Presence:** She never appears in person in the summaries. Every item is a mention of her, or her word carried by a sprite emissary of the Seelie Court [ch 050 / entry; ch 051 / entry; ch 052 / 052.04].
- **Contested legitimacy:** Card players tied to the Gloaming Court curse "the false Queen Titania." Oblivillish insists Thistle use her title, "Queen Titania" [ch 015 / 015.08; ch 015 / moment].

## Personality and Motivations
Everything below is how others describe her, or what her emissary reports. None of it is her own speech.

- **Others' view of her temperament:**
  - Thistle calls her cold, "but that is about her heart" [ch 040 / 040.03].
  - He also calls her "Vengeful, spiteful, cruel, and heartless" [ch 051 / 051.04].
  - He warns that her gifts come with strings [ch 052 / 052.04].
- **Punishes affronts:**
  - She sent Prince Thornacious into exile and made his court follow him [ch 014 / 014.06].
  - She punished Halisa and Helia after Halisa "expressed herself freely" at a ball [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.04].
  - She told the two dryads that if one left, the other would be safe and the one leaving would die [ch 050 / 050.05]. The party concluded this was a lie [ch 050 / 050.07].
- **Prizes trickery and self-interest:**
  - Through the sprite, she "commends your subterfuge and subtlety in freeing the two Dryads." She is pleased that it served "a personal and selfish goal" [ch 051 / 051.04].
  - Thistle recalls "the time that Queen Titania made a fool of that fomorian" [ch 066 / 066.04].
- **Political use of events:** She plans to use Thornacious' involvement "as another reason to keep him out of the Feywild" [ch 051 / 051.04].
- **Hostility to the Unseelie:** She tasked the party with dealing with a Fomorian of the Unseelie Court [ch 051 / 051.04]. When it was dead, she rewarded them with a Blessed Horn [ch 052 / 052.04].
- **Alleged backing of Iymrith:** Iymrith names the Seelie Court among her investors. She laughs at the idea that Titania's subjects were experimented on without the queen's knowledge [ch 084 / 084.04]. This rests on Iymrith's claim alone.
