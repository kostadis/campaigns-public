## Identity
Grik is the Goblin chief of the Ambush Gap gang, tasked by Bad Fruul to ambush travelers passing through the area using poison [ch 013 / entry]. He carries a gold stylus and a note from Bad Fruul ordering his gang to "Defend the pass with your lives! We will seize Parnast!" [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Grik is loyal to Bad Fruul, relaying his orders and promises of poison to his gang [ch 013 / 013.04]. He demands travelers surrender all equipment and treasure to leave naked, or be stripped and eaten, citing Bad Fruul's authority [ch 013 / 013.04; ch 013 / moment]. When threatened, he pragmatically uses his subordinates as shields against incoming attacks [ch 013 / 013.04]. He also displays pragmatic avoidance of specific magical threats, specifically avoiding Unla Key’s spells while engaging in the fight [ch 013 / 013.04].

## History with the Party
- Grik is the Goblin chief leading the Ambush Gap gang, tasked by Bad Fruul to ambush anyone passing through the area using poison released in the pass [ch 013 / entry].
- Upon hearing the adventurers approaching, Grik announced, "This pass is under the control of Bad Fruul!" and demanded they surrender all equipment and treasure to leave naked, or face being stripped and eaten, declaring, "thus spake Bad Fruul!" [ch 013 / 013.04].
- During the ensuing combat, Grik was bound by Unla Key's binding ice spell, which also affected his henchman and a wolf [ch 013 / 013.04].
- While bound, Grik used his last remaining goblin as a shield against Thistle's arrow and the drake's acid breath [ch 013 / 013.04].
- Orsik killed Grik after charging up the walls to his tile [ch 013 / 013.04].
- Loot found on Grik's body included a gold stylus and a note from Bad Fruul reading: "Defend the pass with your lives! We will seize Parnast!" [ch 013 / 013.04].

## Last Observed State
