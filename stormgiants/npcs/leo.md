## Identity
Leo is the owner of the Lionshield Coster franchise in Llorkh [ch 003 / 003.02; ch 006 / entry]. He presents himself as immaculately dressed, with perfectly combed hair, a flawlessly cut beard, freakishly white teeth, and smooth skin [ch 003 / entry; ch 006 / entry]. He speaks in a gruff husky voice [ch 003 / entry]. He insists his appearance is "100% natural" despite party assumptions of magic involvement [ch 006 / 006.01; ch 006 / moment]. Status not established in the summaries.

## Personality and Motivations
Leo is prideful about his status as a franchise owner and explicitly refuses credit to first-time buyers [ch 003 / 003.02]. He is obsessively concerned with presentation and compliance, evidenced by his fear of the "Lionshield Coster best presentation award!" committee [ch 003 / entry]. When Thistle pointed out scuff marks and creases on a scroll tube, Leo reacted with horror, stating, "this is disgraceful," and discounted the item from 300 to 250 gold to avoid disqualification [ch 003 / 003.02]. He maintains a spotless doormat and floor, personally cleaning after the party tracked in dirt while muttering about the possibility of an inspector arriving [ch 006 / 006.01]. He is disdainful of certain business practices, refusing to work with a firm implied by Unla Key and asserting, "we don't work with THAT firm, sir!" [ch 006 / 006.01]. He is commercially pragmatic, buying the party’s treasure at a 10% discount and judging their funds insufficient for rare magical items based on their appearance and coin-counting [ch 006 / 006.01].
