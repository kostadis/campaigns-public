## Identity
Brassik is a scholar and sage formerly associated with Candlekeep [ch 048 / entry; ch 054 / entry]. He describes himself as a "lore smith" who provides information on history, geography, and occult matters for a fee [ch 048 / entry]. He was initially a crew member aboard the Great Airship, a vessel gifted by the red dragon Klauth [ch 048 / 048.09]. Later, he served as a hired scholar aboard the airship *Dragon’s Breath*, under the command of Delsephine [ch 053 / entry].

His professional background includes a prior membership in a cult that planned to end the world, a history he finds uncomfortable to discuss [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / 054.04]. He was bound by a contract to Klauth, which included obligations for both a living and an undead existence [ch 053 / 053.07].

## Personality and Motivations
Brassik is transactional and motivated by payment, though he occasionally exhibits internal conflict about aiding the party’s violent or destructive actions [ch 053 / entry; ch 053 / 053.06]. For example, he hesitated while describing the assault on the Great Worm Cavern but continued because he was paid [ch 053 / 053.06]. He is proud of his scholarly credentials, often noting the completeness of his information compared to the party’s knowledge [ch 048 / 048.09].

He maintains a professional but sometimes pedantic demeanor, distinguishing his faction’s theological views on Dracoliches from Severin’s faction and insisting their difference was "about a contrasting view of the natural order" rather than power [ch 053 / 053.05]. He shows discomfort with grave robbery, though he acknowledges his own morally questionable past makes casting aspersions difficult [ch 054 / 054.04].

Once freed from his contract with Klauth, Brassik became delightfully independent and resentful of the party for not paying him a "fair price" [ch 053 / 053.07; ch 055 / 055.03]. He is vindictive enough to withhold valuable information—such as the location of a treasure map, details about Thistle’s girlfriend, and the history of Uncle Joon—specifically to spite Orsik and Vardis [ch 055 / 055.03].

## History with the Party
- Brassik is a crew member aboard the Great Airship and a self-described "lore smith" who offers information for a fee. [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.09]
- He was paid 50 gold by Orsik and Vardis to warn the party that killing the Dryads has "powerful and dangerous consequences" and that tribe patrols pass through. [ch 048 / 048.09]
- He added that the Dryads have allied with various creatures around the forests to protect the tree. [ch 048 / 048.09]
- Brassik noted that the One Stone lies in the territory of the Sky Ponies and, upon receiving five platinum, described the Sky Pony spirit mound in the Moonwood, including its shape from the air, the central boulder, and the corpses of trespassers lashed to totem poles. [ch 052 / 052.09]
- The party hired Brassik as a scholar aboard the Dragon’s Breath, where he briefed them on Raven Rock’s spirit mound, the Black Raven and Gray Wolf tribes, and the Great Worm Cavern. [ch 053 / entry; ch 053 / 053.01; ch 053 / 053.06]
- He expressed internal conflict over helping assault ancient artifacts but continued due to his payment, and distinguished his faction of the Cult of the Dragon from Severin’s faction regarding Dracoliches. [ch 053 / entry; ch 053 / 053.05; ch 053 / 053.06]
- Orsik and Unla Key purchased the living part of Brassik’s contract from Klauth, freeing him from his obligations to the dragon. [ch 053 / 053.07]
- Brassik described Morgur’s Mound from a map he found at Candlekeep, admitting his past in a cult planning to end the world made him hesitant to cast aspersion on the party’s grave-robbing. [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / 054.04]
- When asked where the artifact was buried at Morgur’s Mound, Brassik stated he had "No clue." [ch 054 / 054.05]
- Brassik was delighted with his freedom and planned to withhold information about Thistle’s girlfriend and Uncle Joon, as well as a treasure map location, because Orsik and Vardis had refused to pay him a fair price. [ch 055 / entry; ch 055 / 055.03]
- Brassik left the party before the arrival at Parnast, as Orsik stated he "decided to look for work elsewhere as their play wasn’t going to have that rewrite we were hoping for." [ch 056 / 056.07]

## Last Observed State
Brassik left the party before they arrived at Parnast. When Delsephine asked where he was, Orsik stated that Brassik "decided to look for work elsewhere as their play wasn’t going to have that rewrite we were hoping for" [ch 056 / 056.07].

Prior to his departure, he was aboard the *Dragon’s Breath*, enjoying his freedom from Klauth’s contract and working on background research and the party's theatrical production [ch 055 / 055.03]. Status not established in the summaries beyond his departure from the group.

## Relationships
**The Party (3HPP):** Brassik acts as a mercenary information source for the party. He has a complex relationship with them, oscillating between professional service and personal resentment. He feels undervalued by Orsik and Vardis, whom he calls "buffoons" for refusing to pay a fair price [ch 055 / 055.03]. He finds Thistle’s anarcho-socialist partnership with his drake "Fascinating" [ch 053 / 053.02] and is aware of Thistle’s personal history regarding his girlfriend and Uncle Joon, which he is withholding [ch 055 / 055.03].

**Klauth:** Brassik was bound by a contract to the ancient red dragon Klauth, held in the dragon's hoard [ch 053 / 053.05]. The party (specifically Orsik and Unla Key) purchased the living part of this contract, freeing Brassik from his obligations to the dragon [ch 053 / 053.07].

**Delsephine:** Delsephine managed Brassik’s contract and facilitated his release by selling the living portion to the party [ch 053 / 053.07]. She employed him as a scholar aboard the *Dragon’s Breath* [ch 053 / entry].

**Cult of the Dragon:** Brassik was formerly part of the Cult of the Dragon but distinguishes his faction’s original Dracolich vision from the Tiamat-summoning faction associated with Severin [ch 053 / entry; ch 053 / 053.05].
