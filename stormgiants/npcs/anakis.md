## Identity
Anakis is a young tiefling associated with the Lady’s College, identifiable by the "Ask Me" sign she wears [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.05]. She serves as a greeter or information point for visitors and students at the institution [ch 048 / 048.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Anakis is described as "bright" and "giddy," displaying intense enthusiasm for her subject of interest, Cambions [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.05]. She demonstrates a pragmatic awareness of her racial stigma, acknowledging that tieflings are not considered a "goodly race" by the broader public while contrasting this with the perceived lack of narrow-mindedness within the College [ch 048 / 048.05]. She holds a realistic, perhaps cynical, view of the College's academic engagement, noting that some lectures have gone unattended for years and that infernal creatures are unseen in Silverymoon [ch 048 / 048.05].

## History with the Party
*   Anakis, a tiefling wearing an "Ask Me" sign, greeted the party upon their arrival at the Lady’s College [ch 048 / 048.05].
*   When Vardis stated the party's interest in Cambions, Anakis expressed enthusiasm and directed him to Sage Vusk's lecture down the hall [ch 048 / 048.05].
*   Anakis acknowledged Orsik's stare by confirming her knowledge of her race not being considered "goodly," while noting the College's relative lack of narrow-mindedness [ch 048 / 048.05].
