## Identity
Grrrk is an old Galeb Duhr who functions as a structural stone within a "great pyramid" of rock [ch 002 / 002.01; ch 002 / entry]. He served as a silent observer and occasional narrator during the party's initial visit to the pyramid [ch 002 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Grrrk values stillness and quiet above all else, viewing his immobility as a professional duty [ch 002 / 002.01; ch 002 / entry]. He exhibits a generational superiority complex regarding stoicism, contrasting his own ability to endure discomfort (such as rock mites) without complaint against the whining of younger stones like Prrrt [ch 002 / 002.01]. His demeanor is characterized by gruffness and pessimism regarding the survival chances of adventurers; he expected the party to be "zapped" at the pyramid's peak, unlike the optimistic Prrrt [ch 002 / 002.01; ch 002 / entry].

## History with the Party
- Grrrk is an old Galeb Duhr acting as a stone in a "great pyramid" of rock [ch 002 / 002.01; ch 002 / entry].
- During the party's visit to the pyramid, Grrrk and the younger rock Prrrt watched the party fight badgers hiding among the rocks [ch 002 / 002.01; ch 002 / entry].
- A fairy from the party tickled Grrrk's nose with its wings, initiating a conversation [ch 002 / 002.01; ch 002 / entry].
- Grrrk told the fairy about seeing "giants and kobolds in great numbers" [ch 002 / 002.01; ch 002 / entry].
- Grrrk expected the adventurers to die when they climbed to the top of the pyramid, unlike Prrrt who was optimistic; the party did not get "zapped" and survived [ch 002 / 002.01; ch 002 / entry].
- Grrrk heard the party climb down into the pyramid and disappear, remarking it was enough excitement for the century [ch 002 / 002.01; ch 002 / entry].
- When the party prepared to leave the pyramid area, Grrrk said "nice seeing you!" and thanked them for dealing with the beetles [ch 002 / 002.03; ch 002 / 002.04].
- Grrrk ordered the other Galeb Duhr "back to your positions" before they turned invisible again [ch 002 / 002.03; ch 002 / 002.04].
