## Identity
Fruf is a dwarf miner residing in the lodge [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.03]. He is present within the vast, tall room of the lodge, which houses at least 200 men and is heated by magic fireplaces that produce no smoke [ch 040 / 040.03].

## Personality and Motivations
Fruf is practical and willing to offer available resources to assist others [ch 040 / 040.03]. When Thistle requested wing muffins to keep his wings warm, Fruf explicitly stated he did not possess the requested items but proactively offered extra socks as a substitute [ch 040 / 040.03; ch 040 / entry].

## History with the Party
