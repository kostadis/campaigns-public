## Identity
- Sandesyl Morgia is a half-elven moon elf vampire, first met in the Port Tower of Blagothkus' castle. [ch 057 / 057.04]
- She rejected Vardis's label "a friendly vampire?" and named herself: "I am Sandesyl Morgia." [ch 057 / 057.05]
- She styles herself castellan of the castle. She says Blagothkus owns it. [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.05]
- She is a necromantic researcher. She uses Ogres and Uthgardt captives as experimental material and food. [ch 057 / 057.06; ch 058 / 058.06]
- She was an operative of the Cult of the Dragon. She seeks a cult tome "for official cult business." [ch 070 / 070.05; ch 070 / moment]
  - Thistle called her "a disciple of Sammaster." [ch 072 / 072.02]
  - The narration of the final fight calls her "the last of the Cult of the Dragon, the last of Sammaster's disciples." [ch 075 / 075.05]
- By Blagothkus's account she was a stowaway on the castle when it left the Miklos Glacier. [ch 074 / 074.07]
- She later carved out her own lair in the ice caves below her tower. [ch 074 / entry; ch 074 / 074.04]

## Personality and Motivations
**Appetite**
- She wants "a fresh supply of bodies to exsanguinate." She wants a steady supply of sentient humanoids. [ch 057 / 057.05; ch 057 / 057.06]
- She bargained over feeding stops. [ch 058 / 058.05]

**Temper and secrecy**
- Her temper "goes from 1 to 50 in an instant." [ch 057 / entry]
- She deflects personal questions with "That would be telling." [ch 057 / 057.06]
- She threatens vengeance on anyone who thinks her "an old helpless vampire." [ch 057 / 057.06]

**Distrust**
- She is not trusting of her minions. [ch 074 / entry]
- Blagothkus predicted she would send spawn to confirm the party's deaths before leaving her lair. [ch 074 / 074.04]

**Caution toward greater powers**
- She refuses to cross House Margaster: "Asmodeus protects them. And I refuse to upset the archfiend’s plans. Only an insane person would do that." [ch 058 / 058.05]

**Ideology**
- She believes salvation comes when all dragons become dracoliches and rule Faerûn. [ch 070 / 070.05]
- She wants a dark lair and to re-establish the cult rituals. [ch 070 / 070.05]
- She scorns Severin's work as a corruption of the Cult. [ch 057 / 057.05; ch 057 / moment]

**Attitude to the party**
- She called Unla Key an abomination on sight. [ch 057 / 057.04]
- Unla Key called her "the worst kind of racist." [ch 070 / 070.04]

**Iymrith**
- She showed lust and passion for Iymrith. She hoped the tome would convince Iymrith to become a dracolich. [ch 070 / 070.06]

**Vampirism**
- She credits the book "Dusk" as why she wanted to become a vampire. [ch 057 / 057.06]

**Experiments**
- She animates the dead. The party burned remains in the courtyard to deny her "animation material." [ch 071 / 071.02]
- She experiments on the undead, including an undead cleaning service. [ch 071 / 071.06; ch 074 / entry]
- Unla Key called the skeletal dragon in her lair "A failed attempt at a dracolich." [ch 075 / 075.02]

## History with the Party
- First met in Chapter 057: Unla Key flew onto the upstairs balcony of the ancient Port Tower on Blagothkus' castle and entered. Thistle followed and saw a half-elven moon elf vampire smiling at Unla Key "like a cat smiling at a mouse." [ch 057 / 057.04]
- She screamed at Unla Key, "You can’t be alive. You’re an abomination." When Vardis called her "a friendly vampire?", she identified herself: "I am no such thing. I am Sandesyl Morgia." [ch 057 / 057.04; ch 057 / 057.05]
- She told Thistle that Severin's work was "a twisted, devilish plot by Zariel to eliminate Tiamat from her plane of Avernus." She also told him that "Severin hired Adimond to create a fey hybrid dragon," and that she does not know where Severin is. [ch 057 / 057.05]
- She described the hybrid fairy as "more a form of lycanthropy than a fusing of the two essences." She confirmed the fairy was the love of Thistle's life and asked, "Can you turn into a dragon?" Thistle tried and failed. [ch 057 / 057.05]
- She styles herself castellan of the castle. She said the castle is owned by Blagothkus and that she does not care who owns it "as long as there is a fresh supply of bodies to exsanguinate." [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.05]
- Orsik asked whether she would leave the party alone if they supplied enough bodies. She said she wants a steady supply of sentient humanoids and silences the screams outside the tower with magic. [ch 057 / 057.06]
- She deflected Orsik's questions about how she became a vampire and how many spawn she has with "That would be telling." She then threatened vengeance on anyone who thinks her "an old helpless vampire obsessed with her necromantic research," and Orsik nodded in approval. [ch 057 / 057.06]
- She told Vardis that the Ogres became part of her necromantic experiments once they were no longer viable as food. She refused Orsik's request to have her zombies perform in his next play. [ch 057 / 057.06]
- Her account of the castle's history: Blagothkus allied with the Cult of the Dragon; the First Faction captured the castle; it crashed in the north and was captured again; the First Faction got rid of her before leaving the north. [ch 057 / 057.06]
- As the party left, she gave Orsik a copy of "Dusk," saying it was why she wanted to become a vampire. Orsik decided against mocking her because her temper "goes from 1 to 50 in an instant." [ch 057 / 057.06; ch 057 / moment; ch 057 / entry]
- Chapter 058 states that she treated the castle as her domain and ate through all of Blagothkus' minions once he parked the castle north of Parnast. Outside her tower, the party felt her watching "through the unseeing eyes of her zombie spawns." [ch 058 / 058.01]
- Esclarotta hates her. The party held back from killing Blagothkus for fear that she would come in. [ch 058 / 058.03]
- After the castle took flight, one of her vampire spawns asked where the castle would stop for a meal. At nightfall Sandesyl stood in the courtyard with her zombies and spawns. Thistle refused to come outside. [ch 058 / 058.05]
- She refused to cross House Margaster: "Asmodeus protects them." She and Thistle bargained over a feeding stop and settled on the Uthgardt barbarians of Flint Rock. [ch 058 / 058.05; ch 058 / entry]
- Her vampires captured Uthgardt nomads near Flint Rock and left evidence and a survivor blaming House Margaster in service of Silverymoon. Her victims' screams echoed through the castle for several days. [ch 058 / 058.06]
- In Chapter 059 she was only spoken of. The giant in the control room shouted that he would not let "Sandesyl, or any of your hell spawn" into his tower. [ch 059 / 059.07; ch 059 / entry]
- In Chapter 064 she was only mentioned, in Unla Key's musing that Laeral Silverhand created the coalition that defeated Severin and Sandesyl. [ch 064 / 064.07]
- In Chapter 070 the party chose to deal with her before going after Hekaton. Unla Key called her "the worst kind of racist," and the party opted for her quest over an attack. [ch 070 / 070.04]
- Thistle visited her tower at midday, where she sat hidden in a shroud of darkness. Thistle could see through it. She offered to make Thistle a fey vampire spawn. [ch 070 / 070.05]
- Her quest: recover a cult tome lost when Severin attempted to bring Tiamat into the world. The tome lies in Xantharl’s Keep, behind an interdimensional puzzle bathed in bright sunlight. Her offered price is the castle. [ch 070 / 070.05; ch 070 / entry]
- She admitted she cannot control the castle because Esclarotta is impervious to her charms. She plans a dark lair and to re-establish the cult rituals. She believes salvation comes when all dragons become dracoliches and rule Faerûn. [ch 070 / 070.05]
- She named the Doom of the Desert as Iymrith and showed lust and passion for her. She let slip that the tome could convince Iymrith to become a dracolich, and said Iymrith needed the Storm Giant’s castle for experiments on hybridizing dragons "and fey." [ch 070 / 070.06]
- She holds Glazhael’s phylactery. Glazhael secretly agreed with Orsik to turn on her "When you have almost won." [ch 070 / 070.07; ch 070 / entry]
- She was not present in Chapter 071. The party burned courtyard remains to deny them to her as "animation material," and Unla Key said she is experimenting on the undead to create alternatives to Dracoliches. [ch 071 / entry; ch 071 / 071.02; ch 071 / 071.06]
