## Identity
Rezmir was a Black Dragon Wyrmspeaker and a member of the inner circle of the Cult of the Dragon, identified by her distinctive purple garb [ch 072 / 072.04]. She was rumored to have been in charge of the castle the party was exploring and responsible for moving the ritual hoard [ch 072 / 072.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Rezmir's motivations are primarily inferred from a note found in her room, which reflects the sentiments of those loyal to or associated with her operations. The author of the note expressed strong resentment toward internal audits, stating, "The idea that I need to be audited beggars' belief," and claimed that auditors who infiltrated the Black Dragon Hatchery were spies [ch 072 / 072.04]. The note also emphasized ideological unity within the cult, asserting, "The five heads of Tiamat do not attack each other and do not distrust each other. They are one and five at once," and demanded that any future audit "must involve all of us or be considered a trick by our enemies" [ch 072 / 072.04]. These quotes suggest that Rezmir's position was contested by subordinates or allies who felt targeted by scrutiny, possibly indicating internal friction or a rigid adherence to Tiamat’s unity that clashed with administrative oversight.

## History with the Party
*   The party discovered the remains of Rezmir, identified by Thistle as the Black Dragon Wyrmspeaker based on her purple garb, lying across the hall from the pantry [ch 072 / 072.04].
*   Thistle identified the remains and noted rumors that Rezmir ran the castle, moved the ritual hoard, and was killed by heroes of the War of the Dragons, though others suspected internal politics [ch 072 / 072.04].
*   Orsik found a note in Rezmir's room supporting the theory that she died due to internal disputes, as the writer protested being audited and called the auditors spies [ch 072 / 072.04].

## Last Observed State
The party encountered the remains of Rezmir lying across the hall from the pantry in a room north of the barracks [ch 072 / 072.04]. Only half of the dragon's remains were present in this location [ch 072 / 072.04]. A note found among the remains supported the theory that Rezmir died due to internal disputes, contrasting with rumors that she was killed by the heroes of the War of the Dragons [ch 072 / 072.04]. Orsik scoured the room for treasure but found nothing [ch 072 / 072.04]. Status not established in the summaries regarding her final moments, though the evidence indicates she is deceased.
