## Identity
- Storm giant who describes himself as "just a shaman." [ch 005 / entry; ch 005 / 005.07]
- Serves the All-Father, whom he names as Annam. [ch 005 / 005.07]
- Appears in one chapter only, at the desecrated shrine. He came after being told several days earlier that the shrine had been desecrated. [ch 005 / 005.07]
- Knows about Tiamat's failed plots, the Cult of the Dragon's aim, and the history of the giant kingdom of Ostoria. [ch 005 / 005.07]

## Personality and Motivations
- **Duty over agreement:** He serves the All-Father even though the All-Father has made decisions he does not agree with. [ch 005 / entry; ch 005 / 005.07]
- **Sad and resigned:** He looks at the "little people" with sadness and worries that "the age of tolerance is over as well." [ch 005 / 005.07]
- **Hopes the war fails to satisfy the All-Father:** His parting words hope the All-Father will learn that "this war will bring no glory to anyone." [ch 005 / 005.07]
- **Stern but lenient:** He confronted Unla Key over the gold plates, then offered him a face-saving excuse instead of punishing him. He says he meant only to invoke a curse, not to kill. [ch 005 / 005.07]
- **Dry humour:** Examples are "There is such a thing as pushing your luck?" and confirming that "thoughts and prayers" means "basically nothing." [ch 005 / entry; ch 005 / moment]
- **Narrow sense of his role:** Asked what he would do about the war, he listed his temple chores (drapes, roof, braziers, frescoes, "alters," the dead body). He said he must pray to understand what the All-Father wants. [ch 005 / 005.07]
- **Open to correction:** He conceded the party's objection to "We're big. You're little." and cited King Hekaton's talk of a newer, inclusive way of speaking. [ch 005 / 005.07]
- **Quietly helpful:** He gave Vardis potions, "perhaps more than just thoughts and prayers," but declined to join the party. [ch 005 / 005.07]

## History with the Party
- Kraddock is a storm giant who calls himself "just a shaman." [ch 005 / entry]
- He confronted the party as they left the desecrated shrine. He said he had been told it was desecrated several days earlier and had come to see its state. [ch 005 / 005.07]
- He saw Unla Key hiding the shrine's large gold plates. Unla Key gave several excuses, and Kraddock offered him a way out. Unla Key then handed back "these sacred gold plates." [ch 005 / 005.07; ch 005 / entry]
- He said he had not meant to kill them, only to "invoke a curse of the All-Father," and then shined the plates. [ch 005 / 005.07]
- He serves the All-Father despite disagreeing with his decisions, and looked at the party with sadness. [ch 005 / entry; ch 005 / 005.07]
- He told the party that Tiamat's failure inspired the giants to war on the little people. [ch 005 / entry; ch 005 / 005.07]
- He explained what the Cult of the Dragon was after: a spell that Tiamat said would let her enter the world. [ch 005 / 005.07]
- He advised the party to warn people to stay away from places where giants live or were rumoured to live. [ch 005 / 005.07]
- The party, led by Thistle, objected to his "We're big. You're little." language. Kraddock conceded the point and cited King Hekaton's talk of a newer, inclusive way of speaking. [ch 005 / 005.07]
- He described the ancient giant kingdom of Ostoria and how the giants were driven north over the centuries. [ch 005 / 005.07; ch 005 / entry]
- When Vardis asked what he would do about the war, he listed his temple chores. He offered only his "thoughts and prayers" and confirmed that this meant "basically nothing." [ch 005 / 005.07; ch 005 / moment]
- As the party left, he handed Vardis some potions. [ch 005 / 005.07; ch 005 / entry]
- He declined Unla Key's request to come with the party. [ch 005 / 005.07]

## Relationships
- **The All-Father (Annam):** Kraddock is his servant. He is loyal but disagrees with the All-Father's decisions, including, by implication, the war. [ch 005 / entry; ch 005 / 005.07]
- **Unla Key:** Caught him hiding the shrine's gold plates and let him hand them back without penalty. Turned down his request to join the party. [ch 005 / 005.07]
- **Vardis:** Vardis challenged him on doing nothing about the war. Kraddock still gave Vardis potions as the party left. [ch 005 / 005.07; ch 005 / entry]
- **Thistle:** Thistle was wary of him ("I think he can kill us without breaking a sweat") and led the objection to his language. Kraddock conceded, "You are so right." [ch 005 / 005.07]
- **King Hekaton:** Mentioned only. Kraddock cited Hekaton's talk of inclusive speech and mentioned "His great wife who disappeared." [ch 005 / 005.07]
- **Party overall:** Sympathetic but not an ally in action. He warned them, shared lore and gave potions, but would not intervene. [ch 005 / 005.07]
