## Identity
A kobold with burn-scarred red scales. [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.04]

## Personality and Motivations
Agra displays a pragmatic and self-preserving nature, prioritizing survival over loyalty or combat. During the failed ambush, he attempted to prevent his companion Gen from engaging the adventurers, urging him to "Let’s sit tight and wait" in hopes the party would trigger a trap. [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 004 / entry] When the trap failed, Agra immediately chose to "surrender and pray for the best," waving a white object and shouting their surrender. [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 004 / entry]

Agra adopts a wry, bureaucratic register to describe his dire circumstances, referring to execution or dismissal as a "negative review of our performance that would result in our termination." [ch 004 / 004.05; ch 004 / entry] He is motivated by fear of punishment from his employers; when questioned by Vardis, he explained that the Fire Giants would terminate them if they failed to return with treasure. [ch 004 / 004.05] This fear manifested physically when he picked up, squished, and swallowed a handful of dirt after explaining this consequence. [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.05]

Agra views himself as a negotiator rather than a subordinate. He was offended when an elf suggested the kobolds wanted to be slaves, insisting they were "employees" who are "very fluid in our loyalties." [ch 004 / 004.05; ch 004 / moment] He readily traded information about the giants and the cult's treasure to secure his release, asking if he could leave now that he and Thistle were "friends." [ch 004 / 004.05]

## History with the Party
- Agra, a kobold with burn-scarred red scales, hid inside the stone building with another kobold, Gen, during the party's ambush. [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 004 / entry]
- Agra attempted to stop Gen from engaging the heavily armed adventurers, clamping a hand over Gen's mouth and telling him to "Let’s sit tight and wait" while hoping the party would be lured into the trap. [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 004 / entry]
- When the ambush failed due to the hidden kobolds' loudness and a sleep spell killing them, Agra decided to "surrender and pray for the best," grabbing something white and stepping outside to shout their surrender. [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 004 / entry]
- Agra pleaded innocence to Thistle regarding the Cult of the Dragon, claiming his service was "an accident" performed "under orders" after seeing Thistle's torture marks. [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 004 / entry]
- Agra informed the party that with the defeat of the Cult and its dragons, the kobold tribes are now working for giants; specifically, a fire giant hired Agra's tribe after their Red Dragon left, while other tribes were hired by hill and cloud giants. [ch 004 / 004.05; ch 004 / entry]
- Agra explained the current mission: the Fire Giants sent the kobolds to find "the treasure of the cultists" in the southern room, which Nug had described as containing a "glowing magical fire symbol, and a river of lava." [ch 004 / 004.05; ch 004 / entry]
- Agra offered his services as an employee rather than a slave, stating that kobolds are "very fluid in our loyalties." [ch 004 / 004.05; ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / moment]
- When Vardis asked if the kobolds would inform the Fire Giants of the party's existence, Agra explained that failure to return with treasure would result in "a, how shall we say, negative review of our performance that would result in our termination." [ch 004 / 004.05; ch 004 / entry]
- Agra was permitted to leave after the dwarf accepted treasure from Gen, with orders to "go and no looting"; Agra then ate a handful of dirt. [ch 004 / 004.05; ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / moment]

## Last Observed State
Agra was last seen in Chapter 4. He traded information to the party, including details on how Fire Giants hired his tribe after the Red Dragon's departure, and how other kobold tribes worked for hill and cloud giants. [ch 004 / 004.05] He described his current mission: finding "the treasure of the cultists" in the southern room, which contained a "glowing magical fire symbol, and a river of lava," as described by their deceased leader Nug. [ch 004 / 004.05]

After Gen offered treasure to the dwarf, the dwarf permitted them to leave with the command "go and no looting." [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.05] Before departing, Agra explained to Vardis that failing to return with treasure would result in their termination, then ate a handful of dirt. [ch 004 / 004.05; ch 004 / moment] Status not established in the summaries beyond his departure from the scene.

## Relationships
**Gen**: A kobold companion hiding with Agra in the stone building. Agra restrained Gen from engaging the party, telling him to sit tight. [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 004 / entry] Gen suggested offering treasure to the party when the halfling proposed using kobolds as shields. [ch 004 / 004.05]

**Thistle**: A fairy tortured by the Cult of the Dragon. Agra recognized Thistle's torture marks and pleaded innocence, claiming his service was "an accident" performed "under orders." [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 004 / entry] Thistle repeatedly questioned Agra about a missing girlfriend, which Agra claimed ignorance of, stating only that they served giant masters. [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 004 / 004.05]

**Vardis**: A member of the party who questioned Agra about whether the kobolds would inform the Fire Giants of the party's existence. [ch 004 / 004.05]

**The Dwarf**: A party member who accepted treasure offered by Gen and commanded Agra and Gen to leave without looting. [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.05]

**Nug**: The former kobold leader (now deceased) who Agra referenced as having identified the location of the cult's treasure and its features. [ch 004 / 004.05]
