## Identity
Slayermighty was a giant who had been shrunk to the size of "a tiny giant." He wore ratty furs and wielded a club [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.08]. His name was Slayermighty, though he referred to himself as "Slayermighty the Giant" [ch 001 / moment].

## Personality and Motivations
Slayermighty displayed a boastful and aggressive temperament, screaming threats upon detecting intruders [ch 001 / 001.08]. He expressed a specific ambition to rule all of Giant-kind, a goal he claimed would soon be realized [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / moment]. His behavior toward the party was hostile and threatening, though he lacked the physical capability to harm them [ch 001 / 001.08].

## History with the Party
- Slayermighty was a giant shrunk to the size of "a tiny giant," dressed in ratty furs and wielding a club, who stood guarding a stone chest in a lantern-lit cavern [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.08].
- Upon detecting the party, Slayermighty screamed threats against them, specifically naming Blooddrinker, while boasting of ruling Giant-kind, but he was unable to lay a hand on the group [ch 001 / 001.08].
- Slayermighty died during the encounter without landing a single blow on the party [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.08].
- Upon death, Slayermighty was found to be carrying two vials of alchemist fire, a potion of healing, and a necklace of 20 copper charms [ch 001 / 001.09].
- The stone chest guarded by Slayermighty did not contain the expected cache, but instead held a taunting note from Clan True-Nimbus of the Clouds [ch 001 / 001.09].

## Last Observed State
Slayermighty was found standing in a lantern-lit cavern before a stone chest that reached up to his eyes [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.08]. When discovered by the party, he screamed threats but was unable to lay a hand on them. He died during this encounter without landing a single blow on the party [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.08]. His death was observed by Orsik, who noted that the giant did not get a chance to swing its club [ch 001 / moment]. Upon death, he was found carrying two vials of alchemist fire, a potion of healing, and a necklace of 20 copper charms [ch 001 / 001.09].

## Relationships
*   **Blooddrinker**: Slayermighty mentioned this bear in his threats, stating he would feed the party to Blooddrinker [ch 001 / moment].
*   **Giant-kind**: Slayermighty expressed a desire to rule this group [ch 001 / moment].
*   **Clan True-Nimbus of the Clouds**: The note found in the chest Slayermighty guarded was signed by this clan, suggesting they were rivals or the source of the shrunk giant's predicament, though the note explicitly mocks "runt-giants" [ch 001 / 001.09].
*   **The Party (Orsik, Vardis, Thistle)**: Slayermighty threatened these characters with death but was killed by them (or died during the encounter with them) [ch 001 / 001.08].
