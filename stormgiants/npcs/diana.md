## Identity
Diana is a female shield dwarf serving as the commander of a strike team [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.03]. She holds direct authority over her subordinate dragon and other team members [ch 024 / 024.03].

## Personality and Motivations
Diana is professional and mission-focused, stating she is "just trying to do my job" when pressed on her tactics [ch 024 / 024.03]. She displays a dry, disdainful sense of humor regarding other races' tropes, grumbling that "no self-respecting dwarf would want a princess in a tower" after Vardis makes a joke about her situation [ch 024 / 024.03]. She is defensive and touchy about her questioning methods, reacting with shock and indignation to Orsik’s inquiry about Cloud Giants by asking, "Are you racist towards Cloud Giants?" [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.03]. She prioritizes efficiency, calling off engagements or missions once they are determined to be errors or "a bust" [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.03].

## History with the Party
- Diana is a female shield dwarf and commander of the strike team. [ch 024 / entry]
- She revealed her authority and removed her helm to introduce herself after her dragon subordinate spoke on her behalf. [ch 024 / 024.03]
- She conceded that the strike team had interfered with the wrong castle once Thistle confirmed they worked for the SEER. [ch 024 / 024.03]
- She stated that the strike team fought the Cult of the Dragon to a standstill. [ch 024 / 024.03]
- She expressed disdain for the trope of a princess in a tower, stating "no self-respecting dwarf would want a princess in a tower." [ch 024 / 024.03]
- After learning that cultists had previously visited to recruit Zephyros, she deemed the situation a bust, said "Should have led with that," and departed. [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.03]

## Last Observed State
Diana is alive and active, having departed with her strike team at the conclusion of Chapter 024 [ch 024 / entry]. After learning from Unla Key that evil cultists had previously visited Zephyros to recruit him, Diana laughed, said "Should have led with that," and flew off with her team [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.03]. Prior to departing, she had conceded that her team had interfered with the wrong castle and confirmed they had fought the Cult of the Dragon to a standstill [ch 024 / 024.03].

## Relationships
*   **The Dragon Subordinate:** Diana commands a dragon subordinate who initially spoke on her behalf due to a "common misconception" that the winged creature was in charge; Diana corrects this by removing her helm and asserting, "I am Diana, and I am in charge of this mission" [ch 024 / 024.03].
*   **The SEER:** Diana respects the authority of "the SEER," immediately calling off the attack against the party once Thistle confirmed their allegiance to her [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.03].
*   **Zephyros:** Diana expressed disdain for the trope of a "princess in a tower" in relation to the castle's contents but departed upon learning cultists had already visited him [ch 024 / 024.03].
*   **Vardis:** Diana was dismissive of Vardis's joke ("Would you say your princess is in another castle?"), identifying it merely as "human humor" before delivering her own grumbling retort [ch 024 / 024.03].
*   **Orsik:** Diana reacted defensively and with indignation to Orsik’s line of questioning regarding Cloud Giants, accusing him of racism before explaining her security protocols [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.03].
