## Identity
Hekaton is the King of the Storm Giants, a quasi-divine figure of immense size and power described as "arguably the most powerful of all Storm Giants" [ch 069 / entry]. Before the shattering of the Ordning, he presided over a court that included representatives of every race of giant, using his strength to bully weaker giants into submission to hold that court together [ch 069 / 069.07]. He is the husband of the missing Queen Neri and the father of Mirran, Nym, and Serissa [ch 069 / 069.07; ch 066 / 066.01]. Following his capture by the Kraken Society and subsequent rescue, he returned to his stronghold, the Maelstrom, to reassert his authority [ch 082 / 082.03; ch 082 / 082.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Hekaton is driven by a desire to restore the Ordning and reassert Storm Giant dominance over other giant races [ch 082 / 082.05]. He views himself as the ultimate authority, insisting on speaking first in council and asserting, "I am King, and I make the decisions!" [ch 085 / 085.01; ch 085 / 085.02]. He prioritizes political and strategic goals over personal emotions, stating, "The affairs of the heart are paramount, but the realms are in danger," when dismissing Thistle’s personal quests [ch 082 / 082.04].

He is deeply affected by the shattering of the Ordning and the death of his wife, Neri, which led him to go on a "rage-filled quest" for revenge rather than maintaining his post, an act his daughter Serissa later condemned as abandoning her [ch 069 / entry; ch 085 / 085.02]. He struggles with guilt regarding these choices and often acts to avoid "losing face," relying on divination to make difficult strategic decisions when he feels trapped by his own pride [ch 085 / 085.04]. He believes his actions are guided by the All-Father and that the party’s involvement is a sign, leading him to insist specifically on their help to secure an alliance with the Lords' Alliance [ch 083 / 083.04].

## Last Observed State
Status established: Hekaton is alive and active in the Maelstrom [ch 082 / entry].

*   **Release and Reintegration:** After being held prisoner on the ship *Morkoth*, Hekaton was freed from his chains by the party in Chapter 82 [ch 082 / 082.02; ch 082 / moment]. Upon waking in the Maelstrom's grotto, he was disoriented ("Where am I? Who are you?") but quickly demanded to go to the throne room [ch 082 / 082.03].
*   **Court Reformation:** In the throne room, he dismissed the old Imperator, Uthor, named Serissa as the new Imperator, and confined his rebellious daughters Nym and Mirran to their chambers without immediate judgment ("I will decide your fates later") [ch 082 / 082.04].
*   **Strategic Engagement:** In Chapter 83, he teleported the party to Ironslag after failing to send them back to their cloud castle due to a *forbiddance* ward [ch 083 / 083.01; ch 083 / 083.02]. In Chapter 85, upon the party's return to the Maelstrom, he asserted his dominance over the conversation, welcomed them, and endured an emotional outburst from Serissa [ch 085 / 085.01; ch 085 / 085.02].
*   **Current Plan:** Confronted with the choice between a piecemeal assassination plot or a direct assault, he asked the diviner Unla Key to determine the better plan [ch 085 / 085.04]. After Unla Key advised a full-force attack, Hekaton agreed to strike at Iymrith’s lair, with the party setting a meeting time for five days later [ch 085 / 085.04].

## Relationships
*   **Serissa (Daughter/Imperator):** Hekaton named Serissa Imperator to replace Uthor [ch 082 / 082.04]. Their relationship is strained; Serissa accuses him of abandoning his post and declaring her sisters unfit to rule, which fueled their rebellion [ch 085 / 085.02]. Hekaton responds defensively, asserting his royal prerogative [ch 085 / 085.02].
*   **Nym and Mirran (Daughters):** Hekaton judged these two eldest daughters unfit to rule years before his disappearance [ch 069 / 069.07]. Upon his return, he confined them for betraying him and Serissa and allying with a dragon, leaving their final judgment pending [ch 082 / 082.04].
*   **Uthor (Brother/Former Imperator):** Hekaton publicly dismissed his brother Uthor, accusing him of insufficient effort in the search for him [ch 082 / 082.04].
*   **Iymrith (The Doom of the Desert):** Hekaton identifies Iymrith as the primary threat to the realms and the architect of the conspiracy against him [ch 082 / 082.04; ch 085 / 085.04]. He views the dragon as a personal and political enemy that must be defeated to restore the Ordning [ch 082 / 082.05].
*   **The Party (3HPP):** Hekaton views the party as "heroic" and essential to his political and military goals [ch 083 / 083.04]. He offered an alliance contingent on them dealing with the five giant lords, but the party declined his partnership, choosing to act independently [ch 082 / 082.05]. Despite this, he insists on their specific involvement to satisfy the Lords' Alliance and the All-Father's perceived will [ch 083 / 083.04].
