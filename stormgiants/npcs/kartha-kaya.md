## Identity
Kartha-Kaya is the Chief of the Yakfolk village [ch 060 / entry]. He presides over a residence described as a 60-foot-diameter hut situated on high ground in the center of the village, featuring a beaded curtain entrance, an indoor fire pit, and a bronze gong [ch 060 / 060.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Kartha-Kaya adheres to specific social protocols, utilizing invitations to meals as a mechanism to assert dominance or impose obligations on visitors; he considers refusing his hospitality "beyond rude" [ch 060 / 060.05]. He displays prejudice, making snide remarks about dwarves specifically when Orsik refused to participate in a cleansing ritual [ch 060 / entry]. His motivations appear hostile or coercive: during the meal, he led a humming ritual during which he and the Yakfolk attempted to take over the heroes' minds [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / moment].

## History with the Party
- Chief Kartha-Kaya invited the heroes to eat in his yurt, a gesture conveyed by a young moon elf who claimed refusing would be "beyond rude" [ch 060 / 060.05].
- Orsik and Vardis cast wards against poison before entering the Chief's 60-foot-diameter hut, while Thistle felt social pressure from Fey court protocols prevented him from avoiding the meal [ch 060 / 060.05].
- Kartha-Kaya insisted on a cleansing ritual during the meal [ch 060 / entry].
- Kartha-Kaya made snide remarks about dwarves when Orsik refused to join the ritual [ch 060 / entry].
- Kartha-Kaya led a hum during which the Yakfolk attempted to take over the heroes' minds [ch 060 / entry].

## Last Observed State
Kartha-Kaya is seen leading a humming ritual intended to mentally subjugate the heroes within his hut [ch 060 / entry]. The attempt failed when Orsik, who had refused to join the ritual, noticed the intrusion and warned the party; this triggered a free-for-all melee [ch 060 / moment]. Status not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
Kartha-Kaya commands the respect or fear of a young moon elf, who acted as his messenger to summon the heroes to dinner, appearing frightened for their safety [ch 060 / 060.05]. He holds a derogatory view of dwarves, evidenced by his snide remarks directed at Orsik for refusing the cleansing ritual [ch 060 / entry]. He is the leader of the Yakfolk who attempted the mental possession of the heroes [ch 060 / entry].
