## Identity
Yazmeen is a gnome with orange hair, a crooked nose, scarred hands, and face tattoos [ch 042 / 042.03]. He serves as the local librarian of Prettyview, maintaining a collection that visitors from neighboring towns use to trade books [ch 042 / 042.03; ch 042 / entry]. The party identified him as the only survivor found in the town after a giant attack destroyed all but two buildings [ch 042 / 042.03; ch 042 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Yazmeen is characterized by an intense devotion to his library and patrons, prioritizing their needs even in dangerous circumstances; he initially refused to abandon his books or leave his patrons despite the threat of giants and a Slaad aberration [ch 042 / 042.03; ch 042 / 042.04]. He displays a habitual politeness centered on hospitality, frequently offering tea to guests [ch 042 / 042.03; ch 042 / 042.04]. Despite his scholarly role, he is physically susceptible to harsh conditions, complaining that he is freezing during travel [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / moment]. He also possesses a degree of magical knowledge or awareness regarding his own circumstances, recognizing the protective properties of his tea against interplanar threats [ch 042 / 042.05].

## History with the Party
*   Yazmeen was discovered by Orsik hiding in the library of Prettyview, one of the only two standing buildings left after a giant attack, and described as a terrified gnome with a crooked nose, scarred hands, face tattoos, and orange hair [ch 042 / 042.03].
*   Yazmeen identified himself as the local librarian and explained that visitors from neighboring towns trade books with his collection [ch 042 / 042.03].
*   Yazmeen offered the party tea and allowed them to sleep in the warm library rather than the empty, cold barn, noting that the barn held only cows [ch 042 / 042.03].
*   Yazmeen refused to join the party initially, stating he could not leave his library patrons or his books [ch 042 / 042.04].
*   During the night, a Slaad aberration emerged from Yazmeen’s bedroom, accusing the party of ruining its breeding program [ch 042 / 042.04].
*   After the Slaad was killed and the library caught fire, Vardis rescued the sleeping Yazmeen from the burning building [ch 042 / 042.05].
*   Yazmeen explained that the Slaad was an extra-planar creature that had been using him as an anchor for a summoning spell, which was disrupted by the Frost Giants’ attack on the town [ch 042 / 042.05].
*   Yazmeen confirmed with Vardis that the tea he provided had a magical property that warded off the Slaad, but the effect faded while he slept [ch 042 / 042.05].
*   Yazmeen travelled with the party through Middle Valley, complaining that he was freezing [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.01; ch 043 / moment].
*   While the group was resting at the Oracle of Annam, Orsik told Yazmeen they should bring him back to civilization; Yazmeen mutteringly agreed, saying, "That would be nice" [ch 047 / 047.01].

## Last Observed State
The last confirmed observation of Yazmeen occurs in Chapter 047. He was travelling with the party to the Oracle of Annam, where he was described as "freezing" [ch 047 / entry; ch 047 / 047.01]. During a rest in one of the rooms of the faithful, Orsik proposed bringing him back to civilization, to which Yazmeen muttered, "That would be nice" [ch 047 / entry; ch 047 / 047.01]. Status not established in the summaries after this interaction; there is no evidence confirming his arrival at civilization or his whereabouts in subsequent chapters.

## Relationships
*   **Orsik:** Discovered Yazmeen hiding in the library [ch 042 / 042.03]. Orsik frequently attempted to persuade Yazmeen to join the group, offering safety in exchange for abandoning his post [ch 042 / 042.04]. Later, Orsik suggested returning Yazmeen to civilization as a side quest or courtesy [ch 047 / 047.01].
*   **Vardis:** Rescued Yazmeen from the burning library after the Slaad attack [ch 042 / 042.05]. Vardis analyzed the magical properties of Yazmeen’s tea to determine how it protected him from the aberration [ch 042 / 042.05].
*   **Unla Key:** Accepted Yazmeen’s offer of tea, which proved to have magical protective properties [ch 042 / 042.04]. Unla Key noted that Yazmeen was sleeping during the Slaad attack and urged the party to check on him [ch 042 / 042.05].
*   **Slaad Aberration:** The creature used Yazmeen as a portal for an interplanar summoning spell [ch 042 / 042.05]. The Slaad accused Yazmeen and the party of ruining its breeding program and threatened to end Yazmeen when its spell was disrupted [ch 042 / 042.04; ch 042 / 042.05].
