## Identity
Glazhael the Undying, formerly Glazhael the Cloudchaser, is a vast white dracolich [ch 059 / 059.05; ch 070 / 070.07]. She was once the steed of Talis the White before being killed by the First Faction (specifically noted as Stroud in chapter 070) and raised as a dracolich by Jorgen using the Blue Mask [ch 059 / 059.05; ch 070 / 070.07]. Her physical form is that of a massive white dragon, currently undead, dwelling in a glacier-based lair beneath Blagothkus’s castle [ch 070 / 070.07; ch 077 / 077.02]. She is defined by her lack of subservience, famously roaring, "I AM A DRACOLICH," and "I do not bow to anyone" [ch 070 / 070.07; ch 070 / moment].

## Personality and Motivations
Glazhael operates with cunning and self-interest rather than heroism or malice. She is described as "not entirely trustworthy" and liable to turn on the party if it benefits her [ch 076 / 076.03]. Her primary motivation is the recovery of her phylactery, which is held by Sandesyl; she fears Sandesyl will destroy it if she acts openly, prompting her to demand its return before assisting further [ch 070 / 070.07; ch 077 / 077.02]. She refuses to share her methods or plans, insisting her tactics are a secret, leading Orsik to note, "Dragons can’t just say things simply" [ch 070 / 070.07; ch 071 / 071.01]. She views the party’s alliance with her as a transactional agreement to seal Sandesyl’s victory, stating, "When you have almost won, I will seal your victory. But not a moment sooner" [ch 070 / 070.07; ch 070 / moment]. She displays a disdain for simple inquiries, laughing and saying "I don't know" when asked how to kill a vampire, despite previously claiming to have a secret method [ch 077 / 077.03; ch 070 / 070.07].

## History with the Party
*   Worug reveals that the castle is built around a glacier containing the den of Glazhael, a white dracolich and former steed of Talis the White, who was killed by the First Faction and later bound to Sandesyl. [ch 059 / 059.05]
*   The 3HPP descend into the glacier, find Glazhael clinging to the ceiling of a vault, and hear her story of being raised by Jorgen and deceived by the First Faction; she agrees to a deal to strike at Sandesyl only when the party is almost winning. [ch 070 / 070.07]
*   The party leaves Glazhael's lair without learning her specific plan, as she refuses to share it, leaving them to rely solely on her word. [ch 071 / 071.01]
*   Glazhael sneaks toward the tower, uses her dragon fear to disorient the party (affecting Thistle, Thuul, and Unla Key), and breathes ice on the tower before flying away and screaming that the party is dead. [ch 073 / 073.07; ch 073 / 073.08]
*   Thistle observes that Glazhael has moved out of eye shot, interpreting it as an invitation for him to approach Sandesyl's tower. [ch 074 / 074.02]
*   Blagothkus notes that Glazhael has left, urging the party to run to Sandesyl's tower while the zombies are disoriented; Blagothkus explains the zombie ogres were meant to trap the party so the dragon could kill them from above. [ch 074 / 074.01]
*   When Orsik demands to know where Glazhael is after finding Sandesyl's coffin empty, Thistle points out that the dracolich has no way to enter the lair; Orsik cites Glazhael's promise to show up if the vampire was deeply injured. [ch 075 / 075.06]
*   Glazhael is described as a cunning undead dragon who struck a deal with the party to retrieve her phylactery, which is hidden in a cavern of ice within the castle; the party must fight through undead and icy dangers to reach it. [ch 076 / 076.03]
*   Glazhael is surprised to see the party alive in her lair; she breaks open an ice wall with her tail to reveal Sandesyl's coffin, demanding her phylactery in exchange for the information. [ch 077 / 077.02]
*   Glazhael receives her phylactery, laughs when asked how to kill a vampire, and leaves once she has it, fulfilling the deal that she would leave once retrieved. [ch 077 / 077.03]

## Last Observed State
In the final recorded events, Glazhael was present in her lair at the center of the glacier when the party returned [ch 077 / 077.02]. She was surprised to find the party alive and asked, "Sandesyl is dead?" [ch 077 / 077.02]. She physically shattered an ice wall with her tail to reveal Sandesyl’s coffin, breaking a plain-looking coffin hidden behind it, demonstrating her strength where a magical fire bolt had previously failed to yield results [ch 077 / 077.02]. She demanded her phylactery in exchange for this information, stating, "Once I have my phylactery, I will leave and you can do whatever you want" [ch 077 / 077.02]. Upon receiving the phylactery, she fulfilled the terms of their deal and departed [ch 077 / 077.03]. As she left, Orsik asked for advice on killing the vampire; she laughed and replied, "I don't know," before exiting the scene [ch 077 / 077.03]. Status not established in the summaries after her departure.

## Relationships
**Sandesyl:** Glazhael views Sandesyl as a captor and enemy who holds her phylactery hostage, controlling her existence [ch 070 / 070.07; ch 076 / 076.03]. She agreed to a secret pact to destroy Sandesyl, though she withheld the specific method to ensure her own safety [ch 070 / 070.07].
**The Party (3HPP/Orsik/Thistle/Unla Key/Vardis):** Her relationship with the party is transactional and tense. They rely on her word but distrust her simplicity and methods [ch 071 / 071.01]. She actively endangered them by using dragon fear and ice breath, screaming "THEY ARE ALL DEAD!" after attacking their position, though this was part of a complex tactical maneuver [ch 073 / 073.08; ch 074 / 074.01]. Thistle interpreted her movements as strategic invitations rather than pure attacks [ch 074 / 074.02]. She ultimately aided them by providing access to Sandesyl’s coffin in exchange for her phylactery [ch 077 / 077.02].
**Talis the White:** Talis was her former rider and partner; Glazhael’s history involves her service to Talis and the subsequent betrayal/destruction of their relationship by outside forces [ch 059 / 059.05; ch 070 / 070.07].
**The First Faction:** She was deceived by the First Faction, who lied to her regarding the location of the Blue Mask and other matters, leading to her downfall and current undeath [ch 070 / 070.07].
