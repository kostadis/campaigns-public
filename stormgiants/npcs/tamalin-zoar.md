## Identity
- Tamalin Zoar, also called Tamalin, is the founder and the law of Calling Horns, a settlement on the Evermoor Way on the road to Everlund. [ch 030 / 030.06; ch 031 / 031.01]
- The chapter 031 entry calls her a retired adventurer who is "still spry at 60". [ch 031 / entry]
- Calling Horns was a trailside inn until she bought it and retired there. Settlers followed and pay her monthly fees for protection. [ch 031 / 031.01]
- Captain Zoar calls her the mayor of Calling Horns. [ch 047 / 047.04]
- Eltio calls her "a legendary member of the Hand of Yartar" [ch 030 / 030.06] and "a legendary figure in these parts." [ch 032 / 032.08]
- Captain Zoar gave her career as cutthroat and brigand, then a gang of women thieves in Triboar, then founder of a town and mayor. [ch 047 / 047.04]
- She is a member of the Zoar family. Her brother is High Captain Horix Zoar of Everlund. [ch 031 / 031.04; ch 033 / 033.11]
- The Zoar family owns Danivarr's House in Everlund, which Dral runs. [ch 031 / 031.04]
- She can issue a Letter of Mark that her contacts in Yartar and Everlund honour. [ch 031 / 031.01; ch 033 / 033.01; ch 047 / 047.04]

## Personality and Motivations
- **Generous and sociable.** When Orsik flattered her, she cleared the main table, called for food and drink, and shared her stories. She then gave the party two rooms for free, saying "I have a soft spot for folks like you." [ch 031 / 031.02]
- The chapter reads her as an adventurer at the end of her career who likes sharing her stories with people at the start of theirs. [ch 031 / 031.02]
- **Frank and businesslike.** She judged the Triboar play: hits-driven businesses are unsustainable, but a big hit is worth more than a dragon's hoard. [ch 031 / moment]
- She corrected Orsik's sales pitch in front of the group, explaining that "I wanted everybody else to benefit." [ch 031 / 031.04]
- **Willing to back down.** When she saw she had upset Orsik, she apologized. [ch 031 / 031.04]
- **Practical about her town's problems.** She was most concerned with the trolls coming down from the Evermoors. She hired the party to find out why, and said that knowing the reason was enough even without a solution. [ch 031 / 031.04]
- **Informed.** She explained the leadership titles of the giant kinds and called a fire-giant and troll alliance "very odd." [ch 032 / 032.05]
- **Proud of her family name.** She mentioned being a Zoar while "waiting for recognition." [ch 031 / 031.04]
- **Has moved away from crime.** Eltio's reaction to the party doing only recon was "Damn, Tamalin has gone legit." [ch 032 / 032.09]

## History with the Party
- Tamalin Zoar was first mentioned, not present, in Yartar. Eltio told the party that information on Harthal would cost 500 gold, or that Tamalin Zoar could waive the fee. [ch 030 / 030.06]
- Eltio described her as "a legendary member of the Hand of Yartar who built her own town on the Evermoor Way on the way to Everlund." [ch 030 / 030.06]
- Before the party met her, Thistle recounted that Calling Horns was a trailside inn until Tamalin bought it and retired there. Settlers came and pay monthly fees for her protection. She is "still spry at 60 and is the law." [ch 031 / 031.01; ch 031 / entry]
- Orsik flattered her business vision and asked to learn from her. Surprised and flattered, she cleared the main table, called for food and drinks, and shared her experiences with the party. [ch 031 / 031.02]
- When Thistle brought up the Triboar play, she judged hits-driven businesses unsustainable but potentially "worth more than a dragon’s hoard." [ch 031 / 031.02]
- She gave the party two rooms in the house for free. [ch 031 / 031.02; ch 031 / entry]
- That night she banged on their doors to report two trolls attacking her stables. The party killed both trolls, but the second slaughtered two horses first. [ch 031 / 031.03]
- After the fight, Orsik pitched her a theater production. She told him, in front of the whole group, that he was being too aggressive with his sales pitch. [ch 031 / 031.04]
- Orsik objected and said "I don’t think this mentee thing is working out." Tamalin apologized, and Vardis was surprised to see Orsik accept the apology. [ch 031 / 031.04; ch 031 / entry]
- She hired the party to go to the Evermoors and find out why trolls were coming down into Calling Horns more often. She said knowing the reason was enough, and offered a Letter of Mark as the reward. [ch 031 / 031.04; ch 031 / entry]
- She revealed she is a member of the Zoar family and that her brother is the High Captain of Everlund. She said Dral, who runs Danivarr’s House for the Zoar family, would act on her note. [ch 031 / 031.04; ch 031 / entry]
- Unla Key, drinking again, balked at working for "a letter." Orsik talked him round, then privately admired Tamalin as a "scoundrel" whose arc he wants for himself. [ch 031 / 031.05]
- The party returned to the Calling Horns Inn and reported that fire giants of Duke Zalto's tribe were working with trolls and seeking the Vonindod. [ch 032 / 032.05; ch 032 / entry]
- In response, Tamalin called the alliance "very odd" and explained giant leadership titles. She suggested asking in Everlund and Silverymoon. [ch 032 / 032.05; ch 032 / entry]
- She told the party that "that note I just gave you is good for a favor" with her brother. [ch 032 / 032.05; ch 032 / entry]
- In Yartar, the party presented the Letter of Mark. Eltio remarked "Damn, Tamalin has gone legit" and recalled her as "a legendary figure in these parts." Tamalin was mentioned only. [ch 032 / 032.08; ch 032 / 032.09]
- Eltio was impressed that the party carried the Letter of Mark from Tamalin Zoar, and gave them information on Harthal and the sword. Tamalin was mentioned only. [ch 033 / 033.01]
- Orsik took Tamalin's note to Dral Thelev at Danivarr's House and praised her there as a "Brilliant business lady." Dral confirmed that High Captain Horix Zoar is her brother. Tamalin was mentioned only. [ch 033 / 033.11]
- In Everlund, Thistle handed her letter of Mark to Captain Zoar. Tamalin was mentioned only. [ch 047 / 047.04]
- Captain Zoar recounted her career: cutthroat and brigand, then a gang of women thieves in Triboar, then building a town and becoming mayor of Calling Horns. He said he was very proud of her, and her letter won the party his help. [ch 047 / 047.04]
- Unla Key listed "Tamalin Zoar, founder of Calling Horns" among the party's allies. Tamalin was mentioned only. [ch 057 / 057.01]
- Orsik named Tamalin Zoar as a possible discreet ally, and Vardis added her brother. Tamalin was mentioned only. [ch 064 / 064.02]
- Status not established in the summaries. [ch 064 / 064.02]
