## Identity
- Imber Chen: a lavender-haired gnome whose clothes match his violet eyes. He has a clear Calishite accent. [ch 028 / entry; ch 028 / 028.05]
- He sells luxurious fabrics at the Fishyard market in Yartar. [ch 028 / entry; ch 028 / 028.05]
- He says he has plied the rivers for many years, including to Silverymoon. [ch 028 / 028.05]
- He formerly had "Cap'n Squint" on his boat. [ch 028 / 028.06]
- Also referred to simply as "Imber." [ch 078 / entry; ch 078 / 078.01]

## Personality and Motivations
- **Quick to take offence and to lecture.** Thistle said "Not my uncle?" Imber explained that he had read fairies are "born through asexual reproduction in egg creches." He then accused Orsik of "heteronormative assumptions about binary reproduction." [ch 028 / 028.05]
- **Politically aligned with the Lords' Alliance.**
  - He called the Lords' Alliance "the only power trying to bring order into the anarchist chaos that the Harpers promote and the Zhentarim exploit through their taxation." [ch 028 / 028.05]
  - He noted that Yartar's Waterbaron allied with the Alliance to stay in power. [ch 028 / 028.05]
- **Exclusionary view of "the goodly races."** He listed Humans, Dwarves, Gnomes, Elves, Halflings and Half-Elves. He refused to include half-orcs. When Orsik pushed back, he glared. [ch 028 / 028.05]
- **Mercantile and firm in negotiation.**
  - He scolded the party for repaying his "generosity without considering my wares or making a fair offer." [ch 028 / 028.06]
  - He pitched his white cloth for a banner, then his own stall. [ch 028 / 028.06]
  - He refused a commission and Orsik's 10% coupon-code scheme. He held to a flat fee of half a gold piece for 43 days. [ch 028 / 028.06]
- **Guarded but willing to share contacts.** He would not take the party to Silverymoon himself. He did refer them to Cap'n Squint, with the remark "Friends come and go. It's the enemies that accumulate." [ch 028 / 028.06]
- **Informed about local politics.** He knew that Lord Khaspere Drylund owns the Grand Dame and "wants to be the new Waterbaron when the old one dies." [ch 028 / 028.06]

## History with the Party
- Imber Chen is a lavender-haired gnome with violet eyes and a clear Calishite accent who sells fabrics at the Fishyard in Yartar. [ch 028 / entry; ch 028 / 028.05]
- First met when Thistle wanted someone who could report on Silverymoon and the magic user shouted, "To Imber!" [ch 028 / 028.05]
- Imber was startled to see a fairy. He took offence at Thistle's "Not my uncle?" and accused Orsik of "heteronormative assumptions about binary reproduction." [ch 028 / 028.05; ch 028 / entry]
- Orsik and Vardis used the exchange to promote their "progressive" play at the Talking Troll, on "the 5th day of the 3rd tenday of Flamerule." [ch 028 / 028.05]
- Imber said he had plied the rivers for many years, including to Silverymoon. [ch 028 / 028.05]
- He described Silverymoon as a powerful Lords' Alliance member populated by "the goodly races." He also said Yartar's Waterbaron had allied with the Lords' Alliance to stay in power. [ch 028 / 028.05; ch 028 / entry]
- He refused to include half-orcs among "the goodly races," which put him in friction with Orsik and Vardis. [ch 028 / 028.05]
- He declined to take the party to Silverymoon himself. [ch 028 / 028.06]
- He pointed them instead to "Cap'n Squint," a halfling formerly on Imber's boat who now owns his own barge and was likely leaving the next day. [ch 028 / 028.06; ch 028 / entry]
- Imber told the party that Squint would gamble that night on the Grand Dame. He said the boat is owned by Lord Khaspere Drylund, who "wants to be the new Waterbaron when the old one dies." [ch 028 / 028.06]
- He scolded the party for not considering his wares. He offered white cloth for a banner and an introduction to a member of the Hand of Yartar who could get them a stall. [ch 028 / 028.06]
- He then offered his own stall for the banner. [ch 028 / 028.06]
- He rejected Unla Key's commission idea and Orsik's 10% coupon-code proposal. He settled on a flat fee of half a gold piece for 43 days of banner space, and Orsik shook on it. [ch 028 / 028.06; ch 028 / entry]
- In Chapter 078 Imber was only spoken of in recollection, as the merchant on the docks who told the party that Lord Khaspere Drylund owns the Grand Dame and is manipulating votes. [ch 078 / entry; ch 078 / 078.01]
- That information is why the party went to Yartar and the Grand Dame. [ch 078 / 078.01]
