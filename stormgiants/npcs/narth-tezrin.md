## Identity
Narth Tezrin is a co-owner of the Lionshield Coster outpost in Triboar, known locally as Lion’s Share [ch 024 / 024.11; ch 025 / 025.05]. He and his partner, Alaestra, manage an interplanar supply chain that allows the store to bypass local markets, a practice that causes local merchants to view the outpost with "caution and disgust" [ch 025 / 025.05]. Narth is described in town records as "the goods store owner" [ch 027 / 027.06; ch 028 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Narth prioritizes operational security over transparency. When Alaestra began explaining the mechanics of their interplanar supply keys to outsiders, Narth actively attempted to silence her to prevent the leakage of proprietary methods [ch 024 / 024.11; ch 025 / entry]. In negotiations, he is tight-fisted and resistant to pressure; when confronted by the adventurer Orsik, he raised his offer from a 20% discount to 50 Gold only after being stared down, finally stating, "I can't go higher," before closing the deal [ch 024 / 024.11]. He appears reluctant to renegotiate terms even after a successful outcome, as seen when he and Alaestra refused to improve the payment despite the key’s safe return, leaving Orsik to feel their employers were more dangerous than he was [ch 026 / 026.04]. Narth also demonstrates a strong attachment to his inventory or assets; he refused to provide horses to the party when they requested them for travel, forcing the group to walk to Yartar instead [ch 028 / 028.03; ch 028 / entry].

## History with the Party
- Narth is the co-owner of the Lionshield Coster outpost known as Lion’s Share alongside Alaestra, where they manage an interplanar supply chain [ch 024 / 024.11; ch 025 / 025.05; ch 026 / entry].
- After their vault was breached and special inventory keys were stolen, Narth and Alaestra hired Orsik to recover the items; Narth attempted to silence Alaestra when she disclosed how the key-based supply chain worked during negotiations [ch 024 / 024.11; ch 025 / 025.05].
- During the negotiation for the recovery contract, Narth offered a 20% discount, then 50 Gold, but conceded to Orsik’s stonewalling with "I can't go higher." [ch 024 / 024.11].
- Upon the return of the key, Narth and Alaestra were delighted and relieved, but they refused to renegotiate the terms despite Orsik’s attempt to extract more value [ch 026 / 026.04].
- Narth was present in the market square after a fire giant attack destroyed Urgala’s inn and other structures; he shouted about the destruction to Urgala and Othovir [ch 027 / 027.05; ch 027 / 027.06].
- Narth refused to hand over horses to the party when they requested them for travel, forcing the group to walk to Yartar [ch 028 / 028.03].

## Relationships
*   **Alaestra**: Narth’s business partner and co-owner of Lion’s Share [ch 024 / 024.11; ch 025 / entry]. Their dynamic is marked by Narth attempting to control Alaestra’s verbose disclosures about their supply chain secrets, such as when he tried to silence her regarding the stolen keys [ch 024 / 024.11]. They present a united front in negotiations and in their refusal to renegotiate contracts [ch 026 / 026.04].
*   **Orsik**: An adventurer hired to recover the stolen supply keys. Narth viewed Orsik’s negotiation tactics (silent staring) as aggressive but ultimately settled with him [ch 024 / 024.11]. Narth later denied Orsik’s request for horses, showing no willingness to assist the party’s travel logistics beyond the initial contract [ch 028 / 028.03].
*   **Urgala**: Owner of The Northshield House, which was destroyed in the fire giant attack. Narth expressed shock at the damage to her property [ch 027 / 027.05].
*   **Othovir**: A local harness shop owner with whom Narth was present in the market square discussing the aftermath of the giant attack [ch 027 / 027.06].
