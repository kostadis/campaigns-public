## Identity
Elspeth is a resident of Parnast who works at the Golden Tankard, where she serves food and drink to the establishment's patrons [ch 017 / 017.02]. She is Wallace’s girlfriend and, by his own admission in earlier chapters, a friend he does not love [ch 013 / 013.06; ch 014 / 014.02]. She receives payment (coin) from Azam in the party's absence [ch 016 / 016.04].

## Personality and Motivations
The evidence is silent on Elspeth’s internal personality, motivations, or personal agency. She appears in the record primarily as a figure in Wallace’s life and as a staff member at the Golden Tankard. Her interactions are defined by her role serving customers and her relationship with Wallace, rather than by independent actions or dialogue [ch 017 / 017.02; ch 017 / 017.04].

## History with the Party
- Elspeth is Wallace's girlfriend; Wallace initially stated he did not want to marry her while protecting his hometown of Parnast [ch 013 / 013.06].
- Orsik teased Wallace about Elspeth, questioning if she would find out about his actions in Parnast [ch 013 / 013.06].
- Wallace mentioned Elspeth as a friend he did not love to help convince Orsik to assist with the Ettin mission [ch 014 / 014.02].
- Azam left coin for Elspeth and Ragnar while waiting for the party to return with rewards [ch 016 / 016.04].
- Elspeth works at the Golden Tankard and served the party cheese, fresh bread, oil, ale, and mugs when Ragnar Redtooth called for food [ch 017 / 017.02].
- Wallace slipped outside the Golden Tankard to talk to Elspeth while the party spoke with Ragnar [ch 017 / 017.02].
- Wallace kissed Elspeth outside the Golden Tankard and promised to talk later before Orsik pushed the party to continue their investigation [ch 017 / 017.04].
- Wallace decided to "hook up" with Elspeth and stay in Parnast as the new guard captain instead of continuing to adventure [ch 023 / 023.07].
