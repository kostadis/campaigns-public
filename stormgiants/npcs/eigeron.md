## Identity
Eigeron is a Cloud Giant and the son of Blagothkus, currently existing as a ghost trapped within the Eye of Annam [ch 057 / 057.06; ch 058 / entry; ch 077 / entry]. He is the former heir to Blagothkus's castle, which he sought to claim or avenge [ch 050 / 050.01; ch 057 / 057.06]. His existence is defined by his death at the hands of his father and his subsequent transformation into a spectral entity seeking retribution [ch 057 / 057.06; ch 077 / 077.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Eigeron is consumed by a desire for revenge against his father, Blagothkus, whom he holds responsible for his death and his current state as a ghost [ch 057 / 057.06; ch 077 / 077.04]. Prior to his death, he possessed enough agency to direct the rogue Unla Key to the exact location of his father's castle, likely to facilitate a claim or confrontation [ch 050 / 050.01]. As a ghost, his mental state is unstable; upon learning his father is alive and nearby, he immediately loses his composure, going "mad" and attempting to possess a living hero to enact his will [ch 058 / 058.07]. He is singularly focused on killing Blagothkus, directing the party to the Eye of Annam with the specific intent of turning the heroes into instruments of his vengeance [ch 077 / 077.04].

## History with the Party
*   Eigeron previously told Unla Key exactly where to find Blagothkus, allowing Unla to consider claiming the Cloud Giant’s castle when he saw it [ch 050 / 050.01].
*   Eigeron’s death is recounted as an event where he slew his father’s attempt to control him at the Eye of Annam, leading Blagothkus to slay Eigeron in self-defense or rage before retiring in grief [ch 057 / 057.06].
*   Eigeron had previously asked the heroes to avenge his death by killing his father, Blagothkus [ch 057 / 057.06].
*   Esclarotta expressed regret that Blagothkus did not let Eigeron kill him years prior, as she believed it might have made her queen [ch 058 / 058.04].
*   Eigeron exists as a ghost trapped in the Eye of Annam who sought out the party to ask about his father [ch 058 / entry].
*   Upon learning his father was alive and his castle was present, Eigeron’s ghost went mad, attempted to possess one of the heroes, and was turned away by Vardis using the power of Corellon [ch 058 / 058.07].
*   The party left Eigeron’s ghost howling for revenge behind them in the collapsing Eye of Annam [ch 058 / 058.08].
*   Thistle revealed Eigeron’s presence as a ghost trapped in the Eye of Annam to Esclarotta and Blagothkus, stating that Eigeron wanted revenge on the person who made him a ghost [ch 077 / 077.04].
*   Esclarotta learned Eigeron was a ghost and demanded his father be killed to free their son [ch 077 / 077.04].
*   Thistle theorized that the dragon Iymrith attempted to use Eigeron in her plan to pervert the Oracle of Annam and start a Giant war, until Blagothkus killed Eigeron [ch 081 / 081.04].

## Last Observed State
Eigeron was last physically observed as a ghost in the main temple of the Eye of Annam in Chapter 058. When confronted with the truth of his father's survival, he attempted to possess one of the party members but was forcibly turned away by Vardis using the power of Corellon [ch 058 / 058.07]. The party subsequently fled the Eye of Annam as the structure collapsed, leaving Eigeron behind; he was left "howling for revenge" amidst the destruction of the temple [ch 058 / 058.08]. In later chapters (077 and 081), he is referenced only through dialogue regarding his status as a ghost and his involvement in Iymrith's earlier schemes [ch 077 / 077.04; ch 081 / 081.04]. Status not established in the summaries beyond his abandonment in Chapter 058.

## Relationships
**Blagothkus (Father)**
Eigeron views Blagothkus as his murderer. Their relationship ended violently when Blagothkus slew Eigeron in a conflict at the Eye of Annam, reportedly triggered by the oracle's advice that Eigeron must step out from beneath his father's "dark shadow" [ch 057 / 057.06]. Eigeron’s ghostly existence is defined by his hatred for his father, whom he wants killed [ch 057 / 057.06; ch 077 / 077.04]. Blagothkus claimed the killing was an "accident" [ch 077 / 077.04].

**Esclarotta (Mother)**
Esclarotta, Eigeron's mother, learned of his spectral existence in Chapter 077. Upon being told Eigeron was a ghost, she reacted with extreme distress, demanding Blagothkus be killed to "FREE MY SON" [ch 077 / 077.04]. Prior to this, she had expressed regret that Blagothkus did not allow Eigeron to kill him years earlier, suggesting she might have become queen if the succession had occurred differently [ch 058 / 058.04].

**Iymrith (Manipulator)**
Eigeron was intended to be a pawn in Iymrith’s plan to pervert the Oracle of Annam and ignite a Giant War. The dragon planned to use Eigeron until Blagothkus killed him, thereby interrupting the scheme [ch 081 / 081.04].

**Unla Key**
Unla Key knew Eigeron well enough to receive precise directions to Blagothkus's castle from him. Unla considered claiming the castle based on this information but decided to wait [ch 050 / 050.01].

**Vardis**
Vardis is the only party member recorded as actively opposing Eigeron, successfully turning the ghost away with the power of Corellon when Eigeron attempted possession [ch 058 / 058.07].
