## Identity
Clarion is a silver dragon [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.02]. He wears a harness specifically used by a strike team of shield dwarves to hang onto and use as transport [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.02]. He identifies himself by name as part of this team: "the strike team and I, Clarion, destroy the orb" [ch 024 / 024.02]. Clarion confirms his involvement with official institutions when asked if his mission is official SEER business [ch 024 / 024.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Clarion is literal-minded and prone to immediate hostility when encountering unexpected allies of perceived enemies [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.02]. When Thistle mentioned striking a deal with the cloud giant Zephyros, Clarion immediately concluded the party were enemies, stating, "Ah, you are enemies! We will fight you in combat, defeat you, and feast on your bodies. It was nice knowing you!" [ch 024 / 024.02; ch 024 / moment]. He engages in dry, precise banter, correcting Thistle’s assumption about his height with the statement, "I didn't say you were short. I said you weren't very tall" [ch 024 / 024.02].

His primary motivation is to destroy the castle's orb to prevent the cloud giant Zephyros from bombing towns and villages in the north [ch 024 / 024.02]. He justifies this mission by claiming to have seen "distinct evidence" of such attacks and making a "reasonable inference" about the giant's guilt [ch 024 / 024.02]. Clarion dismisses unrelated requests, telling Unla Key, "I have not been sent to help halflings fly their castles" [ch 024 / 024.02]. He adopts a parental tone when admonishing the party for associating with "evil cloud giants" [ch 024 / 024.02].
