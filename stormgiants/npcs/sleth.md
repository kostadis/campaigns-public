## Identity
Sleth is a dryad assigned to tend the western cardinal tree within the Grandfather Tree [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.06]. She describes this role as an assignment she loves, stating that it is "positively amazing" to care for the greatest tree in the Universe [ch 050 / 050.06]. Unlike the party's initial assumptions, Sleth is not trapped or imprisoned at this location [ch 050 / 050.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Sleth is highly knowledgeable about the Grandfather Tree’s growth patterns, exhibiting professional pride mixed with irritation at poor management. She is critical of Queen Titania’s judgment in sending other dryads, referring to them as "dregs" [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.06]. Specifically, she is frustrated by the negligence of Helia and Halisa, noting a branch growing in the wrong direction that will cause imbalance in 30 years [ch 050 / 050.06]. She possesses dry wit and patience, having endured decades of aggravation while others believed in a false curse [ch 050 / 050.07].

## History with the Party
- Sleth tends the western cardinal tree as an assignment she loves, though she is critical of Helia and Halisa’s neglect and of the dryads sent by Queen Titania [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.06].
- She revealed to Thistle that she is not trapped at the tree and expressed annoyance that a branch is growing in the wrong direction, which will cause an imbalance in 30 years [ch 050 / 050.06].
- She appeared next to the party after Helia and Halisa departed, confirming that there was no curse and that the dryads had cursed each other [ch 050 / 050.07].
- She was seen speaking with the Tree Ghost Tribe just before the party reached their airship, after the tribe discovered their artifact had been stolen [ch 050 / 050.08].

## Last Observed State
Sleth was seen speaking with barbarians of the Tree Ghost Tribe, led by the son of the Great Chief Boorvald Orcbane, near the party’s airship [ch 050 / 050.08]. This occurred after the tribe discovered their artifact had been stolen and the leader promised vengeance [ch 050 / 050.08]. During this interaction, Sleth remarked to the tribe, "Prince Thornacious was so gracious and helpful!" as the party sneaked away [ch 050 / 050.08; ch 050 / moment]. Her specific whereabouts or status after this encounter are not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
**Helia and Halisa**: Sleth is annoyed by their prolonged misunderstanding of their situation. She reveals to Thistle that there was no curse trapping them, but rather that they had cursed each other, resulting in a century of unnecessary aggravation [ch 050 / 050.06; ch 050 / 050.07].
**Queen Titania**: Sleth is critical of Titania, blaming her for sending incompetent dryads ("dregs") to tend the trees [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.06].
**The Party (Thistle, Orsik-Thornacious)**: Sleth appeared alongside the old Dryad to confirm the dryads' departure to the party [ch 050 / 050.07].
**Tree Ghost Tribe**: Sleth was observed interacting with the tribe just before the party departed, potentially deflecting attention from the party or commenting on Orsik-Thornacious’s behavior [ch 050 / 050.08].
**Orsik-Thornacious**: Sleth publicly praised him to the Tree Ghost Tribe, stating he was "so gracious and helpful" immediately after the party stole the tribe's artifact [ch 050 / 050.08; ch 050 / moment].
