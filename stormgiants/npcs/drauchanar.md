## Identity
Drauchanar is an elven hunter of rare game, who also goes by "Chan" and his nom-de-guerre, "The Knife" [ch 051 / entry; ch 052 / entry]. He possesses a sing-songy, high-pitched voice and refers to himself in the third person or by his alias [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.06]. He explicitly corrects Thistle on his title, stating, "I am not a knife, I am THE Knife. It’s a nom-de-guerre" [ch 052 / 052.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Drauchanar is driven by a desire for trophies, specifically wanting to keep the head of a Fomorian as his prize [ch 051 / 051.06]. He is pragmatic regarding social conventions, willing to circumvent them if it ensures his prize; he agreed to the hunt only after Thistle suggested Orsik disguise himself as a dwarf so that "the Knife can keep the head, and all the forms of society and culture can be preserved!" [ch 051 / 051.06]. He displays a tendency toward self-aggrandizement and revisionist history, claiming after his rescue that his capture was a tactical choice due to his "spectacular leadership" [ch 052 / 052.02]. He also exhibits strong sycophantic tendencies toward perceived nobility, fawning over Orsik under the belief that Orsik is Prince Thornacious, while strictly enforcing social decorum against those he deems inferior [ch 052 / 052.04].

## History with the Party
*   Drauchanar, also known as "Chan or the Knife," identifies himself as an elven hunter of rare game who desires the head of the Fomorian as a prize [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.06].

*   Following the combat where the party defeats the monsters, Vardis frees Drauchanar [ch 052 / entry; ch 052 / 052.02].
*   Drauchanar claims credit for the victory, asserting he let himself be captured to lead the party to the location due to his "spectacular leadership" [ch 052 / entry; ch 052 / 052.02].
*   Drauchanar believes Orsik is Prince Thornacious and fawns over him, while correcting Thistle on the nature of his title "The Knife" [ch 052 / entry; ch 052 / 052.04].
*   Drauchanar rebukes Thistle for questioning Orsik, insisting that Thistle should accept Orsik's answers out of respect for "Prince Thornacious" [ch 052 / 052.04].
*   Status not established in the summaries; however, Drauchanar is present in the Feywild sunlight with the party after the Fomorian's defeat [ch 052 / 052.04].

## Last Observed State
Drauchanar is currently in the Feywild sunlight with the party following the defeat of the Fomorian and the receipt of Queen Titania's gift [ch 052 / 052.04]. Earlier in the session, he had been found hanging from a chain in Maergun’s throne room, bound alongside a cyclops, a displacer beast, and the Fomorian [ch 052 / 052.01]. Vardis freed him from this chain after the party defeated the monsters [ch 052 / 052.02]. Drauchanar claimed to be "free, exhausted, tired, and free!" immediately upon release [ch 052 / 052.02]. He remained present through the discovery of the treasure vault and the party's departure through the portal [ch 052 / 052.02; ch 052 / 052.04].

## Relationships
*   **Orsik:** Drauchanar operates under the mistaken belief that Orsik is Prince Thornacious. Consequently, he speaks to him with extreme deference, calling him "Lord" and stating, "You are so gracious, Lord, to speak so kindly to -- him" [ch 052 / 052.04].
*   **Thistle:** Drauchanar has a contentious but active relationship with Thistle. He agreed to lead Thistle on the hunt [ch 051 / 051.06]. In Chapter 052, Drauchanar rebukes Thistle for questioning Orsik's vague answers, insisting that "You should accept it" out of respect for the Prince [ch 052 / 052.04].
*   **Vardis:** Vardis physically freed Drauchanar from his chains in the throne room [ch 052 / 052.02]. Drauchanar expressed admiration for Vardis's ability to see through the waterfall illusion [ch 052 / 052.02].
