## Identity
Count Vaal was a Cloud Giant lord and one of the four giant lords stationed in the Maelstrom. [ch 066 / 066.05] He possessed the ability to fly, a trait that surprised the party's dwarf, Orsik, and allowed Vaal to bypass Vardis's wall of fire by rocketing into the air. [ch 066 / 066.05] He wielded a morning star. [ch 067 / 067.01] In death, Vaal was found carrying a diamond-studded platinum earring, a vest of platinum embroidery, a teleportation conch, and a potion of water breathing. [ch 067 / 067.02]

## Personality and Motivations
Vaal engaged in combat with a degree of disdain for his opponents. When Orsik questioned whether Cloud Giants could fly, Vaal mocked the dwarf, stating, "Yes, little one, you should have." [ch 066 / 066.05; ch 066 / entry] He fought alongside Duchess Tartha, Jarl Hellenhild, and Thane Braxow, with whom he introduced himself as part of a unified front. [ch 066 / 066.05] During the battle, he was notably vulnerable to psychic attacks and fire magic that did not affect the Fire Giant Duchess Tartha, suffering significant damage from Unla Key's psychic lance and Vardis's magical flames. [ch 066 / 066.05; ch 067 / 067.01]

## History with the Party
- Count Vaal is introduced as a Cloud Giant lord alongside Duchess Tartha, Jarl Hellenhild, and Thane Braxow during the battle against the giant lords in the Maelstrom [ch 066 / 066.05].
- When Vardis cast a wall of fire across the hallway entrance, Count Vaal clicked his tongue and rocketed into the air to bypass it [ch 066 / 066.05].
- Vaal mocked Orsik for his ignorance of Cloud Giant flight capabilities before the party engaged him [ch 066 / 066.05; ch 066 / moment].
- During the battle, Unla Key caused an explosion of fire between Vaal and Duchess Tartha; the fire did not affect Tartha but seared Vaal and Hellenhild [ch 066 / 066.05].
- In the subsequent phase of the fight, Count Vaal crushed Orsik with his morning star [ch 067 / 067.01; ch 067 / entry].
- Orsik taunted Vaal after taking the morning star blow, praising the swing's grace and style while continuing to fight alone behind Vardis's wall of fire [ch 067 / 067.01; ch 067 / moment].
- Vaal suffered heavy damage from Unla Key’s psychic lance (which caused him to scream, float, and wobble), two hand axes impaling him, magical arrows from a half-pint, and jets of pure flame from Vardis [ch 067 / 067.01; ch 067 / entry].
- The final blows from Vardis and the party ended Vaal’s life; Vardis later described finding Orsik standing among the corpses of the giant lords [ch 067 / 067.01; ch 067 / entry].
- Orsik looted Vaal’s corpse, retrieving a diamond-studded platinum earring, a vest of platinum embroidery, a teleportation conch, and a potion of water breathing [ch 067 / 067.02].
- Vaal’s body was dragged by Orsik into the storeroom alongside the other giant lords to hide their presence from Mirran and Nym [ch 067 / 067.02].

## Last Observed State
Vaal is dead. He was killed during the battle in the Guest Wing of the Maelstrom. [ch 067 / 067.01; ch 067 / entry] After suffering heavy damage from a psychic lance, hand axes, magical arrows, and jets of flame, the final blows from Vardis and the party ended his life. [ch 067 / 067.01] His corpse, along with the other giant lords, was looted by Orsik for platinum items, a teleportation conch, and potions. [ch 067 / 067.02] Orsik then dragged Vaal's body into a storeroom to hide the evidence of the lords' deaths from Mirran and Nym. [ch 067 / 067.02]
