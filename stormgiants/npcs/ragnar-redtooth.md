## Identity
Ragnar Redtooth is the barkeep and owner of The Golden Tankard in Parnast [ch 007 / entry; ch 017 / entry]. He is characterized as tall and heavyset, with brown hair, a large belly, and prominent muttonchops [ch 007 / entry; ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.02]. During the party's initial visit to Parnast, he is described as transactional and openly opportunistic, charging high prices for goods and information [ch 007 / entry; ch 007 / 007.05]. By the time of the orc invasion, he is recognized as a de facto leader of Parnast, taking charge of local defenses in the absence of Commander Orsik [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Ragnar prioritizes survival and the defense of Parnast, motivated by a desire to protect his community from external threats like Bad Fruul’s army [ch 017 / 017.03]. He possesses a pragmatic, mercenary streak, stating that "When a poor man sees a fool throwing coins around, he's a fool if he doesn't try to get some more," and initially attempting to upsell the party or charge for information [ch 007 / 007.05]. However, he also demonstrates loyalty to those he considers friends, intervening to de-escalate conflicts and offering resources when the stakes are high [ch 013 / 013.07; ch 017 / 017.02].

He harbors a complex history regarding the Cult of the Dragon, claiming he joined them to work "from the inside," a stance that caused townsfolk to blame him for past instability [ch 017 / 017.02]. Despite this, he remains a key organizer for the town, acknowledging his limitations in strategy by seeking out individuals like Vardis for their "brains and judgment, not a strong sword arm" [ch 017 / moment]. He is capable of conceding ground to better arguments, as seen when he accepted Orsik's objections to the Lords’ Alliance [ch 023 / 023.05].

## History with the Party
*   Ragnar operates as the barkeep of The Golden Tankard, characterized as tall and heavyset with enormous mutton chop sideburns [ch 007 / entry].
*   He serves the party ale and mead in Parnast, initially attempting to upsell them by noting they had spoken for two minutes without buying anything [ch 007 / 007.05].
*   Ragnar provided information regarding Chandra Stol’s search for an idol, noting that other candidates like Azam had refused due to danger, and guessed the location of a treasure map to be "somewhere in the North" for a gold piece [ch 007 / 007.05].
*   Ragnar charged the party 5 gold for a room and another 5 for sheets, defending his pricing to Orsik when accused of treating them as "marks" [ch 007 / 007.05].
*   During the party's first stay in Parnast, Ragnar intervened to stop a traveler from accusing Unla Key of cheating at gambling, causing the traveler to leave while memorizing Unla Key's face [ch 013 / 013.07].
*   Ragnar utilized a back room at the Golden Tankard to introduce the party to Azam and Hsing, where Wallace explained the threat of the SEER to Orsik [ch 013 / 013.08].
*   Azam left payment for Ragnar and Elspeth on the Golden Tankard while the party was away on a quest [ch 016 / 016.04].
*   In Orsik's absence, Ragnar organized Parnast's defenses, suspecting Gundalin Cartwright of sabotage and offering 50 gold each for information on the matter [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.02].
*   Ragnar revealed to the party that he had previously joined the Cult of the Dragon to work "from the inside," a history that caused the townsfolk to blame him for past problems [ch 017 / 017.02].
*   He detailed three acts of sabotage to Vardis: disappearing tools, the collapse of a watchtower killing Donnal, and a broken cart axle, warning that an attack by Bad Fruul was imminent [ch 017 / 017.03].
*   Ragnar expressed that while Orsik was a good soldier, he needed Vardis for his "brains and judgment" to help coordinate the defense [ch 017 / 017.03; ch 017 / moment].
*   He proposed the party move to a cabin to check on a hostage situation involving Tove Brandimen’s mother [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.01].
*   Ragnar waited at the Golden Tankard during the final confrontation and prepared to fight the orcs [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.01].
*   He noted during defense planning that locals believed the watchtower collapsed because they included logs from the old town [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.06].
*   Before the final battle, Ragnar asked Orsik if he had a plan, prompting Orsik to decide to release the wyvern [ch 022 / entry; ch 022 / 022.05].
*   After the battle, Ragnar shouted "What have you done to this town?" and reported to Vardis that "Half the militia, I think" had died [ch 023 / 023.01; ch 023 / 023.02].
*   Ragnar joined Chandra and Gundalin in arguing about how to fix the town following the destruction [ch 023 / 023.02].
*   Following Orsik's objections to the Lords’ Alliance, Ragnar conceded, telling Orsik, "You make some excellent points" [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.05].

## Last Observed State
After Bad Fruul falls at the start of Chapter 23, Ragnar is alive and confronts the heroes in Parnast [ch 023 / 023.01]. He expresses anger at the destruction, shouting, "What have you done to this town?" [ch 023 / 023.01]. He reports that "Half the militia, I think" died during the conflict [ch 023 / 023.02]. Subsequently, he joins Chandra and Gundalin in arguing about how to repair the town [ch 023 / 023.02]. After Orsik voices objections to the Lords’ Alliance, Ragnar concedes, telling Orsik, "You make some excellent points," and appears to align with the group's growing skepticism of the Alliance [ch 023 / 023.05]. Status established as alive [ch 023 / 023.01].

## Relationships
*   **Orsik:** Ragnar respects Orsik’s military skill but views him as needing guidance in strategy [ch 017 / 017.03]. He organizes defenses while Orsik is away and later concedes to Orsik's political views regarding the Lords’ Alliance [ch 017 / 017.01; ch 023 / 023.05].
*   **Vardis:** Ragnar explicitly seeks Vardis’s counsel over Orsik’s during the defense planning, valuing his intellect for coordinating the town's protection [ch 017 / 017.03; ch 017 / moment].
*   **Chandra Stol:** Ragnar interacts with Chandra as a peer and fellow town leader. He provides information regarding her search for an idol [ch 007 / 007.05] and later argues with her regarding the town's reconstruction after the battle [ch 023 / 023.02].
*   **Gundalin Cartwright:** Ragnar suspects Gundalin of sabotage regarding the town's defenses, specifically the collapse of a watchtower and broken cart axles [ch 017 / 017.02; ch 017 / 017.03]. Despite this suspicion, he acknowledges Gundalin's craftsmen are essential for the town's defense [ch 017 / 017.02]. They stand together as Parnast's leadership after the battle [ch 019 / 019.01; ch 023 / 023.02].
*   **The Party (General):** Ragnar acts as a facilitator for the party, providing them with rooms, food, and introductions to key figures like Azam and Hsing [ch 007 / 007.05; ch 013 / 013.08]. He intervened to protect Unla Key from an accusation of cheating at gambling [ch 013 / 013.07].
