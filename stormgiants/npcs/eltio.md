## Identity
Eltio is a female rogue and member of the Hand of Yartar [ch 030 / entry; ch 033 / entry]. Her attire consists of white clothing, a hat, and a white mask [ch 032 / entry]. She operates as an information broker, specifically providing contacts and locations relevant to the party’s objectives [ch 030 / 030.06; ch 031 / entry]. Vardis described her as "sullen, distant, and dangerous" upon first meeting her at the Wink and Kiss [ch 030 / 030.06]. The chapter 033 entry describes her as "no shrinking violet when it came to violence" [ch 033 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Eltio values discretion and the preservation of her standing within Yartar. She rejected monetary sponsorship offers in favor of social capital, agreeing to a deal only after being promised 50 friends for a play [ch 030 / 030.06]. She displayed disdain for the party’s legitimate methods, remarking that Tamalin Zoar had "gone legit" and expressing disappointment at the absence of criminal activity such as murder or highway robbery [ch 032 / 032.08]. She is highly risk-averse regarding open confrontation with authority figures; she was alarmed by Orsik’s suggestion to cast a fireball in the central market near the Waterbaron’s residence, noting that such an act would result in them being "hunted down and killed" [ch 032 / 032.10; ch 033 / 033.02]. She recognized Orsik by reputation, identifying him as "the guy who does the thing" [ch 032 / 032.10; ch 033 / 033.02]. In crisis situations, she prioritizes speed and secrecy, advising the party to rescue a prisoner immediately to avoid execution and offering a secret, unofficial route through the sewers and an abandoned temple of Bane [ch 033 / 033.03; ch 033 / 033.04].

## History with the Party
- Eltio is a member of the Hand of Yartar, described by Vardis as sullen, distant, and dangerous, and dressed in white with a hat and white mask [ch 030 / entry; ch 032 / entry; ch 033 / entry].
- At the Wink and Kiss, Eltio initially demanded 500 gold for information on Harthal, or required Tamalin Zoar to waive the fee, before agreeing to a deal with Orsik involving bringing friends to a play [ch 030 / 030.06; ch 030 / moment].
- The party obtained a Letter of Mark from Tamalin Zoar, which Eltio indicated would lead them to Harthal Zymorven [ch 031 / entry; ch 031 / 031.05].
- Upon meeting the party in Yartar with the Letter of Mark, Eltio was surprised they were alive and impressed by the document [ch 032 / 032.09; ch 033 / 033.01].
- Eltio informed the party that Harthal Zymorven was imprisoned for murder with an execution date of the 10th, and that his sword was held by Tholzar Brenner, a corrupt guard captain [ch 032 / 032.09; ch 033 / 033.01].
- Eltio guided the party to Tholzar Brenner in the central market of Yartar and objected strongly to Orsik’s proposal to use a fireball near the Waterbaron’s residence, recognizing Orsik as "that guy Orsik who did that thing" [ch 032 / 032.10; ch 032 / moment; ch 033 / 033.02; ch 033 / moment].
- Following Brenner’s death, Eltio advised that Harthal had to be freed immediately to avoid retaliation against the prisoner, and she provided a route to the prison through the sewers and an abandoned temple of Bane [ch 033 / 033.03; ch 033 / 033.04].

## Last Observed State
Eltio guided the party through the sewers of Yartar to the entrance of an abandoned temple of Bane, stating, "I'm sure this will get you to where you want" [ch 033 / 033.04]. She successfully led them to the secret door leading to the prison guards' room [ch 033 / 033.04]. Her presence in subsequent scenes after the temple entrance is not explicitly described in the provided evidence. Status not established in the summaries.

She was surprised they were alive and impressed by their possession of the Letter of Mark [ch 032 / 032.09; ch 033 / 033.01]. She provided them with critical intelligence regarding Harthal Zymorven’s imprisonment and execution date, as well as the location of their sword [ch 032 / 032.09; ch 033 / 033.01].
