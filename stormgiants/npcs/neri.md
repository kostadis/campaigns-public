## Identity
Neri is identified as the late wife of King Hekaton and the Queen of the Storm Giants [ch 069 / entry; ch 069 / 069.07; ch 079 / 079.01]. She was known for her affection toward "small folk" [ch 069 / entry; ch 069 / 069.07; ch 081 / 081.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Neri was characterized by her fondness for small folk and her diplomatic influence over King Hekaton, urging him to respect them [ch 069 / entry; ch 069 / 069.07; ch 081 / 081.04]. Her actions and relationships significantly influenced the political stability of the Storm Giants; her death and the circumstances surrounding it led to the unraveling of the Ordning and the disappearance of King Hekaton [ch 079 / 079.01]. Thistle theorizes that her murder was orchestrated by Iymrith (Adimond Kroskas) to provoke a vengeful reaction from Hekaton, though this was thwarted by their daughter Princess Serissa’s intervention [ch 081 / 081.04].

## Last Observed State
Neri is dead. Her corpse was found on a small island, killed by small folk [ch 069 / entry; ch 069 / 069.07; ch 079 / 079.01]. A golden goose coin was found on the bodies of the small folk killed alongside her [ch 069 / entry; ch 079 / 079.01].

## Relationships
*   **Hekaton:** Her husband and King of the Storm Giants. Neri urged him to respect small folk [ch 069 / entry; ch 069 / 069.07; ch 081 / 081.04]. Hekaton threatened vengeance on Coastal territories upon her death, though he was persuaded to investigate first [ch 069 / 069.07; ch 081 / 081.04].
*   **Serissa:** Neri's daughter and heir apparent. Serissa shared her mother's affection for small folk and persuaded Hekaton to uncover the truth of Neri's death before reacting with rage [ch 069 / 069.07; ch 081 / 081.04].
*   **Mirran and Nym:** Neri's other daughters. The evidence does not specify Neri's direct relationship with them, but Hekaton had previously judged them unfit to rule in favor of Serissa [ch 069 / 069.07].
*   **Iymrith (Adimond Kroskas):** According to Thistle's theory, Iymrith ordered Neri's death after being rebuffed by Hekaton, using her assassination as a catalyst for political chaos [ch 081 / 081.04].
