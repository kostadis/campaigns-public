## Identity
Captain Jolanth serves as the Captain of the Guards of the Iron Circle in the town of Llorkh [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.08]. He is a survivor of the region's recent history, having endured both the presence of the Cult of the Dragon and the decline of Zhentarim trade traffic [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.08].

## Personality and Motivations
Jolanth is characterized as pompous and enjoys asserting his authority over those passing through the town [ch 002 / entry]. His primary motivations for remaining in service are practical and personal: he stays for the reliable pay and because he appreciates the local weather [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.08]. He attempts to intimidate newcomers by blocking the road and demanding identification, though he is easily flustered when his authority is challenged through verbal cleverness [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.08]. Privately, he looks down on those he perceives as physically weak, such as the character Orsik, whom he internally dismissed as too weak to threaten the guard [ch 002 / 002.08].

## History with the Party
- Captain Jolanth is the Captain of the Guards of the Iron Circle in Llorkh, serving for the pay and the weather after surviving the Cult of the Dragon and the decline of Zhentarim traffic [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.08].
- He attempted to intimidate the party at the town edge by blocking the road and demanding identification, but was cut off by Orsik who reinterpreted Jolanth's warning into a promise of protection [ch 002 / 002.08].
- Jolanth privately thought Orsik was too weak to threaten the guard, but became flustered when Orsik verbally cornered him into implying he would protect the party from danger [ch 002 / 002.08].
- Vardis interrupted the exchange to claim the "free protection" before the group walked past him [ch 002 / 002.08].

## Last Observed State
Jolanth was observed at the edge of town in Chapter 002, where he attempted to stop the party [ch 002 / 002.08]. After being verbally cornered by Orsik, who reinterpreted Jolanth’s warning into a promise of protection, Jolanth became flustered and began to explain his position [ch 002 / 002.08]. He was interrupted by the party member Vardis, and the group walked past him [ch 002 / 002.08]. No further actions or status changes for Jolanth are recorded in the provided evidence.
