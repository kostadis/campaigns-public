## Identity
Laela is a female fairy with an extremely high-pitched voice and visible wingtips that require "wing-muffs" [ch 084 / 084.03; ch 084 / 084.05]. She is the romantic partner of Thistle, whom she refers to as the "absolute love of her life" and "girlfriend" status confirmed by multiple sources [ch 033 / 033.07; ch 069 / 069.06]. Iymrith identifies her as a "Fey Dragon hybrid" capable of existing in both the Feywild and the Prime Material Plane, possessing an intrinsic shapeshift ability rather than a magical one [ch 084 / 084.03; ch 084 / 084.05]. Despite this hybrid nature, Laela strongly identifies as a fairy and refuses to assume her dragon form, preferring to practice "fey-fullness" [ch 084 / entry; ch 084 / 084.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Laela is driven by a singular motivation: finding Thistle. This quest leads her to recruit allies, including convincing the rogue Harthal to steal a sword and join the Hand of Yartar, though she abandoned Harthal once she realized he could not help her locate Thistle [ch 033 / 033.07]. She is protective of Thistle, scolding him through a sending stone for endangering himself and asking, "You’ll get yourself killed," while simultaneously demanding to know why he is working with elves, stating, "I thought we said we don’t like elves!" [ch 084 / 084.05]. She displays a pragmatic ruthlessness in survival; during a caravan ambush, she torched the surrounding bandits, resulting in "a blinding flash of mayhem" where everyone died except her and a terrified young man [ch 039 / 039.05; ch 084 / 084.05]. She maintains a firm boundary regarding her identity, refusing to shift into dragon form despite Iymrith’s insistence that she prove her lethality to the dragon clans [ch 084 / 084.03; ch 084 / 084.05].

## Last Observed State
Laela is alive and conscious, currently held in a cage inside the lair of the dragon Iymrith [ch 084 / 084.05; ch 084 / entry]. She recently communicated with Thistle via a sending stone, during which she expressed anger at his choice of companions but confirmed her location [ch 084 / 084.05]. Iymrith has proposed a deal wherein Laela will fight the party in a "demonstration" at a coliseum to prove her hybrid nature to Iymrith’s dragon patrons; the party plans to treat this as a rehearsed performance to ensure Laela’s safety [ch 084 / 084.03; ch 084 / 084.06]. Iymrith states that Laela refuses to shift into her dragon form, which frustrates the dragon, who claims she "can’t get her to turn into a dragon" [ch 084 / 084.05].

## Relationships
*   **Thistle:** Laela is Thistle’s lover and has been searching for him extensively, describing him as the "absolute love of her life" [ch 033 / 033.07; ch 084 / entry]. Their relationship is strained by her isolation and his alliances; she specifically objects to his association with elves and accuses him of endangering himself unnecessarily [ch 084 / 084.05].
*   **Iymrith:** Laela is a captive of the dragon Iymrith, who captured her after Laela destroyed a caravan [ch 084 / 084.05]. Iymrith views Laela as a scientific achievement ("an artiste, a creator") and uses her as a prop to secure investment from dragon clans, though Laela refuses to comply with the demonstration's requirements to shift form [ch 084 / 084.03; ch 084 / 084.05].
*   **Adimond Kroskas:** Laela was previously held by Adimond, who took her as a captive when he fled after the cult's collapse [ch 024 / 024.05; ch 069 / 069.06].
*   **Harthal:** Laela briefly recruited Harthal to aid in her search for Thistle, using his desire for adventure to convince him to steal a sword, before abandoning him when the Hand of Yartar proved insufficient for her needs [ch 033 / 033.07].
