## Identity
Eryn is a villager who co-owns horses with Wald [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Eryn is characterized by dry wit and skepticism toward Wallace's performative behavior. During the horse poisoning incident, Eryn mocked Wallace's dramatic announcement by muttering, "the knowledge one accumulates as an adventurer!" [ch 017 / 017.05]. Eryn also demonstrated immediate concern for their animals, attempting to steady a staggering poisoned horse amidst the chaos in town [ch 017 / 017.05].

## History with the Party
- Eryn is a villager who owns horses with Wald and is described as a childhood chum of Wallace. [ch 017 / entry]
- Eryn attempted to steady a staggering horse when three villagers' horses became poisoned in the center of town. [ch 017 / 017.05]
- Eryn mocked Wallace's dramatic announcement of the poisoning by muttering about the knowledge accumulated as an adventurer. [ch 017 / 017.05]

## Last Observed State
Eryn was present in the center of town during a horse poisoning incident involving three of their horses: one dead, one trembling, and one staggering [ch 017 / 017.05]. Eryn physically attempted to steady the staggering horse while the party arrived to investigate [ch 017 / 017.05]. Eryn remained on the periphery of the scene, rolling their eyes at Wallace's theatrics and making a sarcastic comment about Wallace's adventurer knowledge [ch 017 / 017.05]. The party subsequently departed to find a herbalist; Eryn's current status or location following this departure is not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
Eryn is described as a childhood chum of Wallace [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.05]. Eryn co-owns the affected horses with Wald [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.05]. Both Eryn and Wald expressed visible irritation (rolling their eyes) at Wallace's dramatic behavior during the incident [ch 017 / 017.05].
