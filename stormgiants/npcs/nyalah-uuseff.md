## Identity
*   Nyalah Uuseff is the tavern keeper of the Dancing Goat [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.04].
*   She dresses in formal silk and gold [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.04].

## Personality and Motivations
*   She is motivated to involve authorities in matters she perceives as threats to the establishment, specifically summoning the Silverwatch to investigate reports of "ungoodly races being present!" [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.04].
*   Her actions show a reliance on formal authority: she called the guard to investigate "these tales of ungoodly races being present!", and when the party asked for their dead friend to be raised to testify, she answered, "The city guard will decide that!" [ch 029 / 029.04].

## History with the Party
*   Nyalah Uuseff, the tavern keeper of the Dancing Goat, dressed in formal silk and gold, summons the Silverwatch to the inn in the morning. [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.04]
*   She explicitly requests the guard's presence to investigate "these tales of ungoodly races being present!" [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.04]
*   Her summons leads to the Captain of the Silverwatch arriving to detain Orsik and initiate the process of raising Unla Key for testimony. [ch 029 / 029.04]

## Last Observed State

## Relationships

*   She interacted with Orsik and the party members (Thistle, Unla Key) in the morning of Chapter 029, acting as the catalyst for their detention and subsequent legal proceedings [ch 029 / 029.04].
*   Her relationship with the broader community involves upholding local standards, as evidenced by her objection to the presence of what she terms "ungoodly races" within her tavern or its vicinity [ch 029 / 029.04].
