## Identity
Princess Halani Meliamne is an elf from the Moonwood [ch 061 / 061.02]. She presents herself as a captive, accompanied by Jevin and Vandar, who were captured by Yakfolk [ch 061 / entry; ch 061 / 061.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Halani is motivated by a desire to return to her home in the Moonwood, offering a "great reward" to the party to facilitate her escape [ch 061 / entry; ch 061 / 061.02]. She holds deep bitterness toward Silverymoon, claiming they betrayed the Silver Marches during their hour of need and that their era of dominance has ended [ch 061 / entry; ch 061 / 061.02]. She advocates for a "new organization" to protect the people of the world, a statement she quickly retracts after realizing it implies a plot to establish a "new world order" [ch 061 / 061.02; ch 061 / moment]. Following her slip, she pleads that she has been imprisoned for some time, using her captivity as an excuse for her radical views [ch 061 / entry; ch 061 / 061.02]. She suspects that agents of Silverymoon orchestrated the Yakfolk's attacks against her [ch 061 / entry; ch 061 / 061.02].
