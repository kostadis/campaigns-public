## Identity
Urlam Stockspool is a local agent of the Zhentarim and the owner of The Triboar Travelers [ch 037 / entry; ch 037 / 037.01]. He is an Illuskan male of middle age, distinguishable by his missing eye; the eye patch he wears bears the red-emblazoned symbol of the Zhentarim [ch 037 / entry; ch 037 / 037.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Urlam presents himself as pragmatic and commercially driven, explicitly stating that the current "black network" of the Zhentarim focuses on "profitability, gross margins, and endless growth" rather than apocalyptic goals for the god Bane [ch 037 / 037.01]. He utilizes his position to leverage social and financial benefits, arriving at meetings with a large crew of agents to expect free drinks from contacts like Orsik [ch 037 / 037.01]. He is capable of informal affection with high-status allies, greeting Orsik warmly with kisses on both cheeks [ch 037 / 037.01].

Regarding sensitive intelligence, Urlam adopts a guarded demeanor, particularly concerning the name Adimond Kroskas, which he claims not to have heard in a long time despite providing detailed rumors about the subject [ch 037 / entry; ch 037 / 037.01]. He is knowledgeable about historical Zhentarim operations, identifying Kroskas as a "free agent" and "messed-up dude" involved in creating hybrid magical creatures, while distancing the modern network from the Cult of the Dragon [ch 037 / 037.01]. He is also depicted as willing to haggle aggressively for discounts, asking for a "Zhentarim friends and family discount" regarding a payment of 200 gold [ch 037 / entry; ch 037 / moment].
