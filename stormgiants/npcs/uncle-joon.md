## Identity
Uncle Joon is an older traveler and the maternal uncle of Thistle. He operates as a wandering scholar and intelligence source, known for knowing "everything about the North" and having extensive knowledge of Fey, dragon lore, and regional politics [ch 014 / 014.02; ch 014 / entry]. He is a member of a secretive organization distinct from the Cult of the Dragon, though he shares intelligence that overlaps with their interests [ch 017 / entry]. He was once a member of Queen Titania’s Seelie Court but was expelled for drunkenness and for offending the Sky Pony tribe [ch 052 / entry; ch 052 / 052.09].

## Personality and Motivations
Joon is characterized by heavy alcoholism, which has led to his expulsion from high society and his current destitute state in Triboar [ch 024 / entry; ch 052 / entry]. He is manipulative and self-serving, often providing critical but incomplete information to those around him, particularly his nephew. While he claims to be a victim of temple politics, his actions frequently complicate the party's situation [ch 026 / 026.02; ch 055 / 055.05]. He appears to harbor no ill will toward the party, viewing his interactions with them as a means to secure drinks or attention, as evidenced when he publicly claimed Thistle would buy him a drink during an interrogation [ch 024 / 024.05]. His loyalty is fluid; he shared sensitive information about Adimond Kroskas with the archivist Rebecca Deakin, potentially endangering Thistle’s personal relationships, while simultaneously acting as a key intellectual resource for Thistle’s survival [ch 055 / 055.05].

## Relationships
*   **Thistle:** Joon is Thistle’s uncle. The relationship is defined by a mix of familial dependence and deep exasperation. Thistle relies on Joon’s extensive lore for survival and tactical advantage but frequently threatens to kill him for providing damaging intelligence (specifically regarding Thistle’s girlfriend and Adimond Kroskas) and for his drunken antics [ch 014 / entry; ch 055 / 055.05; ch 057 / 057.01]. Thistle has physically attempted to sober Joon with buckets of water multiple times [ch 024 / 024.04; ch 039 / 039.06].
*   **Queen Titania:** Joon was once a member of her court but was banished for intoxication and offending the Sky Pony tribe [ch 052 / entry; ch 052 / 052.09]. The Queen’s sprite emissary later noted that Titania "appreciated" how the party "dealt with" Joon, implying his removal was viewed positively by the Seelie Court [ch 051 / 051.04].
*   **Old Dryad:** An old dryad in the Feywild claims Joon owes her a "good joke," indicating he was a known presence in the Feywild prior to his expulsion [ch 050 / 050.05].
*   **Sky Pony Tribe:** Joon fell out of favor with this tribe due to a dispute over honor and remarks deemed "beyond the pale" by court standards [ch 052 / 052.09].
