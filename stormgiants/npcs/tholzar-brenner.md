## Identity
Tholzar Brenner (also referred to as Brenner) is a Damaran guard captain [ch 032 / entry; ch 033 / entry]. He is described by Eltio as a "thug of a captain of the guard" and a corrupt official [ch 032 / 032.09; ch 033 / 033.01]. He is in possession of the giant slayer sword [ch 032 / entry; ch 033 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Brenner extorts protection money from merchants in Yartar’s central market [ch 032 / entry; ch 033 / entry]. He displays the "smug satisfaction of a man with some power who loved to wield it" [ch 033 / 033.02]. His actions have made him widely disliked; Eltio notes that "no one would be upset to lose" him or "no one would miss" him [ch 032 / 032.09; ch 032 / 032.10].

## History with the Party
- Tholzar Brenner is identified as a Damaran guard captain who possesses the giant slayer sword and collects protection money from merchants in Yartar’s central market [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.09; ch 032 / 032.10; ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.01].
- The party is informed by Eltio that Brenner is holding the sword and is considered a corrupt official whom "no one would be upset to lose" or whom "no one would miss" [ch 032 / 032.09; ch 032 / 032.10; ch 033 / 033.01].
- Eltio leads the party to the market near the Waterbaron's house to locate Brenner, pointing out him and his armed guards collecting protection money [ch 032 / 032.10; ch 033 / 033.01].
- Orsik suggested ambushing Brenner and his guards with a fireball, a plan Eltio strongly opposed due to the proximity to the Waterbaron's residence [ch 032 / 032.10; ch 032 / moment; ch 033 / 033.02].
- Late in the evening, Brenner arrived in the market demanding his cut from small stores, with the giant slayer sword at his side, surrounded by bored and annoyed city guards [ch 033 / 033.02].
- The party ambushed Brenner in the market; the encounter resulted in Brenner's death and the death of all but one of his guards [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.02].
- Following Brenner's death, Eltio warned the party that the authorities would soon discover his body and suspect the prisoner Harthal’s friends, necessitating an immediate prison break [ch 033 / 033.03].

## Last Observed State
Tholzar Brenner was killed in the market ambush [ch 033 / entry]. The encounter resulted in his death and the death of all but one of his guards [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.02].
