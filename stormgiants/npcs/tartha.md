## Identity
Tartha is a Fire Giant holding the title of Duchess [ch 066 / entry]. She identified herself as one of four giant lords guarding the Queen, alongside Count Vaal, Jarl Hellenhild, and Thane Braxow [ch 066 / 066.05]. She wielded a giant sword and possessed immunity to fire, being invigorated rather than harmed by magical fire effects [ch 066 / entry; ch 066 / 066.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Tartha was motivated by duty to the Queen, stating explicitly that she and her companions would kill the party because the party had come to kill the Queen [ch 066 / 066.05]. She displayed confidence and amusement during combat, laughing upon seeing the party fight and taunting her opponents [ch 066 / 066.05]. She acknowledged the threat posed by the party’s leader (Orsik), noting, "Perhaps the Queen did see something in you," while simultaneously assuring him he would not survive to meet the Queen [ch 066 / 066.05]. She was also prone to mockery, taunting the Jarl Hellenhild for her failed ranged attack [ch 066 / 066.05].

## History with the Party
- Duchess Tartha introduced herself alongside Count Vaal, Jarl Hellenhild, and Thane Braxow, declaring that they would "do our duty and kill" the party because the party had come to kill the Queen [ch 066 / 066.05].
- Tartha marched through Vardis's wall of fire unaffected and struck Thistle with her giant sword, taking a strike from Orsik as she passed [ch 066 / 066.05].
- Tartha was struck by Thistle's lightning-charged arrows and Thuul's breath of ice, and by Unla Key's fire mote, the latter of which invigorated her rather than harming her [ch 066 / 066.05].
- Orsik struck Tartha twice; the first strike was resisted, but the second left her prone [ch 066 / 066.05].
- Orsik later looted a necklace of gilded Dragon's teeth and a conch from the corpse of Duchess Tartha [ch 067 / 067.02].

## Last Observed State
Tartha is deceased. Her body was looted by Orsik for a necklace of gilded Dragon's teeth and a conch [ch 067 / 067.02]. Orsik subsequently dragged her corpse into the storeroom to hide it from Mirran, who later descended to find the area empty and mistakenly assumed the giants had retreated [ch 067 / 067.02].

## Relationships
- **Count Vaal, Jarl Hellenhild, and Thane Braxow:** Fellow giant lords who fought alongside Tartha against the party [ch 066 / 066.05]. Tartha mocked Hellenhild’s combat ability and seemed to engage in a dynamic of shared glory-seeking with the group [ch 066 / 066.05].
- **The Queen:** Tartha stated she served the Queen and would kill the party to prevent them from reaching her [ch 066 / 066.05].
- **The 3HPP (Party):** Hostile combatants. Tartha engaged them in direct combat, striking Thistle with her sword [ch 066 / 066.05]. She specifically targeted Orsik, acknowledging his skill but condemning him to death [ch 066 / 066.05].
- **Mirran:** An antagonist who fled the battle; Tartha and the other lords were left behind in the confrontation with the party [ch 066 / 066.05; ch 067 / 067.02].
