## Identity
Zephyros is a cloud giant whose personal castle travels through the sky, currently carrying the party toward Triboar [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.01]. He is a known entity to local authorities, specifically the Lords’ Alliance strike team led by Diana, who distinguishes him from other cloud giants by checking if he is "one of the good guys" [ch 024 / 024.03]. His name is frequently mispronounced by those unfamiliar with giants, requiring correction to "Zephyros" rather than "Zephyr Oh-Es" [ch 024 / 024.01; ch 024 / moment].

## Personality and Motivations
Zephyros is courteous and engaged with his guests, taking care to correct mispronunciations of his name with humor rather than anger [ch 024 / 024.01; ch 024 / moment]. He acts as a guide, eagerly narrating landmarks such as the High Forest, the Star Mounts, and the Dessarin Hills, expressing nostalgia for previous associations with dwarves in those regions [ch 024 / 024.01]. He demonstrates empathy and generosity, noting when the party’s rations were low and sending down his own food [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.01]. He is reflective, asking the party if they still believe their current course of action is correct, indicating an awareness of the moral weight of their journey [ch 024 / 024.01].

## Last Observed State
During the journey toward Triboar, Zephyros hosted the party in his castle, sharing food and conversation until a strike team arrived [ch 024 / 024.01]. When the strike team, led by Clarion and later identified as Diana, attempted to destroy the castle's orb, combat sounds including swords clanking and furniture breaking were heard coming from Zephyros' room, though he was not described speaking or acting directly in the scene [ch 024 / 024.02]. After Diana verified that the party were not allies of evil giants and that Zephyros was not bombing towns, she called off the attack, concluding it was the "wrong castle" [ch 024 / 024.03]. The evidence does not explicitly state Zephyros' physical condition or location immediately after the strike team departed, only that the party remained on the castle. Status not established in the summaries beyond the cessation of hostilities against the castle [ch 024 / 024.03].
