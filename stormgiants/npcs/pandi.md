## Identity
Pandi is a small dog with a vibrantly-colored mane who manifests as a disembodied voice speaking telepathically, specifically into Thistle’s head [ch 015 / entry; ch 015 / 015.02]. Pandi’s speech is characterized by rolled "r"s, evident in quotes such as "Travelerrrrs!" and "rrreal fun" [ch 015 / 015.02]. Pandi appears in a blue-marble room with a plush velvet floor, accessible through a door that materializes in a tree glowing with iridescent blue flame [ch 015 / entry; ch 015 / 015.02]. A friendly, toothy maw forms in the swirling flames of the door before vanishing, which may be a visual manifestation of Pandi, though this is not explicitly confirmed [ch 015 / 015.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Pandi is playful and insistent on engagement, introducing itself with the declaration, "My name is Pandi, and I want to play a game! Can we play a game? CAN WE?" [ch 015 / 015.02]. Pandi acts as a gatekeeper or puzzle-master for the location, using a word game and a Tangram puzzle to determine the party's worthiness or intelligence [ch 015 / entry; ch 015 / 015.02]. Pandi expresses disdain for ignorance, complaining to Thistle that "they don't teach fairies anything these days in fairy school" after incorrect guesses [ch 015 / 015.02]. Pandi also displays practical concern for the players' comfort, sighing, "this might take a while. I hope you have food and water," during the guessing phase [ch 015 / 015.02]. Pandi is eager to please and reward success, asking "Have I been a good boy?" before granting the "gift of passage" [ch 015 / 015.02].

## History with the Party
- Pandi appeared in a blue-marble room, accessible through a door in a tree, and spoke telepathically into Thistle's head [ch 015 / 015.02; ch 015 / entry].
- Pandi presented the party with a word game requiring them to guess what they wanted, complaining about the lack of fairy knowledge after incorrect guesses from Thistle and Vardis [ch 015 / 015.02].
- Pandi expressed concern about the duration of the game, hoping the party had food and water, after Unla Key began observing the wrong guesses [ch 015 / moment].
- Pandi rejoiced when Unla Key correctly guessed "a passage?" and initiated the next phase of the game by producing a Tangram puzzle [ch 015 / 015.02; ch 015 / moment].
- Pandi traded the "gift of passage" to the party in exchange for the completed Tangram puzzle, which allowed Unla Key to exit the room to an area with hills and smoke [ch 015 / 015.02].

## Last Observed State
Pandi was last observed in Chapter 015, facilitating the party's exit from the blue-marble room [ch 015 / 015.02]. After Unla Key correctly guessed the answer to the word game and the party completed the Tangram puzzle, Pandi traded the "gift of passage" in exchange for the finished puzzle piece, which attached itself to the wall [ch 015 / 015.02]. This action transported Unla Key out of the room and into an area with hills, smoke, and drums rising from an encampment [ch 015 / 015.02]. Pandi’s physical location or status after the transport is not described; the NPC effectively vanished from the scene upon completing the transaction [ch 015 / 015.02]. Status not established in the summaries beyond this departure.
