## Identity
Slerdax is described by Unla Key as a "dark lord" of the Ravenloft setting who presides over a small domain [ch 073 / 073.04; ch 073 / entry]. He operates a restaurant specifically designed to trap the unwary [ch 073 / 073.04; ch 073 / entry]. Unla Key identifies Slerdax as a "minor lord" encountered in the context of "minor lords with small domains" during an advanced course on forbidden lore [ch 073 / 073.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Slerdax possesses a desire to be praised for his "brilliant cooking and excellent service" [ch 073 / 073.04; ch 073 / entry]. However, he is cursed to doubt his own brilliance, creating a conflict between his desire for acclaim and his compelled self-doubt [ch 073 / 073.04].

## Last Observed State
