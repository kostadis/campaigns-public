## Identity
Hellenhild was a Frost Giant serving as a Jarl within the retinue of Duchess Tartha [ch 066 / 066.05; ch 066 / entry]. She was one of four giant lords—alongside Duchess Tartha, Count Vaal, and Thane Braxow—tasked with confronting the party to prevent them from reaching the Queen [ch 066 / 066.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Hellenhild displayed insecurity regarding her combat performance, offering a weak excuse ("I was trying some new powder for a better grip") after a failed throw [ch 066 / entry; ch 066 / moment]. She was motivated by professional pride and rivalry, worrying that Duchess Tartha would "steal the glory" if she did not act against the flying archer Thistle [ch 066 / 066.05]. Her primary motivation was duty; she explicitly stated the intent to "do our duty and kill" the intruders [ch 066 / 066.05].

## History with the Party
- Hellenhild was introduced as a Frost Giant Jarl accompanying Duchess Tartha, Count Vaal, and Thane Braxow to confront the 3HPP in the hallway [ch 066 / 066.05].
- Unable to cross the wall of fire cast by Vardis, Hellenhild attempted to throw a rock at Thistle, who was flying above the wall, but missed by a significant distance [ch 066 / 066.05].
- Hellenhild was seared by a mote of fire exploded by Unla Key between Count Vaal and Duchess Tartha, causing her physical condition to deteriorate [ch 066 / entry; ch 066 / 066.05].
- Hellenhild was found dead alongside the other giant lords; Orsik looted a cloak of white dragon scales and a teleportation conch from her body [ch 067 / 067.02].

## Last Observed State
Hellenhild was found dead in Chapter 067 [ch 067 / 067.02]. Orsik looted a cloak of white dragon scales and a teleportation conch from her body [ch 067 / 067.02]. He subsequently dragged her corpse along with the other giant lords into a storeroom north of the guest rooms to hide them from Mirran, who arrived moments later to find them missing [ch 067 / 067.02].
