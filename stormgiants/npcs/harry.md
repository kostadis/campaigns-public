## Identity
Harry is a male giant elk [ch 054 / entry]. He appears alongside his wife, Mary [ch 054 / 054.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Harry is characterized as grumpy and opinionated [ch 054 / entry]. He is strongly anti-Fae and anti-Feywild, declaring that he and Mary belong to "an atheistic club and do not worship the gods of the Fae" [ch 054 / 054.02]. He views the Feywild as "just a tool of the oppressor to keep the Giant Elk subservient to a doomed religion" [ch 054 / 054.03], and dismisses Thistle’s claim to be a "Fairy from the Feywild" as "nonsense" [ch 054 / 054.02].

His primary motivation is to regain "peace and quiet" [ch 054 / 054.03]. He expresses frustration with bipedal creatures, complaining, "Another bipedal stealing our grass!" [ch 054 / 054.02], and urging the party to leave the area so he and Mary can return to enjoying their meal [ch 054 / 054.02; ch 054 / 054.03]. He frequently complains about other giant elk, specifically "the Joneses," whom he describes as irritating and superior [ch 054 / 054.02].

## History with the Party
- Harry, a male giant elk, approached the party alongside his wife Mary while they were digging for a stone [ch 054 / 054.02].
- Thistle used spells to speak with animals and asked the elks if they had seen other creatures, prompting Harry to complain about "the Joneses" [ch 054 / 054.02].
- Harry declared that he and Mary belong to "an atheistic club" and do not worship the gods of the Fae, contradicting Thistle's claim of being fey [ch 054 / 054.02].
- After Thistle identified as fey from the Feywild, Harry muttered that the Feywild is "just a tool of the oppressor to keep the Giant Elk subservient to a doomed religion" [ch 054 / 054.03].
- When Orsik and Vardis attempted to smash the stone, Harry expressed frustration that "the sooner you get out of here, the sooner we get our peace and quiet" [ch 054 / 054.03].
- Harry and Mary ultimately pushed the stone out of the way themselves to remove the party from the area [ch 054 / 054.02; ch 054 / 054.03].

## Last Observed State
Harry was last seen in Chapter 054. After complaining about the noise and the party's presence, Harry and Mary pushed the stone out of the way themselves to clear the area [ch 054 / 054.02; ch 054 / 054.03]. Status not established in the summaries beyond this action [ch 054 / 054.03].

## Relationships
**Mary:** Harry's wife [ch 054 / 054.02]. Mary frequently corrects or scolds Harry, such as when she says, "Harry, how many times must I tell you to stop speaking ill of the Joneses" [ch 054 / moment]. She apologises for his behavior to the party, stating, "My husband and I are trying to enjoy a quiet meal" [ch 054 / 054.02]. Despite his grumpiness, they act together, ultimately moving the stone as a unit [ch 054 / 054.03].

**Thistle:** Harry is hostile toward Thistle, a fey creature. He calls Thistle’s identity "nonsense" and mutters anti-Feywild sentiments [ch 054 / 054.02; ch 054 / 054.03]. When Mary calls Thistle a "Pretentious flying creature," Harry echoes this sentiment by disparaging Thistle’s origins [ch 054 / 054.03]. He views Thistle’s actions as another instance of bipedal intrusion into their lives [ch 054 / 054.02].
