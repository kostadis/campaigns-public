## Identity
Aunt Nonnie (also referred to as Nonnie) is a short, ruddy-cheeked woman who operates an inn in Llorkh known as Nonnie's Place [ch 003 / entry; ch 005 / entry; ch 006 / entry]. She is particularly insistent on being addressed as "Aunt Nonnie" and takes pride in her establishment's strictly vegetarian-only menu [ch 003 / entry]. Her inn serves as a lodging and dining spot for visitors to Llorkh who do not stay with the Iron Circle, as well as local residents [ch 003 / 003.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Aunt Nonnie is characterized by a friendly and welcoming demeanor toward patrons, specifically treating refugees and strangers with dignity; a client noted that Nonnie "treats everyone like a human being. Even refugees like me" [ch 003 / 003.06; ch 005 / entry]. She is protective of her reputation and reacts strongly to perceived disrespect or outbursts, raising her prices in response to an argument from the dwarf Orsik [ch 006 / 006.06]. She is willing to share local intelligence, informing guests of increased kobold activity and the inactivity of the town guards [ch 005 / 005.08]. She has a sharp wit and a long memory for past incidents, as demonstrated when she rebuked Orsik for a past action ("that THING you did") in response to his comment about the guards staying in their tower [ch 005 / 005.08; ch 005 / moment].

## Relationships
*   **The Party (Vardis, Orsik, Thistle, Unla Key):** Aunt Nonnie views the party positively, initially thinking them "a nice group of people" [ch 003 / 003.06]. She welcomes them warmly on their returns [ch 005 / 005.08]. However, her relationship is transactional and sensitive to conduct; she served them vegetarian entrees but raised prices after Orsik shouted at her [ch 005 / 005.08; ch 006 / 006.06]. Vardis noted her hospitality, stating it was "nice to be treated like a human being" [ch 003 / 003.06].
*   **Orsik:** She has a specific, sharp history with this individual. She rebukes him for past behavior, citing "that THING you did" when he makes comments about the guards [ch 005 / 005.08; ch 005 / moment]. She took offence at his verbal outburst regarding their reputation and retaliated by increasing their lodging costs [ch 006 / 006.06].
*   **The Middle Person:** The middle person was last located at Nonnie's Place, where she told the party she did not think they would be welcome there again [ch 003 / 003.01].
*   **Refugees:** Nonnie is known by refugees, such as a woman from Greenest, for treating them humanely [ch 003 / 003.06].
*   **Vardis:** Vardis harbored suspicion about her motives, remarking that she was "probably affiliated with an evil cult," though Nonnie continued to welcome him and the group [ch 005 / 005.08].
