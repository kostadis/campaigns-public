## Identity
Gauche the Young is the successor to Kolbaz at the Harper headquarters in Yartar [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.08]. He is of average human size, heavily built, with amber eyes, a broad nose, and gray hair that indicates he is not young despite his name [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.08]. He refers to the party as the "Three Halfpints and a Pint" [ch 033 / 033.08].

## Personality and Motivations
Gauche expresses strong disapproval of the Harper policy of hiring adventurers, explicitly stating to the party: "You are not Harpers. You are mercenaries" [ch 033 / 033.08]. He permits the party to use the teleportation circles only because of the historical success of Ezra’s program and his own recent promotion, though he asserts, "But I don't like it. Not one bit" [ch 033 / 033.08]. He is guarded regarding internal operations, refusing to answer whether others had come through the teleportation circle by claiming it is "Harper's business and no business of yours" [ch 033 / 033.08]. He appears openly pleased with his new role, describing his predecessor's death as a tragedy but his appointment as a "plum position" he had been looking forward to [ch 033 / 033.08; ch 033 / moment]. He takes an active interest in investigating wizards who masquerade as demons [ch 033 / moment].
