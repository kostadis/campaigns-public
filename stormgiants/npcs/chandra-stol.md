## Identity
Chandra Stol is the local cleric of Parnast, serving as both a spiritual leader and a civic figure who interacts with the town council and militia [ch 017 / 017.05; ch 019 / entry]. She is a descendant of Luistarra, an ancestor who bound the drow priestess Narcellia into a headband of intellect using hag magic centuries ago [ch 019 / 019.05; ch 019 / 019.07]. Chandra possesses a pendant once owned by Luistarra [ch 019 / 019.05]. She is the owner of the town’s pet wyvern [ch 023 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Chandra is primarily motivated by the survival of her town and the permanent destruction of Narcellia, the drow spirit who has tormented her family for centuries and caused her mother’s death thirty years prior [ch 019 / 019.07; ch 023 / 023.03]. She exhibits a stoic and guarded demeanor, often stating "You can do nothing for me" when approached with solutions that do not address the core existential threat, though she ultimately cooperates when convinced the stakes are high enough [ch 019 / 019.07; ch 019 / moment].

She displays pragmatic courage but also deep attachment to familiar things, mourning her wyvern’s death and defending its "heroic" performance despite criticism from the party [ch 022 / 022.05; ch 023 / 023.01]. Chandra is quick to anger when her safety or the town’s cohesion is threatened, reacting forcefully to Orsik’s attempts to hide information about the headband or to dismiss the suffering of the townspeople [ch 023 / 023.03; ch 023 / 023.04]. She possesses a skeptical view of external authorities, questioning the trustworthiness of the Lords’ Alliance and the SEER’s organization [ch 023 / 023.05].

## History with the Party
*   Chandra Stol is the local cleric in Parnast who sought a replacement idol for her temple, specifically a statue of Angharradh. Ragnar Redtooth, a barkeep at The Golden Tankard, mentioned she had been "looking for fools to get her idol for weeks" and that another NPC, Azam, refused to help due to dangers [ch 007 / 007.05].
*   When three horses fell sick in Parnast's center, Chandra arrived but stared silently and initially could not cast spells. She directed the party to the late wife of Silas, a herbalist, who might have a cure, and begged the adventurers to prevent a riot among the villagers [ch 017 / 017.05; ch 017 / 017.06].
*   The SEER revealed that her organization had manipulated events to encourage Chandra to seek this statue as a lure to draw out Narcellia, a drow priestess trapped in a headband who had plagued Chandra’s family for centuries [ch 019 / 019.05].
*   Chandra confirmed to the party that Narcellia was responsible for her mother’s death 30 years prior and that her ancestor Luistarra had used hag magic to bind Narcellia into the headband. Initially stoic, she told the party, "You can do nothing for me," but agreed to join them after Unla Key warned that her refusal meant "the end of the world" [ch 019 / 019.07].
*   During the siege of Parnast, Chandra explained that the transdimensional market of the Lionshield Coster was unavailable, forcing local merchants to rely on Zhentarim physical transport, meaning no magic was available for sale [ch 021 / 021.06].
*   Bad Fruul demanded Chandra, referred to as "the offspring of the horrid Luistarra," and the statue of Angharradh, threatening to enslave or destroy the town [ch 021 / 021.08].
*   During the battle, Chandra assisted in attacking the orc chieftain Kurglow as he charged Orsik [ch 022 / 022.03].
*   Orsik decided to release the town's pet wyvern to fight Bad Fruul. Chandra explained how to free the wyvern from the stables (using dead bodies as bait) but warned that it would "kill everyone." She cast *bless* on Orsik, Thistle, and Wallace [ch 022 / 022.05; ch 022 / 022.08].
*   Chandra refused the party's offer to rest before the final assault, stating that "my family’s nemesis is guiding his actions" and she knew Narcellia would not wait [ch 022 / 022.05].
*   After Bad Fruul’s death, Chandra found the sound of swords striking his body "satisfying," though she mourned the death of her wyvern, insisting it fought "heroically" while the party criticized its performance [ch 023 / 023.01].
*   When the party debated handing the headband to the SEER, Chandra demanded the SEER "get rid of" the soul of Narcellia, which had tormented her family for too long. She later accused Orsik of endangering her life and questioned the trustworthiness of the Lords’ Alliance, noting that the Zhentarim also helped defeat Tiamat [ch 023 / 023.03; ch 023 / 023.04; ch 023 / 023.05].

## Last Observed State
As of the end of chapter 023, Chandra is alive and remains in Parnast [ch 023 / 023.01; ch 023 / 023.02; ch 023 / 023.05]. She is actively involved in the town's post-battle recovery, arguing with Ragnar and Gundalin about how to repair the devastation [ch 023 / 023.02]. She expresses satisfaction at Bad Fruul’s death but grief over the loss of her wyvern [ch 023 / 023.01]. During the debate regarding the headband of Narcellia, she demanded the SEER destroy the soul within and challenged the party's blind trust in the Lords’ Alliance [ch 023 / 023.03; ch 023 / 023.05]. Her final known action was siding with those who began to question the SEER's motives, acknowledging that spymasters lie [ch 023 / 023.05].

## Relationships
**Orsik:** Chandra has a tense and volatile relationship with Orsik. He vowed to protect her and forced a confession from her about the SEER's plot [ch 019 / 019.06; ch 019 / 019.07]. She accepted his protection only when warned of the end of the world, reacting with shock and intimidation to his aggressive arguments [ch 020 / entry; ch 020 / 020.01]. Post-battle, she accused him of endangering her life and labeled him a "mean person" for his dismissive attitude toward the wyvern and casualties [ch 023 / 023.02; ch 023 / 023.04].

**Narcellia:** Chandra views Narcellia as her family’s nemesis. Narcellia was responsible for Chandra’s mother’s death thirty years ago and has plagued the family for centuries [ch 019 / 019.07; ch 022 / 022.05]. Chandra believes Narcellia’s soul must be destroyed, not just contained [ch 023 / 023.03].

**Bad Fruul:** Chandra is the target of Bad Fruul’s demand, described by the giant as "the offspring of the horrid Luistarra" [ch 021 / 021.08].

**Ragnar Redtooth:** Ragnar is a barkeep who initially mocked Chandra for looking for "fools" to retrieve her idol, but later stood with her in refusing Bad Fruul’s demands and argues with her on town recovery matters [ch 007 / 007.05; ch 021 / 021.08; ch 023 / 023.02].

**The SEER/Lords' Alliance:** Chandra is skeptical of the SEER and the broader Lords' Alliance, questioning what is truly known about their motives and noting that their opposition (the Zhentarim) also helped defeat Tiamat [ch 023 / 023.04; ch 023 / 023.05].
