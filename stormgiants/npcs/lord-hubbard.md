## Identity
Lord Hubbard is a white-haired noble [ch 078 / 078.05; ch 078 / entry]. He is identified as a wealthy individual, referencing "the coin I’ve spent a lifetime acquiring" [ch 078 / entry]. He is currently engaged in games of skill and chance [ch 078 / 078.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Lord Hubbard appears pragmatic regarding financial losses, expressing a desire to stop playing against Lady Atalia "lest I squander all the coin I’ve spent a lifetime acquiring" [ch 078 / entry]. He acknowledges his opponent's skill with admiration or resignation, noting that she seemed to know his moves before he did [ch 078 / 078.05]. His primary motivation in the observed scene was to cease playing against a specific opponent to preserve his wealth, leading him to seek alternative tables [ch 078 / entry].
