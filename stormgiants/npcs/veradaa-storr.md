## Identity
Veradaa Storr is a treasure hunter and a rumor mill for Cult gold maps [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.02]. She was known to possess a map of hidden Cult treasure, described by a sage as a possible "plan B" or result of "plain avarice" by Cult leaders rather than handing wealth to Tiamat [ch 001 / 001.02]. A Harper acquaintance described her to Thistle Wendrod as "the real deal" [ch 001 / 001.03].

## Personality and Motivations
The provided evidence does not establish Veradaa Storr's personality, personal motivations, or behavioral traits, as she does not appear in person in any recorded scene. Her professional reputation was strong enough to attract four distinct strangers—Orsik, Vardis, Thistle, and Unla—each seeking employment or information [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.02; ch 001 / 001.03]. The party relied on her written notes to locate specific treasure sites, indicating she was thorough in her investigations, though no direct evidence of her personal methodology survives beyond her written output [ch 003 / entry; ch 004 / entry].
