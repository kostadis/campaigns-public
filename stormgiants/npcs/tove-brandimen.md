## Identity
Tove Brandimen is a young half-elf [ch 017 / entry]. He is known to Ragnar for providing military intelligence regarding the movements of invading forces [ch 017 / 017.03]. Tove also identifies himself as the son of a woman he claims is being held hostage [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Tove appears motivated to present intelligence to the local defense, reporting that "monsters are on the move" and that an "attack by Bad Fruul's army is imminent" through Ragnar [ch 017 / 017.03]. He also seeks the party's aid to rescue his mother, producing evidence to support his claim that she is held at a specific cabin [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.01]. However, prisoners captured at the location identified by Tove described a half-elf matching his description as being "thick as thieves with Kragota," an ally of the enemy [ch 018 / 018.04; ch 018 / moment]. Vardis and Orsik suspect Tove of utilizing a "hostage scam," recalling a past instance of such deception [ch 018 / 018.01].

## History with the Party
- Tove provided military intelligence to Ragnar, reporting that monsters were on the move, forces were destroying resources, and the main camp could not be located [ch 017 / 017.03; ch 017 / entry].
- Ragnar reported that Tove could not find the main camp of the invading forces [ch 017 / 017.03; ch 017 / entry].
- Tove claimed his mother was being held hostage at a cabin and produced evidence supporting this claim the night before the party went to investigate [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.01].
- Vardis and Orsik doubted Tove's hostage claim, recalling a past "hostage scam" after reviewing a note attributed to Kragota Threeteeth [ch 018 / 018.01].
- Prisoners rescued from the cabin confirmed they had seen a half-elf matching Tove's description who was allied with Kragota [ch 018 / 018.04; ch 018 / moment].
- Prisoners confirmed they had not seen a woman matching Tove's description of his mother [ch 018 / 018.04].

## Last Observed State
Status not established in the summaries. Prisoners rescued from the cabin confirmed they had not seen a woman matching Tove's description of his mother, but they did see a half-elf matching Tove's description who was allied with Kragota Threeteeth [ch 018 / 018.04].

## Relationships
*   **Ragnar:** Ragnar received military intelligence from Tove and subsequently briefed Vardis on the threat Tove identified [ch 017 / 017.03].
*   **Kragota Threeteeth:** A half-elf matching Tove's description was reported by prisoners to be "thick as thieves" with Kragota [ch 018 / 018.04]. Kragota is also the purported author of the note Tove used as evidence for the hostage claim [ch 018 / 018.01].
*   **Vardis and Orsik:** These characters doubted Tove's claims, with Vardis explicitly noting a resemblance to a past "hostage scam" involving a note attributed to Kragota [ch 018 / 018.01].
*   **Tove's Mother:** Tove claimed she was being held hostage at the cabin, but prisoners stated they had not seen a woman matching her description [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.04].
