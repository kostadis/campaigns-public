## Identity
Fzoul Slovis is a Zhentarim operative and intelligence gatherer [ch 008 / entry]. He authored a report on the state of the Black Road during the 3rd tenday of Ches, detailing threats to trade routes including hobgoblin tolls and goblin attacks [ch 008 / 008.04]. In Chapter 026, Darz Helgar identified Fzoul as the individual who killed the wizard Manshoon, noting that Manshoon subsequently cloned himself [ch 026 / 026.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Fzoul is pragmatic and financially oriented, prioritizing intelligence gathering over direct conflict; when faced with a hobgoblin toll "too many for us to fight," he paid the fee rather than engaging, stating, "An expense report has been filed" [ch 008 / 008.04]. He is frustrated by the Zhentarim leadership’s "piecemeal attempts" against goblinoids, arguing that these half-measures have "emboldened them" and undermine the organization's credibility: "We will not be taken seriously if Goblins threaten traders" [ch 008 / 008.04]. Consequently, he advocates for "a more systematic approach of extermination of the Goblin menace" [ch 008 / 008.04]. He is also focused on talent acquisition, identifying a dwarf named Orsik as a useful potential recruit due to his unemployability as a standard guard, and stating, "I intend to recruit him at the earliest opportunity" [ch 008 / 008.04].

## History with the Party
- Fzoul Slovis is the author of a Zhentarim report on the state of the Black Road from the 10th day of the 3rd tenday of Ches [ch 008 / entry].
- In his report, Fzoul noted that his group encountered hobgoblins setting up a toll on the edge of the desert, which were "too many for us to fight" [ch 008 / 008.04].
- Fzoul's group paid a toll of "15 GP per camel" to the hobgoblins rather than fighting them, stating, "An expense report has been filed" [ch 008 / 008.04; ch 008 / moment].
- Fzoul recommended dealing with the hobgoblin toll location and noted a caravan near Vuerthyl had been attacked by goblins who "lacked the sophistication of the attacks of the earlier groups" [ch 008 / 008.04].
- Fzoul argued for "a more systematic approach of extermination of the Goblin menace," warning that "The piecemeal attempts have emboldened them" [ch 008 / 008.04].
- Fzoul identified a potential new recruit, the dwarf Orsik, stating, "I intend to recruit him at the earliest opportunity" [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.04].
- Darz Helgar mentioned that Fzoul killed Manshoon, but Manshoon cloned himself and is alive and well [ch 026 / 026.01].

## Last Observed State
Status not established in the summaries. Fzoul was last observed acting through his written report in Chapter 008 [ch 008 / entry]. In Chapter 026, he was only mentioned by Darz Helgar, who noted that Fzoul had killed Manshoon in the past [ch 026 / 026.01].

## Relationships
**Zhentarim Leadership**: Fzoul is subordinate to the Zhentarim leadership but openly criticizes their strategy regarding the Black Road, demanding a firmer stand against goblin threats [ch 008 / 008.04].
**Manshoon**: Fzoul killed Manshoon, though Manshoon survived via cloning, a fact revealed by Darz Helgar in Chapter 026 [ch 026 / 026.01].
**Orsik**: Fzoul identified the dwarf Orsik as a potential new recruit for the Zhentarim, intending to recruit him quickly due to his specific skill set and legal troubles [ch 008 / 008.04].
**Hobgoblins**: Fzoul’s group was forced to pay a toll to hobgoblins on the edge of the desert because their numbers made combat inadvisable [ch 008 / 008.04].
**Darz Helgar**: Darz spoke of Fzoul in Chapter 026, recounting that Fzoul had killed Manshoon [ch 026 / 026.01].
