## Identity
Klauth is an ancient red dragon known as "the Great Dragon of the North" [ch 047 / 047.02]. He is explicitly identified as a necromancer who maintains contracts with his agents, holding them in his hoard [ch 053 / 053.07]. His status as a red dragon is confirmed by Delsephine’s warning regarding the danger of angering such a creature [ch 053 / 053.07].

## Personality and Motivations
Klauth operates as a strategic patron who seeks to manage threats to his dominion through proxy violence rather than direct intervention. He views giants as an obstacle to the eventual domination of the North by dragons, leading him to be "very supportive" of the party’s plans against them [ch 053 / 053.05]. He desires to prevent the Lords' Alliance from investigating the Spine of the World, reasoning that the party are allies by necessity ("The enemy of the enemy") [ch 047 / 047.02]. He exercises indirect control, issuing commands through intermediaries to "forge ahead and face your destiny" and urging the party to "Slay their leaders" [ch 047 / 047.02]. Despite his power, he adheres to contractual obligations; he is willing to negotiate the buyout of servants if the financial terms are met, valuing the "living part" of a contract highly [ch 053 / 053.07].

## History with the Party
*   The party accepts a gift from Klauth, "the Great Dragon of the North," in the form of an airship crew to help them cross his dominion; the offer includes advice to "Slay their leaders" and a reward if they quell the giant threat. [ch 047 / 047.02]
*   Orsik reasons that the party should accept the offer because Giants and Dragons are rivals, and Klauth does not want the Lords' Alliance investigating the Spine of the World; the party agrees to take the airship. [ch 047 / 047.02]
*   The Great Airship is explicitly described as a gift from the ancient red dragon Klauth. [ch 048 / 048.09]
*   Delsephine refuses to reveal Klauth's location but states he will talk to and reward the party once they have dealt with the giants. [ch 053 / entry; ch 053 / 053.07]
*   The party negotiates a buyout of the "living part" of Brassik’s contract from Delsephine (acting on Klauth’s behalf) for 500 gold upfront and capped residuals, which releases Brassik from his obligations to Klauth. [ch 053 / 053.07]
*   Thistle expresses concern about angering a Red Dragon, but Orsik dismisses the worry, suggesting they can manage the narrative to avoid conflict. [ch 053 / 053.07]
*   After the party secures a feeding arrangement with Sandesyl, Delsephine conveys that Klauth wishes them the best of luck in ending the war of the giants. [ch 058 / 058.05]

## Last Observed State
Klauth did not appear personally in the final chapters of the provided evidence. In Chapter 058, his agent Delsephine conveyed his message to the party, wishing them "the best of luck in ending the war of the giants" [ch 058 / 058.05]. Prior to this, in Chapter 053, Delsephine refused to reveal his location, stating only that he would talk to and reward the party once the giants were dealt with [ch 053 / entry; ch 053 / 053.07]. Status not established in the summaries regarding his physical whereabouts, though he remained active in directing operations through Delsephine.

## Relationships
*   **Delsephine:** Her captain and primary agent, who acts on his behalf and holds authority to negotiate his contracts [ch 053 / 053.07]. Delsephine displays a deep, barely hidden sexual attraction to Klauth [ch 047 / 047.03].
*   **Brassik:** A crew member bound by a contract to Klauth, which covers both his living and undead states [ch 053 / 053.07]. The party purchased the "living part" of his contract from Klauth’s agent, releasing Brassik from his obligations to the dragon [ch 053 / 053.07].
*   **The Party (3HPP):** Klauth views them as useful tools to quell the giant threat. He provided them with a gift (an airship and crew) to cross his dominion and offered rewards for success [ch 047 / 047.02]. He avoids direct contact with them until their task is complete [ch 053 / 053.07].
*   **Giants:** Klauth considers them rivals and obstacles to dragon dominance in the North [ch 047 / 047.02; ch 053 / 053.05].
