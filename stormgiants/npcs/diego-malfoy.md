## Identity
Diego Malfoy is a guard in the town of Llorkh [ch 002 / entry; ch 003 / entry; ch 006 / entry]. He is identified as the "paramour" of Fiora Bendaega [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Diego displays a protective loyalty toward Fiora Bendaega, advancing with a growl and reaching for his sword when she signals distress or perceives a threat to her [ch 003 / 003.01]. He is attentive to Fiora’s non-verbal cues, backing down immediately when she winks or signals him to stand down [ch 003 / 003.01]. His protective nature is noted by Fiora, who claims he is "so fond of me" [ch 003 / 003.01], and later by Unla Key, who identifies him as her paramour [ch 006 / entry].

Diego appears to have a low opinion of the general populace's intelligence regarding local hazards, remarking "Most people aren't that stupid" after scolding Orsik for mishandling a supernatural situation [ch 002 / 002.10; ch 002 / moment]. He is quick to recognize previous acquaintances, specifically recalling Orsik and Vardis failing a job interview due to intoxication [ch 002 / 002.10]. He is susceptible to manipulation when it aligns with his protective instincts or official duties; in chapter 6, he was convinced by Unla Key to launch a Guard investigation into a tower [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.06].

## History with the Party
- In Llorkh, Diego is a guard who scolds Orsik for calling a bartender a ghost and recognizes Orsik and Vardis as the pair who previously failed to show up sober for a job interview; he directs them to the Lionshield Coster outpost when they ask for a scroll of dispel magic [ch 002 / 002.10; ch 002 / entry].
- When a dwarf threatens Fiora Bendaega with stealing her job, Fiora signals Diego; Diego advances growling and reaches for his sword until Fiora signals him to back down, after which he is part of the scene where the party is dismissed [ch 003 / 003.01; ch 003 / entry].
- Vardis mentions Diego later while shopping at the Lionshield Coster, claiming Diego wants to talk to him as an excuse to leave the interaction [ch 003 / 003.02].
- In a later meeting near Aunt Nonnie's, Diego is identified as Fiora's "paramour" and questions the party about whether they entered the tower; Unla Key convinces him to lead a Guard investigation into the tower [ch 006 / 006.06; ch 006 / entry].

## Last Observed State
In Chapter 6, Diego was encountered on the way to Aunt Nonnie's place. He questioned the party about whether they had entered a specific tower [ch 006 / 006.06]. After Unla Key convinced him that the party had escaped the building and that a Guard investigation was essential, Diego left to organize that investigation [ch 006 / 006.06].

Status not established in the summaries beyond this point.

## Relationships
*   **Fiora Bendaega:** Diego acts as Fiora's guard and protector. Fiora signals him to intimidate potential threats, and he obeys her cues instantly [ch 003 / 003.01]. Fiora describes him as "so fond of me" [ch 003 / 003.01], and he is explicitly identified as her "paramour" by Unla Key [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.06].
*   **Unla Key:** Unla Key manipulated Diego's beliefs in Chapter 6 to persuade him to lead a Guard investigation into the tower [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.06].
*   **Orsik:** Diego recognizes Orsik as someone who failed to show up sober for a previous job interview [ch 002 / 002.10]. He directed Orsik to the Lionshield Coster outpost when asked about dispel magic scrolls [ch 002 / 002.10]. Orsik greeted Diego casually with "heya, Diego" in Chapter 3, which coincided with Fiora signaling Diego to back down [ch 003 / 003.01].
*   **Vardis:** Diego recognized Vardis alongside Orsik in Chapter 2 [ch 002 / 002.10]. In Chapter 3, Vardis claimed "Diego wants to talk to me" as an excuse to leave an interaction at the Lionshield Coster, though there is no evidence Diego actually initiated contact [ch 003 / 003.02].
