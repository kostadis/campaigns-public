## Identity
Blagothkus is a cloud giant who once served as the lord of a flying castle and allied himself with the Cult of the Dragon and Tiamat [ch 056 / 056.07; ch 076 / entry]. He is the husband of Esclarotta and the father of Eigeron, a ghost seeking vengeance for being slain by his father in a moment of despair and envy [ch 046 / 046.06; ch 072 / 072.06]. Blagothkus was responsible for triggering the chaos of the Ordning through his alliance with the Cult [ch 056 / 056.07]. After his death in Chapter 077, his existence ends; he is no longer active in the campaign timeline [ch 077 / 077.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Blagothkus is driven by guilt and a desire to restore his family’s honor [ch 076 / entry]. He displays a pattern of self-preservation and avoidance of direct conflict, preferring to "stand back and protect" others rather than engage in melee unless coerced [ch 075 / 075.01]. He frequently deflects responsibility, insisting that his mistakes were not his fault or were accidents [ch 072 / 072.06; ch 077 / 077.04]. Despite his past actions, he seeks redemption through assistance to the party, though he often lacks the conviction to act without external pressure from figures like Orsik or Esclarotta [ch 073 / 073.05; ch 075 / 075.01]. He possesses a cynical worldview, viewing the debate over whether ends justify means as the cause of his family's ruin [ch 065 / 065.07]. He maintains a belief that keeping different peoples apart is necessary, a stance identified by Unla Key as racism [ch 070 / 070.03].

## History with the Party
*   Blagothkus is a cloud giant who slew his son, Eigeron, in a fight born of despair and envy after the divine oracle at the Eye of Annam told him he could never impress the gods enough to earn their favor. Eigeron’s ghost asked the party to kill Blagothkus, who retired to his castle to mourn. [ch 046 / 046.06]
*   Unla Key recalls that Eigeron had told him precisely where he could find Blagothkus, and considered claiming Blagothkus' castle for himself before deciding to wait. [ch 050 / 050.01]
*   Thistle points out that the party could always take Blagothkus' castle during a discussion about finances in Silverymoon. [ch 055 / 055.06]
*   The party arrives at Parnast and recognizes the castle as the home of Blagothkus, who had allied himself with Tiamat and triggered the chaos of the Ordning. The castle was previously secured by the First Faction from Blagothkus and a Dracolich. [ch 056 / 056.07]
*   Sandesyl Morgia, the vampire castellan, tells the party that Blagothkus is still in the castle but "has been moping. It’s been weeks," and jokes about dining on him. She recounts that Blagothkus allied with the Cult of the Dragon, which led to the First Faction capturing the castle twice, and that Blagothkus went to the Spine of the World and returned to find his son dead. [ch 057 / 057.06]
*   The party visits the stern tower, Blagothkus' chambers, which are in shambles and guarded by ogre zombies not under Sandesyl's control. [ch 057 / 057.07]
*   The party finds Blagothkus in the control room, harried, unshaven, and disheveled, trapped in an endless argument with his wife Esclarotta, who controls the castle and wants him dead. Esclarotta blames him for helping the Dragons bring Tiamat and for killing his son. [ch 058 / 058.02]
*   When Thistle suggests killing Blagothkus, Blagothkus prepares for combat, but stands down when Vardis clarifies it is a hypothetical, relieved to have someone on his side against his wife. [ch 058 / 058.03]
*   Esclarotta agrees to fly the castle after Blagothkus and Thistle bond over jokes about Fire Giants. [ch 058 / 058.04]
*   At the Eye of the All-Father, the party tells Eigeron's ghost that Blagothkus is not dead but that his castle is present, causing the ghost to go mad and attempt to possess a hero. [ch 058 / 058.07]
*   Ogres Worug and Tak report that Blagothkus has been "holed up in the control room" since the castle arrived at Parnast, crying that he had killed his son. They explain that Blagothkus took control of the castle when the return of Tiamat was thwarted. [ch 059 / 059.04; ch 059 / 059.05]
*   Unla Key and Thistle explore Blagothkus' battle-torn bedchamber in the stern tower and take treasure from his chests. They speculate on how Blagothkus regained control of the castle. [ch 059 / 059.02]
*   Blagothkus speaks for the first time in the control tower, noting that the debate over whether ends justify means is "precisely how we ended up in this emotional conflict between me and my wife." [ch 065 / 065.07]
*   Back at the castle, Blagothkus lifts Thistle to see his dragon eyes, calling them "cool," and claims he no longer hates dragons, having a relationship with them now. He defends keeping different peoples apart, which Unla Key identifies as racism. [ch 070 / 070.03]
*   Blagothkus urges the party to find King Hekaton to defeat the blue dragon (Doom of the Desert), stating, "We need his strength to defeat the dragon." He argues it is not the time to remove unwanted guests (the Vampire). [ch 070 / 070.04]
*   Blagothkus watches the party clear skeletal remains from the lower courtyard and shouts loudly, thanking them for doing a task he had looked for someone to do for almost a year. He explains that undead like the dark, implying they are hiding from the sun. [ch 071 / 071.02]
*   Blagothkus was revealed to be Esclarotta's husband and blamed by her for their current state, having visited the Oracle where the All-Father declared their son the leader giants needed if he killed his father [ch 072 / 072.06].
*   Esclarotta committed Blagothkus to helping the 3HPP defeat Sandesyl after Orsik convinced her that direct action was necessary [ch 072 / 072.06].
*   Blagothkus joined the party, dropping boulders on zombies and mocking Unla Key's magic, while stating he has "a whole binder full of better magic users" [ch 073 / 073.05; ch 073 / moment].

*   Blagothkus urged the party to flee the Undead Ogres to run to Sandesyl's tower, arguing the ogres were merely a trap for the dragon, but Orsik refused to leave [ch 074 / 074.01].
*   Blagothkus explained that Sandesyl had carved a lair in the ice caves below because the party had previously damaged her forces, and he initially offered to stay behind to guard the entrance [ch 074 / 074.04].
*   Blagothkus explained the history of the Frost Giant Jarl Brunvild, noting that Sandesyl kept him alive as a snack source and that Esclarotta had a "different version of the facts" regarding the castle's history [ch 074 / 074.07].
*   Brunvild, when freed by Thistle, bitterly accused Blagothkus of being the reason he was trapped and his men were dead [ch 074 / 074.08; ch 074 / moment].
*   In the battle with Sandesyl, Blagothkus initially tried to protect the magic user, but was shamed and threatened by Orsik into charging into battle, where he destroyed a vampire spawn [ch 075 / 075.01].
*   Blagothkus was bitten by the Skeletal Dragon and asked to be healed, but Orsik threatened to kill him, prompting Blagothkus to throw a rock at the dragon [ch 075 / 075.04].
*   Blagothkus and Thistle struck the last of the Cult of the Dragon with ranged attacks as Sandesyl was driven to mist [ch 075 / 075.05].

*   In the control room, Thistle revealed the ghost's identity as Eigeron, leading Esclarotta to order Blagothkus's death [ch 077 / 077.04].
*   Blagothkus was killed by the combined attacks of the 3HPP, with Brunvild delivering the final blow using one of Blagothkus's old hammers [ch 077 / 077.04].

## Last Observed State
Blagothkus is dead. He was killed in the castle's control room during Chapter 077 [ch 077 / 077.04]. The killing blow was delivered by Brunvild, the Frost Giant Jarl, using one of Blagothkus's old hammers [ch 077 / 077.04; ch 077 / moment]. Prior to his death, Blagothkus was exposed by Thistle as the killer of Eigeron, leading Esclarotta to scream "Kill him! FREE MY SON!!!!" and order the attack [ch 077 / 077.04]. In his final moments, he whimpers, "That's not what happened. It was an accident," before being struck by the combined attacks of the party and Brunvild [ch 077 / 077.04]. In Chapter 081, Thistle references Blagothkus in the past tense, noting that he "killed" Eigeron [ch 081 / 081.04].

## Relationships
*   **Esclarotta:** His spectral wife and controller of the castle. Their relationship is defined by mutual blame and a standoff regarding their son's death and the castle's ruin [ch 058 / 058.02; ch 072 / 072.06]. Esclarotta eventually ordered his death to free their son's ghost [ch 077 / 077.04].
*   **Eigeron:** His son, whom Blagothkus slew in a fight born of despair. Eigeron's ghost is trapped until Blagothkus is slain, a fact central to the plot [ch 046 / 046.06; ch 076 / moment].
*   **Brunvild:** The Frost Giant Jarl who harbors a bitter hatred for Blagothkus, blaming him for his own imprisonment and the deaths of his men [ch 074 / 074.08; ch 074 / moment]. Brunvild ultimately delivers the final blow to Blagothkus [ch 077 / 077.04].
*   **The 3HPP (Orsik, Thistle, Vardis, Unla Key):** Blagothkus was committed by Esclarotta to help this party defeat Sandesyl [ch 072 / 072.06]. Orsik frequently bullied Blagothkus into combat, dismissing his pleas for healing or retreat [ch 075 / 075.01; ch 075 / 075.04]. The party ultimately assisted in his execution [ch 077 / 077.04].
*   **Sandesyl Morgia:** A vampire castellan who served under Blagothkus. Blagothkus viewed her forces as obstacles to his goals and helped the party defeat her [ch 057 / 057.06; ch 075 / 075.05].
*   **Tiamat and the Cult of the Dragon:** Blagothkus allied with these entities, which led to the capture of his castle and the current state of the Ordning [ch 056 / 056.07; ch 057 / 057.06].
