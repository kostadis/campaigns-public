## Identity
Countess Sansuri is a cloud giantess and the elder sibling of Count Thullen [ch 038 / 038.02]. She is identified as a noble of Lyn Armaal who was making a play for rulership and the title of Queen of all giants [ch 058 / 058.02; ch 072 / 072.06]. Her family is noted to have held magical artifacts when Ostoria fell [ch 058 / 058.02]. Esclarotta describes her as a "classless poseur of a magic-wielding cloud giant" who falsely claims to be favored among cloud giants [ch 072 / 072.06; ch 063 / 063.11]. Harshnag the Grim characterizes her negatively in comparison to her brother, stating he is a good person while she is "not so much" [ch 038 / 038.02].

## Personality and Motivations
The evidence presents Countess Sansuri primarily through the hostile perspective of those who oppose her. Esclarotta characterizes her as "arrogant, selfish, horrible" and a "vain, glorious, greedy" creature who should be dead [ch 058 / 058.02; ch 058 / 058.04]. Her primary motivation appears to be the seizure of power, specifically attempting to claim the Ordning to rule over all giants [ch 072 / 072.06]. Esclarotta asserts that Sansuri attempts to leverage her family's possession of artifacts from the fall of Ostoria to legitimize her claim to rulership [ch 058 / 058.02]. No direct actions or spoken lines from Sansuri herself are recorded in the provided evidence; her characterization is entirely derived from the descriptions of Esclarotta, Harshnag, and Hekaton [ch 038 / 038.02; ch 058 / 058.04; ch 082 / 082.05].

## History with the Party
- Countess Sansuri is the younger brother's sister; Harshnag describes her as "not so much" a good person compared to her brother Count Thullen [ch 038 / 038.02].
- Esclarotta identifies Sansuri as the cloud giantess of Lyn Armaal currently "making a play for rulership" [ch 058 / entry; ch 058 / 058.02].
- Esclarotta berates Blagothkus for failing to seize magical artifacts from Sansuri, stating that her family held them when Ostoria fell [ch 058 / 058.02].
- Esclarotta makes the death of Countess Sansuri a mandatory condition for the party receiving the Cloud Castle's aid, describing her as "arrogant, selfish, horrible" [ch 058 / 058.04].
- When the party initially intends to attack Sansuri, Esclarotta overrules the plan, citing their lack of practice, and redirects them to help Thistle find his love, Laela [ch 060 / 060.01].
- Esclarotta expresses violent hatred toward Sansuri, calling her an "excrement of cumulus cloud" who falsely claims to be favored among cloud giants, and urges the party to kill her immediately after the Duchess is dispatched [ch 063 / 063.11].
- In a recount of the Ordning's corruption, Esclarotta names Sansuri as a "classless poseur" attempting to claim the title of Queen of all giants by making a play for the Ordning [ch 072 / 072.06].
- King Hekaton lists Sansuri among the giant lords he intends to force to kneel, offering to join the party in their final battle only if the party kills them or convinces them of their errors [ch 082 / 082.05].
- Thistle realizes that Esclarotta will be displeased if Sansuri is not defeated, as this violates the terms of their alliance [ch 082 / 082.05].
