## Identity
Prince Thornacious is a Royal Fairy Prince currently in exile from the Feywild [ch 013 / entry]. He was recognized as a minor member of the Seelie Court, which explains his previous familiarity with the party's companion Thistle [ch 036 / 036.04]. His court, described as "lowly" due to the exile imposed by Queen Titania, includes the satyr Blasius and the sprite Oblivillish [ch 014 / 014.06; ch 013 / 013.07].

## Personality and Motivations
Thornacious is characterized by his subordinates as foolish and empire-building [ch 015 / 015.06; ch 015 / 015.07]. He is possessive of his game, evidenced by exiling Oblivillish for killing a stag Thornacious claimed belonged to him [ch 014 / 014.06]. He is politically active, seeking to expand control into the territory of the Ettin Gralm and disrupting local power balances [ch 015 / entry]. He maintains relationships through transaction and status, inviting the party to dinner to cultivate alliances [ch 020 / 020.01] and later noting that Thistle is "being noticed" by Queen Titania [ch 023 / 023.07]. While often absent, his influence persists through his courtiers and the Zhentarim agent Orsik, who has served in his court [ch 013 / 013.07].

## Last Observed State
Prince Thornacious was not physically present in the final recorded event (Chapter 084). He was indirectly observed through the actions of Orsik, who utilized a magical gift previously given to him by Thornacious to avoid poisoning from a dragon-worm hybrid's stinger [ch 084 / 084.01]. The Prince himself was otherwise absent from the scene. Status not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
**Blasius and Oblivillish:** Thornacious restored Blasius to his post as Satyr and Oblivillish as Chief of Staff after the party defeated an Unseelie camp, reversing prior exiles [ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / moment]. Blasius claims Thornacious wants to build a great empire while Blasius wishes to return home [ch 015 / 015.07].

**Orsik (Zhentarim):** Orsik served as a Zhentarim agent in Thornacious’s court [ch 013 / 013.07]. Orsik possesses a gift from Thornacious that protects him from poison [ch 084 / 084.01]. Orsik also wore a disguise to impersonate Thornacious, staging a "cure" of muteness and pardoning dryads [ch 050 / entry].

**Queen Titania:** Titania uses Thornacious’s involvement in the dryad incident as a reason to keep him out of the Feywild [ch 051 / 051.04]. Thornacious informed Thistle that he had mentioned Thistle to Titania, who was pleased [ch 023 / 023.07].

**Thistle:** Thistle knows Thornacious through his friend Blasius and was recognized by Thornacious as a member of the Seelie Court [ch 013 / 013.07; ch 036 / 036.04]. Thistle has invoked Thornacious’s name during battles [ch 022 / 022.10].

**Kragota:** The hill giant leader Kragota screamed that her faction had a deal with Prince Thornacious during a battle at a cabin [ch 018 / 018.03].

**The Knife:** The Knife fawned over Orsik while he was disguised as Thornacious, misinterpreting his silence for graciousness [ch 052 / 052.04].
