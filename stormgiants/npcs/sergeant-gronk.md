## Identity
Sergeant Gronk is a goblin who commanded the defenders of a cursed cave [ch 001 / entry]. He was the sergeant responsible for holding the cave after a mission involving two giants and their soldiers went wrong [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Gronk was shrewd regarding terrain, utilizing partial cover and maintaining a southern escape tunnel [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.06]. He harbored bitterness toward his allies, complaining that "half our soldiers are little toys" and that one of their two accompanying giants was "a tiny little creature" while the other had died in a cave-in [ch 001 / moment; ch 001 / 001.06].

His primary motivation was defensive containment; he ordered his troops to hold the cave and "not let anything pass until whatever magical curses we triggered end" [ch 001 / 001.06]. He believed the mission had "turned into a disaster" [ch 001 / 001.06]. Despite the poor circumstances, he displayed confidence when hearing the party debate at the entrance, smiling because he believed "he's got them where he wants them" and subsequently positioning his troops in partial cover [ch 001 / 001.06].

## History with the Party
- Sergeant Gronk is a goblin sergeant commanding the cave's defenders after a mission gone wrong [ch 001 / entry].
- He died after a thunderwave spell cast by a magic-user killed two of his goblins [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.06].
- Upon hearing the party debating at the entrance, he smiled, set his troops in partial cover, and believed he had them where he wanted them [ch 001 / 001.06].
- He ordered his goblins to hold the cave and "not let anything pass until whatever magical curses we triggered end" [ch 001 / 001.06].
- His tactical position included a southern tunnel for retreat and a cavern with plenty of cover; a giant lay buried in a cave-in in the east tunnel, raising dust [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.06].
- He initially believed the battle was going well for his side, noting "two of the invaders are dead" [ch 001 / 001.06].
- His final thought before death was wondering if "that giant" would ever recover [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.06].

## Last Observed State
Sergeant Gronk is dead [ch 001 / entry]. He died after a magic-user cast a thunderwave spell, which killed two of his goblins [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.06]. Prior to his death, he believed the battle was going well, noting that "two of the invaders are dead" and that "The pompous dwarf is slaughtered. And the flying creature is put down." [ch 001 / 001.06]. His final thought was wondering, "will that giant ever recover?" [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.06].

## Relationships
**The Party**: Gronk was hostile to the party members, whom he identified as "invaders" [ch 001 / 001.06]. He targeted specific individuals, referring to one as a "pompous dwarf" and another as a "flying creature" [ch 001 / 001.06].

**The Giants**: Gronk commanded a force that included two giants. One giant died in a cave-in in the east tunnel, the source of dust that tickled Gronk's nose [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.06]. The surviving giant was described by Gronk as "a tiny little creature" [ch 001 / moment; ch 001 / 001.06]. Gronk's final thoughts were occupied by whether this giant would recover from its injuries or state [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.06].

**Goblin Troops**: Gronk was their commanding sergeant. He ordered them to hold the cave and utilize partial cover [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.06]. He lamented that "half our soldiers are little toys" [ch 001 / moment; ch 001 / 001.06].
