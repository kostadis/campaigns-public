## Identity
Amy is the lead actor in the production [ch 035 / entry]. Amy is described as a woman who presents a "fluffy and joyful" exterior while being "status-driven and evasive" underneath [ch 035 / entry; ch 035 / 035.03]. Amy consistently responds to questions with the phrase, "I don’t know, and I am not sure," which Thistle interprets as an attempt to hide a mysterious past [ch 035 / entry; ch 035 / 035.03].

## Personality and Motivations
Amy is motivated by status and requires others to "pander to their sense of being a star" to function effectively [ch 035 / entry; ch 035 / 035.03]. Amy is timid and unable to push her own ideas, often deferring to authority or the director's vision [ch 035 / entry]. Amy becomes evasive and claims ignorance ("I don’t know, and I am not sure") when pressed, potentially to mask insecurity or past issues [ch 035 / entry; ch 035 / 035.03]. Amy complies with aggressive direction, shifting from questioning authority ("Did Kaelen sign off on this?") to meek submission ("You’re the boss. Whatever you want…") when browbeaten [ch 035 / 035.04]. Amy credits external validation for their success, thanking "Mr. Orsik" for the coaching that helped them deliver lines with energy [ch 035 / 035.04].
