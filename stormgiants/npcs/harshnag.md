## Identity
- **Name and aliases:** Harshnag. He introduced himself as "Harshnag, the Grim of Force Grey" [ch 037 / 037.04], and the summaries also call him "Harshnag the Grim" [ch 045 / 045.02]. Orsik and Vardis call him "Frosty" [ch 038 / 038.01; ch 046 / 046.03].
- **Kind:** A frost giant [ch 038 / moment; ch 046 / entry].
- **Reputation:** Orsik described him as:
  - a legendary good-aligned frost giant;
  - the largest member of Force Grey;
  - believed to be hundreds of years old [ch 037 / 037.04].
- **Age:** He denies being old by giant reckoning [ch 045 / 045.06], but chapter 045 states that he was old [ch 045 / entry; ch 045 / 045.07].
- **Appearance:** Plate armor, a white dragon's skull worn as a helm, and a jagged axe [ch 037 / entry; ch 037 / 037.04].
- **Role toward the party:** Guide to the Oracle at the Eye of the All-Father [ch 037 / 037.04; ch 038 / 038.01]. He knows the names and locations of all nine spirit mounds, and drew the map of them that the party later used [ch 046 / 046.08; ch 052 / 052.09].
- **Home:** He told the party he has no fixed home: "My roof is the stars, my walls are the trees, and my bed is the ground" [ch 045 / 045.05].

## Personality and Motivations
- **Main concern: the broken Ordning and the giant war.**
  - He frames the Ordning as what "has kept the peace for so many centuries" [ch 038 / 038.01].
  - He blames Tiamat's bid for power for its sundering [ch 039 / 039.03].
  - He pushed the party to go straight to the Oracle [ch 040 / 040.01; ch 042 / 042.06].
  - He berated them for spending Oracle questions on trivial matters [ch 046 / 046.07].
- **Devout toward the All-Father.**
  - He prays to the All-Father [ch 041 / 041.03; ch 041 / 041.04] and reads events as signs ("The All-Father sent a sign") [ch 042 / moment].
  - He was furious to find a dragon in the holy place [ch 044 / 044.02].
- **Tests would-be heroes rather than shielding them.**
  - When the party ignored his warnings, he refused to defend them against two frost giants. He treated the fight as a test and concluded "They are the heroes" [ch 041 / 041.03; ch 041 / 041.04].
  - The control-room giant claimed that Harshnag hides needed items in the temple to test little people. This is that giant's account; Harshnag was not present [ch 059 / entry; ch 059 / 059.07].
- **Proud and easily needled.**
  - He privately resents "Frosty" as disrespectful [ch 038 / moment].
  - He bristles at being called homeless or old [ch 045 / 045.05; ch 045 / 045.06].
  - Twice he brought his axe close to Unla Key when provoked [ch 038 / 038.02; ch 039 / 039.03].
- **Brusque, but protective in a crisis.**
  - He dug the party out of an avalanche, with a mock apology [ch 042 / 042.07].
  - He carried the dead party out in his satchel [ch 044 / 044.04].
  - Against Adimond Kroskas he chose to cover the party's escape at his own risk [ch 058 / 058.08].
- **Self-serving or face-saving streak.**
  - He asked for a 100 gold cut of the bounty [ch 037 / 037.04].
  - Faced with the remorhaz, he proposed grabbing the Ax of Thrym and running [ch 046 / 046.03].
  - After the fight he claimed he had been "encouraging" the party all along [ch 046 / 046.04].
- **Opinions:**
  - Rejects the idea that biology determines morality [ch 038 / 038.02].
  - Spits at mention of the Lords' Alliance [ch 038 / 038.03].
  - Disdains cloud and fire giants [ch 044 / 044.01].
  - Holds that between giant and dragon there are "Only ceasefires" [ch 039 / 039.03].
- **Habits:** Fond of sleep and comfort. He napped repeatedly in the temple's giant beds [ch 044 / 044.06; ch 045 / 045.06; ch 045 / 045.07], and begged for soup from the Gilded Horseshoe [ch 038 / 038.03].

## History with the Party
- Harshnag arrived at the party's roadside campfire, announcing himself as "Harshnag, the Grim of Force Grey," while Unla Key readied his spells. [ch 037 / 037.04]
- Orsik recognised him and described him as a legendary good-aligned frost giant, the largest member of Force Grey, believed to be hundreds of years old. [ch 037 / 037.04]
- He appeared in plate armor, wore a white dragon's skull as a helm, and carried a jagged axe. [ch 037 / entry; ch 037 / 037.04]
- He told the party that the ordning was broken, that the storm giants could no longer keep the smaller giants in check, and that King Hekaton was missing. [ch 037 / 037.04]
- He said Krowen Valharrow had told him the group had agreed to investigate the giants. He offered to guide them to the oracle at the Eye of the All-Father, under the Spine of the World. [ch 037 / 037.04; ch 038 / 038.01]
- He asked for a 100 gold share of the bounty. The group accepted it as a small tax on their 5000 gold. [ch 037 / 037.04]
- The bounty was the 5,000 gold piece reward for the brigand Worvil Forkbeard, also known as the Weevil. [ch 038 / 038.02]
- Orsik and Vardis called him "Frosty," which he privately resented as disrespectful. He privately judged Unla Key narcissistic and careless of personal safety. [ch 038 / 038.01; ch 038 / entry]
- He explained the Ordning as a caste system created by Annam. He warned that its end meant the giants would destroy each other and everyone in between. [ch 038 / 038.01]
- He reminded the party of the quid pro quo for their access to the teleportation circles, and "watches them slump." [ch 038 / 038.01]
- When Unla Key asked whether Cloud Giants were more powerful than him, he brought his ax next to Unla Key. Unla Key was not fazed. [ch 038 / 038.02; ch 038 / moment]
- He mocked the idea that biology determines morality. He named Count Thullen, younger brother of Countess Sansuri, as a good cloud giant. [ch 038 / 038.02]
- He guided the party through Longsaddle, spat at the mention of the Lords' Alliance, and warned that the Harpells live nearby at Ivy Mansion. [ch 038 / 038.03]
- He begged the group to bring him a pot of soup from the Gilded Horseshoe. [ch 038 / 038.03]
- On the way to Mirabar he lectured the party on the city. When Unla Key needled him about making snow, he slammed his battleaxe inches from the halfling. [ch 039 / 039.03]
- He explained that between giant and dragon there can be "Only ceasefires." He blamed Tiamat's bid for power for the sundering of the Ordning. [ch 039 / 039.03]
- Fire Giants under Duke Zalto searching for an artifact, as the 3HPP had told him. [ch 039 / 039.09]
- He urged the party to go to the Eye of the All-Father at once. He was stunned when they chose the Mines of Mirabar first, and invoked Krowen Valharrow's alliance. [ch 040 / 040.01]
- After consulting his map, he agreed to Vardis's route. [ch 040 / 040.01]
- He recognised the location marked on a map recovered from a corpse in the valley, and said it would not add much time. [ch 040 / 040.06]
- At a village destroyed by giants, he told the party three times to leave. When they refused, he said "I will not help you anymore" and sat down to pray. [ch 041 / 041.01; ch 041 / 041.02]
- As two frost giants approached, he refused to defend the party. He left the village and prayed to the All-Father, ignoring Orsik's scream for help. [ch 041 / 041.03; ch 041 / 041.04]
- He treated the battle as a test. On hearing the party prevail, he concluded "They are the heroes." [ch 041 / 041.04; ch 041 / entry]
- In ruined Prettyview he told Orsik he had grown since Waterdeep, while recalling "that Thing" Orsik did as a guard. [ch 042 / 042.02]
- In the barn he argued with Orsik for going straight to the Oracle instead of following the treasure map. He mumbled "The All-Father sent a sign." [ch 042 / 042.06]
- He shouted the avalanche warning, escaped being buried, and dug the party out with a hearty laugh and a mock apology. [ch 042 / 042.07]
- He was grouchy on the trek while Orsik followed the treasure map. [ch 043 / 043.01; ch 043 / entry]
- At the causeway he explained the six giant bas-reliefs, with disdain for the cloud and fire giants. He was confused by the fresh human tracks Thistle asked about. [ch 044 / 044.01]
- He joined the fight against the Uthgardt barbarians and their white dragon, furious at a dragon in the holy place. He identified the dead as members of the Great Worm tribe. [ch 044 / 044.02; ch 044 / 044.03]
- He opened the doors of the Temple of Annam and lectured on Ostoria. He called Vardis "A creationist!" [ch 044 / 044.03]
- When the Storm of Vengeance killed the whole party, he carried Orsik, Thistle, Unla Key and Vardis out in his satchel. He shoved a healing potion into Vardis' mouth. [ch 044 / 044.04]
- He asked why the giants' weapons were removable, which led Orsik and Vardis to the solution. [ch 044 / 044.05; ch 044 / moment]
- He tested the hill rune himself, suffering confusion, and then the frost rune, suffering an ice storm. He noted that the frost giant's weapon was missing. [ch 044 / 044.05]
- He lay down on the furs in the giant sleeping halls while the party went into the spider tunnel. [ch 044 / 044.06]
- Harshnag the Grim was found asleep on a bed in the main temple, covered in rags and snoring loudly, when the party brought Noori out of the warrens. [ch 045 / 045.02]
- Orsik poked him awake. Orsik and Vardis then mocked his sleeping arrangements and called him homeless, and he answered angrily. [ch 045 / 045.05]
- He was caught in the granite-ball trap in the bedroom hallway with the party. He smashed at a stone wall without breaking through and was hurt by the ball. [ch 045 / 045.06; ch 045 / entry]
- He proposed another nap soon after waking, which led the party to ask whether he was old. He denied it. [ch 045 / 045.06]
- The chapter states that he was in fact old. [ch 045 / entry; ch 045 / 045.07]
- On the 6th day he woke in a giant-sized bed and urged the group to "find this giant axe." [ch 045 / 045.07; ch 045 / entry]
- He lifted the levers in both secret lever rooms, melting the ice off the first lever with Thistle's torch, and each lever opened a portcullis. [ch 046 / 046.01; ch 046 / 046.02]
- When the remorhaz appeared, he proposed taking the Ax of Thrym while the party held the creature off, and then running away. Orsik refused. [ch 046 / 046.03]
- He then charged the remorhaz, and his attacks cracked its carapace while it was held in binding ice. [ch 046 / 046.03]
- After the fight he claimed he had believed in the party all along. Vardis called this a "Ret-con." [ch 046 / 046.04]
- Orsik planned for Harshnag to take the Ax of Thrym and strike the rune alone. Harshnag nodded, and Unla Key followed him. [ch 046 / 046.05]
- In the Eye of Annam he whispered to Unla Key how to address the cloud giant ghost. [ch 046 / 046.06; ch 046 / entry]
- He berated the party for spending Oracle questions on trivial matters. [ch 046 / 046.07]
- Standing in the circle, he accidentally used one of the Oracle's questions himself. [ch 046 / 046.07; ch 046 / moment]
- He spent an hour drawing a crude map of the nine spirit mounds. He said he did not know which relic lay at each mound or which giant lord was tied to each relic. [ch 046 / 046.08]
- Orsik: "Just when I finally need Harshnag." [ch 051 / 051.08]
- His map was later used to choose the party's targets: Vardis studied it on the ship. [ch 052 / 052.09]
- Orsik suggested giving him the gong if it turned out to be a shield. [ch 053 / 053.06]
- When the party returned with the castle, he was waiting in the main temple of Annam and asked about Blagothkus. The party gave non-committal answers. [ch 058 / 058.07]
- He touched the rune on the portal with Thrym's great axe and walked into the Eye. [ch 058 / 058.07]
- When Adimond Kroskas appeared and became an Ancient Blue Dragon, Harshnag told the party to go and save the realms. [ch 058 / 058.08; ch 058 / entry]
- When they hesitated, he struck the statue of Annam the All-Father and brought the mountain down. [ch 058 / 058.08; ch 058 / entry]
- The dragon attacked Harshnag, and the hallway and temple collapsed behind the fleeing party. [ch 058 / 058.08]

- He was not present in chapter 059. The giant in the control room said Harshnag brings people to the Oracle, hides items in the temple to test little people, and that "no one has been there in a thousand years" is "a standing joke." [ch 059 / entry; ch 059 / 059.07]
- Orsik told Blagothkus that Harshnag "died when the Oracle fell on his head." This is Orsik's statement and not a narrated event. [ch 073 / 073.06]
