## Identity
Mama Manyknuckles, also known as Mama Mia, was a hag who led a band of half-ogres in the swamp [ch 009 / entry; ch 010 / entry]. She commanded a faction in a civil war between mud mephits, rivaling the grabby water monster for loyalty [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.03]. Feetor described her as "the particularly ugly leader of a band of half-ogres" [ch 009 / entry]. She resided on a kidney-shaped island in the marsh, where her "bedroom" was actually the sunken roof of the Wizard of the Crag's tower [ch 009 / 009.03; ch 010 / entry; ch 011 / 011.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Mama Manyknuckles operated from a position of defensive superiority, hoping intruders would leave if she did not immediately attack [ch 010 / 010.06]. However, she was prepared to ambush threats, signaling her guards and half-ogres to "quickly get into position for an ambush" when detecting flanking maneuvers [ch 010 / 010.06]. Her loyalty was not personal but contractual or situational; her final thoughts blamed her employer, Bad Fruul, stating, "Ever since my gang signed up with you, I knew this was inevitable" [ch 010 / 010.06; ch 010 / entry]. She held contempt for the mud mephits under her command, calling them "Damn annoying creatures" when they failed to report intruders [ch 010 / 010.06]. She possessed a "death gaze" capable of killing those who did not look away in time [ch 010 / moment; ch 010 / 010.06].

## History with the Party
*   Mama Manyknuckles (also known as Mama Mia) was described by Feetor to the party as "the particularly ugly leader of a band of half-ogres" who commanded the loyalty of some mud mephits in a civil war alongside the grabby water monster [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.03].
*   Feetor suggested the party defeat Mama Manyknuckles to become "King of the swamp," noting she lived deeper in the marsh on a kidney-shaped island [ch 009 / 009.03; ch 009 / 009.04].
*   The party identified the northern route toward the sinkhole as likely where Mama Manyknuckles lived [ch 010 / 010.04; ch 010 / 010.05].
*   During a raid on the northern camp, a half-ogre screamed "Mama Mia!" before being killed by the party [ch 010 / 010.05].
*   Mama Manyknuckles was a hag who commanded half-ogres, guards, and mud mephits from the sinkhole [ch 010 / entry].
*   She ambushed the party in the sinkhole, striking Orsik down with her death gaze, but the party overwhelmed her forces, including her ogres and her "beloved Belg" [ch 010 / 010.06; ch 010 / moment].
*   Mama Manyknuckles died while attempting to flee, blaming her employer Bad Fruul in her final thoughts [ch 010 / 010.06; ch 010 / entry].
*   On her body, the party found a stone pendant with a jade inlay of Stein, the stone rune [ch 010 / 010.08].
*   Thistle determined that Mama Mia's pendant served as the key to open the stone table, which was actually her bed and the entrance to the hidden staircase in the sunken wizard's tower [ch 011 / 011.01; ch 011 / entry].
*   In Chapter 021, Bad Fruul deployed half-ogres described as being "from Mama Mia" to attack Parnast, and later stated that "Mama Mia’s ogres have breached the wall" [ch 021 / 021.09].

## Last Observed State
Mama Manyknuckles died in Chapter 010. During an ambush at her sinkhole stronghold, her death gaze struck Orsik down, but the party overwhelmed her ogres and her "beloved Belg" [ch 010 / moment; ch 010 / 010.06]. She attempted to flee but failed and died while blaming Bad Fruul [ch 010 / 010.06; ch 010 / entry]. Her body was looted for a stone pendant with a jade inlay of the Stein rune, which later served as the key to the wizard's tower [ch 010 / 010.08; ch 011 / 011.01]. In Chapter 021, Bad Fruul referred to "Mama Mia’s ogres" and "half-ogres from Mama Mia" attacking Parnast, indicating her forces survived her death but the NPC herself did not return [ch 021 / 021.09].

*   **Bad Fruul**: Mama Manyknuckles worked for Bad Fruul, though she viewed the partnership as doomed, expressing regret in her final moments [ch 010 / 010.06; ch 010 / entry].

*   **Mud Mephits**: She fought a civil war against the grabby water monster for their loyalty, though they were unreliable and failed to warn her of the party's approach [ch 009 / entry; ch 010 / 010.06].
