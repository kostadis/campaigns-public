## Identity
Wallace is a young local resident of Parnast who serves as the town's guard captain and travels with Orsik's group [ch 009 / entry; ch 023 / entry]. He is identified as the childhood friend of Eryn, a local who tends horses, and has been known since birth by Chandra Stol, who loves him like a son [ch 017 / entry; ch 022 / entry]. He is the son of a local father who is sometimes suspected by Orsik of colluding with the Lords' Alliance, though Wallace dismisses these accusations [ch 017 / 017.04]. He functions as Orsik’s protégé, explicitly referring to Orsik as his mentor [ch 023 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Wallace resents being treated as a boy and is eager to prove his competence, often internally resolving to "step up my game" after combat failures [ch 009 / entry; ch 010 / 010.05]. He possesses a pragmatic, cautious streak that frequently checks Orsik's impulses, warning against offending powerful factions like the SEER, the Zhentarim, or the Lords' Alliance [ch 010 / 010.08; ch 012 / 012.01; ch 013 / 013.06]. He values stability and institutional support, arguing that the town requires the backing of the Lords' Alliance via the SEER [ch 023 / 023.04]. He is physically capable, using a freaky ability to understand distances within a 60-foot radius to plan his charges, and is willing to use brute strength in combat, such as punching an ogre to death [ch 016 / 016.01; ch 018 / 018.03; ch 022 / 022.02; ch 022 / 022.09]. He is romantically interested in Elspeth, a woman in Parnast, which eventually drives his decision to resign from his official duties [ch 013 / 013.06; ch 023 / 023.07].

## Last Observed State
As of Chapter 023, Wallace was present in Parnast for the celebration following the ritual that granted Orsik the headband of intellect. During this event, he formally announced his resignation from the position of guard captain to Orsik, stating, "Elspeth and I decided to hook up, and the town needs a new guard captain," which Orsik approved with a grunt [ch 023 / 023.07]. In Chapter 057, Unla Key listed Wallace and his father among the party's current allies while preparing to land on the Cloud Giant Castle [ch 057 / 057.01]. In Chapter 064, Wallace was not physically present; Orsik considered calling on the Parnast guards for aid but refrained, remembering Wallace’s departure and stating, "Let the folks in Parnast be in peace" [ch 064 / 064.02]. Status established: Active in Parnast as of Chapter 023, no longer guard captain.
