## Identity
Kurglow is the orc chieftain of the Spine Ripper Clan [ch 022 / entry; ch 022 / 022.02]. He is the father of one of the orc whelps captured by the party and brought to Parnast [ch 021 / 021.04; ch 022 / 022.02]. He is described by an orc guard as "a terrible husband, but a good father" [ch 021 / 021.04]. He wears a blue sash, distinguishing him from the other orc chieftain who wears a green sash [ch 022 / 022.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Kurglow’s primary motivation is the safety of his child. He is willing to abandon his alliance with the giant Bad Fruul to ensure his son's return [ch 021 / 021.04]. He reacts with fury to insults regarding his parenting skills, particularly suggestions that his child is soft or pampered; threats involving nursery punishments like missing dessert or having to eat vegetables do not intimidate him but rather provoke anger [ch 022 / 022.02; ch 022 / 022.03]. He is capable of ruthless abandonment; he cut ties with Naz, the female caregiver who protected his whelp, because she remained alive while his son was a prisoner and she prioritized her own child’s safety over his [ch 022 / 022.04].

## History with the Party
- Prisoner children reported that Kurglow threatened to kill them all if their fathers, Jarlor and Larrish, did not spy on him [ch 020 / 020.07].
- One of the captured orc whelps is the son of Kurglow; the orc guard protecting the whelps stated that Kurglow would be willing to abandon Bad Fruul to save his child [ch 021 / 021.04].
- The party sent a message to Kurglow through the spy channel using Jarlor and Larrish, delivering a threat that the whelp would be sent to bed without dessert and denied a bedtime story [ch 021 / 021.05].
- During the battle, Orsik threatened Kurglow that his whelp would be forced to eat all his vegetables; Kurglow was unimpressed by this threat [ch 022 / 022.02].
- Kurglow charged Orsik in fury, angry at the threats to his son and the insults to his fathering, allowing Chandra, Vardis, Wallace, and Unla Key to attack him as he passed [ch 022 / 022.03].
- Kurglow demanded the return of his son in exchange for his withdrawal; after the female caregiver Naz screamed at him, he abandoned her and left [ch 022 / 022.04].
- Bad Fruul howled in anger at Kurglow's betrayal and prepared his final assault with ettins and orcs [ch 022 / 022.05].

## Last Observed State
In Chapter 022, Kurglow is observed at the battle near Parnast. After his ally, the green-sashed chieftain, was killed by Orsik, Kurglow demanded the return of his son in exchange for his withdrawal from the battle [ch 022 / 022.04]. The party agreed; Orsik had a villager return the child, and Kurglow confirmed he would leave [ch 022 / 022.04]. Kurglow then verbally attacked Naz, accusing her of caring only for her own child, and stated, "‘Unlike you, I can take my whelp and leave you to die.’" before walking away [ch 022 / 022.04].

Following his departure, Bad Fruul howled in anger at Kurglow’s betrayal and prepared his final assault [ch 022 / 022.05]. The evidence does not state Kurglow's physical condition after the party attacked him as he passed them, nor does it confirm his survival beyond that moment. Status not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
*   **His Son (Whelp):** Kurglow prioritizes his son’s safety above all else, including his clan’s alliance [ch 021 / 021.04; ch 022 / 022.03]. He became enraged when threats implied his son was weak or overly civilized [ch 022 / 022.03].
*   **Naz:** Kurglow holds Naz in contempt for surviving as a caregiver while his son was held hostage. He accused her of neglecting his son’s needs to protect her own and abandoned her to die with Bad Fruul [ch 022 / 022.04]. Naz vowed revenge against him [ch 022 / 022.06].
*   **Bad Fruul:** Kurglow betrayed Bad Fruul by withdrawing his support to save his son, causing the giant to attack Parnast with reduced but still significant forces [ch 022 / 022.05].
*   **The Green-Sashed Chieftain:** This orc was Kurglow’s ally during the battle. His death by Orsik triggered Kurglow’s desperate charge and subsequent bargain for his son [ch 022 / 022.03; ch 022 / 022.04].
*   **Jarlor and Larrish:** Kurglow held their families hostage to force them into spying for him [ch 020 / 020.07].
