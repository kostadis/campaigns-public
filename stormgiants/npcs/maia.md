## Identity
Maia is a tiefling server at the Wink and Kiss tavern in Yartar [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.04]. She introduces herself as "Maia, short for Qumaia" [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.04]. She first appears as a "tiefling barhop" when the broke party visits the Wink and Kiss [ch 030 / 030.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Maia is professionally aware of her clientele, recognizing the party as the group that defeated a giant in Triboar and noting that their play has been advertised [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.04]. She takes pride in the local lore, correcting Orsik’s claim that they defeated "Two" giants to specify "two fire giants" [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.04].

She acts as a facilitator for information and introductions within the tavern's network, selling an introduction to a contact for a fee [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.06]. When the party is broke, she offers to introduce them to "a gal" [ch 030 / 030.04]. On a later visit, she explicitly sells "the information you need to get the information you need" for five gold, directing them to Eltio [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.06].

Maia appears enthusiastic about the party's accomplishments. When Orsik displays the Letter of Mark, she attempts to celebrate with a high-five [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.08; ch 032 / 032.09]. She is also noted as being "happy to see" the party upon their return [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.09].

## Relationships
*   **Eltio:** Maia has a working relationship with Eltio, a member of the Hand of Yartar. She serves as a broker, selling introductions to Eltio for a fee [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.06]. In chapter 032, she seats the party next to Eltio [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.08; ch 032 / 032.09]. Eltio once scolded Maia and sent her away when she was involved in a conversation about fees [ch 030 / 030.06].
