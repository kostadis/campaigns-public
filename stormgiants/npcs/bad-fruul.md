## Identity
Bad Fruul is a hill giant who established himself as a local power base in the region surrounding Parnast [ch 019 / entry]. He wears bone-festooned plate armor and rides a mammoth into battle [ch 021 / 021.08]. He possesses a headband of intellect that contains the trapped soul of the priestess Narcellia; her advice allowed him to overcome the typical limitations of his kind, enabling him to cast spells like *hold person* and devise complex military strategies [ch 019 / 019.05; ch 021 / moment]. He is widely viewed as the nemesis of Chandra Stol’s family [ch 022 / 022.05; ch 023 / 023.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Bad Fruul is driven by a desire to rule and destroy Parnast, a goal instigated by the priestess Narcellia within his headband, who urged him to unite monstrous humanoids under his banner [ch 019 / 019.05]. He is sensitive to being underestimated due to his species' reputation for lack of intelligence; internally, he seethes at the thought that "They think Bad Fruul is dumb!" [ch 021 / moment]. Despite this self-awareness, his execution often lacks nuance: he uses goblins as expendable probes and, when his *hold person* spell fails against Thistle, he bangs his head and shouts, "That didn’t work! That didn’t work!" [ch 021 / 021.09; ch 022 / 022.07]. He harbors a specific hatred for flying creatures, screaming "I hate fairies that fly!" before smashing through houses [ch 022 / moment]. He is prone to violent outbursts when betrayed or frustrated, howling in anger upon learning of Kurglow’s betrayal [ch 022 / 022.05].

- Ragnar warns Vardis that an attack by Bad Fruul’s army is imminent due to monsters moving and destroying resources near Parnast [ch 017 / 017.03].

- Freed prisoners identify Bad Fruul as the one who sent Kragota to destroy their commune, though they do not know Bad Fruul personally ("Bad who?") [ch 018 / 018.04].
- The SEER reveals that Bad Fruul is a hill giant who owns the headband of intellect containing the priestess Narcellia, and that Narcellia has urged him to draw monstrous humanoids to his banner to destroy Parnast [ch 019 / 019.05; ch 019 / entry].
- Vardis suggests assassinating Bad Fruul as a group action, a plan Unla Key supports [ch 019 / 019.06].

- Orsik warns Chandra that while Bad Fruul might not come personally, he has minions who will do his "evil work," including sending someone to kill her [ch 020 / 020.01].

- Bad Fruul arrives personally on a mammoth, wearing bone-festooned plate armor, and demands Chandra and the statue of Angharradh [ch 021 / 021.08].
- During the battle, Bad Fruul uses goblins as expendable probes, internally monologuing about being underestimated despite his intelligence [ch 021 / 021.09; ch 021 / entry].
- Bad Fruul led the final assault on his mammoth, accompanied by ettins and orcs, preparing to attack Parnast [ch 022 / 022.05; ch 022 / entry].
- Upon learning of Kurglow's betrayal, Bad Fruul howled in anger [ch 022 / 022.05; ch 022 / entry].
- Bad Fruul advanced on the center of Parnast, smashing through houses and casting *hold person* on Thistle, who resisted the spell [ch 022 / 022.07; ch 022 / entry].
- When Thistle resisted his spell, Bad Fruul banged his head and shouted, "That didn’t work! That didn’t work!" [ch 022 / 022.07; ch 022 / entry].

- The two sprites claimed that their poisoned arrows brought down Bad Fruul, though Orsik thanked them graciously while Vardis joked about the sprites being preferable to the wyvern [ch 023 / 023.01].
- After his death, Bad Fruul’s body remained on the field; Orsik attempted to steal the headband holding an evil soul from his corpse but was caught by Thistle [ch 023 / 023.03].

## Last Observed State
Bad Fruul is dead [ch 023 / 023.01; ch 023 / entry]. He was killed during the Battle of Parnast; Chandra Stol described the sound of swords striking his body as "a satisfying thud" [ch 023 / 023.01]. His body remained on the battlefield after the combat ended [ch 023 / 023.03]. During the battle, he had been struck by a released wyvern and attacked by Orsik and the party, who had also utilized sprites and a wyvern to turn the tide against him [ch 022 / 022.12; ch 023 / 023.01].

*   **Narcellia:** The priestess whose soul is trapped in Bad Fruul's headband; she is the architect of his campaign against Parnast [ch 019 / 019.05].
*   **Chandra Stol:** Views Bad Fruul as a nemesis and expresses satisfaction at his death [ch 022 / 022.05; ch 023 / 023.01].
*   **Mama Manyknuckles:** Her gang initially signed up with Bad Fruul, a move she regretted when his power became a liability [ch 010 / 010.06; ch 010 / entry].
*   **Gralm (Ettin):** Bad Fruul attempted to coerce Gralm into joining his army; Gralm viewed Bad Fruul as an enemy and allied with the party against him [ch 014 / 014.01; ch 015 / 015.04].
*   **Kurglow:** An orc chieftain who abandoned Bad Fruul to save his child, causing the giant to howl in rage [ch 021 / 021.04; ch 022 / 022.05].
*   **Chief Grik:** A bugbear chief who relayed Bad Fruul's orders to defend the pass and seize Parnast [ch 013 / 013.04].
*   **The Party:** Targeted by Bad Fruul’s minions and eventually killed him in direct combat. Orsik attempted to steal Bad Fruul’s headband from his corpse immediately after his death [ch 020 / 020.01; ch 023 / 023.03].
