## Identity
Noori is a female human attired in the garb of an Uthgardt tribe member [ch 045 / entry; ch 045 / 045.02]. She belongs to the same tribe as the chieftain that the party had previously killed [ch 045 / entry; ch 045 / 045.02]. She identified herself only as "Noori" when introduced to Orsik [ch 045 / entry; ch 045 / 045.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Noori is driven by the immediate desire to escape her location and locate her tribe [ch 045 / entry; ch 045 / 045.02]. She displays intense hostility or hatred toward Unla Key, staring at her with great hatred during their encounter [ch 045 / entry; ch 045 / 045.02]. Noori is credulous regarding Orsik's account of her tribespeople's fate, failing to see through his deception that they died trying to enter the temple rather than being killed by the party [ch 045 / 045.02].

## History with the Party

## Last Observed State
Noori was found alive in the third cocoon in the warrens [ch 045 / entry; ch 045 / 045.02]. After being transported by the party from the warrens back to the main temple, she was warned by Orsik to be quiet due to the presence of Harshnag the Grim [ch 045 / 045.02]. Following this warning, Noori smiled at Orsik and ran off [ch 045 / 045.02]. Status not established in the summaries.
