## Identity
Othovir is the harness maker and owner of the harness store in Triboar [ch 024 / entry; ch 025 / entry; ch 027 / entry; ch 027 / 027.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Othovir is nervous and grudging when interacting with the party [ch 027 / entry]. He is motivated by a deep hatred for the Margaster family, explicitly stating, "I hate the Margasters. We have a history. And that's all I will share." [ch 027 / 027.13]. Despite having no money of his own to offer, he is willing to provide intelligence to the party because he believes they "did me a favor," suggesting a debt of gratitude or a desire to see the Margasters harmed [ch 027 / 027.13].

## History with the Party
- Othovir is Triboar's harness maker and harness store owner [ch 024 / entry; ch 025 / entry; ch 027 / entry; ch 027 / 027.06].
- He identified a leather harness found at the Lionshield Coster outpost as likely made in Waterdeep, about a decade old, and fitting a person of about 150 pounds, sending the party to Harriet for the grappling hook [ch 024 / 024.11; ch 025 / 025.06].
- Othovir was among the townsfolk discussing the recent fire damage and losses in the market square with Urgala and Narth Tezrin [ch 027 / 027.05; ch 027 / 027.06].
- Nervous and grudging, Othovir approached the party to share information about the Margasters, stating he hates them and "We have a history," and provided details on their emergency magic stash in Silverymoon [ch 027 / entry; ch 027 / 027.13].
- He directed the party to the Margasters' detached carriage house in Silverymoon, specifying the location of the magic items within a compartment under the passenger seat, alongside warnings about an arcane lock, guards, and an alarm spell [ch 027 / 027.13].
- Unla Key came to understand the tension between the Margasters and Othovir while observing Imber Chen's comments in Silverymoon [ch 028 / 028.05].
- Urlam suspected that Othovir had told the party how to hurt House Margaster, noting that Othovir has a relationship with the Margasters [ch 037 / 037.02].
