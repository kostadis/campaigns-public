## Identity
Iymrith is an ancient blue dragon known by the aliases Adimond Kroskas and the Doom of the Desert [ch 069 / 069.04; ch 070 / 070.06; ch 081 / entry]. She disguises herself as a human male named Adimond Kroskas to infiltrate the Storm Giant court, posing as an alchemist and advisor to Queen Serissa [ch 067 / 067.04; ch 069 / 069.04]. She is identified as a leader of the Cult of the Dragon and is credited with creating drakes via hybridization experiments [ch 069 / 069.04; ch 069 / 069.07]. Her true form is that of a massive blue dragon, though she has also been observed in the form of a storm giant [ch 069 / 069.03].

## Personality and Motivations
Iymrith is a manipulative scientist and artist who views herself as a creator rather than a slave keeper or villain [ch 084 / 084.03; ch 084 / 084.05]. Her primary motivation is conducting hybridization experiments to combine the innocence of fairies with the power of dragons, claiming Queen Titania’s approval for experiments on Seelie subjects [ch 067 / 067.04; ch 084 / 084.04]. To fund these expensive projects, she refuses to spend her own hoard and instead cultivates relationships with entities such as Szass Tam, the Red Wizards, the Seelie and Unseelie Courts, and giant lords, often deceiving them [ch 084 / 084.04; ch 084 / 084.07].

She displays a volatile and dramatic temperament, becoming horrified by the financial implications of using her hoard and frustrated by the "drama" of her failures, such as her inability to transform the fairy Laela into a dragon [ch 084 / 084.05; ch 084 / 084.06; ch 084 / 084.07]. While she claims to be "just an alchemist," she is politically ambitious, having attempted to recruit Hekaton and manipulated Serissa by offering hybridization weapons to retain storm giant power [ch 069 / 069.06; ch 081 / 081.04]. She is capable of extreme violence when her cover is blown or her interests are threatened, utilizing dragon fear and physical transformation to combat the party [ch 058 / 058.08; ch 069 / 069.03].

## Relationships
**The Party (3HPP):** Iymrith has a complex and adversarial relationship with the party. She disguises herself as Adimond Kroskas to manipulate Thistle, giving him "dragon eyes" and True Seeing [ch 067 / 067.04; ch 070 / 070.01]. She engages in direct combat with them, attacking Harshnag and Orsik before escaping [ch 058 / 058.08; ch 069 / 069.03]. In later chapters, she attempts to negotiate, inviting them to a festival and proposing they fight her captive, Laela, in exchange for information and access to her lair [ch 084 / 084.03; ch 084 / 084.06]. Orsik views her as a dragon that must be killed, while Thistle acknowledges her manipulative utility despite his personal distress over her gifts [ch 081 / 081.03; ch 083 / 083.01].

**Laela:** Laela is Iymrith’s "girlfriend" and captive, held in Iymrith’s lair [ch 069 / 069.06; ch 084 / 084.05]. Iymrith uses Laela as the centerpiece of her hybridization experiment, admitting failure in transforming her into a dragon due to Laela’s "fey-fullness" [ch 084 / 084.05].

**Queen Serissa and Imperator Uthor:** Iymrith infiltrated the Storm Giant court, advising Queen Serissa on hybridization experiments to maintain power against other giants [ch 067 / 067.04; ch 069 / 069.06]. Serissa and Uthor are deceived by Iymrith’s disguise as Adimond Kroskas until the party reveals her true nature [ch 069 / 069.04; ch 069 / 069.05].

**King Hekaton:** Hekaton identifies Iymrith as the dragon who attempted to steal his kingdom and kidnapped him, expressing a willingness to join the party in a final battle against her [ch 081 / 081.04; ch 082 / 082.05].

**Investors and Allies:** Iymrith maintains precarious alliances with Szass Tam, Red Wizards, and both Seelie and Unseelie Courts, admitting she deceives giant lords and entities like the SEER to secure funding [ch 084 / 084.03; ch 084 / 084.04; ch 084 / 084.07]. The demon Bob seeks to disgrace her by exposing her fraud at her festival [ch 085 / 085.07].
