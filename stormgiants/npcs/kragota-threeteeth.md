## Identity
**Kragota Threeteeth**, also known as **Mother of Bones**, was an ogre who commanded a warband of goblins and orcs stationed at a cabin near Parnast [ch 018 / entry]. She is identified in the text as the specific ogre engaged in combat by the party [ch 018 / entry]. She had been sent by Bad Fruul to destroy a local commune [ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.04]. Additionally, agents and farmers associated with Kragota were responsible for revealing the new statue of Angharradh in Parnast [ch 019 / 019.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Kragota displayed impatience and disdain for her subordinates, frequently screaming orders such as "Crush the Puny creatures!" and complaining, "Do I have to do everything?" [ch 018 / 018.03; ch 018 / moment]. She possessed arcane capability, surprising the party by casting a *spiritual weapon* in the form of a hammer during combat [ch 018 / 018.03]. Her actions were motivated by orders from Bad Fruul to destroy a specific commune, resulting in the death of several of its members [ch 018 / 018.04]. She held a grudge against fey creatures, referencing a failed agreement with Prince Thornacious during her final moments [ch 018 / 018.03; ch 018 / moment].

## History with the Party
- The party received a note supposedly from Kragota Threeteeth, known as the Mother of Bones, which prompted their travel to the cabin [ch 018 / 018.01].
- Vardis expressed skepticism regarding the note, whispering to Orsik that he suspected a hostage scam orchestrated by Kragota [ch 018 / 018.01; ch 018 / moment].
- At the cabin, Kragota commanded her goblin and orc troops, screaming orders such as "Crush the Puny creatures!" and complaining, "Do I have to do everything?" [ch 018 / 018.03].
- Kragota cast a spiritual weapon in the form of a hammer, using it to strike Unla Key and later attacking Wallace and Orsik during the battle [ch 018 / 018.03].
- When brought to 1 HP by Thistle, Kragota screamed, "Fairies! We had a deal with Prince Thornacious!" before being killed by Wallace [ch 018 / 018.03].
- The freed prisoners confirmed that Kragota had been sent by Bad Fruul to destroy their commune, resulting in the death of several members [ch 018 / 018.04].
- One of the prisoners stated that the half-elf matching Tove's description had been "thick as thieves" with Kragota [ch 018 / 018.04].
- Agents and farmers associated with Kragota were responsible for revealing the new statue of Angharradh in Parnast [ch 019 / 019.05].

## Last Observed State
Kragota was killed in Chapter 018. During the battle at the cabin, she commanded troops and engaged the party, casting a *spiritual weapon* hammer to strike Unla Key, Wallace, and Orsik [ch 018 / 018.03]. Thistle reduced Kragota to 1 hit point, at which point she screamed, "Fairies! We had a deal with Prince Thornacious!" before Wallace punched her, killing her [ch 018 / 018.03; ch 018 / entry].
