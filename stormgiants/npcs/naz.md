## Identity
Naz is a woman in Parnast who acts as a caregiver for Kurglow’s captive son [ch 022 / 022.04; ch 022 / entry]. She is identified by the militiaman as Kurglow's female caregiver [ch 022 / 022.04; ch 022 / 022.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Naz prioritizes the safety of Kurglow’s child, insisting during a quarrel that she had kept the boy safe despite Kurglow’s accusations that she cared only for her own child [ch 022 / 022.04; ch 022 / entry]. Her motivation shifts decisively from grief to vengeance after Kurglow abandons her in the town [ch 022 / 022.04]. She declares a specific vow to exact revenge on Kurglow, contingent upon the defeat of Bad Fruul and his forces [ch 022 / 022.06; ch 022 / entry].

## History with the Party
*   Naz was involved in a quarrel with Kurglow regarding the safety of his captive son; Naz insisted she had kept the child safe, while Kurglow accused her of prioritizing her own child [ch 022 / 022.04].
*   Following the quarrel, Kurglow abandoned Naz in Parnast with the words, "Unlike you, I can take my whelp and leave you to die," leaving her sobbing by the town well [ch 022 / 022.04; ch 022 / entry].
*   After the last of the local militia were overrun by advancing orcs and giants, Naz ceased weeping and stood up [ch 022 / 022.06; ch 022 / entry].
*   Naz declared a vow of revenge against Kurglow contingent upon the death of Bad Fruul and his orcs [ch 022 / 022.06; ch 022 / entry].
*   Naz participated in the battle against the mammoth summoned by Bad Fruul by striking it with a guiding bolt [ch 022 / 022.10; ch 022 / 022.11; ch 022 / entry].

## Last Observed State
Naz was observed striking a mammoth summoned by Bad Fruul with a guiding bolt during the battle against the orc and giant forces [ch 022 / 022.10; ch 022 / 022.11; ch 022 / entry]. Prior to this combat, she ceased weeping and stood up after the local militia were overrun [ch 022 / 022.06; ch 022 / entry]. Status not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
**Kurglow**: Naz was in a caretaking relationship with Kurglow’s son. During a confrontation, Kurglow shouted at her to return his son and accused her of neglect; he subsequently abandoned her in Parnast with the words, "Unlike you, I can take my whelp and leave you to die" [ch 022 / 022.04; ch 022 / entry]. Naz views Kurglow as a target for revenge [ch 022 / 022.06; ch 022 / entry].

**Bad Fruul and Orc Forces**: Naz identifies Bad Fruul as "this idiot of a giant" and joins the fight against him and his orcs, though this participation appears motivated by her desire to reach Kurglow for revenge [ch 022 / 022.06; ch 022 / entry].

**Militia and Townspeople**: A militiaman explained Naz's quarrel with Kurglow to others in Parnast [ch 022 / 022.04]. She fought alongside others, including Unla Key and Blasius, against the mammoth, though her interactions with them are not detailed beyond the combat action [ch 022 / 022.10; ch 022 / 022.11].
