## Identity
Gen is a kobold with red scales marked with burns and clothing that smells strong [ch 004 / 004.04]. He is the companion of another kobold, Agra, and was hiding with him inside a stone building during the events of Chapter 004 [ch 004 / 004.04]. Gen is noted by Agra for having heard rumors that their current location was cursed [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Gen demonstrates a pragmatic survival instinct, prioritizing immediate physical safety over pride or combat [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 004 / 004.05]. When the group's ambush on the adventurers failed, Gen expressed despair but subsequently proposed offering treasure to save himself and Agra from becoming shields for the halfling [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 004 / 004.05]. He appears inclined to rely on Agra’s leadership, having been physically stopped by Agra from acting prematurely [ch 004 / 004.04]. Gen is susceptible to superstitious information, as his knowledge of the site being cursed directly influenced their decision to hide [ch 004 / 004.05].

## Last Observed State
In Chapter 004, Gen was alive and hiding with Agra inside a stone building [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 004 / 004.05]. Following a failed ambush by their tribe and the casting of sleep spells, Gen asked, "what will we ever do?" before Agra surrendered [ch 004 / 004.04]. When a surviving halfling suggested using kobolds as shields, Gen intervened by proposing a bribe: "perhaps if we offered some treasure?" [ch 004 / 004.05]. A dwarf accepted the treasure and dismissed them with the command to leave without looting [ch 004 / 004.05]. Gen's status after this exchange is not established in the summaries; while he was released, there is no evidence confirming whether he escaped or remained within the dungeon [ch 004 / 004.05].

## Relationships
**Agra**: Gen is in constant proximity to Agra, who acts as the de facto leader of their pair [ch 004 / 004.04]. Agra has previously silenced Gen physically to prevent reckless action [ch 004 / 004.04]. While Agra is verbose and prone to panicked bargaining, Gen provided the crucial suggestion that secured their life when Agra was paralyzed by the threat of being used as a shield [ch 004 / 004.05].

**Nug**: Nug is identified as the leader of the kobold tribe to which Gen belongs [ch 004 / 004.05]. Gen does not interact with Nug directly in the provided evidence, but Nug's actions (leading the tribe into the southern room) contributed to the situation that forced Gen and Agra to hide [ch 004 / 004.05].
