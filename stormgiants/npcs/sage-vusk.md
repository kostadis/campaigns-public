## Identity
Sage Vusk is a "traveling scholar" who gives lectures at the Lady’s College in Silverymoon. He is not a professor, a distinction he explicitly makes when corrected [ch 048 / 048.06; ch 048 / moment]. He is currently teaching a lecture on the origins and nature of Cambions [ch 048 / 048.06]. He is described by others as a "sweetheart" whom few students attend [ch 048 / 048.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Vusk is pedantic about his professional title, correcting Orsik with the specific assertion, "I am not a professor; I am a traveling scholar" [ch 048 / 048.06; ch 048 / moment]. He holds traditionalist views on fiendish nature, arguing that Cambions lack free will because of their infernal makeup, contrary to "modernist sages" who believe otherwise [ch 048 / 048.06]. He is deeply pragmatic regarding magic in Silverymoon, dismissing any idea of banning it by citing the city’s history with Mystra and the invention of Magical Schools [ch 048 / 048.06]. He is aware of social dangers associated with dispelling magic in public, noting it can lead to duels due to breaches of etiquette [ch 048 / 048.06].

## History with the Party
- Sage Vusk was directed to by Anakis, who stated he was giving a lecture on Cambions. [ch 048 / 048.05]
- Sage Vusk gave a lecture to a nearly empty room on the origins and nature of Cambions. [ch 048 / 048.06]
- Vusk explained that Cambions are the progeny of a union between a demon and a non-demon, are foot soldiers of the Blood War, and lack free will due to their infernal nature. [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.06]
- When asked if Cambions have free will, Vusk noted that while some modernist sages believe they do, he believes their infernal nature makes it impossible. [ch 048 / 048.06]
- Vusk identified that Cambions are disposed of by their masters. [ch 048 / 048.06]
- Vusk warned that Cambions possess a fiendish charm capable of defeating adventuring groups, such as The Noble Daggers. [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.06]
- Vusk suggested that magical darkness, blindness, or invisibility might protect against a Cambion's charm, as some sages believe the charm requires the Cambion to see its enemy. [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.06]
- Vusk corrected Orsik’s address of "Professor," stating he is a traveling scholar. [ch 048 / 048.06]
- Vusk confirmed that Dispel Magic works against Cambion disguises, but warned that casting it in public is considered a grave breach of etiquette and has led to duels. [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.06]
- Vusk laughed off the idea of banning magic in Silverymoon, citing the city's history with Mystra and Magical Schools. [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.06]
- Anakis referred to Sage Vusk as a "sweetheart" to whom nobody goes. [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.05]
