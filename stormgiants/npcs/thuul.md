## Identity
Thuul is a summoned beast, specifically described as a drake, that fights alongside the party [ch 052 / entry; ch 082 / entry]. He possesses a variety of breath weapons and physical capabilities, including acid breath [ch 061 / entry; ch 068 / entry; ch 071 / entry; ch 082 / entry], ice breath comparable to an ice dragon [ch 066 / entry; ch 066 / moment], fire breath (implied by rushing to burn chains) [ch 082 / entry], and a vicious bite [ch 052 / 052.01; ch 075 / entry]. He has a tough drake armor [ch 075 / entry] and can carry heavy objects, such as a dead body, in his jaws [ch 080 / entry]. He was summoned to accompany Thistle, whom he sometimes carries [ch 057 / 057.03] and whom Vardis refers to as "The fairy's creature" [ch 067 / entry]. Thuul is capable of complex maneuvers, such as moving between party members and attacks [ch 052 / 052.07] and utilizing environmental hazards, like stuffing a bag of holding into another [ch 065 / entry]. He was initially located by Hsing’s advice: "where there is the yellow sky, there is the drake you are looking for" [ch 007 / moment].

## Personality and Motivations
Thuul operates in an "anarcho-socialist" dynamic with Thistle, where they take turns being in charge [ch 053 / entry; ch 053 / 053.02]. He exhibits initiative and tactical awareness, such as surprising the party with an unexpected ice breath attack [ch 066 / entry; ch 066 / moment] and executing complex actions during combat [ch 065 / entry; ch 080 / 080.01]. Thuul demonstrates loyalty to Thistle, having mumbled agreement to help him when called [ch 067 / entry], and consistently engages threats to protect or assist the party [ch 052 / 052.10; ch 054 / entry; ch 066 / 066.05]. His actions, such as carrying bodies and engaging enemies directly, suggest a role dedicated to frontline utility and combat support [ch 080 / entry; ch 082 / entry]. UNSUPPORTED: Thuul's internal motivations or feelings are not detailed, as his communication is limited to actions and mumbled agreement [ch 067 / entry].

## Relationships
**Thistle**: Thuul has a close working relationship with Thistle, described as "anarcho-socialist" where they take turns leading [ch 053 / entry; ch 053 / 053.02]. Thuul obeys Thistle's orders [ch 049 / entry; ch 052 / 052.07; ch 068 / entry] and participates in scouting and combat alongside him [ch 052 / entry; ch 052 / 052.10; ch 057 / 057.03]. Thistle has covered for Thuul's actions, such as shrugging off questions about his ice breath [ch 066 / entry].

**Unla Key**: Thuul has interacted with Unla Key in combat, such as moving between them and a Manticore [ch 052 / 052.07] and participating in strikes [ch 052 / 052.10]. However, Thuul was burned by Unla Key's fireball during a battle on a ship, following a nod from Thistle [ch 082 / entry; ch 082 / 082.01].

**Vardis**: Vardis refers to Thuul as "The fairy's creature" [ch 067 / entry] and urged Thuul to help Thistle [ch 067 / entry]. Thuul was present during battles involving Vardis [ch 054 / 054.06; ch 082 / entry].

**Other Party Members**: Thuul was mentioned as present during battles involving Orsik [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / 054.06; ch 080 / 080.01]. He was also affected by dragon fear alongside the party [ch 073 / entry] and was captured with Thistle and Laela [ch 069 / entry].
