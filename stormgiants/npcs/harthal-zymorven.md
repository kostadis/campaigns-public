## Identity
Harthal Zymorven is the heir to Zymorven Hall and son of Lord Zymorven [ch 032 / 032.08; ch 033 / entry]. He was imprisoned in Yartar on charges of murder, with an execution scheduled for the 10th day of the first tenday of Flamerule [ch 032 / 032.09]. During his incarceration, he was held in a richly appointed room where a vampire jailer conducted experiments on prisoners [ch 033 / 033.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Harthal appears naive and eager for adventure, driven by a desire to gain "experiences like dear old dad" [ch 033 / 033.07]. However, his foray into adventuring led to him feeling "used" after his partner, Laela, abandoned him [ch 033 / 033.07]. Consequently, his primary motivation has shifted to returning home to assume his role as the next Lord Zymorven, explicitly stating he never wants to be used by others again [ch 033 / 033.07; ch 033 / entry]. He displays a mix of fear and awe; during a battle with crag cats, he hid behind Vardis and screamed for assistance, later staring in awe at the wizard Unla Key after the threat was neutralized [ch 034 / 034.01]. Despite the trauma of prison and the deception involved in his rescue, he remains enthusiastic about his experience, telling his father, "That was a great bit of adventuring, Dad!" [ch 034 / 034.02].

- The party debated how to persuade Harthal, the heir to Zymorven Hall, to join them; Eltio returned with news that Harthal was imprisoned for murder and scheduled for execution on the 10th [ch 032 / 032.08; ch 032 / 032.09].
- Thistle requested to meet Harthal in a richly appointed prison room; the vampire jailer initially refused to let Thistle enter to avoid contaminating experiments, but allowed him to see Harthal after Orsik (disguised as Lord Zymorven) provided fresh bodies [ch 033 / 033.06].
- Upon appearing, Harthal mistakenly identified Orsik as his father, shouting, "Father, you came for me," and was released by the vampire on the condition that they leave Yartar that night [ch 033 / 033.06].
- Harthal recognized Thistle Wendrod and blamed Thistle's girlfriend, Laela, for his imprisonment, explaining that she convinced him to steal a sword and go adventuring before abandoning him to find "the absolute love of her life" [ch 033 / 033.07].
- Harthal expressed that he felt used by Laela, who had joined the Hand of Yartar to find Thistle but left because the operation was "too local," and he declared his desire to go home and become the next Lord Zymorven [ch 033 / 033.07; ch 033 / entry].
- At Moongleam Tower, Orsik transformed back to his true self just before Harthal could see the deception regarding the Lord Zymorven disguise [ch 033 / 033.10; ch 033 / moment].
- While escorting Harthal to Zymorven Hall, the group was attacked by crag cats; Harthal hid behind Vardis and screamed at Unla Key to intervene, later staring at Unla in awe after the wizard killed the beasts [ch 034 / 034.01].
- Harthal arrived at Zymorven Hall with the 3HPP and was welcomed by his father, who handed Thistle the 1000 gold piece reward [ch 031 / 031.05; ch 034 / 034.02; ch 034 / entry].
- Status: Harthal was escorted home to Zymorven Hall and is currently with his father, Lord Zymorven [ch 034 / 034.02; ch 034 / entry].

## Last Observed State
Harthal is at Zymorven Hall, safely reunited with his father, Lord Zymorven [ch 034 / 034.02; ch 034 / entry]. He arrived with the 3HPP after an escort through the Rauvin Road north of Silverymoon [ch 034 / 034.01; ch 034 / 034.02]. His father paid Thistle the 1,000 gold piece reward, and Lord Zymorven supposed that he himself must avoid visiting Yartar for a while [ch 031 / 031.05; ch 034 / 034.01; ch 034 / 034.02].

## Relationships
*   **Lord Zymorven (Father):** Harthal’s primary relationship is with his father, whom he initially mistook for the disguised Orsik, shouting "Father, you came for me" [ch 033 / 033.06]. Upon returning home, Harthal expresses deep remorse to his father for his misadventures and declares his intent to settle into his inheritance [ch 033 / 033.07].
*   **Laela:** Harthal blames Laela, a rogue and former partner, for his imprisonment. He explains that she convinced him to steal a sword and go adventuring, then abandoned him to find "the absolute love of her life" (Thistle) after he killed someone [ch 033 / 033.07].
*   **Thistle Wendrod:** Harthal recognized Thistle and blamed him indirectly through Laela, stating, "your girlfriend is why I ended up here" [ch 033 / 033.07]. Thistle advised Harthal to stay in his castle to avoid being used by others, which Harthal accepted [ch 033 / 033.07].
*   **The 3HPP (Orsik, Vardis, Unla Key):** Harthal was rescued by the party. He hid behind Vardis during combat and sought protection from Unla Key, whom he respected for his combat prowess [ch 034 / 034.01]. He was rescued from a vampire jailer who traded his freedom for fresh bodies provided by Orsik (disguised as Lord Zymorven) [ch 033 / 033.06].
