## Identity
Senneth is a Cleric of Mielikki and a High Priest associated with Mielikki's Glade in Silverymoon [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.05]. She is known for having raised Unla Key from the dead [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.04]. Senneth acts as a knowledgeable local figure, providing the party with information regarding infernals, local factions, and academic resources within the city [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.04; ch 048 / 048.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Senneth exhibits a pragmatic and somewhat cynical worldview regarding the supernatural presence in the city, dismissing the novelty of devils with the assertion that in a large population, fiendish infiltration is inevitable [ch 048 / 048.04]. She views cult activity as largely suppressed, stating that active cultists fled the city following the defeat of the Cult of the Dragon [ch 048 / 048.04]. While initially dismissive of the party's concerns, she becomes sharply alert and knowledgeable when specific details align with her historical knowledge, identifying Cambions as followers of Lady Margaster and recalling prior conflicts between the party and the Margaster family [ch 048 / 048.04]. She displays suspicion toward the party's inquiries when they involve sensitive topics like devils and demons, questioning the specificity of their questions [ch 048 / 048.04]. Senneth is cooperative in providing academic direction, recommending The Lady’s College for research on Cambions and personally guiding the party through the city, including across an invisible bridge [ch 048 / 048.05].
