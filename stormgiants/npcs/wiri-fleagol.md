## Identity
Wiri Fleagol is a gnome prisoner held in Ironslag [ch 062 / 062.02]. She identifies herself as having been incarcerated "for quite some time" [ch 062 / 062.02]. She possesses detailed knowledge of the upper levels of Ironslag, including the Foundry, the Great Wheel, and the prisoners' sleeping quarters, though she states she was a slave and never visited the lower levels [ch 062 / 062.03; ch 062 / 062.04]. She trades information for money, charging 20 gold for a detailed route description [ch 062 / 062.03].

## Personality and Motivations
Wiri is pragmatic and transactional, offering help only "for a price" [ch 062 / 062.02]. She is highly perceptive, immediately identifying Thistle as a fairy [ch 062 / 062.02], and skeptical, rejecting Thistle’s story about House Margaster as unbelievable [ch 062 / 062.02]. She harbors deep resentment toward Silverymoon for failing to aid the Silver Marches during the Orc invasion, calling the inhabitants "bigoted, self-important, righteous bastards" [ch 062 / 062.02]. However, she distinguishes between her political anger and general vengeance, noting that Silverymoon contains "possibly innocent gnomes" [ch 062 / 062.02].

Her primary motivation is escaping underground life; she plans to "buy a farm and never spend another day underground" [ch 062 / 062.04]. She considers Yartar and Triboar as potential locations for this new life [ch 062 / 062.04]. She is wary of the party, explicitly stating she does not want a "deep personal relationship" with them, particularly after realizing they negotiated her fee down to their maximum budget [ch 062 / 062.03].
