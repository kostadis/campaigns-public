## Identity
A halfling residing in Silverymoon, described as sharply dressed and possessed of a well-appointed drawing room with fine wine [ch 029 / 029.02; ch 029 / entry]. He is identified as a "paramount citizen" whose testimony carries weight in the local judicial system [ch 029 / 029.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Vinby appears to prioritize the sanctity of his property and personal comfort over the larger existential threats presented to him. While he initially reacts to danger by shouting "DEMONS!", his subsequent actions in the Star Court suggest a focus on the immediate violation of his household rather than the magical assault that led to it [ch 029 / 029.02; ch 029 / 029.05]. He positions himself strategically within the court, sitting as far from the judge as possible, indicating a desire to avoid direct conflict or scrutiny from the higher authorities [ch 029 / 029.05].

## History with the Party
- Orsik barges into Vinby Hearthgood's house in Silverymoon, which was unlocked, and interrupts Vinby while he is drinking wine [ch 029 / 029.02].
- Orsik shouts at Vinby that demons are chasing them, causing Vinby to drop his glass [ch 029 / 029.02].
- Vinby survives the encounter in his house, as later evidence notes he is alive in court [ch 029 / 029.02].
- Vinby sits in the Star Court as far from the judge as possible [ch 029 / 029.05].
- The Judge asks Vinby for his account of the events [ch 029 / 029.05].
- Vinby testifies that Orsik is the person who smashed into his house [ch 029 / 029.05].

## Last Observed State
Vinby was last observed in the Star Court during Chapter 029 [ch 029 / 029.05]. He testified against Orsik, stating, "That dwarf is the person who smashed into my house," in response to the Judge’s inquiry [ch 029 / 029.05]. Following his testimony, he remained in the courtroom while the Judge proceeded to question Orsik and Lady Margaster [ch 029 / 029.05]. There is no indication in the provided text of his movement or actions after this scene; Status not established in the summaries beyond the conclusion of the court session.
