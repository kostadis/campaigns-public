## Identity
- A woman in a purple dress with short black hair and a necklace of black stones, who plays Sava. [ch 078 / entry]
- She was first seen at a gaming table aboard the Grand Dame, where she beat Lord Hubbard. [ch 078 / 078.05]
- She presented herself as "a poor lady playing these games of skill, chance, and treachery." [ch 078 / 078.05]
- She showed telepathy. Orsik noticed her probing his mind, and she spoke directly into his mind. [ch 078 / 078.05; ch 078 / 078.06]
- She later suggested that her mind-reading depends on people believing in it, and on her potions: "I would if I had drunk my potions." [ch 080 / 080.05; ch 080 / moment]
- Her refined manner gave way to "a very plebeian dock accent" when she was angry. [ch 080 / 080.05; ch 080 / entry]
- She was under the protection of a small group of female rogues. [ch 080 / 080.05]

## Personality and Motivations
- **Deceptive and calculating at the table.** She posed as an out-of-her-depth lady. Orsik saw that she was playing them, and she won by countering a move she was clearly aware of. She denied reading Orsik's mind when he accused her. [ch 078 / 078.05; ch 078 / 078.06]
- **Discreet.** She moved the conversation off the boat with "Not here." Orsik guessed that she wanted no whiff of scandal; this is his reading, not her stated motive. [ch 078 / 078.06]
- **Uses what she reads as leverage.** She raised Orsik's secret, "your fake identity as your father," at the tavern. [ch 079 / 079.02]
- **Deflects about Hekaton.** Thistle saw her steering away from King Hekaton, as Drylund had. She assumed the party wanted Lord Artan's kidnappers. [ch 079 / 079.02]
- **Claims to be coerced.** She said Oosith runs the operation to kidnap lordlings and that she was blackmailed into helping after Lord Drylund figured her out. She described herself this way: "I am not an evil person. I was blackmailed. And I do so desperately want to go back to the light!" [ch 079 / 079.02; ch 079 / moment]
- **Wants Oosith dead.** She begged the party to kill him. When she learned the aboleth had escaped, she was furious: "You were supposed to kill him." Orsik equated her request with killing the aboleth. [ch 079 / 079.02; ch 080 / 080.04; ch 080 / 080.05]
- **Fears the Kraken.** She warned that "The Kraken doesn't like it when anyone disrupts its plans." [ch 080 / 080.05]

## History with the Party
- Lady Atalia is described as a woman in a purple dress with short black hair and a necklace of black stones, who plays Sava. [ch 078 / entry]
- On the Grand Dame, Thistle spotted her beating Lord Hubbard, who conceded with a stack of golden geese. [ch 078 / 078.05]
- Unla Key sat down to play her. She called herself "a poor lady playing these games of skill, chance, and treachery" and proposed stakes of 5000. Unla Key believed her; Orsik saw that she was playing them. [ch 078 / 078.05]
- Orsik noticed her probing his mind with telepathy and pulled Thistle and Vardis out of what he hoped was her range. [ch 078 / 078.05]
- Vardis noted the party had no magic to counter her. Thistle suggested she might have information worth trading. Vardis ruled out threatening her in a dark alley and proposed losing the game to persuade her to share information. [ch 078 / 078.05]
- Orsik pushed Unla Key out of the chair and played her himself, with some 10,000 gold in golden geese at stake. She appeared to lose, then countered him and won. [ch 078 / 078.06]
- Orsik quietly accused her of reading his mind, and she denied it. [ch 078 / 078.06; ch 078 / moment]
- When Orsik suggested a private conversation, she replied telepathically, telling him to follow her when the boat returned to the docks. [ch 078 / 078.06; ch 078 / entry]
- Lord Drylund later asked the party, "I see you already gambled with Lady Atalia?" An unattributed voice added, "She’s perfect." [ch 078 / 078.07]
- At dawn at the Yartar Docks, she appeared to be waiting for the party. She led them to a tavern and recommended a hangover drink. [ch 079 / 079.02; ch 079 / entry]
- She told Orsik she knew he wanted to learn whether "your fake identity as your father is working out." Infuriated, Orsik handed the talking to someone else. [ch 079 / 079.02; ch 079 / entry]
- She assumed the party was hunting Lord Artan's kidnappers. Thistle asked instead about the connection between the Grand Dame and King Hekaton. Thistle saw her deflecting away from Hekaton, as Drylund had. [ch 079 / 079.02]
- She pointed the party to "An evil spell caster named Oosith." [ch 079 / 079.02; ch 079 / entry]
- Lord Drylund figured her out, and she was blackmailed into helping. [ch 079 / 079.02; ch 079 / moment]
- She said Oosith hides in the sewers, protected by "Fish People," and begged the party to kill him. [ch 079 / 079.02; ch 079 / entry]
- The party walked away from her without committing to help. [ch 079 / 079.02]
- Thistle later proposed her to fill the power vacuum. Orsik objected that she had wanted them to kill the aboleth, which was "very much not dead." Thistle insisted they see her first. [ch 080 / 080.04; ch 080 / entry]
- At a fashionable cafe she learned the aboleth had escaped, cursed the party and stormed out. [ch 080 / 080.05; ch 080 / entry]
- A small group of female rogues stopped the party from following, saying she was under their protection. [ch 080 / 080.05]
- Outside her house she dropped her refinement for a plebeian dock accent and cut ties with the party. [ch 080 / 080.05; ch 080 / entry]
- She warned that "The Kraken doesn't like it when anyone disrupts its plans," and that Drylund was going to come back. [ch 080 / 080.05]
- She admitted that her apparent mind-reading depends on people believing it ("I would if I had drunk my potions"), then closed the door on the party. [ch 080 / 080.05; ch 080 / moment]
- In chapter 081 she was only spoken of. Thistle argued that she and Lord Khaspere Drylund lacked the resources to steal and hide a giant. [ch 081 / 081.03]
