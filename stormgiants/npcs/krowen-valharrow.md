## Identity
- Krowen Valharrow is a Turami man and a Harper [ch 029 / entry]. He named himself to the party at Moongleam Tower, after Vardis recognised him as a Harper [ch 029 / 029.10].
- He is an old-timer Harper who believes in the old ways [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.01]. Harshnag called him "the Harper Agent Krowen Valharrow" [ch 038 / 038.01] and "the mage" [ch 041 / 041.03].
- He has a tower with an inner sanctum [ch 030 / 030.01], an apprentice, Nespril Menk [ch 033 / 033.10], and an agent in Yartar [ch 030 / 030.01].
- Gauche the Young said Krowen follows the school of Ezra [ch 033 / 033.08].
- He acted on screen only in chapters 029–030. Every later appearance is a mention, a report, or a message carried by someone else.

## Personality and Motivations
- **Strategic concern about the giants.** He holds that the factions are busy watching one another while "no one is paying attention to the giants" [ch 029 / moment]. He wants the cause of the fall of the Ordning uncovered, and saw the party as a possible tool for that [ch 030 / 030.01].
- **Reluctant pragmatist.** He believes in the old ways, but accepted hired adventurers because the times are desperate and Darathra herself had sent the group [ch 030 / 030.01]. Privately he worried that he was "coming off desperate" [ch 030 / 030.01]. His own apprentice says he backs "the Ezra model" [ch 033 / 033.10], and so does Gauche [ch 033 / 033.08].
- **Covert and manipulative.** He used a teleport ruse to keep the party from being seen at his door [ch 029 / 029.10]. He then tried to send them out by the front door to "burn Orsik," whom reports placed close to the Zhentarim. When the elf caught the contradiction, he feigned forgetfulness: "I can’t believe I forgot that!" [ch 030 / 030.01].
- **Big-picture over detail.** Nespril says Krowen, "doing the big picture stuff, never pays attention to the details." He did not tell the party about the sewer exit [ch 033 / 033.10].
- **Generous terms at first.** He asked no payment ("Nothing!") [ch 030 / 030.01]. He gave the party the map of circles and Darathra's coffer of scrolls [ch 029 / 029.10].
- **Protective toward the party.** He talked Vardis out of fighting a cloud giant [ch 030 / 030.01].
- **Impatient once the party drifted.** Through a messenger he demanded that they "stop this random questing and go save the North." He did not renew their contract [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.07].
- **Judgment of the party.** Darathra remembers him saying "They betrayed the circles" [ch 064 / 064.05].

## History with the Party
- Krowen Valharrow is described as a Turami man and a Harper. [ch 029 / entry]
- He greeted the party in person when they arrived at Moongleam Tower. Vardis recognised him as a Harper, and he named himself. [ch 029 / 029.10]
- He explained that Dral teleported the party to the tower so that a secret agent would not be seen at the door, which would wave "a red flag at the Zhentarim and Lords’ Alliance." [ch 029 / 029.10]
- Vardis told him the Ordning of the Giants had been sundered, cause unclear. Krowen offered help if the party would find out the cause and fix it. [ch 029 / 029.10]
- He showed them a teleportation circle and gave them a map of circles for instantaneous travel through the North. Orsik was shocked, because the Zhentarim have nothing like it. [ch 029 / 029.10]
- He passed on gifts from Darathra for anyone who would take the quest: a coffer holding several spell scrolls. [ch 029 / 029.10; ch 029 / entry]
- In his tower and inner sanctum, he weighed whether the party could be the tool to unlock the secrets behind the fall of the Ordning. [ch 030 / 030.01]
- He is described as an old-timer Harper who believes in the old ways. He accepted the group because these are desperate times and Darathra herself had sent them. [ch 030 / 030.01; ch 030 / entry]
- He asked the party whether they were in, and privately worried that he was coming off as desperate. The dwarf accepted. [ch 030 / 030.01]
- He told the party to leave by the front door, recalling reports that the dwarf is close to the Zhentarim. The elf pointed out that this contradicted the teleport ruse. Krowen privately conceded that "he saw through my ruse to burn Orsik," and feigned forgetfulness. [ch 030 / 030.01]
- He sent the party to "Yartar to find the sword." When Vardis asked what the party must pay, he said they owed nothing. [ch 030 / 030.01]
- He explained that the orb needed for a flying castle comes only from the Cloud Giants, by persuasion or violence. He warned Vardis off fighting a cloud giant, then teleported the party to his agent in Yartar. [ch 030 / 030.01; ch 030 / entry]
- Gauche the Young named the party as Krowen Valharrow's hired agents and said that Krowen follows the school of Ezra. Krowen was mentioned only, not present. [ch 033 / 033.08]
- Nespril Menk is Krowen's apprentice. He called the party "Krowen's latest project" and said Krowen had neglected to tell them about the sewer exit. He also said he does not believe in Krowen's support for the Ezra model. Krowen was mentioned only. [ch 033 / 033.10]
- Harshnag said Krowen Valharrow had told him that the party agreed to take on the giant quest. Krowen was spoken of, not present. [ch 037 / 037.04]
- Harshnag said Krowen insisted the party were his agents, and invoked the quid pro quo for access to the teleportation circles. Krowen does not appear. [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / 038.01]
- Harshnag said that if the party did not care, Krowen had "allied with the wrong people." Krowen was mentioned only. [ch 040 / 040.01]
- In his thoughts, Harshnag planned to bring the party's bodies to Krowen if they died, so the mage could find new heroes. Krowen was mentioned only. [ch 041 / entry; ch 041 / 041.03]
- Dral Thelev told the party he was sure Krowen chose them for a reason. Krowen was mentioned only. [ch 047 / 047.04]
- Krowen sent an elf Harper messenger asking whether the party planned to deal with the Giants. The messenger warned that the party would lose access to the magical pathways if they did not move that day. [ch 048 / 048.07; ch 048 / entry]
- Through the messenger, Krowen offered treasure maps and told the party to "stop this random questing and go save the North." [ch 048 / 048.07; ch 048 / entry]
- The messenger said the party's contract would not be renewed. Orsik declared, "We’re done," and the party agreed. The elf left with the maps. [ch 048 / 048.07]
- Orsik joked about approaching the Harpers in his father persona, then laughed that Krowen Valharrow would not go for it. Krowen was mentioned only. [ch 064 / 064.02]
- Darathra Shendrel had vouched for the party to Krowen, and it blew up in her face. She remembers Krowen saying, "They betrayed the circles." Krowen was remembered only, not present. [ch 064 / 064.05; ch 064 / entry]
