## Identity
Cap'n Squint is a small halfling wearing a tricorne hat [ch 028 / entry; ch 028 / 028.06]. He is a former friend and crewman of Imber who now owns and captains his own barge [ch 028 / entry; ch 028 / 028.06]. Imber describes him as someone who "barks orders at men twice his size and stomps the planks as if he were ten feet tall" [ch 028 / 028.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Squint presents himself as far more imposing than his physical stature suggests, commanding respect through loud authority and aggressive presence [ch 028 / 028.06]. He is ambitious regarding local politics; Imber reports that Squint "wants to be the new Waterbaron when the old one dies" [ch 028 / 028.06]. Additionally, he is a gambler, frequenting the Grand Dame, a luxury boat owned by Lord Khaspere Drylund, for high-stakes gambling [ch 028 / 028.06].

## History with the Party
- Cap'n Squint is described as a small halfling in a tricorne hat, a former friend and crewman of Imber, and now the owner of his own barge [ch 028 / entry; ch 028 / 028.06].
- He was recommended by Imber as a captain who could take the party to Silverymoon, with a departure expected the following day [ch 028 / 028.06].
- The party was told that Squint would be gambling on the Grand Dame, a luxury boat owned by Lord Khaspere Drylund, the night before their departure [ch 028 / 028.06].
- In the morning, the group departed with Captain Squint aboard his barge, traveling north on the River Surbrin [ch 028 / 028.07; ch 028 / moment].

## Last Observed State
Squint was active in the morning of the departure day, preparing his barge to leave north on the River Surbrin with the party [ch 028 / 028.07; ch 028 / moment]. The group successfully departed with him [ch 028 / entry; ch 028 / moment]. Status not established in the summaries beyond the commencement of this journey.

## Relationships
**Imber**: Squint is Imber’s former friend and crewman [ch 028 / entry]. Imber recommends Squint to the party for transport to Silverymoon but declines to take them himself [ch 028 / 028.06]. Imber notes that Squint’s gambling habits are tied to Lord Khaspere Drylund [ch 028 / 028.06].
**Lord Khaspere Drylund**: Squint gambles on Drylund’s boat, the Grand Dame [ch 028 / 028.06]. Imber implies a political connection between Squint’s ambitions and Drylund’s current status, noting Drylund’s own ambition to become the new Waterbaron [ch 028 / 028.06].
