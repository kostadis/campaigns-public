## Identity
Nespril Menk is an apprentice of Krowen Valharrow at the Moongleam Tower [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.10]. Physically, he is described as tall, coltish, gangly, and green-eyed, with a broad nose and light brown hair [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.10]. He speaks with the sophistication of a Chondathan [ch 033 / 033.10].

## Personality and Motivations
Nespril demonstrates a strong ideological commitment to the Harpers, holding "the old ways" where membership implies belief over transactional relationships [ch 033 / 033.10; ch 033 / moment]. He reacts with anger to suggestions of using independent contractors or the "Ezra model," viewing them as contrary to the group's core values [ch 033 / 033.10]. He perceives his mentor, Krowen, as someone who focuses on "big picture stuff" while neglecting practical details, such as security exits [ch 033 / 033.10]. Nespril acts as a pragmatic facilitator when necessary, providing the party with a secret sewer exit to ensure the Zhentarim do not spot them, correcting Krowen's oversight [ch 033 / 033.10]. He is cautious about internal security, specifically warning that Krowen would be upset if a book detailing a "special random system" were lost [ch 033 / 033.10].
