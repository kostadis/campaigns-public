## Identity
Everard Barney is the director of a theatrical production and a member of the party's investor circle [ch 035 / 035.03; ch 035 / entry]. He is unimposing and heavy-set, wearing a multi-colored shirt with a purple scarf over his head that barely hides a buzz cut. He has enormous ears [ch 035 / 035.03; ch 035 / entry]. He hails from a family of bookkeepers [ch 035 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Everard describes himself as "completely committed to Kaelen's vision," a stance he maintains despite admitting privately that Kaelen "creeps me out" [ch 035 / 035.03; ch 035 / entry]. He is emotionally volatile, prone to sobbing and whining when stressed [ch 035 / entry]. His primary motivation is the success of the production to avoid failure, which would force him to return to Waterdeep and work as an accountant like the rest of his family [ch 035 / 035.03; ch 035 / entry]. He is eager for insight and coaching, actively seeking advice from others on how to manage his production [ch 035 / entry].

## History with the Party
