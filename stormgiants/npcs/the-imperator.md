## Identity
Uthor is a middle-aged Storm Giant who served as the Imperator of the storm giant court in the Maelstrom [ch 067 / 067.04; ch 069 / entry]. Physically, he stands over 30 feet tall with steel-gray hair and wields a trident crackling with latent electricity [ch 067 / 067.04; ch 069 / 069.04]. He was dismissed from his position in Chapter 082 when King Hekaton installed Queen Serissa as the new Imperator [ch 082 / 082.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Uthor displays a condescending, professorial demeanor, frequently regarding those around him with "perpetual condescension" and attempting to maintain a sense of intellectual superiority [ch 067 / 067.04; ch 069 / entry]. He is manipulative and controlling, preferring a Queen he can influence and physically restraining Serissa when she attempted to act out of turn [ch 067 / 067.04]. His primary motivation appears to be the preservation of his own authority and status through proxy power; he advised Serissa to accept the support of Adimond Kroskas to ensure stability, only to stammer that he "didn’t know" about the dragon’s true nature when confronted [ch 067 / 067.04; ch 069 / 069.05]. He is dismissive of external help, rejecting Thistle’s offer to find King Hekaton with a curt "Nonsense" and mocking the party’s concerns as irrelevant [ch 069 / 069.05; ch 070 / 070.02]. Uthor is willing to deceive his king, attempting to claim credit for searching for Hekaton despite having offered little support, though this was exposed by the party [ch 082 / 082.04].

## History with the Party
*   The Imperator, Uthor, was present in Queen Serissa’s throne room, where he was observed by Thistle sitting beside the Queen and regarding her with "perpetual condescension" [ch 067 / 067.04].
*   During a confrontation in the throne room, Uthor physically restrained Queen Serissa by grabbing her arm when she attempted to rise during an argument with her sisters [ch 067 / 067.04].
*   Uthor silenced Adimond Kroskas when the alchemist suggested a change in leadership, stating, "Do not confuse your privilege with the right to speak about these matters" [ch 067 / 067.04].
*   Uthor advised Queen Serissa to accept the support of Adimond Kroskas, a suggestion Serissa later turned against him when the truth about the dragon was revealed [ch 069 / entry; ch 069 / 069.05].
*   Uthor dismissed Mirran and Nym’s claim that the party were assassins, ordering the guards to take the sisters to their quarters [ch 069 / 069.04].
*   Uthor dismissed Thistle’s offer to help find the missing King Hekaton with a single word: "Nonsense" [ch 069 / 069.05].
*   Uthor provided the party with historical context regarding King Hekaton, the shattering of the Ordning, Queen Neri’s death, and the succession crisis, delivering the information in a "condescending, professorial tone" [ch 069 / entry; ch 069 / 069.07].
*   Uthor laughed when Unla Key requested 50,000 gold for research, calling it "Preposterous," but was overruled when Queen Serissa granted the request using the royal golden armor [ch 069 / entry; ch 069 / 069.09].
*   Uthor attempted to calm Thistle after Adimond Kroskas transformed the fairy’s eyes into dragon eyes, pointing out that the curse came with the blessing of True Seeing [ch 070 / entry; ch 070 / 070.01].
*   When King Hekaton returned and asked if Uthor had left "no stone unturned" to find him, Uthor attempted to deceive the King, but the party clarified that he had offered little support [ch 082 / 082.04].
*   King Hekaton dismissed Uthor from his position, stating, "Brother, Uthor, you are dismissed," and installing Queen Serissa as the new Imperator [ch 082 / 082.04].
*   Uthor accepted his dismissal, handed his insignia of office to Serissa, and stated, "Yes, my brother, it is time I took care of other business," before marching out of the throne room [ch 082 / 082.04].

## Last Observed State
In Chapter 082, Uthor was publicly dismissed by King Hekaton, who stated, "Brother, Uthor, you are dismissed," and declared Serissa the new Imperator [ch 082 / 082.04]. Uthor accepted this dismissal, handing his insignia of office to Serissa, and stated, "Yes, my brother, it is time I took care of other business," before marching out of the throne room [ch 082 / 082.04]. In Chapter 083, he is no longer present in the teleportation chamber or the court proceedings; Serissa, who has assumed his title, is the active Imperator alongside King Hekaton [ch 083 / 083.01]. Status not established in the summaries regarding his whereabouts after leaving the throne room in Chapter 082.

## Relationships
*   **Queen Serissa:** Uthor acted as her Regent/Imperator, treating her with condescension and physically restraining her during arguments [ch 067 / 067.04]. Serissa later turned on him, exposing his role in inviting Adimond Kroskas into the court [ch 069 / 069.05]. He handed his insignia to her upon his dismissal [ch 082 / 082.04].
*   **King Hekaton:** Uthor is Hekaton’s brother [ch 082 / 082.04]. He attempted to deceive Hekaton about his efforts to find the missing king [ch 082 / 082.04]. Hekaton dismissed him from office [ch 082 / 082.04].
*   **Adimond Kroskas:** Uthor was an ally and advocate for Adimond’s presence in the court, having suggested accepting his support [ch 069 / entry; ch 069 / 069.05]. He silenced Adimond when the alchemist suggested changing leadership, maintaining the status quo [ch 067 / 067.04].
*   **Mirran and Nym:** Uthor dismissed their accusations against the party, ordering the guards to remove them from the room [ch 069 / 069.04].
*   **The Party (3HPP):** Uthor viewed the party with contempt, laughing at Thistle’s size and dismissing their offers of help [ch 069 / 069.04; ch 069 / 069.05]. He was ultimately outmaneuvered by them when they exposed his lack of effort in finding King Hekaton [ch 082 / 082.04].
