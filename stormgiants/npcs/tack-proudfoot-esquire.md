## Identity
Tack Proudfoot Esquire is a minor noble residing in Mirabar [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.07]. He is a knight serving with the elite guards [ch 039 / 039.07]. He is betrothed to Clan Battlehammer [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.07]. While described by Yip Hammaver as a "dandy" who "read one too many romance novels," his appearance in combat demonstrates significant martial capability [ch 039 / 039.07].

## Personality and Motivations
Tack values honor and formal procedure, challenging offenders to duels to settle slights [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.07]. He prioritizes the preservation of his betrothal alliance and the social reputation of his house, aware that his death would be avenged [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.07]. Following a resolved conflict, he displays a desire for social connection, enthusiastically seeking to integrate the offending party into Mirabar’s noble dating circles [ch 039 / 039.07].

## History with the Party
*   Tack Proudfoot Esquire was offended by John-Orsik (Orsik disguised as John Husteem), leading to a challenge to a duel at dawn [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.07].
*   Tack is betrothed to Clan Battlehammer; his death would be avenged [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.07].
*   Tack appeared at the duel in the finest armor John-Orsik had ever seen, revealing himself as a capable knight despite Yip Hammaver's description of him as a dandy [ch 039 / 039.07].
*   After John-Orsik cast sanctuary and refused to fight, Tack declared honor satisfied, removed his helmet, and embraced John-Orsik [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.07].
*   Tack insisted that the single John-Orsik be introduced to eligible bachelors and bachelorettes of Mirabar's noble houses [ch 039 / 039.07].
