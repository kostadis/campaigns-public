## Identity
Miery is an older woman who acts as a street-corner prophet preaching on a main thoroughfare [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.08]. She identifies herself explicitly as "Miery – not Mary," explaining that her mother worshipped the goddess Mielikki and named her accordingly [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.08]. She represents an order dedicated to fighting an "ancient evil," which she identifies as the Kraken [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.08].

## Personality and Motivations
Miery is motivated by her belief that the Kraken will one day return to destroy the city with "powerful tentacle and mind control" [ch 029 / 029.08]. She actively recruits members to her group to fight this "ancient evil," demanding five gold pieces for membership [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.08]. While she charges a fee, she is flexible if the party lacks resources, offering to waive the cost in exchange for the recovery of an artifact called the "star of light" [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.08]. She describes this artifact as having "purely symbolic value" and claims its actual function is "Nothing" [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.08]. Miery displays a dismissive demeanor toward irrelevant inquiries, specifically ignoring Unla Key’s question regarding the spelling of the artifact's name [ch 029 / 029.08; ch 029 / moment].
