## Identity
Blooddrinker is a bear described as "adorably ferocious" and the size of a cat [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.09]. He was formerly owned by the tiny giant Slayermighty [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.08].

## Personality and Motivations
Blooddrinker displays aggression toward those who threaten his master, as Slayermighty threatened to "feed you to Blooddrinker" [ch 001 / 001.08; ch 001 / moment]. However, he lacks immediate aggression toward strangers; upon Slayermighty's death, he rolled onto his belly expecting to be rubbed rather than attacking the party [ch 001 / 001.08; ch 001 / moment]. He is easily engaged by Thistle, playing with him while the party looted the chest [ch 001 / 001.09].

## History with the Party
- Blooddrinker is a bear the size of a cat, described as "adorably ferocious" and belonging to the giant Slayermighty [ch 001 / entry].
- When Slayermighty died, Blooddrinker convulsed; Thistle saved the bear's life with quick thinking while the others looted the chest [ch 001 / 001.09].
- Blooddrinker bonded to Thistle immediately after being saved [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.09].
- The bear rolled onto his belly expecting to be rubbed immediately after his master died, displaying no aggression toward the party at that moment [ch 001 / 001.08; ch 001 / moment].

## Last Observed State
Blooddrinker is bonded to Thistle, the party member who saved him from convulsing [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.09]. The evidence does not establish his current location or status beyond this bond [ch 001 / entry].

## Relationships
**Thistle:** Blooddrinker bonded to Thistle immediately after Thistle saved his life when he began to convulse [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.09].
**Slayermighty:** Blooddrinker was Slayermighty's "fearsome bear" [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.08]. Slayermighty threatened enemies with him [ch 001 / 001.08]. Upon Slayermighty's death, Blooddrinker rolled over to be rubbed [ch 001 / 001.08; ch 001 / moment].

## Notable Quotes
> "Slayermighty the Giant will tear you apart and feed you to Blooddrinker! I'll be ruling all of Giant-kind soon!" — Slayermighty [ch 001 / 001.08; ch 001 / moment]
