## Identity
Clan True-Nimbus is a clan of Cloud Giants identified as the signatories of a note found in a chest, alongside the title "Clan True-Nimbus of the Clouds" [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.09]. They claimed ownership of the treasure originally stored there and asserted their presence before the party arrived [ch 001 / 001.09].

## Personality and Motivations
The clan displays condescension and arrogance toward other giants, referring to hill giants specifically as "Stupid runt-giants of the hills" and "idiots" [ch 001 / 001.09; ch 001 / moment]. They consider themselves superior to their peers, stating that these other giants are "too slow and dim-witted to challenge" them [ch 001 / 001.09]. Their motivation appears driven by possessiveness over loot, as they explicitly stated, "The treasure is ours" [ch 001 / 001.09]. They also demonstrated a tendency to mock the education of their rivals, offering unsolicited and insulting definitions of words, such as noting that "prevail means win" [ch 001 / moment].

## History with the Party
- Clan True-Nimbus of the Clouds left a note in the chest, claiming ownership of the treasure and mocking the "runt-giants of the hills" [ch 001 / 001.09].
- The note contained a condescending correction regarding the use of the word "prevail" [ch 001 / 001.09; ch 001 / moment].

## Last Observed State
Clan True-Nimbus was not physically present in the final observed scenes; they were mentioned by Orsik as having visited the chest location earlier [ch 001 / 001.09]. The clan left a taunting note in the chest in place of the expected treasure [ch 001 / moment]. Status not established in the summaries [ch 001 / 001.09].

## Relationships
The clan views "runt-giants of the hills" (hill giants) with contempt, describing them as slow and dim-witted [ch 001 / 001.09]. They have a direct adversarial relationship with the party regarding the contents of the specific chest opened in Chapter 1, having successfully claimed the primary treasure before the party arrived [ch 001 / 001.09].
