## Identity
Dral Thelev is a one-eyed half-orc who serves as the host and manager of Danivarr’s House, a tavern in Everlund owned by the Zoar family [ch 027 / 027.14; ch 031 / entry; ch 047 / entry]. He is a member of the Harper organization, identified as a secret agent and a friend of Darathra Shendrel [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.09; ch 029 / 029.10].

## Personality and Motivations
Dral operates as a discreet gatekeeper for the Harpers, utilizing his position to facilitate secure movement for agents; he teleported the party to Moongleam Tower specifically to avoid raising suspicions among the Zhentarim and Lords’ Alliance [ch 029 / 029.09; ch 029 / 029.10]. He harbors a personal resentment toward the citizens and leadership of Silverymoon, deriving spiteful pleasure from consuming the city's finest wine [ch 029 / 029.09]. Professionally, he is knowledgeable about Everlund’s political landscape, including the Zoar family’s high connections and the High Captain’s grudge against Silverymoon, which he shares to assist the party’s entry into that city [ch 033 / 033.11]. He places trust in the judgment of fellow Harper Krowen Valharrow, expressing confidence in the party’s suitability for their quest despite their ongoing struggles [ch 047 / 047.04].
