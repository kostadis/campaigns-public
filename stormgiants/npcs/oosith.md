## Identity
Oosith is described by Lady Atalia as "An evil spell caster" [ch 079 / entry; ch 079 / 079.02] and by Orsik as "a sorcerer" who specializes in kidnappings [ch 079 / entry; ch 079 / 079.03]. Lord Drylund refers to him euphemistically as his "campaign manager in charge of special persuasion operations" [ch 079 / entry; ch 079 / 079.03]. In Chapter 080, Oosith revealed himself to be an aboleth [ch 080 / entry; ch 080 / 080.02]. He operates out of the sewers of Yartar, where he is protected by Kuo-toa who refer to him as "the boss" [ch 079 / entry; ch 079 / 079.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Oosith’s primary motivation appears to be executing a kidnapping operation for Lord Drylund, specifically targeting the children of voters to coerce them into voting for Drylund [ch 079 / entry; ch 079 / 079.03]. He maintains operational security, with Drylund claiming he cannot see Oosith personally for "plausible deniability," though a Kuo-toa guard implies this is because the boss does not want to see Drylund's "ugly face" [ch 079 / 079.04]. In combat, Oosith utilizes mind domination abilities, attempting to command foes directly [ch 080 / 080.02]. His final actions suggest an allegiance to the Kraken, as he shrieked, "The Kraken will have its revenge!" upon fleeing [ch 080 / entry; ch 080 / 080.02].

## Last Observed State
In Chapter 080, Oosith engaged the party in the sewers. He revealed his true form as an aboleth and dominated the party member Unla Key [ch 080 / entry; ch 080 / 080.02]. Vardis blinded Oosith using light-cleric powers, which disrupted his attack on Thistle [ch 080 / 080.02]. When Thistle wounded a Merrow, the aboleth's domination of Unla Key was broken [ch 080 / entry; ch 080 / 080.02]. Seeing the situation turn against him, Oosith fled the confrontation, shrieking, "The Kraken will have its revenge!" [ch 080 / entry; ch 080 / 080.02]. Lady Atalia later accused the party of letting him escape, confirming he was not killed in the sewer [ch 080 / 080.05]. Status beyond fleeing the sewer not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
**Lord Drylund**: Oosith serves as Drylund's "campaign manager" for kidnappings [ch 079 / entry; ch 079 / 079.03]. Drylund claims he cannot see Oosith for operational security [ch 079 / 079.04].
**Kuo-toa**: The Kuo-toa in the sewers protect Oosith and refer to him as their "boss" [ch 079 / entry; ch 079 / 079.04]. They acted as his guards until the confrontation in Chapter 080 [ch 080 / 080.02].
**Lady Atalia**: Atalia was blackmailed by Drylund into helping Oosith's operation [ch 079 / 079.02]. She despises Oosith, describing him as an "evil spell caster," and wanted him killed [ch 079 / 079.02]. She severed ties with the party after Oosith escaped [ch 080 / 080.05].
**The Party (3HPP)**: Oosith was targeted by the party for removal. He dominated Unla Key during the fight [ch 080 / entry; ch 080 / 080.02]. The party later referred to him generically as "A sorcerer named Oosith" when speaking to Atalia's guards [ch 080 / entry; ch 080 / 080.05].
