## Identity
The Foreman is the unnamed leader of the Mines of Mirabar [ch 040 / entry]. He operates from the compound, delegating security to guards while he manages external interactions and labor assignments [ch 040 / 040.02].

## Personality and Motivations
The Foreman is dismissive of those who claim heroism without offering immediate utility, as seen when he told Orsik, "You got paid for your work. Now please go" [ch 040 / 040.02]. He is deeply suspicious of outsiders, specifically wondering if the party are agents of the Weevil or if the Weevil shared information with them [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.05]. He verifies claims before acting; he used unique sending stones to contact the Axe and confirmed that Orsik exaggerated his role in the Weevil's capture [ch 040 / 040.05; ch 040 / entry]. His primary motivation in this instance is to rid himself of the party while addressing a genuine pest problem, noting internally, "I need to get rid of these yahoos" [ch 040 / 040.05]. He is pragmatic about his resources, recognizing that crag cats are "rather annoying" and need removal regardless of the party's motives [ch 040 / 040.05].

## History with the Party
*   The Foreman is the unnamed leader of the Mines of Mirabar who initially dismisses the party, stating they have already been paid for their work regarding the Weevil, and directs them to the lodge for dinner and a morning meeting. [ch 040 / 040.02]
*   Internally, the Foreman views the party with suspicion, wondering if they are agents of the Weevil or if the Weevil shared information with them, and seeks a way to rid himself of them. [ch 040 / 040.05]
*   The Foreman verifies Orsik's claims about the Weevil's interrogation via the Axe using unique sending stones, learning that Orsik exaggerated his role as the Axe actually extracted the information. [ch 040 / 040.05; ch 040 / entry]
*   To keep the party occupied, the Foreman assigns them a job to hunt crag cats a day's hike out of the mines, which aligns with his own need to remove the pests. [ch 040 / 040.05; ch 040 / entry]
*   The Foreman is upset when the Frost Giant and the Ranger attempt to discourage Orsik from accepting the job, but he is satisfied when Vardis persuades Orsik to accept by highlighting the profit from the pelts. [ch 040 / 040.05]
