## Identity
Silas Carras is the widower of the local herbalist in Parnast [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.07]. He resides in a property featuring a backyard puzzle garden designed by his late wife, intended to hide herbs from those unwilling to pay [ch 017 / 017.07].

## Personality and Motivations
Silas is skeptical and somewhat dismissive of outsiders, particularly when they fail to identify themselves clearly or when they underestimate the complexity of his wife’s garden [ch 017 / 017.07]. He assumes visitors are members of the militia ("the Defenders of Parnast") and offers them a remedy for hangovers based on this assumption [ch 017 / 017.07]. When the party attempts to solve the garden's puzzles, he expresses low expectations for their speed, muttering that solving them requires significant thought and time [ch 017 / 017.07]. He is motivated to maintain his late wife’s legacy while keeping the garden’s secrets secure, as evidenced by his shock at the party's rapid solution [ch 017 / 017.07].
