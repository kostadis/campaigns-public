## Identity
Narbeck Horn is the Lord of Xantharl’s Keep [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / 038.04; ch 039 / 039.01]. He holds authority over the Keep’s military and legal matters, specifically regarding the "Ax of Mirabar" [ch 038 / 038.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Narbeck is dismissive and self-important, prioritizing high-level oversight over detail-oriented work [ch 038 / entry]. When approached, he responds with irritation, viewing persistent adventurers as "unbelievably annoying" [ch 038 / 038.04]. He defines his role strictly as policy-setting, explicitly telling others, "My job is to set policy," while refusing to handle the specifics of criminal investigations himself [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / 038.04].

He delegates operational tasks to subordinates, evidenced by referring the party to his lieutenant while smiling "evilly" at the man, implying the lieutenant would face the consequences if the operation failed [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / 038.04]. Narbeck demonstrates a capacity for malice or schadenfreude, particularly in combat situations; upon learning that a Frost Giant was leading the attackers against the Keep, he smiled, reacting to the threat with relish rather than alarm [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / 038.04]. He maintains a reputation for strict, unforgiving justice, warning that "The Ax of Mirabar shows no mercy to those who arrest the wrong man" [ch 038 / 038.04].

## Relationships
**The Party**: Narbeck viewed the party as a nuisance, initially answering their request to arrest a criminal with "So?" and dismissing them by delegating the matter to his lieutenant [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / moment; ch 038 / 038.04].

**The Lieutenant**: Narbeck is the superior of the Keep’s lieutenant. He delegates operational authority to him and appears to take pleasure in assigning him risky or undesirable tasks, evidenced by his "evil smile" when passing the bounty hunters to him [ch 038 / entry; ch 038 / 038.04].

**The Weevil (Worvil Forkbeard)**: Narbeck possesses knowledge that would allow him to identify the dwarf Worvil Forkbeard, known as The Weevil, by the specific braiding of his beard [ch 039 / 039.01].
