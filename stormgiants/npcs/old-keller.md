## Identity
Old Keller is a dwarven stonemason operating out of a hut in Llorkh [ch 003 / 003.05; ch 003 / entry]. He is described as a "kinsman" to Orsik, though he actively rejects this kinship due to the party's recent actions [ch 003 / 003.05]. Keller serves as a figure of judgment within the local dwarven community, holding specific views on what constitutes acceptable behavior for dwarves [ch 003 / 003.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Keller is characterized by his refusal to engage with those he perceives as disgracing their heritage. He explicitly accuses the group of "disgracing all dwarves" through their involvement in "that thing in the dwarf's place of work" and "The thing in the keep!" [ch 003 / 003.05]. His motivation for severing ties is rooted in a desire to maintain the honor of his profession and his kin; he views the group's actions as incompatible with dwarf identity [ch 003 / 003.05; ch 003 / entry].

He is direct and confrontational, shouting at Orsik to stop approaching him and demanding he not "take a step closer" [ch 003 / 003.05]. Keller establishes clear, conditional terms for any potential reconciliation: Orsik must cease the offending behavior ("Stop doing that thing!") and return only when he is "less likely to be goblin food," implying a need for both moral reform and improved survival skills or circumstances [ch 003 / 003.05; ch 003 / moment]. Keller is unwilling to offer guidance or forgiveness under the current conditions, prioritizing the cessation of the "thing" over immediate dialogue [ch 003 / entry].

## History with the Party
*   Orsik attempted to approach Old Keller at the stonemason's hut in Llorkh, despite warnings from Vardis that Keller did not like the group [ch 003 / 003.05].
*   Old Keller refused to engage further, accusing the group of "that thing in the dwarf's place of work, disgracing all dwarves in the process" and shouting at Orsik not to step closer [ch 003 / 003.05].
*   Keller set specific conditions for future reconciliation, stating that Orsik must stop "doing that thing" and return only when "less likely to be goblin food" [ch 003 / 003.05; ch 003 / moment].
*   Old Keller did not appear in Chapter 004; Orsik mentioned that Keller's refusal to speak to him had deeply affected him, stating it made him feel inadequate compared to Clan Licehair standards [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.06].

## Last Observed State
Old Keller did not appear in Chapter 004 [ch 004 / entry]. In Chapter 003, he was last seen at his stonemason's hut in Llorkh, where he successfully rebuffed Orsik's attempt at reconciliation [ch 003 / 003.05]. He remained in the hut during the encounter, maintaining a distance that prevented physical approach [ch 003 / 003.05]. There is no evidence of Keller taking further action, leaving Llorkh, or engaging with the party after this refusal [ch 003 / 003.05; ch 004 / entry]. Status not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
**Orsik**: Keller has a strained and hostile relationship with Orsik. Despite Orsik's assertion that "dwarves stick together," Keller rejects Orsik's appeal to kinship, shouting at him to stay away [ch 003 / 003.05]. Keller views Orsik as complicit in the events "in the keep" that disgraced the dwarves [ch 003 / 003.05]. He refuses to speak to Orsik until specific conditions are met, effectively shunning him [ch 003 / 003.05]. This refusal had a profound negative impact on Orsik, who felt it made him feel inadequate compared to Clan Licehair standards and "not even good enough" to be a member of that clan [ch 004 / 004.06].

**Vardis**: Keller’s hostility extends to the group Vardis is associated with, as he accuses "the group" of disgracing dwarves [ch 003 / 003.05]. Vardis warned Orsik before the approach, stating, "I don't think he likes us," indicating that Keller's disapproval of the party is known and established prior to Orsik's direct interaction [ch 003 / 003.05].
