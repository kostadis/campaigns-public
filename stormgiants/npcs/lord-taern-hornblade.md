## Identity
Taern Hornblade is the High Mage of Everlund [ch 086 / 086.02] and a hero of the War of the Dragons [ch 048 / 048.08; ch 086 / entry]. He is also referred to as the former ruler of Silverymoon [ch 048 / 048.08; ch 086 / entry]. Uzorel "The Viper" Hornblade, a Zhentarim agent, is identified as a distant relative of Taern [ch 048 / 048.08]. Thistle recalls stories describing Taern as a "pompous blowhard" [ch 086 / entry; ch 086 / 086.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Taern displays a cautious and suspicious demeanor regarding the party's proposals. He declared the party's evidence—a feather from the dragon Iymrith's lair—"very suspicious" and argued that the Lord's presence at the rescue attempt would be a disaster if the plan failed [ch 086 / 086.02]. Conversely, he prioritized security over ceremony, ordering the Royal Guard to cordon off the ritual site because mourners had turned it into a memorial with statues and ribbons [ch 086 / 086.02].

In tactical discussions, he argued for deploying "Four" battle mages rather than five [ch 086 / entry]. Despite his public skepticism, he possesses a sense of dark humor or appreciation for political maneuvering; after Thistle publicly committed Lord Aerasumé to the cause, Taern laughed and whispered an approval [ch 086 / 086.05; ch 086 / moment]. His presence at the final battle of Tiamat's temple is noted in Thistle's recollections [ch 086 / 086.05].
