## Identity
Sir Cedric Hollowcrest is the Captain of the gate guard at Silverymoon [ch 055 / entry; ch 055 / 055.04]. He appears to hold sufficient rank to be summoned by a subordinate guard when a dispute arises at the city gate [ch 055 / 055.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Sir Cedric is courteous and deferential toward notable figures, apologising immediately for his subordinate's rudeness upon realizing who the party is [ch 055 / 055.04; ch 055 / entry]. He appears well-informed about current cultural trends among the populace, specifically attributing the party's fame to "Bob Saget’s play" rather than their own deeds [ch 055 / 055.04]. His motivation in the observed interaction was to maintain the gate's decorum and properly acknowledge high-profile visitors.

## History with the Party
- Sir Cedric Hollowcrest is the Captain of the gate guard at Silverymoon. [ch 055 / entry]
- He arrived at the gate of Silverymoon moments after being sent for by a subordinate guard who had antagonized the party. [ch 055 / 055.04]
- He apologised for his guard's behavior and greeted the party members, identifying them as the "famous 3HPP." [ch 055 / 055.04; ch 055 / entry]

## Last Observed State
Sir Cedric arrived at the gate of Silverymoon moments after a subordinate guard antagonized the party [ch 055 / 055.04]. He apologized for the guard's behavior regarding the party's alleged partnership with "non-goodly races" [ch 055 / 055.04]. He then greeted the party members individually, using specific epithets: "The redeemed Orsik," "The brokenhearted Thistle," and "The honest Vardis," attributing their status to Bob Saget's play [ch 055 / 055.04]. The evidence ends with the resolution of this introductory exchange [ch 055 / 055.04]. Status not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
*   **The Party (3HPP):** Sir Cedric treats them with respect and recognition, calling them "the famous 3HPP" [ch 055 / 055.04; ch 055 / entry]. He identifies specific members: Orsik ("The redeemed" and later "Orsik Sr"), Thistle ("The brokenhearted"), and Vardis ("The honest") [ch 055 / 055.04].
*   **Bob Saget:** Sir Cedric cites him as the reason for the party's fame, stating, "His performance was divine!" [ch 055 / 055.04].
*   **Silverymoon Gate Guards:** Sir Cedric acts as their superior, intervening to correct a subordinate guard's antagonistic behavior [ch 055 / 055.04; ch 055 / entry].
