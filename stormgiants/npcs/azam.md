## Identity
Azam is a merchant and caravan organizer who operates primarily in the North, specifically linking the Oasis of Vuerthyl in Anauroch to Parnast [ch 007 / entry; ch 008 / entry]. He is a lean half-elven man with a long, craggy face, dark skin, and hair indicating Rashemi ancestry, noted for his impressive mustache [ch 007 / 007.07; ch 007 / entry]. He acts as the public face and logistical manager for his patron, Hsing, a golden pseudodragon, often deferring to the creature’s authority in all matters [ch 007 / entry; ch 009 / 009.01]. In his capacity as a merchant, he manages caravan logistics, including wagon contents, water, and weapons, and keeps a detailed journal of his travels [ch 008 / 008.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Azam is practical, skeptical, and professional, valuing order and hierarchy. He initially viewed the party with disdain, describing them as "independents" rather than faction agents, though he revised his assessment after they proved competent [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.02]. He is deeply loyal to Hsing and the Lords’ Alliance, to the point of being pompous and easily offended when their honor or knowledge is questioned, reacting with rage before being silenced by his patron [ch 014 / 014.01; ch 014 / entry]. He is a strong advocate for the Lords’ Alliance, arguing that it offers "order and centralization and safety," and frequently invokes the authority of the SEER to persuade or intimidate others [ch 016 / 016.04; ch 014 / 014.01]. Azam is willing to use bribery and secret incentives to secure agreements, as seen when he whispered a private deal to Unla Key and admitted to misreading notes on public rewards to manage expectations [ch 014 / 014.01; ch 014 / 014.02].

## History with the Party
*   The party travelled to the Oasis of Vuerthyl specifically to meet Azam, finding him seated in a tent tavern where a waiter noted "he will be here shortly" [ch 006 / 006.08; ch 007 / 007.06].
*   Azam was introduced as a merchant in Vuerthyl, described as a lean half-elven man of Rashemi ancestry with an impressive mustache, who defers to his patron, the pseudodragon Hsing [ch 007 / entry].
*   Azam tasked the party with delivering a statue of Angharradh (the Triune Queen) to Parnast, noting it was five feet tall, four feet wide, and very heavy [ch 007 / 007.07].
*   Azam revealed that his patron, Hsing, had further work for them if they completed the mission, and cryptically answered Thistle’s inquiry about the Drake Thuul by citing Hsing’s words regarding "the yellow sky" [ch 007 / 007.07].
*   Azam organized the caravan from Vuerthyl to Parnast, keeping a journal where he expressed skepticism about the party, describing them as "independents" rather than faction agents, though his view softened after Thistle’s night watch thwarted a goblin raid [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.02; ch 008 / 008.03; ch 008 / moment].
*   During the journey, Wallace recounted that Vardis explained the hierarchy of the situation by stating, "Azam belongs to the pseudo-dragon," clarifying that Hsing is the true authority [ch 009 / 009.01; ch 009 / moment].
*   Azam waited in Ragnar’s back room in Parnast for the party’s return from their detour, where he and Hsing confronted Orsik regarding the SEER [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.08].
*   Azam relayed a mission from the SEER to recruit the Ettin Gralm’s forces against Bad Fruul, presenting two magical contracts and whispering a private incentive to Unla Key [ch 014 / 014.01].
*   Azam flew into a rage when Thistle questioned Hsing’s knowledge, shouting about the honor of the Lords’ Alliance and the SEER, before being silenced by Hsing [ch 014 / 014.01; ch 014 / moment].
*   Azam corrected an error regarding rewards promised during the festival, admitting, "it would appear that I misread my notes," while clarifying the terms for potions of healing [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.02].
*   Assuming the party had failed after two weeks of absence, Azam nevertheless paid Unla Key the agreed reward in the main square and attempted to recruit him to the Lords’ Alliance, a pitch that Unla Key rejected with a burp [ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / 016.04; ch 016 / moment].
*   Azam later informed Thistle that his Uncle Joon was located in Triboar and had joined a secretive organization unknown to the SEER [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.04].
*   Azam delivered a message from the SEER stating that protecting Parnast from Bad Fruul was of "paramount importance to the Lords' Alliance," successfully dissuading the party from leaving to find Joon by emphasizing the strategic stakes [ch 017 / 017.04].

## Last Observed State
In the final observed chapter, Azam was physically present in Parnast. He approached the party to inform Thistle that his Uncle Joon was located in Triboar, approximately 400 miles away, having joined a secretive organization unknown to the SEER [ch 017 / 017.04]. Azam acted desperately to dissuade the party from leaving Parnast, delivering the SEER’s message that the defense of Parnast from Bad Fruul was of "paramount importance to the Lords' Alliance" [ch 017 / 017.04]. He assured Orsik that Uncle Joon had settled in Triboar and would be tracked by the Alliance if he left, thereby securing the party’s decision to remain in town [ch 017 / 017.04]. His status remains active and engaged in local diplomatic and logistical efforts on behalf of the SEER.

## Relationships
*   **Hsing:** Azam’s patron. A golden pseudodragon who holds the true authority; Azam defers to Hsing’s nods, sleeps when Hsing sleeps, and is physically silenced by Hsing when he oversteps [ch 007 / 007.07; ch 013 / 013.08; ch 014 / 014.01]. Wallace notes that "Azam belongs to the pseudo-dragon" [ch 009 / 009.01].
*   **The Party (Unla Key, Thistle, Orsik, Vardis, Wallace):** Azam views them with initial skepticism but respects their utility. He manages their contracts, pays their rewards, and attempts to recruit Unla Key to the Lords’ Alliance [ch 008 / entry; ch 016 / 016.04]. He acts as a liaison, providing intelligence (such as Uncle Joon’s location) and framing missions within the broader strategic context of the Lords’ Alliance [ch 017 / 017.04].
*   **The SEER:** Azam claims to act as a messenger and agent for the SEER, invoking the SEER’s power and information network to compel the party’s cooperation and justify the importance of tasks [ch 014 / 014.01; ch 017 / 017.04].
*   **Ragnar Redtooth:** The barkeep of the Golden Tankard in Parnast. Azam frequents his establishment, leaves coin for Ragnar and Elspeth, and uses the tavern’s back room for meetings with Hsing [ch 013 / 013.08; ch 016 / 016.04].
