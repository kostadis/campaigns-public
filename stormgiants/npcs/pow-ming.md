## Identity
Pow Ming is a Shou human who serves as the Chief Operating Officer (COO) of the Grand Dame’s gambling hall in Yartar [ch 078 / entry; ch 078 / 078.04; ch 080 / entry]. He defines his role pragmatically, stating he is "in charge of everything so Lord Drylund can do his job" [ch 078 / entry; ch 078 / 078.04]. Following the party's intervention in the power dynamics aboard the ship, he became a partner in the venture to convert the flying castle into a mobile casino, specifically the *Key and Wing Casino* [ch 080 / 080.06]. He speaks with the accent of a citizen of Shou [ch 080 / entry; ch 080 / 080.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Pow Ming is driven by profit, efficiency, and the logistical advantages of their new venture. He immediately identified that the flying ship allows them to "avoid any taxation and protection fees," viewing the mobile casino as a "tremendous opportunity" he had not previously considered [ch 080 / 080.06]. He is practical and direct, managing entry by asking "How many chips will you be buying?" and directing high-stakes players to the Sava tables [ch 078 / 078.04].

He demonstrates a keen ability to read social cues and adapt to his partners' expectations; upon witnessing Thistle's erratic behavior regarding his eyes, Pow initially attempted to offer a compliment but correctly read the negative body language of the rest of the 3HPP and immediately shifted his expression to look horrified [ch 081 / 081.03; ch 081 / moment]. He is also willing to make concessions for goodwill, agreeing to Unla Key’s demand for naming rights to secure the deal [ch 080 / 080.06; ch 080 / moment]. His moral compass regarding entertainment appears flexible; upon learning the party possessed a script that was actually a demon-summoning ritual, he was delighted by its potential to attract crowds, unaware of the magical danger until Vardis intervened [ch 081 / entry; ch 081 / 081.06]. He operates with a business-first mentality, patiently explaining to Unla Key that rigging gambling to ensure wins would result in financial loss [ch 081 / 081.08; ch 081 / moment].

## Last Observed State
During the departure of the flying castle for the Northern Isles of the Trackless Sea, Pow Ming was aboard, overseeing the launch [ch 081 / 081.08]. He had successfully hired a crew of approximately ten Shou laborers for cleanup and demolition, estimating initial costs at around 1,000 gold, though he presented budget estimates of 50,000 gold for a serviceable casino and 500,000 gold for a grand one [ch 081 / entry; ch 081 / 081.05; ch 081 / 081.06]. He coordinated with the party to prioritize cleaning the castle tower and installing gold trim and a nameplate, acting on instructions from Thistle regarding Esclarotta’s requests [ch 081 / 081.07]. As the ship took off, he answered Unla Key’s request to rig the gambling games, noting, "Yes, but then we would lose money" [ch 081 / 081.08; ch 081 / moment]. Status not established in the summaries beyond this departure.

## Relationships
**Lord Khaspere Drylund:** Pow Ming serves as the operational lead for Drylund’s gambling interests, ensuring the lord can focus on other matters [ch 078 / entry; ch 078 / 078.04]. He facilitated communication between the party and the lord, relaying invitations to private drinks after overhearing party members' intentions [ch 078 / 078.07].

**The 3HPP (Orsik, Thistle, Vardis, Unla Key):** Pow Ming transitioned from casino staff to business partner with the party. He signed on to the mobile casino plan after Orsik proposed it, agreeing to Unla Key’s term of naming it *Key and Wing Casino* in exchange for his participation [ch 080 / 080.06; ch 080 / moment]. He manages the physical transformation of the castle, hiring laborers and seeking direction on budget and priorities from Thistle and the others [ch 081 / 081.05; ch 081 / 081.07]. He maintains a patient, explanatory tone with Unla Key regarding business fundamentals [ch 081 / 081.08].

**Shou Community:** Pow Ming’s loyalty or network appears tied to his heritage; he hired a crew of laborers who all appeared to be from Shou, prompting Thistle to wonder to whom they were personally loyal [ch 081 / 081.06].
