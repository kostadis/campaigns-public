## Identity
Tlega is a large goblin with a nasal, squeaky voice [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.09]. He is a goblin boss/leader who identifies himself by name, though Orsik mocks the pronunciation as "Treasure Chest" [ch 051 / 051.09]. Tlega is visibly moved when his name is pronounced correctly by Thistle [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.09].

## Personality and Motivations
Tlega seeks to remove the Fomorian Maergun from power because Maergun has become abusive rather than remaining merely a "bully" in the traditional goblin social dynamic [ch 051 / 051.09]. He believes that dispatching Maergun is necessary for the tribe's advancement [ch 051 / 051.09]. However, Tlega lacks the resolve to overcome social inertia; when confronted by Mirk with the argument that the Bully/Bullied relationship is core to goblin society, Tlega deflates and agrees only after being distracted by Unla Key’s magic [ch 051 / 051.09]. In combat, Tlega prioritizes self-preservation and command over direct engagement, explicitly stating "We lead!" when questioned about fighting, indicating he views leadership as directing others from safety [ch 052 / 052.01; ch 052 / moment].

## History with the Party
*   Tlega is a large goblin with a nasal, squeaky voice who was introduced to the party by Thistle, who touched on Tlega's pronunciation [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.09].
*   Tlega explained that the Bully/Bullied dynamic with the Fomorian Maergun had failed because Maergun had become abusive rather than just a bully, stating that "It's time for him to go" [ch 051 / 051.09].
*   Thistle confirmed Tlega’s truthfulness to the suspicious Orsik by looking into Tlega's eyes and seeing their soul [ch 051 / 051.09].
*   Tlega initially urged the goblins to assemble and attack, but this was disputed by another goblin boss, Mirk, who argued that the Bullied/Bully relationship was core to goblin society; the argument deflated Tlega until Unla Key used a spell to distract Mirk and Tlega, leading both to agree that dispatching Maergun was the right choice [ch 051 / 051.09].
*   During the assault on Maergun’s throne room, Tlega led the goblins from the rear, shouting "Move ahead. I’m right behind you" to his troops, which caused Orsik to glare and sigh [ch 052 / 052.01].
*   When Tlega shouted "ATTACK!" from the rear during the fight, Orsik questioned if Tlega would fight; Tlega replied "We lead!", prompting Orsik to mutter that this meant from the rear [ch 052 / 052.01; ch 052 / moment].

## Last Observed State
As of Chapter 052, Tlega was present in the corridor outside Maergun’s throne room, where he acted as a rear-guard commander for the goblin forces during the assault [ch 052 / 052.01]. He shouted commands to the party and the goblins, including "Move ahead. I’m right behind you" and "ATTACK!" [ch 052 / 052.01; ch 052 / moment]. He remained in the rear while the party and other goblins fought the Fomorian, Cyclops, and displacer beast [ch 052 / 052.01]. Following the death of the Fomorian, the goblins broke into a celebratory dance, shouting "WE ARE THE CHAMPIONS!" [ch 052 / 052.01]. No summary records Tlega being harmed; he was last seen leading the goblins from the rear during the fight [ch 052 / 052.01].
