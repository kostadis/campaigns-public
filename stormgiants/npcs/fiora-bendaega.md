## Identity
*   **Role**: Fiora Bendaega identifies herself as a "middle person" who connects those needing people with those needing jobs [ch 003 / 003.01].
*   **Appearance/Association**: She is associated with a location referred to as "Nonnie's place," from which she directs people for future work [ch 003 / 003.01].

## Personality and Motivations
*   **Transactional Nature**: Fiora views interactions through a business lens, prioritizing the closure of deals and the interests of her clients (tradespeople) over emotional alignment with the mercenaries [ch 003 / 003.01].
*   **Pragmatic Empathy**: Her attempt to build rapport by calling the Cult of the Dragon "terrible for business" was misaligned with the mercenary Thistle's desire for revenge, causing her approach to backfire [ch 003 / 003.01].
*   **Social Engineering**: She utilizes social leverage to maintain control of situations, such as signaling a guard for security and using Diego’s affection for her to de-escalate threats [ch 003 / 003.01].
*   **Perception Management**: She acknowledges the party's competence indirectly, noting their skepticism about themselves ("poked the ghost") while still closing the deal based on their availability [ch 003 / 003.01].
*   **Professionalism**: She maintains professional distance, explicitly stating that the party is not welcome at her location again, though leaving open the possibility of future business elsewhere [ch 003 / 003.01].

## History with the Party
* Fiora Bendaega is the "middle person" who hired the party for a job on behalf of tradespeople working the black road, offering a one-month window for the task [ch 003 / 003.01].
* When a dwarf approached threateningly during the hiring, Fiora signaled a guard to intervene and whispered to a companion named Diego to approach the group, using his fondness for her to de-escalate the situation [ch 003 / 003.01].
* Fiora winked at Diego when he reached for his sword, causing him to back down, and noted the party's skepticism about their competence ("poked the ghost") before closing the deal [ch 003 / 003.01].
* Fiora was mentioned by an elderly elven woman in Parnast as having promised to find mercenaries for a job involving a merchant named Azam stuck in Vuerthyl with a religious artifact [ch 007 / 007.04].
