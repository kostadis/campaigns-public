## Identity
Harriet Uldinath is a craftsperson in Waterdeep, specifically recognized as a maker of grappling hooks and likely other equipment [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.06]. She operates a shop and has a professional competitor [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Harriet possesses professional integrity and humility; she frankly admitted that a piece of craftsmanship was "better than anything she could make," indicating she values quality and acknowledges limits in her own work [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.06]. She was helpful in an investigation, not only clarifying what she did not make but also directing inquiries to the likely correct source [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.06].

## History with the Party
- Harriet Uldinath was mentioned as having been approached regarding a masterfully made grappling hook, though she was not present in the scene description itself [ch 025 / 025.06].
- She did not recognize the grappling hook and insisted it was not from her shop [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.06].
- She conceded that the hook was better than anything she could make [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.06].
- She pointed the party to her competitor, Ghelryn Foehammer, as the likely maker of the hook [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.06].
