## Identity
Alaestra Ulgar is the co-owner of the Lionshield Coster outpost, which is referred to as Lion's Share in later chapters [ch 024 / 024.11; ch 025 / 025.05; ch 026 / 026.04]. She operates alongside Narth [ch 024 / 024.11; ch 025 / 025.05; ch 026 / 026.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Alaestra appears to be direct and candid, often speaking without considering the strategic implications of her disclosures [ch 024 / 024.11]. During the investigation into the stolen key, she "blurts out" critical information despite Narth’s attempts to silence her, revealing that the thieves had stolen the specific key required for the store’s interplanar supply chain [ch 024 / 024.11]. This tendency towards impulsivity contrasts with Narth, who appears more cautious [ch 024 / 024.11].

Despite her earlier distress over the breach, Alaestra demonstrates firmness in business matters when the situation is resolved. When Orsik attempted to renegotiate the terms of the reward after returning the key, Alaestra and Narth refused to budge, maintaining that the original agreement must be honored [ch 026 / 026.04]. Her motivation during the crisis was primarily the restoration of their disrupted interplanar supply chain [ch 024 / 024.11; ch 025 / 025.05].

## History with the Party
- Alaestra is the co-owner of the Lionshield Coster outpost (referred to as Lion's Share in later chapters), alongside Narth [ch 024 / 024.11; ch 025 / 025.05; ch 026 / 026.04].
- Alaestra was in a state of disarray and distress because the outpost's vault had been breached and their key stolen, which disrupted their interplanar supply chain [ch 024 / 024.11; ch 025 / 025.05].
- The party members Orsik and Vardis (and earlier Orsik alone) investigated the theft; Alaestra revealed the details of the theft to them and negotiated a reward [ch 024 / 024.11; ch 025 / 025.05].
- Alaestra was relieved when the party returned the key to them [ch 026 / 026.04].
- Alaestra and Narth refused to renegotiate the terms after the key was returned, maintaining that the party should honor the previous agreement, and the party used their 20% discount to purchase goods [ch 026 / 026.04].

## Last Observed State
In Chapter 26, Alaestra was at the Lion's Share outpost [ch 026 / 026.04]. She was described as "relieved" and "delighted" when the party returned the stolen key [ch 026 / entry; ch 026 / 026.04]. The evidence does not specify her physical location, health, or actions beyond this interaction. Status not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
*   **Narth:** Co-owner of the Lionshield outpost/Lion's Share. Alaestra operates with him; he appears to take a more reserved or controlling role in communications, as seen when he attempted to stop Alaestra from revealing details about the stolen key [ch 024 / 024.11; ch 025 / 025.05].
*   **Orsik:** A party member who investigated the theft. Alaestra negotiated directly with him, offering a 10% discount initially, which was later raised to 20% plus 50 gold through Orsik’s persistent silent negotiation tactics [ch 024 / 024.11; ch 025 / 025.05]. She maintained a firm stance against him when he later tried to renegotiate the deal [ch 026 / 026.04].
*   **Vardis:** Another party member who accompanied Orsik during the shopping trip that led to the second phase of the investigation; Alaestra was relieved to find him and Orsik to help [ch 025 / 025.05].
