## Identity
Wald is a resident of Parnast who co-owns horses with a villager named Eryn [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.05]. He is identified as one of the owners of the livestock involved in the horse poisoning incident [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Wald displays annoyance or impatience toward dramatic displays, specifically rolling his eyes at Wallace’s announcement that the horses were poisoned [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.05]. His past actions during a previous Greengrass festival are cited by villagers as a potential motive for the poisoning, though the nature of these actions is not detailed in the evidence [ch 017 / 017.06].

## History with the Party
- Wald owns horses with Eryn [ch 017 / entry].
- During the horse poisoning incident, Wald was identified as one of the horse owners alongside Eryn [ch 017 / 017.05].
- Wald and Eryn both rolled their eyes at Wallace's dramatic announcements about the poisoned animals [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.05].
- Wald's actions during a previous Greengrass festival were cited by villagers as a possible motive for the horse poisoning, with some arguing it was retribution [ch 017 / 017.06].

## Last Observed State
Wald was last observed during the horse poisoning incident in the center of Parnast [ch 017 / entry]. He was present when Wallace dramatically declared the animals poisoned and dead, reacting by rolling his eyes alongside Eryn [ch 017 / 017.05]. Status not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
*   **Eryn:** Co-owns horses with Wald and was present during the poisoning incident, where both owners reacted similarly to Wallace's behavior [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.05].
*   **Wallace:** Wald reacted with visible irritation (rolling eyes) to Wallace's dramatic announcements regarding the poisoned horses [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.05].
*   **Villagers of Parnast:** Wald is a subject of local gossip and debate; some villagers argued that the horse poisoning was retribution for his past actions, while others debated his responsibility in the incident [ch 017 / 017.06].
