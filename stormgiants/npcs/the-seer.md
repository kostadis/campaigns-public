## Identity
The SEER is a Shou woman wearing a blue robe who serves as the head of secret police and spymaster for the Lords’ Alliance [ch 013 / 013.08; ch 019 / 019.05]. She is identified as the top Spymaster of the organization, commanding "the spies, assassins, and cut-throats" [ch 013 / 013.08]. While some, like Wallace, imply the SEER could be of any gender ("she, he or they"), the SEER reveals herself physically as a female Shou [ch 014 / moment; ch 019 / 019.05]. She claims to have manipulated events for decades, operating through intermediaries like her emissary Hsing (a golden pseudodragon) and agents like Azam [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.08; ch 014 / 014.01; ch 019 / 019.05]. Iymrith, a chromatic dragon, identifies the SEER as the "real power of the Lords’ Alliance" rather than a mere figurehead [ch 084 / 084.03].

## Personality and Motivations
The SEER operates with a long-term strategic focus, often prioritizing political alliances and stability over the immediate desires or moral qualms of the party [ch 017 / 017.04; ch 083 / 083.04]. She believes in imposing order and utilizes "impeccable" intelligence gathering to exploit the weak points and desires of those she hires [ch 083 / 083.04]. She is confident in her abilities, stating, "I have manipulated events for some time," and relies on divination to locate targets [ch 019 / 019.05; ch 083 / 083.04]. When faced with refusal, she can become furious and threatening, as seen when she warned Orsik that his name would be "cursed for a thousand generations" [ch 083 / 083.04]. Despite this, she is pragmatic and willing to leave conciliatory notes after conflicts to keep future options open [ch 083 / 083.05]. Iymrith describes her as "evil" and committed to imposing her own order on the realms [ch 084 / 084.03].

## Last Observed State
The SEER appeared at the party's camp in Chapter 083 alongside her pseudodragon, Hsing, to propose a deal regarding the giants [ch 083 / 083.04]. After all four party members refused her offers, the situation turned violent; Orsik attempted to strike her, Vardis cast a fireball, and Unla Key trapped her in a forcecage [ch 083 / 083.04; ch 083 / 083.05]. The SEER was protected by a sanctuary spell during the initial assault and countered the fireball [ch 083 / 083.05]. She escaped the forcecage moments later, presumably using *time stop* and teleportation, and left a physical note for Orsik advising him to consider that defeating Iymrith might be a favor to the realms [ch 083 / 083.05]. Status not established in the summaries following this escape; she is absent in Chapter 084, where Thistle considers calling her but does not do so [ch 084 / entry].
