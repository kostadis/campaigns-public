## Identity
Oblivillish is a sprite, styled Sir Oblivillish or Lord Oblivillish, who serves as the Zhentarim’s agent within the court of Prince Thornacious [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.07; ch 014 / entry]. He previously held the position of Chief of Staff to Prince Thornacious before his exile [ch 014 / entry; ch 016 / 016.02]. He was exiled and cursed by Prince Thornacious for killing a stag the Prince considered his own, a sentence he describes as "double exile" because Queen Titania had already sent the Prince’s court into exile [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.06]. His punishment manifested as a malfunctioning invisibility that flickers between visible and invisible states [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / moment].

## Personality and Motivations
Oblivillish is haughty and deeply concerned with noble status, stating, "I am noble. People grovel to me," and initially refusing to grovel to regain his position [ch 015 / 015.01; ch 015 / moment]. He maintains a strong self-image as a competent intelligence operative, asserting, "I am a great spy," and using his skills to infiltrate the ettin Gralm’s encampment [ch 015 / 015.03]. He is sensitive to how he is perceived professionally; he became outraged when referred to as a "master hunter," clarifying to Prince Thornacious, "We had clarified this point that I am not a hunter!" [ch 019 / 019.08]. His primary motivation during his exile was returning to his position with glory, for which he was willing to command others to attack perceived enemies and accept dubious narratives regarding the death of the Prince's stag [ch 015 / entry; ch 016 / 016.01].

## History with the Party
*   Oblivillish was introduced to the party as the Zhentarim's agent in Prince Thornacious’ court; the Zhentarim had lost contact with him in the Forgotten Forest and asked Orsik to find him [ch 013 / 013.07].
*   The party located Oblivillish in the Forgotten Forest, where he was identified as the sprite spy Orsik was assigned to protect [ch 014 / 014.06].
*   Oblivillish explained that he had been exiled and cursed with flickering invisibility after killing a stag that Prince Thornacious considered his own [ch 014 / 014.06; ch 014 / entry].
*   Oblivillish admitted to killing the stag by accident and, at Thistle’s instruction, practiced groveling to regain the Prince’s favor, despite his noble pride [ch 015 / 015.01].
*   Oblivillish provided intelligence that he had previously worked for the ettin Gralm until the party eliminated his goblin workforce, which Gralm likely viewed as a cause for his termination [ch 015 / 015.01].
*   Oblivillish successfully infiltrated Gralm’s encampment by posing as a survivor of a goblin group, wearing goblin attire and talking the party past the guards [ch 015 / 015.03].
*   During the audience with Gralm, Oblivillish presented the party as allies who had saved him from the agents of Bad Fruul [ch 015 / 015.04].
*   Oblivillish advised the party that Gralm’s refusal to ally would result in violence against the Fey if they did not resolve the conflict regarding the disruptive Fey music in Gralm’s area [ch 015 / 015.06].
*   Upon encountering Blasius the Satyr, Oblivillish attempted to attack him, citing Blasius as the reason for his banishment, but was deflected by Thistle and Orsik and subsequently put to sleep by Unla Key [ch 015 / 015.07].
*   During the battle with the Unseelie dryads and creatures, Oblivillish claimed he was "framed," asserting that a lion and bear had actually eaten the Prince's stag [ch 016 / 016.01; ch 016 / moment].
*   Oblivillish was restored to his position as Prince Thornacious’ Chief of Staff after the Prince accepted the narrative that the Unseelie were responsible for the stag’s death [ch 016 / 016.02].
*   Oblivillish expressed outrage when Thistle referred to him as a "master hunter" while seeking aid against the mists, clarifying his non-hunter status to the Prince [ch 019 / 019.08].
*   During the battle against the mammoth, Blasius privately blamed Oblivillish for his presence in the fight, viewing it as retribution for Blasius’s earlier indiscretion [ch 022 / 022.10; ch 022 / 022.11].

## Last Observed State
Oblivillish was present at the battle against the mammoth in Chapter 22. During the fight, Blasius the Satyr privately blamed Oblivillish for his presence in the battle, viewing it as retribution for a past indiscretion [ch 022 / 022.10; ch 022 / 022.11]. The evidence notes that "sprites" (plural) poisoned the mammoth but did not put it to sleep, though it does not specify if Oblivillish was among them or acting alone [ch 022 / 022.10; ch 022 / 022.11]. Status not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
*   **Blasius the Satyr:** Blasius inadvertently caused Oblivillish's exile by drunkenly accusing him of killing the Prince's stag [ch 015 / 015.07]. Oblivillish harbored a violent grudge against Blasius, attempting to attack him upon reunion [ch 015 / 015.07]. After the false narrative of the Unseelie killing the stag was established, the two reconciled, hugging and calling each other "dear friend and brother" [ch 015 / 015.08; ch 016 / 016.02]. However, during the battle with the mammoth, Blasius viewed his participation as revenge against Oblivillish [ch 022 / 022.10].
*   **Prince Thornacious:** Oblivillish is the Prince's Chief of Staff and former spy [ch 016 / 016.02; ch 014 / 014.06]. He was exiled by the Prince for the stag incident but was restored to his position after the Prince accepted the Unseelie narrative [ch 016 / 016.02]. Oblivillish is deferential to the Prince, correcting his own status when asked about his hunting skills [ch 019 / 019.08].
*   **The Party:** Oblivillish relies on the party to regain his position, accepting Thistle’s plan to infiltrate Gralm’s camp and later accepting the group's narrative about the stag's killers to return to service [ch 015 / 015.01; ch 016 / 016.02]. Orsik initially considered killing Oblivillish upon learning he was the Zhentarim spy, but recognized him as his protectee after re-reading his mission orders [ch 014 / 014.06]. Thistle and Vardis assisted in the deception regarding the stag's death [ch 016 / 016.01].
