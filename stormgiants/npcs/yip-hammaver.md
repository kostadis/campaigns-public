## Identity
Yip Hammaver is a 32-year-old bachelor of House Hammaver [ch 039 / entry]. He is noted for possessing "the strangest red eyes" [ch 039 / 039.07].

## Personality and Motivations
Yip acts as a social guide and advisor within the noble circles of Mirabar, specifically befriending those integrated into high society [ch 039 / 039.07]. He provides tactical intelligence on potential adversaries, though his assessments may lack precision; he advised Orsik that Tack Proudfoot Esquire was a dandy who had "read one too many romance novels," while noting the political danger posed by Tack's betrothal to Clan Battlehammer [ch 039 / 039.07]. Yip appears to value social propriety and avoidance of awkward political consequences, such as the fallout of an avenged death [ch 039 / 039.07]. He possesses a dry wit, evidenced by his justification for Tack's combat prowess: "Yes, for a noble knight of the elite guards" [ch 039 / moment].

## Last Observed State
Yip was last observed in Chapter 039 during a party hosted by House Hammaver and the subsequent duel between Orsik (disguised as John Husteem) and Tack Proudfoot Esquire [ch 039 / 039.07]. ## Relationships
Yip befriended Orsik, who was attending under the disguise of John Husteem [ch 039 / 039.07]. Yip maintained a cordial but advisory relationship with Orsik, warning him about social pitfalls and the combat capabilities of mutual acquaintances [ch 039 / 039.07]. Yip spoke of Tack Proudfoot Esquire to Orsik, characterizing him as a romantic dandy who is nevertheless a formidable opponent due to his status as a knight of the elite guards [ch 039 / 039.07; ch 039 / moment]. Yip noted Tack's political connection to Clan Battlehammer through betrothal [ch 039 / 039.07].
