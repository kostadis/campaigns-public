## Identity
Duke Zalto is the leader of the Fire Giants [ch 032 / 032.05]. In the hierarchy of giantkind, the title "Duke" is the specific designation for Fire Giant leaders, distinct from chiefs (Hill), thanes (Stone), and counts (Cloud) [ch 032 / 032.05]. He rules from a stronghold named Ironslag [ch 062 / 062.05]. He is one of the "evil giant lords" whose plans threaten the realm [ch 046 / 046.07].

## Personality and Motivations
Zalto seeks to expand his dominion over all giants, driven by a desire for "Power, glory, and the favor of their gods" so that he may be elevated to the top when the Ordning is remade [ch 027 / 027.09; ch 046 / 046.07; ch 047 / 047.01]. To achieve this, he is scouring the lands for an artifact called the Vonindod, a weapon capable of killing dragons [ch 027 / 027.09; ch 032 / 032.05; ch 039 / 039.09]. He commands a tribe of fire giants that operates in coordination with other giant factions, including Hill, Stone, and Frost giants [ch 032 / 032.05; ch 039 / 039.09]. His ambition is viewed with disdain by other powerful entities; for instance, the storm giants Esclarotta and Blagothkus dismissed his ambitions as "absurd" [ch 058 / 058.04].

## Relationships
**Brimskarda**: Zalto’s duchess. She oversees the kitchen at Ironslag, wears a gown made from a young black dragon’s hide, and commands smoke mephits and goblins [ch 062 / 062.06].
**Hell Hounds**: Zalto keeps a pack of hell hounds, which he plays with using a metal ball containing a body [ch 062 / entry; ch 062 / 062.05].
**The Fire Giants**: He is the supreme ruler of this giant tribe. His soldiers search for the Vonindod and operate under his direct command [ch 027 / 027.09; ch 032 / 032.05; ch 039 / 039.09].
**King Hekaton**: Zalto is an adversary to the Storm Giant King. Hekaton intends to bring Zalto to his court to force him to kneel, viewing him as one of the lords who must be defeated or convinced to restore the Ordning [ch 082 / 082.05].
**The 3HPP (Party)**: The party views Zalto as a primary target. Thistle attempted to poison Zalto’s table during a heist at Ironslag but failed due to a disturbance [ch 062 / moment]. The party is aware that Zalto holds Laela [ch 046 / 046.07] and possesses the Vonindod [ch 062 / 062.05].
