## Identity
Darz Helgar is the caretaker of the Northern Campgrounds cottage in Triboar [ch 024 / entry; ch 025 / entry]. He is an ex-criminal from Waterdeep who served ten years in the Waterdeep dungeon [ch 024 / entry; ch 025 / entry]. He was formerly a member of the Coffin Nail, a secretive thieves' guild in Waterdeep that had informants in the city guard [ch 026 / 026.01]. Darz also identifies himself as a member of the Zhentarim, confirmed by a specific hand signal [ch 026 / 026.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Darz is pragmatic and transactional in his worldview. He confessed to stealing from the Lionshield Coster not out of malice, but because he was blackmailed by Kaelen Sarssir into doing so [ch 024 / 024.11; ch 025 / 025.07]. His primary motivation at the time was survival and the avoidance of his past being revealed; he was "tired of running" [ch 024 / 024.11]. He accepted a bargain with Orsik for silence about his criminal history in exchange for information on the Thieves' Guild and revenge against Kaelen [ch 024 / 024.11; ch 025 / 025.07].

Darz maintains a detached, observational perspective on his own circumstances. Upon being resurrected, he analyzed who deserved credit for his return: Orsik ("likes me, not because he needs more information"), Cindell Trueshot ("simply 'what you do'"), and Darathra ("she raised me, and I like living") [ch 027 / 027.10]. This transactional mindset extends to gratitude; he repaid the party by giving up a lucrative bounty tip (the location of the dwarf Weevil, worth 5000 gold) which he had been saving for his own retirement, stating, "I am old, and you guys did bring me back from the dead" [ch 027 / 027.11]. He views life through a lens of debt and exchange, noting he would have retrieved a buried artifact himself if he had known about it [ch 027 / 027.10].

## Last Observed State
Darz was killed during the battle in Triboar, lying dead near the campground entrance [ch 027 / 027.01; ch 027 / 027.02]. Orsik prioritized raising him from the dead, and Cindell Trueshot performed the resurrection [ch 027 / 027.06; ch 027 / 027.07; ch 027 / 027.10]. Darz is alive [ch 027 / 027.10; ch 027 / 027.11].

Following his resurrection, Darz met the party for breakfast at the Talking Troll the next day [ch 027 / 027.11]. During this meeting, he revealed the location of a wanted dwarf named Weevil, who was cleaning stables in Xantharl's Keep, and then left [ch 027 / 027.11].

## Relationships
*   **Orsik:** Darz entered a coercive alliance with Orsik, trading information about the Thieves' Guild and the Zhentarim for silence regarding his past crimes and the promise of revenge against Kaelen [ch 024 / 024.11; ch 025 / 025.07]. They exchanged Zhentarim hand signals [ch 026 / 026.01]. Orsik was motivated to resurrect Darz, feeling "wrong about" leaving him dead [ch 027 / 027.06]. Darz perceives Orsik as liking him for his own sake, rather than just for information [ch 027 / 027.10].
*   **Cindell Trueshot:** Cindell raised Darz from the dead, ostensibly for a discount to strengthen community ties, though Darz notes her act was simply part of her nature [ch 027 / 027.07; ch 027 / 027.10].
*   **Darathra:** Darz acknowledges Darathra's role in his resurrection, thinking, "she raised me, and I like living" [ch 027 / 027.10].
*   **Kaelen Sarssir:** Darz harbored intense animosity toward Kaelen, who ran the Talking Troll and blackmailed Darz into the theft. Darz begged Orsik to "get that bastard; get him good!" [ch 024 / 024.11].
