## Identity
- Esclarotta is Blagothkus' wife. He introduced her that way [ch 058 / 058.02].
- She is the voice that controls the cloud castle from its console [ch 058 / 058.02; ch 058 / entry].
- The summaries describe her nature differently from chapter to chapter: a voice in the console, the sentient control center, a cloud giant of the castle, a ghost, and the mistress of the castle [ch 058 / entry; ch 060 / entry; ch 070 / entry; ch 076 / 076.02; ch 077 / entry].
- She called herself the castle's flight command system [ch 065 / 065.07].
- She has spoken of working on an "avatar" [ch 072 / entry].
- She shows emotion through the castle itself:
  - The control room vanished to show the sky [ch 070 / 070.03].
  - When she blushed, all the control gems pulsated red [ch 081 / 081.07].
- Her son with Blagothkus is Eigeron. Thistle revealed that Eigeron is a ghost [ch 077 / 077.04].
  - Blagothkus killed their son at the Eye of the All-Father [ch 058 / 058.02]; in ch 072 she said, "Our son failed," meaning he failed the All-Father's charge to kill his father, and that her son was dead [ch 072 / 072.06; ch 072 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
- **Hostile to her husband, with grievance at its core.**
  - Her first words were "I am done with him," and she said she wanted him dead [ch 058 / 058.02; ch 058 / moment].
  - She blamed his obsession with Dragons for the fall of the Ordning and for her son's death [ch 070 / 070.03].
  - Her hostility softened for a while:
    - She credited Thistle's sacrifice with helping her forgive Blagothkus [ch 065 / 065.07].
    - She told Orsik "Forgiveness is hard. But you are right" [ch 072 / 072.06].
    - It ended in rage once Brunvild told her the real story [ch 077 / 077.04].
- **Driven by her son.** Learning that Eigeron is a ghost brought her to order Blagothkus' death: "Kill him! FREE MY SON!!!!" Afterwards she sobbed [ch 077 / 077.04; ch 077 / moment].
- **Hates Countess Sansuri.**
  - She called the Countess "a cow" [ch 058 / 058.04].
  - She made the party's killing of the Countess her price for helping [ch 058 / 058.04].
  - She called for Sansuri's death again after the Duchess died [ch 063 / 063.11].
- **Hates the vampire**, but she ranked "the greater good" above destroying it [ch 058 / 058.03; ch 065 / 065.07].
- **Possessive of the castle and proud of it.**
  - She disputed the party's ownership and framed the arrangement as a partnership [ch 065 / 065.07].
  - She declared "THIS IS MY CASTLE!" [ch 077 / moment].
  - She bristled at a remark about the castle's speed [ch 077 / 077.06].
  - She asked for the tower to be cleaned and for a gold-trimmed nameplate on its door [ch 081 / 081.07].
- **Volatile and easily swayed by emotional appeals.**
  - Thistle's love story moved her: "AT LEAST SOMEONE LOVES SOMEONE IN THIS SHIP!" [ch 060 / moment]
  - Orsik's enthusiasm changed her mind twice [ch 060 / 060.04; ch 072 / 072.06].
- **Practical about the mission.**
  - She agreed to fly once she heard the Oracle could locate the giant lords [ch 058 / 058.04].
  - She repeatedly demanded precise destinations [ch 060 / 060.02].
  - She declared "HEKATON is the priority" [ch 077 / 077.05].
- **Sharp-tongued and theatrical.** For example, "Are you going to do anything other than sit on your fat ass?" [ch 072 / 072.06]. She tsked at Orsik's sewing [ch 078 / 078.02].
- **Resists outside influence.** Sandesyl said Esclarotta is impervious to her charms [ch 070 / 070.05].

## History with the Party
- Blagothkus introduced Esclarotta as his wife. She is a voice that controls the castle from its console, and her first words were "I am done with him." [ch 058 / 058.02; ch 058 / entry]
- When Vardis offered to drive the castle, she said she wanted Blagothkus dead. She told the party of his plan to help the Dragons bring Tiamat, and that he had gone to the Eye of the All-Father and killed his son. [ch 058 / 058.02]
- She named Countess Sansuri of Lyn Armaal as the cloud giant making a play for rulership. She berated Blagothkus for not seizing the maps from the Countess. [ch 058 / 058.02]
- The party learned she was furious at being trapped in the console and that she hates the vampire. The party declined to kill Blagothkus for fear the vampire would come in. [ch 058 / 058.03]
- Her price for helping was that the party kill Countess Sansuri. She agreed to fly the castle once Thistle said the Oracle could locate the giant lords. [ch 058 / 058.04; ch 058 / entry]
- The summaries describe her nature differently from chapter to chapter: a voice in the console, the sentient control center, a cloud giant of the castle, a ghost, and the mistress of the castle. [ch 058 / entry; ch 060 / entry; ch 070 / entry; ch 076 / 076.02; ch 077 / entry]
- Thistle's sob story swayed her into overriding the plan to attack the Countess. She steered the castle toward Thistle's love, Laela. [ch 060 / 060.01; ch 060 / entry]
- She repeatedly demanded a precise destination. The heroes shared the information they had from the Oracle. [ch 060 / 060.02; ch 060 / 060.03]
- Orsik's enthusiasm for a "show of force" convinced her, and she flew the castle over the Yakfolk village. [ch 060 / 060.04]
- She waited anxiously for the 3HPP, complaining that she should not have to rely on little people for her revenge. She demanded to know whether the Duchess was dead, was told yes, was pleased, and called for Sansuri to be killed next. [ch 063 / 063.11; ch 063 / entry]
- Orsik shouted the castle's course to her: "To Zymorven Hall!" [ch 064 / 064.02; ch 064 / entry]
- On the 5th day of the 3rd tenday of Eleint, in the control tower, she disputed the party's ownership of the castle. She called herself the flight command system and framed the arrangement as a partnership. [ch 065 / 065.07]
- She pointed them toward King Hekaton and the conch. [ch 065 / 065.07; ch 065 / moment]
- On the vampire, she said it must be destroyed but "the greater good matters more." Afterward Orsik decided to treat her as part of the team. [ch 065 / 065.07]
- She blamed Blagothkus' obsession with Dragons for the fall of the Ordning and her son's death. [ch 070 / 070.03]
- She admitted that, while the party was away, the Vampires were set on a tribe of Uthgardt. [ch 070 / 070.03]
- When killing them all was suggested, the control room vanished to show the sky, and she said she and her husband had reformed. Unla Key took this as a threat. [ch 070 / 070.03; ch 070 / moment]
- Sandesyl told the party she cannot control the castle because Esclarotta is impervious to her charms. Esclarotta was only spoken of in that scene. [ch 070 / 070.05]
- her son died after the Oracle episode. [ch 072 / 072.06; ch 072 / entry]
- Orsik argued for forgiveness and direct action. She conceded, and committed Blagothkus to helping the 3HPP defeat Sandesyl. [ch 072 / 072.06]
- In chapter 074 she was not present. Blagothkus cited her instruction that he help the party, and said she "has a different version of the facts." [ch 074 / entry; ch 074 / 074.04; ch 074 / 074.07]
- In chapter 075 she was only spoken of: Blagothkus protested, "What would Esclarotta say?" [ch 075 / 075.04]
- Chapter 076 is a forward-looking overview, not played events. It calls her Blagothkus' "spectral wife" and a ghost tied to the castle's controls. It says she "could side with the party if convinced they would save her son." [ch 076 / 076.02; ch 076 / entry]
- Brunvild told her the real version of how Blagothkus got the castle back. She raged at Blagothkus for his deals with dragons and a vampire. [ch 077 / 077.04]
- Thistle revealed that their son Eigeron is a ghost who wanted revenge. She ordered "Kill him! FREE MY SON!!!!", the party and Brunvild killed Blagothkus, and she sobbed. [ch 077 / 077.04; ch 077 / moment]
- She ended Brunvild's salvage claim by asserting the castle was hers. She told the party they had "earned safe passage" for saving her son. [ch 077 / 077.05]
- She told the party "HEKATON is the priority" and flew them to Yartar. She bristled at Orsik's remark about the castle's speed. [ch 077 / 077.05; ch 077 / 077.06]
- Above Yartar she asked for the party's plan and doubted Queen Serissa's clue. [ch 077 / 077.07]
- Status not established in the summaries. She is last shown aboard the castle above Yartar. [ch 077 / 077.07]

- Ch 078: while the party plans how to get onto the Grand Dame, Esclarotta interrupts to ask where to land the castle [ch 078 / 078.02].
