## Identity
Maergun is a Fomorian giant who held a position of authority among the local goblin tribe, previously functioning within a "Bully/Bullied" social dynamic [ch 051 / 051.09]. He maintained a newly finished throne room characterized by smooth walls and a massive throne, where he kept the sword Drauchanar hanging from a chain and owned a displacer beast used as a pet [ch 052 / 052.01]. Maergun was known to possess supernatural abilities, specifically attempting to use an evil eye on opponents during combat [ch 052 / 052.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Originally fulfilling a role as a "bully" in the tribe's social structure, Maergun's behavior escalated to become "abusive," which caused the breakdown of his relationship with the goblins [ch 051 / 051.09]. Despite his reputation for wickedness and deformity as a Fomorian [ch 052 / 052.01], Maergun displayed affection for his displacer beast, referring to it as a "puppy" and becoming distraught when it was killed [ch 052 / 052.01]. He possessed an aggressive demeanor, questioning the audacity of those confronting him by shouting, "Who do you think you are?" [ch 052 / 052.01].

## History with the Party
*   The goblins report that Maergun, previously a bully, has become abusive, causing the Bully/Bullied dynamic to fall apart; Tlega states, "It's time for him to go." [ch 051 / 051.09]
*   Following a dispute between goblin bosses Tlega and Mirk, which was resolved by Unla Key casting *Jim's Glowing Coin*, the goblins agree that dispatching Maergun is the right course of action for the tribe. [ch 051 / 051.09]
*   The 3HPP and the goblins, led by Tlega and Mirk, enter Maergun’s newly finished throne room to confront the Fomorian. [ch 052 / 052.01]
*   During the battle, Maergun attempts to use an evil eye on Vardis, who avoids the worst of the effect but suffers damage from Maergun’s club. [ch 052 / 052.01]
*   Maergun shouts, "You killed my puppy!" after Vardis’s *Fireball* inadvertently kills the displacer beast (named Haerbega/Spiky/Fluffy), leading to a brief argument over the pet's name. [ch 052 / 052.01]
*   Maergun is left dying by Orsik’s attacks with the Giant Sword of Lord Zymorven, and is finished off by Vardis with a cantrip. [ch 052 / 052.01]

## Last Observed State
Maergun was killed in his throne room [ch 052 / 052.01]. He sustained significant damage from the 3HPP and goblins, including attacks from Orsik using the Giant Sword of Lord Zymorven, which left him dying [ch 052 / 052.01]. Vardis delivered the final blow with a cantrip, completing Maergun's death [ch 052 / 052.01]. Status: Deceased [ch 052 / 052.01].

## Relationships
*   **Tlega:** The goblin boss declared "It's time for him to go" because Maergun's abuse had destroyed their social pact [ch 051 / 051.09]. Tlega led the forces against Maergun but remained in the rear during the final confrontation [ch 052 / 052.01].
*   **Mirk:** Another goblin boss who initially argued against removing Maergun, viewing the Bully/Bullied dynamic as essential to goblin society, but agreed to his dispatch after being distracted by *Jim's Glowing Coin* [ch 051 / 051.09]. Mirk participated in the battle, cheering when Maergun was injured [ch 052 / 052.01].
*   **Displacer Beast (Haerbega/Spiky/Fluffy):** Maergun's pet. He mourned its death from *Fireball* as if it were a puppy, engaging in a dispute with the party over its name [ch 052 / 052.01].
*   **3HPP (Vardis, Orsik, Thistle, Thuul):** Maergun was their direct antagonist in Chapter 052. He attacked Vardis with an evil eye and club, and was ultimately killed by Vardis [ch 052 / 052.01].
