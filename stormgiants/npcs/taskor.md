## Identity
Taskor is the individual who killed the Wizard of the Crag in a duel [ch 012 / entry; ch 012 / 012.04]. This duel is recorded in the Wizard's ledger as an event intended to address the Wizard's debts [ch 012 / entry; ch 012 / 012.04]. Taskor's action resulted in the loss of the funds required to maintain the Wizard's castle [ch 012 / 012.05].

## Personality and Motivations
The evidence does not establish Taskor's personality, personal motivations, or specific reasoning for engaging in the duel beyond the financial context provided by the ledger [ch 012 / 012.04].

## History with the Party
*   Taskor killed the Wizard of the Crag in a duel, an event linked to the Wizard's debts [ch 012 / entry; ch 012 / 012.04].
*   Taskor was mentioned in the memory of Elquen, the wight, who learned of the Wizard's death via a guard's shout that "Taskor killed him!" [ch 012 / 012.05].
*   Taskor was mentioned by the Wizard's manservant, who clarified that the Wizard died in a duel with Taskor and consequently lost the funds to maintain the castle [ch 012 / 012.05].
