## Identity
Nym is a Storm Giant, the second-eldest daughter of King Hekaton and sister to Mirran and Serissa [ch 066 / 066.01; ch 069 / entry]. She is a musical performer, playing whalebone mallets on the shell of a living crab to accompany her sister Mirran's singing in the great hall of Maelstrom's citadel [ch 066 / 066.01]. The Imperator Uthor describes her as "cold and unloving," a judgment rendered by King Hekaton years prior when he declared Mirran and Nym unfit to rule [ch 069 / 069.07].

## Personality and Motivations
Nym holds open contempt for smaller races, explicitly identifying her attitude as racism rather than fetishism and stating, "Giants are superior to little people" [ch 066 / 066.02]. She is politically ambitious and resentful, having allied with the blue dragon Adimond Kroskas after being furious at losing the throne to Serissa [ch 069 / 069.08]. Her behavior is characterized by cruelty and deception; she taunts Serissa with nicknames for her dwarf suitor, claims missing party members were "well digested" by giants to cover up murders, and screams about "loose ends" while attempting to hide the deaths of giant lords [ch 066 / 066.02; ch 067 / 067.02; ch 067 / 067.04]. She displays a lack of affection or loyalty, described as "cold" by authority figures, and resorts to performative apologies or feigned happiness when confronted by powerful family members or the Imperator [ch 069 / 069.04; ch 082 / 082.04].

## Relationships
*   **Mirran:** Nym's sister and musical partner; they consistently act in concert to undermine Serissa and were both judged unfit to rule by their father [ch 066 / 066.01; ch 067 / 067.03; ch 069 / 069.07].
*   **Serissa:** Nym's younger sister and Queen. Nym and Mirran view Serissa as a rival, mocking her affection for small folk and allying against her [ch 066 / 066.02; ch 069 / 069.08].
*   **King Hekaton:** Their father. Nym attempted to deceive him upon his return with feigned happiness, but he subsequently accused her of betrayal [ch 082 / 082.04].
*   **Adimond Kroskas:** Nym allied with the blue dragon after he offered magical weapons, joining forces with Mirran to oppose Serissa's rule [ch 069 / 069.08].
*   **Nym's Whale:** A killer whale guardian that attacks intruders, complaining that Nym "promised better tasty snacks," and identifies itself as her favorite pet [ch 068 / 068.04].
