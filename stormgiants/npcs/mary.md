## Identity
There are two distinct entities named Mary in the campaign evidence; they are not the same character.

1.  **Mary (Human NPC):** An older woman in Everlund who identifies herself as "Miery," a name reflecting the goddess Mielikki, and explicitly states "not Mary" [ch 029 / 029.08]. She is the head of an organization called the "Fighters of the Light" [ch 049 / entry; ch 081 / 081.01]. Party members Vardis and Thistle refer to her as Mary, though she insisted on the spelling "Miery" [ch 049 / 049.03; ch 081 / 081.01].
2.  **Mary (Giant Elk NPC):** A female giant elk, the wife of Harry [ch 054 / entry]. This Mary is a separate individual from the human organizer in Everlund [ch 054 / 054.02].

## Personality and Motivations
**Mary/Miery (Human):**
*   **Motivation:** She is driven by a belief that the Kraken will return to destroy Everlund using "powerful tentacle and mind control" [ch 029 / 029.08]. Her goal is to recruit members to the "Fighters of the Light" to combat this threat [ch 029 / 029.08].
*   **Method:** She utilizes financial barriers for recruitment, charging a 5 gold piece membership fee and offering a certificate [ch 029 / 029.08]. However, she is willing to waive the fee in exchange for labor, specifically to recover an artifact of "purely symbolic value" [ch 029 / 029.08].
*   **Demeanor:** She acts as a street-corner prophet warning of ancient evil [ch 029 / 029.08]. She is dismissive of technical details or questions regarding spelling, ignoring Unla Key’s query about the word "light" [ch 029 / 029.08].

**Mary (Giant Elk):**
*   **Personality:** She presents as polite and apologetic, contrasting with her husband Harry’s rude behavior [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / 054.02].
*   **Social Norms:** She enforces civility within her marriage, scolding Harry for speaking ill of neighbors ("the Joneses") [ch 054 / 054.02; ch 054 / moment].
*   **Attitude toward Outsiders:** She expresses concern about whether the party has permission to dig in their area [ch 054 / 054.02]. Despite this, she and Harry eventually assist the party by pushing a stone out of the way after being persuaded [ch 054 / 054.02; ch 054 / 054.03].

## History with the Party
*   An older woman in Everlund warned of the Kraken and recruited the party to her group, the "Fighters of the Light," offering a waiver on the 5 gold piece membership fee in exchange for recovering the star of light from kobolds; she introduced herself as "Miery – not Mary," named after the goddess Mielikki [ch 029 / 029.08; ch 049 / entry].
*   Vardis recalls that Mary was the head of the "Fighters of the Light" and that the party took the quest to avoid paying the 5 gold piece fee [ch 049 / 049.03; ch 081 / entry].
*   A female giant elk named Mary, Harry's wife, assisted the party by pushing a stone out of the way after scolding Harry and questioning the party's right to dig [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / 054.02; ch 054 / moment].
*   Thistle remembers that Mary sent them on a "goofball quest" for an artifact (a trinket) located in the Nether Mountains [ch 081 / 081.01].

## Last Observed State
