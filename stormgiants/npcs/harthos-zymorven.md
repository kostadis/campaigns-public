## Identity
- **Name and alias:** Harthos Zymorven, also called "Lord Zymorven" [ch 027 / 027.12; ch 034 / 034.02].
- **Station:** a wealthy knight who lives at Zymorven Hall on the Rauvin Road, northwest of Silverymoon [ch 027 / 027.12].
- **Past:** a former adventuring companion of Urgala [ch 027 / 027.12]. The ch 065 entry calls him "Urgala's ex" [ch 065 / entry].
- **Family:** father of Harthal Zymorven, who is a separate character. Do not merge the two [ch 033 / 033.06; ch 034 / entry].
- **The sword:** he owned a giant slaying longsword and gave it to the party. Orsik carries it [ch 027 / 027.12; ch 034 / 034.06; ch 034 / 034.02; ch 066 / 066.05].
- **Capabilities:**
  - He has scrying magic, which warned him of the party's approach [ch 064 / 064.03].
  - He met them in full armour and called himself "the guard of the road" [ch 064 / 064.03].
  - He fought with sword and bow against Bob's zombie hordes [ch 065 / 065.01; ch 065 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
- **Honours debts:**
  - He paid Thistle the reward for his son's rescue [ch 034 / entry; ch 034 / 034.02].
  - He acknowledged the debt and joined the party's effort [ch 064 / 064.03; ch 064 / entry].
  - He later declared the debts between the families cleared [ch 065 / 065.03].
- **Prefers proper channels:**
  - He wanted to warn the city's leaders [ch 064 / 064.03].
  - He wanted Kaelen interrogated rather than killed [ch 064 / 064.08].
  - He asked whether the party would tell the Lord of Silverymoon before attacking [ch 064 / 064.09].
- **Conscience about civilians:**
  - He was briefly outraged that living citizens were caught in the bag-of-holding blast [ch 065 / 065.01].
  - He kept fretting over the disappeared people [ch 065 / 065.02; ch 065 / 065.03].
- **Protects his reputation and deflects blame:**
  - He praised Orsik's ruthlessness but insisted "Orsik and Orsik alone made that decision" [ch 065 / 065.03; ch 065 / moment].
  - He shrieked at the idea of admitting to fire spells and supplied an official story that blamed Bob [ch 065 / 065.03; ch 065 / entry].
  - He promised to spread word of the party's "courageous, heroic sacrifice" [ch 064 / 064.09].
- **Temperament:**
  - He reacted to Thistle's account with escalating alarm that turned to good humour [ch 034 / 034.02].
  - He was horrified by the vampire and the Dracolich [ch 064 / entry].
  - He is long-winded and resists being cut off [ch 065 / 065.03].
  - He quietly left the scene when talk turned to race [ch 065 / 065.05].
- **Credulous:** he was taken in by Orsik posing as "Orsik's father" [ch 064 / 064.03; ch 064 / moment]. He sympathised with that persona over covering up a son's failings [ch 064 / 064.09].

## History with the Party

- **Ch 027: the sword lead (mentioned only).** Urgala tells the party he will part with the sword "if you drop my name and tell him that you plan to defeat giants and save the realms." [ch 027 / 027.12]
- **Do not confuse with Harthal.** His son is Harthal Zymorven, a separate character. [ch 033 / 033.06; ch 034 / entry]
