## Identity
Mirran is the eldest daughter of King Hekaton and the sister of Queen Serissa [ch 066 / entry; ch 067 / entry]. The Imperator Uthor identifies her as "tempestuous and prone to emotional outbursts," noting that she was deemed unfit to rule by King Hekaton years prior to his disappearance [ch 069 / 069.07].

## Personality and Motivations
Mirran is ambitious but prone to public displays of frustration and pouting, such as stamping her foot to demand the throne in front of the Imperator and Queen Serissa [ch 067 / 067.04]. She employs deception and manipulation, stalling suitors with mock warmth [ch 066 / 066.02] and luring enemies into traps with feigned kindness [ch 066 / 066.03]. She is quick to accuse others of treason, falsely labeling the party as assassins to justify violence [ch 066 / 066.04; ch 069 / 069.04]. Her desire for power led her to ally with the blue dragon Adimond Kroskas, accepting his magical weapons to support her claim against Serissa [ch 069 / 069.08].

## History with the Party
- Mirran is the eldest daughter of King Hekaton and sister to Queen Serissa [ch 066 / entry; ch 067 / entry].
- When Orsik claimed to be Serissa’s matched suitor, Mirran stalled him with mock warmth, claiming Serissa was busy with state affairs and needed time to prepare [ch 066 / 066.02].
- Mirran later approached the party in the guestrooms, answering Vardis’s question about the room’s design by attributing it to her mother, Queen Neri [ch 066 / 066.03; ch 066 / moment].
- Mirran lured the party to a cavernous room with a pool and teleportation circle, where she revealed four giant lords and shouted in Giant that the party were assassins, commanding the giants to kill them [ch 066 / 066.03; ch 066 / 066.04].
- During the resulting battle, Mirran swiped at Orsik but missed, and before departing, she hurled a lightning bolt as a "parting gift" that struck Vardis and Unla Key [ch 066 / 066.05; ch 066 / entry].
- Thistle warned the party not to kill Mirran, reasoning that her death would make the Queen believe the sisters' claim that the party were assassins [ch 066 / moment].
- After the party hid the bodies of the giant lords they defeated, Mirran descended to the Portal Chamber, found it empty, and incorrectly assumed the lords had killed the party and fled to spread rumors about Serissa [ch 067 / 067.02].
- In the throne room, Mirran pouted and stamped her foot, insisting to Imperator Uthor and Queen Serissa that the visitors were assassins and that she and her sister Nym should be Queen instead of Serissa [ch 067 / 067.04].
- Mirran allied with Nym and the blue dragon Adimond Kroskas, who provided them with magical weapons to support their claim to the throne [ch 069 / 069.08].
- When the party exposed Adimond’s identity, Mirran charged into the throne room and claimed the party had been trying to assassinate Queen Serissa [ch 069 / 069.04].
- Imperator Uthor dismissed Mirran’s claims, ordered guards to take her and Nym to their quarters, and later provided context stating that Mirran was "tempestuous and prone to emotional outbursts" and had been deemed unfit to rule by King Hekaton years prior [ch 069 / 069.04; ch 069 / 069.07].
- Upon King Hekaton’s return, Mirran falsely exclaimed her happiness to see him alive, only to be corrected by Unla Key [ch 082 / 082.04].
- King Hekaton learned of Mirran and Nym’s betrayal and alliance with a dragon, ordering them to go to their chambers while he decided their fates later [ch 082 / 082.04].

## Relationships
*   **King Hekaton:** Mirran is his eldest daughter. He previously deemed her unfit to rule [ch 069 / 069.07]. In Chapter 082, she betrayed him by allying with a dragon, resulting in his command for her to go to her chambers [ch 082 / 082.04].
*   **Queen Serissa:** Mirran’s younger sister and the current Queen. Mirran consistently undermines Serissa, claiming the sisters should be Queen instead [ch 067 / 067.04; ch 069 / 069.08]. Serissa noted that Mirran and her sister Nym allied with Adimond after being furious about the succession [ch 069 / 069.08].
*   **Nym:** Mirran’s sister and co-conspirator. They act in concert, stalling suitors [ch 066 / 066.02] and claiming the throne together [ch 067 / 067.04; ch 082 / 082.04]. Nym was observed screaming at Mirran about "loose ends" regarding the missing party members [ch 067 / 067.02].
*   **Adimond Kroskas:** A blue dragon who allied with Mirran and Nym, providing them with magical weapons to support their claim to the throne [ch 069 / 069.08].
*   **The Party (3HPP):** Mirran deceived Orsik regarding his suitor status [ch 066 / 066.02], led the party into an ambush by the giant lords [ch 066 / 066.04], and accused them of being assassins on multiple occasions [ch 066 / 066.04; ch 069 / 069.04].
*   **The Killer Whale:** A whale in the Maelstrom regards Mirran as its "pet," bringing her back to its pool when she wanders into awkward spaces without water [ch 068 / 068.06].
