## Identity
Hyuth Kolstaag is a 55-year-old retired adventurer and magic user [ch 036 / entry]. He is described as having a paunch, grey hair, and "crow's eyes" [ch 036 / entry]. He is a wealthy resident of Triboar, though his wealth has not translated into social acceptance [ch 036 / entry; ch 036 / 036.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Hyuth is characterized as whiny, screeching, and paranoid [ch 036 / entry]. His primary stated motivation is self-preservation, expressed through the belief that "Everyone is out to kill me" [ch 036 / entry]. He is considered self-centred, arrogant, and lacking in class [ch 036 / 036.02; ch 036 / 036.05]. While he could be relied upon in combat, he is viewed by former peers as a "pain-in-the-ass" who brings trouble that makes others feel unsafe [ch 036 / 036.05]. He is notably stingy and uncaring regarding the collateral damage his enemies cause; he refused to pay for a stolen horse or support the family of a murdered stable hand [ch 036 / entry; ch 036 / 036.02; ch 036 / 036.05].

## History with the Party
- Hyuth Kolstaag is a 55-year-old, tired man with a paunch, grey hair, and crow's eyes who is described as whiny, screeching, and paranoid; he believes "Everyone is out to kill me" [ch 036 / entry].
- He is a rich retired adventurer and magic user plagued by enemies; a few weeks prior to this chunk, assassins attempted to kill him, and the town woke to "peals of thunder and blasts of lightning" [ch 036 / entry; ch 036 / 036.02].
- During the assassination attempt, Hyuth summoned gargoyles to protect himself, but he refused to pay for the horse stolen from the Cart and Coin and refused to buy a table at a luncheon for the murdered stable hand's family [ch 036 / entry; ch 036 / 036.02; ch 036 / 036.05].
- Cindell states that Hyuth rejected every invitation she extended to introduce him into Triboar society and has been "a frightfully bad presence for the town" [ch 036 / 036.02].
- Urgala describes Hyuth as a self-centred adventuring magic user who could be relied on in a fight but now brings "the kind of trouble that makes people feel unsafe" [ch 036 / 036.05].
- Cindell presents a contract to the party to deal with Hyuth, noting that Lord Protector Darathra Shendrel has only warned him with "notices and stern words" [ch 036 / 036.02].
- Thistle presented the contract to Urgala, noting that Cindell asked the party to kill Hyuth, which caused Urgala to question if they "should kill him" before demuring [ch 036 / 036.05].

## Last Observed State
The evidence confirms Hyuth was a target of an assassination attempt a few weeks prior to Chapter 36, during which he summoned gargoyles for protection [ch 036 / 036.05]. As of Chapter 36, he lives in Triboar and is the target of a contract on his life that Cindell offered the party [ch 036 / 036.02; ch 036 / 036.05]. He is currently under the watch of Lord Protector Darathra Shendrel, who has only issued warnings regarding his behavior [ch 036 / 036.02].

## Relationships
*   **Cindell:** Cindell holds a deep grudge against Hyuth, describing him as "a frightfully bad presence for the town" and stating he rejected all her social invitations [ch 036 / 036.02]. She actively hires the party to "deal with" him, viewing his removal as a political tool to discredit Lord Protector Darathra Shendrel [ch 036 / 036.02].
*   **Darathra Shendrel:** The Lord Protector of Triboar has dealt with Hyuth only via "notices and stern words" [ch 036 / 036.02]. Cindell cites Hyuth’s unresolved presence as evidence of Darathra’s "ineffectual leadership" [ch 036 / 036.02].
*   **Urgala:** Urgala knows Hyuth from his adventuring days, recalling him as self-centred but reliable in a fight [ch 036 / 036.05]. She is distressed by his current behavior, noting that his lack of responsibility "Makes all of us retired adventurers look bad" [ch 036 / 036.05].
*   **Thistle:** Thistle is aware of the contract to kill Hyuth and presents it to Urgala [ch 036 / 036.05]. He lies to Urgala about not knowing the details of Hyuth's recent trouble with assassins [ch 036 / 036.05].
