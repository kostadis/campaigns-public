## Identity
Delsephine is the captain of the *Dragon’s Breath*, an airship commanded by a crew of masked cultists [ch 047 / 047.02]. She serves Klauth, the red dragon of the North, delivering his offers and executing his logistical support for the party [ch 047 / 047.02; ch 053 / entry]. The ship features sleigh runners and a giant red balloon, with a hold stocked with climbing gear and other supplies for sale [ch 047 / 047.02; ch 053 / 053.06]. Her crew members include Nyzroth, Brassik, Laz, Oriskus, Perella, Tralt, and Zalthia [ch 047 / 047.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Delsephine is pragmatic and driven by opportunity. She left the Cult of the Dragon because Dracolich research was shelved in favor of securing loot for a ritual, seizing Klauth’s offer instead [ch 047 / 047.03; ch 047 / entry]. She holds contempt for former cult leader Severin, viewing him as a fool who trusted unreliable creatures [ch 047 / 047.03]. While she displays a barely hidden sexual attraction to Klauth, she remains professional regarding the dragon’s strategic interests, noting that dragons view giants as obstacles to dominion [ch 047 / entry; ch 053 / 053.05].

She is sharp and observant but keeps her own counsel, seeing through deceptions such as Orsik’s fake aging but choosing not to reveal them, indicating she understands the value of second chances [ch 052 / 052.05; ch 052 / entry]. She is firm in boundaries, refusing to leave the ship to join ground expeditions [ch 052 / 052.09]. She negotiates aggressively for her patron, valuing contracts based on necromantic implications and securing upfront payments for buyouts [ch 053 / 053.07]. She exhibits a detached, sometimes impatient demeanor regarding the party's delays, such as sighing at the lack of artifact progress during the dig at Morgur’s Mound or ordering her crew to give a dirty Orsik space when he refuses to clean up [ch 054 / 054.05; ch 054 / 054.07]. She fears being mistaken for a participant in crimes, such as moving the ship away from Raven Rock to avoid being viewed as anything other than a bystander [ch 053 / 053.04].

## History with the Party
*   Delsephine is introduced as the captain of Klauth's airship, the Dragon’s Breath, commanding a crew of masked cultists. She delivers Klauth’s offer of passage across the Spine of the World, urges the party to slay the giant leaders, and bids them to his vale for a reward. The party accepts the offer and requests transport to Everlund; Delsephine expresses uncertainty about how this aids their mission before ordering her crew to set sail [ch 047 / 047.02].
*   During flight, Thistle questions Delsephine about the Cult of the Dragon. She reveals she left the cult when Dracolich research was shelved in favor of securing loot for a great ritual, having seized Klauth’s offer instead. She expresses contempt for the former cult leader Severin and notes Klauth’s support for the party’s plan, as dragons view Giants as obstacles to domination over the North [ch 047 / 047.03; ch 053 / 053.05].
*   When the party returns aged Orsik to the ship, Delsephine waits on the portal side and sees through Orsik’s claim to be his father. She smiles beneath her mask and keeps the secret, understanding the concept of second chances [ch 052 / 052.05].
*   Delsephine firmly refuses Orsik’s invitation to join the party on the ground, cutting off the suggestion with a definitive no [ch 052 / 052.09].
*   At Raven Rock, Delsephine sells climbing gear to the party for 40 gold and moves the Dragon’s Breath closer to the extraction point, fearing the local Ravens might view her as a participant rather than a bystander [ch 053 / 053.04; ch 053 / 053.06].
*   Delsephine explains the "payout clause" in Brassik’s contract to Orsik, noting Klauth is a necromancer and the contract covers two lives (living and undead). She values the living portion at 1300 gold. Unla Key negotiates a deal involving residuals capped at 6000 gold with a 500 gold upfront payment, which Delsephine accepts [ch 053 / 053.07].
*   While the party digs at Morgur’s Mound, Delsephine comments "Still no artifact" when they find non-target items. She offers to take Orsik somewhere to clean himself after five days of digging; when refused, she orders her crew to give the dirty dwarf space [ch 054 / 054.05; ch 054 / 054.07].
*   Upon arriving at Parnast, Delsephine asks for Brassik’s location; upon learning he sought other work, she drops the party on the upper tower of the castle [ch 056 / 056.07].
*   Before landing at the Cloud Giant Castle, Delsephine shouts "So are you going?" and subsequently maneuvers the Dragon’s Breath to drop Orsik and Vardis near the tower, avoiding the zombies in the courtyard [ch 057 / 057.01; ch 057 / 057.03].
*   After the party negotiates feeding locations with the vampire Sandesyl, Delsephine informs the party that Klauth wishes them luck in ending the war of the giants [ch 058 / 058.05].

## Relationships
*   **Klauth:** Delsephine serves the red dragon, relaying his offers, negotiating contracts on his behalf, and acting as his logistical arm [ch 047 / 047.02; ch 053 / 053.07]. She harbors a "barely hidden deep sexual attraction" to him [ch 047 / entry; ch 047 / 047.03].
*   **The Party (3HPP):** Delsephine acts as their transport and supplier. She finds their requests occasionally opaque, such as their choice of Everlund or their operational security excuses, but fulfills them [ch 047 / 047.02]. She maintains a professional distance, refusing to join them on the ground [ch 052 / 052.09]. She observes their personal dynamics, such as amused interest in Unla Key’s wings [ch 053 / 053.02] and annoyance at Orsik’s hygiene or frustration [ch 054 / 054.05; ch 054 / 054.07].
*   **Brassik:** Delsephine holds Brassik’s contract, which is for two lives (living and undead), and manages his terms for Klauth [ch 053 / 053.07]. She facilitates his buyout when the party negotiates to hire him [ch 053 / 053.07].
*   **Severin:** Former cult leader whom Delsephine despises, blaming him for misdirecting the cult’s resources and trusting failed agents [ch 047 / 047.03].
*   **Orsik:** She sees through his deceptions, such as his "father" disguise, and keeps his secrets [ch 052 / 052.05]. She expresses impatience with his grumpiness and dirtiness on the ship [ch 054 / 054.07].
