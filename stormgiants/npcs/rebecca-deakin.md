## Identity
Rebecca Deakin is an archivist in the cataloging department of Wyrmworks, characterized by her unparalleled mastery of dragon-related topics [ch 048 / entry; ch 055 / entry]. By Chapter 086, her role has expanded; she serves as an advisor to the Lords of Silverymoon [ch 086 / entry; ch 086 / 086.03]. She explicitly defines her professional methodology as traditional: "I open books. I do research. I use my brain" [ch 055 / 055.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Rebecca is outspokenly anti-magic and critical of magical institutions. She believes that "Silverymoon must abandon magic" and describes the Spellguard as "a crypto-fascist tool of the mages and a force of control" [ch 055 / entry; ch 055 / 055.05]. She views wizards as treating non-magic users as lesser and is critical of the Wizard school's out-of-towners and plague-creating experiments [ch 055 / 055.05]. She distinguishes her hostility toward these institutions from individuals, clarifying that her antagonism is directed at "the Silverymoon and the Spellguard" rather than people like Unla Key [ch 055 / 055.05].

She is cynical regarding the dangers of dragon-related research, assuming that anyone investigating figures like Adimond Kroskas would be dead [ch 055 / 055.05; ch 055 / moment]. In her later role as an advisor, she displays skepticism toward the party's competence and motives, suggesting they might be responsible for recent events rather than believing they uncovered the truth independently [ch 086 / 086.03].

## Relationships
