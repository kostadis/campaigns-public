## Identity
Feetor is a mud mephit bound to the swamp [ch 009 / entry]. He speaks in a folksy register, using phrases like "coupl’a day" and "Most’a we mud-folks" [ch 009 / entry]. He refers to himself variously as a "dumb swamp creature" (a persona he adopts when first approaching the party) [ch 009 / 009.02], "number one Mud Mephit" [ch 009 / moment], and the "New king of the swamp" (a title Unla Key attributes to him, though he also claims to be Unla Key's "#2") [ch 010 / 010.07].

## Personality and Motivations
Feetor is characterized as opportunistic, theatrical, and quick to find an angle [ch 009 / entry]. His primary motivation is survival and status through association with the strongest power in the swamp. He decided to join the party immediately upon realizing they had the power to unhide the grabby water monster, stating, "A new power! I must join this new power!" [ch 009 / moment]. He actively maneuvers to secure his own position within the party's hierarchy, turning a negotiation about mud mephits into a personal promotion ("number one Mud Mephit") and a gold coin [ch 009 / 009.05]. He is sensitive to being stereotyped, taking offense when Orsik calls him an expert, retorting, "You tokenizing me? Not every mud mephit knows everything about every other mud mephit" [ch 009 / moment]. Feetor is also a gambler and broker; he organized bets among the mephits regarding the new king and fronted special mud to Unla Key to facilitate the wager [ch 009 / 009.07]. When faced with conflict or failure, he attempts to defuse tension with gifts (special mud, a summoning stone) and titles ("honorary Mud Mephit") [ch 010 / 010.07].

*   To defuse tension over the payment in mud, Feetor declares Unla Key an "honorary Mud Mephit" and gives him a stone to summon mephit help [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.07].
*   Feetor asks to be Unla Key's "#2" and proclaims the civil war ended with everyone following Unla Key, but the mephits immediately begin squabbling over other leaders (Orsik, Vardis, Thistle), restarting the conflict [ch 010 / 010.07].

## Last Observed State
Feetor was present with the party after they successfully killed the remaining half-ogres at the fire [ch 010 / 010.01]. He paid Unla Key’s betting winnings in "special mud" instead of gold, claiming it "heals you from bad wounds" [ch 010 / 010.07]. To placate the party and the bubbling mephits, he declared Unla Key an "honorary Mud Mephit" and gave him a stone to summon mephit help [ch 010 / 010.07]. Following this, Feetor proclaimed the civil war over and declared all mephits followers of Unla Key [ch 010 / moment]. However, this claim failed immediately as the mephits began squabbling over other potential leaders (Orsik, Vardis, and Thistle), reigniting the civil war [ch 010 / 010.07]. Feetor shrugged and said, "oh well" as the conflict resumed [ch 010 / 010.07]. His specific whereabouts or status after this resurgence of the civil war are not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
*   **Unla Key:** Feetor views Unla Key as a new power worth befriending. He fronted Unla Key special mud for a wager and later paid out in the same substance [ch 009 / 009.07; ch 010 / 010.07]. He named Unla Key an "honorary Mud Mephit" and sought to be his "#2" subordinate, a request Unla Key granted with a sigh [ch 010 / 010.07]. Unla Key was initially crestfallen at the payment in mud and proposed a duel bet to resolve the dispute [ch 010 / 010.07].
*   **Orsik:** Feetor approached Orsik's party after they defeated the grabby water monster. He was offended when Orsik referred to him as an "expert," feeling tokenized [ch 009 / 009.05]. He negotiated a neutrality deal with Orsik, receiving a gold coin in exchange for brokering a "wait and see" policy among the other mephits [ch 009 / 009.05]. He pitched the overthrow of Mama Manyknuckles to Orsik [ch 009 / 009.03].
*   **Vardis:** Feetor pitched the idea of the party becoming "King of the swamp" to the group, which Vardis acknowledged favorably ("Three days as the King. Not so bad, eh?") [ch 009 / 009.03]. Later, Vardis demanded the original gold contract rather than mud, warning the mephits that the deal had been altered [ch 010 / 010.07].
*   **Thistle:** Feetor provided information to Thistle, who used it to locate Mama Manyknuckles' hideout after correcting a map error [ch 009 / 009.04]. During the confrontation over payment, Thistle shouted to stop the party from fighting the mephits, allowing Feetor to defuse the situation [ch 010 / 010.07].
*   **The Mephits:** Feetor is a peer and leader among the mud mephits. He brokered their neutrality by claiming the title "number one Mud Mephit" [ch 009 / moment]. He organized betting pools on the outcome of the party's attack [ch 009 / 009.07]. His declaration of Unla Key as the new leader failed to hold the faction together, leading to renewed squabbling [ch 010 / 010.07].
*   **Mama Manyknuckles:** Feetor identified her as the leader of a band of half-ogres who mocked him for being fat and muddy [ch 009 / 009.03]. He avoided her camp but pitched her defeat to the party to resolve the swamp's civil war [ch 009 / 009.03].
