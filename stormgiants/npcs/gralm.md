## Identity
Gralm is a two-headed Ettin, approximately nine feet tall, who rules a military encampment in the Lonely Moor [ch 015 / 015.03; ch 015 / entry; ch 015 / moment]. He commands a force of Hobgoblins and Goblins [ch 014 / 014.01]. He resides in a hut over thirty feet tall with a floor made entirely of skulls, seated upon a ragged wooden throne while wearing a loincloth and deerskin boots [ch 015 / 015.03]. The two heads possess distinct personalities: the head with fancifully braided blonde hair is patient and formal, while the bald head is aggressive and easily agitated [ch 015 / 015.04; ch 015 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Gralm’s primary motivation is the cessation of sleep deprivation caused by Fey music expanding into the Forgotten Forest, which keeps the bald head awake and furious [ch 015 / 015.04; ch 015 / entry]. Consequently, Gralm harbors a deep hostility toward the Fey, viewing their presence as an intrusion that disrupts his rest and territory [ch 015 / 015.04; ch 015 / 015.06]. He opposes Bad Fruul and recognizes that Bad Fruul’s power wanes as Gralm’s waxes, leading him to seek an alliance against the ogre [ch 015 / 015.06]. Gralm values strength and endurance, evidenced by his requirement for "feats of strength and drinking" to prove worthiness, which include contests involving ogres, boar lifts, wall destruction, and drinking games [ch 015 / 015.05]. He is pragmatic regarding Ettin law, relying on magical contracts to enforce terms due to the poorly developed nature of Ettin legal customs [ch 014 / 014.01]. Once his conditions are met, he is hospitable, inviting guests to celebrate with his homemade brews, such as Pixie Sangria and The Everyday Ettin [ch 015 / 015.05; ch 016 / 016.02].

## Relationships
**Azam and the Lords’ Alliance**: Gralm is a target of Azam’s diplomatic efforts, with Azam providing the magical contracts intended to secure an alliance or non-aggression pact against Bad Fruul [ch 014 / 014.01].
**Bad Fruul**: Gralm is currently being pressured by Bad Fruul to join an army intended to destroy Parnast, a move Gralm resists [ch 014 / 014.01; ch 015 / 015.06].
**The Fey**: Gralm views the Fey as adversaries due to their music disrupting his sleep and Prince Thornacious expanding his sphere of control into Gralm’s area [ch 015 / 015.04; ch 015 / 015.06]. He hunts Fairies for food [ch 014 / 014.01].
**Oblivillish**: Oblivillish previously worked for Gralm until his group of goblins was killed, ending his employment [ch 015 / 015.01]. Oblivillish later used a cover story involving this history to facilitate the party’s audience with Gralm [ch 015 / 015.01; ch 015 / 015.03].
**Grindle**: Gralm commands a massive ogre named Grindle, whom he orders around during contests and who expresses disappointment when the party outperforms him [ch 015 / 015.05].
