# Chapter 49 - Killing a dragon

Date: about 6 Eleasis 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
The chapter opens with Vardis and a deflated "Well that was awkward." His master plan to obliterate the army of kobolds he was sure was hiding in a chamber was foiled by the fact that only a single kobold was hiding there, and worse, his arrogance had caused him to fall into a pit. After clambering out, he picked over the charred remains of the kobold, and a shiny idol of Tiamat piqued his interest.

Outside, Orsik shouts, "hey, let's go down this passageway." Vardis groans, since that would mean squeezing for him. Through the twisty passage Orsik keeps pointing out the shoddy workmanship and how Dwarves do a better job — "No Dwarf would ever make a mess of things this way." — while Vardis tries hard to stifle a yawn. At an intersection Orsik shouts, "right hand rule." They follow it and re-emerge into the same large area they had left, just some 20 or so feet further down. "What a waste of time."

Then Vardis hears a very specific, unmistakable sound, and remembers that Orsik had said, and Thistle had repeated, that the Nether Mountains was home to blue dragons. "That's a blue dragon," mutters Thistle. Vardis tries to remember why he is here. When they had first arrived in Everlund, they stumbled across an old lady called Mary who was shouting that the Kraken would return to destroy the city with its powerful tentacle and mind control, and that it would come with allies more powerful than anything they had seen before. She was head of an organization called the "Fighters of the Light", which had a 5 GP membership fee. "We were broke," he remembers, and then thinks, "I am about to fight a blue dragon to save 5 Gold." Then he says it: "Seriously, saving 5 gold?"

Orsik answers that "there are three things of value in these mountains, granite, dragon hoards and Netherese Artifacts. The first is the most valuable, but the last the most powerful magic items in the realms. If there is a chance." Vardis asks, "So what's the plan." While the two of them start to sketch one out, Thistle shouts, "Pro or anti-Tiamat?" The dragon roars. Thistle reaches for his bow — "you're dead." When Vardis whispers to ask what the dragon said, Thistle mutters, "Tiamat for-ever!" "I guess we are doing this." "Yes, we are," says Orsik, readying to attack.

The battle opens with Thistle flying off, telling Thuul to advance on the dragon, and firing two shots that hit. The dragon roars in pain just in time to be stung with a squirt of acid from the guard drake. Orsik dashes to the dragon and slashes at it four times, making it bleed while sheathing it in booming energy. Before Vardis can act, two kobolds seemingly blink into existence and attack. Seeing its situation as precarious, the dragon disengages and flies from the eastern most part of the enormous cavern 80 feet to the middle, just before the cavern turns south.

Unla Key is pleased that the dragon is just inside Fireball range — but as he reaches for the components, Orsik shouts, "hey dude, you cast all of your third and fourth level spells." "Huh?" "Yeah." "Well what am I supposed to do with these first and second level spells?" "Kill Kobold." Unla Key visibly pouts and casts sleep. Thistle orders Thuul forward and fires two arrows at the follower of Tiamat, both of which miss. Vardis shouts, "it's always the light cleric." The fireball hits, but it is a glancing blow; the dragon dodges most of the damage. Orsik, having run towards the dragon, throws his hand axes from more than 30 feet away and hits once. The dragon howls, breathes lightning on Orsik — the bolt courses through his body and reduces his strength in half — and flies west, out of their field of vision.

Unla Key and Vardis find themselves surrounded by kobolds that keep blinking into existence. The wizard shouts, "good thing it's just kobolds" as he unleashes Rime's binding ice, killing four of the materialized creatures. Thistle flies towards the dragon but cannot see it, so rather than dash into combat he finds a nook to hide in, ready to fire if the dragon appears. Orsik, unable to reach the dragon, spots a corridor the group had ignored that should drop him behind it. The dragon, seeing everyone sneaking around, investigates and spots Thistle; Thistle sees it and fires an arrow, which misses. The dragon breathes on Thistle, but this fairy has faced a lot of dragons and knows what to do.

At the other side of the cavern Unla Key shouts, "running out of 2nd level spells." Vardis shouts back, "magic missile?" Three kobolds die. Another light blinks, and a kobold materializes and critically misses. Thistle moves closer to where the dragon was and hides. Orsik emerges from the tunnel and hits the dragon with a Fire Bolt, leaving Thistle staring, shocked: Orsik had a perfectly good bow that could have hit the Dragon twice, but wanted to use a spell.

Bloodied and bruised, the dragon shouts in common, "do you think we could reach an accommodation?" Orsik growls, "No." "I just felt I should ask." "What a polite dragon." "But still no?" "Yes." Vardis overhears and mutters, "he came out charging at us." Unla Key corrects him: Thistle had asked the dragon about Tiamat, the Dragon answered poorly, and Thistle attacked. Vardis, missing an attack on a kobold with his sword, agrees: "right."

The Dragon flies south into a cave with a 60 foot steep climb and hides deep inside. Orsik knows this is its last stand — there is nowhere else for it to run — and charges up the hill to within five feet of the top. Thistle, too far away to get into position, flies closer and stays hidden. The kobolds keep coming at Unla Key and Vardis: one of the dog like creatures charges Unla Key and misses, another strikes at Vardis and misses, another snores. Unla Key has had enough and runs towards the dragon, and Vardis kills, with his bow, a kobold about to strike Unla Key.

The Dragon laughs and strikes Orsik with a lightning bolt, but Orsik dodges the bulk of the blast. The Dragon stops laughing: it is holding on by a thread with only 6 HP, while Orsik hangs by another thread of 8 HP. Thistle, certain the Dragon is dead, was counting his chickens before they hatched — he misses twice. Orsik glares: "twice?" Vardis, keeping an eye on the combat, mutters, "three times?" The dwarf, who had planned to use his spell, decides this is not the time, rushes the last five feet, and swings his sword at the dragon, killing it. "Hallelujah!"

Orsik is breathing heavily and Thistle fires a few more arrows into the body as the cleric and magic user race towards them. Unla Key arrives at the dragon's corpse and smiles: "Time to extract the most valuable body parts." Thistle and Orsik head back to the dragon's hoard, almost 300 feet away, and find 2,000 gp, fourteen gems worth 250 gp each, a potion of mind reading, a bag of beans, a potion of superior healing, and a peculiar gem in the shape of a star. By the time they return, the bloodied Unla Key is admiring his carving: dragon meat, dragon blood for potions of dragon strength, dragon teeth for dragon arrows "for our favorite fey", dragon bone for dragon weapons, and a dragon horn for a dragon wand. Unla Key shouts, "well shall we continue?" Orsik looks at the bloody mess, at how barely he survived, and at the spell casters out of spells, and announces, "nah, we got what we came for."
