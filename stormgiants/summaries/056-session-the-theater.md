# Chapter 56 - The Theater

Date: 24 to 28 Eleasis 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
Hidden from sight by the invisibility potions that Orsik provided, the 3HPP stand before the double doors of the Royal Theater. Each door has a small, grilled view port with a shutter blocking the view beyond, and the door is decorated with magical runes made of many thousand small mosaic pieces. Vardis asks, "Where is everyone?" Orsik agrees it is odd that there are no other patrons: "where did all those patrons that bought the tickets and mobbed us go? Are we too early?" The answer comes back: "No, we arrived 5 minutes before the play started."

Orsik moves boldly to the door and opens it. As he is about to enter he notices a glyph, which the 3HPP turns off — but he does not notice the alarm spell that had been cast. Its shrill sound turns the audience towards the door. Orsik sees that one audience member is a suppurating, bloody mess with one eye missing; Vardis sees another clad in blood-soaked peasant clothes, clearly having suffered horrendous wounds before death; Thistle stares at a gaping wound in a rib cage exposing desiccated organs. They all think, "Zombies." The theater has two levels: on the first, a stage at the end with a raised red curtain, benches arranged to give the zombies an excellent view, and a staircase right next to the door.

On the stage stands Bob Saget, dressed in a purple jacket, purple tie and purple pants. He shouts, "We have guests? Could it be the Three Half Pints and a Pint?" Hearing nothing, he says, "Well, it doesn’t matter," clicks his fingers, and the doors close behind the 3HPP. He warns, "You can still enjoy the show; if you are here to disrupt it, you might want to think twice." Several drow knights and two cambions come from backstage and stand in front of him, blocking the zombie patrons' view; the zombies don't seem to mind.

Bob then explains himself. "The fool Kaelen’s summoning spell brought me here." The 3HPP, he says, thought he would bring joy and light to this world, and profit — "But that was boring." His tone turns to rage: "Who wants to bring joy and light? I am a demon. I cause chaos and mayhem!" Then, conspiratorially, he reveals that he has been casting his magic to twist the folks of Silverymoon against the Spellguards, whom he calls their fascist oppressors, while working on his own little spell that needs victims: "why not have four aspiring thespians pretend to be the 3HPP and die trying to kill me?"

Four terrified, crying, screaming commoners are brought out, dressed like the 3HPP — one in black and carrying a stick with a fairy attached to it. Breaking the fourth wall, Bob tells the audience, "I love the authenticity of their terror." He tut-tuts at the screaming faux-Orsik, then, almost taunting his uninvited guests, asks, "Do you want to take their parts?" The 3HPP stay silent. Bob mocks the victims: "They come into Silverymoon, and they run afoul of the Spellguard and nobody cares if they disappear." He spins in a circle and claps his hands: "I am not killing people. I am solving a problem for the city." After every performance, he announces, "the cast joins the audience and passes judgment on their performance!"

The crying cast members are pushed on stage. A gnome dressed like Unla Key waves his hands frantically trying to cast something, but he is no spell caster; Bob laughs and sticks his claw through the gnome's chest. The Tabaxi dressed like Orsik charges and swings his sword. Bob shouts, "I’ve been injured. I am going to die," the Tabaxi smiles — and Bob kills him, saying gleefully, "No, I am not." The 3HPP can watch no more and close their eyes. When they open them there are four corpses on the stage. A Drow Mage animates them using some kind of device, and the sad victims join the audience. Bob laughs and leaves the stage.

Horrified, the 3HPP leave the theater. Standing invisible outside, they see a blue shimmer appear, and then their fans march out — delighted, excited and very alive. Orsik understands: "He’s using the Theater to conduct his bloodthirsty ritual, and he’s creating some kind of demi-plane to send the theatergoers to."

Each of them sits silently and wonders what to do. Unla Key thinks, "I wanted to attack right then and there. Now, I don’t." Orsik decides the people of Silverymoon did nothing for him; he remembers vividly how the Knights treated him, while forgetting what he did to deserve it. Vardis will do what his best friend wants — Silverymoon is not an elven problem. Thistle wrestles with himself: "This is awful! I must do something!" — then, "But I also have to save my sweetie!" — "We have 30 days!" — "Can I really make that trade off?" — and finally decides, "I am the only one who can save her, nobody else. I will save her and then deal with this."

Vardis mutters, "I don’t think we could take Bob. He’s too powerful. We need allies." The reply: "We seem to have accumulated enemies, not allies." Unla Key quibbles, listing the Harpers, the Zhentarim and the city of Silverymoon as unfriendly, but also the city of Everlund and the Hand of Yartar — "We have friends. And I have a fireball. And I can assure you that this is not how the world ends." Orsik suggests, "We could tell the Lord of this city?" but they realise it is unclear whether the Lord would think them innocent of these crimes. Asked "What’s the vote?", they all agree to deal with this secretly: first the giants, then Bob. And with that, they turn to Unla Key — "Let’s get you that tower." / "A flying tower?" / "A flying castle!"

The following day the 3HPP are once again the celebrated heroes of Silverymoon, and Bob Saget is once again the toast of the town, his play continuing its fantastic run. The 3HPP, however, know the evil they have unleashed on Silverymoon.

On Eleasis 8th day, 3rd tenday, reaching Parnast, Orsik, Thistle and Unla Key recognise the castle as the Dragon's Breath floats past: the same castle that buzzed them so many months ago on their quest on the black road — the quest that set Orsik on his peculiar journey of redemption and led to the 3HPP’s uncompromising hate towards the Lords’ Alliance and its head of secret police, the SEER. The castle was also the home of Blagothkus, who triggered the chaos of the Ordning and allied himself with Tiamat to destroy the world of the small people; the cult used his tower to transport treasure until the First Faction stopped him and secured the castle from Blagothkus and a Dracolich. As they near it, Delsephine asks, "Where is Brassik?" Orsik answers, "He decided to look for work elsewhere as their play wasn’t going to have that rewrite we were hoping for." With that, she drops them on the upper tower of the castle.
