# Chapter 39 - Downtime in Mirabar

Date: about 20 to 25 Flamerule 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
The chapter opens with Fred and George's report on the fighting at Xantharl's Keep. Kaltivar's attack on the keep, they write, "was a ruse": the plan was to draw off the main force of the guard while a detachment of bugbears went over the walls to secure a prisoner named Worvil Forkbeard, who goes by the alias "The Weevil." The main attack was thwarted thanks to the support of a group known as the Three Halfpints and a Pint, or 3HPP. The 3HPP had wanted to leave before the battle ended and demanded the gates be opened, but the report's authors stood their ground and insisted they wait. The 3HPP had "a raucous debate": one member insisted they leave at once because his lordship Narbeck Horn would be duped by The Weevil into calling it a case of mistaken identity. The authors chose not to tell them that His Lordship Horn would know The Weevil by the distinctive braiding of his beard, "A braiding that all shield dwarfs can recognize." The Lieutenant arrived in time to convince the 3HPP to wait, and "no further events occurred."

On the Long Road North to Mirabar, Orsik keeps staring at The Weevil, who plainly wants to tell him something. Finally Orsik ungags the prisoner. The Weevil offers treasure for his freedom; Orsik answers, "You are worth 5000 gold to me." The Weevil knows him by name, and when Orsik asks, "You know me?" the reply is "Who doesn't." Orsik notices Vardis staring at him and feels momentarily bad that his good friend the elf is a relative unknown while he himself is infamous. The Weevil makes his pitch: "I know how we can isolate a caravan from the mines of Mirabar and rob it. There is a secret pattern that I happen to have figured out. All I need is a crew, and I promise we will make a fortune." Orsik reflects that the other Orsik, "the less intelligent one that had been a disgrace," might have taken the offer, but this Orsik, "the Captain of the Guards of Parnast, Defender of Parnast," will not. Remembering the day the old dwarf called him a disgrace, he gags The Weevil again — and notices that everyone except Unla Key is pleased.

Unla Key is grouchy. For two days he has glared at Orsik, who seems interested in going straight, and he cannot get close enough to The Weevil to learn his secret method of robbing the caravans. While he works through his hundredth extraction plan, Harshnag delivers a lecture on Mirabar: "Mirabar," he begins, "is the richest city of the North by far." Unla Key perks up — wealthy cities have rich people who like to gamble. Harshnag describes the city sitting atop a knoll on the north side of the Mirar River, ringed by sloped outer walls "as wide at the base as many city blocks in Waterdeep," and the diviner smirks inwardly at the Waterdhavians who never talk about this place. When the giant mentions that defenders can pour water down the walls in winter to make ice slides, Unla Key cannot resist: "Can we go down the slides?" Told it is not winter, he presses: "Can't you make snow, like a white dragon? I mean, you are a FROST giant." Harshnag slams the bit of his battleaxe inches from the halfling — "How dare you!" — and Orsik sniggers, "That's a bit of a racial slur." Unperturbed, Unla Key asks, "No ice sledding?" Harshnag the Grim solemnly explains that the ancient enemy of the giant is the dragon: "Between our two kinds, there can be no quarter. There can be no peace. Only ceasefires." "Isn't that peace?" asks the halfling. Harshnag answers that the dragons started this disaster between the giants and the little people: "Tiamat tried to seize power and weakened the North. The Giant lords saw an opportunity in that vacuum and sundered the Ordning." Thoroughly confused, Unla Key asks, "So your racial war is why we can't have an ice slide?" The Frost Giant glares, bellows, and continues his monologue about Mirabar's hidden second city below the surface, dominated by dwarves, "a city of lit residential caverns, superheated forges, foundries that operate day and night, and tunnels leading to the mines."

In the city, the 3HPP hand over The Weevil for their reward. The sergeant of the guard, a dwarf named Herald, is delighted and congratulates the group. The guards of Mirabar are known as the Axe of Mirabar, whose crest is a deep-red double-bladed axe on a black field, and the Axe army numbers almost 2000 well-trained soldiers. The Weevil tries to claim he is not who they say, but Herald points to his braid: "Silence! You are the criminal." Drawing other guards with his shouting, Herald smirks, "You wanted ore? Well, you will have as much ore as you want," and tells the 3HPP "he will be working in the mines to the end of his days." When Orsik mentions that The Weevil apparently cracked some code, Herald is intrigued and tells the prisoner "we have ways of getting that information from you." Flush with 5000 gold pieces marked with the symbol of Mirabar, the group agrees to meet again at the entrance in five days.

Thistle spends the time searching for word of his lost love Laela. From the Axe he learns that a fairy matching her description passed through Mirabar and headed east to the Lurkwood. A merchant named Gale Wonderbrow promises information if Thistle helps him arrange an elaborate wedding; as a fairy of the Seelie Court Thistle cannot resist, and "the result is spectacular." In return Gale tells of a fairy matching Laela's description who was on a caravan surrounded by brigands — "in a blinding flash of mayhem, everyone but her and a young man who was cowering in one of the wagons was dead."

Unla Key gambles his entire share at the tables of Mirabar. As a newcomer he is not invited to the high-stakes tables, "And it's a good thing." His luck holds at first, but over the following days he makes more and more outrageous bets to cover his losses and loses half his money. Thistle finds him bleary-eyed, clutching his cash and ready to throw it all on one last bet, and dumps cold water on him, muttering, "Worked for Uncle Joon."

Orsik uses his newfound skills in magical disguise to pose as a member of House Husteem, a minor noble family of Waterdeep with a reputation as belligerent and forceful — a house whose rough-and-tumble style excuses Orsik's rough manners. As John Husteem he is invited to the parties of Mirabar's rich and powerful, including a dinner thrown by House Hammaver, where Yip Hammaver, a 32-year-old bachelor with "the strangest red eyes," befriends him. At one party John-Orsik offends Tack Proudfoot Esquire, which leads to a duel at dawn. Yip assures him Tack is a dandy who "read one too many romance novels that talked about duels," but warns that his death would be awkward, since Tack is betrothed to Clan Battlehammer, and would be avenged. On the morning of the duel Tack appears in the finest armor John-Orsik has ever seen, prompting the quip "I thought you said he was a terrible fighter?" Yip replies, "Yes, for a noble knight of the elite guards." Seeing no way to win, John-Orsik casts sanctuary on himself and refuses to fight. Tack declares honor satisfied, takes off his helmet and embraces him. Delighted, Tack insists that the single John-Orsik be introduced to eligible bachelors and bachelorettes of the noble houses of Mirabar. John-Orsik smiles and leaves quickly.

Vardis answers a notice for a strong sword arm. Hork Gethorn, owner of a meat pie shop, "whose face looks like a meat pie and whose size reflects his love of his product," needs help with an infestation beneath his establishment. Vardis takes the job and returns with a reward and some treasure.

The chapter closes on Harshnag, sitting under a tree some distance from the city on the 5th day of the 3rd tenday of Flamerule. The war against the little people is getting worse: rumors of Jarl’s raiding settlements in Icewind Dale, a refugee's report that Bryn Shander was laid waste by Frost Giants looking for some artifact, Hill giants attacking Goldenfields, and — what frightens him most — stone giants attacking settlements on the surface, for "The Stone Giants view the surface world as an abomination that must be destroyed." And the 3HPP have told him that the Fire Giants under Duke Zalto are scouring the lands for an artifact. "This is not good, not good at all."
