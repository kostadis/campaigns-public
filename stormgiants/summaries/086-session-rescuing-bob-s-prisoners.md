# Chapter 86 Rescuing Bob's Prisoners

Date: about 9 Uktar 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
Once inside the city of Silverymoon, Orsik had an idea: "We can't just be heroes, we need allies." He proposed blackmailing the mayor to get them. Thistle protested, "We don't have time for this," Unla Key wanted money instead, and Vardis cut through it with the deadline: "Bob's going to free the prisoners at 9 am." Thistle settled the matter: "Let's bluff him."

The party marched up the steps to the Lord Methrammar Aerasumé palace and showed their papers from Everlund. The Chamberlain was at first reluctant to disrupt his lordship, but Vardis stared him down and he let them through. The 3HPP were led to a large room with a rotunda, stained glass windows and a large table, where a haggard-looking Lord was eating breakfast. He greeted them with "You?" and the accusation that they had caused nothing but disasters. Thistle answered, "We come with solutions!" and Vardis added, "We are here to do something other than cause disaster!"

Lord Aerasumé laid out his problem: 200 notables had disappeared during the destruction of the Royal Theater after a play by Bob Saget, no one could remember the play, and there was a suspicion of magical infernal powers. He had sent all of his wizards and nobody had figured it out. Thistle declared triumphantly that they had discovered who was behind it, and that the culprit had been riling up the giants. Asked for proof, Thistle presented a feather, calling it a relic found in the lair of a dragon who had been behind all of the chaos in the North, a dragon who held other prisoners as well. Asked which dragon, he answered: "Iymrith."

The head of the Royal Guard was sceptical of the feather: "This trinket is the secret to bringing back our people?" High Mage Taern Hornblade, hero of the War of the Dragons, called it "very suspicious" and warned that having the Lord present if it failed would be a disaster. Another courtier countered that if it worked and the Lord was not present, it would be an even bigger disaster. Orsik pressed the point: "Lord Aerasumé, you must be there." Taern said he would also be there to ensure it was not a trap, and ordered the Royal Guard to cordon off the area, because mourners had left statues, paintings and ribbons, turning it into a memorial.

The Chamberlain asked whether it could wait until tomorrow, given his Lordship's hectic schedule. Vardis shrugged, "Busy, busy, busy!" and Thistle added that they had to return to Iymrith's lair to rescue other hostages. Rebecca Deakin, the sage from Wyrmworks whom Thistle had asked to research Iymrith and who was now an advisor to the Lords of Silverymoon, doubted that the four of them could have figured anything out: "It is almost absurd." Unla Key, annoyed, invoked the Wizard of the Crag and the Flying Castle. Rebecca suggested that perhaps the party was behind the events, Orsik asked whether she was accusing Everlund, and the Captain of the Royal Guard shouted that "that city would be the bane of us" and that they should have crushed it in the last war.

Lord Aerasumé silenced the shouting courtiers with "I see." He confirmed the event would happen in a few minutes and asked whether Iymrith, the Doom of the Desert, had also been behind the Giant Civil War, and what the party wanted in return. Thistle spoke before Orsik could begin negotiating, saying they would discuss it after they had shown they were serious. His Lordship was confused, and Orsik, realising this would cost his trust, smoothed it over "with an air of sleaze": "Thistle is very noble and wants to do the right thing. But we want to end Iymrith." The Lord said he would be willing to consider some aid, especially if Silverymoon's involvement could be kept secret.

Orsik then tried for revenge: "Could you deal with house Margaster?" His Lordship called them "an irrelevant house." Thistle claimed they had allied themselves with Iymrith, but a big wink to Orsik, a tell where he scratched his jaw, or Orsik's groan at what a lousy liar Thistle was, kept the Lord from believing him. The Lord admitted the Margasters had never been a goodly house and were rumoured to be involved in unsavory businesses, and asked why the party disliked them. Thistle blurted out, "Cambions." His Lordship stood up outraged: "Are you accusing me of having Devils in my city?" An awkward silence followed, since some demonic or diabolical presence had stolen 200 notables from the city, and the creature was an ally of his Lordship. He reconsidered, allowing that the recent failures to protect his citizens made it less righteous to dismiss warnings, and Taern gruffly agreed: "I never liked the House. They always struck me as corrupt. Look into them, I say."

A few moments later Thistle stood outside the Theater, among the ribbons and flowers left by mourners, trying not to remember that the ribbons were for the dead victims, not the possibly living victims of Bob. He asked Unla Key, "Can you craft me a pedestal?" and Unla Key cast mold earth, raising Thistle 10 feet off the ground. Holding the feather and hoping Bob would keep his end of the bargain, he began, "Ictum. Antium." and then paused: "What was the last word?" After a stunned silence he smiled: "Right, Merthium." A magical door appeared where the door used to be, and the 200 guests walked out very happy, talking about how great the play was, and were very confused to see the theater burned down behind them.

The Chamberlain shouted, "Huzzah for his Lordship!" and the crowd answered. Orsik's "Huzzah for Everlund!" met an awkward silence. Thistle credited the goodly races and the fairies, Orsik interjected "And the good people of Everlund!", and Thistle glared at him and announced to the crowd that his Lordship planned to help the 3HPP rescue the remaining prisoners Iymrith held and end her tyranny. The crowd cheered; his Lordship smiled tightly. Taern Hornblade laughed and whispered, "Well played fairy." Thistle remembered that Taern had been at the final battle when Tiamat's temple was destroyed, and the stories that the wizard was a pompous blowhard.

Back in the rotunda, Orsik asked for all of the leading wizards of Silverymoon. His Lordship laughed: "They have more important things to do." Vardis coughed, "They are protecting cambions?" Taern answered icily with a Silverymoon saying: "Do not meddle in the affairs of wizards, for they are subtle and quick to anger." Sensing Taern's irritation but feeling he should do something, the Lord offered five of their battle mages. Taern muttered, "Four." "Five," said his Lordship, and Orsik and Vardis could not tell whether it was out of pique or something else. As Taern wrote the orders, the Lord remarked that he had never expected the diplomats of Everlund to help him, and the rotunda laughed.

Lord Aerasumé then asked to speak to Thistle alone. Over his allies' concern, Thistle agreed. The Lord promised: "It is time the fairies became a goodly race of Silverymoon," then added, "In a few months, assuming you have dealt with Iymrith." He confessed he wished he had thought to blame a dragon, and said, "I am not fool enough to think it was the Dragon and wise enough not to care." As Thistle was about to say something, his Lordship dismissed him: "Go."

Leaving the building, Orsik declared, "We have a lot of gold, and this city has a lot of magic. Let's go spend it." The party toured the schools of magic and magical stores, purchasing scrolls and spells for their final fight. Along the way Vardis asked Orsik whether he was going to try and kill Iymrith. Orsik answered, "It's how my story ends. Heroically defeating a dragon or dying." Vardis choked up: "I guess I am happy to have been your friend all these years." On the edge of an emotional moment, Orsik coughed and said, "Remember NRRRR"
