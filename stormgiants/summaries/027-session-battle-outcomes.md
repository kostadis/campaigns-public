# Chapter 27 - Battle Outcomes

Date: about 30 Mirtul to 1 Kythorn 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
The battle in Triboar rages on. Urgala looses an arrow from her short bow at a fire giant and misses. Orsik, wounded, judges that the arrow "would have barely pierced his armor even if you had hit," while Vardis mutters that they need "those sprites with their sleep arrows."

From the top of a two-story tower in the middle of the market, Thistle surveys the field. The market is a shambles of shattered stalls and scattered goods. To the north of the market a second fire giant is frantically digging with its longsword; it had marched toward the spot carrying a large rod, "like a diving rod," which it has now tossed aside. Near the entrance of the campground lies Darz, "the former thief from Waterdeep," dead. To the northeast an orc mounted on an axe beak attacks Urgala, who seems to ignore him; beyond her, her home, the Northshield House, is burning, and Urgala bears burn marks ("Probably fought off the magmin."). To the east a giant is frozen in ice. The Lord Protector is dead, Orsik is about to die, Unla Key is barely alive, and Vardis has his sword extended toward the giant.

"I got it!" Thistle casts a spell to revive the Lord Protector, then fires his bow twice and both arrows hit; he smirks and thinks, "Urgala needs to attend my classes." Unla Key reasons that the giant "should be able to resist this spell with its constitution," and casts Thunderwave anyway. The blast shatters the ice that held the giant and it falls back. Vardis and Orsik swing their blades but cannot kill it.

Darathra stares at the immobilized giant and weighs whether to stay and fight, and perhaps become a legend, or run and probably live. Orsik makes the choice for her: "run!" She runs, and as he runs into the tower he shouts, "stay away from the giants." Inside, the hiding Unla Key asks, "are we safe?" and Darathra mutters, "as long as the giants don't want to come inside." Vardis's arrow misses the giant, but his feet carry him into the tower in time to see Orsik crushed by the giant's sword. Orsik's last thoughts: "I told them that we shouldn't fight these giants. Frankly, I don't think these giants are important." The wounded giant flees; Thistle will not let it escape, bringing it down to the point where Unla Key's magic missiles kill it. Vardis runs out and pulls Orsik into the tower.

The other fire giant is still digging. When Thistle prepares to fire, Darathra forbids it: "you will not fire an arrow at that giant. We will sit here and pray that it leaves without killing anyone or destroying anything else." Thistle offers that he could kill the orc; she answers, "You will not fire another arrow." Orsik calls it a "pointless battle" and tells Thistle it was "not your best work"; Thistle admits, "It's like I was possessed by someone else." Darathra notes that "the rest of us almost died while you were unscathed." Vardis tells Orsik he has patched him up and to go fight; Orsik replies, "Nope, I'm out." Darathra settles on monitoring the situation and taking "copious notes."

Thistle "flies discretely toward the giant" to monitor it. A few minutes later the giant drags out a large 11-foot metal band. Orsik and Unla Key conclude it weighs "between 1 and 10000 pounds." Thistle flies closer but cannot tell what the band is made of; the giant does not notice the tiny fairy. On his way back Thistle takes in the damage: Urgala's inn, The Northshield House, is burned, as are the Apothecary and several cottages and farms next to it. Orsik, feeling wrong about Darz, commits to raising him from the dead. In the market square, Urgala, Narth Tezrin (the goods store owner) and Othovir (the harness store owner) discuss what happened. Narth shouts that her house "burned to the ground"; Urgala whispers, "Houses burn," and "they can be rebuilt, especially if I am in a strong community of friends." They all hug, and Darathra asks how they are going to clean up the place.

Orsik lays out his priorities: collect the dead fire giant's treasure "before anyone else shows up," raise Darz, cast "speaks with dead" on the giant, and meet "this powerful cleric." Thistle suggests selling tickets to see her and promising "some play about her god," to Orsik's annoyance. A tall, striking woman with a "smirking green grin," long ash blond hair under a large brim hat exposing an elven ear, and leather armor dyed blue, red and yellow sits down near them: "what a fine mess you have gotten yourselves into." She needles Darathra ("it's good to see that only half the town was burned to the ground") and her re-election campaign, tells Orsik she saw the battle from her ranch, and Orsik realizes she is the cleric. When Thistle asks whether there are other candidates for mayor, she answers, "Me, Cindell Trueshot." Darathra calls her "a carpetbagger"; a farmer mutters to Orsik that Darathra is from Waterdeep and was voted in because they thought she brought "some Waterdeep magic"; Orsik declares, "You're both carpet baggers." The chapter notes Triboar's two factions: long-time farm people, and those who came to take advantage of Waterdavian nobles who vacation there. Cindell describes her "vacation home" of "Just six rooms" and "a couple of acres," says she was not dressed to fight, and claims Darathra would never let the guards leave the town unprotected; Darathra retorts, "I sent the twelve to protect the ranches!" Cindell says the skull was brought to her ("I have people for that"), and accepts Vardis's word for her home: "A bungalow."

Vardis finds her "one of those clerics who doesn't want to be a cleric." Cindell prices her services: some woodwork around the house, "90 gold for the speak spell, and 1250 to raise someone from the dead," then mocks Darathra's wounds and the Lord Protector hiding "in a tower while a giant rampages around the town." Thistle, who keeps track of the kitty, offers "Three free admissions to the opera house!"; Cindell is "Not a big fan of theater and Kaelen's work." On the giant's body the party finds a ruby gem that should cover the raise dead spell. Thistle removes toenails to make potions of giant strength, then remembers the Apothecary is destroyed. Vardis finds a bag of salt ("probably a gourmet who liked his food seasoned"). Ghelryn, a smithy, whispers that the heart "can be turned into a living lamp"; Thistle thinks it "would be useful for the theater," but Vardis fails to remove the heart.

Unla Key, "the narcissistic neutral evil magic using halfling," is eager to question the dead: "Let's see what this giant has to say!" Orsik asks the first question, "why did you come to Triboar." The dead giant's mouth moves and its eyes turn to Cindell, and Unla Key translates for her: "To rule over the giants! Duke Zalto will soon rule over all giants!" Darathra has never heard of Duke Zalto and insists they must stop him; Unla Key, privately thrilled, urges gathering more information first. Asked what the other fire giant was digging, the giant answers with a question ("A question with a question," grumbles Vardis), and Cindell confirms that answering it would count as a question. Thistle asks what the band was; the giant declares, "A piece of the Vonindod." Thistle explains: "That's an artifact that is related to the killing of dragons." Unla Key reflects that more pieces may be out there and, if brought together, could lead to the world's end, and feels a twinge of jealousy toward Thistle's knowledge, credited to his uncle Joon's travels. Asked where to find Duke Zalto, the giant answers, "In his throne room!" Asked where that is, it turns angry, refuses further questions ("We will rule overall!"), and begins cursing the other giants, particularly the storm giants.

From Cindell's point of view, the dwarf tries to claim the ruby is worth thousands, but she sees a better opportunity: to make Darathra look bad. She agrees to raise Darz "for a significant discount. Because we are a community, a family." The exchange that follows (dinner, friends, a pitch for the Talking Troll's wine list) is read by her inner voice as a flirt, then as a sales pitch. She raises Darz. "I'm alive." Darz's inner voice judges each claimant to the credit: the dwarf, who "likes me, not because he needs more information"; Cindell, whose raising is simply "what you do"; and Darathra, of whom he thinks, "she raised me, and I like living." Staring at the hole in the campground, he thinks he would have dug up the artifact "12 feet down" himself had he known.

The following day Darz finds his benefactors at breakfast in the Talking Troll. "I've never been dead before." He decides he owes them his "retirement job": a merchant from Mirabar saw a dwarf cleaning stables in Xantharl's keep, "a fortified village on the Long Road," and recognized him from a wanted poster while traveling through Longsaddle. "The dwarf is Weevil, and the wanted poster makes him worth 5000 gold to the Axe of Mirabar." Then he leaves.

Urgala comes into the dilapidated Talking Troll and sits with the heroes, "an elf warrior, a dwarf with a thick beard, a fairy with shimmering wings, and a halfling mage." She chides them for not helping with the magmins ("The giants were just looking for something"); the dwarf mumbles "1st of the tenday quarterbacking." After thanking them for what they did for "my poor Miraz" and the town, she tells them of a former adventuring companion, the wealthy knight Harthos Zymorven, owner of a giant slaying longsword, last living in Zymorven Hall on the Rauvin Road northwest of Silverymoon. He will part with it "if you drop my name and tell him that you plan to defeat giants and save the realms." She leaves, then returns: "I did save the map." It is "The map to the mine I didn't explore that my wife died near."

Othovir, the harness maker, comes to the table looking worried. He has no money but will tell them where to find some: "I hate the Margasters. We have a history." The family keeps an emergency magic stash in the detached carriage house of its three-story tower in Silverymoon. The carriage house is "a 30' square room with two floors"; its door has an arcane lock, two human guards are upstairs, the carriage has an alarm spell, and the magic items are "hidden in a compartment under the passenger seat." He leaves.

Darathra, the Lord Protector, arrives with two members of the 12. She makes the Harper hand gesture and explains her organization needs to know what happened: "The idea that fire giants would make it so far into the Dessarin Valley without anyone knowing is worrisome." She produces a platinum badge with the Triboar insignia and sends them to Danivarr's house in Everlund, east along the Evermoor Way, to give the badge to Dral Thelev, "the one-eyed half-orc proprietor."
