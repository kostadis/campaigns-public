# Chapter 54 When the 3HPP Met Harry and Mary, and then Orsik got his fingers dirty.

Date: 15 to 20 Eleasis 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
At Flint Rock, Orsik approaches the site already certain of what he will find: "It’s under the rock, it’s always under the rock. And the rock is always inconveniently placed." Thistle and Unla Key land up ahead, Vardis sighs and starts marching, and after a few sweaty minutes Orsik asks where the thing is. Thistle's answer is a question of its own: "Under the rock?" Vardis casts detect magic and confirms the location, which prompts Orsik to object on the rules: "No, that’s not how detect magic works. See, with 1 foot of stone, you shouldn’t be able to detect anything." Vardis answers with deadpan sarcasm that they must abandon the entire quest and start all over again, until Unla Key sniggers and explains that the stone surface is weather-worn, so the stone is now "just ever so slightly less than 1 foot. And as a result, the spell worked." Orsik scratches his head, and Vardis stops walking away.

As they return to start digging, two giant elk approach. Thistle casts spells to speak with animals, flies within shouting range, and asks whether they have seen any other creatures. The male elk, Harry, complains about the Joneses, "the most irritating giant elks," and the female elk, Mary, scolds him: "Harry, how many times must I tell you to stop speaking ill of the Joneses." Unla Key laughs, "Keeping up with the Joneses…" while Orsik digs furiously. Mary squints into the sky and asks Thistle, "Are you a fly?" When he answers that he is fey from the Feywild, she wonders whether that is a river, a watering hole, or a grove of trees. Harry declares that the two of them are "part of an atheistic club and do not worship the gods of the Fae," and Mary apologises that she and her husband are trying to enjoy a quiet meal. Asked about anything dangerous, Mary recalls nothing. Thistle thanks them and flies off, hearing Harry moan, "Another bipedal stealing our grass!"

While Orsik and Vardis try to smash the rock, the noise draws Mary back: "Yoo-hoo! Pretentious flying creature!" Harry mutters that "The Feywild is just a tool of the oppressor to keep the Giant Elk subservient to a doomed religion." Mary asks whether the party is sure they are allowed to do this, since other bipedal creatures think the area is important. Thistle persuades them, and Harry concludes, "Well the sooner you get out of here, the sooner we get our peace and quiet." Mary and Harry then push the stone out of the way themselves. A bit later, the 3HPP is aboard Delsephine’s ship, the Dragon’s Breath, with a mithral spear tip, and when asked where the ship should go next, Vardis mutters, "Morgur’s Mound."

On the 6th, approaching Morgur’s Mound, Brassik is told to share what he knows and produces a map he found while at Candlekeep. He describes the spirit mound of the lost Thunderbeast tribe, standing in the Crags and named after Uthgar’s brother; the tribe disappeared and "Nobody knows" why. The mound is shaped like "a long-necked, wingless dragon," with a ridge of dragon bones, enormous cairns over the bones of revered warriors, and several dig sites left by earlier treasure hunters. Vardis mutters, "And they call us grave robbers." Brassik coughs uncomfortably and, in one long sentence, admits that given his own past in a cult planning to end the world, he is not sure he can cast aspersion on anyone. At the mound Brassik has "No clue" where the item lies. Vardis's detect magic gives him a hint of something below his feet: "I am feeling something and it’s not the gas." Orsik sniggers. Asked whether he has mold earth, Unla Key shrugs, "No." Orsik, furious but resigned, starts to dig, and Vardis joins him while Thistle and Unla Key stand watch. Eight hours pass without result.

On the 7th the digging continues. Vardis asks Thistle, "Maybe you can dig?" and Thistle says, "No." Vardis tries again: "Why don’t you take your pretty little eyes and check if you can see anything?" After a few more hours the two friends find a magic item, though not the artifact: an abjurer’s bangle that absorbs damage from attacks, which they hand to Unla Key. On the 8th they move to the creature's head and turn up a Helm of Comprehend Languages. Orsik curses, "Seriously?" and Delsephine sighs, "Still no artifact." Vardis gives her the stink eye. The 9th brings nothing.

On the 10th, Orsik finds a gold-plated fire giant’s tooth, a nonmagical relic of giantkind small enough to fit in a human's hand. He waves Thistle over, and as Thistle approaches, four animated thunderbeast skeletons erupt from the mound around Orsik's hole. Thistle flies "as fast as he can, and no faster." Orsik scrambles out and wounds the first creature; Thistle's arrows strike another, which he identifies as "ankylosaurus’"; Thuul dashes closer. Three of the creatures attack: Orsik's skill and armor keep him clear, Vardis casts a "warding flare," and Unla Key is bitten. Vardis's fireball destroys the creature Orsik had injured and wounds two more. Unla Key draws his artifact, which produces effects similar to a wand of wonder, and gets a gust of wind out of the rock. Orsik laughs bitterly, but Unla Key uses the wind to push one skeleton into the pit the dwarf and Vardis had dug, where it cannot climb out against the gale. Orsik mutters, "Excellent!" Orsik and Thistle keep up the attack, Vardis strikes one creature down, and Unla Key blows another into the hole, then chooses a fireball over his artifact of wonder and casts it into the pit. Heat rushes from the ground, and then silence.

Back on the airship, Delsephine asks whether Orsik would like to be taken somewhere to clean himself. "No." Covered in dirt from five days of digging, and annoyed that the cult leader seems to care only about her ship, Orsik plops down on the cleanest spot he can find, pulls out the tooth, and mutters, "Five days of digging in the dirt to find this." Delsephine tells her crew to give the grumpy dwarf space and tells Vardis, "The only places left are Raven Rock and the Great Worm Cavern." The answer: "To Raven Rock."

### Harry
A male giant elk. Grumpy and opinionated: he dislikes the Joneses, calls Thistle's Feywild "nonsense," says he and Mary belong to "an atheistic club," and complains about bipedals stealing their grass. He and Mary push the stone out of the way so the party will leave.

A female giant elk, Harry's wife. Polite and apologetic, she scolds Harry for speaking ill of the Joneses, mistakes Thistle for a fly, and asks whether the party is allowed to dig there before helping move the stone.

The cult leader whose ship, the Dragon’s Breath, carries the party. She comments "Still no artifact" during the dig, offers to take Orsik somewhere to clean up, tells her crew to give him space, and lists the remaining sites.
