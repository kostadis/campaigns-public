# Chapter 16 - Return of the Spy

Date: 23 Tarsakh to 1 Mirtul 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
The chapter opens on a standoff in the grove: the heroes stare at the Unseelie, and the Unseelie stare back, their game of cards disrupted. One of the Dryads, whose leaves and branches remind one of a conifer, casts entangle on the group, and the ground sprouts branches and vines. Thistle, Vardis and Unla Key are trapped, while Orsik and Wallace use their strength to break free. The Coniferous Dryad rushes to a tree and reappears next to another tree some 50 feet away, prompting the Dwarf to mumble, "Teleportation!" A halfling mage in a dirty unseemly robe then hits Orsik with a magic missile. Orsik grumbles, "Can’t resist that," and credits the buffs of his friend Vardis with absorbing some of the damage.

Vardis answers with a scorching ray aimed at the Coniferous Dryad and the Deciduous looking Dryad, but misses, and grouses, "I can’t move and can’t hit." The Deciduous-looking Dryad calls to Orsik, "This isn’t a battle you want to fight in, and we are your friends!" and Orsik shockingly agrees and sits down. Thistle shouts that the charm will break if the creature dies; Wallace rolls his eyes, "really? Really?" and promptly misses. Thistle gives a command to Thuul and fires an arrow, and shortly after, the Deciduous Dryad is covered with acid, an arrow sticking deep into its body. Its hold on Orsik breaks and the Dwarf stands up, ready to kill. Unla Key finishes the job with a pair of magic missiles and directs the last missile at the Coniferous Dryad.

The Coniferous Dryad, recognizing the danger, steps through the forest to get close to Unla Key and tells him, "now is a good time to sit down and get a drink." The halfling pulls out his flask and begins to drink, mumbling that Blasius and Oblivillish are just sitting and drinking, so why shouldn’t he? The creature then disappears into the trees and reappears some 40 feet away on the other side of the grove. The Unseelie Halfling casts magic missile again, this time at Wallace. Vardis, realizing the entanglement is making it harder to hit, casts faerie fire on the Dryad and the Halfling; only the magic user is affected and is covered in an eerie pink glow. Wallace cuts the halfling deeply with his sword, but the wizard lives, and Orsik charges and throws a handaxe that hits.

Thistle pleads with the drinking Unla Key to get up, to no avail: the Dryad's beautiful voice makes his colleague's voice sound like a screeching bleating whine. Then a lion and a bear show up. Oblivillish shouts, "those creatures ate the stag!" Vardis looks at him, "I thought you did?" and the sprite answers, "I was framed." Vardis understands. The Dryad steps out of a tree next to Vardis, swings her staff and misses, and steps away again; Vardis misses as she flees and fails to break free of the roots, branches and vines.

Wallace kills the halfling mage and dodges the charging bear. Thuul is commanded to close in on the Dryad and an arrow is fired at it that misses badly. Unla Key's drunken stupor ends when he sees the lion charging at him; he raises a finger and casually casts Rime’s Binding Ice, though the lion escapes the worst of the blast. The lion stares at Thistle, recognizing him as a Seelie, and pounces. Thistle is dropped, and the lion prepares to tear his throat but gets tangled in the branches. Vardis wonders what to do — "can’t hit anything with my hands caught in these weeds, but maybe …" — and the scorching rays kill the lion and wound the bear. Vardis rubs the ring of Corellon and shouts, "that is how it’s done!" Wallace kills the bear and roars as if he had killed Tiamat; Vardis shouts back, "I softened him up for you!" and Wallace glares. Thistle and Thuul strike the Dryad again, but even covered in acid it keeps its control of Unla Key, until Orsik knocks it prone and cuts it in half with his blade. Oblivillish, freed from the entanglement, flits about and shouts, "I am free!"

Blasius wakes from his own drunken stupor asking, "Unla Key, another drink? Anyone another drink?" Seeing the Dryads dead, he declares it a great day, the day he is no longer the Chief of Staff of Prince Thornacious. Oblivillish is so happy that he kisses Blasius and tries to hug Orsik, Vardis, Thistle and Wallace, who all glare at his unwanted advances. Blasius hands Unla Key a ring of jumping, declaring, "To my true honored drinking friend," and promptly falls asleep.

With the Unseelie singers dead, the group heads back to Gralm to get him to sign the contract. Orsik's mastery of persuasion, the victory in the drinking games and the magnum sound-canceling headphone are enough to sway Gralm, who announces that he will deal with Bad Fruul, and the ettin signs in Gralm's precise script. He asks, "Will you stay for a small celebration and a drink?" Unla Key stares, "how about a flight?" — "Finally, a civilized adventurer!" Gralm's goblin butler brings fine crystals filled with the ettin's various brews. Everyone stares at the halfling, expecting some magical effect, but he only asks, "Some more pleeeze?" Worried because some of the booze's effects can be alarming, Vardis shouts, "hey buddy, we have some much better booze in our tent!" and Unla Key follows him out.

In the morning, heads spinning, the group meets Blasius and Oblivillish in the clearing with the dead Unseelie. Blasius opens another Fey portal and shouts, "come on!" Vardis, remembering the puzzles of the last portal, asks, "what about the trickster fey?" Blasius answers, "Not a problem. I am chief of staff. A perk of being chief of staff. The faster way to get there!" The group appears in front of the Prince, who demands, "What are you doing with this traitorous scum?" Blasius claims he was drunk when he made those accusations, credits the leadership of Oblivillish and the heroes with finding "the true killers: a bear and a lion," and adds that Lord Oblivillish commanded the heroes to attack the Unseelie agents, averting "The threat of the Unseelie incursion". Prince Thornacious scoffs, then asks Thistle, "is this what happened?" Thistle and the rest of the group nod. The Prince proclaims, "Blasius, you are now once again my Satyr! And you Oblivillish are once again my chief of staff!" Orsik smiles — the mission is complete.

On the 5th day of the 3rd Tenday of Tarsakh, the overjoyed Satyr proposes "a vision quest, my friends. We will drink and ponder and think!" Orsik replies, "anything is better than doing the bidding of a blind pseudo dragon." The vision quest expands the mind, poisons the liver, and puts the heroes in contact with the essential elements of the forest and the Fey, showing them how to neutralize poison should they ever be poisoned. Blasius warns, "only works once per vision quest," and Unla Key slurs, "sign me up!" Six days later, the heroes arrive in Parnast.

The final section follows Azam on the 1st day of the 1st Tenday of Mirtul. In his thoughts, two weeks have passed since the party left; he assumes they failed and that the SEER will be disappointed. He has sat in Parnast for days, where the only place to drink is the Golden Tankard, though he admits the Festival of Greengrass is far more pleasant in a small village than in the desert. Small children squealing "Thistle is coming!" break his reverie. Azam leaves some coin for Ragnar and Elspeth, walks into the main square of Old Parnast and shouts, "congratulations! You have secured your rewards!" handing a magical potion and a pouch full of gold to Unla Key. When Unla Key does not betray his friends and tells them that the deal he struck was for 200 GP, Azam concludes the halfling wizard could perhaps work for the SEER and asks, "would you like to join the Lords’ Alliance?" Asked who they are, Azam delivers a pitch dismissing the Harpers, the Zhentarim, the Order of the Gauntlet and the Emerald Enclave, ending: "It is the Lords’ Alliance that is in favor of order and centralization and safety." Azam is convinced the pitch will work. Unla Key takes a swig and burps, "no."
