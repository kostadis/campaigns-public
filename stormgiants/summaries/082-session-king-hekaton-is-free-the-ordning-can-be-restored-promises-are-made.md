# Chapter 82  - King Hekaton is free. The Ordning can be restored. Promises are made.

Date: about 1 Uktar 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
The chapter opens mid-battle aboard the ship where Hekaton lies chained. Orsik sees the Wizard and throws two hand axes; the first hits, and the second embeds itself in the mast behind the wizard. Someone asks, "What the hell happened?" and Unla Key sniggers, "He had a contingency spell and probably teleported to somewhere safe." Orsik picks up the hand ax that did not vanish with the wizard and kills one of the cultists climbing up the mast.

Vardis looks down into the sleet storm, and Orsik shouts, "Light them up." A mote disappears into the storm and a bright spherical light forms; Corellon's cleric is unsure how many died, but he hears several screams. One drenched, shivering cultist appears on the top aft deck, outside the storm, sees Vardis, and wounds him with the armed ballista. Unla Key, flying with his pegasi wings, looks at Thistle, who nods. Unla Key then unleashes a fireball on the few remaining cultists, burning Thuul in the process. Thistle's eyes of True Seeing do not reveal an invisible Wizard. Orsik shouts, "Free the Giant, then let’s use the Conch to get out of here." Thistle nods but wants to kill the Wizard, so he scouts the fore part of the ship and sees nothing.

Orsik and Vardis climb into the sleet storm and reach the chains binding Hekaton. Thuul tries to burn the chains with his acid and fails. Orsik swings his blade and breaks one chain: "This will work!" Vardis is about to swing his sword when Orsik realizes that magical weapons must break the chains; Vardis casts a spiritual weapon, which strikes the chains to no effect.

The archmage appears inside the aft cabin, casts prismatic spray, and closes the door. A blue light envelops Orsik and freezes him; his body aches from the cold, but he lives, and the pain does not distract him from keeping the spell working. An indigo light covers Vardis but does nothing. Unla Key shrieks, "This sleet storm is a nuisance. I can’t burn anything I can’t see!" He remembers that he can cast haste, flies into the storm, but cannot fly there and lands prone on the deck. The first mate then opens the door of the same cabin and strikes Unla Key down with a poisoned weapon.

Orsik screams, "Just get some healing to me, and I will break these chains!!!" Thistle flies toward him, but a chunk of sleet knocks him onto the deck, and he mutters, "This storm is getting in the way." Orsik shrugs, slips moving through the storm, gets up, and breaks one chain: "Two chains left, and it’s all on Vardis." He shouts, "YOU CAN DO THIS!!!!" and privately frets that his DNRRR has no Do not Reanimate clause, and that with his luck the wizard is a necromancer.

The wizard opens the door, glares at the storm, and casts a cone of cold, but Unla Key sees him and counters the magic; the wizard re-enters his room. The first mate shouts, "You will die, halfling." and Unla Key is brought to death's door. Thistle, ready, strikes the first mate with an arrow, and the first mate staggers inside; Thuul uses that opportunity to rush in. Orsik staggers to a chain, misses with one swing and lands only a glancing blow with the next. Vardis's spiritual weapon damages one chain, but its other slash is as ineffective as the first.

The Wizard steps around Thuul and, twirling his hand, unleashes a whirlwind of snow and ice shards that fans out from his fingertips over the remaining members of the 3HPP. This time the sleet storm is gone: Orsik has survived one murderous attack after another and kept focused, but this last one is too much. As the spell evaporates he sees the carnage: burned corpses soggy from the storm, blood dripping from the bodies that fell onto the deck, Unla Key barely hanging on, and Vardis scarcely alive. Thistle's first arrow at the first mate hits the broken door he is using for cover, but his second shot is true. Vardis's spiritual weapon then slashes through one chain and then another, and Vardis destroys the damaged chain with his magical mace.

The Wizard considers turning into a Tyrannosaurus Rex, but thinks, "Perhaps one of them might have some other trick up their sleeve? No, I need to kill them all at once." He repeats the icy blast. Vardis tries to avoid the worst of it but is still barely alive, and Orsik is out. The first mate, thinking the cleric can be finished shortly, attacks Thuul, and the summoned beast disappears; he then turns on Vardis, but Vardis summons Corellon's light and the first mate misses.

Thistle, barely alive, shouts, "I have a plan!" Vardis thinks, "This is going to be another one of those hare-brained schemes of his." and shouts, "JUST HEAL ORSIK SO WE CAN TELEPORT OUT OF HERE!" Thistle answers, "I plan to pop a goodberry into Orsik’s mouth." and does. Orsik recovers, pulls out the conch, and blows.

Moments later they are in the grotto in the basement of the storm giant stronghold, the Maelstrom, sitting on an arcane gate. Hekaton stands up, filling the space beneath a vaulted ceiling carved from a single colossal geode: skin of a thunderhead's deep, bruised purple laced with lines like lightning scars, hair the color of winter moonlight, eyes the color of a storm-churned sea swirling with grief and suppressed rage, armor of enormous scales, and a belt of woven lightning hung with talismans. He asks, "Where am I? Who are you?"

Thistle tells him, "We rescued you from a boat you were chained into. The Kraken Society had captured you." Orsik adds, "We were sent by your daughter to rescue you." Thistle helpfully adds, "Yes, the one not trying to betray you," and Orsik finishes, "And not trying to steal the throne." Hekaton roars, "My daughters?" and insists, "I must go to the throne room at once." Orsik sits down and supposes they should follow; Vardis mutters, "You need healing, buddy," and Orsik is healed and runs after the King.

In the throne room, Queen Serissa is delighted to see her father. Orsik tries to look as dashing as possible, since she will someday be the Queen, and she smiles at him. The Imperator shouts, "So wonderful to see you alive!" and Nym and Mirran shout, "Daddy, we’re so happy to see you!" Unla Key, who has caught up, mutters, "No, you aren’t."

After the reunion, King Hekaton tells the 3HPP, "This is a moment of great joy, but we have problems." Thistle replies, "Yes, we have to rescue my girlfriend." The King's stern face softens: "The affairs of the heart are paramount, but the realms are in danger." He is told the danger comes from "the same blue dragon, Iymrith, who tried to steal your kingdom." He asks, "The Doom of the Desert?" and the answer is "Precisely!" Hekaton tells Serissa to explain, and as she does, Mirran and Nym's failures are laid bare.

Startled and angry but careful, the King asks the Imperator, "I presume you were the one who left no stone unturned to find me?" The Imperator attempts to deceive, but the 3HPP clarify that Serissa remained hopeful while the Imperator offered little support. The King sits on the Wyrmskull Throne, tells Serissa, "You are now the Imperator.", and tells the old Imperator, "Brother, Uthor, you are dismissed." Uthor smiles, "Yes, my brother, it is time I took care of other business.", hands his insignia of office to Serissa, and marches out into stunned silence.

Hekaton turns on Nym and Mirran: they betrayed him, betrayed Serissa, who was their Queen, and allied themselves with a dragon. "Go to your chambers. I will decide your fates later." When they protest, the new Imperator orders the guards to take them away and follows, enjoying the moment too much.

Putting on his obsidian crown, the King tells the 3HPP that his kingdom is in ruins: the All-Father broke the Ordning, and now, thanks to them, he can put it back as it was. Yet it galls him that Iymrith will live while he brings Chief Guh of the Hill Giants, Thane Kayalithica of the Stone Giants, Jarl Storvald of the Frost Giants, Countess Sansuri of the Cloud Giants, and Duke Zalto of the Fire Giants to kneel before him. If the party kills them all or convinces them of their errors, he could join them in one final battle against the Doom of the Desert.

Orsik answers, "Thanks for the offer, but we’re good. We can do this on our own." The rest of the 3HPP are less sure. Thistle knows the love of his life is somewhere near Duke Zalto's kingdom. Vardis cares about the map to the treasure, which is near Zalto's abode. Thistle also knows that Esclarotta will not be happy to discover that Countess Sansuri will not be defeated. And Unla Key is already running past the King to the teleportation chamber, to get back to his cloud castle and kill an ancient blue dragon.
