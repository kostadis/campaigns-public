# Chapter 75 The first defeat of Sandesyl

Date: 27 Eleint 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary

The battle in Sandesyl's lair opens from the point of view of a Vampire Spawn that sees what is coming and turns to warn the others. The chapter is careful to say it is not fleeing but warning, and the spawn hopes, "Sandesyl will see it that way too." Vardis sees it run and sends a raw blast of coruscating divine energy after it, and the spawn burns with the concentrated power of Corellon’s wrath. Thistle floats like a fairy and strikes at one of the other spawns from his position.

Blagothkus, seeing the battle, declares, "I will stand back and protect the magic user." Orsik will have none of it: "No, you will not defend the magic user. You will charge into battle." The giant looks miffed, so Orsik, the motivator, tries again: "Look, you defeated all those armored horrors by yourself. You are the key to this victory." The giant sniffles, "Really?" and charges into the fray, destroying one of the spawns of Sandesyl.

Vardis senses the battle is almost over, but to the south another of the Vampire's minions advances with several guard drakes, and from a chamber to the east comes a giant skeletal dragon. Unla Key identifies it: "A failed attempt at a dracolich, that’s why she needed the book." Thistle mutters that he could have scouted and they could have come up with a better plan, "but Orsik wanted to charge." The dwarf is upset at first, then realizes that a leader must listen to the great people he works with, and sighs, "You’re right. My bad." Vardis raises an eyebrow.

Looking for the vampire, someone spots a coffin in a chamber whose entrance is in the western wall of the cavernous chamber: "I can see a coffin, but there is no vampire." With Sandesyl nowhere to be found, he shoots one of the drakes with his bow, and Thistle puts two more arrows into it. Orsik rushes in, kills the drake, and strikes savagely at the spawn directing the creatures.

Then, seemingly out of nowhere, a fireball engulfs Vardis, Blagothkus, and Unla Key. Blagothkus cannot avoid the heat; Unla Key does, and keeps his concentration. As the fire recedes the Skeletal Dragon's jaws snap at Blagothkus's arms and he howls in pain. The creature also strikes at Thuul, whose tough drake armor protects him. Orsik, surrounded by guard drakes and confident they are not dangerous barring a lucky strike, is cut by the jaws of a drake that got lucky.

Unla Key opens his eyes, sees Orsik surrounded, does some quick math, and drops a fireball on Orsik and the drakes, reckoning it will hurt five times more enemies than allies. Orsik dodges every one of the angry drakes' bites. When Vardis shouts, "Another fireball?" Orsik screams, "I can handle the burn." Vardis closes his eyes as the fire bursts, and opens them on Orsik's roar of approval: where there were five drakes, there are now two badly wounded creatures and one badly burned vampire spawn. Thistle kills one.

Orsik screams at Blagothkus, "Kill the dragon!" The giant mutters, "I am injured. Can someone heal me?" The enraged dwarf answers, "You don’t want to fight; I will kill you myself." The cloud giant sputters, "What would Esclarotta say?" and Orsik replies, "I don’t care." Realizing Orsik is not joking, the giant picks up a rock and tosses it at the skeletal dragon. Orsik rushes past the last drake, which dies moments later from Thistle’s bow, and lays into the dragon with a slice across its chest, a strike to its claw and a crushing blow to one of its legs. It is in pain, and would flee if it were not undead.

From her perch in her cave, Sandesyl rages that her minions have failed her. She is not worried about being killed: she has a secret place to escape to, where she can safely regenerate, and nobody knows its location. "But first, I need to kill that wizard." She moves invisibly through the crowd and grabs at him with one claw, misses, and catches him with the other. Unla Key smiles; he is ready for that and keeps his concentration. But Orsik’s haste ends when the Dragon strikes the Wizard, though the wizard does not die.

When Sandesyl moves to hide behind the dragon, Unla Key casts a spell that reaches to the celestial sphere and throws out a searing line of radiance, "directing the brilliant power of the Sun". The vampire feels fate's twist against her and screams, "A diviner!" Before she can recover, Vardis summons the power of the blazing sun of Olympus from Corellon’s domain. The cavern is brightly lit, the skeletal dragon collapses with its magic spent, and Sandesyl is burned. Blagothkus and Thistle strike "the last of the Cult of the Dragon, the last of Sammaster’s disciples" with ranged attacks. Unable to escape the bright light, Sandesyl screams in rage and turns into mist. Deep in the caverns, Thuul kills the last drake with a vicious bite.

Orsik, burned, bloodied and annoyed, demands, "WHERE IS THE DRACOLICH?" Thistle points out that they chose to attack in her lair, where the dracolich has no way to enter. Orsik protests, "Glazhael said she would show up if we got the vampire deeply injured," and Thistle answers, "Yes, and there was no way for her to come." Orsik stomps his foot like a petulant child but concedes, "Fine, we need to kill that vampire." He throws open the coffin; it is empty. Thistle stares at the ice and mutters, "Endless cracks go deep into the caves. I have no idea where she is."

"Okay, fine, we open the chests and then find this Dracolich." Thistle is about to scream, "Wait!" but is too late: Orsik opens the chest, and a lightning bolt strikes him, Vardis, and Unla Key.
