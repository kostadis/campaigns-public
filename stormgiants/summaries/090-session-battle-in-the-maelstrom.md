# Chapter 90 Battle in the Maelstrom

Date: about 13 Uktar 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-15; recorded 2025-03-26)

## Summary
The battlefield at Iymrith's lair remained in chaos as the party and their storm giant allies clashed against fire and frost giants. Unla Key began the session already transformed into a storm giant, using the giant's abilities in place of his usual powers. The green dragon fled the battlefield, while the red dragon descended into the treasure pit. Above, Orsik's Sleet Storm sent two giants sprawling, but the dwarf remained frustrated that he could not reach an enemy with his Giant Slayer Sword. Instead, he dealt six damage to the Frost Giant Jarl at range and invited retaliation: “Come at me, Jarl.”

The Jarl closed within ten feet and struck Orsik with a cleaving swing for 24 damage. His follow-up great axe attacks missed, and Orsik maintained concentration on Sleet Storm. Vardis's summoned djinn moved behind the Jarl and landed three attacks for 41 damage, excluding its ineffective lightning damage. The Jarl struck the elemental for ten damage and knocked it back, but missed the follow-up attack. Vardis abandoned a proposed Fireball after the table treated Sleet Storm as suppressing it, instead directing Flame Strike at the Jarl for another 24 damage. Thistle struck for 33, Thuul added three reaction damage, and the Jarl's last desperate kick missed. Thuul's seven-damage bite finished him.

The cloud giants immediately saw an opportunity in Hekaton's weakened state. Orsik suggested that they attack the dragon and take its treasure instead, then briefly explored joining them himself. Serissa was shocked. Countess Sansuri answered with condescension, inviting Orsik to pursue the dragon and leave the conflict to the adults. She declared that her rule would bring giant domination across the north, replacing Hekaton's policy of peaceful coexistence. Hekaton meanwhile told the remaining fire and frost giants that their leaders were dead and the fight no longer concerned them. They sat down and withdrew from combat.

The cloud giants advanced and struck Hekaton with three rocks for 30 damage apiece. Their sorcerer moved into casting range, while Sansuri enclosed herself in a globe-shaped Wall of Force and urged her troops onward. Unla Key initially declared a lightning strike against the cloud giants, but their lightning immunity was recognized after damage had been recorded. That damage, along with a storm giant ally's subsequent lightning damage, was reversed. As a retroactive replacement for his earlier lightning strike, Unla Key dealt 35 rock damage to Sansuri before her barrier went up. Vardis healed Hekaton for 19, and the party supplied healing potions as the king continued taking punishment.

Orsik cast Sanctuary on himself so he could survive long enough to carry out his plan. His slow movement remained a problem until Hekaton offered to arrange a throw. A nearby storm giant launched him across the battlefield toward Sansuri. There, Orsik used the party's Scroll of Disintegrate to destroy her Wall of Force. He carefully explained that he had neither attacked an enemy nor damaged a creature, preserving Sanctuary. With the barrier gone, he called for everyone to concentrate their attacks on the Countess.

The djinn struck Sansuri, Thistle fired arrows from long range, and Vardis accepted a storm giant's throw to reach Orsik. Once there, he cast Death Ward on the dwarf despite Orsik's objections. The table treated the spell as outside Orsik's DNRRR (do not revivify, raise dead, or resurrect), his stated wish not to be brought back from the dead. Across the battlefield, Thuul positioned himself to protect Serissa as she endured morning star blows and Fireballs. Hekaton and Serissa both drank healing potions. Sansuri escaped her immediate attackers with Misty Step and struck the djinn with a rock despite Vardis's Warding Flare, but Orsik finally reached her with his Giant Slayer Sword. His three hits dealt 31, 28, and 28 damage; the table recorded her at 18 hit points afterward. Vardis's djinn closed in and killed her, ending the battle above.

Below, Unla Key pursued the red dragon into the treasure chamber. The ancient creature was examining the hoard behind a wall, initially out of sight rather than hidden by darkness. Before combat began, it claimed a deal with “your king”: the dragon would keep the artifact, and the others would keep the treasure. Unla Key chose to attack. His lightning strike dealt 45 damage and prompted the dragon to cry, “Knazreponnium Ketternoppapux, we had a deal.” The connection between that appeal and its earlier claim remained unexplained.

The dragon retaliated with Scorching Ray and Fireball, while Unla Key advanced, threw a rock, and eventually fought with his giant greatsword. A rending attack and a renewed blast of fire breath inflicted heavy damage, but Unla Key remained in the fight. With its legendary resistances already exhausted, the dragon was vulnerable, though it retained legendary actions. The DM treated those actions as belonging only to the duel below, separate from the battle upstairs.

When the fight turned against it, the ancient dragon chose survival. It fled through a passage that led toward the far side of the battlefield near the fire giants. Unla Key struck it as it withdrew, leaving it with 25 hit points, but could not reach a position with line of sight for another attack. He considered pursuit and even Control Weather before accepting that the dragon had escaped. He claimed the treasure and mysterious artifact it left behind.

Hekaton and Serissa survived, Sansuri was dead, and the red dragon remained at large. The dragon's claims about a deal had been heard by Unla Key alone; reporting them to the others was proposed but not played out. The artifact's identity and purpose remained undecided. Orsik, despite his hopes for a heroic death, also survived. The group agreed to return the following week for an epilogue addressing loose threads and the consequences of their actions.

## Locations
### Iymrith's Lair
A battlefield where a massive battle between various giant factions and the party takes place. The atmosphere is chaotic, filled with magical sleet storms and the sounds of clashing steel, with a central pit leading to a lower treasure vault.

The battlefield's large distances repeatedly delay melee combat and spellcasting. Storm giants solve part of the problem by throwing Orsik and Vardis toward Sansuri. Once their leaders are dead, the surviving fire and frost giants sit out the remaining conflict. A separate exit from the lower tunnels emerges near their position, far from the party's fight against the cloud giants.

### The Treasure Room Pit
A lower chamber filled with gold and a mysterious artifact where a red dragon was found examining the treasure. The site of Unla Key's solo duel against the ancient red dragon.

Walls initially conceal the dragon from Unla Key's sight. A passage provides the dragon with an escape route that breaks line of sight and leads to another exit from the lair. Unla Key remains behind to claim the hoard and artifact after pursuit becomes impractical.

## NPCs
### Serissa
The daughter of King Hekaton who fights alongside the party. She is shocked by the suggestion of potentially aligning with the treacherous Cloud Giants and takes significant damage from the Cloud Giant Sorceress during the battle.

She also suffers a 62-damage morning star attack. Thuul positions himself to protect her, and a storm giant arrives to help. After a healing potion restores only 16 hit points, she says, “I fear that Annam is not with us.”

### King Hekaton
The Storm Giant King who leads the defense against the rebellious Cloud Giants. Though heavily wounded by cloud giant rocks and implicated in the red dragon's unverified claim about an arrangement with “your king,” he remains a commanding presence, commands the Fire and Frost giants to stand down, and even offers to toss smaller allies into combat.

The cloud giants target him after noticing his diminished strength. Three opening rock hits deal 90 damage, and later attacks continue the pressure. Vardis restores 19 hit points with divine healing, while two party-supplied potions restore another 20 and 18. He approves Orsik's plan and helps arrange the dwarf's launch toward Sansuri.

### Frost Giant Jarl
The giant leader who charges Orsik after being taunted, uses a cleaving swing and a great axe, and is brought down by the party and their djinn. Thuul delivers the killing bite.

His cleaving swing deals 24 damage to Orsik, but his axe attacks miss. He also uses legendary kicks, first knocking the djinn back and later failing to dislodge Thuul before the fatal bite.

### Countess Sansuri
A calculating and ambitious Cloud Giant leader who seeks to overthrow the current giant order and establish Cloud Giant dominance across the north. She protects herself with a Wall of Force, uses teleportation to evade attackers, and directs her troops from behind magical barriers before being defeated.

She rejects Hekaton's peaceful coexistence with smaller peoples. Her strategy is to claim victory if Hekaton dies and flee if he survives. Orsik destroys her barrier, then eventually deals 87 damage with three Giant Slayer Sword hits. Vardis's djinn finishes her.

### Cloud Giant Sorcerer
A powerful magic-user among the cloud giant ranks who uses fireballs and movement to harass the storm giants and their allies.

The sorcerer initially has to dash into range. The djinn and Thistle attack the caster, but it continues launching Fireballs at Serissa and the storm giants. Its armor class is established as 14.

### The Djinn
A powerful elemental creature summoned by Vardis to assist the party. It provides flanking maneuvers, deals significant damage to the Frost Giant Jarl and Countess Sansuri, and absorbs heavy punishment from cloud giant attacks.

Its three attacks deal 41 damage to the Jarl after lightning damage is excluded. Later it damages the cloud giant sorcerer, strikes Sansuri for 37 damage, and ultimately kills her. It endures the Jarl's kick, 42 damage from a cloud giant's morning star attacks, and a 30-damage rock from Sansuri.

### The Red Dragon
An ancient chromatic dragon found in the treasure pit, distracted by the treasure. It tells Unla Key it had an arrangement with “your king” to keep the artifact while the others kept the treasure; later it calls out to Knazreponnium Ketternoppapux about a deal, without establishing how the two claims relate. It fights Unla Key before fleeing through a secret passage when the battle turns against it.

It has already spent its legendary resistances, though it retains legendary actions. Its attacks include Scorching Ray, Fireball, a rending attack, and Fire Breath. It escapes with 25 hit points after taking an opportunity attack, leaving the treasure and artifact behind.

## Spells
### Sleet Storm
A magical storm that created treacherous, icy terrain on the battlefield, forcing two giants to fall prone when they failed to maintain their footing.

Orsik maintains the spell with a successful concentration save after taking 24 damage from the Jarl. Its DC is 17. The table treats it as suppressing a proposed Fireball, prompting Vardis to change spells.

### Flame Strike
A vertical column of divine fire called down by Vardis to strike the Frost Giant Jarl, dealing significant damage.

The Jarl fails his Dexterity save and takes 24 damage, leaving him at 40 hit points before Thistle and Thuul finish him.

### Lightning Strike
A natural ability of the Storm Giant form assumed by Unla Key, allowing him to call down a bolt of lightning. A declared strike against the cloud giants is rolled back and replaced with a rock attack; a later strike hits the red dragon. A proposed second strike against the dragon is replaced with sword attacks.

Its range is discussed as 500 feet with a ten-foot-radius area. The first cloud giant attack rolls 73 damage before being reversed. The dragon takes 45 damage from the later strike. Unla Key subsequently rolls to recover the ability during the duel.

### Sanctuary
A protective ward cast by Orsik upon himself, making it difficult for enemies to direct attacks against him. Crucially, it remained active after he used a scroll of disintegrate on the Wall of Force, since that action targeted an object rather than a creature.

Orsik initially compares it with its implementation in Baldur's Gate, then recognizes that enemies can still attempt to attack him after making a saving throw. The table also rules that he does not provide a flanking benefit to the djinn while protected and not threatening Sansuri.

### Death Ward
A protective spell cast by Vardis on Orsik to ward against a potentially fatal blow.

Vardis casts it after being thrown into range. Orsik cannot decline or resist it under the spell's wording, and the table rules that it does not violate his DNRRR restrictions. Its eight-hour duration becomes part of the joke about waiting it out.

### Fireball
The cloud giant sorceress casts Fireballs at Serissa and storm giants upstairs. The red dragon separately casts Fireball at Unla Key in the treasure chamber below.

An upstairs blast deals 33 damage to two storm giants and 16 to Serissa after her successful save. A later blast deals 21 each to Serissa and another storm giant. The dragon's Fireball deals 32 damage to Unla Key after he fails against a spell save DC of 23.

### Wall of Force
A globe-shaped Wall of Force created by Countess Sansuri to block attacks, subsequently destroyed by Orsik’s Scroll of Disintegrate.

Sansuri remains inside while directing her troops. Orsik's destruction of the barrier allows the djinn and other attackers to reach her.

### Misty Step
A quick teleportation spell used by Countess Sansuri to reposition herself away from the party's melee attackers after her Wall of Force was destroyed.

After teleporting, she throws a rock at the djinn rather than remaining in close combat.

### Scorching Ray
Beams of intense heat fired by the Red Dragon at Unla Key during their duel in the treasure vault, landing all three rays.

The attacks deal 19 total damage to his storm giant form.

### Fire Breath
A devastating torrent of flames unleashed by the ancient red dragon during its duel with Unla Key in the treasure vault, dealing massive damage.

The dragon initially fails to recover the ability and uses Fireball instead. When Fire Breath later recharges, it deals 96 damage, following a 38-damage rending attack. The DM acknowledges resolving the breath before Unla Key by mistake; play continues without cancelling the damage.

### Healing
Divine magic employed by Vardis to close the wounds of King Hekaton during the battle against the cloud giants.

Vardis restores 19 hit points to Hekaton, explaining that his larger healing spell was already spent on Serissa. Party-supplied healing potions provide additional recovery during the battle.
