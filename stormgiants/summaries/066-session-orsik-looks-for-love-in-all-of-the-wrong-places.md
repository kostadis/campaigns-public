# Chapter 66 Orsik looks for love in all of the wrong places.

Date: 25 Eleint 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
Having explored the rooms to the north, the 3HPP — Orsik, Vardis, Thistle, and Unla Key — goes upstairs, moving silently across a polished stone floor out of a 40-foot-wide hall. Rounding the corner, they find a great hall like "a cathedral of hewn rock bathed in the ethereal glow of a colossal crystal window," its pillars "encrusted with barnacles and draped in vibrant coral." Through the window they realize that Orsik was right: they are deep under water. Unla Key whispers, "How does light get to this deep a place?" and Vardis rolls his eyes: "Magic."

On a raised platform to the north stand two figures. Mirran, "the eldest daughter of King Hekaton," sings with a voice "soaring with the power of a storm," while her sister Nym plays whalebone mallets on the shell of a hulking crab used as a living instrument. Below them a gathering of giants mingles: a cloud giant talking with a fire giant, and a frost giant sharing a laugh with a stone giant.

Orsik stares, then boldly walks in. His footsteps echo over the music, he coughs, the music stops, and everyone stares. He announces that he is looking for Serissa, that he joined a matchmaking service and was told to come here when they were matched. After a silence Mirran laughs, says she is sure "Queen Serissa will be delighted that love came through," but that the queen is not ready to meet anyone, has "important activities of state to manage," and that "our sister will want to look her best for her new beau." Asked when the queen will be free, Nym whispers, "In a few hours?"

Orsik realizes they are overmatched and asks Unla Key, "You got anything?" — the halfling surprisingly shakes his head. Orsik agrees to wait and asks whether the queen has said anything about him. Nym smiles evilly and says the queen was "like our mother, always interested in the little people." Orsik groans, "Casual racism." Vardis corrects him, "It's fetishism." Nym cannot help herself: "It is not a fetish. It is racism. Giants are superior to little people." Playing the part of a boyfriend whose girlfriend's family disapproves, Orsik grabs Vardis and mutters, "We'll go downstairs and wait for the queen." Two storm giant guards march them to the easternmost hallway, where the guestrooms for the little people are, and they settle in for the night.

Mirran then appears in the doorway. Vardis asks her, "Who designed this, midgets?" She answers sweetly, "It was my mother. She had a thing for your kind." The 3HPP suspects everything. She tells them, "She's waiting for you! Come, come!" Vardis whispers to Orsik, "How big of a trap is this?" and gets the answer, "We're going to find out."

Mirran leads them to the large cavernous room with the pool and teleportation circle and, with a wave of her hand, lights it brightly, revealing four giants — the same giants that were upstairs. Rushing past them, she tells them to show their loyalty by getting rid of "these unwanted guests," pausing with visible disgust before the word "dwarf." Unla Key asks, "Are they the unwanted guests?" The four giants laugh and advance, and Thistle mutters, "I think we are."

Orsik shouts that the guest-room door is too big for the giants to march in and that they should fight from there. Vardis runs back, and Thistle and Unla Key follow after a pause. Unla Key asks, "So we doing the bag of holding trick?" and Orsik answers, "No." As she passes the giants, Mirran shouts in Giant, "They are assassins. Kill them all. Show your loyalty." Orsik tells the crew, "We have been accused of worse," and they all nod.

Thistle, who has experience in court intrigues, is reminded of "the time that Queen Titania made a fool of that fomorian," and it clicks just in time to stop Orsik: "Don't kill the sister." If they kill her, he explains, "the Queen will believe the other sister that we are assassins." Orsik replies, "Damn, that's cold." Unla Key shouts, "So, everyone but the Storm Giant?" and sends a bolt of lightning at the fire giant that splits on contact into three new currents ricocheting to the other giants.

The giants laugh. The fire giant names herself and her companions — "I am Duchess Tartha, Count Vaal, and to my right is the Jarl Hellenhild, and the quiet one is Thane Braxow" — and declares, "You have come to kill the Queen, and we will do our duty and kill you." Unla Key runs for the room but hears the tail end of the exposition.

Orsik touches the giant slayer blade Lord Zymorven granted him, whispering, "Well, this is what I saved his son for." He casts Expeditious Retreat, rushes the stone giant thane, and strikes him twice; the giant is unaffected by the sword's magic beyond the bleeding. Two more strikes bring the giant down prone under the sword's power, and one more kills him. Duchess Tartha laughs, "So this will be a challenge. Perhaps the Queen did see something in you. But I can assure you you will not see her in this life." She prepares to strike, but Orsik moves out of her reach, and Mirran swipes at him as he runs and misses.

Vardis casts a wall of fire across the hallway entrance, separating the 3HPP from the giants. Count Vaal clicks his tongue and rockets into the air. Orsik grumbles, "Flying? Can cloud giants fly? I should have known that," and Count Vaal mocks him, "Yes, little one, you should have." The Duchess marches through the fire unaffected and hits Thistle with her giant sword, taking a strike from Orsik as she passes him.

Thistle answers with an arcane, lightning-charged arrow that explodes on Tartha, then strikes her with another arrow before she can recover. Then Thuul breathes "a rush of ice like that of an ice dragon" onto the fire giant. Vardis, Orsik, and Unla Key turn to the ranger: "Thuul can do that?" Thistle shrugs. Unla Key recalls that at the climactic battle of Parnast Thistle did not use his bow as efficiently as he could, and first thinks Thistle likes to create challenges he can overcome — then decides instead that he is hiding his abilities in case one of them betrays him. Unla Key's estimation of Thistle grows.

As a parting gift before she leaves, Mirran hurls a lightning bolt that hits Vardis and spreads to Unla Key; the wizard and cleric are hurt, but not severely. The Jarl, unable to run into the wall of fire and worried the Fire Giant Duchess will steal the glory, notices Thistle flying above the wall and throws a rock that misses by a lot. Vaal stares at her open-mouthed, Tartha taunts her, and Hellenhild mutters, "I was trying some new powder for a better grip."

Unla Key, dazed by the lightning, pokes his head out and sends a mote of fire to a point between Count Vaal and Duchess Tartha, where it explodes. The fire does not affect Tartha and seems to invigorate her, but it sears Vaal and Hellenhild, and Hellenhild begins to look worse. Orsik strikes Tartha, remembering that his flaming sword is less effective against a fire giant; she resists the magic of the first strike, but the second leaves her prone, and Orsik moves away.

Vardis is about to cast a guiding bolt when Unla Key giggles. Asked whether he has anything to share, he says, "Cloud Giants can not only fly but cast misty step." The chapter ends as Vardis moans, mid-sentence.

## Locations
### The great hall
An upstairs hall of hewn rock with vaulted, carved ceilings, coral-draped pillars, and a colossal crystal window looking out deep under water. Mirran and Nym perform here for the gathered giants.

### The guestrooms for the little people
Rooms in the easternmost hallway, downstairs. Mirran says her mother designed them. Their door is too big for the giants to march in, so the party falls back here to fight, and Vardis walls the hallway entrance with fire.

### The room with the pool and teleportation circle
A large cavernous room where Mirran springs the ambush with the four giant lords.

## NPCs
### Mirran
The eldest daughter of King Hekaton, with a voice "soaring with the power of a storm." She stalls Orsik's suitor claim with mock warmth, lures the party out with "She's waiting for you! Come, come!", turns four giant lords on them, and shouts in Giant that they are assassins. She swipes at Orsik and misses, then hurls a lightning bolt "as a parting gift before she leaves."

### Nym
Mirran's sister, who plays a living crab with whalebone mallets. She guesses the queen will be free "In a few hours?" and openly declares, "Giants are superior to little people."

### Duchess Tartha
A fire giant who names the four giant lords and vows they will "do our duty and kill you." She marches through Vardis's wall of fire unaffected, hits Thistle, is invigorated rather than hurt by Unla Key's fire, and is knocked prone by Orsik.

### Count Vaal
A giant lord who clicks his tongue and rockets into the air, mocking Orsik: "Yes, little one, you should have." Unla Key's fire sears him.

### Jarl Hellenhild
A giant lord who cannot run into the wall of fire, throws a rock at Thistle and misses by a lot, and excuses herself: "I was trying some new powder for a better grip." The fire sears her and she begins to look worse.

### Thane Braxow
The quiet stone giant. Orsik knocks him prone and kills him.

### Thuul
A creature the party associates with Thistle — they turn to him when it acts — who surprises the party by breathing "a rush of ice like that of an ice dragon" onto the fire giant.

### Orsik
Poses as Serissa's matched suitor to get into the hall, takes the giant lords' bait, then leads the retreat to the guest room. With Expeditious Retreat and the giant slayer blade he kills Thane Braxow and drops Duchess Tartha prone.

### Vardis
Rolls his eyes at Unla Key, calls the queen's interest "fetishism," and blocks the hallway entrance with a wall of fire. He is hit by Mirran's lightning bolt.

### Thistle
A ranger with experience in court intrigues, who works out that killing Mirran would make the queen believe them assassins. He fights with lightning-charged arrows and flies above the wall of fire.

### Unla Key
A halfling mage who comes up empty when Orsik asks for ideas, opens the fight with a splitting lightning bolt, and later sends a mote of fire between Vaal and Tartha. He revises his opinion of Thistle and points out that "Cloud Giants can not only fly but cast misty step."

## Items

The sword Lord Zymorven granted Orsik, which he says is "what I saved his son for." Its power fells Thane Braxow. The chapter also calls it his "flaming sword," less effective against the fire giant.

### The crab instrument
A hulking crab whose shell Nym plays with whalebone mallets, "a living instrument that pulses with each note."
