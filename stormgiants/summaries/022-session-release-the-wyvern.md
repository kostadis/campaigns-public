# Chapter 22 - Release the Wyvern

Date: 8 Mirtul (night) 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
The chapter opens mid-battle in Parnast with an orc's axe cutting Unla Key down. "Die Unla Key!" screams the orc; "The prophecy says otherwise," thinks Unla Key as he falls. The orc chieftain laughs over the fallen body and, seized with blood lust, charges at Vardis. The cleric calls on Corellon to blind his enemy, but the blinded chieftain still lands his blow for 15 hit points.

Thistle eyes the two orc chiefs, one in a blue sash and one in a green sash, and remarks, "these look like they are pretty tough!" Vardis judges the blue orc to be already hurt: "he’s been hurt a few times. It’s not going to be too hard." Studying the injured chief, Thistle realizes he is the father of one of the orc whelps the party brought from the caves, and wonders why he was not afraid of their threats. Jarlor, the one-time spy, mumbles that it may be because Orsik's threat was to *send the whelp to bed without dessert and will not read a bedtime story*.

Wallace, meanwhile, runs simulations in his head to find the optimal charging path. He runs 20 feet north, turns at a sharp angle, runs 10 feet east, and kills an orc with one blow, sending it flying 10 feet. Vardis casts a scorching ray that kills one injured orc and seriously wounds another, and suggests to Orsik, "try threatening him like your mom used to?" Orsik shouts in common, "if you don’t stop fighting, your whelp will be forced to eat all of his vegetables." When the whole town stares at him, he explains, "I hated vegetables." Kurglow, the orc chieftain of the Spine Ripper Clan, is unimpressed, and Orsik shouts, "I AM NOT NEGOTIATING WITH MONSTERS AGAIN!" Thistle agrees and kills two orcs.

Unla Key, healed by Vardis, casts a mind sliver on the green-sashed chieftain to no effect, and that chieftain hits Orsik for 32 HP of damage; Orsik stays standing. Kurglow is now furious with Orsik, both for threatening his son and for insulting his fathering: the threats only made sense if his whelp were soft, overly civilized and pampered, and if Kurglow had spoiled him. Unable to bear it, he charges Orsik, and Chandra, Vardis, Wallace and Unla Key each get a wack at him as he passes. Wallace hits the other orc chief and screams, "healing!" Vardis answers, "many need healing. Let me see what I can do!" and heals Orsik. Unla Key's second mind sliver finally penetrates the green orc's mental defenses, and Orsik, mumbling "enough talking, more killing," strikes twice with his blade and kills him.

Seeing the other chieftain dead, Kurglow shouts, "give me my son, or I will kill you, you piece of shit!" Orsik stares at him, confused. The orc that protected the whelps appears and screams at Kurglow in orcish; afterwards Kurglow repeats his demand, "give me my child, or you will die." Orsik asks, "Does that mean you will also leave?" and Kurglow answers, "Yes." Orsik has a villager of Parnast return the child. The female caregiver then screams at Orsik and Kurglow, cries, and throws dirt at everyone; Kurglow snarls, points at her and shouts, and she falls to her knees beating the ground as he walks away. One of Orsik's militia who speaks common explains: Kurglow was furious at Naz because she was alive while his son was a prisoner; Naz insisted she had kept his child safe; Kurglow accused her of caring only for her own child; Naz begged to go with him, and he answered, as the militiaman renders it, ‘Unlike you, I can take my whelp and leave you to die.’ Naz sits sobbing near the well in the middle of the town.

Bad Fruul howls in anger at Kurglow's betrayal and prepares a final assault on his mammoth, with several ettins and a horde of orcs. Thistle feeds everyone good berries, citing the saying "Uncle Joon always said you shouldn’t fight on an empty stomach!" When Ragnar asks Orsik, "do you have any plan?", Orsik answers, "it is time for us to release the wyvern!" Chandra shouts, "are you mad? He will kill us all!" and Gundalin screams, "it’s a desperate ploy of a madman!" Orsik laughs that it is time to risk everything: "We will free the Wyvern and hope they and the giant fight." Just then Blasius arrives with two sprites, greeting "My drinking buddy Unla Key!" and promising help against Bad Fruul; Thistle is delighted, "Blasius, you came!"

Chandra explains how to free the wyvern: it is tied to a post in the middle of the former stables, and it likes fresh meat. "Throw dead bodies in one corner, and then rush and remove the chain from the hoop. The Wyvern will notice it’s free and charge at the nearest living thing." Orsik concludes, "So I better make sure it’s the giant!" As Bad Fruul climbs his mammoth and advances on the center of Parnast, Thistle asks whether they have time to rest. Chandra answers no: "my family’s nemesis is guiding his actions. She knows that she must attack now, or she will lose her chance for vengeance." Vardis proposes drinking their potions of healing. The voice in Orsik's head says, "There is no shame in fleeing," and Vardis, seeing his friend's eyes, answers, "yes, there is."

From Orsik's point of view, orcs advance from the south and north and Bad Fruul and his giants from the east; the last of the militia kill one orc from the south and are overrun. As he wonders what help he can count on, Naz stops sobbing, stands, and declares, "I will get my revenge on Kurglow after we kill this idiot of a giant and the orcs that follow him to their doom." Orsik heads to the stables himself; when Chandra asks why he is not sending the guards, he answers, "No, it is my deadly plan, and I am the leader, and I will do it." For a moment he realizes that the thing that always got in his way may be a thing of the past.

Thistle flies up, considers attacking an ettin, and instead strikes an orc trying to block any escape. He watches Blasius complain about the rudeness of an orc who hit him with a Javelin. The hill giant shouts, "I hate fairies that fly!", smashes through the houses of Parnast and casts hold person; Thistle wills himself to keep moving, thinking that *Uncle Joon never said anything about spell-casting Hill Giants*, and Bad Fruul shouts, "That didn’t work! That didn’t work!" as he bangs his head.

Vardis, who has been saving his fireball for a special occasion, catches the orcs from the north in it: three die and three survive. He smiles as Chandra casts bless on Orsik, Thistle and Wallace. Wallace, with his freaky ability to understand distances within a 60-foot radius, attacks the orc that hurt Blasius and misses ("Need to rethink my planning."). A Javelin hits Vardis ("T’is but a flesh wound!"), an orc strikes Wallace, and the mammoth charges through the houses, sending glass, wood and trinkets flying.

Thistle shouts, "For Thornacious!" Blasius, privately thinking *Certainly not for that buffoon*, blames Oblivillish for his presence here, takes cover, and fires an arrow at the orc that struck him with a Javelin. He signals the sprites to attack the mammoth; they poison it but do not put it to sleep, and Naz strikes the mammoth with a guiding bolt. Unla Key, sure that *No one can kill me until the end of times*, uses his wand of web; the mammoth is caught in the web, and he moves directly in front of the giant.

Orsik opens the stable doors closest to Bad Fruul. The wyvern is feasting on the dead corpses, and Orsik uses that distraction to get past it and free it. The wyvern sees the giant outside, smacks its lips, and rushes out with a quick flap of its wings. Orsik hears Vardis attacked by an orc from the north, Thistle commanding Thuul against an orc to the south, the thud of an orc's body, and a fireball. He smiles and rushes out the other door. He hears the wyvern strike the giant, then an ettins mace connect with the wyvern, and a scream: "I thought you said they would not free the Wyvern!" The chapter closes with Thistle flying to a roof and noticing plenty of wounded orcs.
