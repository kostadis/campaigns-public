# Chapter 19 - All is revealed and plans are set in motion

Date: 4 Mirtul 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
The heroes arrive at the Golden Tankard tired, bedraggled and exhausted, and find Ragnar, Gundalin and Chandra waiting for them inside. Chandra asks "So?", Orsik grouses "No, mother," and the reply comes back: "Charred meat it is." Orsik perks up and asks that the captain of the guard feed the Wyvern; Chandra stares, smiles, and with a gesture of noblesse oblige tells him to go ahead. A few minutes later Orsik watches the Wyvern use the thigh bone as a toothpick and finds the sight quite relaxing. Walking away, he pats Wallace on the back and tells him, "someday, you’ll be feeding people to the Wyvern." Wallace stares at him funny.

On the way back toward the Golden Tankard, villagers come screaming that a horde of orcs is approaching from the south, and a corporal of the militia shouts, "boss man, I think there is a group of orcs coming from the south. They may have followed you." Orsik draws his sword, leads his men around behind the Tavern from the east, and charges screaming at the top of his lungs, only to find there are no orcs. The militia insists, "Bossman, I promise you there were orcs!", and Thistle, perceiving no tell-tale signs of lying, confirms, "they were telling the truth about the orcs." Ragnar and Gundalin come out ready to fight and see nothing.

As Orsik is about to laugh it off, he sees in his mind an ornate door with Shou’s writings, and he can tell his companions see the same thing. The door then appears in the wall of the Golden Tankard, followed by a mental image of Orsik opening it. Orsik rejects the suggestion, but Unla Key rushes forward muttering, "when I stop taking risks, the world ends!" When the door opens a small white bird flies out; Thistle thinks it crucial until the sound of hundreds of birds from the octagonal room beyond convinces him otherwise. Hsing, the golden blind pseudo dragon, beckons Orsik in. He refuses, and when Hsing conjures images of death in cities that the Lords’ Alliance protects, Orsik shrugs, "maybe if you had been nicer, I would listen to you." Vardis and Wallace stay with him while Thistle shrugs and heads in. With the villagers and the militia staring at him, Orsik realises he is close to doing something he won’t discuss, and he calls on his militia to follow him through.

Inside is an octagonal room of shelves holding scrolls, books and birds; one militiaman stares slack jawed at the calmly preening creatures. Unla Key and Thistle are examining a map of the Sword Coast on a large desk, held down with small marble statues of Shou gods. Thistle tells Orsik, "this map might help you find your map," and Vardis glares, "we have seen many maps." Double doors open onto what appears to be a garden, where a 50’ish-year-old Shou woman in a blue robe embroidered with birds stands holding a silver staff topped with a crane’s head with crystal eyes. She shouts, "you aren’t in Parnast anymore." Unla Key grabs a cookie from a silver tray and heads out, Thistle follows, and after a request to "be a dear, and then grab the tea cookies," Orsik has one of his militiamen carry the tray.

The second section is headed SEER, and opens in her italic thoughts: Unla Key came through the door, so perhaps the world’s end is not as close at hand as she feared, but the giants are engaged in a civil war to win favour with their gods and the battles between the giant tribes will not end well for anyone. With a sigh she does "that thing" of demonstrating her organisation’s reach through snippets from their files: Uncle Joon is still in Triboar; the nemesis Adimond Kroskas has disappeared into the North; and the beloved Laela is rumoured to live, perhaps captured by some allies of Fire Giants. Thistle stares. She whispers something to Orsik that only he can make out, and he growls, "that’s not me anymore, and we don’t talk about it." She tells Unla Key that the world’s end may require him to take significant risks, notes "and I heard you own a tower," and tells Vardis that the map’s location is "that of an isle that will appear but has not yet." Vardis asks whether that is "the most useless clue of all time or what?"

She declares, "I have manipulated events for some time, and now I need you to actively participate in my plans." Orsik is about to storm out, but the door is gone. She explains that for 30 years her organisation has sought Narcellia, a priestess of Lolth trapped for several hundred years in a headband of intellect by Luistarra, a follower of Angharradh, after Narcellia destroyed Luistarra’s village near Evereska. They lost the trail during the rise of the Cult of the Dragon, knowing only that the headband was somewhere near the Graypeak Mountains. To flush Narcellia out, they encouraged Chandra Stol to seek a replacement for the statue of Angharradh in Parnast, which the last owner of the headband had defaced, inspiring an artist in the Anauroch Desert to make it and Chandra to find him. When Vardis wants to interrupt, she scowls, "I am not done with the opening remarks. Can you please wait until I am done?"

In parallel, the magical tomes the party provided from the Marsh of Chelimber yielded a ritual to scry the owner, who proved to be Bad Fruul. Narcellia has been urging the hill giant to increase his wealth and girth, taught him the basics of arcane magic, and with the collapse of the Ordning convinced him to draw monstrous humanoids to his banner. Kragota Threeteeth’s agents and farmers revealed the new statue, and Bad Fruul, urged by Narcellia, has decided to destroy Parnast. Worse, Chandra Stol has a pendant once owned by her ancestor Luistarra, and Narcellia has "particularly gruesome plans for the cleric." The SEER’s job: protect Chandra at all costs, determine when Bad Fruul will attack, defend Parnast, and bring her the headband, for 600 gold for trying, an extra 200 if no one dies, and an extra 100 if Chandra is kept safe. When Vardis asks what the Ordning is, she explains it is "the social structure of the Giants that keeps them in check and preserves the peace," and that no one knows who or what dissolved it. She offers more tea and cookies, walks them out as the door rematerialises, and hands Unla Key a book: "prophecies of the apocalypse."

Outside, Vardis asks his old friend what they will do. Orsik vows to protect Chandra, tell her about her pendant and reveal everything; Vardis would like to assassinate Bad Fruul "as a group action," and Unla Key smirks, "I am cool with an assassination." Orsik tells the militia, "this is what I have trained you to do!" Wallace explains that Old Parnast, to the northeast, is more defensible because it used to be a Zhentarim fortress; Vardis notes its repairable walls but warns that the locals think it cursed, recalling that they felt the tower Ragnar set up collapsed because they included logs from the old town. Thistle agrees the old city is the better position, but Orsik flips out at the idea of making people leave their homes and declares, "we will make our defense here." He orders trenches, pickets, fortified buildings and "some traps, pits," then confesses to Vardis the gaps in the militia’s training, ending, "what have I done?" Vardis answers, "the best you could have?" Thistle assures Orsik he trained their archers well; a bow breaks, and Vardis says, "no, he didn’t."

At the Shrine of Axes, Orsik tells Chandra everything the SEER said. She confirms it: Narcellia has plagued her family for centuries, was responsible for her mother’s death 30 years ago, and Luistarra went mad and "used hag magic to bound the drow into the headband." She adds that Narcellia "was deathly afraid of the fey, sprites in particular," and that Prince Thornacious might be of use, then says stoically, "You can do nothing for me." Orsik asks, "what if I’m not good enough for you?" Thistle fetches herbs from Silas’s garden and rubs Orsik with them, telling Chandra the herbs will help with his smell. Impressed, Chandra agrees, but first asks Unla Key whether the plan makes sense; he answers "what plan?", and after she describes it, tells her, "if you do not come with us, it will mean the end of the world." She agrees.

With Chandra in tow, the group decides to seek help from Prince Thornacious. Silas tells them of an antidote his wife made against the blue mists of the Weathercote Forest; Thistle is confused, Orsik walks away, Vardis wonders if the herbs can be smoked, and Unla Key works out the preparation and makes enough for four. Orsik volunteers to go unprotected. After scanning the horizon and talking to locals, they conclude the most likely attack date is 4 days hence, the 8th day of the 1st Tenday of Mirtul. At the forest boundary Thistle says, "I know how to do this," and leads them to Prince Thornacious’ camp. His pitch goes well with Blasius until he asks Oblivillish, as a hunting master, to help; Oblivillish is outraged at being called a hunter, and when Prince Thornacious asks, "you are a hunter?", the answer is "No, sire!" Blasius then announces that the prince is sending him and two sprites to fight Bad Fruul. Chandra Stol begs Orsik to let her stay with the fey; he assumes he should stay too, she sighs, "no, I just need the fey," and Orsik, his pride hurt, asks, "am I not good enough for you?"
