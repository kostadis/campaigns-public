# Chapter 20 - The Spine Ripper Tribe

Date: 4 to 6 Mirtul 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
The chapter opens with Orsik staring at Chandra, hurt and confused. He presses his case that she is in danger: "you don't know these people. Bad Fruul can't come here personally, but he could send someone here to kill you." Sensing he has a winning argument, he continues that the giant need not do the work himself, because "He has minions to do his evil work for him, so he's just gonna send someone here." He ends with a shout: "I'm the only one who can guarantee your safety." Chandra, shocked and intimidated by the forcefulness of his plea, says "yes." Blasius then turns to Thistle with "Well, my boy! I am off." Orsik is about to follow him when Prince Thornacious shouts, "a fine dinner for our friends."

The following day the heroes decide to find out where Bad Fruul is. To maximize their efforts they split up, promising to return to Parnast later that day to share what they have learned. Thistle finds evidence of how Bad Fruul intends to invade Parnast by doing an aerial survey; Vardis confirms the route by carefully investigating the forest trails near Parnast; and Wallace, leveraging his local knowledge, further confirms the most likely direction. The three confer and agree that the attack will come from the north, then inspect the fortifications and are pleased to see the town center with the roads barricaded and pits dug in front of the barricades. Orsik, meanwhile, finds some goblins and deceives them with information about Bad Fruul; the goblins reflect on what they have learned, and fewer join the hill giant's banner. Unla Key recognizes the banner of Bad Fruul in the hands of some hoard members, extrapolates the size of the hoard, and from that infers that a tribe of Orcs has not yet joined the giant's army. From his knowledge of the area he guesses the name, "The Spine Rippers," and a location for the orcs.

In the morning of the 6th day, the companions head out to find the orcs' den. Thistle shouts, "I got this!" and leads them straight to where the orcs are hidden, then explains that Uncle Joon, whom he is sure is on the good guys' side, told him all about every Orc tribe and the best places for Orcs to create a den. Orsik stares at him and mumbles, "or you can fly up 50 feet and see them." Thistle grouses and flies at an altitude of 7 feet, leading the group deeper into the forest and then towards some hills. He grows quieter and points with his fingers towards the edge of a cliff wall, but it is too late: an Orog standing beside the cave entrance runs inside.

Thistle proposes that they wait outside, ready to attack anyone who exits. An ambush is prepared, time passes, and no one comes out. Thistle decides to enter the caves to see what is happening. Time passes, nothing changes — "And then all abyss breaks loose."

The orcs had set up a trap deeper in the cave, and that trap was dealt with through the use of fireballs. "FINALLY" shouted Orsik when the first fireball exploded. Unla Key fared badly: in the hallway from which he cast his spell, an orog and an acolyte of Gruumsh erupted, and he was promptly knocked unconscious. Orsik, Wallace, and Thistle were able to kill the enemy.

Exploring the cave complex and the dead Orcs and Orog, the group finds that one of the Orcs was an Eye of Gruumsh, a shaman of Gruumsh, and that the acolyte was clearly his follower. According to Vardis, the room with the trap was a War Hearth, with plentiful shrines to the orc gods and accommodations for many orcs. The passageway the orog had used opened into a nursery full of orc children, guarded by an orc claw of Luthic. The companions at first considered killing them all, until they realized the monstrosity of the crime — and observed that they had leverage over the Orc Claw of Luthic, since one of the whelps was hers. Threatening an attack, they watched the Orc Claw of Luthic grab her Whelp and move away.

The companions then noticed a pit full of human prisoners: two families. When the prisoners saw Orsik they rejoiced, thinking the militia had come to save them, and quickly revealed that they were the families of Jarlor and Larrish. Orsik remembered the two men from his dealings as a town council member. Asked what the orcs wanted with them, the answer came: "They threatened to kill us if Jarlor and Larrish didn't spy chief Kurglow." Seeing the murderous rage in Orsik's eyes, one of the children shouts, "daddy didn't want to do that, but the orc chief Kurglow said he would kill us all."
