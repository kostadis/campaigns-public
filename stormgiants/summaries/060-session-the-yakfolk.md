# Chapter 60 - The Yakfolk

Date: 11 to 16 Eleint 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
Esclarotta, the sentient control center of the Cloud Castle, decides the heroes are not ready for their next fight: "you need practice. I will not have you attack the Countess and lose. And I love a good love story; we will help Thistle find his love, Laela." The rest of the 3HPP had hoped to go after Sansuri first, but Thistle had used his charming wit and sob story to sway the giantess. As the ship moves away from the collapsing Eye of the All-Father, Esclarotta shouts, "AT LEAST SOMEONE LOVES SOMEONE IN THIS SHIP!"

Thistle smiles and watches through the view port as the ship moves silently across the clouds. Because it is daylight, he steps outside, confident that the Vampire will not try and kill him. He tries to remember what he knows about Ancient Blue Dragons, but all that comes to mind is blue fairies and blueberries. Inside the control center, Esclarotta shouts, " Does anyone have any ability to tell me exactly where I am supposed to go?" The heroes share the information they have from Oracle, and three days later they get closer.

On the 6th day of the 2nd tenday of Eleint, Esclarotta once again asks, "Anything more precise?" Orsik turns to Unla Key with "Diviner?", and the halfling only shrugs. Vardis prays to his god for divine intervention. Corellon doesn't listen, or maybe he does, and Vardis ends up somehow covered in bird shit. Wiping himself clean, Vardis remarks, "not exactly endearing me to you right now."

It is Thistle, still outside, who spots a village on a mountain spire and shouts, "It's there!" Ahead lies the great dungeon forge of Ironslag. Thistle remembers from Uncle Joon that the dwarves of the Silver Marches call it Mount Hamarhaast ("hammer of ashes"). Carved into the base of a 500-foot-high mountainside cliff are a pair of 50-foot-tall adamantine doors, which Uncle Joon says are airtight valves sealed by ancient magic. Atop the cliff sits the village of Yakfolk.

When Esclarotta asks, "Where shall I stop?", Thistle argues for the subtle approach and points out a stairway they could climb. Orsik will have none of that and insists on what he calls a "show of force!" His enthusiasm convinces Esclarotta again, and the ship glides over the village at the top of the mountain. The castle's front gate opens, and the heroes climb down to stand in the middle of the yurt nearest the iron gate.

A young, frightened moon elf greets them: "Chief Kartha-Kaya would consider it beyond rude not to come and eat in his yurt!" Thistle stares at her and is convinced she is afraid for them, but he cannot say. He tries at first to avoid the meal, but the social graces he learned in the Fey court impose specific protocols on him. Orsik and Vardis, suspicious, cast wards against poison before entering.

The Chief's home is a 60-foot-diameter hut built on high ground in the middle of the village, with a beaded curtain entrance facing an outdoor campfire. Inside, a shallow circular fire pit keeps the hut warm; cushions, baskets of foodstuffs and painted clay vessels of water, wine and broth are piled toward the back, and an ornately engraved bronze gong hangs from a wooden frame with a yak-hide-padded mallet. The food spread across the hut is fresh vegetables, cooked meats and bread. Orsik eats first, expecting poison, and is pleasantly surprised by how good and unpoisonous the food is. He nods, and the rest of the 3HPP dig in; the monotony of the cleric's created food and water has begun to grate on everyone's nerves.

When the meal is done, the Chief insists they must perform a cleansing ritual. Thistle and Vardis try to object, but the Chief is insistent and suggests that ill omens are likely if the ritual is not performed. Orsik will have none of it and stays outside, and the Chief, disappointed, makes some snide remarks about dwarves. The rest form a circle: the Chief holds Thistle's left hand and Vardis's right hand; Imberu, the Chief's "first wife", holds Thistle's right hand and Unla Key's left; and Nahala, the Chief's other wife, holds Unla Key's right hand and Vardis' left, closing the circle.

The Chief begins to hum. The heroes feel the cleansing, but they also sense the Yakfolk minds trying to take them over. Orsik, outside, notices something amiss and warns his friends, who are then better able to repel the attempted possession. The failed attempt to control the minds of the 3HPP triggers a free-for-all melee.
