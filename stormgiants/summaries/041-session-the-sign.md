# Chapter 41 - The Sign

Date: 28 Flamerule 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
The chapter is told from the point of view of Harshnag the Grim. Harshnag stood at the edge of a small village on the route to the Oracle and read the signs plainly: giants had been there. The houses had been destroyed with rocks, and bodies littered the grounds. His counsel was short: "We must go." Thistle agreed with him, but Orsik would have none of it, because there was a treasure to be found. Harshnag sat down and said, "I will not help you anymore." Orsik glared at him and charged into the village with the rest of the 3HPP.

The village held an ambush. Although it was well thought out, the heroes won the fight, and the Crag Cats lay dead. Harshnag shouted again, "We must go." Again Orsik disagreed, and his friend Vardis set about stripping the pelts from the corpses of the monstrous cats. "Now!" thundered Harshnag. Orsik objected a third time: "We need to see if there are any survivors or treasure." Harshnag sat down and began to pray. He had seen enough.

Off in the distance, two frost giants were approaching. Harshnag resolved that he would not fight to defend the 3HPP from their folly: either they would survive the combat, or he would bring their bodies to Krowen Valharrow, and the mage would find new heroes. He overheard Thistle plead with the others to flee. Vardis would not leave his buddy, and the crazed mage was convinced he had nothing to fear, because he could not be killed: he alone knew how to save the world from the end. The fairy's pleas fell on deaf ears, and Harshnag's were scoffed at.

So Harshnag left the village, saying, "if you live, then you are the right hero. I cannot interfere." Soon rocks flew. Harshnag overheard Orsik scream for help, and the frost giant ignored the dwarf and prayed to the All-Father. With his eyes closed he heard fireballs roaring into life and felt the ground shudder from the sound of falling rocks. He sighed, believing his quest was coming to an end.

Then came the most surprising of sounds: the body of a giant falling to the ground. "Can it be?" he wondered, and he prayed harder to the All-Father. Next he heard the whooping of the 3HPP. A few minutes later he opened his eyes and smiled: "They are the heroes."
