# Chapter 59 - Enter Blagothkus

Date: 28 Eleasis 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
With the Ogre Zombies in the stern tower destroyed, Vardis and Orsik fall into a debate about free will. Vardis opens with "We have a Bob problem." He is evil, and when Orsik suggests he could choose to change, Vardis explains that "Creatures that are native to the prime material plane have agency." Orsik accuses him of planism — "So the prime material plane is superior to other planes? Wow. That’s rabid planism." — and the argument wanders on to whether clerics hate the undead because they lack agency.

"The conversation becomes tedious," so Unla Key turns invisible and Thistle sneaks, and the two climb to the second floor of the tower. The room shows the marks of a series of battles: scorch marks from magical fires, gouges on the bed from swords, broken arrows, and frozen blood pools. Icy murals of cloud giants riding giant birds line the walls, and a smashed bed with a partly melted ice headboard dominates the room, with two large wooden chests at its foot. In the middle stands a large Frost Giant skeleton. Thistle asks, "Why would a Frost Giant be in a Cloud Giant’s castle?" Unla Key, unsure whether he is remembering a class, a story or a vision of the future, answers that "This castle played a prominent role in the final defeat of Tiamat." That leaves Thistle asking "How did Blagothkus get control of the castle again?" Both begin to suspect that the friendly vampire is not as pleasant as she appeared, and may be the castle's actual proprietor, wanting a base in the sky for necromantic experiments. What is inside the glacier is an open question.

Thistle quietly empties the chests of Blagothkus' treasure, and the two return unnoticed to Orsik and Vardis, who are still debating. Unla Key shouts, "Screw Bob and his play," and points to the haul: "This is enough to retire on." They all stare at him.

Leaving the stern tower, the party sneaks along the starboard side of the castle past a room containing a Frost Giant. While the others debate, the invisible Unla Key wonders, "Are they immune to psychic magic?" He remembers he was drunk when that topic was covered, trusts his gut, and casts psychic lance. The giant skeleton is immobilized and then destroyed. Unla Key declares, "it doesn’t matter what I thought I knew. I know the future, and the future said the spell worked." The room itself holds nothing but detritus.

In the central outer courtyard at the aft of the cloud castle, a staircase leads to a second tower whose door is barred heavily shut. Orsik casts knock, and the door opens on two gaunt-looking ogres who scream in terror. Orsik is confused: everyone else is a zombie, but these two are alive. He asks, "do you speak common?" The ogre with a scar on his face answers, "My name is Worug. Do you have food?" The other, with a bandaged right hand, adds, "My name is Tak, and we are hungry." Thistle, staring at gnawed ogre bones, offers "Goodberries?" After eating several, the ogres look less feeble. Worug tries to hug Thistle.

Asked why they are not dead, Worug explains, "It turns out that if you don’t let a Vampire in, it can not enter your house." The other ogres let in a purple lady who called herself the big boss lady, and then came "chaos, mayhem, and blood." Unla Key infers that the ogres' chambers and Blagothkus' chambers are safe from the Vampire. The ogres describe the last few months: the crazy vampire lady killed everyone, and Blagothkus was crying and saying that he had killed his son. Since coming to Parnast, Blagothkus has been "holed up in the control room." The vampire fed on his retinue, and when the remaining creatures attacked, the vampires turned the dead into undead. Lately there has been "a bit of an ogre and elf game" as the two sneak around for food by day, when the vampires do not come out.

A bored Thistle asks, "What’s in it for us?" and Vardis asks him, "When did you get so - Orsik?" Worug says he has a secret he will tell if they are taken off the castle. Vardis guesses that one of the ogres likes the other; Tak coughs "a little too eagerly" that "That’s ridiculous," and Worug asks Tak, "Why didn’t you say something?" After a long, awkward pause, Tak promises to tell if the party will get both of them off the castle, and Unla Key shouts, "Sure, I’ll gamble."

Worug reveals that inside the glacier the castle is built around is the den of a dracolich, Glazhael. The white dracolich was once the steed of Talis the White; the First Faction killed Glazhael, and the dragon, now a dracolich, tried to capture the castle but was sent on a wild goose chase after some artifact. When the return of Tiamat was thwarted, Blagothkus took control of the castle "and Glazhael came back here."

Orsik whistles: "A vampire, a dracolich, and an army of undead. How badly do we want this castle?" Unla Key puts on a sorrowful face. Tak, his arm around Worug, declares that they will not leave the tower and that the party can come there for rest and safety from the vampire. Walking away, Orsik wonders why the vampire would kill them, and Unla Key says "because the vampire might conclude it needs three, not four heroes." Thistle announces he is sleeping on the ground, and Vardis wonders if he can get Delsephine to come and pick them up.

Descending from the ogres' tower, the party reaches the starboard barracks on the upper deck and finds what the ogres described: dead bodies, chaos and mayhem, with several ogres still decomposing. Across the upper courtyard on the port side, another Frost Giant Skeleton waits in one of three guest rooms. Unla Key again incapacitates it with a psychic lance, but this time his allies cannot kill it, and it strikes back with a Freezing Stare. Unla Key barely survives; the creature is killed. Aft of the guest rooms is a room that housed kobolds, which the Vampire used as a snack room: the kobolds are tied to their beds and dead, used as "a living blood bank." Vardis suggests, "maybe we kill the Vampire?" Orsik shuts it down: "We need the vampire."

Orsik marches to the control room at the aft of the cloud castle and knocks. A male giant's voice booms, "I will not let you, Sandesyl, or any of your hell spawn enter my tower. Do you hear that?" When the party protests that they are not vampires, the voice shouts, "That’s what she said last time!" A periscope rises over the top of the tower, and the voice concedes, "Yes, you are not Vampires." Calmer, it asks what they want. Asked whether he has spoken to the Oracle, the giant starts to cry: "That’s how it all began! Me talking to the Oracle!" When the party says the chamber had not been opened in a thousand years, he laughs — "Harshnag! You must have talked to Harshnag! It’s a standing joke." Harshnag, he says, hides the items he needs in the temple and judges little people worthy if they can figure them out and survive. Vardis is aghast, having had to solve a puzzle and nearly die. Orsik asks why the giant does not go to the Oracle now; the answer is "Because my, she won’t let us go!" Asked whether he means the Dracolich, the giant scoffs: "that interloper? No. My wife."

Built around a glacier. The chapter places rooms on its starboard and port sides, a central outer courtyard at the aft, an upper courtyard, starboard barracks on the upper deck, three guest rooms, a kobold room, and the control room at the aft.

## NPCs
### Worug
A gaunt ogre with a scar (later "a scar") on his face, alive behind a barred door. He explains the vampire rule, describes the massacre, tries to hug Thistle, and reveals the dracolich in the glacier.

### Tak
The ogre with a bandaged right hand. He denies liking Worug "a little too eagerly," promises the secret in exchange for passage off the castle, and offers the tower as a refuge.

### Blagothkus
Whose lair is the stern tower, and who took control of the castle when the return of Tiamat was thwarted. Per the ogres, he cried that he had killed his son and is holed up in the control room; his treasure is taken from the stern tower.

### The male giant in the control room
An unnamed giant voice behind the control room door. He refuses entry to "Sandesyl," checks the party with a periscope, weeps about the Oracle, laughs at Harshnag's standing joke, and says it is his wife who won't let them go.

### Sandesyl
Named by the giant in the control room as the one he will not let into his tower. The ogres describe a vampire "purple lady" who called herself the big boss lady and killed everyone.

### Glazhael
A white dracolich whose den is inside the glacier; once the steed of Talis the White, killed by the First Faction.

### Harshnag
Not present. The control-room giant says he brings humans to the Oracle, claims no one has been there in a thousand years, and hides items in the temple to test little people.
