# Chapter 40 - Heading into the Spine of the World

Date: 25 to 27 Flamerule 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary

The chapter opens with Harshnag the Grim sitting under a tree beside a fire, weighing what he has heard of the giants. To the North, the Frost Giant Jarl has been laying waste to settlements while searching for a magical artifact that would let him turn the lands of the North into ice. To the east, the Fire Giant Duke wants to build and repair a magical device that would give him the power to master the dragons. To the south, refugees speak of the mad Thane who wants to wipe out the non-Giant races and has already laid waste to many towns near Orlbar and Llorkh. The cloud giants are "being mighty secretive and hiding in their clouds," and of the Hill Giants he thinks, "And as for the Hill Giants, surely no one should take them as a serious threat."

When he spots the 3HPP advancing toward him, he stamps out the fire and smiles. "We must go to the eye of the All-Father, now." The answer comes back: "Nope. We intend to go to the Mines of Mirabar to check out the mines." Harshnag is stunned, and the chapter gives him the line "Look, if you folks don’t care about your people being destroyed, then Krowen Valharrow has allied with the wrong people." The reply is that they care, but want to stop by the Mines first. The elf interrupts to ask where they are going. Vardis offers a route — over the pass to the south, or through this valley and up to the Eye of the All-Father — and Harshnag scratches his head, consults his map, and agrees: "Yes, that might work." Orsik shouts, "So great the mine is on the way," and Harshnag sighs.

A day later Orsik arrives at the Mines of Mirabar, which turn out to be "more like the picket fence and the guard post at the edge of the land that may contain the Mines of Mirabar." A guard tells them they can go no further without the appropriate passes. A very cold Thistle shouts, "I need to buy some wing muffs! I NEED TO BUY SOME WING MUFFS!" — and a second, gruff guard grunts back "Wing muffs!", clearly unconvinced such a thing should exist. Orsik, doing his best — "Hey, I am the hero of Mirabar because I caught the Weevil routine" — persuades the guard to fetch the Foreman, who is in turn convinced only that Orsik has already been properly paid: "You got paid for your work. Now please go." Aghast, Orsik asks with a hint of desperation tinged with malice, "Perhaps you need a bounty hunter?" Something twinkles in the Foreman's eye: "I may need it in the morning. Why don’t you have dinner tonight in the lodge, and we can speak in the morning." The 3HPP enter the compound and hand their weapons to the guards.

Thistle is cold. The Feywild is not a cold place; Queen Titania is cold, but that is about her heart rather than the temperature. He needs wing-tip warmers. In the lodge — a single vast, tall room holding at least 200 men, heated by several fireplaces lit with magic since there is no smoke — miners eat ravenously after a shift. Thistle approaches a dwarf named Fruf, asks for "Wing muffins" and then "Wingtip muffins," and is offered extra socks instead: "Well, I don’t have any of those, but I have some extra socks." Looking at the warm poofy socks, Thistle remembers that one of the half-pints has a knack for making things, and turns to Orsik, who sighs, "Fine."

That evening, Vardis, cleric of Corellon Larethian, is interrupted in his drinking by another elf who asks, "Are you a cleric of Corellon?" Vardis's relationship with the Cormiira, the college of the high-ranking priests, is so toxic that he is not welcome in any temple of his god, so he hedges: "I have a relationship with that god." The elf makes a sign with his hand; Vardis, unsure what it means, answers "Peace be with you," and the elf stiffens. Orsik adds, "he’s from Waterdeep." The elf spits out "A progressive cleric of Larethian" and adds, "I am not even sure you are a cleric of our god," then turns away. When Orsik asks what that was about, Vardis explains that elves live a long time, that there is much interpretation of religious symbols, influences and rituals, and that "Some of us believe that the great war that split the elves was a mistake, and others think that we have not done enough to end the great war." Orsik, he knows, did not hear a single word.

In the early morning the chapter shifts to the Foreman's thoughts: "I need to get rid of these yahoos. I know the type pretending to be interested in helping. For all I know, they are agents of the Weevil, or maybe he told them things." He recalls Orsik's claim from the night before to have been responsible for getting Weevil to reveal the details of the caravan code; he had spoken to the Axe using the unique sending stones and learned that was an exaggeration — Orsik had told them about Weevil’s offer, but the Axe had extracted the information. Still, he offers a job: "There are crag cats a day's hike out." The Frost Giant and the Ranger try to urge Orsik to drop the quest, which upsets the Foreman, but the progressive cleric of Corellon Larethian comes to the rescue by pointing out how much money is to be made from the pelts.

The 3HPP head out of the restricted areas of the mines into hilly frozen terrain, toward a valley whose name is unknown to those in the warmer south, lying between two arms of the Spine of the World, the range that blocks the Reghed Glacier from advancing south. After a day’s hike they find a tent with a burning fire and a corpse. Before they can pillage the body, four frost drakes attack. The battle is short and brutal: three drakes are killed and one flees. "No crag cats," mutters Orsik, who had already spent the pelt money on fine ale in his head. But the body carries a map with an X on it, and Harshnag recognises it: "I know where that is. It’s a bit out of the way, but we can get there, and it won’t add too much time to our trip."
