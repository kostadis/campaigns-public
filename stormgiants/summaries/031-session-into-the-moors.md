# Chapter 31 - Into the Moors

Date: 30 Kythorn to 1 Flamerule 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
After a two-day journey, the 3HPP arrive at Calling Horns and head straight to the Inn. As Thistle recounts it, Calling Horns was nothing more than a trailside inn until a few years ago, when Tamalin Zoar bought the establishment and retired there. She attracted settlers to the region, giving rise to a small village whose citizens pay monthly fees for her protection; she is still spry at 60, and she is the law. According to Eltio, she can provide the 3HPP a letter of mark that can be redeemed for the service they requested. Broke, and desperate to get their sword, the heroes knock on the Inn's door. Outside lie a gruesome pile of bones from dead orcs who wandered too close, and a large stable that houses several horses. Orsik's verdict before they even meet her: "she sounds like an amazing person."

Inside, the main room has several large tables and a kitchen in the back, and the tavern is full of local villagers enjoying the food and company. When Tamalin enters, Orsik approaches her and declares, "I’m really interested in meeting you. Your business skills and vision lead to this investment. You created this thriving economy." After a pause he adds, "I’d like to learn from you." Surprised and flattered, Tamalin clears the main table of its patrons, shouts to the kitchen for drinks and food, and begs the 3HPP to sit so she can share her experiences. The chapter reads her as an adventurer at the end of her career who finds the idea of sharing her stories with people beginning theirs appealing. When Thistle brings up the play in Triboar as an initial investment, she is impressed but mutters, "hits driven businesses are not very sustainable. But if you get a big hit, it’s worth more than a dragon’s hoard." The talk drags into the night until, muttering about not getting older, she gives them two rooms in the house for free.

Orsik and Vardis take one room, Unla Key and Thistle the other. Just as they are about to sleep, Tamalin bangs at their doors: "trolls! I have a pair of trolls attacking my stables and several fine horses there." Thistle flies out of the window and strikes one troll outside the stables with his bow, directing Thuul to cover it in acid to stop its regeneration. Unla Key opens a window and kills the troll from a distance with fire bolts. Orsik and Vardis grab their shields and weapons and rush out in their pajamas. The first troll dies outside the stable; the second is killed inside the stables, but not before it slaughters two horses.

Having seen the 3HPP in action, Tamalin invites them for a drink before bed and tells them, "I have a problem." Orsik asks whether it is something other than trolls; it is trolls. Orsik demurs anyway, pitching her: "No, your problem is a lack of entertainment options for your patrons. You need a theater production." She likes his gumption and his style, but has "a more immediate problem", and then, in front of the whole group, tells him he is being too aggressive with his sales pitch. Orsik is taken aback, objecting that he thought it was a one-on-one conversation; she says she wanted everybody else to benefit. Orsik glares at her: "I don’t think this mentee thing is working out." Tamalin realizes she has upset the dwarf and apologizes, and Vardis is surprised to see his old friend accept the apology.

Once everyone has settled and drunk a first glass, Tamalin lays out the job. Trolls have been coming down into Calling Horns far more often of late. There are many trolls in the Evermoors, "or Trollmoors as we call them", a big swamp to the north. She wants the 3HPP to go there and find out what is happening and why the trolls are coming down; solving the problem would be great, but knowing is enough, and in return she can provide a Letter of Mark. She then mentions, waiting for recognition, that she is a member of the Zoar family. Thistle remembers something about the family, and that a Zoar commanded the army of Everlund. Tamalin confirms that her brother is the High Captain of Everlund: if they go to Everlund and show Dral her note, he will get the captain to do whatever they need. Dral runs the tavern Danivarr’s House, which the Zoar family owns.

Unla Key, no longer sober, reacts badly. He had been alcohol-free for two weeks, the sobriety was starting to hurt his brain, and the fresh alcohol soothed his nerves. He turns to Tamalin: "let me get this straight, we do this work for you, and we get a letter? We have better things to do." Orsik pulls his hair out and spells out the chain: the letter gets Eltio to lead them to Harthal Zymorven, who gets them their sword, and dragging Harthal back to Zymorven Hall earns a 1000 gold piece reward. Unla Key stares at Orsik and concedes, "I guess we can do this." As Orsik falls asleep, he thinks admiringly that Tamalin is a scoundrel: "She went from thief and crook to scoundrel. I want that kind of arc."

On the 1st day of Flamerule the 3HPP head out into the Evermoors, looking for whatever is driving the trolls out of the moors to hunt for food. After a day of mud, bugs and sweat under the hot summer sun, they find a campsite near a stream. Orsik grumbles that apart from bugs they found nothing. Thistle is less convinced: there are signs, though he is not sure of what, and he knows they need only go deeper into the marsh. Then he has it. The chapter has it that "He points out to Unla Key some people walking in the marsh": the same nomadic humans that attacked them when they left Triboar, and asks if he can understand what they are saying. Unla Key casts comprehend languages and rushes off; Thistle swears and follows him.

It is a good thing he does. Unla Key overhears the nomads talking about trolls moving out of their usual hunting grounds and encroaching on the nomads’ territory, but he does not move quietly getting into position. The warriors and the wizard stare at each other while he tries to be friendly, until a survivor of the last attack recognizes him as that annoying wizard and charges at Unla Key and Thistle. Vardis, who saw Unla Key's noisy approach, hits the warriors near the shaman with a fireball that lights up the night sky, filling the air with the smell of charred flesh and burned trees. Thistle strikes one warrior with his bow, then another, which sets off an incredulous exchange with Unla Key, who learns Thistle has been able to shoot two arrows that quickly "Ever since before we faced the hill giant in Parnast" and simply "Wanted the challenge." The mage laughs, decides he really likes this guy, then finishes the fleeing warriors with magic missiles. Orsik arrives ready to fight and finds only dead bodies: "But I didn’t get a chance to kill anyone!!!"

## Locations
### Calling Horns
A village that was once only a trailside inn. Settlers came after Tamalin Zoar bought the inn and retired there, and they pay her monthly fees for protection. Trolls have been coming down into it more often of late.

### Danivarr’s House

### Zymorven Hall
Where the party means to drag Harthal Zymorven back to for a 1000 gold piece reward.

## NPCs
### Tamalin Zoar
A retired adventurer who is "still spry at 60 and is the law" in Calling Horns. Generous and talkative with the 3HPP, she gives them her table and two free rooms, offers frank business advice ("you gotta know when to make the sales pitch"), and apologizes when her public correction upsets Orsik. She hires the party to find out why the trolls are coming down from the Evermoors, in exchange for a Letter of Mark, and reveals she is a member of the Zoar family.

### Orsik
Plays the eager mentee, flattering Tamalin's business vision, then pitches her a theater production in the middle of her troll problem. Stung by her public critique, he declares the mentoring is not working, then accepts her apology. He talks Unla Key into the job and admires Tamalin as a scoundrel. In the Evermoors he arrives at the fight too late to kill anyone.

Recounts the history of Calling Horns and recalls the Zoar family's link to Everlund's army. Flies out a window to shoot a troll and directs Thuul's acid. ### Unla Key
Kills a troll with fire bolts from a window. Drunk after two weeks of sobriety, he dismisses the job ("We have better things to do") until Orsik explains the chain of favours. In the marsh he casts comprehend languages to eavesdrop on the nomads, is spotted, and finishes the fleeing warriors with magic missiles. The chapter calls him "the mage" and has the nomads call him "that annoying wizard".

### Vardis

### Thuul
Directed by Thistle to cover a troll in acid to stop its regeneration.

### Dral
Runs the tavern Danivarr’s House in Everlund for the Zoar family. Per Tamalin, if he is shown her note he will get the captain to do whatever the party needs.

### Eltio
The source of the information that Tamalin can provide a letter of mark. Per Orsik, the letter gets Eltio to lead them to Harthal Zymorven.

### The nomadic humans
The same nomads who attacked the party when it left Triboar. They are overheard complaining that trolls are encroaching on their territory.
