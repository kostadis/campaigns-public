# Chapter 10 - The SEER

Date: 1 to 2 Tarsakh 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
The chapter opens at night, with the heroes creeping up "with cat-like tread" on half-ogres sitting around a fire and catching them unawares. Vardis opens with a guiding bolt that streaks through the night sky into one of the half-ogres. Unla Key, preparing his spell, thinks about his wager: "The way I see it, if I win, I get money. If I am dead,  it doesn't matter." He mutters "For the 50!", charges up to the pair and casts Rime's Binding Ice. Manipulating the fates just a little, he kills the first; the second, too groggy from being woken to react, is wounded and trapped by the ice. Orsik throws a handaxe, and Vardis kills the remaining ogre with a single arrow.

Wallace, who had been surprised, is told "That's how it's done, Wallace." and smiles sheepishly, then asks whether they hang out a lot and went to school together. The dwarf, annoyed, answers "He's my best friend." An obviously hurt Wallace repeats "Your best friend?", and gets back "I met you like one week ago." Orsik stares at Wallace; Wallace stares back in awe.

Meanwhile Thistle explores the razorvine alone and flies into a swarm of poisonous snakes that drop onto him and bite; he barely escapes with his life. He remembers Unla Key saying "I think we should stick together." and his own flippant answer, "I am a scout. I scout." As he retreats, he thinks, "I scouted!" The rest of the group searches the bodies and the huts. In one hut lies a dead dwarf, and Orsik stares: "my kinsman." The narration notes that among dwarves the word is used only within a clan, and offers two explanations: that Orsik takes a pan-dwarven view, or that his relationship with Vardis got him kicked out of his clan. Orsik shows Thistle the golden beard clips taken from the dead and asks what he found. Thistle answers "Snakes." and munches some goodberries.

The group pauses to weigh routes: north to the sinkhole, probably where Mama Manyknuckles lives; northwest into razorvine with poisonous snakes; northeast along razorvine and a beach toward the northern camp. They stealthily reach the northern island near an open wooden pavilion stained with gore, with six ramshackle huts past the gazebo around a massive fire pit. Orsik mutters "perfect." Thistle notices the camp could hold 12 giants but sees only 2 half-ogres, and his attempt to read the tracks fails. While Wallace is not paying attention, the rest surprise the two creatures. Thistle shoots and sends Thuul ahead, Orsik dashes, and Vardis kills one half-ogre. The other, slow to react, screams "Mama Mia!" Wallace misses and thinks, "I gotta step up my game." After more handaxes, another strike from Vardis and a magic missile from Unla Key, Thuul smashes the half-ogre at Thistle's command and it dies.

The chapter shifts to Mama Manyknuckles' point of view. Thinking of "Those knuckleheads", she signals two guards toward the north ramp, gets no answer from her mud mephits ("Damn annoying creatures!"), and hopes the intruders taking a breather means "maybe they will go away". When she hears them trying to flank her from the south, she whispers to her half-ogres to set an ambush. The ambush is a partial success: the invaders are surprised and frightened, but their will is too strong and she can kill only one with her death gaze. Mama Mia reveals herself as a hag. Orsik is struck down when he does not look away in time, but the rest of the group overwhelms and kills her ogres and her beloved Belg. She tries to flee and cannot. Her last thoughts blame Bad Fruul: "Ever since my gang signed up with you, I knew this was inevitable."

From Unla Key's point of view ("I gambled and won, and the world continued for another day."), he hails Feetor as "New king of the swamp!" Feetor hands over his winnings, which turn out not to be gold. Orsik demands "Where's the gold?", and Feetor protests: "this is special mud! It makes you look prettier and heals you from bad wounds!" Vardis, in a deep raspy voice, says "We have altered the deal, Mephit," and demands the gold. Unla Key is about to attack when he notices the swamp bubbling, and Thistle shouts "we are not fighting these Mud Mephits." To defuse things, Feetor hugs Unla Key and declares "you are an honorary Mud Mephit!", and offers a stone that can be used to ask for the mephits' help. The dwarf snickers about being part of the tribe and whispers to Wallace, "Good lesson. Never give your money to a mud mephit." When Wallace repeats this lesson to Unla Key, Unla Key has had enough and proposes a new bet: "How about we bet this mud that I can kill you in a duel?" Impressed and frightened, Feetor promises the money and asks "But can you say I am #2?" Unla Key sighs, "sure." Feetor proclaims "Today is a great day! The civil war has ended. We are now all followers of Unla Key!" But the mephits are not convinced: factions back Orsik (because Wallace already follows him), Vardis (for his grace), and Thistle (for his command of the Drake), and the civil war erupts again until the place falls quiet. Feetor shrugs: "oh well."

Orsik and Wallace explore the sinkhole and realize it is the roof of a wizard's tower, where they find a statue. On Mama Mia they find a stone pendant with a jade inlay of Stein, the stone rune. After resting, they are woken in the morning by a whistling noise as a golden pseudodragon with milky-white eyes arrives. Orsik mumbles "That again". Hsing whistles that he has just teleported in as the emissary of the SEER. He explains that the SEER believes they have found "the tower of the Wizard of the Crag" and wants them to explore it for the repository of Lore he is rumoured to have had, offering to pay handsomely for manuscripts on ancient forms of magic. Orsik muses aloud that "Killing it would be awesome! We could get some serious cred with the Zhentarim." Wallace urges caution: "The SEER is the top Spymaster of the Lords' Alliance. And if she can find us here, she can find us anywhere." Orsik lets Thistle question Hsing, and they learn that the Wizard was a master of at least the elemental powers of water and earth, worked with all of the elements, and was a devotee of both Mystra and Oghma. They also learn that the SEER needs any kind of help to deal with Bad Fruul. Hsing closes: "Coming out with just one miserable spellbook is not acceptable. Use the pendant that you found." Then he apparently teleports away.

## NPCs
### Wallace
A young companion who has known Orsik for about a week and looks up to him in awe. He misses in the fight, thinks "I gotta step up my game," and warns that the SEER, "the top Spymaster of the Lords' Alliance", can find them anywhere.

### Feetor
A mud mephit who let Unla Key bet on the half-ogres. He pays out in special mud instead of gold, makes Unla Key an honorary Mud Mephit, gives him a stone for summoning mephit help, and asks to be his #2.

### Mama Manyknuckles
Also called Mama Mia. A hag who commands half-ogres, two guards and mud mephits from the sinkhole. She ambushes the party, strikes Orsik down with her death gaze, and dies trying to flee, blaming Bad Fruul in her last thoughts.

### Belg
Mama Manyknuckles' "beloved" creature, overwhelmed and killed by the group along with her ogres.

### Thuul
A creature under Thistle's command, which he sends ahead; it smashes the second northern half-ogre at his command.

### Hsing
A golden, blind pseudodragon with milky-white eyes, the emissary of the SEER. He arrives by teleport, is fond of talking about himself and his naps, commissions the party to explore the tower, and teleports away.

### The SEER
Hsing's mistress. Wallace calls her the top Spymaster of the Lords' Alliance. She wants the Wizard's manuscripts and needs help against Bad Fruul.

### Bad Fruul
A power Mama Manyknuckles' gang had signed up with. His emergence as a local power thwarted the Zhentarim.
