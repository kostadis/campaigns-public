# Chapter 23 - Orsik

Date: 9 to about 12 Mirtul 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary

The battle for Parnast ends with a thud. Chandra Stol savours the sound of swords striking Bad Fruul's body: "It was a satisfying thud." The death of the giant and her nemesis makes her smile, until she remembers that her pet wyvern is dead too.

The aftermath is loud. Ragnar shouts, "What have you done to this town?" and Gundalin is about to agree. Orsik, covered in blood, guts, and gore, shouts back, "Yes, look what we did, saved your town!" Vardis, laughing, adds that they also got rid of the wyvern and "some of those architectural eye sores." The heroes agree the wyvern was "a disappointment" — "Not a single attack landed; even Chandra caused more harm." The two sprites claim the credit, insisting their poisoned arrows brought down Bad Fruul, and Orsik graciously thanks them. Vardis declares that if they ever have to choose between a wyvern and sprites, "we are so totally taking the sprites!" The sprites tumble, high-five, and chant "Sprites FTW!"

Blasius emerges from his hideout with a flask and offers Unla Key some fine wine, which Unla Key accepts. Blasius then hails Orsik as "My brother, my friend, my hero!" — but as he waves at the battlefield the cost becomes plain: most of Orsik's militia is dead, half the town is devastated, the ground has seeped with blood, and the flies have come to feast. Orsik only shouts, "Later."

Chandra, who had watched the fight from near the well, appears. Orsik tells her she was going to be the new head of the guard; she says she never wanted the job, and he answers that after the wyvern's performance it was no longer hers. Vardis snarks, "Win-win for everyone." Chandra sobs that her wyvern fought heroically; Orsik and Vardis agree only that he was "a bold distraction" who stood there and allowed himself to be killed. The local cleric sobs, "You’re a mean person." Orsik insists the outcome is good and the town is saved, and the surviving militia, Ragnar, Gundalin and the rest stare at him in disbelief. When Vardis asks about casualties, Ragnar mumbles, "Half the militia, I think," and Vardis asks about civilians. Chandra erupts: in this town the distinction between civilians and militia is lost — "These were our friends, our family, and our townspeople." Chandra, Ragnar, and Gundalin begin to argue about how to fix the town while everyone else sits down to breathe.

Orsik uses the moment to sneak over to Bad Fruul. He carries a headband he won at cards while still a guard in the city, and he plans to swap it for the one on the giant's head, because he has never trusted the SEER or the pseudodragon. He reasons that the SEER "set up this complicated plan that almost killed me to find this band." But his internal monologue runs too long: Thistle flies up and sees him holding two bands, and Orsik trips while pulling the other headband from between his legs, ends up with the cursed one in his mouth, and tries to shove the fake onto the giant's head. Thistle says, "Um."

Orsik argues that they should not give the headband to the SEER. Thistle counters that the SEER said she would discuss it and that they would get it back, and that although she has led them into danger without warning, he does not know if she has broken her promises yet. Orsik worries that the artifact has an evil soul and that the SEER may have plans for it. Chandra explodes that the soul has been tormenting her family for too long and that the SEER should get rid of it. Then Unla Key announces, "I want the soul in me," explaining that with it he would have access to the magic of the drow and the lore of a 500-year-old wizard. Vardis notices the second headband and asks where it came from; Orsik answers, "Not important."

Things then turn very upsetting for Orsik. Everyone wants him to hand the headband to the SEER, and no one will explain why they trust her. Chandra accuses him of endangering her life and sneers when he asks why she trusts the SEER; one of his guards claims he is putting the town at risk and rolls his eyes at Orsik's question; Wallace argues that the SEER is an essential member of the Lords’ Alliance whose support the town needs. Then Hsing, the gold pseudodragon, shows up and demands the headband, and the townspeople and his allies side with the creature. Orsik feels marginalized and distrusted at the very end of his redemption. He says, "I’m done," drops the magical object, and marches out of town.

Thistle flies after him. Orsik says he is done with everyone, not just Parnast: he never liked the pseudodragon, does not know what "this headband" is, and nobody thinks his distrust matters. Ragnar, racing after him, says, "My dear friend. You make some excellent points." Chandra mumbles, "What do we really know about the Lords’ Alliance?" Gundalin, out of breath, says that from what Wallace and Orsik understand, the Lords' Alliance's machinations caused the death and destruction in the town. Thistle offers that they did defeat the Cult of the Dragon; Chandra shrugs that so did the Zhentarim, who figured they would either help defeat Tiamat or get devoured by a five-headed dragon. Everyone begins to see Orsik's point. Even Hsing, after insisting that the SEER is "the top spymaster in the whole world," concedes, "I guess you shouldn’t trust a spymaster. They do lie." Orsik is smug.

Just then a door materializes and the SEER steps through, demanding the item from Thistle. The answer is no, and Orsik refuses her offer to step into her garden. Pointing at her with the cursed headband, Orsik demands, "What are you going to do with the evil soul?" The SEER laughs and says she will "send it to where it was supposed to go." Seeing that Orsik has changed profoundly, she agrees to perform the ritual in the town the next morning. Orsik is left staring, confused, at the wall from which she appeared.

That evening the town throws a party. An unexpected guest, Prince Thornacious, tells Thistle that he has mentioned him to Queen Titania of the Seelie Court and that she was pleased: "You are being noticed!" Thistle asks whether that is good; the Prince laughs that to be noticed by the Queen "is all we should ever want." Thistle replies, "I just want my beloved."

A drunk Blasius and Unla Key are still singing in the morning when the SEER arrives and says, "Well, let’s get this done." A few moments later, Orsik is holding a headband of intellect. As the sun sets, Wallace tells his mentor that he is quitting: "Elspeth and I decided to hook up, and the town needs a new guard captain." Orsik grunts his approval and sheds not a single tear.

A few days later, Orsik and the other companions begin their long trip to Triboar to find Uncle Joon. Orsik, who had the intelligence of an ox, now has a genius-level intellect; he was the only one who wanted the headband. On the march, his new mind addresses his old self, congratulating him on completing a redemption arc — "I suppose redemption doesn’t need intellect, just a good heart." He looks forward to "knowing more about the world and its history," wonders aloud, "I wonder if I should become a mage?" and then hits himself on the forehead: "Oh, that’s why people kept getting upset at that thing."
