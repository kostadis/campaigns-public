# Chapter 73 - The Wand of Ill Fortune

Date: 27 Eleint 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
In the morning, the members of the 3HPP find a letter sealed with Orsik's logo. Each of them looks up at Orsik and sees his smile, and they open it. The letter lays out a problem: the dracolich has agreed to help them kill the vampire in exchange for the party locating and returning his phylactery. Orsik calls this "a Bad Idea" — "A freed dracolich could wreak untold horrors upon Faerûn" — and declares that "heroes do not free dracoliches." But they need help in the battle, and Orsik proposes an old acquaintance: Bob Saget.

Anticipating objections, Orsik's letter explains that the party had to fight Bob, who had a zombie army and "was going to Waylay Silverymoon." Yet Orsik admits, "the truth is, I kind of liked Bob. He was funny. He made us a LOT of money." He says he talked Unla Key into sending Bob to the astral plane rather than killing him, along with one of his drow allies and four stooges, while letting his other drow get away. Now, Orsik proposes, Vardis can use the spell Planar Ally to bring Bob to them. Bob would fight the vampire alongside the party and help retrieve the phylactery, and in return the dracolich would be his to keep. Orsik lists the benefits — the dracolich and Bob for the battle, no "pesky dracolich" afterward, and Bob removed as an adversary — and closes with "I await your acquiescence."

Thistle is impressed and immediately pens a reply, raising three objections: Bob might betray them just to see them fail, Bob might betray them hoping to get the dracolich for free, and Bob might use the dracolich for something even worse. Orsik tut-tuts the grammar and capitalization and answers point by point: Bob is plotting against them anyway; neither they nor Bob can get the phylactery until the vampire is dead; and "A free dracolich is Our Fault," while "Bob's actions are His Fault." Orsik glares. Thistle stares.

Unla Key suggests they examine exactly what the rules for Planar Ally are, and the rules are laid out: the caster beseeches a known entity, which sends a creature that is under no compulsion to behave in any particular way; services are bargained for payment; and if no price is agreed, the creature immediately returns to its home plane. Vardis asks, "What prevents him from sending the Dracolich to attack us?" Orsik suggests giving Bob something else of value, like a wand of wonder; Unla Key says, "No." Vardis adds that Bob would go back to the Abyss with an emissary on the prime material plane, invulnerable because the phylactery would be somewhere in the abyss: "I’m not sure giving a demon a dracolich is -heroic-."

The new Orsik, rather than getting angry, tries to incorporate the good points and floats a double cross: give Bob the phylactery and then send the Dracolich to get it. Thistle asks if that is "A back stab?" Vardis observes, "Not really, because he was never on our side," prompting, "So, a stab?" Orsik grouses that "The Bob plan was good, but the problem of the Dracolich remains. Let’s table it for now," privately confident that the team will come round. Unla Key is disappointed — the potential for mayhem and chaos was high.

Later, when the sun has risen, the heroes climb to the top of the control tower, some 70 feet above the upper courtyard. Orsik asks, "So we do this with no spells, right?" and the rest of the 3HPP nod. Thistle, Vardis and Orsik fire at the Ogre Zombies, which head for the tower and start to climb. Orsik asks whether they can use mold earth on the stone, and Unla Key, sighing, launches into the arcane lore of the mists of Ravenloft and the story of Slerdax, a dark lord who runs a restaurant that traps the unwary, who "wants to be praised for his brilliant cooking and excellent service but is cursed to doubt." He explains that he took an advanced course from a forbidden lore teacher at his school of magic. Then Unla Key uses toll the dead on one of the Zombies; Vardis mutters that it is "Not exactly the best cantrip to use" and then uses it on the same monster, "but when the monster is injured, it is the best cantrip to use." Unla Key is irritated, and the zombies keep climbing.

Blagothkus, the cloud giant, joins in. When Orsik asks if he has another boulder, Vardis snarks, "Of course he does," adding, "He only has the best boulders." The giant agrees — "Why, yes. Only the very best." — before dropping it on one of the Zombies. Thistle fires two arrows at two zombies to soften them for toll the dead, then groans as Unla Key pulls out the small boulder they found at the one stone: the boulder of wonder. It produces a puff of green, noxious smoke — "Stinking cloud." Vardis notes that it "was almost useful. But they are immune to poison." Orsik is about to start screaming and sighs, "I can’t." Blagothkus remarks, "I have met better magic users. I know better magic users. I have a whole binder full of better magic users." Someone growls at Unla Key that he will dismiss the spell, and the wizard agrees, muttering that "Every moment is not about optimizing your actions; it’s about living." Orsik brings up Bob again — "The demon of dad jokes" — and Vardis sighs, "And give him a real dracolich." Meanwhile, somewhere in the Astral Plane, Bob wonders why he hears the faint sounds of his name.

Vardis kills a zombie and leaves the weakened one for Unla Key. Orsik tells Blagothkus that he is the second giant friend the party has had — the first was Harshnag, who died when the Oracle fell on his head — and that they learned about this place from Blagothkus's son, a ghost who wanted to kill them and who said Blagothkus wasn't good enough for the All-Father. Blagothkus clenches his fists and considers tossing the dwarf down, but remembers he's all about personal growth and tosses a boulder on a zombie instead.

While the others banter, Thistle flies off to the side as a lookout and spots Glazhael the Undying sneaking toward the tower: "The dracolich is coming!" Orsik, distrustful of Thistle's eyesight lately, ends one of the zombies. Unla Key summons the arcane powers of his artifact and darkness envelops the zombies. Orsik cheers, "SEE! I knew this was going to be useful!" — and Vardis mutters, "Except now we can’t use toll the dead!"

Glazhael advances and uses her dragon fear on the 3HPP from a safe distance; Vardis mutters, "Shadowboxing us." Thistle, Thuul and Unla Key are affected; Orsik and Vardis are not. Blagothkus announces he will go downstairs and come back up once the dragon does its thing, and tells Orsik, "I am not standing here to get killed. You might think of doing the same." Vardis shoots an arrow into the darkness and kills a zombie. Blagothkus, heading down for more rocks, adds that he has figured out the zombies' purpose: "Their job was to keep anyone fighting long enough to give her pet dracolich a chance to kill them." With the dragon's fear having overcome Unla Key, Orsik tells Vardis, "I told you we needed Bob." He then uses his decanter of endless liquids to dump two gallons of mayonnaise on an approaching Zombie, which loses its footing and falls but is not killed, and heads for cover. Thistle moves away from the tower and shoots two more Zombies.

Unla Key, ever the gambler, picks up the giant artifact and conjures its wild magic: the tower shimmers green and is covered in green grass. Glazhael, visibly annoyed, breathes on the tower and covers it in ice. As she flies away she screams, "THEY ARE ALL DEAD!" — and Orsik shouts after her, "Bob’s still an option!"
