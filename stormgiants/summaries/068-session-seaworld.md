# Chapter 68 SeaWorld

Date: 26 Eleint 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
Underwater, Vardis, Thistle, and Unla Key watch as Orsik advances on a pair of crabs, and the narration is blunt about it: they are wasting valuable time and resources for Orsik to get some gold. Thistle looks for a mechanism that would drop the portcullis, but realizes the storm giants simply use brute strength — they can swim underwater for protracted periods and are very strong, so raising the gates by hand is more straightforward.

When the crabs notice Orsik, Vardis reaches for sulfur and bat guano and points between the two beasts; a mote of magical fire rushes forward, stops in mid water, and a burst of fire engulfs them. "Magic operates very differently than fire underwater." Thistle signals to Thuul, and while the crabs are fixated on Orsik and Unla Key, Thistle hits one with his arrows and Thuul spits acid. Vardis rings a single chime of a dolorous bell through the water and the remaining crab writhes under Toll the Dead; two more arrow bolts from Thistle follow. Unla Key, impressed, casts the same spell, but this time the Crustacean ignores the awful sound. With one badly injured beast left, Orsik tosses two hand axes — the first bounces off the crab's armor harmlessly, the second kills it.

Digging through the treasure, Orsik shouts, "Anyone who thought this was a bad idea doesn't get any treasure. Except Vardis, who's my friend and always has my back." The haul is unimpressive — gold pieces and a goblet — until Orsik stumbles on two obvious magic items: a Magma Warpick (which, the narration notes, he is to learn later) and Weaving Wasp Needles, which can be used to craft clothing or to restrain an enemy. Orsik is very pleased with himself.

Thistle takes stock. They stand on the edge of a vortex that swirls above a sinkhole 100 feet deep and 180 feet across, with a drain at its bottom that would require a 50-foot plug; going through the vortex would be hard but possible. Shipwrecks ring its base, though the crabs and the storm giants have probably picked them clean. "Let's continue counterclockwise," he mutters.

Further on, an underground tunnel ends in a portcullis with air visible beyond it, and right in front of the portcullis is a killer whale. Thistle tells his allies, "I don't think it is the right way," but Vardis, Orsik, and Unla Key are not convinced, and Vardis points out that Thistle is the master of infiltration and could look. The fairy slips through the gate. Unlike the Fire Giants, the whale is observant, notices Thistle, and swims straight at him. Wanting to avoid killing another beast of nature, Thistle casts "Speak with Animals" and shouts, "We are not your enemy." The whale mutters, "Nym promised better tasty snacks. You are barely a mouthful. But you will do." It snaps at Thistle as he swims up, wearing him down slightly, but he escapes into the air above the pool, where he finds a stairwell going up — and snacks.

Thistle throws a pile of snacks into the water and the whale devours them in glee. When Thistle coughs, "Ahem?", the whale asks, "You are feeding me. You are not Nym. Why are you feeding me?" Thistle answers, "Nym sent me." The whale apologises for the bite — "They don't usually come that way. She comes from the shore." — but when Thistle asks, "Have you seen any dragons?", it explodes, insisting that if there had been a dragon it would have defended Nym and killed it: "Do you see any dragon bones? Of course, you don't." Thistle's explanation that "The dragon is a friend of Nym's" only confuses it, and it declares, "If you have more fish, I'll be happy to talk; if not, this conversation has become very boring." Meanwhile, the rest of the 3HPP swim past the whale onto the shore.

Fed, the whale announces itself: "I am Nym's Whale. I am her favorite pet." It grows suspicious — "Are you sure you are a friend of Nym's? I have never seen anyone but her here." — and Thistle's reply, "I am usually invisible.", does not persuade it. "I don't think you are her friend. But you fed me. I just want you to know that I must eat you if you get into the water. It won't be personal." It submerges. Thistle guesses the stairway up probably goes to her quarters and wonders what is down the south hallway.

The hallway leads to an air-filled cave under 5 feet of water, and a staircase in the south wall rises to a cavern holding a cultivated coral garden, colorful and immense. Thistle knows the coral is worth a fortune, but Adimond is here, and mining it would take time, resources, and risk exposure — so he tells the rest of the 3HPP, "This coral is worthless."

As the ranger floats past a stalagmite, it sprouts a single red, blinking eye above a slobbering, fanged mouth, and tendrils leap out at every member of the 3HPP; only Thistle and Unla Key are grappled. It drags Thistle to its mouth and bites, but he dodges the teeth. Vardis measures the room carefully and places a fireball so that the explosion reaches but does not touch Thistle. Orsik's sword is stopped by the tough hide, and Thistle fails to get free. Unla Key prepares a fireball of his own, using Vardis's carefully positioned blast as his guide, and not one of Thistle's whiskers is singed. The creature flails at Vardis and Orsik to no effect, and shrugs off Vardis's Toll the Dead. Unla Key scoffs at Vardis, reaches for the bag of guano and sulfur, and fills the room with fire once more; the creature is clearly hurt but alive until Orsik whacks it with his sword and kills it. Pleased with himself but finding no treasure, Orsik turns to Thistle: " Are you sure this Coral is worthless?" "Yes."

The southern passageway opens into another large room with a pool and a killer whale, cases of whale food, and several exits: a passageway to the southeast blocked by a portcullis, a spiral staircase going up, and a staircase going west. In the southeast corner is an anemone chest. Thistle throws fish into the water; the whale pokes its head up — "Well, hello there. You are tiny." — and, told "I am a friend.", replies, "Yes, you are. Mirran usually feeds me. Why are you here? Is she okay?" It turns out this whale regards Mirran as its pet: "She needs this awkward space with no water to survive, so I bring her back whenever she goes out." It keeps only one — "I'm very much a one pet animal." — and mentions that she spends much time hitting something outside the pool; Thistle notes the sparring dummy down the west passageway. The whale points its nose at the spiral staircase as the way she arrives, saying she comes here to rest after a frustrating day. It has no way to open the portcullis and would not use one if it did: "I am pretty progressive. By the way, have you met the other whale? It thinks it is the pet. A disgrace."

While Thistle talked, Unla Key examined the anemone, recognising it as an engineered animal used to store valuables. He noticed it was trapped, applied a delicate and careful series of touches, and opened it successfully, finding 400 platinum pieces from various countries. Thistle floats down and asks the team, "Do we wait here for Mirran to come down, or do we go up?" — but Unla Key is already moving up the spiral staircase. "Up it is…" Thistle groans. As they climb, Thistle notices a secret door.

## Locations

## NPCs
### Nym's Whale
The guardian at the first portcullis — a killer whale naming itself "Nym's Whale." Hungry, territorial and loyal to Nym; it bites Thistle, is pacified with snacks, denies any dragon is present, and warns it must eat him if he enters the water, though "It won't be personal."

### Mirran's whale
The whale in the pool room. Friendly and chatty; it believes Mirran is its pet, takes her out for a ride to relax, calls itself "pretty progressive," and disdains the other whale as "A disgrace."

### The stalagmite creature
A monster disguised as a stalagmite, with a single red, blinking eye, a fanged mouth and grappling tendrils. It grapples Thistle and Unla Key, survives three fiery explosions, shrugs off Toll the Dead, and dies to Orsik's sword.

### The crabs
Two monstrous crabs that Orsik attacks for treasure; they are killed with fire, arrows, acid, Toll the Dead and Orsik's hand axes.

### Thuul
Spits acid at a crab on Thistle's signal.

### Thistle
The fairy ranger. Scouts, talks to both whales with Speak with Animals, lies about the coral's value, and is grappled by the stalagmite.

### Vardis
Places fireballs with care around Thistle, casts Toll the Dead, and is the one Orsik exempts from his treasure rule.

### Orsik
Picks the crab fight for gold, kills the last crab and the stalagmite creature, and finds the magic items.

### Unla Key
Casts Toll the Dead and fireballs, opens the trapped anemone chest, and heads up the spiral staircase without waiting.
