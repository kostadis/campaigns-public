# Chapter 12 - Securing the books, and freeing Elquen

Date: 2 Tarsakh 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
The chapter opens inside Orsik's head with a single blunt thought: "I want to kill that Pseudo-dragon." Wallace sees the look in the dwarf's eyes and turns to him with a warning — the creature is "a member of the Lords’ Alliance and works for the SEER." Orsik's answer is a flat "So?", and Wallace spells out the danger: "the SEER can teleport this creature within 5 feet of us. I suppose it can teleport something more dangerous?" Orsik counters that the Zhentarim might reward them, and the reply comes from someone who has "lived in Parnast my whole life": the Zhentarim "don’t like wars. They don’t like freelancers," and they are "just as likely to hand you over to the Lords’ Alliance as a reward." Orsik grumbles. When it is suggested he simply ask the creature for something — "Like about the map?" — he refuses on principle: "Be in his debt? Orsik is in no creature’s debt!" Vardis rolls his eyes.

Unla Key coughs, Orsik stares at him, and someone finally points out the obvious: "If you are all done debating your plans, can we deal with the magmins in front of us?" The Wizard is about to cast a magic missile when Orsik shouts, "a magic missile for these puny creatures?" The dwarf watches in stunned amusement as Unla thinks it over and then swings wildly with his shortsword. A magmin retaliates and knocks the halfling out, and Orsik sighs, "I guess maybe the magic missile would have been the better choice." But Unla can manipulate the fates, and the killing blow is not a killing blow. After a few more moments of fighting the puny creatures die, and Unla muses that this "probably worked better when the wizard could show up and kill the trapped thieves."

Thistle then notices a secret door. Everyone struggles to open it, and just as they are about to give up, he casually walks over to a stone and presses it, and the door swings open.

Vardis volunteers — "I’ll go in!" — and marches into what is obviously a treasure room, with the words "The Wizard of the Crag’s Treasure Room" inscribed quite ostentatiously on the back wall. He sees nothing of value, but he picks up a ledger. It reveals that the Wizard of the Crag made real estate investments that didn't pan out and ran construction projects with overruns, and that there was apparently some duel with Taskor that was supposed to help with the debts.

The chapter shifts into the mind of the wight, who watches more people arrive and expects the worst: "And they will want powerful magical artifacts that I will have to deny. And then more people will die." He reminisces. A guard once shouted, "Elquen, The Wizard of the Crag is dead!" and, through a string of bewildered "What?"s, Elquen learned that Taskor had killed him. His manservant rolled his eyes: "Elquen, it’s not that complicated. The Wizard of the Crag died in a duel with Taskor and no longer has the money to maintain the castle." Elquen's thoughts went at once to the water elementals — "If I don’t control them, they’ll destroy everything." He remembers the wild magic surge that turned him into a wight and trapped the two guards and his manservant; he tried to help them, and they turned into Skeletons. Returning from his reverie, he mumbles, "never again." Knowing that the newcomers want the books, he tells them there will be no removal of books and no use of magic: "It is my curse and burden to protect the world from this." He waves his hand at the library: "magic."

Orsik is asked, "Orsik, are you sure?" and answers "Yes, I am." Unla Key, who had not yet entered the library, makes his entrance, and Orsik declares, "But what if the Wizard of the Crag lives?" Elquen stares at Unla for a moment, and Unla manipulates the fates. Elquen shouts, "Your arrogance," and on Unla's "Yes?" bursts out, "It’s so good to see you are alive!" From here on, Elquen appears to treat the halfling as his master. Unla asks, "Can I have my library?" and receives "Why yes!" — followed by "there is the small matter of back pay." Elquen names his price: "You never could hold onto money, so how about you give me 150 GP for walking-around money?" Unla looks at his allies, nods, and hands it over — "here you go!" — and with "And best of luck, sir!" Elquen walks out through the hallways to the laboratory. Vardis observes, "well, that’s interesting. We released a wight into the world." Orsik offers, "Probably won’t hurt anyone," and gets a "Yep!"

The group collects every book from the shelves. Lacking the space to carry them, they turn one of the bookshelves into a sled and pile the books onto it. Once on the surface of the island, they turn to Thistle, who privately thinks, "My mad mapping skills are fantastic!" He leads them successfully to the same point on the map where they had found the dead hobgoblin, within a few hundred feet of the end of the Marsh of Chelimber. "We’re done!" — except they weren't. The Water Weird had rematerialized, this time with a band of Mud Mephits as allies. The battle was difficult, but Vardis' quick thinking in calling in the group's own mephit allies turned the tide: the Mud Mephits once again fell into their civil war and fought each other to a standstill, and the heroes only had to dodge the muddy explosion each dying mud mephit leaves behind. All that remained was to head back to Parnast and claim their prize.
