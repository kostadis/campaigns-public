# Chapter 88 The Feast Before the Battle

Date: about 13 Uktar 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-15; recorded 2025-03-12)

## Summary
The night before the great confrontation, the party shared a magical feast conjured by Vardis to steel their bodies and spirits for battle. Vardis replaced Blade Barrier with Heroes’ Feast, and the DM accepted that the party had consumed it the previous evening. The meal granted everyone 16 additional maximum hit points for twenty-four hours, along with immunity to the frightened and poisoned conditions and resistance to poison damage. These were increased maximum hit points, allowing temporary hit points to be added separately. Their preparations also exposed a tactical disagreement: Orsik warned that spending actions healing him could leave both him and the healer dead.

Deep within the lair, Thistle faced a painful choice. His rescued companion wanted them both to escape through a side passage, away from the dragon battle above. Thistle tried to persuade her to accompany him through the top entrance, rolling only a 3. She refused, then proposed that he distract their enemies while she escaped separately. His subsequent Insight check of 26 confirmed that she sincerely feared being killed and wanted them both to flee. If he insisted on fighting, however, his appearance above would strengthen the belief that the rescue had merely been a prank by Bob. With a tearful farewell, she slipped toward the side exit while Thistle flew upward to join his companions.

The battle erupted when Bob, the Balor demon, rushed Iymrith. Around them, other ancient dragons approached one another, while Serissa, Hekaton, and the storm giants advanced across an enormous battlefield. The DM distinguished an inner circle of dragon mayhem from an outer circle where the party might still be struck. The distances forced several combatants to spend their opening turns running or flying into position. Orsik used his Cube of Summoning to call an air elemental, chosen because it could fly and deal thunder damage against a dragon immune to lightning.

Iymrith concentrated her attacks on Bob, while gargoyles operating a trebuchet added a crushing projectile to his troubles. Hekaton vaporized the crew of one siege engine with lightning, and the other giants cleared nearby gargoyles. Iymrith’s priests repeatedly healed her, including one restoration of 10 hit points and another of 33. Bob’s own fire damaged the dragon even when his sword and whip failed to penetrate her defenses.

The party coordinated its magical assault around exhausting Iymrith’s legendary resistances. Unla Key, flying in Pegasus form, left the central danger zone and cast Fireball, forcing the dragon to expend her first resistance. Serissa landed a critical boulder throw for 80 damage. Bob landed two attacks for 36 damage. Five allied mages then advanced and cast five Fireballs, drawing out three more resistances before Iymrith finally struck Bob down with a legendary action. He vanished in a puff of smoke; the table confirmed that this was a return to his home plane rather than permanent death.

After Bob fell, Vardis summoned a Djinn on Iymrith’s opposite side to provide flanking support, although all three of its opening melee attacks missed. Thistle marked the dragon with Hunter’s Mark, moved Thuul forward, and fired two unsuccessful arrows. Hekaton and the other giants continued landing boulder hits, while Unla Key’s next Fireball consumed Iymrith’s fifth and final legendary resistance.

Iymrith answered with lightning breath for 103 damage, destroying the summoned air elemental and leaving Orsik grievously wounded. His concentration failed as well. The session ended before the allies could respond: the DM stated that Iymrith intended to kill Orsik and would wait for an intervening action before using a legendary action to become invisible, hamper opportunity attacks, and evade the giants’ next volley of stones.
