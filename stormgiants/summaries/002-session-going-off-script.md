# Chapter 02 - Going off script

Date: 30 Alturiak to 1 Ches 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
The chapter opens inside the head of Grrrk, an old Galeb Duhr who is one of the stones of a "great pyramid" of rock. Grrrk prizes the quiet and the stillness of the job, and is irritated by Prrrt, a younger rock who keeps complaining about its feet and its mites. Grrrk remembers having rock mites as a young rock without fidgeting: "A Galeb Duhr of my generation knows how to do their job." From that stillness the two stones watched a fairy, a dwarf, a halfling and an elf fight the badgers hiding in the rocks. The fairy flew up and tickled Grrrk's nose with its wings, and the two talked; Grrrk told it of the giants and kobolds he had seen "in great numbers." When Prrrt whined about his mites the fairy was happy to help, and Prrrt in turn told them what the dead giant had said. The adventurers climbed to the top. Grrrk was sure they would die, Prrrt was optimistic, and Prrrt was right: they were not zapped the way the giant had been. Grrrk heard them climb down into the pyramid and then disappear, and decided he had had "Enough excitement for this century."

The view then shifts to the group. Vardis reads Veradaa's note, which says the cache was "carefully hidden behind a secret entrance in the wall of a green-sided mountain," that there is only one such mountain in the Graypeak range, and that the cultist who hid the treasure "was an expert in magical curses." Vardis asks why they would want to "go get shrunk like this cute bear," and Thistle sighs over how cute the bear is. Orsik asks why they are going there at all, and Thistle answers, "Because treasure!" Unla Key then asks the question that names the chapter: "what if we went off script? Why should we let this dead treasure hunter set our fate? What if we did something different?" Vardis agrees, suggesting they get "something that fights magical curses."

Their funds are nearly gone: a ledger of the Cult of the Dragon, 100 gold coins, a necklace of copper charms and a scroll of comprehend language. Orsik pulls out a map and lays out the choices. Llorkh is "ruled by a bandit king" and its guards had given Orsik and Vardis some trouble over employment without throwing them out; Parnast is the other nearby town, but smaller. Thistle Wendrod whispers what his uncle told him: Llorkh sat at the western end of the black road the Zhentarim controlled through the desert and was once that group's last outpost. Vardis remembers that treasure hunters swapped tall tales in the local tavern and that tribesmen from the great desert came there to sell strange artifacts. Orsik turns to the halfling and asks where to; Unla Key rolls a die and announces, "Llorkh."

After a short rest the group sets out. Grrrk and Prrrt call out, "nice seeing you!" Vardis thanks them for the treasure, Prrrt replies, "Wasn't ours! And thank you so much for dealing with the beetles!", and Grrrk orders, "alright back to your positions," at which the Galeb Duhr become invisible again. The day is hot and sunny for spring in the Graypeak Mountains until a foul smell hits them. Unla Key stops to wonder about it, while Vardis and Orsik push on ("It's a stench, why do we care?"). Unla Key and Thistle, with their tender noses, start vomiting, and the chapter notes that the amount a fairy can vomit is "damn near incomprehensible."

While the two "ruffians" drag their companions clear, four bandits surround the group and demand treasure or their lives. Orsik shouts, "Good news there is none!" The bandits suspect the group is hunting the treasure of the Cult of the Dragon hidden in these hills. Unla Key does not want to hand over anything, and Orsik, "Channeling every officer, sergeant that had ever screamed at him," tells them to back off and calls them easy to slaughter. Unla Key whispers that Orsik should demand their treasure too, and Orsik growls back, "don't push it!" The bandit leader confers with his men, announces that "old-one-eye doesn't have a soft spot for down on their luck mercenaries" can never be said of him, and the bandits melt into the rocks. A few more hours of scrambling through the foothills brings the group near the road to Llorkh. They make camp at sunset: Vardis and Orsik sleep apart from the others but close enough to respond to an attack (not sure whether responding means fleeing, joining in or defending), Unla Key sleeps in his own corner, and Thistle falls asleep cuddling his new pet bear.

The next section follows Eden "Gleeful" Gleeson, who has been traveling for days to his own wedding and has just realized he forgot to buy his bride a gift. He has despaired of finding one since leaving Llorkh. On the road east he meets the fairy, elf, dwarf and halfling, who shout back "Not interested" and "goodbye" at first. He pleads with them, and they start offering their goods: a copper necklace for 500 (Vardis), a magnifying glass with no glass (Orsik), and a polished gold piece for two (Unla Key). When Vardis offers "A giant silver nose ring?", Eden answers, "That will do just fine! I will turn it into a necklace!" Vardis and Eden haggle. The bidding opens at "200 GP!", Eden is told to sell one of his servants (the chapter does not say by whom) and indignantly refuses ("How dare you suggest I am part of the slave trade!"), and Vardis settles it with "150 seems fair," Eden concedes, "You drive a hard bargain!" Then his manservant Cisco, who wears an eyepatch and cradles a small teddy bear, steps in: "Eddie, you can't make these impulsive purchases! You just ate into the whole desert budget!"

That evening, a few miles out of Llorkh, four wolves come out of the trees on both sides of the road. Unla Key is killed, Orsik kills a wolf, and Thistle waits for a wolf to emerge and misses. Vardis is brought to death's door. Orsik decides to revive the mage, warning Unla Key, "don't make me regret my choice to heal you!" Unla Key casts a thunder wave that blasts the wolves back, but the third wolf brings Thistle to death's door. Orsik kills another wolf, leaving two combatants on each side. The wolf standing over Vardis's body takes Vardis's bow and runs into the forest, and the wolf over Thistle takes his arrows. Orsik rages about it, ending with "AND WE LOST A BOW!" After a short rest under the trees where the wolves had set their ambush, the group reaches Llorkh near sunset: a small town under a keep on a hill, with one main road and a few side streets.

At the edge of town Captain Jolanth of the Guards of the Iron Circle sees them coming ("Harumph, newcomers!"). He has survived the Cult of the Dragon in Llorkh and the falling-off of Zhentarim traffic, and he stays because the pay is good and he likes the weather. He stops them in the road with "who goes there!", and the halfling shouts back, "we are we!" Jolanth announces that the Guards of the Iron Circle protect the town and send troublemakers to the keep. Orsik cuts him off, and Jolanth privately sneers at him as too weak to threaten the guard. Orsik then turns the warning around: "so that means if we are in danger, you will protect us? Is that what I heard?" While the flustered captain is still answering, Vardis interrupts with "free protection! I like it!", and the four walk past him.

Orsik recalls that the town has a tavern, a store and a temple. Vardis mumbles, "I can go to that one," but they settle on the tavern, the only one in town, where the bartender is a ghost. Vardis asks whether it can talk, the exchange turns to "Your condition?", and the local patrons tense up at the question. Someone orders the pine nut salad once the ghost confirms it is vegan (the chapter does not attribute the order). Vardis wonders why a ghost cares about money and why everyone acts as though this is normal. The tavern is full of Guards of the Iron Circle. Orsik asks the ghost how it came to be a ghost; the bartender says it is not one, and the room starts to empty or brace. Orsik insists, "No, you really ARE a ghost!" The bartender turns into a monstrous creature that terrifies Orsik, and within moments he has aged 30 years. The guards he expected to help him have already fled.

Outside, the guard Diego Malfoy shouts, "are you mad? We never tell him that he's a ghost!" When Orsik complains that no one warned them, Diego answers, "Most people aren't that stupid," then recognizes him: "You are Orsik! And that one is Vardis! You wanted a job, and you couldn't even show up sober on your first day!" Orsik insists, "Look, we're nobodies." Vardis asks where they can find a scroll of dispel magic, and Diego sends them to "The Lionshield Coster outpost." Then a woman appears almost out of nowhere, wearing boots, breeches, a sturdy belt with a steel buckle, a shirt, and a brown hooded cloak over it. She says she saw their courage and has a job for them. Orsik rolls his eyes ("you saw a bunch of fools poke a ghost"), and the stranger admits she has few people to choose from and "the clock is ticking." When she adds that the job pays, Vardis smiles: "why didn't you start with that?"

## Locations
### The great pyramid
A pyramid of living rock in which Grrrk, Prrrt and other Galeb Duhr sit as stones. The group fought badgers in its rocks, climbed to the top, climbed down into it, and left after a short rest.

### Graypeak Mountains
The range where Veradaa's note places the cache in the only "green-sided mountain" there. The group crosses its foothills on a hot spring day on the way to the road to Llorkh.

### Llorkh
The group's chosen destination, decided by Unla Key's die roll. Orsik says it is "ruled by a bandit king"; Thistle's uncle described it as the western end of the black road and the Zhentarim's last outpost. It is a small town under a keep on a hill, with one main road and a few side streets, guarded by the Guards of the Iron Circle. Orsik recalls it has a tavern, a store and a temple.

### Parnast
Mentioned by Orsik as "the other town nearby, but it's smaller." Not visited.

### The black road
The route through the desert that, according to Thistle's uncle, the Zhentarim controlled, with Llorkh at its western end.

### The tavern in Llorkh
The only tavern in town, run by a ghost bartender and full of Guards of the Iron Circle. Orsik is aged 30 years here.

### The Lionshield Coster outpost
The local store, where Diego says a scroll of dispel magic can be found. Named only.
