# Chapter 76 Dealing with Blagothkus and Yartar

## Summary
In contrast to a played-session recap, this chapter is written as a forward-looking adventure overview: it sets out where the adventurers stand, the places and people that matter next, and the plot threads that could unfold, mostly in the conditional ("may", "could"). Nothing below is recorded as having happened at the table; it is what the chapter lays out as the situation and its possibilities.

The adventurers are described as "caught between the mortal world and the machinations of powerful beings such as Dracoliches, vampires, and giants." Their current mission is "to secure control of a legendary flying castle," a stronghold the chapter says "can change the course of their fate and the balance of power in the realm." Along the way they must navigate treacherous alliances, uncover dark secrets and face dangerous foes, all while "seeking to restore the storm giant king, Hekaton, to his throne."

The first arena is the Flying Castle itself, "A massive, enchanted fortress floating among the clouds, with various levels, dungeons, and hidden chambers." Its control systems are tied to Esclarotta, a ghost who "can either aid or hinder the party depending on their actions." Esclarotta is Blagothkus' "spectral wife," and the chapter calls her "a complex entity—sometimes helpful, sometimes obstructive—who holds her own desires for the fate of the castle and her family." Blagothkus, a cloud giant "who once allied with the Cult of the Dragon," now stands as a pivotal figure in the castle's control; his fate is tied to his son, "who is trapped as a ghost until Blagothkus is slain." The chapter frames this as a power struggle: Blagothkus, "driven by guilt and the desire to restore his family’s honor," may help the party or become a hindrance, while Esclarotta "could side with the party if convinced they would save her son." The adventurers will decide the fate of the giant family and the flying fortress.

Deep within the castle lies the Dracolich's Ice Cavern, a frozen lair where the Dracolich guards its phylactery. The cavern is "filled with ancient magic, treasure, and hidden dangers." The Dracolich Glazhael is "A cunning undead dragon who struck a deal with the party to retrieve its phylactery," powerful but "not entirely trustworthy," and liable to turn on the party if the situation benefits it. To solidify their alliance with the Dracolich, or to destroy it and free the castle from its influence, the party must "battle through undead and icy dangers to reach the artifact."

The second arena is Yartar. Moored there is The Grand Dame, a gambling boat the chapter calls "a den of vice and secrets," where the party must infiltrate to learn about the storm giant king's fate amid wealthy nobles, hired guards and criminal elements. Its owner, Lord Khaspere Drylund, is "The charismatic and secretive owner of the Grand Dame gambling boat," connected to the plot involving Hekaton, though "extracting information from him will not be easy." The chapter offers two approaches: a subtle one, "using disguises, gambling, and gathering intelligence," or direct action — disrupting the boat's operations, defeating its guards, and "potentially sinking it to the riverbed if they choose open conflict." High-stakes gambling aboard could bring "tremendous riches or devastating losses," win or lose the favour of the boat's elite patrons, and, if they win big, unlock access to Drylund's private quarters and darkest secrets.

Beneath Yartar's surface, the chapter says, the city is run by a secretive guild of thieves that "holds the key to finding Hekaton’s kidnappers." The adventurers may have to negotiate with these criminals, trade favours, or make enemies of the underworld, and navigating it "may force the adventurers to make shady deals, face double-crosses, or earn valuable allies."

Over all of this hangs Queen Serissa, acting head of the storm giant court, who has tasked the party with finding her father, Hekaton. She provides guidance but is desperate, knowing that the storm king's disappearance threatens the balance of power in the realm. The chapter closes on a wider conspiracy: as the adventurers dig into the plot around Hekaton, they uncover a conspiracy "involving human factions and ancient powers, all vying for control of the giants’ kingdom," with betrayals from unexpected sources, "such as one of Hekaton’s closest advisors."
