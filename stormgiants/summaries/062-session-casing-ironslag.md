# Chapter 62 - Casing Ironslag

Date: 16 Eleint 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
With the far Salamander banished, Orsik struck the one within reach and killed it. Vardis rushed in a moment too late, shouting "DIE! SALAMANDERS!", and Orsik could only shake his head.

The 3HPP turned to the dwarves and gnomes they had just freed. Orsik tried to get them to help, but they all wanted to flee. When Thistle pointed out that the party possessed a cloud castle, the freed prisoners shouted "Huzzah!" — and Orsik turned to Thistle with a flat "Really?" Thistle shrugged and offered some advice: "Just be careful. Stay out of the dark places." Orsik muttered the rest of it: "And if someone asks to come in, just say no." One of the dwarves saw through the innuendo and asked, "There are vampires in the castle?" Vardis corrected him: "Only one Vampire and many spawn." The dwarves and gnomes decided that going down the stairs was a safer bet, thanked their saviors, and left.

One gnome stayed behind. She introduced herself as Wiri Fleagol, saying "I have been here for quite some time." Vardis needled her, asking whether the giants had taken over her village or whether she had done something to deserve it. Wiri ignored the taunt: "Does anyone deserve to be locked up in chains mining for giants?" Squinting at Thistle, she told him "You are a fairy. I will help you. I know things about it down below. And I will do it for a price." Orsik moaned that "Everyone wants a cut," and Vardis taunted her about wanting "Money to buy a house in Silverymoon."

That struck a nerve. Wiri wanted no house there: "They are nothing but a bunch of bigoted, self-important, righteous bastards." When the Orc tribes invaded, she said, Silverymoon chose not to help the Silver Marches, and "The Dragons weakened them!" Asked whether she wanted vengeance, she recoiled — there were possibly innocent gnomes there. Thistle then told her the sordid story of Bob and the play in Silverymoon, leaving out his own involvement and making it sound as though House Margaster was to blame. Wiri did not believe a word of it.

She did, however, have something to sell: "I can tell you about what's ahead." Wiri described the route into Ironslag proper. The rail tracks end at a pair of wooden barriers in the first room, where three pillars of black basalt support the ceiling, the bucket chain passes through gaps in the western and eastern walls, four dwarves with ankle chains keep putting ore into the buckets, and a fire salamander stands watch. West of there is the Foundry, scorching enough that anyone who lingers suffers extreme heat, with a rusted iron gantry 50 feet above the floor and the ceiling 40 feet above the gantry. Through a southeast entrance lies the Great Wheel, an enormous wheel of iron gears and wooden spokes rigged to the bucket chain and turned by ten shield dwarves and ten humans with iron manacles around their ankles — and "If you stop the wheel, everyone will come quickly to find out why." South of that room are the prisoners' sleeping quarters, a long, foul-smelling hall sealed by iron portcullises, with thirteen 10-foot-tall iron cages lining the walls, each with a padlock.

Thistle asked "Can they fight?" Wiri answered "No, they are exhausted and dehydrated from the heat." Then she named her price: "That will be 20 gold." Orsik shouted "What a hard bargain you drive," which puzzled the gnome — "Did I ask for too little." — and the reply came back, "Nope, that was our maximum." Wiri glared: "I am beginning to sense that you are not the kind of guys I want to have a deep personal relationship with." A mischievous Vardis asked "Would you rather be on our good or bad side?" and she conceded, "Point taken."

As she was about to leave, Wiri offered to help further; Orsik convinced her that it would not end well. He could not resist asking what she would do with the money: "What will you do with the 20 gold? Buy a nice dinner?" She demanded a trade — he would have to tell her about his hero. Orsik declared "My grandfather." Smiling, she said "Well, I plan to buy a farm and never spend another day underground. Do you have any suggestions?" Thistle offered "Not Silverymoon," and Orsik genteelly suggested "Yartar and Triboar are nice." Wiri decided to try them out, and invited them to come find her if they were ever there.

Vardis had a flash of divine inspiration and remembered that their objective was to get the Conch, not to kill the Giants. He asked Wiri "Do you know where the conch is?" She had been a slave and had never gone there, but hazarded a guess: "it's in his room, which is on the lower level." Before leaving, the gnome asked Orsik "So you're Orsik, spelled with a K?" He answered "No, a C. K is my derelict son." When she asked whether mentioning his name would be okay, he told her "Just mention the 3HPP. We helped the mayor in Triboar."

With Wiri gone, the 3HPP considered their next move. Thistle proposed casing the place: "Unla Key makes me invisible, and then I sneak around until I find the bedroom." Orsik added "Then we can come up with a plan," pleased that where he had once been the muscle, he was now the brain.

An invisible Thistle flew off, making a map and notes as he floated around the top part of Ironslag. "I love sneaking around." He floated through the eastern passage into the foundry, where he recorded the gantry, the din of "clanging metal, rattling chains, whooshing bellows, and bubbling molten iron," and four ogres overseen by a bored-looking juvenile giant. From his perch in the top western corner he headed south and east to a vast chamber of iron gantries where the Vonindod's helmed head and upper torso hung in midair from chains, while cranes on the western and eastern gantries held two more fragments of the colossus: an enormous greatsword and a giant gauntlet-like hand and forearm. An iron portcullis at the top of the stairs led to a dark hallway to the prison. Below, Duke Zalto was playing with his hell hounds, tossing a metal ball with a body in it. Thistle studied the cranes and noted that a few well-placed reduce spells could cause havoc.

Next he slipped into the pressing mill, where eight orcs worked under the watch of a fire giant, tipping, sorting, and reloading ore from the bucket chain. Then he went down to the mustering room, whose frescoes depict fire giants forging armor and weapons, marching to war, and binding red dragons in chains; there three ogres were cheering two Fire Giants who were arm wrestling. Checking his internal clock, Thistle returned to Unla Key for another invocation of invisibility, then finished exploring the lower level.

From the mustering hall he went down into the kitchen. There Brimskarda, the fire giant duchess, stood before a fireplace sampling soup from a fat cauldron, while five smoke mephits danced around the cauldron, two ogres in grease-stained leather aprons stood by the tables, and twenty goblins scurried about. She wore a gown made from a young black dragon's hide and wings, with her orange hair wrapped around a black dragon's horn "like a curving pillar of flame." When Brimskarda shouted to the goblins, they carried food down the hallway, and Thistle followed them into the Dining Hall.

Off the dining hall to the northwest he found the war room: a basalt table sculpted like a fountain of black fire, bare marble shelves, and an iron golem standing against the northwest wall "with a rune-carved iron ingot embedded in its forehead." "No treasure," he thought. Back in the dining room, Thistle remembered he had some poison and tried to pour it into the liquids at Duke Zalto's table, but caused a disturbance in the attempt. The nearby goblins looked around while Thistle flew quietly away, and from their chattering and complaining he inferred they were convinced one of them had made the mess.

Finally Thistle reached an alcove against the East wall with two doors — "The bedrooms." He considered shrinking a door, but realized that even shrunk it would be too big for him to hold in place; it would fall with a considerable noise, and worse, he would stop being invisible. So he sighed and headed back to Unla Key with his map and notes.
