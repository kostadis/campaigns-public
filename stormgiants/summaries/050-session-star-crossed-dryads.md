# Chapter 50 - Star crossed Dryads

Date: about 9 Eleasis 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
Unla Key is the first to notice the Grandfather Tree from the airship. Its crown is over 350 feet high and spreads 800 feet in diameter, and as the ship descends he notices four more trees under the canopy and, just before landing, two large rings of raised earth — an outer and an inner ring — circling the tree. Facing the long hike, he remembers that he could be the proud owner of the Cloud Giant’s castle: Eigeron had told him precisely where he could find Blagothkus. But he considers that his current allies might want to share the castle or, worse, control it, while Thistle, on an errand of love and vengeance, might not care. He decides to put it aside: “No,” he realizes, “this will have to wait.”

Thistle is in a rush to find the artifact, get back to the Oracle, get the Conch, and find the Maelstrom where Adimond Kroskas is hidden — and, only if strictly necessary, deal with the threat to the realms. Approaching the southernmost tree under the canopy, he is told by a dryad’s voice to go no further. The dryad, Halisa, explains she has been exiled from the Fey because she offered her honest opinion about the Queen’s dress; Thistle squirms visibly and covers his eyes with the tips of his wings. She does not know where the artifact is and says the Tree cannot be touched under the terms of her punishment. “Tree Protection Schedule D,” asks Thistle. “The very one!” she replies. Orsik intervenes and infuriates her; she tells him to leave because he’s a dwarf, and though Orsik explains that he was once a dwarf scout who understands the importance of not damaging nature, Halisa shoos him off. Orsik marches off in a huff — as he did in Parnast. Halisa suggests other Dryads might be willing to help.

As Thistle is about to leave, he is startled to see Prince Thornacious. Halisa, terrified at the idea that royalty is coming, demands they leave immediately. From the awkward flying, the expression on the Prince’s face, and his inability to understand Fey, Thistle works out what has happened: “Orsik?” / “Yes.” — “We can use this.” / “I hope so!”

At the easternmost tree, a dryad named Helia is delighted to see Thornacious and unleashes a stream of insults hoping to provoke the sprite, but Orsik-Thornacious understands nothing and misinterprets some of the gestures, and Helia gives up. Helia explains he was Halisa’s plus-one at the ball; when Halisa expressed herself freely, Queen Titania demanded that the plus one present itself. “I tried to explain that I wasn’t her date or interested in Halisa, and I even dumped her on the spot. But Queen Titania didn’t care. And now I am stuck here for an eternity!” He also rebuffs Thistle, but offers an option: “I would give you permission if you could convince Halisa to apologize!” Orsik, Thistle, and Vardis discuss their options: Vardis points out they can bend the fates using Unla Key’s powers, Orsik proposes violence, and Thistle, unsure, suggests checking the other dryads.

The tree on the northern cardinal point holds an old dryad with a grumpy, breathy voice. When Thistle mentions searching for his Uncle Joon, the dryad exclaims “Joon!” — “How much does he owe you,” sighs Thistle. “He owes me a joke. A good joke! I want to hear a good joke.” Thistle remembers an old joke he used to share with his beloved, one that only works if you are a fey, about a Dryad and how the charm interacts with the movement of a tree. He lands it nicely and the Dryad laughs heartily — but still refuses permission “unless you get Helia and Halisa to stop arguing. I am tired of hearing their bickering.” The old dryad is not sure how they were trapped, but says Queen Titania told them that if one left, the other would be safe, and the one leaving would die; “They hate each other so much that they can’t find the love to sacrifice themselves. So they sit there bickering.” He slouches and adds, “and thus we are canceling the moon festival.” Thistle is shocked — performatively, but shocked nonetheless. “Get them to stop bickering or to leave, and I will give you permission.”

At the last tree, on the western cardinal point, Thistle meets Sleth, who is upset that Helia and Halisa are not caring for their trees. Thistle sees only a perfect tree, but Sleth rolls her eyes: “that tree branch is growing in the wrong direction. Why, in the next 30 years, it will create a terrible imbalance. And this is exactly what happens when Queen Titania sends whatever dregs she can muster.” Surprised, Thistle asks, “what? You aren’t trapped here?” Sleth laughs: “Oh no, this is a positively amazing assignment. Or it was. This is the greatest tree in the Universe, and I get to tend to it.”

Thistle hits his head, and as Orsik-Thornacious stares at him, the realization comes (the chapter does not make clear which of them voices it): “Of course! Queen Titania would pull this kind of stuff all of the time. She’s told them a lie, and neither loves the other enough to sacrifice himself. So they sit there stubbornly for all time.” Orsik-Thornacious asks, “So now what.” Thistle’s plan: “So now we say that Prince Thornacious came to this tree to be cured of his muteness and to tell both of these lovelorn dryads that they can leave!” The dwarf in the sprite’s body signals his friend to create the right effect as he touches the tree, then shouts, “Blessed be Queen Titania, I can speak.” — and mumbles, “but only in COMMON!”

Halisa’s reaction surprises them: “Helia is going to be stuck here, right? That knot dumped me just as Queen Titania was about to send me here. We could have been happily in love instead of rotting away.” Thistle’s eyes roll, and she declares, “I plan to come here every 100 years and taunt Helia!” Once she is far enough away, Orsik-Thornacious and Thistle fly to Helia, who is upset that Halisa is leaving — odd, given how much he insists he hates her. Told that this is a pardon and that he too can go, Helia stares a moment and then flies off. With both gone, the old Dryad and Sleth appear next to the 3HPP. “FINALLY LEFT!” groans the elderly Dryad. The reply, not explicitly attributed but in the voice of one who stayed by choice: “Yes, and as I kept telling them, there was no curse. They had cursed each other! Imagine a hundred years of aggravation because you believe it’s a prison. What’s worse is our aggravation, but now – finally – peace.”

Thistle suggests they bid everyone farewell. With permission given, the 3HPP quickly finds the artifact hidden in a chamber in the tree's roots: a nonmagical electrum torc with Giant runes, formerly the nose-ring of a mighty hill giant chief. As they leave, a group of rank centaurs approaches, muttering about a cursed temple somewhere deeper in the forest; they have come for the tree’s magical powers to cure the disease they acquired, since spending the night here has the same effects as the Greater Restoration spell. Just before reaching the airship, the party sneaks up on a group of barbarians of the Tree Ghost Tribe, led by a young man they understand to be the son of the Great Chief Boorvald Orcbane, talking to Sleth. The leader is furious to overhear that his tribe’s precious artifact has been stolen and promises vengeance. As the party sneaks away, Sleth says, “Prince Thornacious was so gracious and helpful!”

## Locations
### Grandfather Tree
A vast tree whose crown is over 350 feet high and 800 feet in diameter, circled by two rings of raised earth, with four more trees under its canopy at the cardinal points, each tended by a dryad. Sleth calls it “the greatest tree in the Universe”. The artifact lies hidden in a chamber in its roots, and spending the night there has the same effects as the Greater Restoration spell.

### Parnast
Mentioned only as the place where Orsik previously marched off in a huff.

## NPCs
### Halisa
Dryad of the southernmost tree, exiled from the Fey for offering her honest opinion about the Queen’s dress. She drives off Orsik for being a dwarf, is terrified of royalty, and on being freed reveals she and Helia could have been happily in love; she plans to return every 100 years to taunt Helia.

### Helia
Dryad of the easternmost tree, Halisa’s plus-one at the ball, who insists he dumped her and is not interested in her. He insults the Prince in hopes of provoking him, offers permission if Halisa apologizes, and is upset when she leaves before flying off himself.

### The old Dryad (unnamed)
Elderly dryad of the northern tree with a grumpy, breathy voice. Owed a joke by Uncle Joon, he laughs at Thistle’s, but only grants permission once Helia and Halisa stop bickering or leave. “FINALLY LEFT!”

### Sleth
Dryad of the western tree who tends the Grandfather Tree as an assignment she loves, critical of Helia and Halisa’s neglect and of “whatever dregs” Queen Titania sends. At the end she is seen speaking with the Tree Ghost Tribe and praising Prince Thornacious.

### Prince Thornacious
Fey royalty whose form Orsik wears in this chapter (“Orsik-Thornacious”), flying awkwardly and unable to understand Fey. Staged as cured of his muteness, he pardons both dryads.

### Queen Titania
Not present. The dryads describe her as the one who punished Halisa and Helia and told them the lie that binds them.

### The Tree Ghost Tribe leader (unnamed)
A young man the party understands to be the son of the Great Chief Boorvald Orcbane, who leads a group of barbarians to the tree and promises vengeance on learning the artifact was stolen.

### The centaurs
A rank group come for the tree’s cure for a disease they acquired, muttering about a cursed temple deeper in the forest.

## Items
### Electrum torc
The artifact the party sought: a nonmagical electrum torc with Giant runes, formerly the nose-ring of a mighty hill giant chief, hidden in a chamber in the tree's roots. The Tree Ghost Tribe regards it as their precious artifact.
