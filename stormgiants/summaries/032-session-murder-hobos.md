# Chapter 32  - Murder Hobos

Date: 2 to 5 Flamerule 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
The sun rises over the Evermoors, which stretch before the heroes "like a plate of overcooked pasta." Humidity clings to their skin, bugs crawl over their tents, and the stench of decaying plants and flesh overwhelms their packed food. Orsik frames the day's work as simple: "So, all we have to do is find the cause – a bit of recon, and we can return to collect our reward." Thistle straps his bow to his back and flies off to scout. He returns having heard "some snarling, whooping, and hollering," but cannot say what it is. Orsik, described as "the suddenly intellectually enhanced fighter," strains to listen and declares, "That’s them." When Thistle stares, Orsik explains, "Look, I don’t want you to feel bad, but I am **really** smart. There are two trolls and a fire giant." Vardis shakes the fairy: "My friend used to be so cool until he got this headband."

An argument follows. Thistle is determined to kill the Fire Giant and the trolls, Unla Key has to be held back from charging, and Orsik insists they only need to confirm that the trolls are coming out because of the Fire Giant. They agree to sneak up and learn what the creatures are arguing about. Thistle goes alone, silenced by his boots of elvenkind, and finds a Fire Giant shouting at two trolls in an opening in the swamp. The giant waves a rod at the trolls, identical to the one the Fire Giant at Triboar used to find a peculiar artifact made of adamantine. The giant looks hungry, frustrated and annoyed; the trolls stare back uncomprehendingly. Thistle thinks, "They are working together! Like Cats and Dogs!"

Back at camp, where Orsik is swatting flies with his sword, the debate repeats: "We should kill them!" / "It was a bad idea last time, and we almost all died." Thistle then asks Unla Key for a comprehend languages spell so he can understand the creatures. A few minutes later he is back at the squabble and overhears the giant shout, "I want something tasty. Not mud pies." The trolls look at the mud pies at her feet, confused, and she whines, "I can’t go back to Duke Zalto until I find a piece of the Vonindod, and this Rod isn’t helping!"

Thistle reports back, and the debate rages longer. The fairy and the mage want to kill the giant, the cleric is ambivalent, and Orsik wants to return for his reward: "The quest is completed," he shouts. "But treasure!" comes the reply. Unla Key, lying bored on the floor of his tent, asks, "Wait, we aren’t going to attack?" and is met with glares. He then proposes enticing the trolls with the bodies of the Uthgardt nomads while he sneaks up to steal the giant's rod. Orsik and Vardis confer — "I’ll do what the group wants," muses the elf; "I have grown attached to them," grouses the fighter — and a speaker agrees to go along while needling Thistle for drawing the line at stealing but not at "just going and killing creatures that have done nothing to us to harvest their organs and steal their magic items." He mutters "Murder hobo" to Vardis, who nods. Thistle reflects that this is a different Fire Giant from the one that attacked Triboar, that he has no vendetta against it, and that they could move on.

A few hours later, covered in the stench of the Evermoors, the heroes reach the Calling Horns Inn. Tamalin finds the news odd: "Well, that is very odd. Fire Giants are working with Trolls. Cats and Dogs, working together." Thistle answers, "That’s what I said." The party tells her the giants are part of Duke Zalto's tribe and are looking for something called the Vonindod. Tamalin has never heard of it, explains that fire giant leaders are all dukes — hill giants are led by a chief, stone giants by thane, cloud giants by a count — and suggests asking in a major city like Everlund and Silverymoon. Orsik grumbles about "the good people of Silverymoon with their Cambion demons." As they leave, Tamalin tells them she has a brother who is the High Captain of Everlund, and that the note she gave them is good for a favor.

The following day the heroes take the Evermoor Way toward Yartar, leaving Calling Horns behind. The road is dusty and quieter than expected. They see a hill giant with a sack, eating anything edible or stuffing it in the bag; Thistle scouts and finds two bugbears and an ogre trailing it, bored and unhappy. Thistle asks, "Shall we kill this giant and do the travelers on this road a good deed?" Orsik replies, "You’re bloodthirsty today?" They set an ambush: Thistle, Orsik and Vardis hide in the bushes while Unla Key stands in the middle of the road with his hands over his eyes, shouting, "I am hiding." It works — the giant decides it can ignore the halfling and turns to an edible fungus, while its escorts slow down.

Thistle fires two arrows and hits once. Orsik strikes three times, wearing a unique shield he crafted overnight in the Calling Horns Inn. Vardis's guiding bolt misses ("It happens."). Unla Key opens his eyes, thinking "See, I knew the giant would be surprised!", and casts a lightning bolt that the giant takes in full. Down to 15 of its 105 hit points, the giant swings at Orsik, who tricks it into attacking Thuul, the guard drake; Vardis uses his mastery of light to blind the giant for a moment, and it misses. It swings at Orsik again, Orsik points at Thuul again, and the giant misses the drake once more. Then Thistle's arrow goes through the giant's eye and into what passes for its brain. The giant falls with a loud thud, and the bugbears and ogre, seeing their dead boss and Vardis holding onto giant toenails, run the other way.

The next day the heroes wave down a goblin caravan. Fritz, a short middle-aged goblin in a red velvet shirt, blue pants and green suspenders, introduces himself: "My name is Fritz, and this is my store." His clan is heading east because "the giants have gotten crazy," and the nomads "attack anything that moves." Asked whether it is a going-out-of-business sale, he cites his mother's advice never to sell for anything less than a reasonable profit.” His goods do not interest the party, but Fritz happily buys a bag of salt that belonged to Duke Zalto ("salt fit for a duke!"), an empty chest, a drinking horn, and fairy leather armor. Orsik stops Thistle before he can explain that it is a fairy's leather armor.

On the 5th day of the 1st tenday of Flamerule the heroes arrive late in the evening in Yartar and go to the Wink and Kiss. Maia is happy to see them; when Orsik shows her the Letter of Mark she tries to high-five him, and he leaves her hanging. She seats them at the bar at the center of the tavern's maze, next to Eltio, a member of the Hand of Yartar dressed in white with a hat and a white mask. Told they did "Recon," Eltio is disappointed: "No murder? Thievery? Highway robbery? Damn, Tamalin has gone legit." She leaves to make inquiries, and the group debates how to persuade Harthal Zymorven, the heir to Zymorven Hall, to come with them. Orsik proposes intimidation, Thistle a faked arrest with an offer of escape to Daddy, and Vardis telling Harthal his dad is ill — to which Orsik says, "I don’t think that will work," then adds, "There are no bad ideas during the brainstorming phase."

Eltio returns with news: "So your boy is in prison for murder. The bloke will be executed on the 10th." The sword is in the possession of Tholzar Brenner, a Damaran guard captain whom no one would miss, and she offers to take them to him "where an ambush would be possible." That evening, in the market near the Waterbaron's house, she points out Tholzar's armed guards and their brutish boss collecting protection money. Orsik observes, "They are so close together, we could fireball them all." Eltio, recognising him as "that guy Orsik who did that thing," weighs the blowback to herself and the Hand of Yartar and asks whether he wants to "create a 20’ radius fireball that will explode in the main market of Yartar near the Waterbaron’s residence?" Meanwhile Thistle thinks, "And he called me a murder hobo."
