# Chapter 48 - The end of a relationship

Date: about 7 to 8 Eleasis 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
After a day and a half in Silverymoon, Orsik is pleased with himself. The 3HPP have armed themselves with magic and weapons, spent their coin, and eaten in the fine restaurants of the city. "Yes, life was good." Then a patrol of the guards of Silverwatch stops in front of him, and what appears to be a captain announces that High Lord Methrammar Aerasumé, "The High Lord of the Silver Marches as well as the ruler of Silverymoon and the High Marshal of the Argent Legion", wishes to speak with him. The captain insists that the party are "diplomatic envoys from the City of Everlund" who are expected to present their credentials. Orsik tries "Diplomatic Immunity", and the captain, in a tinny nasal voice, replies that "you can be expelled regardless of said immunity." Orsik, realizing the opportunity, agrees, as does Thistle, and ten minutes later the 3HPP enter the audience chamber.

The chamber is filled with courtiers discussing matters of state, budgets, and trivia. The letters of introduction are handed to the High Mage, Taern "Thunderspell" Hornblade, who declares them to his satisfaction. Thistle offers "a cultural exchange with the city of Everlund", and the courtiers laugh; a lord calls Everlund "a mere village town, a hovel." Orsik mutters in outrage, and Thistle worries for a moment that the dwarf will do something foolish, but he stays quiet. When Thistle mentions "a traveling play", the High Lord interrupts: the party are the play's producers, and he is "in love with it". He has seen it twice already, and he loves the segment "Mordenkainen's Best Home Illusions", where local mages present a short imaginary tale and the audience votes. When Vardis shouts, "There are 20 different endings!", the High Lord demands "No spoilers" and orders the Chamberlain to arrange an extended run in the royal theater.

Unla Key then shouts for "50,000 gold pieces", and though the fairy and the dwarf try to stop him, he explains it is "for the pre-production costs, not for us!" The High Lord agrees, but the High Treasurer, appalled, interrupts with words about budget, fiscal policy, deficit spending, and inflation. High Lord Aerasumé settles on a thousand, plus the Royal Theater for free. Thistle notes the Chamberlain's tight and very fake smile. Unla Key offers in return "a discounted pair of free tickets", which does not land well, and Orsik steps in to smooth things over, promising two reserved seats.

Thistle then asks for help with "the extradition of Xamlyn Margaster", claiming she has committed unpardonable sins in Everlund. The High Lord refuses: Lady Margaster is an ally of the High Lord, a member of a respectable Waterdhavian noble house, and a guest in the city, and he will not deport her. She is free to speak with anyone or not. He adds that High Captain Horix Zoar corrected the earlier misunderstanding when the party were thrown out of the city, and the party are shown out of the High Lord's Residence.

Outside, Thistle declares, "Killing Xamlyn is too easy. We need to disgrace her and to do that, we need to expose her devils." Vardis suggests Dispel Magic; Thistle mutters that it is "equivalent to pulling off someone’s wig", a fact he learned from "Uncle Joon." After discussion, they decide to ally with Senneth, the Cleric of Mielikki who raised Unla Key from the dead. Senneth is not surprised that there are devils in the city, but she dismisses the idea of cults operating: after the Cult of the Dragon was defeated, there was a general crackdown, and "The smart ones packed their bags and left the city." When Thistle describes the Cambions that showed up at the trial, Senneth is shocked: "those are the men who follow Lady Margaster." She recalls "some unpleasantness with you and the Margasters", and Thistle changes the subject. Unla Key brings up the play, but Senneth sees unwanted connections: "interesting that you bring up a story about a devil and a play with a demon."

Vardis, sensing disaster, asks which library to visit for research on Cambions, and Senneth names "The Lady’s College!" The heroes leave Mielikki's Glade, and the high priest crosses the Moonway and an invisible bridge to the north to reach the college. Thistle recalls from his uncle that the college favours classroom-style education over a "master-apprentice" form. There a bright young tiefling wearing an "Ask Me" sign, Anakis, greets Vardis, is giddy that "Cambions are my favorite!", and points him to a lecture by Sage Vusk down the hall, explaining that "Sage Vusk is a sweetheart, and nobody goes to his class." When Orsik stares at her, she acknowledges, "Yes, I know, not a goodly race."

Vardis sits in the nearly empty room as Sage Vusk explains that a Cambion is the progeny of any union between a demon and a non-demon, that some modernist sages believe a Cambion can have free will while Vusk believes its infernal nature makes that impossible, and that the Cambion is the foot soldier of blood war. Vusk warns of their fiendish charm, noting that "A single Cambion did in many adventuring groups, like The Noble Daggers", and suggests that magical darkness, blindness or invisibility could protect against it, since some sages believe the charm requires the Cambion to see its enemy. Orsik asks about Cambions disguising themselves with magic; Vusk, who corrects him that "I am not a professor; I am a traveling scholar", confirms Dispel Magic works but warns that so many people use illusions that casting it is a breach of etiquette and "It’s not unheard of for there to be duels!" Asked about a movement to ban magic, Vusk laughs: "In Silverymoon? The city that was ruled by the chosen of Mystra, the goddess of magic? The city that invented Magical Schools? No." Outside, Thistle proposes that Bob Saget "create a groundswell against magic as a hidden theme in his play", and Vardis and Orsik like the idea.

Late in the evening, an elf in a purple robe, obviously carrying a symbol of the Harpers, approaches on behalf of Krowen Valharrow, who wants to know whether the party plan to deal with the Giants after their visit to the Oracle. Orsik calls it "micromanagement". The Harper agent flashes anger: "A Frost Giant practically sacked Bryn Shander, and Hill Giants attacked Goldenfields." He warns that their access to magical pathways will be lost if they don't move today and accuses them of allying with cultists, to which Thistle bristles, "former." Orsik, who has dealt with managers before, compares the ultimatum to a "performance improvement plan". The Harper replies that the party are "a contract" that would not be renewed, but offers treasure maps on Krowen's behalf. Orsik tells him to "take those maps and dig that treasure" and declares, "We’re done." The elf blames the idea of using contractors on Ezra: "Mercenaries don’t do things because it’s right; they do it because they have to." Orsik asks the 3HPP if they are done; Unla Key sips from a glass and says "done", and the others nod. The elf leaves with the maps.

The next day the group splits up. Thistle hires Rebecca Deakin, an archivist in the cataloging department of Wyrmworks with unparalleled mastery of dragon-related topics, to research Adimond Kroskas for 1800 gold pieces; she tells him, "Come back in a week." Orsik meets the local Zhentarim agent Uzorel "The Viper" Hornblade, a distant relative of Taern Hornblade, described here as "the former ruler of Silverymoon". The Viper is reluctant until he learns this is "the Orsik of Parnast and Triboar fame." Orsik betrays the location of the circles he knows, in Everlund and in Yartar, asking nothing in return. When the paranoid Viper reaches for his sword at the word "Friends?", Orsik explains that he had "a beef with a high-ranking Harper" and wants "the higher-ups to know I am one of them." The Viper smiles toothily: "They will. And they will be pleased." When the others hear, they realize Yartar and Everlund may become too hot to visit, and Thistle sees a pattern in Orsik: "he gets angry when someone doesn’t give him the respect he thinks he deserves, and then lashes out by leaving."

Later that evening the group leaves Silverymoon aboard the Great Airship, a gift from the ancient red dragon Klauth. Flying above the Evermoors, Delsephine asks "where to?" and Thistle answers "the Grandfather tree", explaining that they need to collect artifacts to find a giant, get the conch, and reach King Hekaton, and that he has information "that my beloved is with the Fire Giant Duke". Vardis and Orsik think, "Touchy." The crew member Brassik offers lore for a fee. Thistle shares what he knows: the Uthgardt Tree Ghost Tribe treats the tree as sacred ground, and two Seelie Dryads, Halisa and Helia, were punished by the Queen to protect it. Orsik and Vardis pay Brassik 50 gold anyway; he warns that killing the Dryads has "powerful and dangerous consequences" and that tribe patrols pass through. Thistle and Orsik joke about their party's makeup, Orsik naming "a neutral evil wizard and a wanted criminal!", and Vardis asks, irritated, "Aren’t you supposed to be disguised?"

Vardis realizes the cultists have become friendlier since Orsik hurt the Harpers, a little payback for the Cult of the Dragon's sake and for the role of the Harper Remallia Haventree in organizing the North against Tiamat. Orsik sarcastically complains about the Harpers, Vardis mutters, "Don’t remind me of the work I quit", and an elven-looking cultist asks, "Is that why you were kicked out of the Cult of Corellon?" Brassik interrupts to add that the dryads have allied with various creatures around the forests to protect the tree.
