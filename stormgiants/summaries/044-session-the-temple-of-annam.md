# Chapter 44 - The Temple of Annam

Date: 1 to 2 Eleasis 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
The journey from the Golden Dragon’s cave to the causeway leading to the Temple of the All-Father is uninterrupted and takes two days. On the way, Thistle notices fresh human tracks and asks Harshnag about them, but the Frost Giant is confused. The causeway ends before a stone dome carved from the mountainside, held up by six 30-foot-tall stone pillars. Orsik declares that there is no danger of the dome collapsing even if the pillars were destroyed; when Vardis stares at him, he answers, "I know stuff." Vardis replies, "Apparently."

Each pillar carries a wraparound bas-relief, and Harshnag walks the party along them from north to south, giving his own commentary on each kind of giant. The hill giant is "lifting a rock above its head," recognisable "from the small size of the rock and the large belly." The frost giant is "proud and honorable and great," cutting down a pine. Of the cloud giants he says, "Birds in the sky, and the bird brained Cloud Giants are flying with them." The storm giant tosses lightning at a ship, the stone giant climbs down a mountain, and he saves his scorn for the last: "See Orsik, the Fire Giants are a plague on all of us," depicted as slavers with dwarves chained against the wall.

The 3HPP advance. Unla Key rushes ahead toward the noises while Thistle urges caution about traps. Thistle spots massive stone blocks held over the hallway by an unclear contraption, but before he can warn him, the halfling has already run past the two blocks into the main hallway. Just before the stairway, Uthgardt barbarians and their pet white dragon ambush the group, shooting from the ledges and hiding in the alcoves. The battle eventually turns, especially once Harshnag joins, furious at the idea of a dragon in this holy place. Only the Shaman escapes, fleeing into the temple.

Standing over the dead, Harshnag tells Orsik, "These are members of the Great Worm tribe. They found this Temple and came to plunder it. They were foolish." He heaves open the doors at the end of the hallway, declaring, "Only a giant could open the door to the Temple of Annam." When Vardis asks whether this is "some kind of ante-chamber," and Orsik points out that the tribesmen got in anyway, Harshnag explodes: "Antechamber? Penetrated? Bah!" Giants on those ledges, he insists, "would toss rocks, not arrows! And those blocks would have crushed you or trapped you!" He then spends another 10 to 15 minutes on the giant civilization of Ostoria: "40,000 years ago, magic-wielding Giants built this." Vardis objects, "Wait a sec, the Toril is only 5,000 years old!" Harshnag rolls his eye, makes the Giant gesture for Loon, and calls him "A creationist!" Before going in, Vardis takes an amulet from the chieftain and hangs it around his neck under his armor.

Inside the Temple of Annam, Vardis notices that the face of the All-Father's statue is blank. Harshnag explains, "The sculptors did not know what he looks like and chose to not offend." Unla Key runs to a giant portal at the far eastern edge of the room. The arch is 40 feet wide and 40 feet tall, carved with six runes inlaid with mithral and filled with glowing mist, with empty, ice-covered iron sconces in the niches beside it. He runs through the mist, hits a wall, and reports, "Nothing to see behind here." Harshnag shouts that they need to do something with the runes. Orsik asks Unla Key for a divination spell, and the diviner replies, "I know the future; what else is there to divine?" It turns out that Unla Key has no divination spell memorized, or even in his spell book. Orsik reads the runes from left to right as hill, cloud, fire, storm, stone, and frost. Harshnag says one of them is "an old version of the rune," and he starts to point out that all of the giants have weapons, but Orsik shushes him.

Thistle suggests starting with the Storm Giant rune, since "It does match the ordning," and the fairy flies up and touches it. A storm cloud spreads across the room, thunder roars, and touching the rune again does nothing. Then acid rain falls. Orsik remembers what Harshnag said, notices that the Frost Giant figure has no weapon, and touches the Frost rune. A magical ice storm hits the whole group and knocks Unla Key unconscious. Harshnag shouts, "We should run!" and Orsik heads for the ledge, but the room is too long. Thistle's touch has set off a Storm of Vengeance, and six powerful lightning bolts kill everyone in the group. Harshnag looks at the bodies and says, "Well, shit." He stuffs Orsik, Thistle, Unla Key, and Vardis into his satchel and runs for the entrance, thinking, "Thank goodness it’s three tiny people and an elf." In the hallway outside, he dumps them on the ground, finds a healing potion among their gear, and shoves it into Vardis’ mouth.

By the next day the group is healed. Unla Key says, "That was fun! Let’s go try that again!" and the others just stare at him. Orsik calls for a better plan. Harshnag asks, "Why are the weapons removable?" and Orsik and Vardis say together, "It’s the weapons." Orsik concludes that they must use each giant's weapon to touch the matching rune. Harshnag chooses the hill giant rune, since the storm giant rune was deadly. He touches it with the hill giant statue's 500lb bone club, and howling, maddened voices pour from giant distended mouths around him. He seems unaffected, but then acts as if under a confusion spell. When it wears off, he comes back and says, "Well, that didn’t work." On Orsik's suggestion that he is immune to frost damage, he touches the Frost Giant rune; it glows blue and an ice storm pelts him. He comes back grumbling, "That didn’t work either." Vardis asks about the Frost Giant weapon, and Harshnag answers, "Didn’t I mention that it’s missing?" Orsik suggests it might be somewhere in the temple.

The 3HPP go through the double doors north of the Temple of Annam into a massive room with stairs and a stone giant statue in an alcove, then down a hallway lined with fourteen 30-foot-tall archways. Each opens onto a 60-foot-square chamber with frost-covered stone couches used as beds. Harshnag sighs, "Beds fit for a giant." Vardis spots a crack in one room's wall, and the 3HPP head through it into a medium-sized tunnel while Harshnag lies down on the furs and sighs contentedly. Thistle leads them down the dark, snaking 10’ high, 5’ wide tunnel to an intersection, where spiders hiding in the shadows attack. Thistle has one thought: "I hate spiders." Fortunately for him, Orsik likes killing them.

## Locations
### Temple of the All-Father / Temple of Annam
A giant temple reached by a causeway that ends at a stone dome on six carved pillars. Inside are a trapped hallway with ledges and alcoves, giant-sized doors, a hall with the blank-faced statue of the All-Father, a six-rune portal at its eastern end, and double doors to the north.

### The Rune Portal
An archway 40 feet wide and 40 feet tall with six mithral-inlaid runes and glowing mist. Behind the mist is a wall. Touching the runes sets off storms, acid rain, ice, lightning, and mad voices.

### The Hallway of Giant Chambers
A hallway past a 60’ archway, lined with fourteen 30-foot-tall archways that open onto 60-foot-square chambers with frost-covered stone couches and tattered furs. A crack in one chamber leads into 10’ high, 5’ wide, snaking dark tunnels where spiders lie in wait.

### Ostoria
The ancient giant civilization that, according to Harshnag, built the temple 40,000 years ago.

## NPCs
### Harshnag
The Frost Giant guide, also called "The Grim." He explains the pillar carvings with plenty of bias against other giant kinds, joins the fight against the barbarians, opens the temple doors, and lectures on Ostoria. He carries the dead party out in his satchel, revives Vardis with a potion, and then tests the hill and frost runes himself. He notices first that the giants' weapons are removable.

He says "I know stuff." about the dome, reads the runes, and touches the Frost rune, which hits the group with the ice storm. He works out the weapon solution with Vardis, suggests the frost rune because Harshnag is immune to frost damage, and likes killing spiders.
