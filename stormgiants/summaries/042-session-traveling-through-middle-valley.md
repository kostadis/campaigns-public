# Chapter 42 - Traveling through Middle Valley

Date: 29 Flamerule 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
The chapter opens in the aftermath of a fight with Frost Giants. "Time to loot," shouts Orsik. Thistle glares at him: in Thistle's view, Orsik's greed, impulsiveness, and insistence on fighting Giants almost cost him his chance to find the love of his fairy heart. "Laela is in terrible danger, and I must find her!" Orsik walks confidently up to the Frost Giant corpses, goes through their bags, and shouts, "HA! SEE!" His hands come up full of gold and silver coins, with the unmistakable sound of more gold behind them.

"We went from being broke to rich!" shouts Vardis. Thistle's thoughts drift toward a first home back in the fey — "Having more money will help finance our first home back in the fey...." — until Orsik turns to Vardis and says, "Imagine how much money we will make from the merchandising of the demon Bob Saget." Thistle does not know the word, and Vardis explains it: "Selling little dolls of the demon while the play goes national."

Someone asks the name of the town. The answer is muttered — "Prettyview" — with a gesture toward a damaged sign pointing at a large building holed by boulders the frost giants hurled. The sign reads, "Welcome to the finest Inn of Prettyview!" The tavern's own name is not visible. Thistle and Orsik look inside and are disappointed: the place is in shambles, full of dead bodies crushed by falling stones or cleaved by giant swords. "No place to sleep," shouts Orsik. "And no treasure," Thistle thinks. The next few houses are the same, demolished and empty; in one lies a man dressed in armor as if about to go and fight, squished by a boulder.

"We need to bury the bodies. Can't have them turn into carrion." Harshnag the Grim smiles at this and tells Orsik he has grown — "You are not the dwarf I knew in Waterdeep. The guard that did that Thing everywhere he worked all across the North." Orsik is both pleased and annoyed: "Will they ever let it go? REALLY?"

Stepping outside, Orsik sees that only two buildings still stand: a barn and a house. The house is a massive building, 100 feet long and wide, holding an enormous library, and behind a couch hides a tiny, terrified gnome. The gnome, Yazmeen, is described as having a crooked nose, scarred hands, face tattoos, and orange hair, and seems both terrified and relieved. He tells the fighter that giants attacked the town, destroyed everything in their path, and left their feline companions behind; he escaped because he was well hidden. While Orsik peruses the books, Yazmeen explains that he is the local librarian, and that visitors from neighboring towns come to exchange books with his collection. "And of course," he adds, "we always offer tea. Would you care for some? I could use a cup myself." Orsik declines. Yazmeen notes that the barn is empty except for some cows and is uncomfortable to sleep in, and offers them the library for the night.

The magic-user Unla Key shouts, "I'll have some tea!" and is soon drinking it; the tea turns out to be magical. Orsik tries to persuade Yazmeen to come with the group, but the gnome is determined to stay despite the cold and the loneliness: "I have other library patrons, and I can't lose my books," he insists, and offers yet again: "Some tea?" Orsik decides to spend the night in Prettyview, in the warm room, and Unla Key is spared a watch shift because he is not very buff.

In the middle of the night, a monstrous aberration that looks like a giant bi-pedal toad steps out of Yazmeen's bedroom into the library where the 3HPP are sleeping and hisses, "YOU RUINED MY BREEDING PROGRAM." Unla Key is awake. While the others try to work out what happened to Yazmeen, he sees the Slaad casting a fireball and counters it. Outmatched and furious, the Slaad hurls flame at the books, setting the library ablaze, and tries to escape through the window in Yazmeen's bedroom. Unla Key traps it in the room with a wand of web. The Slaad casts more fire at the halfling mage to break his concentration, but misses, and a few moments later the creature is dead.

"Can we get out of this burning building" shouts Vardis. Unla Key mutters, "Shouldn't we check on Yazmeen?" "Fine," says the cleric, who rushes in, picks up the sleeping Yazmeen, and carries him out. Yazmeen is fine, and he has a story. The creature, he says, "was from another plane of existence." It wanted to use the inhabitants of Prettyview as interplanar anchors to summon its kind; the spell was almost complete when the Frost Giants attacked and disrupted it by killing everyone. It had been using Yazmeen as a kind of portal and threatened to end him, but sipping the tea seemed to ward it off — "Maybe there's some magical property to that brew?" Vardis works it out: "the tea was only effective for a period. When you went to sleep, the tea would wear off. When it wore off, the creature could return to our dimension." Yazmeen confirms: "Yes! That is it!"

Later that evening the group gathers around a fire in the barn, next to the cows. Vardis overhears Harshnag and Orsik arguing again. "We should go straight to the Oracle!" claims the giant; Orsik counters with "The treasure map?" Watching them, Vardis sees Harshnag look up into the sky as if searching for words, and then mumble under his breath, "The All-Father sent a sign."

After Prettyview, the 3HPP continue eastward, deeper into the Middle Valley of the Spine of the World. A large sound comes from the towering peaks on one side of the valley. "Avalanche!" screams Harshnag. With only minutes before tons of snow come down and nowhere to hide, Orsik and Unla Key devise a plan: Unla Key casts water breathing on the group, and Orsik throws fire bolts to melt ice, so that no one risks asphyxiation. Orsik then proposes using the fire bolts to cut through the snow. As the group huddles and Orsik cuts, they hear a hearty laugh and see sunlight through an opening in the snow. Harshnag, who managed not to get covered, has dug them out: "I am so sorry for helping you, I know how you little people don't want to be helped by us Giants!"

## Locations

### Prettyview
A town destroyed by frost giants: houses smashed by hurled boulders, bodies everywhere. Its inn bears the sign "Welcome to the finest Inn of Prettyview!" Only a barn (holding some cows) and a large house with a library survive. The library burns down during the fight with the Slaad; the party spends the rest of the night in the barn.

### Yazmeen's Library
A massive house, 100 feet long and wide, holding an enormous library where neighboring towns' visitors trade books. The 3HPP sleep there until the Slaad sets the books ablaze.

### Middle Valley
The valley of the Spine of the World through which the 3HPP travel eastwards after Prettyview, and where the avalanche falls on them.

## NPCs

### Harshnag the Grim
The giant travelling with the 3HPP. He tells Orsik he has grown since Waterdeep, argues that the group "should go straight to the Oracle," and mutters, "The All-Father sent a sign." He gives the avalanche warning and, having escaped being buried, digs the party out with a hearty laugh and a mock apology.

### Yazmeen
A gnome, the local librarian of Prettyview and the only survivor found. Terrified and relieved, he constantly offers tea, refuses to leave his books and patrons, and after the Slaad's attack explains that the creature was using him and the town as part of an interplanar summoning, kept at bay by the tea.

### The Slaad
An aberration resembling a giant bi-pedal toad, from another plane of existence. It accuses the sleeping party, "YOU RUINED MY BREEDING PROGRAM," tries to cast a fireball, burns the library, and is killed after Unla Key traps it with a wand of web.

### Laela
Named only by Thistle, who says she is in terrible danger and that he must find her.
