# Chapter 36 - The Hit

Date: 14 to 15 Flamerule 1492 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-06)

## Summary
With their options in front of them, Orsik grumps, "Let's stay for the party," and so that evening the 3HPP head out to where the rich and powerful live, in a series of ranches that ring the town of Triboar. Their destination is Westpharl Ranch, the home away from home of Cindell, with sprawling grounds and a large master's house. By the time they arrive the party is in full swing: there is music, Bob Saget the demon is cracking jokes, folks are dancing, and there is food of every kind and variety.

As the night goes on, a page comes to the 3HPP and invites them to a private audience. Orsik is shocked: "are you sure she said all of us?" In her study, a large room with bookshelves, art, a lush carpet and a fireplace, Cindell smiles, and Orsik can't help but ask, "You want all of us simultaneously?" Cindell realizes Orsik thought this was a different kind of private audience and looks at him with the sorrow of a rich person regarding someone without the class or the money to be seriously considered. Orsik is oblivious. Vardis tries not to laugh and mutters, "Old sport." Then, with all the grace of her noble upbringing, she says, "Sit down."

Cindell lays out her grievance. Hyuth Kolstaag, she says, has been "a frightfully bad presence for the town" ever since he arrived: a rich retired adventurer, arrogant, without a touch of class, who has rejected every one of her invitations to introduce him into Triboar society. He has made enemies who periodically show up to try to kill him, and she worries that "The violence will cause a drop in the property values of Triboar." When the expected gasp does not come, she continues: a few weeks ago a pair of assassins slipped into town and attempted to kill him, and the town was awoken to "peals of thunder and blasts of lightning." When the attempt was foiled, the assassins stole a horse from the Cart and Coin and murdered the stable hand.

Orsik grunts, "We will track down and kill the assassins," but Cindell has a different job in mind. Hyuth refused to recompense the owners of the Cart and Coin for their lost horse and refused to buy a table at the luncheon she threw in support of the stable hand's family. Orsik says, "I am not sure what the issue is." She insists, "He's not a good person. He's a bad person." The Lord Protector of Triboar, Darathra Shendrel, has "only thought it appropriate to warn him with notices and stern words." Still nobody understands, so she explains: she ran for high office and was defeated by "that nobody", and that nobody is a Harper Agent. She believes Triboar must be allied to the Lords' Alliance, which will not happen while Darathra is here, and getting rid of this problem would help her win the next election by showing Darathra's "ineffectual leadership and unwillingness to deal with the riff-raff."

Orsik gets the hint: "So you want us to finish the job the assassins botched?" She answers, "Whatever you think is appropriate." Orsik then turns to Thistle and says, "We need to talk." Orsik is not in favor of killing random people; it is not who he is now. But Thistle tells him he has a plan, so the fighter shrugs and nods.

Thistle asks Cindell for an IOU made out to them, since they have had some bad luck with other people. She is outraged and tries to get Vardis to intervene, but he refuses: "They have a mind of their own." Thistle insists. Cindell tries to appeal to Orsik directly by offering to clear his name in Waterdeep; when he asks for all of the North, she laughs, "I am not a miracle worker." Finally she concedes, "Name your price." Orsik proposes 10,000 gold, and she snorts that for that price she could get a magic user to erase him "for every timeline of every existence." She counters: "How about 500? 550 if I win the election?" Orsik shrugs, wishes her good luck with her election, and leaves. Seeing him go, she shouts to Thistle, "What about you?" Thistle says, "I'll do it if you sign the IOU." She resists at first, but relents when he unleashes the twinkle of his fairy eyes, the same eyes that Thistle's lost love fell in love with. To her "Is this really necessary?" he answers, "Ever since the Dragon Wars, you can't rely on people to pay their bills." She sighs and hands him a paper outlining the nature of the deal without getting into too many specifics.

Leaving the ranch, Orsik asks, "What are you thinking?" Thistle's plan: they don't want the Lords' Alliance here, so they will tell Hyuth about the assassination, then tell Darathra about this, and ensure that Cindell doesn't win the election. Orsik fist-bumps him.

The chapter reveals what the rest of the gang does not realize: Thistle is a member of the Seelie Court. It is why Prince Thornacious recognized him, and why Blasius was his friend. He was a minor member, but still a member, and for him intrigue is what members of the court do for fun. Seeing how bad Cindell was at conspiracy, he had to show her how it was done.

The following day Thistle goes to Urgala, in her drawing room in the Northshield House, and tells her about the odd shenanigans Cindell has asked of him, making it known that Cindell's plans for the next election of the Lord Protector are underhanded. When Urgala is confused, he says, "Well, she did ask us to kill Hyuth Kolstaag," and presents the contract. Urgala is curiously not surprised ("I'd expect anything from her"), then pauses: "But why him?" Staring at a corner of the room, she describes Hyuth as "one of the more pain-in-the-ass magic users who went on the adventuring circuit," someone it was always about, but who could be relied on in a fight; now retired, he brings enemies and "the kind of trouble that makes people feel unsafe." She asks whether Thistle heard what happened a couple of weeks ago; Thistle lies, "No." She repeats the story of the assassins and adds that Hyuth summoned some gargoyles to protect him, refused to pay anything, and won't even send a note to the family: "Makes all of us retired adventurers look bad." Asked "So we should kill him?", she demurs: "My book has some bad pages, and I have sinned." Thistle feigns surprise. She will not judge, but "if someone were to get him to be a better neighbor, that would be a good thing…." As they part she mumbles, "I never imagined that Cindell would be that cold-blooded."

Thistle rejoins the 3HPP crew at the Talking Troll, and they all go to Darathra at the central tower. She is pleased to see them, congratulates them on the success of their play, and remarks on all the new business they have brought to town: "Thank goodness you aren't running for office," she says with a wink. Thistle wastes no time, telling her they have a contract Cindell signed to kill Hyuth, and that "since we are blood brothers, I thought we should bring this to you." Darathra smiles like a cat about to pounce on a rat: "You did the right thing. I am going to bury her with this." She will use it to get Hyuth to calm down, and "when he calms down, without murder, I will win in a landslide." She means to go talk to "that shitty magic user right now," but Orsik offers to do it and make sure Hyuth knows it is from her. She gives them the message: she, the Lord Protector, intervened to stop this incident, may not be able to intervene in the future, and he may want to reconsider "being a productive citizen and thus protected by the 12." As they leave she shouts, "Bring the contract back when you are done. I'll need it for safekeeping."

At Hyuth's mansion the 3HPP note its size, its opulence, and the missing roof, obliterated and covered in scorch marks. A whiny voice screeches, "Who are you?" On Thistle's suggestion, Orsik answers, "Here to repair your roof." The voice does not want it fixed, does not trust them or anybody, and declares, "Everyone is out to kill me." When told the Lord Protector asked them to fix the mansion because the roof damage affects property values, he screeches, "PROPERTY VALUES! THIS IS HARASSMENT!" then pauses: "She offered to pay?" Inside they find a 55-year-old, tired man with a paunch, grey hair, and crow's eyes, who observes, "Most construction workers don't carry great axes, swords, spells, and a bow." Orsik says, "We use magic." Hyuth sighs and leads them to his second-floor study, a complete disaster area of burned papers, a smashed desk, and a trashed roof, with rain, wind and sun damaging whatever was left. Orsik turns to Thistle, "So what's the next move." But poor Thistle wasn't very good at Seelie Court intrigues and is unsure.
