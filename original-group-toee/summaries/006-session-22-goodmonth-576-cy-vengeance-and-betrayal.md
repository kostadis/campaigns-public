# Chapter 06 - 22 Goodmonth 576 CY Vengeance and Betrayal

Date: 22 Goodmonth 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
"The Earth, Water, and Fire Temples were crushed. Now, the question of what to do next lingered." Well, not really: "Kelno had to go." The offer to kill "the sniveling Kelno" was too good a suggestion for Oohlgrist to pass up, and so, "armed with a new Troll, the Party goes to face Kelno."

"Ah, those Clerics and their pesky Gods." Kelno uses his ability to create an illusion to avoid getting slaughtered when a combined troll and party attack deals staggering amounts of damage to him. "Seeing his bugbears hurt and his personage defiled, Kelno decides the time has come to seek safer places and dimension doors out of the room." Where he went is anyone's guess: "Does he go to another room? Does he go outside? 500 feet in every direction is a lot of distance."

Once Kelno is dealt with, Oohlgrist wants his fair share of the treasure. "The two parties had an honest difference of opinion on the meaning of the word fair." Chance and Furnoc tried to convince Oohlgrist that "¼ of the volume of goods was a quarter share." "Oohlgrist, mysteriously, disagreed."

Battle ensued. At one point Oohlgrist tried a surge attack and "missed with both of his bites and all four of his claw attacks." He "screamed at the Troll gods for forsaking him and bought new dice." The Party then quickly slaughtered him, and with Oohlgrist dead, found a few more bugbears and quickly dispatched them.

"Bereft of healing spells and wounded", the Party decided that the better part of valor was a return to "the wretched hive known as Nulb" rather than continuing their quest for treasure, glory and honor in the Temple.
