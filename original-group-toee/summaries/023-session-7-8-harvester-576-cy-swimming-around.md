# Chapter 23 - 7–8 Harvester 576 CY Swimming around …

Date: 7–8 Harvester 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
The chapter picks up after the party has dealt with the swarm of fish and found a portal to the node of air. After a brief respite they search the node, heading east toward a piece of dry land. As they approach, the water grows shallow and warm with a faint mistiness, and floating plants with bright pink blossoms thicken toward the shelf. Hyramm, "proving his superior intellect," deduces that these are not safe, and the party gives them a wide berth.

From fifteen feet out, the shelf shows itself: lush vegetation and "lots and lots of spiders." While the others debate, Furnoc acts, approaching the land with a torch to burn the spiders to the ground. A Drow emerges and attacks him — Furnoc is "a little bit surprised to discover that a Drow cleric might be here," having expected a giant spider. Benefitting from surprise and using her ability to stun him, the Drow cleric kills Furnoc, who "on death’s door told death to shove it and survived." The party springs into action: Honiel casts a spell that stuns the cleric, and the adventurers, Berc in particular, kill her before anyone can ask "what an adherent of Lolth’s was doing in the watery node." Furnoc, back from the dead, discovers there was no giant spider.

Honiel then becomes very interested in what lies under the floating plants. The rest of the party tries to dissuade her; she is unimpressed, dons her necklace of adaptation, swims under, and finds "nothing, nothing of any value or interest under the plants."

Heading south, the party finds a lagoon home to a few Ixitxachitl. Honiel, being a genasi, speaks their tongue. The creatures had been lured to the node with the promise of slaves to build their labyrinth, which is central to their worship of Demogorgon. Honiel spends some time trying to negotiate, but the demonic creatures want the rest of the group as slaves and she cannot talk them out of it. They keep referring to promises Zuggtmoy made about enough slaves to build a labyrinth, and to how "she never sent enough slaves to feed all the monsters in the deep." It becomes clear the Ixitxachitl have become "a little bit unhinged" without a labyrinth to focus their society and worship. Battle follows. For a moment it is scary: the monsters use hold person powers and Berc drops to the bottom of the lagoon, but the party breaks the Ixitxachitl's concentration and he does not drown.

Further south the water grows shallower and cloudier from volcanic vents in the floor, and the group avoids the vents as much as possible. They find a hidden grotto filled with fireflies, a pool of air plants and a low mist, recognise it as an excellent place to rest, and rest. Afterwards they reach a hidden shelf, barely visible through the misty water, with damp soil and mushrooms that grow more exotic closer to a portal to the node of Earth. Finding only food there, they swim north to a rocky outcropping with a boat, a hooded lantern with a continual flame shining from its prow. Furnoc wonders aloud, "Why is there a boat?"

The answer: the boat is a lure. Captives who land in area 1 flee to the ship, where a hezrou demon devours them — "What hezrou demon, you ask? Why the one attacking the party." The Hezrou demon almost kills Hyramm, but Hyramm escapes, and the rest of the party prepares to take the beast down.
