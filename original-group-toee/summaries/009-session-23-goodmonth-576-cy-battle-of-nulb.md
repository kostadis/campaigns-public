# Chapter 09 - 23 Goodmonth 576 CY Battle of Nulb

Date: 23 Goodmonth 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
After securing the keys from the trolls, Furnoc and Chance decided that the time had come to return to Nulb. "Short on magic, and short on hit points, the continued battle seemed pointless." On the winding forest path to Nulb, they stumbled on "a strike force from the Temple of 15 bugbears, and a hill giant all dressed in black."

The Party quickly donned its black cloaks. Chance slunk into the forest and headed back to Nulb, while Furnoc approached the hill giant. The Hill Giant quickly revealed that the bugbears were heading to Nulb "to get rid of Tolub and replace him with Grud": Grud was willing to work with the Temple of Elemental Evil, and Tolub was not. Furnoc convinced the hill giant that they were supposed to help the force "take out the newly self-appointed mayor."

Chance, using his cloak of elvenkind and his stealth, reached Nulb before the strike force. He went first to Otis and remarked that perhaps he might want to do something about the "rather large and imposing strike force coming to destroy the town." Otis "seemed remarkably nonplussed" and said something about this sort of thing happening all the time, and about the higher good calling for him to keep his cover. Chance went next to the Canoness Y’dey, a little more frantic this time, explaining that the situation was dire — because, well, Walter was his connection to a supply chain of magic. Chance was about to demand that Walter stay close to him when Otis intervened: Walter "was a crucial part of Otis’ plans, and Otis would ensure he survived." The Canoness, too, was unwilling to get directly involved, and suggested Chance talk to the Lord Mayor and to "Lundbeams, the leader of the Black Hand."

Chance ran to Lundbeams’ house. "Lundbeams is a bald, green-eyed cleric who worships Heironeous." Not originally from Nulb, she was led there by her faith and set up shop to deliver Nulb from the Temple. She hasn’t had much success, other than setting up a gang called the black hand "that sometimes acts against the agents of the Temple of Elemental Evil." She quickly agreed to commit her gang to fight the strike force.

Chance then ran to the Boatmens’ Tavern and Nulb Market, "the Lord Mayor’s seat of power", and told him of the imminent danger. "Tolub freaked out and ordered his gang to move out." Chance became freaked out as well: with no one to defend Nulb, "his carefully cultivated connections with Walter would come to naught." Surprisingly to Chance, Grud suddenly became an advocate of staying to fight. He pointed out that Tolub wanted the respectability and profit of running a town, that if he turned and fled this would be lost forever — "And after all, wasn’t he the Lord Mayor of Nulb, Tolub the first of his name?" Tolub reflected and agreed to stay and fight.

Chance quickly arranged the defenses. Two pirates, Koldo and Max, stood on the roof with Chance, all armed with bows. Lundbeams hid out of sight, ready to flank the bugbears as they arrived.

When the hill giant and the rest of the Party came, the battle was engaged. The Hill Giant, unaware that the Party were not agents of the Temple, let the bugbears charge toward the Tavern and "threw a giant boulder at Chance and the Pirates on the roof." Honiel, Berc and Furnoc carefully flanked the giant, and "after he threw his stone, the Party killed him in one round with a series of well-placed attacks."

Lundbeams and the Black Hand came around the tavern and flanked the bugbears from the south. "Surrounded and not too bright, the Bugbears split their forces into three": some to fight Lundbeams and her crew, some to confront the Party coming from the North, and a few toward the tavern. Split that way, the bugbears could not get "critical mass against any single target." The Party slowly killed the bugbears nearest them, which meant Lundbeams and her gang took the brunt of the attack; by the end of the battle, Cornelius, Jirard and Dante were dying on the battlefield.

Meanwhile, three bugbears made it to the door of the Tavern. Chance, Koldo and Max killed them, but not before they broke the door down — and when it broke, Grud turned on Tolub to try to kill him. "Seems Grud wanted Tolub to stick around to get killed so that Grud could become the new Lord Mayor or Pirate King …"

Berc, Honiel and Furnoc killed the bugbears closest to them. Hyramm, Berc and Honiel went to help Lundbeams and the Black Hand gang, while Furnoc "rushed to see what the hell was going on in the Tavern." There he saw Tolub and Grud fighting and four pirates guarding the door. Furnoc stood outside wondering what to do while the pirates stared at him. He thought about just waiting for the fight to end, "and then realized that the right thing to do was to help the damn mayor they installed in the city", and rushed in. The pirates at the door fired their bows at him and missed. "Accidentally? Who is to say." As the pirates said, they served the king.

Inside, Furnoc killed Grud. Tolub was delighted. Furnoc tried to get the pirates to help him fight off the rest of the bugbears, but they insisted their job was to protect Tolub, and Tolub had no interest in leaving. Furnoc left the Pirates and went to help Lundbeams.

"The Black Hand gang had become a party of one as the Bugbears overwhelmed the guards." Fortunately, Berc, Hyramm and Honiel were able to lend a hand and kill all of the remaining bugbears. Koldo and Max, with Tolub safe, were reluctant to continue the fight but used their arrows on the remaining bugbears, and after a short while the bugbears were all dead. Lundbeams and Honiel saved the lives of Cornelius, Jirard and Dante, "who were grievously wounded and unconscious and not dead."

In the aftermath, the Lord Mayor, freaked out by his near-death, offered the position of mayor to Lundbeams, who refused. He thought about abandoning his job, then realized that he was a marked man: either the Party would defeat the Temple or he would be killed, and "better to be assassinated as Lord Mayor than a rat on a ship." After this open break with the Temple, Lundbeams decided she should no longer operate covertly and "declared herself Sheriff of Nulb."

As Lundbeams and Tolub negotiated the new power structure, Otis and Y’dey showed up with "this peculiar expression on their face." This battle between the forces of the Temple and the forces of Nulb had happened many times before. "This was the first time where the outcome was not the destruction of the upstart Nulb mayor." "Perhaps, this time the Temple would be crushed…"
