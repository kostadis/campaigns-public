# Chapter 33 - 15 Harvester 576 CY Efreeti and Angels

Date: 15 Harvester 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
The party left the water node, bringing Jacer along with them; the rest of the group of civilians were left behind in the water node.

The party met up with the local Azer war camp, which was "more than happy to ally itself with the party to attack and kill the Efreeti." Unfortunately the Azers cannot actually get to the Efreeti, because of "a carefully placed set of* the magic circle* that wards them out." To reach him, the Azer would have to go through Salamander-controlled territory, and "apparently killing a degenerate Efreeti is one thing. Killing Fire Salamanders is something else." The party was told where the Salamander territory was.

The party then met a Deva, who told them his role was to kill the former leader of The Greater Temple, Gemmel. When Gemmel died, the Deva was trapped — "Something about a poorly worded quest." It turned out that no one had a "remove curse" spell handy.

The Deva told the party how to use The Orb of Golden Death, along with "some essential concerns and advantages." Whoever possesses the Orb "will shift permanently to a more evil alignment." Iuz and Zuggtmoy "will know precisely where the possessor is." Iuz and Zuggtmoy cannot directly attack the possessor, "Although they can send the hordes of hell to kill the possessor." With the Orb, the possessor "can enter Zuggtmoy's interdicted prison through the throne room in the ground level of the Temple." And with the gems in place, "the possessor of the Orb can gate to the throne room."

The Deva also shared that the Efreeti plane-shifts, and that any attack has been thwarted because of that ability: "You either kill him in the first round, or he flees."

The group then fought a set of water weirds. Finally, they discovered one of Gemmel's private chambers, with some of his personal paperwork.
