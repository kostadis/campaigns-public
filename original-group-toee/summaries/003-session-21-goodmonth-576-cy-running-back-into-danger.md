# Chapter 03 - 21 Goodmonth 576 CY Running back into danger.

Date: 21 Goodmonth 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
"After an eventless night of rest, the Party woke and headed across the street to talk to the Canoness." This is where things get interesting. Chance shows Canoness Y’dey a scarab he had found "when he first arrived in Hommlet with the word TZGY", and the Canoness tells him it belongs to Zuggtmoy, "the demon queen of fungi."

The Party learns that the real power behind the Temple was the Demoness Zuggtmoy, and that the real purpose of the Canoness and Otis, and of the Church of St. Cuthbert, was to make sure she didn't escape. The Temple itself was uninteresting. "Seems St. Cuthbert has a visceral hatred of Zuggtmoy after she aligned herself with Iuz."

Then comes a chain of consequences. After the defeat of the Earth Temple, Fire Temple agents or Water Temple agents burned or drowned Earth Temple agents: "Every action in the Temple affected the real world." If the Party defeated the Water Temple entirely, the Fire Temple would use that as an opportunity to crush the Water Temple allies in Nulb. The townies of Nulb would then see their chance to get rid of the Fire Temple faction and reclaim their town; if the Fire Temple were removed, The Greater Temple would intervene; and the ensuing chaos would probably bring "the good Kingdom of Furyondy and the saintly Archclericy of Veluna, and the Viscount of Verbobonc" to send forces down. Nulb lies south of the Kingdoms that want to defend the world from the Evil Kingdoms of the North West.

Chance asked "why wasn't the Viscount of Verbobonc doing more?" His job, after the battle of Emridy Meadows, was to keep the peace. The Canoness's answer was quite interesting: the Viscount "seemed peculiarly disinterested." He could resolve the situation with a small garrison, and yet he seemed very disinclined to do so — "Almost as if he was letting the Temple encroach on his lands."

And then there was the risk of war with Iuz and the Horned Society. If the forces of good were distracted and sent their armies south, Iuz might decide to do the same into Furyondy and Veluna "with disastrous consequences." Zuggtmoy had forged an alliance with Iuz to build the Temple, and "she had an artifact buried in the Temple that Iuz wanted no one to find." Iuz wanted that artifact, and the church of St. Cuthbert did not want him to find it, so Iuz would invade to stop the armies from going south. "A decade of peace ruined because a bunch of adventurers was too clumsy in their explorations." But as the Chaotic Neutral Party put it: "So what?"

The Canoness, in exasperation, started to wonder if she could kill the Party, when Murfles — "now known as Lady Pain, her daughter" — who was "a little bit more practical than the average Cleric of St. Cuthbert", had an insight. If the Armies of Good arrived, small-time adventurers might find themselves imprisoned or executed for creating the mess, or at best find the treasure locked in the Temple out of reach behind the armies; and even if it were reachable, "the tax-free plunder would suddenly be taxed." "Taxation, imprisonment, and loss of plunder got everyone's attention."

Murfles offered a solution. The problem was the Fire Temple and the Water Temple not being decapitated at the same time. If both were defeated, the groups loyal to them would flee, since without the power of the Temples their position was too tenuous. Even if Fire Temple agents stayed and the locals slaughtered them, that was unlikely to provoke further action; the changes in leadership in the Temples "had produced many such bloodlettings." The Air Temple was "too weak and had no agents in the village." To outside observers, "it would look like another day in the Abyss", and The Greater Temple would not have to intervene: "Rebuilding the Temples was something they did a lot of over the last decade."

With that insight, the Party returned to the Temple with only one question: "how do you take down an 8th level cleric who can paralyze your Party at once." Everyone remembered Kelno and his bugbears. If they could find Kelno, they could use his bugbears to deliver what Kelno wanted, "dominance over his antagonists." "The problem was where in the world was Kelno?"

Exploring the dungeon, they discovered another faction in the Temple (241-242), "the guys wearing black robes": The Greater Temple Faction. Keeping the peace among all of the lesser Temples is The Greater Temple, and Brunk and Feldrin "were the faces of The Greater Temple." It was a story Brunk exchanged for his life. Feldrin "was the man in charge, and Brunk was just a cop"; Brunk insisted he was just a man who got paid and worked the beat, who didn't believe anything and "just wanted to make a buck."

Chance made him an offer: hand over the shield, and tell them where the Air Temple was. Brunk was pleased to oblige (Room 210). The Party got his shield, "to prove that the forces of The Greater Temple were defeated", and a map to the air Temple. They also learned, again, that a "deal with an evil person is not quite what you think it is": there were more guards in room 243, "Guards happy to try and kill intruders in the Temple."
