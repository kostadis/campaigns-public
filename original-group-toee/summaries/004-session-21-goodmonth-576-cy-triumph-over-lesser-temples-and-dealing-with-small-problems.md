# Chapter 04 - 21 Goodmonth 576 CY Triumph Over Lesser Temples and Dealing With Small Problems.

Date: 21 Goodmonth 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
"The Party finally reached the Temple of fire." There can be no doubt about it: a huge hall of red granite, "lit by scores of flambeaux and pervaded by the odor of heated metal and sulfur." Great doors of beaten brass reflect the leaping flames, tapestries on the walls of the wider north section depict "scenes of fire in evil form", and motes of rust-colored luminescence hang in the air, so that the streaks of blue and mica flakes in the polished granite "appear to leap and dance as if flames, caught within the rock."

A brass tube, 20' long and 2' in diameter, hangs from the ceiling on brass chains, with a shallow fire pit just to its north and low brass stands of charcoal blocks to either side. East and west, beyond lines of pillars, stand great copper cauldrons filled with "some sort of glowing amber liquid", each with an octagonal brass table before it holding 16 bowls and a copper dipper. At the south end stands the altar: "a translucent block of golden apatite, three feet wide and high and nine feet long", its sides set with skulls inlaid in hammered gold, topped by "the eight-pointed fire symbol set in wrought gold." Before it lies a diamond-shaped fire pit of glowing coals where "Tiny forms seem to cavort", perhaps small denizens of the Elemental Plane of Fire.

The two rogues head for the altar. "And just as Chance and Furnoc reach the altar, Alrrem and his bodyguards enter the room from the North": 5 bugbears and a paladin, who surround Berc, Hyramm and Honiel. "Surrounded and with no magic, the Party wonders if this will be their final stand."

Berc is quickly dispatched. Hyramm and Honiel withstand the assault "just long enough for Furnoc and Chance to make it back." The battle is "fast and furious, with the paladin keeping everyone on their toes." First Alrrem is defeated, then the paladin, and then the bugbears. With that defeat, the Party decides that perhaps it is time to return to Nulb.
