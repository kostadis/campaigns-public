# Chapter 30 - 12 Harvester 576 CY Fleeing dragons

Date: 12 Harvester 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
The chapter opens where the last left off, in a cave where the party faces Ashrem. The recap explains that Ashrem was the brother of Alrrem and "was once the head of the Air Temple on the 2nd level." Dourakine and Chance were paralyzed, but Furnoc, Honiel, and Berc were not, and Ashrem "thought he had the upper hand." A series of stunning reversals followed: Furnoc killed a Yeti, then severely wounded Ashrem, Berc finished him off, and soon the yetis that "were seemingly guarding him" were dead as well. The party's happiness lasted only until they realized Ashrem might have useful information, so Honiel cast spare the dying to give them a chance to talk to the former head of the air Temple.

When they revived him, they were surprised to discover "that he wasn't Ashrem." When Chance pointed out that he was in a room full of Yetis, faux-Ashrem told them they had "completely misread the situation." He was a prisoner of Ashrem, "put here to cool his heels, and to be fed to Ashrem's Air Elemental Toadie" — and yes, the chapter confirms, the name of Ashrem's pet air elemental was "Toadie." Asked what happened, Muirtone began to whine: "Do you have any idea what it's like being the side-kick of Ashrem?" When Kelno backstabbed Ashrem with Alrrem, Ashrem's brother, the two of them were both sent here, and for ten years it had been "my brother this and my brother that." Every day for ten years he took care of toadie and fed the yetis, until one day he had enough, and Ashrem put him with the Yetis so that he could change his mind. "And then you guys come here and try to kill me! I am a lowly functionary, midlevel management."

Furnoc looked at him in disgust: "So you are useless to us?" Suddenly terrified that his audience might decide to kill him again, Muirtone quickly "back-pedals on how useless he is": "No, I said I lack leadership abilities." The party huddled and stared at their prisoner, and Muirtone turned to them with a sly gaze and asked, "Why are you here?" Furnoc said they were looking for the gem of power. Muirtone realized they had "the Orb of death" and was very intrigued. Chance picked up on his change in demeanor and asked what he wanted: "I want out! I want out of this place."

Asked whether he would help the party, Muirtone thought it over: "I am a lackey. I can have an evil boss or an excellent psychotic boss. If there are no live dissections, I am okay." Chance, puzzled, repeated "Live dissections?", and Muirtone, as if explaining introductory evil cleric 101, said: "Yes, do you have any idea how much noise people make when you dissect them, and they are alive? I don't have earplugs." Furnoc was disgusted — "I wouldn't know about that because I am not evil" — and Muirtone answered petulantly, "You are too good for me." Furnoc: "I am too good for live dissections. Listen Muirtone, I don't like you and (pointing to Berc) he doesn't like you either". Chance, amused at the repartee, waited for the two to stop staring at each other, then got the lackey to tell him where Ashrem was.

As the party was about to go to Ashrem for the gem, Muirtone said, "Ashrem doesn't have the gem." He had been looking for it himself, because "it would have helped upper management and made them look good." An annoyed Chance demanded, "What are you useful for?" Muirtone explained his role in life: "I am the guy upper management screams at when they are frustrated. When the boss needs to kick someone, he kicks me." For the first time, Furnoc saw his value: "a punching bag, or a body to be thrown to the Dragons."

Asked what else was in the node, Muirtone pointed out that to the north were goblins with "a pretty slick business." As Furnoc thought of attacking them, Muirtone observed that he always thought the Cloud giant had the gem. Furnoc objected, "But the giant said he didn't." Muirtone stared at him as at a child: "you are a good person. You trust people. You probably don't lie. Of course, he has it and for years." Asked whether he had gone to look, he pointed to his small size — "Big deadly giant, a minor middle-management cleric with no leadership potential" — then paused and offered the other option, the dragons. If it was the dragons, then after the Battle of Emridy Meadows the Orb "was split up into its constituent parts, the Orb, and the four gems," and "Hedrack or whomever Hedrack replaced, sent couriers into the nodes with the gems to hide them." If the dragons had the gem, they had found whoever brought it, killed him, and carried the thing to their hoard.

The party decided to head to the goblins first. At the goblin trading post they were quickly admitted to the leader's cavern: "I am goblin chief Gobluk, and I offer poisons and potions." Chance said, "We are looking for a gem of power." The chief exclaimed, "You are looking for gem power!", waved his finger in the sky with an "AH, Aaaaah!", and then asked in confusion, "What is the gem of power?" An aide spoke to him, and Gobluk suddenly understood: "You want the gem of power of The Orb of Golden Death. Why?" The party stayed non-communicative. The chief insisted he did not have the gem; what he had was the map of all the gates, and he knew the gates form a circle, with no way to escape the nodes. Furnoc tried to buy the map, and was handed "a scroll with random notations on it" — "what a rogue calls a map and what Gobluk calls a map are two different things." While the party pondered, the chief observed that if he could escape, he would go back to the Earth Temple "and beat the head of the chief cleric of the Earth Temple." Chance seized on it and offered him a way out if he would help. The chief agreed.

With this new-found ally, the party headed to the dragon. The plan was "relatively basic": Furnoc and Chance would sneak up and see if the gem was there, and, unbeknownst to Muirtone, he was plan B if the dragons detected them. The two rogues scaled the cliffs to the napping white dragons. Furnoc was detected by one of them; Chance, wearing a cloak of elvenkind, stayed hidden. The dragon breathed on Furnoc, who barely survived and ran down the cliff with the white dragon in pursuit, screaming, "Plan B." Berc, very confused, wondered "what the hell plan B is," then remembered that Muirtone was plan B, killed Muirtone, and hid. The dragon saw the dead body, seized it, and left the rest of the party alone. Furnoc breathed again.

Meanwhile Chance was alone with the other dragon. Looking around, he saw some gems and "the carcass of Gwalihur the Frost Giant," and inferred that the dragons did not have the gem "because they are rolling over everything." He slowly slunk back to the party.

The party then went to deal with Ashrem. Chance tried to sneak into the cave holding him and stepped on a glyph of warding; a gust of wind almost threw him off the cliff, but he dropped to the ground just in time. He got up and pretended to be Muirtone. Ashrem fell for it and asked Muirtone what he was up to and how he had escaped the Yetis. Chance and Furnoc used the opportunity to sneak in, surround Ashrem, and kill him. "Ashrem's pet air elemental toadie" tried to take revenge for his old master and failed. The party searched the room and found no gem.
