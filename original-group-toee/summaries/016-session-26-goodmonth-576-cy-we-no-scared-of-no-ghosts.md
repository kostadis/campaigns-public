# Chapter 16 - 26 Goodmonth 576 CY We no scared of no ghosts.

Date: 26 Goodmonth 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
The chapter picks up with the party inside the Opulent Bedchamber of "a groaning spirit (aka a Banshee room 326)". The fight was decided before it could begin: "Turns out winning initiative and charging boldly was the winning play." Berc and Furnoc charged, and in the chapter's own astonished words, "the Eldritch Fighter and the Cowardly thief charged at the Banshee and killed her before she wailed."

Next came the Black Room, where the party faced a set of shadows and "another shadow demon". The narrator is careful to distinguish it from an earlier room: it is "very different from the Red Room. Except for the Demon and Shadows." The shadow demon also carried "that annoying staff that protects the undead from being turned", which made the fight harder than the bare head-count suggests.

To finish the day, the party found a laboratory, room 330. Its outer area, 10' wide and 50' long, "seems to have been meant principally for the reading and writing of spells": cressets on the walls at ten-foot intervals, a long slanted shelf along the north wall with stools along its 30' length, sheets of vellum and parchment still lying on it, quills, small pots with oddly carved stoppers and candlesticks on an upper ledge, a small writing desk at the west end and a lectern at the far east, each facing an arched doorway leading north, and several small chairs along the south wall.

Furnoc and Chance decided to scout ahead and "leverage their fantastical ability to surprise", and went deeper. The inner chamber, 80' x 20', was "obviously an abandoned laboratory for magical research and experimentation": four long benches covered in paraphernalia, a flowing fountain of water in the southwest corner, and nearly every other stretch of wall given over to shelves, cupboards and bins of magical ingredients — "ape teeth, basilisk scales, cockatrice feathers, elephant hide, fox dung, gorgon tongue, hydra blood", along with earths, seeds, fur, dried leaves, flower petals, bark chips, and metals in rods, wire, ingots, strips, chunks, filings and powder.

There they confronted a pair of ropers. Ropers "have this annoying ability to immobilize you and then chew on you", and they are very good at noticing intruders — but "Fortunately, Furnoc is very good at seeing Ropers."

Then the party's greed got the better of it. "Seems there is something about cursed books the party loves to touch": they opened another cursed book and faced a chasme demon, whose "annoying noise" knocks everyone unconscious. "And yes, Berc once again used the wand of wonder." The result was a fireball. Hidden under the blast of the fireball was Furnoc, who had been knocked unconscious by the chasme demon — "And because he was unconscious, he was fully impacted by the blast." The chasme itself had resistance. The narrator's verdict: "So Berc, once again, hurt his team more than the other side." The chapter adds, in Berc's defence, that "when it mattered the flaming rapier took out the banshee."

Eventually the party stopped being knocked unconscious "by the buzzing bug demon" and was able to defeat "the horrifically horrendous spawn of demonic evil." With that, the group decided to head back to Nulb.
