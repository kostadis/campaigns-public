# Chapter 24 - 8–9 Harvester 576 CY Monsters of the deep

Date: 8–9 Harvester 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
The chapter opens with a joke: the party, "chillaxing in a safe, quiet, and homey space," wakes ready to get coffee at the Boatmens’ Tavern or the Farmer’s Market with Tolub… "Wake up, wake up!" That was several weeks ago, before they jumped through a portal into the astral plane "to assemble an artifact that they still kept hidden in a box."

In fact they are fighting a Hezrou demon. The beast injures Hyramm, finds itself surrounded, and dies before it can summon more demonic help. The group leaves the lure boat where it is and continues through the water node to its southwestern corner. Honiel, enjoying her freedom to explore and dive, notices a cavern; the group suggests she explore it. What is there is an aboleth. The party tries to outswim it, fails, and makes a stand on a rocky outcropping. "It almost turned into a last stand."

The aboleth enslaves Honiel first, then screams in their heads, demanding the Orb of death and saying that Hedrack promised to let him out if he found it. Having enslaved Honiel, it knows that Furnoc has the Orb and tries to make him another thrall. The chapter says "The power of the aboleth was no match for Furnoc’s free spirit." then asks, "Or perhaps St. Cuthbert intervened to save him?" Using what it learned from the enslaved Honiel, the aboleth kills Hyramm — "it seems nobody bothered to heal Hyramm after the battle with the Hezrou demon." It hits the rest of the party, and again St. Cuthbert intervenes to keep his champions from contracting a horrible disease — "Or at least that will be the story Terjon, the priest of St. Cuthbert who lives in Hommlet, will tell." The party learns that the aboleth can suck life energy from its slaves and command its thralls to heal it. The battle looks very grim, and yet the group defeats it.

Freed by the aboleth's death, Honiel's first act is to scream: "Morons! You should have negotiated with the monster, not killed it." An aboleth is a trove of ancient and mysterious knowledge, and the party's best hope of learning the Orb's powers is lost; the Genasi know this, and the aboleth made "a minor tactical error in enslaving her." Furnoc and Chance are flabbergasted — they had just saved her, and she is pissed. Then Honiel asks where Hyramm is. The party remembers he is at the bottom of the water; Honiel fetches the unconscious, nearly dead Hyramm from the deep, and he is quickly healed.

Healed, the group swims cautiously into the aboleth's lair and finds a Rod of the Pact Keeper — "A handy magic item for a warlock, an article that would prove very handy, very shortly." They rest and head into the blue sea, where five chuuls attack. Chuul pincers grapple and a poisonous tentacle paralyzes, and Chance and Honiel are quickly grappled, poisoned and removed from the fight, leaving Furnoc, Berc and Hyramm. Furnoc wonders if the group's story will end here, killed by "The enraged former thralls of the aboleth." "But little did they know what kind of killing machine Hyramm was." With Berc mostly ineffective in the water and Furnoc "peculiarly unable to hit," Hyramm the warlock comes to the rescue. The party survives a final furious assault on a rocky outcropping from the last three chuul: the monsters cannot grapple Furnoc, who escapes, nor Berc, while Hyramm, swimming from the side, blasts them. The Rod of the Pact Keeper gives Hyramm the last spell that saves the group. The chapter closes with Chance, "still unconscious on the rocky outcropping," and Honiel, who had sunk to the bottom, recovering from their paralysis "only to see a smug, proud Warlock looking at the bloody water surrounding the rock they were standing on."
