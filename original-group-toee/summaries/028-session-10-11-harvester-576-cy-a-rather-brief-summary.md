# Chapter 28 - 10–11 Harvester 576 CY A rather brief summary

Date: 10–11 Harvester 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
The party takes a long rest outside, then heads south, where they discover Dourakine trapped in ice and under attack by Ravens. They free Dourakine, "whose background is very mysterious."

Just as they free him, a Demon appears and kills Hyramm, announcing that he is the protector of Node and will kill the spell caster who dared to mess with the weather patterns: "My job is to protect the node from meddling magic users who disrupted the ecology we suffered so hard to put together. And now you will pay for your insolence." Hyramm says, "Fake weather, so #SAD!" The demon is unamused, gets a critical hit, and kills Hyramm, thoroughly. Hyramm's last thought: "Shouldn’t have cast that control weather spell." Dourakine, watching, starts to wonder whether he will be rescued at all, and whether staying in the ice is the safer bet. The Ravens who see Hyramm dead say: "Hyramm is Nevermore."

Honiel wonders whether to revivify Hyramm, but realises he is now "a marked spell caster." Instead she shoves him into the ice to preserve his body for the exit out of this node.

Heading south again, the group finds a ledge full of frozen people. Going up the ridge they see a Yeti, run down the shelf, and fall down; the Yeti gets ready to attack, but the party kills it first. Exploring the dead, frozen victims of "the Yeti’s frost breath," they find no meaningful treasure.

The party continues to the cloud giant's lair and enters a hidden cavern. While they explore it, they hear the cloud giant Burium: "Fie fie whatever, something’s there, guards go find out what’s there. The frost giant Gwalihur is trying to steal my treasure!!!"
