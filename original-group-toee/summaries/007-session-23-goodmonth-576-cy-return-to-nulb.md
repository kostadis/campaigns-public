# Chapter 07 - 23 Goodmonth 576 CY Return to Nulb

Date: 23 Goodmonth 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
"After exacting their vengeance on Kelno and their betrayal of Oohlgrist, the Party returned to Nulb." After a night in their home, they wake to Grud, "Tolub’s right-hand man", knocking at the door. Grud says "in a somewhat irritated voice" that his Lord Mayorship wants to meet with the Party at once, and since there is nothing else to do, they go to the Lord Mayor’s new seat of power, The Boatmens’ Tavern And Nulb Market, near the docks (location 4).

Tolub asks them for "the excellent news that The Greater Temple is defeated." The Party is confused: "They just crushed the Fire, Water, and Earth Temples, what is this Greater Temple they speak." Tolub is in a state of panic. As they learn, "the lesser Temples were just for show": no real power and no actual operations, "more like a fraternity in an army than an army." The Greater Temple holds real power, and now that the lesser Temples are defeated, it "will start to wonder what is going on at their vassal town of Nulb." Grud, who had warned Tolub of these dangers, looks on.

The Party takes Tolub's problems under advisement and heads to Walter to buy a lot of goods. Walter has been bringing in many things from his suppliers, "as Otis, and Burne and Rufus are determined to get their armies ready for battle." With their magic purchased and equipped, the Party heads out to the dungeon.

Their first stop is Room 226, where some bugbears are "offered a Chance to surrender now that Kelno is dead." The bugbears demand money. "Berc uses burning hands as a way to extend the hand of friendship." One bugbear flanks Honiel, and "she swings and misses and thinks perhaps the money idea wasn’t such a bad idea." Meanwhile, Goblins dart in, attack, and dart out. The Party dispatches the bugbears very quickly. A lone Goblin offers to help the Party; the Party decides the Goblin has nothing to offer, and so the Goblin is killed — "With a critical."

Next is Room 213, "where they last fought the leader of the Water Temple, Canon Belsornig". They find the destroyed and despoiled Water Temple, and "Belsornig’s decapitated head bobbing in this altar". Chance can’t resist: "Yo, Dude!" In room 214, a mess now, Kelno "didn’t bother or forgot to explore the hallway to the south, where much treasure was found."

In room 216, "Honiel jumps in to save the triton." The rest of the Party is stunned. "It turns out Honiel’s heroism is not rewarded. She dived into an evil elemental force." Honiel is almost crushed, and "only loses her armor and a magical shield." When the monster dies, the roof collapses, and a poem is found among the shards.

Honiel immediately recognizes the poem as "referring to Prince Thrommel and a jape at the powers of good." The whelp of Mitrik is a reference to Thrommel; Mitrik is the capital of Veluna. The Answerer is Thrommel’s sword Fragarach, "the original Sword of Answering", "A weapon that would always strike anyone who struck Thrommel." The poet was mocking the weapon’s inability to protect him.

The poem matters. "Thrommel is alive. And perhaps in this Temple." If he could be found, "much could change in the world, for Good", and the rewards would be great for his rescuers. But if he is alive, the Viscount’s inability to find him or even get any clues to his location is unusual — and more interesting still, "the Temple not only isn’t dead, but it’s also alive", and maybe holding a mighty leader of the forces of good prisoner. "A leader, the Viscount, should have been moving heaven and earth to find…"
