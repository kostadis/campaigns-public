# Chapter 31 - 12–14 Harvester 576 CY Food! Food! Food!

Date: 12–14 Harvester 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
The chapter opens with the party facing a set of vrocks that had stunned and poisoned several of its members. The party killed one of the vrocks and sent it "to the hell dimension it came from" — just in time for one of the other vrocks to bring it back from the hell dimension. "Which was fine." Except that Furnoc found himself trapped between two vrocks. He told Dourakine not to worry and cast fireball, which "did a limited amount of damage."

Meanwhile "the destroyer of evil, the brash brother of Hyramm, was not doing his usual mayhem." It was disappointing. He claimed it was the fire-resistance of the demons; the party noticed it was "his poorly placed sword attacks." The vrocks "did not do a limited amount of damage on Furnoc," landing him on death's door. The party "was able to overcome the loss of the good rogue and defeated the rest of the demons."

With that, the party headed to the last part of the air node they had not yet explored. At the entrance the goblins saw them and asked, "Did you find the escape passageway?" Furnoc answered with a question of his own: "Did you find the gem?" Both sides stared at each other sullenly, and the group continued on its way.

On entering the room, they were hit by a stinking cloud and "a bunch of ildriss that appeared out of nowhere." Strategic use of gust of wind eliminated the stinking cloud, and a fireball killed all the monsters in the room — "Those that were visible, and those that were not." A few ildriss tried to use their invisibility to materialize from behind, but they were slaughtered quickly. With the guardians of the gem of power dead, the rogues found "the third gem of power, a large aquamarine gem."

The party then went back to the goblins. The goblin guards were delighted: "You have come back, you have come back! Trade? You want a deal?" Furnoc, casually: "Yes, what do you have?" The guards, excitedly: "Potions!" Furnoc, showing interest but not too much: "Okay, what does it cost?" The guards, delighted at the possibility of a trade: "Food!" Furnoc, relieved that he would not have to part with treasure, reassured them a trade could be made.

Before any trade could proceed, the goblin king arrived and declared there would be no trade until a quest was completed. Honiel smirked and asked, "What is your quest?" The goblin king puffed up his chest and said in a grandiose voice, "Kill the frost giant!" Furnoc, realizing they had already killed the frost giant, said excitedly, "Did it!" The king was beyond delighted. The Frost Giant had taken over the fire gate and broken the goblin trade routes through the nodes. He offered the treasure for completing the quest, which included 20 rations of food.

Reflecting on the treasure, Furnoc asked, "Do you have potions of gaseous form?" The goblin king was willing to trade that potion for food, to which Furnoc replied, "We have 28 days of food…" "The green king was not fooled." He pointed to Honiel and demanded she create food and water: for each incantation, the party would get one potion. Honiel sighed and cast create food and water. On seeing the magical food, the goblins shouted in delight: "No more mushroom food!!!! YAY! Tasty food!"

Berc, confused over their delight, tasted the goblin-supplied food. It tasted horrible. "No wonder they want the food." After devouring the food, the goblins rolled on the ground "contented," and the goblin ruler was so happily fed that it begged, "You stay another day? PLEASE! PLEASE!" A day passed, the spell was cast, the goblin bellies were happy again, and they pleaded, "One more day, please one more day?" The party offered one more day in exchange for information about the fire node.

The goblins had much to offer. The fire node is "a dungeon hewn out of the rock," holding "a dragon and an Efreeti." Most importantly, the dungeon is heated: in the past, goblins had died of heat exhaustion if they were not careful. Honiel, "who is really not interested in being stuck in a fire node, being native to water and all," heard about the Efreeti and asked, "Will it grant wishes?" The goblin guards responded sadly, "Not to a goblin."

The party then worked out a strategy to survive. First they looked for a gem of power that would send them back to the air node as an escape mechanism, and realized "that the missing gem is the air node gem." Then they decided to use their fire resistance rings, "except they only have four." After some debate, the plan was for everyone but Honiel to use a ring of fire resistance, and "to use the gems to escape if things get too hairy."
