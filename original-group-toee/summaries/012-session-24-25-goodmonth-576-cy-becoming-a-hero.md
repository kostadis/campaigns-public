# Chapter 12 - 24–25 Goodmonth 576 CY Becoming a Hero.

Date: 24–25 Goodmonth 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
The chapter opens with a recap: the Party "had defeated a local giant king, survived some traps, and fought a group of monsters hidden in a faux tableau." "Injured, bereft of spells, and just plain sleepy," the group decided to head back to Nulb. Arriving late at night at their home, they "crashed in their adventure pad."

In the morning Chance reminded the group that the magic items they had purchased from Walter (5) were finally available. Walter, "who had converted to Beory's neutrality," was really into the whole "balancing of the scale" view of the universe: he provided a product, and the scale was balanced with the team's gold — "such symmetry, such beauty."

Once armed, the adventurers left "The House of Goods" and were preparing to get to the Temple when Lundbeams intercepted them. The new sheriff was concerned that the Lord Mayor had aligned with some local brigands who had set up shop in the Temple, and that their chief Falrinth and Smigmal were pleading with Tolub to help them find whoever had started a fire in their chambers. When the Party asked how the brigands had managed to use the Temple as a base of operations, she mentioned "offhand that Falrinth had something that The Greater Temple needed, some magical device apparently." Possessing it "made him untouchable." Lundbeams described the brigands as occupying the northwest tower.

The Party's motives split along familiar lines. Furnoc, "whose morality was more aligned with doing the right thing," saw Lundbeams' point. Chance, "whose virtue was a little more mercantile," saw the chance at gold and treasure. The rest agreed it would be "an excellent way to increase their gold pile."

Just before they left for the Temple, Tolub's new second in command, William, came by: the boss wanted to meet the group about "some official town business." Furnoc immediately guessed that Tolub was going to ask them to back off. At the "Boatmens' Tavern and Nulb Market (4)," where Tolub kept his court, he offered drinks to everyone; when the barman looked at him, Tolub raised two fingers, which Furnoc, "who had spent some time in the area," knew meant "increase the prices by only 20%." Tolub gave Furnoc — who had killed Grud and saved Tolub's skin in the "Battle of Nulb" "just three days ago" — a warm embrace, and then, predictably, asked the Party not to bug Falrinth and Smigmal. They had come to him for protection, and as Lord Mayor it was his job to protect them.

Then Furnoc, "out-of-nowhere," told the new Mayor "to become the Mayor in action, not just in the title." He reminded Tolub that he was no longer "some random smuggler, but the Lord Mayor," whose first loyalty was to Nulb, "not protecting some crooks that happened to be hiding in the Temple." Tolub was taken aback and, surprisingly, agreed: he was a Pirate King and Lord Mayor, "And neither of those titles covered protecting brigands." It also made him look good — "And well, there is the small matter of getting rid of the local competition." As the Party left, Tolub told the bartender: "Next time my friend Furnoc comes here, the drinks are free."

On the way to the Temple, the team realised that the only fire they had started was in the chamber with the phantasmal basilisk (room 335).. Chance proposed beer. "The plan was simple, leave some beer in casks where the brigands can find them," and while the brigands drank, go down to the 3rd level and kill Falrinth. "And it worked." The brigands decided "that drinking some free booze was better than sitting around doing nothing," and with the brigands drunk, the Party headed down.

The approach was to use a detect magic spell to learn what spells had been cast, a dispel magic to remove the spell traps, and then to send the rogues stealthily into the chamber. "And it would have worked if the Unseen Servants had not started another fire when they dumped oil on the dais." An invisible Falrinth and Smigmal then tried to kill the Party. "Unfortunately for the bad guys, Smigmal missed." Falrinth "forgot to prepare his dimension door and pre-cast his magic mirror." And the brigands meant to come down and help "were too drunk to listen to desperate appeals for help." Smigmal used a ring of spell storing to charm Chance, but Honiel used her last dispel magic to free Chance. After killing Falrinth, the Party held Smigmal and killed her.

With the bad guys dead, they moved south of the dais into room 337, "apparently the chamber of a magic-user": a thaumaturgic triangle at the threshold, shelves of stuffed and mummified animals, a cloak with strange runes by the east door, a bed, a writing table "with an oddly branched candle holder," a parchment with runes on the east wall, and on the south wall what "seems to be a map of the dungeon level." The chapter ends as Honiel, who "still had her detect magic going," notices "that the map was radiating magic."
