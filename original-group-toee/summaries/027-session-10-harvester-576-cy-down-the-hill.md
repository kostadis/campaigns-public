# Chapter 27 - 10 Harvester 576 CY Down the hill.

Date: 10 Harvester 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
The party arrives in the Air Node "with a big massive bang" on top of a 250-foot hill, in a valley surrounded by 1000-foot ice-covered walls. The valley is part of a rock circling the Air Node; its floor is fogged, visibility is limited, and gaps in the sheer walls suggest caverns or tunnels. Above the mountain the Astral Sky shows "a weird night," and the wind howls hard enough to make it clear that some kind of cover will be needed.

Walking down, Chance, Furnoc and Honiel slip and slide down the slope, taking damage; Berc and Hyramm try to arrest their fall and fail. Once everyone has stopped, recovered their bearings, and is covered in snow and starting to get cold, Hyramm casts Control Weather, halting the winds and dispersing the fog on the ground.

Before the fog can be dispersed, a gang at the base of the mountain, led by Grunk, "a ninth level cleric," attacks. Their plan, apparently, is to hold the party with hold person and kill them with arrows. "Chance discovered that being held, and being attacked with arrows and sacred flame, hurts." From the party's perspective the plan does not work; Chance, "who held onto death’s door – of course – disagreed with that rosy assessment."

The battle hinges on Hunger of Hadar. Hyramm uses it to trap the enemy; Berc is caught in the void too, but that is part of the plan — it keeps the pressure on the cleric, who is also trapped. Furnoc and Berc together kill the cleric, though not before he uses dispel magic and forces Hyramm to cast the spell again. Two bad guys escape the void. The first sees Hyramm and uses his arrows to break "the sorcerer’s concentration," and succeeds: the control weather is dispelled. The second tries the same thing and is disappointed to learn "that Hunger of Hadar is not a spell that requires concentration." The other bandit screams at him: "I tried that too, it doesn’t work." One bad guy escapes into the now swirling fog, and another escapes by hiding in the shadows from Furnoc. One last foe remains and surrenders.

He is Baki, average height, stocky, greying brown hair, brown eyes, a previously broken nose, looking "tired, exhausted, and terrified but still trying to make any kind of angle work for him." He volunteers that Grunk was told by "some high ranking muckety-muck from the Temple of Elemental Evil" that if they got The Orb of Golden Death back, they would be freed from the nodes. Furnoc's questioning turns into comedy. Asked whether they have the Orb, Baki says, "We were told you would have the Orb?" Furnoc laughs: "Oh yeah, that’s right. We have it. So how long have you been here?" "We’ve been here for many years." Furnoc, flabbergasted, points out that this was their one shot after years of preparation, and Baki takes offence: "No, no, we’ve been stuck in the air nodes and had a sweet, sweet deal with the giants."

The deal: Grunk had an arrangement with a Frost Giant, who protected the gang from the scary monsters there in exchange for ambushing anyone who showed up and splitting the goods. Chance asks where the Frost Giant lives. Baki's directions are south to a cave opening, south again, and south once more to "Gwalihur’s home," with a warning: "there is this other cloud giant Burium and the whole air node is caught in this civil war between the two giants." Furnoc asks whether Gwalihur has any unique gems for the Orb, and Baki, confused, answers: "Well, he’s got lots of gems and many unique gems, and if I say yes, will you like me more?" The party realises Baki's useful information has run out.

The first order of business is to take the warm clothes off the dead bandits and find shelter in the valley; the party's magic and strength are too depleted to fight anyone else. "Ironically, Hyramm’s loss of concentration and the dispelling of the spell made it possible to hide in the valley…"
