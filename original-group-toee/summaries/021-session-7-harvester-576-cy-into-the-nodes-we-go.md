# Chapter 21 - 7 Harvester 576 CY Into the nodes, we go

Date: 7 Harvester 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
The party jumps through the portal and emerges in a cavern full of fine dust. Visibility is cut to 60 feet and the sound is badly distorted. They move through several caverns until they reach a dusty, dank, depressing dark room where a skeleton hangs from a moldy noose with a note pinned to it. Furnoc, "of course," decides to explore, and is trapped in quicksand that he quickly escapes. He gets the note, which reads: "Romag betrayed me forever. I know I rejoin the dirt and through that will get my revenge."

While the party debates how to explore the quicksand, several black puddings pour into the room and are quickly defeated. Honiel then goes through the quicksand herself. As she goes through, her center of gravity shifts — "what is up becomes down" — and she emerges on the surface of a ball. Around her she sees three other balls, one of water, one of air and one of fire, connected by golden transparent tubes, with green tubes that "seem to come from far far away." Staring further out, she realizes she is in the astral plane. Her report shocks the party: everyone knows the elemental planes are connected to the ethereal planes, so this means that Zuggtmoy "has somehow built four pseudo elemental planes in the astral plane."

The party heads south into a damp, foul-smelling cave with thinner dust, covered in fungus and lichens, and discovers that some of the rocks are shriekers. As they sit wondering what to do, a gorgon enters. Wondering if this is "the END of the line," they use the wand of wonder, and the gorgon is petrified. "Yup, THE GORGON is petrified. The cosmic irony is not lost on anyone."

Sargen, "the evil mage who was philosophically misaligned with the Temple," then appears, raving mad and convinced the party are demons. He offers anything to be free. From his insane babbling the party learns that Romag is still in charge, and that "somehow Romag didn’t escape from his former bosses." Honiel offers him a path of escape — the quicksand pit. Sargen goes through it and jumps into the astral plane; Honiel finds his floating body and hears his screams.

The group enters the sizeable southern cavern the gorgon came out of, with several pathways through a cavern fetid with fungal life, "highly reminiscent of the fungus in the 3rd level," and a shrine to Zuggtmoy in the east. They head on into another grotto. At a fork in the cavern they see a few dead bugbears and six oversized porcupine-looking creatures tearing up the dirt. When the monsters see the party, they charge.
