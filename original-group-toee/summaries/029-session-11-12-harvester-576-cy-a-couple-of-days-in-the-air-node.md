# Chapter 29 - 11–12 Harvester 576 CY A couple of days in the air node.

Date: 11–12 Harvester 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
The party enters the Cloud Giant's antechamber. A pack of bugbears surprises them but misses on all but one attack. Furnoc disengages, attacks one of the monsters and almost kills him, and the others, seeing one of their group severely wounded, ask for a parley. As they lead the party to the cloud giant, the Bugbears ask whether the party would act as if they had been led to the big boss, "instead of the monsters surrendering…"

The group agrees and meets the Cloud Giant, deciding to let its good members negotiate; Furnoc in particular is convinced that all Cloud Giants are good. Chance defers to Furnoc to begin. Furnoc adopts his Good voice: "We seek the gems of the death skull to escape the nodes and defeat the Temple." Burium asks, "You wish to escape the nodes. But why do you desire to defeat the Temple?" Furnoc: "Why would you not want to defeat the Temple?" Burium: "Because they promised me riches and power!" Furnoc thinks, "you’re not a good cloud giant, are you…" Burium goes on: "I was promised riches and power. And it was Prince Thrommel’s armies that defeated us at Emridy Meadows, and I got trapped in here…"

Chance steps in to negotiate, and the party quickly pivots to offering to work with the giant. Burium agrees, provided they take the head of the Frost Giant Gwalihur and bring it to him. Asked whether he will join them, he says there is no "Giant to Giant combat." Asked whether the Bugbears will come, the Bugbears look panicked, and Burium says, "They are my personal guard, of course not."

The party enters the chamber of the frost giant, whose allies are now dead, and kills him in one round. Walking back, they hear white dragons above them, flee back into "the frost giant cave," and miss the breath weapon attack. They throw "the body of the frost giant" outside the cave, and the white dragons eat "their feast of giant food" and flap away.

With the frost giant's head, the party returns to Burium and asks about the power gem. The cloud giant is clueless, but says Ashrem might know. Ashrem was the former head of the air Temple and the brother of Alrrem, leader of the Fire Temple. According to the chapter, Kelno made a deal with Alrrem "to get rid of his brother Ashrem so that Kelno could take over," after which Kelno found himself under Ashrem's thumb until Belsornig, the Water Temple ruler, got the upper hand. The party recalls that Kelno was the cleric whose deal let them defeat the lesser Temples in one swoop: "Kelno crushed a weakened Belsornig, while the party killed the fire Temple, and then the party killed a weakened Kelno."

Now they face Ashrem, hiding with a bunch of Yetis. Dourakine casts fireball. Furnoc is amazed — "The party could now cast Fireball on demand." "Well, except for the part where Dourakine got paralyzed by the Yeti’s stare."
