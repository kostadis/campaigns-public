# Chapter 19 - 27 Goodmonth 576 CY Rock Throwing Ettins

Date: 27 Goodmonth 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
The trolls the party had been facing "soon died. Then the exploration began." First came the trolls' own room, Room 409, with some beds and a chest and "a huge painted mural depicting a great battle, apparently between the forces of Good and Evil", in which shining humans and winged humanoids are blasted down by blue bolts from the fingers of "a tall horned humanoid in wizard's garb."

Behind the painting Honiel found a concealed door, and at the end of the passage beyond, a secret door into the planning room, Room 416. Its ornate wall hangings and twin fireplaces showed it was used "by those at the highest levels of command": a magnificent oak table with a dozen plush chairs, "both normal and giant-sized", books on strategy and tactics, parchment covered in unintelligible scribbles, three quills and two inkwells in a jeweled silver stand, maps of the dungeon levels, nearby towns and countryside on the north wall, four costly tapestries, twin crystal chandeliers, and a brass candle-rod by the southwest fireplace.

From there the group went to a Private Room, 417. Furnoc and Chance sneaked in ahead and found a room "full of untold riches" occupied by "a young lady lounging on the bed." It was Hedrack's abode — "Though only 20 by 30 feet in size, Hedrack's abode is impressive" — thick layered rugs, tapestries of geometric designs including "the eight-pointed fire symbol", brass braziers and a fireplace, a painting over the mantel of the united forces of The Greater Temple "with their human leaders before them (Hedrack clearly visible in the center position)", bookshelves, a desk, a wooden bed with gargoyle-head posts and silvery furs, a wardrobe, and "an ornate unholy symbol" on the wall.

"At first, Furnoc can't decide what to do. Kill her? That's the safe thing to do". The narrator observes, "If Chance has all the luck, Furnoc has none of it." Furnoc and Chance began to argue — "we should kill her, no we shouldn't" — and then Honiel marched in to find out what was going on, "and the lady screams." She screamed that "Hedrack" would kill them all and that they would rue the day they entered his chambers. When the chaos subsided, she became "suddenly very willing to offer what limited information she can provide about the dungeon": Hedrack had taken a position in The Greater Temple, "and there the trap is set for the group. The central Temple is impregnable." Pressed for tactical details, she reminded them of her role: "pretty face that sleeps in the bed of the commander." She did tell them one useful thing — where the key to the desk was.

The desk held parchments and record-books: the numbers and types of creatures "recruited by all the dungeon's Temples in the last year", troop pay records, disciplinary notes, and one small volume sorting humans and humanoids into four sections labeled A, E, F, W — "a record of sacrifices sent to the nodes". Among those prominently mentioned were "Ashrem, Jacer, and Sargen." The desk also held notes between the Viscount of Verbobonc and Hedrack: "Apparently, the Viscount and Hedrack have been working together for some time." The last record mentioned the party, with Hedrack demanding that the Viscount "actually do something to rein them in", and the Viscount answering that it was Hedrack's own demand that no troops be deployed beyond Hommlet that "created the Nulb safe zone for the group." Both were frustrated by "the emergence of the new lord mayor and the complete destruction of all assets in Nulb." The Viscount could not move on Nulb without endangering alliances, and lacked the troops anyway "because, once again, Hedrack insisted he has a weak army."

The group left the Private room for a common room, Room 410, then the north storeroom, full of provisions. North of that was a secret room holding two prisoners: "a human named Alderaan, and an ogre named Tilde." Tilde told them that The Greater Temple "has portals that lead to prisons on the various elemental planes", and that prisoners went either to "the deadly jail, which is the actual elemental plane", or to "another smaller, safer prison." He thought it was every other hallway — "or is it the other way around?" After his tortured debate over the pretty lady, Furnoc helped Alderaan leave the fourth level, reminding himself "that he does all of this in the name of all that is good. And good sometimes is a reward in and of itself."

Furnoc and Chance then sneaked into a large room, Room 414, found 4 giants, and left. In another large room, Room 415, they found "three rock wielding Ettins." Chance achieved surprise and critically wounded one, "tilting the battle heavily in their favor." Facing one wounded Ettin and two fit ones, the group used the wand of wonder, "And the room turns green with grass." The Ettins "momentarily stretch their fingers on the grass" and then resumed their assault. At the very end an Ettin tried to flee, "almost killing poor Hyramm. But, the man with the flaming blade, his brother, saves the warlock!" The chapter closes: "Chance sighs as he thinks about the fireballs that could be cast."
