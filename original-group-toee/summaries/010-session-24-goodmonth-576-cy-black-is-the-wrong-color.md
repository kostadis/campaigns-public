# Chapter 10 - 24 Goodmonth 576 CY Black is the wrong color

Date: 24 Goodmonth 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
The chapter opens after "the victory at the battle of the Boatmens' Tavern" and "the installation of the new sheriff." With a new political order in Nulb, the Party judged that the Temple forces needed crushing even more urgently, and once again prepared to enter the Temple of Elemental Evil. Consulting their map, they chose to go in through the secret door they had found earlier, when they fought the troll guards.

Furnoc, "the brains of the Party," proposed a disguise: the Party should wear black, on the assumption that the faction ruling this level "was probably aligned with The Greater Temple." The bugbears in room 315 "seemed to bear that theory out." Furnoc and Chance convinced them that the group was "on official Greater Temple business," and the Party walked through 315 "without getting molested."

Then they reached room 314, a triangular chamber whose plastered walls are covered with paintings of eyes and the symbol of the Eye of Fire, the eyes all seeming to "stare in hatred, hunger, or fright." The room had seen rough use — piles of rubbish, gnawed bones, the marks of a large fire often burned in the middle of the floor — and held "a large brass gong, suspended from a hornwood frame," with two smoky cressets burning on the east and west walls.

As the Party stood there, a giant walked in. When he saw the black cloaks, he said that he would never go back to serving The Greater Temple. He moved to the gong and banged it, and "all of the monsters that lived in this part of the Temple decided to attack." The disguise had backfired: this was "another faction in the dungeon that had somehow managed to grab control of an area within the dungeon itself."

Hyramm used Hunger of Hadar on the bugbears in room 314, and "The Party was feeling pretty smug at that point. And then it got worse." Five trolls and two bugbear chieftains entered the room. The monsters were smug in turn, until the Party once again used Hunger of Hadar and this time "blocked the monsters from moving out of the negative energy zone." The giant "used all seven of his attacks," but the spell's effects made him attack at a disadvantage, and he "failed to hit Honiel once." Furnoc, caught between several bugbears and a troll, survived by using "his ability to avoid some attacks."

Honiel then used a flame strike spell on the trolls, who had begun to work out how to escape the trap the Party had sprung. After the flame strike the monsters weakened, and, trapped in Hyramm's spell, "the damage accumulated." Eventually they got out. The giant who had started the fight "decided that he didn't want to be a dead king" and fled the room, up to the second level, "and presumably out of the dungeon."

"Although the trolls were amazingly ineffective, and the giant a coward, the bugbears were able to kill Hyramm." But "the fortunes of war and fate ensured that his death was but a flesh wound." At last the Party killed the remaining monsters and secured the room. "The stench of body parts was not improved."
