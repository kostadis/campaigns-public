# Chapter 18 - 27 Goodmonth 576 CY To The Greater Temple We Go!

Date: 27 Goodmonth 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
The chapter opens by recalling where things stood: Furnoc "had managed to enrage the future king and hope for all good people with his greed, and almost started a fight with a Genasi cleric." After that high point, the party was in the Museum of the dungeon, with a lurker attacking.

Lurkers, it turns out, "have this uncanny ability to frighten their prey with their wail", and this one used it. "However, it did not save the Lurker." Because it could not hit the party, the fight was "a brief, albeit temporarily frightening battle."

With that, the group decided to descend into The Greater Temple — "the power-nexus of the Temple of Elemental Evil, the source of all that was dangerous and despairing in the area." The chapter lists what had come from it: "assassins, assaults on good folk, an attempted raid on the Village of Nulb, and the reestablishment of the moat-house." Facing so much power, the party "decided that there was no need to scout ahead, to see what evils lurked below, instead they just charged in."

Walking down the eastern stairs into the Temple, they were immediately attacked by a pair of trolls. "Berc's flame tongue, once again proved handy against the regenerating beasts." With the trolls dead, the party explored. The stairs emptied onto a large landing, from which a passage 20 feet wide and 30 feet tall sloped down to the north toward more stairs, an intersection and more corridor. The landing's walls bore "mosaic murals depicting a nauseating scene of frolicking demons, vile goops and molds, and giant humanoids", capering among their torn and tortured victims. The stair showed signs of frequent use: fresh and old footprints of giant humanoids, scraps of food and bits of bone. In one corner lay a small piece of torn parchment bearing a faded drawing, visible only when held to a light: "a crude depiction of a crowned skull", with four lines running out from the crown and a circle at the end of each.

They went down the hallway and west into the West Wing. "At first, the guys of questionable moral standing found themselves in a room with two trolls", but soon it was a room with 6 trolls, two coming in from the west and two from the south. Looking at "the grim battle scene of Trolls decked in Greater Temple black", the adventurers said "ATTACK." And they did — "summoning monsters, casting spells, and in general unleashing such mayhem that any guards and leaders in the Temple would be well aware of their arrival." They killed all but two of the trolls. The chapter leaves them there, "facing down the last of the Troll ambuscade".
