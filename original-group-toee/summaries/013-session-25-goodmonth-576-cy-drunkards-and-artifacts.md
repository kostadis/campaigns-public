# Chapter 13 - 25 Goodmonth 576 CY Drunkards and Artifacts

Date: 25 Goodmonth 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
The chapter opens just after the Party "had just defeated Falrinth and Smigmal, the two local brigand chiefs." Honiel had entered Falrinth's chambers when "her detect magic spell warned her of a trap on a map." The room looked as if it was Falrinth's. Honiel removed the trap using dispel magic and found that the map "contained detailed references to the occupants of parts of the 3rd level dungeon."

After reviewing the chart, Chance and Furnoc opened the small room to the east (337a) and found a chest. "Furnoc tried and failed to find a trap." A noxious poison began to affect them both until Honiel used her lesser restoration and cured them. They were "rightfully annoyed that they had almost died and had not found any magical treasure." Fortunately they noticed a secret door to the south (338), which led on to the actual chambers of Falrinth.

This 10' x 30' room was "undoubtedly where the magic-user inhabiting the room to the North actually studied his works": a workbench, shelves of bins and containers, kettles over a small fireplace, a brazier, furnace, and bellows, a high writing desk flanked by bookstands "each with an unopened tome," and at the far end a table and an open cabinet. On the table were "a green cup, an iron box, a scattering of gems, several potion bottles, and a leather case"; the cabinet held "three iron coffers, a crystal ball, a tall pointed black hat, a box of reddish wood, a long brass wand, five large books, and a small ceramic statuette."

A systematic investigation followed. Among the magical objects was a Vacuous Grimoire that "some members of the Party opened and suffered a loss of ability." The Party also discovered that Falrinth was a follower of Lolth: "In a Temple that Iuz and Zuggtmoy created," the group had found another reference to the Drow Queen, "the first being Lareth the Beautiful in the moat house."

The most intriguing find was in the iron box: "a golden half skull." It is "a gold sphere three inches in diameter shaped to resemble a human skull without a lower jaw." Atop its crown is "a raised diadem with depressions as if to accept four gems." Furnoc tried to fit the jewels in the room, but none matched.

With the magic and treasure plundered, Chance argued for "killing the rest of Falrinth's gang." The plan: Berc would come up from the tunnel connecting the tower to Falrinth's quarters, "posing as a very angry minion of the big boss," to put the bad guys in a dangerous position and perhaps disarm them before the attack. Furnoc and Chance would sneak around for surprise attacks, and Honiel and Hyramm would come in from the main door, blocking escape.

"The plan almost works." Berc roused the drunk guards and, despite their doubt, corralled them into the northwest corner around a table, while Chance climbed onto the stairwell unnoticed — "The drunkenness of the guards helps this plan a lot." When Berc tried to get them to disarm, they resisted and combat broke out. "Chance does a spectacular jump from the stairwell onto the table," killed one of the bandits, and used his disengage ability to escape retribution. Furnoc attacked, as did Berc, now "playing his actual role." Honiel and Hyramm joined the fray, Honiel using a javelin of lightning to burn several bandits. When Hyramm thought about using his Hunger of Hadar spell, Furnoc turned and shouted: "why bother?"

The guards died fast, until only two were left, named Faen and Yip. Both dropped their weapons. Faen, "a short, stocky man, with black hair and previously broken nose begs for mercy." Berc was tempted to just kill them. Yip — "a tall, lean, and compact man with copper-red hair, blue eyes, with a line drawing of a blue ship tattoo on his left arm" — said: "You're Tolub's men, right? Tolub needs extra muscle, and we're extra muscle. We don't owe anything to Falrinth or Smigmal."

Furnoc, "the Party's resident softie," looked at Faen and Yip, sighed, and stopped the rest of the adventurers. After some discussion — and since they were out of spells and were heading back to Nulb anyway — they agreed to take Faen and Yip back to Tolub "and let the Lord Mayor decide what to do."
