# Chapter 35 - 17 Harvester 576 CY Wraiths are undead are alive and dead

Date: 17 Harvester 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
Chance and Furnoc sneak into room 24 and discover that Gemmel's place is populated with four wraiths. The two rogues decide to attack Gemmel with surprise. Chance opens with 34 HP of damage and Furnoc follows with 35; Gemmel answers with 9 on Furnoc. Dourakine then drops a fireball for 37HP that covers the whole room, catching his own companions: Chance avoids the damage, Furnoc takes half, "sensing the attack at the last minute", and the wraiths dodge the blast and turn out to be resistant to flames anyway. A wraith flanks Furnoc for 19 HP of damage.

Chance kills Gemmel, who had been supporting the flank on Furnoc, and slides to the side of another surviving wraith. Furnoc does 33 to that wraith and repositions so Berc can flank it. Berc realizes his flame tongue is ineffective against these creatures, but rushes into the room anyway and kills the wraith Furnoc is flanking.

Honiel turns the two remaining undead. Dourakine follows with a lightning bolt for 27hp against one of the turned wraiths, "but guess what wraiths are also resistant to lightning." Chance does 35 HP to the injured wraith and sets up a flank for Berc, who kills it and moves to threaten the last one. Furnoc, meanwhile, decides to "flee bravely" to the entrance of the room, where the mercenary Jacer is guarding against intruders, and breaks out a bottle of wine to share with him. Jacer says that he never drinks on duty, but if the boss wants to drink, he will drink.

The last wraith flees toward the exit where Furnoc and Jacer stand and stops in front of Jacer. Jacer realizes the monster is turned and just smiles, since a turned monster will not attack unless it is attacked. Then Chance sees the stopped wraith and charges it for 26, and Jacer "turns pale as he realizes the monster can kill him if it gets a Chance." Furnoc, with 27 HP of damage, and Berc make sure it never gets that chance. Jacer drinks the bottle.

The group moves on to room 23, a well-appointed room with a strange centerpiece: a sword clutched in a gauntleted crawling claw, held on a small table protruding from a silver stand, with two suits of armor against the far wall. The suits start to move, and the hand grabs the sword and rushes the party. The armor attacks Chance twice and misses both times. Chance does 37 to the first helmeted horror and Furnoc kills it with 47, but the horror explodes and showers everyone near it with shrapnel from its armor.

Honiel sees a construct and rushes toward it to use her mace of power, exposing herself to an attack of opportunity. The horror hits her with a critical for 18 and then uses its hellish rebuke for 28 hp. Honiel's reaction lightning damage does nothing: the monster is immune to lightning. Berc kills the crawling horror and moves in to help Honiel. Dourakine "cracks his knuckles" and casts magic missile, and discovers the horror is immune to that too. Jacer snores. Chance does 17 hp to the remaining monster, Furnoc flanks it and hits twice for 35HP, Honiel lands the mace against the construct for 16 hp, and the monster explodes again.

Exploring the room, the party discovers the writings of Gemmel: "a lot of self-pity, loathing, lust for revenge, and the usual nuttiness a crazed evil man would write." From them the party learns his history. Gemmel was one of the leaders of The Greater Temple until he was deposed by Two-Face-Slime-Bucket-Excrement, who was in turn deposed by Hedrack. Gemmel, "surprisingly for a chaotic evil villain", had expected to be deposed and prepared for it: he had a working relationship with the Salamanders and used a portable hole to bring whatever he needed to survive in the fire node. His writings dwell on his plans for revenge on Two-Face-Slime-Bucket-Excrement. Hedrack, the current ruler, sent the deposed leader into the fire node knowing full well that Gemmel was waiting for him, and Gemmel was delighted. The joy he takes in describing torture and grisly deaths ("after all revivify is the sadist's dream spell") makes it clear that Gemmel was evil and very disturbed.
