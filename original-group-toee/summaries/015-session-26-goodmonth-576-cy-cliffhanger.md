# Chapter 15 - 26 Goodmonth 576 CY cliffhanger

Date: 26 Goodmonth 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
The Party had just defeated a pair of Umber Hulks. "Armed with the Falrinth's map, and still wondering where the prison of the Demoness was," they decided to deal with an Ettin.

The original plan was to sneak in from the side, through "door d," instead of charging into the room, but that door was locked from the inside. Rather than break it down, the group came in from the southern passage. The room was a "large 50' square chamber" with a ceiling at least 40 feet high, a cold fire pit hung with chains and a great iron pot, eye bolts with 10' chains along the walls, four locked iron doors, and in the southwest corner "A great heap of cloth, pillows, and skins" with a tun, barrel, and sack nearby.

"Unfortunately, Falrinth's map was marginally inaccurate. Turns out in addition to an Ettin, we had a great bear." The two rogues, Chance and Furnoc, decided to try to surprise the bear and kill it, "And they almost succeeded in the surprise round." They finished it quickly after that with the group's help. Before the two Rogues could finish off the Ettin in another flanking maneuver, "Berc decided to use the wand of wonder managing to engulf everyone but Hyramm in the blast." Furnoc "almost died from his burn injuries and reflected on Berc's other brilliant ideas." Still, after the Ettin was killed, "everyone agreed" that the wand "was a great tool to possess."

The noble adventurers then continued north, first through the "Hall of Black Feasting" (Room 321): a 40' square room floored in alternating red and black stone, its walls painted with "scenes of horrid feasts" in which "ghouls, ghasts, shadows, and vampires join Temple clerics and demons in an unspeakable repast," its long tables still holding dishes and vessels, all dusty and long deserted. After sifting through it, they went on to the "Hall of Armored Skeletons" (Room 322), where "Thirteen human skeletons stand at attention along each wall," each armored and holding a different polearm or missile weapon, beneath six pairs of crossed swords and six shields.

While the group made "a racket picking through weapons," several shadows and a shadow demon surprised the Party. "Turns out that shadows have a nasty ability to destroy your strength. Turns out Berc has gauntlets of ogre power. Turns out that your muscles begin to atrophy, but hey magic! Magic solves all the problems." While the team fought the shadows, "a shadow demon surprised the group. And missed. And then was killed."

With the shadows defeated, the Party went through the secret door to the east into a storage area lined with shelves of empty bottles, flasks, alembics, and retorts, with chalk, charcoal, turpentine, fuel oil, and candles on the floor — enough "to stock a small shop and supply a magic-user or two as well." "But of course, the Party doesn't have a magic user, they have Hyramm."

From the storeroom they went south (red line), reconnected with the room of the Ettin and Giant Bear, then took the other northern door and stopped at a fountain (308) "that had sulfurous water that was potable, as Berc discovered." Next came "The Dining room (orange line, room 327)," with a long wooden table, a large chandelier, and twelve chairs; finding "nothing of any interest," they went southwest (green line) to the kitchen (room 325), which "is still used occasionally" and holds enough food "to feed several score people for many days." "Obviously, the dining area was serviced from this room."

Finally the group headed North (purple line) and found "an opulent bed chamber (room 326)": thick carpets and cushions, and just beyond the entryway "the mummified body of an elf, face down" with a dagger hilt in its back, dressed in "rotting splendor" with jewels on earrings, necklace, bracelet, and two rings, its skeletal right hand clutching "a carved stone of some sort." A curio cabinet held mummified body parts and obscene carvings; the walls and tapestries showed scenes of decadence and depravity; a huge bed, couches, and a muskwood folding screen completed the room.

As the group examined the room, "a translucent elf-like character emerges from the shadows, a Banshee!, Honiel realizes too late." All but Berc and Furnoc were frightened of her horrible visage. "And so while the rest of the group runs, Berc and Furnoc advance."
