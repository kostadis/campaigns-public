# Chapter 37 - 18 Harvester 576 CY Facing Zuggtmoy

Date: 18 Harvester 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
The party jumps on the throne at the ground level, and it takes them down to the Hall of Dread: Zuggtmoy's prison and former throne room. The place is "horrible and disgusting, loathsome, and fearsome", its stonework "leprous and mottled", its columns writhing with intertwined fungi and its walls dripping with slimes. In a swirl of revolting colors squats "a huge sprawling purple throne", sculpted with human forms crying out in pain as fungi shoot up from their flesh, standing on a four-tiered dais. Passages and doors lead east and south; twin valves of bronze in the south wall carry bindings and silvery glowing runes; to the west, serpentine steps lead down into a high domed chamber of changing light. The double doors to the south have the permanent antipathy spell cast on them.

The party goes through the southwestern door into room 342, the room of Crowned Skulls, whose murals show humans, demihumans and humanoids being robed and given crowns of gold adorned with skulls, under a painted golden skull wearing a silvery crown set with gems of red, blue-green, crystal and orange-brown. The south wall shows scenes of elemental devastation, each with a chair beneath it. Chance stares at the water chair. Honiel sits on it and finds it "not entirely designed for Genasi proportions but still comfortable", and is somewhat disappointed that she is not suddenly imbued with power.

East lies room 343, the Hall of Merriment, its walls showing revelry and debauchery and its couches, tables and spilled dishes left in disorder "as if those once here left hurriedly." Chance declares there must be treasure here and finds none. In the northeastern room 341, the "Place of Earth and Golden Chains", a chamber of polished pink stone, golden chains and deep black earth, Chance uses his investigation ability to discover a glyph of warding at the entrance. Honiel attempts to dispel the ward and fails, and the group decides not to explore further.

In the easternmost room, room 339, the "Hall of Elemental Magic", four great symbols are set into a floor of polished black stone: a triangle of dull ecru stone to the north, a square of blue-green translucent stone to the east, a circle of translucent crystal to the south, and a mottled red and amber diamond to the west, each giving off its own light. Berc goes "Oh!!! Oh!!!" and explains: "I could have teleported here! That's what this place was." He had seen this room in his visions without realizing what it was, and the party could have come here directly from the nodes. Asked whether the symbols lead to the nodes, Berc confirms it: "Yeah, totally, you can go to the nodes from here. I mean, you wouldn't be able to escape because I have the Orb unless you did that astral plane thing that Furnoc suggested." Chance mumbles, "An escape route!" Furnoc asks Jacer to guard the room, and he happily agrees.

When Chance looks around for a node control system, Berc points at the Orb: "Dude! Node control system! I am the dude, now. I got the control system." The whole group rolls its eyes, and Berc is suddenly not happy: "You know what you need to start respecting me more. I am the man with the Orb." Furnoc, in a patronizing tone, reminds him that "The prince will give us a big reward only if we defeat the demoness and give up the Orb." Berc, pained, answers almost silently: "Yes." Then, grouchy at the idea of losing the Orb, he turns on the group: "You guys killed my brother." Chance is surprised — "We didn't kill your brother." — and Berc whines, "Right, you left him behind!" Chance again: "No, we put the body in the portable hole!" Seeing "the silent but not particularly bright Berc" suddenly more engaged, Furnoc suggests they move forward and face Zuggtmoy "before the artifact takes over Berc."

Room 344, the room of demons, holds murals of lesser demons paying homage to evil humans and a plain black armchair; Chance dismissively declares there is no loot. Further east, a frescoed room hangs six suits of black, fluted, spiked plate armor enameled with red, white, green and brown glyphs around a golden skull, each bearing a huge two-handed sword. Chance and Furnoc investigate stealthily and carefully, but "a combination of bad luck and Honiel's spellcasting" sets the suits moving: they are helmeted horrors. The battle is long but uneventful, except when Berc uses The Orb of Golden Death to cast flame-strike, and Furnoc, seeing Berc's glee at the Orb's power, becomes deeply concerned that Berc "is going to be an enemy combatant when this is all said and done."

During the short rest after the battle, Furnoc finds 24 topazes embedded in the armor, worth 12,000gp, and offers them all to Jacer. Jacer is delighted. Chance is stunned, and more stunned still when Furnoc reassures him that he can get it back. Berc is furious, "like an older child whose toys were given to a younger sibling": "Why did Jacer get it? What exactly did he do? And who made you in charge of giving our - my stuff away?" Furnoc shouts angrily, "Berc you are an asshole, fuck you, and I don't want to fight with you anymore. I don't want you in the party anymore," and mumbles mostly to himself, "We are not going to defeat Zuggtmoy because we will be fighting each other." Berc ignores the rant: "No, no dude, it's just my money. You don't give away my money." Furnoc: "We have 100k gold pieces, we will be okay." Berc: "I came from a poor family! I don't come from a rich family. Money matters to me." Chance stops the escalation: "Let's go west."

Before they go, the party notices frescoes of crowned humans, minor demons and slaves bearing rewards, parading before "a horribly bloated figure upon a throne-like seat" with a fungous body-sphere, a toadstool-like growth bearing a face, and four elephantine legs, seated beside "an obese male demonic figure". The party recognizes the image of Zuggtmoy and Iuz.

In room 346, a plain carpeted chamber, Honiel perceives a concealed door to the west, and behind it a hallway. Asked what to do, Furnoc says: "Whatever. We can't surprise Zuggtmoy. She knows Berc is coming." Chance tries to sneak to just behind the dais (location B), but a mysterious crone steps out of the shadows: "You, welcome! I have been waiting for you for a very loooooong time!" Chance steps back through the door very quickly. Furnoc, in a pissy mood, suggests Berc go in — "He's the one that is so sure of himself." — and Dourakine agrees: "Yeah!" Chance remains surprised that Zuggtmoy noticed him despite his feat of stealth.

The party starts to notice some facts: Zuggtmoy seems able to see anyone in her lair; even though she knows where Berc is with her artifact, she has not approached him; and she is only a few feet from an escape route, room 339, but has never used it. Chance concludes that she is trapped in her room, and that the room has a bunch of traps and she is trying to lure them. Furnoc wonders if she is an illusion. While they think, Zuggtmoy announces in a wailing voice, "I have a prize for you! A wonderful prize!" Furnoc is very dismissive. Berc: "Dudes, we should totally get the prize." Furnoc rolls his eyes: "Yeah, man, it's all you."
