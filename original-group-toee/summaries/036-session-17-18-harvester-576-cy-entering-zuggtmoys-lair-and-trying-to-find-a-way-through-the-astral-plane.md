# Chapter 36 - 17–18 Harvester 576 CY Entering Zuggtmoy’s lair and trying to find a way through the Astral Plane

Date: 17–18 Harvester 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
As the party contemplates its next move, Furnoc holds the portable hole and the bag of holding close together and asks whether anyone wants to go to the astral plane. Everyone looks at him in confusion, so he swings the bag precariously close to the hole and explains: "if we put the bag of holding in the portable hole, we could go to the astral plane." Everyone stares aghast. Chance, in a calm and soothing voice, suggests "we keep those two things far apart". Furnoc whines that he was not going to combine them, though they could if they wanted to, and Berc says, "We don't need to go to the astral plane that urgently." Furnoc thinks it would be a twist, something different, and "Better than being railroaded by fate and the gods to fight Zuggtmoy by assembling The Orb of Golden Death."

In room 21 the party finds a goblin, Grizzum, whom they recognize as a member of the goblin tribe they found on the air node. Chance greets him with "Yo, Dude!" Grizzum asks who they are and what they are doing there. Furnoc insists they are friends on the same team; Chance prefers "more like a business partner" of the king; Furnoc stops him mid-stride, and the two rogues agree that they gave the tribe food and finished a quest for them. Grizzum perks up at the mention of food and wonders why the tribe gets food while he is stuck here. He explains that he was part of a group of goblins who would come to the fire node, collect stuff, and return to the air node. Asked if he has a way back, he blurts out: "No, that's what the king is for." He has been holed up all this time and "Nobody is coming, NOBODY IS COMING!" When Furnoc tells him to chill, the goblin answers, "No, it's quite hot."

Furnoc looks for what Grizzum has to offer. Grizzum mines coal on the fire node, which the goblins on the air node use to make potions of gaseous form, and he agrees to trade 2 potions for his freedom. Along the way the party learns why Brazebul locked the air node portal: he did not like coal dust in his rooms. Hilarity ensues when Furnoc discovers that the goblin mines coal ("There is coal? C-O-A-L? You trade coal?") and cannot work out who gives Grizzum the coal, until Dourakine figures it out: "He's a miner." Grizzum points at Dourakine and says "YES!", then explains to Furnoc as if to a child that he sneaks into the fire node to mine coal and back to the air node "without getting noticed by the Drow, the Azers, the Salamanders, and the Efreeti." Furnoc finally puts it together: "Ah, so we get you to the air node, and then you get us a pair of gaseous potions, so we all have them."

The party heads through the portal to the goblins, carefully avoiding falling down the mountain or attracting unwanted dragons. The guard cries, "Grizzum, you are alive!" and Grizzum answers, "Not thanks to you…", and the goblins start arguing in goblin. Furnoc interrupts to remind everyone that what matters is "OUR potions of gaseous form", but the guard declares there are none "unless kings say so." The king sees Grizzum, Grizzum screams at him, and there is general goblin disagreement. Furnoc's "Hail King, we have saved your miner!" leaves the king wondering why that matters; Chance's news that they re-established the trade route gets a very positive reaction, which Furnoc immediately claims he was about to mention. When the king asks why he should give them anything now that the route is open, Furnoc observes nonchalantly that they have the key and could lock it up again, and that the king has not shown any gratitude. "Here's two!"

Chance points out that Jacer needs a potion too. Jacer says he is cool with anything, but if he is the only non-gaseous member of the party, all attacks would be directed at him, and that would make him "think hard about my employment choices". Chance tells him to go trade with the goblins himself, and Jacer is taken aback: they are supposed to take care of him, and he has been guarding the rear. Furnoc acknowledges his contribution and how much he enjoyed drinking with him, "But you need to work on your alcohol tolerance buddy. You're a lightweight." The goblins ask for food in exchange for the last potion of gaseous form, and Honiel casts create food and water, feeding the group.

The party heads to the water node for a long rest. Furnoc uses the night to attune to dragon scale mail; Honiel researches any way to make herself invulnerable to dragon breath and finds none. Back in the fire node, the coal mine turns out to be just coal.

The two rogues sneak into the room they think holds the dragon while Jacer stands far away. The room opens into the roaring fiery inferno of the fire node, and a red dragon sits on its hoard at the back "looking cranky." Furnoc and Chance search the hoard for the gem for two stealthy minutes and find nothing, and then the dragon notices Chance. Chance tries flattery: "Truly, the tales understated your power and greatness…" The dragon breathes on him, and between his ring of fire resistance and his rogue ability to avoid damage, Chance takes 14 HP — "The ultimate 'get off my lawn!'"

Honiel casts ice storm for 12HP, Furnoc attacks with advantage for 33 HP, and Berc dashes from the hallway into melee range. The dragon flies up into the opening of the room and breathes on Chance, Furnoc and Berc; Berc takes 28 HP. Chance, realizing the airborne dragon is immune to his sneak attack, pokes around the treasure instead, finds the gem and swoops it into a bag while the dragon screams at the thief. Dourakine reasons out loud that fireball would incinerate everyone, the dragon has complete immunity to fire, and he cannot use his fire bolts — "So… Lightning Bolt." The dragon turns into it and takes 36 HP: "That was unexpected."

The dragon roars and the chamber shakes, knocking Honiel and Berc prone. Jacer shouts, "We are all good here, nobody seems to be attacking," and Furnoc mutters that when the dragon chases them, it is coming for Jacer too. Honiel decides flame strike may not be the best choice and casts call lightning for 20 HP. Furnoc shoots his bow for 7. Berc levitates himself to the dragon's level ("Cool!" says Dourakine), and the dragon answers with claws and bite for 47 HP, leaving Berc at 10 HP; Berc holds his concentration and does not fall. Chance considers helping his party member, then uses his bow for 11. Dourakine settles on another lightning bolt, to a big thumbs up from Furnoc, while Berc secretly hopes it only almost kills the dragon so he can land the killing blow. It does not: the dragon crumples, and Dourakine raises his hand in triumph — "I killed the dragon! YOU CAN NOT TAKE THAT AWAY FROM ME!" Berc sighs.

In the hoard the group finds a letter from Prefect Alrrem trying to form an alliance with the dragon; how Alrrem meant to get the dragon's help is unclear. Chance and Furnoc agree, "Well, we got all of the gems …", and when Dourakine asks what the point of all these gems is, the party cracks up: he is the only person in the nodes who does not seem to know about the gems and the Orb.

The party faces a dilemma. Assembling the Orb would let them return to the prime material plane, but the moment they do, Iuz and Zuggtmoy will be notified of who holds it and where. Furnoc proposes finding a path through the astral plane. The problem: "in an infinite plane, with an infinite number of gates that manifest randomly, it would still take an infinite amount of time to find one gate!" Furnoc observes that they cannot die there, so they have an infinite amount of time. The party checks its spells and scrolls for another way back and finds none, and goes to Darley.

Darley points at the Orb as the way out. The astral plane, she says, is "a great way to stay alive and never die and be lost for eternity", and the way not to be lost is "Don't go to the astral plane…" — or to go with a magic user who can cast plane shift. When Furnoc insists there are portals, she explains the infinite-gates problem as "higher-level math" beyond their "puny brains", adding that "Further revelations will have to wait until your civilization hits the 1+1=2 stage." Chance takes it in: "So what you are saying is that there is a chance…" Darley smiles and thinks to herself that the Orb's creators arranged things so that assembling it would reveal your location and force you to the one place they could injure you — "It's almost as if it was an EVIL plan by an EVIL demon."

The party stares at the Orb. Chance looks at Furnoc and the Orb and says, "Furnoc?"; Furnoc looks at Dourakine and says, "Dourakine?" Berc says: "Orb of death? Looks cool. And invulnerability in the final battle with the demoness, sign me up." Honiel begins to prepare to dispel evil. At the water Temple, where the escaped prisoners live, Berc takes the Orb and the gems from Furnoc and, using gloves, assembles it. Honiel reminds the team about some good Ettins they should bring along, but they are gone; apparently the Deva rescued them. Berc attunes to the Orb while the party holds its breath: "Nope, I am not evil."

Berc perks up — "Aha! I know how to get out of this mess!" — and offers the ground level of the Temple or The Greater Temple. Furnoc and Chance agree on the ground level. Chance feels they should prepare; Berc disagrees: "I'm after all invulnerable! You guys, not so much, but whatever." Berc uses the Orb to teleport the whole group to the ground floor of the Temple of elemental evil. Then, in front of the throne, someone the chapter calls only "He" steps on the brown, red, white and green tiles in order, says Zuggtmoy, and asks, "Wanna come?" The group piles onto the throne and ends up in the interdicted prison of the demoness.
