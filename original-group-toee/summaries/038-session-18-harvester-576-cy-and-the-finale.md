# Chapter 38 - 18 Harvester 576 CY And the finale.

Date: 18 Harvester 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
The party has entered "the inner sanctum and now prison of Zuggtmoy", but instead of a bulbous, grotesque fungus creature they find an old crone calling to them. Realizing that stealth will not work in her lair, they carefully approach her throne. "She looked at them. And sighed. And smiled. Malevolently."

The room is split into an East Hall and a Great Hall. Polished steps descend into the East Hall, with black marble walls veined in green and purple, runes "with glyphs that seem to run and shift into other forms when not viewed directly", rainbow-flamed cressets, and a ceiling sheathed in gleaming silver. Three large pedestals stand there, each 12 feet tall and topped with a metal skull: "The south pillar is electrum, the center, gold, the northern, platinum." Beyond them the Great Hall broadens, with a magical device set in the floor before a dais of translucent alabaster, a hexagon of interlocking triangles and a ring that "seem to be made of molten electrum", its tips glowing red, orange, yellow, green, blue and indigo and its center pulsing "a pure purple light." On the dais stands a huge silver throne set with hundreds of gems forming demon faces, skulls and fungi, before a deep purple tapestry. The west end is shrouded in black mist. On the throne sits a small, fat old crone, almost lost in the huge seat, who cringes and shrieks, "You won't get my treasure!", fumbles with the arm of the chair, and booms out, "You may take my pillar of electrum. Begone!"

The old crone is, of course, Zuggtmoy. The party surrounds her and sees the pillars in the distance, and their greed almost overwhelms them, but "their greed can not overcome their fundamental distrust of the demoness." Except for one person: "That neutral man, that Every Man, that guardian of all that is good and just, Jacer." Furnoc had told Jacer to come into the room from where he was hiding, and so Jacer stood right next to the pillars. Zuggtmoy tells him: "You, take my pillars. Take that wealth! It's yours!" The party watches in horror — "What would The Every Man decide?" Furnoc pleads with him, and Jacer weighs the rogue's generosity and his ill-treatment at the hands of Zuggtmoy's minions, and shakes his head.

With that, Zuggtmoy has had enough. The crone fumbles with the arms of the throne again: an olive ray eats a small hole in the floor, a maroon one disintegrates some of the metal sheathing in the ceiling, a prismatic sphere of odd colors springs up, and green and red rays bounce around inside it in swirls of radiance and sparks. "Hideous shrieks come from the figure bathed in the rays—and the hapless crone is blasted, and turns into a fine gray ash".

The group wonders whether it is now safe to explore the room, except for Furnoc and Chance, "who have to look behind the curtain." Then the bulbous, angry Zuggtmoy explodes and decides to exact her revenge at last. She had been waiting for a decade for someone to come into her lair and free her, "and these buffoons were not them." Worse, her Orb was within reach and yet so far. The battle is swift and brutal. Chance is killed. Honiel, "who had avoided death for so long, managed to dodge it one last time." And it is done: Zuggtmoy is dead.

Furnoc turns to Jacer and gives him a signal, and Jacer moves up behind Berc. Furnoc asks Berc to give up the Orb; Berc says no. Furnoc says, "I'm done doing this the nice way," and attacks him. Just as the party is about to tear itself apart, "the old one entered the fray. Yup, Iuz." The death of Zuggtmoy had eliminated the barrier interdicting her prison, and the Orb called out to Iuz. Iuz uses magic to convince Berc to give up the Orb, and Berc starts to move toward him. The party is not going to let the monster get his hands on the Orb, and the easiest way to stop that is to stop Berc, so they surround him. Iuz is not going to let them: he kills Honiel with a death ray, and Furnoc is knocked unconscious.

"And just when all seemed lost, Iuz made his fatal mistake." When the Circle of 8 created the prison, they realized that whoever made it there would probably have the Orb; that if that person killed Zuggtmoy, Iuz would be able to come; and that he would overwhelm anyone who had the Orb. So they installed a failsafe: if Iuz came within 30 feet of the Orb, St. Cuthbert would show up. St. Cuthbert does show up, and raises and heals the party, and "Iuz and St. Cuthbert decided to take it outside." With that, the party has defeated the demoness — though it does beg the question of "why did the Circle of 8 not just kill Zuggtmoy?"
