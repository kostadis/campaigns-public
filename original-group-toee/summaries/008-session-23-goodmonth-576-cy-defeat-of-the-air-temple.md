# Chapter 08 - 23 Goodmonth 576 CY Defeat of the Air Temple

Date: 23 Goodmonth 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
"The Party confronted the werewolves in room 208. It’s unclear if the monsters got a hit." In the rubble of the room, the Party found plenty of treasure "from all the victims of the werewolf scam": a silver service (450gp), 18 sprigs of wolfsbane, a dagger +1, a short sword +1 and 5 silvered daggers. Under a flagstone, found with a DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) check, lay 1,629sp, 22 arrows, 19 silver-tipped arrows, a longbow, a short bow, 4 light crossbows, 82 crossbow bolts, 3 daggers, "1 holy symbol of St. Cuthbert, 2 holy symbols of Rao", a mace, a hand mirror, 2 spears and a longsword.

The Party then left 208 and headed into the Air Temple, where they fought and defeated its guardian, an Air Elemental. Unfortunately, after they defeated the guardian, the Party activated a failsafe. "It seems Chance had to look at the treasure. Had to." Furnoc, once again, "pressed his thumbs to his temples and asked: Why?" This time Chance paid for his carelessness: "The demon killed him. Fortunately, it was a temporary death."

With the demon dead and the Air Temple guardian defeated, Furnoc suggested they go down to the 3rd level; the rest of the monsters "were no match for the awesome power of the Party." The group agreed after they revived Chance. "After all, Furnoc was the smart one."

"The plan quickly ran into some troubles after the Party went to the 3rd level." Each of the side doors in the polygonal rooms opened into an identical room, and each room housed a troll. The trolls came out one at a time: "Initially, there was one, and as one died, the others came out to replace the dead monster." "The Party was fortunate that Berc did not miss."

After the trolls died, the Party explored the rooms around the hexagonal room. In each room they found a key, and in location 302 they found a secret passage. Only after the combat was over did the Party notice "that the Trolls wore the black cloaks of The Greater Temple."
