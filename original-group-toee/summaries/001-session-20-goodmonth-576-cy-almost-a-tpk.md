# Chapter 01 - 20 Goodmonth 576 CY Almost a TPK

Date: 20 Goodmonth 576 CY (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-09)

## Summary
The chapter announces itself in one line: "The best-laid plans go awry." The Party enters a huge hall, "40' x 90'", with an arched ceiling "some 30 feet overhead" lost in a tracery of shadowy dimness. Floor and walls are slabs of polished stone, "apparently azurite-malachite from the swirls of blue intermingled with the deep green", and "a soft cloudy greenish luminosity seeps from the walls and floor", so that the whole chamber looks "as if deep underwater." The bronze doors, the fountain and the other work are covered with verdigris, and "Even the damp air seems to smell of the sea."

Several weird things stand out. In the middle of the west wall is "a great sheet of bronze, a bas relief of an underwater vista", from which projects the head of a fish-like thing, "a most hideous visage", whose "ghastly maw emits a stream of water" into a tiered series of four basins; the fourth and largest never overflows. Near the middle of the hall stands a round bronze altar sculpted with marine creatures, its concave top a shallow basin of water whose bottom "is filled with coins, a sprinkling of gems, and many small seashells." In the mouth of a 20' wide alcove south of the altar stands an idol of serpentine stone, "a writhing, amorphous agglomeration of fish, eels, octopi, and other marine monsters", about eight feet high, and behind it the alcove's back wall is hung with "a drapery apparently fashioned from seaweed and water!" Each corner of the hall holds a triangular plinth topped by "a gargoyle-like statue made of bronze and coated with verdigris."

Furnoc, "the only intelligent member of the Party, it seems," wants to explore the room carefully, and works his way around the edge of the hall toward the bronze sheet and its fountain on the west wall. Berc "thwarts the careful, maneuvering, and pokes one of the gargoyle statues, who just happens to be a gargoyle."

Battle ensues. The gargoyles are quickly defeated. Then the idol in the alcove turns out to be a juggernaut and "enters the fray." The Party dispatches it too, although it is a monster that "had some scary attacks, including the ability to roll over adventurers and cause them crushing damage while hitting with pseudopods."

"And just as they are about to catch their breath, Canon Belsornig enters the room with his clerical acolytes." The Canon is protected at first by a mirror image spell. An attack that is supposed to hurt him doesn't, and missile attacks hit his mirror images. The acolytes are dispatched very quickly, "But the Canon turns to a blitzkrieg of hold person spells." Using 4th level variants, he almost paralyzes the entire Party: Honiel is paralyzed, and Chance is paralyzed. When an attack finally hits "his high armor class", the Party discovers that they have been fighting an illusion. "The Canon then enters the room with crazed blood lust and the scent of victory."

The arithmetic is grim. Of the members still standing, Berc has 8 hp left, Furnoc has 5, and Hyramm is still okay. No one has successfully hit the Canon yet, and he still has "plenty of hold person spells left to paralyze the entire Party. And then methodically kill everyone."

"Confronted with a potential TPK (Total Party Kill), Furnoc grabs Honiel and flees." Berc thinks about it for a moment, then grabs Chance, "muttering something about brotherhood and friendship." Hyramm, seeing everybody running, makes a break for the door as well. "The Canon, fortunately, decides not to pursue."
