# Chapter 39 Contracts, Plans, and Fungi

Date: Jan 19th, 2026 is source-document metadata; neither the session date nor an in-world date is verified.

## Summary

The party enters a binding contract with Maram il Vass to retrieve the Lexicon of Oaths from his rival Vraxen in Maerimydra. Maram supplies an hourglass marking the remaining time and suggests provoking conflict between Vraxen’s yugoloth administration and the demon-powered giants.

Felkur learns the significance of the Watcher's Eye Brooch. He picked it up from a table, accidentally triggered its antiscrying effect, and sold it for 40 silver to drink beer. The artifact allowed free passage into the Tower of Blades of Mulmaster. Its theft and rapid return caused conspiracy theories.

At Sporedome, Solom Ned'razak and Xaven Tanor'this research a ritual to remove demon taint. They need blood from a living fire giant infused with fiendish blood. The party agrees to help and discusses combining this mission with its effort to save Felkur.

The proposed route into Maerimydra passes beneath Black Ember Outpost through the Path of Shadows, a tunnel Solom built to the Shattered Tower. Xaven knows the entrance. The party discusses transporting a shrunken giant in a portable hole and escaping through a teleportation circle Felkur could recalibrate. The notes do not record a giant’s capture, completion of the ritual, or infiltration of Maerimydra.

### Felkur Discovers What He Stole

1. Felkur had regarded the Watcher's Eye Brooch as a random trinket. It was an artifact that allowed free passage into the Tower of Blades of Mulmaster.
2. Felkur picked it up from a table, accidentally triggered an antiscrying effect, and sold it for 40 silver to drink beer.
3. The brooch’s theft and rapid return caused conspiracy theories.
4. Maram tells Felkur that the Brotherhood of the Cloaks spent thousands of gold pieces on divination, searched the Astral Plane, and executed three High Inquisitors for failing to find him.
5. According to Maram, the searchers imagined a Level 20 Wizard or a chosen of Mask: a master infiltrator, a shadowy threat to the Moonsea, and the leader of an assumed army.
6. Maram contrasts those perceptions with Felkur’s conspicuous adventures, citing saving Hillsfar from Graz'zt and pursuing the Red Plumes. He attributes the failure to find Felkur to the accidentally triggered ward and Felkur’s apparent implausibility as a suspect. These are Maram’s explanations and claims, not independently established outcomes.

### Solom Explains the Situation

1. Solom approaches with determination, accompanied by a nervous Xaven who repeatedly wrings his hands.
2. Their proposed ritual would remove demon taint from fire giants’ blood, or potentially anyone’s blood. Completing it requires blood from a living fire giant with fiendish blood in its veins.
3. Solom provides a slightly outdated map of Maerimydra.
4. He describes a frontal assault as suicidal: Hledh, a powerful fiendish giantess, keeps the gates locked and heavily guarded.
5. Solom built a secret tunnel from the Shattered Tower to travel beyond the notice of the Lolth-worshipping matron mothers. Xaven worked with him there when Maerimydra was a drow city and knows the secret entrance.
6. Kurgoth Hellspawn and the fire giants demolished the Shattered Tower when they took the city over a century ago.
7. The tunnel exited into the Underdark four miles from Maerimydra. Fire giants subsequently built Black Ember Outpost over the exit without recognizing its significance.
8. The entrance lies in the southernmost portion of a large pool beneath the outpost. Solom hopes time or the giants have not blocked the passage.
