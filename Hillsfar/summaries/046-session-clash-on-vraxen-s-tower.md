# Chapter 46 Clash on Vraxen's Tower

Date: May 9th, 2026

## Summary
Atop the dizzying heights of Vraxen's Tower, the party resumed their assault against the cunning wizard Veris and his Stone Golem in round four. Veris had just teleported, revealing that Felkur's manipulation of the teleport system had locally weakened the Faerzress protections that should have prevented such movement. Daein repositioned without provoking the Golem and loosed two unsuccessful arrows at Veris. The wizard spent two legendary actions to conjure a 20-foot cube of sticky webbing, restraining combatants and turning the area into difficult terrain.

The Giant Owlbear tore into the Stone Golem, landing a damaging claw attack despite the webbing. Veris then deliberately struck his own construct with Firebolt, dealing only 6 damage while burning away its restraints. Freed to attack the still-restrained Daein with advantage, the Golem dealt a punishing 48 damage. Bramgrim's Spirit Guardians continued wearing down the construct, while Bramgrim cast Freedom of Movement on himself and restored 17 hit points to Daein with Mass Healing Word. Felkur's cannon burned away the remaining webs around Daein and the Owlbear, dealing 16 damage to each ally as the price of their freedom. Akritas finished the Stone Golem and transferred Hunter's Mark to Veris.

With his first plan defeated, Veris moved and conjured a Wall of Force around Felkur, declaring, “Now, Felkur, there is no escape.” A Shield Guardian arrived from the stairs, with more reinforcements close behind. The party increasingly focused on buying time for Felkur's ongoing work rather than winning every exchange. Bramgrim used a fifth-level Dispel Magic to remove Veris's barrier under the session's ruling, and Daein's prepared arrow struck the wizard for 8 damage and reduced his speed. The Owlbear knocked the Shield Guardian prone, while Felkur devised a diversion: send his cannon down the passageway toward Vraxen's library and detonate it among the archmage's possessions.

The party also spent one of Felkur's remaining tokens to produce a Scroll of Wall of Force, considering a protective dome around themselves rather than merely blocking the entrance. Obtaining the scroll used Felkur's action, so he moved the cannon down the passageway but did not detonate it. Veris retaliated with a critical Firebolt against Akritas, mitigated by Daein's Protective Field. Akritas then brought Veris down to 11 hit points with an arrow and finished him with a third-level Hail of Thorns. The DM revealed that Veris had been about to cast Fireball.

The Shield Guardian stood, missed Bramgrim with both fists, and moved aside to admit its allies, taking damage from Spirit Guardians. At the start of round six, a Mezzoloth reached the battlefield before Daein could cast the party's barrier. It took 29 damage from the spirits, then cast Cloudkill over the party. Successful saves reduced the poison damage from 35 to 17; the Owlbear took the full damage. Felkur passed the resulting concentration check, but Bramgrim's remained unresolved. The session paused with the poisonous cloud still active, the scroll uncast, the cannon undetonated, and the party reconsidering who should raise the barrier.

## Scenes
### The Battle Continues: Webs and Golems
#### The party continues their intense combat against Veris and a Stone Golem atop Vraxen's Tower, facing magical webs and teleportation anomalies.
- Combat resumes in round four, immediately after Veris's previous action.
- Daein considers killing Veris and imitating his voice to command the Golem, but the party proceeds with direct attacks.
- The party notes that Felkur's manipulation of the teleport system has weakened the tower's protections, allowing Veris to teleport unexpectedly — a problem flagged for later in the adventure. The DM specifies that the weakening of the Faerzress is localized.
- Felkur jokes about filing a support ticket over the unexpected teleportation.
- The Stone Golem remains standing and dangerous. Daein chooses a position that avoids an opportunity attack, then attempts to snipe Veris with a longbow but misses both shots.
- Veris spends two legendary actions to cast Web, filling a 20-foot cube with sticky, flammable strands that restrain several party members and make the area difficult terrain and lightly obscured.
- The party discusses the tactical implications of restraint. The DM clarifies that restrained creatures have disadvantage on their attacks, while attacks against them have advantage; when both attacker and target are restrained, those effects cancel.
- The Giant Owlbear delivers a critical strike against the Stone Golem. During the subsequent restraint ruling, the DM confirms that the second attack hits for 26 damage.
- A Roll20 malfunction interrupts the battle. The map repeatedly scrolls left, and attempts to change browsers do not immediately resolve it. The DM later identifies a mouse issue.
- The DM treats Felkur's active invisibility as Greater Invisibility so he can continue operating his cannon while invisible, explaining that the adjustment keeps him involved in the encounter.
- Veris casts Firebolt at the Stone Golem, dealing 6 damage and burning away the webbing restraining it. The construct can now attack Daein with advantage.
- The Stone Golem pounds Daein for 48 damage. A mistaken address briefly makes Felkur think he has been hit despite being invisible and elsewhere; the DM clarifies that Daein is the target.
- Bramgrim's Spirit Guardians deal 26 damage to the Golem after it fails its DC 17 Wisdom save, even with advantage. Its speed is halved.
- Felkur proposes pretending to surrender to buy time. The party discusses stalling for six rounds rather than needing to defeat every enemy.
- Bramgrim considers Freedom of Movement's touch range, but Daein is five feet too far away. An Arcana check of 3 yields no further tactical hint from the DM.
- Bramgrim casts Freedom of Movement on himself and uses Mass Healing Word as a bonus action to restore 17 hit points to Daein.
- Felkur aims his cannon's flamethrower at the Golem and the webs holding his allies. The Special Orb does not increase the cannon's save DC, leaving it at 19.
- The Golem takes full damage from the cannon. Restrained Daein and the Owlbear make Dexterity saves with disadvantage, both fail, and each takes 16 damage.
- The flames free Daein and the Owlbear. Bramgrim is already able to move freely because of Freedom of Movement.
- Akritas attacks the Stone Golem with her marked-target damage, including Favored Foe and Hunter's Mark. Her follow-up attack destroys the construct.
- Akritas transfers Hunter's Mark to Veris; the table confirms that the mark grants no saving throw.

### Veris Cornered: Wall of Force and Shield Guardian
#### With the Stone Golem destroyed, Veris makes a desperate last stand while a Shield Guardian arrives as reinforcement.
- With his first strategy defeated and his high-level resources depleted, Veris moves across the battlefield and conjures a Wall of Force to trap Felkur.
- Veris declares: “Now, Felkur, there is no escape.”
- Asked how Veris knows where the invisible artificer is, the DM explains that he inferred Felkur's presence from the teleportation interference and the missing visible party member. The earlier dinner party is also cited as a point of familiarity.
- A Shield Guardian emerges from the stairwell to support Veris, joining the combat for round five. More enemies can be heard following it.
- Felkur asks whether his cannon can reach the library. The DM reminds him that the party is at the tower's top and reached it through a chute; the library lies much farther below.
- Felkur proposes dropping the cannon down the chute and detonating it among Vraxen's possessions. The DM confirms that this could divert Vraxen and guarantee he does not arrive at the fight above.
- Daein prepares a ranged attack for the removal of the barrier. The DM rules that he cannot ready the proposed melee approach from his current distance, and that Spirit Guardians cannot pass through the force barrier.
- Bramgrim casts Dispel Magic at fifth level, automatically removing Veris's Wall of Force under the session's ruling. Veris screams in frustration.
- Bramgrim moves to bring Veris within Spirit Guardians. Veris succeeds on the DC 17 Wisdom save with his +7 modifier.
- Veris spends a refreshed legendary action to move away from Bramgrim's spirits.
- Daein's prepared bow shot hits Veris for 8 damage and reduces his speed by 10 feet until the start of his turn.
- The Owlbear shoves the Shield Guardian. It rolls 9 against DC 15 and is knocked prone, allowing the Owlbear to move away under the table's ruling.
- Seeing more enemies rushing up, the party considers using a token to block the entrance. Alternatives discussed include Web, Evard's Black Tentacles, Wall of Stone, and Wall of Fire.
- Arcane Lock is ruled unsuitable because the door has already been smashed. Wall of Fire is considered less useful because the approaching fiends resist fire.
- The DM clarifies that the first three tokens were usable by anyone, while the last two belong to Felkur.
- Felkur suggests shaping Wall of Force into a dome around the party. The group discusses keeping most enemies outside while surviving the remaining four rounds.
- Wind Wall is briefly considered as extra protection, but its concentration requirement conflicts with Felkur's ongoing work.
- The party uses one of Felkur's magical tokens to produce a Scroll of Wall of Force that another character can cast. Creating the scroll consumes Felkur's action; it does not immediately raise a barrier.
- Felkur moves his cannon down the passageway toward the library chute as a bonus action. He cannot also perform the planned detonation that turn.
- Veris uses a legendary action to strike Akritas with a critical Firebolt for 40 damage, targeting the archer threatening him.
- Daein uses Protective Field, spending psionic energy to reduce Akritas's damage to 36.
- Akritas designates Veris as her sworn enemy and spends her final Favored Foe use. Hunter's Mark is already on him.
- Akritas misses her first shot and hits with her second for 28 damage, leaving Veris at 11 hit points. The DM considers Shield but determines it would not prevent the hit.
- Before the turn ends, Akritas uses her remaining bonus action to cast third-level Hail of Thorns for 3d10 damage.
- Veris fails the DC 14 Dexterity save and dies. The DM reveals that he was about to cast Fireball.

### Demonic Reinforcements: The Mezzoloth Arrives
#### With Veris slain, the party must now contend with the Shield Guardian and a wave of demonic reinforcements, including a deadly Mezzoloth.
- The Shield Guardian spends half its movement standing, approaches Bramgrim, and makes two fist attacks. Both miss; the first attack totals 15.
- It moves aside to clear the stairwell entrance for its allies, exposing itself to opportunity attacks from the Owlbear and Bramgrim.
- The party resolves its outstanding Spirit Guardians damage. It fails its save and takes 17 damage.
- The DM rolls initiative for incoming reinforcements, including a Mezzoloth, a Nycaloth, and additional lesser enemies.
- Daein plans to cast the Scroll of Wall of Force. The DM confirms that the scroll uses its fixed spell parameters and requires concentration from its caster.
- At the start of round six, the Mezzoloth's initiative places it ahead of Daein. It reaches the tower top before the planned dome is cast; the Nycaloth and other enemies remain outside the proposed barrier area.
- The stairwell opens near the Owlbear, where the Shield Guardian had cleared space.
- The Mezzoloth enters Bramgrim's Spirit Guardians, fails its DC 17 Wisdom save despite advantage, and takes 29 damage.
- The DM initially proposes Dispel Magic, then retracts that action when reminded that the party's barrier does not yet exist.
- The Mezzoloth instead casts Cloudkill, filling the area with poisonous gas and forcing DC 13 Constitution saving throws.
- The poison roll totals 35 damage. Successful saves halve this to 17; most party members resist the worst of it, while the Owlbear takes the full damage.
- Felkur succeeds on his poison save and then passes the DC 10 Constitution check to maintain concentration. Bramgrim's concentration check is not resolved before the pause.
- The party reconsiders whether Daein should damage the Mezzoloth and leave Akritas to cast the scroll.
- The DM emphasizes that the poisonous cloud remains a danger every round the party stays inside it.
- The session pauses mid-round before Daein commits to an action. The Wall of Force scroll remains uncast, and Felkur's cannon has not detonated.

## Locations
### Vraxen's Tower (Top Floor)
The summit of a high tower where the party is engaged in a desperate combat against Veris, a Stone Golem, a Shield Guardian, and demonic reinforcements. It features a chute leading down to a library and a stairwell from which reinforcements are emerging.

- The DM describes the battlefield as the tower's top, around the thirtieth floor. The library is substantially deeper inside the tower.
- The party previously climbed a chute to reach this level. Felkur moves his cannon down the passageway toward that chute, intending to detonate it among Vraxen's possessions; neither descent down the chute nor detonation is confirmed before the pause.
- The stairwell opens near the Owlbear's position. The Shield Guardian deliberately moves aside to admit the next wave.
- The entrance door has been smashed, preventing the proposed use of Arcane Lock.

- Cloudkill remains over the battlefield when the session ends.

## NPCs
### Veris
A powerful magic-user and “big bad” who utilized legendary actions, Web spells, firebolts, and a Wall of Force to control the battlefield. He was ultimately slain by Akritas's Hail of Thorns before he could unleash a fireball.

- Identified by the DM as High Cloak Veris and a fourteenth-level wizard.
- Uses two legendary actions for Web, then deliberately burns his own Golem free so it can attack restrained opponents.
- Attempts to isolate Felkur while reinforcements arrive, inferring the invisible artificer's presence from events.
- Has a +7 Wisdom saving throw modifier and successfully resists the worst of Bramgrim's Spirit Guardians.
- Uses legendary movement to escape the spirits and a critical Firebolt to punish Akritas.
- Dies after Akritas reduces him to 11 hit points and follows with Hail of Thorns.

### Owlbear
The party's loyal sidekick who fought bravely against the Stone Golem despite being caught in magical webbing and scorched by Felkur's cannon, and later helped shove the Shield Guardian prone.

- Lands a confirmed 26-damage attack against the Stone Golem during the restraint exchange.
- Takes 16 damage from Felkur's flamethrower while being freed.
- Knocks the Shield Guardian prone with a DC 15 shove.
- Stands near the entrance through which reinforcements arrive.
- Takes the full 35 damage from Cloudkill at the end of the session.

### Shield Guardian
A large magical construct that arrived as reinforcement for Veris. The Owlbear knocked it prone; it then stood, approached Bramgrim, missed both attacks, and took damage from Spirit Guardians.

- Arrives as round five begins.
- Fails its save against the Owlbear's shove with a roll of 9.
- Clears the stairwell so its allies can emerge, provoking attacks from Bramgrim and the Owlbear.
- Fails its Spirit Guardians save and takes 17 damage.
- Remains active when the session pauses.

### Mezzoloth
A demonic entity that emerged from the stairwell as reinforcement and immediately cast Cloudkill, filling the tower top with a poisonous cloud to choke the adventurers.

- Arrives ahead of Daein in round six, before the party's Wall of Force can be raised.
- Takes 29 damage from Spirit Guardians after failing its Wisdom save despite advantage.
- Has access to Dispel Magic, which the DM briefly considers before recognizing that the intended barrier has not been cast.
- Casts Cloudkill with a DC 13 Constitution save, rolling 35 poison damage.
- Accompanies a larger incoming force that includes a Nycaloth.

## Items

A magical item used by Veris to conjure a massive 20-foot cube of sticky, flammable webbing that restrained the party and made the area difficult to traverse.

- The DM explicitly identifies Veris's use of the wand while reviewing his resources.
- The webbing becomes central to his plan to immobilize the party while freeing the Stone Golem with targeted fire.

### Felkur's Cannon
An artificer's invention used by Felkur to fire a flamethrower blast that burned away the magical webs restraining the party. Felkur also moved it toward the library chute with the intention of detonating it as a diversion to draw Vraxen away from the tower.

- Uses a DC 19 save, without an increase from the Special Orb.
- Deals 16 damage to both Daein and the Owlbear during the rescue from the webs.
- Can move as a bonus action, allowing Felkur to send it down the passageway after using his action to obtain the scroll.
- Its proposed self-detonation among Vraxen's belongings is intended to divert the archmage.
- Has not exploded when play pauses.

### Special Orb
An item previously stolen by Felkur that grants a bonus to ability checks involving artificer abilities. The party debated whether it also affected spell save DC, ultimately determining it did not.

- The DM specifies a +2 bonus to ability checks involving artificers.
- Felkur initially proposes DC 21 for the cannon, then returns to DC 19 after the clarification.

### Magical Token (Scroll of Wall of Force)
A consumable token belonging to Felkur that, when used, produced a Scroll of Wall of Force. The party used it to obtain the scroll, planning a barrier against incoming reinforcements; the barrier had not been cast when the session paused.

- The DM distinguishes the first three party-usable tokens from the last two, which Felkur must use.
- Spending the token establishes that the party brought the needed scroll; another character can subsequently cast it.
- Producing the scroll uses Felkur's action.
- The plan develops from blocking the stairwell to enclosing the party in a dome.
- Daein is initially chosen to cast it, but the party considers handing that task to Akritas after Cloudkill changes the situation.
- The scroll's eventual caster must maintain concentration.

## Spells
### Web
Cast by Veris as a legendary action, this spell conjured a 20-foot cube of sticky, flammable webbing that restrained party members within it, imposed disadvantage on their attack rolls, and granted advantage to attackers targeting them.

- Costs Veris two legendary actions in this encounter.
- Creates difficult terrain and light obscurement.
- The DM clarifies that ordinary webbing does not deal damage simply because a creature starts its turn inside it.
- Burning webs are discussed as dealing 2d4 damage, with exposed five-foot cubes burning away in one round.
- Veris's Firebolt frees the Golem first; Felkur's flamethrower subsequently frees Daein and the Owlbear.

### Firebolt
Used by Veris to target the Stone Golem and burn away the webs restraining it, and later used as a legendary action to deal heavy damage to Akritas.

- Deals 6 damage to the Stone Golem while freeing it.
- Later critically hits Akritas for 40 damage.
- Daein's Protective Field reduces Akritas's damage to 36.

### Spirit Guardians
A concentration spell maintained by Bramgrim throughout the battle, creating a protective aura of spirits that damaged the Stone Golem, Shield Guardian, and Mezzoloth as they moved through the area, and reduced the Stone Golem's speed on a failed saving throw.

- Uses a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw and is identified as cast at fifth level.
- Deals 26 damage to the Stone Golem after a failed save.
- Cannot pass through Veris's Wall of Force under the session's ruling.
- Veris succeeds on his save and then uses legendary movement to get away from the aura.
- Deals 17 damage to the Shield Guardian and 29 to the Mezzoloth.
- Bramgrim's concentration after Cloudkill remains unresolved at the pause.

### Freedom of Movement
Cast by Bramgrim on himself, allowing him to move freely through the magical webs without being restrained.

- Discussed as a touch spell without concentration.
- Daein is five feet beyond Bramgrim's touch range when the spell is considered.
- Bramgrim's self-casting frees his movement without ending Spirit Guardians.

### Mass Healing Word
A bonus action spell used by Bramgrim to restore health to Daein during the heat of battle, returning 17 hit points.

- Cast on the same turn as Bramgrim's Freedom of Movement.
- Helps Daein recover from the Stone Golem's 48-damage assault.

### Wall of Force
Cast by Veris to create an invisible, physical barrier to isolate Felkur, declaring there was no escape. It was later successfully dispelled by Bramgrim. The party also used a token to obtain a scroll of this spell to block demonic reinforcements from the stairwell.

- Veris's barrier prevents the party's direct approach and blocks Spirit Guardians under the table's ruling.
- The party considers both an entrance barrier and a dome enclosing themselves.
- Their proposed protection needs to buy four more rounds.
- The scroll requires a separate casting action and concentration; obtaining it does not immediately create the wall.
- Remains uncast when the Mezzoloth uses Cloudkill.

### Greater Invisibility
The GM treated Felkur's active invisibility as Greater Invisibility so he could operate his cannon while remaining invisible, explicitly making this adjustment to keep the game engaging.

- The ruling lets Felkur contribute through the cannon while continuing his ongoing work.
- Felkur relies on remaining unseen as part of his argument that the party can stall rather than win outright.

### Dispel Magic
Under the GM's ruling for this session, Bramgrim cast Dispel Magic at fifth level to shatter Veris's Wall of Force, freeing the party to strike back at the wizard.

- The DM discusses a DC 15 spellcasting ability check for a lower-level casting against the barrier.
- Bramgrim chooses fifth level for automatic success under that ruling.
- The Mezzoloth also has Dispel Magic available, though it uses Cloudkill instead because the party's wall has not yet been cast.

### Hail of Thorns
Cast by Akritas as a bonus action at third level, turning her arrow into a burst of magical thorns that rained down on Veris and ultimately killed him before he could unleash a fireball.

- Added after Akritas's successful bow attack leaves Veris with 11 hit points.
- Deals 3d10 damage at the declared level.
- Veris fails the DC 14 Dexterity saving throw.
- The killing blow prevents his imminent Fireball.

### Cloudkill
Cast by the Mezzoloth upon entering the battlefield, conjuring a billowing cloud of poisonous gas that forced the party to make Constitution saving throws or take heavy poison damage. The cloud persists and threatens the party's concentration spells each round.

- Uses a DC 13 Constitution saving throw.
- Deals 35 poison damage, reduced to 17 on a successful save.
- The Owlbear takes full damage; most of the party succeeds.
- Felkur passes the resulting DC 10 concentration check.
- The persistent cloud prompts the party to reconsider casting a dome around their current position.
- Its continuing effects and Bramgrim's concentration remain unresolved when the session pauses.
