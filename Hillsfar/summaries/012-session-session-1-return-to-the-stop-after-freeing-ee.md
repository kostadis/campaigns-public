# Chapter 12: Session 1: Return to The Stop after Freeing EE

Date: 17–22 Mirtul 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); played 1494 DR, between 14 Mirtul and 2 Kythorn (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-15); between 2023-06 and 2023-10 (GM ruling 2026-11-14); report written 2023-11-20

## Summary
The session opens with a debt settled. Elanil Elassidil's relative is freed from the oath, and in thanks she leaves her rare lute to Daein.

Serinia then gives the heroes a new charge: investigate the forest and see whether anything can be done about the demonic influence there, perhaps by talking to Brightleaf, the Treant. They find that Brightleaf himself has been possessed by a demonic force. The heroes free him, but the Treant tells them this is "beyond anything he can do."

The demonic turn costs the party a companion. Falofa leaves the group to head out to her tribe, saying "something about not wanting to deal with demonic wars."

Serinia also brings news from The Stop. The former magistrate, Thaddeus Zervous, was in league with the Duergar, selling slaves. La Resistance staged a coup against him, and he "has fled the city." In response the City of Hillsfar withdrew the Red Plume Guards, and in the power vacuum a new Magistrate was elected: Jhanos Myrkeer. The Emerald Enclave asks the heroes to go to The Stop and support him and the new order by guarding it.

On the way, the heroes spend the night at Goodroot Beet Farm to avoid meeting the guards. Constance Goodroot is pleased to see all of them except Daein, who rudely tries to probe what kind of "half-elven" she is. Given the current state of affairs in the Hillsfar region, she assumes he wants to know whether she is half-human, and she claims to be half-Aarakocra. When he is surprised, she scowls at him. Akritas steps in and saves the situation, but Daein spends the night in a closet while the rest of the group sleeps in nice beds.

Traveling near the edge of the Cormanthor forest but outside the Rimwood, the heroes spot a goblin war party and stage an ambush. Felkur pretends to be drunk, with Owly beside him, while the rest of the group hides in the trees. The goblins try to threaten Felkur; Felkur fails to threaten them back, and a battle breaks out. The goblin war party is decimated. As the goblin chief runs off, he shrieks that The Ringmaster will not be happy.

The heroes arrive at the Stop and meet Karl and Jhanos, Rogi, and Kendrick.
