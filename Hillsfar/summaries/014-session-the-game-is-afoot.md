# Chapter 14: The Game is Afoot

Date: 1 Kythorn 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); played 1494 DR, between 14 Mirtul and 2 Kythorn (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-15); between 2023-06 and 2023-10 (GM ruling 2026-11-14); report written 2023-11-20

## Summary
The adventurers visit the Plodding Plow and meet up with Glenna Moonsmith. She is very upset about the death of Jhanos Myrkeer, and in particular about the claims that Karl is to blame. She demands that the new police of the town quickly uncover the murder.

Asking around the tavern, the heroes piece together the politics behind the killing. Bickleford "Biff" Bitterwood was the social organizer working for more rights for non-humans. When Thaddeus Zervous and the city of Hillsfar cranked up their enforcement of the Law of Humanity, and non-human farmers started to lose their farms, folks like Karl took up arms. The heroes also recall that Jhanos Myrkeer had decided to hand over the Magistrate to Biff: the Merchant House had been sold, the HAMR no longer had the dominant share of the town's holdings, and so it could not appoint him.

That night the heroes are attacked by a Slithering Tracker. Wudu is assassinated, and Daein is almost killed.

The list of suspects is long. The HAMR and its supporters exist to ensure that the humans of The Stop are at the top of the economic food chain: non-humans are paid less for their goods and services, and pay more to get goods and services from the merchants of The Stop. Then there are the non-humans who hate HAMR and would resent any human Magistrate it supports. And then there is Biff, Jubal, Andros and others.

The heroes decide it is time to close some threads, starting with Dr. Jubal. At the Carnival they meet Andros and Fabian Greeneyes. They learn that Petra, Andros' daughter, had tried to convince Fabian to stick around and work at The Way Station, but Andros had lied and said that no non-human could work there. Petra tried to come with Fabian; Fabian told her no, and he has not seen her since. Andros confirms the story. The heroes then recall Fabian Greeneyes waving at them as they arrived in town, and a young woman matching Petra's description riding a horse in the opposite direction.
