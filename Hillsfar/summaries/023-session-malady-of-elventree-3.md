# Chapter 23: Malady of Elventree (3)

Date: 21 Eleasis 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); played 2024-06-30 (Zoom meeting date)

## Summary

The party's journey down the river ran into an unexpected ambush. A fresh map showed three large creatures and rocky riverbanks, and the question was who had been surprised. The attackers were duergar, some of them invisible; the location of one was revealed through the perception of the owlbear. That duergar attacked Bramgrim with advantage but missed, and Bramgrim moved into a position to gain advantage against it. Then a "mind blast" was used against Bramgrim, who failed the wisdom saving throw against it. The fight that followed involved stunned and grappled conditions and a protective field to reduce damage. The party used the area effect of the Spirit Guardians to hit several opponents at once, Felkur's cannon came into play, and Bramgrim took the duergar out with a critical hit.

After the fight came a negotiation. The mind flayer Galuum put an offer to the party: valuable information in exchange for their assistance. The party, led by Felkur, at first distrusted the offer because of past experiences with "mind flayers". Once the situation with the mad duergar was made clear, they agreed to help, and decided to give the mad duergar to the mind flayers, removing the potential for more problems in the future. The one making the offer had once intended to use the duergar as slaves, but their self-proclaimed deity status and the killing spree that followed had made them useless to him. A gift exchange followed, adding 50 gold pieces each to the party's income, and the party took a short rest before continuing.

Next came a gate and a statue. The party had to open the gate without breaking it. Felkur noticed a locking mechanism in the statue's eyes and mouth, and the party kept that knowledge to itself. Strength, dexterity and thieves' tools all failed on the lock. So they tried something else: entertaining the Laird. The Laird was unimpressed at first, but eventually let them pass once Felkur convinced him that they were not boring.

Beyond the gate the party met enslaved svirfneblin fighting for their freedom from the Duergar. Vernon, one of the svirfneblin, apologized for his friends' aggression and said they had been mistreated for five years. The Duergar had recently become delusional and were attacking each other, and most of the town had succumbed to madness. Vernon asked for food and offered to help the party if they could provide it. The session ended with open questions: what had become of the duergar and deep gnomes of Zortagra after the mind flayer attack, whether the mind flayers might take over the deep gnomes, what the new god Deep Duerra and the duergar's madness meant, food rations from Akritas for the escaped svirfneblin workers from the Zortagra smeltery, and whether to negotiate with the mind flayer Galuum.
