# Chapter 58 The Council at Sporedome

Date: 18 Nightal 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); played 2026-08-23

## Summary
The party arrived at Sporedome, the great fungal amphitheater that served as the staging ground for the coalition's march on Maerimydra. The cavern buzzed with a myriad of voices — secretive Drow mingling with tall mushroom men, stout dwarves conversing with red-feathered humans, and faction leaders locked in furious debate. Among the assembled faces were Solom Ned'razak, the cryptic Drow Archmage; Sovereign Agaricus of the Myconids, who communicated through drifting spores; Huum, a Mind Flayer representative, watching from the shadows; and the newly appointed First Lord of Hillsfar, a man the party recognized as the very guard they had once met at the city's gates. Perched atop a tall mushroom, a little girl in a dirty dress sat alone with her arm around a blind black goat — the mysterious Elisande, who had a habit of appearing at the most critical junctures of their journey.

Sovereign Agaricus conveyed through his connecting spores that a fierce battle had recently taken place between his colony and the demon queen of fungi, Zuggtmoy, fighting alongside the demon lord Juiblex — and both demon lords had been defeated by five outside adventurers. The revelation reminded the party of Alder's grand design — her "Plan A" had been another adventuring party working to collapse the demonic incursion from within, while the party themselves were her "Plan B," tasked with intercepting the demon lord Graz'zt when he inevitably manifested in Maerimydra. Solom Ned'razak, for his part, announced that his experiment to purge fiendish taint from a fire giant had succeeded, though the process required the subject to be subdued for ten full days, making it useless as a battlefield solution. The news sparked excited debate among the assembled factions, but the hard truth was clear: there was no shortcut. The city would have to be taken by force.

Elanil Elassidil, known as the Moonsilver Herald, raised her arms and a magical hush fell over the amphitheater. She addressed the crowd with calm authority, outlining the plan: three coalition forces would deploy to advanced positions and establish bases for a coordinated assault on Maerimydra, while two smaller teams would scout alternate approaches and gather local allies. The primary objective, she made clear, was not to seize the city but to kill Hledh Hellspawn, the fiend-born fire giant who commanded the demonic forces. Without their leader, the fire giants would descend into chaos and begin tearing each other apart. When Akritas asked bluntly whether they could actually take the city, Elanil answered with equal bluntness — they had enough to make the enemy look the wrong way for a while, and that had been enough before.

Knowing what the rest of the coalition did not, the party pulled Elanil aside to warn her about Graz'zt. At first, she dismissed the warning as one of many dire predictions she heard before every campaign. But when Bramgrim produced Alder's autograph — written on his sword during their legendary encounter with the Harper hero — Elanil's expression shifted from dismissal to bitter recognition. She accused Alder of engineering the entire situation, sending them to die cleaning up a mess the Harpers had made. After a tense exchange, however, she acknowledged that nothing about the plan could change: the fire giants had to be defeated regardless, as it was the only path to Graz'zt. She told the party plainly that once they were inside Maerimydra, they would be on their own — and when Daein pointed out they always had been, she offered no argument.

The party then turned to the Mind Flayer, hoping to gain intelligence on Graz'zt before the battle. The Mind Flayer initially claimed that all debts between them were settled, having already provided the party with two powerful magical items. Bramgrim and Felkur appealed to the creature's pride, arguing that the demonic incursion was a threat worth finishing, and the Mind Flayer — eyeing Felkur's brain with unsettling interest — eventually agreed to provide a detailed accounting of Graz'zt's known abilities. Akritas kept her bow quietly at the ready throughout the entire negotiation, just in case the Mind Flayer's interest in Felkur's brain became more than academic.

No sooner had the Mind Flayer departed than a short halfling with the look of a seasoned rogue pulled Bramgrim aside and delivered a grim warning. The Drow alliance, she explained, was held together by a single thread: fear of Solom Ned'razak. Drow society operated on a brutal principle — if any faction attempted to kill another and failed to eliminate every last member, the remaining factions were honor-bound to destroy the attacker. This was what kept the coalition from collapsing into civil war. The danger, however, was that Dorina T'sarran — a vampire Drow cleric and the former ruler of Szith Morcane — had been spotted in the area and had sworn revenge against Solom. Daein recognized the name immediately: Dorina shared a surname with Velasta T'sarran, and both were clerics of the same dark goddess. Dorina was Velasta's aunt, and if she found Solom alive and told her niece, the lie that had kept Velasta focused on Maerimydra would unravel, and the entire coalition could come apart before a single blow was struck.

With the political landscape mapped and the dangers catalogued, the party turned to the practical work of assigning their allied factions to the battle's objectives. The Stone Giants, well-matched against fire giants, were sent to the Northern Forward Base. The Hidden, a faction of skilled thieves, were assigned to a scouting mission deep in the Underdark. The leaderless Nerevenans were paired with the friendly undead forces, while Rilira's former slaves and Grundar's Duergar were sent to the Eastern Base. Quil'bryn's Drow were kept in reserve as a tactical backup — a decision Rilira objected to loudly until Daein's persuasive reasoning won her over. Solom Ned'razak watched the entire arrangement with a quiet, cryptic smile.

As the factions were organized, two more parties approached with private requests. A representative of the Order of the Gauntlet told the party of the Cave of Black Whispers, where a dwarven leader had risen as a powerful mummy and held his kin in undeath — a mission brought to them by Glenna Moonsmith, who begged for her ancestor to be laid to rest. Then, in a far darker corner of the preparations, a Zhentarim operative approached Felkur with an expensive bottle of rare wine and a proposition: in exchange for cementing a smuggling partnership, the Black Network wanted the party to retrieve newborn children of Graz'zt from Castle Maerimydra, to be raised as demonic operatives. Felkur listened with apparent interest, privately planning to give the Zhentarim minor items as a decoy while keeping the real prize — a powerful ledger — for himself, ensuring that any magical investigation would lead to the Black Network rather than him. As the armies began to march out of Sporedome to the sound of cheering, Elanil shared a final somber moment with Daein, confessing her fear of what awaited them. Daein reassured her by mentioning the extremely strong alcohol he had been brewing — enough to knock someone out if they were captured. Elanil smiled despite herself, put on her helmet, and headed off to lead her forces into the dark.

## NPCs
### Elanil Elassidil
Known as the Moonsilver Herald, she is an elven woman who acts as the overall military commander of the coalition. She is pragmatic and weary, focused on the immediate threat of the fire giants. She is visibly shaken and bitterly frustrated when Bramgrim reveals that Alder is behind the party's intelligence, accusing the Harpers of sending them to clean up their own mess. She ultimately accepts that the plan cannot change and that the party will be on their own once inside Maerimydra.

### Solom Ned'razak
A powerful Drow Archmage whose presence keeps the fractious Drow factions in line through fear. He has successfully developed a method to remove demonic taint from fire giants, though it requires the subject to be subdued for ten days. He watches the battle preparations with a cryptic smile and is currently the target of a vampire's vendetta.

### Sovereign Agaricus
The leader of the Myconids in Sporedome who communicates through connecting spores. He reveals that a battle occurred between the demon queen of fungi and his colony, which was defeated with outside help. He is deeply disturbed by the rapid changes affecting the Underdark.

### Elisande
A mysterious figure who appears at critical junctures of the party's journey. She is seen sitting atop a tall mushroom in the Sporedome, her arm around a blind black goat with milky white eyes, dressed in a dirty dress.

### First Lord Vuhm Yestral
Formerly a simple guard the party once encountered in Hillsfar, he has risen to become the city's First Lord amidst the chaos. He attends the war council and is recognized by Felkur. He is eager to associate himself with the party's heroics and publicly praises Daein as a protector and hero of Hillsfar.

### Quil'bryn
A Drow warrior who once believed he had killed Solom Ned'razak. He leads a Drow faction that is ultimately kept in reserve as a tactical backup force, per an arrangement with the party.

### Hledh Hellspawn
The leader of the fire giant and demonic forces in Maerimydra. She is a fiend-born fire giant and the primary assassination target of the coalition's military operation, as her death is expected to throw the enemy forces into disorganizing chaos.

A legendary Harper hero described as a 21st-level rogue who was involved in trapping Lolth in a dimensional prison. Bramgrim obtained her autograph on his sword.

### Dorina T'sarran
A vampire Drow cleric of Kiaransalee and the former ruler of Szith Morcane. She is the aunt of Velasta T'sarran and has been seen in the area, having vowed revenge against Solom Ned'razak. Her potential interference poses a serious threat to the coalition's stability.

### Rilira
Leader of the former Drow slaves who vociferously objects to keeping forces in reserve, arguing that the Drow knights should be on the front lines. She is ultimately persuaded by Daein's strategic reasoning to accept the plan.

### Glenna Moonsmith
The owner of the Plodding Plow and The Stop, a familiar contact of the party. She has approached the Order of the Gauntlet and begged them to lay her dwarven ancestor — now risen as a powerful mummy in the Cave of Black Whispers — to rest permanently.
