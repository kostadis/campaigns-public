# Chapter 15: The Defeat of Rogi, and Dr. Jubal is in trouble, Again!

Date: 2 Kythorn to about 12 Kythorn 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-15); between 2023-06 and 2023-10 (GM ruling 2026-11-14); report written 2023-11-20

## Summary
After defeating Rogi, Thaddeus Zervous and the members of the cult in a battle in The Livery in The Stop, the heroes plot their next move. Daein wants to have the Owlbear eat the remains of the Vargouille, but Bramgrim, Akritas and Felkur will have none of that. He then proposes that the owlbear eat Thaddeus and Rogi, and is vetoed again.

Daein points out a real problem: with Vonhilda, Eve Saint Clair, Andros, Chef Bertram and Captain Remy Leduc turned into vargouilles, they have no evidence to clear Biff. Searching the room solves it. In Thaddeus Zervous's spell book they find a spell to create a slithering tracker, the evidence they need to confirm that Biff is innocent.

The heroes go into the courtyard at The Way Station and explain to Biff and the locals what happened, presenting the evidence. There is much celebration and joy. Karl is made the new Captain of the Guard of The Stop, and the heroes are offered free room and board. The Chateau, The Livery, Von's Trading House and the Merchant House will all need new owners and management, and many of the local non-humans are excited at the employment opportunities.

Karl declares that he isn't going to stop fighting for La Resistance. Glenna Moonsmith begs him to stop and leave that to other people: he has to protect The Stop from Hillsfar, and others can try to overturn Hillsfar.

Owly, the Owlbear, decides to follow Felkur. Daein's own attempt is so bad that Owly pees on him.

Ten days later, while the heroes are settled in, Karl, who is now known as Karl, not Charles, arrives to tell them that Bickleford "Biff" Bitterwood wants to meet them. Biff tells them that Dr. Jubal's caravan was supposed to be in Hillpicket but never arrived. It was found on a herder road used during the winter, not the summer. Only one wagon was found; the other nine are missing, and no one has seen any carnie or Doctor Jubal since. Karl has cordoned off the area. Karl is obviously not happy with his new job, but he reveals that he is in love with Glenna Moonsmith and has decided that, for her, he will abandon La Resistance and the cause.

At the site, the heroes' investigation shows that goblins ambushed the caravan 10 days ago, and that the goblin tracks and the caravans headed north. The abandoned wagon holds nothing of value, except that inside a barrel labeled PICKLES they find Pickles, the halfling, who survived ten days eating pickles and pickle juice. Pickles is one half of the two-person act Pickles and Jam: Pickles will eat anything, and Jam will bite the head off small animals and spit them at people.

Pickles isn't thrilled that his rescuers are the adventurers, given his criminal activity in the Stop 10 days earlier, when he tried to trigger a riot so he could rob the locals. He quickly explains that Karl freed him when Dr. Jubal paid a small fee. The Carnival tried to leave, but it rained, and his wagon fell into a ditch. Goblins attacked and, surprisingly, took prisoners instead of killing everyone. Dr Jubal went to fight, his red eye blazing. Pickles hid in the barrel: "I am an entertainer, not a fighter!" The goblins took the unconscious prisoners and the wagons north.
