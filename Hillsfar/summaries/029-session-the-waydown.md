## Summary
The party entered the temple cavern. Its layout held statues, doors, and "a sacrificial hall with an archpriest and a shackled drow." Daein questioned Bramgrim's motivations, and Bramgrim defended them before the party moved on. They prepared to confront the archpriest in the sacrificial hall.

The party chose to save the drow. They planned to sneak in on the enemies and to fight with long-range attacks, since the enemies had "an annoying property" that "could cause their weapons to stick in their bodies."

The plan worked: the party surprised the enemies "in the middle of a ritual." Daein and Bramgrim attacked the arch priest, "dealing significant damage." Felkur hit the arch priest "with firebolt and pistol attacks." Akritas used her bow to deal 25 damage to the arch priest. The owl bear charged one of the enemies and dealt 29 damage. Bramgrim's new spiritual weapon was shown: a hammer that could be spawned 60 feet away.

The kuo-toa fought back with magic. One cast control water, a spell that dealt damage and could knock characters prone. Another cast bane, imposing a penalty on attack rolls and saving throws. The kuo-toa Archpriest used a heal spell "to restore health to his allies," and the Owlbear "was dealt significant damage." The Archpriest then submerged himself in water for cover while the kuo-toa attacked Akritas.

The party ground the enemies down. Spirit guardians slowed the speed of certain characters, and attacks traded back and forth, with "some critical hits and misses." The combat ended "with the defeat of the kuo-toa and the Drow's escape."

Afterward the party looked ahead. They would go through the middle passage and explore "the underwater passage in the temple," with Felkur and Bramgrim riding the Owlbear "through the water-filled area." The rescued drow had mentioned a toad demon, and the party meant to be ready for it. They also meant to investigate "the connection between the twin gods, demons, and fire giants."
