# Chapter 35 From Hillsfar's Aftermath to the Howling Pits

Date: Nov 22nd, 2025

## Summary
Hillsfar's ruling elite lay dead, and the Red Plume guards found themselves caught between fractured factions, uncertain of their place in the new order. The party first advanced to level 10, though persistent D&D Beyond problems required the DM to help update character sheets and provide a PDF workaround. Daein took another fighter level and increased his Charisma by two, from 14 to 16.

Isthana, the head of the Thieves Guild, surveyed the blood-soaked temple, shattered windows, and destroyed artifacts with barely concealed delight. The party had created extraordinary opportunities for her organization. She repeatedly offered them positions in Hillsfar's new political order, but Felkur preferred to keep his head attached and refused to let her manipulate him. When Daein asked why she did not take power herself, she admitted she shared Felkur's concerns. She knew ruling was a dangerous idea; she had merely hoped the party might think otherwise.

Instead, Isthana helped them acquire equipment from the castle's stores. Akritas received Studded Leather +1 and Bracers of Archery to complement her Oathbow, bringing her armor class to 16. Felkur also acquired Studded Leather +1 and reported an armor class of 15. Daein exchanged his scale mail for Plate Mail +1, reaching armor class 21. Bramgrim chose Mithral Plate, accepting armor class 20 in exchange for removing disadvantage on stealth checks. The equipment discussion also revealed that Akritas could cast Pass Without Trace, potentially giving Daein a +20 stealth bonus while helping the entire party, including their owlbear.

Isthana departed through the sewers, while the party walked out through the castle's main gate. Red Plume guards stood ready to fight, but the party neither threatened them nor raised weapons, and the guards let them pass. Outside, the adventurers debated their next steps: Felkur wanted materials for a bomb, Bramgrim suggested a restaurant called the Heroes of Hillsfar, and Daein questioned whether the public would regard them as heroes or criminals. Seeking endorsement from Selûne's clerics was suggested, but the restaurant remained only an idea.

Their immediate priority became protecting Owly, the owlbear who had accompanied them since level 3. They commissioned custom scale mail from a garishly painted smithy. After considerable discussion of costs and armor options, they settled on 200 gold pieces for armor that would bring Owly's armor class from 13 to 15. Akritas would handle the arrangements while the party rested at a tavern for several days. During the downtime, Felkur explored artificer infusions and ways to increase his spellcasting resources; a Wand of Fireballs was deferred until level 14, while Spell Refueling Rings were discussed as an available alternative.

Charles interrupted their vacation with congratulations and urgent news from the Emerald Enclave. His exuberant performance briefly became an acting critique before he explained that the drow had brought alarming intelligence about Graz'zt's fire giants. The party already knew about the giants, but Charles emphasized that the danger would not remain confined to the Underdark. Demons sought chaos and destruction for their own sake, and destroying Hillsfar as well as killing the adventurers would suit Graz'zt perfectly.

Daein wanted to take the fight to the enemy and pressed for troops or meaningful support from the Emerald Enclave. Akritas cautioned that an army was not how the Enclave worked. Charles acknowledged that the party had already accomplished enough to owe nothing further, citing their rescue of victims of the war against demi-humans and of Constance. Nevertheless, he appealed to their shared desire to make a better world. He directed them to Silver Shadow Cave near Elventree, warning that the price of fighting evil increased the longer they waited.

The party arrived at the sacred cave of Selûne in the evening. Moonlight illuminated drifting pollen, short grass, and wildflowers around the entrance, then filtered mysteriously into the chamber below. Already blessed by Selûne, the adventurers could feel her power there. Murssvayas Dhuurniv emerged from the shadows: a drow with silver hair, thick sideburns, emerald green eyes, nearly purple skin, black clothing, and a sword at his side. He greeted each adventurer individually and spoke to Owly in a way that caused the owlbear to recognize him.

Murssvayas represented Solom Ned'razak, the powerful wizard whom the party had previously helped escape Szith Morcane. The outpost had fallen to slaves, fiendish fire giants, and demons striking from Maerimydra. Solom had taken refuge in the Sporedome, where other survivors gathered after hearing of his arrival. When Bramgrim requested proof of Murssvayas's authority, the emissary presented Solom's recognizable pendant. Given Solom's power as an 18th-level wizard, Bramgrim accepted the evidence, and Daein trusted his judgment.

The drow had been developing a ritual to remove the fiendish blood that gave Graz'zt's giants their advantage. Their chief alchemist, Xaven Tanor'this, had been captured before the work could be completed. A now-collapsed spy network had traced his sale to Maram il Vass, a creature residing in the Den of the Whispered Word, an extraplanar brokerage of goods and services in the Howling Pits. The party would need to recover Xaven and return him to Solom in the Sporedome so the ritual could be completed.

Bramgrim led the demand for compensation. Murssvayas initially emphasized the opportunity for future dealings with Szith Morcane and the chance to save the surface world, but the party insisted that risking their lives required a tangible reward. Solom had anticipated the question: if the mission succeeded and Szith Morcane was recovered, he would grant an item from the drow hoard. Daein questioned the value of that promise, and the DM left the specific reward to be determined. The party accepted the rescue mission; their journey into the Howling Pits remained ahead.

## Locations
### Hillsfar
A city recovering from weeks of chaos after the party defeated Malkyn Grenefeld and killed the ruling elite. The Red Plumes are caught between factions, and the Thieves Guild sees opportunities in the power vacuum. A temporary atmosphere of remorse and reconciliation allows the party to move openly, though they debate whether ordinary citizens still regard them as criminals. Its safety remains threatened by Graz'zt's vengeance and the empowered fire giants.

### Hillsfar Castle
The former seat of power, now marked by blood, shattered windows, and destroyed artifacts after the recent battle. Isthana helps the party obtain armor here. She exits through the sewers; the party leaves through the main gate, passing wary Red Plume guards without raising weapons.

### Tavern in Hillsfar
An unnamed establishment where the party rests for several days while Owly's armor is made. The downtime includes discussion of artificer infusions and additional spellcasting resources. Charles finds the party here and interrupts their vacation with the invitation to Silver Shadow Cave.

### Smithy in Hillsfar
An extraordinarily garishly painted workshop where the party commissions custom scale mail for Owly. After comparing and correcting several options, they agree on 200 gold pieces for armor class 15. The work takes a few days, with Akritas handling the arrangements.

### Silver Shadow Cave
A sacred site near Elventree for communing with Selûne. Moonlight bathes a clearing of short grass and wildflowers, revealing motes of pollen drifting through the air. A wide entrance descends into a broader chamber, where a natural phenomenon allows moonlight to penetrate underground. The party feels Selûne's power through its existing blessing. Murssvayas meets them here in the evening.

### Elventree
A settlement near Silver Shadow Cave and one of the surface communities concerned about Graz'zt's potential retaliation. Charles uses it as the geographical reference for the meeting.

### Szith Morcane
A drow outpost sacked by slaves, fiendish fire giants, and demons striking from Maerimydra. Solom Ned'razak escaped through the party's earlier intervention. Its recovery is one of the conditions attached to the promised reward, and Murssvayas raises the prospect of future dealings between the restored outpost and surface factions.

### Sporedome
A fungal forest in the Underdark where Solom took refuge. Other survivors gathered there after hearing of his arrival. It is the destination to which the party must return Xaven so the ritual can be completed.

### Maerimydra
The Underdark city from which the fiendish fire giants, demons, and accompanying slave forces struck against Szith Morcane.

### Den of the Whispered Word
An extraplanar brokerage of goods and services in the Howling Pits. Maram il Vass resides here and has purchased Xaven Tanor'this. The party accepts a mission to recover the alchemist from Maram's custody; no specific rescue method is chosen.

### Howling Pits
A twisted realm of the Underdark containing Maram il Vass's refuge, the Den of the Whispered Word. It is the party's next intended destination, but they have not entered it by the session's end.

## NPCs
### Isthana
The pragmatic, opportunistic head of Hillsfar's Thieves Guild. She thanks the party for removing the ruling elite and creating opportunities for her organization. She repeatedly offers them political power while declining to seek it herself because she also values keeping her head attached. After helping fit the party with armor from the castle, she leaves through the sewers.

### Charles
A messenger connected to the Emerald Enclave and La Résistance. His theatrical congratulations prompt an impromptu critique of his acting, which he admits he has been practicing. Beneath the performance, he brings a serious warning about the fire giants and directs the party to Silver Shadow Cave. He acknowledges their past sacrifices, including saving Constance, and says they owe nothing further, but appeals to their commitment to a better world.

### Murssvayas Dhuurniv
Solom Ned'razak's drow representative. He has silver hair, thick sideburns, emerald green eyes, nearly purple skin, black clothing, and a sword at his side. He greets every party member and communicates with Owly in a way that prompts recognition. He carries Solom's pendant as evidence of his authority, explains the rescue mission, and conveys the conditional promise of an item from the drow hoard.

### Solom Ned'razak
The leader of Szith Morcane and an 18th-level wizard whom the party previously helped escape. He now shelters in the Sporedome with other survivors. His collapsed spy network traced Xaven's sale to Maram il Vass. Unable to attend the meeting, he sends Murssvayas with his pendant and anticipates the party's demand for compensation.

### Xaven Tanor'this
The drow chief alchemist developing a ritual to remove fiendish blood from Graz'zt's empowered fire giants. Captured during the sack of Szith Morcane, he was sold to Maram il Vass. Solom needs him returned to the Sporedome so the ritual can be completed.

### Maram il Vass
A creature residing in the Den of the Whispered Word in the Howling Pits. Maram purchased Xaven after his capture. The party receives no further description of Maram's appearance, capabilities, or motives during this session.

### Graz'zt
The demon lord whose plot the party thwarted by defeating Malkyn Grenefeld. He remains on the prime material plane and has threatened revenge. His blood empowers the fire giants, giving them an advantage over the drow. Charles warns that demonic retaliation may embrace widespread destruction as readily as a direct attack on the party.

### Malkyn Grenefeld
The recently defeated antagonist whose death triggered the current upheaval in Hillsfar and Graz'zt's wrath. Charles describes her as a focal point for the demon's activity.

### Red Plume Guards
Hillsfar's guards, left between fractured factions after the ruling elite's death. Those at the castle gate remain ready to fight if the party raises weapons, but permit the adventurers to walk past peacefully. The uncertainty over who now holds authority helps frame the encounter.
