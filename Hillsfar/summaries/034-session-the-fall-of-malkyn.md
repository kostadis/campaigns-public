# Chapter 34: The Fall of Malkyn

Date: 15 Eleint 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); played 2025-11-15 (Zoom meeting date)

## Summary

The battle against Malkyn resumed. Malkyn opened with her Eldritch Blast against Bramgrim, dealing 24 points of damage, reduced to 19 by Daein's Protective Field. Akritas moved to avoid a fireball and killed two sergeants with her attacks. Bramgrim moved to attack a thug and accidentally killed him because of the mirror's effect, and the owlbear suffered one unit of madness after being near the mirror. Malkyn spent her remaining legendary actions on two more Eldritch Blasts at Bramgrim.

The enemy line held thugs and a Red Plume Captain. Bramgrim planned a healing spell before moving, and Malkyn attempted to dominate another character. The party was reminded not to target Constance with fireball spells. Malkyn, who has fire resistance, took 5 damage from a fire attack; Felkur cast a fireball and moved for cover. Malkyn used a legendary action to attempt to frighten Daein, and Daein made the saving throw. The Red Plume Captain and his men considered not attacking Daein, but after a brief moment of uncertainty they attacked.

The mirrors were the heart of the problem. They are divine powers that cannot be dispelled and must be smashed. The owlbear was gripped by fear; grappling was raised as a possibility, but dispel magic would not work on the fear effect. The party weighed Sacred Flame and the limits of Lesser Restoration. Using paint to identify the mirror images was not feasible, since the spinning images blocked attacks. Akritas put Hunter's Mark on one of the duplicates, dealing extra damage and keeping the option to switch targets as long as concentration held. An attack roll missed.

Malkyn's fear spell frightened Daein, who managed to resist fleeing. Bramgrim used his mass healing word to restore hit points. Malkyn attacked Daein with disadvantage and missed, and screamed in frustration, realizing that divine powers were interfering with her plans.

The party then defeated Malkyn. The victory caused a significant shift in the balance of power in Hillsfar. The demon lord Graz'zt, who had been using Malkyn as a conduit for his power, was furious. He threatened to confront the group in the Underdark, challenging them to face his true power. Graz'zt's current gambit was thwarted, but the threat remains as long as Graz'zt is on the prime material plane.

Hillsfar began to recover from the pervasive abyssal madness that had gripped it. The party gained a level, to level 10, and is now considered heroes in Hillsfar, with a few weeks of rest and relaxation ahead. Looking forward, the party wants to become "filthy rich" to afford better magical armor; magical armor rewards are to be arranged through Isthana.
