# Chapter 21: Malady of Elventree (1)

Date: 16 Eleasis 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); played 2024-06-09 (Zoom meeting date)

## Summary

At a party, the talk turned to Doctor Jubal, a legendary carnival performer, and a persuasion check drew out the local rumors. The rumors included the construction of a garrison "by the Red Plumes in the west", increased activity by the Cormanthor forest, and the alleged involvement of Bramgrim with a band of dark elves and in the kidnapping of a local family, a family later rescued by a group of adventurers. Bramgrim remained silent about these matters.

A new performer took the stage: a dwarf woman with stark white hair and dusky grey skin, whom the group recognized as "a duergar from the underdark". She played a sweet fiddle that captivated the crowd. The mood of the party shifted when one of the guards used excessive force against a party-goer. Daein tried to defuse the situation with telekinetic movement, moving a chair so that the angry woman fell into it and scooted away. His successful deception earned him the woman's ire.

The fiddler's frenzied music was behind abnormal behavior among the patrons. Akritas used a magical bow to silence the musician, and the peacekeepers took notice. Bramgrim noticed that a young lady with a goat had disappeared, and the party split its attention between investigating the temple of Mielikki and following the lady with the goat.

The party then met Serinia, the gnomish cleric of Mielikki, who had earlier sent them on a quest related to a tree and a trance. They had helped her with a demonic possession in the forest, and she had been happy to see them. Now she was delirious, handing out tokens, and the party's owlbear followed them to her. Daein recognized that Serinia was in the advanced stages of a mind-degenerating madness. Bramgrim cast lesser restoration, and the affected person noticeably improved. Some people had been affected by the duergar's music more than others, raising the suspicion that it was being spread intentionally as a weapon.

Elanil left the party with a charge: find where the duergar and her fiddle came from, likely the Duergar settlement of Zortagra near a Mind Flayer enclave, and search for a cure for the contagious madness. Bramgrim's knowledge of the Duergar clan tattoo and its history would aid the investigation.
