## Summary
The session picked up in the middle of the fight on the river. Monsters acted on one side; Daein and Felkur took their turns attacking them on the other. The javelin of lightning and the Warhammer came into play, and being slowed mattered because a slowed character could not keep up with the boat. A failed saving throw meant damage, and the spiritual weapon was brought in, with a plan to push an opponent into the water.

Shoving decided the fight. Under the new shove action, a character could either push someone 5 feet away or knock them prone. Characters were pushed into the water or slowed down, and a character slowed down or unable to take an action was out of the combat. Positioning mattered, as did the limits of certain spells. The fight ended with "the death of a character."

Victory music played, though it "didn't sound very victorious," and the party took a long rest.

The party then went on into the dark. A scene was described of "a musician is playing in a catacomb," and there was some confusion over whether a "silence spell" affected that sound.

The plan was to arrive at an abandoned temple "to meet a drow who would guard supplies for Szith Morcane." On arrival there was nothing to see but "a big, massive, black area" ("old school, fog of war"). The cavern they had reached "was nearly 50 feet high and had rubble from destroyed statues." The Correction Key names the location as the **Waydown** entrance (Shrine of Twofold Sin).

The temple belonged to the kuo-toa god Blibdoolpoolp, the Sea Mother, and the party was urged to understand and respect the religious traditions of the place and to take part, so as "to gain the trust of the locals." The statues held pearl eyes, and the pearl eyes were a trap: a gas trap, held in by wax seals. The party chose to disable it safely and to come back for the pearls later.

The plan they settled on was precise. Felkur would "poke small holes in the wax seals around the pearl eyes to allow the trapped gas to dissipate safely." The party would wait 5 minutes after that. Only then would Daein remove the pearl eyes. Beyond the trap, the party meant to go "through the large stone doors into the next area," where there were stagnant water areas to check for hazards, a "blood-stained black stone slab and shackles" for Bramgrim to examine, a "ruined hall dedicated to the new god," and a room "with pillars and collapsed walls" whose approach and formation they still had to decide.
