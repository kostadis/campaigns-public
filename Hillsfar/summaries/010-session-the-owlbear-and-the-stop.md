# Chapter 10: The Owlbear and The Stop

Date: 9–14 Mirtul 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-15); written 2023-05-13 (Drive createdTime; play date not stated)

## Summary

On the 9th day of the 1st tenday of Mirtul, Bramgrim exults, "We found the owlbear!" as the party approaches a small cave in a rock that pops out of the forest floor, from which come the telltale sounds of a sleeping owlbear. Bramgrim's suggestion is "Let's kill it!", but Daein has other ideas: ever since the Hermit, Norden, told him of the Owlbear riders, he has wanted his own pet. He has their prisoner stand to one side and bring the bodies and carcasses to tempt the beast. It does not go to plan. The owlbear slaughters the prisoner, eats the carcasses and heads back into the cave, and Daein's attempts to convince it they are friends only convince it that they are food. Bramgrim is annoyed, wanting his reward and a return to Hillpicket, but Daein insists, and they spend a night in the woods to try again the next day.

On the 10th day, Bramgrim marches out of the cave on the Owlbear. "That is so not cool!" grumbles Daein. When he tries to pet the creature it almost snaps his hand off, and the cleric smirks: "I think you will be a tad bit more respectful to us going forward." Daein asks whether he can ride it. "No, but Felkur can." The Owlbear was Daein's idea, and now he walks while the Artificer and the Cleric ride; worse, he can no longer physically intimidate them.

On the 2nd day of the 2nd tenday of Mirtul the party arrives in Hillpicket with the owlbear in tow. Mira Roper is super excited: they have defeated the Green Needles that had been terrorizing the locals, they have defeated the owlbear, and, as Daein realizes from her point of view, she is still alive. At the Hillpicket Inn, the mayor and owner, Hope Kellar, is pleased to see Daein, a little bit too pleased, and suggests he might settle down in a town that can use his strong arms and mighty sword. The confused Daein politely rebuffs her, and when he sees Bramgrim laughing hard he blushes and promises himself the dwarf will pay. He asks Berl Cuttler, the dwarven smith the group had saved from Tak the Doppelganger, to build a cage for the creature: "I'll do it at cost for you, Daein, and your friends. Come back in a week!" With time to fill, Daein asks whether to find the mushroom for Weet Greenspen or go to The Stop to meet "Sharles" aka "Karl." Bramgrim does not care, Felkur is distracted by his beer, and the barbarian still has not gotten over not getting to kill the creature. Remembering that Karl wanted them right away, Daein decides on The Stop.

On the 4th day, Felkur rides the owlbear ("Traveling on an Owlbear is exciting") while Daein trudges behind, and enjoys the growl on the big human's face. Cresting a hill, they see The Stop on the road to Hillsfar, and Daein knows where to go: "The Plodding Plow! We are to meet Sharles there." The arrival of a gnome and a dwarf on an owlbear confuses the locals: "The Law of Humanity" is plainly enforced here, but two non-humans on such a beast command their own respect, and even the Red Plume Guards are loath to approach. Bramgrim ties the creature to a post outside and Felkur marches in.

Sharles, already drinking, shouts, "Come here! I have something to zay to you," and complains he has waited several days. Felkur notices how he looks at the tavern's owner as she approaches, and thinks Karl would have waited for months in this tavern. She asks, "are you the help Sharles promised?" and before Felkur can say no, Sharles declares them "Allies of La Resistance, avengers of the ills perpetrated on the non-humans of this area!" She introduces herself as Glenna Moonsmith: "The drinks are on me if you listen."

Glenna's story: south of The Stop but north of the forest of Cormanthor lie scattered farms and ranches. Frantic ranch hands from the Arcrus Ranch, whose family is highly regarded, rushed in claiming the attackers were "goblin knights wearing crowns." Few took them seriously, since they are known to drink too heavily, but Glenna could tell they were stone sober and scared out of their wits. When Karl asks Daein whether they will help her and La Resistance, Felkur, thinking of free beer, jumps in with "Yes!" Over beers she relays the ranch hands' account: Arduro and Doster were woken by shouting from the main house, and with other hands they saw the Arcrus Ranch ablaze and, silhouetted in the firelight, goblins in plate armor riding monsters ("two-headed wolves, frogs the size of horses, and so on"), each wearing a black crown. Other goblins capered about, slaughtering livestock and setting fires, and when ranch hands tried to fight back with knives or shovels, a goblin on a big centipede-like monster killed them with bolts of fire from his hands. The hands ran all night and saw goblins attack other neighbors, most likely the Callidell's homestead and the Lumbus' farm.

Bramgrim decides at once: "Witches or Halflings? Why it's clear what we shall do, to the Lumbus'. I pray we are not too late." Daein glares at him because Bramgrim forgot to get a reward. Bramgrim reminds him that they have been "the victims of demonic forces three times": the evil powers at the farms, a whole city possessed in madness at Hillsfar, a Doppelganger making a deal with a demon that almost destroyed a town. "We must go and investigate." "But money?" asks Daein. Glenna sees through it and offers 50 gold, and before Daein can argue for more, Bramgrim shouts, "Done!"

Daein asks where he can get a horse and about the town. Karl tells him its history: it was founded by Maalthiir, the archwizard and first ruler of Hillsfar, as a Way Station on the Tradeway; it grew from a roadhouse and barn into a small town; with investment from the Hillsfar Alliance of Merchant Representatives a regal chateau and livery were built, and The Stop became a destination outing for the well-to-do of Hillsfar, who would "brave the dangers" outside the walls with Red Plume escorts, see a play at Cloverwall Roadhouse, then spend a day in the shops capped by an evening meal at The Chateau. Horses can be had at the Livery, run by Kendrick, who will make a good deal, but "If you go in with a non-human, the prices will be much higher." Daein is outraged but sees the wisdom.
