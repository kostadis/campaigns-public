# Chapter 16: Hillsfar Reclaimed

Date: 29 Flamerule – Midsummer 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); played 2024-01-21 (Zoom meeting date)

## Summary
The party is still wanted for the events leading to the destruction of the Colosseum, and their next mission is to infiltrate the First Lord's tower to obtain Torin Nomerthal's schedule. To get in, they plan to disguise themselves using what the Zoom summary calls "long-acting change self", and Bramgrim, Daein and Akritas consider half-elf transformations. The city around them is changing under the influence of a demonic lord, Graz'zt, with shifts in its architecture and color schemes.

The infiltration runs through job interviews, where providing rumors works better than giving answers. The rumors that surface include recent improvements in trade imports and complaints about reckless driving by nobles, and later a rumor of increased patrolling that suggests an important visit. A safe is found in the Chamberlain's office. Roles are assigned: Chad serves food, Daein heads to the kitchen, and Akritas acts as a messenger. The servers are required to wear racist stereotype costumes. The party also weighs how to persuade or intimidate a person to assist Safire, and the idea of creating a diversion.

The schedule, they confirm, is on the ground floor, in the safe. There are three ways toward the office of Malkyn Grenefeld: the stairways, the iron gates, and the red plume guards, who are walking all over the place.

Then the party spots Thessaly Malaeda, betrothed to the First Lord, who bears a striking resemblance to her twin brother, Davon Malaeda. Akritas takes on the task of following her. Thessaly is found speaking into a polished stone, which she then crushes in her hand before vanishing. The party speculates about what this means, particularly for the First Lord's affections.

To get past the increased guard presence, the plan is for Akritas alone, or the group including Bramgrim and Felkur, to use their attractiveness to distract the guards. Akritas, Daein and Safire manage to sneak past. In the office of the Chamberlain, Malkyn Grenefeld, they secure an official letter confirming a meeting between the leaders of Damara and the First Lord. As they leave, Red Plume guards surprise them, but Daein distracts the guards and defuses the situation. With the date and location of the meeting in hand, the mission counts as a success.

At the party, Torin Nomerthal gives a speech. Present are Garos, the Chancellor, and the two similar-looking twins, Thessaly and Davon, handsome, with striking violet eyes and clothing similar to the First Lord's. During the Midsummer celebration a slender male elf attempts to assassinate the First Lord, and chaos follows. When it is over, Thessaly and Davon have disappeared.

What follows is a scramble: the party divides stolen gold pieces with an eye to fairness, then searches for the missing pair, weighing intimidation, creative ways to locate them, and secret doors or passageways. Whether to keep the crowd down while making a run for a door is left open. A fight breaks out, with weapons and disguises in play, and by its end Akritas is in a critical state.

The session closes on a mission to find the First Lord's schedule. The party meets a screaming woman and guards, decides to avoid a direct confrontation, and sneaks into the sewers. The mission is completed, and each of them is rewarded with 50 gold pieces.
