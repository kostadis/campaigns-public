The vault activities may have overlapped; their precise order is not established.

The approaching elevator and grinding stone signaled pursuit while the vault guardian's fail-safe began reactivating. The party found a hidden door and climbed a secret passage to the peak of the Spire of Inevitable Reckoning. Accounts differ over whether Bramgrim or Felkur located the door. At the summit, they entered a circular chamber open to poisonous clouds that prevented flight as an escape route. A permanent teleportation circle offered their way out, but Felkur first had to recalibrate it.

Felkur worked with his artificer tools, collected crystals, and stabilizing nodes received during the earlier Singed Veil visit to Vriska. Those nodes were separate from the newly stolen shard. The recalibration required sustained concentration and multiple adjustments over ten rounds. Felkur completed early stages, installed the shattered nodes, and rewired crystals into the mechanism, providing additional time should his concentration falter. This progress did not complete the portal. Akritas covered the staircase with her bow while the others prepared to defend the chamber; the exact assignments at the iron door and possible floor hatch remain disputed. Bramgrim cast Sanctuary on Felkur, and Daein used his magical mandolin to cast Invisibility, allowing Felkur to continue behind a pillar unseen.

An injured High Inquisitor Veris approached with the stone golem, threatening revenge over the forgery involving his name in the Lexicon. The golem was initially held at the iron door before Veris ordered it torn away and the construct smashed through. It was the same guardian that had begun reactivating in the vault. Daein failed to shove it back, the Owlbear attacked with magical claws, and Bramgrim's Spirit Guardians damaged both the golem and Veris, with Veris partially resisting. Felkur deployed his eldritch cannon against the golem while continuing his portal work. Akritas used Hunter's Mark and landed a shot.

Veris teleported during the confrontation. The accounts describe this either as entering past the frontline or repositioning within the chamber; they do not establish separate teleports or a settled entrance sequence. The chapter ends after that teleport, during round four, with combat and recalibration unfinished. Six rounds remained in the notes' count, but whether that meant time until completed recalibration or portal collapse is unresolved.
