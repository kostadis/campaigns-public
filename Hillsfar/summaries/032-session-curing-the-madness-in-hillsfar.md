# Chapter 32: Curing the Madness in Hillsfar

Date: 13 Eleint 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); played 2025-08-31 (Zoom meeting date)

## Summary

The session picked up mid-fight against the cultists who had defiled the Temple of Selûne. Their cult leader cast a fireball that damaged several of the party, and Daein was particularly affected, taking 21 points of damage from a hellish rebuke reaction. It was likely to be the last combat of the day.

The party defeated the cult and cleansed the defiled temple of the goddess Selûne, and watched its architecture slowly restore to its former state. The temple granted them blessings, meant to aid them in their fight against Malkyn Grenefeld; the GM's notes name the blessing **Moonsilver Clarity**, Selûne's protection (the Zoom next-steps list writes it "Moonsilver Clarity"). The restored temple became a safe haven for them.

The party searched the bodies of the cultists and their treasure. Rolling to discover what lay in a chamber, they turned up a silver pendant, ritual components and religious texts, and a note that contained a message from a captured character.

The note's story was grim. Constance Goodroot had been captured and was being tortured by Malkyn to fuel a ritual. Malkyn believes Constance loves Daein, and that belief fuels her rage. The party resolved to infiltrate the castle where Malkyn is likely holding Constance, but to rest at the Temple of Selûne first. Selûne told them that Constance is safe for now, but warned that Malkyn is waiting to fight them, as she senses their presence.

The temple's treasure was shared out. Among the magic items were a belt of frost giant strength, which raises strength to 27 and went to Daein, and a ring of evasion, which regains charges and requires attunement, assigned to Bramgrim. Felkur was offered an all-purpose tool and a wand of the war mage. An Aetheric Chronometer of Clarity was shared in the Discord chat without a detailed explanation, and Bracers of Archery were named. Daein learned he could attune up to three items. The talk turned to the laws of magical energy conservation: magic items are crucial for rituals and cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed.

Before heading to the castle, Daein suggested they first talk to a friend in the sewers or a Red Plume guard they had helped. Noting the city's chaotic atmosphere, the party decided to approach the sewers casually and search for the thieves' guild there.

Taking stock, they had thwarted one of two rituals. Isthana, who appeared to be in a leadership role, advised that while saving Constance is heroic, the essential goal is to kill Malkyn: Constance's death might weaken Malkyn but is not sufficient on its own. Bramgrim, a cleric, suggested that killing Constance could be bad due to her connection to Selûne, which led to a tense exchange with Isthana, who criticized his perspective as unclerical.

The party worked out a plan to rescue Constance from a chapel in the castle, and debated how best to persuade a thieves' guild leader to join their cause, settling on persuasion backed by Daein's bardic inspiration. The GM's notes record Isthana Ro as their ally, facilitating the "Shadow Path" entry, and Malkyn Grenefeld as confirmed to be at the Obsidian Keep. The session ended at the Obsidian Keep entrance, on a cliffhanger before the big battle.
