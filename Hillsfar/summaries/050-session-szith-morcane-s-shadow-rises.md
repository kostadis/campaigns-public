# Chapter 50 Szith Morcane's Shadow Rises

Date: Jun 14th, 2026

## Summary
The fight continued against both an exposed intellect devourer and a Githyanki whose brain had already been consumed by another. Felkur destroyed the exposed creature and dealt 17 damage to the surviving host. With his Action Surge already spent, Daein dashed ahead to intercept the fleeing warrior while Akritas landed a 19-damage arrow. Bramgrim summoned a Spiritual Weapon despite being unable to see the target directly, reducing the warrior to 6 hit points. The creature disengaged and ran; Felkur's pistol shot missed, but Daein caught and killed the host. A second intellect devourer emerged, and Akritas finished it off. With both brain-eating creatures destroyed, Bramgrim healed the badly injured Owlbear.

Searching the area yielded two opals worth 25 gold pieces each, a circlet worth 250 gold pieces, and a chainmail pouch containing 35 gold pieces. The DM announced a division of 75 gold pieces each. Beyond the final cold doorway, the party discovered a freezing chamber surrounded by columns of ice. A large block of opaque ice held suspended brains, small vials of yellowish liquid, and strange otherworldly symbols. A hidden copper plate bore further markings. The party suspected a nearby chest was trapped and left it alone: they already had the four brains needed for the magically sealed doors. Recognizing the chamber's purpose as biological preservation, they dubbed it a brain fridge.

The party returned to the main area for a short rest, restoring Daein's Action Surge and bringing the Owlbear back to full health. They learned that placing one brain at each sealed door would unlock all the doors. After considering a single entrance, they chose to approach through different entrances, reasoning that the distances between them suggested short passages into a shared central room.

The inner sanctum was decorated with graven images of skulls and brains, illuminated only faintly by concentrated Faerzress. Three tall brain jars stood beside a pile of cushions, with a fourth tilted against the soft surface. The smell of raw flesh carried an acidic undertone, and the room's hard surfaces amplified even quiet whispers. More disturbing whispers infiltrated both the party's ears and their minds. Rogue Thought sat sipping from his brain jars, accompanied by four Githyanki warriors. His greeting was simple: “I have been waiting for a long time.”

The battle immediately turned against him. A warrior failed twice to strike Daein, and Rogue Thought's Faerzress lair attack missed Bramgrim, whom the Mind Flayer had identified through mind-reading as a dangerous spellcaster. Akritas suffered a 16-damage greatsword strike, but Bramgrim answered with fifth-level Spirit Guardians. Akritas combined her archery with Favored Foe and Hunter's Mark for a 23-damage hit. Two warriors tried unsuccessfully to break Bramgrim's concentration before using Misty Step to escape the guardians. The Owlbear found a viable route through another entrance and struck Rogue Thought for 16 damage.

Felkur then carefully positioned a Fireball using its 20-foot radius and the table's partial-square ruling. The final placement caught Rogue Thought and all four Githyanki while sparing the Owlbear. The spell dealt 24 damage before saving throws; warriors one and two failed, while warriors three and four and Rogue Thought succeeded. Daein killed the fourth warrior, then used his recovered Action Surge to strike Rogue Thought twice, dealing 28 damage and leaving him with only 6 hit points before his first regular turn.

Facing imminent defeat, Rogue Thought severed his own tentacles, dropped them, and Plane Shifted away. He threatened revenge from both himself and Graz'zt. One remaining warrior still cried that their master would protect them and struck Akritas again. Bramgrim advanced with Spirit Guardians, killing all but one of the remaining warriors, then used Toll the Dead to finish the final survivor. The party collected the severed tentacles as proof of victory; Rogue Thought's chamber contained no treasure.

The return journey to Sporedome was uneventful. Arrizz accompanied the party before departing with a sardonic farewell: he hoped never to meet them again, but expected any reunion would mean another rescue. In Sporedome, the hiring Mind Flayer accepted Rogue Thought's tentacles. When asked whether the rogue was dead, the party emphasized the evidence they had brought rather than claiming a confirmed kill. The contract required the tentacles. The employer initially offered a Rod of the Pact Keeper, then substituted a Wand of the War Mage +2 for Felkur after determining that the rod would not benefit the party's spellcasters. He also promised to give the relevant intelligence to the Order of the Gauntlet, fulfilling the arrangement that had brought the Emerald Enclave and Akritas into the mission.

Solom Ned'razak declared that they now had what they needed to move against Graz'zt, who was hiding in Maerimydra. With some time available, the party chose to return to Hillsfar rather than linger in Sporedome. They began exploring the possibility of establishing a business and approached Isthana, who invited the entire party to a private restaurant dinner and arranged a feast for their Owlbear, Owly.

Isthana brought Felkur three bottles of rare wine; he allowed one to be shared. Over dinner, the party discussed a business that might support their eventual retirement, with a restaurant among the possibilities. They declined an offer to become limited partners in her existing businesses. Isthana offered a ready-made business, protection, supplies, favorable critics, customers, and even a chef, while making clear that ventures outside her patronage would struggle. The DM explained that she wanted powerful resident adventurers to protect Hillsfar after its previous defenders had disappeared amid the upheaval involving Malkyn Grenefeld and Graz'zt. Daein indicated that the party would tolerate her business provided it did not interfere with theirs. They signed no agreement.

The session ended with a telepathic message to Akritas from the Emerald Enclave. Allied forces had rallied, and the secured braincap mushrooms were pushing back Graz'zt's madness. However, the fire giants controlling Szith Morcane were nearing completion of their work and looking toward the Dalelands and Hillsfar. The party was summoned to join the Drow resistance and meet the returned wizard Murssvayas Dhuurniv, drive out the giants, and prevent the corruption from reaching the surface.

## Locations
### Rogue Mind Flayer's Lair
A dark, dangerous complex filled with moss-covered chambers, magically sealed doors requiring brains to open, and freezing rooms used for preserving biological specimens. Intellect devourers occupy Githyanki hosts within the complex. Supplying a brain at every sealed door unlocks all the entrances to the central chamber.

### The Brain Fridge
A freezing chamber surrounded by columns of ice, containing a large opaque ice block that preserves brains and small vials of yellowish liquid among otherworldly symbols. A hidden copper plate bears further markings. The party leaves a suspicious chest unopened because it already has enough brains to operate the doors. No living creatures are seen inside.

### Rogue Thought's Chamber
A room decorated with graven images of skulls and brains, faintly illuminated by concentrated Faerzress. Three tall decorative brain jars stand beside cushions, with a fourth tilted against them. Raw flesh and a faint acidic smell fill the air. Its hard surfaces amplify whispers, while haunting voices reach directly into visitors' minds. Multiple entrances allow the party to approach from different directions. No treasure is found after the battle.

### Sporedome
A fungal city in the Underdark where the party reports its success and receives its magical reward. The return journey is uneventful. Given an opportunity to stay longer, the party chooses to return to Hillsfar.

### Hillsfar
A major surface city where the party considers establishing a business and meets Isthana over a private dinner. Its former powerful protectors are no longer present, giving Isthana a reason to encourage the party to settle there. Hillsfar is also one of the surface communities threatened by the fire giants in Szith Morcane.

### Szith Morcane
An Underdark settlement near abundant Faerzress, currently controlled by fire giants. Their work is nearing completion, and they are looking toward the Dalelands and Hillsfar. The Drow resistance and the returned wizard Murssvayas Dhuurniv are the party's intended contacts.

## NPCs
### Arrizz
A drow previously encountered by the party, rescued from Githyanki torment and cured of demonic madness with magical mushroom in the previous session. Dry-witted and pragmatic, he had agreed to travel briefly in exchange for a beer. He remains available after the final battle, accompanies the party on its uneventful return journey, and departs with the observation that another meeting would probably mean another rescue.

### Theara Grabelthum
A Duergar encountered in the previous session, found unconscious in a moss-covered chamber and claiming to be an innocent traveler. Under intimidation, she admitted to spying on Duergar operations for the Zhentarim Black Network. She turned invisible during the ambush. In this session's opening recap, the GM clarifies that she is already dead when play resumes.

### Githyanki Warriors
Greatsword-wielding warriors who use teleportation to reposition and escape Spirit Guardians. Some tormented Arrizz in the previous session, while others serve Rogue Thought despite their people's hostility toward Mind Flayers. Two encountered during the ambush conceal intellect devourers inside their bodies. Four defend Rogue Thought's sanctum, and their loyalty persists after he escapes. In the previous session, a Githyanki knight escaped and declared the party permanent enemies.

### Intellect Devourer
A pulsating brain on legs capable of inhabiting a creature whose brain it has consumed. In the previous session, one emerged from a slain Githyanki and left the Owlbear stunned and helpless. Felkur destroys that exposed creature; a second emerges when Daein kills the remaining host, and Akritas finishes it.

### Rogue Thought
A mad Mind Flayer who consumes brains from decorative jars in his Faerzress-filled sanctum. Blessed by Graz'zt and protected by four Githyanki, he uses mind-reading to identify Bramgrim as a magical threat and directs a lair attack at him. The party reduces him to 6 hit points before his first regular turn. He cuts off his own tentacles and Plane Shifts away, leaving the required evidence behind while promising revenge from himself and Graz'zt.

### Owlbear
The party's loyal sidekick, identified during the Hillsfar invitation as Owly. After being stunned and badly injured by an intellect devourer, it receives healing and returns to full health during the short rest. Its 40-foot movement allows it to reach Rogue Thought through an alternative entrance and deal 16 damage. Isthana later arranges a feast for it.

### Solom Ned'razak
A drow archmage who declares that the allies now have everything they need and that the time is approaching to confront Graz'zt in Maerimydra.

### Isthana
Hillsfar's influential criminal leader, who courts the party with private hospitality, rare wine, and offers of commercial support. She wants powerful adventurers invested in protecting the city and willing to coexist with her criminal enterprises. Her proposed assistance includes a ready-made business, supplies, favorable critics, important customers, and a chef. The party leaves her invitation open without signing a deal.

### Murssvayas Dhuurniv
A Drow wizard who has returned to his people and is ready to receive the party at Szith Morcane. The Emerald Enclave directs the party to join him and the Drow resistance against the occupying fire giants.
