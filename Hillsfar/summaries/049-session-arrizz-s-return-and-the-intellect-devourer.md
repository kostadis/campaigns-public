# Chapter 49 Arrizz's Return and the Intellect Devourer

Date: Jun 7th, 2026

## Summary
The party stood before two sealed doors deep within the rogue mind flayer's lair. Their mission remained the bargain struck with Glor: hunt the renegade illithid in exchange for information on disabling the fire giants’ arcane siege weapons threatening the surface.

From behind one door came the voice of Arrizz, a drow acquaintance from earlier adventures: “I was weak once, never again.” The party chose the left door, and Daein eased it open quietly enough to catch the occupants absorbed in tormenting their prisoner.

Six Githyanki — five warriors and a knight carrying a massive silvery blade — surrounded Arrizz, who was bound to a post. They shouted guttural insults and poked at the colorful scars left by his earlier disease, apparently both curious and repulsed. The party’s assessment and Daein’s knowledge of arcane lore helped establish the danger: these were ancient enemies of mind flayers, but their own bitter war and contempt for other peoples offered little reason to expect mercy. Arrizz was unlikely to survive without intervention. Another brain basin also made the captors’ usefulness as potential keys grimly apparent.

Daein, Bramgrim, and Akritas gained the opening attacks. Daein struck the nearest warrior, Bramgrim brought several enemies within Spirit Guardians, and Akritas marked and killed a target before turning her remaining attack on another. The Githyanki answered with repeated Misty Steps, avoiding opportunities for retaliation and trying to reach vulnerable targets. Several warriors attempted to break Bramgrim’s concentration, but their attacks repeatedly missed.

The knight proved much more dangerous to the Owlbear, landing two greatsword blows for a combined 46 damage. Restricted by the narrow hallway, the Owlbear could attack only the foe it could reach. Daein used Action Surge to finish another warrior with a critical hit and wound a second. Akritas continued picking off wounded enemies, while Felkur’s Firebolt missed the knight. Bramgrim brought the remaining opponents into his guardians’ reach, killed a warrior with Toll the Dead, and conjured Spiritual Weapon. The knight then cast Plane Shift and disappeared, leaving the impression that the party had acquired a permanent enemy. Four Githyanki lay dead — enough to provide four brains.

Arrizz was grateful, but erratic and suspicious. He recounted abandoning the surface, which he called the “overbright,” after finding both life there and his smuggling work dangerously unreliable. At least the Underdark presented dangers he understood, and its darkness did not hurt his eyes. An insight check revealed that demonic madness had taken hold of him. The party gave him a piece of their magical mushroom, restoring his familiar calculating demeanor.

Now lucid, Arrizz confirmed that his captors had intended to cut out his brain and put it in the jar. He could not identify exactly when the madness had overtaken him, but warned that this very place was infected. He wanted to leave, then reconsidered the protection offered by traveling with such powerful companions. He declined to join their fighting, offered to cook, and agreed to wait nearby while they finished exploring. The party proposed a test of his cooking once they returned hungry.

The party next headed northeast, deciding to investigate the remaining rooms before placing the brains in the basins. With assistance, Felkur detected an acrid smell resembling freshly harvested alfalfa. Beyond the door lay a humid, stale chamber covered in fungus and slick moss. Large orbs with stalks protruding from their central masses bobbed in the air, and another brain basin stood within.

Searching the muck revealed an unconscious Duergar, Theara Grabelthum. The party woke her and gave her mushroom, which produced no apparent change. She thanked them and immediately suggested walking out through the open door. Her account of traveling from an outpost toward Sporedome and being captured by Githyanki did not withstand scrutiny. Under intimidation, she admitted that her employer was not simply a random outpost, then claimed to have accepted Black Network money to spy on other Duergar and gather information around Sporedome. Another insight check found that she was still lying.

Before the questioning could continue, two Githyanki rose from the muck. Theara turned invisible, and all three enemies acted in a surprise round. The warriors wounded the Owlbear and Daein. The party noticed something wrong about their opponents, and the presence of bodies and brains in the chamber prompted a belated observation that the earlier fight had not been their only possible source of keys.

The Owlbear retaliated with a critical hit. Felkur landed a Firebolt and followed with a pistol shot, though poor damage rolls limited their effect. Daein struck the invisible Duergar, while Akritas killed one warrior with the help of Favored Foe. An Intellect Devourer emerged from the fallen warrior’s head. Akritas marked the walking brain and struck it with her remaining attack, but its psychic assault still stunned the Owlbear.

Bramgrim chose Guardian of Faith, deliberately trying a spell other than Spirit Guardians. The guardian struck the invisible Theara; another Intellect Devourer emerged and also failed its saving throw, leaving it with one hit point. That creature skedaddled when its turn came, and the remaining Githyanki used Misty Step. The session ended mid-encounter, with the Owlbear stunned, a badly wounded Intellect Devourer still loose, and the rogue mind flayer’s secrets waiting beyond the unopened mechanisms.

### Opening the Left Door — The Githyanki Tormentors
#### The party chooses to open the left door and discovers a group of Githyanki tormenting their old acquaintance Arrizz.
- Daein opens the door with a Stealth result of 15. The captors are sufficiently absorbed in tormenting Arrizz that the party can assess the room.
- Six green-skinned Githyanki occupy the chamber: five warriors and a knight, wearing ornate exotic armor. One carries a massive silvery blade.
- Arrizz is tied to a post while the captors shout guttural insults, poke him, and prod at the colorful scars left by his earlier disease.
- The DM recalls the party’s first meeting with the afflicted Arrizz in a basement. His old illness has left visible facial scarring.
- Bramgrim makes an Insight check while the party studies the situation.
- Daein’s Arcana result of 21 helps identify the Githyanki and recall their history: legends describe them as humans twisted and tormented by mind flayers who rebelled and helped destroy the illithid empire.
- Their continuing war with mind flayers has not made them benevolent allies. The party understands that their culture views others as subordinate and useful primarily in that war.
- The DM also recounts legends that their queen is some kind of demon.
- Another brain basin is identified near the room’s further door, reinforcing the possibility of using the captors’ brains as keys.
- The party concludes that Arrizz will probably die without intervention and decides to rescue him while obtaining the brains needed for the mechanisms.
- The Owlbear struggles to squeeze through the approach. The DM suggests an enlargement-and-reduction wand as a possible future solution; none is acquired.
- Initiative and further Stealth checks determine who can exploit the opening. Daein rolls 25, Akritas 20, and Bramgrim 15; they gain the initial attacks. Felkur and the Owlbear roll 4.
