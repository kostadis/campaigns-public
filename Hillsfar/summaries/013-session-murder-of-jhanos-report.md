## Summary
The heroes arrive at The Stop and head to the Plodding Plow. There they meet Karl, who tells them La Resistance has defeated Thaddeus.

Nearby, Jhanos Myrkeer is talking to a halfling. The two exchange something, and then the halfling runs off. Jhanos is delighted to meet the heroes and tells them he has provided them with rooms in the chateau. He asks Rogi to take them there, but Rogi informs Jhanos that he must talk to Kendrick first.

Kendrick is pleased that the heroes have returned from their mission to Elventree for EE, but displeased that his assistant Rogi is being asked to be a bellhop. He is trying to scam the heroes out of some money to stable their horses, and is annoyed when Daein reminds him that Jhanos said everything is being paid for.

Then the evening turns. Karl and Jhanos get into an argument outside the Chateau, and Jhanos dies mysteriously.
