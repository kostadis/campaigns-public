# Chapter 31: Curing the Madness in Hillsfar: An Abyssal Adventure

Date: 8–12 Eleint 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); played 2025-07-13 (Zoom meeting date)

## Summary

The session opened with the party taking stock of their adventure in Hillsfar, where they had discovered a cure for madness caused by a powerful demon. They had successfully treated some individuals, including Bramgrim and Charles, but the city's madness had worsened under the new mayor, Malkyn Grenefeld. The Emerald Enclave instructed them to enter the city and find the source of the madness to defeat it, because the cure's effectiveness depends on eliminating the evil maintaining the madness. The GM's own notes for the session add that Isthana Ro, the long-tenured Head of the Thieves Guild, provided the sewer-to-dock transport that brought them in.

On the docks they came upon a ritual in progress: a red plume guard captain and a Barlgura demon, with the demon's cultist followers chanting around it. (The GM's notes call it an "Abyss-touched enforcer".) Rather than charge in, the party wanted to understand what the chanting was doing before acting. They planned an illusion of a loud explosion to test the cultists' reaction and draw out information about their powers. The demon could see through their illusions, but they managed to hide from him for a while.

The party set a narrower goal than slaughter: try to capture one of the fanatics, or save the Red Plume Guard captain, rather than kill all the enemies. They debated whether to attack the demon or keep hiding, and chose to wait for the right moment to act.

When the moment came, Bramgrim opened the attack with a 3rd level Guiding Bolt. It landed: Bramgrim dealt 19 points of damage and gained advantage on his next attack, while Felkur readied his cannon for a follow-up. The demon's aura forced wisdom saves on the party. Spirit Guardians, called in the Zoom text "a high-level spell", dealt damage to the enemies and reduced their speed, and the demon was fighting at a disadvantage.

Felkur's cannon fired into a 15-foot cone and killed one enemy with a hit of 14 damage. Some of the demon's attacks missed, but it hit Felkur for 16 points of damage, reduced by 8 points by a protective field (the protective field is Daein's in the session's own next-steps list). After a long exchange of blows that also involved spiritual weapons and grappling, the demon was defeated by the combined efforts of the team.

With the demon down, the party turned to the guards. At first the Red Plume Guards took them for enemies, but the party convinced them to help by telling them they had killed a demon and saved their captain, bringing the guards to them rather than attacking directly. They then captured one of the fanatics with a combination of spells and melee attacks, Felkur dealing the final blow, and killed another enemy before the session ended.

The GM's notes record the outcome: Captain Varen, of the Red Plume leadership, was rescued, and the objective now was to clear the docks and pivot toward the Temple. The party's open tasks were to interrogate the captured cultist about their master, heal the unconscious Red Plume captain, and press on to find and defeat the source of madness in Hillsfar so the potion would work. Felkur meant to research and create grenades in his next downtime.
