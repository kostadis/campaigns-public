# Chapter 7: Ambushed Again!

Date: 5 Mirtul 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); written 2023-01-07 (Drive createdTime; play date not stated)

## Summary

Riding with the farmers turned brigands, Daein reflects that "desperate people do desperate things." The brigands do not follow the expected paths but cut through trails only they know; more than once Daein almost makes a wrong turn, and more than once he thinks being a brigand is a tough life.

They reach the road, and it is quiet, until the thunderous hoof beats of horses and the rattle of wagons fill the air. A trio of armored wagons speeds past, fortified and led by four large draft horses. Sharles shouts, "Allons-y!" and Daein and his allies breach the forest line and attack, spells and swords at hand. The Red Plume Captain shouts, "Riders behind! Horses, spears, and bows!" and the chase begins.

Daein and Falofa pull up to the wagons, throw their opponents off and grab the horses' reins. Felkur uses his artifices to some effect. A Red Plume Guard spell caster almost turns the tide but is quickly tossed aside. A few new Red Plume Guards appear seemingly out of nowhere and attack Bramgrim and Felkur, "But they are no match for the dwarf and the artificer." And it ends.

Sharles spots a roadside shrine ahead and has the wagons pull off there. Felkur is eager to see what is so valuable. The shrine stands among fields with several large mass grave cairns; a small building for dedicated offerings to the fallen is the only structure. "Good place for an ambush," mutters one of the brigands. "Never say zat!" shouts Sharles. Then the Red Plume Guards they had defeated but not killed, with some reinforcements they had found, attack. The chapter ends there.
