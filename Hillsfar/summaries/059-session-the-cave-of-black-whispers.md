# Chapter 59 The Cave of Black Whispers

Date: 18–27 Nightal 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); played 2026-09-05

## Summary
The party moved ahead of the army toward the Cave of Black Whispers, knowing that four members of a small party could move faster than any marching force. They found Charles waiting for them deep in the Underdark, seated on the tailgate of a farm cart hundreds of miles from the nearest road, wearing an ill-fitting constable's uniform and a beret, eating from a cloth. Before anyone could ask the obvious questions, he raised both arms and declared that this time it was not for la résistance — it was for Lamour, for love itself. He had built a long pine coffin by hand, made larger than standard to accommodate the tall stature of the old Moonsmith clan, and had come to carry Vragni Moonsmith's remains home for a proper burial on behalf of his beloved Glenna. When Daein playfully patted him and sent him tumbling off the tailgate, Charles declared from the ground that this was precisely why he could not go into combat — he lacked the necessary stability — and settled in to wait for the party to do the dangerous work.

The cave itself was warm as breath and smelled of dry stone and old iron, with the bones of the fallen dwarves glowing from within in patient blue-white light, drifted against the walls like snow. In the center stood a regimental standard — a banner pole topped with a skull, planted firmly with taut guy ropes still maintained — and Bramgrim recognized it immediately as the mark of a unit that had intended to hold its ground. Vragni Moonsmith rose to meet them, a powerful mummy atop a nightmare horse, surrounding the party with a shield wall of spectral dwarves and ghosts on three sides. He offered them a dark bargain: betray their allies the Nerevenans, bring them to the cave to be slaughtered and turned into undead, and he would serve the party's war effort for one day. The party debated the morality of it briefly, with Bramgrim raising an objection he did not press very hard, and Akritas noting with cool pragmatism that the spectral dwarves' hatred of the drow might cause some collateral damage — before shrugging and deciding it was not their problem. The decision was made, and when Oxania, a high-ranking Nerevenan leader, overheard the deal and came forward to confront them, she did not beg for mercy. She unbuckled her sword belt, dropped to her knees, and asked only that the party do it with their own hands rather than walk away. Bramgrim obliged, and when the rest of the Nerevenans arrived and were guided inside, the party locked the door behind them. The dwarves slaughtered the cult in what could only be described as spectral joy, and the party marched away with an undead army at their disposal — and a quiet plan to return and destroy Vragni Moonsmith once the war was won.

The party received word on the march that the stone giants were ahead of schedule on the northern approach and very pleased with themselves, and a cheer went up among the coalition forces. They arrived at the Mirror Cavern, the western approach to Maerimydra, where every surface was polished to a perfect sheen and stalactites burned with unnatural purple and green light, each one standing on its own reflection so that the room appeared twice as tall as it truly was. A mysterious glow-gray drift sat in the center of the floor — something that was clearly not stone. The party walked through the middle of the cavern, and without warning, three creatures erupted from the ground to ambush them, as the battle for the western approach began.

## NPCs
### Charles
A flamboyant and idealistic man who adopts the persona of a revolutionary hero. He traveled deep into the Underdark with a handmade coffin to ensure his beloved Glenna Moonsmith's ancestor receives a proper burial, declaring he is there 'for Lamour.' He has no intention of fighting and plans to wait for the party to do the dangerous work.

### Glenna Moonsmith
The owner of the Plodding Plow tavern and Charles's love interest, whose ancestral connection to Vragni Moonsmith drives the mission to the Cave of Black Whispers.

### Vragni Moonsmith
A powerful mummy and former dwarven commander slaughtered by the drow of Szith Morcane. He sits atop a nightmare horse, bound by duty and fury, and holds his fallen kin in spectral enslavement. He offers the party one day of military service in exchange for the souls of the Nerevenan cultists.

### Oxania
A high-ranking leader of the Nerevenan death cult who overhears the party's deal with Vragni Moonsmith. She willingly drops to her knees and offers herself as a sacrifice, embracing the transition to undeath as a religious fulfillment of her cult's beliefs. She is executed by Bramgrim.

## Locations
### Sporedome
A luminous fungal cavern city serving as the staging ground for the coalition's march on Maerimydra. Its heart contains a great amphitheater where the council of war is held, buzzing with drow, mushroom men, dwarves, and Hillsfar soldiers.

### Cave of Black Whispers
A warm cavern in the Underdark where the bones of dwarves slaughtered by the drow of Szith Morcane glow with an eerie blue-white internal light. A regimental standard planted in the center marks the position the dwarves intended to hold forever, and Vragni Moonsmith holds his kin in spectral enslavement here.

### Mirror Cavern
The western approach to Maerimydra, where every surface is polished to a perfect sheen and stalactites burn with unnatural purple and green light. The reflections make the room appear twice as tall as it is, and the party is ambushed by creatures bursting from the floor.
