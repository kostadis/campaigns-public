# Chapter 51 Berets, Braincaps, and Backup Plans

Date: Jun 22nd, 2026

## Summary
The party found themselves in Hillsfar, still catching their breath from past battles, when Charles — a former revolutionary leader who had fully embraced a dramatic French resistance aesthetic, complete with a beret and an affected accent — approached them with urgent news. His dwarven partner had adopted the same mannerisms, although their conspicuously new, clean clothes made the revolutionary presentation somewhat theatrical. He led them to a tavern he insisted on calling a café, where over strong drinks and fine food, he reminded them of their shared victories against Hillsfar's tyranny. Before the party defeated Malkyn Grenefeld and helped replace the regime, Charles could not even have eaten openly in this establishment. Behind those troubles lay the symbol of the six-fingered demon, Graz'zt.

Charles warned that the fire giants of Szith Morcane were nearing the completion of their work and turning their attention toward the surface, including the Dalelands and Hillsfar. The party's earlier work with the braincap mushroom had created an opportunity to resist Graz'zt's madness and build an alliance. They needed to return to Szith Morcane and meet the Drow resistance, which Murssvayas Dhuurniv had gone ahead to prepare. Daein complained that they had only recently been near Szith Morcane and had just returned to Hillsfar, but Charles insisted that the opportunity could not wait several days. The discussion also revived memories of Bramgrim's naked mushroom revelry and the exceptional alcohol brewed from those mushrooms.

Before the evening was over, Bramgrim suggested asking the Emerald Enclave for teleportation assistance, prompting Felkur to propose a commercial network of teleportation circles called “Stargate.” Charles immediately dropped his revolutionary persona and became Accountant Carl, eagerly discussing an artificers' guild, tax advantages, city guard protection, and exclusive operating rights in Hillsfar. Isthana Ro's interest in making the party Hillsfar's protectors might provide an opening for guild approval. Felkur authorized preliminary conversations about land and property while the party was away; Charles explicitly promised to make no purchases or commitments.

The following day, an Emerald Enclave contact helped the party pass the guards at The Waydown. In the Underdark, a three-foot halfling woman bearing a Harper symbol appeared before them. She introduced herself as Alder, the Harper overseeing Underdark operations, and deflated their pride by observing that most of the people whose exploits she had read about were now dead. She had dispatched five adventurers — Celia, Mor, Spots, Draco, and Connor — toward Menzoberranzan to address the demon lord invasion.

Alder explained that she and other legendary heroes had previously imprisoned Lolth in an interdimensional prison supplied by Vhaeraun. Gromph Baenre subsequently cast a spell that concentrated the Faerzress's anti-teleportation power into an energy surge, freeing Lolth and accidentally opening a passage through which demon lords entered the material plane. The other adventurers' operation involved Vizeran DeVir's counterspell, intended to summon the demon lords into a battle against one another. Graz'zt, however, had a contingency involving Maerimydra. The party needed to prevent his escape from undoing the larger plan.

Alder also warned that Solom Ned'razak was dead and that the resistance would need leadership neither he nor Murssvayas could provide. Felkur's insight suggested that Alder knew more about him than she was saying. Bramgrim obtained her autograph on his sword, and she sent the party deep into the Underdark with a snap of her fingers, sparing them days of travel.

Near Szith Morcane, the party began searching for their allies. A Galeb Duhr emerged from the stone, weeping over its appearance. One half bore a beautifully carved stone giant face with a shattered nose; the other remained rough-hewn. Its concern with beauty was unusual for its kind and suggested that something was wrong. Exiled from the Lodge of Faces by the stone giant elder Decha for being imperfect, it described giants who had become so obsessed with stonework that they ignored fire giant incursions. Encouragement from the party lifted its spirits, and it provided directions to the lodge and its perfect carving of Skoraeus Stonebones.

The party next encountered four Drow, two Quaggoths, and six Duergar scavenging the bodies of defeated slavers. The escapees had been forge slaves sent to search for ore and had seized their chance to rebel. Their Duergar spokesman resented the resistance for failing to free them, but gave the party directions to its camp.

The resistance was in disarray. Murssvayas explained that Quil'bryn had murdered Solom eight days earlier while half the other Drow watched. Although the earlier blood ritual had succeeded, the army it was meant to support now wanted to flee. Felkur climbed onto a table and urged the Drow to unite, earning a natural 20 that counted as two successes. When Quil'bryn opposed him, Bramgrim recognized the warrior's madness and proposed using a braincap mushroom to settle the dispute.

Felkur challenged Quil'bryn publicly and threatened to force-feed him the mushroom at gunpoint, while Daein supplied the successful intimidation. Quil'bryn ate it to prove his sanity. His manner and voice changed, and he realized with horror that he had killed Solom. Drawing his sword, he pledged himself to Felkur and brought the remaining Drow into the alliance, while reserving the question of relations between Drow and gnomes until after the crisis.

With twenty Drow committed, Quil'bryn identified four possible sources of reinforcements: the Hidden, the Lodge of Faces, the Nerevenans, and escaped slaves. Felkur wanted all four. The Hidden followed another Drow god, whom Daein identified as Vhaeraun, and would need to be approached without Lolth-worshipping escorts. Quil'bryn also proposed using allied troops to absorb losses while the Drow and party struck decisive blows, keeping his own role as second-in-command discreet beneath the appearance of a council of war.

Akritas's hostility toward the Drow nearly caused a confrontation before Daein intervened. Discussion then turned to stealing an Arcanaloth's ledger under the identity of the Phantom Thief. An insight check exposed Akritas's concealed interest: the Emerald Enclave High Council wanted to examine particular entries. Felkur insisted the ledger itself would remain his. Outnumbered, Daein agreed to the attempt, provided they abandoned it if the danger became unacceptable and ensured the theft was attributed to the Phantom Thief. The session ended with the main objective clarified: recruit allies around Szith Morcane, then stage the assault on Maerimydra from Sporedome. Fighting fire giants might be necessary to secure recruits, but gathering the army came first.

## Locations
### Hillsfar
A city recently liberated from tyranny and racism against non-humans, now transitioning under a new regime focused on openness and community. Charles emphasizes the change by noting that he can now eat and drink openly in an establishment previously closed to him.

The party receives its mission in a tavern Charles insists on calling a café. Hillsfar is also the proposed base for Felkur's Stargate guild, with possible city protection and exclusive operating rights discussed through Isthana Ro.

### The Waydown
A village serving as an entrance to the Underdark. The Emerald Enclave contact Double E gets the party past its guards with a scowl; the soldiers look over the group, shrug, and permit passage.

### The Underdark
A vast subterranean world through which the party travels toward Szith Morcane. Alder intercepts them here and teleports them deeper, saving days of walking.

Near their destination, the tunnels contain the distressed Galeb Duhr, escaped forge slaves, and the fragmented Drow resistance. Graz'zt's madness threatens to spread upward from this region.

### Lodge of Faces
The home of stone giants led by Decha, whose obsession with stonework has displaced their former honor and attentiveness to threats. According to the exiled Galeb Duhr, they even ignore incursions by Maerimydra's fire giants.

The lodge contains a perfect carving of Skoraeus Stonebones, the Stone Father. The party obtains directions and later identifies its inhabitants as potential allies.

### Szith Morcane
A Drow outpost occupied by fire giants and still ruled by a fire giant lord. Charles warns that their work is nearing completion and their attention is turning toward the surface.

The surrounding region contains the resistance camp and several possible sources of recruits. The session's final clarification makes recruitment the immediate objective; fighting giants may be necessary to win particular allies.

### Maerimydra
A major Drow city conquered by fire giants and influenced by Graz'zt. It is the ultimate target of the planned campaign and the destination associated with his escape contingency.

Alder warns that the operation at Menzoberranzan will be insufficient if Graz'zt can fall back here.

### Sporedome
The designated staging ground where the recruited allies will assemble before the assault on Maerimydra. This sequence is explicitly confirmed at the end of the session.

### Menzoberranzan
The most famous Drow city in the Underdark and the seat of Gromph Baenre's office as chief wizard.

Celia, Mor, Spots, Draco, and Connor are heading here as part of the broader response to the demon invasion. Vizeran DeVir's counterspell is intended to summon the demon lords into a destructive confrontation in the city.

## NPCs
### Charles
A dwarven former revolutionary leader who has adopted a French resistance persona, complete with beret, dramatic accent, and a partner sharing his presentation. His new, clean clothing contrasts with the performance of an underground revolutionary.

He connects the party's liberation of Hillsfar with the unfinished struggle against Graz'zt and urges an immediate return to the Underdark. When Felkur proposes Stargate, he becomes Accountant Carl and enthusiastically discusses guild formation, taxes, exclusivity, and property. Felkur authorizes exploratory discussions, with no purchases or commitments.

### Alder
A legendary Harper rogue overseeing Underdark operations while managing crises across the North. She appears as a three-foot halfling wearing a Harper symbol.

She helped imprison Lolth in Vhaeraun's interdimensional prison and now coordinates multiple responses to the consequences of Lolth's escape. Her manner combines sharp assessments, impatience, and weary humor.

Felkur gets the impression that she knows more than she discloses. She warns the party about Solom's death and Graz'zt's contingency, signs Bramgrim's sword, and teleports the group close to Szith Morcane. The DM describes her disappearance as that of a twentieth-level rogue with divine boons.

### Gromph Baenre
The Archmage of Menzoberranzan whose spell freed Lolth and accidentally enabled the demon lord invasion.

Alder explains that Lolth persuaded him to perform it and suggests he may not have understood its true purpose. The spell concentrated the Faerzress's anti-teleportation power into a surge capable of breaking Lolth's confinement.

### Murssvayas Dhuurniv
A Drow mage and resistance ally who previously sent the party to obtain blood for a giant ritual. He returned to his people to prepare them for the party's arrival.

Although that ritual succeeded, Solom's murder left him with a fracturing resistance. He urgently asks the party to restore unity, warning that they cannot confront the giants' army alone.

### Quil'bryn
An elite Drow warrior who murdered Solom Ned'razak while afflicted by demonic madness. Before his cure, he leads the argument for abandoning the campaign and insists that he finally sees clearly.

A braincap mushroom restores his sanity in public. Horrified by what he has done, he pledges his sword to Felkur and helps rally the remaining Drow.

He remains calculating after the cure: he proposes recruiting expendable allied troops, concealing his role as second-in-command, and maintaining a public council of war. His approval of ruthless planning is presented as a sincere compliment within his outlook.

### Solom Ned'razak
The former resistance leader, murdered by Quil'bryn eight days before the party's arrival while half the other Drow watched without intervening.

He was meant to lead the force against Szith Morcane's giants and support the advance toward Maerimydra. His death leaves that plan without effective leadership.

### Decha
The stone giant elder leading the Lodge of Faces. Decha exiled the Galeb Duhr for being imperfect.

The creature describes the lodge's giants as consumed by their stonework and neglectful of fire giant incursions.

### Galeb Duhr
A sentient rock creature whose right half bears a beautifully carved stone giant face with a shattered nose, while its other half remains rough-hewn.

Its fixation on ugliness is unusual for its kind. Exile from the Lodge of Faces has left it distraught at losing access to the perfect carving of Skoraeus Stonebones.

After Bramgrim's encouragement, it cheers up and provides directions to the lodge.

## Items
### Braincap Mushroom
A specialized fungus capable of pushing away Graz'zt's demonic madness. Charles credits the party's earlier work with it for creating an opportunity to organize resistance.

The party retains a supply and uses one to cure Quil'bryn before the assembled Drow. His immediate change in voice, posture, and understanding proves that his earlier certainty was an effect of madness.

The party also recalls exceptional mushroom alcohol from its previous adventures, alongside Bramgrim's much less universally appreciated mushroom revelry.

### Bramgrim's Sword
Bramgrim's personal weapon, presented to Alder with a quill for an autograph during her briefing.

She signs it carefully, wishes him luck, and adds that she is keeping her eyes on him too.

### The Ledger
A powerful magical record belonging to an Arcanaloth. The Emerald Enclave High Council wants to inspect particular entries, an assignment Akritas initially keeps secret.

Felkur wants to steal the ledger under the Phantom Thief identity and retain it himself, while permitting the Enclave to examine the relevant contents. Daein agrees only with limits: abandon the attempt if it becomes too dangerous and ensure the theft is attributed to the Phantom Thief.

Bramgrim believes their earlier actions have weakened the owner's defenses and reduced his support. Rumors of other powerful magical artifacts at the same location add to the party's interest, but no theft takes place this session.

## Spells
### Teleportation
A powerful magical effect Alder uses with a snap of her fingers to transport the entire party deep into the Underdark. They arrive close to Szith Morcane, saving several days of walking, but must still locate the resistance themselves.

Teleportation also inspires Felkur's proposed Stargate business: a commercial network of teleportation circles operated through an artificers' guild. Charles offers to begin preliminary arrangements in Hillsfar while the party is away; the network is not established during this session.
