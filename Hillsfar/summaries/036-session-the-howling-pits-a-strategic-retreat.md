# Chapter 36 The Howling Pits: A Strategic Retreat

Date: Dec 7th, 2025

## Summary
The party began in Elventree at the Silver Shadow Cave before returning to the familiar sinkhole known as The Waydown, a half-day's journey from Hillsfar. They had traveled this route so many times that they joked about becoming tour guides. This time, they found a partially built wall surrounding the entrance, guarded by two nervous soldiers in splint armor. A nearby tent could accommodate four people and contained a small fire pit. The soldiers recognized the party as the heroes who had saved Hillsfar from demonic possession, but were initially unsure whether they were supposed to stop them or cheer them. Their commander, Vuhm Yestral, had returned to the city with a larger contingent of the Red Plumes, leaving these two guards with no desire to venture below. Daein's invitation to accompany the expedition met with emphatic refusals.

Before descending, the party made preparations for the journey ahead. Felkur crafted uncommon magical gloves for their Owlbear companion for one hundred gold pieces, improving its claw attacks. They also clarified the Owlbear's martial sidekick abilities: its proficiency bonus had increased to plus four, it could score critical hits on nineteen or twenty, and it could use Extra Attack or Multiattack on a turn, but could not combine them. Daein, the only member without natural darkvision, purchased Goggles of Night for two hundred gold pieces. After enduring the others' pointed looks, he could finally appreciate the appearance of the Underdark himself.

The party descended the muddy, hundred-foot sinkhole to a swift-flowing underground river and began their two-day journey to the Howling Pits. Murssvayas's directions and map proved accurate, and the journey was surprisingly uneventful. They traversed increasingly rough, less worn tunnels amid growing patches of fungus and darkness that seemed to choke out all light. As they approached the Howling Pits, a faint piping sound grew louder, and a breeze rose from the branching passages ahead. The howling resembled an unearthly moan and the sound of bones rattling in the wind. The constant wind masked sounds more than seventy-five feet away. The party chose to proceed at a normal pace, keeping Akritas's Pass Without Trace available if needed.

Inside a large cave, a deep howl full of hatred and horror preceded an ambush by two fire giants. They leaped screaming from behind stalactites. One had leathery wings too small for flight; the other possessed an extra eye, signs of the madness affecting the giants. Felkur, whose fire cantrip was poorly suited to these enemies, opened with Catapult instead. His damage dice came up one, one, and two, dealing only four points to a single giant.

The giants retaliated with devastating greatsword attacks. Daein's Protective Field reduced the first hit on Bramgrim from thirty-two damage to twenty-eight. The Owlbear struck back with its newly enhanced claws, while Bramgrim cast fifth-level Spirit Guardians, catching both giants and halving their speed. The second giant then landed a critical hit, ultimately calculated at sixty damage, and knocked Bramgrim unconscious. Daein had already spent his reaction and could not use Protective Field again. Recognizing the danger of losing their healer, the party disengaged and fled, with Daein carrying Bramgrim. The giants laughed and returned to resting in their cave.

After Bramgrim recovered, the party reconsidered its approach. Daein wondered whether the extra eye and wings might be weak points, while Akritas observed that they needed to pass through the area rather than kill everything in it. The encounter made the danger of these giants reaching the surface much more immediate. Following a short rest that restored forty-one of Bramgrim's hit points, Akritas cast Pass Without Trace. Most of the party moved quietly, but Felkur rolled a natural one and stubbed his toe, alerting one giant. Akritas and Daein both rolled natural twenties on initiative and grabbed their slower companions, escaping despite Bramgrim's objection to dwarf tossing. The frustrated giant drove its sword down behind them, then returned to sleep.

The party next reached a section riddled with deep pits and relentless winds. Daein initially proposed granting everyone flight through his Canaith mandolin, but learned that each of its spells could only be cast once before the next dawn. He preserved its Fly spell and instead used Psi-Powered Leap to ferry everyone across. Strong winds still required his passengers to maintain their balance, but the entire party, including the Owlbear, crossed safely.

Beyond the pits lay a wide cave divided by a fifteen-foot underground river, with gray and white fungi covering its banks. Akritas could not identify the growths, even with Daein's help. Bramgrim succeeded and smugly explained that these were Cave Creepers, fungi that could drive someone mad through contact. Daein offered one hundred gold pieces to anyone willing to lick them. Burning the growths was considered, but the possibility of dangerous fumes discouraged that approach. Daein used the mandolin's Fly on himself to scout, discovered that either of two onward passages would work, and followed Felkur's choice of the northern route. He then ferried everyone across without touching the fungi.

Farther on, Akritas recognized poisonous yellow mold just as rising winds threatened to lift its spores into a cloud. Given only a brief opportunity to act, the party held their breath and hurried through the narrow tunnel. Most resisted the poison, but Bramgrim took eleven damage despite his resistance. An initial assumption that he was immune was corrected during the encounter. He succeeded on a subsequent saving throw, and the party continued once the wind carried the spores away. Akritas answered his earlier mushroom expertise with a silent, contemptuous stare.

The next passage narrowed to five feet or less near the floor, while its ceiling rose thirty feet and widened overhead. Bramgrim refused to lead and warned that creatures could attack from above. Felkur investigated and spotted bat-like predators; Akritas identified them as cloakers. After confirming that they hunted with darkvision rather than sonar, the party used Pass Without Trace again and slipped through without becoming their prey.

The session ended as it approached time for the party to sleep, leaving their next rest unresolved. The closing discussion acknowledged that the party was underleveled and that another level was tied to getting through the upcoming combat. The DM praised their creative use of abilities and their willingness to bypass encounters. The players noted that Bramgrim's collapse had forced the change in tactics: without their main healer, continuing the fight had become too dangerous.
