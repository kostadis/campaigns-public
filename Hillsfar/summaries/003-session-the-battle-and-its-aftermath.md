# Chapter 3: The Battle and Its Aftermath

Date: 23–25 Tarsakh 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); written 2022-10-01 (Drive createdTime; play date not stated)

## Summary

At the bridge over the river leading to the ruined elvish village, Deriel runs from the companions into one of the ruins, and Red Plume Guards emerge armed with crossbows, nets and scimitars. Their opening volley of crossbow bolts misses. Some advance on Falofa and Felkur on the bridge while others try to cross the river. For a while the battle is in doubt, but "Falofa’s aura, Felkur’s cannon, Daein’s sword, and Bramgrim’s spells finally turn the tide."

The guards try to snare the companions with nets, and everyone escapes easily except Felkur, who stays in his net for the whole fight and directs his tiny cannon from inside it. At one point he tries to use his snared state to deceive a guard: "I want to surrender! I don’t want to die." The guard answers "I don’t think so." Felkur hits him with firebolts and mumbles "you’re right, I didn’t."

The guards' leader appears in bloodied armor and tries to rally his troops. Daein overhears his name from the guards in melee and shouts "that man is Captain Erlich, and he’s the brother of Deirk Erlich!" The companions realise that Deirk was seizing property his brother had taken from the Tinfellows. "Ethnic Cleansing!" shouts Falofa. Even Captain Erlich cannot turn the tide, and he and his few remaining guards flee.

Afterwards Bramgrim finds Deriel: "Here’s Deriel," he shouts, "she’s dead!" Daein grumbles "Well, that’s anti-climactic." Felkur crows that the guards thought they could trap the companions: "Bet we ruined their plans!" Falofa shouts "death to the ethnic cleansers!"

Bramgrim searches Deriel's body and finds four notes. In the first, Arrizz tells Deriel he is still alive and will stay alive if she does as the Captain says, mentioning his illness and an escape from Duergar bounty hunters. In the second, Erlich sets the price of Arrizz's freedom: "a steady supply of non-humans for Hillsfar Arena." In the third, Erlich calls the raid on the Tinfellows a success, says the other attacks Deriel arranged have also supplied warriors for the arena, and orders her to lead any non-humans who come investigating to "the village of Shadowlight, where my guards and I will wait to ambush them." The last is Deriel's own note to Arrizz: "I have done many horrible things to save you," including sending children, women and farmers to their deaths in the arena, ending with a promise that they "will feast on Erlich’s heart and burn the city of Hillsfar to the ground."

## Locations

### The Bridge and the Ruined Elvish Village
The bridge crosses the river to an elvish village now in ruins. The Red Plume Guards wait in the ruins, and the battle is fought on and around the bridge.

### Shadowlight
The ruined elvish village at the bridge. In Erlich's note, the village where Deriel is to lead investigating non-humans to be ambushed by Erlich and his guards.

### Hillsfar Arena
The destination of the captives. Erlich wants "non-humans for Hillsfar Arena," and Deriel's note says she has sent children, women and farmers to their deaths in the arena.

## NPCs

### Deriel
The guide who led the companions into the ambush. She flees at the start of the battle and is found dead afterwards. Her notes show she has been supplying non-human captives to Erlich for the arena to keep Arrizz alive, and that she hoped to free him and take revenge on Erlich and Hillsfar.

### Captain Erlich
Leader of the Red Plume Guards and brother of Deirk Erlich. He appears in bloodied armor, fails to rally his troops, and flees. His notes show he is holding Arrizz to force Deriel to supply non-humans for the arena.

### Arrizz
Deriel's beloved, held by the Captain. His note to Deriel mentions his illness and a fortunate escape from Duergar bounty hunters.

### The Red Plume Guard
The guard Felkur tries to deceive by pretending to surrender: "I don’t think so."

### Bramgrim
Casts spells in the battle, finds Deriel's body and discovers the notes on it.

## Items

### The Nets
Red Plume Guard nets meant to snare the companions. Everyone escapes them except Felkur, who fights from inside his.

### Felkur's Tiny Cannon
Directed by Felkur from inside his net throughout the battle.

### Deriel's Notes
Four notes found on Deriel's body: Arrizz to Deriel, two from Erlich to Deriel, and Deriel's own to Arrizz. They reveal the deal that drove her betrayal, the purpose of the raids, and the planned ambush at Shadowlight.
