# Chapter 19: Cedric and the Ghost in The Stop

Date: 10–12 Eleasis 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); played 2024-03-10 (Zoom meeting date)

## Summary
The session opens with the party searching after their victory in Hillsfar. The papers, as the GM describes them, are reports on the Wall's construction and a proposed deal to sell Damaran gemstones through Hillsfar's trade network, and on a dead body they find a 'bracers of archery', which the GM's Correction Key records as now equipped by Akritas. They also find a purple gemstone worth 50 gold pieces. A potential mission takes shape involving the Emerald Enclave and a rebellion against Lord Torin: find a sample of the strange purple gems that cause madness, for an additional 50 gold pieces.

They search the rooms, identifying Davon's room and Lord Torin's room, with large rugs, fur-covered cots and iron stoves. Daein works at the locked chest in Lord Torin's room. It holds clothing, grooming supplies and a pouch with a hundred gold pieces; the question of poison in the lock comes up, but no one is poisoned.

The party has ended Torin Nomerthal's regime, which threatened the non-humans, and changed the Moonsea, "changing the course of Hillsfarian governance for the better." Daein asks what the other lords will expect. With guards arriving, the party flees the First Lord's Pavilion. They weigh Hillsfar, The Stop, and Elventree, and decide to go to The Stop to consult with Charles.

At The Stop, the talk with Charles and others turns to the move from the pre-revolutionary phase into a new one, and to where the group stands on social issues and political nominations. A drunken dwarf wants to give hugs and toast to the revolution. The Correction Key sums up the party's state: an "investigative phase" following their successful political disruption in Hillsfar.

Downtime follows, with a religious service and talk of training an Owlbear and socializing at a bar. The party meets a mercenary and a traveler who could be useful for future information. The new government in Hillsfar is volatile after the execution of the former leadership. Bramgrim achieves a minor miracle, and Akritas now owns an owlbear.

Daein asks to go on a small adventure and finds two quests. The first involves a local villager whose child came home with a stolen pure platinum crown. The second is a ghost story: Cedric tells of a restless spirit keeping him awake at night and asks for help confronting it. Daein is interested in helping. Talk turns to whether magic and gods exist, and Daein and some villagers are skeptical, but everyone agrees to help with the ghost.

Cedric leads the group to his house, where there are many workers and a multitude of wood carvings. Daein investigates the carvings and discovers a recurring motif; the group notices an amulet on each carving. The party believes Cedric is an adventurer from elsewhere and wonders how he came to own such a large house and why he carves wooden statues, each with an amulet. The GM suggests Cedric might be suffering from post-traumatic stress. The party considers asking Charles and Biff about him, and the session's next steps repeat it (Biff and Karl, that is, Charles), and asking Liz how long Cedric has stayed in the town.

Standing outside Cedric's house, ready to ask about a ghost, the party hears Cedric's account: a ghost appears at night and is attracted to his amulet. He has been using wooden statues to ward it off, but fears they no longer work, and he asks for help dealing with it, through intimidation or persuasion. The party is confused and skeptical, but agrees to investigate whether there is a ghost at all and, if so, how best to handle it.
