# Chapter 1: The Cloverwall Roadhouse and the Puppet Play

Date: 18–21 Tarsakh 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); written 2022-09-17 (Drive createdTime; play date not stated)

## Summary

The chapter opens in the aftermath of a victory. "We did it!" shouts Falofa: "The shadow is dead, and the evil cultists are caught up in whatever horrible place cultists go." Her arm hurts and the shadow's strength drain has worn her down, so she sits. The man the shadow had possessed then stands up, declares "Well, then! I am free," and introduces himself: "Why I am Ezra." When Daein asks who Ezra is, he answers "Why I am a cleric of Jergal!" Ezra, a man of about thirty, laughs at the companions in confusion until Falofa is close to whacking him with her weapon, and then explains that "priests of Jergal catalog cemeteries and graveyards to make sure everything is done correctly." When Falofa notices Bramgrim about to explode, Bramgrim explains that Jergal is an ancient deity, "a seneschal of Kelemvor, God of the dead," rarely worshipped.

Falofa listens with half an ear while the two clerics, Bramgrim and Ezra, talk. She hears that the temple was dedicated to Waukeen, but its members were secret followers of Graz’zt, the demon lord. That catches her attention. Something had released the soul of the chief priest of Graz’zt, imprisoned there "for eons," and it was that soul that captured Ezra: "Apparently, Graz’zt’s power has grown in the prime material plane." Falofa connects it to children with six fingers, demonic goats and madmen, concludes "plunder, violence, glory, and adventure," and goes to sleep.

When Bramgrim proposes heading back to Ebenz and Rea Farbough for the food, Daein refuses: the cooking is good, but the endless noise and chores are too much. He says one word, "Constance," and everyone agrees. A day's hike later they reach the Goodroots farm, where Constance welcomes them, gives them beds and asks nothing of them. Daein wonders where he can buy armor, since Hillsfar is off-limits to his companions. A gnome merchant named Clidnep arrives in a pony-pulled cart bearing a small tent marked "wondrous emporium." The tent is far bigger on the inside than the outside, and Daein buys equipment there.

The next morning Constance wakes Daein with news: "You’ve been invited to a play!" Personalized invitations call each companion to The Poppet’s History of Hillsfar, one night only at the Cloverwall Roadhouse. Daein is suspicious ("no one knew we were here") and mutters "Not to be trusted," but Constance, who has no invitation, wants to go and sways the group. On the road she talks about the Red Plumes terrorizing the non-human sentients. Daein dismisses this as the talk of a frightened person attacked by rogue elements; to him, "They were always honorable soldiers." Felkur, who has been testing his new cannon on the farm's beets ("I NEED TO BLOW SOMETHING UP!"), takes Constance's warning more seriously: to him the Red Plumes are like guards in many cities, corrupt and self-serving.

The Cloverwall Roadhouse, almost a day's ride from both Constance's farm and Hillsfar, turns out to be a ramshackle two-story inn with a blacksmith. Constance rates its food "reasonable." Laughter rises from the basement, guarded by a man from Shou under a banner reading Poppet’s Playhouse, who refuses Constance entry, grumbling "She can’t come in!" Daein talks him round by asking whether whoever sent the magical invitations would want them to turn back at the door.

The puppet show presents the recent history of Hillsfar: puppet Red Plumes defeat pompous elves, obstinate dwarves and scheming halflings, and the show ends with a puppet in golden armor greeted by cheers of "Torin!" and "Lord Nomerthal!" Felkur finds it racist. As he is about to complain, a regal female elf in a traveler's cloak steps out from behind the stage holding the golden-armored puppet. She knows Daein bought his armor from her friend Clidnep, and she stuns Felkur by calmly saying "the guild had too much money, but I don’t think that was the right way to do things." She introduces herself: "My name is Olisara Lightsong," and says "We who harp wish to enlist your services."

Olisara explains that over the past two months farmsteads between Hillsfar and the Cormanthor Forest have been raided and their farmers have vanished; the latest is the Tinfellows, a halfling family who had aided her allies. She says the raiders are unknown, the farms lie mainly north and west of Hillsfar, and she has found no witnesses, learning of the disappearances only through rumors or messages from concerned relatives. The Tinfellows are sympathetic to the Harpers. She asks the companions to discover what became of them "and rescue… or avenge them." Daein, confused, objects that the propaganda makes the Red Plumes look like racist thugs when he knows them as honorable; Olisara answers "something has changed, Daein. And they no longer are." She gives them a rough map to the Tinfellows' farm, less than a day away on the border of the Cormanthor forest, and describes the Harpers. Felkur's own view of the Harpers is far less flattering: "They are like teenagers armed with a wish spell determined to keep themselves from growing up." He mutters "I work for my guild only."

Olisara adds that the Red Plumes have been seen kidnapping people, which she had thought a tall tale, until Constance insists "no, it’s true" and tells her story. Olisara concludes that a Red Plume patrol up to no good might reveal where the victims are taken, and warns that non-violence will work better given the Harpers' tense relationship with Hillsfar's leadership. Falofa grunts "not fun." When Felkur raises money, Olisara offers 40 gold for expenses and 60 gold each on their return and report.

## Locations

### The Temple
A temple dedicated to Waukeen whose members were secret followers of Graz’zt. The soul of a chief priest of Graz’zt had been imprisoned there; once released, it captured Ezra. The companions have just destroyed the shadow there as the chapter opens, and Falofa says the cultists are "caught up in whatever horrible place cultists go."

### Goodroots Farm
Constance's farm, a day's hike from the temple. The companions rest there, Daein shops with Clidnep, and Felkur's cannon nearly destroys some of the beets.

### Cloverwall Roadhouse
A ramshackle two-story inn with a blacksmith, almost a day's ride from Constance's farm and a day's ride from Hillsfar. Its basement hosts Poppet’s Playhouse, where the puppet play is staged and Olisara makes her offer.

### Hillsfar
The city the puppet show glorifies. It is off-limits to Daein's companions. Felkur is glad to be a day's ride away from it.

### The Tinfellows' farm
The farm of the missing halfling family, "less than a day away, on the border of the Cormanthor forest." Olisara supplies a rough map.

## NPCs

### Falofa
The barbarian of the group. She celebrates the shadow's defeat, suffers from the strength it drained ("A shadow stole my strength. But it shall return."), takes an interest at the mention of Graz’zt, and sums up her outlook as "plunder, violence, glory, and adventure." She finds Olisara's call for non-violence "not fun."

### Ezra
A man of about thirty, possessed by the shadow until its defeat; a cleric of Jergal whose order catalogues cemeteries and graveyards. He laughs at the companions in confusion before explaining himself, and reveals that the soul of a chief priest of Graz’zt captured him.

### Bramgrim
A companion who proposes returning to the Farbough household for the food. Falofa notices him "about to explode" during Ezra's explanation.

### Constance
The companions' host at the Goodroots farm, happy to see them and undemanding. She is excited by the invitations ("But I want to go!"), warns about the Red Plumes, and confirms Olisara's kidnapping rumours with her own story.

### Clidnep
A kindly gnome merchant about 3 feet tall, in tight-fitting overalls, with earrings in his ear, nose, eyebrow and lip and no hair. His pony-drawn "wondrous emporium" is far bigger on the inside than outside. Olisara calls him her friend.

### The Shou Doorman (Gai)
A man from Shou guarding the basement door of Poppet’s Playhouse, who tries to keep Constance out ("She can’t come in!") until Daein talks him round.

### Olisara Lightsong
A regal female elf with long white hair, pale skin and piercing blue eyes, in a simple traveler's cloak. She stages the puppet show as a demonstration of Hillsfar propaganda, knows more about the companions than they expect, and recruits them for the Harpers to find the Tinfellows, offering 40 gold for expenses and 60 gold each on return.
