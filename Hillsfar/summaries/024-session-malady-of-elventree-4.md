# Chapter 24: Malady of Elventree (4)

Date: 22 Eleasis 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); played 2024-08-18 (Zoom meeting date)

## Summary

The party was still on its mission to investigate the source of the madness, in the dark tunnels beneath Zortagra (the Zoom summary places the madness "in the area surrounding Hillsfar and Elventree"). So far they had found a settlement previously occupied by "deep gnomes", now under Duergar control, and had met a mind flayer and a female Duergar who asked for their help to rescue her family. They had discovered a pyramid temple where Duergar danced while a leader played a lute. A samovar was part of the investigation, but they chose not to drink from it after Felkur's unpleasant experience.

The fight came in the dark. Against them stood a Duergar mob, a Succubus with dark vision and a vulnerability to necrotic damage, and Dancing Duergars who could grapple their opponents and compel them to dance, while the party's Owlbear approached. Then shadow demons attacked. The party worked out that torchlight was the answer, since the demons were vulnerable to bright light, and the party tried to keep itself inside the torchlight radius to deny the demons their advantage in darkness. Akritas took heavy damage from the demons' attacks, and Daein prepared to light another torch to extend the safe area.

Akritas was set to defend the fire. A succubus put out the torch, but the party regained the light and kept attacking. Felkur, Daein and Bramgrim took turns on the demons, and the enemies singled out Felkur as their next target. Guiding bolt, spiritual weapon and fireball were in play, along with the favored enemy feature and a sworn enemy that could be activated even if the first attack missed. Healing potions were weighed against how much damage each target had taken. The battle ran over several rounds, and several enemies fell. What the fight made clear was that the party needed better ways to see in the dark: Daein meant to buy dark vision goggles, a Light spell or cantrip for Bramgrim was to be considered, and torches would have to be placed and used better in future.
