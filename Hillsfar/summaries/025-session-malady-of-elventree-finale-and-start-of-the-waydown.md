# Chapter 25: Malady of Elventree Finale and Start of The Waydown

Date: 23–30 Eleasis 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); played 2024-08-25 (Zoom meeting date)

## Summary

The session turned on a samovar. The party debated how to approach a self-proclaimed goddess, the duergar laird, who believed she was "the reincarnation of a goddess after drinking from a samovar", while also weighing the search for the Amulet or phylactery. A hysterical woman had to be handled; persuasion was judged the best approach, and Felkur took the lead in the scene. The party learned of a previous failed rescue mission and of the items stolen from Hledh's lair, among them a magical samovar, and heard about Faerzress, a magical substance of the underdark that prevents easy attacks or assassinations. Maerimydra, a city in the underdark, came up for the first time.

The Duergar who held the samovar were believed to be protecting themselves from the illithid, and the party reckoned they could be convinced to give it up. The gold holy symbol, which the party had used before to defeat the mind flayers, might be used to weaken them. In a trade, Bramgrim tried without advantage, then cast guidance to make sure the trade succeeded. The party also came away with an alchemy jug that could produce various liquids, including beer and basic poison, though not at the same time or in large quantities, and added it to their list of magical items.

The party then traveled to the Emerald Enclave and back to Yûlash, and a guard was pleased with their contribution of beer. They learned that Hillsfar was in chaos following the death of its first lord, and that a female magic user, rumored to have been in love with the first lord, was seeking revenge against them. They decided to avoid Hillsfar and went on to the Goodroot Beet Farm. Along the way they heard of a group mistakenly seeking revenge on Felkur for a crime it believed he had committed, of a possible threat from a human with dark vision, and of wanted posters for the party. The bounty, they concluded, was likely meant for a different group made up of a cleric, a human fighter, a gnome and an elf. Constance has feelings for Daein. They were warned not to stay long in Maerimydra, and the problem of the phylactery remained.

Back in Elventree, the party was told to stay a few days while contacts in the Harpers and the Emerald Enclave gathered information, and Elanil was to examine the samovar carefully and research its properties. Then they met a bubbly-haired red-headed woman and her companion, a drow with fungus-covered skin, both of whom had developed a great distaste for normal food and preferred mushrooms and fungus. From them came the plan: sneak into the underdark through the Waydown Sinkhole to rescue Szith Morcane and the retreating drow, the last bastion of resistance against the drow and the threat of the giants. The party would disguise themselves as minstrels to get into the garrison, with the Owlbear as part of the act, sneak through the garrison to the Waydown after nightfall, and travel 18 miles downriver to an abandoned temple to meet a drow caravan, avoiding major battles along the way.
