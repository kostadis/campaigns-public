# Chapter 5: Rough Summary

Date: 29–30 Tarsakh 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); written 2022-12-10 (Drive createdTime; play date not stated)

## Summary

The chapter is the GM's "rough notes" on the aftermath of the arena. The heroes were victorious in the arena, surviving the Battle Royale and defeating Breex. In the course of the fight Breex's makeup fell off, revealing that he was a half-orc. While the Battle Royale raged, the city of Hillsfar erupted into a riot.

The heroes were able to find the Tinfellow Valley villagers. Deriel Rethslane, the moon elf, then left them to go to Goodroot Beet Farm and find her love Arrizz, promising to help the heroes if she and Arrizz ever can.

Olisara Lightsong was delighted to learn that the Tinfellows were freed, particularly the agent Elbus Tinfellow. She was disturbed to hear about the madness of Hillsfar, and she was not convinced that the First Lord Torin Nomerthal's reign had ended. She offered the heroes membership in the Harpers, and they refused.

Daein wanted to press on, but Bramgrim insisted they take up Olisara's offer of a meal at the Cloverwall Roadhouse, explaining that "some of us ate moldy cookies, and some of us ate well-cooked meat!"

The heroes then returned Elbus and his family to Tinfellow Valley. The Widow Vigdas Miller was pleased to see them. Baar Tworgeir, the Carpenter, apologized to Elbus and offered his free services to repair Elbus' farm. Elbus forgave Baar and decided to create a standing militia, the Tinfellow Guard, and he asked the heroes to go to Elventree and see if any non-human refugees might want to become members of it.
