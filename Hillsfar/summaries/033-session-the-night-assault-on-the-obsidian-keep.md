# Chapter 33: The Night Assault on the Obsidian Keep

Date: 14 Eleint 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); played 2025-10-19 (Zoom meeting date)

## Summary

The party looked back on their recent adventure at the Temple of Selûne, where they acquired magical items and blessings, and turned to the mission ahead: defeating Malkyn, who is holed up in the Obsidian Keep in Hillsfar. Isthana, a thief who had joined their party, laid out two possible entry points. The sewers might be dangerous due to ongoing chaos; the front door could lead to a fight. Malkyn was rumored to be in the possessed chapel, but they would need to confirm her exact location before proceeding.

The party weighed flying in, going through the sewers, or using invisibility, considering visibility, alarm risks and the ability to hide. Flying in looked best despite the risk of raising alarms, because it would let them land directly in the chapel. Going invisible one by one had its own problems: visibility, and the risk of being attacked while alone in the courtyard.

The plan that emerged leaned on distraction and stealth. There would be two distractions: noise-making devices to simulate multiple groups flying in, and illusions to make guards chase after a bait. They would fly into the courtyard at night, when visibility is low, with a fireball for additional confusion. Isthana, "a 10th-level rogue", might climb over the walls to drop the noisemakers. Once the distractions were set, they would fly down to the chapel and get inside quickly, before the guards could respond effectively. If it all failed, Isthana was prepared to claim she was charmed and forced to serve Malkyn.

The details drew argument. Daein proposed different entry points and heights. Felkur suggested a fireball attack, and Daein reminded them that they don't want to kill the guards. The fight was set up with an initiative order, Felkur at 7.

In the night assault, Felkur cast a fireball in a specific direction to draw the guards away from the residence. It distracted everyone and threw the guards into confusion, and Felkur followed up with another fireball, further intensifying the chaos. Guards were redirected to various parts of the castle, including the chapel and the dining room, while the captain ordered some guards to stay at the entrance and others to check the residence for intruders.

The chapel had been defiled and repurposed as a shrine to Graz'zt. Using invisibility and silence spells, the party checked what was inside before moving. Felkur looked in first and found a grotesque and oppressive place, filled with abyssal energy and twisted architecture. Then the fight began, and Malkyn joined it, using her magic weapon ability to gain an advantage against the thugs.

In the battle, Malkyn delivered a dramatic monologue about her lost love and the corruption of Hillsfar. Felkur prepared to cast healing spells; Daein used a protective field to reduce damage from enemy attacks; and Bramgrim planned to cast a second-level Spiritual Weapon spell against Malkyn. Malkyn cast Mirror Image to protect herself from attacks, and the session ended with the battle against Malkyn in the chapel still to be finished.
