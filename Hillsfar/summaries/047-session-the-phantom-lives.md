# Chapter 47 The Phantom Lives

Date: May 10th, 2026

## Summary
The battle was already chaos when the Mezzoloth made its move. With the Wall of Force protecting the party’s escape preparations and Bramgrim’s Spirit Guardians active, the creature considered attacking the caster responsible for the barrier. Bramgrim had cast the barrier. Rather than risk accomplishing nothing with Dispel Magic, the cruel Yugoloth chose the satisfaction of hurting Daein—but both trident attacks missed Daein’s armor class of 21. When the party raised the damage from Bramgrim’s spirits, the Mezzoloth teleported away. Daein pursued through the pillars, landed a blow for 10 damage, and imposed disadvantage on the creature’s next attack.

Meanwhile, the Mezzoloth’s Cloudkill continued to choke the battlefield. Passing a saving throw did not end the danger: the party had to keep making Constitution saves, and successful saves still meant taking damage. Daein and the Owlbear each took 7 poison damage; Daein maintained concentration with a result of 24. Bramgrim also resisted the poison but lost concentration on Spirit Guardians. His attempt to dispel Cloudkill failed after the table clarified that the check used his spellcasting ability modifier rather than his spell attack bonus. With the Owlbear dangerously wounded, Bramgrim weighed healing against offense and summoned a fourth-level Spiritual Weapon, though it did not attack that turn.

The tide turned when Felkur interrupted his invisible vigil to hit the Mezzoloth with Firebolt. Fire resistance reduced the damage from 21 to 10, but the creature still failed its concentration save and Cloudkill dissolved. Felkur’s cannon could not help finish it: he had already sent it away to create a distraction. Akritas followed with an Oathbow hit for 8 damage, without a sworn enemy active and with Hunter’s Mark still on the Shield Guardian. The weakened Mezzoloth tried unsuccessfully to dispel the Wall of Force, teleported again, and was finally killed by Daein.

Meanwhile, three Apprentice Arcanaloths had appeared outside the barrier and shouted for reinforcements before the wounded Mezzoloth’s final turn. By the seventh round, only three rounds remained before escape. The DM clarified that Felkur’s Firebolt had not cost him a round of the escape concentration. The party repositioned and prepared for battle; Bramgrim healed the Owlbear back to 51 hit points, and Daein recovered 18 hit points with Second Wind. Two Fire Giants climbed over the wall. Felkur became invisible again and moved, remaining beside a pillar as required. Another Mezzoloth arrived and repeatedly failed to dispel the barrier.

With one round left, another creature threw a wand to the surviving Mezzoloth, which pressed it against the Wall of Force and disintegrated the barrier. The Fire Giants attacked Daein four times and missed every strike. Daein disengaged toward the green escape circle, and the Owlbear moved into position. An Apprentice Arcanaloth then engulfed the party in a fireball. Every party member made the saving throw, taking 16 damage each, and Felkur maintained concentration. The initiative order gave them the time they needed: the tenth round completed, and the party teleported away in a ball of light.

They materialized in Sporedome, where Solom Ned'razak and Xaven Tanor'this rushed out to greet them. Solom and his drow assistants carefully secured Brandyr, the captured Fire Giant, and moved her into a nearby tent for experimentation. The party emphasized that they had delivered the giant alive, with the blood Solom needed. Solom explained that the ritual would take a ten-day and instructed them to return to Hillsfar. He would remain in contact through Murssvayas Dhuurniv, who had sent them on the quest. The victory mattered, but the battle was not over.

With the heist complete sooner than the DM expected, the party gained a level and discussed new abilities. Akritas took Sharpshooter and increased her Dexterity; Bramgrim improved his Constitution and concentration defenses, reached 111 hit points, and selected Word of Radiance. Daein considered advancing Fighter toward a third attack rather than pursuing more Bard abilities, while the table also explored Bard colleges. Felkur and the DM agreed on a proposed infusion-powered device holding six elemental bolts per day, with damage types chosen when loading it and another casting required for a later change. The device was not built during the session. The legend of the Phantom Thief survived—and Felkur immediately wanted to know whether he could sell merchandise.

## Scenes
### Battle in the Poison Cloud
#### The party finds themselves trapped within a poisonous cloud while battling a Mezzoloth and other Yugoloth threats.
- The session resumes with Bramgrim’s Wall of Force and Spirit Guardians already active. The Mezzoloth attacks Daein.
- The Mezzoloth considers Dispel Magic but chooses to attack Daein with its trident instead. Both attacks miss his armor class of 21.
- When the party raises Spirit Guardians damage, the Mezzoloth teleports away from Bramgrim’s reach.
- Daein has already used Action Surge. He pursues between the pillars after the DM confirms that his position fits under their established 50-percent space ruling.
- Against the Mezzoloth’s armor class of 18, Daein misses once and hits once, dealing 10 damage and imposing disadvantage on its next attack roll.
- The Owlbear moves forward and misses with its claws.
- The table confirms that Cloudkill requires repeated Constitution saves, rather than a single successful save granting ongoing protection. The save DC is 13, and success still causes half damage.
- Daein rolls a natural 20 against the poison; the Owlbear also succeeds. Both take 7 damage.
- The Mezzoloth has moved toward the direction in which its poisonous cloud is drifting. The DM describes the whole area as filled with poison.
- Daein passes his subsequent concentration check with a 24.
- Bramgrim succeeds on his poison save after invoking his advantage against poison, takes 7 damage, and then fails the separate concentration check. Spirit Guardians disappears.
- Bramgrim has no fifth-level slot available to guarantee dispelling Cloudkill. He chooses a third-level Dispel Magic, which requires a check against DC 15.
- After discussion distinguishes his +5 spellcasting ability modifier from his +9 spell attack bonus, Bramgrim’s result is confirmed as 12. The dispel fails, and the DM rules that another character cannot help with the check.
- Bramgrim debates healing the badly wounded Owlbear or increasing pressure on the Mezzoloth. The table notes that healing remains possible if the Owlbear falls and discusses Revivify as a further contingency.
- Bramgrim summons a fourth-level Spiritual Weapon as a bonus action. The table rules that he does not attack with it that turn.
- Felkur weighs preserving his escape preparations against helping kill the Mezzoloth. The DM confirms that completed rolls do not need to be repeated; later, he clarifies that casting Firebolt does not itself cost Felkur a round of concentration.

### The Final Stand at the Wall of Force
#### The party holds their ground against a Mezzoloth and encroaching reinforcements while waiting for their escape window.
- Felkur attacks the Mezzoloth with Firebolt, ending his invisibility. Its resistance reduces 21 fire damage to 10, but it fails its concentration save and Cloudkill ends.
- Felkur cannot follow up with his cannon because it was previously sent away to create a distraction. The Mezzoloth has 16 hit points remaining.
- Akritas fires her Oathbow for 8 damage and misses her second attack. No sworn enemy is active, Favored Foe is unavailable, and Hunter’s Mark remains on the Shield Guardian.
- Three Apprentice Arcanaloths emerge outside the barrier, each with 32 hit points. The DM first states armor class 17 and later enters 18 while creating their tokens.
- The apprentices cannot yet reach the party through the barrier. They shout down for assistance, giving the party the impression that another Mezzoloth is coming.
- With 8 hit points left, the original Mezzoloth attempts Dispel Magic against Wall of Force. Its roll of 7 fails.
- It teleports away without provoking an opportunity attack. Daein follows and kills it.
- The party reaches round seven of the escape preparations, leaving three rounds. The DM confirms that Felkur’s Firebolt did not interrupt the countdown.
- The party repositions and prepares actions for the coming battle. Bramgrim heals the Owlbear back to 51 hit points, and Daein uses Second Wind to recover 18.
- Two Fire Giants climb over the wall. Felkur becomes invisible again and changes position, remaining next to a pillar as required.
- Another Mezzoloth appears, fails to dispel the barrier, and shouts to someone below. A further attempt also fails before round ten.
- Another creature arrives and throws a wand to that Mezzoloth. It presses the wand against Wall of Force and disintegrates the barrier, leaving one round to survive.
- The first Fire Giant attacks Daein in anger over the party’s treatment of its master, naming Veperyd. Both attacks miss; even a result of 20 does not beat Daein’s armor class.
- Reminded to reach the green escape circle, Daein disengages and retreats into position.
- The second Fire Giant targets Daein as a fellow fighter. Both of its attacks also miss.
- The Owlbear moves into the escape area.
- An Apprentice Arcanaloth casts Fireball across the party. The blast deals 32 damage before saves and requires a DC 13 Dexterity save; the Fire Giants are immune to its fire.
- Daein, Felkur, the Owlbear, Akritas, and Bramgrim all succeed and take 16 damage each. Felkur also passes his concentration check.
- The DM initially says the concentration check required 16, then corrects the DC to 10.
- The remaining party members finish moving into position. With the tenth round reached and initiative favoring their escape, the party teleports away in a ball of light.

### Post-Heist Leveling and Planning
#### Following their successful escape, the party levels up and discusses future strategies and new abilities.
- The party receives one level.
- Daein weighs Fighter advancement against more Bard levels, particularly the eventual Fighter benefit of attacking three times. The DM also recommends considering the special abilities available at Bard level three.

- The group jokes about selling Phantom Thief merchandise to recover the money they did not make from the ship.

## Locations
### Sporedome
A safe haven where the party returns after their mission. It is inhabited by Drow and serves as a base for Solom Ned'razak’s magical research. Solom Ned'razak and Xaven Tanor'this rush out to greet the party upon arrival.

Solom’s assistants secure Brandyr in a nearby tent for experimentation. The ritual will occupy a ten-day, during which the party is instructed to return to Hillsfar and await further contact through Murssvayas Dhuurniv.

## NPCs
### Mezzoloth
A cruel Yugoloth that uses a trident and teleportation to harass the party. The original Mezzoloth attacks Daein twice and misses, teleports away from Spirit Guardians, and maintains Cloudkill until Felkur’s Firebolt breaks its concentration.

Daein deals 10 damage and imposes disadvantage on its next attack. Felkur then deals 10 damage after fire resistance, and Akritas deals another 8. Left with 8 hit points, it unsuccessfully attempts to dispel Wall of Force, teleports again, and is killed by Daein.

A second Mezzoloth arrives outside the barrier, repeatedly fails to dispel it, and calls for assistance. Another creature supplies the wand it uses to disintegrate Wall of Force in the final round.

### Owlbear
The party’s sidekick is caught in Cloudkill, takes 7 damage despite a successful save, and misses a claw attack against the enemy. Its declining health prompts Bramgrim to debate healing versus offense.

During the respite before the barrier falls, Bramgrim heals it back to 51 hit points. It reaches the escape area and succeeds on its Fireball save, taking 16 damage before escaping with the party.

### Apprentice Arcanaloth
Yugoloth spellcasters who arrive as reinforcements, call for aid, and blast the party with a fireball. Three appear, each with 32 hit points; the GM gives conflicting armor-class entries of 17 and 18 while creating their tokens.

Initially blocked by Wall of Force, they shout for reinforcements. Once the barrier falls, one casts Fireball for 32 damage before saves. Every party member succeeds and takes half damage.

### Fire Giant
Massive warriors who climb the walls to attack the party in retaliation for the assault on their stronghold. Two appear and attack Daein repeatedly, but miss all four strikes.

The first invokes the insult to their master, Veperyd. The second pursues Daein after he retreats, with the DM explaining that it considers another fighter the honorable opponent. Their fire immunity lets the Apprentice Arcanaloth engulf the area without harming them.

### Solom Ned'razak
A Drow Archmage who receives the party upon their return to Sporedome. He and his assistants secure Brandyr and move the captured giant into a nearby tent for experimentation.

He thanks the party, explains that the ritual will take a ten-day, and instructs them to return to Hillsfar. He will maintain contact through Murssvayas Dhuurniv and warns that the larger battle continues.

### Xaven Tanor'this
A Drow Alchemist who greets the party alongside Solom Ned'razak after their successful escape.

### Brandyr
A Fire Giant captured by the party and delivered alive to Solom Ned'razak’s Drow assistants for experimentation and blood-borne rituals. The assistants take precautions against escape and move her into a nearby tent.

## Spells
### Cloudkill
A sphere of poisonous yellow-green fog cast by the Mezzoloth that fills the battlefield and forces repeated Constitution saves. The table uses DC 13; successful saves still cause half damage.

Daein, the Owlbear, and Bramgrim each take 7 poison damage during the resolved saves. The damage forces separate concentration checks: Daein succeeds, while Bramgrim loses Spirit Guardians.

The cloud ends when Felkur’s Firebolt damages the Mezzoloth and breaks its concentration, even after fire resistance reduces the damage.

### Spirit Guardians
Protective spirits summoned by Bramgrim that threaten the Mezzoloth, which teleports away when their damage is raised.

Bramgrim later loses concentration after taking poison damage from Cloudkill. The spell is not restored during the remaining battle.

### Wall of Force
An invisible barrier cast by Bramgrim, protecting the party during their escape preparations. It remains until a wand of disintegration destroys it in the final round.

The original Mezzoloth unsuccessfully attempts to dispel it. A second Mezzoloth also makes unsuccessful attempts before using the supplied wand of disintegration to destroy it in the final round.

### Dispel Magic
Attempted by both the Mezzoloths and Bramgrim during the session.

Bramgrim uses a third-level slot against the fifth-level Cloudkill. The table sets the check at DC 15 and clarifies that his spellcasting ability modifier applies rather than his spell attack bonus. His total of 12 fails.

The original Mezzoloth rolls 7 on its attempt against Wall of Force. The later Mezzoloth’s attempts also fail before it receives the wand.

### Spiritual Weapon
A floating spectral weapon summoned by Bramgrim as a fourth-level bonus-action spell after losing Spirit Guardians.

Bramgrim chooses it after weighing the Owlbear’s need for healing against the value of pressuring the Mezzoloth. The table rules that the weapon does not attack on the turn it is summoned.

### Fireball
A massive explosion cast by an Apprentice Arcanaloth after Wall of Force falls. Its 20-foot radius engulfs the party, while the nearby Fire Giants are immune to the fire.

The blast deals 32 damage before a DC 13 Dexterity save. Every party member succeeds and takes 16 damage. Felkur also maintains concentration, allowing the escape to complete.

### Word of Radiance
A divine cantrip newly selected by Bramgrim upon leveling up, which causes a burst of divine light to strike nearby foes.

The DM chooses it while filling Bramgrim’s fifth cantrip slot, after considering alternatives including Mending and Spare the Dying. It is selected during advancement and is not cast in this session.
