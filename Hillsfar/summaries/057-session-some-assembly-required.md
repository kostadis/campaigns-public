# Chapter  57 Some Assembly Required

Date: 8–17 Nightal 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); played 2026-08-10

## Summary
The dust had barely settled over the wreckage of King Dengor's palace when Velasta, the terrifying undead revenant priestess, swept into the chamber with blazing eyes and a demand to know who had slain the fire giant king. The party held their breath as Bramgrim stepped forward and carefully persuaded her that the fire giant she truly sought was not the one lying dead at their feet, but rather enemies still lurking in Maerimydra. What made the moment all the more striking was Felkur's quiet realization — Velasta had actually wanted to be convinced. As a revenant, completing her centuries-long revenge would cause her to cease to exist entirely, and so she had been secretly relieved to believe her quarry still lived. With a sharp snap of her fingers, she turned and vanished — though not before leaving behind a fire giant she had previously banished, who now returned to find his king dead and his allies scattered.

The remaining fire giant refused to be cowed. When Daein climbed atop a rock to look more imposing and offered the giant an easy way out, the towering creature simply laughed, sneering that he was not about to be intimidated by a scrawny-looking halfling. The stone giants hurled massive boulders while Felkur sniped from the shadows, and Bramgrim called upon the tolling of a deathly bell that rang out against the giant's very soul. The fire giant managed one last desperate swing, landing a punishing blow on Bramgrim that shattered the cleric's concentration and dispelled the protective spirits swirling around him. But it was not enough — the combined might of the party and their stone giant allies overwhelmed the creature, and the last fire giant fell.

No sooner had the battle ended than a new crisis erupted from above. Daein noticed that the rhythmic hammering from the forge had gone eerily silent, and the party quickly pieced together what it meant — with King Dengor dead, the magical binding that had sustained all the Azers simultaneously collapsed, and every last one of them ceased to exist in an instant. The forge itself began to groan under the stress of untended molten channels, and fire salamanders ran rampant through the chamber above, tearing apart the floor plates with reckless fury. The party had only moments before the area became completely impassable — but sharp eyes spotted several large crates containing finished Hellfire Projectors and a fortune in rare ceremonial metals sitting in the chaos.

Moving with practiced precision, Akritas cloaked the group in a veil of magical silence and shadow while Daein rendered Felkur invisible. The two slipped through the crumbling forge, carefully prying open the giant-sized crates and extracting three finished Hellfire Projectors along with components for four half-assembled ones and roughly a thousand gold pieces worth of rare metals. They moved crate to crate with breathtaking efficiency, narrowly avoiding the rampaging salamanders on each pass. On their final run out, the salamanders finally realized what was happening and let out a howling shriek of rage — but the party was already gone, sprinting clear of the collapsing forge with their prizes in hand. Felkur immediately began eyeing the disassembled ballista components with the gleam of someone already planning their next invention.

Back in the caverns of Szith Morcane, the party arrived to find every faction they had ever promised anything standing in the same room, all of them wearing the expectant look of people who believed they were about to collect. Rilira stepped forward and publicly thanked the adventurers for restoring the city to her, which immediately set off Asala — Velasta's assistant — who asserted a century-old family claim to the goddess and temple sites within the city. Hamadh, the representative of the Cult of Vhaeraun, stood to one side watching the whole scene with barely concealed suspicion, while the stone giants milled about cheerfully, entirely unbothered. Felkur, for his part, ignored the entire political standoff and crouched over the pieces of a massive ballista, muttering to himself about which part went where as the factions nearly came to blows around him.

Then the situation turned truly dire. A bolt of annihilating magic screamed out of nowhere and reduced Asala to a fine pile of gray dust on the spot, silencing the room in an instant of horrified shock. A magical clone of the Drow Archmage Solom Ned'razak materialized, claimed the kill — "now that I've gotten rid of that problem" — and attempted to seize the moment, suggesting smoothly that the question of who owned Szith Morcane could wait until after the war — and that whoever wished to challenge him for it afterward was welcome to try. Daein quietly bolstered Felkur's resolve with bardic inspiration, and Felkur fired a massive ballista into the air with a thunderous crack that cut through every voice in the room. He then delivered a blunt, commanding speech — pointing out that none of them were a global peacekeeping force, that the city's ownership could be sorted out later, and that right now they had a massive army and a city to take back. He even invited the crowd to raise their hands if they had ever been part of a good invasion, drawing a few sheepish hands from the crowd. The Solom clone, faced with Felkur's iron will, fell silent. One of the stone giants began to clap — slowly at first — and then another joined in, and then another, until the entire assembled coalition of Drow, cultists, giants, and undead was united, however reluctantly, behind the march on Maerimydra.

## Spells
### Mass Healing Word
Cast by Bramgrim during the battle in the palace to bolster the wounded stone giants and keep them fighting alongside the party.

### Toll the Dead
A cantrip used by Bramgrim to inflict necrotic damage on the final fire giant, sounding a funereal bell that severely weakened the creature. The giant failed its wisdom saving throw, taking significant damage.

### Spirit Guardians
Protective spirits maintained by Bramgrim that damaged nearby enemies. The fire giant's heavy retaliatory strike against Bramgrim broke his concentration on the spell.

### Pass Without Trace
Cast by Akritas to provide a magical veil of shadows and silence, allowing the party to move through the forge and loot the Hellfire Projector crates undetected by the fire salamanders.

### Invisibility
Used by Daein to conceal Felkur from the sight of the fire salamanders during the heist in the forge.

### Misty Step
A short-range teleportation spell considered by the party as a means to bypass obstacles and reach the crates in the forge quickly.

### Telekinesis
A psionic ability used by Daein to manipulate and move large objects from a distance, considered for moving the heavy Hellfire Projector crates within the forge.

### Disintegrate
A devastating spell cast by Solom's clone during the faction negotiations at Szith Morcane, instantly reducing Asala to dust in a shocking ambush.

### Enhanced Ability
Cast by Bramgrim upon Daein to bolster his presence and resolve during the tense negotiations at Szith Morcane. It was Felkur, with Daein's bardic inspiration, who finally intimidated the Solom clone into silence.

## NPCs
### Velasta
An undead revenant priestess driven by an unquenchable thirst for vengeance. She arrived demanding to know who killed King Dengor, but was persuaded that her true target lay in Maerimydra. Felkur realized she actually wanted to be convinced, as completing her revenge would cause her to cease to exist.

### King Dengor
The fire giant king who was slain by the party during a devastating ambush in his own palace, triggering the collapse of the Azer binding ritual and the destabilization of the forge.

### Fire Giant
A stubborn warrior who refused to be intimidated by the party after Velasta's departure, choosing to die fighting rather than surrender. He managed to land a heavy blow on Bramgrim before being overwhelmed by the party and stone giants.

### Stone Giants
Freed prisoners who assisted the party by hurling rocks at the remaining fire giant forces. They celebrated after the battle and later joined the assembled army at Szith Morcane, where one of them started the slow clap that rallied the factions.

### Fire Salamanders
Destructive elemental creatures that began wrecking the forge floor plates and tearing apart the chamber once the fire giant leadership fell. They eventually noticed the party looting the forge and howled in rage as the party escaped.

### Rilira
A leader who publicly claimed ownership of Szith Morcane and thanked the adventurers for restoring the city to her, sparking tension with other factions.

### Asala
Velasta's assistant who asserted a century-old family claim to the goddess and temple sites within Szith Morcane during the tense faction negotiations.

### Hamadh
A member of the Cult of Vhaeraun who watched the party with growing suspicion during the negotiations at Szith Morcane, feeling that his interests were being ignored.

### Murssvayas
A figure present at the Szith Morcane negotiations who deferred the question of leadership to Daein, expressing a preference to move on to other matters.

### Solom Ned'razak
A magical clone of the Drow Archmage appeared during the faction negotiations at Szith Morcane, attempting to mediate the dispute over the city before being silenced by Felkur's intimidation.

## Locations
### King Dengor's Palace
The site of the final battle against the fire giant king, now filled with wreckage and the bodies of the fallen. The party fought the remaining fire giants here alongside freed stone giants.

### Forge of Surtr
A massive industrial complex within the fire giant palace. Following King Dengor's death, the magical bindings sustaining the Azers failed, causing the molten channels to breach and fire salamanders to run rampant, turning the area into a crumbling death trap where the party looted Hellfire Projectors.

### Szith Morcane
A recently liberated Drow city serving as the staging ground for a diverse and fractious army. Multiple factions clashed over ownership of the city before Daein united them toward the common goal of marching on Maerimydra.

## Items
### Hellfire Projectors
Large, powerful siege weapons found in crates within the Forge of Surtr. The party looted three finished units and components for four half-assembled ones. Intended to be used as ballistas, they are earmarked to arm Rilira's forces for the assault on Maerimydra. Felkur assembled one at Szith Morcane as a show of force during the faction negotiations.

### Ceremonial Metals and Rare Materials
Valuable materials worth roughly 1,000 gold pieces discovered in the forge's storage crates during the collapse, looted by the party alongside the Hellfire Projectors.

### Pistol
A firearm used by Felkur to snipe at the fire giants from the shadows during the palace melee, and later fired into the air at Szith Morcane to silence the arguing factions.

### Bag of Holding
A magical storage pouch used by the party to transport looted goods. The party discussed disassembling the large Hellfire Projectors in order to fit them inside.
