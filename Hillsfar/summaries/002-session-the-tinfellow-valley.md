# Chapter 2: The Tinfellow Valley

Date: 22 Tarsakh 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); written 2022-09-24 (Drive createdTime; play date not stated)

## Summary

Daein weighs the job: "60 Gold to find some missing halflings and 40 gold for expenses. I have had worse jobs. How hard could it possibly be?" He bids Olisara goodbye, and the companions set out for Tinfellow Valley. On the way Constance tells him about the Tinfellows, who laid their claim in the narrow valley over a hundred years ago, just north of the Cormanthor forest, raising sheep and farming roots and hard grains; as they prospered, other settlers joined them.

After several hours they reach the valley. They pass a humble millhouse, its wheel turning in the alpine stream, fruit trees and wheat fields. The ground is well tended though a bit overgrown. Two men and several young boys are laying the base of a new log cabin, and several cottages lie ruined with burnt timbers. "Something bad happened here," Daein mutters. A female moon elf, mud-covered and smelling, emerges from the brush and asks "Are you heroes?" Told they work for the Harpers, she says "I suppose that will have to do." She gives her name as Deriel and says she was there when the Red Plumes raided the valley twelve days ago: "They burned and killed and took prisoners." She claims to have some Tinfellows hidden nearby and needs guards for them. Felkur shouts "We will help!"; Daein adds "After we do a little bit of investigating."

Mrs. Vigdas, a grey-haired widow who has lived alone in the millhouse since her husband's death two winters ago, sees the four strangers through her window and invites them in for tea and cookies. She was a friend of the Tinfellows ("Oh, a beastly business!"). The Red Plumes came twelve days ago and "burned and killed," but spared her: "They opened the door, saw me and said – ‘she’s human’ and then slammed the door." They went "North!"

Felkur, helping himself to a few more cookies, notices the five people building the house (by the module, DDEX3-02: Deirk, his sons Grey, Chort and Jermee, and a hired carpenter). The older man is bony and missing teeth; the younger ones show long-term malnutrition, which is odd in a valley Constance described as well off. The man names himself Deirk Erlich and claims "Marauding elves" damaged the house. Felkur knows he is lying. The young men repeat "we aren’t allowed to talk to folk like you," and Deirk snarls "we have work to do here." Mrs. Vigdas confirms that Deirk and his boys are new, arriving after the valley was sacked: "I think they are from the poor parts of Hillsfar." Felkur decides they will deal with the Erlichs after freeing the Tinfellows.

Falofa takes the tracking. She finds a trail of heavy footprints near the barn heading towards Hillsfar, with halfling footprints mixed among the boot prints. On the bank of the alpine river she finds a dead halfling. Bramgrim observes "he’s dead", then examines the body: the arrow did not kill him, but it was poisoned, and he fell into the river and died. "Evildoers!" Falofa shouts. "And dishonorable guards." Even Daein concedes: "the Red Plumes are not the Red Plumes I used to know."

They look for Deriel, and Daein commands her to lead them to the Tinfellows. She points them over the bridge to "that old elven village," but Daein has figured out that she is not telling the truth. At the old wooden bridge Falofa thinks "it’s a trap" and hears a whistle. Deriel claims she is calling the Tinfellows and urges them into the middle of the village; twice the answer is "No." The ambush springs, as 12 Red Plume Guards and their Captain charge the companions.

## Locations

### Tinfellow Valley
A narrow valley just north of the Cormanthor forest with a long southern exposure and a fresh alpine river, settled by the Tinfellows over a hundred years ago. Twelve days before the companions arrive, the Red Plumes raided it, burning cottages, killing and taking prisoners.

### The Millhouse
A humble millhouse beside the alpine stream, home of the widow Vigdas.

### The Alpine River
Where Falofa finds the poisoned halfling, who fell into the river and died there.

### The Old Elven Village
A small village of destroyed houses across an old wooden bridge, where Deriel claims the Tinfellows are hiding. It is the site of the ambush.

## NPCs

### Constance
Tells Daein the history of the Tinfellows on the road to the valley.

### Deriel
A female moon elf in dirty, mud-covered clothes with tough leather beneath. She claims to have witnessed the Red Plume raid and to be hiding some Tinfellows. She leads the companions to the bridge; Falofa hears a whistle, and Deriel claims she is calling the Tinfellows before the ambush. Daein sees through her.

### Mrs. Vigdas
A grey-haired widow living alone in the millhouse. Hospitable and talkative ("Eat! Eat!"), a friend of the Tinfellows. The Red Plumes spared her because she is human. She confirms the Erlichs are newcomers.

### Deirk Erlich
An older man, bony and missing teeth, rebuilding a house in the valley with his boys. He lies that "Marauding elves" did the damage and snarls the companions away. Mrs. Vigdas thinks he comes from the poor parts of Hillsfar.

### Falofa

### Bramgrim
