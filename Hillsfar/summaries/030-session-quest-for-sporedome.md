# Chapter 30: Quest for Sporedome

Date: 6–7 Eleint 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); played 2025-04-06 (Zoom meeting date)

## Summary
The party, bound for Sporedome to reach Sovereign Agaricus, came upon "a corpse of a derro in a fungal area." After some hesitation Felkur sent his homunculus servant to inspect the body. It confirmed the corpse was "dead and not undead," and reported that "someone will need to physically open the smoldering satchel found on the corpse."

In the satchel was Mallag's journal: "insane ramblings and maps written in blood." Felkur used his insight to decipher it. The journal spoke of "a sacred seer, a myconid visionary who leads her followers," and of "the presence of demon drow in the area," and it gave the location of "the Seer's Dell of Doom, estimated to be a four-hour journey away." The satchel also held gold and gems, which the party divided equally, "with each member receiving 100 gold pieces."

With two choices before them, the Myconids or "the Seer's Dell of Doom," the party went first to the Myconids at the Court. There they found "a collection of injured Myconids and several glass-eyed drow." The Myconids, "led by the sovereign Agaricus," welcomed them and told them about younglings captured "by a group of drow." The sacred seer then announced a reward for freeing them: "a braincap mushroom and everything they could imagine."

The scene left the party uneasy. Agaricus stood over the sacred seer. The influence of Zuggtmoy, a demon lord, on fungi was weighed, along with the chance that the sacred seer was lying, and the connection between Mallag (the child-sized derro corpse) and the sacred seer, with the sacred seer "being confused and possibly possessed by demons." The party decided "to find the stolen children to gain more information about the situation."

They found a camp near a rocky outcrop. It was chaotic, full of "drow humanoids and myconid slaves." The party attacked the drow from a distance with their bows. Felkur won initiative and attacked first, hitting one of the drow. Bramgrim used a spell to kill another drow, but "failed to save himself from the explosion of the dead drow's body." Everyone had to roll a wisdom saving throw, "and no one failed."

Then a demon appeared. The party worked at it with a longbow and "a magic bow to deal extra damage," and in the end the creature took "a total of 22 points of damage." Felkur's next attack against it was a roll of 5. Attacking individual creatures instead of using area of effect spells led to a different outcome than intended. Killing the smaller creatures, it turned out, could turn them into demons. The demon "turned invisible and moved to a different location," and attacks against it were made with disadvantage.

A savant joined the fight, moving and casting spells. The party weighed fireball, lightning arrow and phantasmal killer, and "Spirit Guardians" against the invisible creature, while protective fields and invisibility shaped the movement of the fight. Daein planned "to use a Psi-Powered Leap to gain an advantage."

In the end Bramgrim used an area-of-effect spell "to kill the savant and damage another creature." Akritas and the Owlbear attacked the remaining enemies, "with mixed results." The Barlgura dealt significant damage to Daein and knocked him prone. But the party defeated the main enemy, "leaving only one creature alive for potential questioning." The next session is to question the captured drow.
