# Chapter 8: Hillpicket, Doctor Jubal's Carnival, and the Fools

Date: 6–7 Mirtul 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); written 2023-03-04 (Drive createdTime; play date not stated)

## Summary

The chapter is a brief summary in note form. The adventuring group arrives in Hillpicket to hide after their successful raid on the Red Plume Guards. They stay at the local tavern owned by Hope Kellar, and there they encounter Goodwoman Aelia, an old woman convinced that there is something evil going on in the Carnival. Bramgrim convinces the group to help her find whatever she might be looking for; after all, "it is a free 50 gold." The head of the local guards, Mira Roper, shares Goodwoman Aelia's concerns.

Doctor Jubal's Carnival of Curiosities has arrived. At the carnival the heroes visit several of the curiosities: several games of chance, a set of dancing rats, and a disturbing performance by Pickles and Jam, who will eat anything offered up by the crowd. The heroes offer them a small vial of perfume, and Pickles eats the vial "to no ill-effect. So far."

A group of carnival folk wearing motley and white featureless masks begins to follow the heroes. They are known as fools. They become increasingly annoying until the heroes glare at them, when a carnival worker named Eliah Coastoar shoos them off and the Fools run into the mirror maze.

Talking to a carnie, the heroes learn that Doctor Jubal runs the carnival, pays the wages on time and asks no questions; rumor has it he lost his magic before he started the carnival. He recently brought four new attractions: Madame Darkness, a mirror maze, dancing rats and fools. The fools are trouble and obey him unquestioningly ("never see one without its mask on, never talked to one."). The mirror maze is at the center of the carnival and free to enter: "The fools hang out there. They seem to like it."

They find Goodwoman Aelia sitting on the ground behind a tent, cackling uncontrollably and babbling incoherently: "Fools……listen…remember…someone…mirrors…they…call…us …the…mirrors…". As the final words leave her lips her head lolls, she lapses further into feverish babbling, and a spider web of cracks begins to spread across her wrinkled skin. Without warning, swarms of rats, giant rats, and several fools attack. The heroes unmask a fool and see a face heavily distorted, with drooling lips and madly twitching eyes in a mushed-up face, caught in grinning expressions. Goodwoman Aelia looks a lot like the Fools.

The chapter ends with Daein taking one of the Fools to Doctor Jubal's office to talk to Doctor Jubal.
