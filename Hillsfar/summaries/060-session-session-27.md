# Chapter 60: Session #27

Date: 2026-11-15

## Summary
Combat resumed in the Mirror Cavern on the western approach to Maerimydra, against the three Umber Hulks whose eruption had ended the previous session. Before the fight resumed, Felkur declared his intention to claim the disputed ledger for himself: “I'm getting that ledger, by the way. No one else's. That is my ledger that I'm keeping.”

The cavern’s polished surfaces and purple and green fire created disorienting reflections. As the flying shards were resolved, Felkur used Flash of Genius to help Akritas avoid the worst of the damage; both she and the Owlbear ultimately took 7 damage.

The mirrored chamber made the Umber Hulks’ confusing gaze especially dangerous. Within 30 feet, looking away was insufficient: the party had to close their eyes, escape the creatures’ range, or find another way to block sight. Daein considered Earth Tremor to break the reflective stone, but its small area could not solve the room-wide problem. Felkur’s opening firebolt missed while he fought blind, though his cannon burned the first creature. Daein’s opening longbow critical dealt only 7 damage, prompting considerable table commentary, while Akritas followed with a much heavier, 40-damage volley. Bramgrim and the Owlbear repeatedly succumbed to confusion, leaving them unable to contribute for much of the battle.

Felkur’s Fireball struck all three enemies and caught the helpless Bramgrim in its blast. Then Semdran and a band of Harper rangers arrived from the west. These were troops from the coalition muster, brave enough to enter a fight well above their own strength. Invoking Mielikki, they outlined two Umber Hulks with Faerie Fire and followed with arrows. Their magic helped attackers who chose to look, but did not remove the danger of the gaze or the disadvantage of fighting with closed eyes.

Daein initially spent an Action Surge on a second Vicious Mockery after his first insult dealt only 5 damage; the second casting was resisted. Later, fighting blind and spending luck points, he cut down the first Umber Hulk with his sword, then shot another with his bow. When that wounded creature burrowed underground, he killed it with a single contemptuous magical insult that reached it beneath the stone. Bramgrim finally recovered and cast Spirit Guardians, then maintained concentration through another attack. The Harper archers left the final creature barely alive, and Felkur finished it with a firebolt followed by flame from his eldritch cannon as it collapsed. The ambush was over, the western approach held, and the coalition was one step closer to Maerimydra.

## Scenes
### The Mirror Cavern Ambush
#### Combat resumes against the three Umber Hulks whose eruption ended the previous session; Akritas and the Owlbear resolve damage from the flying shards.
- The party is already in the Mirror Cavern on the western approach to Maerimydra. Its polished surfaces and purple and green fire create disorienting reflections.
- The three Umber Hulks erupted at the end of the previous session. Combat resumes with Akritas and the Owlbear resolving the flying shards.
- Akritas and the Owlbear must make DC 15 Dexterity saving throws. Felkur uses Flash of Genius to turn Akritas’s failure into a success; the Owlbear’s save bonus is clarified as +6 because of its Warrior abilities. Both take 7 damage.
- The party learns that the mirrored surfaces prevent simply looking away from the Umber Hulks’ eyes. Closing their eyes avoids the gaze but forces them to attack with disadvantage.
- Felkur considers burning the mirrors or coating them with soot from Fireball. The DM rules that fire will not solve the problem of the polished stone; magical darkness or effects that damage the surfaces could help.
- Daein proposes Earth Tremor, but its 10-foot area is too small to neutralize the cavern’s reflections. He also considers removing his night-vision goggles; the DM rules that only magical darkness would accomplish the intended protection here.
- The gaze’s 30-foot range becomes an important tactical constraint. The DM adds visible range circles to the map, and Felkur moves farther away.
- Felkur closes his eyes and fires a firebolt at Umber Hulk 1, missing with a 16. His flamethrower-like cannon blast succeeds, dealing 15 damage after the creature fails its Dexterity save.
- Daein moves away with his eyes closed and fires three longbow attacks at the first creature. Only one hits—a critical for 7 damage.
- The group discusses Haste as a future way for Felkur to support Daein, though Felkur does not cast it during this exchange.
- Umber Hulk 3 uses its confusing gaze on the Owlbear. The Owlbear fails with a 14 and loses its turn.
- The same Umber Hulk charges Bramgrim and lands one attack for 12 damage.
- Akritas maneuvers through the cavern and hits Umber Hulk 1 with both attacks. After sorting out her damage bonuses, including Colossus Slayer and Favored Foe, the total is 40 damage.
- Akritas and Felkur also succeed on additional DC 15 Wisdom saves against madness.
- Bramgrim prepares to cast a sixth-level Spirit Guardians, but the start-of-turn gaze check takes precedence. He fails, stands dazed, and cannot cast; no spell slot is spent.
- Umber Hulk 1 closes on Daein and hits him for 12 damage. Daein’s closed eyes protect him from its gaze.
- Umber Hulk 2 reaches Felkur and deals 12 damage. The DM subsequently clarifies that Felkur’s established practice of keeping his eyes closed also protects him from the gaze.

#### As the party struggles against the Umber Hulks' confusing gazes, a group of Harper rangers led by Semdran arrives to reinforce them, and the party begins to gain the upper hand.

- Daein then moves and shoots Umber Hulk 3 with his bow, using Faerie Fire’s advantage to deal another 17 damage.

## Locations
### Mirror Cavern
A cavern on the western approach to Maerimydra where every surface is polished to a mirror sheen. Stalactites and stalagmites blaze with purple and green fire, creating a disorienting environment of endless reflections. The site of the party's battle against three Umber Hulks on the western approach to Maerimydra.

- The reflective surfaces make it difficult to distinguish actual stone formations from their images.
- The eruption at the end of the previous session scattered hundreds of mirror-like fragments, each reflecting the adventurers’ faces; damage from those shards is resolved in this session.
- Reflections make the Umber Hulks’ gaze especially difficult to avoid within its 30-foot range. Distance, closed eyes, or magical darkness offer possible protection.
- The polished material cannot simply be burned away with Fireball. Earth Tremor is considered as a means of damaging it, but its coverage is too limited.
- The Umber Hulks can burrow beneath the floor to reposition beyond the reach of ordinary weapon attacks.

### Sporedome
Prior-session context: Sporedome was the coalition muster site where the party received its orders for the assault on Maerimydra. It is referenced here as the muster attended by Semdran’s reinforcements.

- In an earlier session, the muster brought together the factions, stone giants, myconids, and the party; the coalition was ordered to mobilize within an hour.
- Semdran’s reinforcements were among the troops present at that muster.
- Prior-session context recalled by the quest log: the mind flayer at Sporedome promised a full accounting of Graz’zt’s capabilities. The read-aloud reports that it remains outstanding; this session does not resolve that promise.

## NPCs
### Umber Hulks
Three massive, insectoid subterranean predators with a magical gaze that causes confusion in those who look into their eyes. Their eruption through the floor of the Mirror Cavern ended the previous session; during this fight they could also burrow underground to reposition.

- Their confusing gaze operates within 30 feet and requires DC 15 Charisma saves during this encounter.
- Their attacks are resolved as three rend attacks, with successful hits repeatedly dealing 12 damage.
- Umber Hulk 1 is worn down by cannon fire, Akritas’s arrows, Fireball, and Vicious Mockery before Daein kills it with his sword.
- Umber Hulk 3 burrows after taking heavy damage, only to die underground to Daein’s Vicious Mockery.
- Umber Hulk 2 survives longest, attacking Felkur and later Bramgrim before falling to the combined efforts of Spirit Guardians, Harper arrows, and Felkur’s fire.

### Semdran
A Harper leader who arrived from the west with a group of rangers to reinforce the party during the ambush in the Mirror Cavern. He and his troops used Faerie Fire and archery to support the fight, shouting praises to the goddess Mielikki.

## Spells
### Fireball
Cast by Felkur to engulf the Umber Hulks in a blast of flame. Felkur cast while keeping his eyes closed to avoid the creatures' confusing gaze. The fireball struck all three Umber Hulks but also caught Bramgrim in the area of effect.

- The third-level casting deals 20 damage: Umber Hulks 1 and 3 take full damage, while Umber Hulk 2 saves for 10.
- Bramgrim fails his Dexterity save and is caught in the blast while confused.
- Felkur also considers using Fireball to obscure the mirrors with soot, but the DM rules that it does not work that way.

### Spirit Guardians
Cast by Bramgrim at a higher level to summon protective spirits that swirl around him and damage enemies who draw too close. Confusion at the start of his earlier turn prevented him from casting, without spending a spell slot; he successfully cast it later in the fight.

- Bramgrim initially chooses a sixth-level casting, but confusion at the start of his turn prevents him from casting; no spell slot is spent.
- Once he finally saves against confusion, he casts while keeping his eyes closed.
- The final Umber Hulk fails its Wisdom save and drops from 36 to 16 hit points.
- Bramgrim maintains concentration with a 13 after taking 12 damage from the creature’s subsequent attack.

### Faerie Fire
Cast by Semdran's Harper rangers to outline the affected Umber Hulks in shimmering light, granting advantage to attackers who could see them. Closing their eyes still meant fighting blind.

- Umber Hulks 1 and 3 are affected; Umber Hulk 2 resists.
- The spell does not neutralize the confusing gaze. Looking at an outlined creature within range still risks a saving throw.
- Daein later uses the advantage against Umber Hulk 3 to land a 17-damage bow shot.

### Vicious Mockery
Used by Daein to assault the Umber Hulks' minds with psychic insults. The first casting dealt psychic damage, the Action Surge repeat was resisted, and a later casting — a single insulting word — killed a burrowing Umber Hulk.

- The first successful casting deals 5 damage.
- The second casting consumes Daein’s Action Surge but deals nothing after a successful save.
- For the killing underground casting, Daein confirms that the creature can still be heard; the DM allows the insult to reach it despite the intervening floor.
- The killing word is explained as meaning “Unqualified,” fitting the session’s recurring military humor.

### Hunter's Mark
Used by Akritas to magically mark a specific Umber Hulk as her quarry, increasing the damage of her strikes against it. She transferred the mark to a different creature mid-combat as targets were eliminated.

- Akritas spends a bonus action transferring the mark to Umber Hulk 2 after Umber Hulk 3 burrows.
- Both attacks in that volley miss.
- The group clarifies that Hunter’s Mark benefits Akritas’s own attacks, rather than the Harper archers’ shots.

### Firebolt
A mote of fire hurled by Felkur at the Umber Hulks. Despite the disadvantage of fighting with closed eyes, he used it to steadily wear down the creatures.

- Early attack totals of 16 and 17 miss.
- The DM corrects a momentary rules mix-up: Firebolt remains one attack whose damage scales, rather than becoming multiple attacks.
- Felkur combines later firebolts with his cannon’s separate flame attack.
- The final firebolt kills the last Umber Hulk before the cannon burns it as it falls.

### Earth Tremor
Considered by Daein as a potential way to shatter the mirrored surfaces of the cavern and neutralize the Umber Hulks' gaze advantage, but ultimately deemed insufficient due to its limited area of effect.

- The DM demonstrates the spell’s 10-foot coverage on the map.
- Daein concludes that the affected area is too small for the chamber-wide problem.
- The spell is discussed but never cast.
