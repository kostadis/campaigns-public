# Chapter 37 The Devil's Bargain

Date: Dec 14th, 2025

## Summary
Exhausted and beaten from their journey through the Underdark, the party found a defensible cave branching off the interconnected tunnels. Bramgrim recommended resting there because it had only one entrance. His companions remained suspicious of his judgment after his earlier madness and his suggestion that they follow the river deeper underground. Bramgrim protested that he had merely been suffering bouts of insanity. The party nevertheless settled behind a tall rock formation, rigged an alarm from string and a loud metal object, and positioned Felkur's cannon. The cannon could not be connected to the trap because it required a magical keyword. Bramgrim also suggested spreading out their sleeping positions in case of fireballs. Eight hours passed without incident, whereupon he professed bafflement that anyone had expected trouble.

While the party prepared breakfast, a ragged scream rose above the mournful winds and abruptly stopped. It came from farther along their route, so they investigated under Pass Without Trace. They found Tegan comforting her brother Drystan, who was rocking with his knees clutched to his chest. Both were filthy, dressed in torn rags, and covered in lash marks. Dried blood ran from Drystan's ears and pooled beside a sharpened stick. Tegan stepped protectively between him and the adventurers, suspecting that they served Maram il Vass and had come to continue their torture.

Bramgrim offered to heal Drystan, but before he could begin, two Barlguras appeared behind the party, howling about “the pets.” The demons wanted to recapture the siblings, and the cavern ceiling offered them a possible climbing route over the adventurers. One attacked the Owlbear, dealing 20 damage and knocking it prone. The companion stood and recovered 16 hit points with Second Wind. Bramgrim advanced with fourth-level Spirit Guardians, and Daein struck with his weapons. The second demon afflicted Daein with Phantasmal Killer, dealing 18 psychic damage and impairing his attacks. Akritas then killed the first demon with her bow. Felkur's Catapult, the Owlbear's claws, Bramgrim's guardians and Toll the Dead, and Daein's final attack finished it. The defeated demon became dust and ichor.

Afterward, Tegan explained that she and Drystan had been farmers in the Dalelands before orc raiders captured them five years earlier and sold them to Maram. They had served his guests and attended to his personal needs. They escaped when taken outside to feed on cave mushrooms, choosing the dangerous winds over continued captivity. Although they described the escape vaguely, the party determined that it had happened only a day earlier: the Den of the Whispered Word was close. Tegan remembered a Drow being brought to Maram, though he had not been kept in the palace's main section. She warned of a gray-skinned gnome who answered only to Maram and disliked discourtesy. Drystan had already broken down before their escape; the maddening winds then drove him to thrust their only weapon into both ears. Asked specifically about fire giants in the palace, Tegan said there were none.

The party advanced to 11th level and prepared for their arrival. Akritas gained Volley, Felkur chose a +2 armor infusion for himself, and the Owlbear received 24 additional maximum hit points. Bramgrim prepared magic suited to negotiation and deception, including Command, Tongues, and True Seeing. Felkur considered storing ten uses of Cure Wounds in an item. The GM allowed a long rest for the new preparations and infusion.

About a day's travel later, with Tegan and Drystan refusing to accompany them farther, the party reached a vast cavern containing the Den. A central black and red marble building stood 50 feet high between two 30-foot buildings, its domes edged with blue, green, and purple Faerie Fire. Crossing roughly 100 feet of open ground, they knocked at ornate brass doors decorated with images of a half-elf acquiring wealth and power. Grabber Forth stood beneath his shield guardian and demanded their business. An Arcana check suggested that direct negotiation would serve them better than bluffing, so they simply asserted that they had business with his master. Grabber admitted them.

The closed doors completely shut out the howling winds. Expensive incense and perfume filled halls decorated with fresh flowers, unusual captive creatures, and tapestries celebrating the same half-elf's success. The party saved Bramgrim's sole sixth-level spell slot for the audience itself. In the grand chamber, Maram sat behind a purple wooden desk. True Seeing revealed the apparent half-elf as a Yugoloth; later, Maram abandoned that appearance and stood about seven feet tall with demonic features.

Maram spoke casually of his former involvement in the endless Blood War, named Felkur without an introduction, and knew that an organization believed Felkur had stolen something. He offered to help with that problem for an unspecified consideration, then turned to their immediate business. He confirmed holding Xaven Tanor'this, whom fire giants had sold to him unconscious and badly injured. Maram claimed to have provided exemplary hospitality. The party's objective was to rescue Xaven, and Maram rejected the suggestion of merely lending him out.

His opening price was Felkur. Maram explained that he could exchange the Drow for Felkur, then sell Felkur to the people pursuing him. Daein briefly suggested hearing him out, but Bramgrim firmly refused. Maram proposed Bramgrim instead, then settled on 10,000 gold pieces or two rare magical items. Felkur suggested a con using infused equipment that he said would last only eight hours.

The party requested privacy to decide which items to offer. Maram offered accommodation and promised not to spy, but when Daein extended a hand to seal that promise, he produced a contract. The party insisted on reading its exact terms; Maram reclaimed it as the wrong contract. A replacement privacy contract was signed, covering deliberation without spying or disruption. Maram clarified that any two rare magical items would suffice because he intended to resell them. Two more demons emerged from the back room as the party entered the side office. The exchange for Xaven remained unfinished.

## Locations
### Underdark
A vast subterranean realm, described in the opening recap as becoming almost a daily commute for the party. Its recent hazards include bottomless pits, madness-inducing mushrooms, poison spores, dangerous giants, and ceiling-dwelling cloakers. The current journey follows interconnected tunnels toward Maram's domain.

### Cave (Resting Spot)
A defensible side cave with one entrance, allowing the party to rest without abandoning its route through the larger tunnel system. A tall formation described as a stalactite provides cover. The party rigs an entrance alarm and positions Felkur's cannon before completing an uninterrupted eight-hour rest.

### Howling Pits
Underdark tunnels filled with persistent, mournful winds, also referred to during the session as the Howling Winds. Although supposedly empty, they contained crazed giants, poisonous fungi and spores, and bottomless pits. The wind is dangerous enough that Tegan knowingly risks it to escape captivity; Drystan succumbs to its maddening effects. The scream leading the party to the siblings is loud and close enough to carry over it.

### Den of the Whispered Word
Maram il Vass's marble palace and extra-planar marketplace, situated in an expansive cavern. The cavern's limits extend beyond sight, with slender stalactites and tall stalagmites surrounding the buildings.

The central black and red marble structure stands 50 feet high between two 30-foot buildings. Its domes are outlined in blue, green, and purple Faerie Fire. Visitors must cross about 100 feet of open terrain to reach the ornate brass entrance. Once the doors close, the howling winds are completely excluded.

### Entrance Foyer (Maram il Vass's Palace)
The initial entry area contains two large tapestries on its east and west walls, continuing the front doors' account of a half-elf's rise to wealth and influence. Tall vases hold fresh flowers, while separate glass containers display unusual small fish, birds, reptiles, and other curiosities. Expensive incense and perfume scent the interior.

### Grand Chamber (Maram il Vass's Palace)
Reached through a wide, long hallway and an archway. Its polished white marble floor is veined with red and black. Maram conducts business behind a large desk of unusual purple wood. Other archways lead east and west into long halls, and brass doors stand in the southeast and southwest corners.

### Side Office (Maram il Vass's Palace)
The room Maram offers for the party's private deliberations over the price of Xaven's release. The party secures assurances against spying or disruption before entering. Two demons emerge from the back room during the transition, revealing additional fiendish attendants nearby.

### Xaven Tanor'this
The Drow the party seeks to rescue. Fire giants brought him to Maram unconscious and in poor condition, apparently unaware of his value.

Tegan remembers his arrival but says he was not kept in the palace's main section. Maram claims to have extended exceptional hospitality and refuses to lend him out, insisting on an upfront exchange. Xaven remains captive and does not appear directly before the session ends.
