# Chapter 48 Saving the world with brains.

Date: May 25th, 2026

## Summary
The party had settled into a rare stretch of rest deep within Sporedome, the luminous fungal expanse of the Underdark. Felkur used his artificer skills to construct specialized distillation equipment, and Akritas helped him refine the local mushrooms into increasingly potent concoctions. Daein explored opportunities to make money selling soups and other goods. Bramgrim communed with nature, expanded his horizons, and spent at least one afternoon running naked through the fungus forest. Meanwhile, the consequences of the party's earlier thievery remained unresolved: Felkur still wanted to know what he was supposed to do with the orb.

An Emerald Enclave messenger interrupted this holiday with a summons for Akritas. Information the party had gathered had reached the factions' higher ranks, who believed the Maerimydra situation would require direct intervention. The proposed mission could help with that eventual confrontation. Akritas, swirling a mushroom-infused drink, argued that they should at least attend the meeting. Daein suspected the familiar arrangement of an impending apocalypse being used to recruit indispensable adventurers for free labor. Bramgrim, now dressed but with his hair pointing in every direction, found the prospect of becoming an agent of destiny considerably more appealing.

The debate expanded into Akritas's standing within the Emerald Enclave and Daein's financial ambitions. Akritas could request a scout, had learned Druidic, and could purchase common and uncommon potions, spells, and scrolls through the faction. Resolving Maerimydra would earn her a promotion associated with green eyes and the default friendliness of centaurs, treants, unicorns, and beasts. Daein wanted “FU money”: enough to buy what he wanted without settling down. He favored buying profitable businesses, while Akritas argued that treasure from adventuring could supply the necessary capital—and that personal power mattered when demons and gods could threaten those investments. The party also revisited the ledger from their previous heist, regretting that they had neither stolen nor examined it, though Daein insisted there had been no time.

The meeting took place inside a mostly stable, hollowed-out dead mushroom the size of a small house. A cowled figure named Glor greeted them telepathically. Several attempts to identify something unusual about their host failed, and Akritas's attempt to discreetly alert Felkur was compromised by her drinking. Glor ultimately explained that he was a mind flayer from Ryxyg, an illithid encampment several miles below Sporedome that traded peacefully with myconids. Bramgrim raised his weapon and challenged the idea of helping a member of a species known for slavery. Glor maintained that Ryxyg had found peaceful cohabitation more productive.

Glor claimed that Maerimydra's demon-tainted fire giants had constructed devastating arcane siege weapons and intended to attack the surface, beginning with Elventree and Hillsfar. He possessed information that could disable those engines from a distance while keeping the saboteurs' identities secret. Surface devastation was not, in itself, a reason for Ryxyg to intervene: the GM explained that war-ravaged populations could provide mind flayers with food and opportunities. Glor therefore wanted something valuable in return—the destruction of a “rogue thought,” a mind flayer that had rejected the elder brain's collective.

The party questioned both the ethics of killing an independent creature and the reliability of Glor's promised payment. Felkur objected to taking away free will and demanded assurance that the intelligence actually existed. The GM explained that an independent mind flayer could still be a dangerous predator, while leaving the decision to the party. Bramgrim proposed threatening consequences if Ryxyg lied. With help from his companions, Daein delivered a warning strong enough to make Glor reassess them. Glor acknowledged that the Order of the Gauntlet might have been right to recommend them, then disclosed why his own community had failed: the rogue's lair was saturated with Faerzress, which disrupted their abilities, and the rogue likely possessed powers unusual for its kind. The party accepted the bargain, including a small additional reward for returning a severed tentacle, and received a map.

The ten-day journey passed without encounters. During the final two days, the Faerzress became so pervasive that everyone experienced a constant buzz inside their heads. At the entrance, the party checked for sounds before opening the door. Daein led, Bramgrim followed, and Akritas stayed behind with her bow. Felkur selected ice bullets from his specialized ammunition.

Inside, colored pools cast dim light across a chamber containing four bodies arranged around a table: three human men and a drow woman. Each skull bore four holes through which its brain had been removed. None of the bodies carried valuables, allegiance insignia, or possessions identifying their origins. The table held fresh-looking food, empty scroll tubes, playing cards, commercial agreements involving an unnamed drow outpost and the Red Blooms, and detailed maps of Hillsfar, Phlan, and Mulmaster. Felkur recognized the destruction marked on Mulmaster's newer map as the work of elemental cults; much of the city was expected to starve. The GM connected the devastation to accusations against the Phantom Ghost for exposing the city's supposedly impenetrable protections.

A basin carved to resemble the underside of a human brain proved to be the key to an arcanely sealed door. A brain would have to be placed inside it; neither removing the hinges nor Dispel Magic would bypass the mechanism. With no usable brain found among the corpses, the party investigated the other routes. Beyond the northwest door, guttural voices hurled insults at someone responding defiantly in Undercommon. Daein recognized Arrizz, the drow associated with their earlier Hillsfar and beet-farm adventures and the beginning of his relationship with Candace. Rather than enter immediately, the party checked the northeast door. It was markedly colder than the surrounding area. They ended the session there, with neither side door opened and the brain lock unresolved.

## Locations
### Sporedome
A vibrant fungal environment in the Underdark filled with edible and hallucinogenic mushrooms. The party spends several days resting here, experimenting with distillation and considering a return to the surface. Its fungus forest provides the setting for Bramgrim's uninhibited communion with nature.

### Ryxyg
An illithid encampment several miles below Sporedome that engages in peaceful trade with myconids. Glor claims its mind flayers favor cohabitation over enslavement. The community nevertheless regards a mind flayer's rejection of its elder brain as a threat and has unsuccessfully sent its own forces against the rogue.

### Hollowed-out Mushroom Dwelling
A mostly stable dead mushroom roughly the size of a small house, used for the meeting with Glor. No creatures are visible outside. Windowed doors lead into a single large chamber containing stools, a low table, and a desk.

### Maerimydra
A drow city occupied by fire giants with demonic ties. Glor says the giants have been unusually productive and constructed arcane siege engines for attacks on the surface, beginning with Elventree and Hillsfar. The factions anticipate direct action against the city, while Akritas also sees potential treasure there.

### The Rogue Thought's Lair
A foreboding Underdark complex reached after ten days of travel. Faerzress becomes especially pervasive during the final two days of the approach, producing a constant mental buzz.

The entrance chamber contains colored luminous pools, four brainless corpses, and a table crowded with intelligence about surface cities and Underdark trade. A brain-shaped basin controls an arcanely sealed door. The northwest route leads toward Arrizz's apparent captivity; the northeast door is unusually cold. Neither side chamber has been entered.

## NPCs
### Emerald Enclave Messenger
A representative who locates the party in Sporedome and brings Akritas a mission request involving several factions. The messenger's arrival interrupts the party's mushroom experiments and business discussions and leads to the meeting with Glor.

### Glor
A cowled mind flayer from Ryxyg who communicates telepathically. His community trades peacefully with myconids, and he claims it has rejected the enslavement practiced by many illithids.

Glor offers secret methods for disabling Maerimydra's arcane siege weapons in exchange for killing a rogue mind flayer. His concern is the rogue's rejection of the collective, rather than the surface settlements endangered by the giants. He also offers a small reward for a severed tentacle.

Initially dismissive and willing to seek other adventurers, Glor becomes impressed after Daein's assisted warning. He then explains his community's failures in the Faerzress-saturated lair and warns that the rogue likely possesses unusual powers. His dry threats about eating the party punctuate the negotiations.

### Arrizz
A drow acquaintance connected to the party's earlier Hillsfar adventures and beet-farm rescue, remembered alongside the beginnings of Daein's relationship with Candace. He is apparently imprisoned beyond the northwest door in the rogue's lair. The party hears him answering his tormentors' guttural insults with defiant curses in Undercommon but has not yet seen or reached him.

## Items
### Hallucinogenic Mushrooms
Unique Underdark fungi used in Felkur and Akritas's distillation experiments and Bramgrim's nature communion. Felkur offers to bring a newly developed variety on the mission. Bramgrim eagerly accepts and begins undressing in preparation, drawing Akritas's objection. Akritas also drinks a mushroom-infused alcoholic concoction during the summons discussion.

### Mushroom Distillery
Specialized equipment constructed using Felkur's artificer skills. Akritas helps refine the mushroom-distillation techniques. Their experiments become both a source of recreation and a potential business opportunity.

### Map of Hillsfar
A large, highly detailed map showing every street, building, and some infrastructure. It lies spread across the rogue's table beneath other materials and could fetch a substantial price from the right buyer. Daein explicitly directs the party to take it.

### Map of Phlan
A detailed map approximately one or two years old. It depicts the Thicket, a dense dome of brambles and briars erected by the green dragon that conquered the city. Found among the rogue's extensive collection of surface intelligence.

### Maps of Mulmaster
Two maps folded into quarters. The newer map gives limited attention to the harbor and marks destroyed sections with large red Xs and repeated “ha-ha-ha” annotations. The older map records trade routes and guard rotations and schedules in its margins.

Felkur recognizes the devastation as the result of elemental cult attacks, with starvation expected across much of the city. The guard details make the maps valuable to an interested buyer.

### Elemental Ice Bullets
Specialized ammunition selected by Felkur before entering the lair. His equipment allows him to choose among different attack types; he settles on ice after discussing possible enemy resistances. No shots are fired during the session.

### Brain Basin
A stone basin carved to resemble the underside of a human brain, with space for a brain of human size. Bramgrim recognizes the carving, and further examination establishes its role as the key to the nearby arcanely sealed door.

An actual brain must be placed inside to open the door. The four nearby corpses have already lost theirs, and the party has not yet supplied a replacement.

### Map to the Rogue's Lair
Glor's map shows the path of least resistance through the Underdark to the rogue's lair. The journey takes approximately ten days and passes without encounters; the strongest Faerzress effects occur during its final two days.

## Spells
### Magically Sealed Door
A door in the rogue's lair is magically and arcanely locked. The GM rules that removing its hinges will not bypass the enchantment and that Dispel Magic will not open it. Examination of the associated basin reveals the required method: place a human-sized brain inside. The mechanism remains unresolved at the end of the session.
