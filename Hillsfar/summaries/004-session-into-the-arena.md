# Chapter 4: Into the arena!

Date: 26–28 Tarsakh 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); written 2022-11-11 (Drive createdTime; play date not stated)

## Summary

The abandoned elven village falls quiet after the battle. "Now what?" asks Daein, the hulking fighter. Everyone they could question is dead. Bramgrim suggests following tracks, but Felkur, the artificer, disagrees: "we’re wounded. We can’t face another group of soldiers." Daein decides to return to the Cloverwall Roadhouse and consult Olisara about freeing the halflings. Falofa growls that they should first deal with "that cretinous brother of the Red Plume Guard captain, Deirk Erlich," and Daein agrees: "I never liked people who took advantage of ethnic cleansing."

At the Roadhouse Olisara Lightsong sits with her henchman, a broad-shouldered Shou human. Felkur reports that the Red Plumes are raiding the farmsteads for participants in the arena's bloodsports, and that the companions were ambushed and could not find the Tinfellows: "We felt the information was more important." Olisara answers coldly: "you must go into Hillsfar and free the Tinfellows and any other victims of these raids." A frontal assault would be suicidal; "Gai has a plan." Gai explains that the companions will be sold into the city as slaves for the Arena, find the Tinfellows, and he will help orchestrate an escape. Daein offers to go in as a guard too, and Gai welcomes the idea: "With two of us, our chances will increase." Felkur offers distractions: three infused rocks producing the sound of a dragon roaring, an image of a fireball explosion and the sound of screaming crowds, to be placed around the town. Gai knows just where to put them.

The next morning Daein and Gai, dressed as Red Plume Guards, set out for Hillsfar with Falofa, Felkur and Bramgrim as their prisoners. On the road they meet Captain Erlich leading a smaller squad with a wagon of prisoners. Erlich offers to take the prisoners off their hands, but Gai insists they be present at the auction, so Erlich lets them join his group. Gai explains to Daein that Erlich had wanted to pocket all the profit. Daein watches his friends loaded into the wagons, their weapons taken and the spellcasters' fingers bound and gagged. The caravan reaches Hillsfar after the gates close and camps outside.

That night a Red Plume sorcerer, Breex Vandermast, portly and nasty-tempered, gloats to the prisoners that he will sell them to Mags, who will put them in the Arena, "and then you will die in various horrible ways to entertain the pure folk of Hillsfar." He describes a pool of acid ("Watching their bodies melt as they tried to swim was so much fun.") and the battle royale against the reigning champions. Daein notices the other wagon holds the Tinfellows. Breex laughs at their screams: "that screaming will earn us so much gold!"

Bramgrim, Felkur and Falofa decide not to reveal who they are and play ordinary non-human prisoners. At the Hillsfar Gate, Captain Erlich forces his way past the waiting caravans. Inside, the city is strange: a fat man in silky robes shakes a six-fingered fist at Bramgrim and shouts gibberish; two pregnant women each carrying a baby quarrel over milk; two men in identical red velvet capes duel to the death over the insult of wearing the same cape. Bramgrim, remembering a family that suddenly had six children, thinks "there must be something in the water." Daein sees the adventurers' tavern, the Diamond in the Dung, packed with drinkers in the middle of the day.

At the Arena, Breex presents his haul to Mags, a middle-aged woman with spear-straight posture in a blue gown, attended by Horac and a shirtless Goliath leashed to her belt. The Goliath shakes Bramgrim's wagon to test the prisoners. Mags has "no use for the halflings," but after haggling she tells Horac to pay Breex. Gai leaves to set up the traps and sends Daein inside. Daein bluffs his way in as a Red Plume Guard; Horac backs down. Horac leads them through tunnels lined with cells holding exotic creatures and people, and the Tinfellows are thrown into a small cell: "You will be fighting tomorrow." Bramgrim, Felkur and Falofa are dragged into a 15 by 30-foot barred cage on the arena floor and their manacles are unlocked.

From the cage Felkur watches workers building elaborate wooden structures and waterproofing the interior walls: "I hope it’s not an acid pool. I hate acid pools." The cage is covered with filthy sailcloth. Daein brings them water and moldy biscuits, which Bramgrim pronounces "edible, not dangerous." At sunset guards throw a struggling female elf into the cage: "Where is he? Where is Arrizz?" It is Deriel, whom the companions left for dead. She explains that Erlich took her body, found a cleric of Tempus to raise her, and means to keep raising and killing her because he blames her for his defeat. She tells how she met Arrizz in the Cormanthor Forest, lost him, tracked him to a beet farm crawling with Red Plume Guards, and held a dagger to Erlich's throat in his bath to demand Arrizz's release; Erlich promised it if she helped him. Felkur realises the companions found Arrizz at that beet farm, very sick, and promises to take her to him; the farm's owner, Constance Goodroot, is a friend. "You’ll help me after all the evil I did?" Deriel asks. Bramgrim answers: "young lady, we all do crazy things for the ones we love. You did it out of love."

## Locations

Flat, rolling plains on a well-used, rutted, dry road, where the disguised party joins Erlich's caravan.

Crowded with caravans of finished goods and carts of fish and timber. Captain Erlich pushes to the front.

### Hillsfar
A city in the grip of madness: six-fingered fists, quarrelling pregnant women, and duels over matching capes. The streets smell of rotting trash and market goods.

### Diamond in the Dung
A famous adventurers' tavern, packed and reeking of alcohol in the middle of the day. Daein remembers it as a place for sophisticated folk.

Winding tunnels and corridors full of cells holding exotic creatures and people. On the arena floor stand a 15 by 30-foot barred cage and new wooden structures, and the interior walls are being waterproofed.
