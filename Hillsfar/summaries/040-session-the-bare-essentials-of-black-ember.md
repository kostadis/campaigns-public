# Chapter 40 The Bare Essentials of Black Ember

Date: Feb 1st, 2026 — source-document metadata, not a verified session or in-world date.

## Summary

The party reached Black Ember Outpost, a polished obsidian fortress built into an Underdark cavern wall. Through arrow slits, they watched a fire giant sergeant drill two ogres and three hell hounds. Felkur removed one ogre’s uniform with Mage Hand and created a female-hellhound illusion using sound supplied by Daein, distracting the defenders before the ambush.

The party achieved complete surprise. Felkur attacked with fire and his modified cold-infused cannon, Bramgrim entered with spirit guardians, and the Owlbear finished both ogres. Akritas killed the sergeant before he could strike. Felkur’s cannon killed Hell Hound 2, Akritas killed Hell Hound 1, and Hell Hound 3 escaped; whether it raised an alarm remains unknown.

Daein supplied the giant’s voice for Felkur’s auditory illusion, portraying a giant victory and adding praise for the human prisoner’s beauty. No listener response is established. Choosing to seek a route through the outpost, the party passed a probable kennel identified only by sounds and smell, then reached a storeroom containing two ogres, another fire giant, and a well. They regarded the well as a likely route to the pool, Path of Shadows, and Maerimydra, but the notes record no confirmation or traversal.

Bramgrim cast silence before the storeroom assault. Daein suffered a heavy giant strike, reduced slightly by his psionic ability. Bramgrim later cast spirit guardian and lost concentration after an ogre struck him; the end of the earlier Silence remains unknown. After two misses, Daein killed Ogre 1, used Action Surge, and struck the giant twice while winking at Bramgrim. Bramgrim answered with Mass Healing Word and Toll the Dead, dealing 27 necrotic damage with the latter. Akritas killed the giant, securing the room.

## Scenes

### 1. Arrival at Black Ember Outpost and ambush preparations

#### The party surveyed the fortress through its arrow slits and used magic to disrupt a drill involving one fire giant, two ogres, and three hell hounds.

- The Underdark tunnel opened into a vast cavern with a smooth floor, few natural formations, and scattered stalactites several hundred feet overhead.
- Black Ember Outpost occupied the southeastern cavern wall. Fungal illumination reflected from its polished stone, firelight showed through wide arrow slits, and laughter and shouting carried outside.
- Obsidian double doors appeared to be the only true entrance.
- Through the arrow slits, the party observed the Great Hall, nearly one hundred feet wide and long.
- A frustrated fire giant sergeant drilled two hungry, unhappy ogres and three bored hell hounds. The ogres feared disobeying him; the more disciplined hounds sat at attention.
- The ogres wore poorly made, ill-fitting uniforms barely held together.
- Felkur proposed removing the uniforms with mage hand and distracting the male hell hounds with the sound of a female hell hound.
- Akritas and Felkur remembered the female hellhound sound; Daein reproduced it for Felkur’s illusion.
- Felkur’s initial attempt to remove clothing failed on a Sleight of Hand roll of 5. A natural 20 let the magical hand go unnoticed, and a subsequent 18 brought down **one ogre’s uniform**.
- The giant and ogres were distracted by the fallen uniform. Felkur’s female-hellhound illusion distracted the hounds.

### 2. Fight in the parade ground

#### The ambush killed the sergeant, both ogres, and two hell hounds, while the third hound escaped with its alarm outcome unknown.

- The party achieved complete surprise.
- Felkur cast fireball at the ogres through an arrow slit and targeted the giant with his cannon, modified with a cold infusion.
- Akritas used her oathsworn bow, cast hunter's mark on the giant, and landed a critical hit for over 50 damage.
- Daein opened the door.
- Bramgrim cast spirit guardians and moved between the two ogres and the giant. After his enemies were hurt, he remembered too late that he could have moved out of melee range.
- Felkur struck an ogre with Fire Bolt, then struck the giant with his modified cannon.
- The Owlbear finished **both ogres**. Akritas then struck the giant again.
- Daein missed his first attack against the giant, drawing Bramgrim’s disapproval, then landed a critical hit for 17 damage.
- Bramgrim expressed disappointment that Daein had not used an action surge and called on Akritas to demonstrate.
- Akritas killed the giant before it could strike, moved hunters mark to a hell hound, and attacked it.
- Felkur asked about keeping a hellhound as a pet. Akritas indicated that attacking them had complicated that possibility; Felkur remained excited about trying in the future.
- A hell hound bit Daein. The notes also record fire breath against the Owlbear and Bramgrim, without assigning these attacks to numbered hounds.
- Bramgrim lost concentration on spirit guardians, and Daein returned his earlier gesture of disapproval.
- Bramgrim cast spiritual weapon and struck a hell hound.
- Hell Hound 3 attacked Bramgrim and the Owlbear with what the notes describe as a fire weapon. Both made their saving throws; the nature of that attack remains unresolved.
- Felkur’s cannon dealt 17 ice damage to Hell Hound 1 and Hell Hound 2, killing **Hell Hound 2**.
- Akritas killed **Hell Hound 1**.
- **Hell Hound 3 escaped.** Whether it raised an alarm is not established.

### 3. Deception and movement through the outpost

#### The party staged an auditory account of a giant victory, chose to seek a route onward, and investigated nearby rooms by listening.

- Daein supplied the fire giant’s voice for Felkur’s auditory illusion, presenting the battle as a victory for the giants.
- The performance included praise for the human prisoner’s beauty. This was part of the deception, not evidence that a prisoner was present.
- The intended deception was to convince nearby occupants that the sergeant had won; no listener response or successful deception is established.
- The party debated finding a route through the outpost versus clearing every room. Felkur argued for finding the route, and the party agreed.
- They chose healing magic instead of a short rest; the notes give no healing amounts.
- They opened the western Great Hall door into a hallway with doors to the north, east, and southeast, and a westward turn roughly 35 feet away.
- At the door directly opposite the Great Hall exit, they smelled burned hair and heard a fire giant mocking an ogre whose hair had been burned by a hell hound.
- These sounds and smells suggested a kennel. The notes record listening and smelling, without entry or visual confirmation.
- At the southeastern door, they heard one ogre hauling water from a well, another grumbling, and a fire giant ordering them to hurry.

- The Owlbear dealt 38 damage to Ogre 2.

- Bramgrim cast a 5th-level spirit guardian and entered combat. The giant and surviving ogre failed their saving throws and took 19 damage each.
