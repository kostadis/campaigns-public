# Chapter 22: Malady of Elventree (2)

Date: 17–20 Eleasis 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); played 2024-06-24 (Zoom meeting date)

## Summary

The party set to work on the duergar fiddler whose music induced madness in its listeners. They learned that the fiddle was made of alchemically treated hallucinogenic mushrooms and "the hair of duergar, an insane dwarf race". Elanil, the ranking member of the Harpers, assigned them to investigate where the duergar and the fiddle came from and to find a potential cure for the madness, and warned them that someone might be using the madness as a weapon.

They were given a map of the area surrounding Yûlash, believed to be the most likely location because of its proximity to "a mind flayer enclave". There were still people guarding the ruins of Yûlash, and a significant evil presence in the area. One suggested option was to find a way past the sentries guarding the ruins. At the temple, Serinia was happy to see the party and offered her assistance; after talk of recent events and of the decision to protect the city for its strategic value, she provided a map of the area.

On the road, the party was mistaken for heroes and granted free passage by some militia guards, and heard of a businessman who collects the signatures of adventurers as a valuable commodity. They passed through a decayed town to an underground entrance. The descent was harrowing; after a narrow chimney they reached an underground tunnel system of narrow passages, where survival checks were needed. Following the recommendation of Elanil, they took the northern tunnel, which seemed to lead upstream. They brought the owlbear along, its size an advantage in the narrow tunnels, and had to manage light sources, spider webs, difficult terrain and the risk of being swept away by the strong current. Artifice and platform shoes helped them over obstacles and made them appear taller.

In the tunnels between the Waydown and Zortagra they met the Kuo-toa. The Kuo-toa believe in their own delusions, as many creatures of the underdark do; they believe they are the chosen ones, and they are ruled by a theocracy of archpriests. A secret enclave of Kuo-toa who had been enslaved was seeking an alliance to fight the Mind Flayers. The Mind Flayers and the duergar had formed an alliance against the kuo-toa, and an attack was expected. Bramgrim advised helping the kuo-toa fortify their home. The kuo-toa leader Shagah gave the party a route to the duergar settlement of Zortagra, near a mind flayer enclave. On the way they ran into an ambush, and the session ended before that story could be finished.
