# Chapter 54 Died of Entirely Natural Causes

Date: 2026-07-26

## Summary
The party pressed deeper into the slave-worked tunnels of Szith Morcane, continuing through the three terraced levels of the fungus farms toward the slave pens. They were trying to assemble an army from the enslaved population, working within a three-day window before the missing cart and patrol would be noticed. Their Drow ally Sorn rejoined them after dealing with the overseers blocking their path. Neither Daein nor Felkur wanted to inquire too closely into his methods. Akritas was tempted to press him, but Daein persuaded her to wait with a natural 20, and she accepted that this dark place was not her enclave.

That decision soon became harder to ignore. Near the bridge, Rylzyn Veth questioned Sorn in Undercommon about whether he had dealt with everyone. Sorn clarified that three overseers were dead; the other two were “a different count.” Rylzyn pointed toward something in the shadows and disclaimed responsibility if the other two wanted Sorn dead; their fate remained unclear. The party again chose to leave the matter alone.

The slave pens south of the bridge were a miserable sprawl of shacks, lightly patrolled because the enslaved population mostly policed itself. A Duergar named Grundar approached, apparently recognizing someone. Felkur’s investigation revealed that he was addressing Rylzyn rather than the party: the Drow had once been imprisoned here and had bought his freedom by killing several of Grundar’s cousins. Rylzyn grew visibly nervous and reached for his sword rather than risk being handed over. A second investigation established that Grundar valued his own escape more highly than revenge. General promises of liberation did not satisfy him, but a personal assurance of help did. When he accepted, a startling number of armed Duergar nearby lowered their weapons.

Grundar brought the party toward Rilira, whose position was watched by a bored but overwhelmingly dangerous fire giant and three Azer sentinels. Before the party could settle on an approach, the hobgoblin Korkan arrived with followers and challenged Rilira’s claim to leadership. He dismissed her authority as inherited privilege and threatened the party if they supported her. His ability to shout and alert the giant made even this relatively weak rival an immediate problem.

Felkur escaped the argument by casting Misty Step into Rilira’s guarded quarters. She received him with contempt and suspicion, demanding to know how he could still cast spells when the prisoners’ magic had been taken away. He explained his arrival as the work of a mechanical device. Outside, Daein denied that the party had ever had an artificer. Korkan and Grundar meanwhile insisted that the fire giant had to be removed before anyone would rise up or escape.

The party considered incapacitating the giant, supporting Korkan, supporting Rilira, or using their previously rescued ally Quil'bryn as a commander. They rejected killing both rival leaders merely to create chaos: they needed an organized force, and the Drow were unlikely to accept a hobgoblin commander. Inside, Rilira offered the Drow slaves’ support in return for Korkan’s head and the fire giant’s death. She wanted Szith Morcane back and threatened pursuit throughout the Underdark and Overbright if betrayed. Felkur answered her intimidation with, “I think me shivers have been timbered,” then used a second Misty Step to return.

Sorn proposed convincing Korkan to join an attack and stabbing him during the fighting. Rylzyn refined the idea: let the hobgoblin charge first. Daein sold the false promise of support with a deception result of 19. Korkan and his three hobgoblin companions charged, missed their attacks, and discovered that the party had stayed behind. The giant wounded Korkan, and the Azer killed all three followers. With the guards occupied, the party received a full free round to attack.

The Owlbear mauled an Azer before turning on the giant. Sorn and Rylzyn initially missed with their hand crossbows, then closed with swords. Bramgrim’s Spirit Guardians killed the wounded Azer, damaged and slowed the giant, and battered another guard. Daein struck the giant twice, while Akritas named it her Oathbow’s sworn enemy and unleashed two powerful arrows. Felkur’s attacks missed.

Korkan was also subjected to Bramgrim’s divine aura. After failing his saving throw and taking its damage, he retaliated against the cleric, landing one blow for 15 damage. Bramgrim maintained concentration, and Korkan died within the spirits. The party’s verdict was succinct: “Natural causes.”

The remaining Azer also fell, but the giant managed two final sword strikes against Bramgrim for a total of 62 damage. The cleric survived, although the second concentration check failed and Spirit Guardians ended. Bramgrim immediately cast it again. The giant failed its saving throw and died, leaving Rilira smiling in satisfaction. The party retained her as the commander needed for an organized uprising. Their agreement concerning Szith Morcane remained important: Rilira wanted the city back, while Felkur’s final assessment was that the party had promised to free it.
