# Chapter 38 Buzzing Demons and Binding Deals

Date: Dec 30th, 2025

## Summary
The party stood in Maram il Vass's opulent office, ready to present their payment for the drow prisoner. Felkur had used his magical abilities to create two sets of armor that would remain real magical items for eight hours. The party trusted the wording of their existing contract to make this acceptable payment, despite the DM's pointed reminder about fiendish revenge. To most of the party, Maram appeared as a handsome half-elf; Bramgrim's enhanced sight continued to reveal a seven-foot-tall yugoloth with demonic jaws and claws.

Maram questioned the armor's origins, condition, and history, looking for stories that would increase its resale value. Felkur claimed one set came from bandits and the other from a castle raided weeks earlier, explaining that the undamaged armor had been displayed on a stand rather than stripped from a corpse. When Maram asked whether the bandits had shared any interesting lore, Felkur answered, "Killed him too fast." Maram then mentioned the death of Graz'zt's Chosen, Malkyn Grenefeld, in Hillsfar. Anything associated with Graz'zt's failure would be especially valuable to his enemies. Daein encouraged that interpretation without making an explicit claim. Rewarded with advantage and an additional +4 for the party's roleplaying, Daein rolled a natural 20 and secured the exchange.

Maram directed them downstairs to the Hanging Gardens to collect Xaven Tanor'this themselves. He unlocked the adjoining door, left the key behind, and departed with a chest. The party took the key and descended through a storage room filled with empty containers. The long staircase grew rocky and uneven before opening into a cavern one hundred feet tall, illuminated by ceiling fungus whose pale glow resembled moonlight. Nine visible cages were occupied, suspended high above the floor on heavy chains. An unsettling buzzing filled the chamber.

After three creatures were heard screaming and running down a hallway, two Chasme Demons appeared. Daein identified them as intelligent Abyssal taskmasters and interrogators created to punish deserters from the Blood War. His knowledge also revealed their elemental resistances, poison immunity, magical defenses, climbing ability, and dangerous drone. The party plugged their ears. Pass Without Trace was suggested, but they had already been detected. The DM explained the contractual trap: Maram had fulfilled his obligations, but the party had failed to account for the prisoner's custody having been handed to someone else.

The demons initially flew fifty feet above the floor, complicating the party's attacks and rescue plans. Bramgrim's Toll the Dead failed, but his fourth-level Spiritual Weapon struck one demon. Felkur's Firebolts dealt reduced damage through their fire resistance. Daein and the Owlbear readied attacks while Akritas marked a target and fired her bow. When one demon swooped toward Bramgrim, the Owlbear clawed it, and its attack failed against the cleric's armor. The second demon struck Akritas for 40 damage.

Bramgrim summoned Spirit Guardians as the fight moved to ground level. Meanwhile, the party discovered that Xaven's cage hung one hundred feet overhead. Simply breaking its chain risked a lethal fall, but the DM clarified that the cages used a pulley system accessible from the floor. Bramgrim's Spiritual Weapon broke the chain while the Owlbear caught and held it, allowing Xaven to be lowered safely. Daein attacked the demon blocking the exit and used Action Surge, while Felkur and Akritas concentrated their attacks on it. Akritas's two arrows dealt 28 damage, leaving it nearly defeated.

The demons taunted their opponents telepathically in buzzing, clicking voices that sounded like several speakers talking at once. One threatened to find the weakness inside Bramgrim's armored exterior. The other lunged at Akritas, initially appearing to inflict another devastating wound; the attack was then ruled a miss because Daein's Sap had imposed disadvantage. Bramgrim disengaged while maintaining Spirit Guardians, and the aura finished the wounded demon. The Owlbear carried Xaven's cage toward the stairs.

The party retreated, leaving Bramgrim briefly at the rear. The surviving demon mocked their formation and struck him critically for 45 damage: 23 piercing and 22 necrotic. His poison resistance offered no protection against that necrotic attack. The other prisoners pleaded for rescue, but the party's plugged ears prevented them from hearing. Xaven had been able to hear Daein's earlier call because the drone's range was only thirty feet; he had identified himself by shaking his cage. Bramgrim disengaged again and escaped with the others. The surviving demon commented that Maram would need a new partner to guard the prisoners, and three hags arrived, complaining about hired help.

Back in Maram's office, Daein brushed off their ordeal and claimed they had deliberately spared the remaining demon so Maram would retain some protection. Maram showed little concern about replacing the dead guard. He then turned to Felkur and revealed that he had promised another fiend information about Felkur's whereabouts in connection with a stolen item. Felkur freely admitted the theft. Maram offered to withhold that information if Felkur retrieved another magical item, one that lay along the party's intended route and that Maram owed to another demon.

Questions about whether the disclosure would reveal Felkur's location once or continuously met a wall of nested nondisclosure agreements. Challenged on whether these contracts existed, Maram became offended and produced examples of his work. He insisted that he was famous for his contracts and described his business as an independent way of participating in the Blood War. He would not explain the requested item's intended use. Its exact identity remained unspecified at the table, although Felkur was treated as recognizing Maram's description.

Felkur insisted on a written agreement and negotiated with intelligence, supported by Daein's Bardic Inspiration, which supplied a six. He secured a clause requiring disclosure of the item's location and defenses, then rolled 34 to establish an airtight no-betrayal provision. Maram could not switch sides without suffering a cosmic curse or forfeiting his true name. Felkur sealed the agreement through a magical binding ritual with a result of 25, earning Maram's admiration for his knowledge of binding law. The finalized contract bound Felkur to retrieve the item and granted him a bonus on saving throws against fear while under its terms.
