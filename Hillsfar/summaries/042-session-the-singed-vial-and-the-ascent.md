# Chapter 42 The Singed Vial and the Ascent

Date: 2026-02-15

## Summary
The party moved through the demon-occupied streets of Maerimydra like ghosts, cloaked in magical silence as they navigated the grim spectacle of a once-proud drow city brought low. Akritas maintained Pass Without Trace rather than spending a token on disguises. Braziers burned along the thoroughfares, roasting the flesh of unknown creatures, while drow — once the feared masters of the Underdark — shuffled past in chains, enslaved by the fire giants who now ruled the city. The adventurers pressed on from the Shattered Tower toward the plateau marked with Graz'zt’s six-fingered hand.

Their mission carried both practical and personal stakes: capture a fiendish giant for a cure, provoke a conflict between Vraxen and Veperyd, steal the Lexicon of Oaths, and escape through a teleportation circle. Maram il Vass had promised to conceal Felkur’s identity as the thief behind an earlier Mulmaster security disaster in exchange for the Lexicon. The Mulmaster wizards believed a powerful rival had defeated their protections and contemptuously sold the stolen artifact for almost nothing. In reality, Felkur had stolen it for quick money, accidentally activated its nondetection properties while fiddling with it, and sold an object worth tens of thousands of gold pieces for just 45 gold.

Tucked beneath the crushed marble staircase of a ruined drow manor, the party discovered a hidden magic shop called The Singed Vial. The air inside smelled of ozone, old blood, and burnt cinnamon, and cracked jars of Faerzress cast a sickly glow across the cramped space. Its proprietor, Vriska, was a female drow mage in ash-covered, tattered finery, with half her face beautiful and the other half ruined by fire giant branding scars. She was suspicious of customers who were neither drow nor giants and baffled that anyone had entered her supposedly secret shop despite its conspicuous sign.

Vriska offered a scroll of Seeming, three potions of fire resistance, and Dust of Disappearance. The shopping prompted a clarification of the heist: Vraxen, Veperyd, and the Mulmaster agents would all attend dinner in the tower, giving the party an opportunity to expose Veperyd’s theft and start the intended conflict. The party also discussed how to conceal their portable hole and its breathing apparatus. Felkur checked his artificer infusions and established that they did not expire after eight hours, although discovery of the missing giant would still put pressure on the escape.

Felkur identified crystalline Faerzress shards that would ease activation of the teleportation device. The party won a discount by feigning shared contempt for Solom Ned'razak, whom Vriska despised as a cowardly academic who had abandoned the city. Felkur’s experience with gems supplied another advantage: the coral gemstones recovered from the Black Ember Outpost had been prized currency in Maerimydra before its fall. Vriska accepted them at double value, and the party used part of their coral treasure to obtain the shards. They retained Solom’s notes, potentially saving that leverage for a later purchase of the Seeming scroll.

With their supplies secured, the adventurers approached the western plateau, where important giants kept broad estates. They rejected a noisy diversion that might attract the city’s eager fire giant warriors and chose to climb rather than cross the bridge in disguise. Tied together with rope, they scaled the hundred-foot cliff. Felkur rode the owlbear, while Bramgrim slipped and needed his companions to pull the rope toward the rock so he could regain his grip. At the top, he thanked Daein repeatedly. Akritas renewed Pass Without Trace before they advanced.

The courtyard above had a polished obsidian floor and six towering ebony pillars marked with Graz'zt’s six-fingered hand. It initially appeared empty. Then Brandyr entered: an eighteen-foot-tall fiend-born fire giant clad in obsidian plate and flowing black robes, with leathery wings, jagged horns, a third eye, and cold green eyes above skin the color of cooling lava. Four cambions hovered twenty feet overhead, chanting in Abyssal and firing black and green energy into the pillars. Akritas understood the ritual as Brandyr raised a greatsword resembling a slice of midnight sky and proclaimed destruction in Graz'zt’s name.

The party remained hidden while debating how to capture her alive. Their preferred sequence was Sleep, Reduce, and the portable hole, with companions stopping the cambions from waking her. Sleep was not yet available to them, and using a token to give Felkur access to it remained a possibility. The DM warned that the ritual’s fires might amplify Brandyr’s power. Initiative was rolled, the ritual was completed, and the enemies still had not detected the party. The session stopped before the ambush began.

## Scenes
### The Streets of Maerimydra
#### The party navigates the demon-occupied city of Maerimydra, moving stealthily toward their objective while observing the grim reality of drow enslavement.
- The party sets out from the Shattered Tower toward the elevated courtyard marked with the six-fingered symbol.
- The DM reminds them that they are conspicuous outsiders and could spend a token to disguise themselves as drow. They discuss invisibility before choosing stealth supported by Akritas’s Pass Without Trace.
- The spell provides a +10 bonus to Dexterity checks for stealth and requires concentration for up to an hour. The DM judges their concealment sufficient to pass the city’s inhabitants undetected.
- They pass a creature’s flesh roasting over one of the many braziers. They continue without intervening, seeing no useful way to help.
- Drow serving as slaves to fire giants make the city’s reversal of power unmistakable.
- Before the journey, the DM clarifies Felkur’s predicament: Mulmaster’s search for the earlier artifact thief has driven paranoia, security upgrades, and the execution of several High Inquisitors.
- The Mulmaster authorities imagine a powerful wizard deliberately humiliated them. Felkur’s actual theft, accidental use of nondetection, and sale for 45 gold have escaped their suspicions.
- Maram il Vass apparently knows the truth and demands the Lexicon of Oaths for his silence.
- Along their route, the party notices a magical shop sign reading The Singed Vial and decides to investigate.

### The Singed Vial
#### The party enters a hidden magic shop and meets its scarred proprietor, a rare female drow mage, before negotiating for magical supplies.
- The shop lies beneath a ruined drow manor near the Shattered Tower. A crushed marble staircase forms its low ceiling; cracked jars of Faerzress provide sickly light.
- Its air smells of ozone, old blood, and burnt cinnamon. Vriska wears tattered finery dusted with ash.
- Half her face remains beautiful; the other half bears extensive scars that the party recognizes as a fire giant brand.
- Vriska immediately notices that the visitors are neither drow nor giants. She suspects Pipyap directed them to her and questions why they are in the city.
- Daein points out the large sign outside. Vriska explains that people were supposed to see it and keep walking.
- Daein’s knowledge of elven religion helps establish how unusual a female drow mage is in a society where females typically become clerics. The DM also explains the deep hostility drow mages bear toward Lolth’s clergy.
- Vriska threatens to turn the outsiders over, then explains why she prefers doing business: they need her goods and cannot safely expose her without revealing themselves.
- Her stock includes a scroll of Seeming for 600 gold, three potions of fire resistance at 300 gold each, and Dust of Disappearance for 500 gold.
- The scroll prompts a review of the heist. Vraxen, Veperyd, and Mulmaster agents will all attend dinner in the tower. Pipyap does not know why they are meeting; the party infers that the Mulmasters also have information about the Lexicon.
- The intended conflict begins by telling Vraxen that Veperyd is stealing from him. Capturing the giant alone will not start it.
- The DM explains Vraxen’s role as Graz'zt’s yugoloth agent: enforce the arrangement under which the giants received fiendish blood and promised to spread destruction through the Underdark.
- Seeming could disguise the party’s appearance and equipment, including their portable hole and its breathing apparatus. Felkur’s stated spell save DC of 19 would apply if he cast it.
- Felkur initially worries that an infusion’s duration creates an eight-hour limit, then checks and confirms that infusions have no such expiration. The DM still warns that the missing giant will eventually provoke a search.
- Felkur proposes hiding inside the portable hole with the captive. The DM points out that a conscious giant inside could attack them.
- They discuss adding an anesthetic to the breathing apparatus. The DM offers a token as a way to establish that preparation, but no such modification is committed to.
- Felkur’s Investigation result of 20 reveals crystalline Faerzress shards useful for the teleportation escape. The device can work without them, but the shards make the required skill-check process easier and faster.
- Daein wants to preserve Bardic Inspiration for the eventual teleportation attempt.
- The party prioritizes the shards. They defer Seeming, judge the fire resistance potions unnecessary for now, and decline Dust of Disappearance because they already have several invisibility options.
- Vriska initially asks 500 gold for the crystalline Faerzress.
- Bramgrim’s Insight result of 13, combined with the party’s knowledge, suggests that Vriska must know Solom Ned'razak.
- Felkur rolls a natural 20 on an Insight check made with advantage because of his gem-working background. He remembers that their coral treasure was a prized form of local currency before Maerimydra fell.
- Daein considers Charm Person, but the possibility of Counterspell and an enraged drow mage makes it a last resort.
- Daein casually mentions Solom to Bramgrim and watches Vriska’s reaction. The group overhears her condemning Solom as a coward who fled and left her behind.
- Daein joins in the criticism. Vriska approves and reduces the shard price from 500 to 450 gold.
- The coral gemstones soften her demeanor further. She accepts 400 gold worth of coral as having 800 gold in purchasing value; the party chooses to trade only enough to cover the shards.
- The DM suggests that Solom’s notes could interest Vriska because they make him look worse. The party declines, mindful that exposure could earn the hatred of a twentieth-level drow wizard.
- They retain the notes as possible leverage for the Seeming scroll if they return.

### The Ascent to the Courtyard of Lolth
#### The adventurers scale a hundred-foot plateau to reach the high-altitude district of Maerimydra where the fiendish giant resides.
- At the plateau’s base, the party considers drawing their target down rather than climbing.
- A proposed diversion using explosion sounds is abandoned: the city is full of fire giants eager for a fight, and the target is occupied with a religious ritual.
- The available approaches are to cross the bridge in disguise or climb the rock face. Their concealment is sufficient for the latter, leaving the physical ascent as the obstacle.
- The cliff is 100 feet high, with a DC 15 Athletics check required to climb it.
- Daein considers using psionic power to fly before the group proceeds with the climb.
- They tie themselves together so successful climbers can catch a companion who slips.
- Felkur, with a −1 Athletics modifier, chooses to hold onto the owlbear.
- Daein, Akritas, and the owlbear climb successfully. Bramgrim rolls an 8 and loses his footing.
- An additional Athletics check allows the others to draw the rope toward the cliff, giving Bramgrim something to grasp so he can continue.
- The DM offers an opportunity for a snarky remark at Bramgrim’s expense; Daein declines.
- At the top, Bramgrim thanks Daein repeatedly. Daein turns their attention back to stealing the giant without setting off an alarm.
- Akritas casts Pass Without Trace again.
- The plateau holds broad estates for important giants. Slaves here are better dressed, and the fiery chaos of the lower city is more subdued.

### Observation of the Fiendish Ritual
#### From the shadows, the party watches a powerful fiend-born fire giant perform a ritual to Graz'zt and begins planning their ambush.
- The southern courtyard has polished obsidian flooring and six ebony pillars marked with Graz'zt’s six-fingered hand.
- The courtyard initially appears empty, with the intended giant nowhere in sight. The party chooses to hide and observe.
- Daein asks whether Bramgrim’s True Seeing could locate the target. The DM explains its uses against illusions and concealed magical features, clarifying that it does not function as an X-ray. It is not cast.
- Felkur proposes dropping an open portable hole over the giant. The DM explains that the target must first be reduced to fit the hole’s ten-foot opening.
- The DM rules that an unconscious target would not receive the saving throw against Reduce, making unconsciousness central to the proposed capture.
- Brandyr arrives with four cambions. Her horns, wings, and extra eye identify her as one of the fiend-born giants they seek.
- She stands eighteen feet tall in obsidian plate and black robes. Her skin resembles cooling lava, her eyes burn green, and her wings twitch from her shoulders.
- The cambions hover twenty feet above the courtyard, chanting in a low Abyssal harmony and firing black and green rays into the pillars.
- Brandyr raises her midnight-dark greatsword and proclaims the ritual’s destructive purpose. She then sweeps the courtyard with a hunting gaze but fails to detect the party.
- Akritas confirms that she speaks Abyssal and understands the ritual.
- Brandyr proceeds around the pillars in a circle. The DM warns that fire can amplify power in such rituals and that she may become stronger as it progresses.
- The party considers moving between the pillars to prepare an ambush while still seeking a way to avoid a prolonged battle.
- The DM explains that they could knock her unconscious by reducing her to zero hit points with a final melee weapon attack. The party prefers to avoid fighting her down.
- Their favored plan becomes putting Brandyr to sleep, reducing her, and loading her into the portable hole while the others occupy the cambions.
- They recognize that a cambion could wake her by striking her; Silence would not prevent that.
- Daein considers a four-against-four engagement with someone ready to stop a guard who moves to wake Brandyr.
- Darkness and Polymorph are discussed as possibilities. The party notes that they lack Darkness and have not yet secured Sleep.
- Sleep requires two failed saves to produce unconsciousness. Felkur’s stated DC of 19 makes him the preferred prospective caster over Bramgrim’s 17.
- Initiative rolls are generally poor: the owlbear gets a 3 despite advantage, and Felkur gets an 8.
- The ritual is completed while the enemies remain unaware of the hidden party. The session ends before an attack or capture spell is attempted.
- In the closing discussion, Hold Monster is suggested as another option, but Felkur reiterates the appeal of Sleep: unconsciousness would remove the saving throw against shrinking the giant.

## NPCs

- Vriska suspects he revealed her shop’s existence.
- His information places all three parties at dinner in the tower, although he does not know why they are meeting.

### Maram il Vass
An influential figure who is blackmailing Felkur, demanding the Lexicon of Oaths in exchange for keeping Felkur's identity as the thief secret from the Mulmasters.

- He apparently discovered the truth behind a theft that the Mulmasters attribute to an exceptionally powerful wizard.
- His promised silence concerns Felkur’s earlier artifact theft, separate from the current Lexicon retrieval.

### Vriska
A female drow mage and proprietor of The Singed Vial. Half of her face is a ruin of scars given to her by fire giants as a brand. She harbors a deep resentment for Solom Ned'razak, viewing him as a cowardly academic who fled the city and abandoned her. She softened toward the party after they insulted Solom and traded her prized coral gemstones.

- She wears ash-covered, tattered finery and has a red eye visible through her scarred face.
- She regards outsiders as useful customers because exposing her would endanger them too.
- Her remaining stock and position as one of the few surviving shops give her little incentive to lower prices.
- She grants a 50-gold discount for the party’s criticism of Solom and doubles the purchasing value of their coral gemstones.

### Vraxen
A Yugoloth described as an 'evil lawyer' and mercenary who acts as an agent for Graz'zt, ensuring the giants fulfill their contracts. He is expected to attend a high-stakes dinner party alongside Veperyd and agents of the Mulmasters.

- The DM distinguishes yugoloths from both devils and demons, describing their role in making and enforcing deals.
- Graz'zt employs him to ensure the empowered giants spread chaos through the Underdark rather than destroy themselves.
- His tower is the intended site of both the dinner infiltration and the theft.

### Veperyd
A giant involved in the local power struggle, suspected of stealing from Vraxen. He is expected to attend the dinner party at the tower.

- The party intends to expose his theft to Vraxen to provoke the conflict that will cover their heist.

### Brandyr
An 18-foot-tall fiend-born fire giant twisted by the blood of the abyss. She wears obsidian plate armor and flowing black robes, possesses massive leathery wings, a crown of jagged horns, and a third eye that burns with cold green light. She wields a greatsword that appears forged from the midnight sky and leads a ritual to Graz'zt in the courtyard.

- Her skin is the color of cooling lava.
- She circles the courtyard’s pillars during the ritual and actively scans for threats without finding the party.
- The DM characterizes her as an already arrogant fire giant made even more confident by demonic power.
- She is the party’s intended live captive; they have not attacked or incapacitated her by session’s end.

### Cambions
Four winged, partly human fiends that hover 20 feet in the air, assisting Brandyr by firing rays of crackling black and green fire into the ritual pillars while chanting in Abyssal.

- Their leathery wings beat slowly as they hover, and their low chanting makes the observers’ teeth ache.
- They pose a particular obstacle to Sleep because any one of them could strike Brandyr to wake her.
