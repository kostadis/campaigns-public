# Chapter 9: The Madness of the Maze

Date: 8 Mirtul 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); written 2023-03-11 (Drive createdTime; play date not stated)

## Summary

Doctor Jubal, a dark-haired man dressed in somber clothing, steps out of his covered wagon flanked by four of his fools and advances toward the small campfire. "What's all this? What's all this? We'll have no fighting here, not at my carnival. We are peaceful folk. Entertainers. Take your fight elsewhere." He sends two fools to "go collect the bodies of your comrades and take them to the maze." He stares at Daein as if trying to peer into his soul, then says, "I am so sorry they attacked you."

Daein and Felkur question him. He insists that "People enjoy my carnival because they can briefly escape their lives. People join my carnival to escape their past. This carnival is a refuge for those persecuted and a home for those without one." When they point to the fools he scoffs that "the area around Hillsfar seems to breed them." When Daein insists on a connection between illnesses and the Fools, Doctor Jubal answers, "If we were to blame for sickness, wouldn't my people also fall ill?" and "I give a home to the Fools. I don't create them." Then, with a hint of malevolence and hate, he mutters, "I give these Fools sanctuary from the mad world that would kill them." The group hands him the Fool; he does not seem concerned that several are dead, and the injured Fool walks up to him like a dog begging to be loved by its owner. Bramgrim mutters, "we need to go investigate this maze."

First, though, they have their fortune read by Madame Darkness, a drow who painted her skin golden and declared herself a sun-elf. Her act is not very good; confronted, she admits it is all an act, and her accent and demeanor take on the look of a drow from the Underdark. Later that day Daein is puzzled why so many denizens of the Underdark have been seen recently: the Derro raiding party makes sense, but the Drow hate the sunlight, and this is the second Drow to seek refuge on the surface lands. That is a mystery for another day.

The maze is dimly lit with low lanterns and relies on reflection to spread the light, so the whole tent is full of flickering light and dancing shadows. Felkur hears giggling and shouts: "people seem to be having fun." Bramgrim casts detect magic: "the wall and ceiling mirrors radiate magic."

Falofa declares "this isn't that hard," Daein is "not so sure….", and Falofa collides face-first into a mirror while Bramgrim and Felkur laugh. In the mirror the interior looks far larger than the exterior, and their shadows seem to move in menacing ways. Then madness strikes. Daein, Felkur, and Bramgrim are disoriented and start fighting each other. "The only one who is not affected is Falofa." Daein recovers, and the Barbarian and fighter immobilize the artificer and the dwarf just long enough. "Let's be careful," mutters Falofa. Daein shatters the mirror ("well, that's the end of that.") and to his horror it reassembles. "I have a bad feeling about this," mutters Falofa.

Moving carefully through the turns, the adventurers stumble into a large open space where many villagers are chained to the floor. Bramgrim notices a black six-fingered hand, "the symbol of Graz'zt, the demon lord." Daein deciphers the arcane runes around it: "These are symbols involved in rituals to create copies of the individual at the expense of their soul." When everyone stares, he says, "what? I read a lot as a kid."

Felkur picks out Goodwoman Aelia, the smith Berl Cuttler, and a man dressed like Doctor Jubal; some of the prisoners have the facial disfigurements of the Fools. The alchemist Weet Greenspen offers his healing potion in return for being freed. The gentleman shouts, "I am Doctor Jubal," and begs them to "Get us out of here before the demon comes back." Aelia cries, "Bramgrim, I knew you would save us!" and explains: "the maze is evil. It makes duplicates of people! The maze plans to use the Duplicate Doctor Jubal to lead the carnival into the Underdark and sell us to the demons below."

With a burst of sickening laughter, one of the villagers stands up, slowly clapping, and its features melt into silvery putty. "Is everything you can't understand a demon to you yokels?" She mockingly bows to the party, holds out her hands as if to be manacled, then grins, drops them, and shifts through the shapes of Aelia, Berl, and Daein. "I admit that the rumors about the sickness were inconvenient, but it is the price you pay for the master's gift. Soon you will know his blessing."

Fools rush in and rats swarm the heroes. The Doppelganger wields strange magic through the mirrors, shifting into her enemies' appearance and almost driving them mad. But when one of the mirrors is shattered the magic turns against her; Felkur notices and uses his cannon to smash another mirror. The battle is gruesome, but in the end the evil Doppelganger Doctor Jubal is dead.
