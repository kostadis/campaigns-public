# Chapter 26: Waydown

Date: 1–2 Eleint 1494 DR (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-16); played 2024-09-08 (Zoom meeting date)

## Summary

The session began with a look back at the party's past adventures: the groups they had run into, the influence of the demon lord "Graz'zt in Hillsfar", and the chaos caused by the false Dr. Jubal, the doppelganger of the mirror maze (Ch. 9), remembered as exacerbating the existing problems "in a demonic way". The recap then turned to the party's time in the Elventree and the plan ahead: rescue Szith Morcane from fire giants, by way of a garrison, an abandoned temple and a drow caravan, with corrupt officials and vengeful groups among the dangers. Daein proposed an alternative route, but it would not accommodate the supplies and equipment the party needed.

The Emerald Enclave had a new mission that required the party's help. Daein was hesitant at first, since it lay close to their current destination, but agreed to meet the Emerald Enclave members in the Hall of the Unicorn to learn more. The party also learned that Solom, previously believed to be a captive, was alive and leading the Szith Morcane outpost, which made the investigation of the outpost all the more crucial. Felratha and her troupe were given the mission of investigating the control of Szith Morcane under Solom.

The party joined the troupe in disguise. They had to conceal their non-human identities and watch for impostors. Felkur became "Jose the Gardener", and Daein was given a colorful mustache and the name "Goozym". Arrizz went disguised as Lioneth the sword juggler. Felratha's minstrels arrived at the garrison with a plan to sneak out and escort a wagon of supplies, and put on their show. Daein performed a juggling act using telekinetic movement, and Felratha introduced him with some misunderstanding, while she provided music on a harp. Somewhere along the way came a mock wrestling match decided by strength, which Daein won by pinning Zern Xerkstil, and telekinetic movement was used to get past a gate that called for Strength.

At the garrison, the target was the Great Lift at the Waydown Sinkhole. Captain Helck kept the pass phrase hidden under his mattress, and the party succeeded in stealing the passphrase and activating the descent. Daein would keep up his disguise and the performance, Felkur would keep playing the gardener and field inquiries about future haircuts, and the party would stay in cover, gather what it could about the situation in Szith Morcane, and prepare for creatures in the river below.
