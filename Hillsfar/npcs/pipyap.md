## Identity
Pipyap is an imp devil with a barbed tail and a bloodstained, button-fastened satchel. He serves as the party’s contracted guide and informant in Maerimydra. [ch 041 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Talkative, opportunistic, and persistently hungry, Pipyap treats information as something to bargain over. His attention to contractual wording extends from employment loopholes to attempts to charge separately for follow-up questions. [ch 041 / 041.06; ch 043 / 043.04]

He values the Material Plane’s food and freedom from his infernal employment obligations. Food and protection are explicit incentives for cooperating with the party; he also expresses confidence in manipulating volatile people through their competing interests. [ch 041 / 041.06; ch 043 / 043.04]

His knowledge has limits: he provides local intelligence but acknowledges that he has never explored the Spire’s upper rooms. [ch 043 / 043.04]

## History with the Party
- The party rescued Pipyap from four ogres in Maerimydra; during combat, he agreed to help subject to his existing contracts. [ch 041 / 041.05]
- Pipyap’s first written contract would have made the party serve him. They rejected it and negotiated an agreement requiring confidentiality, obedience, and protection against harm and theft. [ch 041 / 041.06]
- Pipyap explained that his infernal employment contract omitted service on the Material Plane, allowing him to remain there without serving his employer. [ch 041 / 041.06]
- Pipyap repeatedly requested food, offered Daein a soul sale, and complained about the provisions’ lack of salt after being fed. [ch 041 / 041.06]
- Pipyap identified the fiendish giants at the Coliseum and Six-Fingered Courtyard and reported a visitor from Mulmaster dining with Vraxen. [ch 041 / 041.06]
- Pipyap proposed exposing Veperyd’s theft from Vraxen to provoke a conflict; the party incorporated this distraction into its plan. [ch 041 / 041.07]
- Vriska suspected Pipyap had directed the party to The Singed Vial; the summary does not confirm her suspicion. [ch 042 / 042.02]
- Before entering the Spire, Pipyap insisted he was the party’s friend rather than captive and demanded food for each question. He resumed answering after Bramgrim intimidated him and the party reminded him of their protection agreement. [ch 043 / 043.04]
- Pipyap described the dining hall and magical table, revealed that Vraxen carried the key to the upper rooms, and acknowledged that he had never explored those rooms himself. [ch 043 / 043.04]
- Pipyap recommended joining the dinner to manipulate its volatile attendees and identified High Inquisitor Veris as a guest. [ch 043 / 043.04]
- At the session’s close, the DM revealed that Pipyap was no longer accompanying the party. His whereabouts, disappearance timing, and reasons remained unresolved. [ch 043 / 043.06; ch 043 / entry]

## Last Observed State
His last confirmed interaction with the party was their consultation before entering the Spire. At the session’s close, after the party fled the dinner conflict upstairs, the DM explicitly established that Pipyap was no longer accompanying them. His whereabouts, disappearance timing, and reasons remain unresolved. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 043 / 043.04; ch 043 / 043.06; ch 043 / entry]

## Relationships
- **The party:** Pipyap calls them friends rather than captors. Their working relationship rests on an agreement exchanging his assistance for protection, with requirements of obedience, confidentiality, and prohibitions against harming or stealing from them. [ch 041 / 041.06; ch 043 / 043.04]
- **Bramgrim and Felkur:** Both scrutinize his contractual bargaining; Bramgrim also uses intimidation to secure his continued cooperation. [ch 041 / 041.06; ch 043 / 043.04]
- **Daein:** A frequent target of his requests for food and infernal business pitches. [ch 041 / 041.06]
- **Infernal employer:** An unnamed devil whose service requirements Pipyap avoids through the contract’s omission of Material Plane work. [ch 041 / 041.06]
- **Vriska:** Suspects him of revealing The Singed Vial’s existence. Her suspicion is unconfirmed. [ch 042 / 042.02]
