## Identity
Captain Erlich is the leader of the Red Plume Guards and the brother of Deirk Erlich. [ch 003 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Erlich uses captivity and promises of release to coerce cooperation, demanding non-humans for Hillsfar Arena in exchange for Arrizz’s freedom. His orders show deliberate planning of raids and ambushes. [ch 003 / 003.03]

He uses threats of arrest to secure priority and seeks personal profit from prisoners, according to Gai’s explanation of his offer to take the party’s captives. [ch 004 / 004.05; ch 004 / 004.03]

## History with the Party
- Captain Erlich leads the Red Plume Guards and is Deirk Erlich's brother. [ch 003 / entry]
- He appeared in bloodied armor, failed to rally his troops, and fled with his remaining guards. [ch 003 / 003.02]
- The companions realised Deirk was seizing property Captain Erlich had taken from the Tinfellows. [ch 003 / 003.02]
- Notes Bramgrim found on Deriel's body showed Erlich demanded a steady supply of non-humans for Hillsfar Arena as the price of Arrizz's freedom. [ch 003 / 003.03]
- Erlich's notes called the Tinfellows raid a success and instructed Deriel to lead investigating non-humans to Shadowlight, where he and his guards would ambush them. [ch 003 / 003.03]
- Daein and Gai, disguised as Red Plume Guards, met Erlich's prisoner caravan while escorting Falofa, Felkur and Bramgrim as prisoners. [ch 004 / 004.03]
- Erlich offered to take their prisoners, then allowed the group to join his caravan after Gai insisted on attending the auction; Gai explained that Erlich wanted all the profit. [ch 004 / 004.03]
- At Hillsfar Gate, Erlich forced his way past the waiting caravans by threatening arrest. [ch 004 / 004.05]
- Deriel told the companions she had held a dagger to Erlich's throat in his bath to demand Arrizz's release, and that Erlich promised it if she helped him. [ch 004 / 004.07]
- Deriel said Erlich recovered her body and had a cleric of Tempus raise her; she believed he intended to keep raising and killing her because he blamed her for his defeat. [ch 004 / 004.07]

## Last Observed State
Last directly observed alive at Hillsfar Gate, forcing his prisoner caravan past waiting traffic by threatening arrest. [ch 004 / 004.05]

He is subsequently spoken of by Deriel, who says he recovered her body and arranged her resurrection through a cleric of Tempus. She believes he intends to kill and raise her repeatedly because he blames her for his defeat; this intention is her assessment. [ch 004 / 004.07]

## Relationships
- **Deirk Erlich:** His brother, whom the companions identified as seizing property Captain Erlich had taken from the Tinfellows. [ch 003 / 003.02]
- **Red Plume Guards:** His troops; he commands them in battle and leads a squad escorting prisoners. [ch 003 / 003.02; ch 004 / 004.03]
- **Deriel:** A coerced supplier of arena captives through his leverage over Arrizz. Her note expresses murderous hatred toward Erlich. [ch 003 / 003.03; ch 003 / moment]
- **Arrizz:** Held as leverage over Deriel, with freedom conditional on her supplying arena prisoners. [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.03]
- **The companions:** Targets of his planned ambush. Later, Daein and Gai gain access to his caravan disguised as Red Plume Guards, escorting Falofa, Felkur and Bramgrim as prisoners. [ch 003 / 003.03; ch 004 / 004.03]
