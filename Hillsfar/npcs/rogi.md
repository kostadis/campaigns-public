## Identity
Rogi is Kendrick's assistant, encountered by the heroes at the Stop. [ch 013 / entry; ch 012 / 012.07]

## Personality and Motivations
Rogi defers to Kendrick's authority: when Jhanos asks him to escort the heroes to the Chateau, he says he must speak to Kendrick first. His underlying motives are not established by that exchange. [ch 013 / entry]

## History with the Party
- The heroes met Rogi when they arrived at the Stop. [ch 012 / 012.07]
- Rogi was Kendrick's assistant. [ch 013 / entry]
- Jhanos asked Rogi to escort the heroes to the Chateau; Rogi said he had to talk to Kendrick first. [ch 013 / entry]
- Kendrick was displeased that Rogi was being asked to act as a bellhop. [ch 013 / 013.03]
- The heroes defeated Rogi, Thaddeus Zervous and the members of the cult in The Livery in The Stop. [ch 015 / 015.01]
- Daein proposed having the owlbear eat Thaddeus and Rogi; the proposal was vetoed. [ch 015 / 015.01]

## Last Observed State
Last recorded as defeated alongside Thaddeus Zervous and the cult members in The Livery in The Stop. Daein's subsequent proposal to feed Rogi and Thaddeus to the owlbear was vetoed. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 015 / 015.01]

## Relationships
- **Kendrick:** Rogi's employer; Kendrick objects to his assistant being asked to act as a bellhop. [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.03]
- **Jhanos:** Requests Rogi's help escorting the heroes to the Chateau; Rogi makes his response conditional on consulting Kendrick. [ch 013 / entry]
- **The heroes:** Initially encounter Rogi at the Stop; later defeat him in The Livery. [ch 012 / 012.07; ch 015 / 015.01]
- **Thaddeus Zervous and the cult:** Defeated alongside Rogi; the precise nature of their association is not established. [ch 015 / 015.01]
