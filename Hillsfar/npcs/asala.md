## Identity
Asala was a Drow Inquisitor and Velasta's assistant, acting as an intermediary between the party and Velasta's group. [ch 056 / entry; ch 057 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Asala was willing to open negotiations with potential allies and assert a century-old family claim to the goddess and temple sites within Szith Morcane. Her response to Rilira's claim suggests a firm commitment to defending those claimed rights. [ch 056 / 056.05; ch 057 / 057.05]

## History with the Party
- Asala, a Drow Inquisitor, intercepted the party in the crypts and brought them to her leader, Velasta, to discuss a potential alliance. [ch 056 / entry]
- As Velasta's assistant, Asala asserted a century-old family claim to the goddess and temple sites within Szith Morcane during faction negotiations. [ch 057 / entry]
- Solom's clone killed Asala with disintegrating magic during the negotiations, reducing her to dust. [ch 057 / 057.05; ch 058 / 058.06]
- After Asala's death, the leaderless Nerevenans were assigned to support the friendly undead forces. [ch 058 / 058.06]

## Last Observed State
Asala is dead: Solom's clone reduced her to dust during the negotiations at Szith Morcane. Chapter 058 explicitly confirms her death when describing the subsequent assignment of the leaderless Nerevenans. Her mentions in that chapter concern her death and its consequences, rather than a new appearance. [ch 057 / 057.05; ch 058 / 058.01; ch 058 / 058.06]

## Relationships
- **Velasta:** Asala served as her assistant and facilitated contact with the party. [ch 056 / entry; ch 057 / entry]
- **The party:** Asala approached them as potential negotiating partners and provided access to Velasta. [ch 056 / 056.05]
- **Rilira:** Their claims within Szith Morcane conflicted; Rilira's public assertion of ownership prompted Asala to assert the family claim. [ch 057 / 057.05]
- **Solom's clone:** Killed Asala and publicly treated her removal as resolving a problem. [ch 057 / 057.05]
- **Nerevenans:** Her death left the faction leaderless, establishing that she held a leadership role. [ch 058 / 058.06]
