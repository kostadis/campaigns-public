## Identity
Madame Darkness is a drow fortune teller at Doctor Jubal’s carnival. Her performance persona is a sun-elf, supported by golden skin paint. [ch 008 / 008.04; ch 009 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
She uses a false identity in her performance but admits the deception when confronted. Her accent and demeanor then become those of a drow from the Underdark. Her reasons for adopting the persona are not established. [ch 009 / 009.02]

## History with the Party
- A carnie mentioned Madame Darkness as one of four attractions Doctor Jubal recently brought to the carnival. [ch 008 / 008.04]
- The heroes had their fortunes read by Madame Darkness, a drow who painted her skin golden and declared herself a sun-elf. [ch 009 / 009.02]
- When confronted, she admitted her fortune-telling was an act; her accent and demeanor became those of a drow from the Underdark. [ch 009 / 009.02]
