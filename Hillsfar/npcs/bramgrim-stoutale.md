## Identity
Bramgrim Stoutale, also called Bramgrim, is a cleric of Sharindlar who identifies healing as his calling as a cleric of life. The DM describes him as lawful good. [ch 006 / 006.05; ch 040 / 040.04; ch 052 / 052.01]

He describes himself as an Underdark creature, demonstrates knowledge of its fungi, and speaks Undercommon. His companions refer to him as a dwarf. [ch 036 / 036.05; ch 036 / 036.07; ch 049 / 049.01]

## Personality and Motivations
Bramgrim combines compassion with concern for practical security. He responds sympathetically to betrayal motivated by love, encourages a despondent stranger, and argues that saving the world must still leave the party with money and somewhere to live. [ch 004 / 004.07; ch 051 / 051.03; ch 035 / moment]

He wants to oppose spreading demonic influence and welcomes the prospect of the party becoming indispensable heroes. Payment also influences his advocacy for missions and his negotiations on the party’s behalf. [ch 010 / 010.06; ch 048 / 048.02; ch 008 / 008.01; ch 035 / 035.06]

His religious objections do not consistently govern his actions. He resists cooperation with an evil clerical cult but accepts a temporary alliance against Graz'zt after Daein persuades him. Later, he weakly objects to sacrificing the Nerevenans for undead military support, then personally executes Oxania at her request. [ch 056 / 056.02; ch 059 / 059.02; ch 059 / 059.03]

He can be argumentative about his judgment and his companions’ tactics, while also indulging in exuberant mushroom communion. Earlier madness remains a source of friction when companions question his advice. [ch 037 / 037.01; ch 040 / 040.02; ch 048 / 048.01; ch 048 / 048.02]
