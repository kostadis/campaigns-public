## Identity
Arrizz is a drow and former smuggler who moved supplies between the surface and the Underdark. He has distinct facial scars left by an earlier disease. He has used Lioneth the sword juggler as a disguise while traveling with minstrels. [ch 049 / entry; ch 049 / 049.04; ch 026 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Arrizz is calculating, pragmatic, and dry-witted. He prioritizes personal safety, seeking the party’s protection while declining combat and offering cooking as his contribution. [ch 049 / 049.04; ch 050 / entry]

He prefers familiar Underdark dangers to surface life and dislikes painful sunlight. His suspicion and erratic behavior during his rescue accompanied demonic madness; treatment restored his familiar calculating demeanor. [ch 049 / 049.04]

His concerns have also extended to other drow: he sought the party’s help for Szith Morcane against fire giant attacks. [ch 027 / entry]

## History with the Party
- Bramgrim found notes on Deriel's body identifying Arrizz as her beloved, held by the Captain; Arrizz's note reported illness, an escape from Duergar bounty hunters, and survival conditional on Deriel obeying the Captain. [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.03]
- Erlich demanded a steady supply of non-humans for Hillsfar Arena as the price of Arrizz's freedom; Deriel's note admitted sending children, women and farmers to their deaths to save him. [ch 003 / 003.03]
- Deriel said she met Arrizz in the Cormanthor Forest, lost him, and tracked him to a beet farm, where she threatened Erlich to demand his release. [ch 004 / 004.07]
- Felkur said the companions had found Arrizz very sick at the beet farm and promised to take Deriel to him. [ch 004 / 004.07]
- Deriel Rethslane left the heroes for Goodroot Beet Farm to find Arrizz, promising help if she and Arrizz ever could. [ch 005 / 005.02]
- Arrizz traveled with the minstrels disguised as Lioneth the sword juggler. [ch 026 / entry]
- The chapter 027 recap described Arrizz as a drow the party had helped previously and said he wanted their help for the drow of Szith Morcane, who were being attacked by fire giants. [ch 027 / 027.01]
- Daein recognized Arrizz's voice answering insults defiantly in Undercommon beyond the northwest door of the rogue mind flayer's lair; the party deferred opening that door. [ch 048 / 048.05]
- The party subsequently found Arrizz tied to a post, surrounded by six Githyanki who tormented him and prodded the facial scars left by his earlier disease. [ch 049 / 049.02]
- The party fought the Githyanki and freed Arrizz. [ch 049 / 049.03; ch 049 / 049.04]
- Arrizz recalled helping the party smuggle supplies to the drow alongside a cheerful woman, during an expedition involving demons and demon-worshipping kuo-toa. [ch 049 / 049.04]
- Arrizz said he had abandoned his work between the surface and the Underdark, returned underground seeking safety, and been captured. [ch 049 / 049.04]
- The party identified Arrizz's demonic madness and cured it with a piece of magical mushroom, restoring his familiar calculating demeanor. [ch 049 / 049.04]
- Arrizz could not identify when or where his madness began, but said the place was infected and confirmed that his captors intended to remove his brain for the jar. [ch 049 / 049.04]
- Arrizz agreed to travel with the party temporarily for protection, declined to fight, offered to cook, and waited nearby while they explored. [ch 049 / 049.04]
- Arrizz accompanied the party on its uneventful return journey toward Sporedome, then departed along the way, expecting another meeting would involve another rescue. [ch 050 / 050.04]

## Last Observed State
Arrizz was alive and traveling with the party on its uneventful return toward Sporedome, then departed along the way. His farewell anticipated another rescue if they met again; his destination was not established. [ch 050 / 050.04]

Before that departure, the party had freed him from Githyanki captivity and cured his demonic madness with magical mushroom. His facial scarring remained visible at the rescue. [ch 049 / 049.02; ch 049 / 049.04]

- **The party:** Former smuggling collaborators and repeated rescuers. Arrizz regards their strength as protection and accepts temporary companionship while maintaining firm limits on his involvement in danger. [ch 049 / 049.04; ch 050 / 050.04]

- **The drow of Szith Morcane:** Intended beneficiaries of Arrizz’s request for the party’s aid against fire giants. [ch 027 / entry]
