## Identity
Korkan was a hobgoblin slave leader who claimed the right to lead the revolt in the slave pens of Szith Morcane. [ch 054 / 054.02]

## Personality and Motivations
Korkan was confrontational and hostile to inherited authority: he rejected Rilira’s birthright as grounds for command and demanded allegiance through threats. He sought leadership of the uprising and regarded removing the fire giant as a prerequisite for revolt. [ch 054 / 054.02; ch 054 / 054.03]

## History with the Party
- Korkan challenged Rilira’s inherited authority, wanted her dead, and threatened the party if they supported her. His ability to alert the nearby fire giant made the threat consequential. [ch 054 / 054.02]
- Korkan insisted that the fire giant had to be removed before a revolt could succeed. [ch 054 / 054.03]
- Rilira demanded Korkan’s head or equivalent proof of his death in exchange for the Drow slaves’ support, alongside the fire giant’s death. [ch 054 / 054.03]
- Daein deceived Korkan into believing the party would charge alongside him. Korkan and three hobgoblin companions attacked while the party stayed behind. [ch 054 / 054.04]
- The giant wounded Korkan, and the three Azer each killed one of his companions. Their charge gave the party an opening to attack. [ch 054 / 054.04]
- Bramgrim deliberately allowed Spirit Guardians to affect Korkan. Korkan retaliated, striking Bramgrim once before dying within the divine aura. [ch 054 / 054.04]
- In chapter 055, Rilira spoke contemptuously of Korkan, comparing his usefulness to that of a dog. [ch 055 / moment]

## Last Observed State
Korkan is dead, killed by Bramgrim’s Spirit Guardians in the slave pens after retaliating against the cleric. His three hobgoblin companions also died in the fighting. [ch 054 / 054.04]

In chapter 055, Korkan was spoken of contemptuously by Rilira; this was a reference to him after his death, not a new appearance. [ch 055 / entry; ch 055 / moment]

## Relationships
- **Rilira:** Rival for leadership of the uprising. Korkan rejected her authority and wanted her dead; she made proof of his death a condition of Drow support and later dismissed his usefulness with contempt. [ch 054 / 054.02; ch 054 / 054.03; ch 055 / moment]
- **Daein:** Exploited Korkan’s willingness to attack the fire giant by falsely promising the party would charge alongside him. [ch 054 / 054.04]
- **Bramgrim:** Deliberately subjected Korkan to Spirit Guardians. Korkan responded by attacking him before the aura killed Korkan. [ch 054 / 054.04]
- **Hobgoblin companions:** Three followers joined Korkan’s charge; each was killed by an Azer. [ch 054 / 054.04]
