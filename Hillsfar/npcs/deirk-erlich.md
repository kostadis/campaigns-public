## Identity
Deirk Erlich is an older, bony man missing teeth, encountered rebuilding a house in the valley. Mrs. Vigdas identified him as a newcomer who arrived after the valley was sacked and thought he came from the poor parts of Hillsfar. [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.03]

## Personality and Motivations
Deirk is deceptive and hostile toward the companions: Felkur recognised his account of the damage as a lie, and Deirk dismissed further conversation. His seizure of property taken from the Tinfellows establishes a material interest in their dispossession; no broader motivation is established. [ch 002 / 002.03; ch 003 / 003.02]

## History with the Party
- Felkur encountered Deirk Erlich rebuilding a house with his sons Grey, Chort and Jermee and a hired carpenter. Deirk was bony and missing teeth. [ch 002 / 002.03]
- Deirk claimed “Marauding elves” damaged the house; Felkur knew he was lying. Deirk dismissed the companions, saying “we have work to do here.” [ch 002 / 002.03]
- Mrs. Vigdas said Deirk and his boys arrived after the valley was sacked and thought they came from the poor parts of Hillsfar. [ch 002 / 002.03]
- Felkur planned to deal with the Erlichs after freeing the Tinfellows. [ch 002 / 002.03]
- During the battle, Daein identified Captain Erlich as Deirk’s brother. The companions realised Deirk was seizing property his brother had taken from the Tinfellows; Deirk was mentioned, with no presence established in that scene. [ch 003 / 003.02]
- After the battle, Falofa wanted to deal with Deirk before returning to consult Olisara, and Daein agreed. Deirk was spoken of; no confrontation with him is recorded in this scene. [ch 004 / 004.01]

## Last Observed State
Deirk was last directly observed rebuilding the house with his sons and a hired carpenter. [ch 002 / 002.03] His latest mention concerns Falofa and Daein’s intention to deal with him before consulting Olisara; no confrontation or subsequent outcome is recorded. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 004 / 004.01]
