## Identity
Vraxen is a fox-faced Arcanaloth who enforces Graz'zt’s contracts in Maerimydra and serves as his accountant. His residence is the Spire of Inevitable Reckoning. [ch 043 / entry; ch 052 / entry; ch 041 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Vraxen is meticulous about financial accuracy, suspicious of embezzlement, and violently punitive when he perceives betrayal. His confrontation with Veperyd escalated from missing tribute to an order for flaying. [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.06]

His journal establishes plans to betray his Yugoloth superiors; the evidence does not establish his intended outcome beyond that betrayal. [ch 045 / 045.01]

He expresses contempt through accounting language and dismissive descriptions of the adventurers, treating their interference as repeated financial loss and humiliation for Graz'zt. [ch 052 / 052.01]

## History with the Party
- The party contracted with Maram il Vass to retrieve the Lexicon of Oaths from Vraxen in Maerimydra; Maram suggested provoking conflict between Vraxen and the demon-powered giants. [ch 039 / 039.01]
- Pipyap identified Vraxen’s residence as the Spire of Inevitable Reckoning, containing both the desired book and the party’s escape teleportation circle. [ch 041 / 041.06]
- Pipyap reported that Veperyd had been stealing betting money from Vraxen and proposed exposing the theft as a distraction. [ch 041 / 041.06]
- Vraxen was described as Graz'zt’s yugoloth agent enforcing the giants’ agreement to spread destruction through the Underdark. [ch 042 / 042.02]
- The party learned that Vraxen carried the key to the upper rooms and settled on entering his dinner as Hillsfar traders offering information about the Phantom Ghost. [ch 043 / 043.04]
- The party bluffed past the entrance guards; Vraxen asked them to introduce themselves and invited them to sit with Veperyd and High Inquisitor Veris. [ch 043 / 043.05]
- At dinner, Vraxen accused Veperyd of withholding 3,000 gold pieces of Colosseum tribute; Daein and Bramgrim intensified the financial dispute to divert attention from Felkur. [ch 043 / 043.06]
- With the owlbear creating a distraction on the table, Daein telekinetically stole Vraxen’s runic key unnoticed. [ch 043 / 043.06]
- The party used an illusion of Veris’s voice to escalate the dispute; Vraxen ordered his guards to flay Veperyd, a three-way fight began, and the party escaped upstairs with the key. [ch 043 / 043.06]
- Felkur extracted Vraxen’s scent from the Owlbear and applied it to satisfy the vault golem’s recognition, gaining passage. [ch 044 / 044.03]
- In the vault, the party discovered Vraxen’s journal documenting plans to betray his Yugoloth superiors, a soulcoin ledger with blackmail potential, and blank infernal contracts. An unnamed party member took the contracts; removal of the journal, ledger, or Lexicon was not established. [ch 045 / 045.01]
- Felkur proposed detonating his cannon among Vraxen’s possessions to divert him from the rooftop fight. The cannon was moved toward the library chute, but the detonation had not occurred. [ch 046 / 046.02; ch 046 / moment]
- The players later reviewed Vraxen’s letter to Graz'zt accounting for the party’s damage in soul coins, including riots and destroyed soul-trapping apparatus. The letter concerned the party but was not a direct demand for payment addressed to them. [ch 052 / 052.01]
- Vraxen’s separate magical ledger remained a target of interest to the Emerald Enclave High Council. [ch 052 / entry]

- **Graz'zt:** Employer and recipient of Vraxen’s accounting. Vraxen oversees the giants’ contractual obligation to spread destruction through the Underdark. [ch 042 / 042.02; ch 052 / 052.01]
- **Veperyd:** Betting associate whose theft undermined their arrangement; their financial dispute erupted into violence. [ch 041 / 041.06; ch 043 / 043.06]
- **Maram il Vass:** Rival who commissioned the party to retrieve the Lexicon of Oaths from Vraxen. [ch 039 / 039.01]
- **High Inquisitor Veris:** Dinner guest who became involved in the three-way fight after the party manipulated the confrontation. No broader alliance is established. [ch 043 / 043.05; ch 043 / 043.06]
