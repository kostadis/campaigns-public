## Identity
Xaven Tanor'this is the drow chief alchemist developing a ritual to remove the fiendish blood empowering Graz'zt's fire giants. [ch 035 / 035.05]

## Personality and Motivations
His haste to reach the drow encampment and subsequent research with Solom Ned'razak suggest urgency about resuming the work. His documented objective is completing the ritual to remove demon taint; broader personal motives are not established. [ch 039 / 039.03; ch 039 / 039.07]

## History with the Party
- Murssvayas Dhuurniv told the party that Xaven Tanor'this, the drow chief alchemist, had been captured while developing a ritual to remove the fiendish blood empowering Graz'zt's fire giants. [ch 035 / 035.05]
- Murssvayas reported that Xaven had been sold to Maram il Vass and asked the party to recover him and return him to the Sporedome so the ritual could be completed. [ch 035 / 035.05]
- Maram confirmed holding Xaven, who had arrived unconscious and badly injured, and demanded an upfront exchange. His eventual price was 10,000 gold pieces or two rare magical items; the exchange remained unfinished at the session’s end. [ch 037 / 037.07]
- In the Hanging Gardens, Xaven heard Daein calling and identified himself by shaking his cage, which hung one hundred feet above the floor. [ch 038 / entry]
- The Owlbear lowered Xaven safely and carried him toward the exit inside his cage; the party brought him back to Maram’s office. [ch 038 / entry]
- At Sporedome, Xaven hurried the party to the drow encampment, received a welcome from several drow, and joined Solom in a tent to research. [ch 039 / 039.03]
- Xaven and Solom Ned'razak researched a ritual to remove demon taint that required blood from a living fire giant infused with fiendish blood. The party agreed to help. [ch 039 / 039.07]
- Xaven greeted the party alongside Solom Ned'razak when they returned to Sporedome with Brandyr alive. Solom said the ritual would take a ten-day. [ch 047 / 047.03]

## Last Observed State
Alive and present at Sporedome, Xaven rushed out alongside Solom Ned'razak to greet the returning party. They had delivered Brandyr alive for the ritual; Solom estimated a ten-day to complete it. Xaven’s specific duties at that stage are not stated. [ch 047 / 047.03]

## Relationships
- **Solom Ned'razak:** Research collaborator on the ritual to remove demon taint. [ch 039 / 039.07]
- **The party:** Rescued him from his hanging cage and returned him to Sporedome, where he subsequently greeted them upon their return with Brandyr. [ch 038 / entry; ch 039 / 039.03; ch 047 / 047.03]
- **Maram il Vass:** Former captor who acquired him from fire giants and demanded an exchange for his release. [ch 037 / 037.07; ch 038 / entry]
- **The drow at Sporedome:** Several welcomed him upon his return to their encampment. [ch 039 / 039.03]
