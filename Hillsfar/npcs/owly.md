## Identity
Owly is an Owlbear and a longstanding companion of the party. [ch 015 / entry; ch 035 / 035.03]

## Personality and Motivations
Owly shows a preference for Felkur as his companion. The summaries do not establish broader motivations or a consistent temperament. [ch 015 / 015.05]

## History with the Party
- Owly stood beside Felkur as he pretended to be drunk during the party's ambush of a goblin war party near the Cormanthor forest, outside the Rimwood. [ch 012 / 012.06]
- Owly, an Owlbear, chose to follow Felkur. [ch 015 / 015.05]
- Owly peed on Daein after Daein's failed attempt to win him over. [ch 015 / moment]
- The party recalled encountering Owly at level 3 and keeping him as a companion through seven further levels. [ch 035 / 035.03]
- Daein requested custom armor for Owly; the party commissioned scale mail for 200 gold pieces, intended to raise his armor class from 13 to 15. [ch 035 / 035.03]
- Akritas was assigned to handle the armor arrangements while the smith worked for several days. [ch 035 / 035.03]
- At Silver Shadow Cave, Murssvayas Dhuurniv addressed Owly in a way that caused Owly to recognize him. [ch 035 / 035.05]
- During the approach to the Den of the Whispered Word, the Owlbear's name was clarified as Owly rather than Alfred. [ch 037 / 037.06]

## Last Observed State
Owly was last explicitly shown responding at Silver Shadow Cave, where he recognized Murssvayas Dhuurniv after being addressed. [ch 035 / 035.05]

He was subsequently mentioned during the approach to the Den of the Whispered Word, when his name was clarified; that mention does not establish his presence or entry into the Den. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 037 / 037.06]

Custom scale mail had been commissioned for him, but its completion and delivery are not established. [ch 035 / 035.03]

## Relationships
- **Felkur:** Owly's chosen companion; their association also includes Owly standing beside him during an ambush. [ch 015 / 015.05; ch 012 / 012.06]
- **Daein:** Despite an unsuccessful attempt to win Owly over, Daein is invested in protecting him and sought armor for him. [ch 015 / moment; ch 035 / 035.03; ch 035 / moment]
- **Akritas:** Responsible for arranging Owly's commissioned armor. [ch 035 / 035.03]
- **Murssvayas Dhuurniv:** Owly recognized him when addressed; the basis of that recognition is unspecified. [ch 035 / 035.05]
