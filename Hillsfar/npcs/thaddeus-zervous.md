## Identity
Thaddeus Zervous is the former magistrate of The Stop, overthrown by La Resistance. He was involved in selling slaves in league with the Duergar. [ch 012 / 012.04]

## Personality and Motivations
His documented conduct includes slave trading and increased enforcement of the Law of Humanity alongside the city of Hillsfar; non-human farmers lost their farms as enforcement intensified. These actions establish his policies, but the summaries do not establish his personal motives or temperament. [ch 012 / 012.04; ch 014 / 014.02]

## History with the Party
- Serinia reported that Thaddeus Zervous, former magistrate of The Stop, had been selling slaves in league with the Duergar; La Resistance staged a coup, and he fled the city. [ch 012 / 012.04]
