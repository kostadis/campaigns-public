## Identity
Margery "Mags" Thrier buys prisoners for the Arena. She is a middle-aged woman with spear-straight posture who wears a blue gown. [ch 004 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Her bargaining suggests a practical, selective approach to buying prisoners: she disputes Breex's price and questions the usefulness of the halflings. Her broader motives are not established. [ch 004 / 004.06]

## History with the Party
- Breex told the prisoners he would sell them to Mags, who would put them in the Arena. [ch 004 / 004.04]
- At the Arena, Mags arrived with Horac and a shirtless Goliath leashed to her belt; the Goliath shook Bramgrim's wagon to test the prisoners. [ch 004 / 004.06]
- Mags haggled with Breex, said she had no use for the halflings, and then told Horac to pay Breex and bring the prisoners inside. [ch 004 / 004.06]

## Last Observed State
Mags was last observed at the Arena directing Horac to pay Breex and bring the prisoners inside. Her subsequent whereabouts are not established. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 004 / 004.06]

## Relationships
- **Breex:** A supplier of prisoners with whom she negotiates purchases for the Arena. [ch 004 / 004.06]
- **Horac:** Acts under her direction in paying for and admitting prisoners. [ch 004 / 004.06]
- **Goliath:** Accompanies her on a leash attached to her belt and tests the prisoners by shaking Bramgrim's wagon; the relationship beyond this is unexplained. [ch 004 / 004.06]
- **The prisoners:** She deals with them as prospective Arena purchases, assessing their usefulness during the negotiation. [ch 004 / 004.06]

## Notable Quotes
> "We will sell you to Mags, and then she will put you into the Arena." — Breex [ch 004 / 004.04]

> "But I have no use for the halflings." — Mags [ch 004 / 004.06]
