## Identity
Quil'bryn is an elite Drow warrior and resistance leader allied with the party. He sought a discreet position as second-in-command beneath public party leadership and a council of war. [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.06]

## Personality and Motivations
His restored sanity brings genuine horror at his killing of Solom Ned'razak, but leaves his calculating military outlook intact. He prioritizes preserving elite Drow and party strength, treating other recruits as expendable protection. [ch 051 / entry; ch 052 / moment]

He favors concealed authority and pragmatic cooperation across factional divisions. His commitment to cooperation between Drow and gnomes is explicitly temporary; their future relations remain unresolved. [ch 051 / 051.05; ch 051 / 051.06]

## History with the Party
- Quil'bryn murdered Solom Ned'razak while afflicted by demonic madness, eight days before the party reached the resistance camp. [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.05]
- He opposed Felkur's effort to rally the Drow, arguing that survival mattered more than continuing the campaign. [ch 051 / 051.05]
- After Bramgrim recognized his madness, Felkur threatened to force-feed him a braincap mushroom and Daein supplied the intimidation; Quil'bryn ate it to prove his sanity. [ch 051 / 051.05]
- The mushroom restored his sanity, leaving him horrified by Solom's murder. [ch 051 / 051.05]
- He pledged his sword to Felkur and declared a temporary alliance between Drow and gnomes; the remaining Drow rallied to the party's cause. [ch 051 / 051.05]
- He identified the Hidden, the Lodge of Faces, the Nerevenans, and escaped slaves as potential reinforcements. [ch 051 / 051.06]
- He proposed using allied troops to absorb casualties while the Drow and party delivered decisive attacks. [ch 051 / 051.06]
- He wanted his position as second-in-command concealed beneath public party leadership and a council of war. [ch 051 / 051.06]
- He recommended a secret Drow passage to the supply-cart ambush point. [ch 052 / 052.03]
- Sorn later proposed Quil'bryn as an alternative commander for the uprising during negotiations involving Rilira and Korkan; this was a discussion of him, not an established appearance. [ch 054 / 054.03]
- Rylzyn and Sorn invoked his plan while discussing weapons for Rilira's followers; Rilira sneered at his mention. [ch 055 / 055.01]
- Bramgrim and Akritas followed his advice to wait visibly on the webs until the Hidden contacted them. [ch 055 / 055.02]
- He agreed to provide a guide toward Kiaransalee's followers, limiting his men's involvement to the edge of their territory. [ch 056 / 056.04]
- He agreed to keep his troops silent about his killing of Solom to preserve the alliance with the revenant. [ch 056 / 056.06]
- The party discovered Solom's magical clone, a contingency prepared before Quil'bryn killed the original body. [ch 056 / 056.07]
- At the assault planning, his Drow were assigned to reserve duty as a tactical backup; Daein persuaded Rilira to accept the arrangement despite her objections. [ch 058 / 058.06]

## Last Observed State
The latest account assigns Quil'bryn's Drow to reserve duty as a tactical backup for the assault on Maerimydra. Daein secures Rilira's acceptance despite her objections. This establishes his faction's assignment, without establishing Quil'bryn's personal presence or location at the planning scene. [ch 058 / 058.06]

Status not established in the summaries. [ch 058 / entry; ch 058 / 058.06]

## Relationships
- **Felkur:** Receives Quil'bryn's personal allegiance and sword pledge. Quil'bryn approves of his recruitment ambition and supports party leadership while seeking private strategic influence. [ch 051 / 051.05; ch 051 / 051.06]
- **Solom Ned'razak:** Quil'bryn killed his original body under demonic madness and was horrified after his cure. The later discovery of Solom's prepared magical clone establishes that the original body's death did not end Solom's involvement. [ch 051 / 051.05; ch 056 / 056.07]
- **Daein:** Supports concealing Quil'bryn's intended use of allied recruits and later persuades Rilira to accept his Drow's reserve assignment. [ch 051 / 051.06; ch 058 / 058.06]
- **Rilira:** Displays contempt when his plan is invoked and objects to keeping his Drow in reserve. [ch 055 / 055.01; ch 058 / 058.06]
- **The Hidden:** Quil'bryn provides useful contact instructions, but says the party must approach without him because the Hidden will not welcome Lolth's followers. [ch 051 / 051.06; ch 055 / 055.02]
- **Kiaransalee's followers:** Limits his troops' assistance to guiding the party to the edge of their territory. He also agrees to suppress the truth about Solom's killing to preserve the alliance with the revenant Velasta. [ch 056 / 056.04; ch 056 / 056.06]
