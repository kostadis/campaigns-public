## Identity
Veperyd is a mutated fire giant whose skin weeps oily black smoke. A general, he runs Maerimydra’s Colosseum, duels, and betting pool. [ch 043 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Veperyd responds to financial scrutiny with anger, defending his usefulness through arena management, sacrifice, and the subjugation of slaves. He invokes drow rebels as a more urgent concern than accounting; when threatened, he promises violent retaliation. His smoke intensifies with his anger. [ch 043 / entry]

## History with the Party
- Pipyap identified Veperyd as the fiendish giant running the Coliseum and its betting pool, and reported that he was stealing from his business partner Vraxen. [ch 041 / 041.06]
- The party planned to exploit Veperyd’s theft to provoke a fight with Vraxen and cover their theft of the book. They chose the courtyard’s giant as their capture target after initially considering Veperyd. [ch 041 / 041.07]
- Pipyap advised that an accusation against Veperyd would carry weight if it appeared to come from a dinner guest. [ch 043 / 043.04]
- The party encountered Veperyd at dinner with Vraxen and High Inquisitor Veris in the Spire of Inevitable Reckoning. [ch 043 / 043.05]
- Vraxen accused Veperyd of withholding 3,000 gold pieces of Colosseum tribute; Veperyd defended his arena management and invoked drow rebels as a more urgent concern. [ch 043 / 043.06]
- Daein and Bramgrim redirected attention from Felkur by questioning Veperyd’s betting commissions, intensifying the financial dispute. [ch 043 / 043.06]
- The party used an illusion of Veris’s voice to provoke a three-way fight. Vraxen ordered Veperyd flayed, Veperyd threatened repayment in blood, and the party escaped upstairs with the stolen key. [ch 043 / 043.06]
- Status not established in the summaries. [ch 043 / 043.06; ch 047 / 047.02]
- During the party’s escape preparations, a Fire Giant attacked Daein in anger over their treatment of its master, naming Veperyd; Veperyd himself was only mentioned in that encounter. [ch 047 / 047.02]

## Last Observed State
Veperyd was last directly observed fighting Vraxen and High Inquisitor Veris at the dinner after Vraxen ordered him flayed. The party left while the fight continued. [ch 043 / 043.06]

He was subsequently mentioned by a Fire Giant attacking Daein over the party’s treatment of its master, Veperyd; Veperyd himself was not shown present. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 043 / 043.06; ch 047 / 047.02]
