## Identity
Hamadh leads the Hidden, a group of Vhaeraunite rebels, and represents the Cult of Vhaeraun in negotiations over Szith Morcane. [ch 055 / entry; ch 057 / 057.05]

## Personality and Motivations
Cautious and politically alert, Hamadh values stealth and cunning over combat prowess. He scrutinizes prospective allies’ commitments before offering support. [ch 055 / entry; ch 055 / 055.02]

He seeks drow control of Szith Morcane and recognition of his faction’s interests. His willingness to accept a successful mission despite its alarm and fighting shows flexibility in judging results. [ch 055 / 055.04; ch 057 / entry]

## History with the Party
- Bramgrim and Akritas met Hamadh, leader of the Hidden, at gnome ruins near Szith Morcane. [ch 055 / 055.02]
- Hamadh accepted that the party had promised drow governance without committing to a particular faction, and warned them of the danger of choosing sides. [ch 055 / 055.02]
- Hamadh required the party to prove its stealth and cunning by recovering the contents of a giant-held weapon cache, ideally without a fight. [ch 055 / 055.02]
- Despite the alarm and skirmish, Hamadh was impressed and pledged the Hidden's support, conditional on defeating Dengor and allowing the drow to reclaim Szith Morcane. [ch 055 / 055.04]
- During the competing claims to Szith Morcane, Hamadh represented the Cult of Vhaeraun and watched the party with growing suspicion, feeling his interests were being ignored. [ch 057 / 057.05; ch 057 / entry]
- Chapter 058 mentions Hamadh in the recap of the Szith Morcane dispute; it does not establish his presence at Sporedome. [ch 058 / 058.01]

## Last Observed State
Hamadh was last directly observed at the negotiations in Szith Morcane, watching the party with growing suspicion and feeling that his interests were being ignored. [ch 057 / 057.05; ch 057 / entry]

Chapter 058 mentions him only in the recap of that dispute; it does not establish his presence at Sporedome. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 058 / 058.01]

## Relationships
- **The Hidden / Cult of Vhaeraun:** Hamadh leads the Hidden and represents the Cult’s interests in the contested city. [ch 055 / entry; ch 057 / 057.05]
- **The party:** A conditional ally whose initial approval has become strained by perceived neglect of his interests. His pledged support required Dengor’s defeat and drow reclamation of Szith Morcane. [ch 055 / 055.04; ch 057 / entry]
