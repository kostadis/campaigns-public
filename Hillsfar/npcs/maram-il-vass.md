## Identity
Maram il Vass is a Yugoloth who conducts business at the Den of the Whispered Word in the Howling Pits. He initially presented himself as a handsome half-elf, a disguise Bramgrim penetrated with True Seeing. [ch 035 / 035.05; ch 037 / 037.07]

He trades in information and power and claims fame for his contracts. [ch 037 / moment; ch 038 / moment]

## Personality and Motivations
Maram treats captives, information, and secrecy as bargaining assets. His negotiations make personal vulnerability a source of leverage, particularly Felkur’s exposure to pursuit. [ch 037 / 037.07; ch 038 / 038.06; ch 042 / entry]

He is willing to revise contractual terms under scrutiny, but securing protections against betrayal required explicit negotiation and magical binding. His concern over the loss of a demon guard was limited to its replacement. [ch 037 / 037.07; ch 038 / 038.06]

His established objective was obtaining the Lexicon of Oaths from his rival Vraxen. The evidence supports an attempt to exploit competing interests around Felkur’s identity; his broader purpose for the book is not established. [ch 039 / 039.01; ch 043 / 043.04; ch 044 / 044.05]

## History with the Party
- Murssvayas Dhuurniv told the party that captured alchemist Xaven Tanor'this had been sold to Maram il Vass at the Den of the Whispered Word in the Howling Pits. The party accepted the mission to recover Xaven. [ch 035 / 035.05; ch 035 / 035.06]
- Escaped captives Tegan and Drystan described their enslavement by Maram; Tegan initially feared the party served him and had come to continue their torture. [ch 037 / 037.02; ch 037 / 037.04]
- The party met Maram behind a purple wooden desk in the Den. Bramgrim’s True Seeing revealed a Yugoloth beneath his handsome half-elf appearance; Maram later abandoned that appearance. [ch 037 / 037.07]
- Maram knew Felkur’s name and the theft accusations against him, offering help for a price he initially left unspecified. [ch 037 / 037.07]
- Maram confirmed holding Xaven. He proposed exchanging him for Felkur, then suggested Bramgrim, before setting a price of 10,000 gold pieces or two rare magical items. Bramgrim refused to trade Felkur. [ch 037 / 037.07]
- When the party requested careful examination of a proposed privacy contract, Maram withdrew it as the wrong contract. A replacement agreement covered deliberation without spying or disruption. [ch 037 / 037.07]
- Maram accepted two armor sets created by Felkur that would remain magical for eight hours. Felkur supplied false provenance, and Daein encouraged Maram to associate the merchandise with Graz'zt’s defeat in Hillsfar. [ch 038 / 038.01; ch 038 / 038.02]
- Maram required the party to collect Xaven from the Hanging Gardens themselves. They returned with Xaven after killing one demon guard; Maram showed little concern about replacing it. [ch 038 / 038.02; ch 038 / 038.05; ch 038 / 038.06]
- Maram offered to withhold Felkur’s whereabouts from a pursuing devil if Felkur retrieved a magical item. Felkur negotiated disclosure of its location and defenses, a no-betrayal provision backed by a cosmic curse or forfeiture of Maram’s true name, and magical binding. [ch 038 / 038.06]
- The retrieval contract identified the item as the Lexicon of Oaths, held by Maram’s rival Vraxen in Maerimydra. Maram supplied an hourglass marking the remaining time and suggested provoking conflict between Vraxen and the demon-powered giants. [ch 039 / 039.01]
- While Maram was spoken of in Maerimydra, the party planned to steal his requested book and escape through Vraxen’s teleportation circle. [ch 041 / 041.06; ch 041 / 041.07]
- Chapter 042 described Maram’s demand as blackmail: the Lexicon in exchange for concealing Felkur’s identity as the thief behind an earlier Mulmaster artifact theft, separate from the Lexicon retrieval. [ch 042 / entry; ch 042 / 042.01]
- Before entering the Spire, Felkur deduced a probable double cross: Maram il Vass had likely directed High Inquisitor Veris toward the Lexicon’s information about the Phantom Ghost while threatening Felkur with exposure unless he retrieved it first. [ch 043 / 043.04]
- Reading the Lexicon in the vault supplied recorded details of Maram il Vass’s sale of Felkur’s information and the contract requiring the party to retrieve the Lexicon, supporting their existing suspicion of his double cross. [ch 044 / 044.05]

## Relationships
- **Felkur:** A target of recruitment, bargaining, and blackmail. Maram used threatened exposure to secure the Lexicon retrieval; the negotiated contract imposed a cosmic curse or forfeiture of Maram’s true name if he switched sides. [ch 037 / 037.07; ch 038 / 038.06; ch 042 / entry]
- **Bramgrim:** A resistant negotiator who exposed Maram’s disguise and refused to exchange Felkur for Xaven Tanor'this. Maram also proposed Bramgrim as an alternative exchange. [ch 037 / 037.07]
- **Xaven Tanor'this, Tegan, and Drystan:** Captives associated with Maram’s trade. Maram confirmed holding Xaven Tanor'this; Tegan and Drystan described enslavement by him, and Tegan feared renewed torture by supposed agents of Maram. [ch 037 / 037.02; ch 037 / 037.04; ch 037 / 037.07]
- **Vraxen:** Maram’s rival and the holder of the Lexicon he commissioned the party to retrieve. [ch 039 / 039.01]
- **High Inquisitor Veris:** A suspected parallel customer or beneficiary. Felkur deduced that Maram had probably directed Veris toward the Lexicon’s information about the Phantom Ghost; the exact arrangement remains unconfirmed. [ch 043 / 043.04]
