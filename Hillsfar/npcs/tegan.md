## Identity
Tegan is a former Dalelands farmer and Drystan’s sister, enslaved by Maram after orc raiders captured the siblings five years earlier. [ch 037 / 037.04]

## Personality and Motivations
Protective of Drystan and initially distrustful of strangers, Tegan becomes willing to cooperate when the party demonstrates that it can oppose their pursuers. [ch 037 / 037.02; ch 037 / 037.04] She values escape from captivity enough to risk the dangerous winds, and recalls serving Maram with disgust. [ch 037 / moment; ch 037 / 037.04]

## History with the Party
- The party found Tegan comforting her brother Drystan in the tunnels; both wore torn rags and bore lash marks. [ch 037 / 037.02]
- Tegan stepped protectively before Drystan and accused the adventurers of serving Maram il Vass in another scheme to torture them. [ch 037 / 037.02]
- Tegan screamed when two Barlguras appeared to recapture the siblings. [ch 037 / 037.03]
- Reassured by the party's defeat of the demons, Tegan shared information about her captivity. [ch 037 / 037.04]
- Tegan explained that she and Drystan had been Dalelands farmers before orc raiders captured them five years earlier and sold them to Maram. [ch 037 / 037.04]
- She described with disgust serving Maram's guests and attending to his personal needs. [ch 037 / 037.04]
- Tegan fled when the siblings were taken outside to feed on cave mushrooms; the party established that their escape had occurred only a day earlier. [ch 037 / 037.04]
- Tegan remembered the Drow's arrival and said he was not kept in the palace's main section. [ch 037 / 037.04]
- She pointed toward the palace and warned that its gray-skinned gnome guard, Grabber Forth, answered only to Maram and disliked rude responses. [ch 037 / 037.04]
- Tegan said the palace contained no fire giants. [ch 037 / 037.04]
- Tegan and Drystan refused to accompany the party farther toward the Den of the Whispered Word. [ch 037 / 037.06]

## Last Observed State
Tegan’s last recorded decision is to refuse to accompany the party farther toward the Den of the Whispered Word, alongside Drystan. Her subsequent location and fate are not established in the summaries. [ch 037 / 037.06]

## Relationships
- **Drystan:** Her brother; she comforts him and places herself between him and unfamiliar adventurers. [ch 037 / 037.02]
- **Maram il Vass:** Her former captor and enslaver, whom she associates with torture and degrading service. [ch 037 / 037.02; ch 037 / 037.04]
- **The party:** Initially suspected torturers; their victory over the pursuing demons earns enough trust for her to share information, though she declines to accompany them farther. [ch 037 / 037.02; ch 037 / 037.04; ch 037 / 037.06]
