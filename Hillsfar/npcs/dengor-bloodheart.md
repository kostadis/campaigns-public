## Identity
Dengor is a fire giant king whose palace in Szith Morcane was identified as the principal obstacle to the city's liberation. [ch 055 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
His recorded activities center on manufacturing weapons for the surface and using enslaved stone giants to build his palace. These establish his operations, but provide little direct evidence of his personality or personal motives. [ch 052 / 052.02; ch 055 / 055.01]

## History with the Party
- Dengor was mentioned during planning as manufacturing weapons for the surface, with finished goods shipped out rather than stockpiled locally. [ch 052 / 052.02]
- Dengor was identified as the fire giant king occupying the palace in Szith Morcane, marked Area F, and the primary obstacle to liberating the city. [ch 055 / entry]
- The party discussed approaching Dengor's palace through the House Morcane ruins, allowing the forge and palace to be attacked during the same operation. [ch 055 / 055.01]
- The party had learned that three enslaved stone giants were forced to build Dengor's palace. [ch 055 / 055.01]
- Hamadh pledged the Hidden's support on condition that the party defeat Dengor and allow the drow to reclaim Szith Morcane. [ch 055 / 055.04]
- The Stone Giant King pledged assistance in exchange for freeing his enslaved kin from Dengor's palace. [ch 056 / 056.03]
- Felkur initiated the ambush by restraining Dengor and nearby guards with black tentacles. [ch 056 / 056.10]
- Felkur used passwall to connect Dengor's chamber to the main chamber, allowing Bramgrim to reach and heal the wounded stone giants. [ch 056 / 056.10]
- Dengor was slain in the battle; Daein delivered the final blow with Vicious Mockery. [ch 056 / 056.10]
- After Dengor's death, Velasta arrived demanding to know who had killed him so she could take vengeance upon them. [ch 056 / moment]

## Last Observed State
Dengor is dead, slain during the party's assault; Daein delivered the final blow with Vicious Mockery. [ch 056 / 056.10]

After his death, Velasta arrived demanding to know who had killed him so she could take vengeance upon them. [ch 056 / moment]

## Relationships
- **The party:** Opponents seeking to end his occupation of Szith Morcane; their assault killed him. [ch 055 / entry; ch 056 / 056.10]
- **Enslaved stone giants:** Three were forced to build his palace. The Stone Giant King made their release a condition of his assistance to the party. [ch 055 / 055.01; ch 056 / 056.03]
- **Hamadh and the Hidden:** Hamadh conditioned the Hidden's support on Dengor's defeat and the restoration of drow control over Szith Morcane. [ch 055 / 055.04]
- **Velasta:** She sought vengeance against whoever killed Dengor; the cited account does not explain the origin of that demand. [ch 056 / moment]
