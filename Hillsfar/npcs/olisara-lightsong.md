## Identity
Olisara Lightsong is a female elf and Harper agent and recruiter. She has long white hair, pale skin, piercing blue eyes, and a regal bearing, and wears a simple traveler's cloak. [ch 001 / entry; ch 004 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
She is composed and well informed about the companions’ affairs, revealing personal knowledge that surprises them. Her information has limits: she initially acknowledges having no witnesses to the farmstead raids. [ch 001 / 001.04; ch 001 / 001.05]

Her priorities include protecting the Harpers’ allies and rescuing the raids’ victims. She advocates non-violence because of political tensions with Hillsfar’s leadership, but responds coldly and firmly when the companions return without the Tinfellows. [ch 001 / 001.05; ch 004 / 004.02]

She shows concern for the rescued Tinfellows and caution about claims that First Lord Torin Nomerthal has lost power. [ch 005 / 005.03]

## History with the Party
- At the Cloverwall Roadhouse, Olisara Lightsong staged a puppet show demonstrating Hillsfar propaganda and introduced herself to the companions as a Harper recruiter. [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.04]
- She knew that Daein had bought armor from her friend Clidnep and spoke of Felkur's dealings with his guild, surprising him. [ch 001 / 001.04]
- She asked the companions to discover what had happened to the missing Tinfellows and rescue or avenge them, providing a rough map to their farm. [ch 001 / 001.05]
- She urged non-violence because of the Harpers' tense relationship with Hillsfar's leadership. [ch 001 / 001.05]
- She offered 40 gold for expenses and 60 gold each on their return and report. [ch 001 / 001.05]
- After Felkur reported that the Red Plumes were abducting farmers for arena bloodsports, she ordered the companions into Hillsfar to free the Tinfellows and other victims, referring them to Gai's plan. [ch 004 / 004.02]
- She was delighted to learn that the Tinfellows had been freed, particularly the agent Elbus Tinfellow. [ch 005 / 005.03]
- She was disturbed by the news from Hillsfar and doubted that First Lord Torin Nomerthal's reign had ended. [ch 005 / 005.03]
- She offered the heroes Harper membership; they refused. [ch 005 / 005.03]
- The companions accepted her offer of a meal at the Cloverwall Roadhouse at Bramgrim's insistence. [ch 005 / entry]
- Felkur's retrospective recalls a few days with Olisara Lightsong and the Tinfellows during which he ate well. [ch 006 / 006.01]
- Olisara was not present for Elbus Tinfellow's request for help recruiting a militia; Elbus cited her report that the First Lord had not been deposed and remained firmly in charge. [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.02]

## Last Observed State

## Relationships
- **The Harpers:** Her organization; she recruits agents and commissions work on its behalf. [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.05]
- **The companions:** Hired operatives whom she later invites to join the Harpers. They decline membership but accept her hospitality. [ch 001 / 001.05; ch 005 / entry]
- **Elbus Tinfellow and the Tinfellows:** Elbus is her friend and a fellow Harper agent. His family has aided her allies and is sympathetic to the Harpers. [ch 006 / 006.02; ch 001 / 001.05]
- **Clidnep:** Her friend, whose sale of armor to Daein she knows about. [ch 001 / 001.04]
- **Gai:** A collaborator whose infiltration and escape plan she endorses for the rescue mission. [ch 004 / 004.02]
