## Identity
Brandyr is an eighteen-foot-tall fiend-born fire giant twisted by abyssal blood. She has cooling-lava-colored skin, leathery wings, jagged horns, and a third eye burning with cold green light. She wears obsidian plate and flowing black robes and wields a midnight-dark greatsword. [ch 042 / entry]

She speaks Abyssal and leads a ritual dedicated to Graz'zt. [ch 042 / 042.04]

## Personality and Motivations
The DM explicitly characterizes Brandyr as arrogant, with demonic power further increasing her confidence. Her vigilance accompanies that confidence: she actively searches the courtyard for threats during the ritual. [ch 042 / entry]

Her ritual proclamation expresses allegiance to Graz'zt and a destructive purpose framed as an offering to the Prince of Pleasure. Personal ambitions beyond that religious commitment are not established. [ch 042 / moment]

## History with the Party
- Brandyr led a ritual to Graz'zt with four cambions in an obsidian courtyard. She scanned for threats without detecting the hidden party; the ritual finished before their ambush began. [ch 042 / 042.04]
- The party planned to capture Brandyr alive using Sleep, reduction magic, and a portable hole. [ch 042 / 042.04]
- Felkur put Brandyr to sleep; Bramgrim and Daein shrank her; Akritas approached invisibly and placed her in a portable hole equipped with a breathing apparatus. [ch 043 / 043.02]
- The party exploited the cambions’ belief that Graz’zt might have removed Brandyr. Felkur supplied projected sound while Daein performed Graz’zt’s voice, convincing all four guards to abandon their posts and spread the supposed revelation. [ch 043 / 043.03]
- Brandyr remained in the party’s portable hole without release or further interaction during chapter 043. [ch 043 / entry]
- At Sporedome, the party delivered Brandyr alive to Solom Ned'razak and his drow assistants. They secured her against escape and moved her into a nearby tent for experimentation; Solom said the ritual would take a ten-day. [ch 047 / 047.03]

## Last Observed State
Brandyr was last observed alive and captive at Sporedome. Solom Ned'razak and his drow assistants secured her against escape and moved her into a nearby tent for experimentation involving her blood. Solom said the ritual would take a ten-day; its outcome is not established. [ch 047 / 047.03; ch 047 / entry]

## Relationships
- **Graz'zt:** Brandyr conducts a ritual in his name. The evidence establishes her allegiance, but no direct response from Graz'zt to her actions. [ch 042 / 042.04]
- **Four cambion guards:** They support her ritual, but their discussion reveals resentment toward demonically empowered giants as potential rivals. Their belief that Graz'zt removed Brandyr was reinforced by the party’s impersonation. [ch 042 / 042.04; ch 043 / 043.03]
- **The party:** Her captors; Felkur, Bramgrim, Daein, and Akritas carried out her abduction and subsequently delivered her alive for experimentation. [ch 043 / 043.02; ch 047 / 047.03]
- **Solom Ned'razak and his drow assistants:** Her custodians at the last observation, holding her for experimentation and blood-borne rituals. [ch 047 / entry]
