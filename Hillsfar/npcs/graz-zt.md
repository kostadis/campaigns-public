## Identity
Graz'zt is a demon lord identified by a black six-fingered hand symbol. His influence extends through cult worship, demonic agents, and fire giants empowered with fiendish blood. [ch 009 / 009.04; ch 033 / 033.05; ch 042 / 042.02]

## Personality and Motivations
Graz'zt responds to setbacks with fury and threats of personal revenge, as shown after Malkyn's defeat. His stated challenge emphasized confronting the party with his true power. [ch 034 / entry]

His arrangements use intermediaries to advance destruction: Vraxen enforced an exchange of fiendish blood for the giants spreading devastation through the Underdark. He also maintained a contingency to flee to Maerimydra if the adventurers sent toward Menzoberranzan succeeded. [ch 042 / 042.02; ch 052 / entry]

## Last Observed State
Graz'zt was spoken of during the preparations at Sporedome; these scenes do not show him present. The party expected his arrival in Maerimydra and understood intercepting him there to be their role in Alder's “Plan B.” His actual location and arrival were not confirmed. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 058 / 058.01; ch 058 / 058.03]

Elanil maintained that defeating the fire giants was necessary to reach him and that the party would face him without coalition support inside Maerimydra. The Mind Flayer agreed to supply an accounting of his known abilities; the scene does not provide that accounting. [ch 058 / 058.03; ch 058 / 058.04]
