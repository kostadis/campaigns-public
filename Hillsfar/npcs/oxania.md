## Identity
Oxania was a high-ranking leader of the Nerevenan death cult, described as a cleric. [ch 059 / entry; ch 059 / moment]

## Personality and Motivations
Her religious devotion encompassed willingly accepting sacrifice and viewing transition to undeath as fulfillment of her cult’s beliefs. Her calm surrender and insistence that the party perform the killing themselves show the importance she placed on their direct participation. [ch 059 / entry; ch 059 / moment]

## History with the Party
- Oxania was a high-ranking leader of the Nerevenan death cult. [ch 059 / entry]
- After overhearing the party's bargain with Vragni Moonsmith to sacrifice the Nerevenans for military support, she confronted the party, unbuckled her sword belt, and knelt, asking that they perform the killing themselves. [ch 059 / 059.02]
- She willingly offered herself as a sacrifice, embracing transition to undeath as fulfillment of her cult's beliefs. [ch 059 / entry]
- Bramgrim executed Oxania with his sword to initiate the transformation process; completion of that transformation is not established in the summaries. [ch 059 / 059.03]

## Last Observed State
Oxania was executed by Bramgrim with his sword at the Cave of Black Whispers to initiate her transformation. Completion of that transformation is not established in the summaries. [ch 059 / 059.03]

## Relationships
- **Nerevenans:** Her cult and the group she helped lead; she shared its belief in undeath as religious fulfillment. [ch 059 / entry]
- **The party:** Allies of the Nerevenans who agreed to sacrifice them for military support. Oxania confronted them and asked them to carry out her killing personally. [ch 059 / 059.02]
- **Bramgrim:** Carried out her requested execution. [ch 059 / 059.03]
- **Vragni Moonsmith:** Offered the bargain that made her cult’s sacrifice the price of his army’s service for one day. [ch 059 / 059.02]
