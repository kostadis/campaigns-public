## Identity
Akritas is an Emerald Enclave member whose position carries meeting obligations and assignments from its High Council. She uses an Oathbow and provides magical stealth support through Pass Without Trace. She speaks Abyssal, Undercommon, and Druidic. [ch 048 / 048.02; ch 051 / 051.06; ch 035 / 035.01; ch 042 / 042.01; ch 042 / 042.04; ch 049 / 049.01]

## Personality and Motivations
- **Cautious and resource-conscious:** She favors bypassing avoidable threats and reminds companions that her stealth magic requires concentration and limited resources. [ch 036 / 036.05; ch 035 / 035.02]
- **Protective and ambitious:** She wants personal wealth preserved from demonic destruction and accepts morally troubling alliances when protecting the Forgotten Realms takes priority. [ch 048 / moment; ch 056 / 056.02]
- **Distrustful of Drow allies, but capable of compromise:** She has threatened lethal action over misconduct, yet accepted Daein’s intervention and limits on her authority outside her enclave. [ch 053 / moment; ch 054 / 054.01]
- **Alert to dangerous consequences:** She questioned the risks of possessing the stolen artifact and warned that spectral dwarves could harm allied drow forces. [ch 048 / 048.02; ch 059 / 059.03]
