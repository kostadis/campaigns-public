## Identity
Vragni Moonsmith is a deceased dwarven commander, slaughtered by the drow of Szith Morcane, who appears as a powerful mummy mounted on a nightmare horse. [ch 059 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Duty and fury bind Vragni to his command; he holds his fallen kin in spectral enslavement. His authority depends on the regimental standard: while it stands, the undead dwarves must obey him. [ch 059 / entry; ch 059 / 059.02]

He treats military service as something to bargain with, demanding the Nerevenans’ slaughter and transformation into undead as his price. [ch 059 / 059.02]
