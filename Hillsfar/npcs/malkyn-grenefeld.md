## Identity
Malkyn Grenefeld served as Chamberlain and later became Hillsfar's mayor. Maram identified her as Graz'zt's Chosen when discussing rumors of her death. [ch 016 / 016.05; ch 031 / entry; ch 038 / 038.02]

## Personality and Motivations
Malkyn used Constance's captivity and torture to fuel a ritual. Her belief that Constance loved Daein intensified her rage; this establishes Malkyn's belief, without establishing Constance's feelings. She sensed the party's presence and waited to fight them. [ch 032 / entry]

## History with the Party
- As Chamberlain, Malkyn Grenefeld had an office the party infiltrated; Akritas, Daein and Safire secured an official letter confirming a meeting between the leaders of Damara and the First Lord. [ch 016 / 016.05]
- Malkyn Grenefeld pursued Elanil after Elanil left a package and fled; the party decided to inspect the package. [ch 017 / 017.04]
- By chapter 031, Malkyn Grenefeld was Hillsfar's new mayor, under whom the city's madness had worsened. The party did not meet her that session. [ch 031 / entry]
- Malkyn Grenefeld held Constance captive and tortured her to fuel a ritual; her belief that Constance loved Daein fueled her rage. [ch 032 / entry]
- Malkyn Grenefeld sensed the party's presence and waited to fight them. [ch 032 / entry]
- The restored temple of Selûne granted the party Moonsilver Clarity to aid their fight against Malkyn Grenefeld. [ch 032 / 032.02]
- Isthana advised that killing Malkyn was essential and that Constance's death alone would not suffice. [ch 032 / 032.05]
- While planning Constance's rescue, the party received confirmation that Malkyn Grenefeld was at the Obsidian Keep. [ch 032 / 032.05]
- Chapter 035 establishes Malkyn Grenefeld's defeat and death, linking her death to upheaval in Hillsfar and Graz'zt's wrath. [ch 035 / entry]
- Maram mentioned rumors that Graz'zt's Chosen, Malkyn Grenefeld, had been killed in Hillsfar; he considered evidence of Graz'zt's failure valuable to Graz'zt's enemies. [ch 038 / 038.02]
- The party declined a proposed approach to Vraxen's dinner in which they would present themselves as Malkyn's killers seeking an alliance. [ch 043 / 043.04]
- Charles later recalled the party's defeat of Malkyn Grenefeld and their help replacing Hillsfar's anti-nonhuman regime, which allowed him to eat and drink openly in the tavern. [ch 051 / 051.01]

## Last Observed State
Dead: chapter 035 explicitly establishes her death and links it to upheaval in Hillsfar and Graz'zt's wrath. Her latest mention is retrospective: Charles recalls the party's victory over her and the replacement of Hillsfar's anti-nonhuman regime. She does not appear in that scene. [ch 035 / entry; ch 051 / 051.01]

## Relationships
- **Constance:** Malkyn's captive and torture victim, used to fuel a ritual. [ch 032 / entry]
- **Daein:** The person Malkyn believed Constance loved; that belief fueled Malkyn's rage. The evidence does not establish Malkyn's own feelings toward Daein beyond this connection. [ch 032 / entry]
- **Graz'zt:** Maram called Malkyn his Chosen, and Charles described her as a focal point for the demon's activity. Her death provoked Graz'zt's wrath. [ch 038 / 038.02; ch 035 / entry]
- **The party:** Adversaries whom she sensed approaching and awaited in battle; Charles later credited them with her defeat. [ch 032 / entry; ch 051 / 051.01]
- **Elanil:** Malkyn pursued Elanil after Elanil left a package and fled; the reason for the pursuit is not supplied. [ch 017 / 017.04]
- **Isthana:** Urged the party to kill Malkyn, warning that Constance's death alone would not suffice. [ch 032 / 032.05]
