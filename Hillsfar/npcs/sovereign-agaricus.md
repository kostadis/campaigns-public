## Identity
Sovereign Agaricus leads the Myconids at the Court and is identified as their leader in Sporedome. He communicates through connecting spores. [ch 030 / entry; ch 058 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
His welcome to the party shows a hospitable approach to outsiders. Bringing the captured younglings to their attention suggests concern for his people, though the summaries provide little detail about his broader aims. He is explicitly described as deeply disturbed by the rapid changes affecting the Underdark. [ch 030 / entry; ch 058 / entry]

## History with the Party
- Sovereign Agaricus led the Myconids at the Court, welcomed the party, and told them that drow had captured younglings. [ch 030 / entry]
- Agaricus stood over the sacred seer while the party considered whether the seer was lying or possessed; the party decided to find the stolen children to learn more. [ch 030 / 030.04]
- The party encountered Sovereign Agaricus again at Sporedome among the leaders assembled for the coalition's march on Maerimydra. [ch 058 / 058.01]
- Through connecting spores, Sovereign Agaricus reported a battle involving his colony and the demon lords Zuggtmoy and Juiblex; five outside adventurers had defeated both demon lords. [ch 058 / 058.01]
- He was deeply disturbed by the rapid changes affecting the Underdark. [ch 058 / entry]

## Last Observed State
Last observed at Sporedome among the leaders assembled for the coalition’s march on Maerimydra, actively communicating through spores. He reported that five outside adventurers had defeated Zuggtmoy and Juiblex following a battle involving his colony. His reported emotional state was deep disturbance at the Underdark’s rapid changes. [ch 058 / 058.01; ch 058 / entry]

## Relationships
- **The Myconids:** His people and colony; his leadership and concern over their captured younglings define his documented responsibilities. [ch 030 / entry; ch 058 / entry]
- **The party:** Welcomed visitors whom he informed about the younglings; later recipients of his report at Sporedome. [ch 030 / entry; ch 058 / 058.01]
- **The sacred seer:** Agaricus stood over the seer, but the nature of their relationship is not established. The party’s suspicions concerned the seer. [ch 030 / 030.04]
- **Zuggtmoy and Juiblex:** Opponents of his colony in the battle he reported. [ch 058 / 058.01]
