## Identity
Solom Ned'razak is a Drow Archmage and resistance leader whom Murssvayas Dhuurniv described as the force’s general. His original body was killed by Quil'bryn; his magical clone retains the original’s memories and personality. [ch 056 / entry; ch 052 / moment; ch 051 / 051.05; ch 056 / 056.07]

## Personality and Motivations
- **Proud and protective of his work:** He treats his research as essential to the drow’s survival, resents interruptions, and has dismissed the party as replaceable. [ch 039 / 039.04; ch 039 / 039.07]
- **Pragmatic and ruthless:** He appreciates Daein’s willingness to promise Szith Morcane to competing leaders. His clone uses lethal force to remove opposition and asserts his own claim to authority. [ch 056 / 056.07; ch 057 / 057.05]
- **Focused on survival and military victory:** His work supports removing fiendish influence from fire giants and confronting Graz'zt in Maerimydra. His papers also express opposition to Lolth’s clerics. [ch 039 / 039.06; ch 050 / 050.04; ch 041 / 041.03]
- **Capable of yielding under pressure:** Felkur successfully intimidated his clone into silence during the faction dispute. [ch 057 / 057.05]

## History with the Party
- The party learned that Solom, previously believed captive, was alive and leading Szith Morcane; Felratha and her troupe were assigned to investigate his control of the outpost. [ch 026 / 026.03]
- A recap described Solom Ned'razak as an archmage allied against the giants’ threat; he was mentioned, without appearing. [ch 027 / 027.01]
- The party had previously helped Solom escape Szith Morcane. He sheltered in the Sporedome, where other survivors gathered. [ch 035 / 035.05]
- Unable to attend the meeting at Silver Shadow Cave, Solom sent Murssvayas Dhuurniv with his pendant; Bramgrim recognized it and accepted the emissary’s authority. [ch 035 / 035.05]
- Solom’s subsequently collapsed spy network traced captured alchemist Xaven Tanor'this to Maram il Vass. The party accepted the mission to recover Xaven and return him to Solom. [ch 035 / 035.05; ch 035 / 035.06]
- Through Murssvayas, Solom offered an item from the drow hoard if the mission succeeded and Szith Morcane was recovered. No particular item was selected. [ch 035 / 035.06]
- At Sporedome, Solom thanked the party for recovering Xaven and retired with the alchemist to research. [ch 039 / 039.03]
- Solom became furious when interrupted during research and refused recruitment, describing himself as essential to the drow’s survival and the party as replaceable. [ch 039 / 039.04; ch 039 / 039.07]
- Solom and Xaven’s proposed ritual required blood from a living fire giant carrying fiendish blood; the party agreed to help. [ch 039 / 039.06; ch 039 / 039.07]
- Solom supplied a slightly outdated map of Maerimydra and explained his secret tunnel beneath Black Ember Outpost, leading toward the ruined Shattered Tower. Xaven knew its entrance. [ch 039 / 039.06]
- Solom explained portable holes and identified The Spire of Inevitable Reckoning’s teleportation circle as a possible escape route during planning for the giant’s capture. [ch 039 / 039.08]
- The party discovered Solom’s private office along the Path of Shadows and read his papers; this was a discovery of his writings, without an encounter with him. [ch 041 / 041.02; ch 041 / 041.03]
- His papers described opposition to Lolth’s clerics, blamed powerful wizards and liches from Menzoberranzan for the Blink without identifying a certain culprit, and recorded failed attempts to bargain with demons. [ch 041 / 041.03]
- The party feigned agreement with Vriska’s condemnation of Solom to secure a discount. They retained his notes rather than expose them, keeping them as possible leverage for a later purchase. [ch 042 / 042.02]
- Solom and his assistants took custody of the living captive Brandyr at Sporedome. He thanked the party, said the ritual would take a ten-day, and directed them to Hillsfar with continued contact through Murssvayas Dhuurniv. [ch 047 / 047.03]
- Solom later declared that the allies had everything needed and that the time was approaching to confront Graz'zt in Maerimydra. [ch 050 / 050.04]
- Alder warned the party that Solom was dead and that the resistance needed leadership. Murssvayas subsequently reported that Quil'bryn had murdered him eight days before the party’s arrival while half the assembled Drow watched without intervening. [ch 051 / 051.02; ch 051 / 051.05]
- Murssvayas confirmed that the blood ritual had worked, but Solom’s death had left the resistance politically fractured. [ch 051 / 051.05]
- After eating a braincap mushroom, Quil'bryn recognized his responsibility for killing Solom; Murssvayas attributed the murder to the Underdark’s madness. Quil'bryn pledged himself to Felkur, and the other Drow rallied. [ch 051 / 051.05]
- Murssvayas subsequently described Solom as the force’s general and acknowledged his own lack of military experience. [ch 052 / 052.02; ch 052 / moment]
- Velasta accused Solom Ned'razak of betraying and murdering her mother, her sister, and herself. Daein deceived her into believing fire giants had killed him, redirecting her vengeance toward them. [ch 056 / 056.05]
- Quil'bryn had actually killed Solom; he agreed to keep his troops silent to preserve the alliance with Velasta. [ch 056 / 056.06]
- Murssvayas Dhuurniv directed the party to Solom's private quarters for possible memory-altering magic, providing Solom's signet ring and the password 'Kural' to bypass the Inverted Tower's defenses. [ch 056 / 056.06]
- The party found Solom in the Inverted Tower as a magical clone prepared before his original body's death, retaining the original's memories and personality. [ch 056 / 056.07]
- Solom explained that memory magic was unlikely to work on a revenant because of the nature of its existence. [ch 056 / 056.07]
- Solom laughed approvingly at Daein's promises of Szith Morcane to multiple faction leaders and gave the party a spellbook whose spells could serve as scrolls. [ch 056 / 056.07]
- During the faction dispute at Szith Morcane, Solom's clone disintegrated Asala and proposed postponing the city's ownership dispute until after the war, inviting subsequent challengers to oppose him. [ch 057 / 057.05]
- With Daein's inspiration, Felkur intimidated Solom's clone into silence while rallying the coalition to march on Maerimydra. [ch 057 / 057.05]
- At Sporedome, Solom announced that he had successfully removed fiendish taint from a fire giant; the process required ten days of subdual and was impractical as a battlefield solution. [ch 058 / 058.01]
- The party learned that fear of Solom held the Drow alliance together and that Dorina T'sarran, a vampire Drow cleric and Velasta's aunt, had vowed revenge against him. [ch 058 / 058.05]
- Fearing that Dorina could reveal Solom's survival to Velasta and fracture the coalition, the party concluded that they must secretly protect him despite distrusting his motives. [ch 058 / 058.05]
- Solom watched the faction assignments with a cryptic smile. The added recap identifies the Solom at the muster as his clone and states that Velasta had left before seeing him and had not yet heard from her followers. [ch 058 / 058.06]

## Last Observed State
His clone was present at the Sporedome muster, watching faction assignments with a cryptic smile. The added recap says no one at the muster knew he was a clone. [ch 058 / 058.06]

He reported successfully removing fiendish taint from a fire giant, but the required ten days of subdual made the procedure impractical for battlefield use. [ch 058 / 058.01]

Dorina T'sarran’s vendetta threatens both him and the coalition. Velasta had left before seeing his clone, and her followers had not yet contacted her. [ch 058 / 058.05; ch 058 / 058.06]

## Relationships
- **The party:** Useful but distrusted allies. He supplies knowledge and magical resources; they regard protecting him secretly as necessary to preserve the army despite their doubts about his motives. [ch 039 / 039.06; ch 056 / 056.07; ch 058 / 058.05]
- **Murssvayas Dhuurniv:** Former right-hand man and trusted emissary, who recognizes Solom’s military leadership and his own limitations as a replacement. [ch 056 / 056.06; ch 035 / 035.05; ch 052 / moment]
- **Xaven Tanor'this:** Research collaborator on the ritual involving fiendish fire giant blood. [ch 039 / 039.03; ch 039 / 039.06]
- **Quil'bryn:** Killer of Solom’s original body; keeping that fact from Velasta is necessary to sustain her alliance with the coalition. [ch 056 / 056.06]
- **Velasta and Dorina T'sarran:** Vengeful enemies. Velasta accuses him of betraying and murdering her family and herself; Dorina, her aunt, has sworn revenge against him. [ch 056 / 056.05; ch 058 / 058.05]
- **Drow factions:** Their cohesion depends on fear of him, making his continued presence a political necessity for the coalition. [ch 058 / 058.05]
