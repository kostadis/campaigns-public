## Identity
Deriel Rethslane is a female moon elf, introduced as Deriel. At her first encounter with the companions, she wore dirty, mud-covered clothes over tough leather. [ch 002 / entry; ch 005 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Her love for Arrizz drove her to supply captives to Erlich to keep Arrizz alive. She acknowledged the harm she caused while expressing a desire to free Arrizz and take revenge on Erlich and Hillsfar. [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.03]

She used deception to draw the companions into danger. Later, her surprise at their willingness to help shows that she recognized her actions as evil and did not take their assistance for granted. [ch 002 / 002.05; ch 004 / 004.07]

## History with the Party
- Deriel introduced herself in Tinfellow Valley, claimed to have witnessed the Red Plume raid, and said she needed guards for hidden Tinfellows. [ch 002 / 002.01]
- She led the companions toward the ruined elven village; Daein recognized her deception before the Red Plume ambush. [ch 002 / 002.05]
- Deriel fled into a ruin as the battle began. [ch 003 / 003.01]
- Bramgrim found her dead after the battle and recovered four notes from her body. [ch 003 / 003.03]
- The notes revealed that she supplied non-human captives to Erlich to keep Arrizz alive and hoped to free Arrizz and take revenge on Erlich and Hillsfar. [ch 003 / entry]
- Raised by a cleric of Tempus at Erlich's order, Deriel was thrown into the companions' cage. [ch 004 / entry]
- She said she believed Erlich intended to keep raising and killing her because he blamed her for his defeat. [ch 004 / 004.07]
- Felkur promised to take her to Arrizz; Bramgrim responded sympathetically when she questioned their willingness to help her. [ch 004 / 004.07]
- Deriel Rethslane left for Goodroot Beet Farm to find Arrizz, promising help if she and Arrizz ever could. [ch 005 / 005.02]

## Last Observed State
Alive at her last observed departure, Deriel left the companions for Goodroot Beet Farm to find Arrizz. She promised assistance if she and Arrizz ever could provide it. Her arrival and reunion with Arrizz are not established in the summaries. [ch 005 / 005.02]

## Relationships
- **Arrizz:** Her love and the central motive for her service to Erlich. She sought his freedom despite the suffering she inflicted on others. [ch 003 / entry; ch 005 / 005.02]
- **Erlich:** He conditioned Arrizz’s freedom on her supplying non-human captives. She wanted revenge against him and later believed he intended to kill and raise her repeatedly as punishment for his defeat. [ch 003 / 003.03; ch 004 / 004.07]
- **Felkur:** Offered to take her to Arrizz despite her betrayal of the companions. [ch 004 / 004.07]
- **Bramgrim:** Responded sympathetically to her admission of wrongdoing, attributing her actions to love. [ch 004 / moment]
- **Daein:** Recognized her deception before the ambush. [ch 002 / 002.05]
- **The companions:** Former targets of her betrayal who subsequently offered help; she left them with a conditional promise of future assistance from herself and Arrizz. [ch 002 / 002.05; ch 004 / entry; ch 005 / 005.02]
