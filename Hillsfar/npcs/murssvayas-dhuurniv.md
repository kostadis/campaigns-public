## Identity
Murssvayas Dhuurniv is a drow wizard, Solom Ned'razak's representative and former right-hand man, who inherited responsibility for a fractured resistance after Solom's death. [ch 035 / entry; ch 052 / entry; ch 056 / entry]

He has silver hair, thick sideburns, emerald green eyes, and nearly purple skin. He wears black clothing and carries a sword. [ch 035 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
He is protective of drow standing: during reward negotiations, he reacted with disbelief to demands for payment and took Daein's doubts about the drow hoard as an insult. [ch 035 / 035.06]

He recognizes his limits as a military leader. Overwhelmed by responsibility, he relies on the party's experience and presses for practical action against the giants' weapons production. His stated priorities are maintaining effective resistance and stopping weapons before they reach Maerimydra. [ch 051 / entry; ch 052 / 052.02]

He acknowledges difficult obstacles to cooperation, including the drow's former ownership of the enslaved forge workers. When consulted about concealing Solom's murder, he offers a possible source of memory-altering magic. [ch 052 / 052.02; ch 056 / 056.06]

## History with the Party
- Murssvayas Dhuurniv met the party at Silver Shadow Cave as Solom Ned'razak's representative and presented Solom's recognizable pendant as proof of his authority. [ch 035 / 035.05]
- He greeted the adventurers individually and spoke to Owly in a way that prompted recognition. [ch 035 / 035.05]
- He requested that the party recover the captured alchemist Xaven Tanor'this from Maram il Vass and return him to the Sporedome to complete a ritual against the fiendish giants. [ch 035 / 035.05]
- During reward negotiations, he conveyed Solom's conditional offer of an item from the drow hoard if the rescue succeeded and Szith Morcane was recovered; the party accepted the mission. [ch 035 / 035.06]
- His directions and map guided the party through an uneventful two-day journey to the Howling Pits. [ch 036 / 036.02]
- Solom later promised continued contact through Murssvayas Dhuurniv after the party delivered Brandyr to the Sporedome; Murssvayas was mentioned in that exchange. [ch 047 / 047.03]
- The Emerald Enclave's summons reported that Murssvayas Dhuurniv had returned to his people and was ready to receive the party at Szith Morcane. [ch 050 / 050.06]
- Alder warned the party that the resistance needed leadership and explicitly ruled out relying on Murssvayas to provide it. [ch 051 / 051.02]
- At the resistance camp, Murssvayas confirmed that the ritual had worked but reported that Quil'bryn had killed Solom eight days earlier, leaving the resistance divided and many survivors wanting to flee. [ch 051 / 051.05]
- He warned that the party would face the giants' massed army alone without the resistance. [ch 051 / 051.05]
- After Quil'bryn ate a braincap mushroom and recognized what he had done, Murssvayas attributed Solom's murder to the Underdark's madness. [ch 051 / 051.05]
- Hoarse and exhausted, he briefed the party with a scratched-out map and urged them to destroy Dengor's weapons production before its output reached Maerimydra. [ch 052 / 052.02]
- He identified possible support among the Hidden, the Lodge of Faces, Kiaransalee's followers, and approximately two hundred enslaved forge workers. [ch 052 / 052.02]
- He acknowledged that the enslaved workers had formerly been Drow property, complicating their recruitment. [ch 052 / 052.02]
- When consulted about concealing the truth of Solom's death, he suggested that a spellbook containing memory-altering magic might be in Solom's private quarters in the Inverted Tower. [ch 056 / 056.06]
- He supplied Solom's arcane signet ring and the password 'Kural' to bypass the Inverted Tower's defenses. [ch 056 / 056.06]
- His mages provided gaseous form spells that enabled the party to infiltrate the Forge of Surtur. [ch 056 / 056.08]
- At the Szith Morcane negotiations, he deferred the question of leadership to Daein and expressed a preference to move on to other matters. [ch 057 / entry]

## Last Observed State
At the Szith Morcane negotiations, Murssvayas was present, deferred the leadership question to Daein, and preferred to move on to other matters. The entry does not establish a settled leadership role for him. [ch 057 / entry]

## Relationships
- **Solom Ned'razak:** His former principal; Murssvayas served as his representative and right-hand man. Solom's death left him carrying responsibilities he considered beyond his military experience. [ch 035 / 035.05; ch 056 / entry; ch 052 / moment]
- **The party:** Resistance allies whose strength and experience he relies upon. He supports them with intelligence, access to Solom's defenses, and magic supplied by his mages. [ch 052 / 052.02; ch 056 / entry]
- **Daein:** Their dealings include friction over drow prestige, but Murssvayas later defers the leadership question to him. [ch 035 / 035.06; ch 057 / entry]
- **Quil'bryn:** Murssvayas identifies him as Solom's killer and attributes the murder to the Underdark's madness after Quil'bryn eats a braincap mushroom. [ch 051 / 051.05]
- **Owly:** Their initial exchange prompts recognition from Owly; the nature of any earlier connection is unexplained. [ch 035 / 035.05]
