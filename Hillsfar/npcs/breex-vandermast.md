## Identity
Breex Vandermast is a half-orc Red Plume sorcerer, described as cloaked and somewhat portly. His makeup concealed his half-orc identity until it fell off during battle. [ch 004 / entry; ch 005 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Breex is nasty-tempered and openly delights in prisoners’ suffering. His gloating links cruelty with profit: he treats their anticipated screams as a source of gold. [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.04]

## History with the Party
- Breex Vandermast, a Red Plume sorcerer, gloated to the prisoners about selling them to Mags and the horrors awaiting them in the Arena. [ch 004 / 004.04]
- Breex described prisoners dying in a pool of acid and laughed at the prospect of earning gold from the prisoners' screams. [ch 004 / 004.04]
- Breex sold the prisoners to Mags after haggling; she ordered Horac to pay him and bring them inside. [ch 004 / 004.06]
- The heroes defeated Breex during the Battle Royale in the arena. [ch 005 / 005.01]
- Breex's makeup fell off during the battle, revealing that he was a half-orc. [ch 005 / entry]
- Status not established in the summaries beyond his defeat. [ch 005 / entry]

## Last Observed State
Last observed defeated by the heroes in the arena’s Battle Royale, where his makeup fell off and revealed his half-orc identity. Status not established in the summaries beyond his defeat. [ch 005 / 005.01; ch 005 / entry]

## Relationships
- **The heroes:** Breex treats them as prisoners to sell and torment; he subsequently faces them as an arena opponent. [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 004 / 004.06; ch 005 / 005.01]
- **Mags:** A buyer with whom Breex haggles over the prisoners. She directs Horac to pay him and bring them inside. [ch 004 / 004.06]
- **Red Plume:** His stated affiliation; he is identified as a Red Plume sorcerer. [ch 004 / entry]
