## Identity
Serinia is a gnomish cleric of Mielikki who serves as a source of guidance and assistance for the party. [ch 021 / 021.04; ch 022 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Serinia’s requests show concern about demonic influence in the forest. Her warm welcome and offer of assistance at the temple show a friendly, supportive attitude toward the party. Broader personal ambitions are not established. [ch 012 / 012.02; ch 022 / entry]

## History with the Party
- Serinia asked the heroes to investigate demonic influence in the forest, suggesting they speak with Brightleaf, the Treant. The heroes found him possessed and freed him. [ch 012 / 012.02]
- Serinia reported that Thaddeus Zervous had fled The Stop following a coup and that Jhanos Myrkeer had been elected Magistrate. The Emerald Enclave asked the heroes to support the new order by guarding it. [ch 012 / 012.04]
- The party later encountered Serinia, the gnomish cleric of Mielikki, delirious and handing out tokens. Daein recognized advanced mind-degenerating madness. [ch 021 / 021.04]
- Bramgrim cast lesser restoration, noticeably improving an affected individual; the summary does not explicitly identify that individual as Serinia. [ch 021 / 021.04]
- At the temple, Serinia welcomed the party, offered assistance, and provided a map of the area surrounding Yûlash. [ch 022 / 022.02]

## Last Observed State
Serinia was at the temple, happy to see the party and willing to help, providing a map of the area surrounding Yûlash. [ch 022 / 022.02]

Her recovery from the previously identified mind-degenerating madness is not established: the recipient of Bramgrim’s lesser restoration was not explicitly identified, and the later temple account does not address her condition. [ch 021 / 021.04; ch 022 / entry]
