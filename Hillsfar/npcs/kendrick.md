## Identity
Kendrick owns and runs the livery at the waystation in The Stop. Rogi is his assistant. [ch 010 / 010.07; ch 011 / entry; ch 013 / 013.03]

## Personality and Motivations
Kendrick is described as racist; Karl reports that his prices rise when a customer brings a non-human companion. [ch 011 / entry; ch 010 / 010.07]

He seeks money through dishonest charges, attempting to collect a stabling fee despite the heroes’ expenses being covered. He reacts with annoyance when challenged. [ch 013 / 013.03; ch 013 / moment]

## History with the Party
- Karl told Daein that Kendrick runs the Livery and charges higher prices when a non-human accompanies the customer. [ch 010 / 010.07]
- Daein secured horses for the party from Kendrick's livery at the waystation. [ch 011 / 011.02]
- The heroes met Kendrick when they arrived at the Stop. [ch 012 / 012.07]
