## Identity
Veris is a mage and Mulmaster official known as High Inquisitor Veris and High Cloak Veris. He hunts the Phantom Ghost. The DM identifies him as a fourteenth-level wizard. [ch 043 / entry; ch 046 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Cold, calculating, and intensely watchful, Veris probes suspected identities through physical clues and pointed questions. He becomes furious when others obstruct his interrogation. [ch 043 / entry]

The forgery involving his name in the Lexicon gives him an explicit motive for revenge. In combat, he uses restraint and isolation to expose opponents to attacks, including freeing his own golem while leaving its target restrained. [ch 045 / 045.04; ch 046 / 046.01; ch 046 / 046.02]

## History with the Party
- Pipyap identified High Inquisitor Veris as a Mulmaster official hunting the Phantom Ghost; Felkur inferred that Maram il Vass had probably directed Veris toward the Lexicon while requiring Felkur to retrieve it first. [ch 043 / 043.04]
- Veris attended Vraxen’s dinner, where he was also addressed as High Cloak Veris. [ch 043 / entry]
- Veris singled out Felkur’s smith-stained hands and apparent familiarity with Mulmaster imprisonment; Daein and Bramgrim diverted his interrogation toward Colosseum finances, angering him. [ch 043 / 043.06]
- After Daein stole the runic key, Veris noticed the party moving, reached for his staff, and looked toward the key’s former position. [ch 043 / 043.06]
- The party used an illusion of Veris’s voice to provoke a three-way fight among Veris, Vraxen, and Veperyd, then escaped upstairs with the key. [ch 043 / 043.06]
- Felkur replaced his own identifying name in the Lexicon with Veris of Mulmaster to misdirect future investigators. [ch 044 / 044.06]
- An injured Veris approached with the reactivated stone golem, threatened revenge over the forgery, and ordered the golem to tear away the iron door. [ch 045 / 045.04]
- Veris teleported during the ensuing battle; the chapter’s accounts leave his exact entrance and repositioning sequence unresolved. [ch 045 / 045.04]
- Veris restrained party members with Web, then burned his own Stone Golem free with Firebolt so it could attack the restrained Daein. [ch 046 / 046.01]
- Veris inferred the invisible Felkur’s presence and attempted to trap him with Wall of Force; Bramgrim removed the barrier with Dispel Magic under the session’s ruling. [ch 046 / 046.02]
- Veris struck Akritas with a critical Firebolt; Daein reduced the damage with Protective Field. [ch 046 / 046.02]
- Akritas killed Veris with Hail of Thorns before he could cast Fireball. [ch 046 / 046.02]

## Last Observed State
Dead atop Vraxen's Tower: Akritas killed Veris with Hail of Thorns before he could cast Fireball. The subsequent fighting against the Shield Guardian and demonic reinforcements occurs after his death. [ch 046 / 046.02; ch 046 / 046.03]

- **Felkur:** A focus of Veris’s suspicion and later revenge. Felkur substituted Veris’s name for his own in the Lexicon; Veris subsequently sought to trap him despite his invisibility. [ch 043 / 043.06; ch 044 / 044.06; ch 045 / 045.04; ch 046 / 046.02]

- **Vraxen and Veperyd:** Fellow dinner attendees drawn into a three-way fight through the party’s imitation of Veris’s voice. [ch 043 / 043.06]
- **Daein and Bramgrim:** Opponents who obstructed his interrogation and later countered his attacks and battlefield control. [ch 043 / 043.06; ch 046 / 046.02]

- **Stone Golem and Shield Guardian:** Veris commanded the Stone Golem and deliberately freed it from webbing; the Shield Guardian later arrived to support him. [ch 045 / 045.04; ch 046 / 046.01; ch 046 / 046.02]
