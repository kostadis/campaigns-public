## Identity
Falofa is a member of the adventuring group described as its barbarian, though she privately resents that label. She uses a great axe and demonstrates tracking skills. [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.02; ch 002 / 002.04]

## Personality and Motivations
She seeks spoils, combat, renown and adventure, and finds appeals to non-violence unappealing. Her hostility toward the Red Plumes also reflects outrage at their persecution of non-humans and seizure of the Tinfellows’ property. [ch 001 / moment; ch 001 / 001.05; ch 003 / 003.02; ch 006 / 006.03]

She can act impulsively, but also recognizes danger and exercises restraint when protecting companions. Her initial interest in demonic disturbances eventually gives way to an explicit unwillingness to become involved in demonic wars. [ch 006 / 006.03; ch 002 / 002.05; ch 009 / 009.03; ch 012 / 012.03]

## History with the Party
- Falofa celebrated the shadow’s destruction but suffered its strength drain, struggling with her great axe while resting at the Goodroots farm. [ch 001 / 001.01; ch 001 / 001.02]
- Hearing about Graz’zt’s growing power, she connected it to six-fingered children, demonic goats and madmen. [ch 001 / 001.01]
- She told Daein to stop questioning Constance about the mysterious invitations. [ch 001 / 001.03]
- She disliked Olisara’s advice to use non-violence, responding “not fun.” [ch 001 / 001.05]
- She tracked heavy boot prints and halfling footprints toward Hillsfar and found a dead halfling beside the river. [ch 002 / 002.04]
- She suspected a trap at the wooden bridge and heard a whistle before the Red Plume ambush. [ch 002 / 002.05]
- Her aura helped turn the battle against the Red Plume Guards. [ch 003 / 003.01]
- She denounced the seizure of the Tinfellows’ property as “Ethnic Cleansing!” and later wanted to confront Deirk Erlich. [ch 003 / 003.02; ch 004 / 004.01]
- She posed as a prisoner alongside Felkur and Bramgrim during the infiltration of Hillsfar, continuing the disguise through the gate. [ch 004 / 004.03; ch 004 / 004.05]
- She was placed with Bramgrim and Felkur in a barred cage on the arena floor, where their manacles were unlocked. [ch 004 / 004.06]
- At the creek, she charged into the fight against the Red Plumes; their invocation of “humanity” enraged her, and the soldiers were dead a few sword strokes later. [ch 006 / 006.03]
- She accepted a purse from the dwarf who introduced himself as Charles not Karl and agreed to attack a Red Plume caravan. [ch 006 / 006.04]
- During the wagon chase, she and Daein threw opponents off the wagons and seized the horses’ reins. [ch 007 / 007.02]
- She alone remained unaffected when the maze’s magic made her companions fight each other; after Daein recovered, they restrained Felkur and Bramgrim. [ch 009 / 009.03]
- She roasted a goat without permission; Daein ate some and insisted she pay, proposing payment through Karl to the Bitterwoods. [ch 011 / 011.04]
- She fought alongside Felkur and Daein against the animated scarecrows, which were eventually destroyed. [ch 011 / 011.07]
- She left the group to head to her tribe rather than deal with demonic wars. [ch 012 / entry; ch 012 / 012.03]

## Last Observed State
Falofa has left the group, heading toward her tribe because she does not want to deal with demonic wars. Her departure is explicit; the account does not establish her arrival. [ch 012 / entry; ch 012 / 012.03]

## Relationships
- **Daein:** A combat partner with whom she also clashes over conduct, including his questioning of Constance and his insistence that she compensate the owners of the goat she roasted. [ch 007 / 007.02; ch 001 / 001.03; ch 011 / 011.04]
- **Felkur and Bramgrim:** Fellow adventurers whom she helps restrain for their protection when the maze’s magic turns them against their companions. [ch 009 / 009.03]
- **Constance:** Their host; Falofa objects when Daein presses her with suspicious questions. [ch 001 / 001.03]
- **Red Plumes and Deirk Erlich:** Targets of her anger over persecution and dispossession; she favors direct confrontation. [ch 003 / 003.02; ch 004 / 004.01; ch 006 / 006.03]
- **Her tribe:** Her chosen destination after leaving the party; its name and individual members are not established in the departure account. [ch 012 / 012.03]
