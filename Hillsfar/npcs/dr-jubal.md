## Identity
Dr. Jubal runs Doctor Jubal's Carnival of Curiosities and was later spoken of as a legendary carnival performer. A carnie reported that he had lost his magic, but this was explicitly a rumor. [ch 008 / 008.04; ch 021 / 021.01]

Distinguish him from the mirror-maze doppelganger: a chained prisoner identified himself as Doctor Jubal, while Aelia described a duplicate intended to take control of the carnival. [ch 009 / 009.04]

## Personality and Motivations
A carnie described him as an employer who pays wages on time and asks no questions. Pickles credited him with paying for his release from Karl, suggesting practical support for his performers. [ch 008 / 008.04; ch 015 / 015.08]

Pickles also reported that Dr. Jubal went to fight the attacking goblins. His motives were not stated. The campfire figure's claims of offering sanctuary should be treated cautiously when assessing Dr. Jubal's character, given the subsequent discovery of a prisoner identifying himself as Doctor Jubal and the duplicate plot. [ch 015 / 015.08; ch 009 / 009.01; ch 009 / 009.04]

## History with the Party
- The heroes toured Doctor Jubal's Carnival of Curiosities, visiting games, dancing rats, and Pickles and Jam's performance. [ch 008 / 008.02]
- A carnie told the heroes that Doctor Jubal paid wages on time, asked no questions, and was rumored to have lost his magic before starting the carnival. [ch 008 / 008.04]
- The carnie said Doctor Jubal had recently added Madame Darkness, a mirror maze, dancing rats and fools; the fools obeyed him unquestioningly. [ch 008 / 008.04]
- Daein took one of the Fools to Doctor Jubal's office to talk to him. [ch 008 / 008.06]
- The figure identified as Doctor Jubal confronted the party at the campfire, prohibited fighting, and ordered two fools to take their comrades' bodies to the maze. [ch 009 / 009.01]
- Questioned by Daein and Felkur, Doctor Jubal denied creating the Fools or causing sickness; the party handed the captured Fool over to him. [ch 009 / 009.01]
- Inside the maze, the heroes found a chained man dressed like Doctor Jubal who identified himself as Doctor Jubal and begged for escape before the demon returned. [ch 009 / 009.04]
- Aelia said the maze made duplicates and planned to use the Duplicate Doctor Jubal to lead the carnival into the Underdark and sell the prisoners to demons. [ch 009 / 009.04]
- The heroes killed the evil Doppelganger Doctor Jubal after breaking mirrors turned her magic against her. This death concerns the duplicate. [ch 009 / 009.05]
- Dr. Jubal remained on the heroes' list of possible suspects; they chose his thread as their first unfinished matter to address. [ch 014 / entry]
- Biff reported that Dr. Jubal's caravan had failed to reach Hillpicket: one wagon was found on a winter herder road, nine were missing, and no carnie or Doctor Jubal had been seen since. [ch 015 / 015.06]
- Pickles reported that Dr. Jubal had paid a small fee securing his release from Karl. [ch 015 / 015.08]
- According to Pickles, goblins attacked the carnival, Dr Jubal went to fight with his red eye blazing, and the attackers took unconscious prisoners and wagons north. [ch 015 / 015.08]
- Later rumors mentioned a “successful mission against Dr. Jubal”; they did not specify his fate. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 017 / 017.02]
- Doctor Jubal was spoken of at a party as a legendary carnival performer. [ch 021 / 021.01]
- A later recap remembered the false Dr. Jubal, identified as the mirror-maze doppelganger, as worsening existing problems “in a demonic way”. [ch 026 / 026.02]

## Last Observed State
The latest reported action by Dr. Jubal himself is Pickles' account of him confronting goblins with his red eye blazing. Pickles said the attackers carried unconscious prisoners and wagons north, but did not explicitly identify Dr. Jubal among those prisoners. [ch 015 / 015.08]

Later references concern an unspecified “successful mission against Dr. Jubal,” his reputation as a performer, and a retrospective mention of the false Dr. Jubal. These do not establish his subsequent location or fate. Status not established in the summaries. The explicitly recorded death is the duplicate's. [ch 017 / 017.02; ch 021 / 021.01; ch 026 / 026.02; ch 009 / 009.05]
