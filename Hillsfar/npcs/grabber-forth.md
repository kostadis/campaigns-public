## Identity
Grabber Forth is a gray-skinned deep gnome who serves as Maram's guard and majordomo at the Den of the Whispered Word. [ch 037 / entry; ch 037 / 037.06]

## Personality and Motivations
Tegan describes him as mean beneath deceptive politeness, obedient only to Maram, and intolerant of guests returning his rudeness. [ch 037 / entry]

He enforces access to his master's residence but accepts a direct assertion of business with Maram as grounds for admission. [ch 037 / 037.06]

## History with the Party
- Tegan described Grabber Forth as a gray-skinned gnome guard who answered only to Maram, was deceptively polite, and disliked guests responding rudely. [ch 037 / 037.04]
- At the Den of the Whispered Word, Grabber Forth stood beneath the shield guardian's legs, demanded the party's identities, and explained that they were uninvited. [ch 037 / 037.06]
- After the party insisted it needed to speak to Maram, Grabber Forth told the shield guardian to let them through and introduced himself. [ch 037 / 037.06]

## Last Observed State
Last observed at the Den's entrance beneath the shield guardian's legs, Grabber Forth instructed the guardian to admit the party and introduced himself. He was alive and acting as gatekeeper in that encounter. [ch 037 / 037.06]

## Relationships
- **Maram:** His master; Grabber Forth serves as his guard and majordomo. According to Tegan, he answers only to Maram. [ch 037 / entry]
- **Shield guardian:** Guards the entrance alongside him and receives his instruction to let the party through. [ch 037 / 037.06]
- **The party:** Initially challenged as uninvited visitors, then admitted after asserting business with Maram. [ch 037 / 037.06]
- **Tegan:** Provides the party's advance warning about his manner and exclusive obedience to Maram. [ch 037 / 037.04]
