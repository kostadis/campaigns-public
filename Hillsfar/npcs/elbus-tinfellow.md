## Identity
Elbus Tinfellow is a Harper agent, patriarch of the Tinfellow clan, and founder of the Tinfellow Guard. [ch 006 / entry; ch 005 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
His forgiveness of Baar shows a willingness to reconcile. His militia request establishes the defense of Tinfellow Valley as a priority, while his route advice and cover story show caution about possible Red Plume patrols. [ch 005 / entry; ch 006 / 006.02]

## History with the Party
- Olisara Lightsong was delighted to learn that the Tinfellows, particularly Elbus Tinfellow, had been freed. [ch 005 / 005.03]
- Elbus returned home with his family. [ch 005 / entry]
- He forgave Baar. [ch 005 / entry]
- He founded the Tinfellow Guard. [ch 005 / entry]
- Elbus is a Harper agent, patriarch of the Tinfellow clan, and friend of Olisara Lightsong. [ch 006 / entry]
- He asked the heroes to recruit a militia from Elventree, where refugees from the Red Plumes were rumored to have established a military presence; the heroes agreed. [ch 006 / 006.02]
- He directed them north above The Stop, then south along the main road, to avoid possible Red Plume patrols. [ch 006 / 006.02]
- He supplied a cover story that they were fleeing Phlan. [ch 006 / 006.02]
- He relayed Olisara's report that the First Lord had not been deposed and remained firmly in charge despite a riot. [ch 006 / 006.02]

## Last Observed State
Last observed sitting in the sun and arranging recruitment assistance for Tinfellow Valley. The heroes accepted his request; Daein gathered his allies and headed toward The Stop. Elbus's own subsequent movements are not established. [ch 006 / 006.02]

## Relationships
- **Tinfellow clan:** Its patriarch; he returned home with his family after being freed. [ch 006 / entry; ch 005 / entry]
- **Olisara Lightsong:** His friend and an information source he trusts concerning the First Lord's continued rule. She expressed particular delight at his freedom. [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.02; ch 005 / 005.03]
- **Baar:** Elbus forgave him; the supplied evidence does not explain the underlying grievance. [ch 005 / entry]
- **The heroes:** He relies on their assistance to recruit militia members and provides travel guidance and a cover story. [ch 006 / 006.02]
