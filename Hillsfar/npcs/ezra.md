## Identity
Ezra is a man of about thirty and a cleric of Jergal, whose order catalogues cemeteries and graveyards. [ch 001 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
His confused laughter and casual description of undead encounters as an occupational hazard suggest a matter-of-fact attitude toward his work’s dangers. [ch 001 / 001.01]

## History with the Party
- After the shadow’s defeat, Ezra stood up, declared himself free, and introduced himself to the companions as a cleric of Jergal. [ch 001 / 001.01]
- Ezra laughed in confusion until Falofa nearly struck him, then explained that priests of Jergal catalogue cemeteries and graveyards. [ch 001 / 001.01]
- Ezra spoke with Bramgrim; Falofa overheard that the temple was dedicated to Waukeen but its members secretly followed Graz’zt. [ch 001 / 001.01]
- Ezra revealed that the released soul of a chief priest of Graz’zt had captured him. [ch 001 / entry]

## Last Observed State
Ezra was freed from the shadow’s possession when it was defeated. He was subsequently shown speaking with Bramgrim about the temple and its concealed allegiance to Graz’zt. [ch 001 / 001.01]

## Relationships
- **Jergal:** Ezra serves as a cleric of this deity. [ch 001 / entry]
- **Bramgrim:** A fellow cleric with whom Ezra discusses the temple’s religious history. [ch 001 / 001.01]
- **Falofa:** His confused laughter nearly provokes her to strike him before he explains his clerical work. [ch 001 / 001.01]
- **Chief priest of Graz’zt:** The priest’s released soul captured Ezra. [ch 001 / entry]
