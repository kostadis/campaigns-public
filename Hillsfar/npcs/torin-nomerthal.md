## Identity
Torin Nomerthal is identified as Hillsfar’s First Lord, whose rule reinstated the laws of Trade and Humanity, including the exclusion of non-humans from the city. [ch 006 / 006.01]

## Personality and Motivations
His policies privileged humans and protected legitimate trade. They establish his governing priorities; the summaries do not establish his private motives. [ch 006 / 006.01]

Friedrich Watson described him as initially seeming reasonable before returning to Hillsfar’s former exclusionary practices. This is Watson’s assessment, not an explanation of what caused the change. [ch 006 / 006.05]

## History with the Party
- At Poppet’s Playhouse, the companions watched a golden-armored puppet greeted with cheers of “Torin!” and “Lord Nomerthal!” [ch 001 / 001.04]
- Olisara Lightsong was not convinced that First Lord Torin Nomerthal's reign had ended. [ch 005 / 005.03]
- Torin Nomerthal reintroduced Hillsfar’s laws of Trade ("Do not interfere with legitimate trade") and Humanity ("Only humans are allowed within Hillsfar"); Felkur agreed with the first and found the second appalling. [ch 006 / 006.01]
- Friedrich Watson described the new First Lord’s return to the old ways; Charles linked his takeover and Hillsfar’s closure to merchants forcing farmers to accept lower prices. [ch 006 / 006.05]
- The party planned to infiltrate the First Lord’s tower to obtain Torin Nomerthal’s schedule. [ch 016 / 016.01]
- Torin Nomerthal gave a speech at the Midsummer celebration, during which a slender male elf attempted to assassinate him. [ch 016 / 016.06]
- Torin Nomerthal’s personal fate: Status not established in the summaries. The assassination attempt and the end of his regime do not specify his fate. [ch 016 / 016.06; ch 019 / 019.03]
- The party ended Torin Nomerthal’s regime, which threatened non-humans, and fled the First Lord’s Pavilion as guards arrived. [ch 019 / 019.03]

## Last Observed State
His last depicted personal action is giving a speech at the Midsummer celebration, where a slender male elf attempts to assassinate him. The outcome for Torin Nomerthal is unspecified. [ch 016 / 016.06]

The later account establishes that the party ended his regime, but does not establish his presence during their flight from the First Lord’s Pavilion. **Status not established in the summaries.** [ch 019 / 019.03; ch 016 / 016.06]

## Relationships
- **The party:** Opponents of his regime; their actions ultimately ended it. [ch 019 / 019.03]
- **Felkur:** Rejected his exclusion of non-humans while agreeing with his law protecting legitimate trade. [ch 006 / 006.01]
- **Charles and Friedrich Watson:** Critical commentators on his rule, linking Hillsfar’s closure and return to exclusionary practices with the farmers’ worsening circumstances. [ch 006 / 006.05]
- **Garos, Thessaly and Davon:** Present at his Midsummer speech. Their personal relationships with him are not specified; the twins’ similar clothing does not establish kinship. [ch 016 / 016.06]
