## Identity
Grek is a Galeb Duhr who serves as a guide, translator, and messenger among the stone giants. His right half bears a carved stone giant face with a shattered nose; the other half remains rough and unfinished. [ch 055 / entry; ch 056 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Grek shows an unusual concern for his appearance for an elemental and remembers the Lodge as honorable before its obsession with stonework displaced concern over the fire giants’ actions. Exile troubled him partly because it denied him access to the Stone Father’s image. [ch 055 / entry; ch 055 / 055.05]

His reverence for sacred images coexists with recognition of the harm caused by fixation on perfection. His horror at the statue’s destruction gave way to visible relief when it released the stone giants from their madness. [ch 055 / 055.05; ch 056 / 056.01]

## History with the Party
- Grek is a Galeb Duhr whom Decha exiled for imperfection; his right half bears a carved stone giant face with a shattered nose, while his other half remains rough and unfinished. [ch 055 / entry]
- Grek accompanied Daein and Felkur to the Cathedral of Faces and translated for them because neither spoke Giant or Undercommon. [ch 055 / 055.05]
- Grek explained that carving a perfect face leaves the carver with nothing to improve, causing them to stop forever. [ch 055 / 055.05]
- Grek watched in horror when Felkur shattered the sacred crystal statue with a beer bottle; the envoys then fled. [ch 055 / 055.05]
- Grek subsequently emerged from the rock and told the party that destroying the statue had broken the stone giants' seven-year madness; their now-lucid king wished to meet them to finalize an alliance. [ch 056 / 056.01]
- At the Stone Lodge, Grek explained to Daein that the king's lowered head was a formal stone giant apology. [ch 056 / 056.03]

## Last Observed State
Grek was last observed at the Stone Lodge, quietly explaining the king’s formal apology to Daein during the alliance meeting. He was present and speaking in that scene; his subsequent whereabouts and status are not established in the summaries. [ch 056 / 056.03]

## Relationships
- **Decha:** Exiled Grek for imperfection, despite Grek’s attachment to the Lodge and its sacred image. [ch 055 / 055.05]
- **Daein and Felkur:** Grek serves as their interpreter and guide to stone giant customs. Felkur’s destruction of the statue initially horrified him, but he subsequently brought the party news of its beneficial consequences. [ch 055 / 055.05; ch 056 / 056.01; ch 056 / 056.03]
- **The stone giants:** Grek speaks of their seven years of helpless watching in collective terms and acts as their messenger to the party. His latest appearance places him alongside their king during the alliance meeting. [ch 056 / moment; ch 056 / 056.01; ch 056 / 056.03]

## Notable Quotes
