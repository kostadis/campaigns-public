## Identity
Constance Goodroot owns Goodroot Beet Farm and describes herself as an eligible beetroot farmer. [ch 004 / 004.07; ch 020 / 020.03] Her heritage is unresolved: she claims to be half-Aarakocra when challenged, while a later summary gives conflicting half-orc descriptions. [ch 012 / 012.05; ch 020 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Constance is hospitable and undemanding toward the companions, offering rest without chores or payment. [ch 001 / 001.02] She is enthusiastic about entertainment and persistent enough to persuade the group to attend a play. [ch 001 / 001.03] She seeks romantic companionship and expresses frustration at the shortage of local bachelors. [ch 020 / 020.03]

Her warmth has limits: she responds to Daein’s intrusive questions about her heritage with a scowl and markedly less comfortable hospitality. [ch 012 / 012.05]

## History with the Party
- Constance welcomed the companions to the Goodroots farm, gave them beds, and asked nothing of them during their rest. [ch 001 / 001.02]
- Although she had no invitation, she persuaded the party to attend The Poppet’s History of Hillsfar and accompanied them to the Cloverwall Roadhouse. Daein persuaded the doorman to admit her. [ch 001 / 001.03; ch 001 / 001.04]
- She warned the companions that the Red Plumes were terrorizing non-humans and supported reports of kidnappings with her own story. [ch 001 / 001.03; ch 001 / 001.05]
- On the road to Tinfellow Valley, she told Daein the history of the Tinfellows and their settlement. [ch 002 / 002.01]
- During his conversation with Deriel, Felkur identified Constance Goodroot as a friend and the owner of the beet farm where the companions had found Arrizz very sick. Constance was spoken of in this scene. [ch 004 / 004.07]
- When the party stayed at Goodroot Beet Farm to avoid guards, Constance welcomed everyone except Daein. In response to his probing her heritage, she claimed to be half-Aarakocra and scowled at his surprise. [ch 012 / 012.05]
- Akritas rescued that conversation; Daein slept in a closet while the other companions received nice beds. [ch 012 / 012.05]
- In Elventree, Constance greeted everyone except Daein, who repeatedly rebuffed her. She invited the party to her farm and described herself as an eligible beetroot farmer struggling to find bachelors nearby. [ch 020 / 020.03]
- The summaries state that Constance has feelings for Daein. [ch 025 / 025.06]
- The party learned that Malkyn had captured Constance and was torturing her to fuel a ritual. Malkyn’s belief that Constance loved Daein fueled her rage. [ch 032 / 032.03]
- Selûne told the party Constance was safe for now; they planned to infiltrate the castle to rescue her. [ch 032 / 032.03]
- Bramgrim opposed killing Constance because of her connection to Selûne, while Isthana argued that Constance’s death might weaken Malkyn but would not replace the need to kill Malkyn. [ch 032 / 032.05]
- Constance was present during the battle against Malkyn, and the party was reminded not to target her with fireball spells. [ch 034 / entry; ch 034 / 034.02]
- Charles later credited the party with rescuing Constance; she was spoken of during his appeal for further help. [ch 035 / 035.04]

## Last Observed State
Charles credits the party with rescuing Constance; she is spoken of in his account, rather than shown present. Her location and condition after that rescue are not established in the summaries. [ch 035 / 035.04] Her latest direct presence is during the battle against Malkyn, when the party is reminded to avoid hitting her with fireball spells. [ch 034 / entry; ch 034 / 034.02]

## Relationships
- **The party:** A recurring host and friend; Felkur explicitly identifies her as a friend. Charles later credits the companions with her rescue. [ch 001 / 001.02; ch 004 / 004.07; ch 035 / 035.04]
- **Daein:** She has feelings for him, while he repeatedly rebuffs her. Their interactions also include tension over his questions about her heritage. [ch 025 / 025.06; ch 020 / 020.03; ch 012 / 012.05]
- **Akritas:** Intervenes to defuse the uncomfortable exchange between Constance and Daein. [ch 012 / 012.05]
- **Malkyn:** Captures and tortures Constance to fuel a ritual; Malkyn’s belief that Constance loves Daein fuels her rage. [ch 032 / 032.03]
- **Selûne and Bramgrim:** Constance has a connection to Selûne whose nature is unspecified. Bramgrim invokes that connection when opposing her death. [ch 032 / 032.05]
