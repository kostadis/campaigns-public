## Identity
Jhanos Myrkeer was the elected Magistrate of The Stop following Hillsfar’s withdrawal of the Red Plume Guards. The Emerald Enclave supported his administration and sent the heroes to help guard the new order. [ch 012 / 012.04]

## Personality and Motivations
Jhanos showed warmth and hospitality toward the heroes, expressing delight at meeting them and providing accommodation in the chateau. [ch 013 / 013.02]

His decision to hand the Magistrate over to Biff establishes an intention to transfer the office; his personal reasons are not stated. [ch 014 / 014.02]

## History with the Party
- Jhanos Myrkeer was elected Magistrate of The Stop after Hillsfar withdrew the Red Plume Guards; the Emerald Enclave asked the heroes to support him and guard the new order. [ch 012 / 012.04]
- The heroes met Jhanos upon arriving at The Stop. [ch 012 / 012.07]
- Jhanos exchanged something with a halfling, who then ran off. [ch 013 / 013.02]
- Delighted to meet the heroes, Jhanos provided rooms in the chateau and asked Rogi to take them there; Rogi said he first had to talk to Kendrick. [ch 013 / 013.02]
- When Kendrick tried to charge for stabling their horses, Daein reminded him that Jhanos had said everything was being paid for. [ch 013 / 013.03]
- Jhanos died mysteriously after arguing with Karl outside the Chateau. [ch 013 / 013.04]
- Glenna Moonsmith was upset about Jhanos's death and claims blaming Karl; she demanded that the town's new police quickly uncover the murder. [ch 014 / 014.01]
- The heroes recalled that Jhanos had decided to hand the Magistrate over to Biff. [ch 014 / 014.02]

## Last Observed State
**Dead:** Jhanos died mysteriously following an argument with Karl outside the Chateau. [ch 013 / 013.04]

In the subsequent chapter, he was spoken of during inquiries into his death and the intended succession. Claims blamed Karl, but the supplied summaries do not establish the killer or the cause of death. [ch 014 / 014.01; ch 014 / 014.02]
