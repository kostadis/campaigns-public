## Identity
Vigdas, also called the Widow Vigdas Miller, is a grey-haired human widow. She lives alone in the millhouse; her husband died two winters before the companions met her. [ch 002 / 002.02; ch 005 / 005.04]

## Personality and Motivations
Her hospitality toward strangers suggests a welcoming disposition. Her distress over the Tinfellows’ suffering and pleasure at their return indicate affection for them. [ch 002 / 002.02; ch 005 / 005.04]

Daein anticipates that she might match him with eligible brides she knows; her own reasons for matchmaking are not given. [ch 006 / 006.02]

## History with the Party
- Vigdas invited the four strangers into her millhouse and served them tea and cookies. [ch 002 / 002.02]
- She told Daein she had been friends with the Tinfellows. [ch 002 / 002.02]
- She reported that the Red Plumes had come twelve days earlier, spared her because she was human, and gone north. [ch 002 / 002.02]
- The Widow Vigdas Miller was pleased to see Elbus and his family when the heroes returned them to Tinfellow Valley. [ch 005 / 005.04]
- Daein wanted something to do partly to avoid Vigdas matchmaking him with eligible brides she knew. [ch 006 / 006.02]

## Last Observed State
Her latest direct depiction places her in Tinfellow Valley, pleased to see Elbus and his family return. [ch 005 / 005.04]

In the subsequent chapter, she is mentioned through Daein’s wish to avoid her matchmaking; she is not shown acting or speaking. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.02]

## Relationships
- **The Tinfellows:** Vigdas identifies herself as their friend and welcomes Elbus and his family’s return. [ch 002 / 002.02; ch 005 / 005.04]
- **Daein:** A recipient of her hospitality who later wants to avoid becoming the subject of her matchmaking. [ch 002 / 002.02; ch 006 / 006.02]
- **The Red Plumes:** She reports that they spared her during their violence because she was human. [ch 002 / 002.02]
