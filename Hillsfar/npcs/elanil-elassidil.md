## Identity
Elanil Elassidil, known as the Moonsilver Herald, is an elven woman and the coalition’s overall military commander. She is also identified as the ranking member of the Harpers. [ch 058 / entry; ch 022 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Pragmatic and weary, Elanil projects calm authority in public and speaks bluntly about her forces’ limitations. Her military priority is to break the enemy’s command by defeating Hledh Hellspawn. [ch 058 / entry; ch 058 / 058.02]

Her earlier assignments show concern with identifying the source of the contagious madness, finding a cure, and determining whether it was being weaponized. [ch 022 / 022.01]

She expresses bitter frustration toward Alder and the Harpers when she recognizes their involvement, yet maintains the assault’s immediate objectives. In private, she admits fear of the coming battle and Graz'zt; Daein’s reassurance briefly makes her smile. [ch 058 / 058.03; ch 058 / 058.06]

## Last Observed State
At the departure from Sporedome, Elanil put on her helmet and headed off to lead her forces toward the assault on Maerimydra. She was alive and acting as commander at that moment; the battle’s outcome and her subsequent condition are not established in the summaries. [ch 058 / 058.06]

## Relationships
- **The party:** Elanil serves as a mission giver and adviser, assigning investigations and recommending a route that the party follows. Their military cooperation has a stated limit: she tells them they will be on their own inside Maerimydra. [ch 022 / 022.01; ch 022 / 022.05; ch 058 / 058.03]
- **Daein:** She confides her fears to him before the march and responds to his reassurance with a smile. [ch 058 / 058.06]
- **Alder and the Harpers:** Elanil holds Harper rank but bitterly accuses Alder of sending others to die resolving a crisis the Harpers created. This is her accusation, rather than an independently established account of responsibility. [ch 022 / entry; ch 058 / 058.03]
- **The coalition:** She commands its military operation and openly acknowledges the limited diversion its forces can sustain. [ch 058 / entry; ch 058 / 058.02]
- **Malkyn Grenefeld:** He pursued her when she left the package for the party. [ch 017 / 017.04]
