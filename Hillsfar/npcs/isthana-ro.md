## Identity
Isthana Ro is the long-tenured Head of Hillsfar's Thieves Guild and an ally who has provided the party with covert access and practical assistance. [ch 031 / entry; ch 032 / entry; ch 035 / 035.02]

## Personality and Motivations
Pragmatic and opportunistic, she treats political upheaval as an opening for her organization. She favors influence while avoiding the personal danger of ruling directly. [ch 035 / entry; ch 035 / 035.02]

Her hospitality combines generosity with pressure: commercial support comes with an expectation of cooperation, and her warnings imply that independence would be costly. She wants the party invested in Hillsfar's protection and willing to tolerate her criminal enterprises. [ch 050 / 050.05]

She plans for personal survival, including preparing a claim of magical coercion should the assault on Malkyn fail. [ch 033 / 033.03]

## History with the Party
- Isthana Ro, the long-tenured Head of the Thieves Guild, provided the party's sewer-to-dock transport. [ch 031 / entry]
- As the party's ally, she facilitated the "Shadow Path" entry. [ch 032 / entry]
- She argued that killing Malkyn was essential and that Constance's death alone would not suffice. [ch 032 / entry]
- She criticized Bramgrim's perspective as unclerical during their disagreement over Constance. [ch 032 / entry]
- Isthana joined the party and proposed entering the Obsidian Keep through the sewers or front door, warning of danger through either route. [ch 033 / 033.01]
- She considered climbing the walls to place noisemakers for the assault's distraction. [ch 033 / 033.03]
- If the plan failed, she was prepared to claim she had been charmed and forced to serve Malkyn. [ch 033 / 033.03]
- After the ruling elite's removal, she thanked the party for creating opportunities for the Thieves Guild. [ch 035 / entry]
- She repeatedly offered the party political power; Felkur and Daein declined. [ch 035 / 035.02]
- She declined to seek power herself because she shared Felkur's concern about keeping her head attached. [ch 035 / 035.02]
- She helped the party obtain magical armor and equipment from the castle's stores. [ch 035 / 035.02]
- She gave the party a polite farewell and departed through the sewers. [ch 035 / 035.03]
- When the party returned to Hillsfar seeking business opportunities, she hosted a private restaurant dinner and brought Felkur three bottles of rare wine. [ch 050 / 050.05]
- She offered limited partnerships in her businesses; Felkur declined in favor of the party pursuing its own direction. [ch 050 / 050.05]
- She offered a ready-made business, protection, supplies, favorable critics, important customers, and help finding a chef, while warning that a venture outside her patronage would struggle. [ch 050 / 050.05]
- She wanted the party invested in protecting Hillsfar and accepting her continued criminal enterprises. [ch 050 / 050.05]
- Daein agreed to tolerate her activities provided they did not interfere with the party's business; no agreement was signed. [ch 050 / 050.05]
- In a later meeting with Charles, Isthana Ro was mentioned as a potential source of help securing government approval for the proposed Stargate venture. [ch 051 / 051.01]
- Felkur authorized Charles to discuss possible land and property with Isthana; Charles promised to make no purchases or commitments. [ch 051 / 051.01]
- At the muster, First Lord Vuhm Yestral invoked Isthana Ro's glowing words about the party; this was a mention of her, rather than an established appearance. [ch 058 / 058.06]

## Last Observed State
Her latest direct appearance is the private dinner in Hillsfar, where her business proposal remained open and no agreement was signed. [ch 050 / 050.05]

Subsequent references concern possible assistance with the Stargate venture and, most recently, First Lord Vuhm Yestral invoking her favorable assessment of the party. Neither reference establishes her presence at those meetings. [ch 051 / 051.01; ch 058 / 058.06]

Status not established in the summaries.
