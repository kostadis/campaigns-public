## Identity
Semdran is a Harper leader commanding a group of rangers associated with the coalition muster at Sporedome. [ch 060 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
His willingness to lead comparatively inexperienced troops into a dangerous battle supports a characterization of bravery. He and his troops invoke Mielikki while aiding the party; broader personal motivations are not established. [ch 060 / entry]

## History with the Party
- Semdran, a Harper leader, arrived from the west with Harper rangers to reinforce the party during the Mirror Cavern ambush. [ch 060 / entry]
- His group had attended the coalition muster at Sporedome. [ch 060 / entry]
- His rangers invoked Mielikki, cast Faerie Fire on two Umber Hulks, and supported the party with repeated volleys of arrows. [ch 060 / 060.02]
- The Harper archers’ final volley left the last Umber Hulk with 2 hit points; Felkur finished it. [ch 060 / 060.02]

## Last Observed State
Last observed reinforcing the party in the Mirror Cavern with his Harper rangers. The encounter ended in victory, with the western approach held; his individual condition afterward is not specified. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 060 / 060.02]

## Relationships
- **Harper rangers:** Semdran leads the group that reinforced the party. [ch 060 / entry]
- **The party:** A battlefield ally whose troops provided magical and archery support against the Umber Hulks. [ch 060 / 060.02]
- **Coalition:** His group participated in the muster at Sporedome. [ch 060 / entry]
