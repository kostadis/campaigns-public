## Identity
Rilira is a Drow noblewoman and resistance leader who claims to be the rightful heir of Szith Morcane. Her authority among the enslaved laborers made her central to organizing an uprising. [ch 054 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Haughty, imperious, and suspicious, she initially treated Felkur’s unexpected arrival as a possible assassination attempt. She uses threats of retaliation to demand loyalty. [ch 054 / 054.03; ch 055 / entry]

Her central ambition is to reclaim Szith Morcane. She treats support for the campaign against Maerimydra as conditional on arming her followers and liberating her city. [ch 054 / 054.04; ch 055 / 055.01]

She expresses contempt for non-Drow and rejects hobgoblin claims to rule. Her dismissiveness toward Quil'bryn is explicitly associated with Drow gender politics. Despite her forceful objections, she can accept persuasive strategic reasoning. [ch 054 / 054.03; ch 055 / entry; ch 058 / entry]

## History with the Party
- Rilira was first mentioned as a noblewoman claiming approximately two hundred enslaved forge workers. [ch 052 / 052.02]
- A slave spokesperson directed the party to Rilira in the slave pens, identifying her as the leader whose order could trigger a revolt. [ch 053 / 053.04]
- Grundar led the party toward Rilira; Korkan challenged her leadership and threatened the party if they supported her. [ch 054 / 054.02]
- Felkur teleported into her guarded quarters. Rilira initially suspected an assassination attempt and questioned how he could cast spells when the prisoners’ magic had been taken away. [ch 054 / 054.03]
- Rilira claimed to be Szith Morcane’s rightful heir and demanded Korkan’s death, proof of it, and the fire giant’s death in exchange for the Drow slaves’ support. [ch 054 / 054.03]
- She threatened pursuit throughout the Underdark and Overbright if betrayed; Felkur was unimpressed. [ch 054 / 054.03]
- After Korkan and the fire giant died, Rilira confirmed her alliance with the party. The party retained her leadership to secure an organized uprising. [ch 054 / 054.04]
- Rilira wanted Szith Morcane back; Felkur understood the party’s promise as a commitment to free it. [ch 054 / 054.04]
- Rilira demanded weapons for her followers and committed them to assist against Maerimydra after arming them and liberating Szith Morcane. [ch 055 / 055.01]
- She identified an approach to Dengor’s palace through the House Morcane ruins, where the forge stood. [ch 055 / 055.01]
- Rilira publicly thanked the adventurers for restoring Szith Morcane to her, provoking tension with other claimants. [ch 057 / 057.05]
- Her former slaves were assigned to the Eastern Base alongside Grundar’s Duergar. [ch 058 / 058.06]
- Rilira objected to keeping Quil'bryn’s Drow in reserve, but Daein persuaded her to accept their role as a tactical backup force. [ch 058 / 058.06]

## Last Observed State
Rilira is actively participating in coalition planning at Sporedome as leader of the former Drow slaves. Her followers are assigned to the Eastern Base alongside Grundar’s Duergar; this is an assignment, with no arrival at that base established in the scene. She accepts keeping Quil'bryn’s Drow in reserve after Daein persuades her of their tactical value. [ch 058 / 058.06]

Her ownership of Szith Morcane remains contested: her public claim provoked rival claims, and the coalition agreed to prioritize Maerimydra while postponing the ownership dispute. [ch 057 / 057.05]

## Relationships
- **The party:** Conditional allies whose support she connects to reclaiming Szith Morcane. Felkur understood their promise as liberation of the city, leaving a difference between his interpretation and her ambition to possess it. [ch 054 / 054.04]
- **Felkur:** Her negotiating contact, initially suspected of intending assassination. Her threats failed to intimidate him. [ch 054 / 054.03]
- **Daein:** Successfully persuades her to accept the reserve deployment she initially opposes. [ch 058 / 058.06]
- **Her followers:** She supplies the leadership needed for an organized uprising and presses for their armament before further campaigning. [ch 054 / entry; ch 055 / 055.01]
- **Quil'bryn:** An object of her contempt; she also objects to keeping his Drow out of the front lines before accepting the reserve plan. [ch 055 / entry; ch 058 / entry]
- **Korkan:** A rival for leadership whose death she demanded as a condition of support. She subsequently dismissed his usefulness in demeaning terms. [ch 054 / 054.03; ch 055 / moment]
- **Grundar:** His Duergar and her former slaves share the Eastern Base assignment. [ch 058 / 058.06]
- **Asala:** Their competing claims in Szith Morcane produced an immediate public dispute. [ch 057 / 057.05]
