## Identity
Cedric owns a house containing many wood carvings, each bearing an amulet. The party believes he is an adventurer from elsewhere; his origin is not confirmed. [ch 019 / 019.07]

## Personality and Motivations
Cedric seeks relief from a spirit he says disrupts his sleep. He fears his wooden wards are failing and is willing to seek help through intimidation or persuasion. [ch 019 / 019.06; ch 019 / 019.08]

His distress over being unable to dispose of the phylactery establishes a further concern, but his broader goals are not described. [ch 020 / 020.01]

## History with the Party
- Cedric told the party that a restless spirit kept him awake at night and asked for help confronting it; the party agreed to help. [ch 019 / 019.06]
- Cedric led the party to his house, where many workers and wood carvings were present. Daein found a recurring motif, and the group noticed an amulet on each carving. [ch 019 / 019.07]
- The party believed Cedric was an adventurer from elsewhere and considered asking Charles and Biff about him. [ch 019 / 019.07]
- Cedric said the ghost appeared at night, was attracted to his amulet, and was no longer reliably warded off by his wooden statues. He requested help through intimidation or persuasion; the skeptical party agreed to investigate. [ch 019 / 019.08]
- The party learned that a recovered gem was a failed lich's phylactery holding Cedric's soul, and that destroying it would trap his soul. [ch 020 / 020.01]
- Cedric was distressed that he could not get rid of the phylactery. Daein suggested continuing their journey, and no clear solution was reached. [ch 020 / 020.01]

## Last Observed State
Cedric is distressed about a recovered gem identified as a failed lich's phylactery holding his soul. Destroying it would trap his soul, and no solution is reached. His location and bodily condition are not specified. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 020 / 020.01]

## Relationships

- **Daein:** Shows interest in helping Cedric and investigates his carvings. After the phylactery revelation, Daein suggests continuing the journey without resolving the problem. [ch 019 / 019.06; ch 019 / 019.07; ch 020 / 020.01]
- **The ghost:** According to Cedric, it appears at night and is attracted to his amulet. Its identity and motives are not established. [ch 019 / 019.08]
