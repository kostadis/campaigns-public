## Identity
Hledh Hellspawn is a fiend-born fire giant and leader of Maerimydra’s fire giant and demonic forces. Pipyap identified her as the city’s huge fire giant queen, ruling from its eastern castle. [ch 058 / entry; ch 041 / 041.06]

## Personality and Motivations
Solom described Hledh as powerful and maintaining locked, heavily guarded gates. Elanil Elassidil reported that she was preparing an invasion of the sunlight realms; these reports establish defensive precautions and a military objective, but provide little evidence of her personal temperament. [ch 039 / 039.06; ch 058 / 058.02]

## History with the Party
- The party learned that items stolen from Hledh's lair included a magical samovar. [ch 025 / 025.02]
- Solom described Hledh as a powerful fiendish giantess who kept Maerimydra’s gates locked and heavily guarded, making a frontal assault suicidal. [ch 039 / 039.06]
- Pipyap warned the party to avoid Hledh, Maerimydra’s huge fire giant queen, who ruled from the eastern castle. [ch 041 / 041.06]
- At the war council, Elanil Elassidil identified Hledh Hellspawn as the leader of the fire giant and demonic forces, present in Maerimydra preparing an invasion of the sunlight realms. [ch 058 / 058.02]
- Elanil Elassidil presented killing Hledh Hellspawn as the operation’s primary objective, expecting her loss to provoke infighting among the fire giants. [ch 058 / 058.02]

## Last Observed State
Hledh was last spoken of at the war council, where Elanil Elassidil reported her presence in Maerimydra preparing the invasion. This was a report, not a direct encounter with Hledh. [ch 058 / 058.02]

She was the coalition’s primary assassination target. Elanil expected her death to provoke infighting among the fire giants; the council account establishes an intended operation, not its outcome. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 058 / 058.02]
