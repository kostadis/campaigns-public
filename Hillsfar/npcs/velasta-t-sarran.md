## Identity
Velasta T'sarran is a Drow priestess of Kiaransalee and an undead revenant, with pallid, non-living skin and bright, intelligent eyes. [ch 056 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Vengeance defines her purpose, but preserving her existence complicates it. Felkur recognized that she wanted to accept Bramgrim's redirection because completing her revenge would cause her to cease to exist. Her willingness to be persuaded reflects a desire to prolong that unfinished purpose. [ch 057 / 057.01]

Her wrath extends to those who deny her vengeance: after believing fire giants responsible for Solom Ned'razak's death, she pledged indiscriminate violence against them. [ch 056 / 056.05]

## History with the Party
- The party met Velasta, a Drow priestess of Kiaransalee, in the crypts and recognized her as a revenant. [ch 056 / 056.05]
- Velasta sought vengeance against Solom Ned'razak for betraying and murdering her mother, her sister, and herself. [ch 056 / 056.05]
- After Velasta sensed Solom's death, Daein deceived her into believing fire giants had killed him; she pledged to slaughter every fire giant she encountered. [ch 056 / 056.05]
- The party discussed memory-altering magic to protect their deception, while Quil'bryn agreed to keep his troops silent about his responsibility for Solom's death. [ch 056 / 056.06]
- Velasta arrived after the party slew King Dengor. Chapter 056's narrative and memorable moment describe her demanding to know who killed Dengor, while the scene's final bullet instead names Solom Ned'razak. [ch 056 / 056.10; ch 056 / moment]
- Bramgrim persuaded Velasta that her quarry was still in Maerimydra. [ch 057 / 057.01]
- Felkur realized Velasta wanted to be convinced because completing her revenge would cause her to cease to exist. [ch 057 / 057.01]
- Velasta snapped her fingers and departed, leaving behind a fire giant she had previously banished, who returned. [ch 057 / 057.01]
- In Szith Morcane, Velasta's assistant Asala asserted a century-old family claim to the goddess and temple sites; Solom Ned'razak's clone subsequently reduced Asala to dust. [ch 057 / 057.05]
- The party learned that Dorina T'sarran was Velasta's aunt and was hunting Solom Ned'razak; they feared Dorina could expose their deception to Velasta and turn her against the coalition. [ch 058 / 058.05]
- The added recap details establish that Velasta had left before seeing Solom's clone and that her followers had not yet contacted her. [ch 058 / 058.06]

## Last Observed State
Her last direct appearance ended with her snapping her fingers and departing after accepting that her quarry remained in Maerimydra; a fire giant she had previously banished returned behind her. Her arrival in Maerimydra is not established. [ch 057 / 057.01]

Later discussion established that she had left before seeing Solom Ned'razak's clone and that her followers had not yet contacted her. She was spoken of in these preparations, rather than shown present. [ch 058 / 058.06]

## Relationships
- **Solom Ned'razak:** The object of her vengeance for betraying and murdering her mother, her sister, and herself. Her belief that fire giants killed him rests on Daein's deception. [ch 056 / 056.05]
- **The party:** An ally whose cooperation depends on a concealed truth. Daein redirected her wrath, Bramgrim sustained the deception, and Felkur understood her reluctance to finish her revenge. [ch 056 / 056.05; ch 057 / 057.01]
- **Quil'bryn:** Solom Ned'razak's actual killer, whose responsibility the party concealed from her; he agreed to silence his troops to preserve the alliance. [ch 056 / 056.06]
- **Asala:** Her assistant, subsequently reduced to dust by Solom Ned'razak's clone. Velasta's reaction is not established. [ch 057 / 057.05; ch 058 / 058.06]
- **Dorina T'sarran:** Her aunt and a fellow cleric of Kiaransalee, also seeking revenge against Solom Ned'razak. The party feared that Dorina could expose their deception and turn Velasta against the coalition; no such disclosure is established. [ch 058 / 058.05]
