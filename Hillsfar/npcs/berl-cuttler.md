## Identity
Berl Cuttler is a dwarven smith whom the group saved from Tak the Doppelganger. [ch 010 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
His offer to work at cost for Daein and his friends shows practical generosity toward them; his underlying motives are not stated. [ch 010 / 010.03]

- The chapter 010 entry identifies Berl Cuttler as a dwarven smith whom the group saved from Tak the Doppelganger. [ch 010 / entry]
- Back in Hillpicket, Daein asked Berl Cuttler to build an owlbear cage; Berl agreed to do it at cost and told them to return in a week. [ch 010 / 010.03]
