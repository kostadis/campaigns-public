## Identity
Glenna Moonsmith owns the Plodding Plow tavern and is a familiar contact of the party in The Stop. Vragni Moonsmith is her ancestor. [ch 010 / entry; ch 058 / entry; ch 059 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
She takes frightened witnesses seriously and commits resources to securing help, as shown by her assessment of the Arcrus Ranch hands and her offered reward. [ch 010 / 010.05; ch 010 / 010.06]

Her expressed priorities include resolving Jhanos Myrkeer's murder, addressing the accusations against Karl, and protecting The Stop from Hillsfar. She wants Karl to leave the wider struggle to others. [ch 014 / 014.01; ch 015 / 015.04]

She seeks a permanent rest for her ancestor, appealing to the Order of the Gauntlet for help. [ch 058 / entry]

## History with the Party
- Glenna Moonsmith owns the Plodding Plow. [ch 010 / entry]
- She offered the party free drinks to hear the Arcrus Ranch hands' account of goblin attacks, believing the hands were sober and frightened. [ch 010 / 010.05]
- She offered 50 gold to investigate; Bramgrim accepted. [ch 010 / 010.06]
- Her report prompted the party to set out toward the farms. [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.01]
- At the Plodding Plow, she was upset about Jhanos Myrkeer's death and the accusations against Karl, and demanded that the town's new police solve the murder. [ch 014 / 014.01]
- She begged Karl to leave La Resistance to others and protect The Stop from Hillsfar. [ch 015 / 015.04]
- Karl told the heroes he loved Glenna Moonsmith and would abandon La Resistance for her. [ch 015 / 015.06]
- Karl's subsequent actions with La Resistance upset her; Daein suggested a way to mend their relationship. [ch 017 / 017.03]
- Bramgrim stayed behind with Glenna to create the impression that the whole party had been there while the others departed for Yûlash. [ch 017 / 017.06]
- An Order of the Gauntlet representative relayed her request that her dwarven ancestor, risen as a powerful mummy in the Cave of Black Whispers, be laid to rest. [ch 058 / 058.06]
- Charles waited near the Cave of Black Whispers with a handmade coffin to carry her ancestor Vragni Moonsmith home for burial on behalf of his beloved Glenna. [ch 059 / 059.01]
