Mira Roper is head of the local guards in Hillpicket. [ch 008 / 008.01]

## Personality and Motivations
She shares concerns about evil at the Carnival and responds enthusiastically to the defeat of threats to the locals. Her excitement also includes relief at her own survival, as Daein recognizes. [ch 008 / 008.01; ch 010 / 010.03]
