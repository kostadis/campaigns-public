## Identity
Horac is Mags's man at the Arena, responsible for paying Breex and bringing prisoners inside. [ch 004 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Horac challenges Daein's attempt to accompany the prisoners but yields to his bluff as a Red Plume Guard, showing that claimed authority can overcome his objections. [ch 004 / 004.06]

## History with the Party
- Horac is Mags's man and pays Breex for the prisoners. [ch 004 / entry]
- Horac objects to Daein accompanying the prisoners, then backs down when Daein bluffs his way in as a Red Plume Guard. [ch 004 / 004.06]
- Horac leads the prisoners through Arena tunnels lined with cells holding exotic creatures and people. [ch 004 / 004.06]

## Last Observed State
Horac's last recorded action is announcing that they will go eat after the prisoners are confined in the Arena. His subsequent whereabouts and status are not established in the summaries. [ch 004 / 004.06]

## Relationships
- **Mags:** Horac serves her and carries out her instructions concerning payment and prisoner intake. [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.06]
- **Breex:** Horac pays him for the prisoners on Mags's behalf. [ch 004 / entry]
- **Daein:** Horac initially objects to his entry, then permits him to follow after his Red Plume Guard bluff. [ch 004 / 004.06]
- **The prisoners:** Horac acts as their escort into the Arena's holding areas. [ch 004 / 004.06]
