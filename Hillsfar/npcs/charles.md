## Identity
Charles is a dwarf, a former leader of farmers turned brigands, and a revolutionary associated with La Resistance. He was appointed Captain of the Guard of The Stop. [ch 051 / entry; ch 007 / entry; ch 015 / 015.03]

The summaries also identify him as Sharles and Karl; Accountant Carl is a persona he adopts when discussing business. His presentation includes a beret and an affected French resistance accent. [ch 010 / 010.03; ch 015 / 015.06; ch 051 / 051.01]

## Personality and Motivations
Charles is theatrical and consciously practices his dramatic delivery. His revolutionary idealism centers on opposing exploitation and persecution of non-humans; he urges action against evil before its costs and dangers increase. [ch 035 / moment; ch 006 / 006.05; ch 010 / 010.05]

Love for Glenna Moonsmith competes with his commitment to La Resistance. His stated decision to abandon the cause for her did not prevent later resistance activity from straining their relationship. [ch 015 / 015.06; ch 017 / 017.03]

He also shows enthusiasm for practical business arrangements, readily discussing guilds, taxes, protection, and exclusive operating rights when Felkur proposes Stargate. [ch 051 / 051.01]

## Last Observed State
Charles was physically present deep in the Underdark on the approach to the Cave of Black Whispers, beside a farm cart carrying a handmade pine coffin. He wore an ill-fitting constable's uniform and a beret. His purpose was to bring Vragni Moonsmith's remains home for burial on Glenna's behalf. [ch 059 / 059.01]

He intended to wait while the party confronted the mummy. After Daein's playful pat knocked him from the tailgate, he cited his lack of stability as his reason for avoiding combat. Recovery of the remains and completion of the burial are not established in this scene. [ch 059 / 059.01]
