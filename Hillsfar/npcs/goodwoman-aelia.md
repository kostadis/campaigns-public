## Identity
Goodwoman Aelia is an old woman encountered in Hillpicket who sought help concerning suspected evil at the Carnival. [ch 008 / 008.01]

## Personality and Motivations
Aelia wants the danger at the Carnival investigated and expresses confidence in Bramgrim as a rescuer. These encounters establish her concern and trust, but provide little evidence of her broader personality. [ch 008 / 008.01; ch 009 / 009.04]

## History with the Party
- The party encountered Goodwoman Aelia at Hope Kellar's tavern in Hillpicket; she believed something evil was happening in the Carnival. [ch 008 / 008.01]
- Bramgrim convinced the group to help her, and Mira Roper shared her concerns. [ch 008 / 008.01]
- The party later found Goodwoman Aelia behind a tent, cackling and babbling about fools and mirrors as cracks spread across her skin. She looked a lot like the Fools. [ch 008 / 008.05]
- The party found Goodwoman Aelia chained in the maze; she greeted Bramgrim as her rescuer. [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.04]
- Aelia said the maze made duplicates and planned to use the Duplicate Doctor Jubal to lead the carnival into the Underdark and sell the prisoners to demons. [ch 009 / 009.04]
- The Doppelganger shifted into Aelia's shape during her confrontation with the party. [ch 009 / 009.05]
- Status not established in the summaries beyond her being chained in the maze. [ch 009 / entry]

## Last Observed State
Her last direct appearance places her chained in the maze, able to recognize Bramgrim and explain what she describes as the maze's plan for its prisoners. [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.04]

Her release and subsequent condition are not recorded. Status not established in the summaries beyond her being chained in the maze. The later appearance of Aelia's shape belongs to the Doppelganger. [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.05]

## Relationships
- **Bramgrim:** Advocates for helping her; Aelia subsequently greets him with confidence that he will rescue her and the other prisoners. [ch 008 / 008.01; ch 009 / 009.04]
- **Mira Roper:** Shares Aelia's concerns about the Carnival; no closer relationship is established. [ch 008 / 008.01]
- **The Doppelganger:** Takes Aelia's shape during the confrontation with the party. [ch 009 / 009.05]
