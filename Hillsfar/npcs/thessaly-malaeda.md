## Identity
Thessaly Malaeda is the First Lord’s betrothed and Davon Malaeda’s twin sister, bearing a striking resemblance to him. [ch 016 / 016.04]

## Personality and Motivations
Her motives are not established. Akritas observed her speaking into a polished stone, crushing it, and vanishing, but the account does not explain her purpose. [ch 016 / 016.04]

## History with the Party
- Thessaly Malaeda was betrothed to the First Lord and was Davon Malaeda's twin. [ch 016 / entry]
- Akritas followed Thessaly Malaeda and saw her speak into a polished stone, crush it in her hand, and vanish. [ch 016 / 016.04]
- She disappeared again after the assassination attempt. [ch 016 / entry]
- Thessaly Malaeda was killed; a successful strike from Daein followed. [ch 018 / 018.03]

## Last Observed State
Thessaly Malaeda is explicitly reported killed. The account does not identify her killer; Daein’s successful strike follows her death. [ch 018 / 018.03]
