## Identity
Gai is Olisara's henchman, a broad-shouldered Shou human who plans the companions' infiltration of Hillsfar's Arena. [ch 004 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Gai's approach is practical and cooperative: he favors infiltration over a frontal assault, welcomes Daein's assistance, and incorporates Felkur's distractions into the escape preparations. His stated objective is to help the companions find the Tinfellows and orchestrate an escape. [ch 004 / 004.02]

He shows a shrewd understanding of Captain Erlich's financial motives, using a demand to attend the auction to maintain access to the prisoners. [ch 004 / 004.03]

## History with the Party
- Gai is Olisara's henchman, a broad-shouldered Shou human. [ch 004 / entry]
- At the Roadhouse, Gai proposes having the companions sold into Hillsfar as Arena slaves so they can find the Tinfellows while he helps orchestrate an escape. [ch 004 / 004.02]
- Gai welcomes Daein's offer to pose as a guard and knows where to place Felkur's three infused distraction rocks. [ch 004 / 004.02]
- Disguised as Red Plume Guards, Gai and Daein escort Falofa, Felkur and Bramgrim toward Hillsfar as prisoners. [ch 004 / 004.03]
- Gai insists on attending the prisoner auction, securing permission to join Captain Erlich's caravan; he explains to Daein that Erlich wanted all the profit. [ch 004 / 004.03]
- At the Arena, Gai leaves to set up the traps and sends Daein inside to help the companions if needed. [ch 004 / 004.06]

## Last Observed State
At the Arena, Gai leaves to set up traps and directs Daein inside to assist the companions if needed. The summaries do not establish whether he completes those preparations or what happens to him afterward. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 004 / 004.06]

## Relationships
- **Olisara:** Gai serves as her henchman and supplies the infiltration plan for her rescue assignment. [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.02]
- **Daein:** His partner in the Red Plume Guard disguise; Gai welcomes his help and later assigns him to support the companions inside the Arena. [ch 004 / 004.02; ch 004 / 004.03; ch 004 / 004.06]
- **Felkur:** Provides the infused distraction rocks that Gai intends to position around town. [ch 004 / 004.02]
- **Falofa, Felkur and Bramgrim:** Companions whose entry as apparent prisoners Gai helps arrange as part of the rescue plan. [ch 004 / 004.02; ch 004 / 004.03]
- **Captain Erlich:** A caravan captain Gai negotiates with while disguised as a guard, exploiting Erlich's interest in auction profits to secure passage with his group. [ch 004 / 004.03]
