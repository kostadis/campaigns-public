## Identity
Drystan is Tegan's brother, a former Dalelands farmer and escaped captive of Maram. [ch 037 / 037.04]

## Personality and Motivations
His observed distress offers little basis for establishing his usual personality: Tegan attributes his breakdown to torture and his subsequent ear injuries to the maddening winds. His refusal to proceed toward the Den of the Whispered Word establishes his reluctance to continue there, but no broader goals are recorded. [ch 037 / 037.04; ch 037 / 037.06]

## History with the Party
- The party found Drystan, Tegan's brother, clutching his knees and rocking, with dried blood running from both ears and a sharpened stick beside him. [ch 037 / 037.02]
- Tegan said she and Drystan had been Dalelands farmers before orc raiders captured them five years earlier and sold them to Maram; they served his guests and attended to his personal needs. [ch 037 / 037.04]
- Tegan explained that Drystan broke down the day before their escape after torture; the maddening winds then drove him to injure both ears with their pointed stick. [ch 037 / 037.04]
- Bramgrim offered to heal Drystan, but two Barlguras ambushed the party before healing began. No later healing is explicitly recorded. [ch 037 / entry]
- The Barlguras intended to recapture Drystan and Tegan; the party defeated both demons. [ch 037 / 037.03; ch 037 / 037.04]
- Drystan described their escape as having happened some time ago; the party determined that it had occurred only a day earlier. [ch 037 / 037.04]
- Tegan said they escaped when taken outside to feed on cave mushrooms. [ch 037 / 037.04]
- Drystan and Tegan refused to accompany the party farther toward the Den of the Whispered Word. [ch 037 / 037.06]

## Last Observed State
Drystan's last recorded decision is to refuse, alongside Tegan, to accompany the party farther toward the Den of the Whispered Word. His subsequent whereabouts are not given. Status not established in the summaries. [ch 037 / 037.06]

His ear injuries have no recorded treatment: Bramgrim's offer of healing was interrupted before it began, and no later healing is explicitly recorded. [ch 037 / entry]

## Relationships
- **Tegan:** His sister and fellow escaped captive. She comforts him and places herself protectively between him and the approaching party. [ch 037 / 037.02]
- **Maram:** His former captor, to whom orc raiders sold both siblings. They were forced to serve his guests and attend to his personal needs. [ch 037 / 037.04]
- **Bramgrim and the party:** Bramgrim offers healing with the party's agreement; the adventurers defeat the demons attempting to recapture the siblings. [ch 037 / 037.03; ch 037 / 037.04]
- **Barlguras:** Pursuers seeking to return him and Tegan to captivity; Tegan identifies them as palace inhabitants sent after the siblings. [ch 037 / 037.03; ch 037 / 037.04]
