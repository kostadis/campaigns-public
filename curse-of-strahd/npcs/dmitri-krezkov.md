## Identity

The bourgomaster/Burgomaster of Krezk, a Barovian noble bound by his oath of office to admit them. Grieving the death of the last of his four children, he is angered by Marron's advice to smile: "You have no children. You do not understand the grief." Puts the heroes to work in exchange for beds. [ch 020 / entry]


## Personality and Motivations

Grief over his children makes Marron's demand that he smile intolerable to him. He bargains for work in exchange for lodging. [ch 020 / entry]
