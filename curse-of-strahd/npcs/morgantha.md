## History with the Party


### Chapter 004 — Morgantha

- A hunched old woman in rags with a cart of pastries, who seems relieved when nothing happens to Gaxim after he eats one. [ch 004 / entry]


### Chapter 005 — Morgantha

- A hag and pastry peddler who collects payment house to house and bags a child, then gives him up when the party attacks. She offers Gaxim pastries and lays out Barovia as Strahd's prison: "We are all prisoners of Strahd." She calls herself "the fish that knows it is a fish." She recognises the Death House treasure, tells the Durst story, and points the party to the Order of the Silver Dragon and the temple reached by Tsolenka Pass. Told not to eat any more children, she smiles and walks away. [ch 005 / entry]


### Chapter 017 — Morgantha

- A haggard, heavyset hag whom Gaxim recognizes as the pie peddler. Claims the windmill with a deed bearing the official seal of Barovia, sells pies that Slerdax says are children, summons a dretch from her magic pot, counterspells Slerdax, and casts a lightning bolt on Wukong. Speaks of herself in the third person: "You seem to want to take Morgantha’s property." [ch 017 / entry]


### Chapter 018 — Morgantha

### Chapter 019 — Morgantha

### Chapter 023 — Morgantha

### Chapter 025 — Morgantha

### Chapter 027 — Morgantha

### Chapter 028 — Morgantha

### Chapter 037 — Morgantha
