### Chapter 016 — Davian Martikov

- The Martikov patriarch, who learns through the ravens that the danger is gone. Hospitable and insistent on civility ("we are civilized here and drink from a glass"), bitter toward his son Urwin, and defensive about Strahd: "we are a mission-oriented winery." He explains the three gems, describes Krezk and the Abbey, names the Abbot as Strahd, and gives the party a barrel on condition they find his gems first. [ch 016 / entry]


### Chapter 017 — Davian

### Chapter 019 — Davian

### Chapter 020 — Davian

- The elder Martikov. He sent Adrian off on Gaxim's horse to draw off Strahd, and gave a speech naming the heroes as the ones the Keepers have been waiting for. [ch 020 / entry]
