## History with the Party

- Savid says he sheltered refugees in his mansion and was slain when Strahd's forces destroyed the order. Godfrey adds that Strahd had the bones taken to Castle Ravenloft. [ch 021 / 021.11; ch 022 / 022.09]
- The heroes find his empty mausoleum and have its draconic inscription translated. Godfrey connects returning their lord to rest with Vladimir finding peace. [ch 023 / 023.06; ch 023 / 023.09; ch 022 / 022.09]
- Wukong finds Argynvost's skull in the room of bones and leaves Castle Ravenloft with it. The narration presents Strahd's repeated theft trap as an effort to break Argynvost's remaining hold on the mortal realm. [ch 024 / 024.09; ch 024 / 024.10]

## Last Observed State
