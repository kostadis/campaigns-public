## Identity

A handsome, eternally young man in a brown monk's robe bearing the symbol of the Morninglord. He rebukes the party for killing Mishka, rejects Ireena ("Ireena is not the true savior of Barovia or Tatyana"), insists Vasilka is Strahd's real bride, and wants a bridal gown for her, dealing only with the Burgomaster. Villagers suspect he is Strahd in disguise. [ch 020 / entry]
