### Chapter 028 — Kasimir

- The Dusk Elf guide to the Amber Temple. Wary of betrayal and on the edge of backing out, he is won over by Gaxim's purple grapemash. At the gate he says, "This is a place of great evil. In we must go!" He names Sangzor, cheers the kill, claims the pelt for his own fame, and promises "Kasimir’s famous stew!" — but not "in freezing cold of Tsolenka Pass!" He screams in terror at the roc. [ch 028 / entry]


### Chapter 029 — Kasimir

- A Dusk Elf, "humble", travelling with the party to find the secret that lets Strahd imprison his sister. He declines to lead and offers warm clothes for a nominal fee. (The chapter does not name who then sighs and gives the clothes, nor who fondles the ring of warmth.) He snarls that he is not the one that disturbed the hags. [ch 029 / entry]


### Chapter 031 — Kasimir

- Says he came to find the secret that turned Strahd into a vampire, to defeat him and free his sister. When Marron asks him "why should not I destroy them all?", the reply is that these are the repositories of the dark powers: "We must find the one that my sister used and the one that offered the gift to Strahd." [ch 031 / entry]


### Chapter 032 — Kasimir

- Travels with the party to the temple. Laments the destroyed sarcophagi, runs for the sarcophagus he was looking for, is killed when Slerdax casts dawn on the vampires, and is raised the next morning. Learning Zhudun is destroyed, he weeps for his sister and declares a blood feud, then accepts Gaxim's oath with a one-year deadline. Through Arthrax: a mutilated, grief-stricken dusk elf whose "real endgame was to raise his sister and kill Strahd." [ch 032 / entry]


### Chapter 035 — Kasimir

- Tells Gaxim "363 days to go." [ch 035 / entry]


### Chapter 045 — Kasimir Velikov

- Marches into the inn and explains that the heroes killed his sister after promising she would be saved. When Gaxim walks out, Kasimir attempts to curse him and fails. [ch 045 / entry]
