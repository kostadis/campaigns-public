### Chapter 005 — Ismark

- Ireena's brother. Delighted the party survived, he asks after "my friend Donavich", gives up on the Paladin argument and has the barkeep give them two rooms, then leads them to the Burgomaster's house. He insists to Ireena, "They will help us, Ireena!" [ch 005 / entry]


### Chapter 006 — Ismark

- Ireena's protector and the party's uneasy ally. He knows vampire lore, threatens war if they spare Doru, then begs them to stop chasing him once he flees. He links the sword card to Rictavio's Carnival and warns of werewolves. [ch 006 / entry]


### Chapter 007 — Ismark

- Ireena's guardian, who calls himself "protector of Ireena." The wolves single him out; he takes 20 points and then kills the last three wolves himself, impressing the party. He declares himself "the sworn enemy of Strahd!", argues with Ireena over it, warns that Vallaki is unwelcoming, and confronts the half-elf bard. He sighs when Ireena is kept at the mansion. [ch 007 / entry]


### Chapter 008 — Ismark

- Shrugs and whispers to his friend; she assures him there is nothing to fear. [ch 008 / entry]


### Chapter 012 — Ismark

- Bound in stocks on the festival platform. Freed by Gaxim, he grabs a sword and, with Merrin, kills Izek. At the end he is too distraught to be consoled. [ch 012 / entry]
