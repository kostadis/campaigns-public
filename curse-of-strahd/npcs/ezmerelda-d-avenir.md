## Personality and Motivations

Combative, proud of her expertise and protective of her chosen wolf pack. Her hostility toward van Richten eventually gives way to reconciliation when he explains his curse. [ch 023 / entry; ch 026 / entry; ch 037 / entry; ch 045 / entry]


## History with the Party


### Chapter 022 — Ezmerelda d'Avenir

### Chapter 023 — Ezmerelda d'Avenir

- Self-described "interplanar-vampire-killer, trained by the great van Richten". Lectures the party on killing vampires, opens the coffin, is accused of horse theft by Arrigal, vows to kill Morgantha, and turns on Rictavio: "You are a narcissistic sociopath who has attachment issues!" [ch 023 / entry]


### Chapter 024 — Ezmerelda d'Avenir

- The self-styled expert ("the great Ezmerelda d'Avenir"), irritated that no one consults her. She advises going underground, and is Strahd's chosen target once the theft is revealed. Strahd taunts her as "runt, you spawn of van Richten" and means to turn her. She fights alone: protection from evil, rapier and handaxe for 24 HP of damage, laughter when his bite fails, and finally a knock spell and "a dismissive Vistani salute." To Marron: "we are friends to the end!" [ch 024 / entry]


### Chapter 025 — Ezmerelda d'Avenir

- Declares herself a member of the "wolf pack". She identifies the coffin maker as a vampire, persuades the guardsman, fights with rapier, hand ax, lightning bolt and knock, mocks Strahd with a five-nothing count, and doubts Strahd's threat makes a good trap. She addresses the party as "Monsieur Gaxim" and "Monsieur Slerdax". [ch 025 / entry]


### Chapter 026 — Ezmerelda

- Peppers her speech with ("Monsieur", "Messieurs", "Oui", "magique"). Stays behind with Merrin against Strahd rather than escape — "Merrin, I stand with you!" — and breaks free of Strahd's hold. Curses the Warlock and helps kill him with a lightning bolt. Reveals Van Richten's tower and her wagon, and shows the party a note about him. Calls the party her wolf pack. [ch 026 / entry]


### Chapter 027 — Ezmerelda

- Recognises the hag's work in Slerdax's nightmares, laments her cart ("Not so useless anymore, Monsieur Slerdax?"), fights the ambushers with a lightning bolt, and agrees to wait outside the Vistani camp. [ch 027 / entry]


### Chapter 028 — Ezmerelda

- Recalls demons' resistances, casts magic missile and a firebolt that fizzles, thinks of Gaxim as part of her "wolf pack," and fails to befriend the roc with a spell. [ch 028 / entry]


### Chapter 029 — Ezmerelda

- Travelling with the party; called "Ez" by Slerdax and "the Vistani vampire hunter" by the narration. Fights with rapier and hand-ax, tosses Wukong a magical hand-ax, and is knocked out twice on the balcony, urging "monsieur Gaxim" not to throw his life away. [ch 029 / entry]


### Chapter 031 — Ezmerelda

- Opens locked doors with knock. Distrusts Wukong — "there is something fishy going on about the Rogue" — and nearly reveals the deals before deflecting Marron. Fights the Slaad with rapier and handaxe, and fails to restrain Wukong. [ch 031 / entry]


### Chapter 032 — Ezmerelda d'Avenir

- Fights with blades and rapier; keeps away from Wukong and Slerdax; argues for knock. Speaks with a flourish: "Monsieur Wukong deserves no help!", "Ze Wolf Pack is back! GROUP HUG!", "Non! I do not want to touch this evil." Poisoned and paralyzed by ghasts, she wills herself free. [ch 032 / entry]


### Chapter 033 — Ezmerelda

- Fights alongside the party with magic missile and knock spells; reluctant "to help that villain Wukong," and refers to "Monsieur Gaxim." [ch 033 / entry]


### Chapter 034 — Ezmerelda

- Travelling with the party. Arabelle's rescue clears her: she had been accused of kidnapping the girl and fled the Vistani for it, killing someone and stealing a horse in her escape. She handles Arabelle where Merrin cannot ("This is Vistani business."), and devises the illusion of Strahd that disarms KP. [ch 034 / entry]


### Chapter 035 — Ezmerelda d'Avenir

- Travels with the party; predicts Arabelle will betray them, accused of stealing a horse, and proposes to announce the werewolves' doom. [ch 035 / entry]


### Chapter 036 — Ezmerelda

- Travels with the party. She casts magic missiles and a lightning bolt, fights with her blades, calms Kellen and the children, and tells the group they "should stay away from human children." She keeps the Blinsky slogan on Kellen's doll quiet until Vallaki. [ch 036 / entry]


### Chapter 037 — Ezmerelda

- Defends Gaxim to Rictavio, then humiliates him: "I am not a lone wolf. I found a wolf pack." Offers her itchy fingers for the stone theft, is answered with an unattributed "NO, I AM NOT!" when Merrin calls her a thief, and attacks Morgantha while invisible. [ch 037 / entry]


### Chapter 039 — Ezmerelda

- Climbs with the party, slips on the wall and is saved by KP's feather fall. In the tower she is assigned to run up the stairs, is 60’ behind, runs up, and later falls to the bottom with Merrin when the tower shakes. She shouts, "stairs, stairs are a great workout," and dashes up 60 feet. [ch 039 / entry]


### Chapter 040 — Ezmerelda

- Called Ms. d'Avenir and Ez. Runs up the tower to 70’, is surrounded by three climbing vampires, warns "Monsieur Slerdax" that Strahd "can appear through any room," shoves a vampire off the stairs, and deals 10 HP with a magic missile. Afterwards tests the party on the bodies and awards a "Vampire Spawn Merit Badge." [ch 040 / entry]


### Chapter 041 — Ezmerelda

- Blames KP for inspiring Slerdax's "crazy maneuvers," flies after Slerdax on the broom of flying, is knocked unconscious by Patrina's wail, and after Merrin heals her, turns the empty crypt into "A teaching moment." Her speech in the chapter carries "Zis", "iz", "Mon Dieu" and "Monsieur". [ch 041 / entry]


### Chapter 042 — Ezmerelda

- Charges in with rapier and hand ax after pondering what KP would do; destroys a bat swarm. "Ordinary, pshaw!" [ch 042 / entry]


### Chapter 043 — Ezmerelda

- Also "Ez", and once "d'Avenir". She wants a share ("I will have zome"), is disgusted by Endorovich's inscription, follows Gaxim into the tunnel, and warns of the first fireball just in time ("FIREBALL, HIT THE DIRT!"). She uses her dust of dryness to drain the pool, and when KP objects she replies, "Oui, but it’s easier to cast spells, non?" After the second fireball, Slerdax pours a potion of greater healing down her throat; she hugs him and stares witheringly at KP. [ch 043 / entry]


### Chapter 045 — Ezmerelda

- Touched by van Richten’s confession: "You could have told me, you old fool." In the assault plan she swoops in with Wukong on a broomstick. [ch 045 / entry]


### Chapter 046 — Ezmerelda

- Travels with the party. "You arrogant … " she screams at KP; kills a water creature with a magic missile, casts a lightning bolt through the coffin, and casts mirror image. Declares, "Zis is not over yet," and her suspicion proves justified. Named Ezmerelda d'Avenir in Marron's fate. [ch 046 / entry]


## Last Observed State

Survives the final assault and warns that it is not over. In the post-script she becomes Marron's close friend; Strahd's eventual return vindicates her warning. [ch 046 / 046.09; ch 046 / entry]
