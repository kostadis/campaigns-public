## Identity

A Lich and "master of this temple," found as a confused skeleton and restored by Merrin's Greater Restoration. He refuses to ally against Strahd, recounts the temple's history and Strahd's pact, opens his spellbooks to Slerdax, and takes up the idea of turning the temple into a tourist attraction, the Amber B&B, with Vilnius as his apprentice. [ch 033 / entry]


## Personality and Motivations

Declines to join the fight against Strahd but allows access to his spellbooks and embraces the proposed tourist business. [ch 033 / entry]
