## Identity

The burgomaster's right-hand man, a brutish figure with a "gruesome demonic hand" whose presence terrifies bystanders, guards, the bartender and the maids. He claims Gaxim as his lost half-brother, pays for the party's lodging by intimidation, and escorts them to the Baron. Danika says he burned Udo Lukovich's house "Using some kind of dark magic". [ch 007 / entry]

### Chapter 008 — Izek Strazni

- The Baron's man, with a strong Barovian accent and a demonic arm attached at his shoulder, given to him by the dark powers after he lost his own. He calls himself "the Baron's right arm of justice". He arrested Udo. Gaxim calls him "My brother!"; Father Lucian says "Izek, for example, has no soul." and Willemina calls him "a soulless monster". [ch 008 / entry]


### Chapter 012 — Izek

- Ireena held in his room. At the festival he stands by Ismark, eager to kill him, and is enraged at Ireena's escape, his plans to capture his long lost sister in tatters. Denies Gaxim — "He is not my brother! He is a buffoon." — hurls flame, drops Gaxim to 0 HP, kills Lady Lydia after the Baron dies, and is killed by Ismark and Merrin after Gaxim knocks him prone. [ch 012 / entry]


### Chapter 013 — Izek

- Dead; carried lovingly by Gaxim to the Church and buried there at Gaxim's insistence. [ch 013 / entry]


### Chapter 014 — Izek

- Saw the guards' plan and killed Lady Lydia after the party killed the Baron. [ch 014 / entry]
