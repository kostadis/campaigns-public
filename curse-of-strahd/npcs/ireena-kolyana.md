## Identity

Ismark's sister. She makes her brother prove himself at the door for 5-10 minutes, explains the Burgomaster's death, and refuses to leave until he is buried: "I cannot leave this town until that happens." She is appalled when the party attacks Morgantha, and later orders Merrin to consecrate the grave. [ch 005 / entry]

## History with the Party


### Chapter 005 — Ireena

- Ismark's sister. She makes her brother prove himself at the door for 5-10 minutes, explains the Burgomaster's death, and refuses to leave until he is buried: "I cannot leave this town until that happens." ### Chapter 006 — Ireena

- Worried after the fight. She reminds Wukong of his promise to protect her and proposes the Abbey of St. Markovia as an alternative. [ch 006 / entry]


### Chapter 007 — Ireena

- Daughter of the burgomaster of Barovia, under Ismark's protection. The wolves ignore her. She mocks Ismark's Strahd routine as one that "might attract Strahd’s attention", thanks the guards, and claims nobility: "I am nobility." The Baron demands she stay at his house, and she agrees politely. [ch 007 / entry]


### Chapter 012 — Ireena

- Freed from Izek’s room by Wukong after Gaxim’s attempts hurt her. Speaks with a Barovian accent, calls Izek "a monster", and claims him as her twin. Disapproves of the attic detour ("I thought this was a rescue, not a robbery."), chooses to stay with Merrin, and urges the party to kill Izek. Tries to stop Wukong shooting the Baron, and at the end tries to console Gaxim. [ch 012 / entry]


### Chapter 019 — Ireena

- Agrees to leave Vallaki with Marron. At Yester Hill she squeezes Gaxim tight and, terrified of Strahd, begs Gaxim to leave. [ch 019 / entry]


### Chapter 020 — Ireena

- Brought to Krezk for safety. She asks Marron, "Do you think I will be safe here?" and is dumbstruck — "No!" — when the party proposes leaving her at the Abbey. Realizing Gaxim does not have her best interests at heart, she moves closer to Marron. [ch 020 / entry]


### Chapter 021 — Ireena

- Hears a gentle voice at the pool, greets Sergei — "My beloved Sergei!" — dodges Gaxim's grapple, and is pulled into the water, fading from view with him. [ch 021 / entry]


## Last Observed State

Fades into the pool with Sergei after recognizing him and evading Gaxim's attempt to stop her. Later references to her tomb do not establish that she returned to the party. [ch 021 / entry]
