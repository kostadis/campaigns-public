## Identity

One of the three Vistani owners of the Blood of the Vine Tavern, alongside Alenka and Mirabel. She is described as a woman of about forty with bright eyes and grey-black hair. The GM has ruled that the earlier spelling Sorvia refers to Sarovia. [ch 004 / 004.06; ch 019 / 019.02; ch 037 / 037.05]

No additional private motivation is inferred beyond the cited conduct and accounts.

## History with the Party

- She tells Alenka that the party has made them money. The three women become interested in the Death House trinkets and direct Marron toward Madam Eva. [ch 004 / 004.06]

- The later tavern account names Alenka, Mirabel and Sarovia as the owners and friends of Madam Eva. [ch 019 / 019.02]

- When the party returns to the tavern, she says that some will sleep better and comments that there can always be new additions to the dead adventurers’ parade. [ch 037 / 037.05]

## Last Observed State
