## History with the Party


### Chapter 007 — Urwin

- Danika's husband and the inn's cook. Mentioned only: he reports the delayed Wizard of Wines shipment and would be glad to have the party look into it. [ch 007 / entry]


### Chapter 009 — Urwin Martikov

- Keeper of the Blue Water Inn, Danika's husband, a wereraven and "a high-ranking member of the Keepers of the Feather". Long-winded, takes the locket into his vault, interprets the prophecies, and asks for help with the missing wine. [ch 009 / entry]


### Chapter 011 — Urwin Martikov

- Danika's husband, one of the Keepers of the Feather, who appears as a Raven. Warns the party, directs them to the back room, and calms the bickering in their house. [ch 011 / entry]


### Chapter 013 — Urwin

- Brings news that Milivoj has revealed where the bones are, urges the party to beat the dark, promises to bury Izek with Father Lucian, and declares that the Keepers of the Feather will hold the Church: "THIS IS OUR TIME!" When the bats come he sends the party out, invoking the prophecy and "Madam Eva". Afterward he explains what Strahd did in the Church. [ch 013 / entry]


### Chapter 014 — Urwin

- Survives the night in the church, chases the captain to explain the black-winged aasimar story, and argues for "a transition government" over an election. The party's debate ends with him named transitional Mayor — "Congratulations, Lord Mayor!" The heroes are told they are free to come through the city. Davian is pleased that "his son is now Mayor," and Urwin has sent the heroes to help save the winery. [ch 014 / entry]


### Chapter 019 — Urwin Martikov

- The new Burgomaster of Vallaki. Learns the town must smile, orders a mandatory weekly festival, and lets Ireena go when told his life is otherwise forfeit. [ch 019 / entry]


### Chapter 025 — Urwin Martikov

- The burgomaster. He demands the heroes leave because his wife was killed by one of Strahd’s minions, then accepts Slerdax's offer to have her raised, promising to keep the body safe and reminding them they only have five days. [ch 025 / entry]


### Chapter 026 — Lord Urwin Martikov

- Called Lord Martikov and Lord Urwin. Has the city guard bar the party from Vallaki, but brings his wife's body out in a hearse, calling the promise "a great boon." Refuses Gaxim the hearse horse, citing "the expected increase in deaths." [ch 026 / entry]
