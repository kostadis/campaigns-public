## Identity

Named as "The Devil himself." Per Urwin, he entered the Church, killed Lucian and Ismark, and was about to take Ireena when the heroes arrived. [ch 013 / entry]


## Personality and Motivations

Uses invitations, threats, proxies and selective promises to manipulate the heroes, and explicitly seeks to corrupt them. These are recorded campaign actions, not new tactical plans. [ch 018 / entry; ch 024 / entry; ch 035 / entry]


## History with the Party


### Chapter 013 — Strahd

- Named as "The Devil himself." Per Urwin, he entered the Church, killed Lucian and Ismark, and was about to take Ireena when the heroes arrived. [ch 013 / entry]


### Chapter 014 — Strahd

- Unseen except outside the church, where he bends at one knee, writes a note, seals it, and leaves it at the church's door. [ch 014 / entry]


### Chapter 018 — Strahd von Zarovich

- The Count, "the devil." Arrives late after an illusion greets the party, and scolds Rahadin for it. Sizes up each hero: offers Slerdax employment, taunts Marron about the absent Cleric, offers Gaxim help against the hags. Tells of Knazaporium's failed rebellion, of Tatyana and Sergei, and of his plan for Ireena; threatens to burn down Vallaki. Nearly loses his temper at Slerdax but remembers his vow when shown the letter. Wants entertainment: "I want to corrupt your souls." [ch 018 / entry]


### Chapter 019 — Strahd

- The Count. Taunts the heroes about the winery, recognizes Sergei's sword, and denies Marron her revenge. At Yester Hill he raises the Druids and Berserkers, calls Ireena "Tatyana," fireballs Marron, polymorphs Slerdax's horse into a frog, and pursues Gaxim and Ireena toward the Winery. [ch 019 / entry]


### Chapter 020 — Strahd

- Absent in person. The Dark Powers torment him by trading a glimpse of his old realm for letting the warriors and druids rampage; he chased Gaxim to the Winery. The Abbot holds that Vasilka is his real bride. [ch 020 / entry]


### Chapter 021 — Strahd

- His voice cries "She is mine!" and his lightning strikes the pool. He later sends the party an invitation to dinner to discuss vengeance. [ch 021 / entry]


### Chapter 024 — Strahd

- The host. He confronts the spider in the Hall of Bones, eats it in bat form, and proposes an early dinner. At the table he is "curiously delighted" to talk about his Castle, fails to charm Gaxim, and is appalled by Gaxim's corner ruse. His reasoning is shown directly: a trap he has run on heroes for centuries, held back by his promise of safe passage, "And he was a man of honor." When the promise breaks he attacks Ezmerelda, and ends the chapter frustrated at the doorway, howling in rage. [ch 024 / entry]


### Chapter 025 — Strahd

- Count Strahd von Zarovich. He glares from the shade in daylight, confronts the party outside the Temple — "You stole my prize." — threatens Vallaki through its burgomaster, leaves a note, has the party followed, and sees the beacon's glow from afar. [ch 025 / entry]


### Chapter 026 — Strahd (Count von Zarovich)

- Bored until this chapter; he blocks the road to keep Danika dead as punishment of Lord Urwin for letting Ireena escape. He lets Wukong go, salutes him, and offers him "a place for you in my kingdom." Gaxim resists his charm, and for the first time there is doubt in his head, yet he feels alive. Ezmerelda breaks his hold and his spawn is banished; he leaves as a bat, thinking about how to defeat these heroes. [ch 026 / entry]


### Chapter 028 — Strahd

- Not present. Called The Devil; he trapped the undead adventurers in Barovia rather than killing them. [ch 028 / entry]


### Chapter 029 — Strahd

- Manifests for a moment to whisper to Wukong, hinting he will need "a replacement for that fool Rahadin." [ch 029 / entry]


### Chapter 032 — Strahd

- Not present. Per the Vampyr, he accepted its gift of life eternal; per Arthrax, he punished Kasimir and forced the Dusk Elves to obey him. [ch 032 / entry]


### Chapter 033 — Strahd

- Not present. Per Exethanter, he came seeking immortality, pacted with the vestiges, murdered his brother Sergei, and became a vampire; he "treated me rudely." [ch 033 / entry]


### Chapter 034 — Strahd

- Absent but behind the revenants' ambush: "Strahd said we were to wait here." He defeated KP and, according to KP, did not tell him where in Sergei's crypt the item lies. [ch 034 / entry]


### Chapter 035 — Strahd

- Strahd ordered vengeance on the Martikovs and "subcontracted out the hit on a clan of werewolves". [ch 035 / entry]


### Chapter 040 — Strahd

- Not present. Has told his minions about Wukong's pacts. Rages from Castle Ravenloft, “I defeated you once, mage, and sent you into madness. I will defeat you again!”, and later growls at KP. [ch 040 / entry]


### Chapter 041 — Strahd

- Not present; the thugs fear him more than the party. [ch 041 / entry]


### Chapter 042 — Strahd

- Took KP's gem of true seeing; can scry anyone who eats at his table. [ch 042 / entry]


### Chapter 043 — Strahd

- Materializes in the dungeon and greets them: "what a pleasant surprise, I thought I would have two victims, and now I have three!" He casts fireball twice — the second time from within a wall — moves through walls, and sets his vampire spawn on the party ("kill them for me, my children!"). He singles out KP, whom he claims to have destroyed before: "Again, again, you thwart me Knazaporium! But you are a worthy adversary. A toy that doesn’t break." His attempt to charm KP fails, and he flees, then howls into the winds in Castle Ravenloft, knowing he had them trapped. The chapter also calls him "The Count". [ch 043 / entry]


### Chapter 044 — Strahd

- Count von Zarovich learns from Rahadin how KP got Teleport. He is shocked ("WHAAAT!"), briefly considers killing Rahadin, and resolves to deal with the Lich once "these fools" are dealt with. [ch 044 / entry]


### Chapter 045 — Strahd

- The target of the whole chapter. His wives and the Banshee hold the tomb; KP recalls that Strahd sends minions and comes for the kill once KP is down. On the parapet he escapes when Wukong misses, moving without exposing himself to attacks of opportunity, and "Strahd sinks into the ground beneath the party." [ch 045 / entry]


### Chapter 046 — Strahd

- Flees towards the catacombs using his haste; exposed by KP's mold earth. Waits in his coffin, guarded by four red dragons, Rahadin and vampire spawn. Destroyed when Slerdax's magic missile shatters the coffin — "Strahd von Zarovich, the dark lord of Barovia, is dead and gone." In the post-script he re-forms after months and plots revenge. [ch 046 / entry]


## Last Observed State

Destroyed in the final battle, then re-forms months later through the Dark Powers. He resumes plotting revenge, and Wukong becomes his trapped majordomo. The dossier's current state must include this return. [ch 046 / entry; ch 046 / 046.09]
