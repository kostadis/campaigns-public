## Identity

The Mad Mage, a gnome in ragged robes with messy hair and a beard. He came to Barovia to write a treatise on trade through the mists and the book was trapped there. Driven mad and convinced the heroes were part of a plot by Strahd, he nearly attacked them, until Ezmerelda's illusion persuaded him they were Strahd's enemies. He had cast mind blank on himself to protect what he knows about Bayard's dagger, which trapped him on the plane. Restored by Slerdax's dispel magic, he is vain, scholarly and impatient: "I’m a powerful wizard. I have powerful wizard things to do. Other things." He tells the heroes the third item is in Sergei's grave, tells them to call him with "this stone" when they are ready to attack Strahd, and trades dinner for lessons in mold earth. [ch 034 / entry]


## Personality and Motivations

Vain, scholarly and impatient, but willing to teach and to trade practical help. His elaborate magical plans are accompanied by lectures and occasional admissions of poor judgment. [ch 034 / entry; ch 039 / entry; ch 045 / entry]

### Chapter 039 — KP

- Accompanies the party to Ravenloft and stands just before the entrance into the front courtyard. He casts feather fall to save Ezmerelda on the wall, and again in the tower. He tutors Slerdax on the animated armor, dropping it with dispel Magic and lecturing that "understanding the weak point and taking advantage of that is the true art of the mage." He casts Dimension Door to carry himself and Slerdax to the landing, is attacked by a vampire in a leather jerkin, takes 6 from the halberds, and casts sunbeam, sculpting around the heroes. [ch 039 / entry]


### Chapter 040 — KP

- Fights through the battle despite the leather-jerkined vampire clawing him, breaking his concentration, and drinking his blood mid-fall. Clicks his fingers to burn the halberds, misty steps, kills vampires with magic missiles ("Hm. I can take care of that."), and ends the fight with a sculpted thunderwave. Strahd shouts that he should have killed him. Carries Wukong and then the party by broom to the High Tower. [ch 040 / entry]


### Chapter 041 — KP

- Confers with Slerdax about hiding, kills all but one thug with a fireball, enters Patrina's crypt out of curiosity, is frightened by the Banshee, and casts magic missile and detect magic. Called "The Great but unwise KP." [ch 041 / entry]


### Chapter 042 — KP

- The former gnomish librarian, called by the narration "the greatest wizard of any age ™" and "the great mage". He deduces Strahd's scrying, casts fireball and snowball storm, lectures on inter-planar economics, and supplies lore about Stefan Gregorovich, Kroval Grislek and King Katsky. His friends do not call him KP. [ch 042 / entry]


### Chapter 043 — KP

- Called "Knazaporium" by Strahd. He judges the maul to have no magic, slides down the chute, says he can breathe water, and casts a wall of force as a sphere that holds off the spawn but leaves Merrin outside. Strahd's charm does not affect him. He teleports the group out and notes that they were handed a marginal defeat. [ch 043 / entry]


### Chapter 044 — KP

- The gnomish wizard and the heroes' ally, now able to cast Teleport thanks to the Lich's spell library. He looks for a permanency spell, cannot find it, and teleports the party into the catacombs with a coin. He remembers Vallaki gossip about Lady Isolde, points Gaxim to the Champagne, and admits that assembling an army of villagers to take on Strahd "is something I would do." He is teleported back by the curtain of light and happily entertains Wukong's business proposal. [ch 044 / entry]


### Chapter 045 — KP

- Knocked unconscious by the Banshee and healed by the heroes. He recalls how Strahd defeated him last time, admits his army-of-potential-undead plan "may not have been his most elegant 15th-dimensional chess move," and works out that the key to the Banshee was a dark power locked in amber — now gone. At the inn he answers the question about the cosplayers with deadpan evasion: "I have not seen a young man named Alexa and his three friends dressed as a wizard, a fighter, and a cleric." [ch 045 / entry]


### Chapter 046 — KP

- The mage who runs "their twisted game of chess" against Strahd: time stop, mold earth, a sunbeam, silence on Gaxim, and the teleports in and out. Always casts water breathing in the morning. Called "the gnome" as he clicks his fingers to teleport the party away. Afterwards he pursues interplanar research. [ch 046 / entry]


## Last Observed State

Survives the assault and resumes interplanar research. The post-script also credits him with writing about Slerdax's restaurant. Keep this campaign NPC separate from the module registry's Mordenkainen entry. [ch 046 / 046.09; ch 046 / entry]
