# Chapter 32: The deal with Kasimir

Date: Days 20–21 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
It is decided that Marron will lead the party — "Or rather, she decides." Gaxim agrees, and the rest fall into line. Her first order: "New rules," she shouts. "I alone will touch sarcophagi." Wukong notices her spear and wisely decides to do nothing.

The vestige within the first sarcophagus offers Marron the dark gift of Yog the Invincible, an offering of physical resilience. Marron tells it, "We are looking for the creature that created a pact with Strahd." A violent evil laugh sounds in her head: "Strahd made a pact with the greatest of us. I demand you leave my sarcophagus alone." "No," she says, and shatters the sarcophagus. Kasimir stares at the destroyed sarcophagi: "so much power being lost."

Marron heads north into a breached vault (x33d) with amber-glazed walls and a floor of purplish-black marble, where four hunched creatures, each with a single large, baleful eye, cluster in the middle of the room. The nothics see that Marron is two warring souls, and that the rest of the group hasn't figured out what's going on. "Who is Merrin? Merrin, the one who was tortured, or Marron, the one who wants revenge?" Marron glares: "What are you talking about?" The chapter explains that when Marron or Merrin is in control, she forgets about the other, but when not in control, she knows and sees everything. Marron screams. Sensing the hatred of deals with evil, another nothic says, pointing at Wukong, "I bet you don’t know the deals the other one made." Asked what deals, the answer is "The deals with the evils in the Sarcophagi." Gaxim asks Marron, "Why not kill evil?" The answer: they are "the wizards who protected the tower for centuries", who now sit admiring their sarcophagi and draw power from the dark vestiges inside.

Marron says, "I am familiar with that," and unleashes the divine energy inside her: her eyes turn into pools of darkness and two additional flightless wings pop up from her back. Three nothics move away in fear; a single nothic stands its ground. The frightened three commune with the vestiges in the sarcophagi, while the fourth inflicts necrotic damage on Marron, who is resistant. "Kill evil!" shouts Gaxim, disappointed with his damage (26 HP). Wukong misses with his bow, Slerdax's fireball burns several, Marron kills the nothic in front of her, and Ezmerelda damages the nothics with her blades. Two nothics use the powers of the vestiges to blast the party with two cones of cold; all but Slerdax survive. Gaxim kills one nothic and leaves another barely alive for Wukong to finish, and Marron kills the remaining one. Slerdax is healed "with a touch of her hands."

Marron touches the remaining prisons. The western sarcophagus holds Delban, the Star of Ice and Hate, whose gift is the power to unleash deadly cold. Asked about Strahd, Delban laughs: "Strahd Lives? We have not seen him in days, or is it centuries." Strahd searched there for "a power that I didn’t have." "No," and she smashes it. The eastern sarcophagus holds Khirad, the Star of Secrets, offering divination: "surely Marron, you would like to know what your friends are thinking, what truly goes on in their souls." Of Strahd, Khirad says, "Ah, that man. He took the power of the greatest of us all." Marron destroys it. "Tired!" shouts Gaxim, closing the door for a short rest; the rest of the group thinks, "finally!"

In the plundered treasury (x39), invisible poltergeists attack Marron for 14 HP damage and one attacks Gaxim. The barbarian sends three of them to their "eternal and final doom", and Marron kills the last. The party concludes the dead were thieves who had become poltergeists. Wukong finds a secret door with a stairwell behind it.

At the next door, Ezmerelda keeps as far from Wukong and Slerdax as possible. Slerdax proposes casting find familiar; Ezmerelda argues for knock; while the two spell casters discuss, Gaxim smashes the door into a sealed treasure (x40). Gaxim runs to the piles despite the sword's warning about traps — "being deliberate and careful is not on brand" — telling the sword this is enough wealth to take care of his mom, dad, siblings, "and perhaps even the whole tribe!" The Fire Genasi jumps into a pile head first; it is "the most treasure the group has seen in Barovia."

An invisible Amber Golem pounds Wukong for 32 HP. Slerdax dispels its magic, it appears, and he runs away; "The Morninglord smiles on Slerdax" as the golem misses. The orc, seeing the monster, is enraged that it plans to take his gold; Gaxim deals two critical hits with his saber of light for 74 points of damage; the golem pummels Gaxim for 22. Marron pierces its amber shell with the blood spear for 22. Ezmerelda nearly holds back — "Monsieur Wukong deserves no help!" — but sees "her friend, Monsieur Gaxim" being pummeled and attacks twice with her rapier for 15 HP. Wukong's non-magical arrow bounces harmlessly off; "Everyone groans." Three magic missiles from the mage's hand inflict 15. Gaxim misses once and is beaten by a fist, and then Marron steps forward and kills it.

The haul: Wukong finds a magical bow; there is a figurine of wondrous power that is a raven, a potion of superior healing, fifteen signed copies of the *Snow Dwarf and the Seven Wights* by Nitch Rackmay, a broom of flying, a wand of magic missiles, some dust of dryness and a potion of animal friendship. Wukong dismisses the delicate dresses Ezmerelda shows him: "poor needlework, archaic designs, not haute couture, rags for the cleaner." Gaxim tries to get a Wooden Pony to move, because the sword told him it was real and he trusted his sword.

Through two earthquake fissures in the north of the chamber, the heroes reach a spiral-staircase shaft with amber walls sculpted as tentacles around bas-reliefs of kings, queens, pharaohs, and sultans, and alcoves holding rough blocks of amber. Ezmerelda smiles: "Ze Wolf Pack is back! GROUP HUG!" As Marron heads to the sarcophagus on the eastern wall, Kasimir's eyes light up — "this is the room that I have been looking for!" — and he runs to the sarcophagus, realizing that the crazed paladin will stop him. His decision-making was lacking: the sarcophagus he wanted was on the eastern, not the western wall, and he did not see the vampires that erupted from the earth and surrounded him. Kasimir screams for help; Gaxim shrugs, still angry about the goat that Kasimir held onto instead of offering to the Roc.

The vampires attack Gaxim, Marron, and Kasimir; only Kasimir is wounded. Gaxim does 54 HP. "Get out of the way," screams Slerdax; the barbarian obliges, the mage casts dawn, the vampires scream in agony, and Kasimir dies. Marron holds a fleeing vampire in place; Gaxim kills one; another dies fleeing from sunlight and radiant damage. Wukong, with his magical bow, hits for 20 HP. Ezmerelda: "You have finally become worthy of working with the great Ezmerelda d'Avenir." And she misses. A burning vampire spawn attacks Marron from behind the sarcophagus and misses, screaming, "damn you, Strahd!" Magic missiles kill one, Marron releases her grappled vampire and stabs it with a spear before the daylight kills it, Gaxim's handaxes do 26 HP, and Wukong kills the last.

The vistani guards the southern sarcophagus and the barbarian the western one while Marron approaches the eastern one; Slerdax and Wukong sigh internally. It holds Zhudun, the Corpse Star, whose gift is the power to raise the ancient dead. Zhudun did not gift Strahd: "No, he’s a petty man. Foolish. Power-hungry. I see your friend is dead. I can help if you will accept my gift." Merrin begs Marron to let her free so she can save Kasimir. "No. He wanted to make a deal with evil." Marron destroys the receptacle of Zhudun.

At the southern tomb: "I am Tenebrous and you are not worthy." Marron answers that she is "more worthy than you. I am so much worthier than you that I would never accept your gift!" Tenebrous is silent. The last sarcophagus says, "You have come seeking me," and refuses Marron: "You are not worthy." Pressed for a reason, it shouts, "BECAUSE I SAID SO. Only those with a particular view on the meaning of life." Ezmerelda refuses to touch it — "Non! I do not want to touch this evil." — and Gaxim steps in front of Marron, who relents, then demands Gaxim touch it. The voice tells him, "I alone can offer you immortality."

Slerdax, hearing this, says "I want to be immortal," casts a dimension door onto the sarcophagus, and recoils in horror at the offer. He tells the group it offers "the dark gift of the Vampyr" to any humanoid creature of evil alignment, the immortality of undeath. "maybe I can convince Vampyr to reveal something." He asks, "Is there a power that can defeat Strahd? Some power of good?" "No," and it laughs. "This is the abode of all of the dark powers." The vestige explains that Strahd spent months exploring the temple, learning of all the powers, and finally found the Vampyr; at first he avoided the deal, but the idea of life eternal was too tempting, and one day he accepted the gift, even though he knew what the price might be. Marron and Gaxim smash the sarcophagus.

Merrin wants out, wants to consecrate this place, and seizes control. She sees her friends injured and Kasimir dead, and casts greater restoration on Slerdax, letting him recover from the spell that the hags put on him. Gaxim takes charge: "we go sleep in the room with rats." Vilnius is still in the lecture hall and is pleased he will not eat rats today.

In the morning Kasimir is raised. "Am I a demon spawn?" Gaxim cuts him. "Ow! Ah yes, not a vampire." Merrin says the sarcophagi were "Purged," "of all evil," and, unsure which was Zhudun, confirms "yes! All were destroyed!" Kasimir: "You have consigned my sister to the doom you fools! Without his power, I could not raise her. She has been dead for centuries!" "I am an elf." He sobs — "My sister is now lost to me" — and Slerdax observes, "Well, she was dead for centuries." Agitated, Kasimir spits: "Today is the last day we have been friends," and "As of now, there is a blood feud between the elves of Barovia and you!" As Merrin, Slerdax and Wukong ready to strike, Gaxim stops them: "No. He is sad because his sister died. I sad because my brother died. I will find a good person to bring my sister to life! Not evil one!" "Do you swear to it?" "Yes." "If it is not done in a year, then it will be a vendetta." "Don’t kill goats!" Kasimir nods and leaves.

Slerdax recalls Arthrax Delmuroth, a dragon folk he met in Vallaki, a middle man criminal "between mayors, vampires, vistani, merchants, and occult conspiracies", who told him about Kasimir: a mutilated, grief-stricken dusk elf trapped in Barovia for centuries. His people surrendered to Strahd's armies on the verge of annihilation, and the few survivors were borne by the Vistani to the valley of Barovia. Kasimir took the name of the Vistana who welcomed him into his clan, Velikov, who passed away more than a century ago; Kasimir still lives among Velikov's descendants. His sister, Patrina Velikovna, is sealed in the catacombs below Castle Ravenloft. Convinced she was "the concubine of the devil Strahd", Kasimir and his fellow dusk elves stoned her to death; Strahd butchered all the women in the dusk elf tribe and had Kasimir's ears cut off, and he wears a cowl to conceal the mutilation. Patrina now speaks to him in dreams, claiming guilt and regret have cleansed her soul. Kasimir never believed she had changed: "His real endgame was to raise his sister and kill Strahd." Arthrax points out that the heroes are just a means to an end. Strahd insisted Kasimir rule the other Dusk Elves, who wanted to kill him for revenge, and made clear that disobeying or killing him would be worse. Merrin observes that not raising him would have avoided this new obligation, and that killing Kasimir now would be evil.

The heroes descend through the secret door in the East Shrine (x12) to the lower level, the stench of death growing. In the ghastly vault (x33c), with a floor of dark green marble and three amber sarcophagi, three-eyed ghasts wait — two on the floor, five more clinging to the walls and ceiling. Gaxim tries to reason with them; they attack, one climbing over him to strike from behind. Ghasts paralyze Merrin, though one bite finds "steel, not the fleshy bits." Slerdax casts a chromatic orb; Ezmerelda attacks successfully; Merrin recovers; Ezmerelda is poisoned and paralyzed. Slerdax's tidal wave hits four ghasts and leaves two prone; Ezmerelda wills her body free; Slerdax kills a ghast with a chromatic orb "using a power surge"; Gaxim kills two; Merrin and Gaxim finish the rest and smash the sarcophagi. Wukong mutters, "small-minded people." Slerdax grumbles, "the powers might be useful."

Back up and then down into the lower east hall (x32), glistening amber over a black marble floor, the heroes quickly kill three witches near the south door. Ezmerelda prepares to cast knock on the northernmost door before realizing it is not locked; inside (x35) stands a man-shaped construct of dark wood and riveted iron, cobwebbed to the wrecked furniture. Merrin marches to the southernmost door, convinced the amber repositories of the evil lie there. Wukong grumbles, "it is such a burden to be with the little people." Ezmerelda opens the door: a room of glazed amber and red marble, three amber sarcophagi, and above each "a human skull wreathed in green flame."
