# Chapter 2: A Ghoulish TPK

Date: Day 2 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
The chapter opens with Wukong taking stock of the house: "Kinda scary, but nobody’s died yet." Slerdax directs Gaxim to a bathroom (13), a dark room with a clawed-foot wooden tub, a small iron stove with a kettle, and a barrel under a spigot fed by a rooftop cistern whose plumbing no longer works. Slerdax takes careful note that the 2nd-floor kitchen was spotless and new, while this room is decayed and old.

Seeing nothing of value, Marron opens the door to the south (14), a room of dusty shelves holding folded sheets, blankets and old bars of soap. A cobweb-covered broom of animated attack leans against the far wall, and when the cleric enters further into the room, the broom attacks. Marron misses and retreats. Wukong, seeing no angle of attack, moves away and tells Gaxim, "your turn." The cry "Kill Straw!" follows, and the swing misses. Marron casts toll-the-dead, but the broom is unaffected. Slerdax casts produce-flame and singes the straw, and is disappointed that, unlike in the academy, the broom does not move to clean the room. Wukong hits with his bow, Gaxim misses, and Marron tries her cantrip again, doing 5 HP of damage and killing the broom. Someone exclaims, "You strong! You killed broom!" Wukong sighs at the lack of treasure and turns to Slerdax: "Hey brains, can you tell brawn where to go?"

Slerdax runs into a suite: a dust-shrouded, elegantly appointed bedroom (15A), an adjoining nursery (15B), and stained-glass double doors opening onto a balcony (15C) over the front of the house. A specter screams at the barbarian. Wukong fires an arrow and misses, and Gaxim, who has never seen a specter, is confused: "why firing bow?" Wukong is horrified — "That will kill Wukong! Wukong is Gaxim’s friend!" — and is answered, "Wukong, not Gaxim friend!" Marron enters and has a critical miss. Slerdax casts Tasha’s Hideous Laughter, and the specter laughs hideously and angrily while continuing its assault. Wukong screams, "Couldn’t you have used firebolt," Gaxim makes a poor tactical decision, and Marron hits the laughing specter for 3 glorious points of damage. The specter misses the war cleric; Slerdax misses. Wukong glares — "Gaxim, you are in the worst possible tactical position." — then flanks the specter and hits, and Gaxim hits as well. Marron rejects the idea of necrotic damage and hits the specter with her scythe. The specter strikes the cleric for 6 HP of damage and tries to move away. Slerdax's produce-flame misses, Wukong misses, and after "Fight ghost?" Wukong nods. The barbarian throws an ax. The specter brings Slerdax to 0 Hp, but the mage resists the soul-force sucking, and Wukong walks up and kills "the phantasmal force." The party takes a short rest.

"Wukong, where wolf?" — "Through there?" Gaxim steps onto the balcony (15c), comes back in, sees Marron searching against the wall, and finds a secret door. Before going through it, Marron investigates the nursery (15B): a crib covered with a hanging black shroud, holding a tightly wrapped, baby-sized bundle. Marron unwraps the blanket and finds nothing inside. Slerdax makes the connection that the missing baby is the baby Rose and Thorn said was missing, and the party realizes this dead or missing baby is what the letter from Strahd von Zarovich referred to.

Gaxim goes upstairs to the attic (16), a bare hall choked with dust and cobwebs, and charges south into a spare bedroom (17) furnished with a slender bed, nightstand, stove, writing desk and stool, empty wardrobe and rocking chair; a smiling doll in a lacy yellow dress sits in the northern window box, "cobwebs draping it like a wedding veil." In the storage room, among sheet-draped furniture, an unlocked wooden trunk near an iron stove holds the skeletal remains of the nursemaid, wrapped in a bedsheet stained with dry blood. Slerdax sees that she was stabbed to death by multiple knife wounds, and the party realizes that the specter was the woman who appears to have been the nursemaid. As Slerdax explores the room, a magical door materializes in the northeast corner, revealing a stairwell going down.

Gaxim charges into another web-filled spare bedroom. The door beside it is locked; Wukong unlocks it and encourages Gaxim to enter (20). Inside, a bricked-up window is flanked by two dusty beds sized for children, with a toy chest painted with windmills and a dollhouse that is "a perfect replica of the dreary edifice in which you stand." On the floor lie two small skeletons in tattered but familiar clothing; the smaller cradles a stuffed doll, the same as the one Marron has. The girl smiles at Marron, who asks, "Who are you, and what are you doing here?" The answer: "I am Rose, and this is Thorn. Our parents locked us here to protect us from the monster in the basement. Then they didn’t come back for us. Then we died." Both smile creepily and say, "Please don’t go." Marron and Gaxim leave.

Believing the wolves are below, Gaxim charges down the stairs, which go "down, down, down, down." Entering the dungeon, the party hears "an eerie, incessant chant echoing throughout." Marron directs them north to quarters (24) with a table, four chairs and four alcoves of moldy straw pallets, which Wukong inspects. Wukong declares, "Gaxim is our leader!" and adds, "And our meat shield." Gaxim leads on to a room with a well and more quarters (25): a 4-foot-diameter shaft with a 3-foot-high stone lip descending 30 feet to a water-filled cistern, with a bucket on a rope-and-pulley. Marron tastes the water; it does not taste of sulfur and is quite potable. The quarters hold locked chests. Gaxim offers to smash them and Wukong objects, but when Wukong fails to unlock one — "Gaxim, smash?" — the half-orc smashes the chest, and Wukong opens the remaining locks.

Heading south (26) and then west down the tunnel, the chanting grows discernibly louder. Gaxim falls into a pit trap, and Wukong, seeing the half-orc prone with 3 spikes in his body, remarks, "we should rethink the whole idea of Gaxim being in front." The party heads west (30); Marron insists on going down the stairs until they realize the ghostly chants are below. They head south instead. When Gaxim reaches the midpoint of a four-way tunnel intersection, where the chanting is noticeably louder to the north, four ghouls rise up out of the ground and attack.

By the end of the combat, the party is "down one rogue, one barbarian, and a considerable quantity of hair." The rogue clings to life, though a ghoul had begun to chew off his skin, and his pain is enormous. Afterwards Slerdax realizes that the ghouls must have been former members of the cult called the Priests of Osybus, a reference to which was found in the secret room in the library. The ghouls wear cultists' clothes, and the chambers found earlier (24, 25) may have been the quarters of the now undead cultists.

## Locations
### The bathroom (13)
A dark room with a clawed-foot tub, stove and kettle, and a dead spigot fed by a roof cistern. Its decay contrasts with the spotless 2nd-floor kitchen.

### The linen room (14)
Dusty shelves of sheets, blankets and soap; home to the broom of animated attack.

### The suite (15A, 15B, 15C)
An elegantly appointed bedroom (15A), the nursery (15B) with its shrouded crib, and a stained-glass-doored balcony (15C) overlooking the front of the house. The specter attacks here, and Gaxim finds a secret door.

### The attic (16), spare bedrooms (17) and storage room
A dust-choked upper floor. The storage room hides the nursemaid's remains in a trunk, and a magical door there opens on a stairwell going down.

### The children's room (20)
A locked room with a bricked-up window, two child-sized beds, a windmill toy chest and a dollhouse replica of the house; the skeletons of Rose and Thorn lie on the floor.

### The dungeon
Reached by a long stair, filled with incessant chanting. It includes quarters (24), a well room with more quarters (25), tunnels south (26) and west (30), a spiked pit trap, and a four-way tunnel intersection where the ghouls attack.
