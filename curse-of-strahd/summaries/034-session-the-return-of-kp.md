# Chapter 34: The return of KP

Date: Day 23 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
As the heroes leave the sign of the Amber B&B, Gaxim puts their problem plainly: "We have money. Nowhere to buy!" Their newfound wealth is useless. They were exiled from Vallaki, the prices in Barovia are prohibitive, and Krezk has nothing for sale. They wonder whether this was simply how the closed world of Barovia worked, and whether, trapped in this hell-dimension by Madam Eva, it was inevitable that they would be rich with nothing to spend it on.

The land's answer is no, "pointing at the march of the dead and the crazed mad mage." The chapter walks back through the choices that led here: freeing Tatyana was an option; Ireena could have chosen not to go to Sergei if Ismark had not died; Strahd could have caught up with Gaxim; the decision to help the Martikov’s made it possible for Gaxim and Ireena to elude Strahd; the dark powers might not have intervened, and Gaxim might have been charmed into handing her over. Argynvostholt would not be lit, "reminding Strahd and everyone in Barovia that the change is here." So many little butterflies, random events and random actions, all led to an exile from Vallaki. "There was no fate. There were choices. And the reality that is Barovia is that the prisoners choose to live here."

Merrin stares at the light and reflects on why they ended up in Barovia at all. The Mad Mage, KP, wanted to produce a treatise on trade through the mists. Having already written the definitive text on interplanar trade empires based on abyssal artifacts, he set out to write another authoritative text on the Vistani's power to travel through the inter-dimensional mists and the trade opportunities therein. He did write the book, but the book was trapped in Barovia: another groundbreaking book for the great gnomish library in the Kron Hills, trapped and lost. Merrin realizes the Mad Mage may have the knowledge and power they need, and argues that the group should find him; maybe he knows how to escape. Wukong agrees.

The journey back is uneventful, and the heroes choose to leave the undead adventurers trapped in their circle in their undeath. At the Raven River Crossroads they meet the assassin they killed a few days ago, with his henchmen, all decomposed. "We have come!" Gaxim asks, confused, "how long have you been here?" The answer: "Strahd said you would come here! Strahd said we were to wait here. Strahd said if we killed you, we would find vengeance!" The sword takes over and tells them they are fools: "Help us kill Strahd, and you will be free!" The revenants ponder it for a moment and refuse: "You will not deceive us with your words. You killed me once! I will kill you too! And perhaps we will spend all eternity killing each other!"

Slerdax opens with a fireball. The revenants try to use their magical powers to stop Gaxim, but "the good wisdom of our hero blocks evil again." Another revenant attacks the mage and misses. Wukong "casually picks his spot, casually aims, and misses," and a revenant's longswords slash through his armor for 47 HP of damage. Then, from an unattributed speaker: "Mes dieux! It was to be expected, with all ze people we have killed. We must escape this land. In ze meantime, I attack!" Merrin heals Wukong. Slerdax, leveraging his new spells, casts lightning bolt on the three creatures of vengeance. Gaxim's shield and armor block an attack, and the barbarian kills one creature and injures another. "You may kill us today, but we have eternity to practice our vengeance!" Wukong kills one with his bow. "Death is temporary," says the last. Slerdax steps back and casts a chromatic orb, and he and the creature strike each other; Gaxim finishes it off with two crushing slashes of his sun blade. The heroes all know this is not the last they have seen of these creatures.

From the crossroads, Wukong leads the group to the gates of Vallaki to spread the bills that Exethanter gave them. A guard greets them: "Cleric of Heironeous, you are not welcome." When the other guard asks why, he is told, "this fool convinced the mayor to force everyone to smile!" The guards are unimpressed by the heroes' pitch to post the bills, but the guard who refused Merrin stares at the writing for some time, asks "Is this really a family-friendly vacation spot with the most amazing food supplies ever?", and begins to see a business in it. He decides to keep the bills for himself "and create a nice tidy business," then wonders whether the heroes are offering safe passage to the temple. They are not, so he proposes a side business: "I suppose we could have a tiny little side business taking groups the Amber temple vistas. We are guards." "Exactly," says Gaxim. The other guard nods, they pack the bills away, and the first guard points the heroes away from Vallaki: "We will take care of this part of the business arrangement. Have a nice day."

At the beach of Lake Zarovich the heroes stumble on a lit campfire and a backpack leaning against a rock, and Gaxim decides to go touch the backpack. A soaked man in his underclothes climbs out of the lake with a huge fish and a strange weapon: "Hey, who are you?" "I’m Gaxim." "Who’s Gaxim?" He recognizes Merrin — "wait, you’re the dip shit cleric who convinced the mayor that we have to smile all the time" — then looks at Merrin's bloody spear and says, "No." "Smiling. We’re all smiling". He introduces himself, smiling even harder: "my name is Luka." He offers his giant fish, which "could easily feed a group of five", for "a paltry sum of four gold", and the group is stunned. "I am a unique fisherman with unique fishing skills." The weapon turns out to be a harpoon.

Asked about a strange hermit in the area, Luka describes swimming by the lake after fish "the size of your head," pausing to notice that the heroes' heads range "from the small to the exceptionally large." He heard bubbling from the surface, then "a streak of lightning struck the water and fried the fish," and when he surfaced, "all I saw was a bearded gnome with lightning crackling from his fingers." To cross the lake he suggests walking around, which is probably safest, or borrowing a boat; swimming is out because the water is too cold. The heroes' negotiation with the local fishermen fails until Luka steps in — "they’re friends of mine. They bought some of my fish at fair market value." — and one fisherman names his price: "30 gold pieces." But Slerdax remembers that his robe of useful items contains a boat, and the heroes row out on their own.

In the middle of the lake, a fisherman grabs a burlap sack, throws it into the water, and watches it sink. Gaxim jumps in; Slerdax casts a spell that freezes the water under the sack so it floats. A little girl is screaming inside. "Another hag," thinks Gaxim. The fisherman, distraught and very drunk, is killed by Gaxim, his blood splattered across the deck. Merrin opens the sack, and out pops a young vistani girl who declares herself to be Arabelle. "That drunk captured me and took me from my family." Ezmerelda stares in shock: "I was accused of kidnapping you. And that is why I had to flee the Vistani." Gaxim smiles that Ezmerelda "had nothing to do with the missing child," and Ezmerelda allows that she killed someone in her escape and stole a horse, and "the blood feud might create some complications," but "everyone here must know that I had nothing to do with kidnapping Arabelle."

Gaxim asks the 7-year-old girl, half a mile from shore, "Do you know how to use a rowboat on your own, sweetie?" Merrin intervenes: "I think we should bring her with us for now." Gaxim is confused — in his town, a 7-year-old female orc could row a boat through a typhoon. The group argues whether showing up with Arabelle would be better or worse until Wukong decides: "we keep her." "I bow before incomprehensible logic," groans Ezmerelda. Arabelle demands, "I am the daughter of a vistani lord. I command you to take me back now." Merrin tries to intimidate the child into following them and fails. Ezmerelda tells her, "You have never dealt with any children before, cleric. And you have never dealt with a vistani child." She says something to the child, who sits down quietly: "I will follow you." Merrin asks, "You threatened to kill her?" "This is Vistani business."

The heroes continue to the north end of the lake, where the trees climb the slopes of Mount Baratok, "Its monolithic presence is oppressive at this distance." The ground is rocky and tiring, even the wolves avoid it, and they climb above the blanket of fog that engulfs the valley as thunder clouds roll overhead. A gnome in ragged robes, with messy hair and a beard, shouts, "You can tell the devil of yours that he will not get me so easily. You should have stayed in bed today," and begins to move his improvised staff while reciting words of power. Merrin examines him and finds that powerful magic has driven him mad; a greater restoration might work, or might not. Wukong's investigation reveals that he is preparing spells to kill them all: KP has gone insane and is convinced they are part of a plot by Strahd to kill him.

"We have no choice but to kill him." "We have a choice," says Gaxim. Someone offers acrobatics; Slerdax blurts out, "Or offer to make a dress." Ezmerelda coughs, Wukong points to her and takes the child to safety, and Ezmerelda stands up and declares, "We are friends of strahd!" Gaxim groans. She has Slerdax "cast a minor illusion of Strahd at the right time," casts a spell behind her back, and announces, "See, Strahd’s chasing us." She tells Gaxim, "my friend, go and attack the noise!", and Gaxim and the sword are convinced that Strahd is pursuing them. KP stands confused: "perhaps he is an ally of yours. I will sit and watch and see what happens." Merrin rushes him — "you look ill, mage!" — and bounces off a wall of force. Slerdax snootily calls the Strahd idea "a waste of time"; Ezmerelda answers, "Patience. Wukong shoot at him." An arrow flies through the illusion, Slerdax makes it appear to have an arrow lodged in its heart, has it say "this is not the time," and turns it into a bat. Gaxim runs after the bat.

KP is now much less determined to wipe them off the map: "It would appear that you have defeated Strahd, and you are, in fact, enemies of Strahd. Why have you come looking for me?" Slerdax explains, "We hope you can help us destroy Strahd." KP faced Strahd and lost, cannot remember how, "and I’ve been trying to escape his damn realm ever since." Merrin suggests Strahd "afflicted you with some sort of like vile magic." Merrin attempts greater restoration, but the mage interrupts: "Let’s go have dinner in my castle." He opens a door into his castle, a demi-plane. Merrin's greater restoration doesn't seem to work, until she realizes he has cast mind blank on himself. Slerdax cracks his knuckles and casts the 5th level dispel magic, and the mage rolls on the ground and howls in pain. "I’m back. I’m back. Oh, I am back." He wants to know why he is in this mansion and where his spellbooks are, then realizes, "Oh, I’m still stuck in the plane of dread!"

KP rolls his eyes and guesses the rest: "You’re a group of adventurers. Who have found Madam Eva and collected some artifacts and have been told that I’m going to ally with you and we’re going to go defeat Strahd?" Merrin, taken aback, says, "I talked to the Lich." "Does he still have my book?" Gaxim nods, and the sword says the Lich "gave a five-star review." KP is delighted: "You rarely find another magic-user of a similar caliber to mine." The group decides to reveal all: they had taken an odd job to hunt down a clue for some cult of the dagger. KP's answer: "Bayard is probably afraid that I know about his little dagger and was going to reveal it to somebody." That is why he cast mind blank — "With my mind blanked. No one could figure anything out." — though it had "the unfortunate side effect that I couldn’t escape from this plane."

KP asks Slerdax whether he is a magic-user; the Genasi says he is. KP wants to know whether he engages in "convoluted, intricate plots that use spells in clever ways" or is "one of that point and shoot the fireball." "As you said, I’ve also struggled to find the wizard of my equal, and I continue to struggle." "Excellent. Excellent. Excellent." Asked about the dagger, KP says, "No, I can’t tell you about that. I’d have to kill you." He has to clean himself up, relearn his spells, and "go talk to Exethanter and get a copy of my book." He ushers them to the door: "When you’re ready to attack Strahd and call me with this stone, and I’ll show up at this castle." Slerdax reveals Madam Eva's prophecy and asks where the artifact is. KP: "It is in Sergei’s grave. That’s where you need to go to find the third item. It’s the crypt. I don’t know precisely where.  Strahd didn’t tell me."

Irritated, KP has "powerful wizard things to do," and the group looks longingly at the food. He will feed them only if they have something interesting to teach him. Slerdax smiles: "I have a book about mold earth." KP snorts that it is "a worthless cantrip," and "That’s why I never learned it." Slerdax offers to teach him what they did with mold earth. "This is actually interesting. Have you written a book on it?" "I have." "I have to learn about mold earth. Let us have dinner." The chapter closes on its mirror: "the dinner at Castle Ravenloft began with great wine and almost ended up in a massacre. The spread in Mordenkainen’s Magnificent Mansion almost started with a great slaughter and ended with great wine." Oddly, Gaxim managed to miss both.

## NPCs
### KP
The Mad Mage, a gnome in ragged robes with messy hair and a beard. He came to Barovia to write a treatise on trade through the mists and the book was trapped there. Driven mad and convinced the heroes were part of a plot by Strahd, he nearly attacked them, until Ezmerelda's illusion persuaded him they were Strahd's enemies. He had cast mind blank on himself to protect what he knows about Bayard's dagger, which trapped him on the plane. Restored by Slerdax's dispel magic, he is vain, scholarly and impatient: "I’m a powerful wizard. I have powerful wizard things to do. Other things." He tells the heroes the third item is in Sergei's grave, tells them to call him with "this stone" when they are ready to attack Strahd, and trades dinner for lessons in mold earth.

### Luka
A soaked fisherman in his underclothes, with a huge fish and a harpoon. He recognizes the cleric who made the mayor force everyone to smile, and smiles hard about it: "Smiling. We’re all smiling". He sells the heroes a fish for four gold, tells them of the bearded gnome throwing lightning, and vouches for them to the local fishermen.

### Arabelle
A young vistani girl, 7 years old, whom a drunk fisherman stuffed in a burlap sack and threw into the lake. "I am the daughter of a vistani lord. I command you to take me back now." After Ezmerelda speaks to her, she agrees to follow the group.
