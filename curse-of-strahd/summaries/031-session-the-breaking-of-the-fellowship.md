# Chapter 31: The breaking of the fellowship

Date: Day 20 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
After the defeat of the Flaming Skulls, the heroes explore the area to the south of the chamber (x22) with the specters. In a twenty-foot-wide, seventy-foot-long arched corridor sheathed in amber, its southern half scorched by fire, they find a charred corpse under a burned fur cloak: "the charred remains of Vilnius’s teacher and boss." On the corpse they find a staff of frost. "When Slerdax picks it up, something changes."

The party turns to Wukong to pick a path through the maze of doors. He leads them to the architect’s room (X20), dominated by a twelve-foot-tall model of a dark castle with high walls and tall spires: "It is castle Ravenloft." Behind it, among ruined furnishings, is a wooden chest; Wukong points out that its inside has a false bottom, and underneath it he finds a tome of understanding. Through the other door (X1) Wukong discovers "a long-lost potion storage room", its stone niches filled with hundreds of dusty bottles. The master rogue notices a secret door to the north and finds a staircase leading down, its thick dust undisturbed in ages, with secret doors set into the north and south walls of the uppermost landing.

Before going below, the heroes return to the room Wukong fled into when the arcanaloth attacked (X12) and open the secret door (X14). Stairs descend to a collapsed hall with a high ceiling and amber-glazed walls; a path through the rubble leads to an open doorway, from which "A deathly stench seems to come". At the east archer post (X13) the Sword causes Gaxim to observe, "maybe we fight from arrow slits instead of charge?" The friendly giant takes over and snarls, "No." Someone asks, "Shall we go down?" but the heroes point out that they have not yet explored the northwest balcony (x23) and the north area.

The black marble balcony overhangs the northwest corner of the temple, with the floor thirty feet below; nearly half of it has fallen away. Before anyone can walk on it, Slerdax casts mold earth to reinforce the construction — "Fortunately, because otherwise, the balcony would have fallen." North of it (X24) is a shrine, with a faceless obsidian statue in a raised alcove and two desiccated corpses slumped before it. The chapter turns to the Dark Powers, who "hold their breath; they know Gaxim will march in and be affected by a mighty evil power they placed here." "He does and is not." The narration asks, "Is Gaxim some kind of cosmic joke being played on them?" Gaxim examines the corpses and determines they died of hunger. Wukong points out a secret door to the north, but Gaxim "is bored and decides that it’s just another door", and wants to go down by the stairs near the upper west hall (X17). The rest of the group follows him. On the way, Slerdax sees the Sunsword and asks Gaxim, "I must have your sword." Gaxim stares at him and says, "No." Slerdax looks annoyed that this power is being denied him.

The heroes enter "a great amber hall full of ambered familiars": amber coating walls and ceiling "like sculpted honey", a vaulted ceiling twenty-five feet high, and ledges of life-sized alabaster statues of animals, many fallen and shattered. Gaxim is tempted to pick up all of the amber figurines for resale, but the sword urges him not to, and the friendly giant grunts, "more important things, now." Wukong finds the south door locked with an arcane lock. Ezmerelda cracks her knuckles: "it is good, non, that I have knock?" While they wait, Gaxim wonders aloud, "why here?" Kasimir steps forward: "Ah, my friend, you have come to help me. There is some secret that turned Strahd into a vampire, and if we find that secret, perhaps we can use it to defeat him and free my sister."

Behind the door are three amber sarcophagi in alcoves. Wukong examines the western sarcophagus, immediately goes into some kind of trance, and when it is over "he reeks of filth." Gaxim asks, "Wukong still brave?" Wukong nods: "Just the smell made me dizzy." Asked "Get treasure?", he answers, "No, there is no treasure. Let’s look at the other sarcophagus." Confused, Gaxim touches the same sarcophagus. A dark power reaches out to him and announces itself as Norganas, "one of the dark powers trapped in the amber temple," offering a dark gift, the Finger of Oblivion — "the power to turn life into undeath." Gaxim pulls his hand away: "Wukong, evil offer you power?" Wukong tries to lie, but "whatever power has decided Gaxim will be the force of good sees through Wukong’s lies." Then: "You lying!"

Before Gaxim can react, Slerdax, sensing power, rushes to the southern sarcophagus and is tranced; Wukong rushes to the eastern one and enters another quick trance. The wizard and the rogue exit their trances "looking curiously different." Merrin is in horror: pacts are being made with evil powers, and she feels helpless "as if she is reliving some terrible moment in her life." Her smile falters. Marron screams, "You need me! You need me to protect you!" The white aasimar drops its feathers, and the black feathers of Marron appear. When Slerdax moves to touch the other sarcophagi, Marron blocks him: "Evil must be destroyed!" The chapter says "She and Gaxim destroy them very quickly."

Gaxim points at Wukong: "Wukong not brave. Wukong Lies! Wukong lies". Wukong ignores him and enters what appears to be a wizard’s bed-chamber; Gaxim, upset, refuses to enter. Ezmerelda, standing grumpily beside him, says, "there is something fishy going on about the Rogue." Marron scans to see if the rogue is evil, but nothing pops up; she decides that if her divine powers detect no evil, Wukong is not evil, "and Gaxim is just a child." The half-orc is upset.

Gaxim and Ezmerelda pointedly stand as far away as possible on the eastern wall when Wukong opens the door to a littered bedroom (X38). As Slerdax and Marron enter, a book flies from the shelf, barely missing Marron. Marron picks up a book titled "On the nature of inter-planar economics and the use of artifacts to power permanent trade routes under Infernal Equilibrium, assuming the Abyssal Hypothesis for intelligent behavior." and tosses it in disgust. More books are thrown. Slerdax calls for help; Gaxim, who does not like that no one believes him, stays away, and Ezmerelda mutters that all is not well. Marron calls out, "There are weird books." A sullen Gaxim responds with a broken two-word line about books being weird, and then comes, "I’m not an expert on books, but they shouldn’t be flying at you." Marron uses her divine powers and detects the poltergeist in the room. When she calls to Gaxim for help, he refuses; "The giant is unhappy that he was lied to and that nobody believes him". Meanwhile, Slerdax finds a scroll with the spell "wall of fire."

The elven rogue finds the north door locked as well and asks Ezmerelda to use knock. She is not happy about how things have been going: "I don’t like these deals." Marron suspects something and walks up to her: "what deals?" Ezmerelda realizes that "the Paladin who has taken over the Cleric’s body will not be as forgiving" and looks for someone to interrupt her. Wukong smiles, knowing she will either tell Marron and see a bloodbath or open the door, and insists, "what deals?" Gaxim is too annoyed; Slerdax "sees power and can’t resist." Ezmerelda growls, "perhaps we wish to go into the room to find what other evil we can destroy." Marron: "More evil to destroy."

The door opens on more amber sarcophagi. Gaxim wants to charge in and smash it all. Slerdax slips in but is surprised by a Slaad. Wukong is not, and must choose between the dark gifts and his ally; "Realizing that escape from Barovia is only possible if he is alive, he concludes. First, we must kill the Slaad." The Slaad is injured but still knocks Slerdax out. Seeing "a demon in front of her," Marron shouts a vow of enmity and attacks. A line follows: "Monsieur Gaxim, evil has taken hold of our group. And I do not trust Monsieur Wukong, and the actions of Slerdax are curious, but a demon is a demon and must be killed." The barbarian nods and recklessly attacks; Ezmerelda, "seeing Monsieur Gaxim attack," does the same with her rapier and handaxe. The Slaad casts a poisonous cloud and regenerates, "But it is too late as the unholy alliance of Wukong and Marron destroy the monster."

Ezmerelda sees that Wukong wants to reach a sarcophagus and attempts to restrain him, but fails. Gaxim destroys the one closest to Wukong along the north wall, "with three blows causing 80 points." Wukong, "seeing the enraged Half-Orc barbarian, simply adjusts his plan," somersaulting over Gaxim’s outstretched hands to land on the sarcophagus against the western wall, and goes into a trance. Marron, watching, realizes that "some kind of powerful evil force offers deals of power to people who are willing to accept the consequences." Outraged, she declares, "no one will touch the sarcophagus again, or they will die. There will be no more deals with evil!" The friendly giant is tempted to rush and hug her: "I told you!!! But you didn’t believe me."

Marron turns to Kasimir: "why should not I destroy them all?" The reply: "Because these are the repositories of the dark powers that gave Strahd his dark gift. We must find the one that my sister used and the one that offered the gift to Strahd." "What dark gift?" "His vampirism is a dark gift! Surely you have heard of this?" Everyone looks at Kasimir: "Nope." "Uh-uh." "I mean, curse, sure." Ezmerelda sighs. Then: "The vampires of Barovia refer to their condition as a ‘dark gift.’ The rest of us refer to it as evil." The heroes figure out that the best way forward is to have "the incorruptible Marron test each one and see what the creature has to offer." Wukong is disappointed but goes along with the plan.
