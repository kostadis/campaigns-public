# Chapter 40: The Battle of the tower

Date: Days 26–27 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
The chapter opens mid-battle on the tower stairs. A sunbeam ray has burned the green and blue pantaloon wearing vampires; the one attacking KP, who wears a leather jerkin, and the vampire leader are bathed in sunlight and cannot regenerate. Slerdax blasts several of the floating halberds with a lightning bolt and sears three of the vampires. The vampire with the leather jerkin, enraged at the sunlight, claws at KP and breaks his concentration, and Merrin charges up the stairs.

The vampire leader grapples Slerdax and throws them both over the void, reasoning that Slerdax would fall and die or be injured while his own regeneration would let him survive and "kill the meddling mage." The narration answers: "But fate has two words for this creature of the night, “feather fall.”" With the sunlight gone, the vampire with the green pantaloons attacks Wukong, but does not try to grapple him, because Strahd has informed his minions that "the great Wukong has made pacts with powers." The creature claws at the party's rogue, and "The rogue smirks and kills the Vampire next to Slerdax." Gaxim, surrounded by halberds, sees Slerdax falling and throws one of his javelins of lightning at the vampire; the blast "lights up the tower in a blue glow" and the vampire dies. Seeing its leader die, the vampire with the leather jerkin grabs Slerdax and tries to toss him off the tower, but Slerdax escapes. The floating halberds swing at the heroes and only KP is injured, while Ezmerelda runs up the tower to 70’.

KP clicks his fingers and a blast of fire erupts over his head, engulfing every creature around his allies; the magical halberds "lose what animating force they had and come crashing to the ground." Just then three vampires that had been stealthily climbing the tower with their spider climb ability surround Ms. d'Avenir. Slerdax runs down and is about to cast Dawn when Ezmerelda shouts, “be aware, Monsieur Slerdax, that Strahd can appear through any room. We must be careful.” Somewhere in Castle Ravenloft, Strahd rages, “I defeated you once, mage, and sent you into madness. I will defeat you again!”

Slerdax casts thunderwave instead, knocking a vampire in a pink sash off the stairs. Three lines follow without named speakers: “You have saved my life Monsieur Slerdax!” — “There are 5 more.” — “Only two attacking me, and I have dealt with such odds before.” The vampire with the jerkin grabs KP and jumps with him over the wall, but KP also has feather fall — "Sadly, that doesn’t stop him from drinking his blood." Merrin rushes up to flank a vampire. The green pantalooned vampire rakes Wukong, and Wukong, "using his dark power imbued strength," grapples the vampire spawn and throws it down the 190’ tower to its death. Inspired, Gaxim grapples the purple pantalooned vampire and throws it to its death: "The sickening thud was replaced with a pleasant thud."

Lower down, where Merrin and Ezmerelda face three vampires, one tries to grapple Merrin and fails, and Ezmerelda shoves a vampire off the stairs. KP, floating down some 60’ from Merrin, Slerdax and Ezmerelda at 70’, misty steps about 30’ away; the vampire that had been clutching him falls but is still alive. KP says, “Hm. I can take care of that.” His magic missiles kill it, and a few more darts strike the vampires facing Ez, Slerdax and Merrin. Slerdax burns the vampire still on the stairwell with a chromatic orb for 17 points of acid damage. Merrin heals herself and her friends, and Wukong strikes with his bow. Gaxim jumps across the chasm, meaning to feather fall down to his friends and strike the vampires, but "just barely misses grabbing hold and keeps falling."

The two vampires that had fallen climb back up, one at 60’ and the other at 130’. The one still within reach of Merrin shoves her off the stairs and she falls 60’. KP kills that vampire and strikes the other two. The Dark Powers grumble, “I thought we were going to see some 13th-dimensional chess moves by KP. That was why he saved him. This is boooooooooooring.” Slerdax, apparently listening, uses a thunderwave to throw an approaching vampire off the tower stairs. Merrin cures her wounds, Wukong strikes with his bow, and Gaxim "reaches and grabs and lands near our heroes," then hits a vampire with the light sword for 50 HP of damage. Ezmerelda does 10 HP with a magic missile. Then, "Perhaps it was the grumbling of the Dark Powers," KP sculpts a thunderwave around his allies, deals 16 HP of damage, and throws the climbing and nearby vampires down the tower.

With the vampires dead, there is "the small matter of the bodies." Ezmerelda tests the heroes: “they are spawn; they do not need to be burned.” Gaxim will have none of it, recalling how one vampire spawn returned from the dead. She smiles, scribbles “Vampire Spawn Merit Badge,” on a gold badge, and pins it on the barbarian.

With the battle over and the heart destroyed, the heroes climb on (K20). The stairs end in a dark and dreary room (K60) with manacles on the walls, a wood-framed bed fitted with leather restraints, and a closed iron chest at the foot of the bed, "its lid sculpted with an emblem." A wooden ladder rises to a trapdoor whose rotting wood drips water into a puddle at its base. Wukong cracks his knuckles and opens the chest, finding a bejeweled crown that Slerdax insists on wearing. The heroes climb the last stretch to the top of North Tower and see Ravenloft below them. As they climb into Mordenkainen’s Mansion (k60), Strahd growls, “I SHOULD HAVE KILLED YOU, KP!”

After a long rest, the heroes check outside the door to make sure Strahd has not set a trap. Wukong and KP fly the broomstick to the top of the high tower. As KP grumbles about the lack of windows, the Docent appears as a ghost and explains the glass tax: “The Construction of Castle Ravenloft was done during an era of the glass tax, that limited the number of windows that could be built. Count von Strahd spared no expense, which explains why there are so many windows, but even he had limits.” Someone answers, “I thought it was because Vampires don’t like windows.” The Docent replies that this is "a common misconception about the Castle," that the castle "was built before Strahd made his pact with the Vampyr," and adds, "But you – it would seem – knew that." Then it disappears. Landing on the High Tower and seeing nothing likely to spark an attack, KP uses the broom to ferry everyone else over from the North Tower.
