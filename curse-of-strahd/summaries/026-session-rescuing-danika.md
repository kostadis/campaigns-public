# Chapter 26: Rescuing Danika

Date: Days 15–16 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
The chapter opens on the wider board. "The light of Argynvostholt shines." In her encampment near the Tser Pool, Madam Eva smiles. Strahd's boredom is at an end: "So many fools," he thinks, "have come to Barovia, and so many died. Perhaps these are worthy adversaries?" The Dark Powers hold an emergency meeting because their plots and plans are at risk, and the committee on Strahd's imprisonment and torment is asked for an emergency update on their OKR's. Its report: "Revenge," the committee reports, "these heroes seek Revenge. Revenge is something we can work with." And the Dark Powers rest.

The adventurers stare up at the brightened sky after a great victory. Gaxim starts walking towards Vallaki and shouts out, "raise dead lady!" A few hours later, at the western entrance to Vallaki, an annoyed guard refuses them entry: "As his lordship, Lord Martikov, made clear, you are not permitted to enter the city." Then: "Raise dead lady." The guard, momentarily confused, has a lightbulb moment: "Oh yes, her ladyship is dead, and you promised to raise her! Fantastic, wait here!" A hearse pulls up, and Lord Martikov steps forward: "My friends, and allies, this is a great boon you have promised me." Two guards extract a 250-pound dead body in a shroud from the hearse. "My beloved!" Gaxim pouts at the hearse, "Me want horse!", but Lord Urwin Martikov apologises that he needs it: "With Strahd active and his threats, I must ensure that we have the proper infrastructure to deal with the expected increase in deaths. I am terribly sorry."

A few minutes after the Luna River Crossroads, the weather changes. The wind picks up in a curious and ominous way, and cloud cover appears out of nowhere, blotting the sun. "The warlock," mutter the heroes. An enormous evil-looking bat floats in front of them and transforms into Count von Zarovich. "No," he says, "not a warlock." Strahd declares that Danika is to remain dead: "Lord Urwin betrayed his lawful liege when he allowed Ireena to escape. For that, he must be punished, and so his wife must die." The heroes remember stories from the Wild Coast about petty kingdoms and lords at the border of the orc and non-orc empires, whose notions of honor and vengeance "could only be addressed with blood."

Slerdax stares at the clouds, and Ezmerelda, realizing what he is thinking, warns him: "Monsieur Slerdax, a fireball will not destroy the clouds. These are magique and part of Le Vampire’s powerz." Gaxim stares at Strahd: "No." Disappointed, the Count summons several vampire spawn that surround the heroes, who know that "in Barovia, survival is paramount to victory."

Wukong dashes past Strahd, who lets him go: "My friend Wukong, I expected no less from you. You left the best," he says, pointing at the rest of the party, "behind." The vampire spawn rush Gaxim. He dodges four of them, but the fifth grabs hold and the sixth bites him for 17 HP. Strahd, saying to himself "The idiot will be easy to control," tries to charm Gaxim, who resists again. For the first time there is doubt in Strahd's head, and at the same time he feels alive; the ennui dissipates. "The sword thinks, “2-0”." Gaxim growls, "I good! You bad! I no do bad!" Strahd screams, "Warlock, you disappoint me! You told me barbarian was a fool."

Ezmerelda runs past Strahd. Slerdax runs up to Gaxim, who shouts, "Slerdax Brave!", and before anyone can react the magic-user touches Gaxim and dimension doors him outside the cloud cover. "You will pay for this," Strahd seethes. Slerdax and Gaxim keep running towards Krezk. Merrin pulls out her symbol of Heironeous and turns all but one of the undead, then runs. Wukong, realizing Gaxim is gone, stares at the two women and runs toward Gaxim and Slerdax; Strahd smiles and salutes him. The last vampire spawn fails to grapple Merrin.

Strahd turns on Ezmerelda: "They are not your wolf pack, I am!" He fails to hold her, and as she breaks free she utters one word, "No." She runs, but Strahd keeps up with her. Merrin runs past the snarling spawn, catches up to Ezmerelda, and casts freedom of movement on her; the spawn grabs hold of Merrin and bites her for 13 HP. "The child of Rudolph," growls Strahd, "or the cleric who stands between her and my revenge?" Merrin is already trapped, so he tries to bite the aasimar. Ezmerelda thinks, "I can run! I can escape. But my wolf-pack. Damn it!" She turns, touches Merrin to impart her protection from good, and shouts, "Merrin, I stand with you!" The vampire spawn is banished, and Strahd stares at the Vistani and the Cleric, shocked. Merrin runs.

Wukong, about 100 feet away and inspired by Ezmerelda's heroism, thinks, "yes, I can help from here." His arrow pierces Strahd for 13 HP, but it pops out and drops to the ground "as if the attack never happened." Strahd shouts, "Wukong, we could have been friends. I have a place for you in my kingdom." The two women run past the Count's grasp towards Wukong; when they catch up to the elf, he casts a fireball. Seeing that everyone is still alive, Strahd turns into a bat and heads to his castle, thinking about what he must do to defeat these heroes. "We are heroes! We have defeated Le Vampire three times!"

Meanwhile Gaxim and Slerdax reach Krezk and the Abbey, where two mongrelfolk greet them with surprise and usher them to the Abbot. The Abbot is perplexed and about to refuse until he sees Wukong: "How interesting," he says in a distracted ethereal voice, "you wish to save this woman who is a nobody. How odd." Danika is amongst the living. Then: "Only one more, Wukong. Be mindful of giving this boon to others." From the Abbey the heroes go to Dmitri's house, and he reminds them that to stay in Krezk they must do chores.

The morning hike to Vallaki on the Old Svalich road is tedious, under a cloudless sky. The Warlock appears in the middle of the road. Gaxim walks up to him: "Strahd boss! Go away or Die!" Merrin adds, "or work for us? We have this city that needs a mayor." The Warlock laughs: "I have three options for you. You can hand over the woman. You can walk away from here without her. You can all die." Slerdax casts slow, but the Warlock is unaffected. Danika turns into a raven and flies away. "No!" screams the pawn of Strahd, casting finger of death; the genasi magic-user counters it, and the Warlock thinks that Strahd was right, and that the Dark Powers are playing some exciting games.

The fight follows. Wukong hits the Warlock with another normal arrow. The barbarian rages and slashes him for 39. The Vistani curses the Warlock to be vulnerable to radiant damage, and he snarls, "I am cursed beyond your puny witch’s curse!" Gaxim shouts "big boom attack!"; the reply comes, "It lack style, Monsieur Gaxim." He insists, "Big boom attack!", and is told, "Fine, I will do that next time." Marron strikes twice with the blood spear, and Slerdax's chromatic orb flies past its target. The Warlock heals himself and casts finger of death on Gaxim, who is hurt but not dead. Gaxim strikes back with his sword after extracting a promise from Ezmerelda about casting a "big boom attack." Ezmerelda, with a lightning bolt, and Marron finish the Warlock off. Slerdax uncovers a necklace of fireballs on the body.

"Messieurs, would you like to come to my tower?" The party turns to her: "YOUR TOWER? YOU HAVE A TOWER?" "Oui," she says, acting upset: "I told you that I saw you from my tower! But always here, and there we must go." On the way they agree their next stop will be Barovia, and she is delighted: "Excellent, then we can take my wagon!" When Slerdax asks what the tower is, she answers, "Well, it’s not my tower; it’s Van Richten’s." Told "He’s an ally of ours; you would like him.", she grimaces.

At the tower entrance stands Ezmerelda's wagon: fresh purple paint under layers of mud, gold-trimmed wheels, a brass lantern at each corner, red drapes over a tombstone-shaped window on each side, and a steel padlock on the back door hung with a cheap wooden sign reading "Keep out!" "This is my wagon!" Merrin asks where the horses are; Ezmerelda answers, "I had a horse, but you forced me to return it to the Vistani allies of Strahd." Slerdax asks if there is any treasure inside; she scowls, "no, but I have this note about your ally."

The note makes the heroes realize that Van Richten is a monster. Then, with no speaker named: "I thought I was working for a hero; instead, I was working for a monster that killed monsters. He will kill anything and anyone that crosses his path. He is without pity and without remorse." The speaker says that after abandoning everything to be with him, he declared them unworthy and abandoned them: "But I found him. And I, not he, will kill Strahd." Gaxim says, "we group!" and Ezmerelda answers, "I was a lone wolf, but I have now found my wolf pack! Come let us howl together." Wukong is too cool for that and Marron is confused, but Slerdax and Gaxim join in.

Asked about treasure in the tower, Ezmerelda admits she never went in: she was about to when she saw the party heading towards Argynvostholt and followed them. She came "Because I wanted to find out what Rudolph knew about Strahd. He tells me nothing." She climbs into the wagon: "let’s go kill your Morgantha, Monsieur Gaxim." The mage asks, "can we just admit that the wagon is useless?" Ezmerelda sheepishly explains, "I haven’t had a Wolf pack in a long time, and I wanted you guys to see my cool wagon." Gaxim smiles: "Save children, see wagon?" Merrin wonders, "if this is a place of residence, can The Vampire enter it?" — "Not sure, never tested that." Everyone gets the distinct impression that Rudolph (aka Rictavio) didn't reveal the real reasons he abandoned her. The heroes head to Vallaki.
