# Chapter 42: The search for Sergei’s tomb

Date: Day 27 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
The chapter opens with the gentle clanking of metal boots on stone rapidly approaching the heroes, and everyone rushes into Ireena’s tomb. KP reaches for his gem of true seeing and remembers that Strahd took it. Outside, a voice orders, "They have hidden in Ireena’s tomb; seal them in." The door is shut with planks and nails, and another voice adds, "The master will be pleased."

KP is really annoyed that Strahd and his creatures know what the party is doing at any time. He ponders and remembers that Strahd has the power to scry anyone who eats at his table, and asks, "Did any of you eat anything in his castle?" When everyone stares at him confused, he grumbles, "fools! He knows everything that we are doing! Why would you eat anything from his dinner plates!" Slerdax, who always wanted to be a famous chef, remarks, "It was really good," and the rest of the group notes that the Champagne was remarkably tasty: "There are simply no more bottles of that particular vintage." While they are being sealed in, the group debates different strategies to deal with divination magic. Someone says, "We should probably do something about them." Gaxim nods in agreement with the Genasi: too much talking, not enough destruction of evil.

Slerdax uses mold earth to go through the wall. KP is surprised; the spell is not supposed to work like that, and something is very odd about Slerdax. The party rushes from the tomb and surprises a group of wight carpenters under the direction of a tall, decrepit figure clad in splint armor. Ezmerelda ponders what KP would do, shrugs, and charges in with her rapier and hand ax. Slerdax also surprises the bad guys and casts a fireball. Seeing the power of magic, the Vistani vampire hunter steps back and casts a lightning bolt, killing two carpenters. Using his magic sword, the half-orc kills a wight and wounds the blackguard. KP casts a fireball and kills the other wight, and then the other mage finishes off the blackguard for good. Gaxim smiles: "we powerful! We good team!" The group revels in their new power with their wizard friend — although KP would remind everyone that his friends do not call him KP.

Then Wukong notices that the roof is moving, and bats descend on the magic users, surrounding them. A thunder wave from Slerdax pushes the swarms on him 10 feet away, and as they are moved away he waves his staff at them and hits several. Wukong destroys a swarm with a single arrow — "all about efficiency." Merrin uses her spear and more bats die. Ezmerelda's rapier destroys a swarm, and then her ax is flung at the multitude surrounding KP. The only remaining bats are around the former gnomish librarian, nibbling at him; KP responds with a snowball storm. The bats keep nibbling, and when the other mage tries to help "the greatest wizard of any age ™", his spell misses the target. Wukong's bow destroys another swarm, and Merrin finishes the last one. The bats, the chapter notes, will make exploration more complicated and area-of-effect spells less effective.

"Now, let’s go find Sergei’s tomb." The party begins working through the crypts. The first is Artimus (Builder of the Keep), whose epitaph reads "Thou standest amidst the monument to his life." The docent, a ghost, appears: "Yes, he was a fine gentleman. I’m so glad you are showing an interest in the historical nature of the castle!" Noticing that the party is not actually interested, it sighs, "well, phooey." Someone asks whether this is "one of those: kill the architect after he builds the building so no one can learn its secrets?", and the docent answers, "Why, yes, that’s exactly how it worked!" The party decides to take a bone in case they ever need to commune with Artimus to learn Ravenloft’s secrets. Inside, though, a skeleton draped in rags lies atop a marble slab with no signs of violence, dead for centuries: "The docent lied."

Slerdax looks over the room full of crypts: "this is going to take a long time." The docent reappears to explain that "our Count collects crypts that he likes to walk around as a reminder of happier days." At the crypt of Sir Erik Vonderbucks (22), someone doubts there is much upside in examining every crypt, but Wukong opens the door, having heard from a guy he knows that "this Erik was a dying noble who wanted his corpse dipped in molten gold." Gaxim's sword mumbles, "good luck spending the gold." Wukong replies, "I intend to survive and be very wealthy." Gaxim rolls his eyes — "nothing to buy!" — and KP launches into a lecture on inter-planar economics and "D&D-Bay, an emporium that connected collectors and sellers of worthless curios", on which he was planning to write a treatise. Everyone stares at KP, who did not quite understand what they had been trying to tell Wukong.

At the crypt of Sasha Ivliskova—Wife (22), with no recollection of treasure, the party moves on. Gaxim praises Wukong as a great warrior who plans for his death and is not afraid of danger: "Wukong, brave." KP whispers, "You can come anytime you want, but never leave." The elf ignores the taunting and opens a crypt, releasing a horrid stench of decay and revealing a corpse that looks like Wukong. Thinking it might be a future Wukong with some treasure present Wukong does not have, he touches the skeleton; it disappears, as does the inscription.

Slerdax is intrigued by the crypt of Bascal Ofenheiss—Chef Deluxe, but there are too many crypts to explore. The party skips an open crypt (27). Claws drop on them from the roof, and Slerdax screams, "OH, THIS IS SO COOL! It’s just like EVIL DEAD 2!" and "I LOVED THAT BOOK!" KP shouts, "Me too!" and Gaxim mumbles, "See play in Fax." Slerdax casts thunderclap and destroys them.

At the crypt of Intree Sik-Valoo — "He spurned wealth for the knowledge he could take to heaven" — the gnome walks in saying "Knowledge." A skeleton lies on the slab, its bones dusty and neglected but its skull well polished. The skeleton coughs. Asked "Do you have a cold?", it answers, "Of course, I don’t! That’s your first question." The party thinks they may come back later. They don't.

The search continues. Of Stefan Gregorovich, First Counselor to King Barov von Zarovich (24), KP declares, "Per my research, in life, Stefan was neither observant nor well informed" — without revealing that his research consisted of listening to Stefan and losing portions of his army to the useless advice. Ivan Ivliskovich, Champion of Winter Dog Racing (24), gets a "Pass." At the crypt of Prefect Ciril Romulich (30), Merrin urges everyone to move on from these meddlesome priests who want to take her temples. At the crypt marked "We knew him only by his wealth (31)", Wukong almost falls into the trap, but Slerdax's wand of traps saves him. The door of crypt 32 has no name or epitaph; Gaxim asks "What say?" and is reassured it is not Sergei's tomb. At Sir Klutz Tripalotsky (33), who "fell on his own sword", Wukong — tired of being the one who always misses at critical junctures — thinks, "Perhaps we should add him to the party."

At King Dostron the Hellborn (34), a flying broom attacks while the heroes stare at the epitaph. Slerdax turns to the great one: "oh great mentor, what should I do?" The great mage groans. Wukong destroys the broom and tells the gnome, "you owe us." The reply: "Oh, shall I submit your bill?" "No."

Beucephalus, the Wonder Horse (39), is passed over. At Tatsaul Eris—Last of the Line (40), four wights attack, surprising the heroes and screaming, "the master will have his revenge." Someone warns, "Ze vampyr is bringing all of his monsters to attack! We must be careful!" The heroes destroy the wights without breaking a sweat — "Perhaps zay should be careful."

KP knows things about General Kroval “Mad Dog” Grislek (Master of the Hunt): a well-known master of the hunt, a member of the court, honored with a permanent burial crypt in the catacombs by one of the Lords of Barovia. At Gralmore Nimblenobs—Wizard Ordinaire (37), Ezmerelda exclaims, "Ordinary, pshaw!" and "we have extraordinary!" Claw marks obliterate the name on crypt 36, and even KP's travels give him no specific arcane knowledge of it. Slerdax explores it, finds another skeleton in rags on a marble slab, and uses his wand of secrets: no traps and nothing of value.

The heroes reach a portcullis (86). Gaxim approaches it, but Slerdax says, "let’s explore everything else." The chapter adds: "Later on, we would learn that it was Strahd’s tomb." Slerdax continues to look at each crypt. King Troisky—The Three-Faced King (12) earns a "Boring."

At King Katsky (Katsky the Bright): Ruler, inventor, and self-proclaimed time traveler (13), KP recalls a guy in Vallaki who knew something about this King: a famous inventor of the Barovian dynasty who tinkered with technologies and magic in ways lost to time, and who claimed to travel in time, though many felt that was merely him saying things. The contact added, "Although he did have a curious knack of knowing what was going to happen except when he died." The gnome, who has traveled between dimensions but never along the 4th, goes in. Amid the bones lie a stoppered drinking horn, a fat pouch, and a weird-looking scepter of metal and wood; above, hanging from the domed ceiling by wires, is a wooden flying contraption like a set of folding dragon wings. KP investigates: "Aha! - The scepter is a device that fires silver pellets. And the flying apparatus works! He is indeed a great inventor."

The search runs on: Stahbal Indi-Bhak (14) gets a "Blech", and Khazan (16) a "Not Sergei, skip!" The chapter ends at the crypt of Sir Sedrik Spinwitovich (Admiral Spinwitovich) (17), who "built the greatest naval force ever assembled in a landlocked country".
