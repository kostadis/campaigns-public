# Chapter 45: Encounters with Strahd.

Date: Days 27–28 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
The chapter opens in the aftermath of a desecration. After the heroes defile the tomb of Strahd’s ancestors, several zombies attack and are defeated, but the victory has a cost: "Unfortunately, one of our cosplayers dies. Taken by a Vampire." The surviving cosplayers remain anxious. The heroes do not waver — "No matter, the defeat of Strahd remains the only goal." With the cosplayers staying close, they explore the crypt to the south and discover Strahd’s tomb.

From the earth, three of Strahd’s wives emerge. Wukong is unimpressed with their choice of wedding dress. Slerdax uses a Dawn spell, and the battle seems well in hand until the Banshee shows up. "But she’s dead," the heroes think. Her wail pierces the din of the battle: "You thought you could kill me? You thought ending my suffering was so easy? FOOLS!" KP is knocked unconscious, and so is Wukong. The cosplayers are dead, destroyed by the Banshee; the chapter notes that "Tomorrow our heroes can see them in the march up to Castle Ravenloft." Slerdax, though, is unaffected, and his concentration is not broken.

The heroes heal KP. He recalls that this was how Strahd had defeated him last time: Strahd would send minions, and when KP was wounded or unconscious, he would come for the kill. KP also ruminates that the master plan of bringing an army of creatures that can be turned into undead "may not have been his most elegant 15th-dimensional chess move." He works out that the key to dealing with the Banshee is a dark power locked in amber, and learns, to his annoyance, that the dark force is no more.

Realizing that their time is limited, the heroes kill the last of Strahd’s wives and teleport to Barovia, where they rest and prepare for another assault. In the last room in the catacombs they find the last artifact, the Tome of Strahd. It proves "a bit of a letdown, full of interesting trivia," not the big piece of information that Madam Eva made it sound like — "Except for the detail about the stake. Our heroes missed that detail."

With all the artifacts in hand, the heroes head back to Barovia to plan their final assault. The question is how to kill Strahd, "And who better to ask than the great vampire-hunter himself, van Richten." They teleport to the Blue Water Inn in Vallaki. Before they can ask van Richten, "a customer of the Tavern" asks where they were. Rather than keep silent, they declare that they have entered Castle Ravenloft. Someone asks whether they have seen the cosplayers, and KP responds: "I have not seen a young man named Alexa and his three friends dressed as a wizard, a fighter, and a cleric." The group explains the circumstances of the death, and the town argues about "the perilousness of certain coming-of-age activities."

As the conversation dies down, the heroes approach van Richten. He explains that the only way to kill Strahd is in his coffin after being reduced to mist in combat. Pressed to join them, he refuses; pressed further, he admits that a Vistani curse makes it impossible, and that if he allied himself with the heroes they would be placed in extreme peril. Ezmerelda is touched: "You could have told me, you old fool." The reconciliation that they both want happens at last.

Just then Kasimir Velikov marches in and explains how his sister was killed by the heroes, who had promised that she would be saved. It takes a few moments for folks to realize she is the Banshee the group has been fighting. Gaxim grunts something unintelligible and marches out; Kasimir attempts to curse him and fails.

With that final piece of information, the heroes plan their attack on Strahd in the place Madam Eva foretold they would find him alone: on the parapet. They will teleport to the top of the castle, the magic-user will cast dimension door to bring the fighters next to Strahd, and simultaneously Wukong and Ezmerelda will swoop in on a broomstick. "The plan goes well. Except, despite the use of foresight, Wukong misses." Because he misses, Strahd can escape — he can move without exposing himself to attacks of opportunity, and he can move through the castle walls as if they were air. The chapter ends as "Strahd sinks into the ground beneath the party."
