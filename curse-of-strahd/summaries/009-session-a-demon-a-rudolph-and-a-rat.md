# Chapter 9: A Demon, a Rudolph and a Rat

Date: Day 8 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
The chapter opens with the Dark Powers watching the battle in the orphanage attic and pondering whether these heroes are "genuinely involved in the combat, or are they merely virtually there, participating in a theater performance, where everyone has his turn".

The wounded Demon dissipates into Felix. The boy scowls, puts a knife to his own throat and threatens, "move away, or I will kill the boy." Wukong, having observed that Slerdax gained power when he performed an evil act, asks the gods of the fleur-de-lys for advice; they do not respond. "His moral compass is broken." He shoots his arrow, and the boy dies. The dark powers smile. Wukong feels a power course through his body: he grows fangs, his hands grow claws, and his breath stinks. He knows that pursuing this course of evil would increase his power, and the price "would be – interesting."

Slerdax saves the boy by applying bandages. The conscious boy stares at Gaxim, who is confused. Marron urges Gaxim to grab the boy so they can take him to Father Lucian, but the grapple fails as the boy dodges, and when Marron tries, she "trips over his feet". The possessed boy backs off: "back off, or I will kill him?" Wukong suggests he could kill the boy again but is talked into grabbing him instead, and, "demonstrating his street smarts", holds the boy.

Slerdax then grabs the locket and examines it, and the Demon sees the locket and possesses Slerdax. Wukong screams at Gaxim, "Grab the magic user!" Demon/Slerdax is immobilized, and Marron silences him. The Demon emerges from Slerdax's body, which slumps to the ground, floats just outside the range of the silence spell and growls, "it would appear that we have a stalemate." Marron, staring at the locket, realizes that this kind of Demon doesn't want the host body to die. The Demon offers, "Perhaps we can make a deal?", but the Paladin shakes her head: there will be no deal. The Demon suddenly disappears into Slerdax, who fumbles with the locket, opens it and lets the Demon enter. Slerdax is freed, the Demon is trapped — and the locket cannot be opened anymore.

Mme. Belasco makes it up into the attic, sees Felix bleeding and starts screaming about "horrible sounds: of battle, of children screaming in pain and some horrible scary, disturbing voice", then turns on Wukong: "you monster!" Gaxim is done — "He raged, he missed, and he fumbled" — and shrugs and starts to leave. Marron, an avenging Paladin, will not have the matron think she was party to some evil plot or working with the Demon, and with all her persuasive powers she convinces Mme. Belasco that the heroes are heroes and that Felix is free of demonic possession. "Oh, Angel! You saved this child! And perhaps, Milivoj!" Felix explains that everything was okay until he touched the locket; after that he was a spectator in his own mind while his body killed his parents and his siblings and burned down his house.

The heroes return to the room where the caretaker was lying ill. He is no longer deathly ill but unconscious, and will need several hours to recover, "perhaps even till tomorrow morning." When Slerdax wonders how to carry the locket safely, Mme. Belasco answers, "no problem, dear. Everyone in Barovia has a special box to carry cursed curios," and returns with a lead-lined box and a particular set of tongs. She thanks them and offers them potions of healing. Gaxim asks "treasure?"; she reminds him they are a poor orphanage with no gold. Slerdax tries to argue that four heroes should get four potions of healing, and Marron kicks him.

The positive energy turns Marron back into the cleric, Merrin, who has no recollection of what just happened and announces, "we have come here to bless your children." Mme. Belasco asks, "what kind of magic is this? Is she some kind of Demon?" — "No! I am from Celestia."

Outside, Ernst Larnak, Lady Wachter's henchman, greets them: "Seems you took care of some business? Lady Fiona wants to meet you for dinner?" Slerdax asks "What's for dinner?" and Ernst laughs, "Not you!" The group huddles. They don't like her lawbreaking and suspect she hates Izek, but they acknowledge she offers an alternative to the Baron: where he intends "a totalitarian state, where everyone is forced to smile", she believes Strahd isn't that bad and that people should learn to live with him; to her, lockdown, weekly festivals and mandatory happiness are radical measures with no proof that they work. Merrin replies, "we will pray first and then come for dinner." Ernst, walking away, says slyly, "before you decide you want to be friendsies with the Baron and Izek, you might want to check out who is placed in the stocks." He is gone before they can ask. Someone cries, "My Brother!"

At Father Lucian's, the priest exclaims, "Mon Dieu! What has happened to Milivoj?" Merrin explains that a demon possessed a child and harmed Milivoj, and shows the box. Realizing it is a cursed curio box, Father Lucian screams, "You brought it here?" and then "ARE YOU INSANE?" He says he may have friends who can help, and sends them to the Blue Water Inn to find Danika's husband Urwin: "Tell him that Father Lucian sent you."

At the Blue Water Inn someone calls out, "Rictavio, I told you they would come back! Where is Izek?" Danika, behind the bar, buys Gaxim drinks for him and the Wachter brothers and tells the rest of the party, "I have terrible news!" When they announce "We found an evil artifact!", she rolls her eyes: "This is Barovia! You throw a rock, and the rock turns into an evil artifact while it's in mid-air." The bard suggests, "you should ask the Vampire killer about these artifacts." Urwin — a middle-aged, dark-featured, heavyset man in traditional Barovian garb — comes from the kitchen and takes them to his office, sends out his two children, and draws the curtains. The heroes grab their swords.

"I am Urwin Martikov, and I am a high-ranking member of the Keepers of the Feather!" When no one asks, he explains anyway: "The Keepers of the Feather are wereravens. We are dedicated to the cause of Strahd's defeat and the freedom of Barovia." Gaxim notices that he is a good man hiding some terrible secret. Urwin reveals that Danika is one of them, as are the ravens around the inn and the ravens at the windmill that tried to warn the party off — "But you insisted! Like fools!" — and that "Gaxim's sleepy head is what saved your lives". He talks for a good 5 to 10 minutes before agreeing to take the locket: "We have a special vault that we store them." Wukong's eager interest in the vault draws a rebuke: "We can keep it hidden from you, thief." To Gaxim he says, "Your destiny is to defeat Strahd. Ours is to help you. You can not become one of us."

Urwin knows through his ravens that the party has seen Madam Eva, and offers to interpret their reading: "You are not the first to come through Barovia to try and kill Strahd." He points to "the March of the damned in the village of Barovia that leads up to Strahd's castle"; Gaxim perks up, "I walk with friends!" Urwin hopes he never does: "I wish to see Barovia free of Strahd!" He dismisses the Baron's "silly festivals" and says, "Strahd is no devil," then catches himself — "I have said too much." Slerdax reminds him of his offer to decode the prophecies; Urwin reads them and declares, "The man from the Carnival is Rictavio! Go talk to him!" — the bard at the bar, whom Danika has been steering them toward. Before they go he hints at "such a small family matter". In the bar, four strangers give the party a knowing look.

Rictavio's posture changes, revealing "a secret hidden power", though his very nasal voice "ruins the whole effect". He takes them to his "office" (a bedroom, as he concedes to Danika), and tells them, "You Gaxim and you aasimar I trust. These other two have been seduced by the dark powers." He reveals himself: "My name is Rudolph van Richten." He tells his story — a scholar and doctor from Darkon, married to Ingrid, father of Erasmus, whose son was made a vampire spawn by Baron Metus; he staked his son, the Baron killed his wife, and after destroying the Baron he "sought revenge against the Vistani" and took up hunting evil. He has waited a long time to kill "Strahd von Zarovich". Gaxim stands, "let us go now," but Rudolph says "it is not time" and that he does not work with others: "we are on the same journey but going on a different path." He is frank that if the party dies weakening Strahd, "that would not be so bad". Merrin is appalled; the rest of the group is cool.

Reading their fortunes, he says, "Ah, you need my prized possession, the Sunsword." His price: find out whether the Vistani outside the town "are friends of Strahd or friends of the Vistani". Asked how, he says, "Seriously? You are adventurers. Go and ask. Beat them up." Slerdax proposes "An illusion of Strahd?" — "See, you are capable."

Remembering Urwin's errand, the heroes return to the kitchen, where he whispers that he needs them to find out what happened to the wine that should have arrived from the winery. In the dining room Ernst sits with the Wachters, holding up a beer, and smiles at them. Danika warns them about the wizard the ravens saw them searching for at the waterfalls — Marron names him, "Knazaporium Knatterpox!" Danika calls him "The mad mage", seen north of the lake opening portals and cursing; even the fishermen avoid him now. "He has gone mad. He is dangerous. Do not go there." Wukong promises they won't, offering a contract in blood, and she cuts him off: "The Dark Powers may decide that you just did." Gaxim says his brother will protect them — "Izek is my brother." Danika tells him, "No, friendly giant. He lied to you." Izek wanted to get close to the group to get Ireena, whom he thinks is his sister; Ireena is now trapped in the Baron's mansion, and Ismark is in the stocks, to be killed tomorrow "for the crime of having kidnapped Ireena"; Ismark had tried to fight off the city guard and killed a few before being overwhelmed. Gaxim insists, "I will talk to him. He will listen." Danika shrugs and shakes her head.

Ernst returns, observing that they have learned "how the Baron backstabbed Ismark, took Ireena prisoner, and is generally untrustworthy", and suggests they meet his boss now. Marron: "It is dinner time."

At the Wachterhouse the party notices a basement. Slerdax convinces the group to cast find-familiar; Ernst goes inside believing they will follow shortly. Wukong tells Gaxim the mouse about to appear is Slerdax's pet, to avoid another incident, and Gaxim is pleased. As a rat, Slerdax finds a basement room full of skeletons that ignore him, a stairway up and a secret door. Beyond it, in a candlelit room with a large black pentagram, four robed figures sit in four of five chairs: "a young man who has the face of an angel; a balding hulk of a man; a squat, middle-aged woman; and a taller, younger woman with an unsettling glare." They discuss how, thanks to Lady Wachter and her promises of Demonic power, they will overthrow the worthless Baron. The young man says, "I look forward to killing Father Lucian. His church has protected the people of Vallaki from our Master Strahd for too long."
