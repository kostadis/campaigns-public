# Chapter 28: Tsolenka Pass

Date: Day 17 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
The chapter opens on Gaxim's private theory of Barovia. Some of the locals call the place "The Curse of Strahd," holding that Strahd himself has cursed it. Gaxim disagrees: "The real villain of Barovia isn’t Strahd, but Morgantha." In his reading, Morgantha uses children's bodies for her pastries to enslave the residents and get more children, and she is safe in this locked-up world because anyone powerful enough to threaten her learns that to be freed they must kill Strahd — "And they go and face The Devil, and die." She plagues Barovia because she gets to watch the would-be heroes who might defeat her parade through the streets as ghostly apparitions every night. "No, this isn’t “The Curse of Strahd”; this is “Morgantha’s Curse of Strahd.”" The narration then undercuts him: "Gaxim is done thinking. Or is it the sword?"

The heroes reach the Vistani encampment near Vallaki to find Kasimir, their guide to the Amber Temple. The Dusk Elf is "delighted to see them. Or is it surprising?" The light of the Argynvost has unsettled the Vistani: they know their patron has suffered a defeat, worry that Strahd will ask them to prove their loyalty, and fear fighting those with the power to defeat The Devil. Kasimir refuses to tell the heroes where the temple is, worried they will betray him. The sword, which sees the Dusk Elves as a meat shield to protect Gaxim, urges the Friendly Giant to encourage more of them to come along. Kasimir grows suspicious. Merrin tries to persuade him by suggesting the other Dusk Elves are required for a party to be thrown at the temple. Kasimir knows about the smiling city of Vallaki and the role the White Angel played in it, and he is about to pull out of the deal — until Gaxim offers some of his purple grapemash. Kasimir smiles in delight and asks his friends, "Come drink some wine." When the wine is drunk, he agrees to help.

The road to the Amber temple runs through the Luna River crossroads (P) and then the Raven River crossroads. Halfway to the Tsolenka Pass (T), Slerdax notices a curious encampment: a small camp in a clearing, flanked by huge rocks with rough palisades filling the gaps and ragged tents inside. Slerdax, "curious as always," decides to explore. The camp holds four undead: a male dwarf who is now a wight, a halfling revenant, a gnome ghost, and a veldaken wraith. The revenant screams at the dwarf, "Ulfrig, you really had to try and go kill Strahd?" Ulfrig answers angrily, "It was a group decision! We agreed that we either killed Strahd and escaped or spend the rest of our lives here!" The gnome, Belvenor Legrand, sighs that Strahd "had a funny way of making that rest of our lives, really, really, long," and the veldaken wraith, Ilyanvaz Theron, moans, "Surprisingly, the undead can survive for a very long time without feeding." The argument "appears to have been going on for centuries and probably will go on for centuries." Slerdax notices some magical trinkets among their items but is so engrossed in the conversation that he forgets to point them out.

Through Gaxim, the sword sums up the dead adventurers: "They faced Strahd. They failed to kill Strahd. Strahd wasn’t impressed." Instead of killing them, Strahd offered a third option — "He trapped them in Barovia for the rest of their lives, and that rest is eternity." Hidden, the heroes learn from the banter that Madam Eva brought these adventurers in, and that they found the sword but not the other artifacts or their ally before Strahd defeated them. The revenant screams, "I CAN’T KILL YOU! YOU CAN’T KILL ME! AND WE CAN’T GET PAST THAT LINE!" — and only then do the heroes notice the magical line that encircles the encampment. Gaxim observes that freeing them would be an evil act. Slerdax wonders aloud whether "This evil act might give us more powers?" The party decides not to take him up on it. "The Dark powers, however, noticed."

At Tsolenka Pass the mountain road narrows between icy cliffs and a sea of fog. Ahead stands a high wall of black stone lined with spikes and topped by statues of demonic vultures with horned heads, with a closed iron portcullis at its center and a curtain of green flame behind it; beyond the wall a white stone guard tower is topped by golden statues of mighty warriors. Gaxim "can not wait" and charges; within 10 feet the gate shrieks open, and he goes straight through the shimmering wall of green light. Kasimir declares, "This is a place of great evil. In we must go!"

The two creatures on the gate transform into vrocks, "demons of the abyss," and attack. Gaxim climbs the tower and recklessly slashes the vrock on the northern lookout for 54 points of damage. Slerdax considers a fireball, reconsiders, and casts tidal wave instead: the first vrock takes 10 points of damage and is knocked prone, while the one attacking Gaxim avoids most of the water and takes 5. Ezmerelda recalls that demons resist fire and lightning, considers climbing up to join Gaxim, and is urged by the group to use her spells; her magic missile does 6Hp. A vrock's stunning scream fails, and Gaxim slashes for 25 HP. Wukong hits, "but the standard weapon is only so effective," so the cleric casts Magic Weapon on him. The vrock on the southern tower flies off and shrieks; Merrin is the only one affected, and Marron tries and fails to seize control. Gaxim kills the vrock he was fighting with a flung hand-ax. A chromatic orb from "the magic-user" misses, and "The vistani vampire hunter sneers at Slerdax and casts a magic-missile that does 12 HP." Wukong misses even with a magical arrow. The surviving vrock claws and pecks the stunned Merrin for 48 points of damage; the cleric loses concentration, and again the Paladin fails to take over.

Gaxim leaps across to the southern tower and onto the vrock itself, striking it for 29 points of damage. "There over Tsolenka pass, the Dark Powers notice that a hero of good, armed with a sword of light, is doing battle with the demonic powers in the air." Ezmerelda's firebolt fizzles. Wukong's arrow pierces the demon for 12 HP. The vrock climbs some 60 feet and tosses Gaxim off; from the ground he dusts himself off, hits it with a javelin for 5, and picks up a hand-ax. The two spellcasters' magic missiles deal 32 points of damage, and Wukong, "with his final normal arrow, kills the beast."

The heroes climb over the wall to avoid the green barrier. Gaxim fails to open the tower door; Wukong cracks his knuckles and opens it. The ground floor has a cold hearth and a stone staircase. Gaxim charges up while the rest wait; the upper level is "an icebox" with a ladder to a trapdoor and a dire wolf's head above the hearth. Finding nothing of value, he goes up onto the battlements, where ten-foot-tall, gold-plated statues of female human knights holding lances stand over snow-covered skeletons in rusty mail. When Gaxim examines them as possible treasure, they come to life and scream, "Begone!" The rest of the party hears him and charges up; Ezmerelda thinks, "I will not abandon you, Gaxim, you are part of my wolf pack!" Gaxim kills three, the remaining snow maidens hit him twice, and he kills the remaining three. He picks up a finely crafted shield at their feet and walks back down to a party staring at him, "wondering what the hell happened." He tells them, "strange women," then shows off the rubies he found: "treasure!" Everyone looks at the magical shield in awe, and Kasimir says, "we must go!"

On the steep road beyond, a nine-foot-tall goat with gray fur stands on a crag, "malice glimmers in its eyes," and charges. Kasimir identifies it as "the evil goat Sangzor!" Wukong wounds it with a regular arrow, Merrin wonders about the morality of killing it, and the Dusk Elf shouts, "The pelt will make us famous! Kill the beast!" Slerdax kills the goat. Kasimir seizes it in delight — "this pelt will make me famous in all of Barovia!" — and the mage asks, "Why," then, "because you stood around while we killed it?" Kasimir answers, "That is not going to be my version of the story," and offers to make "Kasimir’s famous stew!" if they let him keep the pelt and tell his story. Asked "Here?", he replies, "I do not make stew in freezing cold of Tsolenka Pass!" and laughs heartily.

At the bridge on the eastern edge of the pass, stone arches thirty feet tall and thirty feet wide stand at each end, each topped by two statues of armored knights on horseback charging toward one another. The trap is so obvious that even Gaxim refuses to cross first; he is convinced the knights are alive. Slerdax proposes using mold earth to dig steps down and freezing a chunk of ice so they can swim across the river, and the party agrees. On the way down a great roc swoops in, "so monstrous that its wings blot out the sky." Gaxim throws his two hand-axes for 22 points of damage, Kasimir screams in terror, "the beast!", and the bird ignores "the tasty morsel of an elf" to seize "the imposing white aasimar." Slerdax's chromatic orb deals 19 points of damage, Wukong's normal arrow deals 20, Ezmerelda tries and fails to befriend the animal with a spell, and finally Merrin casts freedom of movement and escapes its clutches. Gaxim strikes with his sword of light for 54 points of damage. Everyone runs to the gate (T7), and the roc, seeing that the prey is no longer easy, flies away.
