# Chapter 24: Dinner at the Zarovich

Date: Day 14 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
The chapter opens with the party still scouting the Castle through Slerdax's spider. Ezmerelda fumes that no one ever talks to the expert: "The great sage Tomas Nichols is right." When the mage asks where she thinks they should go, her first answer is "Madam Eva? She knows much about the insides of this Castle." That deflates everyone. She then offers a real opinion: given the nature of vampires to live in the dark, the trophy room would be somewhere he could go whenever he wants, "so I urge you to go underground." Marron suggests "the Vampire Strahd may be trapped in his own Castle. Perhaps a frontal assault?" and Merrin screams, "that's the spirit!" The others point out that they have killed precisely zero vampires so far, and Ezmerelda fumes in her head, "YOU HAVE KILLED ZERO! Not I – the great Ezmerelda d'Avenir!"

The exploration continues. Slerdax, the Spider, crawls into the room beside the cake room (K43), a dark chamber whose centrepiece is an ornate iron tub with clawed feet, full of blood. Gaxim approves of the bathroom's proximity to the dining hall. Slerdax wonders, "Blood?", tempted by the academy's teachings to look closer, and Gaxim says, "Blood, bad!" Behind the curtains (K44) is a closet of black capes and formal wear; through its window Slerdax sees a walkway, and Wukong agrees it could serve as an entry point instead of the front door. Crossing the ceiling to the north (K42), Slerdax finds a sweet-smelling, candlelit bedroom with a large "Z." carved into the headboard and a young woman in a nightgown lying in the bed. He recognises her as Gertruda, the daughter of Mad Mary, whom Strahd stole. To the east is the Count's study, its hearth blazing and every surface in order, with a gargantuan painting over the mantelpiece that is an exact likeness of Ireena Kolyana.

Further on, Slerdax steps onto a great spiral staircase (K20), and a reddish light flares overhead: the tower, sixty feet wide at its base, rises to a large crystal heart pulsating with red light, the stairs continuing above it. The spell's range will not let the party go further up, so the spider exits north onto a ten-foot-wide walkway around the keep, in drizzling rain and thunder. "Weird," he thinks, "it wasn't raining earlier." He climbs the parapet and waves to his friends outside. West of the stair (K45) is a long, roofless hall of alcoves holding life-sized human statues. Slerdax notices that in the dark the statues' faces are stoic, and when the lightning is active they change to terror.

Back past the cake room (K36), through the door to the west (K35), some rats notice the spider and ignore it. Past a shining door of engraved steel flanked by two shadowed figures, a spiral staircase leads all the way down to the first floor, across from the room where they first met Strahd, and further still. The heroes wonder whether to go down, having already explored the crypts. Ezmerelda's needling ("Ah yes, your typical newbie vampire hunter who finds one room in one crypt and thinks he found everything.") irritates the sword ("Spider, no, go that far."), and when asked, "Have you considered going down these stairs?", Slerdax answers, annoyed, "yes." and continues down.

At the bottom is a dusty hallway (K61). Walking on the ceiling, Slerdax notices that ten feet in the hallway changes subtly: some kind of trap. To the north is the fog-filled servant's hall (K62), where a mongrelfolk shuffles down the corridor. To the northeast (K65) is a kitchen with a colossal pot bubbling over a fire pit and cooking implements that could double as implements of torture. Next to the kitchen are the butler's quarters, cluttered with thousands of pieces of junk, broken swords, crumpled shields and helmets, beneath a faded tapestry of Castle Ravenloft. The butler is there, caressing his treasures and mumbling, "and this belonged to that ranger the master killed, and this belonged to the rogue who threatened me, and this belonged to that strange wizard." He apparently collects the worthless equipment of the adventurers who make up the parade of the dead. Wukong exclaims, "the treasure room!"

To the west Slerdax enters the Hall of Bones (K67), an ossuary of bone furniture, bone-covered walls, garlands of skulls and a bone chandelier over a long bone table with ten bone chairs. Above the steel-banded double doors in the east wall is mounted the skull of a dragon. Strahd is there, sipping his drink. He smirks, "have you enjoyed your exploration of my house?", and asks whether they have come early for dinner to get a lay of the land. Noting "You can't talk through that creature," he gets up and muses, "Perhaps an early dinner is appropriate. What is it that Ezmerelda calls it? A souper?" Usually, he says, the magic-users try to attack him at this point or dispel their familiars. Slerdax decides to go for broke and spins words in spider silk: "we are locked outside." Strahd laughs, turns into a bat, and eats Spider Slerdax before Slerdax can do anything.

The heroes are delighted all the same: they have found the room of bones that Madam Eva said contained one of their artifacts, and the dragon skull. Slerdax proposes accepting the dinner invitation and having someone sneak out mid-dinner to steal the 250-pound skull and the artifact. Everyone likes the plan, except that the Sword of Sergei will probably seize control of Gaxim and send him on a murderous and suicidal rampage. Marron attempts to reason with it, and Gaxim speaks: "As long as you continue to accumulate power to kill Strahd, I will stay my hand."

Wukong is volunteered to sneak down during dinner, but the weight of the skull means someone must help the rogue; Wukong appeals to Gaxim, "Mon Ami!" Slerdax proposes to cast haste on Wukong, and the reply comes, "What a fantastic idea you have! Perhaps the first?" On distracting Strahd, Marron offers, "I could cast a consecrate spell?" Slerdax stares in disbelief: "Your plan is to start a battle with Strahd in his Castle?" He suggests something less extravagant, like dropping a bottle of fine wine on the tablecloth. Merrin proposes saying grace; Slerdax proposes appealing to Strahd's ego: "Why don't we ask him about his life? Offer to listen to his stories?"

Before the debate can rage, Rahadin, Strahd's chamberlain, arrives: "my master says you were looking for him in the Castle? I am so sorry that I didn't hear you knock." He clearly does not believe what Strahd told him. In the dining room Strahd stares at Ezmerelda, "Ah, Miss d'Avenir, it's still Miss? How is your mentor … Rudolph?" If looks could kill, Strahd would still be dead. Rahadin withdraws: "Dinner will be served shortly. Please enjoy the festivities." The plan: Wukong sits nearest the door, the party distracts Strahd, Slerdax casts haste, Wukong goes downstairs for the bones, and Gaxim follows him.

Slerdax opens: "So, tell us about the Castle. It's pretty amazing." Strahd swirls his wine and describes Castle Ravenloft as "an architectural masterpiece I created many years ago", built where a hamlet once stood, "A tribute to the family of von Zarovich and our impact on the land." He seems curiously delighted to talk about it. Wukong slips out, but Gaxim's attempt to get up is noticed: "Are you not interested in my story? I'm sure Sergei's sword is. Sit, my friend, sit."

Wukong goes on alone, down the stairs (K21) into the hallway (K61). He detects the trap too, finds no mechanism to deactivate it, and jumps over the trigger, some kind of pressure pad, noting that moving the dragon's head through here will be difficult. In the hallway (K62) the mongrelfolk, Cyrus Belview, notices him. Upstairs, Strahd tells Gaxim, "I thought you were the honorable one." Gaxim: "Sword want to kill you! Must go!" Strahd attempts to charm him, but the good giant is protected in this land of Barovia and Strahd fails. Gaxim is unaware of the attempt; Sergei's sword knows precisely what is going on, having seen its revenge thwarted many times before when the wielder got charmed, and urges Gaxim to sit down. He does.

Below, Cyrus greets Wukong, "a visitor." Wukong claims the count sent him to fetch something from the kitchen; Cyrus insists, "No, no. You are in the wrong place. You must follow me to the right room." When Wukong persists, Cyrus interrupts him, "no." Recalling that Cyrus is interested in collecting things, Wukong offers his belt as a "fantastical belt from the isle of Floren. The craftsmen are the greatest in all of the realms." Cyrus is unconvinced: "you lie! You liar! You, not a friend of Cyrus, and I don't trust you. You shouldn't be here." Wukong assures him he won't steal anything and is here on Strahd's behalf. Cyrus: "I will go cook. You stay away from my treasures."

In the dining hall, the sword encourages Gaxim to demand to go to the bathroom. Strahd, who believes he has the barbarian under his control, tells him to sit down, and Gaxim shrugs. Realising what an elitist Strahd is, the sword urges Gaxim to relieve himself in the corner of the room. Strahd, appalled, tells him to go outside. Gaxim goes down the stairwell (K21) into the hallway (K62), where Cyrus emerges from the kitchen: "WHO ARE YOU?" Gaxim and Wukong decide the conversation has gone on long enough, kill Cyrus, and hide the body in the treasure room that has only worthless goods. Upstairs, Strahd is still talking, now about the negotiations to bring the drapes that cover all of the windows.

In the room of bones Wukong finds the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind, the artifact Madam Eva promised, and the skull of Argynvost. Ruling out the trapped hallway (K61), he takes the other stairs up to a dusty servant's entrance (K23), with an open book on a desk and a skeletal figure in chain mail on each side of the stairs. Gaxim heads north into the servant's quarters. Then Gaxim and Wukong exit into the courtyard with the skull and the symbol.

At that exact moment Strahd becomes enraged; the monster, not the man, appears. To Slerdax: "you never cared about my story!" / "No, it was – riveting." Strahd: "Your friends and allies were not going to the bathroom. They stole something of mine." This is the moment he had been waiting for. The chapter turns to Strahd's view. He knew the heroes would come for Argynvost's skull: over the centuries many would-be saviors of Barovia had gone to Castle Argynvostholt seeking allies, found only revenants who wanted them dead, and been driven to his Castle to steal his things; Morgantha had a way of fooling such heroes as if revealing a deep dark secret, "not setting a trap." Every time, Strahd killed them, and every time Argynvost's tenuous grip on the mortal realm would crack, until eventually Argynvost would give up and Strahd's victory over the Dragon would be final. But Strahd had promised these particular fools safe passage, "And he was a man of honor." During Gaxim's ruse he watched the room to see whether anyone knew his plans; the fools ate and drank and hung on his every word. When Gaxim and Wukong left the Castle, the promise was broken and he was free to take his revenge.

And they had brought Ezmerelda, an unexpected prize. She was the mentee of Rudolph van Richten, who had been upsetting Strahd's beloved Vistani and his vampiric spawn and had brought hope to Barovia. Killing her would be easy, but "turning her into a thrall of his would break the old killer." Strahd leaps across the table superhumanly fast and attacks her: "I have been waiting for a long time to kill you, runt, you spawn of van Richten! But I won't kill you, I will turn you into my kind, and then you will feast on Rudolph's blood." He does 44 HP of damage.

Slerdax casts slow, to no effect. Ezmerelda screams, "Run, fools! Run!", and Slerdax runs. Ezmerelda at first thinks she will get her revenge and prove herself to Rudolph; recalling her training, she casts protection from evil, which calms her, and she judges that Strahd is likely to win without the right artifacts. "Not today!" she says, and runs. Merrin casts silence in the room and runs out to the guest's hall (K9), while Ezmerelda holds the door to the dining room (K10) shut. At the other end of the courtyard, Wukong and Gaxim run around to the front carrying the large skull.

Strahd walks through the wall and smiles at the horrified Ezmerelda, grapples her, and drinks her blood for 17 HP of damage. Slerdax runs toward the great entry (K8), sees Rahadin, and conjures minor elementals to surround the chamberlain. Ezmerelda, terrified and angry, screams, "I will not die like this. I will not become one of you!" and attacks with rapier and handaxe for 24 HP of damage. Merrin, annoyed that Ezmerelda did not wait for her to act, casts stoneskin and healing word. Rahadin and eight gargoyles turn on Slerdax's elementals. Strahd tries to bite Ezmerelda again and fails, and she laughs at him.

Past Rahadin and the gargoyles, in the entry (K7), Slerdax finds five red dragon wyrmlings slowly waking and casts web on two of them. Strahd attacks Ezmerelda again, but the spell she cast protects her; he rages that his revenge will not be denied. Slerdax casts haste on Ezmerelda and runs outside. Merrin sacrifices some of her hit points to heal Ezmerelda, and the Vistani is touched: "we are friends to the end!"

Deciding her friends have escaped, Ezmerelda runs for the doorway to the entrance (K7) and finds it locked; the rumors that Strahd controlled all the doors and windows in the Castle were true. "I am trapped! So close!" Strahd sees her struggle and smiles. Then she remembers her knock spell, one van Richten insisted she learn and always prepare, smiles back, and waves her hands at him "in a dismissive Vistani salute." Strahd stands at the doorway, frustrated: his decision to start dinner early, his arrogance, made the escape possible. They have the second of the three artifacts, they have taken Ireena from him, and the Dragon will find peace. He howls in rage. Rahadin has gone white from fear.

## Locations
### Castle Ravenloft
Strahd's Castle, which he calls "an architectural masterpiece I created many years ago", built where a hamlet once stood. The chapter tours it room by room by keyed number: the blood-filled tub (K43), the closet (K44), Gertruda's bedroom (K42), the Count's study, the tower stair with its crystal heart (K20), the hall of statues (K45), the hallway past the cake room (K35, K36), the servants' level (K61, K62, K65), the butler's quarters, the Hall of Bones (K67), the servant's entrance (K23), the guest's hall (K9), the dining room (K10), the great entry (K8) and the entry (K7).

### The Hall of Bones (K67)
An ossuary furnished in human bone, with a dragon's skull mounted above the east doors. It is the room of bones Madam Eva said contained one of their artifacts. Strahd waits here for the spider, and Wukong later finds the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind and the skull of Argynvost here.

### The Count's Study
A warm, ordered room of books and divans; the gargantuan painting over the mantelpiece is an exact likeness of Ireena Kolyana.

### The Butler's Quarters
A cluttered room of broken weapons and armour, "the treasure room" in Wukong's words. Gaxim and Wukong hide Cyrus's body in "the treasure room that has only worthless goods."

### Castle Argynvostholt
Named only in Strahd's reasoning: would-be saviors of Barovia went there looking for allies and found revenants who wanted them dead.

### Barovia
The land in which Gaxim is protected from Strahd's charm, and to which Rudolph van Richten "had brought hope".

## NPCs
### Ezmerelda d'Avenir
The self-styled expert ("the great Ezmerelda d'Avenir"), irritated that no one consults her. She advises going underground, and is Strahd's chosen target once the theft is revealed. Strahd taunts her as "runt, you spawn of van Richten" and means to turn her. She fights alone: protection from evil, rapier and handaxe for 24 HP of damage, laughter when his bite fails, and finally a knock spell and "a dismissive Vistani salute." To Marron: "we are friends to the end!"

### Strahd
The host. He confronts the spider in the Hall of Bones, eats it in bat form, and proposes an early dinner. At the table he is "curiously delighted" to talk about his Castle, fails to charm Gaxim, and is appalled by Gaxim's corner ruse. His reasoning is shown directly: a trap he has run on heroes for centuries, held back by his promise of safe passage, "And he was a man of honor." When the promise breaks he attacks Ezmerelda, and ends the chapter frustrated at the doorway, howling in rage.

### Rahadin
Strahd's chamberlain. He fetches the party to dinner, clearly not believing what Strahd told him. During the escape Slerdax surrounds him with minor elementals, and eight gargoyles defend him. He ends the chapter "gone white from fear."

### Cyrus Belview
The mongrelfolk in the servant's hall. He tries to steer Wukong away, rejects the belt from Floren ("you lie! You liar!"), and goes to cook, warning "You stay away from my treasures." When he challenges Gaxim ("WHO ARE YOU?"), Gaxim and Wukong kill him and hide the body.

### The butler
Unnamed in the chapter. Found in his quarters caressing the worthless equipment of dead adventurers and mumbling who each piece belonged to.

### Gertruda
The daughter of Mad Mary, whom Strahd stole. Slerdax finds her lying in the canopied bed in K42 and notes her location.

### Sergei's sword (the Sword of Sergei)
Expected to seize control of Gaxim and send him on a murderous and suicidal rampage. When Marron attempts to reason with it, Gaxim speaks: "As long as you continue to accumulate power to kill Strahd, I will stay my hand." It knows when Strahd tries to charm Gaxim, urges him to sit, and then engineers his exit by having him demand the bathroom and relieve himself in the corner.

### Slerdax
Scouting through his spider until Strahd eats it. He authors the heist plan and the idea of asking Strahd about his life, keeps Strahd talking at dinner, then flees: slow, minor elementals, web on two wyrmlings, and haste on Ezmerelda.

### Wukong
The rogue, volunteered for the theft. He jumps the trap, bluffs Cyrus with a belt from the isle of Floren, and with Gaxim kills Cyrus and carries out the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind and Argynvost's skull.

### Gaxim
The barbarian and "the good giant". He is protected from Strahd's charm in Barovia, is steered by the sword into the corner ruse, and helps Wukong carry the skull out.

### Marron and Merrin
Marron proposes a frontal assault and a consecrate spell, and reasons with the sword; Merrin proposes saying grace and screams, "that's the spirit!" In the escape Merrin casts silence, stoneskin and healing word, and sacrifices some of her hit points to heal Ezmerelda.
