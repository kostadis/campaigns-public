# Chapter 5: The vampire screams

Date: Days 6–7 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
The chapter opens mid-ambush. The vampire screams, "Father! Thank you! Food!" The Heroes, fully aware of the danger below, are not surprised — except for Slerdax, "who was too busy touching things." The monster charges straight at the barbarian, and both of his claw attacks miss. Slerdax, from the top of the stairs, casts Tasha’s hideous laughter: "The vampire is not amused." Marron does 1 HP of damage, Gaxim recklessly attacks for 7, and Wukong's arrow inflicts nine points. Doru misses again. Slerdax casts web, but Doru escapes it. Wukong misses, and Doru grapples Gaxim and regenerates. Slerdax uses the grapple to walk past the vampire and casts a chromatic orb for 16 HP of fire damage. Marron inflicts 12, Gaxim, grappled but enraged, does 9, and Wukong does 8. Doru bites Gaxim, takes 9 points of damage, and regenerates 16 HP. Slerdax does 30 HP of damage with melf’s acid arrow, and Marron kills the vampire.

Donavich comes down and cries in horror, "you live!" Gaxim smiles: "Gaxim brave!" Donavich answers, "My son is dead, and you live?" and collapses in tears, while Slerdax wonders whether he should cast Tasha’s hideous laughter to cheer him up. "You are not supposed to live. You were supposed to die." The party realizes that Donavich had set them up: his son, mad from hunger, needed to feed, and strangers made "an interesting option." Gaxim is furious when he discovers that Donavich has no treasure and thought they would die — "Mostly when he realizes there is no treasure." Wukong and the Paladin intervene to ensure Donavich lives. Marron, frustrated, says, "hey, ocher jelly, don’t bubble at him."

Leaving the temple, Slerdax asks, "should we burn it down?" A very irritated Marron answers "No," then looks at the temple intently: "We must return it to a proper deity." When Slerdax asks about exploring the cemetery, Marron replies, "Perhaps not in the middle of the night." Just then a parade of ghosts rises from the grave and marches down the main street towards Castle Ravenloft. The party anxiously looks for the gnomish magic user they are nominally looking for; Donavich had said that those who walk in this march were adventurers who had come to fight Strahd and died. Gaxim thinks they are alive and marches with them. The ghosts find it amusing and make a space for him, and he walks with the spirits towards the castle, pleased with himself. The oppressive despair of ghosts who failed to escape Barovia and kill its master causes Marron to turn into Merrin.

"What happened to the vampire?" Everyone stares at the aasimar. Wukong realizes what has happened — "you are the cleric!" — and Merrin answers, "Yes, I am and always have been." Wukong explains that another aasimar, a Paladin, helped them kill the vampire while she was, after a struggle for the word, "sleeping." "Sleeping?"

Back at the Tavern, Ismark is delighted to see them alive: "you live! Fantastic. Tomorrow we shall go and save Ireena. And how is my friend Donavich doing?" Merrin begins, "Distraught," and explains that his son was turned into a horrible monster but died. Wukong jumps in, "it was the Paladin, not the CLERIC!" Gaxim, very confused about this mysterious Paladin, insists, "There is no Paladin!" Wukong tries to explain, and Gaxim grows progressively more annoyed; when Wukong says, "this is Merrin, not Marron," Gaxim looks at the rogue, whom he is convinced is insane: "no, this is Marron!" Ismark, watching the party argue over who is and who is not a Paladin, announces, "I am going to go to bed and let the four of you figure out who is who," and tells the barkeep, "give them two rooms." Merrin points out, "there are four of us, not two." Alenka, the Vistani, scowls: "if you do not like the rooms, you can always go find an abandoned house." The party heads up to sleep.

Morning arrives in Barovia under a hazy, dark sky, "The twilight of dawn and dusk brighter than the mid-day of this land." Ismark greets them — "come, my friends, let us go in and save my sister, Ireena" — and leads everyone to the Burgomaster’s house at the edge of town (E4): a weary-looking mansion behind a rusting iron fence with twisted, torn gates, weeds choking the grounds, heavy claw markings and great black fire marks on the walls, and every glassless window barred with planks "marked with stains of evil omen." "In we must go," says Ireena’s brother. At the door, a female voice demands "Who?", asks "Are you one of Strahd’s creatures?", and insists, "Tell me something that only he would know!" This goes on for some 5-10 minutes before Ireena lets them in.

Inside, the mansion is well furnished but worn, with boarded-up windows and holy symbols in every room. The Burgomaster lies in a simple wooden coffin in a side drawing room, surrounded by wilting flowers and a faint odor of decay. Ireena explains that wolves and other creatures attacked the house night after night for weeks, that the Burgomaster's heart could not stand it and he died three days ago, and that "Strangely, since his death, the house has not come under attack." She pauses for dramatic effect: "I promised Kolyan that he would be buried in a cemetery with all of the rites. I cannot leave this town until that happens." Ismark insists, "They will help us, Ireena!" Merrin observes that she can cast a ceremony spell on the body so that it does not rise as a vampire. There is debate about the timing, but Ireena will have none of it: she made a promise to her old friend and intends to keep it.

The heroes march the coffin toward the cemetery. On the way they notice Morgantha going from house to house, collecting payment from each home where someone is there. At one, she grabs a small child and shoves them into a bag while the mother screams frantically, "you cannot take my son!" Gaxim charges; Merrin follows. Ireena is appalled: "Ismark, did you find the biggest fools in all of Barovia?" Morgantha is in shock. Gaxim attacks recklessly and misses, Wukong does 19 HP of damage to "the shocked peddler", and Gaxim rages and misses again. Morgantha relents: "Wait! Wait! You want the child? There is no reason for us to fight; you can have the child." To Slerdax's "why do you want the child?" she answers contemptuously that strangers will quickly join the procession of ghosts to Strahd's palace — "Bah! Take the child!" The child dashes to his parents.

Morgantha then offers pastries — "Friendly giant, do you want some pastries?" — and while Slerdax ponders "what exactly is in the pastries?", Gaxim gladly takes some. Asked whether they think they can fight Strahd, Merrin replies, "no, we are here to find a wizard." Morgantha scoffs at "The mad wizard": "He thought he could defeat Strahd! No one can." He is not dead only "Because Strahd wills it so." When Slerdax looks her in the eyes and asks what she wants and what her relationship with Strahd is, she gives a long, bitter answer: "We are all prisoners of Strahd." Strahd has mastery over the land and the weather, his spies are everywhere, "including the thrice-damned Vistani", and souls that die are trapped and "reborn into something that will amuse Strahd." "I am a hag, but was I always one? Who knows". "I am the fish that knows it is a fish, and that it lives because the thing on the outside chooses to let it live." Wukong picks that moment to mention "an exit strategy", and she snaps, "You fool! No one leaves Barovia unless Strahd wills it." She explains that when someone dies in Barovia, Strahd can bring them back cursed, turn them into ghosts, or worse. "But that was the right question. Do you want to kill Strahd?"

The party answers at cross-purposes: Merrin, "I want to find the wizard!"; Gaxim, "I want to kill the priest!"; Wukong, "I want to go where the priest goes!"; Slerdax, "can you help us kill Strahd?" Morgantha asks, "What treasure do you have?", and the party shows a bag of treasure from the house they escaped. "You survived the Death House. Well done." She names it "Durst’s house": the Dursts thought "THEY controlled Strahd", amused him until they killed someone who amused Strahd, and were trapped in their house as ghouls, the children starved and kept as ghosts "to lead the unwary and unworthy to their doom." She reveals that Strahd has undead enemies in Barovia — the fallen knights of the Order of the Silver Dragon, revenants found in a ruined mansion west of Vallaki — and that his most carefully guarded secret is a temple of forbidden lore in the mountains, reached by the long and winding Tsolenka Pass. Merrin sternly tells her to eat no more children; she smiles and walks away.

After the delay, the party finds Donavich in the main temple, still crying: "They killed Doru!" Ismark and Ireena try to console the Cleric, but he is inconsolable. Ireena, realizing they must go, turns to Merrin: "Merrin, you said you could consecrate the grave? Then do so now." Someone tells Donavich, "your love for your son was commendable. But you know there is no salvation other than death for him as a vampire. You should be thankful." More hysterics, more crying. "Cleric, let us go and bury the old man." Merrin asks, "what about the vampire?" — and the vampire's body cannot be found.
