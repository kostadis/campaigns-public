# Chapter 39: It is morning in Barovia

Date: Day 26 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
The chapter opens on a morning that should not exist: "There are no mornings in Barovia. There is no sun circling the land lost in the mists. It is whatever Count von Strahd wants it to be, and he wishes it to be morning, and so it is."

The heroes huddle in the Blue Water Inn, where Stefania opens the magic box. The Magic Box is a device the Keepers of the Feather use to protect their hoard and to earn a tidy profit; it shows a small number of the magic items they have collected over the years, while the location of the actual site is "a secret, a secret that nobody has." Merrin announces that the group has 43,000 GP, and Stefania starts to show them the nice stuff. Merrin reaches for a +3 shield and hands over the currency to buy it, but Stefania looks it over and announces that they don’t have 43,000 GP: "They have 7500." Merrin begins to object, recognizes the small numerical error she made, and reluctantly lets go of the shield.

They sit and stare at each other; the long quest is reaching its end. Gaxim observes, "Could have just attacked after entering Barovia." Everyone stares at him and thinks about how quickly they would have died. Stefania remarks, "Some have tried just that," "but they are part of the eternal march." The exchange closes with "Not Gaxim." / "True."

The hike to Ravenloft is brief, but this time there is no invitation and the doors are closed. KP stands just before the entrance into the front courtyard. After a brief discussion the group decides to scale the outer wall. Their first objective is to find the third artifact in the dungeon without going through the main entrance into the castle proper; they recall that there are stairways that will take them all the way down.

As they pass through the external gate into the courtyard, the weather abruptly changes "from a pleasant sunny day to a dark and stormy night with rain drizzling." Wukong notices green slime hanging from the ceiling and warns the others. At the interior walls he looks at the 90’ wall and laughs, "child’s play." He slips from 30’ out; Slerdax casts feather fall on him, and this time Wukong makes it up without falling. The rope is dangled and everyone makes it up except Ezmerelda, who slips — but KP has a feather fall spell memorized and casts it.

They arrive on the second floor, on the parapet that Slerdax explored using his familiar: a ten-foot-wide walkway that encircles most of the keep, with drizzle "punctuated by the occasional clap of thunder or stroke of lightning" and the shining wet cobblestones of the courtyard far below. Walking north along the outside wall, they meet some animated armor bearing "the insignia of Strahd’s house and maybe his." KP turns to Slerdax: "You might want to use your knowledge of the arcana to choose how to fight this creature." The Genasi mage thinks and casts a chromatic orb. KP smirks, casts Dispel Magic, and the construct falls down unconscious; he tells the group, "Fighters, please destroy it." Gaxim stares: "how?" KP turns to Slerdax with the lesson: "Magic constructs are affected by Dispel Magic. Most evokers insist on going boom-boom with the destruction spell, but understanding the weak point and taking advantage of that is the true art of the mage."

After the armor is destroyed, Gaxim gets curious, runs around the parapets and ends up back at the tower. The rest of the group waits for him and enters. They see a mosaic floor from 90’ up, a dark, cold, empty tower rising above, a spiral staircase hugging the outer wall into darkness, and in the center of the room another set of stairs leading down.

The heroes have decided to let Gaxim lead today; recognizing how he has thwarted Strahd at every turn, "it is deemed auspicious." Gaxim charges up. As he steps onto the spiral staircase, a reddish light flares high overhead and settles into a dull, pulsing red glow, revealing the tower's full immensity: sixty feet wide at its base and narrowing as it climbs, and at the pinnacle of the hollow tower "a large crystal heart pulsates with red light," with the stairs continuing above it. Everyone stares at it, and they all turn to each other and say, "I got nothing."

30’ up, the tower begins to shake. KP casts another feather fall, just in case, and Merrin falls 120’ but is still alive because of the spell. As the heroes stare at her falling, a toy rolls down the stairs and lands at Gaxim’s feet. After debate they agree that Slerdax will run towards Merrin, KP will stay put, and Ezmerelda, Gaxim and Wukong will run up the stairs. Still under the feather fall and 120’ from her friends, Merrin starts climbing.

Wukong makes it up to where the evil beating heart is located. Convinced it must be some kind of magical artifact, and seeing Gaxim 30’ away and the wizards even further behind, he shoots an arrow. The arrow affects the heart — and draws four vampire spawn in from the southern passageway. Their leader shouts, "how dare you attack the master’s heart." The vampires are dressed in a mish-mash of clothes that are not Barovian in origin. Animated halberds fly towards Wukong.

Ezmerelda, 60’ behind, runs up. KP, seeing that Ezmerelda is there to help Merrin and that magic will be required to deal with the artifact, casts Dimension Door and brings himself and Slerdax to the landing. Slerdax is about to cast Dawn but decides a fireball is called for first; several halberds are destroyed, but the vampires avoid the bulk of the damage. An enraged vampire charges Slerdax and claws him for 17 HP. At the bottom of the tower Merrin charges up — and then the tower shakes again, and Ezmerelda and Merrin fall to the bottom.

One vampire in a green vest and a blue pantaloon charges Wukong while another in a blue vest and a green pantaloon flanks him; together they claw the rogue for 31 points of damage. Surrounded by vampires, halberds and a beating heart, with the party split, Slerdax shouts, "Let’s try and kill the heart!" Wukong answers, "Not a bad idea, Slerdax. And then we jump." The rogue hits with an arrow, Gaxim throws his Javelin of Lightning, and the heart shatters.

A vampire wearing a leather jerkin attacks KP. The halberds surround the heroes and attack to no avail except on Slerdax, slashed for 11, and KP, for 6. Ezmerelda shouts, "stairs, stairs are a great workout," and dashes up 60 feet. The chapter ends as KP casts sunbeam and sculpts around the heroes in front of him.
