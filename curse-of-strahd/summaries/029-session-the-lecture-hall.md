# Chapter 29: The lecture hall

Date: Days 17–19 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
Kasimir dusts the snow off his hiding place and urges the party on: "Continue onwards! We must go to the temple and find the secret that allows Strahd to imprison my sister!" Gaxim urges Kasimir to take the lead and earn the coveted "Kasimir is Brave!", but Kasimir, "a humble Dusk Elf", answers, "I will follow where you lead." Gaxim's reply is a single "Grr." Merrin heals the heroes.

Wukong observes that he alone has come through uninjured. As he examines his claws and scratches his tattoo of a bow, Strahd manifests for a moment and whispers to him: "think of the rewards. Soon I will require a replacement for that fool Rahadin. When I ask, all you must do is, do."

Ezmerelda looks over the cold heroes — "foolz! Why are you dressed so poorly?" — and Slerdax answers that warm clothes would have been "More useful at the beginning of this trip up the mountain, Ez". Kasimir shouts that they are in luck, for he has "Some warm clothes that I am willing to sell for a nominal fee." Gaxim shivers. Wukong asks, "how exactly do you plan to defeat the monsters and find what you need without us?" and the reply is a sigh: "we are blood brothers! This cursed land has made me forget the duties. Here, I give them to you as we are on the same quest!" "He fondles his ring that our heroes recognize as a ring of warmth."

The party reaches the entrance to the temple: a facade carved into the sheer mountainside, fifty feet high, with six alcoves holding twenty-foot-tall statues, each carved from a single block of amber and depicting "a faceless, hooded figure". Between the two innermost statues a twenty-foot-tall archway opens on a staircase leading down. The sword pushes Gaxim to do something novel: "Wukong, go find traps!" Wukong sneaks down the icy steps into a time-ravaged hallway with arrow slits in the walls, and motions Slerdax to block the slits with mold earth. Kasimir waves him on.

Beyond the hall Wukong comes out on a balcony of black marble with a shattered railing, overlooking a vast temple whose walls and ceiling are covered in an amber glaze, "lending the gloom a golden sheen." Amber doors stand closed at the west end of the balcony and open at the east. Wukong peers through the eastern door into a featureless room with a rough-edged, 10-foot-diameter hole in the floor; it goes down 30 feet, and he "chooses to explore that another day." Heading for the southern door, he notices a secret door — "That is where the traps must be," he thinks out loud — and passes through a frigid room strewn with shattered wood. With "Caution being tossed to the wind", he sneaks past the secret door into another guardroom, where a skeleton in a blue wizard's robe sits slumped in the northeast corner clutching a wand to its chest. Slerdax examines the wand and announces it is a wand of secrets; Merrin, "Sensing foul play", examines the body and declares that cold killed the mage. The other secret door opens to a Scroll Repository (X7), where the wizards once kept magic scrolls in case of attack; the scrolls have crumbled to dust. "I suppose if we had a scroll or a map to put away, this would be useful." The group stares at Slerdax.

Wukong scouts north along an amber-glazed, seventy-foot-long arched corridor and creeps into a side room (X9): a lecture hall lit by red copper lanthorns, its amber walls shaped into bas-reliefs of wizards with spellbooks, with rows of red marble benches descending to an obsidian lectern and a slab of black slate hanging from chains. At the lectern he finds a mage in scorched robes, his hair half burned away and his face and arms blistered from magic fire. Asked who he is, Wukong answers, "Tourist!" and explains he is "Looking around, admiring the architecture, and wondering what is this all for?" The mage, holding a rat that looks cooked, is decidedly uninterested — "You have nothing to share with me." — until Wukong offers "Some food, perhaps?" Then: "My name is Vilnius, and I thank you!"

A sound in the hallway terrifies Vilnius: "you have friends? They woke up the Amber Golem, and it will kill us all! You are safe here. The Amber Golem does not enter this room. That's why I have been stuck here for weeks." Wukong thanks him and chooses not to pass the information to his friends. When he goes to the door, Ezmerelda sees him cowering behind it, spits out his name, and attacks with her rapier and hand-ax. Gaxim rages and attacks recklessly; the wizard uses a fireball; Merrin hits with her blood spear. The monster strikes Gaxim, who resists the deadly attack. Wukong judges it safe to fire an arrow, which leaves the creature unaffected, and Ezmerelda sighs and tosses him a magical hand-ax. Wukong stares at it: "are you expecting me to attack in," he gasps, "melee?" Ezmerelda's rapiers, to a lesser extent, and Gaxim's critical attack defeat the monster. Wukong turns to Vilnius: "I saved you."

Slerdax asks Vilnius what he is doing there. Vilnius's teacher, "that imbecilic, foolish mage Jakarion", came to this land seeking power, followed "some damn fool quest", sought further power and died. "I want his staff. I will trade information for his staff!" The party answers as one: "Madam Eva's fault." Vilnius is unconvinced: "we followed some Vistani. Why we are here is no matter, he thought he would find powerful magic! He found nothing." Once the group learns how close the staff is, the sword prods Gaxim: "why you no get staff?" Vilnius looks horrified — "have you not been attacked by the Flameskulls?" — and mocks them: "Jakarion thought he was so clever as well. Then he died, and I have been stuck here for weeks, eating rats!" He refuses to bargain: "Until you promise me the staff and a way out, I will say nothing. I survived this many weeks. I can survive many more!"

Slerdax casts mold earth on the door and the heroes crash on the pews of the amphitheater. Vilnius stares at the food, Gaxim throws him some, and he answers, "Thank you! But don't think that will get me to trade my precious information!" Gaxim takes the first watch. The three hags visit in the night and disrupt the sleep, and with "all of the mages caught in their horrible nightmares, no one can help them." The cackling laugh of Morgantha and her daughters taunts the heroes. The group sleeps once more in the amber temple, regaled with the annoying tales of Vilnius and his Imp; it becomes clear that Vilnius is from the Prime Material Plane, but not from Oerth.

The next exchange is sharp. "I am not the one that disturbed the hags," snarls Kasimir at the Vistani vampire hunter. Slerdax growls, "I want to kill those hags," and Gaxim grumbles, "Hags die! Hags die!" The sword reflects that Morgantha is "the true horror of this place": "Strahd is fool enough to think he is master. And Eva is fool enough to think she is fighting Strahd. They are both pawns of Morgantha, who is the true horror of this place." Gaxim reflects it back: "Hags boring." Judging that another day might push their luck — and with the room "starting to be a bit rank from the feces" — they head out.

North through a collapsed Annex, Wukong advances on a black marble balcony thirty feet above the floor, overhanging the northeast corner of the temple. As he and Ezmerelda step onto it, a fireball strikes them both from magical darkness around the head of a statue; the rogue's ability to "presciently put someone else in front of him" reduces the blast. Gaxim rushes in, Slerdax walks in to be as far from the group as possible, and Merrin stands at the door. Three flame skulls approach the arrow slits "that lead to the doom of Jakarion"; Vilnius "was not lying about the monsters." The first flameskull's fireball is countered, and a voice declares: "I am the guardian of this temple, and its horrors! How dare you! My name is Neferon, and I have guarded this temple for centuries! You will meet your doom!"

All four heroes are hit with chain lightning, and the arcanaloth is annoyed that they did not die. Merrin casts daylight, breaking the darkness; another flameskull fireball lands. Wukong retreats to an unexplored room, assuring Merrin, who wonders aloud about wights, that it is empty and has a secret door. Slerdax suggests a wall of fire; everyone dismisses it, so he casts a tidal wave for a meager amount of damage. "Out-fireballed, exposed and wounded", the party could retreat, but Gaxim will have none of it: "No retreat! No surrender! No, today!" Another fireball knocks Ezmerelda out; under her breath she says, "do not throw your life away, monsieur Gaxim!"

Gaxim jumps off the balcony onto the statue and engages the arcanaloth, his light sword slashing it for 29 HP. He taunts the flameskulls to fireball him, unaware that they can cast one fireball per day; their magic missiles hit him instead, and the sword thinks, "oops." The arcanaloth, thinking the barbarian "will make an excellent addition to his zombie slaves", casts a finger of death; Gaxim avoids death and sneers. Merrin heals Ezmerelda and Gaxim, "sacrificing 20 HP for the good giant", and Ezmerelda is struck down again. Wukong sends an arrow from his hideaway into the arcanaloth's tough hide for 25 HP. Slerdax, realising the Flameskulls need line of sight for their magic missiles, uses a wall of fire to block it; "The Flameskull hisses and does nothing," and Slerdax thinks maybe they should not have been so quick to dismiss him. Gaxim slashes the monster twice, reducing them to 2, Merrin tries and fails to kill them, and Wukong finishes them off.

The heroes return to rest. Vilnius mocks them: "you didn't believe me when I said they were dangerous?" When Slerdax and Merrin discuss the battle, he is outraged: "you killed Neferon! He was a master of arcane magic and a keeper of secrets. You fools! Do you know what has been lost?" Gaxim walks up and slaps him. Vilnius whimpers, "can I have some food?" The heroes toss him some and prepare to spend another night in this frigid lecture hall.
