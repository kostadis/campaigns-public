# Chapter 27: Morgantha

Date: Days 16–17 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
Our heroes arrive in Barovia late at night. The nighttime is "a great time to find Morgantha, but not the safest of times in this vampire-infested land." The howls of Mad Mary have not ended; she still sits in her home wailing. Remembering that they have news of Mary's daughter, the heroes think they may find some sanctuary there.

Slerdax gently brings Mary up to speed: their trip into Castle Ravenloft, the discovery of her daughter, and that she is not yet a vampire. Mary, who speaks with a thick Barovian accent, asks, "You have seen Gertruda? She is not yet a vampeere?" Merrin answers respectfully that they "don't think so." Mary cries, "Bless the Morninglords!", someone answers "Heironeous!", and Mary replies, "Your god? Then I praise him too!" Merrin, who "will not miss any opportunity to proselytize," promises, "And we will sanctify your local chapel!" Mary agrees: "you will free us from that demon vampire Doru who killed his father Donavich." In her telling, "The poor boy followed the Mad Mage up to the Castle, and his reward was not to join the fallen, but to be a vampire that torments us to this day." She hugs Merrin and spends 20 minutes praising all the gods, "including and particularly Heironeous," then asks, "When do we go to the Castle?" The heroes ponder her value, realise she would die quickly, thank her for her heroism, and say they will take care of this on their own.

They wait for Morgantha. Soon they hear "small, wooden wheels rolling across damp cobbles," leave Mad Mary's house, and trace the lonely sound to "a hunched figure bundled in rags, pushing a rickety wooden cart through the fogs crying, "Pies!"" Gaxim rushes into combat: "You! You are the real evil!" Wukong misses. Merrin rushes in as well and casts Crusader's Mantle. An attack made "recklessly and without concern" deals 36 points of damage; the chapter does not name who made it. Slerdax slows Morgantha. The hag stares at the heroes and says, "I didn't survive this long, to be killed by the likes of you!", and disappears into the ethereal plane. The party stares at the empty spot and howls in rage.

The heroes debate whether to go to the windmill and attack now or wait for an opportune moment. They conclude that attacking the windmill in the evening would be advantageous: Morgantha would not be with her coven, as she would be out on the streets of Barovia selling pies. They head to Mad Mary's for a night's sleep, but Merrin decides to check the local general store, Bildrath's Mercantile.. Bildrath asks whether they are "the heroes of Vallaki" and whether she is "the white angel that convinced the Burgomaster to have everyone smile?" Merrin replies, "Smiling is important!" and smiles grandly. The prices do not change. Gaxim blurts out, "We save Danika, who is raven!", which draws the question, "Danika is a Keeper of the Feather?" Merrin interrupts the flow of the conversation — "silly idea you have! He always has silly ideas!" — and Gaxim, confused, decides to leave.

In the middle of the night, Slerdax wakes everyone up with horrible nightmares; most nights it is his giggling and snoring that wakes them. Merrin examines him and declares, "there is something magical going on here!" Ezmerelda mutters to herself that "the evil genius told me about this. Yes! It is a hag!" Asked what to do, she says, "I know not. If I had my cart, I am sure there would be a book on hags that we could consult. But, Non! You all said that my cart was of no value." Staring at Slerdax, who had called it useless, she adds, "Not so useless anymore, Monsieur Slerdax?" The heroes are unable to stop the nightmares. An hour later they stop, and the heroes hear, from somewhere outside the village, the cackle of Morgantha. By morning Slerdax has "slept frightfully": he cannot recover his spells and is weakened, losing 5 HP.

The heroes decide to head to the Amber Temple to see whether they can find any secrets. Because they are not sure where the temple is and promised Kasimir Velikov they would bring him along, they plan first to stop by the Dusk Elves outside of Vallaki. Before they get there, there is "the small matter of the people following them." Wukong points out an ambush of "some eight to 12 men on either side of the road." The tough men think they are about to surprise the heroes; "Little do they know that they are about to be surprised."

Gaxim kills one of the thugs. Merrin casts stoneskin on Slerdax. Wukong's arrow pierces the hired muscle's armor for 22 HP of damage. Slerdax casts a tidal wave; the muscle avoids being knocked over but still takes 8 points of bludgeoning damage. Ezmerelda strikes the waterlogged bad guys with a lightning bolt for 30. An Assassin suddenly fires a crossbow bolt at Ezmerelda that misses, shouting, "at least Strahd pays well." Gaxim kills one thug and severely wounds another; Merrin attacks the injured thug with her blood spear; Wukong hits one of the enemies Ezmerelda and Slerdax are fighting for 17 HP; Slerdax's magic missile kills one more thug and injures another. Two "veterans of the forests of Vallaki" surround Gaxim and slash him for 17 points of damage, and the assassin smiles and fires a bolt into Gaxim's back whose poison drains him of 24 HP. The battle ends "predictably in the death of the ambushers."

The assassin curses and runs deeper into the forest. Slerdax grabs Gaxim by the hand and casts dimension door, aiming to appear where the assassin was last heard. Wukong fires an arrow at where he thinks the assassin is for 11 Hp of damage. The assassin strikes twice with his poisoned scimitars, killing Gaxim — "But Gaxim has death ward and is still standing." Gaxim hits him, Wukong fires an arrow through the trees and hits, and Slerdax finishes him off with a magic missile. On the body the heroes find a contract from Strahd worth 5000 GP.

A few hours later they appear near the Vistani encampment near Vallaki. Ezmerelda recognises the band as the one with which they had a dispute over horses. The group recommends, "You wait here," and she pauses, thinks, and finally says she "will." They find Kasimir, who is "delighted and a bit surprised to see them again."
