# Chapter 16: A mission oriented winery

Date: Day 10 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
Meanwhile, in the plane of the Dark Powers, there was a mid-quarter update on the heroes. According to Strahd's handler, the manager of the Realm of Barovia, "the plan to keep Strahd enraged was going well." The other objectives — "turn the party evil and keep them trapped in Barovia" — were "trending yellow and red." The handler promised to move some people around, focus on the tough choices, and make the hard decisions "to turn all the OKRs Green by the end." The Dark Powers were pleased with the update.

The party heads up the stairs while the ravens watch without acting, and enters a room with a loading winch (W16): a wooden floor with "a ten-foot-square hole cut into the middle of it," a wooden winch looming over the hole, and perched atop it "a man with wild hair, rotted teeth, and skin painted red with blood." He waves "a gnarled staff made from a black branch" and babbles at them. "The third druid," the party thinks. Wukong misses; Gaxim attacks the Druid recklessly for 16 HP of damage; Slerdax hits with a chromatic orb and the Druid is down to three. The Druid casts thunderwave, but Gaxim is not pushed away. When "the evil nature lover" tries to flee through the hole, Gaxim swings and kills him. The party examines the staff and discovers it is made from the branch of a Gulthias tree — "a spongy, black length of wood" — and magical. Slerdax, who is unarmed, takes it.

Looking out into the vineyard, the party sees the damage the hordes of needleblight caused. Wukong, bored, leads the way into the kitchen (W18), with its rectangular table and eight chairs, L-shaped cupboard, floor-to-ceiling closet pantry and small iron stove. Gaxim enters the master bedroom (W19): a four-poster bed whose headboard is carved "in the likeness of a giant raven," a soft black rug, two slender wardrobes flanking a tapestry of a church, a carved rocking cradle beneath the tapestry, a plain desk and chair under the north window, a wooden chest and a freestanding mirror. Wukong opens the chest, uses sleight of hand to steal "the coinage that belongs to the Martikov," slams it closed, and declares that there is nothing.

The desk holds manifests recording wine shipments for the past century. Recent records show almost all shipments go to "BV" (the Blood o' the Vine tavern in the village of Barovia), "BW" (the Blue Water Inn in the town of Vallaki), "K" (Krezk) and "VISTANI". Looking deeper, Wukong finds entries for "S" (Strahd) that are a hundred years old. He first wants to use this as proof that the Martikovs are evil, but Merrin reminds him that this was a hundred years ago. Wukong then remembers that, according to "the Great Vampire Killer van Richten," Strahd has been inactive for a century — "A century that coincided with peace in Vallaki and the reign of smiles." Slerdax wonders aloud how good that 100-year-old wine of Strahd's is, and what it is worth.

Gaxim, bored, walks outside and runs into two vine-blights and, "to their horror," a fourth Druid. The Druid casts entanglement at Gaxim and fails; a vineblight tries to capture him and fails too. Slerdax misses. Gaxim advances and kills the vineblight. Wukong advances past Gaxim, turns the corner across from W20, and misses; tempted to break his bow, he instead retreats out of sight. Seeing all of his allies dead, the Druid flees down the stairs into the fermentation room, first telling his lackey, the vineblight, "to stop the good guys." The vineblight tries to entangle Gaxim and Wukong on the stairs near W20: Gaxim is trapped and takes 12 Hp of damage; Wukong is not. Merrin races down the stairs next to the kitchen and catches up to the Druid, and Slerdax follows her, leaving Wukong and Gaxim to deal with the vineblight.

"The dark powers smile," hoping Gaxim, "abandoned by everyone to his fate," will turn to evil. "But no, Gaxim has faith in his own strength and Wukong." He throws a handaxe for 18 HP of damage, "And that faith is rewarded. Wukong kills the creature." The cry goes up: "Wukong Brave! Wukong Save!" "The plan to pervert Gaxim has gone horribly awry. The next meeting of the council will not go well."

Downstairs, the Druid casts thunderwave and Merrin is not pushed away. The Druid flees, Merrin misses, pursues him outside, casts a spiritual weapon, and misses again. Slerdax follows Merrin outside. Gaxim jumps out the window down next to the Druid and misses, while the Druid "wonders about the sequence of events that brought him to this point. All of the bad choices, the wrong turns." Wukong follows Gaxim out the window and hits the Druid for 15 HP of damage. "The Dark Powers are appalled. The faith Gaxim had in his friend has changed the evil rogue's luck!" Surrounded, the Druid casts thunderwave, knocking Gaxim unconscious though no one else is pushed away, cheers at the sight of the unconscious barbarian, and runs 25 feet toward the vineyard. Merrin misses with the spiritual weapon, runs up to the Druid, and kills him. The aasimar then saunters over and heals the barbarian 1 HP "through the wondrous application of her mystical arts." Gaxim goes to the loading dock, finds barrels in the cart, and plans to take the cart.

With the danger gone, the Martikov patriarch approaches — he knew because of the ravens. "You have done Barovia a great deed! And now I can tell you about my real troubles!" he says, and, staring intently at Gaxim, hopes to "trouble you for one more act of bravery! But first, we must go and drink some wine and eat some food!" He tells his son Adrian to open the barrel, because Gaxim "looks pale and needs some of our fine wine to be fortified!" When Gaxim is about to drink straight from the barrel, the elder stops him — "No, we are civilized here and drink from a glass" — and hands the barbarian a goblet.

As night falls, Davian explains the real threat to the winery and to Barovia. The Vistani bring wine from outside but rarely trade it, and the only vineyards worth making into wine are here. The winery is famous for three wines: "the unremarkable Purple Grapemash No. 3, the slightly more tantalizing Red Dragon Crush, and the rich Champagne du le Stomp." The heroes recall that they have only seen the Purple Grapemash at the Inns. The Wizard of Wines was founded by a mage "whose name is buried in the annals of history," who fashioned three magic gems, "each one as big as a pinecone," and planted them in the valley soil; these "seeds" gave rise to healthy vines. Gaxim says, "Cows? Plant Seeds! See Giant!" and smiles. Davian is irritated: "we have heard that joke 1000 times!" Even after Strahd's curse settled over Barovia, the gems kept the vines from succumbing to the darkness.

Ten years ago one gem was dug up and stolen, and the winery stopped producing the champagne. Davian blames his middle son, Urwin, who was on watch that night; Davian is convinced Urwin shirked his duty to spend time with his betrothed, and Urwin steadfastly denies it. "I have not spoken to him in 10 years! He destroyed us, for that – " — and here Davian spits on the ground — " – woman!" Then he pauses: "but he is now the burgomaster, so perhaps, I can change my mind." The wereravens have also been fending off frequent attacks by Baba Lysaga's scarecrow constructs, and three weeks ago a second gem was dug up and taken during one; Davian believes Baba Lysaga has it. Five days ago evil druids stole the third and final gem and bore it to Yester Hill; the wereravens' counterattack failed, as "The druids and their blights proved more than a match for the lycanthropes." Two days ago the druids returned with a horde of blights, drove Davian's family from the winery, and poisoned the fermentation vats, leaving only a few bottles and barrels of drinkable wine. "If you do not find the gems, the people of Barovia will be cursed to suffer under Strahd and the Vistani and drink no more!"

The party asks whether he has asked Strahd for help. Davian stares silently, then says that "Lord von Zarovich, when he comes here for wine, is always given the finest for himself and his dinner parties. But he has not asked for wine in 100 years!" Slerdax asks, "Is he on the wagon," and a somewhat inebriated Gaxim growls, "Strahd need a drink!" The Martikovs stare at him: "vampires drink blood, and that's a weird wish." Sensing that the party thinks him allied to Strahd, Davian declares, "we are a mission-oriented winery. We are about wine. We believe that you make wine and sell it to all, regardless of living status. We are blessed with being the oldest and only winery."

Slerdax asks about Krezk, having seen the name on the road and heard of it from Ismark as a safe place for Ireena, and Davian tells him all he knows. The fortified village lies near the edge of Strahd's domain, within sight of the wall of mist; its villagers are so terrified of Strahd and his wolves that they never leave. Inside their walls they grow trees for firewood, draw water from a blessed pool, and keep chickens, hares, small pigs and gardens of beets and turnips; "The only thing they depend on from the outside world is wine." Their burgomaster, Dmitri Krezkov, comes from a noble family. Above the village looms the Abbey of Saint Markovia, "once a convent and hospital, now a madhouse overrun with wickedness." After Saint Markovia and her followers failed to overthrow Strahd, the abbey closed itself off; the clergy fought over food and wine until they were killed by each other's hands or driven insane by Strahd's terror. Over a century ago, a "strikingly handsome and extremely persuasive" pilgrim from a distant land reopened the abbey; "Eternally young," he presides there still, known simply as the Abbot. Davian interrupts himself: "The Abbot is Strahd! It is so much like him to drive his enemies insane, have them kill each other and rule from their place of power over their corpses."

Merrin is appalled — "that does sound very Strahd like." The tale of torture and evil wakes Marron, who screams to let her take over. While Merrin battles Marron for control, Davian tells the party that the only way into Krezk is with wine; the citizens will let no one in otherwise. But he has only three barrels: "Do I send my three barrels to my worthless son, who is now mayor? Or do you want them to enter Krezk?" Gaxim looks at him: "two barrels." Davian smiles: "Of course, I have two barrels. The laws of hospitality require that I give this barrel to you." After some thought the heroes decide to send one to Blue Water Inn and use the other to enter Krezk — "After you find my gems," says Davian.

As they fall asleep, the party ponders their reality: "The only way out is with Strahd's death or their own." They know where he is and can visit him whenever they want, "But as they look around Barovia, they realize that there are fates worse than death for those trapped in this land."
