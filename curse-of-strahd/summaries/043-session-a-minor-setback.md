# Chapter 43: A minor setback

Date: Day 27 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
The chapter opens among the crypts, each section headed by the name carved over a crypt door. At the crypt of *Tasha Petrovna—Healer of Kings, Light unto the West, Servant, Companion (11)*, someone announces, "I’m just gonna keep reading doors unless someone says otherwise."

At the crypt of *Sir Leonid Krushkin (Sir Lee the Crusher): Bigger than life, he loved his jewelry (10)*, Wukong takes the cue: "Otherwise," he says, "anyone else interested, or is it all of me?" KP shakes his head. The party bickers over shares — "Do I share with Mr. Gaxim?" — and Ez answers, "I will have zome." When someone asks, "So KP will forgo his share?", the reply comes back, "No, I was commenting on the question of your integrity." Wukong enters and finds a giant skeleton draped in jewelry and rags atop an elongated marble slab, with a bloodstained maul strung with cobwebs leaning against it. Three jeweled necklaces, worth 750 gp each, lie across Sir Lee’s skeleton. Gaxim picks up the maul and discards it when KP indicates it has no magic.

At the crypt of *Duchess Dorfniya Dilisnya (8)*, Wukong is conflicted: he wants to get out of the Hell Dimension, but he has his retirement to worry about. Gaxim’s sword prompts him to ask: "You like a dragon. No stores to spend money. Just sit on the money." The rogue stares at the barbarian and answers that when he escapes he intends "to have a very long vacation." He adds, "I expect to survive this and escape. I don’t understand what the rest of your plans are.”" Slerdax sighs and opens the door. Inside, a skeleton draped in rags lies on a marble slab, and a handsome quilt depicting a royal feast hangs on the back wall. Someone concludes: "Earlier grave robbers."

The next crypt, its name obscured by fog (7), stands open, its stone door on the floor. A skull, some bones and bits of rusted armor lie on a marble slab with a leering stone gargoyle squatting at each end. While the party debates whether the gargoyles are monsters or statues, Ezmerelda stands up, disgusted at the inscription to *Endorovich (Endorovich the Terrible): What the blood of a hundred wars did not do, the spurn of a woman accomplished*: "What a sexist, misogynistic thing to write". She declares, "There is nothing of value here." Wukong is about to object but thinks this might not be the right time to talk about treasure and the dead.

At the door marked *Herein lie the ones who walk the path of pain and torment (1)*, Slerdax says, "this could be it!" and opens it on a hewn tunnel of stone (81), cut into the Pillarstone of Ravenloft itself — slick-surfaced, its ceiling barely 6 feet high, with a lingering fog that limits visibility to a few feet. Gaxim looks from Slerdax to the fog and back, expecting the magic-user to do something. Slerdax weighs his options: "I could cast thunderwave, but I am running low on spells. Or could I try and freeze it? Or maybe dispel magic?"

Bored, the barbarian marches in with Ezmerelda right behind. They fall into a chute of polished black marble (82) and slide into the darkness at the junction of another hallway. There, a mold-covered ceiling hangs three feet above still, black water five feet deep (74). Gaxim notices ten-foot-square cells lining both sides of the hall behind iron bars; one is dimly lit. Ezmerelda shouts, "do not split the party!" Wukong rushes after her, falling down the chute.

Strahd materializes out of the darkness: "what a pleasant surprise, I thought I would have two victims, and now I have three!" He twirls his fingers, and Ez screams, "FIREBALL, HIT THE DIRT!" — a warning just in time. After the blast, Strahd moves through the wall to avoid being a target of a certain mage. "NON! Rudolph said he had these powers to walk through the castles! We MUST be careful!"

Meanwhile, in the catacombs, vampires jump down silently behind the rest of the party at the entrance of the hallway, and a vampire spawn rises out of the fog in front of them, blocking them from reaching Gaxim and d'Avenir. KP avoids the vampires and slides the chute. Down in the dungeon, Ezmerelda drops her dust of dryness into the standing water, which falls from 5’ deep to 1’ deep. KP is confused — "but I can breathe water; there was no need." — and she answers, "Oui, but it’s easier to cast spells, non?"

The vampire spawn behind Slerdax whispers, "the master demands we keep you here while he toys with the others!" A misty step and a run put Slerdax with the others in the cell, leaving the spawn flailing at the air. Merrin finds the hallway ahead and her retreat both blocked and pauses in fear. Marron tries to take control — "I CAN DO THIS!" — but Merrin regains her wits, charges one of the vampires, pushes it 5 feet, and slides through the gap as they try to grab her. In the cage, Gaxim, with Wukong’s help, opens the cage doors.

More vampire spawns enter the prison: "the master will punish you for your insolence!" Strahd casts another fireball from within a wall, this time without warning: "KP, I do recall how I defeated you last time, you arrogant fool." The chapter records that "The vistani is knocked unconscious." Slipping into the walls, Strahd tells the spawn coming from the chute and from the prison, "kill them for me, my children!" KP casts a wall of force as a sphere to block the vampires, but Merrin is on the wrong side of it. The genasi mage pours a potion of greater healing down Ez’s throat and she is saved; she hugs Slerdax in appreciation and gives KP a withering stare. KP points to the wall of force, where several vampire spawns are trying to bite through it: "Fireballs? I had to do something about them and that."

Strahd is furious: "Again, again, you thwart me Knazaporium! But you are a worthy adversary. A toy that doesn’t break. I destroyed you last time and grew my army. Your arrogance will lead to your defeat again!" Everyone braces, and nothing happens. The Count turns to KP and attempts to charm him, but KP is unaffected, and Strahd flees into the darkness, realizing that victory will be snatched from his fingers this time. A vampire spawn slides down the chute and grapples Merrin. KP smiles and offers a destination: "Barovia, Vallaki, the top of the tower, thoughts?" Merrin shouts, "The Church of Barovia," and the party escapes and hurries into Mordenkainen’s Mansion. KP notes that they were handed a marginal defeat. In Castle Ravenloft, Strahd howls into the winds; he knows he had them trapped.

## NPCs
### Strahd
Materializes in the dungeon and greets them: "what a pleasant surprise, I thought I would have two victims, and now I have three!" He casts fireball twice — the second time from within a wall — moves through walls, and sets his vampire spawn on the party ("kill them for me, my children!"). He singles out KP, whom he claims to have destroyed before: "Again, again, you thwart me Knazaporium! But you are a worthy adversary. A toy that doesn’t break." His attempt to charm KP fails, and he flees, then howls into the winds in Castle Ravenloft, knowing he had them trapped. The chapter also calls him "The Count".

### KP
Called "Knazaporium" by Strahd. He judges the maul to have no magic, slides down the chute, says he can breathe water, and casts a wall of force as a sphere that holds off the spawn but leaves Merrin outside. Strahd's charm does not affect him. He teleports the group out and notes that they were handed a marginal defeat.
