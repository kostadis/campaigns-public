# Chapter 41: Descent into the catacombs

Date: Day 27 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary

The chapter opens with the party on the high tower roof of Ravenloft, looking down through a hole into the room below while the sky is stormy. The spiral staircase ends at a five-foot-wide stone walkway that circles a shaft; in the center of the tower's highest floor, "a fifteen-foot-diameter hole drops into the cold heart of Ravenloft itself." Cold air rushes up out of the shaft, arrow slits line the walls, and aging beams support a steep, cone-shaped roof.

When the party descends into the room, they hear burly men grumbling to each other in a Vistani accent: "Strahd thinks there is something up there. There is nothing." / "You want to tell him that?" Slerdax quickly discusses with KP what he should do, since their plans hinge on Slerdax being able to hide. The exchange runs: "Monsieur Slerdax, do you not have invisibility?" / "Minor Illusion?" (the chapter does not attribute either line).

Slerdax gets inspired. He climbs on the roof, climbs down to an arrowslit, and plans to cast thunderwave on the thugs. He slips. "It's a 380-foot drop." Slerdax casts feather fall and calmly looks for the broom of flying, but he doesn't have it, and reminds himself that none of this would have happened if he had used the broom. Somewhere, somehow, Slerdax heard that and says, "IT WAS MY IDEA!"

Gaxim charges into combat, killing one thug and wounding the other. Ezmerelda turns on KP: "Zis is your fault. He was a good mage, but now you want to inspire him to do crazy maneuvers." She hops on the broom of flying and races after Slerdax. Wukong kills the injured thug with his bow.

As the head of the first thug rolls down and the second body tumbles down the stairs, the surviving thugs talk it over. One says, "I thought you said there was nobody here." The other answers, "well, Fred and Mark’s head suggest otherwise." They weigh running against telling Strahd they ran, and one concludes, "No, I suppose there are worse fates than dying at the hands of the Barbarian." KP kills all but one of them with a fireball. The last one, seeing everyone dead, disengages and runs, turning first to one of the dead thugs: "Frank, my brother, I will take my chances with Strahd!"

Meanwhile, Slerdax ignores Ezmerelda screaming, "I AM COMING FOR YOU, MONSIEUR SLERDAX," misty steps into the tower, and kills the last vistani with a magic missile. The chapter gives the Vistani's last thoughts: "This was supposed to be a simple job. And then I get ganged up by these crazy adventurers. My brother is killed. And then I am killed by a Magic-User who teleports in front of me. Not my day."

The party stares at the 380-foot drop. Slerdax urges that they jump down using feather fall; KP and Merrin decide to walk down, and the group takes the steps.

The group reaches the chapel level and continues down the stairs, another 40 feet. Slerdax smiles: "I am in peak physical condition! Didn’t even break a sweat!" At the bottom of the stairs are the catacombs, buried deep beneath the keep of Ravenloft, with arched ceilings supported by wide, hollow columns that double as crypts. Cobwebs hang limp, a thick fog clings to a floor covered in putrid waste, and "The black ceiling is moving." Gaxim and KP dig deep through their arcane lore and arrive at the same conclusion: "there is something evil here!" The sword reminds Gaxim, who shouts, "treasure in Sergei’s tomb! Must find!"

As KP passes a crypt, he sees the name of "Patrina Velikovna—Bride." The group recognizes her as the sister of Kasimir. Everyone else wants to move on, but KP is too intrigued at the idea of meeting a Bride of Strahd, and he has the spells necessary to raise Patrina Velikovna, so he enters. From the darkness comes "a spectral elf maiden twisted by the horror of her undead existence." She wails.

Slerdax finds the visage pleasing and casts a chromatic orb. "The orb crackles with lightning energy, but the Banshee is resistant." Gaxim is frightened and runs up the stairs. KP, "The Great but unwise KP", is frightened too; as he runs he recalls what he knew about the Banshee and casts a magic missile. Patrina wails again: Ezmerelda is knocked unconscious and all but Gaxim take necrotic damage. Patrina then moves into the crypt across from hers (18). Slerdax follows, reading on the door, "Ireena Kolyana: Wife."

Wukong draws his bow to kill the Banshee and lectures: "Banshees are not always evil female elves. It’s an unfortunate ignorant trope created by the hack sage Gygax." The group is not comforted. Wukong kills her.

KP casts detect magic and finds nothing. Wukong looks in Patrina's tomb and the group plunders Patrina's treasure. Slerdax examines Ireena's tomb: it has swirly mists of perpetual fog and is clean. Merrin has healed Ezmerelda, who, now aware, shouts, "Mon Dieu, this is what he was planning to do!" KP asks, "Bury a dead person?" Ezmerelda answers, "This iz why you were defeated, Monsieur KP!" and turns to Slerdax for "A teaching moment, your thoughts?" The answer comes: "He planned to turn her into a vampire and have this be her crypt?" Then: "Well done! You are correct!" (the chapter does not name the speaker of either line).
