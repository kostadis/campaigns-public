# Chapter 17: The hags

Date: Day 11 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
Our heroes wake up after the drunken celebration and announce their intention to take back the windmill from the hags. That prompts Davian to ask whether they would take two barrels of wine to his son, the new burgomaster of Vallaki. The grouchy and cranky Gaxim answers, "No, go to Vallaki." He is still angry at how his brother was killed and not appreciated by the citizenry. When Adrian is volunteered to make things easier, Gaxim only gets more annoyed: "Easy die! No, take!"

Along the way the party notices a skeleton. Merrin approaches and notices, to her horror, that it looks like her. Marron screams, "Let me take care of this!" Merrin resists and recovers, and as she walks away the skeleton dissolves into ashes. Adrian remarks, "that was odd. Have you displeased Strahd?" Gaxim replies, "Strahd Friend! Strahd invite to Dinner!" Shown the invitation, Adrian warns, "The Count is not very forgiving of people who do not accept his summons." The mage is unimpressed: "He disappears for 100 years, and it’s okay. He sends one stinking invitation, and you have to drop everything. Who does he think he is?" The answer — "The lord of Barovia? The cursed one!" — impresses no one.

After the party takes a turn towards Vallaki, Adrian looks apprehensive. A few moments later a skeleton on a skeletal horse comes racing towards them: "The Izgiben! These are the remains of those foolish enough to attempt an escape through the mists." Gaxim waves the skeleton down, the monster takes that as a signal to attack, and the party makes short work of it.

At Vallaki the captain of the guard is delighted to greet them, salutes them as the town's saviors, and thanks them for restoring St. Andral’s. Merrin corrects him, "Heironeous!" He answers, "Yes, it is true. But we call it by its old name. These new gods, they take time to take."

The heroes continue towards the windmill. After 15 miles of hard work they reach a fork in the road: Svalich road continues, and the fork goes into the hills. They take the fork. Here the Old Svalich Road turns from a winding path through the Balinok Mountains into a lazy trail descending into a fog-filled valley, with a walled town near the shores of a great mountain lake. A branch leads west to a promontory topped by a dilapidated stone windmill, its warped wooden vanes stripped bare.

The onion-domed windmill leans forward and to one side. A decrepit wooden platform encircles it above a flimsy doorway, and on a beam above the door a raven hops about and squawks, seemingly agitated. Gaxim is pleased, convinced the raven is protecting their windmill: "Protector!" The raven tries to warn the party away. Slerdax and Merrin quickly divine that the squawks warn about the dangers, but the party has one word in their head, "The Children!" — except for Wukong, who has the word treasure.

The group enters a filthy ground floor turned makeshift kitchen: baskets and old dishware, a peddler’s cart, a chicken coop, a heavy wooden trunk, and a pretty wooden cabinet with flowers painted on its doors. Toads croak, the sweet smell of pastries blends horridly with a stench from an open, upright barrel in the center of the room, warmth comes from a brick oven, and shrieks and cackles from higher up make the old mill shudder. Wukong opens the trunk and is covered in happy toads running to freedom.

A haggard, heavyset old woman in a bloodstained, flour-caked apron, a long, sharp bodkin impaling her bundled-up gray hair, comes down the stairs. Gaxim recognizes her as the peddler: "More Pies?" She replies, "Here, we do serious business. If you want a pie, buy them like everyone else." "My windmill!" bellows the half-orc, and waves the contract in her face: "This says so!" Apparently Wukong had explained the situation to the barbarian. She is unmoved: "Ah yes, that deed paperwork. You are not the first guests of Strahd to come through that old house with that paper." She asks whether they have seen the march of the fools, and says that every so often one of those poor dead fools comes to her windmill and tells her that "Morgantha and her daughters don’t own this place." She has two daughters — "This is our home!" — and produces "a paper signed with the official seal of Barovia." Merrin examines it: it declares the mill a legitimate business whose owners can store Barovian children and the use thereof, and the mill belongs, in perpetuity, to Morgantha and her progeny. Its date is after the one on the party's deed.

Slerdax is done. He casts produce flame, surprises the hag, and burns the papers. Morgantha approaches the pot: "You seem to want to take Morgantha’s property." "It’s not yours!" says Slerdax, pointing to the deed in Gaxim's hands. "Strahd says otherwise. And I don’t like you. Unless you are going to buy some pastries, begone!" Gaxim offers to buy a pastry, complains at the cost, and wonders if anyone would pay it. Morgantha smiles wickedly: "people will pay a lot more for these pies." When the party presses with questions she grows very angry — "Either buy more pies or get out of my house!" Gaxim asks, "If Buy 1 pie, ask 1 question?" and she stares at him: "If you EAT a pie, you can ask whatever you want." Gaxim shrugs and starts to eat. "Gaxim, the pie is children," says Slerdax. Gaxim is horrified, throws the pie down, and grabs his weapons.

Morgantha taps her magic pot and summons a dretch. Merrin is paralyzed with fear, remembering the horrors the demonic powers inflicted on her; Marron laughs and tells Merrin, "hide behind me!" Slerdax's 2nd level thunderwave flings the Dretch against the wall, catches Wukong, and only marginally affects Morgantha. Gaxim kills the demon, and Wukong does 18 HP of damage with a sword.

Two more hags rush down the stairs. The first casts eyebite on Gaxim, who is unaffected, and the hag curses. The second casts a phantasmal killer on Gaxim, and the barbarian — surprisingly — is frightened: it is an image of his brother repeatedly dying in a tight loop. The third hag hits Slerdax, Gaxim and Marron with a lightning bolt. Wukong evaluates the situation with one thought: "there is a door, and I can get to it without being attacked." Marron misses her attack. Morgantha counters Slerdax's next spell with a counterspell. Wukong rubs his bow tattoo, stares at Morgantha, and nocks an arrow — but his bowstring breaks and the arrow falls down, and he runs the hell out. The frightened Gaxim flees through the open door, dodging a hag's slash. Marron is affected by the eyebite and runs far away. Morgantha casts a lightning bolt on Wukong. The last hag asks, "should I try and kill one too, mother?" — "Of course, dear." She targets Slerdax and kills the mage. Morgantha cackles: "Don’t come to deal with a business you don’t understand. This house is protected by the hags!" The chapter text breaks off mid-sentence at this point.
