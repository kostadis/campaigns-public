# Chapter 23: From one castle to a bigger one

Date: Days 13–14 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
After the end of yet another battle, Merrin heals the party and the heroes discuss what to do next. Gaxim, prodded by the sword, together with "the intrinsic nobility of bringing peace to Argynvost and the knights and the self-interest of avoiding having to deal with homicidal revenants", suggests they visit Strahd while holding the invitation. Slerdax agrees and proposes casting find familiar so they can explore the area. He also poses a hypothetical: what would happen if the revenants saw the group fighting with Strahd? Given Vladimir's promise of vengeance — Vladimir being the revenants' leader — the likely outcome is that the revenants attack the party instead of Strahd, and it is "entirely possible that the revenants may choose to attack the party anyways because they see them as a threat to their vengeance."

The heroes then reflect that Morgantha is "the real day-to-day evil in Barovia." While Strahd sleeps for a hundred years, she goes around passing pasties "made up from ground-up children", sends heroes to find allies where all they can see is vengeance, and she and her coven laugh as they destroy Strahd's enemies. When Slerdax had asked Morgantha what she wanted, she convinced the party that she was perhaps on their side, raging against Strahd as much as anyone in Barovia. "But, perhaps, Barovia is her playground, and that she is the real evil."

After reflecting on the nature of evil, the heroes finish exploring the castle for new clues. Gaxim charges into the chapel balcony, a wooden balcony overhanging the mansion's chapel, with an exquisitely carved wooden throne between two doors and an iron chandelier whose candleholders are shaped "like tiny silver dragons." The half-orc barbarian explores the throne, "intrigued at this artifice of civilized life." Wukong enters a storage room (Q23) where rainwater has pooled on the sagging wooden floor; as he reflects on the piping and the sewage system, the floor collapses and he ends up in the dining room of the first floor. Slerdax confirms Wukong is okay and checks the adjoining bathroom (Q24), whose faded mural of a mountainscape he recognises as the mountain range to the south of Barovia.

Merrin reflects on the betrayal of Morgantha, who promised allies but instead sent the group to their doom — though fate, and Heironeous, had a different plan, bringing Ezmerelda d'Avenir to their group. Gaxim is unmoved: "Morgantha, lied! Morgantha, Die!"

The party heads downstairs to explore the last of the palace. In the plundered kitchen (Q10), an open iron pot rattles on its hook in the blackened fireplace "as though something is inside it." Merrin opens it, and a single harmless bat flies out. She shrugs and moves into what she later discovers is the servants' quarters, a circular room strewn with the wreckage of half a dozen beds. Wukong searches and mutters, "A dragon likes to hoard its wealth, not to share with the help." There is nothing to be found.

Standing next to the northern portcullis, the heroes hear hooves and wagon wheels on gravel. The sword prods Gaxim to ask, "Strahd?" The smiling cleric answers, "No, it’s a draft horse with a wagon". The driver is obviously drunk and must have lost the road on his way up. From the parlor, through tattered velvet drapes, the party watches him: he wears the clothes of a Vistani, parks the cart, relieves himself on the dragon's statue, grumbles, unclips the wagon, and heads off with his horse. The parlor itself holds dust, cobwebs, a damaged brass chandelier and a faded mural of metallic dragons and colorful birds; its valuables are long gone, except for a deck of cards titled "Heroes, the Gathering," which Gaxim picks up. Wukong, "who is always alert", recalls that the Vistani was from Vallaki, part of the group allied to Strahd, on whom Rictavio wanted revenge.

Gaxim rushes to the cart and discovers it carries a coffin. There is a name on it that the sword reads for him, "but he cares not." The heroes wonder aloud how to safely open the coffin and kill a vampire while an obviously irritated Ezmerelda stares at them, until she finally announces herself: "interplanar-vampire-killer, trained by the great van Richten, here." The heroes sheepishly ask for advice, and she delivers a lecture on the silly stories heard "from bards at taverns", concluding, "The ease with which you can kill a vampire makes you wonder how Strahd lives." Gaxim shows her a stake — "how use?" — and Merrin produces some bread — "holy wafers?" — and Ezmerelda answers: "Non! Magical weapons, spells, and someone to chop off his head." Just before opening the casket she notes, "But Monsieur Gaxim, this has your name on it!" Gaxim, neither surprised nor concerned, growls, "open!"

A swarm of bats flies out and attacks Gaxim. From a safe distance Wukong fires a single arrow that produces a miracle shot, skewering many of the bats; the weight of the arrow and its skewered bats cripples many of the remaining bats, and the few uninjured creatures fly away. Gaxim is amused. Instead of treasure, the coffin holds a note: "My master sends his regards, Henrik van der Voort". Apparently, the coffin maker of Vallaki is mocking the heroes.

Slerdax urges the group to rest, but Gaxim pounds his Sunsword against his chest: "Gaxim BRAVE!" On the way to the mausoleum the heroes pass through the castle's fog-shrouded graveyard behind the mansion, enclosed by a seven-foot-tall wrought-iron fence. The graves have been disturbed, and creatures appear to have dug out from them. An unattributed voice explains: "Ah, Oui! Of course! These were used by the revenants." Gaxim points at the graveyard and then at Slerdax — "Rest here?" — and laughs. "Everyone is shocked to hear him crack a joke."

The mausoleum, its roof clung with tarnished, silver-plated gargoyles shaped like dragon wyrmlings, has a white marble door engraved with a name: Argynvost. Inside it is empty, but there is writing on the wall in draconic. None of them speak the language, so Wukong, "master of Haut Couture", demonstrates his forgery skills; the copy is a perfect match. After some debate the heroes decide to head to Vallaki, where Merrin can check on the state of her temple and the party can find a mage to translate the text.

As they prepare to leave the castle, Arrigal, the leader of the Vistani at the Vallaki encampment, arrives with three other men and a pair of dire wolves. Ezmerelda says "Shit"; Merrin asks if it is an old boyfriend; Ezmerelda snarls that "he’s dead" and "it’s much worse." Arrigal screams that she's a horse thief. Marron intones disapprovingly that it is not lawful. Ezmerelda protests, "I saved your lives. And it wasn’t so much theft as a borrowing out of stark necessity." Asked whether she will return it, she mutters something that sounds like "never save a Paladin, that’s what Rudolph said," then says aloud, "Of course, of course!" The Vistani leader is not satisfied: "you have stained the honor of the Vistani and are a horse thief. You must be punished."

Wukong, with some experience of horse-thieves and scoundrels, reads them as blowhards not really interested in a fight. Wukong and Slerdax show the dark powers' manifestations on their bodies, and Slerdax casts a minor illusion that convinces the Vistani to back off. The frustrated Vistani curses the rogue: he can no longer manipulate buttons on his clothes, and the master of Haut Couture needs help to take off his treasures. "The dressmaker can no longer button himself, “laughs the Vistani." The reply, unattributed in the chapter: "Haut-couture, it’s HAUT-COUTURE, you philistine."

The heroes ask Ezmerelda why she stole the horse: "Because I heard it was populated with heroes that could help me defeat Strahd." Gaxim asks, "Morgantha say?" She answers with a question — "The pie maker?" — and when Gaxim nods, she says, "I will kill her." Marron: "That’s our next stop."

The journey to Vallaki is uneventful. On arrival they learn that everyone must smile in Vallaki; posters everywhere require it, and a new happy festival this Sunday is mandatory — "All triggered because Merrin was – oh so persuasive." Merrin is delighted, knowing "that a smile a day keeps the vampire away."

At the Blue Water Inn, Rictavio greets Ezmerelda — "How are you doing?" — and she glares at him. In the moment of extreme awkwardness Gaxim asks, "you lovers?" Wukong and Slerdax conclude it is a platonic mentor-mentee relationship and tell Gaxim, "No." Gaxim shrugs.

Merrin heads off to her new temple of Heironeous, where Milivoj assures her that everyone is smiling and adoring the new god — although all of the rituals appear to be those of the morninglords. She finds the tithe box full and asks herself, "Can I be a big tent cleric?" Weighing the cash, she concludes, "Perhaps the Morninglord is Heironeous’ manifestation in this plane?"

By the time she returns, the Blue Water Inn has become the mayor's guards' watering hole. Lady Martikov greets her: "Our benefactors and heroes!" Merrin asks how things are, and is told, "Well, ever since you convinced Urwin to implement the full smiling policy, everyone has been smiling." Merrin replies, "Excellent." Wukong and Slerdax notice that as far as Lady Martikov is concerned, it is not excellent, and several people around the bar look less than delighted that Merrin is responsible for "this new bout of smiling insanity."

As the party is about to leave in the morning, Rictavio shouts, "You need someone to translate something?" Taking the paper from Wukong, he asks, "Do you speak Draconic? This is excellent penmanship." Wukong, pleased, answers, "No, a forger." Rictavio translates the text: *Here lie the bones and treasures of Argynvost, lord of Argynvostholt and founder of the Order of the Silver Dragon.* Ezmerelda thinks it is again "a hero of good who offers a useless clue". Gaxim invites Rictavio — "You come? We kill Strahd." — and Rictavio, seeing the look on Ezmerelda's face, declines: "No. Not this time. I’m a lone wolf." She shouts, "LONE WOLF? You are a narcissistic sociopath who has attachment issues!" Wukong takes Ezmerelda out of the room before it gets very awkward.

The heroes settle on a new plan: their invitation is for dinner, so they will go to the castle and explore it using Slerdax's find familiar spell. Standing outside the castle doors, with the sun still on the horizon, Slerdax casts it. The familiar uncovers a chapel and some stairs on the ground floor, goes downstairs (K18), finds the underground "incredibly spooky" and comes back up. The tower is further than Slerdax can control his familiar, so it goes back through the chapel and up the stairs on the other side (K16), then takes the circular stairway to the third floor, where it finds "the cake from the failed window from over 400 years ago."
