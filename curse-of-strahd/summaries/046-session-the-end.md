# Chapter 46: The end?

Date: Day 28 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
Strahd has gone below the castle. KP stares and thinks to himself, "excellent, Strahd has tied one hand behind my back! He has hidden!" Before anyone can react, KP casts time stop, and the others' objections are caught mid-word. The rain stops, the lightning stops mid bolt, and "The silence is eerie." KP and Strahd once again engage in "their twisted game of chess." Strahd, taking advantage of his ability to haste, has fled the battle and is running towards the catacombs.

KP flies off the landing and looks underneath, seeing a 900-foot drop to the ground. He casts mold earth where Strahd was, but the Count has already moved on. "Hmm, there must be something magical on the count." He does some quick calculations and computes a flight pattern that will hit every location in a 60-foot sphere where the count could be hiding. The frozen heroes' equipment glows — "Nope." — and then, "in the frozen dusk of Ravenloft, is a hovering bit of glowing magic." Mold earth drops dirt that reveals the outline of one fleeing Strahd. "Gotcha," he thinks. A sunbeam erupts from his hands, and time flows again.

"You arrogant … " screams Ezmerelda. KP appears hovering over a hole he created: "okay, time to go!" Before she can interrupt, he corrects himself — "well, not quite yet. We need to arrive after he rematerializes and before he recovers in his coffin. So, let's wait for a few …." She smiles; "KP has learned much." He is not comfortable with the variables: they are exposed to attacks from Strahd's minions, but they need a place to wait, "So we wait here." The heroes find cover from the rain and sit down.

The puddle on the floor boils, and a cluster of horrible, snakelike heads forms out of the murky water and lunges at them. There are six. KP, Merrin, and the Vistani are surprised. Gaxim rages, kills one "waving his bright sword in the darkness" and wounds another. The wounded creature crawls onto Merrin and suffocates her with its watery body. Another jumps on KP, but he is immune, because he always casts water breathing in the morning as part of his daily rituals — a practice he read about in a story of adventurers who had defeated the Temple of Elemental Evil. Wukong shoots the creature on KP and hits; the nature of the monster makes KP absorb half the damage from the arrow, but it dies. "KP glares." Another creature tries to grapple Ezmerelda and misses. Slerdax casts a fireball and kills two. The creature on Merrin inflicts 20 points of necrotic damage that the Cleric can partly resist, and Ezmerelda kills it with a magic missile, "adding injury to the insult of being suffocated." Gaxim kills the last one. "Time to go," grunts the Half-orc.

The group huddles over what to do. "The Banshee is the problem, " KP mutters. "I could time a thunderwave?" Slerdax proposes. "Good, but not good enough." "Silence!" growls Gaxim. "Yes, a silence spell! What an excellent idea!" — and KP casts silence on the half-orc. Ezmerelda privately thinks "well, they could have used ear wax, but this 17th-dimensional chess has worked so far!" KP teleports the group back into the catacombs. "Poof."

In the catacombs the Docent is waiting, trying to warn them about the dangerous trap Strahd has prepared, but the silence spell makes it impossible. He sighs. The heroes advance to the portcullis guarding Strahd's tomb, and Wukong, drawing on the dark power pact coursing through his veins, opens the gates. Gaxim thinks, "Wukong strong! Wukong Brave!" Not as brave as "our mage, who walks into Strahd's tomb expecting to find a casket, not four red dragons."

Behind them, Rahadin and three vampire spawn appear. Rahadin's speech — "Do you think you are the first heroes who have made it this far? Strahd knew that you would be here." — is one "our heroes would have heard" had they not been under the silence spell. Slerdax suggests a fireball with hand signals; KP objects — "a fireball on RED dragons?" — and he shapes his hands like a sun rising and points to the enemies behind. The junior mage gets the point, moves out of the silent area surrounding Gaxim, and casts Dawn on Rahadin and his vampire spawn. One vampire sees Dawn; the other two shield their eyes in time. Rahadin just stands there, taunting Slerdax, then charges Slerdax and Wukong at the rear. "The sounds of all the victims that he has ever killed penetrate into their minds." Slerdax takes damage but holds concentration; a furious Rahadin strikes him twice with his swords, and Slerdax keeps his focus despite 17 points of damage.

Ezmerelda steps out of the zone of silence and casts a lightning bolt that smashes through the coffin and hits one of the dragons; the coffin is damaged "But still protects Strahd." Gaxim strikes it twice with the sword of Sergei, "the rage of two heroes in every blow", but it won't fall apart. The Cleric advances on the coffin, and Marron screams, "you are going to hit the monster? You? I was made for this! This is my purpose." Merrin hits twice — "I don't need you." The coffin cracks but doesn't break; Merrin just barely misses it, and "Marron laughs in her head."

The dragons roar and summon lava from the ground: KP takes 20, Merrin 21 fire damage, and Gaxim, resistant, only 4. The last dragon summons a poisonous cloud. Slerdax and KP are incapacitated; KP's sunbeam sputters and the Dawn ends. Two vampires try and fail to grapple Slerdax. In the coffin room the first dragon breathes on KP for 36 points of damage; the next breathes on KP, Wukong and Merrin. KP takes less, but Merrin can't hold her concentration, and Marron screams, "YOU FOOL! YOU ARROGANT SELF-IMPORTANT FOOL!" Out of the darkness, Kasimir's sister emerges "ready to wail and kill." The third vampire spawn, "dressed in the fine clothes of a Barovian nobleman", grabs Slerdax and pulls him aside, out of KP's line of sight — and Slerdax knows that when the teleport happens, KP must see him. The two remaining dragons breathe on the heroes, "scorching for 42 damage."

Slerdax misty steps into Strahd's tomb, looks at the damaged coffin, is tempted to use mold earth, "but just casts a magic missile!" The coffin shatters, and "Strahd can't hide his surprise as death takes him into the black abyss." His rage makes the Pillarstone of Ravenloft tremble, shaking dust from the ceiling of the tomb, until his hatred melts away, "replaced at last with relief." His corpse deteriorates until "only bones, dust, and noble garb remain. Strahd von Zarovich, the dark lord of Barovia, is dead and gone."

Rahadin screams, "Master!" Gaxim tries to convince him it is over; Rahadin is unconvinced and unleashes the horror in his mind on Merrin and Wukong, inflicting 28 points of damage on the Cleric. Ezmerelda stares at KP and casts mirror image, hoping the mage will get them out and needing anything to protect herself from the Dragons if he doesn't. Gaxim turns on the injured dragon, swings his sword twice and kills it; with "the dragon's blood over his body", he turns on another creature and does 27 HP. Merrin heals everyone with mass cure and a mass healing word. The Banshee prepares to wail with an evil smile: "If KP can't cast teleport, Strahd will have revenge, even in death." The dragons release poison in the crypt and all eyes turn to KP, but "KP was ready this time and avoided the poisonous gas." As the Banshee is about to wail, "the gnome clicks his fingers, and the party disappears."

The post-script tells what follows. The fog surrounding the land thins and no longer harms those who pass through it; the dark clouds give way to sunshine, "shocking the Barovians out of their despair." Most Barovians realize they have nowhere to go; a few depart. Those with souls can leave the valley, while those without "fade into nothingness" as they step beyond the edge of Strahd's former domain. "Zis is not over yet," declares Ezmerelda.

Then the fates of the heroes who step through the mists. Gaxim returns to Fax and opens a bar; the Knights of the Knife try to recruit him, but "Gaxim wanted nothing more than to drink with his friends." He gives everyone free drinks and says he serves the best drinks, "except nothing will ever be as good as Strahd's champagne", losing money every night, which doesn't matter "because he's rich." A pair of artists often play free there, "a halfling and a very tall and fat cleric." KP moves on to further interplanar research, having discovered that it is possible to visit the dark planes and escape them, and "tells the author of this manuscript to not meddle in his affairs." Merrin is "finally free of Marron. The trial is over." She becomes a founder of a religious sect "that insists on the power of smiles to ward off evil." The vistani leave the valley and warn everyone who wishes to be free not to stay long. The bats, wolves and dire wolves of Barovia lose their supernatural link to Strahd and become ordinary beasts, and Castle Ravenloft remains a haunted place shunned by all Barovians.

"But… Not all is well." Stepping through the mists, Slerdax accepts an offer of power and glory from the Dark Powers "that he could not refuse." He founds Slerdax's Family Winery and Restaurant, famous throughout the planes, with "the coveted 3 Mordenkainen Stars", and even KP writes about it in his tome of "Things to sample for the discerning inter-planar traveler." But Slerdax's hunger for approval and quest for power corrupt him: the mists roll over the restaurant, which now appears in towns; when Slerdax asks for praise, it rings hollow, he sees "the mockery in his patrons' eyes", turns to murderous rage, and over the subsequent courses each client dies "a horrible death, trapped in the demi-plane."

Wukong decides to plunder Castle Ravenloft despite Ezmerelda's warning. Rahadin tries to stop him, and "our elf kills him." Wukong decides the castle should be his: "After all, the Count is dead. And this is prime real-estate on the Wild Coast."

Ezmerelda's suspicion proves justified. The Dark Powers cause Strahd to re-form after a period of months — "long enough for the Barovians to discover what it feels like to live a life of hope." The mists return, hope turns to despair, and Strahd begins plotting his revenge. Wukong finds himself trapped in Barovia; "Strahd finds the irony of another elf serving as his majordomo delightful." Wukong is cursed to live in Castle Ravenloft, to want to kill Strahd, and to always miss; he has tried to escape through death, but the dark powers raise him again. Marron is split from Merrin and manifests in the dark realms, "finally having an endless supply of evil to fight", becomes dear friends with Ezmerelda d'Avenir, and is cursed never to leave — though Marron does not view being trapped there as a curse, "but instead a calling."

One final note: after the mists reappear, Madam Eva and her Vistani return, the beasts fall under Strahd's spell again, and the burgomasters fortify their settlements. One fine evening another group of adventurers arrives at the encampment on the Tser Pool, and Madam Eva smiles. "Perhaps these heroes will be the ones to kill Strahd, she thinks." She pulls some cards and says – "The fortunes of Ravenloft…."
