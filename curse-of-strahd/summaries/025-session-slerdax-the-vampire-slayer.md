# Chapter 25: Slerdax the vampire slayer

Date: Days 14–15 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
The chapter opens in a courtyard with Ezmerelda d'Avenir declaring herself part of the group: "I used to be a lone wolf," she says, and then, turning to her new friends, "but now I am a member of a wolf pack." The group acknowledges her membership. Ezmerelda notices Strahd standing in the shade, glaring, and warns that "Strahd will not come out while there is still daylight, but twilight is soon upon us."

The heroes debate where they would be safe. Ezmerelda explains, "Strahd cannot enter a home without an invitation." They propose Barovia but reject it, and settle on Vallaki instead, reasoning that Merrin’s Temple will provide enough protection from both the revenant’s rage and Strahd. The journey is uneventful. At the gates of Vallaki the guards are smiling, though the smiles are a little more forced than usual, and very forced when Merrin walks by.

Gaxim carries the dragon’s head over his shoulder as the party marches down the main street. As they pass the home of the coffin maker, he steps out of the darkness and says, "my master will be very  pleased to have his prize back, and he will reward me greatly." Gaxim shrugs, convinced the mere cabinetmaker cannot stop him, but Ezmerelda corrects him: "Monsieur Gaxim, this is no common coffin maker. He is a vampire."

Slerdax smiles and casts a tidal wave, intending to catch the vampire in a pool of moving water — a sign, the chapter notes, that he had been paying attention to Ezmerelda and Rictavio. The vampire avoids the rush of water, charges the magic-user, leaves the pool, and inflicts six damage points. Ezmerelda advises him, "next time, make sure the pool of water is where you are standing." She flanks the vampire and hits with her rapier and hand ax for 18 points of damage. Gaxim drops the Dragon head and attacks with his sword; the light blade cuts through for 34 points. Merrin punctures the creature with her spear for 16, and Slerdax finishes it off with a fourth-level magic missile.

Gaxim, prodded by the sword, asks, "how keep vampire dead?" Ezmerelda tells him to "grab the body, and we will deal with it in the church. There are many allies of the devil in the city." Merrin orders a guard, "you, bring this body to the church." The guardsman, Algor, stares at the dead coffin maker — "you killed a citizen of Vallaki." — and prepares to arrest the heroes. The Vistani sighs, "do you see any blood? This is no man. This is a spawn of the devil." Algor takes off his helmet, rubs his graying hair, and admits, "I always knew there was something fishy about the fact that he would never come out in the middle of the day." She turns to the heroes: "congratulations, my friends, you have killed your first vampire." Gaxim, prodded by the sword, asks, "ready kill Strahd now?" The answer is "No."

With the body in tow, the group rushes toward the Temple and finds eight zombies outside. Milivoj cries out, "these are not zombies. They are Strahd zombies." Merrin, undeterred, casts them out with the power of her god; five of the eight run away, and the other three cannot block the heroes from rushing into the Temple, where they are safe from Strahd and the undead.

An enraged Strahd stands outside the Temple. "Funny meeting you here," says Wukong. The cleric asks, "We are busy burning a body here. Would you like to help us?" Wukong adds, "We were having a delightful dinner. I don’t know why you had to attack us." Strahd answers, "You stole my prize." He looks around the village for something to kill, seeming to want to will the town razed to the ground, and just as he is about to lose control he declares: "I am Count Strahd von Zarovich. Tonight you have won a round of this game. You cannot leave Barovia without me letting you. Enjoy your victory."

The heroes cannot leave it there. The magic-user, beyond irritated, tallies the score: "as I see it, we won two times today. We won once at your house in the middle of the day," then, after a theatrical pause, "and another time tonight," and finally, to Strahd, "so that’s two wins." Merrin joins in: "and I guess we could also count the victory over the effigies, so that’s three?" Gaxim smiles, "Ireena, we win?" — he knows that Ireena and Tatyana can no longer be harmed, and Strahd rages as he remembers how the friendly giant saved Ireena from his clutches. Ezmerelda points at herself and holds up five fingers: "I believe it is five nothing count, not one nothing."

The party presses further. Wukong turns to Slerdax and says that Morgantha, on the other hand, "has bested us a couple of times. She’s much more powerful than Strahd." Slerdax agrees: "she is the real evil in this land. In fact, we’ve heard from people in Barovia that they respect her more than you." Ezmerelda chimes in, "We almost died because we followed her tip," and "We’ve never followed one of yours."

Strahd stares and addresses the burgomaster: "burgomaster, if these creatures are here in the morning, I will kill everyone in this town. They are no longer welcome in Vallaki." Slerdax tries once more to intimidate him with a dismissive tone, but the count will have none of it: "I have stared into more beating hearts of adventurers than you have lived days. Of course, they weren’t beating when I was looking at them. No matter, you cannot frighten me." Slerdax cannot be quiet: "did you like to hold the heart up to your eyes, or did you cut open the cavity of the chest and stare?" Before he leaves, Strahd leaves a note: if the party ever used Vallaki and this Temple as a refuge, he would have the town burned to the ground.

The party is excited, seeing a chance to set a trap — facing Strahd in Vallaki would be easier than meeting him in his castle. Ezmerelda is unconvinced: Strahd has many allies, and he doesn’t always have to get his hands dirty to cause mayhem.

In the morning the burgomaster, Urwin Martikov, arrives at the Temple and demands the heroes leave: "My wife was killed by one of Strahd’s minions." Gaxim shrugs, and Slerdax offers, "we happen to know someone who could raise her from the dead. If that’s what you want?" Urwin is delighted and promises to keep the body safe, but reminds them they only have five days.

The heroes decide that returning the skull to its home is a higher priority, since there is still time to talk to the Abbey. Strahd has them followed as they head to Argynvostholt; the warlock they met earlier tries to stay sufficiently far away.

In the graveyard leading to the mausoleum, two revenants stand in front of the door, blocking the way. The magic-user opens with a fireball for 35 points of damage, though one of them dodges the blast. Ezmerelda rushes to flank and casts a lightning bolt that both fail to dodge, frying them for 29 points. Merrin advances and casts a summoning spirit that causes radiant damage on both vengeful undead. Gaxim rushes to the door with the skull, but it is closed and he cannot open it. The two creatures slash the barbarian with their longswords for 17 points of damage, and the heroes hear the clatter of more revenants rushing down to help their brothers. The cleric’s spell burns the revenants for 12 more points; Wukong notches an arrow and hits true for 20; the magic-user casts a chromatic orb for 24.

Ezmerelda mutters, "Monsieur Slerdax, the ability to inflict damage at scale is importante. It is also essential that you be able to open doors." as she casts a knock and opens the mausoleum door. Merrin punctures a flanked former knight with her spear for 16 points. The barbarian disengages, rushes into the mausoleum, and deposits the skull. Nothing happens, so he calmly walks out and closes the door.

Then the dragon’s spirit rushes from the mausoleum and turns into a bright light that streams up to the top of the tower (Q53). The light flashes across the valley like the beacon of a lighthouse. The mountains keep it from reaching Castle Ravenloft directly, but Strahd can see its glow in the sky to the west. Morgantha stares at the sky and scowls, thinking these fools didn’t die; Madam Eva chuckles, her plans slowly coming together.

At that moment Vladimir Horngaard, the head of the order, arrives in the graveyard. His rage dissipates and his hate goes away. He says, "I am so sorry." If he could cry, he would. The dragon comforts him and tells him all is forgiven. Vladimir reaches out and holds his love, Sir Godfrey — "Forgive me!" — and Sir Godfrey forgives him. With that, the revenants are gone, their armor clattering to the ground.

Slerdax turns to Ezmerelda: "so, you think there is anything else to find here?" To her, this was a touching moment of beauty and justice, and the magic-user just wants to see if there is any treasure. She ponders a cutting word and says, "No. Whatever we were to find here, we found."
