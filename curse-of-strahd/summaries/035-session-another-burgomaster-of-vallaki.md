# Chapter 35: Another Burgomaster of Vallaki

Date: Day 23 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
The chapter opens with Gaxim screaming "Strahd!!!!" and running after the bat through the forest, "his eyes mad with bloodlust", the sword egging him on. At the beach he sees something in the distance and, throwing caution to the wind, swims across the lake, then takes the road east to Ravenloft, determined to catch up with Strahd or kill him in his castle. A few miles out of Vallaki the sword comes to its senses and tells Gaxim to return: killing Strahd in his lair without his friends and the other artifacts will be impossible. He trudges back to the town of Vallaki.

Meanwhile the rest of the heroes cross the lake and notice smoke coming from Merrin's temple. Merrin rushes in through the south gate, past the two guards. When asked "Who do you think you are?" she answers "Priest of the burning temple." Seeing her in full battle regalia, the guards argue among themselves — one warns "The new mayor will be unhappy," another answers "I am not going to die on behalf of some mayor, plus he's so smiley!" — and they let the group in: "All right, you can come in. Don't want to offend some gods." The exchange closes with "Wise move Frederick." / "Thank you, Engels."

Inside there is clearly some chaos. Merrin walks past the clustered townsfolk and finds everyone standing around the fire. Slerdax has seen enough and casts a tidal wave, extinguishing the flames "in a roar of water", but the building has already turned into a smoldering wreck. A middle-aged man of hefty build arrives "curiously wearing the regalia of the office of Burgomaster of Vallaki." In a nasal voice he introduces himself: "Hi, my name is Ratan! The old Burgomaster met an unfortunate demise." Merrin is unimpressed: "And are you prepared to meet the next unforeseen demise?" Ratan freezes, recovers, and explains that agents of the powers that be decided the previous Burgomaster's alliance with the party had led to the loss of a bride, and that the insult to the Count's honor "had to be paid for in blood". "Strahd has this thing about losing brides," he says, and adds, "Let's just say there was a red wedding."

Ratan insists he is "just the local authority that was elevated by Count Zarovich" and had nothing to do with the events; the reply comes, "No, you're merely benefiting from them." Gaxim arrives, shouts "You!", and notices on the new burgomaster "the tell-tale sign of the dark power gift – splotchy skin." He bellows "EVIL!" Ratan reveals that the old burgomaster, his wife, and three children "were butchered in a fire by a bunch of werewolves." To the shout of "RAISE DEAD!", Merrin is about to make the same point when she realizes the bodies are mutilated, and she reflects on the decision to destroy the Dark Vestige that could resurrect the dead. Ratan explains that Strahd wanted everybody in Vallaki and Barovia to know that allying with his enemies "would be met with violent retribution", especially for people in positions of authority.

Ratan then offers, at great length, to trade the location of "the villainous creatures who stole into the night and massacred everyone" for his continued life — "No. I'm not an ally. I am merely providing information under duress." Merrin answers, "I don't know that you have started to see duress just yet." More guards show up, smiling; a momentary thought of revenge passes through them before they see the heroes are more dangerous than the last time and reconsider. When Merrin asks for free run of the town again, the Burgomaster shrugs and sighs. He credits the party with freeing Vallaki "from the insane Burgomaster" and "the power-hungry Urwin", but says the Count has made it clear there must be consequences for the previous mayor's alliance, and that the transformation of the Amber Temple "into a B&B" and the loss of his powers was unacceptable. The party grins. With a tear he lists what Vallaki has lost: "we've lost the bones of St. Andral. We've lost father, Lucian."

Gaxim's sword suggests they could tell Strahd they are allies — or the town could let them stay quietly. Ratan, terrified, demands to know which guard let them in; the captain of the guard blames "the guards at the Southern gate" and promises "I will have them hanged for this!" Gaxim is appalled: "YOU EVIL!" Ratan says he cannot have them stay: "If you stay here, who knows how many more innocent people will die and, in particular, me." Slerdax remarks, "We could kill you right now," and the barbarian points at the guards: "these men die!" But Slerdax whispers that Ratan "needs a way out of this mess", and the Burgomaster, "like a drowning man", latches onto it. Strahd's vengeance, he says, was directed at Danika and Urwin Martikov and their family; Strahd "subcontracted out the hit on a clan of werewolves", who did not fulfill the letter of their contract and took the two children, "two little boys, Brom and Bray?" The werewolves went to their den, and werewolf hunters at the Blue Water Inn could give directions. Then he smiles evilly: "What's my leverage? Those children." Gaxim storms out, then turns: "I'll be back."

The Burgomaster recognizes the 7-year-old girl beside Ezmerelda: "Arabelle? I think your parents are looking for you." Slerdax realizes the girl is leverage — if the Vistani lords learned Ratan let their daughter come to harm, they would kill him in painful ways — but Merrin and Gaxim will have none of it: "We're taking Arabelle home." Slerdax is overruled.

At the Blue Water Inn the mood is sadder: Danika, "the life-of-the establishment", is dead, and the youngest of the Martikov children, Stefania, and her husband Dag Tomescu are now running it. Davian Martikov "will never let a tragedy get in the way of business"; the Inn is the premier distribution channel for the winery. The group saved the family when they found the gem, but they were also the cause of Urwin's death. The patriarch decided no vendetta would be required, but Stefania, particularly close to her brother, is conflicted. She greets them — "Welcome," "You've come to drink at my bar?" — and pours the barbarian a glass of "Purple Grapemash". Rictavio looks at his old friend Ezmerelda; she glares at him. When Stefania asks after "the other gem", the sword explains that "people keep getting kidnapped"; hearing what happened to her nephews, she springs into action and points them to Szoldar Szoldarovich and Yevgeni Krushkin at a table in the back.

Szoldar will tell them where the werewolves are, but wants payment — "I just want the pelts. I am a special kind of furrier." Yevgeni explains that they make special coats for people whose preferences involve dressing up in werewolf suits; Gaxim spits out his drink. "I do not judge! I just provide what they need." The heroes cut him off, Merrin asks "Where are the werewolves?", and Szoldar, smirking, gives directions.

On the way to the Vistani encampment southwest of the city, Ezmerelda predicts of the girl, "she's going to betray us." The girl insists "I am Vistani. I do not need rescuing. Everything was under control," then reverses: "I am Vistani Princess, and I am always willing to be rescued, especially if it comes with some gold?" Merrin glares — "this wizard wants to drop you off on the other side of the lake" — and the rage of being tormented by demons shows in her eyes, so the girl offers instead to be "very  thankful". The girl tells Ezmerelda "You are not from my village," and says she will be forgiven for helping, but "you also stole a horse, and there can be no forgiveness of that." Gaxim nods: "fair!" Ezmerelda is upset; someone tells her "Let it go, Ez!" Arabelle runs to her encampment, and her brother Luvash is thrilled to see her. Kasimir tells Gaxim "363 days to go." Gaxim ignores him, and the Dusk elf shouts that "the days – they do not work the same way they work in other places."

A few hours later the heroes reach a broad, torchlit cave above the tree line, carved into a rocky mountain spur, "that looks like the gaping maw of a great wolf." They wonder why the day stretches on and on — the morning in the Amber temple, the lake, "a mad mage", now werewolves — and why they are not tired; perhaps Kasimir was making a point. From deep inside comes the sound of a flute, some notes "discordant — painfully so."

Slerdax sends in a familiar. Slerdax-as-spider passes a guard post where two feral-looking women with spears stand on a ledge between two tunnels. To the left is a large cave with a smoldering campfire, a floor of gnawed bones, a pack of wolves, and an old werewolf in hybrid form playing an electrum flute. Further in, the voices of children: "good! Fight! One of you will live tonight. The rest of you will die!" To the right is a cavern with an underground spring, a pool roughly forty feet across and ten feet deep, with ledges and a few crates. A child screams, and a voice says, "kill him, or I will kill you both myself."

The group agrees on a distraction. Ezmerelda proposes to announce their doom — "I am Ezmerelda d'Avenir! Bane of vampires, killer of werewolves" — standing shoulder to shoulder with Gaxim while Wukong rushes in. Merrin isn't happy, and Slerdax has a better idea: run in "acting as if something far worse than us was chasing us". Slerdax casts an illusion of smoke and the party rushes in screaming "dragon!"

The werewolf guard Aziana flees deeper into the caves. Slerdax surrounds the wolf den with a wall of fire; the wolves and their werewolf flutist all die, Skennis grinning as he dies: "When Kiril returns," "he'll skin you alive." Wukong rushes to the room of children; the other guard, Davanka, follows and shouts "what is going on?" Ezmerelda, Gaxim, and Merrin rush towards the children as a hunting party of werewolves arrives. Wukong hits Zuleika Toranescu. Davanka screams "Kiril, there is a dragon," and the leader of the hunting party screams back, "you fool, it's a trick!" Davanka charges Ezmerelda, who disengages; Gaxim charges Toranescu, who turns hybrid; Kiril's pack chases Slerdax, who casts a fireball for 31 HP of damage — the wolves all die, the werewolves are badly injured, and Kiril's force of personality keeps them charging. Wukong fires another arrow into Toranescu, Davanka scratches Gaxim for 5 HP, and Gaxim kills Toranescu and cuts into Aziana with his blade of light for 27. Merrin's magical blood spear lands for 12. A second fireball leaves only three of the original pack of 7, all badly injured. Wensencia and Bianca join the attack. Gaxim, "consumed with the bloodlust of the barbarian", kills Aziana, then Bianca, and injures Wensencia, whom Merrin attacks but does not kill. Kiril, bloodied and burned, charges past Gaxim to rend Slerdax, and Slerdax casts thunderwave: one remaining wolf is killed outright, another dies to Merrin's attack of opportunity, and Kiril is pushed back.
