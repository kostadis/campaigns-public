# Chapter 11: A simple plan

Date: Days 8–9 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
The chapter opens in the middle of a fight: "Skeletons everywhere, our heroes smile. The nest of evil will be destroyed!" Slerdax casts a magic missile, injuring a skeleton, and Gaxim, enraged, recklessly attacks the same monster, critically hitting it and "destroying the magical forces that bound them." Wukong kills the skeleton next to Gaxim, to the cry "Wukong Brave!" The skeletons attack and miss, "ALL OF THEM! The Dark Powers are confused." The Avenging Paladin attacks and misses a skeleton, Slerdax's produce flame fizzles, and Gaxim charges and damages another.

Wukong then weighs the situation. The Paladin is surrounded and the Barbarian is close to a target, but the most pressing threat is that Slerdax stands between Wukong and a skeleton, and "It’s unclear that the mage can survive another" attack. Wukong shoots and kills the skeleton. When everyone stares at him, wondering why he would help the mage, he explains: "I don’t trust a mage meat-buffer." The fight grinds on. Gaxim and Marron each take 6 points of damage; Marron smites a skeleton, "sundering its magic and reducing it to a pile of bones"; Slerdax produces flame to no effect; Gaxim kills another skeleton; Wukong disengages and misses. Gaxim is critically hit "for an astonishing amount of damage, 4 HP," Marron takes 16 HP of damage, and Gaxim does 20 HP of damage to one of the skeletons. Wukong misses and blames the absent: "THIS IS ISMAARK’s FAULT! I don’t know how, but it is!" Marron, down to 2 HP, does 11 HP of damage, and Slerdax kills the last remaining skeleton. Wukong pats his back: "You the man!" The chapter says "Marron heals herself."

Gaxim, too excited, charges at the secret door. Inside are four cultists in black robes: "What have you done to the mistress! How will we have our revenge on the Baron!" "Kill EVIL!" screams Gaxim; "Kill, GOOD!" scream the cultists. Slerdax asks Wukong and Marron whether they should try to get the cultists to work for them instead of killing them. Wukong is game; Marron utters a single word, "No." and charges. Slerdax tries to tempt Gaxim with the idea that the cultists could be his slaves, but Gaxim "has become curiously righteous" — perhaps the Dark Powers missed something — and finds the idea repugnant.

Gaxim kills one cultist, and Slerdax uses his last magic missile spell to kill another. One of the remaining cultists tries to hold Gaxim and fails, while a spiritual weapon strikes Slerdax for 7 HP; Gaxim, surprisingly, is unaffected. The second cultist casts command on Gaxim, who is again unaffected, and the cultists are in shock and frightened. "Strahd has abandoned us!" Gaxim scowls: "you mean devil!" Marron is pleased with Gaxim's observation of the law. Gaxim takes 8 points of damage from the spiritual weapon but kills a cultist; Wukong directs the Half-orc to move next to "another of Lady Fiona’s patsies," then shoots and kills the remaining cultist. Marron is annoyed that she did not get a chance to kill "these demon-worshipping scum" and pleased that they are dead.

On the streets a Raven squawks. The heroes realize it is trying to communicate and remember that it must be one of the Keepers of the Feather. It leads them to a secluded spot and turns into a human: Urwin Martikov, Danika's husband. "You must go to the Blue Water Inn for safety. Izek has declared that you are traitors to the city and must be put to death, apparently for lack of smiling." Gaxim laughs, because "He knows that his brother would never hurt him." Marron is outraged: "I am a law-abiding citizen, and I have been continuously smiling!" "Are you fools?" hisses the wereraven. The Barbarian and the Paladin, offended, are determined to find Izek and talk to him, until Slerdax asks why Izek suddenly says they are not smiling. "Because he wants you dead. He got what he wanted, Ireena."

At the bar Danika shouts, "They made it here!" The Wachter brothers declare, "These are the criminals Izek wants!" Some patrons reach for their swords, others for their drinks, and then one notices Stella: "Stella? Why is she here?" The continued assault on Marron's integrity as a law-abiding citizen triggers another transformation: her wings molt, and in their place are the white feathers of Merrin. The room stares for a moment, declares "It’s Barovia." and goes back to drinking. The cleric tries to restore the troubled Stella, but fails.

Nikolai Wachter stands and shouts, "You, you tried to help my sister!" He asks why she is there; Wukong mumbles, "we are taking her to the St. Andral’s." Asked "And my mother?", Merrin answers, confused, "I have no idea." — honestly, since she was not present at her death. Gaxim has lost track of all the characters involved in this plot and grunts. Slerdax says, "I can’t contradict my friends," and persuades Nikolai Wachter to ask no more questions, then deceives him that they found his mother dead and rescued Stella from cultists about to practice a vile ritual on her. The brothers see their chance to be rid of their annoying sister and secure the house and family fortune: "You have saved my sister! You are heroes!" / "Nikolai, you know what this means?" / "Yes, Karl, the house and the treasures are ours!" Gaxim blurts out, "More drinking!" and Danika mumbles that nobody has yet gone to the winery and there is still no booze.

When the brothers try to take Stella, Merrin intervenes, saying they plan to take her to Father Lucian. Nikolai smiles: "Mother felt Father Lucian was a quack. But if you think he could help, by all means. We will collect her tomorrow." Their plan, the chapter says, is to abandon her there. Merrin works out that Stella's trouble is some kind of traumatic abuse; the best she can figure is that the Baron's son, Victor, her one-time fiancé, somehow abused her and broke her mind.

Rictavio asks whether they found the information about the Vistani. Slerdax: "yeah, we had a prior dinner engagement." Wukong laughs. "You have not done my quest." "But we know that Fiona was aligned with Strahd. And that she is dead." Rictavio answers that since they have attacked one of Strahd's allies he must tell them one more thing, and declares that he intends to visit his friend Blinsky. The heroes remember the doll Mad Mary gave them, which bears the signature of Blinsky of Vallaki; Mad Mary had told them to find her daughter, whom Strahd had stolen.

Urwin directs them to the back room, and Rictavio comes too. Danika presses him — "van Richten, it is time you told them everything!" — and the two bicker ("They have done nothing for me!" / "They killed Lady Fiona!" / "Bah!") until Urwin reminds van Richten that he is a guest in their house. Van Richten relents: the reason Strahd has not attacked is that "the Devil follows a peculiar cycle of activity and inactivity. He was inactive for 100 years." The baron, he says, "is like a child that sees the sunrise every day because it screams and thinks its screams are why the sun rises." When Marron is confused, he sighs: "The entire domain of Barovia is Strahd’s. There is no place he can not go." Danika adds, "We are concerned Strahd intends to visit the town." Then the grand reveal: "I believe he has an obsession with Ireena. He is after her. We must deny him this prize. And the only safe place is in St. Andral’s." Asked why, Danika explains "as if explaining things to a small child": "Because that is where the bones of the Saint are." Wukong tells them, "they are missing." Everyone stares at everyone, and they all say, "WHAT?"

Wukong argues they have leverage because they killed Fiona, and the Baron will command Izek. Van Richten and the Martikovs laugh cruelly, and Danika explains: "Izek is a power of evil unto himself, haven’t you seen his demonic arm? The Baron is terrified of Izek and is Izek’s prisoner." Confused, the party recaps: Ireena is Izek's prisoner and must be freed; they have no leverage over the Baron; they have to save Ismark; they have to find the bones and take them to St. Andral's; and they must avoid being caught by Izek. Danika adds the wine: "How am I supposed to cook my fondues and my coq au vin?" The plan is laid out: "Ismark will be executed during the early part of the Festival, but it will take time to prepare. You will go into Burgomaster’s house and free Ireena." After Ismark is freed, "we will know where the bones are," and "The Keepers of the Feather will protect Ireena and Ismark from any who dare enter the church."

Slerdax asks, "So if the bones are so awesome, why don’t we just keep them?" and Wukong wonders, "If we have the bones, how much are they worth?" Merrin looks angry; "the stench of evil on Wukong and Slerdax is evident," and she now knows why their features have changed: "these two have been touched by the dark forces." Asked "It is a plan?", Slerdax answers, "Already forgot it, sure. Can we go to bed now?" Merrin: "Destroying evil, sign me up!" Wukong is unconvinced — "surely we could get into some arrangement with him that would work?" — until Gaxim says, "Strahd, Treasure?" Wukong: "Now that is a reason to sign up for this crazy plan." In the morning Slerdax announces, to much rejoicing, "In lieu of Tasha’s Hideous Laughter, I have decided to prepare Thunder Wave."

The heroes sneak to the house while the Baron and Izek have left for the main square to attend the Festival. Slerdax summons a mouse familiar — "Slerdous (Slerdax as a mouse familiar)" — which a maid spots, screams at and chases with a broom before it hides and slips upstairs. Slerdous tours the house: the grand foyer (N3a), the dining room where Lady Lydia Petrovna, the Baron's wife, who has not yet left, talks to eight women about decorations for the Festival and announces, "Izek has found his long-lost sister!" and "We plan to execute him today!" of Ismark; the den (N3e), the servant's quarters, the kitchen, the upstairs gallery, the bathroom, the master bedroom, the adjoining room (N3p) with a white bridal gown on a mannequin, the Master Bedroom Closet (N3n), and the library "where they last met the Baron." In a bedroom full of identical dolls that "all look like Ireena Kolyana," Ireena is on the bed. Slerdax disengages and asks where he should free her; the party observes that they have no idea who else is here, and Wukong notes there is no exterior window, so they will have to enter the house.

Slerdous continues: another bedroom that probably belongs to "the Baron’s only son, Victor," and a closet (N3m) where a badly beaten man in a loincloth is chained to the wall, his wrists cut by iron shackles. Slerdax recognizes him as Udo Lukovich, son of Willemina Rikalova, "who had been imprisoned for speaking against the burgomaster." Merrin observes she has no spells, and Slerdax offers to build blocks of dirt as steps, but Wukong rolls his eyes: "I am a rogue. I have some experience with climbing into 2nd-floor buildings." Marron glares. "Nothing illegal, just a skill I practiced." Wukong climbs the walls, quietly opens the window, climbs in and drops the rope. Gaxim whispers, "Wukong brave!" and Wukong thinks to himself, "I love my old job."

## Locations
### Blue Water Inn
Urwin sends the party here "for safety." The bar is where the Wachter brothers confront them, where Marron becomes Merrin, and where, in the back room, van Richten tells them the truth.

### St. Andral's
Where the party intends to take Stella, and which van Richten calls "the only safe place," because "that is where the bones of the Saint are." The bones are missing.

### Burgomaster's house
The house the heroes sneak into while the Baron and Izek are at the Festival. Slerdous scouts it room by room: grand foyer (N3a) with framed portraits and bundles of twigs heaped against the walls; a dining room with a wrought-iron chandelier; the den (N3e) with "the head of an angry-looking brown bear"; servant's quarters; kitchen; a gallery of landscape paintings; a bathroom; a faded master bedroom with a trapdoor; the adjoining room (N3p) with a bridal gown on a mannequin; the Master Bedroom Closet (N3n); the library with a chair bearing "the symbol of a roaring bear"; Ireena's doll-filled bedroom; Victor's probable bedroom; and the closet (N3m) where Udo is chained.
