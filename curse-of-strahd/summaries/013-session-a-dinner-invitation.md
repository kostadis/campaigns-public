# Chapter 13: A dinner invitation

Date: Day 9 in Barovia (from `docs/arcs/calendar.md`, ruled by the GM 2026-11-07)

## Summary
Gaxim picks up Izek's dead body and carries him "lovingly towards St. Andral's." The crowd applauds the half-orc and then offers advice on how to deal with the corpse: "A stake through the heart!"; another shoves wafers at Gaxim — "remember to put wafers in his mouth!"; another calls, "Chop off his head, and sprinkle holy water!" Gaxim ignores them all. He is given a wide berth, and the rest of the group follows "the sad giant." On the way to the Church they see the outcomes of the revolution: "Burning houses, looting, chaos as old scores are settled."

At the Church, Urwin approaches the heroes: "we have bad news!" Gaxim nods, "Izek is dead!" Urwin stares at him, confused, and Gaxim is comforted that Urwin sees the tragedy of Izek's death. Someone answers "Yes!" and "Bury him!" Urwin agrees but has more important news: "We know where the bones are. Milivoj has revealed everything. But we have a problem." He points to the setting sun. When Wukong asks, "Vampires?", Urwin answers, "Vampires? No. The Devil himself, Strahd, is coming. You must get there before it becomes dark." Someone insists, "Bury Izek, Now!" (the chapter does not name the speaker). Urwin pleads, "Gaxim, I promise to bury him myself with Father Lucian, but you must get the bones before it is dark!" Marron observes that she has no more spells, is at death's door, and must rest. Despite Urwin's pleading, the heroes go forward with the burial and use it as an opportunity to rest.

Willemina, who has followed the party to the Church, approaches Marron and hugs her, and the old woman thanks the Paladin for all she has done. Merrin says, "See, I told you they would love us if we didn't kill but saved lives!" The argument wins the day, and "the feathers molt, revealing the Cleric."

After the burial, Urwin reveals that the bones are inside the coffin maker's home and suggests going right away. But it is dark, and the party argues to wait for first light. Urwin agrees: "Strahd has not come for a 100 years, why would he choose tonight?" The narration answers: "Why indeed." Merrin looks at everyone and says, "because you are not smiling." Everyone stares at the Cleric and laughs at her innocence. Merrin says, "I am a law-abiding citizen." Father Lucian replies, "He was a lunatic, my child. Everything the Baron said was lies."

The heroes tell Urwin they are about to head to the Blue Water Inn to sleep. He stops them: "It is not safe. The Keepers of the Feather are here. The Church is the only sanctified place in all of Vallaki. If it falls, there will be no safe place." The Keepers have decided to protect the Church until the bones are found — "This will either be the beginning of the salvation of Barovia, or the end of the Keepers of the Feather! THIS IS OUR TIME!" A bunch of ravens crow as if cheering, and everyone hunkers down for the night.

The silence is shattered by breaking glass as swarms of bats break into the Church. The commoners scream and flee, because they know what it means: "The Devil is here." After 100 years, the locals have forgotten that a vampire cannot enter the Church; the party is confused. The bats surprise Wukong and attack him, and one takes 10 HP of damage. Merrin's scythe does 7 HP to a swarm; Slerdax takes 5 HP and uses a thunderwave on two of the swarms; Gaxim does 8 HP to a swarm; another swarm misses Merrin. Father Lucian turns pale: "Vampires! THERE ARE VAMPIRES OUTSIDE!" Merrin is unconvinced, and he screams, "I have lived here my whole life. I know the sound and signs a vampire makes. There are vampires outside of my Church."

With vampires outside and bats inside, Urwin shouts, "my friends, this is not your fight. The Keepers of the Feather will defend the Church. You must go find the bones." He tells them that if they stay and die, "we will have to wait another hundred years for someone else to free Barovia," and ends: "You are the ones for whom the prophecy has spoken. You are the one who Madam Eva has given the signs. You must live. Go get the bones!" Wukong "does not need an invitation to flee." He runs, and the rest of the party follows; Wukong drinks a potion of healing on the way.

The group navigates riots and looting that seem to have died down and reaches the coffin maker's house: a two-story shop with a sign shaped like a coffin above the front door, every window shutter closed tight, and "a deathly silence surrounds the establishment." The owner is inside and looks distraught. Gaxim marches up: "Bones?" Henrik van der Voort answers, "it was the vampires. They made me steal the bones. I didn't want to." The exchange runs on, unattributed in the chapter: "Bones?" / "Upstairs." / "Bring bones!" Merrin stares at him closely, and Henrik realises his disguise will not hold: "Strahd has blessed me to be one of his. I will rule over this town and feed on the fools who mocked me!" He is a vampire.

Battle begins. Wukong does 10 HP, Merrin 11 HP with the scythe, Slerdax's chromatic orb 13 HP, and Gaxim, attacking recklessly, 10 HP. Henrik grapples the half-orc. Wukong misses. Slerdax says, "We don't have to kill the Vampire. We just have to get the bones." Marron's scythe does 3 HP. Slerdax reasons that getting the bones won't protect him from the Vampire — if the bones are here, why is the Vampire here? — and concludes that the Vampire dying is the best course, casting burning hands. Gaxim hits the Vampire, which then sucks the barbarian's blood and recovers 17 HP: "The Vampire is becoming stronger." Wukong does 5 HP — "we are underpowered." Merrin tries to turn Henrik and fails; Henrik snarls, "your god has no power in my lord's domain!" Marron heaps scorn on Merrin.

Realising the situation is dire, Slerdax breaks off to find the bones, runs out of the room, finds two doors, and picks the wrong one. Wukong, deciding the bones in the Church are the safest option, opens the door Slerdax didn't and finds the stairs up. On the 2nd floor the room has four boxes; "Wukong being a systematic thinker decides to start with the smallest box. After all, it is the smallest. He chooses wisely." Meanwhile Gaxim fails to break the grapple, the Vampire feeds on him, and Merrin heals him. Slerdax, hearing Wukong, runs into the lobby, sees Gaxim grappled, and rushes to help; Gaxim breaks free, but the Vampire grapples him again — "The Dark Powers have plans." Wukong jumps out of the 2nd-floor window. Merrin thinks this would have been easier with a magic weapon, then remembers she has a relevant spell; Marron laughs, and Merrin will not run and give Marron the satisfaction. Merrin shoves the Vampire, breaking the grapple, and runs out as the Vampire howls in rage and claws at her.

With the party all outside and the rain slowing, a pursuit to the Church begins. A beggar steps into Wukong's way; Wukong refuses him money and runs around him. The same beggar blocks Merrin, who intimidates him, and the beggar curses the heroes for avoiding him. Slerdax, avoiding the beggar, ends up in a maze of barrels, plots a course, and gets out "without losing a beat." Gaxim, "proving that the Gods like fools and idiots, runs." The Vampire stumbles in the maze of barrels. Wukong steps in horse feces slick from the rain and is knocked prone. Merrin, seeing Wukong fall, misses the brawl in her way, fails to persuade the brawlers to help her against the Vampire, disengages, and runs on. The Vampire runs into the same brawl and cannot get past: "The brawlers have decided that this Vampire will not get what it wants."

The heroes arrive at the Church with the bones, and the vampires scream and run out. A single vampire stares at the party, bends to one knee, scribbles something, and flies off; Merrin picks up a note addressed to them. Inside, "It's a blood bath. Father Lucian is dead. Ismark is dead. Udo is dead. Danika is at death's door." Urwin explains that Strahd entered the Church, killed Lucian and Ismark, and was about to take Ireena when the heroes arrived. The Keepers of the Feathers have lost many of their brethren; apparently the vampires had found a way to be invited in before the bloodbath began.

Merrin is satisfied that those who didn't follow the law were punished as she predicted. Marron laughs at her: "you see this bloodbath as proof that those who deviate from the straight and narrow get what they deserve. You are just too much of a coward to get your hands dirty. You want someone else to do it. Even if the person who does it is a Vampire." Rictavio appears, looks at the blood bath, and says, "St. Andral's is safe. Excellent." He has a peculiar smirk on his face. The chapter ends as "Merrin or is it Marron, reads the note" — the note itself appears in the chapter only as an image.
