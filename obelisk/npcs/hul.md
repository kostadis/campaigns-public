## Identity
Hul is a tall, furry bugbear with large ears and a milky film over one eye [ch 010 / entry; ch 011 / entry]. He serves as a rank-and-file guard for the orc chief Brughor Axe-Biter, stationed outside the marauder cave at Wyvern Tor, one of four bugbear rank-and-file serving alongside the ogre Gog [ch 010 / entry; ch 011 / entry]. He was seen leaning on a salvaged pike [ch 011 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Hul served as a guard for Brughor Axe-Biter at Wyvern Tor [ch 010 / entry; ch 011 / entry]. During his watch, he displayed boredom and inattentiveness, hunkering behind a boulder and failing to immediately notice the party on the ridge in his initial surveillance [ch 010 / entry]. When confronted, he remained complacent enough to allow the party's distractions to draw him out, though he retained predatory intent, evidenced by a remark interpreted as a "tourist" comment with unmistakable predatory undertones [ch 011 / entry].

## History with the Party
