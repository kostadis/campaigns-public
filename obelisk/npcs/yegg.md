## Identity

Yegg is a Jagged Tooth goblin and head cook of the Cragmaw goblins at Cragmaw Castle. [ch 015 / entry; ch 016 / entry; ch 017 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations

Yegg celebrates tribal victories enthusiastically and takes pride in his cooking, asserting control over the feast’s serving order. The following morning, visibly hungover, he serves food with indifferent brevity. [ch 015 / 015.05; ch 016 / entry; ch 017 / entry]

## History with the Party

- Yegg arrived cheering after the fighting resolved in the Jagged Tooth tribe’s favor. The tribe took control of Cragmaw Castle and began preparing a feast. [ch 015 / entry; ch 015 / 015.05]
- His arrival coincided with Lhupo convincing the other goblins that Zenvon’s prophetic authority had been vindicated; Yegg’s individual beliefs were not specified. [ch 015 / 015.05]
- Yegg presented the slow-roasted Red-Mask hobgoblin feast, reserving the first cut for Zenvon as Prophet and the second for the Paladin. His serving order made Zenvon’s attempted fast an obstacle to everyone eating; Zenvon ultimately took a bite, and the goblins began feasting. [ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / 016.01]
- Yegg announced that food was ready and served cold red mask while visibly hungover. The party shared an unenthusiastic meal before preparing to move on. [ch 017 / 017.03]

## Last Observed State

Yegg was at Cragmaw Castle, visibly hungover from the previous night, announcing and serving a cold meal. His subsequent status is not established in the summaries. [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.03]
