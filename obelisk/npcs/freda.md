## Identity
Freda is a slight Gnomish woman with silver-streaked hair, round spectacles, and a self-woven shawl [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.02]. She is characterized physically by her nervous habits, continually twisting thread or worrying the edges of her sleeves [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Freda exhibits a combination of nervousness and deep resentment toward the Redbrand gang [ch 002 / entry]. While she desires the gang's removal, she strictly insists that her neighbors must not be endangered in the process, viewing outsiders as suitable for taking such risks on her behalf [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.02]. She harbors suspicion that the Redbrands are responsible for Gundren’s disappearance, though this remains a local accusation rather than established fact [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.02]. Freda directs investigative attention toward Halia at the Miner's Exchange, noting the gang conspicuously avoids harassing her, and speculates on whether they fear or owe her [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.02; ch 002 / moment].
