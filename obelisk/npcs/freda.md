## Identity
Freda is a slight Gnomish woman with silver-streaked hair, round spectacles, and a shawl she wove herself. She continually twists thread or worries the edges of her sleeves. [ch 002 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Freda combines nervousness with deep resentment toward the Redbrands. She wants outsiders to remove the gang without endangering her neighbors. [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.02]

She accused the Redbrands of causing Gundren’s disappearance; this was her suspicion, not an established connection. She recommended investigating Halia because the gang conspicuously left her alone. [ch 002 / 002.02]
