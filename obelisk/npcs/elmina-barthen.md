## Identity
Elmina Barthen is the young proprietor of Barthen's Provisions in Phandalin [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.04]. She is described as having a "kindly manner" [ch 001 / 001.04]. Her establishment is the largest trading post in town, stocking ordinary supplies such as backpacks, bedrolls, rope, and rations, but notably excluding weapons [ch 001 / 001.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Elmina is characterized by genuine concern for those she considers close friends [ch 001 / 001.04; ch 001 / moment]. When confronted with evidence that her friend Gundren Rockseeker had been abducted, she reacted with urgency, demanding that the party rescue him [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.04; ch 001 / moment]. Her demeanor is sincere, a trait confirmed by Sister Maela after a successful Insight check [ch 001 / 001.04; ch 001 / moment]. Elmina is well-informed about local history, specifically reiterating Gundren's conviction that he had located the lost mine of the Phandelver Pact [ch 001 / 001.04].

## History with the Party
- Elmina Barthen is the young, kindly proprietor of Barthen's Provisions and a close friend of Gundren Rockseeker [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.04].
- She received the delivery contract from Zenvon Forepot, sent her clerks Ander and Thistle to inspect the wagon, and paid Zenvon the agreed 19 gold pieces [ch 001 / 001.04].
- When asked why Gundren had not arrived with the wagon, she expressed genuine concern; Sister Maela judged her sincerity credible after a successful Insight check [ch 001 / 001.04; ch 001 / moment].
- Elmina revealed that Gundren was a close friend who had been excited about his belief that he had discovered the lost mine of the Phandelver Pact in the nearby hills [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.04].
- After learning of the abduction evidence (horses, ransacked belongings, empty map case, and signs of dragged bodies) from Sister Maela, Elmina urgently demanded that the party rescue Gundren [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.04].
- Zenvon assured her that the party would rest in town that night and begin the search the following morning [ch 001 / 001.04; ch 001 / 001.05].
