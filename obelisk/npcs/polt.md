## Identity

Gap-toothed goblin guard and veteran archer with a dragging left leg and a quiver of nine black-feathered arrows. Sister Maela identifies his limp as an old battle injury. Lhupo supplies him as one of her stronger archers; he has 21 hit points [ch 014 / entry; ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / 016.02].

## Personality and Motivations

Keeps his distance while shooting at the Grell. Echoes the Jagged Tooth’s religious language during the farewell to Lhupo; his personal motivations are not established in the summaries [ch 016 / 016.06; ch 017 / 017.05].

## History with the Party
Admits and escorts the party toward Lhupo; stepping over a tripwire reveals its location. He answers their thanks with a warning not to get too excited [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.01].
- At the feast, recounts the shapeshifter’s death near the storage tower and trees, credits Veyra, and reveals that nobody has searched the body [ch 016 / 016.01].
- Joins the party’s monster hunt as the strong archer Lhupo supplies after Zenvon negotiates for assistance [ch 016 / 016.02].
- Misses the Grick with his shortbow. After its defeat, points through a doorway to attract Lhupo’s attention, but she brushes him off [ch 016 / 016.03].
- Hits Snarl with an arrow for 5 damage before Sister Maela kills the wolf [ch 016 / 016.04].
- Shoots the Grell for 5 damage while keeping as far away as possible; the battle remains unresolved when the session ends [ch 016 / 016.06].
- Lands a hit for six damage during the continuing grell fight; Veyra subsequently kills the creature with Magic Missile [ch 017 / 017.01].
- Zenvon proposes Polt as someone to catch the chest Pip finds above the ruined library; the party instead lowers it safely with rope [ch 017 / 017.02].
- During the farewell to Lhupo, echoes the Jagged Tooth’s creed: “The tooth answers in the dark.” [ch 017 / 017.05].

## Last Observed State

Alive and speaking during the party’s farewell to Lhupo at Cragmaw Castle. The summaries do not establish whether he accompanies the departing party [ch 017 / 017.05].

## Relationships

- **Lhupo:** Supplies Polt as one of her more capable followers. She brushes off his attempt to draw her attention through a doorway after the Grick’s defeat. He later echoes her religious language during the farewell [ch 016 / 016.02; ch 016 / 016.03; ch 017 / 017.05].
- **The party:** Initially serves as their castle escort, then assists against the Grick, Snarl, and the Grell [ch 014 / 014.01; ch 016 / 016.03; ch 016 / 016.04; ch 016 / 016.06; ch 017 / 017.01].
- **Veyra:** Publicly credits her for the shapeshifter’s death [ch 016 / 016.01].
