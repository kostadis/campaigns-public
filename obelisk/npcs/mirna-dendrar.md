## Personality and Motivations
*   **Survival Instinct:** Demonstrated high resourcefulness by fabricating a ransom story about wealthy relatives in Neverwinter to delay execution and hope for rescue [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.02; ch 010 / entry].
*   **Insightful:** Detected that Zenvon was lying about the whereabouts of her husband’s body when he failed a Deception check, prompting Zenvon to ask Sister Maela to explain Thel's fate gently [ch 009 / 009.02].
*   **Grateful:** Expressed relief at the party's honesty and in gratitude revealed the location of a valuable emerald necklace and insisted the party take it [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.02].
*   **Motivation:** Primary concern was the survival of herself and her children; secondary motivation is the respectful recovery of her late husband’s body [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.02].

## History with the Party
- Mirna was one of two human women held in the southern cell [ch 008 / entry]; her children, Nars (13) and Nilsa (18), were also held captive and rescued with her [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.02; ch 010 / entry].
- After being freed, Mirna and her family barricaded themselves in terror at the sight of skeletal guardians outside; Zenvon called to them to open the door, and they were released [ch 008 / entry; ch 009 / 009.02].
- She and her family were held captive by the Redbrands, who murdered her husband, Thel, and planned to kill the rest of the family [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.02; ch 010 / entry].
- To survive, Mirna lied to the Redbrands, claiming she had wealthy relatives in Neverwinter who would pay a ransom, a bluff she later admitted to the party [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.02; ch 010 / entry].
- Mirna found Zenvon’s offer of food and water to be "very thoughtful" [ch 009 / 009.02].
- Zenvon failed a Deception check regarding the whereabouts of Thel's body, and Mirna saw through the lie; Sister Maela then explained Thel's fate gently [ch 009 / 009.02].
- Mirna was satisfied and relieved by the party's honesty [ch 009 / 009.02].
- In gratitude for being saved, she revealed the location of a valuable emerald necklace hidden behind storage shelves in her family's old herb and alchemy shop in the southeast parcel of Thundertree [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.02; ch 010 / entry].
- She insisted the party take the necklace as their own if they ever passed that way [ch 009 / 009.02].
- She asked the party to recover Thel's body if they ever could; Zenvon agreed to try, and the request remains open [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.02; ch 010 / entry].
- The party agreed to escort the Dendrars out of the manor and back to the safety of Phandalin [ch 009 / 009.02].
- The party is reminded of Mirna's treasure in Thundertree before setting out from Phandalin [ch 010 / 010.04].
