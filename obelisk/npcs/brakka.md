## Identity
A deceased Scarlet Fist bugbear with a snapped tusk and a scarlet cloak fastened with a bent nail, identified by Gorl [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Gorl says Brakka knew the route to Wave Echo Cave and kept it secret from him. No further motive is established [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.04].

## History with the Party

- Gorl said Brakka alone had known the cave route within that account. His death left Gorl unable to supply it; this does not rule out Gundren’s map or other sources [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.04].

## Last Observed State
