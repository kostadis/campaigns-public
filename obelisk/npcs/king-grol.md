## Identity
King Grol is the bugbear ruler of Cragmaw Castle. He belongs to Gorl’s tribe but is explicitly distinguished from Gorl [ch 013 / entry; ch 014 / 014.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Grol maintains factional cooperation through payment despite resentment among the goblins and Red Masks. The Black Spider’s money is described as the likely source binding the groups together [ch 013 / 013.05; ch 014 / 014.04].

He asserts his authority over visitors and wants to know who enters his castle. During the audience, his willingness to hear the pilgrims’ prayer competes with his desire to complete Gundren’s profitable sale [ch 015 / entry; ch 015 / 015.01].

## History with the Party
- Outside the castle, Gorl named King Grol as the bugbear in charge [ch 013 / 013.05].
- The party considered exploiting the inhabitants’ competing interests rather than uniting them through an open attack [ch 013 / 013.05].

- According to Lhupo, Grol maintains factional cooperation through payments; her account leaves the party with the impression that goblins and hobgoblins resent him [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.04].
- The entrance watchers reported the party, and Grol sent Vorl to summon them. At the end they reached his quarters and saw him standing over the unconscious Gundren beside Snarl and an unnamed drow woman; conversation had not begun [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.05].
- Grol questioned the party about Nosk, Gorl, and the rumored prophet. Zenvon claimed they were faithful followers of the Jagged Tooth returning an injured Gorl [ch 015 / 015.01].
- Zenvon proposed a prayer for Grol’s well-being while Grol completed Gundren’s sale to Nisrell Bao for seven hundred gold pieces [ch 015 / 015.01].
- After Nisrell revealed herself as a doppelganger and accused the party of conspiring with Lhupo, Grol attacked Zenvon with his morningstar and missed. Zenvon and Pip wounded him; Veyra’s Magic Missile killed him [ch 015 / 015.02].

## Last Observed State
Dead in his quarters at Cragmaw Castle, killed by Veyra’s Magic Missile after Zenvon and Pip wounded him [ch 015 / 015.01; ch 015 / 015.02].

## Relationships
- **Castle factions:** Grol pays the goblins and Red Masks to cooperate. Lhupo reports that both resent him; Thrak leads the Red Masks [ch 014 / 014.04].
- **Lhupo:** Wants Grol killed and his tooth brought back as a trophy [ch 014 / 014.04].
- **Gundren Rockseeker:** Grol held him captive and sold him for seven hundred gold pieces [ch 015 / entry; ch 015 / 015.01].
- **Nisrell Bao:** Paid Grol for Gundren while disguised as a Drow woman, then revealed herself as a doppelganger and exposed the party’s conspiracy [ch 015 / 015.01; ch 015 / 015.02].
- **Snarl:** Grol’s great wolf and pet, present during the audience and subsequent combat [ch 014 / 014.04; ch 015 / 015.02].
- **Vorl:** Delivered Grol’s summons and announced the party to him [ch 014 / 014.05].
