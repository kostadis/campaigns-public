## Identity
The bugbear ruler of Cragmaw Castle, named by Gorl and explicitly distinguished from the captive despite their similar names [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.05].

## Personality and Motivations
No direct conversation or personal motive is established. The GM explains that Grol’s personal strength does not adequately explain the cooperation of groups with separate goals and loyalties; the Spider’s money is the likely binding force [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.05].

## History with the Party
- Outside the castle, Gorl named King Grol as the bugbear in charge [ch 013 / 013.05].
- The party considered exploiting the inhabitants’ competing interests rather than uniting them through an open attack [ch 013 / 013.05].

## Last Observed State
Reported ruler of the castle. Not directly encountered: the party remains outside at the end of the summary. His individual physical condition is not established by an on-screen sighting [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.05].

## Relationships
