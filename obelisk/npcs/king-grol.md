## Identity
King Grol is the bugbear ruler of Cragmaw Castle. He belongs to Gorl’s tribe and is explicitly distinguished from Gorl [ch 013 / entry; ch 014 / 014.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Grol maintained cooperation through payments despite resentment among the goblins and Red Masks. The Black Spider’s money was described as the likely force binding the factions together [ch 013 / 013.05; ch 014 / 014.04].

He asserted authority over visitors and sought to complete Gundren’s profitable sale while entertaining the proposed prayer. The GM later explained that Grol had not cared enough to remove the castle’s remaining monsters [ch 015 / entry; ch 015 / 015.01; ch 016 / 016.05].

## History with the Party
- Outside the castle, Gorl named King Grol as the bugbear in charge [ch 013 / 013.05].
- The party considered exploiting the inhabitants’ competing interests rather than uniting them through an open attack [ch 013 / 013.05].

- According to Lhupo, Grol maintains factional cooperation through payments; her account leaves the party with the impression that goblins and hobgoblins resent him [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.04].
- The entrance watchers reported the party, and Grol sent Vorl to summon them. At the end they reached his quarters and saw him standing over the unconscious Gundren beside Snarl and an unnamed drow woman; conversation had not begun [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.05].
- Grol questioned the party about Nosk, Gorl, and the rumored prophet. Zenvon claimed they were faithful followers of the Jagged Tooth returning an injured Gorl [ch 015 / 015.01].
- Zenvon proposed a prayer for Grol’s well-being while Grol completed Gundren’s sale to Nisrell Bao for seven hundred gold pieces [ch 015 / 015.01].
- After Nisrell revealed herself as a doppelganger and accused the party of conspiring with Lhupo, Grol attacked Zenvon with his morningstar and missed. Zenvon and Pip wounded him; Veyra’s Magic Missile killed him [ch 015 / 015.02].
- At the goblins’ celebration, a musician presented Grol’s severed tooth to Zenvon, who gave it to Lhupo. She tied it into a pendant [ch 016 / 016.01].
- The party recovered the 700 gold pieces the doppelganger had paid Grol for Gundren. Lhupo honored the treasure agreement, and the gold was recorded in the party’s treasure spreadsheet [ch 016 / 016.05].
- The GM explained that Grol had neglected to remove monsters remaining in the castle; Lhupo requested the party’s help against a Grell that sometimes killed goblins [ch 016 / 016.05].

## Last Observed State
Dead: Veyra’s Magic Missile killed him after Zenvon and Pip wounded him. His severed tooth subsequently became Lhupo’s pendant [ch 015 / 015.02; ch 016 / 016.01].

## Relationships
- **Castle factions:** Paid the goblins and Red Masks to cooperate despite their resentment; Thrak led the Red Masks [ch 014 / 014.04].
- **Lhupo:** Wanted Grol dead and his tooth as a trophy; subsequently received the tooth from Zenvon and made it into a pendant [ch 014 / 014.04; ch 016 / 016.01].
- **Gundren Rockseeker:** Held him captive and sold him for seven hundred gold pieces [ch 015 / entry; ch 015 / 015.01].
- **Nisrell Bao:** Paid Grol for Gundren while disguised as a Drow woman, then revealed herself as a doppelganger and exposed the party’s conspiracy [ch 015 / 015.01; ch 015 / 015.02].
- **Snarl:** Grol’s pet wolf, present during the audience and battle; explicitly still alive during the subsequent celebration [ch 014 / 014.04; ch 015 / 015.02; ch 016 / 016.01].
- **Vorl:** Delivered Grol’s summons and announced the party [ch 014 / 014.05].
- **Party:** Zenvon and Pip wounded Grol; Veyra killed him [ch 015 / 015.02].
