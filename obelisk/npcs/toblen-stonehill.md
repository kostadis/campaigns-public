## Identity
Toblen Stonehill is the owner and proprietor of the Stonehill Inn in Phandalin [ch 002 / entry; ch 005 / entry]. He is described as a broad-shouldered young human with sandy hair, tired blue eyes, and sleeves rolled up and dusted with flour and hearth ash [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.01]. He is the father of Tuck Stonehill, a nine-year-old boy [ch 002 / 002.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Toblen is characterized by caution, fear, and a strong desire to avoid drawing attention to himself or his establishment. When questioned about enemies or trouble in Phandalin, he retreats into evasiveness, warning, "Trouble in Phandalin has a habit of walking home with you." [ch 002 / 002.01; ch 002 / moment]. He speaks softly and nervously when discussing the Redbrands, even among friends, because he witnessed their murder of Thel Dendrar in broad daylight and the subsequent disappearance of the victim's family [ch 002 / 002.02; ch 002 / moment].

Despite his fear, Toblen possesses a practical, community-focused morality. He provided free lodging to the party after they cleared the Redbrand threat, stating, "since you guys did us a good deed with the Redbrand, tonight's free" [ch 009 / entry]. He remembers Gundren Rockseeker fondly as a merchant whose business benefited the town, clarifying that they were not related but acknowledging Gundren's positive impact [ch 002 / 002.01]. His advice regarding other locations, such as the ruins of Conyberry, is often based on fear and hearsay rather than firsthand experience; an Insight check revealed he had never visited the site, though his historical facts were correct [ch 010 / 010.03].

## History with the Party
*   Toblen is the owner of the Stonehill Inn in Phandalin, described as broad-shouldered with sandy hair and tired blue eyes [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.01].
*   Toblen offered the party rooms and stew upon their arrival, watching the door nervously when voices rose [ch 002 / 002.01].
*   Toblen remembered Gundren Rockseeker as a good man and merchant whose business mattered to Phandalin, clarifying that Gundren was not his relative [ch 002 / 002.01].
*   Toblen warned the party that trouble in Phandalin tends to follow people home, using this logic to explain his caution regarding enemies [ch 002 / 002.02].
*   Toblen privately revealed that the Redbrands murdered Thel Dendrar in broad daylight, causing his family to vanish [ch 002 / 002.02; ch 002 / moment].
*   Toblen identified the Sleeping Giant as the Redbrands’ haunt and noted that Daran Edermath retired because most adventurers die or quit [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.02].
*   Toblen was present at the inn when the party returned with Sildar Hallwinter, bursting from the doors in relief to greet them [ch 005 / 005.01].
*   Toblen served as a social indicator during the breakfast meeting with Sildar, giving a wary look to a suspicious patron and moving behind the bar to maintain distance, helping Zenvon identify the man as a likely Redbrand associate [ch 005 / 005.02].
*   Toblen provided the party with a free night's lodging and charged separately for food, citing their heroics against the Redbrands as the reason [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.08].
*   Toblen welcomed the party back to the inn after the battle at Tresendar Manor, treating them as local heroes and pouring them celebratory drinks [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.09].
*   Toblen warned the party against traveling through the ruins of Conyberry, describing it as a Spellplague-blasted ruin destroyed by Uthgardt raiders [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.03].
*   An Insight check revealed Toblen had never visited Conyberry and his warnings were based on fear and imagination rather than firsthand experience, though his historical facts were correct [ch 010 / 010.03].
*   Toblen was taken hostage by bugbears and goblins who ambushed the Stonehill Inn while the party rested [ch 012 / entry; ch 012 / 012.06].
*   Nosk, the bugbear leader, held Toblen face-down at knifepoint, using him as leverage to demand the party surrender their weapons [ch 012 / 012.06].
*   Toblen attempted to flee the inn when Nosk signaled the truce, running past goblins blocking the exit during the chaos of Zenvon's illusion spell [ch 012 / entry; ch 012 / moment].

- Toblen survived the fight, protested the broken windows, and was present when the party brought Gorl back for interrogation [ch 013 / 013.02; ch 013 / 013.03].
- He opposed releasing another bugbear and refused responsibility for housing the maimed prisoner. He demanded a council meeting; the party instead took Gorl toward Cragmaw Castle in a cart [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.03].

*   **Gundren Rockseeker:** Toblen regarded Gundren as a good man and merchant whose business mattered to Phandalin [ch 002 / 002.01]. He clarified that Gundren was not his relative [ch 002 / 002.01].
