## Identity

Nosk led the Scarlet Fist, a four-bugbear reinforcement group sent by the Black Spider to support the Redbrands and intimidate Phandalin. His group answered directly to the Black Spider rather than Glasstaff [ch 010 / entry; ch 009 / 009.05].

He wielded a morning star and later returned with singed fur after surviving the fire at Tresendar Manor [ch 009 / entry; ch 012 / 012.06].

## Personality and Motivations

Nosk reacted aggressively to challenges to his authority. Zenvon provoked him into revealing Cragmaw Castle as the Black Spider’s location, after which Nosk attacked to silence the party [ch 009 / 009.05].

Humiliation and his superiors’ displeasure motivated his revenge at the Stonehill Inn. Despite declaring himself trustworthy, he planned to disarm, bind, and betray the party. He nevertheless recognized the danger posed by other people in town and accepted a negotiated partial disarmament [ch 012 / entry; ch 012 / 012.06].

## History with the Party
*   Nosk, the leader of a group of bugbears sent by the Black Spider to reinforce the Redbrands, was confronted in their barracks by the party disguised in Redbrand cloaks [ch 009 / 009.05].
*   Nosk mocked Zenvon’s attempt to assert authority, revealing that his group answered directly to the Black Spider rather than to Glasstaff [ch 009 / 009.05].
*   While being interrogated by Zenvon, Nosk accidentally revealed that the Black Spider was located at Cragmaw Castle; realizing his mistake, he initiated combat against the party [ch 009 / 009.05].
*   During the brawl in the barracks, Nosk struck Zenvon with a morning star, dealing 14 points of damage and nearly incapacitating him [ch 009 / 009.06; ch 010 / entry].
*   Nosk's fate following the barracks brawl was not confirmed in the summaries of the first encounter [ch 009 / entry].
*   Weeks later, Nosk and his bugbears, identifiable by their singed fur, tracked the party to the Stonehill Inn [ch 012 / 012.05].
*   Nosk took innkeeper Toblen Stonehill hostage at knifepoint, demanding the party disarm and surrender to be taken to Cragmaw Castle as retribution for the humiliation at Tresendar Manor [ch 012 / 012.05; ch 012 / 012.06].
*   Zenvon deduced through insight that Nosk intended to betray the negotiation and attack the party once they were disarmed [ch 012 / 012.06].
*   When the standoff collapsed into combat, Zenvon severely wounded Nosk with a sneak attack from his shortbow, dealing 12 points of damage [ch 012 / 012.06].
*   The session concluded with Nosk bleeding heavily and the outcome of the battle undecided [ch 012 / 012.06].

- In the resumed Stonehill Inn fight, Zenvon shot Nosk for 14 damage, leaving him with one hit point. Pip then killed him [ch 013 / 013.01].
- Nosk was mentioned during the party’s audience with King Grol at Cragmaw Castle. Grol asked where Nosk and Gorl were; Zenvon Forepot reported that Nosk had been killed in battle as part of the party’s cover story [ch 015 / 015.01].

## Last Observed State

Dead: Pip killed Nosk at the Stonehill Inn after Zenvon’s hidden shortbow attack left him with one hit point [ch 013 / 013.01].

He was subsequently spoken of at Cragmaw Castle, where Zenvon Forepot reported his death to Grol [ch 015 / 015.01].

## Relationships

- **Black Spider:** Nosk served him directly and accidentally disclosed his location to the party. Gorl later said Nosk’s direct access to the boss meant he would have known more about the operation [ch 009 / 009.05; ch 013 / 013.03].
- **Glasstaff:** Nosk rejected the party’s assumed Redbrand authority, emphasizing that Glasstaff also worked for the Black Spider [ch 009 / 009.05].
- **Zenvon:** Their confrontations involved provocation, a damaging morning-star strike, exposed betrayal plans, and Zenvon’s eventual shortbow attacks against Nosk [ch 009 / entry; ch 012 / 012.06; ch 013 / 013.01].
- **Toblen Stonehill:** Nosk used Toblen as a hostage to demand the party’s surrender and planned to exploit his release as a distraction [ch 012 / 012.06].
- **Pip:** Pip delivered the killing blow against Nosk [ch 013 / 013.01].
- **Gorl:** A Scarlet Fist subordinate assigned to help Nosk clean up his mistakes [ch 013 / 013.03; ch 013 / 013.04].
- **Grol:** Asked the arriving party where Nosk and Gorl were [ch 015 / 015.01].
