## Identity
Nosk is the leader of a group of four bugbears, identified in the campaign record as the "Scarlet Fist," sent by the Black Spider to reinforce the Redbrand faction in Phandalin [ch 009 / entry; ch 010 / entry]. He is a direct subordinate of the Black Spider, operating outside the chain of command of Glasstaff, the local Redbrand leader [ch 009 / 009.05]. Nosk possesses a morning star and commands a small team of bugbears and allied goblins, including a goblin named Droop who belongs to the Scraptops clan [ch 009 / 009.05]. In later chapters, his group is distinguished by singed fur, indicating survival of the fire at Tresendar Manor [ch 012 / 012.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Nosk is aggressive and prone to rash decisions when embarrassed or provoked. In the barracks, Zenvon’s "rage-baiting" caused Nosk to impulsively reveal the Black Spider’s location (Cragmaw Castle), leading Nosk to immediately attempt murder to cover his mistake [ch 009 / 009.05; ch 009 / moment]. He views himself as superior to those he deems subordinate or lesser, mocking Zenvon’s authority as a Redbrand [ch 009 / 009.05].

Nosk is driven by a desire for retribution and restoring his standing with his superiors. He explicitly states that the party "embarrassed" him by destroying his operation without killing him, and that his bosses at Cragmaw Castle are displeased that the party remains alive [ch 012 / 012.05; ch 012 / 012.06]. While he attempts to project honor, claiming to be "a bugbear of my word," this is a facade for a planned betrayal; he intends to disarm the party under the guise of negotiation, then attack them once they are vulnerable [ch 012 / 012.06]. He lacks foresight, admitting when pressed that he had not considered the safety of other townsfolk, and he is easily intimidated when his plans are exposed [ch 012 / 012.06; ch 012 / moment].

## Relationships
**Zenvon**: Nosk’s primary antagonism is directed at Zenvon. Nosk nearly incapacitated Zenvon with a morning star in the barracks and later targeted him during the Stonehill ambush. Zenvon successfully outmaneuvered Nosk through deception, intimidation, and insight, consistently exposing Nosk’s lies and tactical errors [ch 009 / 009.06; ch 012 / 012.06].

**Toblen Stonehill**: Nosk holds Toblen hostage to leverage the party into compliance, showing little regard for Toblen’s life ("I personally don't care if he's fed to the goblins") but using him as a tactical tool [ch 012 / 012.05; ch 012 / 012.06].

**Glasstaff**: Nosk is openly dismissive of Glasstaff, asserting that his bugbear unit reports directly to the Black Spider, not to Glasstaff [ch 009 / 009.05].

**Black Spider**: Nosk is a direct subordinate of the Black Spider. His fear of displeasing the Black Spider drives his aggressive actions, including the attempt to silence the party after revealing Cragmaw Castle’s location and his pursuit of the party to restore his reputation [ch 009 / 009.05; ch 012 / 012.05].
