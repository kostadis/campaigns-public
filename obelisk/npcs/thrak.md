## Identity
Leader of the eight-member Red-Mask Kin, a group of hobgoblins stationed at Cragmaw Castle and equipped with longswords and longbows [ch 014 / 014.04; ch 015 / 015.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Lhupo describes his followers as disciplined soldiers who survived defeats elsewhere. She reports that the Red Masks resent King Grol but continue serving for his payments; Thrak’s individual motives are not separately established [ch 014 / 014.04].

## History with the Party
Lhupo identifies him and his position while describing the disciplined hobgoblins, their longswords and longbows, and their service for Grol’s money. The party does not confront him [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.04].
- Thrak watched the religious proceedings from the doorway of King Grol’s quarters, then attacked Zenvon with a two-handed strike and missed. Zenvon’s two subsequent attacks against him also missed [ch 015 / 015.01; ch 015 / 015.02].
- Veyra’s Web restrained Thrak while the party tried to stop Gundren’s abductor. Veyra later killed him with Magic Missile and ended the spell [ch 015 / 015.03].

## Last Observed State
Dead: Veyra killed Thrak with Magic Missile before ending Web [ch 015 / 015.03].

## Relationships
- **Red-Mask Kin:** Thrak led the eight hobgoblins stationed in the castle [ch 014 / 014.04].
- **King Grol:** Grol paid the Red Masks to maintain their cooperation despite their resentment of him [ch 014 / 014.04].
- **Lhupo:** She required Thrak’s death before committing her goblins to charge. During the battle, she reached and attacked the doppelganger while Thrak was still restrained in Web [ch 014 / 014.04; ch 015 / 015.03].
- **Veyra:** Her spells restrained and then killed him [ch 015 / 015.03].
