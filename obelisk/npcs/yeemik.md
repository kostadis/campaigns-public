## Identity
Yeemik is a goblin boss who commanded the goblin group guarding the ledge where the prisoner Sildar Hallwinter was held [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.03]. He possessed a scimitar and carried three agates and a potion of healing [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.04]. While he was identified by the party as a leader, Sildar Hallwinter clarified that Yeemik was not the true boss of the cave; that title belonged to Klarg, a bugbear [ch 003 / 003.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Yeemik displayed hostility toward the party, referring to them as "Bright Dwellers" when provoked by light [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / moment]. His combat behavior was aggressive; after Zenvon dealt him nine damage, he immediately turned his attention to the rogue, hitting for five damage before missing a second attack [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.03]. He prioritized pressing the attack against Pip Thistlewick, the character most exposed to the goblins' ranged attacks [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.03].

## Last Observed State
Yeemik was slain during the battle for the ledge. He survived a critical hit from Pip Thistlewick that dealt eight damage, but was killed by Sister Maela Dawnforge, who dealt six bludgeoning damage with a melee strike [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.03; ch 003 / moment]. His body remained on the southern ledge, where it was found by the party during the rescue of Sildar Hallwinter [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.04]. The party recovered his carried valuables (three agates and a potion of healing) before withdrawing [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.04].

## Relationships
**Zenvon:** Yeemik was provoked by Zenvon producing a light source, prompting a screech and an assault [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / moment]. Yeemik specifically targeted Zenvon with a retaliatory strike after taking nine damage from him [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.03].
**Pip Thistlewick:** Yeemik pressed the attack against Pip, landing a five-damage hit that initially prompted a knockout call before being corrected by the DM [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.03]. **Sister Maela Dawnforge:** Sister Maela delivered the killing blow to Yeemik with a bludgeoning melee attack, switching from a proposed spell after a mechanics check revealed the target would have succeeded on the saving throw [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.03; ch 003 / moment].
**Sildar Hallwinter:** Sildar identified Yeemik’s corpse and corrected the party’s assumption that Yeemik was the cave's ultimate leader, naming Klarg as the true boss [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.04]. Sildar expressed frustration upon seeing Yeemik's body [ch 003 / moment].
**Klarg:** Yeemik served as a subordinate leader in Klarg's hierarchy; his death did not eliminate the cave's true leadership [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.04].
