## Identity
Klarg was the true leader of the Cragmaw Hideout, identified as a bugbear [ch 003 / 003.04; ch 004 / entry]. While the goblin Yeemik was initially mistaken by the party as the boss, Sildar Hallwinter corrected this assumption, establishing Klarg as the ultimate authority in the cave [ch 003 / 003.04]. Klarg held command over the hideout's defenses and remained deeper in the cave while goblin subordinates handled lower-level encounters [ch 003 / entry; ch 004 / 004.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Klarg was motivated by the preservation of his command and the security of the Cragmaw Hideout. Sildar urged the party to deal with Klarg specifically because he possessed the ability to send word of their escape or attack to allies, suggesting a role focused on communication and coordination of the bandit network [ch 004 / 004.01]. He engaged directly in combat when his hideout was threatened, dealing heavy blows to intruders to protect his domain [ch 004 / 004.04].

## History with the Party
- Klarg is a bugbear leader mentioned by Sildar Hallwinter as a dangerous threat deeper in the cave that the party did not encounter during Chapter 003 [ch 003 / entry].
- Sildar corrected the party's assumption that Yeemik was the ultimate leader, identifying Klarg as the true boss [ch 003 / 003.04; ch 003 / moment].
- Sister Maela confirmed that the party had not killed any bugbears, leaving Klarg as an unresolved threat [ch 003 / 003.04].
- The DM warned that Klarg might send a patrol to find the escaping party, prompting them to conceal their trails [ch 003 / 003.04].
- Sildar identified himself as a member of the Lords' Alliance and urged the party to deal with Klarg before the bugbear could send word ahead [ch 004 / 004.01].
- Klarg remained alive after Sildar's rescue but was killed by Zenvon during the party's return to the hideout in Chapter 004 [ch 004 / entry].
- Klarg joined the battle in the cave, dealing heavy blows to Pip and Sister Maela [ch 004 / 004.04].
- Veyra struck Klarg with Magic Missile before Zenvon delivered the killing blow to Klarg with his scimitar and dagger [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 004 / moment].
- Searching Klarg's cave revealed sacks and crates bearing the Lionshield Coster mark, along with 1,700 copper pieces, two potions of healing, and a jade frog statuette [ch 004 / 004.05].

## Last Observed State
Klarg is dead. After remaining alive following the initial party retreat in Chapter 003 [ch 003 / entry], he joined the fighting when the party returned to the hideout in Chapter 004 [ch 004 / entry]. During the battle, he inflicted significant damage on Pip and Sister Maela before being struck by Veyra with Magic Missile [ch 004 / 004.04]. Zenvon delivered the killing blow using a scimitar and dagger [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 004 / moment]. Following his death, the party searched his cave, discovering Lionshield Coster supplies, 1,700 copper pieces, two potions of healing, and a jade frog statuette [ch 004 / 004.05].
