## Identity
Gundren Rockseeker is a dwarf merchant and the eldest brother of Tharden and Nundro Rockseeker. The brothers claimed to have rediscovered Wave Echo Cave. He originally hired Zenvon Forepot to deliver provisions to Phandalin for 19 gold pieces. [ch 002 / 002.01; ch 005 / 005.02; ch 001 / entry]

He now has a formal mining partnership with the party, granting them ten percent of the Wave Echo Cave operation’s gross, before taxes and expenses. [ch 017 / 017.07]

## Personality and Motivations
Gundren is excited by archaeological adventure and regards Wave Echo Cave as the adventure of a lifetime. He describes its prospects in terms of a magical artifact and valuable Mithral Silver deposits. [ch 015 / entry; ch 017 / 017.07]

He expresses strong gratitude for his rescue, gives the party a finely crafted quarterstaff, and welcomes proper food and a drink after captivity. [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.05; ch 017 / moment]

He needs help finding his missing brothers and developing the mine. He initially offers a share after expenses, then accepts Zenvon’s counteroffer for a share of the gross and puts the agreement in writing. [ch 017 / 017.07]

He questions arrangements that leave him vulnerable but accepts Zenvon’s explanations about the goblins and cooperates. He refuses hobgoblin meat. [ch 016 / 016.01; ch 016 / 016.02]

## History with the Party

## Last Observed State
Alive and back at the Stonehill Inn in Phandalin, Gundren has completed the witnessed mining agreement with the party. The session ends with the contract settled and the party preparing to rest. [ch 017 / moment; ch 017 / 017.07]

His brothers are missing. He suspects they reached Wave Echo Cave or encountered other interested parties, but he does not know their fate. [ch 017 / 017.07]
