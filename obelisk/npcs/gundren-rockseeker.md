## Identity
Gundren Rockseeker is a dwarf merchant and the eldest brother of Tharden and Nundro Rockseeker. The brothers claimed to have rediscovered the entrance to Wave Echo Cave. [ch 002 / 002.01; ch 005 / 005.02]

He hired Zenvon Forepot to deliver provisions to Phandalin for 19 gold pieces, arranging to meet the wagon at Barthen's Provisions after riding ahead with Sildar Hallwinter. [ch 001 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Gundren was excited and convinced he had found the lost mine of the Phandelver Pact. He also collected passage money from the wagon's other travelers while paying Zenvon for the delivery. [ch 001 / entry]

The GM describes him as an adventurous, archaeological sort who regards Wave Echo Cave as the adventure of a lifetime. His likely preference is to accompany the party toward the cave; this remains an expectation rather than an enacted decision. [ch 015 / entry; ch 015 / 015.05]

## History with the Party
- Gundren hired Zenvon Forepot to deliver provisions to Phandalin for 19 gold pieces, then rode ahead with his escort, Sildar Hallwinter, to meet the wagon at Barthen's Provisions. [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.01]
- Gundren also collected passage money from the other travelers on the wagon (Pip, Sister Maela, and Veyra), meaning he did not lose money on the delivery arrangement. [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.01]
- Gundren was excited and convinced he had discovered the lost mine of the Phandelver Pact (Wave Echo Cave) in the nearby hills. [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.04]
- Gundren and Sildar were ambushed by goblins on the Triboar Trail; their riderless horses were found with ransacked belongings, and trail evidence indicated both men were dragged away alive. [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.02; ch 001 / moment]
- An empty leather map case was found at the ambush site, but the map itself was missing. [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.02; ch 001 / moment]
- Elmina Barthen considers Gundren a close friend and was deeply worried when he failed to arrive at Barthen's Provisions. [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.04]
- Toblen Stonehill remembers Gundren as a good man whose business, along with his brother’s, benefited the town, clarifying they were not relatives. [ch 002 / 002.01]
- Freda of the Stonehill Inn accused the Redbrands of being behind Gundren’s disappearance. [ch 002 / 002.02]
- Tuck Stonehill asked the party if they would rescue Gundren, noting that "People who get taken don't always come back." [ch 002 / 002.02; ch 002 / moment]
- Daran Edermath scolded the party for stopping to gather information instead of immediately rescuing Gundren, urging them to act quickly. [ch 002 / 002.03; ch 002 / moment]
- Sister Garaele at the Shrine of Luck recognized the party as the ones who arrived without Gundren and blessed their rescue mission. [ch 002 / 002.04]
- Sildar Hallwinter explained that the ambush on him and Gundren was deliberate, ordered by someone who tipped off the goblins, and that Gundren had been taken away separately from himself. [ch 004 / 004.01]
- The party searched Cragmaw Hideout but did not find Gundren there; his status remained unresolved during this period. [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.06]
- Sildar revealed that Gundren and his brothers, Tharden and Nundro, claimed to have rediscovered Wave Echo Cave, and that Gundren is the eldest brother. [ch 005 / 005.02]
- Sildar asserted that the cave's treasure belongs to Lord Neverember and the Lords’ Alliance, and that Gundren would receive compensation through a contract subordinate to Alliance interests. [ch 005 / 005.02]
- Zenvon negotiated a separate fee for Gundren's rescue, initially asking for 500 gold, but ultimately settling for a separate payment structure distinct from the Iarno Albrek contract. [ch 005 / 005.02]
- Rondar, a captured Redbrand, denied knowing Gundren when asked during an interrogation. [ch 006 / 006.04]
- A letter found in Iarno Albrek's quarters ordered his capture or death and the delivery of "their dwarven maps," connecting Iarno to The Spider and to responsibility for Gundren Rockseeker's capture. [ch 007 / 007.07]

- During the inn interrogation, Gorl said Gundren had passed through Cragmaw Castle and was being held there because the Spider wanted him [ch 013 / 013.03].
- After learning that Gorl did not know the Wave Echo Cave route, Zenvon chose Cragmaw Castle as the best immediate lead for Gundren and the missing map [ch 013 / 013.04].

- Lhupo reported a dwarf held by Grol and a drow visitor seeking him. Zenvon used the fiction of a sacred offering to restrain her intention to eat the prisoner [ch 014 / 014.04].
- On entering the king’s quarters, the party directly saw Gundren badly beaten and unconscious on the floor near the table, with Grol, Snarl, and the unnamed drow woman standing over him. He could not recognize them and expose their disguise while unconscious [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.05].
- In Grol's quarters, the party witnessed Nisrell Bao purchase the unconscious Gundren for seven hundred gold pieces. Nisrell began dragging him away, then attacked Zenvon and revealed a doppelganger beneath the Drow disguise. [ch 015 / 015.01]
- The doppelganger carried Gundren through the battle, knocking Zenvon unconscious while escaping. The party prioritized stopping the abduction, and Veyra's Web temporarily restrained the creature. [ch 015 / 015.02; ch 015 / 015.03]
- Gundren's weight slowed the doppelganger enough for Pip to catch it. Veyra killed it with a second-level Magic Missile, rescuing Gundren. [ch 015 / 015.04]
- Pip secured Gundren before returning to help Lhupo. Gundren regained consciousness by the session's end; the proposed feast and departure deception remained unplayed plans. [ch 015 / 015.05]

## Last Observed State
Rescued and conscious at Cragmaw Castle after Pip secured him. He had been badly beaten and unconscious before the rescue; the summaries do not describe his remaining injuries after he wakes. [ch 015 / entry; ch 015 / 015.05]

No departure has occurred. Zenvon’s player proposed telling the goblins that a divine command required escorting Gundren to Phandalin, while leaving the actual destination undecided. Gundren can potentially provide Wave Echo Cave's location, but no route is supplied in this closing scene. [ch 015 / 015.05]
