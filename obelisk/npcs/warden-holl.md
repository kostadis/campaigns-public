## Identity
Warden Holl is an undead servant and the former apprentice of the Red Wizard Hamun Kost [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.07]. He is distinct from Warden Dreth, the leader of the Kost Wardens [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / moment]. His current form is that of a zombie who visually resembles a Red Wizard, distinguishing him from the other undead servants at Old Owl Well who resemble a former guard or peasants [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.07].

## Personality and Motivations
No independent actions, speech, or internal motivations are recorded for Holl; he acts solely as a waiter during the party's dinner with Hamun Kost [ch 011 / 011.07]. His behavior is defined entirely by Hamun Kost’s remark that his apprentice is "serving me so much better in death than he served me in life" [ch 011 / moment; ch 011 / entry].
