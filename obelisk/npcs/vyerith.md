## Identity

Vyerith was a doppelganger appearing as a white-haired, gray-skinned Drow woman in gray robes. [ch 015 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations

- Repeatedly pressed to complete Gundren’s purchase and depart; Zenvon’s insight revealed marked anxiety about remaining in the castle. [ch 015 / entry]
- Invoked Menzoberranzan while expressing contempt for goblin worship. [ch 015 / entry]
- Attempted to escape with Gundren during the fighting. [ch 015 / entry]

## History with the Party

- Paid seven hundred gold for Gundren. [ch 015 / entry]
- Attempted to attack during the prayer, missed, revealed her featureless humanoid form, and accused the party of conspiring with Lhupo. [ch 015 / entry]
- Repeatedly attacked Zenvon, carried Gundren toward escape, killed two pursuing goblins, and badly wounded Pip. [ch 015 / entry]
- Was killed by Veyra’s second-level Magic Missile. [ch 015 / entry]

## Last Observed State

Dead: the entry explicitly states that Veyra’s second-level Magic Missile killed her. [ch 015 / entry]

## Relationships

- **Gundren:** Purchased him as a prisoner and carried him toward escape. [ch 015 / entry]
- **Zenvon:** Repeatedly targeted him during battle. [ch 015 / entry]
- **Lhupo:** Accused the party of conspiring with her; an actual connection between Vyerith and Lhupo is not established. [ch 015 / entry]
- **Pip:** Badly wounded him during the battle. [ch 015 / entry]
- **Veyra:** Killed Vyerith with Magic Missile. [ch 015 / entry]
