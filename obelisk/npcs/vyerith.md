## Identity

Vyerith was a doppelganger appearing as a white-haired, gray-skinned Drow woman in gray robes. [ch 015 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations

- Repeatedly pressed to complete Gundren’s purchase and depart; Zenvon’s insight revealed marked anxiety about remaining in the castle. [ch 015 / entry]
- Invoked Menzoberranzan while expressing contempt for goblin worship. [ch 015 / entry]
- Attempted to escape with Gundren during the fighting. [ch 015 / entry]

## History with the Party

- Paid seven hundred gold for Gundren. [ch 015 / entry]
- Attempted to attack during the prayer, missed, revealed her featureless humanoid form, and accused the party of conspiring with Lhupo. [ch 015 / entry]
- Repeatedly attacked Zenvon, carried Gundren toward escape, killed two pursuing goblins, and badly wounded Pip. [ch 015 / entry]
- Was killed by Veyra’s second-level Magic Missile. [ch 015 / entry]
- Polt recounted Vyerith’s death near the back of the storage tower and the trees, crediting Veyra. He reported that nobody had touched or searched the body; its possible loot remained unexamined. [ch 016 / 016.01]
- Before leaving the castle, the party searched Vyerith’s corpse outside and recovered Gundren Rockseeker’s map to Wave Echo Cave, identified by his handwriting. Nocturnal creatures had chewed the body. [ch 017 / 017.03; ch 017 / 017.04]

## Last Observed State

Dead: Veyra’s second-level Magic Missile killed her. Her corpse subsequently remained outside the castle, damaged by creatures overnight. The party searched it and recovered Gundren Rockseeker’s map to Wave Echo Cave. [ch 015 / entry; ch 017 / 017.03; ch 017 / 017.04]

## Relationships

- **Gundren Rockseeker:** Purchased him for seven hundred gold and carried him toward escape. His map to Wave Echo Cave was later recovered from her corpse. [ch 015 / entry; ch 017 / 017.03; ch 017 / 017.04]
- **Zenvon:** Repeatedly attacked him during battle. [ch 015 / entry]
- **Lhupo:** Accused the party of conspiring with her. [ch 015 / entry]
- **Pip:** Badly wounded him during the battle. [ch 015 / entry]
- **Veyra:** Killed Vyerith with Magic Missile; Polt’s later account also credited Veyra. [ch 015 / entry; ch 016 / 016.01]
- **Polt:** Recounted her death and reported that her body had not yet been searched. [ch 016 / 016.01]
