## Identity
Rondar is a lieutenant of the Redbrand Ruffians in Phandalin, distinguishable by the gang’s signature red cloak [ch 006 / 006.03; ch 006 / 006.04; ch 007 / entry]. He served as a mid-level operative under the leader known as "Glasstaff," whom Rondar identified as Iarno Albrek, a former Lords’ Alliance agent gone rogue [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / moment]. Rondar suggested that Glasstaff himself answered to an unidentified superior [ch 006 / 006.04; ch 006 / moment].

## Personality and Motivations
Rondar displays a mix of professional pragmatism and self-preservation. He initially attempted to establish a neutral ground, offering a "professional courtesy" truce where the Redbrands and adventurers would avoid interfering with each other [ch 006 / 006.03]. When pressed, he asserted territorial pride, stating, "This town is our town," though he quickly folded under physical intimidation, particularly when Pip placed a blade to his throat [ch 006 / 006.03; ch 006 / 006.04].

He possesses a survival-oriented caution regarding the supernatural threats in the local area, advising, "We don't ask questions about what's down there," and urging others to move away from the guarded passages [ch 006 / 006.05]. Despite his bravado, his courage was fragile; he mocked Zenvon after his intimidation failed but capitulated immediately when confronted with the reality of their violence [ch 006 / moment; ch 006 / 006.04]. In the face of imminent death, he appealed to basic logic, protesting the moral dissonance of his executioners by stating, "you can't expect me to not think it's personal when you're about to kill me" [ch 007 / moment].

## History with the Party
- Rondar is a Redbrand lieutenant who recognizes the party’s role in defeating four of his men; he initially offers a truce where each side stays out of the other’s business [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.03].
- After being intimidated by Pip’s blade at his throat, Rondar identified Glasstaff as Iarno Albrek, a former Lords’ Alliance agent gone bad, and hinted that Iarno answered to an unidentified superior [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.04; ch 006 / moment].
- Rondar denied any knowledge of Gundren and agreed to guide the party to Tresendar Manor via a back entrance, accepting silver instead of gold under pressure [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.04; ch 006 / 006.05].
- He guided the party to a concealed forest tunnel and warned them of a malevolent, intelligent creature guarding the passage, recounting how a Redbrand had returned strangely altered after entering the cellar two months prior [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.05].
- Zenvon paid Rondar 2 silver pieces, promised 3 more, and had him bound and gagged before forcing him to lead the descent into the caverns [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.05].
- The Nothic’s attention fell on Rondar as a potential meal; the party reached a cold agreement to sacrifice him to the creature to secure passage [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.05; ch 007 / 007.01].
- Upon realizing he was to be sacrificed, Rondar bolted and screamed for help; Zenvon wounded him, leaving him on four hit points before Sister Maela delivered the fatal mace blow [ch 007 / entry; ch 007 / 007.02].
- The Nothic dragged Rondar’s body away to eat, satisfying its hunger and granting the party passage [ch 007 / entry; ch 007 / 007.02].

## Last Observed State
Rondar is dead. After being bound and gagged to guide the party through a concealed forest tunnel, he was forced to descend into a cavern guarded by a Nothic [ch 006 / 006.05; ch 007 / 007.01]. The creature demanded a sacrifice to allow the party passage, and the party agreed to offer Rondar [ch 007 / 007.01]. Upon hearing the agreement, Rondar bolted, screaming for help, but was caught by the party [ch 007 / 007.01]. Zenvon wounded him, and Sister Maela delivered the fatal blow with her mace [ch 007 / entry; ch 007 / 007.02]. His body was subsequently dragged away by the Nothic to be eaten, satisfying the creature's hunger and granting the party safe passage [ch 007 / entry; ch 007 / 007.02].

## Relationships
*   **The Party (Zenvon, Pip, Veyra, Sister Maela):** Initially, Rondar treated them as adversaries, attempting to broker a truce [ch 006 / 006.03]. This shifted to an exploitative dynamic where the party coerced him into guiding them to Tresendar Manor, paying him meagerly and treating him as a potential lure or hostage [ch 006 / 006.05]. Ultimately, the party sacrificed him to the Nothic, with Sister Maela justifying the killing as an impersonal judgment on his criminal character, a stance Rondar rejected in his final moments [ch 007 / entry; ch 007 / 007.02; ch 007 / moment].
*   **Halia Thornton:** Rondar was seated with Halia Thornton of the Miner's Exchange at the Sleeping Giant [ch 006 / 006.03]. Halia performed theatrical shock at discovering his affiliation with the Redbrands despite his conspicuous uniform, suggesting a public facade of ignorance or separation from his criminal activities [ch 006 / 006.04; ch 006 / moment].
*   **Glasstaff / Iarno Albrek:** Rondar served as a lieutenant under Glasstaff, who he revealed to be Iarno Albrek. He acknowledged Glasstaff's own subservience to a higher, unnamed power, and feared reprisals if he was heard revealing Iarno’s identity [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.04; ch 006 / moment].
