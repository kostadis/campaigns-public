## Identity
Harbin Wester is the Townmaster and mayor of Phandalin, a role he explicitly cites alongside his identity as a banker [ch 006 / 006.02; ch 006 / entry]. He operates out of the Townmaster’s Hall and is responsible for authorizing work under the leadership of the lord of Neverwinter [ch 006 / 006.02; ch 010 / 010.01]. He is referred to as "pompous" in his chapter entry, though this characterization is contextualized by his defensive behavior regarding finances [ch 012 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Harbin is characterized by nervousness and caution, described as "visibly frightened" when he first emerges from his hall to address the party [ch 006 / 006.02]. He views local gangs like the Redbrands as a "terrible plague" but relies on adventurers to address physical threats rather than acting himself [ch 006 / 006.02; ch 002 / 002.02]. Critically, Harbin prioritizes fiscal conservatism and self-interest over straightforward honor; he attempted to short the party 20 gold pieces by claiming a lower agreed rate, only paying the difference from his private funds after being cornered with a precise recollection of the negotiation [ch 012 / 012.02]. He responds to social criticism with exaggerated sarcasm and disengagement, dismissing the heroes to return to "important Town Master activities," which he defines as playing tic-tac-toe [ch 012 / 012.02]. He is, however, pragmatic enough to recognize valuable skills, having offered Pip Thistlewick potential employment in his personal business due to Pip’s negotiating prowess [ch 006 / 006.02].

## History with the Party
*   Lanar publicly condemned Harbin Wester as useless for relying on notices and wax seals while the roads remained unsafe [ch 002 / 002.02; ch 002 / entry].
*   Harbin emerged from the Townmaster’s Hall in Chapter 6, visibly frightened, and introduced himself as Phandalin’s mayor and townmaster [ch 006 / 006.02].
*   He described the Redbrands as a terrible plague and requested an investigation into marauder attacks on caravans near Wyvern Tor [ch 006 / 006.02].
*   Harbin initially offered a 100-gold reward, which Pip negotiated up to 120 gold pieces by leveraging distance and previous payments [ch 006 / 006.02].
*   Impressed by Pip’s negotiation, Harbin suggested Pip might find work in his personal business, citing his own status as a banker-townmaster [ch 006 / 006.02].
*   Harbin agreed to hold a captured Redbrand in custody, though he expressed uncertainty about what to do with him afterward [ch 006 / 006.02].
*   In Chapter 10, Daran Edermath mentioned that Harbin had reported caravan attacks near Wyvern Tor, though Daran had not followed the details [ch 010 / 010.02; ch 010 / entry].
*   The party acknowledged Harbin’s 120-gp contract for Wyvern Tor as an unfinished, unstarted quest in their review of leads [ch 010 / 010.01].
*   Upon returning to Phandalin in Chapter 12, Harbin attempted to pay only 100 gold pieces, claiming that was the agreed amount [ch 012 / 012.02].
*   Zenvon challenged the deception, and Pip recited the specific negotiation history (110 offered, 140 demanded, 120 settled), forcing Harbin to pay the full 120 gold [ch 012 / 012.02].
*   Harbin paid the additional 20 gold from his private purse, dropped the pile on the desk, and dismissed the party with a curt "go" [ch 012 / 012.02].
*   When Zenvon criticized Harbin’s rudeness, Harbin responded with exaggerated sarcasm and returned to playing tic-tac-toe, calling it an important townmaster activity [ch 012 / 012.02].

## Last Observed State
Harbin was last seen in Chapter 12 inside the Townmaster’s Hall. After being forced to pay the full 120 gold pieces for the Wyvern Tor contract, he dropped the coin pile on his desk, issued a curt "go," and resumed playing tic-tac-toe [ch 012 / 012.02]. He is alive and active in his administrative role [ch 012 / 012.02].

## Relationships
*   **Pip Thistlewick:** Harbin respects Pip’s financial acumen. After the initial negotiation, he offered Pip future employment [ch 006 / 006.02]. When Pip recited his account of the deal ("you offered 110, and I made an offer, I demanded 140, and we settled on 120"), Harbin capitulated and paid the full amount, though he did so with visible irritation [ch 012 / 012.02].
*   **Zenvon Forepot:** Harbin attempts to deceive Zenvon regarding the payment amount but is rebuked by Zenvon for his rudeness following the transaction. Harbin dismisses Zenvon’s moral standing with sarcasm ("you're such noble heroes") and ignores his presence to return to his game [ch 012 / 012.02].
*   **Lanar:** Lanar publicly condemned Harbin in Chapter 2, calling him useless for hiding behind notices and wax seals while roads remained unsafe [ch 002 / 002.02; ch 002 / entry].
*   **Daran Edermath:** Daran mentioned that Harbin had reported caravan attacks near Wyvern Tor, though Daran himself did not follow the details of the reports [ch 010 / 010.02].
*   **The Redbrands:** Harbin acknowledges the Redbrands as a plague but delegates the solution to the party, admitting uncertainty about what to do with captured gang members after the party left a prisoner in his custody [ch 006 / 006.02].
