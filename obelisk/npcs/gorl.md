## Identity
Gorl is a smaller-than-average bugbear with a perpetual nervous twitch. He served in the Scarlet Fist under Nosk. He explicitly spells his name G-O-R-L and is distinct from King Grol [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.04; ch 013 / 013.05].

## Personality and Motivations
He alternates between defiance and terror, describes himself as hired muscle, and says he was ordered to help Nosk clean up his mistakes. Veyra’s intimidation succeeds after Zenvon’s persuasion fails [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.03]. He bargains for release and says he wants to get as far away as his arms can carry him. His agreement to wait is not established as sincere [ch 013 / 013.05].

## History with the Party
- Zenvon pursued Gorl and knocked him unconscious with his shortsword’s hilt; the party bound him and carried him into the inn [ch 013 / 013.02; ch 013 / 013.03].
- Under Veyra’s threats, he identified the Spider as a drow and said Gundren was held at Cragmaw Castle [ch 013 / 013.03].
- Zenvon amputated his legs with a scimitar while preserving his life. After Toblen refused to house him, the party took him in a cart [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.03].
- Gorl identified the dead Brakka as the member who had secretly known the Wave Echo Cave route, which Gorl does not know [ch 013 / 013.04].
- At the castle, he described the groups within and an avoided monster, while muddling the goblin factions [ch 013 / 013.05].
- The party required him to support a cover story that they had rescued him. He objected that they were the attackers; Maela threatened him against betrayal and Veyra proposed cutting out his tongue [ch 013 / 013.06].

## Last Observed State
Alive, without his legs, accompanying the party outside Cragmaw Castle. He has been included in an untested pilgrimage deception. The party had not entered, and his willingness to maintain its cover remained uncertain [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.05; ch 013 / 013.06].

## Relationships
Nosk led his Scarlet Fist group; Gorl says Nosk worked directly for the Spider [ch 013 / 013.03; ch 013 / 013.04]. Brakka kept the cave route from him [ch 013 / 013.04]. Zenvon controls his captivity; Veyra intimidates him; Maela threatens him if he betrays the cover [ch 013 / 013.03; ch 013 / 013.06]. His account of the Arrow Slit Watch and Lhupo’s group is confused and should not be treated as a reliable full faction map [ch 013 / 013.05].

## Notable Quotes
