## Identity
Gog is a massive ogre who serves as a combatant alongside Brughor Axe-Biter. He fights using a great club [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.04]. During his final confrontation with the party, he is identified as an ogre, distinct from the bugbear followers who fled [ch 011 / 011.04; ch 011 / 011.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Gog exhibits extreme loyalty and emotional dependence on Brughor Axe-Biter, with the DM establishing their relationship as romantic [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.05]. His primary motivation during combat was furious grief over Brughor’s death, which drove him to stay behind while the rest of the forces retreated [ch 011 / 011.04; ch 011 / 011.05]. He was verbally abusive toward retreating allies, denouncing them as cowards, and expressed violent intent toward the party by threatening to eat them alive [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.04]. While under persuasion, he revealed a lack of independent strategic knowledge, confirming that Brughor ordered the raids but failing to recognize the name Hamun Kost [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.05].

## Last Observed State
Gog was killed by Pip Thistlewick after an interrogation yielded no useful intelligence regarding Hamun Kost [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.05]. Prior to his death, he took significant damage from the party, including 15 damage from Veyra’s Magic Missile and multiple weapon strikes [ch 011 / 011.05].
