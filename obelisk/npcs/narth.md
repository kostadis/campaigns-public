## Identity
Narth is an older human man in Phandalin, described as having weathered skin, a stooped back, and pale eyes that frequently linger on windows, doorways, and perceived bad omens. He carries a scent of hay, rain, and pipe smoke [ch 002 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Narth is observant and cautious, tending to focus on potential dangers in his environment [ch 002 / entry]. He is motivated by concern for his neighbors, specifically fearing for Sister Garaele after she returned injured and silent about her whereabouts. He deduced that Garaele kept silent to protect the townspeople and sought to leverage Sister Maela’s religious status to gain Garaele’s confidence [ch 002 / 002.02]. Narth displays grudging respect for the party, acknowledging their success in rescuing Sildar while maintaining a skeptical focus on local threats like the Redbrands [ch 005 / 005.01].

## History with the Party
- Narth approached Sister Maela, identifying her as temple folk, to express concern for Sister Garaele who had returned injured and silent about her whereabouts; he hoped another religious person might gain her confidence [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.02].
- Narth was present among the locals in Phandalin when the party returned from Cragmaw Hideout, displaying a mix of grudging respect and recognition for the rescue [ch 005 / 005.01].
- Narth was present at the Stonehill Inn alongside Elsa, Tuck, Toblen, and Freda while the party took a long rest after their escape from the manor [ch 009 / 009.08].
