## Identity

Narth is an older human man with weathered skin, a stooped back, and pale eyes that linger on windows, doorways, and bad omens. He smells faintly of hay, rain, and pipe smoke [ch 002 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations

Narth is watchful and concerned for Sister Garaele. He believes she withholds information about her injured return to protect the locals, and hopes another religious person might gain her confidence [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.02].

## History with the Party
- Narth approached Sister Maela, identifying her as temple folk, to express concern for Sister Garaele who had returned injured and silent about her whereabouts; he hoped another religious person might gain her confidence [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.02].
- Narth was present among the locals in Phandalin when the party returned from Cragmaw Hideout, displaying a mix of grudging respect and recognition for the rescue [ch 005 / 005.01].
- Narth was present at the Stonehill Inn alongside Elsa, Tuck, Toblen, and Freda while the party took a long rest after their escape from the manor [ch 009 / 009.08].
- Narth was present at the repaired Stonehill Inn when the party returned with Gundren and reported to Sildar Hallwinter [ch 017 / 017.06].

## Last Observed State

Narth was present among the villagers at the Stonehill Inn during the party’s midmorning return with Gundren. No individual action or dialogue is recorded for him in that scene [ch 017 / 017.06].
