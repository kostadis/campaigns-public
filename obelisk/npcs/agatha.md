## Identity
Agatha is a powerful and dangerous undead banshee [ch 009 / entry; ch 012 / entry]. She resides in a lair that is accessible via the ruins of Conyberry [ch 010 / 010.03]. While she is described as an "undead creature of considerable power" [ch 009 / 009.10] and a "pissed-off spirit" [ch 012 / 012.01], she possesses knowledge of ancient magical secrets, specifically regarding the legendary mage Bowgentle's spellbook and the wizard who originally built the tower at Old Owl Well [ch 009 / entry; ch 010 / 010.06]. She is also potentially a source of information regarding the whereabouts of Orryn Voss, a lost mentor [ch 012 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Agatha is characterized by profound bitterness and a sense of betrayal. Hamun Kost describes her as a spirit "betrayed in some profound way" who may be willing to answer questions if approached with "appropriate respect" [ch 012 / entry; ch 012 / 012.01]. She displays vanity or nostalgia, speaking "wistfully about the beautiful gems, baubles she had in her life" [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.10]. Her primary dangerous trait is her wailing, which is not merely a weapon but a "lure to draw you into the ethereal plane" [ch 012 / 012.01]. She is not mindless; Sister Maela notes that unlike zombies, banshees are intelligent and deadly, with Sister Maela stating, "the only good banshee is a dead banshee" [ch 012 / moment].

## History with the Party
- Agatha is a powerful and dangerous undead banshee who possesses knowledge of ancient magical secrets, specifically regarding the legendary mage Bowgentle's spellbook, and she is known to speak wistfully about beautiful gems and baubles from her life. [ch 009 / entry]
- Sister Garaele attempted to approach Agatha to ask about the spellbook but barely escaped with her life, leading her to request the party bring Agatha a jeweled silver comb as a gift to win her favor. [ch 009 / 009.10]
- Zenvon accepted the task of visiting Agatha in exchange for three potions of healing, though he noted it was a priority that would follow other high-priority business. [ch 009 / 009.10]
- During the party's review of open leads at the Miner's Exchange, Agatha's Lair was identified as one of the active quests alongside Old Owl Well, Wyvern Tor, Thundertree, and Cragmaw Castle. [ch 010 / 010.01]
- Toblen Stonehill advised the party that the route to Agatha's lair passes through the ruins of Conyberry, if they later undertake Sister Garaele's errand. [ch 010 / 010.03]
- Hamun Kost offered the party a choice between dealing with marauders at Wyvern Tor or asking Agatha for the name of the wizard who built the tower at Old Owl Well; the party chose Wyvern Tor after Zenvon and Sister Maela deemed Agatha the greater risk. [ch 010 / 010.06]
- Hamun Kost stated he did not want to question Agatha himself because he did not want to be killed by a banshee. [ch 010 / 010.06]
- Hamun Kost later characterized Agatha as a spirit betrayed in some profound way, warning that she might answer a question if approached with appropriate respect, while advising the party to ignore her wailing, not to look at her, and above all to not let her touch them. [ch 012 / entry]
- Zenvon expressed unwillingness to visit Agatha ("zombies are fine, but…" no banshees), while Pip suggested using the potential interaction to gain other information, and Sister Maela objected to repurposing Sister Garaele's gift. [ch 012 / 012.01]
- Veyra became highly excited at the prospect of asking Agatha about her lost mentor, Orryn Voss, but grew conflicted about displacing the original request regarding the spellbook. [ch 012 / 012.01]
- Hamun Kost declined to accompany the party to Agatha's lair, and Zenvon decided they would visit Agatha at a later time, preferring to rest between quests first. [ch 012 / 012.01]

## Last Observed State
Agatha remains in her lair, which Sister Garaele described as a static location where "the banshee isn't going anywhere" [ch 009 / 009.10]. The party has not visited her; as of the end of Chapter 12, they had discussed approaching her but decided to postpone the visit to rest first [ch 012 / 012.01]. Hamun Kost advised the party that they must ignore her wailing, avoid looking at her, and ensure she does not touch them, as she is deadly [ch 012 / entry; ch 012 / 012.01]. Status not established in the summaries beyond her continued existence in her lair.
