## Identity
Halia Thornton is the Guildmaster of the Phandalin Miner's Exchange, a trading post and unofficial records office [ch 009 / 009.11]. She styles herself as a "local authority" within the town [ch 009 / entry]. While Freda, a local resident, initially suspected she had an influence over or connection to the Redbrand gang, Halia presented herself to the party as a merchant conducting business [ch 002 / 002.02; ch 006 / 006.03].

## Personality and Motivations
Halia is characterized by shrewdness and theatricality. Freda noted that the Redbrands harassed everyone in town except Halia, leading locals to wonder if they feared her, owed her, or both [ch 002 / moment; ch 002 / 002.02]. When confronted in the Sleeping Giant with Rondar, a Redbrand lieutenant wearing a conspicuous red cloak, Halia performed exaggerated shock and disbelief at his gang affiliation, claiming she was merely sharing a drink with a friend [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / moment].

During the interrogation of Rondar, Halia maintained this persona, attempting to leave and then feigning surprise when her friend was identified as a criminal [ch 006 / 006.04]. She later praised Zenvon’s interrogation methods, suggesting he was suited for "business" [ch 006 / 006.04]. Following the defeat of the Redbrands, Halia’s demeanor shifted from congratulatory to cold when she learned the gang leader, Glasstaff (Iarno Albrek), had escaped rather than died [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.11]. She expressed keen interest in any correspondence recovered from Glasstaff's personal effects, offering 100 gold pieces for the letters [ch 009 / 009.11]. She claimed her motivation was protecting local trade and business from disruption [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.11]. The party found her interest suspicious and felt she knew more about the Redbrands than she let on [ch 009 / 009.11]. *Note: The party has not learned her true affiliations on screen.*

## Relationships
*   **Zenvon Forepot:** Halia invited Zenvon to her establishment to arrange future business after he interrogated Rondar [ch 006 / 006.04]. She later attempted to negotiate with him for Glasstaff's letters, offering 100 gold, which he rebuffed [ch 009 / 009.11]. Zenvon viewed her as suspicious and worth investigating later in private [ch 009 / 009.11].
*   **Rondar:** Halia claimed Rondar was a "dear friend" and stated she was shocked to discover his membership in the Redbrand Ruffians [ch 006 / moment]. She was found drinking and conducting business with him at the Sleeping Giant before the confrontation [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.03]. When Zenvon forced her to stay during the interrogation, she claimed Rondar's fear under threat was proof of his sincere confession [ch 006 / entry].
*   **The Redbrands:** Freda observed that the gang avoided bothering Halia, suggesting a relationship of fear, debt, or mutual protection, though this was only a local suspicion [ch 002 / moment; ch 002 / 002.02]. Halia’s quick pivot to asking for correspondence after their defeat indicated a specific interest in the gang's internal communications [ch 009 / 009.11].
