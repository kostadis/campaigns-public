## Identity
Ssarnak is a malevolent, intelligent, one-eyed Nothic residing in the caverns and crevasses beneath Tresendar Manor [ch 006 / entry; ch 007 / entry; ch 009 / entry; ch 010 / entry]. It possesses long fangs and claws and communicates via telepathy and speech in Undercommon [ch 007 / entry]. While all nearby creatures can hear its voice, only Sister Maela demonstrated the ability to understand the language [ch 007 / entry]. The creature is described as extracting secrets from nearby minds [ch 007 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Ssarnak operates on a transactional basis, guarding the manor's cavern entrance in exchange for food supplied by Iarno [ch 006 / entry; ch 007 / entry]. It has offered the possibility of avoiding bloodshed if fed, though it also bargains for specific victims [ch 006 / entry; ch 007 / entry]. It shows little interest in conversation once feeding begins [ch 007 / entry]. It whispered to Veyra that her teacher was not dead, a message that stopped her cold [ch 009 / 009.07; ch 010 / entry]. It recognizes and answers to Glasstaff, responding to illusions of the wizard's activity [ch 009 / entry; ch 010 / entry].
