## Identity
Hamun Kost is a Red Wizard of Thay and a necromancer distinguished by distinctive tattoos across his shaved head [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.06]. He operates from Old Owl Well, where he commands twelve undead servants known as Kost Wardens [ch 010 / entry]. While he presents himself publicly as conducting an archaeological dig, his methods involve the reanimation of corpses for labor and defense [ch 010 / 010.06]. He possesses significant expertise in Netheril archaeology and language, correctly identifying the sword Talon as the weapon of Aldith Tresendar, the Black Hawk [ch 011 / 011.07].

## Personality and Motivations
Hamun Kost combines cultivated hospitality with ruthless practicality and dark arrogance. He insists on civilized conduct, such as sharing a fine meal before discussing business, yet displays a macabre indifference to the state of his undead servants; he remarked that his undead former apprentice, Warden Holl, served him better in death than in life [ch 011 / moment]. His confidence borders on hubris; he told the party that he would have simply killed them if secrecy required it, rather than relying on their discretion [ch 010 / moment].

Kost is motivated by archaeological discovery and personal convenience rather than malice toward local settlements. He claims no desire to harm nearby communities, describing himself as wanting to be a "positive member of the local community," though he uses undead labor to avoid engaging in combat himself [ch 010 / 010.06]. He exhibits suppressed excitement regarding rare magical artifacts, such as the black obelisk and crystals, though he feigns ignorance of their significance [ch 011 / 011.07]. He is candid about his own vulnerabilities, admitting he avoided confronting the banshee Agatha because he did not wish to be killed by her [ch 010 / 010.06].

## History with the Party
- Hamun Kost is a Red Wizard of Thay and necromancer with distinctive tattoos on a shaved head, who commands twelve undead "Kost Wardens" at the ruins of Old Owl Well for archaeological labor and defense. [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.06]
- The party initially encountered Hamun after he raised zombies from the watchtower ruins; he confronted them personally after being provoked by Zenvon’s taunt, claiming he was merely conducting an archaeological dig. [ch 010 / 010.06]
- Hamun offered the location of Cragmaw Castle in exchange for the party clearing out bugbears and an ogre at Wyvern Tor, explaining he could handle them but did not wish to create more zombies for the task. [ch 010 / 010.06]
- The party accepted the Wyvern Tor contract, killing the ogre Gog and driving off the bugbears, though Gog did not recognize Hamun’s name during interrogation. [ch 011 / 011.05; ch 011 / 011.07]
- Upon the party’s return to Old Owl Well, Hamun insisted on serving a fine dinner before discussing the agreed-upon information, employing his undead former apprentice, Warden Holl, as a waiter. [ch 011 / 011.07]
- Hamun’s demeanor shifted abruptly and he demanded details when shown a charcoal drawing of a crystal-headed goblin, though he claimed ignorance of its meaning while Pip noted his suppressed excitement. [ch 011 / 011.07]
- Hamun identified the sword Talon as belonging to Aldith Tresendar, the Black Hawk, and offered himself as an expert consultant on Netheril archaeology and language for future discoveries. [ch 011 / 011.07]
- During a subsequent meal, Hamun fulfilled his debt by personally marking the exact location of Cragmaw Castle on the party’s map, calling it a "fabulous place" to visit. [ch 012 / 012.01]
- Hamun provided tactical advice regarding the banshee Agatha, citing the *Thayan Necronomicon: The Undead and You* (inventing the author's name on the spot), and warned that banshees are spirits who feel profoundly betrayed. [ch 012 / 012.01]
- Hamun declined to accompany the party to Agatha’s lair, citing ongoing complex archaeological procedures, and refused to let them sleep in his luxurious tent, directing them to camp outside. [ch 012 / 012.01]
- The party reported their encounter with Hamun to Daran Edermath, with Zenvon initially describing a "zombie army" before Pip convinced him to downplay the threat as a familial feud to avoid further dangerous quests. [ch 012 / 012.04]

## Last Observed State
The party’s final direct interaction with Hamun Kost occurred during a meal at Old Owl Well [ch 012 / 012.01]. He fulfilled his debt by personally marking the exact location of Cragmaw Castle on the party’s map, describing it as a "fabulous place" to visit [ch 012 / 012.01]. Status not established in the summaries beyond his active presence at Old Owl Well during this final interaction.

## Relationships
*   **Zenvon**: Kost views Zenvon with patronizing amusement, referring to him as "dear boy" and mocking his attempts to protect local citizens [ch 010 / 010.06]. He holds little respect for Zenvon’s competence, remarking on the low odds of a successful banshee visit if Zenvon were in charge [ch 012 / moment].
*   **Warden Holl**: An undead former apprentice of Kost. Holl serves Kost as a waiter at dinner, a service Kost sarcastically praises as superior to Holl’s service during his life [ch 011 / 011.07; ch 011 / moment].
*   **Sister Maela Dawnforge**: Kost interacted with Maela during the initial negotiation of favors. He did not directly engage with her strong preference for physical combat over banshee diplomacy, though he acknowledged the party's choice to handle the bugbears [ch 010 / 010.06].
*   **Pip Thistlewick**: Pip identified Kost’s suppressed excitement regarding the black obelisk despite Kost’s feigned ignorance [ch 011 / 011.07]. Pip later advised the party to downplay Kost’s threat to Daran to prevent acquiring more quests [ch 012 / moment].
*   **Daran Edermath**: Kost was spoken of to Daran, who expressed alarm upon learning of Kost’s "zombie army" and Red Wizard status, though the party later concealed the full extent of the threat [ch 012 / 012.04].
