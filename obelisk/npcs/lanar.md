## Identity
Lanar is a lean human miner distinguished by sunken cheeks, a dusty gray beard, and cracked hands stained by ore and lamp soot. He wears a crooked cap [ch 002 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Lanar is vocal and direct in his interactions. He speaks habitually with his mug held halfway to his mouth [ch 002 / entry]. His primary motivation at the time of observation was a strong dissatisfaction with local leadership and security; he loudly condemned Town Master Harbin Wester for hiding behind notices and wax seals [ch 002 / 002.02]. He views the threats to Phandalin—including goblins, bandits, orcs, and the Redbrands—as interconnected parts of the same problem: unsafe roads [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.02]. He is also concerned with the immediate safety of travelers, as demonstrated when he welcomed the adventurers and directly asked if they had been attacked on their way to Phandalin [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.02].
