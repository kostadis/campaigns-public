## Identity
The "Redbrand Ruffian" designation in this campaign refers to various low-ranking members of the Redbrand gang, rather than a single unique individual, though one specific captured ruffian plays a notable role. They are red-cloaked thugs who view Phandalin as their territory [ch 005 / entry; ch 006 / entry]. They serve as guards and enforcers in Tresendar Manor and frequent the Sleeping Giant [ch 006 / entry; ch 007 / entry; ch 008 / entry]. The specific ruffian captured by the party is distinct from a man who had previously followed the party, who the DM confirms is dead [ch 006 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Most ruffians are portrayed as violent enforcers willing to ambush and attack the party on order [ch 005 / entry; ch 006 / 006.01; ch 007 / entry]. The captured ruffian exhibits cowardice and self-preservation instincts; he surrendered immediately when cornered by Sister Maela, claiming "alright, alright, I'll surrender!" [ch 006 / 006.01]. He insists he is not "management" and claims his role is limited to collecting coins rather than making decisions [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / moment]. He shows deference to hierarchy, admitting the ambush was ordered by the boss and flinching at the mention of Glasstaff [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.02]. Another fleeing ruffian demonstrates panic and desperate rationalization during an ambush failure, shouting, "Do I look like I'm the kind of person who would lie? That is a killing machine! Kill him!" [ch 008 / moment].

## History with the Party
- The Redbrand Ruffians attempted to ambush the party after one member drew Zenvon away from the inn, regarding Phandalin as theirs to control; Zenvon’s intimidation caused their leader to hesitate before ordering an attack, during which one ruffian died to Magic Missile and the confrontation remained unresolved. [ch 005 / entry]
- In the street skirmish, Sister Maela wounded a ruffian with her warhammer, Zenvon killed a fleeing ruffian with a Sneak Attack, and Maela captured the last survivor who surrendered to avoid death; three Redbrands lay dead and their coins were collected for a four-way split. [ch 006 / 006.01; ch 006 / entry]
- The captured ruffian surrendered to Maela, claimed he only collected coins rather than making decisions, and admitted the boss ordered the party’s elimination; he identified Tresendar Manor as the hideout and the Sleeping Giant as the gang’s haunt, and offered to guide the party there. [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.02]
- Zenvon offered the captive 5 gold pieces for cooperation, but no payment was recorded; the party left him in Harbin Wester’s custody to be held while they scouted the Redbrand locations. [ch 006 / entry; ch 006 / 006.02]
- Four Redbrand Ruffians were found gambling in the common room of the Sleeping Giant; one called for bugbear assistance and a bandit wounded Veyra, but all four died leaving no prisoner, their coins and cloaks became party loot, and their bodies were consumed by the Nothic. [ch 007 / entry]
- Redbrand Ruffians serving as guards and enforcers in the hideout attempted an ambush in the slave pens; one survivor fled to warn the barracks, and Zenvon used the ensuing confusion and the ruffian’s frantic warning to execute a deception involving an impersonation; the surviving ruffian was eventually killed by Wick. [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / moment]

## Last Observed State
In Chapter 008, Redbrand Ruffians serving as guards and enforcers attempted an ambush in the slave pens of Tresendar Manor [ch 008 / entry]. One survivor fled to warn the barracks, but the group was overwhelmed. This fleeing ruffian was eventually killed by Wick [ch 008 / entry]. The party utilized the chaos of this event and the ruffian’s frantic warning to execute a deception involving an impersonation [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / moment]. Prior to this, in Chapter 007, four Ruffians gambling in the Sleeping Giant were killed by the party, with their bodies subsequently consumed by a Nothic [ch 007 / entry]. Status of individual named ruffians beyond those explicitly killed is not established in the summaries.
