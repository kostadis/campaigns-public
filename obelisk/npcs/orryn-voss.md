## Identity
Professor Orryn Voss is a missing mentor and scholar who traveled to Phandalin shortly before his disappearance [ch 002 / entry]. He is associated with the research of black stone fragments believed to belong to a great obelisk connected to minds, memory, and transformation [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.01]. His work is linked to the phenomenon of Veyra’s blue candle reacting to fragments and psionic magic [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.01]. Status not established in the summaries.

## Personality and Motivations
Voss is described by his student, Veyra, as a "good man" [ch 002 / entry]. He was motivated by a desire to investigate the obelisk fragments but was driven by a profound fear of the consequences of their assembly, warning that they were connected to minds, memory, and transformation [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.01]. He proposed a theory that a device was controlling people, a hypothesis Veyra initially dismissed as implausible but which later gained credibility through magical resonance [ch 009 / 009.08; ch 010 / moment]. UNSUPPORTED: Voss knew Elsa’s scholar sister in Neverwinter (Veyra believes this, but it is not confirmed in the text) [ch 002 / 002.01].
