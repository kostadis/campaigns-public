## Identity
Linene Graywind is the proprietor of the Lionshield Coster in Phandalin, one of the only two stores in town that stocks weapons and armor [ch 012 / 012.03]. She handles high-value transactions, including the sale of imported goods and specialized magical items [ch 012 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Linene operates with sharp business acumen, aggressively upselling customers by bundling high-value items—such as a gem-studded scabbard and plate mail—as a personal sacrifice that would cause her "bankruptcy" [ch 012 / 012.03]. She performs theatrical shock when accused of overcharging, reacting with incredulity to Pip’s observation that the Coster carries a "markup" over local artisans [ch 012 / 012.03].

Her primary motivation appears to be profitability and capitalizing on market conditions. She was previously hesitant to stock high-quality goods while roads were unsafe, but on learning how much coin the party carried, and with the roads clearing, she committed to importing "the purest and finest potions brewed in Waterdeep" [ch 012 / 012.03; ch 012 / entry]. While she pricks up her interest at unusual requests—raising an eyebrow at Zenvon’s demand for anti-banshee gear—she respects client privacy and does not press for details once deflected, prioritizing the completion of the sale [ch 012 / 012.03].

## History with the Party
- The party recovered goods belonging to Linene Graywind’s Lionshield Coster during their rescue of Sildar Hallwinter from the Cragmaw Hideout, though they postponed the return of these goods due to a suspicious patron in town [ch 005 / 005.01; ch 005 / 005.02].
- Linene acted as the proprietor of the Lionshield Coster, greeting the party with enthusiasm and attempting to sell Pip a high-value bundle of a gem-studded scabbard and plate mail, which Pip declined in favor of purchasing breastplates for himself and Sister Maela [ch 012 / 012.03].
- Surprised that the party came in with that much coin, Linene promised to procure specialized potions and magical goods from Waterdeep, stating the roads were now safe enough for such trade [ch 012 / 012.03; ch 012 / entry].
- Linene raised an eyebrow at Zenvon’s specific request for items effective against banshees but agreed to see what she could procure after Zenvon deflected questions about his motives [ch 012 / 012.03; ch 012 / entry].

## Last Observed State
Linene was last seen at the Lionshield Coster during the party’s visit to Phandalin in Chapter 012 [ch 012 / 012.03]. She completed the sale of breastplates to Pip and Sister Maela for 400 gold pieces each [ch 012 / 012.03]. Before the party left, she promised to procure specialized potions and magical goods from Waterdeep within a few days, including items effective against banshees as requested by Zenvon [ch 012 / 012.03; ch 012 / entry].

## Relationships
**The Party:** Linene views the adventurers as "good customers" and "flush" clients. She initially attempted to upsell Pip Thistlewick, but accepted his refusal of the expensive bundle when he opted for breastplates for himself and Sister Maela [ch 012 / 012.03]. She interacts with Zenvon by name, acknowledging his deflection regarding banshees but agreeing to fulfill his request [ch 012 / 012.03].

**Lionshield Coster Goods:** The party previously recovered goods belonging to Linene Graywind during the rescue of Sildar Hallwinter at the Cragmaw Hideout. These goods were returned to her ownership, though the physical handover was postponed during Chapter 005 [ch 005 / 005.01; ch 005 / 005.02].
