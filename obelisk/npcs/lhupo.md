## Identity

Lhupo is a female goblin priestess who invented the Jagged Tooth religion, became a paladin through her oath, and took leadership of the Cragmaw band at Cragmaw Castle [ch 013 / entry; ch 014 / 014.02; ch 015 / 015.03; ch 016 / entry].

She wears King Grol’s severed tooth as a pendant symbolizing her faith [ch 016 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations

Lhupo’s desire for religious confirmation made her receptive to Zenvon’s deception. Her resulting faith became genuine; she had previously used the Grick as an instrument of control without truly believing it was divine [ch 014 / 014.02; ch 014 / 014.03; ch 016 / 016.01].

She prioritizes her band’s survival and cohesion. She resisted a suicidal attack against the hobgoblins and later rejected Zenvon’s attempt to requisition her army through a supposed revelation [ch 014 / 014.04; ch 016 / 016.02].

She rewards the party’s help with treasure and offers to punish anyone who steals from Zenvon [ch 016 / 016.05; ch 017 / 017.03].

## History with the Party
- Gorl mentioned Lhupo while describing the castle’s groups; his account muddled the goblin factions [ch 013 / 013.05].
- The party learned of her shrine while developing its cover as religious travelers or prospective converts [ch 013 / 013.06].
- Zenvon recognized the risk of strangers claiming prior knowledge of a cult that might exist only inside this castle [ch 013 / 013.06].

- Lhupo welcomed the supposed pilgrims. Zenvon’s invented village history convinced her that he was a prophet; his discovery of a golden elf statue during her ritual became proof of the Jagged Tooth in her eyes [ch 014 / 014.02; ch 014 / 014.03].
- She recalled the dead cave goblins by name and asked who killed them, unaware she was addressing their killers. Zenvon deflected without exposing the party [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.02].
- She asked for King Grol’s death and tooth, supplied castle intelligence, and expected to eat the dwarf offering until Zenvon restrained that expectation through his religious authority [ch 014 / 014.04].
- She agreed her goblins would attack only after the party killed Thrak. A messenger had already spread the prophet’s demand for Grol’s death. She remained behind when the party was summoned to the king [ch 014 / 014.04; ch 014 / 014.05].
- Sister Maela’s cry that the prophet had been killed drew Lhupo and her goblins toward the battle. Lhupo avoided Veyra’s Web and struck the doppelganger twice, manifesting divine smiting power as her paladin abilities awakened [ch 015 / 015.02; ch 015 / 015.03].
- Relieved that Zenvon lived, she healed him for fifteen hit points. At his direction, she charged the hobgoblins while the party pursued Gundren’s abductor [ch 015 / 015.04].
- Zenvon returned to help her after Gundren’s rescue, killed a hobgoblin beside her, and clinked scimitars with her in celebration [ch 015 / 015.05].
- After the Jagged Tooth tribe’s victory, Lhupo convinced the other goblins that Zenvon’s prophetic authority had been vindicated [ch 015 / 015.05].
- Lhupo welcomed Zenvon to the victory feast and offered him the head of the table. His attempt to fast threatened to halt everyone’s meal until he took a bite [ch 016 / 016.01].
- Zenvon fulfilled his promise by giving her King Grol’s tooth; she tied it into a pendant [ch 016 / 016.01].
- She requested the deaths of the chapel creature and Snarl, offering their guarded treasure. She explained that she must remain to hold the band together [ch 016 / 016.01].
- She rejected Zenvon’s claim that the Jagged Tooth required him to take the goblin army. After negotiation, she supplied Polt, one of her stronger archers [ch 016 / 016.02].
- After Pip killed the Grick, Lhupo celebrated and dismissed her former object of worship as meat [ch 016 / 016.03].
- She honored the treasure bargain when the party found 700 gold pieces, then requested help against a Grell that sometimes killed goblins. Zenvon accepted the same terms and agreed to continue immediately [ch 016 / 016.05].
- Lhupo asked where the party would go next, approved searching the dead doppelganger, and offered to punish anyone who stole from Zenvon [ch 017 / 017.03].
- She accepted that Gundren, the offering, would accompany Zenvon and pledged the Cragmaw goblins’ loyalty and support if trouble arose [ch 017 / 017.05].
- In Phandalin, Zenvon reported her paladinhood and alliance with the party to Sildar Hallwinter. Sildar conditioned the remaining two hundred gold pieces of the castle reward on a formal agreement with Lhupo; Zenvon agreed to pursue it, but she had not signed anything [ch 017 / 017.06].

## Last Observed State

Lhupo remains at Cragmaw Castle with the surviving goblins. She accepts Gundren’s departure with Zenvon and has pledged support to the party [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.05].

Her agreement to a formal relationship with the Lords’ Alliance remains unsecured. She was spoken of during the party’s report to Sildar in Phandalin, rather than shown participating in that conversation [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.06].

## Relationships

- **Zenvon Forepot:** Her recognized Prophet, whom she healed and fought beside. She also claims religious authority of her own and has rejected one of his purported divine commands [ch 015 / 015.04; ch 015 / 015.05; ch 016 / 016.02].
- **The party:** Allies whom she rewards with treasure and promises future goblin support. She was unaware that they killed the cave goblins whose deaths she asked them to explain [ch 014 / entry; ch 016 / 016.05; ch 017 / 017.05].
- **The Cragmaw band:** Her followers; she considers holding them together her responsibility [ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / 016.01].
- **Polt:** A capable archer she supplied after refusing to commit her army [ch 016 / 016.02].
- **Gundren Rockseeker:** Initially an offering she expected to eat, until Zenvon prohibited it. She now accepts that he goes with the Prophet [ch 014 / 014.04; ch 017 / 017.05].
- **King Grol:** She sought his death and tooth, and received the tooth as a religious pendant [ch 014 / 014.04; ch 016 / 016.01].
- **Sildar Hallwinter and the Lords’ Alliance:** Sildar wants a formal agreement with her; her consent has yet to be secured [ch 017 / entry; ch 017 / 017.06].
