## Identity
Lhupo is a female goblin faction leader and priestess who invented the Jagged Tooth religion and maintains its shrine in Cragmaw Castle. She belongs to the Cragmaw goblins [ch 013 / entry; ch 014 / 014.02].

Her devotion, oath, and the shock of Zenvon’s apparent death awakened her as a paladin of the Jagged Tooth, manifesting divine smiting power [ch 015 / entry; ch 015 / 015.03].

## Personality and Motivations
She desperately wants her god to be real. Forepot’s invented account of distant worshippers and the discovery of a golden elf statue convinced her that the Jagged Tooth existed and that Forepot was its prophet [ch 014 / 014.02; ch 014 / 014.03].

She remembers her people as individuals and seeks answers about the cave goblins’ deaths. She wanted King Grol killed and his tooth brought back, but resisted sacrificing her weaker tribe against disciplined hobgoblins without the party’s help [ch 014 / 014.02; ch 014 / 014.04].

Her devotion to Zenvon motivates immediate action: she rushed toward the fighting after hearing that the prophet had been killed, healed him, and eagerly accepted his direction to fight the hobgoblins [ch 015 / 015.02; ch 015 / 015.04].

## History with the Party
- Gorl mentioned Lhupo while describing the castle’s groups; his account muddled the goblin factions [ch 013 / 013.05].
- The party learned of her shrine while developing its cover as religious travelers or prospective converts [ch 013 / 013.06].
- Zenvon recognized the risk of strangers claiming prior knowledge of a cult that might exist only inside this castle [ch 013 / 013.06].

- Lhupo welcomed the supposed pilgrims. Zenvon’s invented village history convinced her that he was a prophet; his discovery of a golden elf statue during her ritual became proof of the Jagged Tooth in her eyes [ch 014 / 014.02; ch 014 / 014.03].
- She recalled the dead cave goblins by name and asked who killed them, unaware she was addressing their killers. Zenvon deflected without exposing the party [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.02].
- She asked for King Grol’s death and tooth, supplied castle intelligence, and expected to eat the dwarf offering until Zenvon restrained that expectation through his religious authority [ch 014 / 014.04].
- She agreed her goblins would attack only after the party killed Thrak. A messenger had already spread the prophet’s demand for Grol’s death. She remained behind when the party was summoned to the king [ch 014 / 014.04; ch 014 / 014.05].
- Sister Maela’s cry that the prophet had been killed drew Lhupo and her goblins toward the battle. Lhupo avoided Veyra’s Web and struck the doppelganger twice, manifesting divine smiting power as her paladin abilities awakened [ch 015 / 015.02; ch 015 / 015.03].
- Relieved that Zenvon lived, she healed him for fifteen hit points. At his direction, she charged the hobgoblins while the party pursued Gundren’s abductor [ch 015 / 015.04].
- Zenvon returned to help her after Gundren’s rescue, killed a hobgoblin beside her, and clinked scimitars with her in celebration [ch 015 / 015.05].
- After the Jagged Tooth tribe’s victory, Lhupo convinced the other goblins that Zenvon’s prophetic authority had been vindicated [ch 015 / 015.05].

## Last Observed State
Alive and victorious inside Cragmaw Castle, Lhupo helped affirm Zenvon’s prophetic authority among the celebrating goblins. The Jagged Tooth tribe controlled the castle; all the Red-Mask Kin were dead, with two goblin losses reported [ch 015 / 015.05].

The goblins began preparing the fallen hobgoblins for roasting. The proposed feast and next morning’s departure deception remained unplayed plans [ch 015 / 015.05].

## Relationships
- **Zenvon Forepot:** Her accepted prophet, recipient of her healing, and companion in battle. His apparent death catalyzed her paladin awakening; his return to help her delighted her [ch 015 / entry; ch 015 / 015.03; ch 015 / 015.04; ch 015 / 015.05].
- **The party:** Allies through their religious deception. She did not know they had killed the cave goblins she remembered by name [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.02].
- **Her goblins:** She feared sending them into a suicidal attack and required Thrak’s death before their charge. Following victory, she reinforced their belief in Zenvon [ch 014 / 014.04; ch 015 / 015.05].
- **King Grol and the Red-Mask Kin:** She sought Grol’s death and fought the hobgoblins. Grol was killed by Veyra, and the Red-Mask Kin were subsequently eliminated [ch 014 / 014.04; ch 015 / 015.02; ch 015 / 015.04; ch 015 / 015.05].
- **Gundren Rockseeker:** She initially anticipated eating the dwarf as an offering, but accepted Forepot’s prohibition [ch 014 / 014.04].
