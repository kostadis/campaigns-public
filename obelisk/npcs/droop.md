## Identity
Droop is a goblin belonging to the Scraptops clan, a group characterized as scavengers and tinkerers. This clan is distinct from the Sawplee goblins led by Ruxithid [ch 009 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Droop is described as miserable and clearly unhappy, currently occupying the lowest niche in the local social ecology [ch 009 / entry]. He was motivated to enter the Redbrand hideout specifically to look for parts [ch 009 / entry]. His temperament appears skittish, as he reacted to the arrival of the party by fainting from terror [ch 009 / entry]. He is the subject of recruitment by the Cragmaw goblins, who want him to join their band [ch 009 / entry].

## History with the Party
*   A miserable goblin of the Scraptops clan (distinct from Ruxithid's Sawplee goblins) who wandered into the Redbrand hideout looking for parts [ch 009 / entry].
*   He was being bullied by the bugbears and occupied the lowest niche in the ecology below them [ch 009 / entry].
*   He had a "thing on his head" and was clearly unhappy [ch 009 / entry].
*   The Cragmaw want him to join their band [ch 009 / entry].
*   He fainted from terror when the party burst into the barracks [ch 009 / entry].
*   The party left him behind during the retreat; he was last seen fainted in the barracks, and his fate after the fire is unknown [ch 009 / entry].

## Last Observed State
Droop was last seen in the Redbrand barracks after the party burst in, causing him to faint from terror [ch 009 / entry]. During the party's retreat, he was left behind while unconscious; his fate following the subsequent fire is unknown [ch 009 / entry]. Status not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
Droop was being bullied by the bugbears, who dominated the hierarchy above him [ch 009 / entry]. He is a target for recruitment by the Cragmaw goblins [ch 009 / entry]. He belongs to the Scraptops clan, distinct from Ruxithid’s Sawplee goblins [ch 009 / entry].
