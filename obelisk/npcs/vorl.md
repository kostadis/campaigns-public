## Identity
Hobgoblin messenger and escort sent by King Grol; announces the party as Lhupo’s pilgrims before the audience [ch 014 / entry; ch 015 / 015.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Expresses contempt for Lhupo when announcing her pilgrims. Approves the party knocking Gorl unconscious and concealing him behind barrels [ch 015 / 015.01; ch 014 / 014.05].

## History with the Party
Brings Grol’s summons, escorts the party through the storage tower, approves the treatment of Gorl, and announces the visitors to the king [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.04; ch 014 / 014.05].
- Announces the visitors as Lhupo’s pilgrims, spitting her name with contempt, and enters Grol’s quarters [ch 015 / 015.01].
- Repeatedly attacks Sister Maela during the battle [ch 015 / 015.02; ch 015 / 015.03].
- Pip wounds him while escaping the chamber. Maela closes the door, and Zenvon sets the table on fire with the brazier [ch 015 / 015.04].
- Bangs on the door while Maela holds it closed; dies from the fire inside the quarters [ch 015 / 015.05].

## Last Observed State
Dead from the fire in Grol’s quarters, explicitly established while Maela keeps the door closed and Vorl bangs from inside [ch 015 / 015.05].

## Relationships
- **King Grol:** Serves as his messenger and announces visitors to him [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.05].
- **Lhupo:** Shows contempt when speaking her name, though both approve the party’s treatment of Gorl [ch 015 / 015.01; ch 014 / 014.05].
- **Sister Maela:** Attacks her repeatedly; she subsequently keeps him confined in the burning quarters [ch 015 / entry; ch 015 / 015.05].
- **Pip:** Wounds Vorl while leaving the chamber [ch 015 / 015.04].
