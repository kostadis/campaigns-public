## Identity
Ander is a clerk employed at Barthen's Provisions in Phandalin [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.04]. He works alongside another clerk, Thistle, under the direction of Elmina Barthen [ch 001 / 001.04].

## Personality and Motivations
No information regarding Ander's personality or personal motivations is available in the provided evidence. He is described only in his professional capacity as a clerk carrying out duties assigned by his employer.

## History with the Party
- Ander was greeted by the party at Barthen's Provisions alongside his fellow clerk Thistle [ch 001 / 001.04].
- Elmina sent Ander and Thistle to unload the wagon after receiving Zenvon's contract [ch 001 / entry; ch 001 / 001.04].
