## Identity
Nezznar, primarily known as the Black Spider and the Spider [ch 004 / entry; ch 005 / entry; ch 007 / entry]. He is a mysterious figure believed to be a villainous mastermind [ch 005 / entry]. The party has not established his identity or motives [ch 004 / entry].

Gorl identifies the Spider as a drow; this dossier’s canonical registry name is not a name the party has learned in play [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.03].

## Personality and Motivations
Nezznar is believed to control a network of bandit gangs and goblins around Phandalin [ch 005 / entry; ch 007 / 007.07]. A spider-sealed letter ordered his agent Iarno to capture the party if possible, kill them if necessary, and send their dwarven maps [ch 007 / 007.07].

## History with the Party
*   Sildar stated that the Black Spider is believed to control a network of bandit gangs and goblins around Phandalin and is connected to the targeting of Gundren and his map [ch 005 / entry].
*   The party found a letter bearing a spider-shaped wax seal in Iarno's quarters, which ordered Iarno to capture the party if possible, kill them if necessary, and send their dwarven maps [ch 007 / 007.07; ch 007 / moment].
*   The letter confirmed Iarno's allegiance to "The Spider," a mysterious figure already associated with the goblins and a wider network of bandit gangs [ch 007 / 007.07; ch 007 / entry].
*   Zenvon copied the spider-sealed letter into his notes [ch 007 / 007.07].

- Gorl identified the Spider as a drow and the real boss, with Glasstaff managing local affairs and Nosk working directly for that superior. He said the Spider specifically wanted Gundren, but disclaimed knowledge of the larger strategy [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.03].
- Outside Cragmaw Castle, the party learned that the inhabitants have separate goals and loyalties; the GM explained that the Spider’s money was the likely unifying force rather than King Grol’s personal strength [ch 013 / 013.05].

## Last Observed State
Still not directly encountered. Gorl identifies the Spider as a drow, describes the chain of command through Glasstaff and Nosk, and reports Gundren held at Cragmaw Castle for the Spider [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.03]. The Spider’s money is the likely binding force among the castle’s otherwise divided groups [ch 013 / 013.05]. The party has not entered the castle; the summary does not establish the Spider’s physical presence there or disclose the canonical personal name to the party [ch 013 / 013.05].

## Relationships

*   **Gundren Rockseeker**: Nezznar is believed to be connected to the targeting and capture of Gundren [ch 005 / entry; ch 007 / 007.07]. The DM connected Iarno to responsibility for Gundren's capture [ch 007 / 007.07].
