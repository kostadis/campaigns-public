## Identity
Nezznar the Spider is referred to as the Spider and the Black Spider. Gorl identified him as a drow and the real boss behind the local operation and goblinoid factions [ch 005 / entry; ch 013 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
The spider-sealed letter ordered Iarno to capture the party if possible, kill them if necessary, and deliver their dwarven maps [ch 007 / 007.07]. Gorl said the Spider specifically wanted Gundren, but did not know his ultimate objectives [ch 013 / 013.03].

## History with the Party
*   Sildar stated that the Black Spider is believed to control a network of bandit gangs and goblins around Phandalin and is connected to the targeting of Gundren and his map [ch 005 / entry].
*   The party found a letter bearing a spider-shaped wax seal in Iarno's quarters, which ordered Iarno to capture the party if possible, kill them if necessary, and send their dwarven maps [ch 007 / 007.07; ch 007 / moment].
*   The letter confirmed Iarno's allegiance to "The Spider," a mysterious figure already associated with the goblins and a wider network of bandit gangs [ch 007 / 007.07; ch 007 / entry].
*   Zenvon copied the spider-sealed letter into his notes [ch 007 / 007.07].

- Gorl identified the Spider as a drow and the real boss, with Glasstaff managing local affairs and Nosk working directly for that superior. He said the Spider specifically wanted Gundren, but disclaimed knowledge of the larger strategy [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.03].
- Outside Cragmaw Castle, the party learned that the inhabitants have separate goals and loyalties; the GM explained that the Spider’s money was the likely unifying force rather than King Grol’s personal strength [ch 013 / 013.05].
- The Black Spider was confirmed absent from Cragmaw Castle. After recovering a map in Gundren’s handwriting, the party suspected Wave Echo Cave as the next place to look, but chose to escort Gundren to Phandalin [ch 017 / 017.04].
- Zenvon told Sildar that Gundren could help them find Iarno and the Black Spider, while avoiding confirmation that the party knew Wave Echo Cave’s location [ch 017 / 017.06].

## Last Observed State
The Black Spider was confirmed absent from Cragmaw Castle; Wave Echo Cave was only the party’s suspected next search location [ch 017 / 017.04]. Status not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
- **Iarno / Glasstaff:** The letter confirmed Iarno’s allegiance to the Spider. Gorl described Glasstaff’s authority as subordinate and local [ch 007 / 007.07; ch 013 / entry].
- **Nosk:** According to Gorl, Nosk worked directly for the Spider and would have known more about his plans [ch 013 / 013.03].
- **Gundren Rockseeker:** Gorl reported that Gundren was held at Cragmaw Castle because the Spider wanted him. The party subsequently brought Gundren back to Phandalin [ch 013 / 013.03; ch 017 / 017.06].
- **Cragmaw factions:** The Spider’s money was described as the likely force holding together groups with competing goals and loyalties [ch 013 / 013.05].
- **Sildar:** Sildar wanted the Black Spider identified and said the Lords’ Alliance would favor whoever brought him to justice [ch 005 / entry].
- **The party:** The spider-sealed letter targeted them for capture or death and demanded their dwarven maps [ch 007 / 007.07].
