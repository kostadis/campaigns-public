## Identity
Brughor Axe-Biter is an orc who served as the leader of the Wyvern Tor band [ch 011 / entry]. Although physically weaker than the ogres and bugbears under his command, he maintained their attention and authority while seated beside the fire [ch 011 / entry]. Pip identified him specifically as the gang’s organizer [ch 011 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Brughor was the primary authority figure issuing orders to the raiding party, including the directive to attack [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.05]. He held a position of command that transcended physical dominance, successfully managing larger, stronger creatures like Gog [ch 011 / entry]. His personal relationship with the ogre Gog was romantic, which influenced Gog’s behavior during combat [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.05]. Brughor was involved in ordering raids connected to Hamun Kost, though the extent of his knowledge or the wizard's direct involvement is not established in the summaries [ch 011 / 011.05].

## History with the Party
- Brughor Axe-Biter is identified as the orc leader of the Wyvern Tor band who commands the attention of ogres and bugbears despite being physically weaker than them [ch 011 / entry].
- Pip identifies Brughor as the gang’s organizer and recommends attacking him first [ch 011 / entry].
- Zenvon’s opening strikes leave Brughor barely alive before subsequent lethal attacks [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.04].
- During the assault, Zenvon strikes Brughor with a short sword to unbalance him and cuts his throat with a scimitar, killing him [ch 011 / 011.04; ch 011 / moment].
- Brughor’s death causes the remaining bugbears to flee, while the ogre Gog stays to fight [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.04].
- Gog reveals that Brughor was his romantic companion and gave the orders to attack [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.05].
- The party confirms Brughor is dead, establishing his role in ordering raids while noting Gog did not recognize the name Hamun Kost [ch 011 / 011.05].

## Last Observed State
Brughor Axe-Biter is dead [ch 011 / 011.04; ch 011 / moment]. He was killed during an ambush by Zenvon, who used an illusion to gain surprise and advantage [ch 011 / 011.04]. Zenvon’s initial strikes left Brughor at 2 hit points, and subsequent attacks with a short sword and scimitar severed his throat, killing him instantly [ch 011 / 011.04; ch 011 / entry]. His death triggered the immediate flight of the remaining bugbears, while Gog remained to fight in a state of furious grief [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.04].

## Relationships
*   **Gog (Ogre):** Gog’s companion and romantic partner [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.05]. Gog’s devastation and refusal to flee were directly tied to Brughor’s death [ch 011 / 011.05].
*   **Wyvern Tor Band (Bugbears):** The bugbears followed Brughor’s commands but fled immediately upon his death [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.04].
*   **Hamun Kost:** Brughor ordered attacks associated with this name, though Gog did not recognize the name during interrogation [ch 011 / 011.05].
