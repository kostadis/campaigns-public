## Identity
Sister Garaele is a scholarly, zealous young elf acolyte who tends Phandalin’s only temple, the Shrine of Luck, dedicated to Tymora [ch 002 / entry; ch 009 / 009.10]. She is described as priestess of the shrine [ch 012 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Garaele exhibits scholarly zeal and a desire to protect her community, evidenced by her refusal to explain her whereabouts after returning injured, possibly to shield townspeople from worry [ch 002 / 002.02; ch 002 / 002.04]. She is pragmatic regarding magical dangers, acknowledging her own limitations when she attempted to approach the banshee Agatha and fled, barely recovering after several days [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.10]. Her motivations center on acquiring the legendary spellbook of the mage Bowgentle; she views the delivery of a jeweled silver comb to Agatha as a necessary step to win the banshee's favor for this information [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.10]. She maintains a professional demeanor with the party, offering blessings and specific tasks rather than casual conversation, and emphasizes urgency regarding the banshee's persistence [ch 002 / 002.04; ch 009 / 009.10].

## History with the Party
- Narth reported to Sister Maela that Garaele had recently returned limping and pale, refusing to explain her whereabouts. Narth hoped another religious person might gain her confidence [ch 002 / 002.02].
- Sister Garaele recognized the party’s connection to Gundren Rockseeker at the Shrine of Luck, admitted she did not know his location, and blessed their rescue mission. She reserved another job for them upon their return but did not disclose it [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.04].
- Garaele greeted the party after they cleared out the Redbrands, praising them as "the new heroes of Phandalin" [ch 009 / 009.10].
- Garaele revealed she had previously attempted to approach the banshee Agatha to ask about the legendary spellbook of Bowgentle, but fled for her life and barely recovered after several days [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.10].
- Garaele tasked the party with delivering a jeweled silver comb to Agatha to win the banshee's favor and information about Bowgentle's spellbook, offering three potions of healing as payment [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.10].
- Toblen Stonehill reminded the party that the route to Agatha's lair passed through the ruins of Conyberry, should they undertake Sister Garaele's errand [ch 010 / 010.03].
- The party debated visiting Agatha's lair, with Sister Maela noting that the comb was given to them by Sister Garaele and that repurposing the gift would not be "good" [ch 012 / 012.01; ch 012 / moment].
- Zenvon consulted Hamun Kost about the comb offering from a friend, identifying the item as part of Sister Garaele's quest [ch 012 / 012.01].
- The party spotted Sister Garaele on their return to Phandalin but waved her off, citing exhaustion, and promised to speak later. Garaele waved back, appearing busy [ch 012 / 012.05].
