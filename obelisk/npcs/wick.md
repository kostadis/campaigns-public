## Identity
Wick was a low-level thug employed by the Redbrand gang [ch 008 / entry; ch 009 / entry]. He self-described his function within the organization as someone who "just carries crates" [ch 008 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Wick prioritized self-preservation above all other loyalties. Upon seeing his allies defeated in combat, he killed an injured ruffian beside him in an attempt to curry favor with the adventurers and beg for mercy [ch 008 / entry; ch 009 / entry]. His motivation for cooperating with the party was purely transactional; although he verbally agreed to join them, stating, "Of course I'm with you, boss," a successful Insight check revealed this was a lie, as his actual desire was for coin and an exit rather than a new employer [ch 008 / entry; ch 009 / entry]. He flinched visibly when the name "Black Spider" was mentioned, indicating fear of that specific figure [ch 008 / entry; ch 009 / entry].
