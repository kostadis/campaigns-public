## Identity
Dosa Rook was a Redbrand bandit stationed in the barracks of the hideout [ch 008 / entry; ch 009 / entry]. He is described in later records as a "hot-headed" member of the faction [ch 009 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Dosa Rook displayed immediate gullibility and aggressive zealotry when deceived. Upon hearing Zenvon’s claim that a fleeing comrade was a traitor, Dosa Rook instantly accepted the story without skepticism, declaring his intent to violently execute the alleged traitor [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.06]. His enthusiasm for violence was notable, stating he would "cut this rat into several pieces" [ch 008 / 008.06]. Unlike a second, more skeptical bandit present in the scene, Dosa Rook did not require verification before acting [ch 008 / 008.06].

## History with the Party
*   Dosa Rook was a Redbrand bandit stationed in the barracks who was immediately and completely taken in by Zenvon's deception. [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.06]
*   Upon believing Zenvon's claim that a fleeing comrade was a traitor, Dosa Rook declared his intent to kill the alleged traitor. [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.06]
*   The deception collapsed when a second, more skeptical bandit attempted to verify the story, prompting a chaotic brawl. [ch 008 / 008.06]
*   Dosa Rook was killed by Zenvon's dagger when the deception collapsed. [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.06]
*   In the following session, Dosa Rook was carried forward in the records as a hot-headed bandit in the cellar who was briefly fooled by the bluff and turned on his own comrade before fighting started, but was not present in Chapter 009. [ch 009 / entry]

## Last Observed State
Dosa Rook was killed by Zenvon's dagger during the chaotic brawl that erupted after Zenvon's deception collapsed [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.06]. He was not present in Chapter 009 [ch 009 / entry].

*   **Unnamed Skeptical Bandit**: Dosa Rook acted on Zenvon's lie before this bandit could verify the truth, who then raised doubts about the blood coverage on both the accuser and the accused [ch 008 / 008.06].
*   **Wick**: A bandit named Wick was the last standing combatant in the brawl where Dosa Rook died, eventually begging for mercy after killing an injured ruffian [ch 008 / 008.06].
