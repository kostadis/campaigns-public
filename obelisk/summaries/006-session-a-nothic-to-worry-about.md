# Chapter 06 A Nothic to Worry About

Date: 2026-07-03

## Summary
The streets of Phandalin rang with the clash of steel as the party finished fighting a band of Redbrand ruffians. Sister Maela struck the enemy beside her with her warhammer, dealing 6 damage without risking an opportunity attack. As the surviving ruffians fled, Veyra’s Fire Bolt sailed wide, and Pip Thistlewick, unable to reach an enemy in melee, missed with a javelin. Zenvon Forepot pursued, landed a 7-damage strike, and added Sneak Attack to kill his target. Sister Maela dashed after the last survivor, whose response to her determination was immediate surrender. Three Redbrands lay dead, and the party collected their coins for a four-way split.

Harbin Wester, Phandalin’s nervous, round-faced mayor and townmaster, emerged cautiously from the Townmaster’s Hall. Zenvon introduced his companions and explained that the Redbrands had ambushed them. Harbin called the gang a terrible plague, then offered the adventurers work investigating caravan attacks near Wyvern Tor, east along the Triboar Trail. Pip reminded Zenvon to ask about payment, then took over the negotiations himself. Harbin’s initial 100-gold reward became 110 and finally 120 gold pieces. Impressed, the banker-townmaster suggested Pip might find work in his personal business if he ever tired of adventuring.

Before leaving their captive with Harbin, the party questioned him about Gundren and Iarno Albrek, Sildar’s missing contact. The ruffian insisted he merely collected coins and was not management. Under physical pressure, he named Tresendar Manor as the hideout and the Sleeping Giant as the gang’s drinking haunt. He also admitted that the ambush had been ordered by the boss. Zenvon offered 5 gold pieces for cooperation, but decided to leave the man in custody while the party scouted. Sister Maela approved once Zenvon clarified that he meant to investigate the Redbrands immediately, rather than leave loose ends while pursuing an unrelated quest.

After a short rest restored Zenvon to full health, the party approached the Sleeping Giant. Zenvon initially proposed that all four companions scout different sides of the building; the plan became a solo reconnaissance by the rogue, with the others close enough to intervene. His attempt to enter unnoticed failed spectacularly. The surly orc bartender spotted him, and he covered by ordering an ale for an exorbitant gold piece. Looking around, he found Halia Thornton of the Miner's Exchange sitting with a broad-shouldered Redbrand lieutenant named Rondar. Rondar recognized him immediately and offered a professional courtesy: a truce in which each side kept out of the other’s business.

When Halia tried to leave, Zenvon called his companions inside. She sat back down and claimed she had merely been conducting business over a pleasant drink, then performed astonishment at discovering Rondar’s Redbrand affiliation despite his conspicuous red cloak. Rondar offered limited information in exchange for peace. Sister Maela recognized that he was exaggerating how much he knew; Zenvon’s own intimidation failed, but Pip’s blade at Rondar’s throat produced the decisive confession. Glasstaff was Iarno Albrek, a Lords’ Alliance agent gone bad. Rondar also suggested that Glasstaff answered to someone else, though that superior remained unidentified. He denied knowing Gundren.

Halia praised Zenvon’s methods and invited him to her exchange to discuss business. Zenvon kept her from leaving and directed her to accompany them as far as Tresendar Manor. Rondar offered to show the party a back entrance for gold, but accepted silver after further pressure. He guided them into the woods to a concealed tunnel roughly 100 feet from the hideout. There, the party debated releasing him, tying him to a tree, or taking him along. Zenvon chose to retain him as a guide and possible lure or bargaining hostage, paying 2 silver pieces and promising 3 more afterward before having him bound and gagged.

Rondar warned that a malevolent, intelligent creature guarded the passage. Veyra excitedly identified it as a Nothic: a tormented seeker of secrets transformed by a curse associated with Vecna. She did not know how to kill it, but was eager to study it. Rondar explained that Iarno kept the creature fed in exchange for its service as a guardian. Two months earlier, a Redbrand pursuing a rat into the cellar had returned speaking strangely and knowing things about his companions that he should not have known.

With a reluctant Rondar forced ahead at shortsword point, the party descended the tunnel into a cold natural cavern smelling faintly of decaying flesh. A crevasse divided the chamber beneath a twenty-foot ceiling, with rough stone columns and two arched wooden bridges. The party spotted the creature, which was already aware of them thanks to Rondar’s noise.

The Nothic spoke directly into their minds and exposed Pip’s secret: he had won a stone shard near Neverwinter’s docks, sold it to a peddler heading toward Phandalin, and since suffered dreams of broken black stone underground and whispers of his name. It also revealed his desire to find the peddler, recover the shard, and understand why it haunted him. Pip went pale and urged the party to kill the creature. Sister Maela doubted it would be easy, and Veyra suspected powerful magic. The Nothic simply wanted food—and looked hungrily at Rondar. The session ended before the party decided how to answer.
