# Chapter 03 Cave Crawl, Goblin Brawl, and a Serpent's Fall

Date: 2026-06-08

## Summary

Zenvon returned to the party to report the massive, coiled serpent he had discovered in the cave. Darkness complicated their approach: Veyra and Sister Maela could see, but Zenvon and Pip could not. After considering how best to protect their frail magic user, the party paired Veyra with Pip and Sister Maela with Zenvon. They slipped past three wolves without alerting them, then approached the snake unnoticed. Fighting without a light hampered their attacks, but Veyra scorched the serpent with Fire Bolt and Zenvon killed it with his second shortbow shot.

The party followed the passage beyond the snake instead of continuing north. Sister Maela's attempt at stealth went badly, and one of three visible goblins spotted her. When challenged, she asked Zenvon for a bright idea. He tried claiming they had come to meet Glasstaff after hearing heroic stories about him, but the deception failed. A fourth goblin shouted in Goblin, and from deeper inside the cave came an unmistakable voice: Sildar Hallwinter calling to Sister Maela.

The ensuing battle became a desperate struggle against goblins with scimitars and archers firing from elevated ledges. Pip took the brunt of their attacks. Veyra spent her last Magic Missile, killing one enemy and wounding another, while Sister Maela used her last Cure Wounds to restore six of Pip's hit points. Zenvon and Pip repeatedly missed in the darkness until Zenvon sacrificed an attack to produce a light.

Yeemik screeched at the “Bright Dwellers” and pressed the assault. Pip survived at one hit point after the DM corrected an initial knockout call, then recovered ten hit points with Second Wind. With light finally available, Zenvon killed a goblin and wounded Yeemik, drawing the boss's attention and suffering a retaliatory strike. Pip then landed a natural 20 against Yeemik, and Sister Maela finished the boss with a bludgeoning blow. The remaining archers remained dangerous: Sister Maela herself fell to one hit point before Pip killed one goblin and knocked the last prone. Maela struck the prone enemy, and Zenvon closed in with his short sword to end the fight.

Sildar was found bound on the southern ledge, alive but reduced to one hit point. He urged the party to leave and asked whether they had killed Klarg. The party learned that Yeemik was not the cave's ultimate leader: Klarg was a bugbear, and Sister Maela confirmed they had encountered no bugbears. Before withdrawing, they recovered three agates worth ten gold pieces each and a potion of healing from Yeemik.

Zenvon advanced to level 2, reaching a maximum of 15 hit points and gaining bonus-action options to Dash, Disengage, or Hide. A review of weapon masteries led to a scimitar-and-short-sword focus, combining Nick and Vex. Outside the cave, the party abandoned the idea of resting beside the slain lookouts and moved to a clearing roughly a hundred feet away. With Zenvon's help, Pip rolled a natural 20 on Survival, concealed their tracks, and delivered the final instruction for their hidden camp: no fires.

## Scenes

### The Snake in the Shadows

#### Zenvon leads the party back into the dark cave to confront a giant poisonous snake he previously scouted, coordinating with those who have darkvision.

- Zenvon returns to tell the party about the giant poisonous snake lurking ahead.
- Veyra and Sister Maela have darkvision; Pip and Zenvon cannot see in the absolute darkness and suffer disadvantage on attacks. Carrying a light would reveal their presence.
- Zenvon initially proposes pairing himself with Veyra and Pip with Maela. After the DM points out Veyra's frailty, they instead pair the magic user with the fighter and Zenvon with the cleric.
- Zenvon and Sister Maela make a group stealth check. His 15 and her 9 are ruled sufficient to pass the wolves unnoticed.
- Maela whispers, “There's three wolves! Pass it on!” Pip overhears the warning.
- Zenvon asks whether the wolves might attack if left alone. After learning they probably will not attack without noticing the party, he chooses to leave them undisturbed and explore toward the snake.
- Zenvon's next Stealth roll is 25, allowing him and Maela to approach the serpent unnoticed. He rolls 21 for initiative.
- With Maela between him and the creature, Zenvon uses his shortbow. His opening attack rolls 10 and misses.
- Veyra hits with Fire Bolt for ten damage.
- Pip throws a javelin with disadvantage and misses.
- Maela attempts Sacred Flame, but the snake succeeds on its saving throw against DC 12.
- Zenvon's second shortbow attack produces results of 23 and 12. The lower result still hits despite disadvantage, killing the snake.
- Maela identifies a route down through a hole. Given the choice between following this passage and continuing north, Zenvon chooses the passage beyond the slain serpent.

### Ambush in the Dark

#### The party attempts to sneak past a group of goblins in the darkness, but Sister Maela is spotted, leading to a tense confrontation.

- A roll of 3 for Maela's stealth leaves her conspicuously noisy. She notices three goblins, and one notices her.
- Maela reacts bluntly, and Zenvon rolls 7 for initiative. The DM leaves room for a bluff before the confrontation becomes a fight.
- The first goblin challenges Maela: “Who are you?”
- Maela asks Zenvon, “do you have any bright ideas, Zenvon?” The alternative is to charge and attack.
- Zenvon briefly considers claiming the party is lost, then tries a story about coming to meet Glasstaff after hearing heroic tales about him.
- The deception fails. A fourth goblin shouts in Goblin, which the party does not understand. Maela warns, “this is not good.”
- From deeper inside, Sildar calls, “Sister Maela, is that you?”
- The DM retroactively credits an earlier short rest, restoring Zenvon to 9 hit points before combat proceeds.
- One goblin fires a shortbow at Maela and misses. Another charges with a scimitar and also misses.
- Veyra's Fire Bolt misses.
- Pip squeezes forward, shouting “DIE, Goblin!” His attack deals six damage, leaving the enemy at one hit point.
- Zenvon tries two-weapon fighting against the wounded goblin, but both his short sword and dagger attacks miss in the darkness.
- He considers producing a light, but his attacks have already used his turn. The light must wait.
- Maela kills the wounded goblin with her mace and steps forward. Another goblin rushes Pip but misses.
- More goblins and a goblin boss enter the fight. The DM clarifies that this boss is not Glasstaff, who leads the Redbrands.

### The Battle for the Ledge

#### The party fights a desperate battle against goblin reinforcements and their leader Yeemik, while trying to protect a heavily wounded Pip.

- Goblin archers concentrate on Pip. One arrow deals six damage, another misses, and a third deals three damage, leaving him badly hurt.
- Veyra spends her last Magic Missile. One target dies, while another is left at two hit points.
- Pip continues attacking with disadvantage and misses twice.
- Maela uses her last Cure Wounds, healing Pip for six hit points. A goblin immediately follows with a five-damage melee hit.
- Zenvon spends his action producing a light, giving up his attack that turn to illuminate the fight.
- Yeemik reacts to the light with cries of “Bright Dwellers!” and charges Pip with his scimitar.
- A five-damage hit initially prompts a knockout call. The DM later remembers Pip's bonus hit point and corrects the result: Pip remains conscious at one hit point.
- Goblin archers shift their attention to Maela, hitting her twice for three damage each.
- Veyra misses with Fire Bolt. Pip uses Second Wind to heal ten hit points, bringing him to eleven.
- Maela tries another spell, but the target succeeds on its saving throw. Pip takes another hit and protests, “this is not funny, guys!”
- Now able to see, Zenvon kills a goblin with a seven-damage short sword attack. The DM points out that the kill did not require his Sneak Attack damage and that he can still attack Yeemik with his other weapon.
- A rules discussion introduces Nick: the additional weapon attack can be folded into the Attack action, leaving the bonus action available for another use.
- Zenvon deals nine damage to Yeemik. The boss turns on him, hits his AC 14 for five damage, then misses with a second attack.
- Pip rolls a natural 20 against Yeemik. The DM resolves the critical hit for eight damage.
- Maela initially proposes another spell, but the DM checks its saving throw and switches her action to a melee attack. Her six bludgeoning damage kills Yeemik.
- Two goblins remain. One is visible to Zenvon; the other is still concealed by the elevated terrain, though its noises and arrows betray its presence.
- The DM explains that Zenvon could ready a bow attack for when Maela moves beside an enemy. Zenvon instead fires immediately, dealing four damage, then moves back toward cover.
- One goblin charges Maela and misses. The unseen archer hits her, leaving her at one hit point after another corrected knockout concern.
- Veyra misses again. Pip kills the nearer goblin, moves up to the remaining archer, and successfully shoves it down to the ground, leaving it prone.
- Maela attacks the prone goblin with advantage. Her one-handed strike deals six damage, leaving it at one hit point.
- Zenvon weighs firing his bow against closing for two chances to hit. After discussing the opportunity attack risk of retreating from melee, he moves in with his short sword.
- His first attack hits for nine damage, killing the final goblin.

### Rescue of Sildar Hallwinter

#### After clearing the goblin chamber, the party discovers and rescues a battered Sildar Hallwinter.

- The party finds Sildar bound on the southern ledge of the large cave, badly wounded and reduced to one hit point.
- Sildar greets them with relief: “You're alive! That's good news!”
- He stresses the need to leave. When Zenvon asks whether he is all right, Sildar replies, “I'm fine now, I'm fine now, but we need to move.”
- Zenvon proposes taking him somewhere safe to be healed and agrees that they must first get outside the cave.
- Sildar asks whether they killed Klarg. Seeing Yeemik's body, he exclaims, “Damn it, you killed Yeemik!”
- Zenvon believes Yeemik was the boss. Sildar corrects him: “No, no, Klarg was the boss!”
- Maela settles the uncertainty over whether they might have killed Klarg among the other enemies: “no, Zenvon, we did not kill any bugbears.”
- The party agrees to withdraw and rest instead of continuing to attack the cave's inhabitants.
- Before leaving, they search for valuables. Yeemik carries three agates worth ten gold pieces each and one potion of healing.
- The party escapes the cave. The DM warns that Klarg may send a patrol to find them.
- Zenvon initially proposes hiding near the previously cleared goblin position. After the DM points out that this means resting beside the corpses, he agrees to move farther away.
- They find a small clearing roughly a hundred feet from the cave. Both Sildar and Maela approve of the decision to move farther out.
- Pip takes the lead on finding concealment because he has a +3 Survival modifier, compared with Zenvon's +0. He asks Zenvon to help, distinguishing his wilderness experience from Zenvon's experience hiding in cities.
- With Zenvon's assistance, Pip rolls a natural 20. The party successfully hides and obscures its trails.
- Pleased with the result, Pip announces that they will sleep and adds, “And no fires.”

- The party records one potion of healing and three agates worth ten gold pieces each (thirty gold pieces for the gems), in the shared inventory.
- The DM shares and pins the inventory spreadsheet in the group's Discord.
- Zenvon asks whether the potion can be used for someone else. The DM confirms that anybody can use it. No consumption is recorded.
- Zenvon Forepot advances from level 1 to level 2. His maximum hit points increase to 15.
- The DM introduces RPGBot as a resource for comparing character options.
- Reviewing the character sheet reveals that Zenvon's weapon masteries had not yet been selected.
- Scimitar is selected for Nick. The group compares short sword and shortbow, both offering Vex, for the other mastery.
- The discussion distinguishes Nick's ability to free the bonus action from Vex's advantage on a subsequent attack after a hit.
- The shortbow option appeals to Zenvon's image of his character as someone who shoots and then hides. He nevertheless chooses the short sword because he wants a weapon in each hand.
- The resulting focus combines a scimitar with Nick and a short sword with Vex.
- The DM reviews movement, actions, bonus actions, and reactions, using examples from the battle: readying a shot until Maela reaches an enemy, Pip's shove, and the danger of moving away from an adjacent opponent.
- At level 2, Zenvon gains the ability to Dash, Disengage, or Hide as a bonus action.
- The DM connects this directly to the earlier fight: with Nick preserving his bonus action, Zenvon can make his two weapon attacks and then Disengage before moving away.
- The DM still needs to level up the accompanying sidekicks when the session ends.
