# Chapter 04 Clearing Cragmaw Hideout

Date: Not recorded in the Zoom summary

## Summary

After rescuing Sildar Hallwinter and resting at their concealed camp near Cragmaw Hideout, Zenvon, Pip Thistlewick, Sister Maela Dawnforge, and Veyra prepared to return to the cave. Sildar was safe, but Klarg, the bugbear commanding the hideout, remained alive deeper inside.

Before they returned, Sildar explained that the ambush on him and Gundren Rockseeker had been deliberate. Someone had tipped off the goblins, and Gundren had been taken away separately. Sildar had overheard the name Black Spider, but the identity behind it remained a mystery. Identifying himself as a member of the Lords' Alliance, he urged the party to deal with Klarg before the bugbear could send word ahead. Zenvon tried to persuade him to accompany them, but Sildar declined because of his injuries and other responsibilities. He agreed to wait at camp and offered fifty gold pieces for an escort to Phandalin.

The party reentered the hideout and fought goblins and wolves in its outer passages. Zenvon used food to draw the wolves aside and fought with a scimitar and dagger, killing a goblin with a surprise strike. The wolves remained dangerous: one bit him, although he kept his footing, and the fighting eventually left him close to collapse. Sister Maela restored him with Cure Wounds. Darkness complicated the advance until she provided light.

The goblins released water from a dam farther inside, sending a flood down the cave passage. Veyra was caught in the surge, but Zenvon grabbed her before the water could carry her away. With the outer defenders defeated, the party continued into the hideout.

Deeper in the cave, Zenvon imitated a goblin voice to lure a guard close enough for an ambush. He killed the goblin without immediately alerting the others. The fighting escalated when Klarg joined the battle, and both Pip and Sister Maela suffered heavy blows. Veyra struck the bugbear with Magic Missile and brought down a fleeing goblin with Fire Bolt before it could escape to warn others. Zenvon finished Klarg with his scimitar and dagger. Sister Maela subdued the remaining wolves, sparing their lives and ending the resistance in the hideout.

Searching Klarg's cave, the party found sacks and crates bearing a blue lion, the mark of the Lionshield Coster. The stolen merchant provisions offered an opportunity to help the company's Phandalin trading post and perhaps earn a reward. The recorded treasure included 1,700 copper pieces, two potions of healing, and a jade frog statuette with tiny golden eyes.

The party reunited with Sildar and set off toward Phandalin. During the return journey, he recalled another troubling detail of his capture: a strange goblin with an elongated head had been present at the ambush. It had whispered, "You're not what Ruxithid wants." Neither Sildar nor the party could explain the name or the remark. Klarg was dead and the hideout cleared, but Gundren remained missing, the Black Spider unidentified, and this second mysterious name unresolved. The party headed for town intending to rest.

A wounded member of the Lords' Alliance, rescued in the preceding session. He waits at camp while the party confronts Klarg, offers fifty gold pieces for an escort to Phandalin, and shares what he remembers of Gundren's capture, the Black Spider, and the strange goblin.

### Pip Thistlewick

Zenvon's companion and a fighter in the assault on the hideout. He takes significant damage during the battle with Klarg.

### Sister Maela Dawnforge

The party's cleric. She heals Zenvon, provides light during the cave fighting, suffers wounds in the battle with Klarg, and subdues the surviving wolves.

### Veyra

The party's arcane spellcaster. Zenvon saves her from the flood; she later wounds Klarg with Magic Missile and kills a fleeing goblin with Fire Bolt.

The bugbear leader of Cragmaw Hideout. Still alive after Sildar's rescue, he is killed by Zenvon during the party's return to the cave.

The missing dwarf captured alongside Sildar and taken away separately. The party seeks his whereabouts but does not find him in the hideout.

A name Sildar overheard among the goblins, associated with the mystery surrounding Gundren's capture. The party has not established the person's identity or motives.

### The Elongated-Headed Goblin

An unidentified goblin recalled by Sildar from the ambush. Its reference to Ruxithid introduces an unresolved lead; neither its identity nor Ruxithid's role is established in this session.
