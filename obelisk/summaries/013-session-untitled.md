# Chapter 13

Date: 2026-10-18

## Summary
The battle at the Stonehill Inn resumed with Pip already facing a bugbear at close quarters. He landed a blow for 9 damage while Sister Maela summoned a floating spectral hammer and killed a nearby goblin with divine radiance. The remaining goblins used the furniture for cover, shooting at Maela and ducking beneath tables. One missed entirely, but two later arrows dealt her 5 damage apiece. One goblin had already recognized that the apparent Zenvon it could see was not the real one and chose Maela as its target instead. Veyra, who had already expended two castings of Magic Missile before the session resumed, used another to kill a goblin beneath a table.

Zenvon slipped to the corner of a table and hid before firing at Nosk. His stealth narrowly defeated the bugbears’ awareness, allowing him to choose either target; he chose their leader. His arrow dealt 14 damage and left Nosk with a single hit point. Pip promptly moved over and finished Nosk himself. When Zenvon complained about losing the kill, Pip replied, “You should have said something, Boss.” Another bugbear vaulted over the bar and struck Pip with a morning star for 12 damage, leaving him with 17 of his 36 hit points. Maela’s spectral hammer struck a bugbear for 7 damage, and Zenvon subsequently killed another with his shortbow.

The last bugbear faced Pip, whose anger at people who preyed on farmers and innocent civilians made him particularly determined to finish the fight. Pip shoved it prone and struck it for 8 damage. When it stood and fled, he landed another 8 damage as it escaped. The final goblin took advantage of the distraction, smashing a window with its scimitar and jumping into the street. Toblen Stonehill protested the cost of the windows as Veyra and Zenvon pursued outside.

Zenvon used his extra burst of speed to catch the fleeing bugbear and strike it with his shortsword for 8 damage. He then told Pip that they wanted it alive for questioning. Pip pursued using Action Surge, but both his attack and Zenvon’s subsequent dagger strike missed as the bugbear escaped their attempted encirclement. Veyra, reluctantly denied permission to kill the bugbear, killed the fleeing goblin instead. Zenvon caught the bugbear once more and knocked it unconscious with his shortsword’s hilt.

The party bound the captive’s hands and brought him into the wrecked inn. Toblen, Tuck, and Elsa were present, and Sildar came downstairs to the aftermath. Zenvon’s promise of freedom failed to persuade the prisoner, who seemed caught between defiance and terror. Veyra then produced her notebook and a complicated map, claiming that she already knew everything and merely wanted confirmation. She threatened to examine his brain if his answers proved useless. This intimidation succeeded where persuasion had failed.

The captive revealed that the Spider was a drow and the real power behind the operation; Glasstaff had only managed local affairs. Gundren Rockseeker was a prisoner at Cragmaw Castle, and the boss had specifically wanted the dwarf. The bugbear insisted that he was only hired muscle and did not know the larger strategy. Nosk, who had worked directly for the boss, would have known more; the prisoner had merely been ordered to help Nosk clean up his mistakes.

Toblen fiercely opposed letting another bugbear escape back to Cragmaw Castle. Zenvon insisted on keeping his promise that the captive would live, then resolved the escape problem by chopping off his legs with his scimitar. Sister Maela expressed reluctant admiration for the ruthlessness of that compromise, while recalling the enemies’ attempts to abduct people, including women and children. Toblen objected to housing the prisoner and demanded a council meeting. Although the party initially discussed keeping him at the inn, they ultimately decided to take him to Cragmaw Castle in a cart so he could provide useful information.

The captive introduced himself as Gorl, a smaller-than-average bugbear with a perpetual nervous twitch. He belonged to the Scarlet Fist under Nosk. He identified Brakka, a slain bugbear with a snapped tusk and a scarlet cloak pinned with a bent nail, as the only member who had known the route to Wave Echo Cave. Brakka had kept that knowledge secret. The party reviewed the cave’s lost history and its connection to magical artifact-making. Zenvon considered seeking Hamun’s advice, but the possible movement of Gundren’s map made delay costly. Cragmaw Castle remained their best lead. Before departure, Sildar reminded them about the money still available for collecting Iarno and clearing the castle.

The party agreed to take a long rest on the way, and their forest journey was otherwise uneventful. Cragmaw Castle had seven crumbling towers, collapsed upper stories, and an entrance beyond broken doors and a short staircase. After scouting its perimeter, the party bargained with Gorl for information. He offered to identify the factions if they let him leave; he intended to get as far away as his arms could carry him. Zenvon suggested that he wait outside until they returned and offered continued care, but could not tell whether Gorl’s agreement was sincere.

Gorl described the Arrow Slit Watch, red-mask hobgoblins, Lhupo, and King Grol, carefully distinguishing the king from himself. His account muddled the goblin groups rather than establishing a reliable full faction map. The different groups had separate goals and loyalties, and the DM explained that the Black Spider’s money, rather than King Grol’s personal strength, held them together. The party could exploit those divisions, though attacking openly might unite the defenders. Gorl also warned of an unidentified monster in an area the goblins avoided.

Zenvon proposed entering as religious travelers, with Veyra and Sister Maela posing as holy figures and himself and Pip as escorts. The plan developed into an apparent conversion to goblin worship. Learning that Lhupo maintained a shrine to the Jagged Tooth gave the scheme a local focus. Zenvon’s strong religion check revealed no real god by that name: it might represent a dangerous creature or something imagined. He also recognized the risk of claiming knowledge of a cult that might exist only inside the castle.

The final addition was Gorl himself. The party would claim to have rescued him after attackers cut off his legs, using their apparent kindness to gain entry and trust. Gorl pointed out that they were the attackers. Zenvon reminded him that his continued survival depended on cooperation. Sister Maela threatened him if he betrayed them, while Veyra proposed cutting out his tongue. Gorl protested that this would prevent him from speaking. The session ended before the party entered the castle, with their pilgrimage deception prepared and their captive’s cooperation still uncertain.

## Locations
### Stonehill Inn

### Cragmaw Castle
An isolated ruined fortress with seven towers of varying sizes and heights, collapsed upper stories, and a short staircase leading to a terrace. Broken double doors open toward a shadowed hall; round towers with arrow slits overlook the approach.

The castle houses several goblin and hobgoblin factions under King Grol and ultimately the Black Spider. Gundren is reportedly imprisoned there. A local shrine inside Cragmaw Castle maintained by Lhupo, who claims priestly authority. The party learned that the Jagged Tooth is no real god, though it might refer to a feared creature or an invented being.

### Wave Echo Cave
A lost dungeon associated with magical artifact-making by humans, dwarves, and wizards. Its location disappeared from common knowledge after an orc raid centuries ago.

Gorl said Brakka knew the route but kept it secret. With Brakka dead, Gundren and his possible map remained the party’s practical lead. The possibility that others were already using that map made a detour to seek Hamun’s advice less attractive.
