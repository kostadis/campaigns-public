# Chapter 16

Date: 2026-11-15

## Summary
The halls of Cragmaw Castle rang with goblin music, table-pounding, and celebration as the Cragmaw band feasted on the slow-roasted remains of the defeated Red-Mask hobgoblins. Lhupo, the newly sworn goblin paladin, rose with her scimitar raised as Zenvon entered: “The prophet, the tooth spoke, and the jaw broke.” She offered him the head of the table, and the goblins chanted “Prophet” until he reluctantly accepted. Gundren, untied and healed since the afternoon’s battle, tried to approach the same place of honor but settled elsewhere after a look from Lhupo. Veyra took notes, Sister Maela rolled her eyes, and Pip discussed defense and combat with the goblin archers.

Yegg, the head cook, proudly presented the feast: “first cut for Prophet, second cut for paladin. Nobody else touches till I say.” Zenvon tried to avoid the meat by announcing that he would fast in honor of their victory. The hall fell silent: if the Prophet would not eat, neither would Lhupo or the tribe. Realizing the consequence, Zenvon took a bite, releasing the goblins into cheering and feasting. His companions appreciated his predicament; Maela laughed, and Pip observed, “better than you than me.” Zenvon also accepted the celebratory alcohol.

A goblin musician then placed King Grol’s severed tooth before Zenvon. Lhupo watched the trophy closely: Zenvon had promised it to her. He handed it over with “It’s yours,” and she knotted it into a pendant, proclaiming that the Jagged Tooth lived. Zenvon maintained a neutral expression through the unfamiliar customs.

Gundren approached privately, asking why the goblins bowed to Zenvon and called him “the offering.” Zenvon admitted that the party was posing as followers of the Jagged Tooth to rescue him, but concealed the goblins’ original intention to cook him. Instead, he claimed Gundren was considered a blessed child of their god. A Deception result of 22 persuaded the dwarf, helped by his reluctance to contemplate any worse explanation. Zenvon warned him that one mistake could compromise their escape. Gundren complied, mentioned that his brothers Tharden and Nundro had gone ahead into the hills, and firmly refused the hobgoblin meat.

During the festivities, Polt recounted the shapeshifter’s death near the storage tower and the trees, crediting Veyra’s magic. The party learned that nobody had searched the body for valuables. Lhupo then silenced the hall and asked Zenvon to kill the creature she had fed in the old chapel, along with Snarl, the king’s surviving wolf. She wanted the castle safe for goblins and offered the party whatever treasure the creatures guarded. Zenvon’s Insight revealed that Lhupo had never actually believed the chapel creature was divine; she had used it to maintain control. Her new oath had given her genuine power, leaving her no further use for the old prop. She insisted that she must remain to hold the band together while the Prophet dealt with its monsters.

The captive bugbear Gorl was brought before the goblins and executed. The goblins remembered his violence and oppression, and Zenvon chose not to intervene. The party then retired to comfortable rooms with actual beds. Gundren questioned the wisdom of leaving him among goblins while his rescuers hunted monsters. Zenvon explained that securing the band’s cooperation offered their best chance of leaving safely, and Gundren agreed to remain in their room.

In the morning, Zenvon gathered the group into a hand-holding circle around the tooth and led several minutes of prayer. He then claimed the Jagged Tooth had commanded him to take the goblin army into battle. Lhupo answered that she too heard from the Jagged Tooth, and that this was a test of her faith in Zenvon’s strength. The bluff failed. After further negotiation, she supplied Polt, one of her strongest archers, whose 21 hit points made him as resilient as three ordinary goblins.

The party entered the chapel with torches, declining the suggestion of using leftover meat as bait. The Grick surprised nobody. Veyra opened with Fire Bolt, Polt missed with his bow, and the creature struck Pip for 13 damage with its tentacles before missing with its beak. Zenvon missed his first scimitar attack but landed a second for 13 damage. Sister Maela attempted a divine cantrip, and Pip killed the creature with his sword. Lhupo dismissed the former object of worship as meat.

Before confronting Snarl, Pip used Second Wind and recovered fully. The wolf was already wounded, with 25 of 37 hit points. Veyra’s fire, Polt’s arrow, and Zenvon’s shortbow weakened it further, and Sister Maela’s magic finished it. Zenvon had considered using Mage Hand with fire again, but the room’s fire had burned out.

The party found 700 gold pieces associated with the doppelganger’s deal with King Grol, and Lhupo honored her promise that the treasure belonged to the Prophet. Zenvon had missed that part of the bargain, prompting Pip to offer his services as a translator. The gold was recorded in the party’s treasure spreadsheet.

Lhupo then requested help with one final predator, a Grell that sometimes killed goblins. She had waited until the party proved itself against the Grick and Snarl before asking. The GM explained that the castle had been abandoned before its current occupants arrived; some monsters had remained, and Grol had never cared enough to remove them. Zenvon accepted the same arrangement: kill the creature and keep its treasure.

Pip entered the Grell’s chamber first. The eyeless, brain-like creature attacked him with its tentacles and missed. Sister Maela attempted her magic, Pip struck for 5 damage, Veyra’s Fire Bolt dealt 3, Polt’s arrow dealt 5, and Zenvon’s shortbow hit for 12 while Pip stood close enough to enable Sneak Attack. The Grell had begun with 55 hit points, and the session ended with the fight unresolved. A proposed additional action by Sister Maela was withdrawn before play stopped.

- The party considers using meat left over from the feast as bait but elects to enter with torches and no food.

- Veyra opens with Fire Bolt, hitting for 9 damage, then repositions.
- Polt shoots his shortbow and misses badly, then moves as well.
- The Grick hits Pip with its tentacles for 13 damage. Its follow-up beak attack misses.
- Zenvon attempts a scimitar attack using Nick, with disengagement in mind, but his attack result of 7 misses.
- He chooses another attack instead of hiding with his bonus action. The second result, 19, hits for 13 damage and makes the creature howl.
- Sister Maela uses an unnamed cantrip; the Grick makes its saving throw.
- Pip hits with his sword and kills the creature.
- Lhupo celebrates and declares that the creature was her god when she had nothing but is now merely meat.
- Polt points through a doorway and attracts Lhupo’s attention, but she brushes him off. The party proceeds toward Snarl.

## Locations
### Cragmaw Castle
The stronghold of the Cragmaw goblins, currently filled with music, dancing, table-pounding, and the smell of roasting Red-Mask hobgoblin meat. Lhupo now leads the band and wants its remaining dangerous inhabitants removed.

The party rests in fancy guest quarters with beds, where Gundren remains during the hunts. The castle had been abandoned before its current occupants arrived; several predators remained in areas the goblins and hobgoblins generally avoided. Grol had shown little interest in making those areas safe.

Polt also identifies the area behind the storage tower, near the trees, as the site of the shapeshifter’s body. Nobody has searched it.

### Cragmaw Castle Chapel
An old chapel where a Grick occupied a ledge. Lhupo fed it and allowed it to eat her enemies, using its supposed divinity to influence the goblins without believing in that divinity herself.

The party enters with torches rather than bait. The Grick fails to surprise them and is killed after wounding Pip.

### King's Room
The area where the wounded wolf Snarl remained and where the party found 700 gold pieces the doppelganger had paid King Grol for Gundren.

The room’s fire has burned out by the time Zenvon considers using Mage Hand during the wolf fight. Snarl is killed through coordinated ranged attacks and Sister Maela’s magic.

## NPCs
### Lhupo
A recently christened goblin Paladin who has taken leadership of the Cragmaw band. She welcomes Zenvon as the Prophet and wears King Grol’s tooth as a pendant symbolizing her faith.

Her earlier treatment of the Grick as a god was a means of controlling the goblins. Zenvon’s previous deception inspired a genuine religious transformation, and her oath gained power when she rushed to save him.

She wants the castle’s threats removed but insists that she must stay to hold the band together. Her independent conviction defeats Zenvon’s attempt to requisition the goblin army through another supposed revelation. She supplies Polt instead and promises the party the treasure found during its hunts.

### Gundren Rockseeker
A rescued dwarf, now untied and healed, who remains confused by Zenvon’s status and his own designation as “the offering.”

Zenvon convinces him that the goblins regard him as a blessed child of their god, concealing their earlier intention to cook him. Gundren accepts the explanation but refuses hobgoblin meat.

He mentions that his brothers Tharden and Nundro went ahead into the hills. Concerned about being left behind during the hunts, he ultimately agrees to stay in the guest room and avoid trouble.

### Yegg
The head cook of the Cragmaw goblins, proud of his slow-roasted Red-Mask hobgoblin feast.

He reserves the first cut for the Prophet and the second for the Paladin, forbidding anyone else to eat until he permits it. This serving order turns Zenvon’s attempted fast into an obstacle for the entire feast.

### Pip Thistlewick
A companion who spends the feast discussing defense and combat with goblin archers. He appreciates Zenvon’s willingness to eat the ceremonial meat and later teases him for missing the treasure bargain.

Pip takes 13 damage from the Grick’s tentacles and delivers its killing blow with his sword. He restores himself fully with Second Wind before opening Snarl’s door.

He enters the Grell’s chamber first, survives its missed opening attack, and deals 5 damage. His position beside the creature enables Zenvon’s Sneak Attack.

### Sister Maela Dawnforge
A party member who observes the festivities with skepticism and amusement, rolling her eyes and laughing at Zenvon’s ceremonial predicament.

She urges the group onward after his failed attempt to secure the goblin army. Her unnamed offensive cantrip is used during the hunts and delivers the killing blow against Snarl.

### Veyra
A companion who takes notes during the victory feast and provides Fire Bolt support throughout the hunts.

Polt credits her with the magic that killed the shapeshifter. Her Fire Bolts deal 9 damage to the Grick, 7 to Snarl, and 3 to the Grell.

### Gorl
The captive bugbear brought before the group and executed during the victory celebrations. King Grol had already died in the previous battle.

The goblins’ desire to kill Gorl reflects his history of tormenting, beating, and killing them. Zenvon considers his own promise fulfilled and chooses not to interfere.

### Polt
A veteran goblin archer assigned by Lhupo to assist the party. He has 21 hit points, compared with an ordinary goblin’s 7, making his contribution more substantial than Zenvon initially assumes.

The party met him on its first day. At the feast, he recounts the apparent drow’s death and credits Veyra, also revealing that the body remains unsearched.

He misses the Grick, deals 5 damage to Snarl, and deals 5 damage to the Grell while maintaining his distance.

Zenvon had previously promised the tooth to Lhupo. He fulfills that promise, and she ties it into a pendant representing the Jagged Tooth faith. The following morning, it becomes the focus of the group’s hand-holding prayer circle.

### 700 Gold Pieces
Gold found in the King’s Room and associated with the doppelganger’s deal with King Grol.

Lhupo awards the treasure to the party under the terms of their monster-hunting agreement. The sum is recorded in the party’s treasure spreadsheet; no additional treasure is found in that immediate search.
