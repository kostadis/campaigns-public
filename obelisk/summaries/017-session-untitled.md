# Chapter 17

Date: 2026-11-15

## Summary
The final battle within the crumbling library tower of Cragmaw Castle left Pip caught in the grell’s grip. The brain-like horror struck him with its tentacles and attacked with its beak while he remained grappled. Pip resisted its poison, but the continuing assault left him fifteen hit points down. Sister Maela’s first attack missed; Pip answered with a longsword strike, Veyra landed a Fire Bolt, and Polt dealt another solid blow. Zenvon repositioned and fired his shortbow, but missed. Maela then burned the creature with Sacred Flame, and Veyra finished it with three unerring Magic Missiles. With the grell dead, the castle was clear of immediate monstrous threats.

The ruined library’s collapsed upper floors had left a thirty-foot hollow silo, its upper reaches lost in shadow. Dust, rubble, broken glass, work tables, and bookshelves filled the room below. At Zenvon’s request, Pip climbed into the upper ruins, crediting childhood practice climbing trees. He discovered a chest and initially asked someone below to catch it, but the party settled on lowering it with rope from Zenvon’s pack. Inside were 90 electrum pieces, 120 gold pieces, a potion of healing, a Scroll of Silence, and a Scroll of Revivify. The discoveries prompted a discussion of scrolls as consumable magic and the difference between copying wizard spells and using clerical scrolls.

Yegg, visibly hungover from the previous night, announced that food was ready and offered cold red mask with little ceremony. The party shared a cold, unenthusiastic meal before preparing to move on. Lhupo asked where they were going next. Before leaving, the party searched the fallen doppelganger outside the castle, whose body had been chewed by nocturnal creatures. They recovered Gundren Rockseeker’s map, recognizable by his handwriting. It revealed Wave Echo Cave’s location and gave the party a new direction: the Black Spider was certainly absent from Cragmaw Castle, and the cave became his suspected destination.

The party considered returning to Phandalin, proceeding directly to Wave Echo Cave, or pursuing the outstanding treasure lead in Thundertree. They chose to escort the famished Gundren back to town. Before departing, Zenvon thanked Lhupo for letting them worship the Jagged Tooth and explained that they would take the offering with them. Lhupo declared that the offering went where the prophet went, while Polt echoed that the tooth answered in the dark. When Zenvon requested support should trouble arise, Lhupo pledged the Cragmaw goblins’ loyalty.

Once outside the castle, Gundren offered a surprisingly light, finely crafted quarterstaff he had found in the hobgoblin’s chamber, estimating its value at ten gold pieces. He expressed heartfelt gratitude for having hired the party. Zenvon suggested that more gold—or a stake in Gundren’s businesses—would make a suitable expression of that gratitude. Gundren explained that his business interests were currently tied up in Wave Echo Cave.

During the roughly day-long journey to Phandalin, Gundren asked for his recovered map. Zenvon initially agreed, then sought information and a firm arrangement before surrendering their means of finding the cave’s precise entrance. Gundren explained that his brothers, Nundro and Tharden, had gone ahead to prospect and were missing. He suspected they had found the cave or encountered others searching for it. Wave Echo Cave reportedly contained a magical artifact and valuable deposits of Mithral Silver.

Gundren offered ten percent of the mining operation’s profits after deductions and expenses, together with first pick of what the party found in the cave. Zenvon countered for ten percent of the gross, before taxes and expenses. Gundren’s impressed expression acknowledged the strength of that negotiation. He also cited the backing of the Lords’ Alliance and Lord Neverember of Neverwinter. Zenvon requested money in advance or a written promise, and Gundren agreed to formalize the arrangement.

At the repaired Stonehill Inn, Toblen welcomed the party as heroes. Tuck rushed to hug Gundren, celebrating Zenvon’s fulfilled promise to bring him back. Elsa expressed astonishment and relief, while Gundren immediately requested a drink. Sildar Hallwinter received the party’s report with greater respect, though Zenvon had to explain that the castle remained occupied by allied goblins under Lhupo, now a paladin.

Sildar paid three hundred gold pieces of the original five-hundred-gold reward. He offered the remaining two hundred if Zenvon could bring Lhupo into a formal relationship with the Lords’ Alliance, since the Alliance did not consider a goblin-held castle cleared. Zenvon agreed. Sildar also approved of captive Gorl’s fate after Zenvon explained that the goblins had dealt with him, praised Pip’s honorable bearing of his sword, and suggested a future place for him in the Alliance. Iarno Albrek remained missing, with a separate two-hundred-gold reward still available. Zenvon avoided confirming that the party knew Wave Echo Cave’s location, although mentioning Gundren’s assistance immediately sharpened Sildar’s interest.

Finally, Gundren drafted the mining contract with Sildar and Toblen as witnesses. Pip disclaimed any accounting expertise; Veyra reviewed the wording, made amendments, and approved it after a brief exchange. The party secured ten percent of the gross from the Wave Echo Cave operation, with the recovered map and the work of clearing the cave central to the partnership. The session ended with the agreement settled and everyone ready to rest.

## Scenes
### The Final Stand Against the Grell
#### The party engages in a final, desperate struggle against a brain-like creature with a beak in the ruins of the castle library.
- The grell strikes Pip for four damage while holding him grappled. Its accompanying beak attack misses spectacularly.
- Sister Maela’s initial attack roll is five and misses.
- Pip fights back with his longsword, hitting with a total of 21 and dealing seven damage.
- Veyra, introduced as Veyra of the Blue Candle, casts Fire Bolt and deals six damage. The creature howls in outrage.
- Polt lands another hit for six damage.
- Zenvon moves and fires his shortbow, but misses. During the turn, the DM clarifies that the nearby ally enabling Sneak Attack must be close to the monster; Pip satisfies that condition.
- Zenvon asks about a further weapon attack, and the DM explains that the bow attack does not permit the proposed additional attack.
- Pip remains grappled because he has not attempted to escape. He succeeds against the grell’s poison.
- The grell continues attacking him. After accounting for an earlier attack and advantage, the DM records Pip as fifteen hit points down; Pip remains standing.
- Sister Maela casts Sacred Flame. The grell fails its saving throw and takes five damage.
- Pip’s next longsword attack misses with a total of eight.
- Veyra casts Magic Missile. The shared damage roll is three per dart, producing nine damage across three missiles and killing the grell.
- The group celebrates, and the DM confirms that the palace is clear.

### Looting the Library Tower
#### After the battle, the party explores the collapsed library tower and discovers hidden treasure.
- The DM identifies the chamber as C13, formerly a library. Collapsed upper floors have created a hollow silo thirty feet high, with shadows obscuring its upper reaches.
- Dust, rubble, and broken glass cover the floor. Old work tables and bookshelves clutter the southern half, and the room appears long unused.
- Zenvon asks whether the wooden rectangles on the map are benches, boxes, or treasure chests; the DM identifies them as work tables.
- Zenvon wants to explore above. Rather than attempt the DC 15 Athletics climb himself, he sends Pip, who succeeds with a total of 22.
- Pip remarks, “well, boss, it's a good thing I knew I practiced climbing trees back when I was a kid.”
- When Zenvon credits his warrior’s strength, Pip adds, “Yes, but as you know, it's not just about how much strength you have, but how you use that strength.”
- Pip finds a chest above and initially pushes it toward the edge, asking whether someone can catch it. Zenvon proposes Polt, but the discussion turns to using rope.
- Pip boasts about preparedness before Zenvon confirms that he has rope in his backpack. Pip ties the chest and lowers it safely.
- Zenvon notes the rope’s listed knot-tying and breaking checks, but no additional check is recorded for lowering the chest.
- The chest contains 90 electrum pieces, 120 gold pieces, a potion of healing, a Scroll of Silence, and a Scroll of Revivify. The treasure is entered into the shared spreadsheet.
- Zenvon expresses relief that snakes did not emerge from the chest.
- The group discusses scrolls as one-use magic items. The DM explains that copying a suitable spell into a wizard’s spellbook consumes the scroll, while casting a scroll offers access to magic beyond the caster’s usual prepared resources.
- In the table’s explanation, Silence is presented as a utility spell Veyra could copy, while Revivify is treated as clerical magic that cannot be preserved through copying. Its value includes access to a spell normally requiring a more advanced caster.
