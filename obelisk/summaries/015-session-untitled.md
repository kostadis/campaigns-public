# Chapter 15

Date: 2026-11-01

## Summary
The party entered King Grol's quarters deep within Cragmaw Castle to find the bugbear king standing over the unconscious and badly beaten Gundren Rockseeker. Thick furs covered the floor, old trophies hung on the walls, and a brazier of coals burned beside the room’s furnishings. Nearby stood the great wolf Snarl and a white-haired woman in gray robes who appeared to be a Drow, negotiating the purchase of the dwarf prisoner for seven hundred gold pieces.

Vorl announced the visitors as Lhupo’s pilgrims, spitting her name with evident contempt. Grol immediately demanded to know where Nosk and Gorl were, noting that the watchers had seen the party carrying Gorl inside. Zenvon Forepot claimed Nosk had been killed in battle and that they had found Gorl badly hurt and brought him back as faithful followers of the Jagged Tooth. The bluff briefly fooled even the GM, who accepted it without requiring a deception roll. When Grol asked about rumors of a prophet in his shrine, Zenvon maintained that the Jagged Tooth had bestowed that role upon him.

Zenvon proposed that everyone close their eyes for a sacred prayer for the king’s well-being. Grol sharply corrected his thanks for the invitation: “I summoned you.” The supposed Drow, Nisrell Bao, repeatedly tried to finish her business and leave. She invoked Menzoberranzan’s supposed customs toward the worship of “lesser species,” while an insight check revealed how anxious she was to depart. Zenvon first urged that the prayer precede the sale, then offered to let the transaction finish if she would remain for a brief blessing to protect her journey. He warned that an angry Jagged Tooth might otherwise cause her to lose her prisoner.

Nisrell paid Grol and dragged Gundren toward the exit. Vorl entered, and Thrak watched the religious proceedings from the doorway. As the prayer began, Nisrell suddenly attacked Zenvon, missing him, and shed her Drow appearance to reveal a blank, featureless humanoid doppelganger. The creature shouted that the party had come to betray Grol and was working with Lhupo. The room erupted into combat.

Grol’s morningstar missed, and Zenvon wounded him with his shortsword before withdrawing. Pip protected Veyra from Snarl with his shield, struck the king, and spent his Action Surge on another attack. Veyra’s first-level Magic Missile killed Grol. The doppelganger, however, began carrying Gundren away. After Zenvon’s player pointed out that the creature had been omitted from initiative, it struck Zenvon twice for fourteen damage. A later pair of blows knocked the prophet unconscious while he tried to block its escape.

Sister Maela cried, “they have killed the prophet,” drawing Lhupo and her goblins toward the fight. Faced with a choice between killing Thrak and stopping Gundren’s abductor, the party directed Veyra to cast Web across the escape route. The spell restrained the doppelganger, Thrak, Zenvon, and two arriving goblins. Lhupo avoided the webs and attacked the shapeshifter. To everyone’s astonishment, her assault erupted in divine power: Zenvon’s fall had completed her transformation into a paladin of the Jagged Tooth. Sister Maela’s response was a startled “shit.”

Maela restored Zenvon to consciousness, though he remained caught in the web and missed a shortbow shot at the doppelganger. Snarl knocked Pip unconscious, the hobgoblins badly wounded Maela, and the shapeshifter broke free. Veyra killed Thrak, ended the web, and moved to block reinforcements. Lhupo healed Zenvon and accepted his direction to hold off the hobgoblins while the party pursued Gundren.

Zenvon then used Mage Hand to hurl the lit brazier onto Snarl. The wolf caught fire and spent its next action rolling out the flames, giving Pip an opening to escape the chamber. Maela healed herself, withdrew, and closed the door. Zenvon used the brazier again to ignite the table, leaving Vorl and Snarl inside. Vorl died from the fire; Snarl’s death was not established.

The doppelganger killed the pursuing goblins and nearly escaped with Gundren. Pip caught up because the prisoner slowed the creature, but suffered another fourteen damage and was left with only four hit points. Veyra finished the pursuit with a second-level Magic Missile, killing the shapeshifter and securing Gundren.

With Gundren safe in Pip’s care, Zenvon chose to return to Lhupo rather than leave her surrounded. He killed a hobgoblin beside her, and they clinked their scimitars together. Veyra dispatched another enemy, while the Jagged Tooth goblins won the fighting elsewhere in the castle. All the Red-Mask Kin were dead; the final reported Jagged Tooth losses were two goblins. Yegg cheered the victory, and the tribe accepted Zenvon’s prophetic status with renewed conviction.

The goblins began preparing to roast the fallen hobgoblins. Sister Maela suggested leaving before their hosts asked too many questions, but Zenvon’s proposed plan was to stay for the feast, dance and drink with the tribe, and warn Gundren to remain quiet. The next morning, he intended to claim that the Jagged Tooth had appeared in a dream and ordered him to escort Gundren safely to Phandalin. This remained an out-of-character plan, not an enacted departure. Gundren was conscious by the session’s end, and the GM noted that the adventurous dwarf would likely want to continue toward Wave Echo Cave.

## Spells
### Magic Missile
Veyra cast this spell repeatedly, using first-level magical force darts to kill King Grol and later Thrak. She also used it against the remaining hobgoblin opposition.

Her decisive second-level casting sent four darts into the fleeing doppelganger after Pip caught up with it. The spell killed the shapeshifter and secured Gundren’s rescue. The GM noted that a sufficiently poor damage roll could have left the creature alive.

### Spiritual Weapon
Sister Maela conjured a floating spectral weapon to attack the doppelganger, landing an early hit for seven damage. She continued directing it during the battle, including moving it toward another enemy while attending to healing.

The table also discussed whether the weapon could move to a place Maela could not see; the GM permitted the movement.

### Web
Veyra chose Web when stopping Gundren’s abductor became more urgent than immediately killing Thrak. Its twenty-foot cube restrained the doppelganger, Thrak, Zenvon, and two goblin reinforcements.

Lhupo avoided restraint and reached the doppelganger. Zenvon failed an Athletics check to escape, but could still attempt attacks. The shapeshifter later broke free, and Veyra ended the spell after killing Thrak.

### Mage Hand
Zenvon used this spectral hand to manipulate the lit brazier under an improvised ruling. He first threw it onto Snarl, dealing eight damage and igniting the wolf. Snarl spent its next action putting out the flames, creating an opening for Pip to escape.

After Maela withdrew and closed the door, Zenvon used the brazier again to ignite the table. Vorl died in the resulting chamber fire. Zenvon’s broader intention was to set the room ablaze and force its occupants to contend with the flames.

### Healing Word
Maela’s healing returned Zenvon to consciousness with six hit points, followed by one temporary hit point. The spell was not named in that exchange.

For Pip’s recovery, the GM announced Healing Word twice, then announced second-level Cure Wounds before reporting Pip at eighteen hit points. The transcript does not separately resolve the Healing Word announcement.

### Cure Wounds
Sister Maela cast this healing spell at second level on Pip, restoring him to eighteen hit points after Snarl knocked him unconscious.

She later healed herself before escaping the quarters; her stated total afterward was twenty hit points. By the later stages of the fight, the GM noted that she had spent all her second-level spells.

### Divine Smite
Lhupo manifested divine power during her sudden awakening as a paladin of the Jagged Tooth. Her attack against the doppelganger produced a blinding smite, surprising even her.

Both attacks in that assault hit for sixteen damage each. The GM explicitly linked her transformation to the fervor inspired by Zenvon and the final shock of seeing the prophet fall.

## NPCs
### King Grol
The mighty bugbear ruler of Cragmaw Castle held Gundren captive and agreed to sell him for seven hundred gold. He had summoned the party to learn who the visitors were and already knew from his watchers that they had carried Gorl inside.

He questioned the party about Nosk, Gorl, and the rumored new prophet. His willingness to hear the pilgrims’ prayer competed with his desire to finish the profitable transaction. Once the doppelganger exposed the conspiracy, he attacked with his morningstar but missed. Zenvon and Pip wounded him before Veyra’s Magic Missile killed him.

### Gundren Rockseeker
Found unconscious and badly beaten beside the table in Grol’s quarters, Gundren was sold to Nisrell Bao and dragged through the battle by the revealed doppelganger.

His weight slowed the shapeshifter enough for Pip to catch up. After Veyra killed it, Pip secured the dwarf before returning to help Lhupo.

Gundren was conscious by the end of the session. The GM described him as an adventurous, archaeological sort for whom Wave Echo Cave represented the adventure of a lifetime, making an immediate return to Phandalin uncertain.

### Nisrell Bao
The apparent white-haired, gray-skinned Drow woman in gray robes was a doppelganger. She paid seven hundred gold for Gundren and repeatedly pressed to conclude the transaction and depart.

She invoked Menzoberranzan while displaying contempt for goblin worship. Zenvon’s insight revealed marked anxiety about remaining in the castle.

Her attempted attack during the prayer missed, but she revealed her featureless humanoid form and accused the party of conspiring with Lhupo. During the battle she repeatedly attacked Zenvon, carried Gundren toward escape, killed two pursuing goblins, and badly wounded Pip. Veyra’s second-level Magic Missile finally killed her.

### Snarl
Grol’s great wolf first targeted Veyra, but Pip’s intervention prevented the hit. It then turned on Pip and eventually knocked him unconscious.

Zenvon’s brazier attack set Snarl ablaze, forcing it to spend an action extinguishing itself. It remained inside the burning quarters after Maela closed the door, later avoiding further burning with a successful saving throw. Its death was not established.

### Thrak
Leader of the Red-Mask Kin, Thrak initially watched the religious proceedings from the doorway. Lhupo’s earlier agreement made his death the condition for her goblins’ planned charge.

He attacked Zenvon, became caught in Veyra’s Web, and participated in the pressure on Sister Maela. Veyra killed him with Magic Missile before ending the web.

### Vorl
The hobgoblin announced the visitors as Lhupo’s pilgrims, expressing his disdain for her name. He entered Grol’s quarters and repeatedly attacked Sister Maela during the battle.

Pip wounded him while escaping Snarl. Maela subsequently shut him inside the quarters, where he banged on the door and died from the spreading fire.

### Lhupo
A fervent goblin follower of the Jagged Tooth, Lhupo believed Zenvon was its prophet. Maela’s cry that the prophet had been killed brought her rushing toward the fight.

She avoided Veyra’s web, attacked the doppelganger, and unexpectedly manifested divine smiting power. The GM established that her devotion and oath had made her a paladin, with Zenvon’s apparent death serving as the final catalyst.

She healed Zenvon for fifteen hit points and eagerly accepted the task of fighting the hobgoblins. His return to help her delighted her, and they clinked scimitars after fighting side by side. Her victory helped convince the tribe of Zenvon’s prophetic legitimacy.

### Yegg
A Jagged Tooth goblin who arrived cheering after the Red-Mask Kin’s defeat. He joined the noisy celebration as the tribe took control of Cragmaw Castle and prepared its feast.

## Locations
### King Grol's Quarters
A furnished living chamber with thick fur carpets, old trophies, a large bed to the north, a round table and chairs near the southeast door, and a brightly burning brazier of coals.

Gundren lay beside the table when the party arrived. The chamber became the center of the fight with Grol, Snarl, Vorl, and the doppelganger, while its doorways became crucial escape routes and defensive positions.

Zenvon used the brazier against Snarl and then ignited the table. Maela withdrew and closed the door, leaving Vorl and Snarl inside. The windows provided ventilation, but Vorl died from the fire.

### Cragmaw Castle
The ruined fortress housed the competing Jagged Tooth goblins and Red-Mask Kin hobgoblins under Grol’s rule. Fighting spread beyond the royal quarters as the factions attacked one another.

The doppelganger attempted to carry Gundren out through the castle, pursued by goblins and then Pip and Veyra. After its death and the Red-Mask Kin’s defeat, the Jagged Tooth tribe controlled the castle.

The final reported losses were all the Red-Mask Kin and two Jagged Tooth goblins. The victors began preparing a feast from the hobgoblin dead while celebrating Zenvon as their prophet.
