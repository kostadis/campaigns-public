# Chapter 01 Phandalin: The Threshold Awaits

Date: 2026-05-22

## Summary
The road to Phandalin began with a contract, a wagon full of provisions, and the promise of easy coin. Zenvon Forepot, a rogue whose criminal work in Neverwinter had grown dangerously complicated, had signed on to deliver supplies for Gundren Rockseeker. Gundren and his warrior escort, Sildar Hallwinter, had already ridden ahead, expecting to meet the wagon at Barthen's Provisions. Zenvon had negotiated 19 gold pieces for the work, but soon realized Gundren had arranged a profitable journey of his own: the wagon's other travelers had paid for passage.

Those companions each had their own reason to seek Phandalin. Pip believed frontier towns needed strong backs and sharper eyes. Sister Maela was answering a troubling letter from an old dwarven contact who worked claims near Triboar. Veyra of the Blue Candle, a tiefling, was following her missing mentor's last known destination. He had vanished while investigating fragments of an ancient stone. She carried a charcoal map covered in frantic, conspiratorial annotations, a blue candle wrapped in cloth, and a half-burned note reading: “Was in Phandalin. The town is not a prize. It is the threshold.”

The journey turned unsettling when a wagon wheel struck a black stone half-buried in the road. It cracked with a sound like breaking glass. Zenvon stopped the wagon to investigate and glimpsed the shadow of a tall, broken obelisk stretching across the road, though nothing stood there to cast it. At the same moment, Veyra's wrapped candle gave a single pulse of light without lighting as an ordinary candle would. Zenvon asked whether the others had seen the apparition and questioned Veyra about the flicker. His investigation yielded no explanation. Veyra admitted she did not understand it either, but connected the candle to her mentor's disappearance and his research into fragments of an ancient obelisk. Zenvon inspected what lay beneath the wheel, pocketed the cracked black stone, and recorded that he was carrying it.

After about half a day on the Triboar Trail, near a side road heading south to Phandalin, the party rounded a bend and found two riderless horses sniffing at ransacked belongings. Woods crowded the road, with steep embankments and dense thickets on either side. Suspecting an ambush, Zenvon intended to move past the horses before stopping and scanning the woods. Four goblins emerged before he could complete that plan.

The resulting fight revolved around the wagon and its cover. Zenvon's opening shot missed, and several more attacks from both sides failed to connect. Veyra, terrified and protesting that she was too young, missed with Fire Bolt and then suffered a wound that reduced her to three hit points. Sister Maela healed her back to eight and told her to shelter beneath the wagon. Pip charged forward with enthusiasm, declaring that he was made for this, but initially struggled to land a blow.

Veyra recovered enough to emerge from beneath the wagon, kill a goblin with Magic Missile, and duck back into hiding. Sister Maela's Sacred Flame failed when its target made its saving throw; a goblin subsequently rushed her with a scimitar and left her at one hit point. Zenvon finally broke his run of misses with two-weapon fighting: his dagger missed, but his shortsword hit for four damage plus five Sneak Attack damage, killing a goblin outright. Veyra's next Magic Missile left another enemy barely alive, Sister Maela healed herself to five hit points, and Pip landed a five-damage finishing blow. Zenvon's final shortbow shot dealt eight piercing damage and ended the fight. He celebrated having claimed two kills.

The horses had fled during the fighting, but the party identified them as Gundren's and Sildar's. They were subsequently collected and brought back. Arrows, torn fabric, and discarded contents of Gundren's bags littered the road. The saddlebags had been looted, and an empty leather map case lay nearby. Zenvon's investigation found nothing further, but Pip spotted a trail heading north behind the thickets. A successful Survival check revealed the passage of about a dozen goblins and signs of two human-sized bodies being dragged away. Gundren and Sildar had been taken alive.

Zenvon initially wanted to follow the goblins, unwilling to proceed while his employer was in danger. The party also needed rest after the fight. After asking how long it would take to find the captives and hearing an estimate of evening, Zenvon reconsidered: they would deliver the provisions first, rest, and return to investigate.

Phandalin emerged from the wooded hillside as forty or fifty simple log buildings among the foundations and crumbling walls of a much older settlement. Children played on the town green, residents tended their chores, and a muddy main street climbed toward a ruined manor on the eastern hillside. At Barthen's Provisions, Elmina Barthen received Zenvon's contract, sent her clerks Ander and Thistle to the wagon, and paid the agreed 19 gold pieces.

Elmina immediately asked after Gundren. Zenvon initially said he would arrive later, withholding his suspicions because he did not yet know whom to trust. Sister Maela judged Elmina's concern sincere and began questioning her. Elmina explained that Gundren was a close friend and had been excited about discovering the lost mine of the Phandelver Pact in the nearby hills.

Veyra supplied the history. More than five hundred years earlier, dwarves and gnomes had agreed to share the mineral wealth and magical power of Wave Echo Cave. Human spellcasters joined them and bound that power into the Forge of Spells, where magic items could be crafted. Phandalin prospered until bandits reinforced by evil mercenary wizards attacked the cave. Its defenders fought back, but the magical battle destroyed much of the cavern. Few survived the cave-ins and tremors, and knowledge of the mine's location was lost. Centuries of treasure seekers had failed to find it; Gundren believed he had succeeded.

After this revelation, Sister Maela ended Zenvon's silence and told Elmina about the horses, the empty map case, and the evidence of two people dragged away. When Elmina feared Gundren might be dead, Zenvon said they did not know, and Maela assured her that the captives had been alive when taken. Elmina urgently demanded that they go save him.

Zenvon told Barthen they would rest that night and leave in the morning. The party went to the Stonehill Inn, a newly built roadhouse opposite the town green, where the proprietor welcomed them to take a table and enjoy a beer. Locals watched the newcomers over mugs of ale or cider; two humans and a gnome glanced over before returning to their own business. The party deferred questioning them until later. The session ended with the delivery complete, the goblin trail still waiting, and the unexplained black stone in Zenvon's pocket.
