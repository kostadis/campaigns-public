# Chapter 02 Welcome to Phandalin, Mind the Goblins

Date: 2026-05-29

## Summary
Having reached the Stonehill Inn in the frontier town of Phandalin, the party settled in, weary from the road and heavy with worry for their missing patron, Gundren Rockseeker, and his escort, Sildar Hallwinter. It was the middle of the day, and the large common room held relatively few people. Everyone had heard that a wagon had arrived without Gundren. Locals pretended not to stare while deciding whether the newcomers were brave, dangerous, or merely passing through. Toblen Stonehill, the broad-shouldered owner with sandy hair and tired blue eyes, offered rooms and hot stew, glancing toward the door whenever voices rose.

Toblen remembered Gundren as a good man whose business, along with his brother’s, mattered to the town. Zenvon Forepot refused to treat his disappearance as a death: “Well, yeah, let's not make any assumptions. We still have not lost him. I'm gonna bring him back.” Questions about enemies and fights made Toblen retreat into caution. Trouble in Phandalin, he warned, had a habit of following people home.

Zenvon chose the bar because it seemed the best place to hear conversations and bought the dwarf bartender Elsa a beer. Elsa poured drinks for them both and proudly described her scholar sister in Neverwinter. Veyra thought Professor Orryn Voss, her missing mentor, had known Elsa’s sister. Voss had come to Phandalin shortly before vanishing, investigating black stone fragments believed to belong to a great obelisk connected to minds, memory, and transformation. Veyra’s blue candle reacted to those fragments and certain kinds of psionic magic. Her mentor had warned against assembling them, and Veyra desperately wanted reassurance that his fears had not been madness.

The discussion prompted the party to compare strange experiences from the road. Zenvon recalled seeing the shadow of an obelisk when they struck a rock; Pip Thistlewick had heard a whisper saying “returned”; Sister Maela Dawnforge had felt a brief chill in her holy symbol. Elsa said other scholarly visitors had come asking about old stones. She recommended Daran Edermath, a retired adventurer at a nearby orchard, though she remained convinced her brilliant sister could sort everything out.

The other patrons supplied more immediate warnings. Freda, a slight Gnomish woman constantly worrying loose threads, blamed the Redbrands for Gundren’s disappearance and wanted the gang removed without her neighbors getting hurt. Her accusation remained a local suspicion. She pointed out that the Redbrands bothered everyone except Halia at the Miner's Exchange. Tuck Stonehill, the innkeeper’s nine-year-old son, reported that his friend Carp Alderleaf had discovered a tunnel in the woods and nearly been caught by the red cloaks. He offered to show the party and asked anxiously whether they would bring Gundren back.

Narth approached Sister Maela with concern for Sister Garaele, who had returned limping and pale but would not explain where she had been. He hoped another religious person might gain her confidence. Lanar loudly condemned Town Master Harbin Wester for hiding behind notices and wax seals while goblins, bandits, orcs, and Redbrands made the roads unsafe. Zenvon confirmed the party had already suffered a goblin attack.

When Zenvon returned to Toblen with these reports, the innkeeper lowered his voice and explained his fear: the Redbrands had murdered Thel Dendrar in the street in broad daylight, after which his wife and children vanished. The gang drank at the Sleeping Giant, a dirty, dangerous tap house at the end of Main Street. Toblen described them as tough men who had taken over because nobody stood up to them. Asked why Daran had not intervened, he observed that adventurers usually died or retired.

The next morning, the party visited Daran’s orchard before leaving town. The retired drow had lived for over five centuries and served as a marshal and herald along the distant Dragon Coast. Pip’s proud declaration that communities needed people with good sword arms earned Daran’s enthusiastic approval. Daran identified the Redbrands’ leader as Glasstaff and placed their safe house beneath Tresendar Manor. He also asked the party to investigate Old Owl Well, a ruined Netherese watchtower a couple of days’ march northeast of Phandalin, where someone was digging and undead had driven prospectors away. He offered his Boots of Striding and Springing as a reward.

Once Daran realized Gundren was still missing, he sharply urged the party to stop delaying the rescue. Pip apologized for spending time gathering information. Zenvon considered visiting Halia but instead chose a brief prayer at the Shrine of Luck before heading out. Sister Garaele, the scholarly elf tending the shrine to Tymora, blessed the rescue mission and reserved another job for their return.

The party retraced its route to the goblin ambush site and followed the trail carefully into the woods. Pip initially led, buoyed by Daran’s encouragement, with Zenvon walking beside him to help watch for danger. About ten minutes along the trail, Zenvon avoided falling into a hidden pit. Another ten minutes later, Pip triggered a snare and ended up suspended ten feet above the ground until his companions lowered him safely.

Five miles from the ambush site, the trail reached a hillside cave screened by briars and thickets. A shallow stream flowed out beside a narrow dry approach. Zenvon scouted a concealed lookout on the stream’s eastern side and found two goblins and a goblin boss, bored and inattentive. He returned to report rather than attacking alone. The party crept into position together, securing surprise despite Pip’s eagerness to draw his sword.

Their opening attacks went poorly: Zenvon missed with his shortbow, Pip’s sword failed to connect, and Maela’s Sacred Flame accomplished nothing. Veyra’s Magic Missile struck all three enemies. Zenvon then killed a goblin with a spectacular critical melee strike, and Veyra killed the other guard with Fire Bolt. The boss’s scimitar attacks missed Pip. Maela’s mace dealt a solid blow, Zenvon followed a dagger miss with a strong short sword hit, and Veyra finished the boss with another Fire Bolt.

The bodies yielded no valuables beyond weapons. Zenvon took a scimitar and javelin, and Pip took another javelin. Moving into the cave, Zenvon slipped past three wolves unnoticed. Darkness soon limited the halfling’s scouting: he lacked darkvision, while Maela and Veyra could see without carrying an obvious light. Near a rubble-choked passage blocked by a steep slope, he heard a hiss and discovered a giant coiled poisonous snake close by. His stealth still held. Zenvon began tiptoeing back toward his companions, leaving the encounter unresolved.

### Black Stone Fragments
Mysterious black stones Professor Orryn Voss believed were pieces of a large obelisk. He feared connections to minds, memory, and transformation and warned that nobody should assemble them.

Discussion of the fragments prompts the party to compare their earlier road experiences: Zenvon’s obelisk-shadow vision, Pip’s whisper, and Maela’s chilled holy symbol. No fragment is recovered during the session.

### Boots of Striding and Springing
Magical boots Daran offers as a reward for investigating Old Owl Well and ensuring no threat to Phandalin is developing there.

He says they helped him during his adventuring career but are no longer needed in retirement. They remain a promised reward, not equipment the party has received.

### Snare Trap
A concealed trap encountered about ten minutes after the hidden pit. Pip fails his Dexterity saving throw and is hoisted ten feet above the ground.

His companions lower him safely without another required roll, then continue along the trail.

### Scimitar
A curved blade recovered from the defeated goblins and claimed by Zenvon. The goblin boss used a scimitar for his unsuccessful attacks against Pip.

Zenvon adds and equips the recovered weapon while reviewing how inventory and weapon proficiency work.

### Javelin
Throwing weapons recovered at the lookout. Zenvon and Pip each claim one.

The DM explains that a javelin can be thrown or used to stab in melee. Zenvon can use his despite lacking proficiency, but his displayed attack bonus is only +1.

## Spells
### Sacred Flame
Divine radiance called down by Sister Maela during the opening surprise attack at the lookout. It fails to affect the goblins.

Maela later switches to her mace and deals six damage to the boss.

### Magic Missile
Veyra sends magical darts through the briars during the surprise attack after her companions’ opening attacks fail.

The missiles strike both regular goblins and their boss, with the DM announcing three damage to each. This supplies the party’s first effective damage of the fight.

### Fire Bolt
Veyra switches to Fire Bolt after deciding to conserve her spells. Her first successful bolt deals five damage and kills the remaining regular goblin.

A later Fire Bolt kills the boss after Maela and Zenvon have wounded him, ending the lookout battle.
