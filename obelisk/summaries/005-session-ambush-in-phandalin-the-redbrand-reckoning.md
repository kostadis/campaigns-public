# Chapter 05 Ambush in Phandalin: The Redbrand Reckoning

Date: Jun 17th, 2026

## Summary
The party rode into Phandalin from the north as the last light of evening faded, weary but triumphant, with Sildar Hallwinter safely in tow and recovered Lionshield Coster goods still awaiting return. Most shops were already closed, making the Stonehill Inn the natural destination. Zenvon, disappointed by the absence of a hero’s welcome, soon received one: Toblen Stonehill burst from the doors in relief, and Tuck — Toblen’s young son — threw his arms around Zenvon in a tearful embrace. Pip reminded Zenvon that the rescue had been a team effort, and Zenvon shared the credit. The locals offered grudging respect, but their questions quickly returned to the Redbrands and whether the defeated goblins had any connection to the gang. Elsa poured celebratory ale while declaring that her sister would someday be an even bigger hero. Sildar promised a proper conversation, drink, and payment in the morning, then retired. The party took a long rest.

At breakfast, Sildar welcomed everyone to a table already large enough for them and recommended the flapjacks. His remark about unexpectedly finding honor in a rogue from Neverwinter’s disreputable neighborhoods soured the greeting. He explained that Gundren Rockseeker had been deliberately targeted for his map to Wave Echo Cave. Gundren and his brothers, Tharden and Nundro, claimed to have rediscovered the ancient mine of the Phandelver Pact, whose Forge of Spells had once harnessed its magical power. A bandit assault reinforced by evil mercenary wizards had devastated the cavern, leaving its location lost for centuries. Sildar asserted that Lord Neverember and the Lords’ Alliance held rightful authority over the treasure; Gundren would receive compensation under that authority.

Sildar also sought Iarno Albrek, the wizard he had recommended for Phandalin’s constabulary assignment, and information about the Black Spider, believed to control local bandits and goblins. His account of Iarno exposed the social hierarchy beneath their professional relationship: Sildar considered himself a mentor, but their families did not mix. Meanwhile, Veyra reported that her blue crystal had flared while she recorded the strange elongated-headed goblin and the name “Ruxithid.” Sister Maela rolled her eyes, Pip took note, and Zenvon questioned Veyra before letting her return to her notebook’s circles and arrows.

Pip urged Zenvon to ask for payment before accepting more dangerous work. Sildar paid the outstanding 50 gold pieces for his escort and initially offered another 200 for Iarno’s safe return. Zenvon challenged the appeal to personal loyalty toward Gundren and refused to let several assignments blur into one bargain. After rejected offers and counteroffers, he demanded 100 gold upfront and 200 upon bringing Iarno back, telling Sildar he could find someone else. Sildar handed over 100 gold without verbally confirming the remaining balance, to Pip’s visible delight. Gundren’s rescue and the Black Spider investigation remained additional concerns.

Across the room, a supposed laborer lingered over his porridge while Toblen kept conspicuously clear of him. Zenvon’s investigation identified the stranger as a likely Redbrand associate. He postponed returning the Lionshield goods and followed the man outside. Around a corner, he spotted the laborer and three other red-cloaked ruffians positioned for an ambush. His bold request for an introduction to the man’s friends briefly unsettled the apparent leader, but a second attempt at intimidation prompted the gang to attack.

Sister Maela suffered repeated shortsword wounds and missed her retaliation. Pip struck a nearby ruffian, while Zenvon took a hit, answered with two attacks dealing 7 damage apiece, and withdrew toward his companions. Veyra cast Magic Missile, finishing the badly wounded ruffian and damaging two others. Maela’s injuries worsened, Pip landed another modest blow, and Zenvon followed with an 8-damage attack including Sneak Attack and a second attack recorded at 11 damage. The session ended immediately afterward, with the fight unresolved.

## Scenes
### Return to Phandalin
#### The party arrives in Phandalin with Sildar Hallwinter and heads to the Stonehill Inn for rest and celebration.
- The party arrives from the north in the late evening after clearing Cragmaw Hideout, bringing Sildar and recovered goods belonging to Linene Graywind’s Lionshield Coster.
- Most shops are closed, but the Stonehill Inn remains open for food and lodging.
- Sildar is exhausted and wants to sleep. His promised escort reward is 50 gold pieces.
- The DM reiterates Sildar’s Lords’ Alliance affiliation and its commitment to centralized authority. The faction overview contrasts this with the Harpers’ decentralized outlook, the Emerald Enclave’s forests, the Order of the Gauntlet’s dedication to good, and the Zhentarim’s trade interests. Internal divisions remain possible: “factions have factions.”
- Zenvon complains about the lack of a hero’s welcome: “I was expecting a hero's welcome, but no.”
- Toblen rushes out in surprise and relief at Sildar’s return, inviting the party inside and offering whatever they need.

- Pip reminds Zenvon that the rescue was teamwork. Zenvon agrees and says, “Everybody contributed.”
- Sildar promises payment, a drink, and a proper conversation in the morning before going to bed.
- Elsa, Freda, Narth, and other locals are present. Their welcome includes raised eyebrows as well as recognition of the rescue.
- A grouchy older woman immediately asks whether the party has dealt with the Redbrands. Another local asks whether the goblins were connected to them.
- Zenvon recalls that mentioning Glasstaff had provoked an attack during the earlier encounter and concludes that a Redbrand connection is likely. He reports killing the bugbear that ran the goblins and ambushed travelers.
- Asked about further discoveries, Zenvon mentions Sildar’s account of a strange goblin but postpones further questions until Sildar has rested.
- Elsa serves ale for rescuing one of their own and clearing out the goblin bandits, while insisting her sister will be an even greater hero when she arrives.
- Zenvon thanks the locals for their support and information. The party celebrates and takes a long rest; the table uses the 5.5 recovery option.

### Breakfast, Revelations, and Negotiations at the Stonehill Inn
#### The party joins Sildar Hallwinter for breakfast, where he reveals the history of Wave Echo Cave, tasks them with finding missing persons, and Zenvon negotiates a substantial fee.
- Sildar is already seated at a table with enough room for the party and invites them over. He praises the inn’s food and ale and recommends the flapjacks.
- Zenvon’s Investigation check totals **24**, a natural 20. He notices Toblen’s uneasy reaction to a patron dressed like a laborer and suspects a Redbrand connection.
- The stranger is eating breakfast and watching the party. Toblen moves behind the bar and keeps as far away from him as possible.
- Sildar expresses surprise that a rogue from Neverwinter’s disreputable neighborhoods has proved honorable. Zenvon objects that this did not sound like a compliment.
- Sildar explains that Gundren’s capture was deliberate: someone instructed the goblins to seize him and take his map.
- Gundren, Tharden, and Nundro Rockseeker recently claimed to have found the entrance to Wave Echo Cave. Gundren is the eldest brother.
- Sildar recounts the Phandelver Pact, made 500 years earlier by dwarven and gnomish clans to share a rich mine. Human spellcasters joined them to channel its magical energy into the Forge of Spells, where magical items could be crafted.
- Phandalin prospered alongside the mine until a powerful bandit force, reinforced by evil mercenary wizards, attacked. The defenders’ spell battle destroyed much of the cavern; cave-ins and tremors left few survivors and erased knowledge of its location.
- Centuries of treasure hunting followed. Sildar presents the Lords’ Alliance as the force that will bring order to the resettled frontier town.
- He asserts that the cave’s treasure belongs to Lord Neverember and Neverwinter. Gundren would be compensated for his discovery through a contract subordinate to Alliance interests.
- Sildar believes a mysterious villain followed the Rockseekers to their discovery through a network of goblins and bandit gangs. He asks the party to identify the Black Spider and investigate Gundren’s disappearance.
- He also asks them to find Iarno Albrek, a wizard and old professional contact sent to Phandalin on Lords’ Alliance business who stopped reporting weeks ago.
- Sildar clarifies that Iarno was his agent and that he recommended him for the constabulary assignment. He describes himself as a mentor, while stressing that they did not visit one another’s families.
- Zenvon’s Neverwinter background lets him recognize the class distinction behind that explanation: Sildar regards Iarno as socially beneath him despite their professional relationship.
- Veyra repeatedly murmurs “Ruxithid” while working through her notes about the elongated-headed goblin.

- Sister Maela reacts dismissively; Pip takes note. Zenvon whispers questions to Veyra rather than dismissing her. She insists there is a connection, then resumes her circles, arrows, and page-turning.
- Pip nudges Zenvon to negotiate compensation, reminding him that the party must eat and Sildar still owes them 50 gold. He also points out Sildar’s apparent wealth and Alliance backing.
- Sildar pays the **50 gold pieces** owed for bringing him safely to town and offers **200 gold pieces** for Iarno’s safe return.
- When Sildar invokes personal loyalty toward Gundren, Zenvon asks him to explain that obligation. Sildar retreats to saying the Lords’ Alliance would look favorably on someone who rescued Gundren and brought the Black Spider to justice.
- Zenvon stresses that the original delivery job did not disclose the dangers they have faced. He asks for **500 gold** to bring Gundren back; Sildar calls this highway robbery.

- Zenvon rejects bundling Gundren into the Iarno agreement and demands **100 upfront and 200 upon Iarno’s return**, adding that his original job ended with delivering the goods.

- The stranger remains conspicuously slow over his porridge, giving Zenvon further reason to watch him.

### The Redbrand Ambush
#### After leaving the tavern, Zenvon tails the suspicious laborer and discovers a calculated ambush by Redbrand ruffians, leading to a tense standoff and a street brawl.
- Zenvon follows the stranger discreetly, rolling **11 Stealth**.
- An **Investigation result of 23** reveals the laborer and three other ruffians positioned around a corner for an ambush.
- The man Zenvon followed stands waiting and looking around for the party. Zenvon’s companions are nearby, around the corner.
- Zenvon calls attention to the trap by asking whether the man intends to introduce his friends.
- His first **Intimidation check of 19** makes the man pause and reassess Zenvon’s intentions.
- Zenvon presses the point: “let me bring some of my friends to introduce to you as well”. He calls for his companions.
- After a second intimidation attempt, the leader orders his gang to attack. The DM confirms that their red cloaks identify them as Redbrands and that the gang resents outsiders challenging its control of the town.
- Zenvon rolls **14 initiative**. Four Redbrand opponents take part.
- A ruffian opens by striking Sister Maela with a shortsword for **4 damage**.
- Pip moves to engage the opponent designated ruffian **2**. An attack roll of **18** hits, dealing **6 damage**.
- Another ruffian taunts Zenvon and hits him with a shortsword for **5 damage**.
- Zenvon chooses the attack sequence discussed at the table as allowing him to disengage afterward. He hits with a **dagger for 7 damage**, then a **scimitar for another 7**, leaving the opponent badly wounded.
- Zenvon disengages and runs back toward the group.
- Sister Maela declares, “I'll take care of him.” Her attack misses.
- A ruffian attacks Pip but rolls **15** against his **16 AC**, missing. Another ruffian charges forward.
- Veyra casts **Magic Missile**. One bolt kills the ruffian left with **2 hit points**; two other ruffians each take **4 damage**.
- A Redbrand hits Sister Maela again for **8 damage**, bringing her recorded damage during the fight to **12**. The DM describes her as hurting.
- Pip hits again but deals only **3 damage**.
- The DM tells Zenvon that the surviving enemies are available targets for Sneak Attack because of the melee positioning.
- Zenvon’s next scimitar attack hits with a **19**, dealing **8 total damage**, including the recorded Sneak Attack damage.
- Zenvon then makes a second attack with advantage after discussing weapon choice. The exchange turns to his shortsword, and the DM records **11 damage**.
- The session stops immediately for the DM’s meeting. No final outcome for that target or the battle is recorded; Sister Maela remains badly wounded and the ambush unresolved.

## Locations
### Phandalin
A rough-and-tumble frontier town where the party returns after their adventure in the wilds. They arrive from the north in the late evening, when most shops are closed.

The town’s gratitude for the rescue coexists with anxiety about the Redbrands. Sildar describes Phandalin as falling under Lord Neverember’s protection and presents the Lords’ Alliance as its prospective source of order.

### Stonehill Inn
A local tavern and inn run by Toblen Stonehill. The party celebrates, rests, shares breakfast, and negotiates its next contract here.

Elsa, Tuck, Freda, and Narth are present during the evening welcome. In the morning, Sildar reserves enough table space for the group and recommends pancakes. A suspicious laborer lingers over porridge while Toblen retreats behind the bar to avoid him.

### Wave Echo Cave
A mythical cavern and site of the ancient Phandelver Pact, described by Sildar Hallwinter. Its mineral wealth and magical power supported the Forge of Spells before a bandit attack and catastrophic spell battle 500 years ago destroyed much of the cavern.

Gundren, Tharden, and Nundro Rockseeker claim to have rediscovered its entrance and intend to reopen the mines. Sildar asserts Neverwinter’s ownership of its treasures and promises that Gundren’s discovery would be compensated under that authority.

### Phandalin Streets
The narrow alleys and corners outside the Stonehill Inn where the Redbrands prepare their ambush.

Zenvon spots four waiting opponents while his companions remain nearby around the corner. The confrontation becomes a street fight, and one ruffian is confirmed dead before the session ends.

## NPCs
### Sildar Hallwinter
A rescued Lords’ Alliance agent who pays the party its promised **50 gold pieces** and provides the history of Wave Echo Cave. He seeks Iarno Albrek, Gundren’s rescue, and the identity of the Black Spider.

Sildar is class-conscious: he expresses surprise at Zenvon’s honor and distinguishes his professional mentorship of Iarno from social intimacy. He considers Neverwinter’s authority over the cave’s riches fundamental, while allowing that Gundren should be compensated.

After negotiation, he hands over a further **100 gold upfront** after Zenvon demands **200 more upon Iarno’s return**; the transcript records no verbal acceptance of that remaining balance. Pip’s enthusiastic support makes the bargaining a small victory over Sildar’s condescension.

### Toblen Stonehill
The proprietor of the Stonehill Inn, who greets the party with relief and calls them the heroes of the hour.

His behavior helps expose the suspicious breakfast patron: he gives the man a wary look, avoids him, and moves behind the bar to maintain distance.

### Tuck
Toblen’s young son, overjoyed by Sildar’s rescue. He runs up and hugs Zenvon, repeatedly celebrating that the missing man has been found. Zenvon answers that he has kept his promise.

### Elsa
The Stonehill Inn barkeep who serves celebratory ale for Sildar’s rescue and the defeat of the goblin bandits.

Even while praising the party, she insists that her sister will be an even bigger hero when she arrives.

### Pip Thistlewick
A companion who insists that the whole party share credit for the rescue. He repeatedly encourages Zenvon to negotiate rather than volunteer, pointing out the party’s living expenses, Sildar’s unpaid debt, and the Lords’ Alliance’s apparent wealth.

He is visibly pleased when Zenvon stands up to Sildar. During the ambush, he engages ruffian 2, deals **6 damage** and then **3 damage**, and avoids a blow that fails to beat his **16 AC**.

### Veyra
A meticulous note-taker investigating the elongated-headed goblin and the name “Ruxithid.” After an evening spent rereading by candlelight, she reports that writing about the subject caused her blue crystal to flare.

She insists that something connects the evidence, then returns to making circles and arrows and comparing pages. In combat, her Magic Missile kills a badly wounded ruffian and damages two others.

### Iarno Albrek
A wizard and Lords’ Alliance agent sent to Phandalin weeks ago to establish a constabulary on Sildar’s recommendation. He has stopped sending reports.

Sildar has known him professionally for years and describes himself as a mentor, though their families did not socialize. Iarno’s safe return is the basis of the negotiated payment.

### The Black Spider
A mysterious villain Sildar wants identified, believed to control a network of bandit gangs and goblins around Phandalin and to be connected to the targeting of Gundren and his map.

Sildar says the Lords’ Alliance would look favorably upon someone who brought the Black Spider to justice.

### Redbrand Ruffian
Red-cloaked gang members who attempt to ambush the party after one of their number draws Zenvon away from the inn. Four participate in the street fight.

They regard the town as theirs to control. Their apparent leader briefly hesitates under Zenvon’s intimidation, then orders an attack when Zenvon summons his friends. One ruffian dies to Magic Missile; the confrontation remains unresolved.

### Sister Maela Dawnforge
A companion who reacts dismissively to Veyra’s latest crystal revelation.

During the street fight, she takes shortsword hits for **4** and **8 damage**, suffering **12 recorded damage** in total. Her attempted retaliation misses, leaving her badly wounded at the cliffhanger.
