## Identity
Crabby is a monstrously sized, sentient crab living on the beach near the Tower of Storms [ch 010 / entry]. He speaks fluent Common and was originally a dumb mute crab granted intelligence and speech by Miraal [ch 010 / entry]. He cannot enter Miraal’s cave due to his size [ch 010 / entry]. He is knowledgeable about the local coastline, including the five shipwrecks, the harpies, and the sharks, though he does not know what truly causes the wrecks [ch 010 / entry]. He was referenced as a source regarding whether harpies were responsible for luring ships to their doom, though the DM remarked, "I don’t think Crabby McGee told us that" [ch 011 / 011.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Crabby is cheerful, literal-minded, and devoted to the memory of Miraal, preferring to remember her as she was rather than as a ghost [ch 010 / entry]. He offered to salvage treasure, specifically a magical weapon from the *Star-Crossed Lover*, if the party laid her spirit to rest [ch 010 / entry]. He thanked the party for eliminating the sharks [ch 012 / entry; ch 012 / 012.05]. He asked whether the party would share the shark meat and eagerly accepted Brewbarry’s offer [ch 012 / entry; ch 012 / 012.05].
