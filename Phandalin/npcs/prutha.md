## Identity
Prutha is an orc warrior, also described as half-orc, who was formerly a member of the Talosian cult [ch 046 / entry; ch 048 / entry]. He was initially identified as an enemy combatant before converting to the worship of Lathander after Vukradin’s final appeal and the sight of Valphine, the Drow Cleric, wielding sunlight [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.04]. He serves as a devoted follower of Valphine’s “Church of the Searing Light,” a militant interpretation of Lathanderian faith [ch 047 / 047.02]. In his final observed role, he acted as the leader of a missionary crusade aimed at converting other orc tribes in the Ice Bear Mountains [ch 048 / 048.07; ch 049 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Prutha’s conversion was pragmatic and driven by a recognition of power; he concluded that Lathander held greater power than Talos, citing Valphine’s ability to wield sunlight as proof [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.04]. He adopted the doctrine of convert or die, advocating for the death of those who refused to convert [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.04]. While he claims never to have killed by choice, serving only to avoid death in his past, he fights with brutal efficiency and genuine fervor during battles, screaming invocations to Lathander and his deceased uncle [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.04; ch 046 / entry; ch 046 / 046.02]. He is fiercely loyal to Valphine, whom he views as the “true prophet” [ch 047 / entry]. Despite his religious zeal, Prutha displays pragmatic dishonesty and opportunism; he attempted to rob Vukradin upon first meeting him [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.03] and later attempted to evade a Zone of Truth spell by producing rocks from his pockets while secretly looting a 25-gold candelabra and a bathrobe [ch 048 / 048.05]. He exhibits blunt, deadpan humor, such as noting that Valphine’s divine light “can kill mosquitoes” [ch 048 / 048.02; ch 048 / moment].

## History with the Party
*   Prutha was initially identified as “orc 2” and charged Vukradin during the initial battle against the Talosians [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.03].
*   He attempted to rob Vukradin after the anchorite’s death, striking him for 9 damage when Vukradin refused his demand for money [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.03; ch 043 / moment].
*   Vukradin successfully persuaded Prutha to abandon Talos; Prutha lowered his axe, said “We friend,” and accepted Vukradin’s blessing [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.04; ch 043 / moment].
*   Prutha concluded that Lathander held greater power than Talos, citing Valphine’s status as a Drow wielding sunlight as proof, and declared himself a follower of the Drow Cleric [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.04; ch 043 / moment].
*   He joined the party; Vukradin objected to proposed names like Thrall as inappropriate for a new friend, and Prutha kept his own name [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.04; ch 043 / moment].
*   Prutha revealed his great uncle was his clan’s shaman, murdered by the Talosians who branded him “thunder meat” to break clan resistance [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.04; ch 043 / moment].
*   He claimed to have fought in three skirmishes but never killed by choice, serving only to avoid death [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.04; ch 043 / moment].
*   Prutha performed the dawn rite by blowing his uncle’s bone whistle for the first time since his uncle’s death, weeping during the act [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.04; ch 043 / moment].
*   He proposed hunting down remaining Talosian clerics, advocating for the death of those who refused to convert, a doctrine Valphine endorsed [ch 043 / entry; ch 043 / 043.04; ch 043 / moment].
*   During the battle at the Circle of Thunder, Prutha fought fiercely, hurling a javelin at a paralyzed anchorite for 11 damage and charging with his greataxe for 13 damage [ch 044 / entry; ch 044 / 044.02].
*   After the battle, Prutha destroyed the Talosian holy symbols found on the fallen anchorites, overriding Vukradin’s desire to return them and Valphine’s desire to study them [ch 044 / entry; ch 044 / 044.03; ch 044 / moment].
*   He acknowledged the death of the anchorites (who were young half-orcs) to Valphine, noting his uncle’s teaching that dawn comes for everyone, even “these ones” [ch 044 / entry; ch 044 / 044.03; ch 044 / moment].
*   Prutha accompanied the party into the caves surrounding the henge, following Brewbarry’s lead in search of alcohol or supplies [ch 044 / 044.04].
*   At Falcon’s Hunting Lodge, Prutha recognized the banners as belonging to the Sun and Stone clan, his own family, and approached the gate alone [ch 045 / entry; ch 045 / 045.03; ch 045 / moment].
*   He performed the dawn-bow and blew the bone whistle dawn prayer, securing safe passage for the entire party into the lodge [ch 045 / entry; ch 045 / 045.03; ch 045 / moment].
*   The party speculated that a note referencing “the boy from the East” might refer to Prutha, though this was not confirmed [ch 045 / 045.04].

[ch 048 / 048.05]

[ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.07]

## Last Observed State
Prutha is currently absent from the party. He departed from the group at Icespire Hold in Chapter 49 to lead five orc followers in a missionary campaign to convert Vorga’s orcs to Valphine’s specific interpretation of Lathander worship [ch 049 / entry; ch 049 / 049.01]. In Chapter 50, the DM notes that Prutha is no longer with the group and is on this crusade, warning ominously that his actions “will have consequences” [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.01]. His specific physical condition or location beyond Icespire Hold is not established in the summaries.

*   **Vorga:** Prutha departed to convert the orcs of Vorga’s tribe, who were located at Icespire Hold [ch 049 / 049.01].
