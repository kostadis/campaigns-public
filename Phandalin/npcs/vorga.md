## Identity
Vorga is the acting war chief of the Eastern Heart band, a displaced orc tribe currently occupying Falcon’s Hunting Lodge before relocating to Icespire Hold [ch 045 / entry]. She holds full operational authority over the tribe following the death of the previous war chief [ch 045 / entry]. She is a middle-aged female orc characterized by gray hair at the temples, a broken nose, and an old scar across her jaw, wearing Falcon’s stolen hunter cloak over orc-style leather [ch 045 / entry]. While she speaks flawless Orcish, the party noted that she speaks Common with a perceived speech impediment, though in-universe explanations suggest orcs are highly intelligent and do not suffer from such impediments [ch 045 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Vorga is pragmatic and militarily minded, prioritizing the survival of her non-combatants above all else [ch 045 / entry]. She refuses to expose her people to open-field risks when fortified positions are available, reasoning that non-combatants cannot outrun pursuing forces [ch 045 / 045.04]. Her strategic approach is concise and direct, summarized by her stance: "Cult summon creature today. We kill creature today. We safely go to Icespire" [ch 045 / entry; ch 045 / 045.04]. She is driven primarily by the desire to return her tribe to their ancestral home, showing only mild interest in plunder compared to the goal of resettlement [ch 048 / entry]. She grew tired of the Falcon’s enthusiasm and reminded the party of their promise to escort her tribe home [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.06]. She used a war cry that granted her allies advantage on attacks [ch 046 / entry; ch 046 / 046.04].

## History with the Party
- Vorga Long-Memory is the acting war chief of the Eastern Heart band, a displaced orc tribe squatting at Falcon’s Hunting Lodge, holding full operational authority after the previous war chief’s death [ch 045 / entry].
- When Valphine warned Vorga of an imminent Talosian attack using henge correspondence and maps as proof, Vorga accepted the alliance but refused to move her non-combatants into the open woods, citing their inability to outrun the threat [ch 045 / 045.04].
- Vorga’s posture shifted entirely upon learning the party had killed the dragon Cryovain, acknowledging that their ancestral home at Icespire Hold was now free [ch 045 / 045.04; ch 045 / moment].
- She chose to defend the lodge using its fortified palisade rather than risk her people in the open, stating her strategy: "Cult summon creature today. We kill creature today. We safely go to Icespire." [ch 045 / 045.04].
- During the defense of the lodge, Vorga used a powerful war cry ability that granted advantage on attacks to allies within 30 feet, including Falcon, Soma, and Brewbarry [ch 046 / entry; ch 046 / 046.04].
- This war cry proved pivotal, allowing Brewbarry to cleave through enemies and enabling Falcon to land a decisive three-strike charge on an Anchorite [ch 046 / entry; ch 046 / 046.05].
- Following the battle, Vorga declared the party honorary members of her tribe, with her warriors echoing the proclamation with cheers [ch 046 / entry; ch 046 / 046.06].
- When the party returned to the lodge, Vorga expressed weariness with the Falcon’s enthusiasm and reminded the party of their prior offer to escort her tribe back to Icespire Hold [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.06].
- Vorga showed mild interest in plunder but prioritized returning home [ch 048 / entry].
- Vukradin fulfilled his escort obligation, delivering Vorga and her tribe to Icespire Hold, which now serves as their base [ch 049 / entry; ch 049 / 049.01].

## Last Observed State
Vorga and her tribe were delivered to Icespire Hold, which now serves as their base [ch 049 / entry; ch 049 / 049.01]. This delivery fulfilled the party’s escort obligation, closing that specific plot thread [ch 049 / entry]. At the time of this relocation, Prutha and his five orc converts part ways with the party to join Vorga’s group at Icespire Hold, with Prutha intending to convert the orcs to Valphine’s version of Lathander worship [ch 049 / 049.01]. Prior to this, in Chapter 048, Vorga was observed at Falcon’s Hunting Lodge, expressing weariness with the Falcon’s antics and reminding the party of their promise to escort her tribe [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.06].

## Relationships
**The Party:** Vorga views the party as allies who earned the tribe's respect through combat prowess and shared objectives. After the defense of the lodge, she declared the party honorary members of her tribe, with her warriors echoing the proclamation [ch 046 / entry; ch 046 / 046.06]. She holds the party to their agreements, explicitly reminding them of their offer to escort the tribe when they returned to the lodge [ch 048 / 048.06].

**Vukradin:** Vorga’s primary relationship with the party is anchored by Vukradin, who fulfilled the final escort obligation to deliver her to Icespire Hold [ch 049 / entry; ch 049 / 049.01].

**The Falcon:** Vorga wears the Falcon’s stolen hunter cloak [ch 045 / entry]. She showed clear irritation at his enthusiasm during the party's return to the lodge, rolling her eyes at his behavior [ch 048 / entry]. However, she cooperated with him defensively, agreeing to signal his arrival to prevent friendly fire [ch 045 / 045.04].

**Valphine:** Valphine served as the primary diplomat, speaking to Vorga in fluent orcish to establish contact and verify intelligence regarding the Talosian threat [ch 045 / 045.04]. Valphine opened the conversation with Vorga in orcish and warned her of the Talosian attack [ch 045 / 045.04].

**Prutha:** Prutha is an orc warrior who fights alongside the party and the Eastern Heart band [ch 046 / 046.03]. He is intended to convert Vorga’s orcs to Lathander worship upon their arrival at Icespire Hold [ch 049 / 049.01].
