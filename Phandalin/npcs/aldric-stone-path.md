## Identity
Aldric Stone Path is a weathered human described by the GM as being in his early forties [ch 041 / entry; ch 041 / 041.04]. He claims to have spent fifty years mapping the mountains and to know the historical appearance of every valley [ch 041 / entry; ch 041 / 041.04]. The source notes a contradiction between his stated fifty years of experience and his described age of early forties, preserved as an apparent table error [ch 041 / entry; ch 041 / 041.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Aldric supported Adabra because she was the first person willing to listen to and ask about what he had seen, including orc incursions and disturbances in nature [ch 041 / 041.04; ch 041 / entry]. He said, “I’ve been told in order that nothing can be done, and nothing needs to be done.” [ch 041 / 041.04]. However, when Soma pressed him on the consequences of Adabra’s philosophy, he stopped speaking [ch 041 / 041.04].

## History with the Party
- Aldric Stone Path was a visible supporter of Adabra alongside Falcon during the council debate, after Vukradin had departed [ch 041 / 041.04].
- He was identified by the GM as a weathered human in his early forties who claimed to have spent fifty years mapping the mountains; this contradiction between his age and experience is noted as an apparent table error [ch 041 / entry; ch 041 / 041.04].
- Aldric explained that his support for Adabra stemmed from her being the first person to listen to his observations regarding orc incursions, disturbances in nature, and the mountains’ decline [ch 041 / entry; ch 041 / 041.04].
- Soma confronted Aldric regarding how Adabra’s philosophy of withdrawal would address the threats he had identified, forcing him to realize her proposed solution offered no protection [ch 041 / 041.04].
- When confronted with the lack of remedy for the dangers he cited, Aldric faltered and stopped speaking, causing his support for Adabra to collapse [ch 041 / entry; ch 041 / 041.04].

## Last Observed State
Aldric was last seen present and acting during the Druid Council Debate in Chapter 041 [ch 041 / 041.04]. When confronted by Soma regarding the lack of protection offered by Adabra’s policy of withdrawal, he faltered and stopped speaking [ch 041 / entry; ch 041 / 041.04]. His public support for Adabra collapsed during this interaction [ch 041 / entry; ch 041 / 041.04]. The evidence does not state his location or status after he stopped speaking. Status not established in the summaries beyond his silent collapse of support during the debate.

## Relationships
*   **Adabra**: Aldric acted as one of only two visible supporters of Adabra during the council debate [ch 041 / 041.04]. His support was based on her willingness to listen to his findings [ch 041 / 041.04], but this alliance dissolved when he realized her solution offered no remedy for the dangers he had identified [ch 041 / 041.04].
*   **Soma**: Soma directly confronted Aldric, forcing him to acknowledge that Adabra’s philosophy allowed threats to proceed unchecked [ch 041 / 041.04]. This confrontation caused Aldric to cease speaking [ch 041 / 041.04].
*   **Falcon**: Both Aldric and Falcon were identified as the remaining visible supporters of Adabra once Vukradin had departed [ch 041 / 041.04].
