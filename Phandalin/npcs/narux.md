## Identity
Narux was a young half-orc Anchorite of Talos. [ch 044 / entry] He bore elaborate storm-themed markings, specifically crossed boar-tusks tattooed down both forearms. [ch 044 / entry] Narux was one of two anchorites who led a summoning ritual at the Circle of Thunder. [ch 044 / entry]

## Personality and Motivations
Narux served the Storm God, leading summoning rituals. [ch 044 / entry] His personal notes, written in Orcish, include the annotations: “feed before unleashing” and “the leash holds only if the stones sing.” [ch 044 / 044.05] He carried a club, hide armor, and a holy symbol of Talos during the confrontation. [ch 044 / entry]

## History with the Party
*   Narux was one of two young half-orc Anchorites of Talos who led a summoning ritual at the Circle of Thunder; he wore crossed boar-tusks tattooed down both forearms. [ch 044 / entry]
*   Narux was paralyzed by Hold Person and slain by the party during the confrontation at the Circle of Thunder. [ch 044 / entry]
*   Narux carried a club, hide armor, and a holy symbol of Talos when slain. [ch 044 / entry]
*   The party explored Narux’s quarters (Cave 3/C3), discovering a buried +1 Shield, a stack of crude vellum sketches of Gorthok annotated with notes such as “feed before unleashing” and “the leash holds only if the stones sing,” and a torn map fragment of Neverwinter Wood. [ch 044 / entry; ch 044 / 044.05]
*   The torn map found in Narux’s quarters indicated that the Circle of Thunder was a sacrificed site, the Woodland Manse was the active backup ritual site, and Falcon’s Hunting Lodge was the target endpoint for the rituals. [ch 044 / 044.05]
*   The party utilized evidence recovered from Narux’s quarters, including his marked map and sketches of Gorthok, to warn Vorga Long-Memory and the Eastern Heart orcs of the imminent Talosian threat at the Hunting Lodge. [ch 045 / 045.02; ch 045 / 045.04]

## Last Observed State
Narux was killed during the confrontation at the Circle of Thunder. [ch 044 / entry] He was paralyzed by Hold Person and subsequently slain by the party. [ch 044 / entry] In the aftermath, the party explored his quarters (Cave 3/C3) at the henge, recovering a buried +1 Shield, crude vellum sketches of Gorthok, and a torn map fragment of Neverwinter Wood. [ch 044 / entry; ch 044 / 044.05] The map indicated the Circle of Thunder was a site intended to be sacrificed, the Woodland Manse was a backup ritual site, and Falcon’s Hunting Lodge was the target endpoint. [ch 044 / 044.05] The party utilized these recovered items in subsequent negotiations with Vorga Long-Memory and the Eastern Heart orcs. [ch 045 / 045.02; ch 045 / 045.04]

## Relationships
