## Identity
Ser Kaelen Thorn (also referred to as Ser Kaelen or Kaelen) is a knight and contact for the Order of the Gauntlet, based primarily in Phandalin [ch 008 / 008.08; ch 013 / entry]. He acts as a liaison between the Order and the party, providing intelligence, logistical support, and political context [ch 013 / entry; ch 026 / 026.06]. He presents himself as a man of “sword and action” with limited expertise in esoteric fields like druidic ritual or interplanar theory, though he maintains a network of contacts, including researchers at Candlekeep [ch 040 / entry; ch 026 / 026.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Kaelen is pragmatic, cautious, and politically conservative. He prioritizes the stability of existing power structures, warning the party against revolutionary actions or direct challenges to Lord Neverember’s authority to avoid military intervention [ch 019 / 019.04]. He values the reputation and influence of the Order of the Gauntlet, often framing requests (such as statue delivery or credit for identifying magic items) in terms of gaining upper-class influence or demonstrating the Order’s commitment [ch 026 / 026.06; ch 034 / 034.03]. He is candid about his limitations, admitting he has “little intelligence” on certain threats or is skeptical of claims that do not fit known patterns, such as Talos clerics illuminating hearts [ch 008 / 008.13; ch 013 / 013.05]. While generally cooperative, he maintains professional boundaries; he declined to personally deliver unofficial supplies like cheese but conceded when persuaded by the party’s practical arguments [ch 032 / 032.07]. He displays discomfort with unconventional religious practices, such as Valphine’s method of Lathander worship, and expresses visible unease at the concept of a Drow blessed by Lathander [ch 027 / 027.06; ch 049 / entry].

## History with the Party
*   **Chapter 008:** Ser Kaelen Thorn introduced himself to Vukradin at the Stonehill Inn, recruiting him into the Order of the Gauntlet not with money but with the promise of reach for his music [ch 008 / 008.08]. He offered membership to Valphine, who privately rejected the fit, and explicitly ruled out recruiting Soma due to doctrinal disagreements regarding her naturalist beliefs [ch 008 / 008.09]. To Brewbarry, Kaelen neither denied nor apologized for the Order’s war against the barbarian tribes, but reframed it as defense and bought the goliath a drink to diffuse tension [ch 008 / 008.10]. After Vukradin accepted membership, Kaelen stated the Order would reach out when needed and “try to do its best” if the bard required help, invoking a sense of brotherhood [ch 008 / 008.11]. When the party proposed destroying the lighthouse, Kaelen was candid that the Order had little intelligence on the curse, offering three possibilities: it required a cleric, it was haunted by a monster (his theory), or the rumors were false [ch 008 / 008.13].
*   **Chapter 009:** Kaelen remained stationed in Phandalin and expressed pleasure that Vukradin was performing small good deeds, stating, “Bringing you into the fold was the right thing to do” [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.05]. Embarrassed by the Order’s current chant, he shouted its lyrics to Vukradin and confirmed that the Order fields magic users, urging the bard to write better music for them [ch 009 / 009.05].
*   **Chapter 013:** Upon the party’s return from the Tower of Storms, Kaelen denied betraying them regarding the ambush, and the GM confirmed he was telling the truth [ch 013 / 013.05]. He clarified that the mission’s details had been public knowledge in Phandalin due to the party’s open discussions [ch 013 / 013.05]. Kaelen reacted with alarm to the Talos cleric’s ability to illuminate his own heart, noting that typical shamans of Talos could not perform such a feat [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.05]. He explained that the Emerald Enclave, to which Adabra belonged, was an ally against existential threats like Tiamat but disagreed with the Order on the philosophy of balance versus active good [ch 013 / 013.05]. Kaelen admitted he remained in Phandalin partly to gather intelligence on the Carver, hoping the party could act as reconnaissance before the Order intervened [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.05]. He dismissed the Whispering Grove blight as a lower priority compared to dragons and bandits, and was skeptical of Adabra’s claims of interplanar interference [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / 013.05]. He promised to investigate the recovered shipwreck treasures and boat names if the party returned in a few days [ch 013 / 013.05].
*   **Chapter 014:** Vukradin remained suspicious of Kaelen, noting that a mysterious letter he received did not help clear the knight’s name, though he still sought alliances with various factions [ch 014 / entry; ch 014 / 014.01].
*   **Chapter 016:** Kaelen approached Vukradin to ask about his work, receiving a wary reception due to lingering distrust [ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / 016.12]. He was unable to provide information on the rightful owners of the shipwreck treasures, sighing about the difficulty of the task [ch 016 / 016.12]. He reviewed Vukradin’s revised musical commission, calling it an improvement but stating it still failed to capture the Order’s spirit, and asked the bard to keep working [ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / 016.12].

## Last Observed State
Kaelen was mentioned but did not appear in the final recorded chapter (Chapter 051). Vukradin recalled that Kaelen had warned the party to “stay away” from House Margaster, but the GM clarified this is Vukradin’s recollection; no campaign record confirms Kaelen issued such a warning, and the documented warning regarding the Neverwinter Commission came from Lord Cassian [ch 051 / 051.04]. In the same chapter, Cullen Sharpe claimed Kaelen reached out to House Margaster to connect the party’s finds with their real proprietors. The GM clarified this claim is likely spin; canon states Kaelen matched the necklace claim in good faith via a source he did not know was compromised by Margaster, and did not knowingly contact the house [ch 051 / 051.08]. Status not established in the summaries for any events following Chapter 049.

## Relationships
*   **Vukradin:** Kaelen recruited Vukradin into the Order, promising reach for his music [ch 008 / 008.08]. He views Vukradin’s membership as full-fledged, while Vukradin views it as friendship [ch 019 / 019.12]. Kaelen often acts as a patron or handler, assigning tasks and providing intelligence, while Vukradin regards him as closer to trustworthy than most contacts but does not extend full trust [ch 039 / entry].
*   **Order of the Gauntlet:** Kaelen is a devoted member who enforces its protocols and seeks to enhance its political capital [ch 008 / 008.11; ch 027 / 027.06]. He utilizes the Order’s resources, such as Candlekeep archives, to assist the party while ensuring the Order receives credit [ch 026 / 026.06; ch 034 / 034.03].
*   **The Party:** He maintains a working alliance with Brewbarry, Valphine, and Soma. He explicitly ruled out recruiting Soma due to doctrinal differences [ch 008 / 008.09]. He questioned Valphine’s religious practices and advised her to consult Brother Aldric about her golden eyes, expressing discomfort at her condition’s implications [ch 027 / 027.06; ch 049 / entry].
*   **Adabra (Emerald Enclave):** Kaelen views Adabra as misguided rather than evil, understanding her political maneuvering against the party as a reaction to witnessed collateral damage rather than malice [ch 040 / entry].
*   **House Margaster:** While Cullen Sharpe alleges Kaelen contacted House Margaster, the GM confirms Kaelen acted in good faith through an unwittingly compromised channel [ch 051 / 051.08].
*   **House Neverember:** Kaelen advises strict adherence to Lord Neverember’s authority, suggesting puppet figures like Townmaster Harbin be retained to maintain political stability [ch 019 / 019.04].
