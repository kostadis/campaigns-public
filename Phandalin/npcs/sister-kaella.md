## Identity
Sister Kaella is an agent of the criminal gang leader known as the Carver, serving specifically as his Spymaster and head of his network in Phandalin [ch 017 / 017.03; ch 018 / entry; ch 018 / 018.03]. In ch 019 she asserts that Valphine’s Lathander devotion is a “front” for being a cleric of Lolth [ch 019 / 019.10].

## Personality and Motivations
Kaella is driven primarily by self-preservation and a desire to displace her employer, stating she has “more important business than working for the Carver” and that “The Carver’s gang needs new management” [ch 019 / 019.10]. Her motivation to betray the Carver stems from fear; he has blamed her for intelligence failures, and she believes eliminating him will increase her own life expectancy [ch 019 / 019.10; ch 037 / 037.03]. She is pragmatic, manipulative, and intellectually arrogant, dismissing the party members as naive, simple-minded, or obsessed, while misidentifying Valphine as a cleric of Lolth [ch 019 / 019.10]. Kaella operates on a transactional basis, offering gold and information to achieve her goals, though her plans often rely on others doing the dirty work for her [ch 019 / 019.10; ch 037 / 037.03].
