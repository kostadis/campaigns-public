## Identity
Jenna Roscoe is a Lord’s Alliance agent based in Neverwinter and a member of the Phandalin council [ch 049 / entry]. She was identified by Spiderman by her insignia and, in his words, their stench [ch 019 / entry]. She presents herself as refined, unassuming, and forgettable, with auburn hair and practical attire [ch 019 / entry]. She named Neverwinter high-society figures associated with the Kraken Society and offered the Alliance’s help to the party [ch 026 / entry; ch 042 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Jenna is pragmatic and focused on the cleanliness of economic flows, arguing that adventuring treasure is stolen from the “people” collectively and becomes “clean money” when redistributed through purchases or community funding [ch 019 / 019.14; ch 020 / 020.02]. She views mind-laundering of wealth via institutions like the Temple of Lathander as morally acceptable [ch 020 / 020.02]. She displays open contempt for those she deems uncivilized, such as Brewbarry, whom she refers to as “Uthgardt” and deliberately mispronounces [ch 019 / moment; ch 042 / 042.02]. She attempts to leverage the Alliance’s resources to secure favorable outcomes for the party, offering connections to nobility in Waterdeep or assistance in investigations [ch 019 / 019.13; ch 042 / 042.02]. She is horrified by populist movements, viewing the Universal Basic Treasure (UBT) proposal as mob rule and a threat to established financial dialectics [ch 049 / entry; ch 049 / 049.03].
