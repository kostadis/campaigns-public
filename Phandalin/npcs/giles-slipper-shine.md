## Identity
Giles Slipper-Shine is a pale, sunburned villager of Phandalin with restless hands [ch 020 / entry; ch 020 / 020.02]. He is an ardent devotee of Lathander who specifically believes in the doctrine of the sun’s "purifying pain" [ch 020 / entry]. He serves as an acolyte and laborer for the Temple of Lathander’s Searing Pain of Justice, formerly Tresendar Manor [ch 020 / entry; ch 026 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Giles is characterized by religious zealotry and physical discomfort. He is visibly excited when encountering figures who align with his extreme interpretation of Lathander’s faith, believing that "Lathander’s glory is in feeling his pain while it sears you and brings you purity" [ch 020 / 020.02]. He and his associate Brin Bundlewine embraced rumors of a drow worshiper of Lathander, operating under the belief that only a drow could truly comprehend this doctrine through the direct experience of the sun’s pain [ch 020 / entry; ch 020 / 020.02].

He is motivated by the establishment and renovation of a physical space for this worship. He agreed to provide free labor for the temple’s founding in exchange for materials funding (twenty-five gold pieces for beams and furniture) [ch 020 / entry; ch 020 / 020.02]. Giles was ecstatic about the church’s progress and Valphine’s sermon [ch 026 / entry; ch 026 / 026.07]. He was noted as looking sunburnt, which Soma described as "Kissed by Lathander" [ch 020 / entry; ch 049 / 049.03].
