## Identity
Ostren Vayle is a high-end armorer operating in the Protector’s Enclave of Neverwinter [ch 049 / entry; ch 052 / entry]. He owns a shop named ‘Dragon Scales R Us’ [ch 052 / entry]. He is a faithful follower of the Morning Lord (Lathander), described by the DM as "one of the devout" [ch 052 / 052.01; ch 052 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Ostren is deeply impressed by displays of divine favor, specifically reacting to Valphine’s status as a chosen of Lathander [ch 049 / entry]. He holds and expresses racist views against Drow, celebrating what he perceives as the "justice of Lathander’s warmth" [ch 049 / entry].

His primary motivation in his interactions with the party appears to be religious merit and professional pride rather than monetary gain. He sought a blessing from Lathander as payment for his work instead of currency [ch 052 / 052.01; ch 052 / entry]. He is described as "very proud of himself" and his craft [ch 052 / entry; ch 052 / 052.01]. The DM clarified that he was not advertising, and Valphine remarked that it was "a great advertisement for his business" [ch 052 / 052.01].

## Last Observed State
Ostren was last observed at the Spire of the Morninglord in Neverwinter during the dawn service attended by the party [ch 052 / 052.01]. He appeared at the temple doors having worked through the entire night to finish Valphine’s gleaming white dragon scale armor [ch 052 / 052.01; ch 052 / entry].

He personally fitted the armor onto Valphine at the threshold of the temple, causing a stir among the gathered crowd [ch 052 / 052.01]. After the service concluded, Valphine visibly blessed him in front of the crowd, fulfilling his request for payment [ch 052 / entry]. Status not established in the summaries beyond his presence at the temple.
