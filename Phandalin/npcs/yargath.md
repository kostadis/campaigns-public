## Identity
Yargath is an Anchorite of Talos who physically manifests his divine allegiance through supernatural elongated claws [ch 042 / entry; ch 042 / 042.08]. He commanded a patrol of ten orcs encountered by the party on the ridge opposite the Circle of Thunder gully [ch 042 / 042.08].

## Personality and Motivations
Yargath is a fervent Talosian cleric who responds to evangelism by Lathander with immediate rage rather than conversion [ch 042 / 042.08; ch 042 / entry]. After Valphine cast Daylight, he screamed “Kill the false cleric of Lathander!” and denounced her as an abomination [ch 042 / 042.08; ch 042 / moment].

A half-burned vellum scrap found in his firepit, signed only “K.,” read: “dawn cannot rise where the storm already lives” [ch 044 / 044.04].

## History with the Party
- Yargath led a patrol of ten orcs and was encountered by the party on the ridge opposite the Circle of Thunder gully [ch 042 / 042.08].
- Vukradin used Universal Speech on Yargath to preach about Lathander, which enraged Yargath rather than converting him [ch 042 / 042.08].
- After Valphine cast Daylight, Yargath screamed for the party to kill her, denouncing her as a false cleric and an abomination, which triggered combat among the hostile orcs while three others remained hesitant [ch 042 / 042.08; ch 042 / moment].
- The party discovered a half-burned vellum scrap signed “K.” from Yargath’s firepit in a cave near the henge, which contained the phrase “dawn cannot rise where the storm already lives” [ch 044 / 044.04].
- The party reviewed the intel from Yargath’s firepit alongside letters from Flenz and sketches from Narux to determine that multiple Talosian summoning rituals were being attempted across the region [ch 045 / 045.02].

## Last Observed State
Yargath’s last direct interaction with the party occurred during the initial confrontation on the ridge, where he ordered his hostile orcs to attack while three others remained hesitant [ch 042 / 042.08; ch 042 / entry]. The outcome of that battle is not recorded in the evidence. Subsequently, his personal firepit was discovered in a cave near the henge during the party’s exploration in Chapter 044 [ch 044 / 044.04]. The firepit contained a half-burned vellum scrap signed “K.” [ch 044 / 044.04]. In Chapter 045, the party reviewed intel from his firepit alongside other documents to confirm multiple Talosian summoning rituals [ch 045 / 045.02]. Status not established in the summaries.
