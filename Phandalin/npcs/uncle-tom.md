## Identity
Uncle Tom is the landlord of a high-efficiency, single-room-occupancy-style building in the Bluelake District [ch 054 / 054.04; ch 054 / entry]. He is a man of business who prioritizes financial gain over ideological alignment, stating, “money’s money, man, I don’t care who’s got it” [ch 054 / 054.04; ch 054 / moment]. While he insists he runs an “equal opportunity housing establishment,” this stance is rooted in apathy rather than progressive ideals [ch 054 / 054.04; ch 054 / moment]. He maintains minimal oversight of his tenants, explicitly noting that he does not scry on his customers [ch 054 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Uncle Tom is driven by pragmatic greed and a desire to avoid conflict with dangerous individuals. Initially confused by the ancestries of his visitors, he displayed ignorance regarding common races, asking Soma if Tortles lived inside their shells and noting Valphine’s Drow heritage as if it were a novelty [ch 054 / 054.04; ch 054 / moment]. However, his demeanor shifted to nervous conciliation immediately after recognizing the party’s heavy weaponry and magical items, such as Valphine’s white armor and Soma’s Staff of Meril [ch 054 / 054.04; ch 054 / moment]. To diffuse potential hostility, he offered them a discount on rooms [ch 054 / 054.04]. He earnestly asked Soma what made a good Tortle room, and she recommended a hearth, plenty of water, and proximity to water [ch 054 / 054.04].

## History with the Party
- Uncle Tom is the landlord of the high-efficiency building in the Bluelake District where Denvar resides [ch 054 / 054.04].
- He greeted Soma, Valphine, and Brewbarry when they arrived to investigate his tenant, initially displaying confusion about their ancestries [ch 054 / 054.04].
- He stated that he runs an equal-opportunity establishment because "money’s money," though he became nervous and conciliatory after recognizing the party’s weaponry and magical items [ch 054 / 054.04; ch 054 / moment].
- He provided the party with Denvar’s name and described him as a quiet, dependable, and unobtrusive tenant who was currently behind on rent [ch 054 / 054.04].
- He offered the party a discount on a room, though they declined as they were only seeking information [ch 054 / 054.04].
- He asked Soma for sincere advice on how to design rooms suitable for Tortles, accepting her recommendation to include a hearth, water sources, and proximity to a body of water [ch 054 / 054.04].
