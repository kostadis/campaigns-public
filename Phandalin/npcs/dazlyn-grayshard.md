## Identity
Dazlyn Grayshard is a Shield Dwarf who works as a prospector and amateur archaeologist alongside Norbus Ironrune [ch 002 / 002.06; ch 003 / 003.09]. He presents himself as a serious academic excavating an ancient temple of Abbathor, though the party’s high insight checks revealed that his expertise is largely feigned; he and Norbus only adopted the title of "archaeologist" a few days prior to the party’s arrival [ch 003 / 003.09; ch 003 / entry]. He operates out of a camp at the excavation site in the foothills of the Sword Mountains [ch 002 / 002.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Dazlyn is defensive regarding the secrecy of his operations, insisting early on that "everything that we’re doing, we’re not sharing" [ch 002 / entry]. He took mild offense when Valphine compared Abbathor to Drow deities, noting that "Dwarven gods are not like your drow gods" [ch 002 / 002.06; ch 002 / 002.07]. During the ochre jelly fight he called out "Is everybody okay there?" and later asked the party, "to the extent possible," not to damage any of the artwork [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / moment; ch 003 / 003.02]. He watched from hiding after the party’s fight with the orcs, weighing his options about the gem, and the party split the found gemstones with the dwarves [ch 004 / entry; ch 003 / 003.04]. He is also a discerning audience for music, preferring popular local ditties over Vukradin’s original compositions [ch 004 / 004.02; ch 004 / entry].

## Last Observed State
As of Chapter 004, Dazlyn is alive and well at the excavation site camp [ch 004 / 004.02]. He had been hiding and watching the party from a distance after their return from fighting orcs, weighing whether to confront them or flee due to their possession of a gem [ch 004 / 004.02; ch 004 / entry]. Seeing the party alive, he emerged and led with the question, "You’re not here for the gem, right?" and subsequently provided the party with a meal and a fire pit for the night [ch 004 / 004.02; ch 004 / entry]. He took part in listening to Vukradin’s flute performance, which he found unimpressive compared to local standards [ch 004 / 004.02; ch 004 / entry].

## Relationships
*   **Norbus Ironrune:** Dazlyn’s partner in excavation. At one point Dazlyn thumped Norbus into rescinding an offer to tell stories, declaring that the party’s bard would tell them instead [ch 003 / 003.03; ch 003 / 003.05; ch 003 / moment]. They share a professional interest in the site but also engage in joint deception regarding their expertise [ch 003 / 003.09].
*   **Valphine:** Dazlyn confirmed the accuracy of Valphine’s lore regarding Abbathor but bristled at her comparison of Dwarven gods to Drow deities [ch 002 / 002.06; ch 002 / 002.07]. He later privately confirmed the gemstone split with her [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.04; ch 003 / 003.09].
*   **Vukradin:** Dazlyn hired Vukradin and his party to clear the ruins. He declared that the party’s bard would tell the stories, overriding Norbus [ch 003 / 003.03; ch 003 / 003.05; ch 003 / moment]. However, he was unimpressed by Vukradin’s original musical compositions during the campfire scene [ch 004 / 004.02; ch 004 / entry].
