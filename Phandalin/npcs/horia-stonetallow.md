## Identity
Horia Stonetallow is a shield dwarf and the leader of a five-dwarf mining group from the Stonetallow clan [ch 033 / entry; ch 034 / entry]. She serves as the manager of the Mountain’s Toe Gold Mine, overseeing operations, finances, and personnel [ch 048 / entry]. Horia leads a lineage that traces its ancestry to a disgraced Gauntlgrym engineer, the only one of his peers who knew how to build a proper archway [ch 034 / entry; ch 034 / 034.01]. The clan name “Stonetallow” is a contraction of “stone to allow,” referring to allowing the arch to pass, a fact Horia explains with great pride and enthusiasm [ch 034 / 034.01; ch 034 / moment]. She asserts a longstanding contractual connection to the mine, possessing a contract signed by Don-Jon Raskin authorizing the dwarven clan to work the mine [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Horia is a proud, professional, and rigidly honest businesswoman who values proper procedure and exact workmanship [ch 034 / entry; ch 040 / entry]. She believes that only dwarves understand mining, and specifically that the Stonetallow clan understands it best due to their fixation on correct architectural supports and arches [ch 034 / entry]. She describes herself as a stickler for rules, procedures, and contractual obligations [ch 040 / entry]. Horia is deeply offended by cynicism regarding contracts; she questioned whether Valphine was truly a cleric of Lathander after Valphine suggested contracts exist to cheat people [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.06].

Motivationally, Horia seeks to protect the clan’s interests and ensure the mine operates safely and profitably. She initially opposed replacing dwarves with wererats, warning that wererats lacked mining expertise and would cause collapses [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.06]. She agreed to a joint operation only as a pragmatic compromise, insisting on wererat apologies for dwarven deaths [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.06]. While she publicly champions strict adherence to the contract, she is pragmatically willing to bend rules if necessary to resolve operational crises, as seen when she negotiated a secret "Brewbarry addendum" to bypass Vukradin’s no-skimming clause for a contingency plan [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.03].

## Last Observed State
Horia was last seen operating the Mountain’s Toe Gold Mine [ch 048 / 048.08]. During the party's visit to collect earnings, she presented a detailed ledger explaining that the party’s dividend was only thirty gold pieces due to high operational costs, insurance, salaries, and the cut taken by the Neverwinter Commission [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.08]. She assured the party that a new vein was close and that the superstitious dwarven miner insisted the mountain was speaking to him, promising future profits [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.08]. She remained at the mine when the party departed [ch 048 / 048.08].

In the subsequent chapter, the party bypassed the mine to head directly to Neverwinter, leaving Horia’s secret addendum regarding the fake gem and the Earthstone Gem retrieval as outstanding obligations [ch 049 / 049.05]. Status not established in the summaries beyond her role as active manager in chapter 048.

## Relationships
*   **The Party (Vukradin, Brewbarry, Valphine):** Horia is in a business partnership with the party. She countered Vukradin’s claim of legal ownership with her own contract and protested that the original agreement should remain intact when the party insisted on renegotiating the clan’s 20% share [ch 033 / 033.06]. She negotiated a secret deal with Brewbarry to authorize spending on a fake gem, agreeing to keep it hidden from Vukradin [ch 040 / 040.03]. She was offended by Valphine’s cynical view of contracts and recognized her as a drow [ch 033 / 033.06].
*   **The Stonetallow Clan:** Horia is fiercely proud of her clan’s history and the etymology of its name, launching into lengthy lectures on dwarven surnames when questioned [ch 034 / 034.01]. She defends the clan’s honor, asking Vukradin if he had an "issue with the proud clan Stonetallow" when he questioned the name’s logic [ch 034 / 034.01].
*   **Dwolin the Grout:** Horia manages this superstitious foreman. She acknowledges his many eccentricities and rituals but vouches for his unmatched ability to locate gold, insisting he is the finest seeker she has ever known [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.03].
*   **The Wererats (Whiskered Gang):** Horia openly disapproves of the wererats’ modifications to the mine site and regards their work with disdain, though she signed off on their employment as guards and laborers to allow dwarves to focus on skilled mining [ch 034 / entry; ch 034 / 034.03]. She initially demanded apologies from them for dwarven deaths before agreeing to the joint operation [ch 033 / 033.06].
