## Identity
Orsik is a dwarf, previously an adventurer and now a retired resident of Parnast. [ch 049 / entry; ch 050 / entry] In the political narrative of the North, he is publicly celebrated as the “Defender of Parnast” and “Champion of the North,” featured on Lord’s Alliance broadsheets; in the disputed accounts of the dragon Iymrith’s fate, some stories credit Orsik’s heroism and others credit other factions. [ch 022 / 022.01; ch 049 / 049.06] His public image, disseminated via Lord’s Alliance broadsheets, depicts him as “blunt-nosed, serious.” [ch 049 / 049.05]

## Personality and Motivations
Orsik is a former adventurer who has retired to Parnast and is living in “peace”. [ch 050 / entry] He has become ignorant of what he has accomplished; the DM describes him as “protected by his buddy from having his peace of mind ruined”, and the Lords’ Alliance “thinks it’s very convenient”. [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.01] He is associated with a significant act of arcane or infernal bargaining: after Vukradin described Orsik as having killed a blue dragon, the GM corrected that Orsik had forced it into an infernal pact rather than killing it. [ch 021 / 021.01] The summaries do not establish his personal motivations.

## History with the Party
*   In the party’s pre-campaign lore, Orsik was mentioned as helping take care of the children of a previous campaign character named Wallace. [ch 002 / 002.01]
*   Vukradin recalled a story about Orsik involving a blue dragon; Vukradin initially believed Orsik had killed the dragon, but the GM corrected the account to state that Orsik forced the dragon into an infernal pact. [ch 021 / 021.01]
