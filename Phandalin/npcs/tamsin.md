## Identity
A human courier in her forties, wearing a postal clerk’s gray coat, ink on the side of her right hand, and a brass parcel stamp at her neck. The reviewed account describes her as slightly neurodivergent [ch 059 / entry; ch 059 / 059.03].

## Personality and Motivations
Highly professional, devoted to completing every delivery, and distressed by the suggestion of putting a person into a parcel-only device. She fears that the first failed delivery will cost her Aletra’s retainer, and continues demanding the manual even while discussing other work [ch 059 / entry; ch 059 / 059.03; ch 059 / 059.05].

## History with the Party
- Waited at the Fallen Tower with an untouched drink, expecting Aletra’s predicted courier. She rejected the Model 1489 manual because the order was for Model 1486, then allowed Soma in rat form to enter the one-way magical satchel Aletra had given her [ch 059 / 059.03].
- Described how generous low-risk jobs and accurate predictions led her to accept Aletra’s retainer. She transported curios, including the familiar wolf pelt, rather than obtaining them herself [ch 059 / 059.05].
- Reported competition from the Collector’s agents and Aletra’s claim of a powerful unnamed employer. She does not know the Collector’s identity or nature, denies a Margaster connection, and cannot explain Aletra’s promise to regain everything [ch 059 / 059.05].
- Considered arranging a cheese-and-gold route for the party’s mine at a suitable price, but Vukradin lost interest when she proposed sending another courier [ch 059 / 059.05].

## Last Observed State
At the Fallen Tower, distressed over the undelivered manual and uncertain whether Aletra will retain her. No termination or replacement employment is confirmed. The Model 1486 manual remains with the party pending a future dead drop [ch 059 / 059.05; ch 059 / 059.07].

## Relationships
Aletra is her retainer client and the source of her magical satchel. Her knowledge of the Collector comes through agents, and her denial of a Margaster connection is testimony. Her knowledge of Aletra’s employer and restoration language is limited to what Aletra told her [ch 059 / 059.05].
