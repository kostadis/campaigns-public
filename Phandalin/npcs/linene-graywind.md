## Identity
Linene Graywind is the manager of the Lionshield Coster’s outpost in Phandalin [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.04]. She was named to Phandalin’s new privy council by Harbin Wester [ch 019 / 019.07]. She has engaged in economic advocacy, serving as a proponent for the Universal Basic Treasure (UBT) and citing academic texts such as “Candle Keeps, the Sage’s Universal Theorem for Income Distribution” [ch 049 / entry; ch 049 / 049.03]. She also acts as a wealth management consultant, providing letters of introduction to institutions like the Counting House in Neverwinter [ch 049 / entry; ch 049 / 049.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Linene initially greeted the returning adventurers with the remark, “Oh, you’re still alive,” [ch 004 / 004.04]. She is commercially astute and curious, questioning the provenance of goods extensively and paying more than twice Barthen’s offer for the pelts, and saying her bosses would be very interested in the planar effect [ch 016 / 016.07]. In the UBT debate she argued that spending treasure recirculates it into the local economy [ch 049 / 049.03]. She demonstrates strong initiative, volunteering to draft rules for UBT distribution and telling Brewbarry, after Valphine’s Insight roll found her sincere, “this is your personal quest, now it is my personal quest” [ch 049 / 049.04].

## History with the Party
- Linene is the manager of the Lionshield Coster’s Phandalin post, described as running the outpost rather than owning it [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.04].
- Upon the party’s return from their initial adventure, Linene greeted them with the remark, “Oh, you’re still alive,” and purchased their salvaged hide armor at full value [ch 004 / 004.04].
- Linene informed the party of disruptions to her trade routes, attributing them to supply chain and tariff dynamics without fully understanding the root cause, and shared a rumor about strange magic pervading Neverwinter Wood [ch 004 / 004.04].
- When the party later returned to Phandalin seeking to sell pelts, Linene was visibly excited, questioned them extensively about the provenance of the goods, and paid more than twice what Barthen offered [ch 016 / 016.07].
- Linene indicated that her superiors (“bosses”) would be very interested in the planar effect associated with the pelts [ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / 016.07].
- Before the party left town, Linene asked if the group would be performing that evening, specifically inquiring if Valphine would play [ch 016 / 016.07].
- Linene was present on the town green when Vukradin convened the town assembly and was subsequently named to the new privy council by Harbin Wester [ch 019 / 019.05; ch 019 / 019.07].
- During the Universal Basic Treasure (UBT) debates, Linene argued that treasure might have been stolen from someone else originally and that spending it recirculates wealth into the local economy [ch 049 / 049.03].
- Linene volunteered to lead a subcommittee to draft the formal rules for UBT distribution, citing economic theory from “Candle Keeps, the Sage’s Universal Theorem for Income Distribution” [ch 049 / entry; ch 049 / 049.03].
- Brewbarry sought out Linene to serve as his wealth management consultant for a bathrobe factory venture, and she agreed to write a letter of introduction to the Counting House in Neverwinter [ch 049 / entry; ch 049 / 049.04].
- Linene flagged a supply chain problem with the Lionshield Coster to the party, noting that distribution costs had risen and asking them to investigate in Neverwinter [ch 049 / 049.04].
- Linene was among the Phandalin residents drawn to the town green for Vukradin’s performance before the Universal Basic Treasure speech [ch 049 / 049.03].
- Brewbarry presented Linene’s letter of introduction to Aurelan Vance at the Counting House to secure funding and shipping arrangements for his business [ch 051 / 051.01; ch 051 / moment].
- Aurelan Vance confirmed that Linene had already communicated the information regarding the Commission’s declaration of a maintenance failure in the shipping manifold to the party [ch 052 / 052.05].
*   The party currently carries a written request for help with supplies from Linene Graywind. [ch 053 / 053.06]
