## Identity
Elara Seasong Meliamne was a wealthy sea elf noblewoman of House Meliamne and a prominent art collector in Neverwinter [ch 027 / 027.06; ch 050 / entry]. She was the cousin of Lord Cassian Meliamne; the two called each other brother and sister [ch 027 / 027.06; ch 050 / entry]. She was the rightful owner of a statue (a mermaid statue) recovered from a lighthouse, which she had last held centuries ago when she was twelve years old [ch 027 / 027.06; ch 050 / entry; ch 049 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
*   **Reward:** She was expected to reward those returning her statue generously [ch 027 / 027.06].
*   **Investigative:** Before her death, Elara was actively investigating the malfunction of the Planar Manifold. She had met with its gnome technician twice at the docks and documented her unease regarding him in writing [ch 050 / entry].
*   **Enthusiastic:** She was described as "ecstatic and eagerly awaiting" the party's arrival at her festival when word came that her statue had resurfaced [ch 050 / entry].

## History with the Party
*   Ser Kaelen identified a statue recovered from the lighthouse as the property of Elara Seasong Meliamne and requested the party escort it to her in Neverwinter, noting she is expected to reward them generously [ch 027 / 027.06; ch 027 / entry].
*   Vukradin strongly favored traveling to Neverwinter to visit Elara, partly to clarify magical loot and partly for personal business [ch 034 / entry].
*   Elara died during a festival disaster officially ruled a tragic accident by Lord Neverember, though her cousin Cassian Meliamne attributes her death to House Margaster [ch 049 / entry; ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.03].
*   Status: Elara is dead [ch 049 / entry; ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.03].
*   The party arrived at the Moonstone Mask intending to present the mermaid statue to Elara, but were informed by a staff member that she had been killed eight days prior [ch 050 / 050.03].
*   Elara’s death was so severe that resurrection was impossible [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.03].
*   Before her death, Elara had been investigating the malfunction of the Planar Manifold, meeting with a gnome technician at the docks and documenting her unease regarding him [ch 050 / entry].

- Cassian showed the party Elara’s records-request slip concerning the manifold and her final note that the failure would be profitable only if someone knew the route would remain closed. He did not know who had seen her inquiry and acknowledged that his accusation against House Margaster still needed proof [ch 058 / 058.02].

## Last Observed State
Deceased; the new evidence consists of her records request and final note, presented by Cassian. He attributes her murder and inability to be raised to House Margaster, but says he lacks a case and seeks further proof. The identity of whoever saw her query remains unresolved [ch 050 / entry; ch 058 / 058.02].

## Relationships
*   **Lord Cassian Meliamne:** Her cousin, whom she called her brother. He hosted a weeks-long gala in Neverwinter that Elara attended before her death [ch 027 / 027.06; ch 050 / entry]. Following her death, the staff directed the party to Cassian at the inn [ch 050 / 050.03]. The staff noted that Cassian did not seem eager to pursue raising Elara, despite the impossibility [ch 050 / 050.03].
*   **Ser Kaelen:** A member of the Order of the Gauntlet who identified the statue as Elara's property and requested the party escort it to her, hoping to gain her financial support for the Order through this service [ch 027 / 027.06].
*   **House Margaster:** A rival family whose agents intended to intercept Elara's statue to embarrass the Meliamnes [ch 027 / 027.06]. Cassian explicitly names them as responsible for Elara's death [ch 050 / entry].
*   **Gnome Technician:** An individual associated with the Planar Manifold whom Elara investigated and noted with unease shortly before her death [ch 050 / entry].
