## Identity
Iymrith is an ancient blue dragon known as the “Doom of the Desert” [ch 021 / entry; ch 022 / entry; ch 032 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
The evidence is silent on Iymrith’s specific personality traits or active motivations. However, Falcon claims that clerics of Talos regard her as favored by their god and seek pieces of her treasure as offerings [ch 021 / entry; ch 021 / 021.02; ch 022 / 022.01]. Falcon the Hunter describes her as “fearsome” [ch 021 / 021.02]. Vukradin theorizes that Iymrith was not necessarily the rightful owner of the lightning ring, suggesting she may have stolen it herself [ch 022 / entry; ch 022 / 022.01].

## History with the Party
*   Falcon the Hunter identifies Iymrith as a blue dragon known as the “Doom of the Desert” and claims his adventuring party once faced her, with Falcon being the only survivor [ch 021 / entry].
*   Falcon states that he later heard reports of Iymrith’s death, but when he visited her plundered hoard, her body was not found, though dragon bodies and other corpses littered the site [ch 021 / entry; ch 021 / 021.02].
*   Falcon claims he found the lightning ring and the coins used to fund his lodge among the remains of Iymrith’s hoard [ch 021 / entry; ch 022 / entry].
*   Falcon asserts that Talos’s clerics regard Iymrith as favored by their god and seek pieces of her treasure as offerings, leading him to believe they broke into his lodge to steal the ring [ch 021 / entry; ch 021 / 021.02; ch 022 / 022.01].
*   Vukradin examines a mechanical blue-dragon-head box in Falcon’s quarters but fails to establish that the box is literally Iymrith’s head or modeled specifically on her [ch 021 / 021.01].
*   Vukradin theorizes that the owner of some elven ears found in Falcon’s room was harmed by a dragon, prompting Falcon to create the dragon-head trophy as an act of revenge [ch 021 / 021.01].
*   The party recalls Orsik having forced a blue dragon into an infernal pact; the GM corrects a claim that Orsik killed the dragon, but this event is not established as explaining Iymrith’s fate [ch 021 / 021.01].
*   Sages dispute the circumstances of Iymrith’s disappearance or death, with competing accounts crediting Orsik, a seer, or other factions [ch 022 / entry; ch 022 / 022.01].
*   Vukradin argues that Iymrith was not necessarily the rightful owner of the lightning ring because she may have stolen it herself [ch 022 / entry; ch 022 / 022.01].
*   An entry identifies Iymrith as an ancient blue dragon who was significantly wounded by Bob and later escaped to Avernus rather than being killed [ch 032 / entry].

## Last Observed State
Iymrith was not present in chapters 21, 22, or 32; she was only discussed [ch 021 / 021.01; ch 021 / 021.02; ch 022 / 022.01; ch 022 / 022.02]. Her physical location is unknown. In chapter 21, Falcon reported that upon visiting her hoard, he found no body, only dragon corpses and other remains, leaving her fate unconfirmed [ch 021 / entry; ch 021 / 021.02]. Sages in chapter 22 disputed whether she was dead, with accounts crediting Orsik, a seer, or other factions [ch 022 / entry; ch 022 / 022.01]. In chapter 32, an entry states that in a previous timeline/campaign context, she was significantly wounded by Bob and escaped to Avernus rather than being killed [ch 032 / entry]. Status not established in the current campaign summaries.

## Relationships
*   **Falcon the Hunter:** Falcon claims his adventuring party once faced Iymrith and that he was the only survivor [ch 021 / entry; ch 021 / 021.02]. He scavenged her ring and coins from her plundered hoard to fund his lodge [ch 021 / entry; ch 021 / 021.02; ch 022 / entry]. Falcon falsely presents himself as the dragon’s slayer to build his reputation [ch 021 / 021.02; ch 022 / entry].
*   **Bob:** In a prior context, Bob significantly wounded Iymrith, and she later escaped to Avernus rather than being killed [ch 032 / entry].
*   **Clerics of Talos:** These clerics view Iymrith as favored by Talos and seek her treasure as offerings [ch 021 / entry; ch 021 / 021.02]. Falcon believes they attempted to steal the lightning ring from his lodge for this purpose [ch 021 / entry; ch 021 / 021.02].
*   **Orsik:** Sages dispute whether Orsik, a seer, or other factions were responsible for Iymrith’s defeat or disappearance [ch 022 / entry; ch 022 / 022.01]. Vukradin recalls Orsik forcing a blue dragon into an infernal pact, though this is not explicitly linked to Iymrith in the current evidence [ch 021 / 021.01].
*   **Vukradin:** Vukradin posits that Iymrith was likely a thief herself and therefore not the rightful owner of the lightning ring [ch 022 / entry; ch 022 / 022.01].
