## Identity
Petunia is a cow owned by Big Al, identifiable by a stamp reading “Big Al’s Cow” [ch 017 / entry; ch 018 / 018.08]. She is described as Big Al’s "prized possession" and "the area’s famous prize cow" [ch 018 / 018.07; ch 033 / 033.03]. She is capable of producing high-quality milk used to make cheese that Zeleen Varnaster identifies as a "famous local delicacy" [ch 033 / moment; ch 034 / 034.02]. Through magical means (*Speak with Animals*), Petunia is capable of human-like speech and expresses distinct preferences and emotions [ch 017 / 017.05; ch 027 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
*   **Contentment with Care:** When communicating via magic, Petunia stated, “Oh, Big Al takes care of me very good. Yep, yep, he does,” indicating she feels well-treated by her owner [ch 027 / 027.02].
*   **Nostalgia for Home:** Petunia expressed missing her "old stomping grounds," specifically Butterskull Ranch, asking, “Do you know when I’ll go back to them?” [ch 027 / 027.02].
*   **Dietary Preferences:** She explicitly missed the taste of alfalfa from Butterskull Ranch, stating, “I miss the taste of the alfalfa there” [ch 027 / moment].
*   **Social and Environmental Needs:** Petunia desires other cows for companionship and prefers a quiet environment, disliking "annoying intruders" [ch 027 / entry; ch 027 / 027.02].
*   **Motivation:** Her primary motivation appears to be maintaining her current comfort level with Big Al while resolving the absence of her preferred diet (alfalfa) and social herd (other cows) lost when Butterskull Ranch burned [ch 027 / 027.02].

## History with the Party
* Petunia is Big Al’s prized cow, identifiable by a stamp reading “Big Al’s Cow,” and is the subject of a rescue quest offered with Mithral Chain Mail as a reward [ch 017 / entry; ch 018 / 018.07].
* Soma used Speak with Animals to locate Petunia within an hour, after which the party moved the cow and Big Al to Corbin’s camp [ch 017 / 017.05; ch 018 / 018.08].
* Petunia’s milk is central to a deal with the Whiskered Gang at the Mountain’s Toe Gold Mine; the gang agreed to work the mine in exchange for regular shipments of cheese made from her milk [ch 026 / 026.11].
* Soma communicated with Petunia, learning that the cow is happy with Big Al’s care but misses the alfalfa from Butterskull Ranch and desires other cows and a quiet environment [ch 027 / entry; ch 027 / 027.02].
* Big Al took Petunia to Qelline Alderleaf’s farm to apologize for past grievances, after which Petunia happily grazed and began producing milk while the reconciliation occurred privately [ch 027 / 027.04].
* Zeleen Varnaster and the wererats recognized Petunia’s cheese as a famous local delicacy, consuming it as part of their mine work agreement [ch 033 / 033.03; ch 034 / 034.02].
* The wererats were working with conspicuous diligence, apparently still intimidated by the party’s last visit, and nervously asked if they could leave posts to fetch their promised cheese rations [ch 048 / 048.08].

## Last Observed State
The most recent confirmed evidence places Petunia in Phandalin, specifically on Qelline Alderleaf’s farm, where she was observed happily grazing and producing milk following the reconciliation between Big Al and Qelline [ch 027 / 027.04]. While the cow herself was not present at the Mountain’s Toe Gold Mine in Chapter 048, her cheese was the subject of active negotiation, with wererats asking permission to fetch their promised cheese (sourced from Petunia’s “happy cows”) [ch 048 / 048.08]. The production pipeline was confirmed active, as the party had previously secured grazing rights at Qelline’s farm to ensure milk supply for this agreement [ch 027 / 027.03; ch 027 / 027.04]. Status of the cow herself is assumed to be alive and under Big Al's care or Qelline's grazing arrangement, as the milk supply remained consistent through Chapter 048.

## Relationships
*   **Big Al:** Petunia is Big Al’s prized cow. Big Al views her as indispensable, stating, “She’s my prized possession. Without her, you know, the Butterskull Ranch is just another…” [ch 018 / 018.07]. He took her to Qelline’s farm to facilitate a personal apology, and they were seen together in Phandalin [ch 027 / 027.04].
*   **Qelline Alderleaf:** Petunia was to graze on Qelline’s farm at a reduced fee of five silver pieces per week, which Qelline offered only if Big Al apologized to her personally [ch 027 / 027.03; ch 027 / 027.04].
*   **Soma:** Soma used *Speak with Animals* to communicate with Petunia, establishing her preferences and happiness levels [ch 017 / 017.05; ch 027 / 027.02]. Soma described her role regarding Petunia as trying to "guide her home" [ch 027 / 027.03].
*   **Zeleen Varnaster & The Whiskered Gang:** Zeleen and the wererats consider Petunia’s cheese a famous delicacy and rely on its regular shipment as payment for their labor at the mine [ch 033 / 033.03; ch 034 / 034.02; ch 048 / 048.08]. Zeleen specifically praised the cheese’s quality, comparing it favorably to Gouda [ch 034 / 034.02].
*   **Butterskull Ranch:** Petunia has a nostalgic attachment to the location, missing the specific alfalfa and herd found there before the ranch burned [ch 027 / 027.02].
