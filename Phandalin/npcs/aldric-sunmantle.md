## Identity
**Aldric Sunmantle** (also referred to as Brother Aldric) is a High Priest at the Spire of the Morning Lord in Neverwinter [ch 049 / entry]. He is a high-ranking cleric of Lathander [ch 052 / entry]. He served as the priest who received Valphine’s conversion approximately four months prior to the party's arrival in Neverwinter [ch 049 / entry]. His formal title within the narrative events of Chapter 052 includes interactions as a senior cleric addressing theological irregularities [ch 052 / 052.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Aldric insisted that last rites be performed for the slain drow and questioned Valphine about the meaning of her “blessing” [ch 052 / 052.01; ch 057 / 057.05]. He gave Valphine a "pointed look" when she announced that the party had discovered drow operating in Neverwinter [ch 057 / 057.05]. He is deeply concerned by what he terms the "searing pain doctrine," viewing Valphine’s teachings on pain and suffering as widely considered heretical [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.05].

He is diligent in administrative and ritual matters, insisting on the performance of last rites for the dead, even when they are enemies of the faithful, and overseeing the proper handling of corpses [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.05]. He demonstrates a protective, albeit conservative, attitude toward the faith, worrying that followers have already begun dressing like Brewbarry and may soon adopt his bathrobes [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.05]. When Valphine challenged him for questioning Lathander’s chosen, he became uncomfortable enough to suggest the conversation not continue in public or before nonbelievers [ch 057 / 057.05].

## History with the Party
- Ser Kaelen advised Valphine to seek guidance from Brother Aldric regarding her golden eyes and divine blessing when the party arrived in Neverwinter. [ch 049 / 049.02]
- An armorer in the Protector’s Enclave recognized Valphine as the Drow follower of Lathander whom Brother Aldric had spoken of in the city. [ch 049 / 049.06]
- Brother Aldric agreed to shelter the gnome Bimble and handle the drow corpse, insisting that last rites be performed. [ch 057 / 057.05]
- Brother Aldric expressed concern over Valphine’s "searing pain doctrine" and summoned Father Jerome to address the heretical teachings. [ch 057 / 057.05]
- Brother Aldric voiced concern that Brewbarry’s influence on church merchandise might lead the faithful to begin wearing bathrobes. [ch 057 / 057.05]

## Last Observed State
Aldric is currently at the Spire of the Morning Lord in Neverwinter [ch 057 / 057.05]. He has granted sanctuary to the gnome Bimble, accepting him into the temple’s protection to shield him from pursuers [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.05]. He is managing the remains of a slain drow, having insisted that last rites be performed despite the drow’s hostility, and arranging for the body to be placed in the temple’s catacombs or mortuary space [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.05].

He has summoned Father Jerome to address Valphine’s teachings, with Jerome expected to arrive in approximately one week [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.05]. During this meeting, Aldric expressed visible discomfort when Valphine challenged his authority as a critic of Lathander’s chosen, leading him to end the public portion of the discussion [ch 057 / 057.05]. Status not established in the summaries beyond his active presence at the Spire during Chapter 057.

## Relationships
**Valphine:** Aldric was her conversion priest and has spoken of her to other devotees in Neverwinter, establishing her reputation in the city [ch 049 / 049.06; ch 049 / entry]. He is currently in a state of theological conflict with her, regarding her "searing pain doctrine" as heretical [ch 057 / entry]. He has attempted to correct her through theological traps and warnings, stating, "I warn you to stay away from this path of pain. This is not the Lathander’s way," though he welcomed her to the pulpit after she successfully defended her views in a prior encounter [ch 052 / 052.01; ch 057 / 057.05].

**Bimble:** Aldric is currently his protector within the temple, having agreed to shelter him from enemies [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.05].

**Father Jerome:** Aldric has summoned him to aid in doctrinal oversight, indicating a professional relationship where Jerome is viewed as an authority who can help guide Valphine toward more acceptable teachings [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.05].

**Brewbarry:** Aldric is wary of Brewbarry’s commercial influence on the church, while Valphine identified Brewbarry as the purveyor of church merchandise, and Aldric worried that followers have already begun dressing like him [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.05].

**Ser Kaelen:** Kaelen suggested that Valphine visit Aldric in Neverwinter for guidance on what Lathander might be telling her through her golden eyes [ch 049 / entry; ch 049 / 049.02].
