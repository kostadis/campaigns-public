## Identity
Bimble Nackle is a gnome technician and the former operator of the planar manifold [ch 057 / entry]. He spent approximately nine years tuning the device and performing its mandatory ten-day maintenance routines [ch 057 / entry]. Although he could tune and operate the manifold, he did not understand how its original parts were constructed or where replacements might be found [ch 057 / entry]. He previously worked for the Commission, attempting to persuade them to reduce increasingly dangerous throughput targets [ch 057 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Bimble is precise about language and displays dry patience and calm firmness even when pressured [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / moment]. When Vukradin used *persuasion* to attempt to force him to change his name to “Bimbo,” Bimble conceded the effectiveness of the argument but refused to alter his identity, stating, "I see your point, but my name is still Bimble" [ch 057 / 057.01; ch 057 / moment]. He responded strongly to suggestions that he had shown poor hospitality, saying, "I would not have my mother say that I demonstrated poor hospitality," and apologized profusely when he believed he had raised his voice in front of his host [ch 056 / moment; ch 057 / 057.01].

His primary motivation is self-preservation and avoiding blame for the manifold’s collapse. He strongly opposed the idea of appearing before Lord Neverember, fearing the Lord Protector would blame him for the resulting loss of revenue [ch 057 / 057.01]. Bimble believed that unauthorized routing changes burned out the machine, asserting, "The machine did not fail. It was finished" [ch 057 / entry; ch 056 / moment]. He fled his position five to ten days prior to the party's discovery of him in the sewers after a drow woman interrogated him about what he had removed from the manifold housing [ch 056 / moment].
