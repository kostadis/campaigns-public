## Identity
Corrin is a short, silent performer and the partner of Rimardo, operating together as a comedy duo and later as “Divine Accountants and Occasional Entertainers” [ch 047 / entry; ch 051 / entry]. He communicates exclusively through non-verbal means, including a deck of cards, magical props, deliberate hand gestures, shrugs, and official stamps [ch 047 / entry; ch 051 / entry]. His primary tools are magical in nature, including a smooth gray stone that causes objects to cease to exist (“unhappens”), a tiny self-animating suit of armor, and cards that illustrate cryptic warnings about future events [ch 047 / 047.01; ch 047 / entry]. He possesses the ability to physically “redact” clauses from a narrative or contract by lowering a finger [ch 047 / 047.01; ch 047 / entry]. He is referred to by Rimardo as “Mr. Corrin” [ch 047 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Corrin is defined by his silence and his role as a bureaucratic enforcer within the duo’s theatrical routine [ch 047 / entry; ch 051 / entry]. He acts as a physical editor and auditor, using his props to strike out rules or validate documents [ch 047 / 047.01; ch 051 / 051.03]. His actions suggest a motivation tied to balancing accounts—whether financial, emotional, or metaphysical—as evidenced by his production of itemized invoices for “emotional damages” and “sentimental value” and his approval of financial instruments [ch 047 / 047.02; ch 051 / 051.03]. He engages with the party through cryptic, visual narratives rather than direct speech, often highlighting the absurdity or inevitability of their situation [ch 047 / 047.01; ch 051 / 051.03]. He displays a willingness to intrude on private spaces, such as appearing uninvited at Falcon’s Lodge or setting up shop in a government office [ch 047 / 047.01; ch 051 / 051.03].

## History with the Party
- Corrin appears with Rimardo at Falcon’s Lodge, making prolonged eye contact with one party member and examining scorched timbers with a gloved finger. [ch 047 / 047.01]
- During the lodge performance, Corrin uses cards and magical props to illustrate warnings, including a submerged coastal town, a drowned gate, a tiny self-animating suit of armor, and a stone that ‘unhappens’ things. [ch 047 / 047.01; ch 047 / 047.02]
- Corrin physically ‘redacts’ the third house rule recited by Rimardo by lowering a finger, striking it from the record. [ch 047 / 047.01; ch 047 / entry]

- After vanishing from the lodge, Corrin is mentioned by the Falcon as part of a comedy duo he wishes to hire; the Falcon gives Vukradin an unsealed letter of offer to deliver to the pair, which Vukradin files in his ‘plot items list.’ [ch 048 / 048.06]
- Corrin reappears with Rimardo at the Counting House in Neverwinter, operating behind a velvet rope as one half of ‘Divine Accountants and Occasional Entertainers.’ [ch 051 / 051.03]
- Corrin produces physical receipts from every establishment the party has visited, including one from the Bronze Sun. [ch 051 / 051.03]
- Corrin stamps the party’s subcommittee charter as ‘comedically compliant’ and their letter of credit as ‘approved’ before leaving with Rimardo. [ch 051 / 051.03; ch 051 / entry]
