## Identity
Dwolin the Grout is the foreman of the Mountain’s Toe Gold Mine. [ch 040 / entry] He is a dwarf. [ch 040 / 040.03] Horia, the dwarven leader, describes him as the finest seeker of gold she has ever known. [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.03]

## Personality and Motivations
Dwolin is characterized by deep superstition and eccentricity. [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.03] He relies heavily on rituals and trinkets in his work. [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.03] He holds the belief that a gold vein is “shy” and will hide if not treated with absolute respect. [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.03] His primary motivation for his actions is to ensure the mine is properly blessed with an Earthstone Gem before digging resumes. [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.03]

## History with the Party
- Dwolin is the superstitious foreman of the Mountain’s Toe Gold Mine. [ch 040 / entry]
- He led the dwarven miners in a strike, refusing to dig another inch until an Earthstone Gem could be found to bless the mine. [ch 040 / 040.03]
- He explained to the party that the gold vein is shy and must be treated with absolute respect or it will hide. [ch 040 / 040.03]
- Horia, the dwarven leader, informed the party that despite Dwolin’s eccentric rituals and trinkets, he is the finest seeker of gold she has ever known. [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.03]
- The party initiated a contingency plan involving Horia to acquire a fake Earthstone Gem for fifty gold pieces, intended to satisfy Dwolin’s rituals if the were-rats failed to retrieve the real gem. [ch 040 / 040.03]
