## Identity
Lim is a tortle chef and the proprietor of The Board Laid Bare in the Blue Lake District [ch 053 / entry]. She is also a long-time Harper agent, having worked for the organization before the Manifold closed and originally gathering intelligence on Soma due to suspicions of naturalist sympathies [ch 055 / entry; ch 055 / 055.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Lim prioritizes the reputation and privacy of her business, explicitly forbidding advertising to rely solely on word-of-mouth [ch 053 / 053.04; ch 053 / entry]. She maintains a supply chain using smugglers and asserts that her goods are moved through the Blue Lake District’s sewer system rather than sourced from it, justifying this method as a necessity under rising taxes imposed by Lord Neverember [ch 053 / entry; ch 053 / 053.05]. She defends her choice of suppliers by claiming they are “not bad people” and that her prices and quality remain good [ch 053 / 053.05]. Lim is protective of her operations, asking the party specifically not to frighten or ambush her suppliers [ch 053 / 053.05; ch 054 / entry].
