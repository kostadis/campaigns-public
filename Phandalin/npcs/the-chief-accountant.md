## Identity
The Chief Accountant is a luminous, glitching dwarven banshee who manifested within Axeholm, appearing like corrupted data with a face of shifting geometric shapes and ink-like shadows leaking from her eyes [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.03]. Her voice combines a dwarven grandmother’s warmth with screeching static [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.03]. She was an employee of the keep who claimed to be uncertain whether she was truly dead or merely “out of phase” [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.05]. After the interdimensional distortions ceased and her final accounts were settled, she disappeared in peace [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.03].

## Personality and Motivations
The Chief Accountant is defined by an obsessive commitment to accounting principles, ledgers, and balance sheets [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.04]. She speaks in accounting metaphors, referencing “depreciation,” “value attrition,” and liquidation even in moments of panic or violence [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.05]. Her primary motivation was the closure of accounts; her banshee wail reduced Valphine to zero hit points, after which she panicked, crying that she “just wanted a signature!” and fearing the auditor would liquidate her [ch 030 / 030.04].

She blamed Valphine’s sister, Aletra, whom she described as “a snake” [ch 030 / 030.05; ch 030 / moment]. The Chief Accountant claimed Aletra killed her for discovering that the books did not balance and for threatening to report the discrepancy to management [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.05]. She harbored anxiety about outstanding debts, which bound her to the location after death [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.03]. She was transparent with the party, revealing that she had found Override Code 77 Beta and offering it in exchange for her freedom and secrecy [ch 030 / 030.05].

## History with the Party
- The Chief Accountant is a luminous, glitching dwarven banshee who appears in the mine amidst out-of-phase dwarves obsessed with efficiency [ch 030 / 030.03; ch 030 / 030.04].
- She recognized Valphine by the “Sotorra scent” and referred to her as “sister of the sun,” referencing Valphine’s Lathander connection [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.04].
- During combat, Vukradin projected an illusory Babbage Difference Engine into the Accountant’s mind, which she believed was attacking her [ch 030 / 030.04].
- The Accountant unleashed a banshee wail that reduced Valphine and Vukradin to zero hit points, but she panicked upon seeing Valphine unconscious and attempted to stabilize her with blue-white static [ch 030 / 030.04].
- She fled through a wall into an adjoining room, where she negotiated with the party after they healed, revealing that Valphine’s sister Aletra had installed a multidimensional machine to increase profits [ch 030 / 030.05].
- The Accountant claimed Aletra killed her because the Accountant discovered the books did not balance and threatened to report it to management, though she is uncertain if she is truly dead or just out of phase [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.05].
- She offered the party Override Code 77 Beta in exchange for secrecy and her freedom, directing them toward the machine’s location [ch 030 / 030.05].
- After the machine imploded and the spatial distortions ended, the party returned to the Chief Accountant, who was calm but anxious about a final unpaid bill [ch 032 / 032.03].
- Upon reviewing a 42,000-gold-piece invoice, the Accountant determined Axeholm owed no more, declared “The book is closed,” and disappeared in peace [ch 032 / 032.03].
- The party later found an iron chest containing her possessions, including a dread helm and gauntlets of ogre power [ch 032 / 032.05].
- Vukradin expressed a desire to find the Accountant’s next of kin to return the items, but the Accountant had already departed [ch 032 / 032.07].

## Last Observed State
The Chief Accountant’s final appearance occurred after the party disabled the multidimensional machine and returned to her. She was calm and relaxed, though initially anxious about a final unpaid bill [ch 032 / 032.03]. When presented with a 42,000-gold-piece interplanetary invoice, she spent approximately ten minutes furiously disputing the charge as “outrageous” and “incorrect” [ch 032 / 032.03; ch 032 / moment]. Upon determining that Axeholm owed no more money, she declared “The book is closed” and disappeared in peace [ch 032 / 032.03; ch 032 / moment]. The party subsequently found an iron chest containing her possessions, including a dread helm and gauntlets of ogre power [ch 032 / 032.05].

## Relationships
*   **Valphine Sotorra**: The Chief Accountant recognized Valphine by her family’s “Sotorra scent” of bleach and betrayal, referring to her as “sister of the sun” due to her connection with Lathander [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.04; ch 030 / moment]. During combat, the Accountant accidentally reduced Valphine to zero hit points but panicked and stabilized her with blue-white static, fearing that “the auditor” would liquidate her for allowing a party member to depreciate [ch 030 / 030.04; ch 030 / moment].
*   **Aletra**: The Chief Accountant accused Aletra of being “a snake” and claimed Aletra killed her for attempting to report that the books did not balance [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.05; ch 030 / moment]. She offered the party Override Code 77 Beta, speculating “That may have been more to do with why she killed me” [ch 030 / 030.05].
*   **The Party (Vukradin, Brewbarry, Soma)**: The Chief Accountant negotiated with the party after combat. She offered the Override Code in exchange for being allowed to leave and for the party keeping the arrangement secret from Aletra [ch 030 / 030.05]. Vukradin used an illusory Babbage Difference Engine to distract her during the fight [ch 030 / 030.04]. Later, the party presented her with a final 42,000-gold-piece bill, after which she declared the book closed, and Vukradin expressed a desire to locate her next of kin to return her magical items [ch 032 / 032.03; ch 032 / 032.05; ch 032 / 032.07].
