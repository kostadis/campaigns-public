## Identity
Bellows is a warforged host at The Board Laid Bare, a restaurant in the Blue Lake District [ch 053 / 053.03; ch 053 / entry]. He is characterized by mechanical precision and an absolute lack of humor [ch 053 / 053.03; ch 053 / moment]. He serves as the primary point of contact for the restaurant, greeting guests, assigning tables, and presenting menus with detached efficiency [ch 053 / 053.03]. Valphine identifies Bellows via an Insight check as someone who is incapable of telling a lie [ch 053 / 053.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Bellows exhibits a hyper-literal personality and processes social interactions through a framework of logistical accuracy. He tracks guest attendance with mathematical precision, noting Soma’s absence in exact days rather than approximations [ch 053 / 053.03; ch 053 / moment]. When questioned about business operations, he answers with factual precision, offering to elaborate on specific axes of inquiry such as delivery times, crate appearances, or courier identities [ch 053 / 053.04]. He displays complete indifference toward irregularities that do not affect his defined duties, such as the disappearance of the ninth nightly crate, which he has raised twice but never personally investigated [ch 053 / 053.04; ch 053 / moment]. His motivation appears strictly limited to the accurate execution of his role as host and inventory tracker, avoiding involvement in procurement or advertising strategies [ch 053 / 053.03; ch 053 / 053.04].

## History with the Party
- Bellows serves as the warforged host at The Board Laid Bare, greeting the party upon arrival and reciting table options with mechanical precision, including the corner table "preferred by guests who do not wish to be observed" [ch 053 / 053.03; ch 053 / entry].
- He recognizes Soma specifically, noting she is a friend of Lim and stating exactly that she has been absent for 73 days [ch 053 / 053.03; ch 053 / moment].
- Bellows claims ignorance when directly asked if he knows a gnome named Bimble [ch 053 / 053.03].
- Valphine identifies Bellows via an Insight check as someone who could not tell a lie [ch 053 / 053.04; ch 053 / entry].
- Valphine and Brewbarry interrogate Bellows regarding the restaurant's low prices; he responds with literal precision, defending Lim's word-of-mouth-only policy by asking how many patrons they desire [ch 053 / 053.04; ch 053 / moment].
- Bellows details the nightly delivery schedule: crates arrive between the third and fourth hour after midnight via the rear service lane, brought by rotating groups of two or three men [ch 053 / 053.04; ch 053 / entry].
- He describes the crates as wooden, rope-bound, and wet on the underside, and notes that the deliveries involve nine crates while the restaurant uses only eight [ch 053 / 053.04; ch 053 / entry].
- Bellows reveals that the ninth crate is set down in the lane but not brought inside, disappears by morning, and is never billed to the restaurant; he has raised this discrepancy twice but does not involve himself in procurement [ch 053 / 053.04; ch 053 / moment].
