## Identity
Old Hesp is a grizzled, long-time regular patron of the Common Chord in Neverwinter [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / 054.01]. He is a local fixture who has witnessed the venue's history over many years, ranging from its days as a dive with empty rooms to its current status as an expensive, fashionable establishment [ch 054 / 054.01; ch 054 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Hesp is characterized by a strong nostalgia for the Common Chord’s earlier, grittier era, which he contrasts sharply with its current polished, commercial, and unaffordable state under Petra’s management [ch 054 / 054.01; ch 054 / entry]. He is dissatisfied with the modern atmosphere, complaining that the venue has become a "money-making operation" and that patrons are now pressured to look fashionable [ch 054 / 054.01]. His dissatisfaction extends to specific amenities, noting that Brewbarry’s table previously lacked leather, polish, and plaques, and that the venue did not serve fancy food [ch 054 / 054.01; ch 054 / entry].

Despite his complaints, Hesp supports the arts and Vukradin’s vision for an independent, low-cost music studio [ch 054 / entry]. He contributed to Vukradin’s crowdfunding effort for this studio, ensuring his name was recorded for future program credits [ch 054 / 054.01]. He retains a personal, albeit critical, history with Vukradin, recalling specific past interactions such as the bard’s promise of refunds for poor ratings [ch 054 / 054.01].

## Relationships
**Vukradin**: Hesp is a long-time acquaintance who recognizes the bard personally [ch 054 / 054.01]. He recalls attending Vukradin’s performances when the rooms were often empty and the bard offered refunds to anyone who did not rate the show ten out of ten [ch 054 / 054.01; ch 054 / entry]. Hesp greeted Vukradin by noting these humble beginnings [ch 054 / moment]. Despite Vukradin’s current fame making the venue expensive, Hesp became a contributor to Vukradin’s nonprofit studio project [ch 054 / 054.01; ch 054 / entry].

**Petra**: Hesp holds Petra’s management of the Common Chord in disdain, blaming her for transforming the venue into a commercial, fashionable, and unaffordable operation [ch 054 / 054.01].

**Brewbarry**: Hesp remembers Brewbarry in a previous state, specifically recalling that Brewbarry’s table lacked leather, polish, and a plaque in the past, contrasting this with its current polished appearance [ch 054 / 054.01; ch 054 / entry].
