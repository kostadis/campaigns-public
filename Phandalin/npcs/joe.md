## Identity
Joe was an undercover agent for the Emerald Enclave stationed at the logging camp [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.06]. "Joe" was a nickname rather than the deceased man’s actual fantasy name [ch 024 / 024.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Joe was expecting a patrol to handle an “interloper” situation at the camp [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.06]. He left behind notes and a diary (or a note prepared for the Enclave) describing his search for the patrol and a possible next location [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.06].

## History with the Party
*   Joe was an deceased Emerald Enclave agent working undercover at the logging camp [ch 023 / entry].
*   Joe was among the loggers killed and digested by ankhegs [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.03; ch 023 / 023.06].
*   Joe left behind notes and a diary (or note prepared for the Enclave) that the party discovered in an unexplored portion of the camp [ch 023 / 023.06].
*   Joe’s writings revealed that a patrol was expected the following day to handle an “interloper” situation, but he could not find the patrol at the expected rendezvous after it arrived [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.06].
*   The party used Joe’s notebook to determine directions to the Emerald Enclave meeting point before departing the camp [ch 024 / 024.01; ch 024 / 024.02].
*   Joe was a nickname, not the deceased man’s actual fantasy name [ch 024 / 024.01].

## Last Observed State
Joe was among those killed and digested by the ankhegs [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.03; ch 023 / 023.06]. Although physically deceased during the events at the camp, his written records remained active evidence; the party used his notebook to determine directions to the Emerald Enclave meeting point during their journey [ch 024 / 024.01; ch 024 / 024.02].

## Relationships
**The Emerald Enclave:** Joe served this faction as an undercover agent [ch 023 / entry]. A message reassured him that a patrol would come the next day, though he failed to connect with their patrol before his death [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.06].
