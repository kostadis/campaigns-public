## Identity
Lord Cassian Meliamne is a sea elf nobleman and the eccentric cousin of the late Lady Elara Seasong Meliamne; the two called each other brother and sister [ch 027 / entry; ch 049 / entry; ch 050 / entry]. He is a prominent figure in Neverwinter high society, known for hosting extravagant parties, including a weeks-long gala, that Jenna Roscoe describes as “wonderful, extravagant, and debauched” [ch 027 / 027.06; ch 050 / entry]. Rumors circulated by Jenna Roscoe suggest he may have ties to the Kraken Society, though she dismissed the society’s broader threat level [ch 027 / 027.06]. Following Elara’s death, he assumed control of her anti-Margaster case and her Neverwinter commercial interests [ch 049 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Cassian is generous toward the party, though he is not much of a music fan [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.04]. He values honor and propriety, framing his reward to Vukradin as a donation to a music studio to avoid insulting the bard’s dignity with direct payment [ch 050 / 050.04; ch 050 / 050.05]. He holds the Order of the Gauntlet in high regard, calling them “truly one of the few heroes of the realms” [ch 050 / 050.05].

Politically, Cassian appears easily intimidated by the power of House Margaster. He publicly succumbed to a shakedown by their agent, Cullen Sharpe, paying 200 gold to settle a fraudulent salvage claim while nervously insisting the transaction was legitimate [ch 050 / 050.06]. Despite this public submission, he seeks to investigate the connection between his cousin’s death and the failure of the Commission’s displacement manifold, but strictly prefers to keep his own distance from the Commission [ch 050 / 050.05]. He is deeply sentimental regarding his family history, becoming visibly emotional upon returning the statue his cousin last held as a child hundreds of years ago [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.05].
