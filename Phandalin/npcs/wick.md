## Identity
Wick is a patron of the Common Chord tavern in Neverwinter [ch 054 / entry]. He is one of the willing donors identified by Vukradin during a crowdfunding pitch for an independent music studio [ch 054 / 054.01]. His name was recorded for future studio program credits alongside Sabbatha, Old Hesp, and Ondrelle Kass [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / 054.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Wick is characterized as an enthusiastic supporter of the arts who did not much care whether the fundraiser, Vukradin, was truly the bard he claimed to be [ch 054 / 054.01]. He does not know the band Lathander’s Death and remains unconvinced of Vukradin’s authenticity, but he enthusiastically supports the project regardless [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / 054.01]. His primary motivation is a love for the idea of patronizing the arts; he assumes that Vukradin’s music studio will function as a school to train musicians [ch 054 / entry].
