## Identity
Gorthok the Thunder Boar is a massive, monstrous boar-like creature associated with the Talosian cult [ch 046 / entry]. Narux’s sketches depict him as a four-legged silhouette as large as a building, with lightning running along his tusks [ch 044 / 044.05]. In combat, his tusks deliver simultaneous slashing/lightning and bludgeoning/thunder damage [ch 046 / entry]. He possesses a breath weapon that functions as a supernatural ability rather than a spell, rendering it immune to suppression by effects like *Silence* [ch 046 / entry; ch 046 / 046.01]. His combat abilities also include a supernatural Revivify trait that allows him to return to 1 hit point if killed [ch 046 / entry]. The DM confirmed his hit point pool at 74 during the siege at Falcon’s Hunting Lodge [ch 045 / entry; ch 045 / 045.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Gorthok acts as a summoning endpoint for the cult’s rituals, with correspondence stating, “The boar comes. The storm comes” [ch 044 / 044.04]. Narux’s notes indicate a requirement to “feed before unleashing” and that “the leash holds only if the stones sing” [ch 044 / 044.05]. During combat, Gorthok demonstrates high resilience, successfully resisting the *Hypnotic Pattern* enchantment and the first attempt at *Command Grovel* [ch 046 / 046.01; ch 046 / 046.03]. He dealt massive damage to Soma, with 38 damage in one attack and later 19 more that knocked her unconscious [ch 046 / 046.02; ch 046 / 046.03].

## Last Observed State
Gorthok was slain at Falcon’s Hunting Lodge during Chapter 046 [ch 046 / 046.05]. Moonbeam damage left him at 3 hit points; a hoof strike then knocked him dead, triggering his supernatural Revivify, which returned him to 1 hit point; the Moonbeam then dealt the final blow at the end of his turn [ch 046 / 046.04; ch 046 / 046.05]. By Chapter 048, the return to the lodge confirms the siege is over and the anchorite force, including Gorthok, is defeated [ch 048 / 048.06].

## Relationships
**Talosian Cult / Anchorites**: Gorthok operates in conjunction with the Anchorites of Talos, who coordinate the assault on Falcon’s Hunting Lodge and aim lightning bolts alongside his melee attacks [ch 045 / 045.05; ch 046 / 046.02].
**Narux**: Narux provided sketches and operational notes regarding Gorthok’s maintenance (“feed before unleashing”) and containment (“the leash holds”) [ch 044 / 044.05].
**Grannoc**: While not directly interacting with Grannoc, Gorthok’s summoning protocol was outlined as a fail-safe in correspondence regarding Grannoc’s failing ritual and the Woodland Manse [ch 044 / 044.04].
