## Identity
Ilsbeth Marrow is the proprietor and family matron of Marrow and Sons, a small independent shipping company operated with her four sons [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.06]. She identifies herself transparently as both the “Marrow” in the company name and the mother of the four sons [ch 057 / 057.06]. She believes that Valphine is an influential advisor to Lord Neverember [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Ilsbeth is motivated by financial survival and political advocacy for her business. Since the failure of the planar manifold, House Margaster’s control of harbor shipping has caused Marrow and Sons’ shipping fees to quadruple [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.06]. She seeks to have the party advocate for her firm and persuade Lord Neverember to treat the repair of the manifold as a matter of “royal urgency” [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.06].

She possesses knowledge regarding the manifold’s history, stating it appeared approximately ten years earlier via an arrangement made by the Commission, though she does not know its original source [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.06]. She assumes Lord Neverember might possess that knowledge [ch 057 / 057.06]. She displays transparent horror when questioned about any ties to House Margaster, a reaction Valphine accepts as genuine [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.06]. She approaches Valphine with the confidence of someone addressing a high-ranking political advisor [ch 057 / 057.06].
