## Identity
Norbus Ironrune is a Shield Dwarf prospector who excavates the ruins of a temple to Abbathor in the foothills of the Sword Mountains [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.06]. He is the partner of Dazlyn Grayshard [ch 002 / 002.06]. The two operate as amateur archaeologists who recently adopted the hobby after months of gold prospecting revealed hints of a significant historical site [ch 003 / 003.09]. Norbus justified the excavation of the dwarven dead by the fact that the temple belongs to the evil god of greed [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Norbus displayed excitement over the "Hall of Greed" and the gemstones found there [ch 003 / 003.04; ch 003 / 003.09]. He exhibits a strong desire to preserve the physical integrity of the site, warning the party against using damaging spells that might damage the interior [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.02; ch 003 / 003.05]. He is timid and risk-averse, admitting he fled from ochre jellies rather than face them, and generally stays hidden during combat while worrying about collateral damage to the ruins or asking if anyone is hurt from a safe distance [ch 002 / 002.07; ch 003 / 003.02; ch 003 / 003.03; ch 003 / moment].

Norbus is prone to exaggeration and ignorance, presenting himself as an expert on historical significance while actually being an amateur; he admits he does not know whether a monster is dangerous or easy [ch 002 / 002.07; ch 003 / 003.09; ch 003 / entry]. He possesses a superficial memory, failing to remember the name of Vukradin, the bard, even after an entire evening of the bard’s songs and stories [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / moment]. He and Dazlyn protested that they would have been useless in combat with four hit points apiece, and Norbus floated that “the museum only needs half of it” in the gemstone split [ch 003 / 003.03; ch 003 / 003.04]. He is more encouraging of Vukradin’s original musical compositions than his partner, though he ultimately prefers familiar local songs [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.02; ch 004 / moment].

## History with the Party
- Norbus Ironrune is a Shield Dwarf prospector excavating the ruins of a temple to Abbathor in the foothills of the Sword Mountains [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.06].
- He initially told the party that their excavation was private and that they were not sharing what they were doing, but warmed up after Vukradin delivered Townmaster Harbin’s warning about a white dragon [ch 002 / 002.06; ch 002 / 002.07].
- Norbus stated he would normally object to digging into the Dwarven Dead, but justified the excavation because the temple belongs to the evil god of greed [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.06].
- He expressed disbelief when Valphine claimed to be a Drow cleric of Lathander, flatly declaring she was lying despite the visible holy symbol [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.07; ch 002 / moment].
- Norbus warned the party about an ochre jelly inside the temple, admitting the prospectors ran out before facing it and that he did not know what made a monster dangerous or easy [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.06; ch 002 / 002.07].
- He offered the party a pair of sending stones as payment if they cleared the ruins of dangerous creatures, flattering the party's swords, spells, and "buff shell" [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.06; ch 002 / 002.07].
- During the battle with the ochre jellies, Norbus shouted warnings from safety about using damaging spells, concerned that they might damage the interior of the archaeological site they were trying to preserve [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.02; ch 003 / moment].
- After the jellies were defeated, Norbus and Dazlyn emerged from cover, asking if the oozes were dead and if it was safe to enter, and admitted they would have been useless in the fight with only four hit points each [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.03; ch 003 / 003.05].
- Norbus offered the party a meal, stories, and a place to rest in exchange for clearing the rest of the complex, though Vukradin conditioned his acceptance on there being no stories [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.03; ch 003 / 003.05].
- When the party discovered a cavity of dwarf skulls and a coffer of fifteen gemstones, Norbus and Dazlyn were excited and rushed in, remarking on the find's dazzle, though they nervously asked if Vukradin was serious about the items being cursed artifacts for a museum [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.04].
- Norbus suggested during the gemstone negotiation that "the museum only needs half of it," leading to a compromise where the party kept eight gems and the dwarves kept seven [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.04; ch 003 / 003.09].
- Norbus and Dazlyn admitted they were amateur archaeologists who had only recently taken up the hobby, having previously spent months prospecting for gold before finding hints of a more significant site [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.09].
- The dwarves rewarded the party with the promised sending stones after Vukradin noted they had warned them and cleared the ochre jellies, marking the job as done [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / 003.09].
- Norbus did not remember Vukradin’s name the morning after an entire evening of the bard’s songs and stories, asking "What was your name again?" [ch 003 / entry; ch 003 / moment].
- When the party returned from an orc ambush, Norbus and Dazlyn had been hiding to see if the party was alive, weighing whether to take the gem the party was carrying, before emerging with Dazlyn asking if they were there for the gem [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.02].
- Norbus was more encouraging than Dazlyn regarding Vukradin’s impromptu original flute composition about the orc battle, telling him to keep at it, though both preferred a popular local ditty [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.02; ch 004 / moment].

## Last Observed State
In Chapter 004, Norbus was located at the dwarven excavation site [ch 004 / 004.02]. When the party returned from an orc ambush, he had been hiding in the area, observing the party to determine if they were alive while weighing whether to take the green gem they were carrying [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.02]. Upon seeing the party alive and reassured that they were not there to reclaim the gem, he emerged with Dazlyn [ch 004 / 004.02]. He participated in a communal meal around a fire pit [ch 004 / 004.02]. He listened to Vukradin perform an original flute composition about the orc battle; while Dazlyn was unimpressed, Norbus was encouraging and told the bard to keep at it, though both indicated a preference for popular local ditties over original works [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.02; ch 004 / moment]. Status not established in the summaries beyond his presence at the camp during the party's long rest.

## Relationships
*   **Dazlyn Grayshard:** His excavation partner and fellow amateur archaeologist [ch 002 / 002.06; ch 003 / 003.09]. They share a professional and social partnership, jointly offering rewards and sharing meals with the party [ch 002 / 002.07; ch 003 / 003.03; ch 004 / 004.02]. In one instance Dazlyn thumped Norbus and overruled Norbus’s offer to tell stories in favor of the bard [ch 003 / 003.03; ch 003 / moment].
*   **Vukradin:** The party bard whom Norbus hired. Norbus viewed Vukradin as a useful asset who had “swords” and knew “how to cast spells” [ch 002 / 002.07]. Their relationship is transactional and slightly awkward; Norbus forgot Vukradin’s name after a night of hospitality [ch 003 / moment]. In Chapter 004, Norbus served as the polite audience for Vukradin’s performance [ch 004 / 004.02].
*   **The Party:** Hired mercenaries employed to clear the ruins of dangerous creatures [ch 002 / 002.07]. Norbus attempted to hire them with sending stones, which he successfully delivered after they cleared the ochre jellies [ch 002 / 002.07; ch 003 / 003.09]. He was wary of them during the gemstone negotiation, eyeing their weapons nervously [ch 003 / 003.04]. He and Dazlyn hid and watched to see whether the party had survived the orcs, weighing whether to flee or try to take the gem the party was carrying [ch 004 / 004.02].
