## Identity
Teega the Smith is a physically powerful woman with heavily developed arms, hands, and fingers, identifying her trade as a blacksmith [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.03]. She previously worked at the Wayside Inn, a roadside tavern complex with an attached forge near the High Road and Triboar Trail, where she repaired horseshoes and performed ironwork for travelers [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.03]. The party discovered that her brain had been manipulated through psionic means rather than conventional magic or Mind Flayer domination, though the specific source of this manipulation remains unknown [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.03].

## Personality and Motivations
Teega views herself as a victim of her circumstances and reacts defensively to criticism regarding her choices [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / moment; ch 025 / 025.03]. Prior to her capture, she suffered from blackouts, and the Sword Coast’s repeated wars and disasters left her susceptible to a cult promising peace [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.03]. A recruiter at the Wayside Inn introduced her to the cult’s promise of “peace” and “the sweet oblivion” following the Sword Coast’s wars and disasters, promising that everyone would be unified into a single whole [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.03]. Teega remembers the recruiter’s message but not his physical appearance [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.03]. She remains conflicted between the Kraken Society’s offer of peaceful bliss and the Church of Lathander’s message that pain can be endured and redeemed [ch 026 / entry; ch 026 / 026.07]. While intrigued by the idea that Lathander’s teachings might stop her bad dreams, she declined immediate recruitment, preferring to return home first [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.03].

## History with the Party
*   Teega the Smith was first encountered by the party standing rigidly by a river, appearing possessed with glassy, unfocused eyes. She spoke in a monotone about “the metal bends” and “the waves are coming” and drew a stylized spiral symbol in the mud [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.02].
*   Teega described the missing Emerald Enclave patrol as having “brittle thoughts” that were being “reformed” by the current, dismissing the party’s questions about wine as “noise” [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.02].
*   After Vukradin successfully persuaded her, Teega briefly regained lucidity and asked why she was there, before wandering into a glade where the party found the mind-controlled patrol [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.02; ch 024 / 024.03].
*   Soma tracked Teega in brown bear form, accompanied by Valphine, and knocked her unconscious without killing her. Teega was then restrained for transport [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.05].
*   The party transported Teega to the Falcon Hunting Lodge along with other prisoners and rescued companions, where Falcon greeted the party with surprise [ch 025 / 025.02].
*   Teega was revealed to be a blacksmith who worked at the Wayside Inn. She remembered working there before awakening in a cart and admitted to suffering blackouts prior to her capture [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.03].
*   Teega recalled that a traveling man at the inn introduced her to the Kraken cult, which promised “peace” and “the sweet oblivion” following the Sword Coast’s wars and disasters. She could not remember the recruiter’s appearance [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.03].
*   Medical examinations determined that Teega’s brain had been manipulated through psionic means rather than conventional magic or Mind Flayer domination, though the specific source remained unknown [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.03].
*   Teega was disturbed by the display of a severed Kraken Society cultist head and the church’s new sacrament of intentional blinding and healing [ch 026 / entry; ch 026 / 026.07].
*   Teega listened to Valphine’s sermon and compared the Kraken Society’s offer of “peaceful bliss” with Valphine’s message that pain could be endured and redeemed. She remained conflicted and returned to her home rather than converting immediately [ch 026 / entry; ch 026 / 026.07].

## Last Observed State
Teega listened to Valphine’s sermon at the partially reconstructed Church of Lathander and was visibly disturbed by the display of a severed Kraken Society cultist head on a pike, as well as the congregation’s new sacrament of intentional blinding and healing via Lesser Restoration [ch 026 / entry; ch 026 / 026.07]. Although the theatrical nature of the sermon unsettled her, Valphine’s message forced her to reflect, comparing the Kraken’s promise of oblivious peace with Lathander’s promise of redeeming pain [ch 026 / entry; ch 026 / 026.07]. She remained conflicted rather than converting immediately and departed from the church to return to her home [ch 026 / entry; ch 026 / 026.07]. Status not established in the summaries beyond her departure to home.

## Relationships
*   **Vukradin:** Successfully persuaded Teega to briefly regain lucidity from her trance [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.02]. He later recounted her rescue story, and his charm helped ease her defensiveness; he offered her work as a blacksmith for the church, which she declined for the moment, saying she might visit the church later [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.03].
*   **Soma:** Tracked Teega in brown bear form and subdued her non-lethally to restrain her for transport [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.05].
*   **Valphine:** Accompanied Soma during the capture of Teega [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.05]. Valphine harshly challenged Teega’s willingness to follow the Kraken cult, leading to a confrontation where Teega insisted she was a victim [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.03]. Teega was disturbed by Valphine’s sermon and the church’s new sacrament but was forced to reflect on Valphine’s message regarding pain [ch 026 / entry; ch 026 / 026.07].
*   **Falcon:** Greeted the party at the Falcon Hunting Lodge when they arrived with Teega, other prisoners, and rescued companions; no exchange with Teega is recorded [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.02].
