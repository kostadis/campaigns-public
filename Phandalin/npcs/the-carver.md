## Identity
The Carver is the true identity of a sentient, black obsidian sword containing a mental amplifier crystal [ch 038 / entry; ch 039 / 039.04]. It used a human veteran as a vessel, and Ser Kaelen described the Carver as hiding his identity behind a name rather than exposing himself to divination [ch 013 / 013.05; ch 038 / entry]. Corbin, who never met him, relayed a second-hand description of the Carver as a thick, powerful man with broad shoulders, a trunk-like neck, and a stooped posture [ch 006 / 006.08]. The sword itself is capable of projecting music directly into minds and was nicknamed the “Bluetooth Sword” by the bard Vukradin [ch 039 / 039.04]. The entity’s essence was permanently dissipated when the white dragon Cryovain died during a failed domination ritual [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.02].

## Personality and Motivations
The Carver engineered the Butterskull Ranch attack specifically to generate a quest that would lure adventurers into an ambush, and the captured brigand Skippy called how the Carver anticipated the party “boringly predictable” [ch 017 / 017.03; ch 018 / 018.03]. The entity demanded increasing amounts of resources from its subordinates and was rumored to keep its identity hidden behind a name while its crew carried out organized raids [ch 006 / 006.07; ch 013 / entry]. In its final phase, the Carver abandoned secrecy, marching alone toward Icespire Hold to possess Cryovain and use the dragon to burn Phandalin, driven by a desire for revenge [ch 036 / entry; ch 037 / 037.03]. During the confrontation the sword spoke into the party’s minds: “Coward’s magic, you mute the ritual because you lack the strength to shatter the vessel,” before declaring, “And now, now I shall have everything I always wanted” [ch 038 / moment].

## Relationships
**Subordinates and Lieutenants:** Sister Kaella served as the Carver’s spymaster in Phandalin before defecting to assist the party against him [ch 017 / 017.03; ch 019 / 019.10]. Borg the Hammer acted as the Carver’s half-orc enforcer [ch 017 / 017.03]. Vukradin disrupted the Carver’s final ritual at Icespire Hold by casting *Silence* over the summoning circle [ch 038 / 038.04]. Brewbarry killed the Carver’s paralyzed human vessel after the entity dissipated, rejecting Soma’s plea that the man was merely a victim [ch 039 / 039.03]. The party currently possesses the physical sword recovered from the Carver’s defeat [ch 039 / 039.04; ch 056 / 056.02].

**External Actors:** Ser Kaelen denied being the Carver, and the GM confirmed he was telling the truth, though the party initially suspected him [ch 010 / 010.03; ch 013 / 013.05]. Sister Kaella hoped that killing the Carver’s lieutenants would force him into the open and said the party would then kill him [ch 019 / 019.10].
