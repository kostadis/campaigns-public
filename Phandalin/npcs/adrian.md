## Identity

Adrian is a well-dressed House Margaster representative who arrived with a contract to take over Cullen Sharpe's business after Cullen's death. His identity as a Margaster agent was confirmed by the GM; no connection to another same-named person is inferred [ch 060 / entry; ch 060 / 060.06].

In Chapter 62 the party directly encountered Adrian as a devil in fine, fashionable clothing alongside Cullen Sharpe [ch 062 / entry; ch 062 / 062.09].

## Personality and Motivations

He showed little concern for the bodies and remarked that he had warned Cullen to be careful. The brief encounter establishes his manner at this arrival, without establishing any broader private motive [ch 060 / 060.06].

At that meeting he treated the warehouse violence as settled business, said the promotion suited them, and reminded Cullen that he had predicted Valphine’s refusal. His private reason for wanting the requested name is not established [ch 062 / 062.09].

## History with the Party
- Adrian arrived after Cullen was killed, carrying a contract and announcing that he was taking over Cullen's business [ch 060 / 060.06].
- The party killed him before negotiations developed. Aldus Hern later saw the bodies of both Margaster representatives at the warehouse [ch 060 / 060.06; ch 060 / 060.07].

- The Seat later described the party's two Margaster killings as promotions, saying special partner contracts continue through death and return slain agents as infernal beings. The two agents are contextually Adrian and Cullen; the party did not witness such a return [ch 060 / 060.06; ch 061 / 061.06].
- Mnemazar mentioned Adrian's terrible week while dealing with Valphine and Brewbarry at the warehouse office; this was a reference to Adrian, not an appearance by him [ch 062 / 062.03].
- The party subsequently met Adrian and Cullen Sharpe as visibly identifiable devils. Adrian treated the earlier warehouse violence as past business and said the promotion suited them [ch 062 / 062.09].
- Adrian assisted Cullen during the patronage offer to Valphine. After she refused, both expressed mild disappointment, and Adrian reminded Cullen that he had predicted her decision. No bargain was signed [ch 062 / entry; ch 062 / 062.09].
