## Identity
Sabbatha is identified as a patron of the Common Chord who contributed to Vukradin’s studio fund [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / 054.01]. She is one of several individuals recorded for future program credits alongside Wick, Old Hesp, and Ondrelle Kass [ch 054 / 054.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Sabbatha’s behavior during the fundraising pitch was that she spent much of the conversation admiring Brewbarry’s imposing physique and dedication to his look [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / 054.01]. She openly admired Brewbarry’s “imposing physique” and dedication to his distinctive aesthetic, which inspired descriptions of him as “bulging” and “rippling” [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / 054.01]. She contributed while admiring Brewbarry’s dedication to his distinctive look [ch 054 / 054.01].
