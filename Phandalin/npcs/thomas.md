## Identity
Thomas is an agent and ally of Lyra within the Emerald Enclave [ch 041 / entry; ch 041 / 041.01]. He operates within the local chapter of the organization, serving as a trusted liaison for Lyra [ch 041 / entry]. He told the party the Enclave lacks a meaningful top-down hierarchy [ch 041 / 041.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Thomas, one of Lyra’s agents, warned the party that Adabra was seeking to ostracize Lyra [ch 041 / entry; ch 041 / 041.01]. He possesses a pragmatic understanding of the Enclave’s decentralized nature, expressing this with the statement: “This is the Emerald Enclave. We don’t really do authority” [ch 041 / 041.01]. He is direct and prideful regarding Lyra’s chapter’s actions, testifying openly that they failed to handle a metastasized treant and expressing pride in the party’s intervention rather than shame [ch 041 / 041.02]. After repeated questioning from Vukradin about fetching Reidoth, Thomas became exasperated, stating, “Look, if you want to go fetch Reidoth, you can go fetch Reidoth. By the time you fetch him, Adabra will have had her meeting and had her say” [ch 041 / 041.01].

## History with the Party
- Thomas is an agent and ally of Lyra who intercepted the party on the road to the Emerald Enclave council to warn them of Adabra’s political maneuvering [ch 041 / entry; ch 041 / 041.01].
- He informed the party that Adabra could not formally ban Lyra but could orchestrate her ostracism, and that Adabra had called the council meeting specifically during the absence of Reidoth, the nominal head of the local Enclave chapter [ch 041 / 041.01].
- Thomas explained that the Enclave lacks a meaningful top-down hierarchy to halt proceedings, stating, “This is the Emerald Enclave. We don’t really do authority,” and warned that fetching Reidoth would take too long to prevent Adabra from making her case [ch 041 / 041.01].
- During the council meeting, Thomas testified that Lyra’s chapter had tried and failed to deal with the metastasized treant before the party intervened, openly expressing pride in that intervention rather than shame [ch 041 / entry; ch 041 / 041.02].
- He became increasingly exasperated by Vukradin’s questioning regarding Enclave hierarchy, telling Vukradin, “Look, if you want to go fetch Reidoth, you can go fetch Reidoth. By the time you fetch him, Adabra will have had her meeting and had her say” [ch 041 / entry; ch 041 / 041.01].
- In a later Sending spell, Vukradin invoked an analogy involving “Justice Thomas’s wife” to demand an apology from Falcon for Falcon’s conduct at the Druid Council, though this reference alluded to a real-world figure rather than the NPC Thomas [ch 045 / 045.02].

## Last Observed State
Status not established in the summaries. ## Relationships
**Lyra**: Thomas serves as one of her trusted agents and allies [ch 041 / entry; ch 041 / 041.01]. He shares her belief that the party’s intervention regarding the treant was correct and supported her position during the council testimony [ch 041 / 041.02].
**Adabra**: Thomas views Adabra as a political threat capable of ostracizing Lyra, though not formally banning her [ch 041 / 041.01]. He recognized that Adabra scheduled the council meeting during Reidoth’s absence to push her agenda without opposition [ch 041 / 041.01].
**Vukradin**: Thomas interacted directly with Vukradin on the road, warning him of the political trap [ch 041 / 041.01]. He grew exasperated with Vukradin’s persistent questions about Enclave hierarchy and the feasibility of summoning Reidoth [ch 041 / 041.01].
**Reidoth**: Thomas identifies Reidoth as the nominal head of the local Enclave chapter but notes that, with no strict hierarchy, even if Reidoth were present he could not stop Adabra from speaking [ch 041 / 041.01].
