## Identity
Alfonse “Big Al” Kalazorn is the owner of Butterskull Ranch and a retired sheriff of Triboar, specifically the predecessor to Darathra [ch 018 / 018.07; ch 018 / entry]. He is a large man, described as a retired veteran [ch 018 / entry]. Initially introduced as a target of an orc raid, he was discovered tied to a chair in his own cellar, held alive as bait for the party [ch 017 / 017.04; ch 018 / 018.06]. His ranch was subsequently destroyed by fire after the failed ambush [ch 017 / 017.06; ch 027 / entry], leaving him financially struggling but still actively engaged in local commerce through cheese production [ch 027 / entry; ch 049 / 049.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Big Al is primarily motivated by the welfare of his prized cow, Petunia, whom he values above physical assets or personal safety [ch 017 / 017.05; ch 018 / 018.07]. Upon his rescue, his immediate distress was not for himself but for the missing cow, stating, "I really need my cow," and "She’s my prized possession" [ch 018 / 018.07]. He is willing to trade valuable equipment, such as his Mithral Chain Mail, solely to ensure her return [ch 017 / 017.05].

He possesses a degree of pragmatic honesty regarding his possessions; when questioned about the provenance of his mithral armor, he simply stated, "I bought it," which Vukradin verified as legitimate [ch 018 / 018.07]. However, Qelline Alderleaf accused him of being too cowardly to come and negotiate with her himself after he sent the party in his place [ch 027 / 027.03]. He is emotionally reserved about his past, deflecting questions about his relationship history as "a personal matter" until pressured by a successful persuasion check, at which point he confessed to abandoning Qelline the night before their wedding [ch 027 / 027.04]. He is also shown to be responsive to economic incentives; he agreed to apologize and reconcile with Qelline primarily when convinced that it would secure a reliable buyer (Don-Jon Raskin) for his cheese, providing the funds needed to rebuild [ch 027 / entry; ch 027 / 027.04]. He participates in community events, such as cheering for political changes in Phandalin [ch 049 / 049.03].

## Relationships
**Petunia:** His prized cow. Petunia stated, "Big Al takes care of me very good," though she missed the alfalfa from his burned ranch [ch 027 / 027.02]. Her milk was central to the party’s plan to supply cheese to the wererats at the gold mine [ch 027 / entry].

**Qelline Alderleaf:** A former fiancée whom Big Al abandoned the night before their wedding, an act he attributed to not being ready to settle down [ch 027 / 027.04]. Qelline initially harbored deep resentment, stating the one-gold-piece price was intentionally high to hurt him financially [ch 027 / 027.03]. Under pressure from the party, Big Al confessed that he had left her the night before their wedding and went to her farm to apologize, after which he and Qelline shut the party out [ch 027 / 027.04]. The planned arrangement had Qelline providing grazing, Petunia providing milk, Big Al making cheese, and Don-Jon buying it [ch 027 / 027.04]. By Chapter 049, Qelline hosted a cheese and wine tasting that Big Al attended [ch 049 / 049.04].

**The Party (Vukradin, Soma, Valphine, Brewbarry):** The party rescued him from Borg the Hammer’s ambush [ch 017 / 017.04]. Vukradin verified his mithral armor before accepting the deal to rescue Petunia [ch 018 / 018.07]. The party pressed Big Al about his past with Qelline after laying out the business case for Don-Jon becoming a consistent cheese buyer [ch 027 / 027.04]. Townmaster Harbin Wester credited the party with healing community pain via this matchmaking [ch 032 / 032.06]. Big Al was in the crowd that cheered at Vukradin’s town green gathering and attended the cheese tasting [ch 049 / 049.03].

**Borg the Hammer:** The half-orc leader who orchestrated the ambush at Butterskull Ranch. Borg intended to kill Big Al and frame the party, but Big Al survived, thwarting Sister Kaella’s plot [ch 017 / 017.04; ch 019 / 019.09].
