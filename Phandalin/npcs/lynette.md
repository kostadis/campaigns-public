## Identity
Lynette is the sharp-eyed proprietor of Cinderwick Hearth and Loaf, a dockside bakery in Neverwinter, and a member of Lathander's church who attended Valphine's sermon. She is the distinct baker confirmed by the GM, not Linene Graywind [ch 061 / entry; ch 061 / 061.03].

## Personality and Motivations
She pursues celebrity endorsement with practical commercial ingenuity, paying a boy to steer the party to her shop and turning Valphine's sermon into Lathander's Death Pie. She raises a skin condition when the party promotes sunburn and pain, and proposes a special cayenne blend with unpredictable heat [ch 061 / 061.03].

## History with the Party
- She paid a boy six coppers to guide the party past her bakery, then sought an endorsement for her pies [ch 061 / 061.03].
- Valphine tasted Lathander's Death Pie, approved it on her own paper and sealed the endorsement as Bishop. Lynette displayed the endorsement behind the counter. Vukradin separately made his endorsement conditional on visiting the church and completing its rites; no completed baptism is recorded [ch 061 / 061.03].
- Lynette agreed to a charitable pie offering and clean bathrobes on consignment. The party promised stock later; delivery is not recorded [ch 061 / 061.03].

## Last Observed State
At Cinderwick Hearth and Loaf with Valphine's endorsement displayed. She has agreed to the charitable offering and consignment arrangement and awaits the promised clean bathrobes. The record establishes no completed baptism or delivery of stock [ch 061 / 061.03].

## Relationships
**Valphine:** Her sermon inspired the pie and her sealed endorsement now supports the bakery. **Vukradin:** Offers his endorsement on a religious condition. **Brewbarry and the party:** Partners in a proposed clean-bathrobe consignment and charitable pie offering [ch 061 / 061.03].
