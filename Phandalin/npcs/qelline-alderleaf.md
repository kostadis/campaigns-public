## Identity
Qelline Alderleaf is a local farmer and cheese producer in Phandalin who supplies goods to local businesses and the Mountain’s Toe Gold Mine [ch 027 / entry; ch 032 / entry]. She owns land suitable for grazing and alfalfa production, which she manages with an eye toward both agricultural utility and commercial value [ch 027 / 027.03].

## Personality and Motivations
Qelline is characterized by a sharp tongue.
*   **Resentment and Retribution:** She harbors deep resentment toward Big Al due to past personal betrayal and business competition that damaged her farm’s income [ch 027 / entry]. She openly expressed anger when he failed to face her personally, sending intermediaries instead [ch 027 / moment]. Her initial pricing strategy was explicitly punitive, described as an “I-want-it-to-hurt-for-him price” [ch 027 / moment].
*   **Quality and Snobbery:** When Valphine asked for “the regular one”, Qelline muttered, “Regular cheese. Philistines” [ch 049 / 049.04]. She called Brewbarry a “discerning customer” [ch 049 / 049.04].
*   **Motivations:** She resented Big Al, set a punitive one-gold-piece fee, and offered to reduce it to five silver pieces per week only if he apologized to her personally [ch 027 / 027.03].

## History with the Party
- In Chapter 027, Big Al mentioned that Qelline Alderleaf had offered to let his cow Petunia graze on her land for one gold piece per week, though he lacked the funds to pay [ch 027 / 027.02].
- The party approached Qelline to negotiate grazing fees for Petunia; she recognized they were sent by Big Al and expressed anger at his cowardice for not coming himself [ch 027 / 027.03].
- Qelline revealed that Big Al’s past success at Butterskull Ranch had flooded the local market and damaged her farm’s income, stating her initial high fee was an “I-want-it-to-hurt-for-him price” [ch 027 / 027.03; ch 027 / moment].
- After the party negotiated and concealed the connection to the gold mine, Qelline agreed to reduce the grazing fee to five silver pieces per week, but imposed the condition that Big Al must apologize to her personally [ch 027 / entry; ch 027 / 027.03].
- In Chapter 032, Townmaster Harbin Wester informed the party that Qelline Alderleaf’s cheese was ready for delivery to the Mountain’s Toe Gold Mine, but no one else was willing to transport it due to threats from dragons and orcs [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.06].
- Harbin Wester praised the party for their matchmaking between Qelline and Big Al, noting it had healed a long-standing source of pain in the community [ch 032 / entry; ch 032 / 032.06].
- In Chapter 049, Qelline Alderleaf was present on the town green when Vukradin delivered a speech declaring the creation of Universal Basic Treasure (UBT) [ch 049 / 049.03].
- Later in Chapter 049, Qelline hosted the party for a lengthy wine and cheese tasting; when Brewbarry correctly selected a creamy blue cheese, she declared him a discerning customer [ch 049 / entry; ch 049 / 049.04].
- Qelline agreed to set aside a fine round of the creamy blue cheese for Aletra and her assistants to collect [ch 049 / entry; ch 049 / 049.04].

## Relationships
*   **Big Al:** Qelline’s relationship with Big Al is complex, shifting from bitter resentment to a functional, albeit conditional, partnership. They share a history of personal betrayal (abandonment) and economic rivalry. The party mediated a reconciliation, leading to a grazing agreement contingent on a personal apology [ch 027 / 027.03; ch 032 / entry]. Townmaster Harbin Wester noted this reconciliation had healed a “long-standing source of pain in the community” [ch 032 / entry]. By Chapter 049, they appeared to interact cordially enough for her to host him, and Big Al admitted ignorance of Brewbarry’s taste, to which Qelline reacted with mild surprise but no hostility [ch 049 / 049.04].
*   **The Party (Vukradin, Brewbarry, Soma, Valphine):** Initially, Qelline was dismissive of the party, particularly Vukradin, whom she called a “pretty-faced bard” sent as a cowardly stand-in [ch 027 / moment]. She was skeptical of their motives regarding Petunia. However, through negotiation and shared business interactions, the relationship improved. By Chapter 049, she hosted them for a social event and respected Brewbarry’s palate [ch 049 / 049.04].
*   **Harbin Wester:** In Chapter 032, Harbin told the party that Qelline’s cheese was ready but nobody else wished to transport it through territory threatened by dragons and orcs [ch 032 / 032.06].
