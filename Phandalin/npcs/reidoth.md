## Identity
Reidoth is the nominal head of the local Emerald Enclave chapter [ch 041 / entry]. He lacks the hierarchical authority to prevent other members, such as Adabra, from speaking or pushing agendas during council meetings [ch 041 / entry; ch 041 / 041.01]. The Emerald Enclave does not operate through a strict top-down hierarchy [ch 041 / 041.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Reidoth favors consensus, discussion, and open communication [ch 041 / entry; ch 041 / 041.01].
