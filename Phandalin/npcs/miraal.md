## Identity
Miraal is the ghost of a sea elf, identifiable by her gills, webbed hands and feet, and spectral garments that drift as if she were underwater [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.06]. She was a spellcaster who utilized an opalescent conch as her focus [ch 010 / entry; ch 012 / 012.01]. Miraal investigated the Tower of Storms after its beacon began pulsing with an unnatural green light, leading to her murder by Moesko, a half-orc anchorite of Talos [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.06]. Her spirit was bound to a nearby cave, compelled to demand the return of her stolen conch from passersby [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.06]. In life, she bestowed intelligence upon a giant crab known as Crabby [ch 010 / 010.05; ch 010 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Miraal is driven by a curse that compels her to retrieve her spellcasting focus; she kills those who refuse her demand [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.06]. Despite this violent compulsion, she expresses kindness and gratitude to those who assist her, such as the party [ch 010 / 010.06; ch 012 / 012.05]. She is observant and analytical, noting that Moesko’s attack was unusually sophisticated for orcish cultists, suggesting external guidance from a larger organization or powerful figure, though she could not identify the specific leader known as the Carver [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.06]. She identified Moesko’s lack of a heart as a disturbing detail [ch 010 / 010.06]. Even in death, she remained perceptive to supernatural disturbances, hearing fragmented whispers from the deep and sensing foreign shadows twisting Moesko’s devotion to Talos into something colder [ch 012 / entry; ch 012 / 012.05].

## Last Observed State
Miraal’s spirit has found peace and vanished after the party recovered her opalescent conch from Moesko’s body and returned it to her [ch 012 / entry; ch 012 / 012.05]. Before dissipating, she thanked the party and delivered a final warning that “The true storm gathers not in the sky, but in the depths below Leilon” [ch 012 / 012.05; ch 012 / moment]. She revealed that Moesko had not acted entirely of his own will but was influenced by a foreign force [ch 012 / 012.05; ch 012 / moment]. Her spirit’s departure ended her haunting, and the DM confirmed that the tower’s curse had ended after Moesko’s death [ch 012 / 012.05].
