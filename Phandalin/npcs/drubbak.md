## Identity
Drubbak is an elderly orc known as "Old Drubbak" [ch 045 / entry; ch 045 / 045.05]. He is characterized physically by leaning on a spear, which he uses like a walking stick [ch 045 / entry]. His spiritual sensitivity is suggested by his ability to sense the absence of a ritual chant that should accompany a storm of such intensity [ch 045 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Drubbak displays a quiet, observant demeanor, acknowledging the dawn with a single word rather than fanfare [ch 045 / entry]. He possesses a heightened sensitivity to magical or spiritual disturbances, evidenced by his immediate recognition that the unnatural storm had come wrong: “It come wrong. There is no chant. Where is the chant?” [ch 045 / 045.05; ch 045 / moment]. This reaction suggests a background of significant spiritual experience or ritual knowledge [ch 045 / entry]. He tends to go very still when detecting a threat or anomaly [ch 045 / 045.05; ch 045 / moment].
