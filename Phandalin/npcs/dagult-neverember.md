## Identity
Dagult Neverember is the Lord Protector of Neverwinter and a member of the Lords’ Alliance council [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.01]. He previously held the leading position in the Alliance but lost it during the War of the Dragons [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.01]. He is a major investor in the Neverwinter Commission, a secret body responsible for trade and revenue, and the majority holder of this entity [ch 052 / 052.05; ch 056 / 056.05]. His government oversees the tax system in Phandalin and funds quests by hiring adventurers as contractors who retain found treasure as tax-free income [ch 002 / entry; ch 027 / entry]. He is attempting to rebuild Neverwinter into a "new Neverwinter" following decades of catastrophe and corruption, a project financed by heavy taxation [ch 027 / entry; ch 049 / entry]. He officially declared the death of Lady Elara Meliamne a tragic accident, ordering the city to mourn and move on despite contradictory reports [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.03]. Rumors suggest he has acquired a new Drow advisor, though the Harpers know of no Drow in his inner circle [ch 053 / 053.07; ch 054 / entry; ch 055 / 055.02]. Status not established in the summaries [ch 050 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Neverember is pragmatic, fiscally oriented, and focused on maintaining control over Neverwinter’s hinterlands and economy. He views adventurers who hoard treasure as economically damaging "dragons" and emphasizes the need to "circulate the wealth and not hoard it" [ch 052 / 052.03; ch 057 / 057.07]. He prefers contractors over permanent staff because they have fewer benefits and at dinner produced a written decree appointing Vukradin mayor [ch 002 / moment; ch 057 / 057.07]. He is interested in the party’s cultural and economic influence, having followed their activities with considerable interest and citing the Candlekeep Report on adventuring parties [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.07]. He is wary of external powers; he appointed Vukradin as mayor of Phandalin specifically to prevent Waterdeep from installing a more convenient authority near Neverwinter’s border [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.07]. He is polite but firm, maintaining European-style hospitality while avoiding business until appropriate, yet he reacts with personal displeasure to unpleasant smells or disruptive behavior [ch 057 / 057.07; ch 057 / moment]. He supports the concept of Universal Basic Treasure only if the financial ledgers balance, refusing to alter Neverwinter’s financial system based on philosophical arguments alone [ch 057 / 057.07; ch 057 / moment].

## Last Observed State
In Chapter 057, Neverember hosted a formal dinner at his estate for the party [ch 057 / 057.07]. He received the party after they were late due to other obligations, as confirmed by his steward Aldus [ch 057 / 057.06]. During the dinner, Neverember expressed approval of Vukradin’s Universal Basic Treasure proposal insofar as the numbers supported his pre-existing intent [ch 057 / 057.07]. He produced a written decree appointing Vukradin as the mayor of Phandalin, replacing Harbin Wester, to secure Neverwinter’s border against Waterdeep’s influence [ch 057 / 057.07; ch 057 / moment]. He tasked the party with protecting Phandalin by assisting with ongoing work in Leilon [ch 057 / 057.07]. When asked about the planar manifold, he admitted he did not know its origins, assuming it was a magical object purchased by the Commission [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.07]. He expressed surprise that the manifold might be repairable, as the Commission had previously insisted it could not be fixed [ch 057 / 057.07]. He promised to ask around for replacement parts and directed the party to speak with the Commission or its Factor [ch 057 / 057.07]. Midway through the meal, when the magical cleaning effects on the party’s clothes wore off, revealing a sewer stench, Neverember offered fresh clothes, clarifying the offer was "not so much for you, it’s for me" [ch 057 / 057.07; ch 057 / moment]. He attempted to explain conventional cutlery to Brewbarry, who used combat blades to eat, damaging fine dining ware [ch 057 / 057.07]. The party departed holding the mayoral appointment and the Leilon mission [ch 057 / 057.07].
