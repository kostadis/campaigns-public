## Identity
Lady Tanamere Alagondar is a figure from the past whom Boney believes he was brought back to help [ch 035 / entry; ch 035 / 035.02]. She is the owner of the barrow in the Dragon Barrow dungeon where the Dragon Slayer Sword was hidden; Boney recalled that he was supposed to be buried “across the way” from her [ch 035 / 035.02; ch 036 / 036.01]. The Necklace of Fireballs was recovered from her tomb [ch 051 / 051.05]. the party convinced Boney that Soma was Lady Alagondar reincarnated [ch 035 / 035.02; ch 035 / moment]. A spectral figure of Lady Alagondar appeared to the party after they secured the Dragon Slayer Sword [ch 036 / entry; ch 036 / 036.03].

## Personality and Motivations
As a ghost, Lady Alagondar is sorrowful and non-aggressive. She does not speak but communicates through gestures, such as staring toward the Icespire Mountains, where the white dragon was reputed to dwell [ch 036 / entry; ch 036 / 036.03]. She is associated with the Alagondar lineage, which Perrin Alagondar claims as the "first family of Neverwinter" [ch 051 / 051.05]. Boney, the skeletal horse who had once served Lady Alagondar, was loyal to her memory [ch 035 / 035.02; ch 051 / 051.06].

## History with the Party
*   Boney, a skeletal horse and loyal companion to the party, serves as the primary link to Lady Tanamere Alagondar, whom he believes has been reincarnated as the party member Soma [ch 035 / 035.02; ch 035 / entry].
*   Lady Alagondar is the figure Boney expected to return; he said he had held onto his sentience for years because he believed she would return, and recalled he was supposed to be buried “across the way” from her [ch 035 / 035.02].
*   A spectral figure of Lady Tanamere Alagondar appeared to the party after they secured the Dragon Slayer Sword, silently staring toward the Icespire Mountains, where the white dragon was reputed to dwell [ch 036 / entry; ch 036 / 036.03].
*   During an encounter with Perrin Alagondar, who claimed descent from the Alagondar line, Boney noted that the Necklace of Fireballs had been recovered from Lady Alagondar’s own barrow and pointed out holes in Perrin’s story that his family had lost it to grave robbers a generation ago, while allowing that Perrin might have a rightful claim if he was an Alagondar [ch 051 / 051.05; ch 051 / 051.06].
*   Boney’s skeletal tack, worn by the horse, was recognized by a stranger in Neverwinter, who incorrectly speculated on the horse’s lineage; Boney answered that he had been “grazing all over the place” [ch 053 / 053.02].

## Last Observed State
The last confirmed manifestation of Lady Tanamere Alagondar was as a ghost in Chapter 036. She appeared silently after the party secured the Dragon Slayer Sword, looking at them sadly before staring toward the Icespire Mountains [ch 036 / entry; ch 036 / 036.03]. She did not attack, speak, or provide precise locations, only a general direction [ch 036 / 036.03]. She was not physically present or mentioned as active in Chapters 051 or 053; references in these chapters are to her tomb, her lineage, or her belongings (Boney's tack) [ch 051 / 051.05; ch 053 / 053.02].

## Relationships
**Boney**: A skeletal horse who was once her steed. He believes Soma is her reincarnation and accepts this despite her different appearance [ch 035 / 035.02; ch 035 / moment]. He still wears her tackle, which a man outside Neverwinter half-recognized in Chapter 053 [ch 053 / 053.02]. He held onto sentience for centuries believing she would return [ch 035 / 035.02].
**Soma**: Identified by Boney as Lady Alagondar reincarnated. Boney bonded with Soma on first meeting, before she was presented as Lady Alagondar [ch 035 / 035.02; ch 036 / 036.01].
**Perrin Alagondar**: A man who claims to be an Alagondar descendant and the owner of the Necklace of Fireballs. Boney pointed out holes in Perrin's story by citing the necklace's location in Lady Alagondar's tomb [ch 051 / 051.05; ch 051 / 051.06].
**Vukradin**: Vukradin attempted to deceive Boney into believing that Soma was Lady Alagondar, and Soma offered reincarnation as the explanation [ch 035 / 035.02]. He asked Boney about the ghost of Lady Alagondar in Chapter 036 [ch 036 / 036.03].
