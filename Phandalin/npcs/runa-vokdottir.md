## Identity
Runa Vokdottir is a muscular woman with black hair and cold blue eyes [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.05]. She is identified as the sister of Syleen Wintermoon [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.05]. During damage resolution in combat, the DM explicitly referred to her as Runa Vokdottir [ch 039 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Runa appears motivated by a shared desire for the treasure claimed by herself and Syleen [ch 039 / 039.05]. In the dragon’s lair she supported Syleen’s claim by echoing religious flattery directed at the party member Vukradin, stating, “Blessed be… blessed be!” [ch 039 / 039.05]. When negotiations failed, she joined the attack against the party [ch 039 / entry].
