## Identity
Alducia is an apparently fictional personage invented on the spot by the steward Aldus. She is described as Aldus’s daughter [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.07]. Her name is the feminine form of her father’s name, a detail the party immediately recognized as suspicious [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.07]. She apparently does not exist; Aldus, too embarrassed to accept the autograph for himself, claimed it was for her [ch 050 / 050.07].

## Personality and Motivations
Because Alducia appears to be a fabrication, she possesses no independent personality or motivations. She functions solely as a prop for Aldus, who used her name to bypass his own embarrassment about being a fan of Vukradin [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.07].

## History with the Party
- Alducia is the fictional name invented by Aldus, a steward of the Lord Protector, when embarrassedly declining an autograph for himself and claiming it was for his daughter [ch 050 / 050.07; ch 050 / entry].
- Vukradin signed the autograph to Alducia “with love,” though the party immediately recognized the name as suspiciously close to Aldus’s own and likely fabricated on the spot [ch 050 / 050.07; ch 050 / entry].

## Last Observed State
Whether Alducia really exists is unresolved. She was observed only in the context of the autograph signed to her “with love” by Vukradin at the Moonstone Mask [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.07]. Valphine later instructed Aldus to ensure his “daughter” knew about the dawn performance at the Spire of the Morninglord, though this applies to Aldus as the recipient of the information [ch 050 / 050.07]. Status not established in the summaries for an entity that does not exist.

## Relationships
*   **Aldus:** Created her as a fictional daughter to accept Vukradin’s autograph [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.07]. Aldus stammered out the name “Alducia” when asked for his daughter’s name [ch 050 / 050.07].
*   **Vukradin:** Signed an autograph to Alducia, realizing the name was invented on the spot [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.07].
