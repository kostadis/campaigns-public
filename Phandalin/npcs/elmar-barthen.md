## Identity
Elmar Barthen is the proprietor of Barthen’s Provisions, a general store located in Phandalin [ch 019 / entry; ch 020 / entry]. He is a merchant who deals in goods such as dried meats, cheese, bread, ale, and oil [ch 020 / 020.03]. He is also a civic leader, having been appointed to the Phandalin privy council alongside Harbin, Toblen, Halia, Linene, and Adabra [ch 019 / 019.07]. He employs two clerks named Ander and Thistle [ch 019 / 019.05]. He is a good friend of Falcon, the proprietor of the Falcon Hunting Lodge [ch 020 / 020.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Barthen is characterized by a pragmatic, business-first mindset. When the party offered him animal pelts with specific planar significance, he offered a "paltry sum," failing to recognize or care about the unique value of the goods [ch 016 / entry; ch 016 / 016.07]. When Brewbarry objected that the crates held no wine, he recommended the Falcon Hunting Lodge, known for fine wine [ch 020 / 020.03].

He could not accompany the party because he had to manage his store [ch 020 / 020.03]. He provided them with supplies, a cart, and an ox named Vincent for a delivery, expecting the assets to be returned [ch 020 / 020.03]. He wrote a signed letter of introduction to his friend Falcon, verifying the party’s standing and ensuring they received a warm welcome and fine wine [ch 020 / 020.03; ch 020 / 020.04]. As a member of the privy council, he participates in the governance of Phandalin, though the summaries do not detail his specific political stances beyond his presence in the body [ch 019 / 019.07; ch 049 / 049.03].
