## Identity
Locutus is an orc [ch 019 / entry; ch 037 / 037.02]. He was working with Valphine and killed Borg [ch 019 / entry; ch 037 / 037.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Locutus came to collect payment for killing Borg [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.10]. He exhibited significant fear of Valphine, apparently anticipating that she intended to betray him by selling him into the Underdark [ch 019 / entry]. Valphine saw that Locutus looked terrified and thought it was good [ch 019 / 019.08]. There is no evidence regarding his personality beyond this fear and his role as a mercenary/killer [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.09].

## History with the Party
- Locutus is an orc working with Valphine who killed Borg [ch 019 / entry].
- Valphine assigned Corbin to watch Locutus while they waited outside town, noting Locutus looked terrified [ch 019 / 019.08].
- Locutus led Valphine to an abandoned building at the edge of town to meet Sister Kaella [ch 019 / 019.09].
- When Sister Kaella asked if he had killed Borg, Locutus nodded [ch 019 / 019.09].
- Sister Kaella immediately struck Locutus down with her offhand dagger after confirming his identity [ch 019 / 019.09].
- The fee intended for Locutus was forfeited and offered to Valphine as payment for cooperation [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.10].
- Xanth the Centaur later stated that Borg was killed by Locutus in the forest after fleeing Butterskull Ranch [ch 037 / 037.02].

## Last Observed State
Locutus is dead. In Chapter 019, he led Valphine to an abandoned building at the edge of town to meet Sister Kaella [ch 019 / 019.09]. After confirming to Kaella that he had killed Borg, Kaella struck him down with her offhand dagger [ch 019 / 019.09; ch 019 / moment]. The fee originally intended for Locutus was forfeited and given to Valphine as payment for cooperation [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.10].
