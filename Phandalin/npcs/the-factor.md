## Identity
The current official speaking for the Neverwinter Commission through the office of Factor; no personal name or hidden identity is established [ch 058 / entry; ch 058 / 058.03].

## Personality and Motivations
Unfailingly helpful and procedurally evasive, using the collective we rather than I. He answers questions about authority with circular institutional explanations and records details carefully [ch 058 / entry; ch 058 / 058.03].

## History with the Party
- Received the party with exactly the required number of teacups and offered access before it was requested. He supplied inspection permission, Bimble’s reports, an access ledger, a harbor continuity file, a replacement-parts research request, and permission for a technical consultant [ch 058 / 058.03].
- Recorded Vukradin’s preferred mayoral title and Bimble’s survival; the party declined protection for Bimble. He took a note when asked about the previous Factor, whose identity was not readily available [ch 058 / 058.03].
- Soma detected reality-warping residue in the writing on the Commission documents, but none on the Factor himself. Illithid or hive-mind explanations remained table speculation [ch 058 / 058.03].

- The Factor received the party with tea and assured them that arrangements with Margaster would restore the machine and maintain slower trade meanwhile, with shareholder returns secure. These are his assurances, not a completed repair [ch 061 / 061.04].
- When Vukradin sought to sell his shares for the studio fund, the Factor immediately produced paperwork, explained the companions' opportunity to buy before an open-market sale, and warned that selling would end Vukradin's right to remain in the shareholder meeting. No price or completed sale was established. The party again noticed his avoidance of the singular first-person pronoun [ch 061 / 061.04].

## Last Observed State
Last seen at the Commission meeting providing repair and shareholder assurances and paperwork for Vukradin's proposed share sale. The sale and repairs remain incomplete; his personal identity and the earlier altered writing remain unresolved [ch 058 / 058.03; ch 061 / 061.04].

## Relationships
Speaks for the Commission and addresses the party as shareholders. His documents record Margaster’s rapid authorization to take over shipping and omit the rewriter described by Bimble; these are documentary findings, not proof that Bimble lied [ch 058 / 058.03].
