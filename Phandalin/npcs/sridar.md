## Identity
Sridar is a drow traveler and refugee currently residing at the Falcon Hunting Lodge under the protection of Falcon the Hunter [ch 020 / entry]. She identifies as a collector of curious artifacts, specifically those with planar connections that are “not quite right” [ch 020 / 020.07]. Sridar is neither an adventurer nor a member of the Emerald Enclave [ch 020 / entry]. Her interest in planar transformations originated after “the blink” in the Underdark, when the Faerzress changed and demons appeared [ch 020 / 020.07].

## Personality and Motivations
Sridar presents herself as an interventionist, motivated by events in Menzoberranzan and a desire to understand planar disruptions [ch 020 / 020.08]. She initially assumed local planar phenomena were natural analogs to the Faerzress but conceded to Brewbarry’s logic that intentional coordination was possible [ch 020 / 020.08]. She offers to pay for stories, clues, and physical artifacts related to planar events, believing artifacts are more valuable than information [ch 020 / entry; ch 020 / 020.07].

Sridar is suspicious of others’ motives, notably doubting Valphine’s worship of Lathander and asking if it was an elaborate Lolthite ruse after hearing Valphine’s death chant, which resembled Lolthite clerical music [ch 020 / 020.07]. She is direct in her inquiries; she asked Bimble what he had removed from the planar manifold’s housing, a question that caused Bimble to flee into hiding [ch 056 / 056.05]. She has ongoing interest in the planar manifold’s housing and its operator’s manual [ch 056 / 056.06].
