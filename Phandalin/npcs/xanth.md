## Identity
Xanth (also known as Xanthopoulos) is a centaur [ch 034 / entry; ch 037 / entry]. He speaks with a Greek accent [ch 037 / entry]. His appearance and nature have been described by others as an “abomination” [ch 034 / moment] and as “a guy. Just ignore the horse half” [ch 034 / moment]. He identifies his place as belonging to fields and forests [ch 037 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Xanth presents himself as a neutral observer rather than a factional ally. He stated to the Emerald Enclave council, “I am not on your side. I’m not on theirs. I’m telling you what my people saw and what happened” [ch 041 / moment]. His theological views on the Cult of Talos are pragmatic and dismissive of ideological justifications; he rejects the idea of necessary destruction, calling Talos a “lunatic god” [ch 042 / moment]. He believes the cult functions like a disease that “explodes, it destroys everything, and then it destroys upon themselves” [ch 042 / moment].

He is motivated by personal connections and practical guidance. He pledged assistance to Soma after recognizing her staff as belonging to his old friend, the druid Meril [ch 037 / moment]. He publicly defended Soma’s selection for the staff at the council, stating that Meril had chosen Soma for the staff [ch 041 / moment]. While he enjoys telling stories about the forest and its history, he respects boundaries; he attempted to discuss Meril’s backstory but accepted the party’s decision to treat it as non-campaign “flavor” [ch 042 / 042.04]. He is capable of dry humor, joking that if the party encountered the Talosian Chosen, “You’d probably die, but at least we’d learn something about him” [ch 042 / moment].

## Last Observed State
At the end of Chapter 043, Xanth was alive and functioning [ch 043 / 043.05]. He accompanied the party to the edge of the treeline below the Circle of Thunder, announcing that he had fulfilled his promise to guide them [ch 043 / 043.05]. During the preceding battle at the gully, he took 14 damage from an anchorite’s *Lightning Bolt*, cursed in his own dialect, and returned fire with a longbow, dealing 6 damage with one of two shots [ch 043 / 043.02]. Valphine subsequently healed 13 of those points [ch 043 / 043.04].

He departed the party by galloping into the wilderness, telling Vukradin, “Vukradinious, this is where a journey separates. You go and take care of the orcs” [ch 043 / 043.05]. Before leaving, he tapped Soma on the shell, stating, “This one, this one was blessed by Meril, and I know that she will do great things” [ch 043 / 043.05], and took a final lingering look at Meril’s Staff [ch 043 / 043.05].

## Relationships
**Soma:** Xanth holds Soma in high regard due to her bond with his old friend, Meril. He publicly affirmed at the council that Meril had chosen her for the staff [ch 041 / moment]. Later, on parting at the Circle of Thunder, he tapped her on the shell and said she “was blessed by Meril” [ch 043 / 043.05]. He praised her recent performance, telling her, “You were superb” [ch 042 / 042.04].

**Vukradin:** Xanth guided the party, and Vukradin said that Xanth’s presence made him feel very safe [ch 042 / 042.04]. Xanth initiated their separation at the Circle of Thunder with a direct address: “Vukradinious, this is where a journey separates” [ch 043 / 043.05]. Vukradin had previously used a *Sending* spell to contact Xanth to arrange this journey [ch 042 / 042.03].

**Meril:** Xanth refers to Meril as an “old friend” and the respected owner of the staff Soma carries [ch 037 / moment]. He expressed that “Perhaps Meril made a good choice” regarding Soma [ch 041 / moment]. He attempted to share details about Meril’s history, though the party declined this lore [ch 042 / 042.04].

**Emerald Enclave (Council):** Xanth acted as a voice of moderation at the council, pushing back against both interventionists and naturalists [ch 041 / entry]. He specifically countered Adabra’s use of Corbin’s death as an ideological weapon [ch 041 / moment].

**Anchorites of Talos:** Xanth identified them as half-orc spellcasters and described their theology as unstable and self-destructive [ch 034 / entry; ch 042 / moment]. He engaged them in combat at the gully, striking the anchorite Yargath with a longbow [ch 043 / 043.02].
