## Identity
An elderly human woman and high-ranking member of the Harpers who manages shipping routes and intelligence [ch 055 / entry]. She operates from the House of a Thousand Faces [ch 055 / 055.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Harper Seat is guarded, skilled at extracting information, and presents ignorance when strategically useful, only becoming indignant when her intelligence network is questioned [ch 055 / entry; ch 055 / 055.02]. She is pragmatic and willing to use dry humor or patronizing compliments to manage relationships, as seen in her interactions with Vukradin and her "friend therapy" quota [ch 055 / 055.02; ch 055 / 055.03]. Her primary motivation is to undercut the economic grip of House Margaster and the Zhentarim by maintaining secret food routes and warning against predatory contracts [ch 055 / 055.02].

## History with the Party
- Harper Seat acted as the high-ranking Harper representative at the House of a Thousand Faces, explaining that the organization was running secret food routes to undercut the economic grip of House Margaster and the Zhentarim [ch 055 / 055.02; ch 055 / 055.03].
- She detailed how House Margaster’s contracts contained hidden traps and loopholes designed to gradually place clients under the house’s control [ch 055 / 055.02; ch 055 / moment].
- Harper Seat more or less confirmed the party’s suspicion that the ninth crate moving through the sewers was intended for the missing gnome mechanic, whom the party called "Bimbo," and noted that Harper attempts to follow the crate’s collector had been thwarted by Rift Weavers [ch 055 / 055.02].
- When Valphine revealed her sister’s name as Aletra of House Sotorra, Harper Seat reacted with surprise, stating she believed the family to be dead and noting that threat assessments would need updating [ch 055 / 055.02; ch 055 / moment].
- The party mocked the Harpers’ intelligence network for failing to know about Aletra, the cambions, or "Bimbo"; Harper Seat became indignant, insisting the Harpers knew more than they disclosed and had known about House Sotorra’s status before the party arrived [ch 055 / entry; ch 055 / 055.02].
- Harper Seat acted as a mediator between Soma and Lim, revealing that Lim had been a Harper agent for years and had initially gathered information on Soma under the assumption Soma was developing naturalist sympathies [ch 055 / entry; ch 055 / 055.02].
- She observed that Soma had become a "determined interventionist" rather than a naturalist threat and concluded the exchange by declaring her allotment of "friend therapy" for the year exhausted [ch 055 / 055.02; ch 055 / moment].
- The party was tasked with clearing a sewer junction of Rift Weavers to reopen the Harper food route, which passed through Dead Rat territory and had become dangerous after the Manifold’s mechanic disappeared [ch 055 / entry; ch 055 / 055.02].
- Harper Seat offered the party washing facilities, clean clothing, and disguises so they could attend Lord Neverember’s dinner without carrying blood and spider remains, promising to make Vukradin "look even more incredible" [ch 055 / entry; ch 055 / 055.03; ch 055 / moment].
- When Vukradin asked if he was being patronized regarding his fame, Harper Seat replied "Absolutely not. You are, in fact, the incredible Vukradin," before listing the various events and rumors contributing to his reputation [ch 055 / 055.03; ch 055 / moment].
- Harper Seat admitted she had not seriously investigated the source of Vukradin’s sudden fame, initially suggesting he might be being manipulated [ch 055 / 055.03; ch 055 / moment].

## Last Observed State
Harper Seat was last seen at the House of a Thousand Faces during a meeting with the party [ch 055 / 055.02]. She tasked the party with clearing a sewer junction of Rift Weavers to reopen a critical Harper food route that passes through Dead Rat territory [ch 055 / entry; ch 055 / 055.02]. She arranged for the party to have access to washing facilities, clean clothing, and disguises to prepare for their evening dinner with Lord Neverember [ch 055 / 055.03]. She acknowledged that her organization’s threat assessments regarding House Sotorra required updating after learning the family survived [ch 055 / 055.02].

*   **Rift Weavers**: Enemies blocking the Harper food route; Harper Seat’s organization attempted to follow the crate’s collector but was thwarted [ch 055 / 055.02].
