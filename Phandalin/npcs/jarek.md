## Identity
Jarek is a groom employed by the merchant Ellarrah [ch 009 / entry]. He first appeared in Chapter 009 as part of Ellarrah’s travel party [ch 009 / 009.01]. In Chapter 046, the GM identified him as a “returning player” character, suggesting his return to the table [ch 046 / 046.06]. No other occupational details, alignment, or backstory are established in the evidence.

## Personality and Motivations
Jarek is initially quiet and reserved, remaining quiet until Brewbarry is out of earshot [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.02]. He appears practical and cautious, criticizing the decision to travel without protection immediately after surviving an ambush [ch 009 / 009.02]. He is observant, capable of corroborating his employer’s accounts of enemy behavior, though he presents his own sensory experiences with hesitation [ch 009 / 009.03]. He reported a “hum” in his head rather than a sound before the attack [ch 009 / 009.03].

## History with the Party
- Jarek was introduced as Ellarrah’s groom, initially quiet but becoming vocal once Brewbarry moved out of earshot. [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.01; ch 009 / 009.02]
- During the party’s departure for the Tower of Storms, Jarek and Ellarrah were found huddled behind an overturned cart on the road out of Phandalin, having survived an orc attack that caused their pack horses to flee. [ch 009 / 009.01]
- When Brewbarry left to recover a bolted horse, Jarek spoke his first line to Ellarrah: “I told you we should travel with some protection.” [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.02]
- Jarek corroborated Ellarrah’s description of the orcs’ “unnerving discipline” and added a personal detail that he had heard a hum, not a sound, “just... in my head,” before the attack. [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.03]
- Jarek was escorted all the way back to Phandalin by the party, as insisted upon by Vukradin. [ch 009 / 009.04]
- In chapter 046, the GM suggested that the current level (7th) was an ideal entry point for returning player Jarek. [ch 046 / 046.06]

## Last Observed State
In Chapter 009, Jarek was huddled behind an overturned trade cart on the road out of Phandalin, appearing visibly shaken and disheveled [ch 009 / 009.01]. He had survived an orc attack that caused their pack horses to flee [ch 009 / 009.01]. After the party helped recover their assets, he was escorted safely back to Phandalin by the party at Vukradin’s insistence [ch 009 / 009.04].

Jarek does not appear physically in Chapter 046. His only presence in that chapter is a meta-narrative mention by the GM, who suggested that 7th level would be an ideal entry point for Jarek’s return to the game [ch 046 / 046.06]. No in-game actions or status changes for Jarek are recorded in Chapter 046.

Status not established in the summaries beyond his safe return to Phandalin in Chapter 009.

## Relationships
**Ellarrah:** Jarek is Ellarrah’s groom [ch 009 / entry]. He travels with her and was found huddled with her behind their overturned cart [ch 009 / 009.01]. He speaks critically of her travel planning but remains with her [ch 009 / 009.02]. They were both escorted back to Phandalin [ch 009 / 009.04].

**The Party (Chapter 009):** Jarek was rescued by the party (Vukradin, Soma, Valphine, Brewbarry) and transported back to Phandalin by them [ch 009 / 009.01; ch 009 / 009.04]. He first spoke only after Brewbarry left to retrieve a horse [ch 009 / 009.02].

**The Party (Chapter 046):** The GM suggested the party send a message to Jarek (“like a group hug kind of thing”) to invite him to return to the table as a player character [ch 046 / 046.06].
