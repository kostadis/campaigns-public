## Identity
Brin Bundlewine is a villager from Phandalin with a pale complexion, a pageboy haircut, and numerous sunburns [ch 020 / entry; ch 020 / 020.02]. He is an acolyte at the Temple of Lathander’s Searing Pain of Justice [ch 049 / entry]. He is paired with Giles Slipper-Shine in their devotion to Valphine and the temple’s work [ch 020 / 020.02; ch 026 / 026.07].

## Personality and Motivations
Brin is a zealous believer in the doctrine of Lathander’s “purifying pain,” having waited years for someone to understand that purity is found in feeling the sun’s light sear the body [ch 020 / entry; ch 020 / 020.02]. He specifically sought out Valphine, a drow, believing that only someone of her heritage could fully comprehend the doctrine through direct experience of the sun’s pain [ch 020 / 020.02]. He finds great ecstasy in serving Valphine and working on the temple’s renovation, viewing it as a source of renewed purpose [ch 026 / entry; ch 026 / 026.07]. He embraces the church’s new sacrament of enduring Lathander’s light and being restored afterward [ch 026 / 026.07]. He is willing to provide free labor for the church, requesting only material costs for construction [ch 020 / entry; ch 020 / 020.02].

## History with the Party
*   Brin Bundlewine, a fair-skinned villager with a pageboy haircut and numerous sunburns, approached Valphine alongside Giles Slipper-Shine to propose establishing a chapel dedicated to Lathander’s “purifying pain,” believing a drow was uniquely equipped to understand their doctrine [ch 020 / entry; ch 020 / 020.02].
*   Valphine appointed Brin and Giles to renovate Tresendar Manor, which was declared their new temple, later renamed the Temple of Lathander’s Searing Pain of Justice [ch 020 / entry; ch 020 / 020.02].
*   Brin and Giles agreed to provide free labor for the temple’s renovation in exchange for twenty-five gold pieces to cover beams, furniture, and construction materials [ch 020 / entry; ch 020 / 020.02].
*   The pair experimented with honorifics for Valphine, eventually settling on “Matron Mother of Lathander” [ch 020 / 020.02].
*   After the party’s absence, Brin was among the workers who had progressed on the church reconstruction; he and Giles expressed ecstasy and renewed purpose upon Valphine’s return [ch 026 / entry; ch 026 / 026.07].
*   At Valphine’s sermon the congregation concluded they must stare directly at the sun to prove their worth, which became a new sacrament of enduring light and receiving restoration; Brin and Giles were ecstatic [ch 026 / 026.07].
*   Brin was present at the town green gathering for the Universal Basic Treasure proclamation, noted by Soma as looking sunburnt and “Kissed by Lathander” [ch 049 / entry; ch 049 / 049.03].
