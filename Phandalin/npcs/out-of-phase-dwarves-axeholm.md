## Identity
The Out-of-Phase Dwarves are the former dwarven inhabitants of Axeholm, warped by a planar anomaly into a state of animated suspension [ch 028 / entry; ch 029 / entry]. They are not undead, despite behaving mechanically like ghouls; the DM explicitly clarifies they are trapped dwarves driven mad by the distortion [ch 028 / entry; ch 030 / entry]. Visually, they possess parchment-like skin covered in shifting ink, gold coins embedded in their flesh, and mouths sewn shut with golden wire or sealed with red tape [ch 028 / entry]. Their limbs move asynchronously through warped reality, creating the illusion of six hands and six legs [ch 028 / entry]. They are difficult to perceive in the static, mathematically cold light of Axeholm until they abruptly materialize into alignment with the party’s reality [ch 028 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
The dwarves are mentally corrupted by the anomaly, rendering them largely unresponsive to normal speech or logic [ch 028 / 028.02]. Their broken vocabulary is centered on bureaucratic and industrial terminology, specifically repeating phrases like "Efficiency. Efficiency. Efficiency.", "Lacking the appropriate tool.", and "Inspector lied." [ch 028 / entry]. In Chapter 030, their obsession with the mine's operational efficiency became frantic; they screamed about "process" and pulled out their hair while muttering the efficiency mantra [ch 030 / entry; ch 030 / 030.03]. Attempts to appeal to this obsession failed catastrophically; when Vukradin successfully persuaded them that the party was there to improve efficiency, the dwarves became furious, suggesting the pursuit of optimization was the cause of their entrapment [ch 030 / 030.03]. Their combat behavior involves pack-animal swarming and psychological paralysis attacks that manifest as sensations of being packaged, sealed, or stored [ch 028 / 028.04; ch 028 / entry].

## Last Observed State
All six Out-of-Phase Dwarves encountered in Chapter 030 were destroyed during the battle with the party [ch 030 / entry]. During the fight, Brewbarry killed the first dwarf; Valphine’s Spirit Guardians killed three, Soma in bear form killed one, and Brewbarry finished the last one after Vukradin ended his concentration on the *Slow* spell to attack the Chief Accountant [ch 030 / 030.03]. Unlike normal corpses, some dwarves blinked away as space folded or appeared momentarily phased upon death or severe injury [ch 028 / entry; ch 028 / 028.03]. Status for any other dwarves in Axeholm is not established in the summaries, though it was noted in Chapter 029 that some had fled during earlier combat and one escaped up a fireplace after being turned [ch 029 / entry].

## Relationships
The Out-of-Phase Dwarves have no known allies; they attacked the party once Brewbarry smashed through the door [ch 028 / 028.03]. They ignore non-magical creatures such as Soma’s rat familiar [ch 028 / 028.02]. The party members Brewbarry, Vukradin, Soma, and Valphine engaged them in combat across three chapters, with Brewbarry mocking their obsession with efficiency and Vukradin attempting to parley [ch 028 / 028.03; ch 030 / 030.03]. The DM serves as the primary arbitrator of their nature, consistently correcting the party's assumption that they are ghouls by emphasizing their status as phased-out dwarves [ch 030 / 030.03].
