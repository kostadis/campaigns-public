## Identity
Corwin is a retainer and chef for Falcon at the Falcon Hunting Lodge [ch 020 / entry]. He is responsible for preparing the lodge’s meals and managing the initial reception of visitors at the gate [ch 020 / 020.04]. He introduces himself simply as “Corwin,” noting that he is called “Chef” only by friends [ch 020 / 020.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Corwin is guarded and formal with strangers. His first action upon meeting visitors at the gate is to demand their identities, asking, “Who are you?” [ch 020 / 020.04]. He establishes strict boundaries regarding familiarity, stating to the party, “My name’s Corwin, although my friends called me Chef. You’re not my friends yet, so you can call me Corwin” [ch 020 / 020.04].

He is loyal to his employer, Falcon, and consistently redirects questions regarding news, other topics, and wine selections to Falcon [ch 020 / entry; ch 020 / 020.04]. Once Barthen’s note was inspected, he shifted from guardedness to efficiency, saying, “Barthen sent you. Well, come in, come in!” and subsequently fetching Falcon [ch 020 / 020.04].

## History with the Party
*   Corwin served as the chef and a retainer for Falcon at the Falcon Hunting Lodge, greeting the party at the gate after Soma rang the bell [ch 020 / entry; ch 020 / 020.04].
*   He demanded to know the identities of the visitors, asking, “Who are you?” and later “Barthen sent you. Well, come in, come in!” after inspecting a note from Barthen [ch 020 / 020.04].
*   Corwin introduced himself, stating he cooked the lodge’s meals and instructing the party to call him “Corwin” because they were not yet his friends [ch 020 / entry; ch 020 / 020.04].
*   He repeatedly supplied wine to the party during dinner and negotiations, including bringing more wine while Vukradin negotiated with Sridar [ch 020 / entry; ch 020 / 020.07].
*   Corwin consistently redirected questions regarding news and other topics away from himself and toward his employer, Falcon [ch 020 / entry; ch 020 / 020.04].

## Last Observed State
Corwin was last seen in Chapter 020 [ch 020 / entry]. After the party arrived at the Falcon Hunting Lodge, he inspected Barthen’s note, welcomed them inside, and alerted Falcon [ch 020 / 020.04]. During the subsequent dinner and negotiations involving Vukradin, Sridar, and Falcon, Corwin was observed bringing more wine to the table [ch 020 / 020.07]. The evidence does not indicate he left the lodge or departed during this scene.
