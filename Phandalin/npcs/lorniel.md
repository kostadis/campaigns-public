## Identity
A musician leading a Vukradin tribute act, with a freakishly convincing nonmagical resemblance to the bard. His performance imitates Vukradin’s earlier arrogant persona [ch 058 / 058.05].

## Personality and Motivations
He regards the act as an homage and insists on its authenticity. He wants to be paid despite stepping aside and has struggled to find a drow cleric of Lathander to fill Valphine’s role [ch 058 / 058.05].

## History with the Party
- Met the party during sound check, discussed his attendance at the Morninglord performance, and agreed to stand aside to avoid confusing the crowd. Petra paid him 25 gold [ch 058 / 058.05].

## Last Observed State
Paid and standing aside for the party’s Common Chord performance; he does not play the opening set that evening [ch 058 / 058.05].

## Relationships
Petra books and pays him. Vukradin is his model and the original performer whose presence displaces his act for this show [ch 058 / 058.05].
