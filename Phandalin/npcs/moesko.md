## Identity
Moesko was a half-orc anchorite of the god Talos [ch 010 / entry; ch 011 / entry; ch 012 / entry]. He served as a cultist who invaded the Tower of Storms, killing the sea elf Miraal and stealing her opalescent conch spellcasting focus as a trophy [ch 010 / 010.06]. He was clad in armor fashioned from giant octopus hide [ch 011 / 011.06; ch 012 / 012.01]. Physically, he was distinguished by a gaping void in his chest where his heart should have been, a condition that persisted despite his continued ability to fight and cast spells [ch 010 / entry; ch 012 / 012.01]. His magical capabilities included powerful lightning and thunder effects [ch 011 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Miraal described something cold, calculating, and foreign that had moved within Moesko, distinct from the ordinary rage of the Stormlord [ch 012 / 012.05]. Miraal revealed that a cold, calculating foreign presence from the deep had influenced him, twisting his devotion to Talos toward an alien purpose [ch 012 / entry; ch 012 / 012.05]. His devotion appeared syncretic or corrupted, as evidenced by a talisman found in his quarters bearing both the lightning bolt of Talos and an inverted eye wreathed in tentacles [ch 012 / 012.01]. Vukradin named this object the “Talisman of Death” upon discovery [ch 012 / 012.01; ch 020 / 020.07]. Miraal noted that the planning behind his invasion of the tower displayed more sophistication than typical orcish cultists, suggesting external direction [ch 010 / 010.06].

## History with the Party
*   Moesko was a half-orc anchorite of Talos who invaded the Tower of Storms and killed the sea elf Miraal, stealing her opalescent conch as a trophy; he had a gaping hole in his chest where his heart should have been [ch 010 / entry; ch 010 / 010.06].
*   He wore armor made from giant octopus hide and used powerful lightning and thunder magic [ch 011 / entry; ch 011 / 011.06].
*   The party confronted Moesko in a chamber at the top of the Tower of Storms, finding him seated in a barnacle-crusted chair with the conch in his lap [ch 011 / 011.06].
*   Moesko nearly caused a total party kill with a single 8d6 lightning bolt dealing 25 damage, but was ultimately slain by the party after Soma transformed into a bear and Vukradin trapped him with a spinning dagger vortex [ch 011 / 011.06; ch 011 / moment].
*   Upon searching his body, the party confirmed the absence of his heart beneath his armor and discovered a talisman bearing symbols of Talos and a tentacled eye, which Vukradin named the “Talisman of Death” [ch 012 / 012.01; ch 012 / moment].
*   The party discovered Moesko’s oversized, still-beating heart powering the lighthouse’s green beacon and destroyed it, ending the tower’s curse [ch 012 / 012.02; ch 012 / moment].
*   Miraal revealed that Moesko had not acted entirely of his own will, but was influenced by a cold, calculating foreign presence from the deep that twisted his devotion to Talos [ch 012 / entry; ch 012 / 012.05].
*   The party sold the “Talisman of Moesko” to the collector Sridar for 120 gold pieces, identifying it as having a planar connection [ch 020 / 020.07].

## Last Observed State
Moesko was slain by the party in a chamber at the top of the Tower of Storms [ch 011 / 011.06]. The battle concluded with Soma delivering the final blow after Brewbarry reduced him to one hit point [ch 011 / 011.06; ch 011 / moment]. Following his death, his body was examined, and the void in his chest was confirmed to be physically empty [ch 012 / 012.01]. His oversized, still-beating heart was discovered at the lighthouse pinnacle, where it had been powering the structure’s green beacon [ch 012 / 012.02; ch 012 / moment]. Vukradin destroyed this heart, ending the tower’s curse [ch 012 / 012.02; ch 012 / moment]. The party later sold the “Talisman of Moesko” to a collector named Sridar for 120 gold pieces [ch 020 / 020.07].
