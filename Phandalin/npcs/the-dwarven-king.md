## Identity
The subject was once the Dwarven King of Axeholm, but by the events of Chapter 029, he had been corrupted into a twisted, three-headed monstrosity [ch 029 / 029.06; ch 029 / entry]. His physical form was a fused amalgamation of medium-sized dwarven flesh, possessing one claw attack and three separate biting mouths [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.06]. He exhibited substantially greater vitality than the lesser "twisted dwarves" roaming Axeholm [ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / 029.06]. Despite his monstrous appearance and diminished intellect—evidenced by a negative Intelligence modifier—he retained enough coherence to wield a natural weapon capable of inflicting paralysis [ch 029 / 029.06]. Upon his death, he carried a signet ring bearing the Axeholm crest, depicting a handaxe clutched in a dwarven gauntlet [ch 029 / 029.07; ch 029 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
In battle he attacked Soma first and later targeted Valphine, shouting "the expert" before attacking her [ch 029 / 029.06; ch 029 / moment]. His mental state appeared fragile and susceptible to illusion. He resisted a *Phantasmal Force* conjuring the image of the god Moradin, narrowly succeeding on a saving throw, but later failed a similar save against an illusion of a giant bear, treating the phantasm as real and suffering psychic damage [ch 029 / 029.06; ch 029 / entry]. He demonstrated high endurance, continuing to advance and attack despite taking twenty-two points of critical damage from Brewbarry [ch 029 / 029.06].

## History with the Party
*   The Dwarven King emerged from behind the limestone throne in the pillared hall, appearing as a twisted, three-headed monstrosity with substantially more vitality than lesser corrupted dwarves [ch 029 / 029.06; ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / moment].
*   In the initial exchange, the King attacked Soma; his claw paralyzed her, and three subsequent bite attacks reduced her wild shape to zero, knocking her unconscious [ch 029 / 029.06; ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / moment].
*   The King resisted Vukradin’s first illusion of Moradin, but succumbed to a later illusion of a giant bear, taking psychic damage while treating the phantasm as real [ch 029 / 029.06; ch 029 / entry].
*   The King targeted Valphine, shouting "the expert" before attacking her with every available natural weapon; however, all attacks missed [ch 029 / 029.06; ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / moment].
*   Soma, having recovered from unconsciousness, delivered the final attack that killed the three-headed Dwarven King [ch 029 / 029.06; ch 029 / entry; ch 029 / moment].
*   Upon searching the fallen King, the party found a signet ring bearing the Axeholm crest, which was worth five gold pieces and functioned as a key to an unidentified lock [ch 029 / 029.07; ch 029 / entry].
