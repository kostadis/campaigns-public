## Identity
Adabra Gwynn is a human midwife, herbalist, apothecary, and druid who resides and works at the Umbrage Hill windmill [ch 007 / entry; ch 013 / entry]. She is a devout follower of Chauntea and an absolutist member of the Emerald Enclave who identifies firmly with the naturalist faction of the organization [ch 007 / 007.07; ch 013 / entry]. She is a known resident of Phandalin and serves as a member of the town’s privy council [ch 019 / 019.07]. While based on the same name as an NPC from the source module, this version of the character is a significantly expanded homebrew addition to the campaign [ch 044 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Adabra is driven by a strict philosophical commitment to natural balance and non-intervention, which she refers to as the “foolish path of imbalance” [ch 013 / entry]. She believes that forcing the multiverse toward good or evil, or attempting to cleanse perceived corruptions, provokes an opposing reaction from planar forces [ch 013 / moment]. Her ideology is one of sincere conviction rather than malice or mental manipulation; she is described as a “true believer” who is neither controlled nor consciously deceptive [ch 041 / entry; ch 041 / 041.03]. She possesses a commanding, intimidating demeanor, using glare to negotiate terms and correcting others’ self-identifications with authority [ch 007 / 007.12; ch 008 / 008.04]. Her rhetoric is sharp and often sarcastic, particularly when addressing those she considers interventionists, and she is deeply skeptical of organizations like the Order of the Gauntlet [ch 013 / 013.02; ch 016 / 016.01]. While Ser Kaelen describes her as misguided rather than evil due to her witnessing of war’s devastation without understanding its causes, her political methods can be viciously personal when she feels deceived [ch 040 / entry; ch 042 / entry].

## Last Observed State
Adabra’s last observed state was following the Emerald Enclave council meeting where her accusations against the party were dismissed [ch 041 / 041.05]. After the council ruled that her case did not hold and that sitting idle to preserve cosmic equilibrium made no sense, she left the meeting in a “seething rage” [ch 041 / 041.05; ch 041 / 041.07]. During this meeting, she personally presented evidence that the sacred treant at the Whispering Grove was dead, with collapsed roots and a black crown, accusing Vukradin of falsifying reports and demanding repayment of the five gold pieces she had previously paid him for information [ch 041 / 041.02; ch 041 / moment]. Following this event, Vukradin sent her six gold pieces via the postal system as a repayment with interest, using “organic fair trade gold” [ch 042 / 042.01; ch 042 / moment]. Status not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
**Soma:** Adabra was Soma’s mentor or former student connection, though the dynamic is strained [ch 007 / 007.08; ch 016 / 016.01]. Adabra attempted to recruit Soma privately, excluding others, but the relationship fractured when Soma’s actions contradicted naturalist doctrine [ch 013 / 013.01]. Soma currently views Adabra’s views as “batshit insanity” and supports Lyra’s promotion specifically to counter Adabra’s influence [ch 020 / 020.08; ch 024 / 024.01].

**Vukradin:** Adabra initially supported Vukradin because she believed his devotion to music represented a balanced, non-aligned cause [ch 013 / entry]. However, their relationship collapsed after she discovered he lied about the Whispering Grove’s condition; she accused him of falsifying reports and demanded financial restitution [ch 041 / 041.02; ch 042 / entry]. Vukradin felt betrayed by her “viciously personal” attacks during the council and settled their dispute with a petty payment [ch 042 / 042.01].

**Townmaster Harbin:** Adabra has a transactional but authoritative relationship with Harbin, leveraging her status to secure party compensation and gaining a seat on the privy council through his appointment [ch 008 / 008.03; ch 019 / 019.07].

**Lyra:** Adabra is an ideological rival to Lyra, attempting to ostracize her from the Emerald Enclave during the druid conclave [ch 041 / 041.01]. Adabra’s case against Lyra was rejected, restoring Lyra’s standing [ch 041 / 041.05].

**Falcon:** Adabra influenced Falcon’s brief turn toward naturalism by providing a philosophical framework for his personal losses and failures, though Falcon later rejected her influence [ch 041 / 041.03; ch 041 / 041.06].
