## Identity
Corbin is a former bandit from Greenmeadow who joined the party after being rescued from brigands [ch 006 / 006.08]. He served as the party’s camp hand, aide, and retainer [ch 007 / entry; ch 010 / entry]. He was formerly a member of the Carver’s gang, a status he used to identify local brigands and former colleagues [ch 008 / 008.04; ch 016 / 016.14]. Corbin was killed by Sister Kaella at Dragon Barrow [ch 041 / 041.04; ch 041 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Corbin was motivated primarily by fear of his former boss, “the Carver,” whom he believed would kill him for leaving the gang [ch 006 / 006.08; ch 017 / 017.03]. He was noted for being frightened of spiders and generally deferential to party members like Brewbarry [ch 007 / entry; ch 010 / entry]. His backstory involved waking up with nothing after his family was killed and joining the bandits because the town rejected him [ch 007 / 007.10].

While he often displayed cowardice—hiding during battles and failing bluffs due to visible fear [ch 010 / 010.03; ch 018 / 018.01]—he also sought revenge against his former gang [ch 018 / 018.04]. He displayed a capacity for cruelty when granted power over former tormentors; Valphine’s Insight check revealed he felt personal satisfaction at guarding Sister Kaella, recognizing that she had hurt him in the past [ch 037 / 037.04]. He attempted to frame taking a prisoner’s armor as “conflict-free,” though this was refused [ch 018 / 018.04; ch 018 / moment].

## Last Observed State

He was killed by Sister Kaella at Dragon Barrow after the party left them both behind under the guard of only a low-level fighter [ch 041 / 041.04; ch 041 / entry]. ## Relationships
*   **Vukradin:** Corbin was a retainer who sought Vukradin’s approval, such as asking to keep scavenged armor [ch 007 / 007.01]. Vukradin entrusted Corbin with guarding prisoners, introducing him as an expert at tying people up [ch 024 / 024.05], but remained naive about Corbin’s violent intent toward Kaella [ch 037 / 037.04].
*   **Sister Kaella:** Corbin held a personal grudge against Kaella, feeling satisfaction at gaining power over her due to past abuse [ch 037 / 037.04]. She killed him while he was assigned to guard her [ch 041 / 041.04].
*   **The Carver:** Corbin feared the Carver would find and kill him [ch 006 / 006.08]. He identified the Carver’s insignia on orc rations [ch 010 / 010.03] and warned of the Carver’s agents in the village [ch 010 / 010.04]. His attempt to contact the Carver in Conyberry failed [ch 017 / 017.03].
*   **Soma:** Soma felt guilt over Corbin’s death, though the Druid Council deemed his death regrettable but insufficient to invalidate her interventionist philosophy [ch 041 / 041.05].
*   **Brewbarry:** Corbin was deferential to Brewbarry [ch 007 / entry].
