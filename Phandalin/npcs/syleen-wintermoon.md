## Identity
Syleen Wintermoon is a lean, brash woman with red hair pulled back into a ponytail [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.05]. She is the sister of Runa [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Syleen is motivated by a belief that she and her sister are the rightful owners of the treasure found in the dragon’s lair [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.05]. She held prior knowledge of Vukradin’s reputation as a bard who returns stolen treasure and viewed him as the answer to their prayers for restitution [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.05]. When Vukradin refused to distribute the treasure without proof, guild consultation, or the use of Legend Lore, Syleen became indignant and accused him of theft [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.05].

## History with the Party
- Syleen Wintermoon appeared in the fortress lair alongside her sister Runa following the defeat of the dragon, claiming ownership of the entire slag block of treasure. [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.05]
- She approached Vukradin, identifying him by his reputation as a bard who returns stolen treasure and characterizing him as the answer to their prayers. [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.05]
- When Vukradin rejected their unsupported claim and Brewbarry asked for evidence, and Vukradin insisted on investigating provenance, consulting guilds, and potentially using Legend Lore, Syleen responded with indignation, calling him a thief. [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.05]
- Syleen led an attack on the party alongside Runa and four numbered veterans, resulting in combat with six opponents in total. [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / 039.05]
- During the conflict, Syleen successfully saved against Spirit Guardians, taking five damage. [ch 039 / entry]
