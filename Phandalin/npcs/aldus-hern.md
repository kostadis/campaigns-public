## Identity
Aldus is a steward and scheduler in the livery of Lord Dagult Neverember, Lord Protector of Neverwinter [ch 050 / entry; ch 057 / entry]. He acts as the primary intermediary for formal communications, summons, and logistical coordination between the Lord Protector and external parties [ch 050 / entry; ch 057 / entry]. He is proficient in *sending* magic, using it to contact the party directly when necessary [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Aldus is described as polite and professional, though prone to nervousness when managing Lord Neverember’s schedule or temper [ch 050 / entry; ch 052 / 052.03]. He harbors a secret, intense fandom for the bard Vukradin’s music; he is embarrassed by this interest and initially fabricated a daughter named “Alducia” to request an autograph [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.07]. His attempts to maintain professional distance collapse easily when confronted with Vukradin’s performance, leading him to declare, “I must say, I am quite the fan” [ch 050 / 050.07].

He is careful and cautious, often apologizing immediately after delivering blunt messages on Neverember’s behalf, such as the Lord Protector’s concern that the party might be hoarding wealth like a large reptile [ch 050 / 050.07]. When the party delays, Aldus attempts to manage expectations diplomatically, assuring them that “Dinner... is going to happen” while noting Neverember’s preoccupation [ch 052 / 052.03]. If patience runs out, he becomes direct and slightly desperate, urging the party to ensure their delay is justified by “a matter of civic importance” [ch 057 / moment]. He is diligent with administrative duties, having reviewed the financial implications of Universal Basic Treasure to ensure the numbers supported Neverember’s pre-existing decisions [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.07].

## Last Observed State
Aldus was last observed attending a formal dinner with the party and Lord Neverember at the Lord Protector’s estate [ch 057 / 057.07]. Earlier in the night, he sent two urgent *sending* messages to the party, noting that Neverember had been waiting for over an hour [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.06]. Upon the party’s arrival, Aldus welcomed them and thanked Vukradin for the autograph intended for his “daughter,” confirming she had been delighted by it [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.07]. During the dinner, Aldus moved in and out of the dining room to attend to Neverember’s needs [ch 057 / 057.07]. He was present in the dining room (with approximately twelve to fifteen others) when the magical cleaning effects on the party’s sewer-caked clothing wore off, causing a sudden stench that prompted Neverember to offer the party fresh clothes [ch 057 / 057.07].

## Relationships
**Lord Dagult Neverember:** Aldus serves as the Lord Protector’s steward, scheduler, and financial auditor. He facilitates the Lord Protector’s meetings and ensures that administrative data, such as the economics of Universal Basic Treasure, aligns with Neverember’s intentions [ch 050 / entry; ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.07].

**Vukradin:** Aldus is a devoted fan of Vukradin’s music. This shared connection allowed Vukradin to successfully persuade Aldus to arrange a charitable gala [ch 050 / 050.07]. Aldus requested an autograph, fabricating a daughter (“Alducia”) to hide his own enthusiasm, and remained visibly moved by the interaction [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.07].

**Valphine:** Aldus contacted Valphine directly via *sending* to manage her group’s tardiness to dinner, characterizing their delay as a matter of civic importance to protect her reputation with Neverember [ch 057 / entry; ch 057 / 057.06].

**Brewbarry:** Aldus endured Brewbarry’s heckling regarding the Lord Protector’s beverages and bathrobes during the initial summons, maintaining strained composure throughout [ch 050 / entry; ch 050 / 050.07].
