## Identity

Mnemazar is an imp clerk serving House Margaster in its warehouse front office, wearing spectacles and handling visitor waivers and count-in/count-out procedures. [ch 062 / entry; ch 062 / 062.03]

## Personality and Motivations

Mnemazar displays bored, bureaucratic indifference, insisting on paperwork and remaining calm after a nearby Thunderwave blast. The clerk describes their responsibility as counting visitors and securing waiver signatures; broader motivations are not established. [ch 062 / 062.03; ch 062 / 062.05]

## History with the Party

- Met Valphine and Brewbarry in the front office, referenced the Cullen Sharpe matter and Adrian's terrible week, and presented visitor waivers. Neither visitor signed. [ch 062 / 062.03]
- Could not authorize Valphine's request to hold the meeting in the front office. Valphine's proposed count-out loophole remained her interpretation, without confirmation from Mnemazar. [ch 062 / 062.03]
- Remained calm after the blast and explained the limits of the front-office role when Valphine asked about responding. [ch 062 / 062.05]
- Brewbarry raged and struck Mnemazar twice with his two-handed weapon, killing the clerk before vaulting the desk into the warehouse. [ch 062 / entry; ch 062 / 062.05]

## Last Observed State

Killed by Brewbarry and disintegrated into a pool of ochre dust. [ch 062 / entry; ch 062 / 062.05]

## Relationships
