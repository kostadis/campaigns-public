## Identity
Toblen Stonehill is the short, friendly human proprietor of the Stonehill Inn in Phandalin. He is originally from Triboar. While serving at the inn, he was named to the newly formed privy council of Phandalin alongside Harbin Wester, Elmar, Halia, Linene, Adabra, and Vukradin [ch 019 / entry; ch 019 / 019.07]. The table improvised a nickname for him, "Spider-Man," based on a childhood incident where a spider’s nest cracked open over him; villagers previously called him "Spider-Boy" until he grew up and persuaded them to upgrade the name [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.05].

## Personality and Motivations
Toblen is commercially motivated by the party's success, noting that their adventures and musical performances are "good for business" [ch 013 / entry]. He acts as a social hub and information broker; he shares rumors regarding orc raids, dragon sightings, and stranger activities, such as linking a local stranger to the Lord’s Alliance [ch 002 / entry; ch 019 / entry]. He possesses a pragmatic, forward-looking philosophy, famously stating, "The past is the past. The future is the future" when forgiving Brewbarry for past associations [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.04]. He is a gentleman regarding privacy; he slipped a letter to Vukradin under their door without reading it, insisting he does not pry into correspondence [ch 013 / entry]. He is observant of the party's behavior, providing free drinks for good performances but monitoring alcohol consumption to prevent excessive drinking [ch 027 / entry].

## History with the Party
*   Toblen is the short, friendly human proprietor of the Stonehill Inn in Phandalin, originally from Triboar [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.04].
*   Upon the party's initial arrival, Toblen welcomed them, served Vukradin ale in exchange for a musical performance, and shared rumors regarding orc raids and a local dragon problem [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.04].
*   He expressed wariness toward Brewbarry upon learning of the Uthgardt barbarian disaster in Triboar, but forgave him after Vukradin and Soma vouched for his character [ch 002 / entry; ch 002 / 002.04].
*   Toblen warned the party that orc tribes were descending from the north, a fact the party later recalled during a skirmish with those same orcs [ch 004 / 004.01].
*   He warmly welcomed the party upon their return from their first quest, specifically requesting Vukradin play the clarinet [ch 004 / entry; ch 004 / 004.04].
*   During a later visit, the table improvised that Toblen’s nickname was “Spider-Man” due to a childhood incident involving a spider’s nest, which Toblen reportedly enjoys recounting to customers [ch 008 / entry; ch 008 / 008.05; ch 013 / entry].
*   Toblen participated in a betting pool regarding the party’s expedition to the Tower of Storms and won by wagering that Vukradin’s music would ensure the party’s safe return; he gave Vukradin ten gold from the winnings [ch 013 / entry; ch 013 / moment].
*   He acted as a middleman for a private letter to Vukradin from Neverwinter, slipping it under their door overnight and insisting he did not read it because he is “a gentleman” [ch 013 / entry].

*   He provided the intelligence that a stranger in town was associated with the Lord’s Alliance in Neverwinter and carried their insignia [ch 019 / entry].
*   Toblen served beer and a celebratory round of drinks to the party following the founding of the Temple of Lathander’s Searing Pain of Justice [ch 020 / entry; ch 020 / 020.02].
*   He is noted as being pleased with the party’s performances, often providing free drinks, though he also monitors the party’s alcohol consumption to ensure they do not drink too much [ch 027 / entry].
*   Toblen welcomed the party back to Phandalin as heroes and provided free drinks for the house after a successful performance by Vukradin [ch 049 / entry; ch 049 / 049.02].
*   He was present among the citizens gathered for the town meeting where Vukradin proposed the Universal Basic Treasure (UBT) program [ch 049 / 049.03].
