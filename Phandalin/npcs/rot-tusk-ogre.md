## Identity
The Rot-Tusk Ogre was a large, brutish creature identified as belonging to the Rot-Tusk Ogre clan. The party had previously established a blood feud with this clan during their earliest forest expeditions [ch 036 / 036.05; ch 036 / entry]. In Chapter 36, this specific ogre acted as a combatant for Jax, the last of the Carver’s agents, participating in an ambush against the party [ch 036 / 036.05].

## Personality and Motivations
The evidence provides no specific personality traits, dialogue, or personal motivations for the Rot-Tusk Ogre itself [ch 036 / 036.05]. The ogre was present as part of Jax’s force, where Jax stated that the conflict was “just business” [ch 036 / 036.05]. The ogre acted under this command, fighting alongside Jax and several thugs against the party [ch 036 / 036.05].

## History with the Party
- The Rot-Tusk Ogre was part of an ambush led by Jax, the last of the Carver’s agents, alongside several thugs. The party had previously established a blood feud with the Rot-Tusk Ogres during their earliest forest encounters [ch 036 / entry; ch 036 / 036.05].
- Vukradin cast *Hold Person*, paralyzing both Jax and the Rot-Tusk Ogre after they failed their Wisdom saves. The ogre repeatedly failed subsequent saving attempts to shake off the paralysis effect [ch 036 / entry; ch 036 / 036.05].
- Valphine cast *Spirit Guardians*, and the ogre took twelve radiant damage after failing its initial Wisdom save. The aura continued to affect the ogre, burning it and slowing its movement [ch 036 / entry; ch 036 / 036.05].
- The Rot-Tusk Ogre remained paralyzed throughout the combat and never had the opportunity to make an attack against the party [ch 036 / entry; ch 036 / 036.05].
- Brewbarry ultimately killed the Rot-Tusk Ogre with two critical hits, ending the creature’s threat permanently [ch 036 / entry; ch 036 / 036.05].

## Last Observed State
The Rot-Tusk Ogre was killed in Chapter 36 [ch 036 / entry]. It was paralyzed by Vukradin’s *Hold Person* spell, failing all subsequent Wisdom saves and never breaking free [ch 036 / 036.05]. While immobilized, it took radiant damage from Valphine’s *Spirit Guardians* aura [ch 036 / 036.05]. Brewbarry then struck the paralyzed ogre twice with critical hits, killing it before the creature ever made an attack against the party [ch 036 / 036.05; ch 036 / entry].
