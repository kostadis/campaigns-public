## Identity
Aurelan Vance is a banker employed at the Counting House in Neverwinter [ch 051 / 051.01]. He is responsible for loan approvals and financial inquiries, adhering to a strict departmental divide where he handles loans while others handle fulfillment [ch 051 / 051.02]. He is meticulous with calculations and profit margins, initially mistaking Brewbarry’s authentic barbarian attire for a costume [ch 051 / entry]. Over time, he has transitioned from a reluctant service provider to a proactive informant for the party, now operating on a basis of mutual favors [ch 052 / 052.05]. He prefers to be addressed by the nickname “Ori” among friends [ch 053 / 053.01].

## Personality and Motivations
Aurelan is professionally composed but easily intimidated by aggressive negotiation tactics, and under Brewbarry’s halberd-tapping pressure he signed the loan paperwork [ch 051 / 051.02]. He is deeply conservative regarding social and institutional norms, visibly unsettling when faced with extreme suggestions such as approaching the Assassin’s Guild or the secretive Commission [ch 052 / 052.05]. He promised to make inquiries into House Margaster by the next day (“You can rely on the Counting House”) [ch 051 / 051.02]. He is capable of independent action when moved by inspiration, as evidenced by his overnight investigation into missing gnome Bimble’s contracts after attending Valphine’s sermon [ch 053 / 053.01; ch 053 / entry]. He deduces truths through financial patterns, prioritizing logical consistency in transactions over external narratives [ch 053 / 053.01].

## History with the Party
*   Aurelan Vance is a banker at the Counting House who initially mistakes Brewbarry’s authentic barbarian attire for a high-end cosplay costume [ch 051 / 051.01; ch 051 / entry].
*   Under pressure from Brewbarry’s halberd and aggressive negotiation, Aurelan signs loan paperwork for Brewbarry’s bathrobe business, despite his own reservations about profit margins and suggesting House Margaster as a cheaper alternative [ch 051 / 051.02; ch 051 / entry].
*   Aurelan recognizes Valphine as “one of the blessed of Lathander” and notes she is a drow when she corrects his financial calculations regarding temple markup [ch 051 / 051.02; ch 051 / entry].
*   He directs the party to the fulfillment department to collect Vukradin’s letter of credit, personally escorting him to the desk where Rimardo and Corrin are acting as divine accountants [ch 051 / 051.02; ch 051 / 051.03].
*   Aurelan commits to investigating House Margaster’s operation by the next day, stating, “You can rely on the Counting House” [ch 051 / 051.02; ch 051 / entry].
*   In the following session, Aurelan appears visibly pained but professionally composed when Brewbarry enters the Counting House, providing context on the Commission’s handling of the shipping manifold failure [ch 052 / 052.05; ch 052 / entry].
*   Aurelan reveals that the Commission is a secretive body led by Lord Neverember and that House Margaster is not a member, while Brewbarry inferred that they stand to benefit from the trade shutdown [ch 052 / 052.05].
*   Valphine’s casual suggestion to consult the Assassin’s Guild to find a missing gnome causes Aurelan to turn pale, a reaction Vukradin attempts to diffuse by noting Valphine is a cleric of Lathander [ch 052 / 052.05; ch 052 / entry].
*   Brewbarry establishes a reciprocal favor-based relationship with Aurelan, cementing their professional dealings with mutual cooperation in mind [ch 052 / 052.05].
*   Inspired by Valphine’s sermon, Aurelan investigates the paperwork of the missing gnome Bimble overnight and rushes to meet the party outside the Spire of the Morninglord to share his findings [ch 053 / 053.01; ch 053 / entry].
*   Aurelan reveals he is on first-name terms with Brewbarry, asking to be called “Ori,” and shares that Bimble’s payments stopped abruptly with no severance or forwarding instructions, concluding the contract was abandoned rather than ended [ch 053 / 053.01].
*   Aurelan deduces that someone must be feeding the missing gnome to keep him hidden, advising the party to find who feeds Bimble to find Bimble [ch 053 / 053.01].

- After seeking the party at the Spire of the Morninglord, Aurelan intercepted them near the Guild Registry and Counting House, sweating from the exertion, and brought a request for an immediate Commission meeting. They accepted [ch 061 / 061.04].

## Last Observed State
Last encountered near the Guild Registry and Counting House after searching at the Spire of the Morninglord. He delivered the Commission's summons, which the party accepted. The record establishes this messenger role, without establishing membership in the Commission or a new financial agreement [ch 061 / 061.04].

## Relationships
*   **Brewbarry:** Initially intimidated by Brewbarry’s barbarian presence and halberd, Aurelan signed loan paperwork under duress [ch 051 / 051.02]. Their relationship evolved into a reciprocal “favor-based” alliance, with Aurelan providing financial intelligence [ch 052 / 052.05]. Aurelan now refers to him as “Brew” and considers him a friend [ch 053 / 053.01].
*   **Valphine:** Aurelan recognizes her status as a cleric of Lathander and her race as drow, reacting with surprise when she corrects his financial calculations [ch 051 / 051.02]. He was visibly horrified by her casual suggestion to use the Assassin’s Guild, requiring Vukradin to clarify she was joking [ch 052 / 052.05]. Her sermon subsequently motivated him to conduct an overnight investigation [ch 053 / 053.01].
*   **Vukradin:** Aurelan interacts professionally with Vukradin, Vukradin smoothed the loan negotiation with an appeal to an acceptable interest rate [ch 051 / 051.02]. He directed Vukradin to the fulfillment department and Vukradin was surprised to learn the Neverwinter Commission owns the Mountain’s Toe Gold Mine [ch 051 / 051.03].
*   **House Margaster:** Aurelan was aware of them as a cheaper shipping alternative and, in Chapter 052, said that to the best of his knowledge House Margaster does not sit on the Commission [ch 051 / 051.02; ch 052 / 052.05].
*   **Linene:** Aurelan identifies her as the party’s wealth management consultant and notes she has already communicated certain facts to them [ch 052 / 052.05].
