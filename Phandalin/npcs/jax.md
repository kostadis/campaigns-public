## Identity
Jax was a lieutenant and agent of the criminal organization known as the Carver, identified as the last of the Carver’s remaining agents active in the area [ch 036 / 036.05; ch 036 / entry; ch 037 / 037.02]. He led an ambush force comprising several thugs and a Rot-Tusk Ogre of the same clan with whom the party had established a prior blood feud [ch 036 / 036.05]. In combat, Jax possessed an Armor Class of 16 and 84 hit points, proving substantially tougher than his initial display suggested [ch 036 / 036.05]. He is confirmed as one of the Carver’s three lieutenants, alongside Borg and Sister Kaella [ch 037 / 037.02].

## Personality and Motivations
Jax framed his actions through a lens of professional detachment rather than personal animus. Upon ambushing the party, he explicitly stated to Vukradin, “I just want you to know, it’s not personal. It’s just business,” and thanked Vukradin for “dealing with that little bit of business” regarding Sister Kaella [ch 036 / 036.05; ch 036 / entry]. This suggests he viewed the elimination of threats as a contractual or organizational duty rather than a vendetta. Sister Kaella’s subsequent testimony confirms the ruthlessness behind this business demeanor: she believed Jax’s victory would have resulted in her certain and prolonged torture, contrasting his methods with the party’s [ch 037 / 037.03].

## History with the Party
*   Jax ambushed the party immediately after Sister Kaella was knocked unconscious, appearing alongside several thugs and a Rot-Tusk Ogre [ch 036 / 036.05; ch 036 / entry].
*   Jax addressed Vukradin, thanking him for removing Sister Kaella and stating that the attack was business, not personal [ch 036 / 036.05; ch 036 / moment].
*   Vukradin paralyzed Jax using *Hold Person*, rendering him unable to act effectively during the battle [ch 036 / 036.05; ch 036 / entry].
*   Soma attacked the paralyzed Jax in bear form, inflicting critical hits and heavy damage [ch 036 / 036.05; ch 036 / entry].
*   Brewbarry entered a rage and struck the paralyzed Jax twice with critical hits [ch 036 / 036.05; ch 036 / entry].
*   Valphine’s *Spirit Guardians* burned Jax and slowed his movement; its next pass finished Jax, and she then repositioned to trigger another round against the thugs [ch 036 / 036.05; ch 036 / entry].
*   Jax was killed by the party’s combined assault, leaving the last surviving henchman to flee [ch 036 / 036.05; ch 036 / entry].
*   Sister Kaella confirmed that a victory by Jax would have resulted in her prolonged torture and death [ch 037 / 037.03; ch 037 / entry].
*   Xanth the Centaur confirmed Jax had died at Dragon Barrow as one of the Carver’s three lieutenants [ch 037 / 037.02].

## Last Observed State
Status: Deceased. Jax was killed at Dragon Barrow during the ambush of the party [ch 037 / 037.02].

During the final confrontation, Jax appeared immediately after the party knocked Sister Kaella unconscious [ch 036 / 036.05]. He engaged the party but was quickly paralyzed by Vukradin’s *Hold Person* spell [ch 036 / 036.05]. Rendered helpless, Jax suffered heavy damage from Soma in bear form, critical strikes from an enraged Brewbarry, and radiant damage from Valphine’s *Spirit Guardians* [ch 036 / 036.05; ch 036 / entry]. Valphine’s Spirit Guardians finished Jax while he remained paralyzed; she then deliberately repositioned her aura to trigger another round that burned through more thugs [ch 036 / 036.05]. Xanth the Centaur later confirmed Jax had died in this battle [ch 037 / 037.02].

## Relationships
*   **The Carver:** Jax served as one of three lieutenants for the Carver, executing the boss’s will [ch 037 / 037.02]. Corbin, a lower-level bandit, mentioned Jax as a direct superior he had worked with, though Corbin had never met the Carver himself [ch 006 / 006.08].
*   **Vukradin:** Jax acknowledged Vukradin directly during the ambush, thanking him for neutralizing Sister Kaella prior to Jax’s arrival [ch 036 / 036.05]. Vukradin was the one who ultimately paralyzed Jax, leaving him vulnerable to the party’s killing blows [ch 036 / 036.05; ch 036 / entry].
*   **Sister Kaella:** Jax was a colleague of Kaella within the Carver’s hierarchy. Kaella feared Jax specifically, believing he would have tortured her extensively if he had prevailed [ch 037 / 037.03].
*   **The Rot-Tusk Ogre:** Jax commanded a Rot-Tusk Ogre from a clan previously fought by the party, indicating a shared history of violence with that group [ch 036 / 036.05].
*   **Borg:** Identified alongside Jax and Kaella as one of the Carver’s lieutenants [ch 037 / 037.02].
