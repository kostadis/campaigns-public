## Identity
Falcon the Hunter is the proprietor of Falcon’s Hunting Lodge in Neverwinter Wood [ch 020 / entry]. He presents himself as a retired adventurer and high-level ranger, boasting that his lodge was financed by a quarter-share of a slain dragon’s hoard [ch 020 / entry]. However, Falcon admits he is "a bit of a fake" and "a bit of a scavenger," having scavenged a ring and coins from the dragon Iymrith’s hoard after hearing of her death rather than slaying her [ch 021 / entry; ch 021 / 021.02]. He is an investor in the Mountain’s Toe Gold Mine and seeks to maintain a hospitality business based on this fabricated reputation [ch 025 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Falcon’s primary motivation is the preservation of his reputation and business model, which relies on guests who, in his words, “want to believe” his dragon-slaying legend [ch 021 / 021.02]. He is pragmatic and financially motivated, prioritizing monetary stakes over moral obligations, as seen when he offered the party a share of the mine rather than immediate cash to solve a poisoning issue [ch 025 / moment].

Falcon exhibits a volatile temperament, shifting between arrogant bravado and nihilistic despair. Initially, he dismissed threats with statements like "If they trouble me, they’ll be dead orcs" [ch 020 / 020.05]. After his lodge was destroyed and his girlfriend killed after the party failed to clear the Woodland Manse, he adopted a nihilistic stance, claiming that "intervention itself was the cause of his suffering" and denouncing the party at a Druid Council [ch 041 / entry; ch 041 / 041.03]. This despair was broken by Valphine’s counsel, leading him to admit he owed Vukradin an apology and recommit to action [ch 041 / 041.06]. He displays a ruthless streak in combat, shooting down fleeing enemies despite protests [ch 046 / 046.06], and pivots quickly to a demanding, hierarchical mindset when given new subordinates, instructing his new orc stewards to learn to "dress properly" [ch 046 / 046.06]. He is also susceptible to wonder and artistic admiration, expressing great enthusiasm for the performers Rimardo and Corrin despite his confusion over their uninvited arrival [ch 048 / 048.06].

## Last Observed State
In the most recent evidence, Falcon is alive and residing at his lodge, which is staffed by three orcish stewards in livery [ch 046 / 046.06; ch 048 / 048.06]. He is in high spirits and actively engaging in business ventures, having gifted Vukradin an unsealed letter offering a performance contract to Rimardo and Corrin [ch 048 / entry; ch 048 / 048.06]. The party also notes that Falcon’s Lodge served as the site where they previously sold planar artifacts to a drow woman named Sridar, who later asked the planar manifold’s operator, Bimble, what he had removed from the machine’s housing [ch 056 / 056.05].

## Relationships
*   **Vukradin:** The relationship is strained but functional. Vukradin previously searched Falcon’s quarters unnoticed; Falcon later confessed to being a fake, and the party agreed to keep the secret [ch 021 / 021.02]. Falcon’s nihilism led him to publicly attack Vukradin, but he later apologized via a Sending spell and joined the defense of the lodge [ch 041 / 041.03; ch 045 / 045.02]. He currently provides Vukradin with business connections, such as the contract for Rimardo and Corrin [ch 048 / 048.06].
*   **Valphine:** Valphine successfully counseled Falcon out of his despair by sharing her personal journey and reminding him of their shared financial interests [ch 041 / 041.05]. She facilitated his alliance with the orc tribe by convincing three orcs to work for him as stewards [ch 046 / 046.06].
*   **The Orc Tribe (Vorga, Prutha):** After a joint defense of the lodge, Falcon accepted honorary membership in the orc tribe and immediately asserted authority over his new orcish employees, focusing on their attire and discipline [ch 046 / 046.06].
*   **Rimardo and Corrin:** Falcon is confused by their uninvited appearance, insisting "I never ordered anything!" [ch 047 / entry]. Despite this confusion, he later became an admirer of their act and drafted a formal contract for their services [ch 048 / 048.06].
*   **Sridar:** The party met Sridar at Falcon’s Lodge and sold her planar artifacts there; Bimble’s description of her later tied her to the planar manifold [ch 056 / 056.05].
