## Identity
Skippy is a brigand and a low-ranking member of the Carver’s gang [ch 017 / entry; ch 018 / entry]. He is described by the narrative as one of the “disposable crew” [ch 018 / entry]. “Skippy” is a nickname acquired when the party captured him; his birth name is not recorded in the evidence [ch 017 / 017.03]. He was captured in Conyberry after Vukradin cast *Sleep* on him during a skirmish with the party [ch 017 / 017.03].

## Personality and Motivations
Skippy is characterized by cowardice and immediate compliance under threat. Upon waking from the *Sleep* spell surrounded by the party’s formidable forms, he “cracked instantly,” was “terrified,” and “smelled strongly of fear” [ch 018 / 018.03; ch 018 / entry]. He possesses no particular loyalty to the Carver, readily betraying operational secrets when physically intimidated [ch 018 / 018.03].

His intelligence manifests in a dry, deflating cynicism regarding the villain’s tactics. When asked how the Carver anticipated their movements, Skippy noted that the ambush was “boringly predictable,” explaining that the ranch attack was merely a lure to trigger a quest on the board for Harbin to hire adventurers [ch 018 / 018.03; ch 018 / moment]. He initially considered withholding information but reconsidered upon seeing blood on Soma’s claws, indicating a pragmatic survival instinct [ch 018 / 018.03; ch 018 / moment].

## History with the Party
- Skippy is a brigand captured by the party in Conyberry after Vukradin put him to sleep; the party tied him up before waking him to extract information [ch 017 / 017.03].
- After the party rescued Big Al and Petunia, they moved them to Corbin’s camp where Skippy was held [ch 017 / 017.05].
- Upon waking surrounded by the party (Brewbarry, Soma in bear form, and Vukradin), Skippy cracked instantly, revealing a planned ambush at Butterskull Ranch engineered by the Carver to lure the party [ch 018 / 018.03; ch 017 / 017.03].
- He identified Sister Kaella as the Carver’s spymaster running a network in Phandalin and Borg the Hammer as the half-orc enforcer commanding the ambush [ch 018 / 018.03; ch 017 / entry].
- Skippy explained that the ambush relied on predictable mechanics rather than spies, noting that the Carver knew knocking out a ranch would generate a quest that Harbin would use to hire adventurers [ch 018 / 018.03; ch 018 / moment].
- When deciding the prisoner’s fate, Vukradin insisted on returning him to Phandalin, but Corbin volunteered to guard him in Conyberry, promising twice to take care of him [ch 018 / 018.04].
- Vukradin agreed to Corbin’s offer on the condition that Skippy remain alive, warning that he “better not be dead” when the party returns [ch 018 / 018.04; ch 018 / moment].

## Last Observed State
Skippy was left in Corbin’s custody in Conyberry [ch 018 / 018.04; ch 018 / entry]. The party left him there while proceeding to Butterskull Ranch [ch 017 / entry]. His status is subject to a strict condition imposed by Vukradin, who agreed to Corbin’s offer to guard him in Conyberry but warned that Skippy had “better not be dead” by the time the party returned [ch 018 / 018.04; ch 018 / moment]. Corbin promised twice to “take care of him,” a phrasing both Soma and Vukradin interpreted as a desire for revenge rather than mere custody [ch 018 / 018.04; ch 018 / moment].

## Relationships
**Corbin**: Corbin identifies Skippy as a former gang mate and volunteered to guard him in Conyberry [ch 017 / 017.03; ch 018 / 018.04]. While Corbin asked about taking Skippy’s armor, Vukradin ruled this out [ch 018 / 018.04]. Both Soma and Vukradin could see that Corbin wanted revenge, and Soma thought he wanted to hide in a shell he does not have [ch 018 / 018.04; ch 018 / moment].

**Vukradin**: Vukradin incapacitated Skippy via *Sleep* during their initial encounter [ch 017 / 017.03]. Vukradin acted as the primary interrogator, demanding details about the Carver’s knowledge and the spy network [ch 018 / 018.03]. He holds ultimate authority over Skippy’s fate, overruling Corbin’s request to take his gear and setting the condition that Skippy must remain alive [ch 018 / 018.04].

**Soma**: Soma’s bear form and the blood on her claws served as the visual catalyst that convinced Skippy to reveal Sister Kaella’s identity as the spymaster [ch 018 / 018.03; ch 018 / moment]. She acted as a deterrent, intimidating Skippy through her physical presence and previous violence in the skirmish [ch 017 / 017.03].

**Brewbarry**: Brewbarry was present during the interrogation, providing the “menacing bulk” that contributed to Skippy’s immediate breakdown and confession [ch 018 / 018.03].
