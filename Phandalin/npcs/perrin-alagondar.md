## Identity
Perrin Alagondar is a man in his thirties who claims to be a descendant of Lady Alagondar and the rightful owner of the Necklace of Fireballs [ch 051 / entry]. He spoke of the necklace as more than jewelry, a symbol of the true heirs of Neverwinter [ch 051 / 051.05]. His lineage was validated by notarized paperwork sealed by House Margaster, a fact the party viewed with suspicion due to the house’s reputation [ch 051 / 051.05; ch 051 / 051.06]. A genealogist later confirmed he is “a descendant, perhaps one of the primary descendants” of the Alagondar family, a conclusion cross-referenced with Boney’s study of family records [ch 052 / 052.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Perrin is anxious, eager, and politically motivated. He waited for the party for hours and had read the recovery office’s letter “about 40 times” before approaching them, displaying a nervous intensity [ch 051 / 051.05]. He speaks with barely contained anxiety but becomes fired with passion when discussing “lineage,” declaring the Alagondars the “first family of Neverwinter” and Lord Neverember an “imposter” [ch 051 / 051.05]. He hastily walked back his revolutionary rhetoric, insisting, “I’m not going to start a revolution, no, no, this is a symbol” [ch 051 / 051.05].

He is closely assisted by House Margaster agents and launched into a tirade suggesting the Neverwinter Commission sabotaged the city’s shipping device to tighten control over trade [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.07]. He is not fully knowledgeable about the history of the artifact; he visibly did not know that the necklace had been in Lady Alagondar’s tomb rather than in family hands during the alleged theft [ch 051 / entry; ch 051 / 051.06]. He professes belief in the Morninglord as a god of good [ch 051 / 051.08]. Whether he is evil or merely controlled by evil actors remains unresolved [ch 052 / entry; ch 052 / 052.04].

## History with the Party
- Ser Kaelen provided a note identifying the Alagondar family as the rightful owners of the Necklace of Fireballs and introduced Perrin Alagondar as the man waiting to be found [ch 049 / 049.04].
- Perrin approached the party in Neverwinter, introducing himself as a descendant of Lady Alagondar and stating he had been waiting for them for hours and had read the recovery office’s letter approximately forty times [ch 051 / 051.05].
- He claimed the Necklace of Fireballs was a political symbol for those opposing Lord Neverember’s rule, referring to Neverember as an imposter, before hastily clarifying he was not starting a revolution [ch 051 / 051.05].
- House Margaster agents provided Perrin with notarized paperwork validating his lineage, which the party viewed with suspicion [ch 051 / 051.05; ch 051 / 051.06].
- Perrin launched into a tirade suggesting the Neverwinter Commission deliberately shut down the city’s interplanar shipping device to tighten their grip on trade [ch 051 / 051.07; ch 051 / moment].
- Boney noted holes in Perrin’s account of the necklace’s history, specifically that the item was in Lady Alagondar’s tomb rather than family hands when the alleged theft occurred, and Perrin visibly did not know all the facts [ch 051 / 051.05; ch 051 / 051.06].
- The party demanded Perrin produce three independent witnesses unaffiliated with House Margaster to prove his identity before surrendering the necklace [ch 051 / 051.06; ch 051 / 051.08].
- Perrin professed belief in the Morninglord as a god of good, allowing Valphine to require he meet the party at the Spire of the Morninglord [ch 051 / 051.08].
- Perrin arrived at the Spire of the Morninglord with three witnesses: a revolutionary enthusiast, a genealogist, and a notary clerk [ch 052 / 052.04].
- The genealogist confirmed Perrin was a descendant, possibly a primary one, of the Alagondar family, a conclusion cross-referenced with Boney’s study of family corpses and records [ch 052 / 052.04].
- The notary clerk revealed that many Alagondar records were purged when Lord Neverember took power, confirming that the remaining records supported Perrin’s claim [ch 052 / 052.04].
- Vukradin handed the Necklace of Fireballs to Perrin, honoring the principle of returning property to its rightful owner despite Brewbarry’s suspicion that Perrin was evil or being manipulated [ch 052 / 052.04].
- Perrin thanked the party greatly, after which Vukradin told him to get out of his sight [ch 052 / entry; ch 052 / 052.04].

## Last Observed State
Perrin was last seen in Chapter 052 at the Spire of the Morninglord, having successfully recovered the Necklace of Fireballs [ch 052 / 052.04]. He arrived accompanied by three independent witnesses: a revolutionary enthusiast, a genealogist, and a notary clerk [ch 052 / 052.04]. During the exchange, he squirmed whenever Brewbarry engaged in intimidating behavior but otherwise waited patiently [ch 052 / entry; ch 052 / 052.04]. After Vukradin honored his claim and handed over the necklace, Perrin thanked the party greatly [ch 052 / entry]. He was then dismissed by Vukradin, who told him to “get out of my sight,” and he departed [ch 052 / moment; ch 052 / 052.04]. Brewbarry advised him to use the necklace for good as a chance at redemption [ch 052 / 052.04].

Status not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
*   **The Party:** Perrin interacts primarily with the party as the claimant to the necklace. Brewbarry treats him with contempt, calling him “Perry” and demanding proof, while Vukradin engages with his claim on principle but ultimately dismisses him personally [ch 051 / 051.08; ch 052 / moment]. Valphine suspects Perrin is “intimidated and controlled by people who are evil” rather than being evil himself [ch 052 / 052.04].
*   **House Margaster:** Perrin is closely associated with House Margaster, who provided him with the notarized paperwork validating his lineage [ch 051 / 051.05; ch 051 / 051.06]. Cullen Sharpe greeted Perrin at the Margaster Logistics Operations, hoping “the letter of introduction we provided was sufficient” [ch 051 / 051.08].
*   **Ser Kaelen:** Ser Kaelen’s note named Perrin Alagondar as the man waiting to be found [ch 049 / 049.04].
*   **The Witnesses:**
    *   **Revolutionary Enthusiast:** A supporter who is genuinely thrilled that Perrin will recover a “symbol of the rightful rulers of Neverwinter” [ch 052 / 052.04].
    *   **Genealogist:** A professional who confirmed Perrin’s descent based on research that included consultations with Boney [ch 052 / 052.04].
    *   **Notary Clerk:** A frightened clerk who revealed that Alagondar records were purged under Lord Neverember; however, Soma’s investigation found the clerk “is not afraid of Perrin” [ch 052 / 052.04].
*   **Lord Neverember:** Perrin openly opposes Lord Neverember, whom he calls an “imposter,” and blames the current administration for the shutdown of the shipping device [ch 051 / 051.05; ch 051 / 051.07].
