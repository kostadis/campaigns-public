## Identity

Cullen Sharpe is a well-tailored associate and business representative of House Margaster in Neverwinter [ch 050 / entry]. He is consistently dressed in a dark green suit [ch 050 / 050.06; ch 051 / 051.08; ch 052 / 052.03]. He functions as a smooth-talking agent and fixer for the house, handling legal claims, extortion, and public relations [ch 050 / entry; ch 051 / entry].

In Chapter 62 the party directly encountered Cullen as a devil in fashionable clothing [ch 062 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations

Cullen is charming, smooth-talking, and heavily reliant on practiced etiquette and corporate slogans like “House Margaster always delivers the goods” [ch 050 / 050.06; ch 051 / entry]. He is unflappable in the face of hostility; when intimidated by Brewbarry, he treated the threats as friendly eccentricity and remained thoroughly unintimidated [ch 051 / 051.08]. He avoids direct conflict when possible, such as stopping his own fixer from drawing a weapon with a mere look [ch 051 / 051.08]. When Brewbarry took his coin pouch in front of the congregation, he was forced to smile and call it a generous donation [ch 052 / 052.03].

He pursued a future name through an offer to fund Valphine’s religious mission, and offered Vukradin studio backing despite expecting refusal. He expected refusal of the studio offer [ch 062 / entry].

## History with the Party
- Cullen Sharpe, a well-tailored associate of House Margaster in a dark green suit, arrived at the Moonstone Mask to assert a legal salvage claim on a recovered statue, introducing himself by spelling his name (“C-U-L-L-E-N, S-H-A-R-P-E”) and addressing each party member by name [ch 050 / 050.06].
- He denied House Margaster’s involvement in Elara’s death when accused by Vukradin, stating, “No, we had nothing to do with that” [ch 050 / 050.06].
- The party examined his documents, with Vukradin rolling a natural 20 on Perception, concluding they were part of a fraudulent racket; Cullen successfully extorted 200 gold from Lord Cassian by transferring the writ with a satisfied smile [ch 050 / 050.06].
- Cullen attempted to recruit the party into a regional distribution partnership for goods found in the Phandalin hinterlands, claiming House Margaster “always delivers the goods” and hinting at city opportunities, but left without donating to the party’s music studio [ch 050 / 050.06].
- At the Margaster Logistics Operations at the Eastern Quay, Cullen greeted the party warmly, denied involvement in the broken shipping device (“perhaps if we had been, it would not have gotten damaged”), and revealed a gnome had been hired to work on it [ch 051 / 051.08].
- Cullen asserted that Ser Kaelen had reached out to House Margaster regarding the party’s finds, a claim the party had no way to verify [ch 051 / 051.08].
- Brewbarry attempted to intimidate Cullen regarding the device, but Cullen remained unfazed, treating the threats as friendly eccentricity and failing to detect Brewbarry’s clumsy deception regarding the device’s repair potential [ch 051 / 051.08].
- A Margaster fixer reached for his weapon during a confrontation with the party, but Cullen stopped him with a look; Cullen also agreed to attend the party’s benefit concert as a house representative [ch 051 / 051.08].
- Cullen attended the benefit concert wearing a green suit, complimented the band’s name (Lathander’s Death), and attempted to pay his tithe with a smile instead of gold [ch 052 / 052.03].
- Brewbarry physically wrestled Cullen’s entire coin pouch away and dropped it into the donation basket; Cullen, surrounded by the congregation, was forced to smile and call it a generous donation [ch 052 / 052.03].
- Cullen assured Vukradin that “we want the same things” regarding returning items to owners, offering to help provide the necessary people for this process [ch 052 / 052.03].
- Vukradin refused to meet with Cullen, so Brewbarry met him alone to collect the gnome technician’s name; Cullen provided the name Bimble Nackle and denied knowledge of his location or employer, stating House Margaster only heard of him after the machinery failed [ch 052 / 052.05].
- The DM confirmed Cullen was not lying about his ignorance of Bimble’s location or House Margaster’s lack of involvement in his hiring [ch 052 / 052.05].

- At tea, Cassian presented a forged harbor writ made by Cullen Sharpe and used against him. The GM confirmed Cullen’s involvement in forgeries [ch 058 / 058.02; ch 058 / entry].

- Cullen arrived unarmed after Aletra's death and revealed Bimble's House Margaster contract and magical non-disclosure restriction. He also asserted a separate commission contract giving the house control over repairs [ch 060 / 060.06].
- Brewbarry tore up the paper, but the agreement remanifested on another sheet and Cullen produced another copy. Cullen emphasized that the contracts belonged to House Margaster rather than to him personally [ch 060 / 060.06].
- The party killed Cullen. Bimble's restriction persisted; Adrian arrived to take over Cullen's business and was also killed. Both Margaster bodies were present when Aldus Hern arrived [ch 060 / 060.06; ch 060 / 060.07].

- The Seat later described the party's two Margaster killings as promotions, saying special partner contracts continue through death and return slain agents as infernal beings. This refers contextually to Cullen and Adrian, the two representatives killed at the warehouse; it is her account, not a witnessed transformation [ch 060 / 060.06; ch 061 / 061.06].
- During the warehouse skirmish, Cullen contacted Valphine through Sending, reiterated that the deal remained available, and invited the party downstairs while requesting that they sign a waiver. The party de-escalated and descended without signing or making a bargain [ch 062 / 062.07].
- After the party examined the restoration contract and discovered a potential route around House Margaster’s repair ownership, Cullen called from the adjoining room and invited them inside [ch 062 / 062.08].
- Cullen offered Valphine the Instrument of Patronage, permitted her to share its initially private terms, and conditioned funding for her religious mission on obtaining a future name. He also offered Vukradin studio funding and remarked that they had chosen not to drug his ink [ch 062 / entry; ch 062 / moment].

This supersedes his previously observed death; the summaries do not depict his transformation or establish his home plane [ch 060 / 060.06; ch 061 / 061.06; ch 062 / entry; ch 062 / 062.07; ch 062 / 062.08].

## Relationships

*   **House Margaster:** Cullen serves as a representative, using the house’s name to legitimize extortion (against Lord Cassian) and monopolistic logistics operations [ch 050 / 050.06; ch 051 / 051.08].
*   **Brewbarry:** Cullen views Brewbarry as a “colorful new business contact” and failed to recognize Brewbarry’s clumsy deception or intimidation attempts as threats [ch 051 / 051.08]. Brewbarry physically wrestled Cullen’s coin pouch away in front of a congregation, forcing Cullen to call it a donation [ch 052 / 052.03]. Brewbarry subsequently planned to spy on Cullen using enchanted coins [ch 052 / 052.05].
*   **Vukradin:** Cullen addresses Vukradin with practiced charm (“Mr. Vukradin, I’m so delighted to see you”) [ch 051 / 051.08]. Vukradin refuses to meet with Cullen directly, forcing Brewbarry to handle the negotiations [ch 052 / 052.05].
*   **Lord Cassian:** Cullen successfully extorted 200 gold from Lord Cassian by presenting salvage writs the party judged to be a fraudulent racket, a transaction Cassian nervously insisted was “totally legitimate” [ch 050 / 050.06].
*   **Ser Kaelen:** Cullen asserted that Kaelen reached out to House Margaster regarding the party’s finds [ch 051 / 051.08].

**Bimble Nackle:** Cullen disclosed the gnome's employment and magical non-disclosure restriction. **Adrian:** A subsequent House Margaster representative announced that he was taking over Cullen's business, commenting that he had warned Cullen to be careful; the party killed him too [ch 060 / 060.06].

**Valphine:** Cullen contacted her through Sending and offered the Instrument of Patronage, permitting her to share its initially private terms. Funding her religious mission was contingent on supplying a future name [ch 062 / 062.07; ch 062 / entry]. **Vukradin:** Cullen also offered studio funding despite expecting refusal [ch 062 / entry].
