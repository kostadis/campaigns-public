## Identity
Tibor Wester is the operator of a logging camp and the half-brother of Harbin Wester [ch 023 / entry; ch 042 / entry]. In the party’s initial encounters, he was identified as the camp chief and the designated recipient of a supply delivery requiring his signature on a notice of delivery [ch 022 / entry; ch 023 / entry]. In chapter 042, he is characterized as the operator of an illegal wildcat logging camp that cuts timber without permits, violating ecological agreements between Neverwinter and local druids [ch 042 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Tibor presents himself as an anxious and evasive individual who claims to have no enemies and asserts that he is friends with everyone [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.05]. He said he survived the ankheg attack by running into his office and locking himself in while the ankhegs took the rest of the crew down [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.05]. When asked whether the strangers’ weapons were magical, he answered, “I’m a logger. I’m not a magician,” and saying he ignored the earlier argument between Emerald Enclave agents and strangers with strange weapons because he viewed them as faction business rather than his own [ch 023 / 023.05]. He gladly signed Vukradin’s paper and refused to take a prisoner to Neverwinter, deeming the journey too risky [ch 023 / 023.05; ch 024 / entry]. In Chapter 042 he is reported to have hired new workers for an illegal wildcat logging camp cutting timber without permits [ch 042 / entry].

## History with the Party
*   Tibor Wester was the designated recipient of a supply delivery to a logging camp; the party arrived to find him missing and the camp deserted [ch 022 / entry; ch 022 / 022.05].
*   Upon discovering Tibor hiding in a hysterical state within his office after the party defeated ankhegs, Vukradin explained the delivery errand; Tibor gratefully signed the notice of delivery, satisfying the signature requirement [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.04; ch 023 / 023.05].
*   Tibor claimed he had survived by locking himself in his office and asserted he had no enemies, though he revealed that people with strange, sharp weapons had argued with the Emerald Enclave the day before the attack [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.05].
*   Tibor suggested the party investigate up the river into the Neverwinter Wood and declined an escort to his half-brother in Phandalin, intending to hire a new crew instead [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.05].
*   Tibor identified the logging area as part of the Neverwinter Wood, pointing to the river as the next investigative lead [ch 023 / 023.06].
*   Tibor refused to transport the prisoner Shrimpy to Neverwinter’s jail, deeming the journey too risky, which left Shrimpy in the party’s custody [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.01].
*   Tibor is the operator of an illegal wildcat logging camp and the half-brother of Harbin Wester [ch 042 / entry].

## Last Observed State
Tibor Wester was not physically present in chapter 042; his status and operations were revealed through his hired workers [ch 042 / 042.06]. The party encountered a group of young druids arguing with Tibor’s newly hired wildcat loggers, who confirmed they were working for Tibor and cutting timber without permits [ch 042 / 042.06]. Vukradin delivered a speech that persuaded the loggers to abandon the operation, cry, apologize, and agree to report Tibor Wester’s illegal camp to Neverwinter [ch 042 / 042.06; ch 042 / moment]. There is no evidence that Tibor was confronted, arrested, or seen during this encounter. Status not established in the summaries beyond the report that his workers agreed to turn him in [ch 042 / entry].

## Relationships
Harbin Wester: Tibor is Harbin’s half-brother [ch 023 / entry; ch 042 / entry]. The party’s initial supply delivery was connected to Harbin, and Tibor declined an escort to Harbin’s location in Phandalin, preferring to stay and hire a new crew [ch 023 / 023.05].

Emerald Enclave: Tibor’s camp hosted Joe, an Emerald Enclave agent [ch 023 / 023.06]. Tibor observed a dispute between Enclave agents and strangers with sharp weapons but chose to ignore it, assuming it was Enclave business rather than his concern [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.05].

Vukradin: Vukradin explained the errand and secured Tibor’s signature on the notice of delivery [ch 023 / 023.05]. Tibor refused to take Shrimpy to Neverwinter, saying it would be too dangerous [ch 024 / 024.01]. Later, Vukradin’s speech directly caused Tibor’s workforce to abandon his illegal operation and agree to report him to authorities [ch 042 / 042.06; ch 042 / moment].
