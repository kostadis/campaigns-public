## Identity
Cryovain is a white dragon characterized by white scales that blend with the snow [ch 038 / 038.01; ch 038 / entry]. The party located the dragon at Icespire Hold, where it was initially spotted apparently sleeping on the main fortress roof [ch 038 / 038.01; ch 038 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
An Animal Handling check with a result of 24 determined that Cryovain was in a state of “seething, psychotic rage” [ch 038 / 038.03; ch 038 / entry]. This rage was so extreme that the DM established releasing the dragon would cause it to attack everything nearby, undermining hopes of an grateful ally [ch 038 / 038.03; ch 038 / entry]. While under the Carver’s incomplete domination, Cryovain’s rage kept it engaged in combat rather than flying away tactically [ch 039 / entry].

## History with the Party
- The party located Cryovain at Icespire Hold, where the dragon was initially seen apparently sleeping on the roof with white scales blending into the snow [ch 038 / 038.01; ch 038 / entry].
- The party discovered Cryovain immobilized within a summoning circle, bound by magical chains while the Carver attempted to dominate it [ch 038 / 038.03; ch 038 / entry].
- An Animal Handling check revealed Cryovain was in a state of extreme rage and would attack everyone nearby if released [ch 038 / 038.03; ch 038 / entry].
- The party interrupted the Carver’s ritual, causing the chains to dissolve and forcing Cryovain into a three-round window of resistance against the Carver’s direct domination [ch 038 / 038.04; ch 038 / entry].
- The party engaged Cryovain in combat on the fortress battlements, where Valphine, Brewbarry, and Vukradin dealt significant damage to the dragon [ch 038 / 038.05; ch 038 / entry].
- Cryovain fought from the air during the resumed battle, using freezing breath against Brewbarry, but was ultimately slain by Valphine with Sacred Flame after being reduced to two hit points [ch 039 / 039.01; ch 039 / 039.02; ch 039 / entry].
- Valphine identified the Eastern Heart orc tribe as refugees displaced by Cryovain from their ancestral home at Icespire Hold [ch 045 / 045.04].
- The party revealed to the orcs that they had killed Cryovain, prompting Vorga to ask whether the home was free to go back to, with the caveat that something worse than the dragon may now occupy it [ch 045 / 045.04; ch 045 / moment].
- The party possessed an untransformed lump of dragon’s gold from Cryovain’s hoard, which they refused to use, later donating the fused slag to the Universal Basic Treasure fund [ch 048 / 048.08; ch 049 / 049.03].

## Last Observed State
Cryovain is dead. The party slew the dragon during combat on the Icespire Hold battlements [ch 039 / 039.02; ch 039 / entry]. After being reduced to two hit points by Vukradin’s attack, Cryovain failed a saving throw against Valphine’s Sacred Flame and died [ch 039 / entry; ch 039 / moment]. The Carver’s essence, which had been attempting to possess the dragon, dissipated upon Cryovain’s death [ch 039 / 039.02]. The party later possessed an untransformed lump of dragon’s gold from Cryovain’s hoard [ch 048 / 048.08; ch 049 / 049.03].

## Relationships
**The Party**: The party engaged Cryovain in combat, with Valphine, Brewbarry, and Vukradin dealing significant damage [ch 038 / 038.05; ch 039 / entry]. Brewbarry suffered freezing breath attacks from Cryovain during the battle [ch 038 / 038.05; ch 039 / 039.02]. Valphine delivered the killing blow [ch 039 / 039.02; ch 039 / moment]. The party’s defeat of Cryovain is the central subject of their recent interactions with the Eastern Heart orc tribe, as the party revealed they had killed the dragon [ch 045 / 045.04; ch 045 / moment].

**The Carver**: Cryovain was the target of the Carver’s ritual, which sought to consume and dominate the dragon [ch 038 / 038.03; ch 038 / entry]. The Carver’s voice spoke into the party’s minds as it attempted to seize control of Cryovain, stating, “And now, now I shall have everything I always wanted” [ch 038 / 038.04; ch 038 / moment]. Cryovain resisted this domination for a three-round window before being killed [ch 038 / 038.04; ch 038 / entry].

**Eastern Heart Orc Tribe**: The Eastern Heart orcs were displaced from their ancestral home at Icespire Hold by Cryovain [ch 045 / 045.04]. Valphine identified the tribe as refugees from this dragon [ch 045 / 045.04]. Upon learning of Cryovain’s death, Vorga asked whether the home was free to go back to, and a caveat was added that something worse than the dragon may now occupy it [ch 045 / 045.04; ch 045 / moment].
