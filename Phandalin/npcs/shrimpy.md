## Identity
Shrimpy is an orc raider and prisoner of the party, also known by his birth name, Rob [ch 022 / entry; ch 022 / 022.04]. He belongs to an independent orc faction that refused to submit to Talosian cultists or brigands following the displacement of their people from the Sword Mountains by a white dragon [ch 022 / entry; ch 022 / 022.04]. The party captured him during a skirmish and renamed him Shrimpy, a title he did not consent to but was forced to accept [ch 022 / 022.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Shrimpy is motivated by a desire for freedom and an end to his forced labor. He asks whether he gets paid, and later asks to leave after carrying the party’s belongings for several days [ch 022 / 022.04; ch 024 / 024.01]. When denied, he attempts to manipulate situations to his advantage, such as trying to convince his guard that the party was dead to secure his own freedom [ch 026 / 026.03]. He adheres to the belief that “orcs work for orcs,” distinguishing himself from those who serve external patrons [ch 022 / 022.04].

## History with the Party
- Shrimpy was captured by the party after Vukradin put him to sleep during a skirmish with orc raiders; the party ignored his objection to be called by his actual name, Rob, and renamed him Shrimpy [ch 022 / entry; ch 022 / 022.04].
- He is an independent orc raider who revealed that a white dragon displaced his people from the Sword Mountains, leading to a fractured society of Talosian cultists, brigands, and independents [ch 022 / entry; ch 022 / 022.04].
- The party forces Shrimpy to pull their supply cart by tying his hands to the handles, with Brewbarry defending the wine on the cart from potential theft [ch 022 / 022.04].
- He remained bound to the cart and forced to pull it during the party’s journey to the abandoned logging camp [ch 022 / 022.05].
- Shrimpy hauled the wagon and supplies for the party at the logging camp, remaining there while the others investigated the silence [ch 023 / entry; ch 023 / 023.01].
- After several days of carrying the party’s belongings, Shrimpy requested release, but Vukradin refused, insisting he must be transported to Phandalin for imprisonment due to the earlier ambush [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.01].
- Tibor, the loggers chief, refused to transport Shrimpy to Neverwinter, leaving the party responsible for his custody [ch 024 / 024.01].
- During an ambush by Talosian orcs, Vukradin attempted to use Shrimpy as proof of the party’s friendliness to orcs, but this backfired because Shrimpy belonged to an independent faction that opposed Talosian leadership [ch 024 / 024.07].
- Shrimpy was one of six prisoners (along with three mind-controlled warriors, a cult fanatic, and a possessed woman) transported by the party toward Phandalin [ch 024 / 024.06].
- He assisted in keeping restrained cult warriors from crawling toward the fanatic during transport planning [ch 024 / entry; ch 024 / 024.06].
- Upon arriving at the Falcon Hunting Lodge, Shrimpy asked if he could leave, but the party refused and introduced him to Falcon as a “genuine criminal” [ch 025 / entry; ch 025 / 025.02].
- While the main party departed for the Woodland Manse, Shrimpy remained at Falcon’s Lodge along with the rescued Emerald Enclave guards and Teega [ch 025 / 025.05].
- At the clearing where Corbin guarded the cart, Shrimpy attempted to persuade Corbin that the party was dead and that he should therefore be released, claiming he knew this from hearing distant lightning [ch 026 / entry; ch 026 / 026.03].
- Shrimpy was delivered to Town Master Harbin Wester in Phandalin upon the party’s return [ch 026 / entry; ch 026 / 026.04].

## Last Observed State
Shrimpy was delivered to Town Master Harbin Wester in Phandalin upon the party’s return [ch 026 / entry; ch 026 / 026.04]. Prior to this, he was guarded by Corbin at a clearing, where he attempted to persuade Corbin that the party had perished at the Woodland Manse, claiming to have heard explosions and seen flashes from a distance [ch 026 / 026.03]. Following this interaction, he was transported with the party to Phandalin and handed over to local authorities [ch 026 / 026.03; ch 026 / 026.04]. His status after delivery to the Town Master is not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
*   **The Party:** Shrimpy was held as a prisoner and laborer by the party. He was forced to pull their supply cart with his hands tied to the handles [ch 022 / 022.04; ch 022 / 022.05]. Brewbarry enforced his labor and defended the cart’s wine from him [ch 022 / 022.04]. Vukradin insisted on transporting him to Phandalin for imprisonment despite Shrimpy’s requests for release [ch 024 / 024.01].
*   **Corbin:** Corbin acted as Shrimpy’s guard during the party’s absence at the Woodland Manse [ch 026 / 026.03]. Shrimpy attempted to manipulate Corbin into releasing him by falsely claiming the party was dead [ch 026 / 026.03].
*   **Falcon:** Shrimpy was presented to Falcon at the Hunting Lodge as “a genuine criminal” while the main party departed for a side quest [ch 025 / 025.02]. He remained at the lodge during the party’s absence [ch 025 / 025.05].
*   **Talosian Orcs:** Vukradin’s attempt to point to Shrimpy as proof the party was friendly to orcs backfired, because Shrimpy belonged to an independent faction opposed to Talosian leadership [ch 024 / 024.07].
