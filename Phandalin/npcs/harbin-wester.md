## Identity
Harbin Wester served as the Townmaster and later Lord Mayor of Phandalin from Chapter 2 through Chapter 57 [ch 002 / entry; ch 027 / entry]. He is the formal leader of the Privy Council established to advise on community decisions [ch 027 / 027.01]. In Chapter 57, Lord Neverember issued a written decree replacing Harbin as mayor with Vukradin, though Harbin retained authority over daily administration while Vukradin provided “big picture” governance [ch 057 / 057.07]. He is the half-brother of Tibor Wester, an owner of an illegal logging operation [ch 042 / 042.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Harbin is characterized by extreme caution and self-preservation, frequently refusing to open his doors to visitors out of fear they might be dragons [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 006 / 006.04]. He justifies withholding payment or resources by citing Lord Neverember’s policies or procedural requirements, such as claiming recovered monster treasure counts as payment [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 008 / 008.03]. He is politically maneuvering and prone to co-opting others’ achievements, framing community victories and new initiatives like Universal Basic Treasure as validation of his own leadership and Privy Council [ch 040 / 040.04; ch 049 / 049.03]. Harbin maintains that his appointment can only be revoked by Lord Neverember, emphasizing his reliance on higher authority for legitimacy [ch 019 / 019.02].

## Last Observed State
As of Chapter 57, Harbin was removed from the position of mayor via a decree from Lord Neverember, which stated “Not according to this piece of paper” in response to questions about his continued mayoralty [ch 057 / 057.07; ch 057 / moment]. He did not appear personally in Chapter 57; his status and role change were established by the decree and Neverember’s explanation that Harbin would continue handling daily administration [ch 057 / 057.07]. ## Relationships
**Vukradin:** Harbin views Vukradin with a mix of political necessity and annoyance, frequently stuttering over his name and attempting to take credit for his ideas [ch 049 / 049.03; ch 049 / moment]. Vukradin eventually replaced Harbin as mayor, leading a Privy Council that Harbin had initially formed [ch 019 / 019.07; ch 057 / 057.07].

**Lord Neverember:** Harbin operates under the authority of Lord Neverember, citing the Lord’s decrees to justify his actions and assert that only Neverember can remove him [ch 004 / 004.04; ch 019 / 019.02]. Neverember ultimately removed Harbin as mayor in favor of Vukradin for strategic reasons [ch 057 / 057.07].

**The Party:** Harbin has a transactional and somewhat dismissive relationship with the party, often treating them with fear or bureaucratic avoidance. He has paid them rewards when pressured but attempted to withhold coin by invoking self-funding policies [ch 008 / 008.04]. The party generally views him with little respect; Vukradin explicitly stated he does not respect Harbin [ch 049 / entry]. Harbin credited the party’s success as proof of his own wise governance rather than their individual heroism [ch 040 / 040.04].

**Tibor Wester:** Harbin is the half-brother of Tibor Wester, whose illegal logging activities were a source of conflict for the party and Soma [ch 042 / 042.06; ch 034 / 034.05].

**Adabra:** Harbin sought Adabra’s help against the dragon threat and later had to negotiate payment when she intervened on behalf of the party [ch 007 / entry; ch 008 / 008.03].
