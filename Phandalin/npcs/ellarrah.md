## Identity
Ellarrah is a middle-aged human merchant traveling with a small trade cart and her groom, Jarek [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.01]. She is devout enough to interpret a shouted deity name as a greeting from a priestess and to offer a reward directed toward that faith [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.01; ch 009 / 009.04].

## Personality and Motivations
Ellarrah is shaken by attack but retains curiosity, noting specific, strange details about her attackers even when those details fade from memory as she speaks [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.03]. She accepts aid readily, asking the party to mend her cart and recover a bolted horse [ch 009 / 009.01; ch 009 / 009.02]. She directs her payment past the bard Vukradin to the drow Valphine, having read Valphine’s shout as devotion [ch 009 / 009.01; ch 009 / 009.04]. Her groom Jarek suggests a lack of caution in her travel planning, remarking they should have traveled with protection [ch 009 / 009.02].

## History with the Party
- Ellarrah is a middle-aged human merchant traveling with a small trade cart and her groom, Jarek, when the party encounters them; their pack horses have been scattered by an orc attack [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.01].
- Upon meeting the party, Ellarrah identifies the attackers as orcs [ch 009 / 009.01; ch 009 / moment].
- When Valphine shouts “Lathander!”, Ellarrah interprets the drow as a devout priestess and expresses admiration for her apparent faith [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.01; ch 009 / moment].
- Ellarrah admits she has never heard the theological teaching Valphine implies (“He’s painful”) [ch 009 / 009.01; ch 009 / moment].
- The party mends Ellarrah’s broken cart, and she asks for help retrieving a bolted pack horse, which Brewbarry recovers [ch 009 / 009.01; ch 009 / 009.02].
- During the party’s work, her groom Jarek remarks to her that they should have traveled with protection [ch 009 / 009.02].
- When pressed for details on the ambush, Ellarrah describes the orcs as having “unnerving discipline” and carrying markings “like a swirl, or an eye,” though she loses the memory of the marking as she tries to recall it [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.03].
- Ellarrah offers a reward for the party’s assistance, directing it past Vukradin to Valphine with the instruction, “For the Morninglord and whomever you feel is worthy” [ch 009 / entry; ch 009 / 009.04; ch 009 / moment].
- The party escorts Ellarrah and Jarek safely back to Phandalin [ch 009 / 009.04; ch 009 / 009.05].
- Vukradin reports the rescue to Ser Kaelen Thorn, and afterward reflects that getting Ellarrah and Jarek home safely and being paid for it shows he can earn coin through genuine assistance [ch 009 / 009.05].

## Last Observed State
Ellarrah was safely escorted back to Phandalin after the party assisted her [ch 009 / 009.04; ch 009 / 009.05]. During the encounter, she provided an account of the ambush that became elusive in the telling, describing the orcs as having “unnerving discipline” and carrying markings “like a swirl, or an eye,” before losing the memory of the marking [ch 009 / 009.03]. She offered a reward to the party, specifically instructing that it be given “For the Morninglord and whomever you feel is worthy” [ch 009 / 009.04].
