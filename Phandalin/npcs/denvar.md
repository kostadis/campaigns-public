## Identity
Denvar is a delivery man operating a recurring smuggling route through the sewers of Neverwinter [ch 054 / entry]. He works as one of two carriers, depositing nine crates during the third hour of the night; eight are for a contact named Lim, while the ninth is separated for a different recipient [ch 054 / 054.02; ch 054 / entry]. He resides in a high-efficiency, single-room-occupancy building in the Bluelake District, requiring significant cash up front, which suggests an income higher than an ordinary crate carrier [ch 054 / 054.04; ch 054 / entry]. The party’s landlord, “Uncle Tom,” describes him as quiet, reliable, and unobtrusive, with no known affiliation to any faction or insignia found in his room [ch 054 / 054.04; ch 054 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
Valphine’s intuition identified Denvar as the more knowledgeable of the two carriers, distinguishing him from the other man who appeared to be merely stacking boxes [ch 054 / 054.02]. Despite his involvement in smuggling, he behaves like an ordinary laborer, deliberately avoiding acknowledgment of his partner to maintain the appearance of independence [ch 054 / 054.02; ch 054 / entry]. He appears to be driven by financial necessity or opportunity, evidenced by his ability to afford a cook shop breakfast at four in the morning and his immediate need to collect payment to settle an overdue rent obligation [ch 054 / entry; ch 054 / 054.04]. His actions are cautious but confident; he acted with discretion when accessing a dead drop and departed smiling after retrieving payment, suggesting a routine familiarity with the arrangement [ch 054 / 054.05].

## History with the Party
*   The party observed Denvar during a stakeout at a sewer grill in Neverwinter, where he acted as one of two carriers depositing nine crates during the third hour of the night [ch 054 / 054.02; ch 054 / entry].
*   Denvar was identified by Valphine as the carrier who placed the ninth crate separately on a ledge and looked into the darkness, suggesting he was more knowledgeable about the operation than the other carrier [ch 054 / 054.02].
*   Valphine, Soma, and Brewbarry followed Denvar as he walked to a cook shop and ate breakfast alone, deliberately behaving as if the carriers did not know each other to avoid drawing attention [ch 054 / 054.02; ch 054 / 054.04].
*   The party tracked Denvar to a single-room-occupancy-style building in the Bluelake District, where he slept during the day [ch 054 / 054.04].
*   Soma’s familiar rat scouted Denvar’s room and discovered delivery chits with the instruction “9 crates, 3rd hour, 8 up, 1 down,” revealing that the most recent chit remained unpaid while previous ones were settled [ch 054 / 054.04; ch 054 / entry].
*   The party’s landlord of record, “Uncle Tom,” confirmed Denvar was a quiet, good customer who owed rent that day, leading the party to conclude Denvar needed to collect payment before the end of the day [ch 054 / 054.04].
*   After waiting several hours, the party watched Denvar wake up and approach a stone wall adjoining the House of a Thousand Faces [ch 054 / 054.05].
*   Denvar retrieved a bag of payment from a hidden stone in the wall, acting with discretion before walking away smiling, confirming a two-way dead drop arrangement [ch 054 / 054.05; ch 054 / entry].
*   The party investigated the building behind the dead drop, identifying it as the House of a Thousand Faces, and subsequently discovered Harper symbols in its basement office via Soma’s familiar [ch 054 / 054.05].

## Last Observed State
Denvar was last observed waking up after a several-hour nap in his Bluelake District room [ch 054 / 054.05]. He walked to a stone exterior wall adjoining the House of a Thousand Faces, looked around to ensure discretion, pulled loose a stone, and retrieved a bag containing his payment for the latest delivery [ch 054 / 054.05; ch 054 / entry]. After replacing the stone, he walked away smiling [ch 054 / 054.05]. The party subsequently identified the building behind the dead drop as the House of a Thousand Faces, which contains Harper symbols in its basement office [ch 054 / 054.05].

## Relationships
**The Other Carrier**: Denvar works with another carrier who deposits eight crates for a contact named Lim. The two deliberately behave as if they do not know each other to avoid drawing attention [ch 054 / 054.02].

**Uncle Tom**: The landlord of Denvar’s residence, who considers Denvar a quiet, good customer despite his overdue rent [ch 054 / 054.04]. Tom confirmed Denvar’s name and character to the party [ch 054 / 054.04].

**The Harpers**: Denvar uses a two-way dead drop connected to the House of a Thousand Faces, which the party identified as a Harper-run facility via its basement symbols [ch 054 / 054.05; ch 054 / entry]. It is not established if the Harpers are arranging the ninth crate or simply acting as intermediaries for another client [ch 054 / 054.05].

**The Construct**: Denvar is responsible for placing the ninth crate on a ledge and pausing to look down into the darkness as if expecting a collector, which was a brass and leather construct [ch 054 / 054.02; ch 054 / entry].
