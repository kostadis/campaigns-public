## Identity
Borg the Hammer is a large half-orc who serves as the Carver’s enforcer and commander [ch 017 / 017.03]. He is identified as one of the Carver’s three lieutenants, alongside Sister Kaella and Jax [ch 037 / 037.02]. Physically, he wears efficient chainmail and carries a shield, and he commands a contingent of rot-tusk ogres [ch 018 / 018.10; ch 018 / 018.06].

## Personality and Motivations
Borg is characterized by a contradiction between outward rage and underlying discipline. Valphine observes that he is “disciplined rather than chaotic,” though his tactical methods are considered crude [ch 018 / 018.10]. He reacts to failure with excessive, gratuitous violence; when his ambush failed, he executed the lone surviving bandit for incompetence and burned the entirety of Butterskull Ranch—including the farmhouse, cornfields, and gardens—rather than attempting to salvage the situation or save his assets [ch 018 / 018.10; ch 017 / 017.06]. Despite this temper, he possesses moments of tactical cunning, evidenced by his decision to split his forces at the ranch by sending his ogres in a direction opposite to his own retreat [ch 018 / 018.11]. His primary operational focus was the execution of specific orders, notably the instruction from Sister Kaella to kill Big Al Kalazorn and leave no witnesses [ch 019 / 019.09].

## History with the Party
- Skippy, a captured brigand, identified Borg the Hammer as a large half-orc serving as the Carver’s enforcer and commander, likely overseeing the Butterskull Ranch ambush [ch 017 / 017.03].
- Big Al Kalazorn explained that after orcs raided Butterskull Ranch, Borg arrived with ogres, killed the orcs, occupied the house, and kept Big Al alive as bait for the party [ch 017 / 017.04; ch 018 / 018.06].
- The party observed from a distance that Borg executed a fleeing bandit (Bandit Number 4) in a fit of rage because the party had not walked into his trap [ch 017 / 017.06; ch 018 / 018.10].
- In a fit of rage after the failed ambush, Borg burned the farmhouse, cornfields, and gardens of Butterskull Ranch while the party watched, choosing to track him rather than save the farm [ch 017 / 017.06; ch 018 / 018.10].
- Vukradin deduced from the ripped and mangled wounds on the dead orcs at the ranch that Borg the Hammer was present, or something of similar size and violence [ch 018 / 018.05].
- Valphine noted that Borg was disciplined rather than chaotic, though his tactics were considered crude, and she rated his decision to send his ogres in one direction while he departed in another as a spark of tactical cunning [ch 018 / 018.10; ch 018 / 018.11].
- Sister Kaella revealed to Valphine that she had assigned Borg the specific task of killing Big Al and leaving no witnesses, a plan that failed and collapsed the attempt to frame the party [ch 019 / 019.09; ch 019 / 019.10].
- Sister Kaella asked Locutus whether he had killed Borg; he nodded and Valphine produced the head, and Kaella then struck Locutus down [ch 019 / 019.09; ch 019 / moment].
- The party later learned that the “disastrous Borg the Hammer incident” weakened the Carver’s position, after which the ogres once aligned with the Carver apparently changed sides [ch 033 / 033.02].
- Xanthopoulos, a centaur, confirmed to the party that Borg was one of the Carver’s three lieutenants and stated that Borg had been killed by Locutus in the forest after fleeing Butterskull Ranch [ch 037 / 037.02].

## Last Observed State
Borg the Hammer was killed by the orc Locutus in the forest after fleeing Butterskull Ranch [ch 019 / moment; ch 037 / 037.02]. When Kaella asked Locutus whether he had killed Borg, he nodded and Valphine produced the head; Kaella then killed Locutus [ch 019 / 019.09; ch 019 / 019.10]. The “disastrous Borg the Hammer incident” weakened the Carver’s position, and the ogres once aligned with him apparently changed sides [ch 033 / 033.02].

## Relationships
*   **The Carver**: Borg served as the Carver’s primary enforcer and lieutenant, probably overseeing the Butterskull Ranch attack the Carver meant to generate a quest that would draw the party into a trap [ch 017 / 017.03]. His failure and subsequent death weakened the Carver’s position among allied monsters like the ogres [ch 033 / 033.02].
*   **Sister Kaella**: Borg and Kaella, the Carver’s spymaster, were two of his three lieutenants [ch 037 / 037.02]; Kaella said Borg’s one job was to kill Big Al Kalazorn and leave no witnesses [ch 019 / 019.09]. Kaella expressed frustration that his failure to do so collapsed her plans [ch 019 / 019.09].
*   **Locutus**: An orc who was to receive money from Kaella for killing Borg. Locutus nodded when Kaella asked whether he had killed Borg, and Kaella then struck him down [ch 019 / 019.09; ch 037 / 037.02].
*   **Rot-Tusk Ogres**: Borg commanded three rot-tusk ogres. While he led them during the Butterskull Ranch raid, he later split from them tactically [ch 018 / 018.11]. After the Borg incident weakened the Carver, the ogres apparently changed sides [ch 033 / 033.02].
*   **Bandit Number 4**: A fleeing brigand from the Conyberry skirmish who joined Borg at the ranch. Borg screamed at and executed this individual, furious that the party had not walked into his trap [ch 018 / 018.10].
*   **The Party**: Borg viewed the party as targets for a pre-planned ambush at Butterskull Ranch [ch 017 / 017.03]. The party agreed to follow him after he burned the ranch [ch 018 / 018.11].
