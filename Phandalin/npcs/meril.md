## Identity
Meril is a highly respected druid who serves as the mentor to the tortle Soma [ch 007 / entry; ch 016 / 016.09]. Meril gave Soma her staff; Xanthopoulos said, “He gave it because he picked her.” [ch 041 / 041.04]. Xanthopoulos, an old friend of Meril, emphasized that Meril was not a wandering figure but a personage of deep standing among the druidic community [ch 041 / 041.04]. The party has chosen to preserve the mystery surrounding Meril’s full history, treating additional background as “flavor” rather than essential campaign information [ch 042 / 042.04]. Meril’s current status is not established in the summaries, though Xanthopoulos referred to Meril in the past tense as a former owner of the staff [ch 037 / 037.02; ch 041 / 041.04].

## Personality and Motivations
The evidence regarding Meril’s personal personality is sparse; motivations are primarily inferred through the symbolism of the staff and the judgments of peers. Meril chose Soma not out of sentimentality, but because Soma was deemed the correct successor to wield the staff [ch 041 / 041.04]. In chapter 44 the staff gained a fourth bud as Soma accepted her role, though in chapter 56 a leaf peeled away when she used it to close a rift [ch 044 / 044.03; ch 056 / 056.03]. As he departed, Xanthopoulos told Soma, “Perhaps Meril made a good choice.” [ch 041 / 041.07].

## Relationships
*   **Soma:** Soma was Meril’s druidic student and the recipient of Meril’s staff. Soma recalls Meril’s teachings on nature and magic, such as identifying will-o’-wisps [ch 034 / 034.05].
