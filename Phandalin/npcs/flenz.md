## Identity
Flenz was a young half-orc Anchorite of Talos [ch 044 / entry]. He wore a rotting boar-head mask and bore elaborate storm-themed tattoos: storm clouds and coiling spirals up his neck, each featuring an open eye [ch 044 / entry]. These markings differed from those of his companion Narux, though both were inked by the same hand [ch 044 / entry]. He carried clubs, hide armor, and a holy symbol of Talos [ch 044 / entry].

## Personality and Motivations
The evidence does not describe Flenz’s personal demeanor, speech, or internal motivations beyond his role as a cultist [ch 044 / entry]. He was motivated by the objectives of the Talosian cult, specifically the completion of summoning rituals [ch 044 / entry; ch 044 / 044.04]. Three letters of cult correspondence were found in a satchel in his quarters, but the evidence does not say to whom they were addressed [ch 044 / 044.04].

## History with the Party
*   Flenz and Narux, two young half-orc Anchorites of Talos wearing rotting boar-head masks, led a summoning ritual at the Circle of Thunder using dancing and lightning magic. The party ultimately paralyzed them with Hold Person and slew them. [ch 044 / entry]
*   Flenz wore storm clouds and coiling spirals up his neck, with each tattoo featuring an open eye; these markings differed from Narux’s crossed boar-tusks, though both were inked by the same hand. [ch 044 / entry]
*   During the exploration of the caves surrounding the henge, Valphine discovered Flenz’s quarters (C2), where a satchel sealed with the lightning-bolt wax sigil of Talos was found. [ch 044 / entry; ch 044 / 044.04]
*   The satchel in Flenz’s quarters contained three letters of cult correspondence. [ch 044 / entry; ch 044 / 044.04]
*   The party reviewed "Flenz’s sealed letters" as part of the intelligence gathered from the henge, alongside Narux’s sketches and a vellum scrap, to identify multiple Talosian summoning rituals being attempted across the region. [ch 045 / 045.02]

## Last Observed State
Flenz was slain by the party after being paralyzed by a *Hold Person* spell during a ritual at the Circle of Thunder [ch 044 / entry]. At the time of his death, he was leading the ritual using dancing and lightning magic [ch 044 / entry]. Following his death, his quarters (cave C2) were explored by Valphine, who discovered a satchel sealed with a lightning-bolt wax sigil of Talos containing three letters of cult correspondence [ch 044 / entry; ch 044 / 044.04]. In Chapter 045, "Flenz’s sealed letters" were reviewed by the party as part of their intelligence gathering, though Flenz himself did not appear or act [ch 045 / 045.02].

## Relationships
*   **Narux:** Flenz’s companion in the summoning ritual at the Circle of Thunder. Both were half-orc Anchorites wearing rotting boar-head masks [ch 044 / entry]. While Flenz had neck tattoos of storm clouds and open eyes, Narux had crossed boar-tusk tattoos on his forearms; both tattoos were inked by the same artist [ch 044 / entry]. They were both slain in the same encounter [ch 044 / entry].
*   **Grannoc:** A rival anchorite at the Woodland Manse, identified in one of the letters found in Flenz’s quarters. The letter described Grannoc as "reckless" and noted that his ritual was faltering [ch 044 / 044.04].
*   **Unnamed Author ("K."?):** One letter found in Flenz’s satchel was signed by an unnamed author who warned that if the henge (where Flenz was stationed) failed, the manse would summon in its place, and if that failed, the author would summon the entity personally [ch 044 / 044.04]. A separate half-burned scrap found in Yargath’s firepit was signed "K." [ch 044 / 044.04]. The connection between the anonymous author of the letter in Flenz’s possession and "K." is not explicitly established in the evidence, though the tone suggests a high-ranking cult figure [ch 044 / 044.04; ch 045 / 045.02].
