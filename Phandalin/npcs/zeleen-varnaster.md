## Identity
Zeleen Varnaster is the female leader of the Whiskered Gang, a group of wererats currently occupying the Mountain’s Toe Gold Mine [ch 026 / entry; ch 026 / 026.11]. Prior to their displacement by orcs and ogres from the Shrine of Savras, the gang’s ancestral home was this ruin located near Conyberry [ch 026 / 026.11; ch 034 / 034.02]. Zeleen operates with a gangster-like demeanor, preferring the role of "muscle" or territorial enforcer over manual labor [ch 034 / entry; ch 034 / 034.02]. She manages a small community of roughly seven wererats, with other members sometimes absent for hunting or other tasks [ch 026 / 026.11].

## Personality and Motivations
Zeleen is pragmatic, self-important, and highly protective of her gang’s comfort and security [ch 026 / entry]. She volunteered two subordinates for a risk she considered herself too valuable to face, and the party determined that the gang was concealing that it liked the mine and did not intend to surrender it [ch 026 / 026.11]. Her motivations are primarily economic and territorial: she values the stability of the mine and the supply of Petunia’s cheese above all else [ch 026 / 026.11; ch 033 / entry; ch 034 / entry].

*   **Self-Preservation:** Zeleen refuses to engage in dangerous tasks she deems beneath her, declaring herself "too valuable" to risk [ch 026 / 026.11; ch 026 / moment]. She prioritizes the gang’s survival and comfort over heroism or contractual honesty [ch 026 / 026.11; ch 040 / entry].
*   **Deception and Evasion:** She has demonstrated a willingness to conceal intentions, such as hiding the gang’s desire to keep the mine despite offering to return it, or falsely reporting that dwarven miners were working when they were on strike [ch 026 / entry; ch 040 / entry]. She also gave a suspiciously evasive account of Don-Jon Raskin’s death, framing it as an "unfortunate incident" while the party interpreted her evasion as meaning the gang lured him into danger or deliberately failed to intervene [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.03; ch 033 / moment].
*   **Greed and Negotiation:** Zeleen attempts to extract extra payments, such as a ten-percent skim or upfront gold, but quickly abandons these demands when faced with overwhelming force or logical coercion [ch 034 / 034.02]. She rapidly reframes failed demands as matters of friendship or duty to avoid conflict [ch 034 / moment].
*   **Insularity:** She is defensive about wererat stereotypes, particularly regarding cheese, though she ultimately appreciates high-quality cheese enough to use it as a transactional currency [ch 026 / 026.11; ch 033 / 033.03]. She perceives the mine primarily as a fortified home rather than a workplace, leading her to dismiss warnings about threats that might breach their defenses [ch 034 / entry; ch 034 / 034.02].

## History with the Party
- Zeleen Varnaster is the female leader of the Whiskered Gang, a group of wererats displaced from the Shrine of Savras who currently occupy the Mountain’s Toe Gold Mine [ch 026 / entry; ch 026 / 026.11].
- During the party’s initial negotiation at the mine, Zeleen claimed the gang would return to the Shrine of Savras if the party cleared it, but the party determined through an insight check that she preferred the mine and did not intend to surrender it [ch 026 / entry; ch 026 / 026.11].
- Zeleen refused to participate in dangerous tasks, declaring herself too valuable to risk her own life, and instead offered two subordinates to the party’s cause [ch 026 / 026.11; ch 026 / moment].
- The party struck an initial accommodation where the Whiskered Gang would live in and work the mine in exchange for regular shipments of cheese made from Petunia’s milk [ch 026 / 026.11].
- In Chapter 033, Zeleen reemerged after the fighting was over and offered a suspicious account of Don-Jon Raskin’s death, claiming he died in an "unfortunate incident" while simultaneously describing him as "a very annoying person" [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.03].
- When Valphine interrogated Zeleen about killing Don-Jon, Zeleen evaded the accusation with the phrase "Kill is such a strong word," leading the party to believe the gang lured Don-Jon into fatal danger or refused to intervene [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.03; ch 033 / moment].
- Zeleen surrendered Don-Jon’s share of the mine to the party in exchange for the removal of the carrion crawler and accepted payment in the form of Petunia’s cheese [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.03].
- In Chapter 034, Zeleen agreed to a new arrangement where wererats would act as "muscle" and protect the mine’s territory while dwarven miners performed the manual labor [ch 034 / entry; ch 034 / 034.02].
- Zeleen admitted the wererats had planned to take ten percent off the top and instead demanded 1,000 gold pieces upfront, but backed down when confronted by Brewbarry, who wielded a weapon covered in orc blood [ch 034 / entry; ch 034 / 034.02; ch 034 / moment].
- Zeleen dismissed a warning from Vukradin that dragons could polymorph into smaller creatures, seeing through the deception and declaring Vukradin the "best boss" the wererats ever had [ch 034 / entry; ch 034 / 034.02; ch 034 / moment].
- Zeleen informed the party that the Shrine of Savras might hold information on the white dragon’s location, which is why the orcs and ogres seized it [ch 034 / entry; ch 034 / 034.02].
- In Chapter 040, Zeleen falsely assured the party that the dwarven miners were working hard, despite the mine being completely silent due to a strike over the missing Earthstone Gem [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.03].
- When confronted by Valphine’s magical Daylight and Brewbarry’s declaration that the gang was a "waste of cheese," Zeleen accepted that retrieving the Earthstone Gem was part of her contractual obligation and dispatched wererats to the orc-controlled forest [ch 040 / entry; ch 040 / 040.03; ch 040 / moment].
- In Chapter 049, the party bypassed the mine on their way to Neverwinter, leaving Zeleen’s Earthstone Gem retrieval and the payment of mine proceeds as outstanding obligations [ch 049 / 049.05].
- In Chapter 053, Zeleen was identified as a potential lead into the sewer networks of Neverwinter, based on the theory that the wererats may have originated there and know other were-rats [ch 053 / entry; ch 053 / 053.06].
- In Chapter 054, the tunnel dweller Rsolk said he knew Zeleen through the Mountain’s Toe Mine and had doubted her claim of a business relationship with Vukradin, which Vukradin then confirmed [ch 054 / 054.03].

## Last Observed State
The last confirmed presence of Zeleen Varnaster acting or speaking was in Chapter 040, where she was found in the Mountain’s Toe Gold Mine attempting to conceal a miner’s strike [ch 040 / entry]. After the party bypassed the mine in Chapter 049, her immediate status and location were not observed, though her contractual obligations regarding the Earthstone Gem retrieval remained outstanding [ch 049 / 049.05].

In Chapter 054, Zeleen was not present, but her connection to the party was confirmed by Rsolk, a tunnel dweller in Neverwinter. Rsolk stated that he knew Zeleen and had previously doubted her claim of having a business relationship with Vukradin, but Vukradin confirmed the relationship existed [ch 054 / 054.03]. No direct observation of her occurred after Chapter 040.

Status not established in the summaries.

## Relationships
*   **The Party (Vukradin, Brewbarry, Valphine, Horia, et al.):** Zeleen views the party as employers ("Mr. Boss") whose primary value lies in providing protection, political cover, and cheese [ch 040 / entry; ch 034 / 034.02]. She respects strength and leverage, rapidly conceding when Brewbarry brandishes a bloody weapon or Valphine uses magical intimidation [ch 034 / 034.02; ch 040 / 040.03]. She considers Vukradin a "kidder" [ch 034 / 034.02; ch 034 / moment].
*   **Don-Jon Raskin:** Former co-owner or manager of the mine. Zeleen describes him as a "very annoying person", though she formally acknowledges his value to the team [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.03]. The party suspects the gang was responsible for his death by luring him into danger [ch 033 / entry; ch 033 / 033.03].
*   **Whiskered Gang:** Zeleen is the leader and acts as their protector and representative. She shields them from danger, assigning subordinates to risky tasks while refusing to join herself [ch 026 / 026.11; ch 040 / 040.03]. The gang shares her preference for cheese and territorial security over labor [ch 034 / entry; ch 034 / 034.02].
*   **Dwarven Miners (Horia, Dwolin):** Zeleen agreed that the dwarves would perform the actual mining while the wererats acted as muscle [ch 034 / entry; ch 034 / 034.02]. In Chapter 040 she told the party that Dwolin said they needed the Earthstone Gem before they could extract any gold [ch 040 / 040.03].
*   **Rsolk (Neverwinter Tunnel Dwellers):** Rsolk, who manages the tunnels in one area of Neverwinter, said he knew Zeleen through the Mountain’s Toe Mine; he had doubted her claim of a business relationship with Vukradin, and Vukradin confirmed it in Chapter 054 [ch 054 / 054.03]. The DM noted that the Mountain’s Toe were-rats may have been from Neverwinter and that were-rats tend to know each other [ch 053 / entry; ch 053 / 053.06].
