# Chapter 22: To the Loggers’ Camp

Date: 2025-11-05

## Summary

The morning after a tense night at the Falcon’s Hunting Lodge, the party gathered their wits and supplies, catching up on Vukradin’s investigation and discussing the previous night’s events. The party caught up on Vukradin’s investigation and Falcon’s accusation that the Cult of Talos had searched for his special ring. Vukradin clarified that he had entered Falcon’s room but taken nothing: “He left it all back where it is, because, you know, he’s not a thief.”

Falcon had publicly built his reputation on a dragon-slayer story, but admitted finding only leftover coins and a ring after the hoard of Iymrith, the fearsome dragon known as the Doom of the Desert, had already been plundered. He had heard that Iymrith was defeated, but when he reached the site he found the cadavers of giants and no dragon corpse. The exact circumstances of Iymrith’s disappearance or death remained hotly contested among sages, with competing stories crediting Orsik, the seer, or other factions. Falcon used the remaining gold to establish his lodge and leveraged the claim that he had defeated a dragon and taken its hoard to attract tourists.

Among the relics Falcon retained was a brass ring inscribed with the word “lightning” in Elvish and Sylvan. Vukradin was determined to trace it back to its rightful owners, explaining that he hoped to take the description and inscription to Ser Kaelen for further investigation. He insisted that he had not stolen the ring because more evidence was needed to identify its provenance. When Brewbarry suggested eventually returning it to Iymrith, Vukradin objected: “She is not the rightful owner of the ring, are you kidding? She stole it too!”

The group debated Vukradin’s methods and moral reasoning. Brewbarry observed that Vukradin wanted to show Falcon the error of his ways but had used deception rather than confronting him openly. Vukradin defended himself by declaring, “I think I have much better morals than most of the people in this, campaign that we’ve encountered.” The others remained amused by the contradiction, dubbing the hoped-for confrontation with Falcon a “persuasion campaign.”

With little more to gain at the lodge, the party hitched up their supply cart and set off through Neverwinter Wood toward the loggers’ camp. Brewbarry led the way, as the tallest member of the group, while Valphine joined him near the front. Their attempt at discretion was limited by Brewbarry’s size and armor, and the group joked that the Goliath might as well be singing at the top of his lungs. The cart—and especially Brewbarry’s wine—remained under close protection.

The forest was dense and quiet as the group pressed forward, but the silence was broken when they spotted an orc scout poorly concealed ahead. Soma recognized that the scout was not a lone wanderer but a lookout for an ambush farther up the trail. Before the party could fully form a plan, Soma transformed into a bear and lumbered toward him as though she were simply part of the local wildlife. She roared, prompting the startled scout to scramble roughly ten feet up a tree and signal frantically to companions to the north.

Brewbarry tried to slip through the woods while the bear occupied the scout, leaving the cart behind with the others. His scale mail and imposing stature made stealth difficult. A second hidden orc spotted him and alerted the ambush party, then furiously berated the scout for being distracted by “a damn bear.” The scout’s relatives and superiors immediately began arguing over whose idea it had been to appoint him, exposing a web of family grievances before the fight had properly begun.

What followed was a chaotic and surprisingly comedic battle. Soma hauled herself up the tree with terrifying ease and bit the scout, who had clearly not considered a bear’s climbing ability when choosing his refuge. The wounded scout disengaged, retreated, and shot Soma with his longbow, while an orc brigand hurled a javelin at her in the tree. Another raider threw a javelin at Brewbarry and missed; Brewbarry theatrically declared, “Aha, I catch the javelin as it passes me, and I break it in two. And growl at him. Grow!”

Vukradin worked mental magic on Orc Raider 2, charming him and compelling him to attack the scout. The raider charged his own lookout while the orcs shouted accusations about treachery, incompetent relatives, and questionable parentage. Their insults drifted into familiar territory involving hamsters and elderberries, while Vukradin observed with satisfaction, “I’m creating… I’m creating a schism in the… in the raiding party that everything is going exactly to plan.” *[Editorial note: “raiding party” corrects the transcript’s “rating party,” including the later “orc-rating party.”]*

Brewbarry fought with his halberd but initially chose not to rage, reasoning that he had only suffered a scratch and was “actually quite happy that we have this opportunity for barbecue.” Soma then leapt from the tree onto the brigand, shoved him northward, and positioned three orcs within a pillar of radiant moonlight. The Moonbeam burned them, though its initial damage was far below Soma’s hopes.

Vukradin subsequently conjured an illusion for Orc Raider 1. With the real Raider 2 on one side and a single illusory duplicate on the other, the raider believed himself surrounded by two versions of the comrade with whom he was already feuding. He wasted his efforts fighting the false orc while the real Raider 2, no longer charmed, became increasingly confused. Vukradin aptly summarized the encounter: “Mind games, this is the combat of mind games.” The illusion eventually dealt fatal psychic damage to Raider 1.

Raider 2 blamed the turmoil on Valphine and attacked her while crying out against drow trickery. Brewbarry entered a rage to defend her, growing enormous and threatening a wide area with his halberd. His first furious swing missed entirely, prompting him to admit, “Well, I swooshed my big halberd. And, I don’t know what I was hacking at, but it wasn’t an orc.” *[Editorial note: “orc” corrects the transcript’s “AUC.”]* He followed it by screaming, “Don’t touch my wine!”

Soma abandoned Moonbeam and returned to melee, landing a critical bite and raking Raider 2 with her claws. The brigand decided that he wanted no further part in the family drama and fled. The scout also escaped into the forest after Valphine failed to pick out his trail, leaving the badly wounded Raider 2 to run. Brewbarry tried to capture him but failed to secure a grapple. Vukradin then cast Sleep, ending the pursuit without killing the final raider.

The party bound the sleeping orc with rope provided by Corbin. Once awake, the prisoner found himself surrounded by an Aasimar, a Drow bearing the symbols of Lathander, a towering barbarian, and a druid who had recently been a bear. Intimidated, he insisted, “Nobody, nobody send us! We’re an independent, we are an independent orc-raiding party!”

The captive explained that a white dragon had driven the orcs from their home in the Sword Mountains. They had descended into the valley, where the situation became “an orc-eat-orc world.” Their chief had gone mad, their shaman had been killed, and the displaced orcs fractured: some joined the Talosian cultists, others followed brigands, while his own party tried to remain independent because “we are orcs, and orcs work for orcs, they don’t work for anyone else.”

That independence ended immediately. When the prisoner cautiously asked, “Do I get paid?” Brewbarry answered, “You get paid with your life. That’s how you get paid.” Although the orc tried to give his actual name as Rob, the party overruled him and renamed him Shrimpy. Brewbarry ordered, “From now on, and until you pull the cart to the loggers’ camp, you’re shrimpy. Now shut up and pull the cart.” *[Editorial note: “cart” corrects the transcript’s “couch.”]* Shrimpy’s hands were tied to the cart handles, leaving him able to do little except pull the supplies—and the jealously guarded wine—toward the logging camp.

The party searched the single fallen raider and recovered fifteen gold pieces in mixed coinage. Vukradin recorded it as blood money. Valphine then used Prayer of Healing to restore the party and even considered healing their captive on the practical grounds that someone needed to pull the cart.

By the time the logging camp came into view along the south shore of the river, the mood had shifted. A dozen tents stood on the sandy beach. Near a dock was a cabin with logs stacked beneath its awning, while only stone chimneys and foundations remained of older cabins that had been torn down. There was no sound, movement, or sign of Tibor Wester. A grim silence hung over the entire camp.

Vukradin immediately worried that, without Tibor’s signature on the notice of delivery, the party would not receive its hundred-gold payment. Soma offered the consolation, “The real goal was the friends you made along the way.” Brewbarry promised that they would investigate, find Tibor, and obtain the signature, though he admitted it was “just more work than it ought to be.”

Before proceeding into the deserted camp, the group clarified that Shrimpy remained bound by the hands to the cart handles. A final discussion turned to whether Valphine’s unusual religious practices allowed sacrificing him. Her Lathander training explicitly ruled out sacrifice, though her emerging doctrine treated causing pain and subsequently healing it as a different matter. No sacrifice took place, and the session ended with the party standing at the edge of the silent camp, their supplies delivered to no one and the unsettling sense that something had gone badly wrong.

## Scenes

### Morning at Falcon’s Hunting Lodge

#### The party discusses the previous night’s investigation, Falcon’s false accusation against the Cult of Talos, and the origins of his treasure.

- The party discussed the previous night’s intrusion and Falcon’s accusation that the Cult of Talos had been hunting for his special ring.

- Vukradin had entered Falcon’s room but had not stolen anything. The party emphasized that “He left it all back where it is, because, you know, he’s not a thief.”

- The discussion connected the Cult of Talos to lightning, the lighthouse previously visited by the party, and the orcs at the woodland manse. Whether the Talosian shamans or the Carver was primarily responsible for organizing the local orcs remained uncertain.

- Falcon’s account held that Iymrith was a famous dragon known as the Doom of the Desert. After the love of Falcon’s life was killed, he went seeking revenge but arrived only after reports that Iymrith had already been defeated.

- At the site of the supposed victory, Falcon found dead giants but no dragon cadaver. The hoard had already been picked clean except for some stray gold and a few remaining objects, including the ring.

- The exact circumstances of Iymrith’s disappearance, death, or survival remained disputed among sages. Some stories credited Orsik’s heroism, others credited the seer, and still others blamed different factions.

- Falcon used the leftover gold to buy and establish his lodge. His claim that he had defeated a dragon and won a dragon hoard helped build his reputation and draw tourists to Falcon’s Hunting Lodge.

- Vukradin explained that his objective was not simple theft: “I wanna… show him the error of his ways, and I was looking for information on the, sort of, the rightful owners of the so-called treasure.”

- Vukradin had acquired a description of the ring and its inscription but found no document clearly identifying an owner. He intended to consult Ser Kaelen and investigate its provenance.

- Brewbarry challenged the morality of the covert entry, noting that Vukradin had used deception while claiming he wanted Falcon to recognize his wrongdoing. Vukradin maintained that Falcon was wrong and that his own methods were justified.

- When Brewbarry imagined returning the ring to Iymrith, Vukradin protested, “She is not the rightful owner of the ring, are you kidding? She stole it too!”

- The group jokingly characterized the hoped-for confrontation with Falcon as a “persuasion campaign.”

- With the investigation complete for the moment, the party prepared to leave the lodge and carry its supplies through Neverwinter Wood to the loggers’ camp.

### Trek Through Neverwinter Wood

#### The party sets off through the forest toward the loggers’ camp, discussing their next steps and moral philosophies.

- The party departed Falcon’s Hunting Lodge and headed through Neverwinter Wood toward the loggers’ camp with its supply cart.

- Brewbarry took the lead because he was the tallest member of the party. Valphine also moved near the front, bringing her sharper passive perception to bear.

- The group intended to travel with some discretion, although Brewbarry conceded they would be only “as much as a Goliath barbarian can be discreet.”

- The party continued discussing Falcon, Iymrith, and the ring. Vukradin wanted to investigate its rightful ownership through Ser Kaelen rather than simply take it or return it to the last known possessor.

- Brewbarry questioned whether Vukradin’s covert actions fit his stated moral principles. Vukradin replied, “I think I have much better morals than most of the people in this, campaign that we’ve encountered.”

- Valphine suggested that forced entry was normal by the standards of the setting, remarking on the prevailing principle that might made right.

- When asked who led the “merry band of pirates,” Brewbarry volunteered on the basis of height: “Boy, it’s gotta be… it’s gotta be me, the tallest one, walking out.”

- The party retained its cart throughout the journey. Brewbarry was especially protective of the wine stored among its supplies.

### Ambush in Neverwinter Wood

#### While traveling through the forest with their cart, the party discovers an orc scout attempting to set an ambush and combat breaks out.

- The party noticed an orc scout attempting to conceal himself ahead in the forest. He appeared to be looking around, though it was initially unclear whether he was searching for the party or merely for food.

- Soma recognized the scout’s actual role. While the others remained uncertain, she realized he was a lookout for a group preparing an ambush farther up the trail.

- The scout had not yet noticed the party and was described as “a very, very poor lookout.”

- Soma transformed into a bear and approached as though she were an ordinary part of the forest. She roared when she and the scout came into view of one another.

- The startled scout scrambled approximately ten feet up a tree and made hand signals toward allies to the north. He had not accounted for the bear’s thirty-foot climbing speed.

- Soma attempted to determine the direction or number of the hidden orcs by scent or investigation but detected nothing beyond the scout’s signaling.

- While Soma made a token attack on the tree, Brewbarry attempted to sneak through the woods. The party left the cart stationary so that he would not drag it—and the wine—into danger.

- Brewbarry’s scale mail imposed a disadvantage on his attempt at stealth. A second hidden orc saw him before Brewbarry saw the orc.

- The hidden orc alerted his companions and began screaming abuse at the scout, calling him useless and condemning him for being distracted by “a damn bear.”

- Valphine, who spoke Orc, understood the raiders’ argument. Their complaints revealed that the scout was somebody’s brother’s nephew and that his appointment had been disputed from the beginning.

- Soma climbed the tree and attacked the scout. Her first swipe missed, but her bite struck for eight damage.

- The scout disengaged, retreated from the tree, and fired his longbow into Soma for nine damage. An orc brigand also threw a javelin at the bear, dealing another six damage.

- Valphine fired a Walloping Crossbow Bolt at the partially concealed enemies, but the cover and forest conditions caused the shot to miss.

- Another raider threw a javelin at Brewbarry and missed. Brewbarry declared, “Aha, I catch the javelin as it passes me, and I break it in two. And growl at him. Grow!”

- The orcs continued their argument in the middle of combat. One raider berated another for appointing his relative as scout, while the other snapped back, “not the time to have this conversation.”

- Orc Raider 1 charged Brewbarry, struck him with a scimitar, and disengaged. Brewbarry initially held back his rage, deciding that the threat did not yet justify expending it.

- Vukradin magically charmed Orc Raider 2 and compelled him to make a melee attack against the scout.

- Raider 2 charged the scout while delivering an insult assembled from the party’s suggestions: “Your mother was a hamster, and your father’s… smell of elderberries.” The attack dealt twelve damage.

- The attack deepened the orcs’ family quarrel. Raider 1 became furious that Raider 2 had struck his brother’s nephew, while Vukradin announced, “I’m creating… I’m creating a schism in the… in the raiding party that everything is going exactly to plan.”

- Brewbarry struck at an orc with his halberd. He chose not to rage yet, explaining, “Because right now, I’m not really enraged, I just have a little scratch, and, you know, I’m actually quite happy that we have this opportunity for barbecue, so…”

- Soma jumped from the tree onto the brigand and successfully landed without mishap. Rather than knock him prone, she shoved him one five-foot square north to cluster him with two other orcs.

- Soma cast Moonbeam over the clustered Scout, Brigand, and Raider 2. Its first burst dealt only four radiant damage to the scout and brigand and two to the raider after his successful save.

- The scout disengaged from the Moonbeam, fired at Raider 2, missed, and moved farther away.

- Valphine moved near the Moonbeam without entering it and attacked the brigand. She dealt four damage to the brigand. The party joked about whether moonlight counted as sunlight, with Valphine dismissing it as “inferior light.”

- Faced with a Drow bearing Lathander’s symbols, a bear casting Moonbeam, a traitorous raider, and the larger party, the brigand cried that the ambush had been his “dumbest idea” and disengaged.

- Raider 1 continued threatening Brewbarry while vowing to deal with Raider 2 afterward. Brewbarry challenged him: “Bring it on, you orc shrimp.”

- When the raider demanded to know who Brewbarry was calling a shrimp, Brewbarry answered, “You. You smell like one.” Asked what a shrimp smelled like, he added, “I don’t know what a shrimp smells like, but I’m sure you do.”

- Raider 1 attacked Brewbarry, missing once and hitting once for six damage before disengaging.

- Raider 2 broke free of Vukradin’s charm. Vukradin then targeted Raider 1 with an illusion, creating one duplicate of Raider 2 on Raider 1’s opposite side from the real orc.

- Raider 1 believed himself surrounded by two copies of his angry companion. The illusion attacked him for nine psychic damage, while Raider 2 became confused because Raider 1 was fighting and shouting at an enemy he could not see.

- Raider 2 blamed the situation on Valphine, crying, “What drow trickery is this?” He struck her twice for a total of twelve damage.

- Brewbarry moved to defend Valphine and finally entered a rage, greatly expanding his effective reach with the halberd.

- Brewbarry’s first raging attack missed. He summarized the effort: “Well, I swooshed my big halberd. And, I don’t know what I was hacking at, but it wasn’t an orc.”

- Still raging, Brewbarry screamed, “Don’t touch my wine!”

- Soma ceased concentrating on Moonbeam, closed on Raider 1 in bear form, and bit him for ten damage, though her follow-up claw attack missed.

- The scout reconsidered the confused state of his relatives, returned to help, repositioned for a longbow shot, and missed.

- Valphine’s crossbow continued to fail her, leading the DM to observe, “The arrows… the arrow… the arrows do not like you today.”

- The brigand abandoned the battle entirely, refusing to deal with the orcs’ “family trauma.”

- Raider 1 wasted his action fighting the illusory Raider 2. At the end of Vukradin’s turn, the illusion dealt thirteen more psychic damage and killed him.

- Raider 2 attempted to strike Valphine twice, missed both attacks, disengaged, and fled.

- Brewbarry pursued through the branches in his enlarged raging state but missed another halberd strike, managing only to collect a toothpick-sized branch.

- Soma pursued Raider 2 and landed a critical bite for fourteen damage, followed by a claw attack that reduced him to eight hit points.

- The scout fled completely into the forest. Valphine attempted to pursue and locate him, but her disadvantaged search failed badly and he escaped.

- The brigand likewise left the battlefield, declaring through his actions that the family drama had become too much.

### The Capture of Shrimpy

#### The party concludes their skirmish with the orc raiders, successfully capturing one survivor to serve as labor.

- With Raider 1 dead and both the scout and brigand gone, the party debated whether to kill, capture, or release Raider 2.

- Vukradin asked the others to decide, partly because he did not want Brewbarry to accuse him of hypocrisy. Brewbarry favored capture if the opportunity arose.

- Raider 2 disengaged and continued fleeing. Brewbarry pursued and attempted to grapple him using his enlarged reach, but failed to secure a hold.

- Soma declined to chase the fleeing enemies further, noting her general objection to running down defeated foes.

- Vukradin cast Sleep at the fleeing Raider 2. The orc failed to resist and collapsed, ending the skirmish.

- Vukradin announced, “Alright, there we go. We wrapped the combat, guys. We can tie him up now.”

- Corbin arrived shortly afterward with rope and asked, “So, more prisoners?”

- The party bound the sleeping orc and later secured his hands to the handles of the supply cart. He might theoretically have run away with the cart, but Brewbarry intended to defend the wine and doubted he would get far.

- Vukradin mocked the captive for having been charmed into attacking his own scout. The orc responded by snarling.

- Valphine asked who had sent the raiders. Vukradin’s intimidation was more effective than her questioning, especially given the captive’s confusion at seeing an Aasimar beside a Drow openly carrying Lathander’s religious symbols.

- The prisoner insisted, “Nobody, nobody send us! We’re an independent, we are an independent orc-raiding party!”

- He explained that the orcs had occupied the Sword Mountains after the war, but a white dragon arrived and drove them into the valley. Since then, it had become a violent free-for-all.

- The prisoner said the orc chief had gone “completely crazy.” After the tribe’s shaman was killed, some orcs joined the Talosian cultists, others joined brigands, and his party attempted to remain independent.

- He summarized his faction’s outlook: “We decided to be in… we… we are orcs, and orcs work for orcs, they don’t work for anyone else.”

- When the captive referred to the situation as “an orc-eat-orc world,” the party briefly misunderstood him as confessing to cannibalism. He indignantly clarified that it was only an expression.

- Valphine told him, “Well, you work for us now,” while Brewbarry added, “Yeah, we’ve got a cart to pull orc.”

- The captive cautiously asked, “Do I get paid?” Brewbarry replied, “You get paid with your life. That’s how you get paid.”

- The party discussed naming him because they could not continue calling him Orc Raider 2. Although the orc attempted to state that his name was Rob, the party settled on Shrimpy.

- Brewbarry informed him, “From now on, and until you pull the cart to the loggers’ camp, you’re shrimpy. Now shut up and pull the cart.”

- The group discussed whether to turn Shrimpy over to authorities, jail him in Phandalin, release him, or keep him as labor. For the immediate journey, he remained bound to the cart and forced to pull it.

- Searching the dead Raider 1 yielded fifteen gold pieces in a mixture of coinage. Vukradin added the money to the party’s “blood money.”

- The party lacked healing potions, but Valphine used Prayer of Healing rather than relying only on a short rest. She was willing to heal Shrimpy as well because “somebody’s gotta pull the cart.”

- Vukradin observed that one hit point would technically be enough for Shrimpy to pull the cart, prompting agreement that this was how health worked in the D&D universe.

### Arrival at the Deserted Loggers’ Camp

#### The party arrives at the logging camp to deliver supplies, only to find the site eerily silent and abandoned.

- The logging camp spread along the south shore of the river. A dozen tents were arranged on a sandy beach.

- Near a dock stood a cabin with logs stacked beneath an awning. Older cabins had been torn down, leaving only their stone chimneys and foundations.

- A grim silence hung over the camp. The party saw no workers, no movement, and no sign of Tibor Wester.

- Vukradin immediately protested, “I brought all these supplies all this way, and I’m not gonna get my hundred gold.”

- Although the supplies had technically reached their destination, Vukradin believed payment required a notice of delivery signed by Tibor Wester.

- Soma joked, “The real goal was the friends you made along the way.”

- Brewbarry promised, “We’ll investigate, find out what happened to them, find the guy, and get your notice signed.”

- Brewbarry acknowledged that locating the missing recipient was “just more work than it ought to be.”

- The party confirmed that Shrimpy remained bound by his hands to the cart handles. This allowed him to pull but made escape difficult.

- The group considered buying manacles for handling prisoners in the future.

- In the closing discussion, the DM asked whether Valphine would explain traditional Drow methods for dealing with orc prisoners. Valphine suggested that the usual technique was to kill or sacrifice them.

- The DM reminded Valphine that Lathander did not approve of sacrifice and that her priestly training placed it off the table.

- Valphine’s developing religious practice distinguished sacrifice from causing pain and then healing that pain, which was described as “not entirely bad.” No sacrifice took place.

- The session ended with the party at the edge of the deserted camp, facing an unsigned delivery, a missing Tibor Wester, and an unexplained silence.

## Locations

### Falcon’s Hunting Lodge

A lodge owned by Falcon the Hunter, who admits that leftover coins scavenged from Iymrith’s already-plundered hoard helped him establish it. Its tourist appeal rests on his public dragon-slayer story, not on his possession of an intact hoard. Falcon accused the Cult of Talos of searching his room for a special ring, unaware or unwilling to acknowledge that Vukradin had conducted the investigation and taken nothing.

### Neverwinter Wood

A dense and ancient forest through which the party travels to reach the loggers’ camp. Its thick trees and foliage provided cover for a disorganized band of orc raiders preparing an ambush. The trees also offered the scout an ill-considered refuge from Soma’s bear form and complicated Valphine’s attempts to land crossbow shots or track fleeing enemies.

### Sword Mountains

The former home of the captured orc’s people. The orcs had occupied the mountains after the war until a white dragon drove them into the valley. Their displacement fractured the surviving groups among Talosian cultists, brigands, and independent raiding parties.

### Loggers’ Camp

A settlement on the south shore of a river, consisting of a sandy beach lined with approximately twelve tents, a dock, and a cabin with logs stacked beneath its awning. Older cabins have been torn down, leaving only stone chimneys and foundations. The party found the camp under an unsettling, grim silence, with no workers and no sign of Tibor Wester.

## NPCs

### Falcon the Hunter

The owner of the hunting lodge who publicly claims to have defeated a dragon and acquired its hoard. He admitted that he arrived after Iymrith had reportedly been defeated and found only dead giants, stray coins, and a ring after the hoard had already been plundered. No dragon corpse was present. The remaining coins helped him establish the lodge, while the embellished dragon-slayer story attracted tourists.

### Iymrith

A famous dragon known as the Doom of the Desert, whose hoard Falcon the Hunter claims to have scavenged. Falcon had heard reports of her defeat but found no dragon body when he reached the site. Her fate remains unconfirmed to the party, and the circumstances of her reported disappearance or death are hotly contested among sages. Competing accounts credit Orsik, a seer, or other factions. Vukradin also believes that even if Iymrith possessed the ring, she was not necessarily its rightful owner because she may have stolen it herself.

### Orc Scout

A clumsy and incompetent lookout who failed to notice the approaching party until Soma confronted him in bear form. He climbed approximately ten feet up a tree to escape, neglecting to consider that bears can climb. He signaled his allies to the north, was savagely berated for his incompetence and family connections, bitten by Soma, and attacked by his own charmed comrade. After briefly returning to help his confused relatives and missing a longbow shot, he escaped into the forest.

### Orc Raider 1

A raider who engaged Brewbarry in melee combat and expressed great frustration with the scout’s incompetence. He was related to the scout through a convoluted family connection and became furious when Raider 2 attacked him. Vukradin’s illusion made him perceive one duplicate of Raider 2 opposite the real orc, causing him to believe he was surrounded. He wasted his actions fighting the illusion and died from its psychic damage.

### Orc Raider 2

An orc warrior magically charmed by Vukradin into attacking his own scout while shouting insults involving hamsters and elderberries. After breaking free of the charm, he attacked Valphine and blamed “drow trickery” for the chaos. Soma badly wounded him with a critical bite and claw attack. He attempted to flee, avoided Brewbarry’s grapple, and was finally put to sleep by Vukradin. The party captured and renamed him Shrimpy.

### Orc Brigand

A raider who attacked Soma’s bear form with a javelin before being jumped upon and shoved into Soma’s Moonbeam. Confronted by a Drow bearing Lathander’s symbols, a spellcasting bear, and a family feud among his allies, he declared the ambush his “dumbest idea” and fled. He escaped the battle unharmed enough to disappear into Neverwinter Wood.

### Shrimpy

Orc Raider 2, captured by the party after Vukradin put him to sleep. He was the raider previously charmed into attacking the scout, not the illusion victim who died. He tried to identify himself as Rob, but the party ignored his objection and renamed him Shrimpy.

Shrimpy revealed that a white dragon displaced his people from the Sword Mountains. Their chief had gone mad, their shaman had been killed, and the surviving orcs fractured between Talosian cultists, brigands, and independent raiding parties. He insisted that his group had no patron and that “orcs work for orcs.” After asking whether he would be paid, he was told that continued life constituted his wages. His hands are tied to the supply cart’s handles, and he is forced to pull it.

### Corbin

The party’s retainer and campmaster, who arrived after the skirmish carrying rope and asked, “So, more prisoners?” He helped provide the means to secure Shrimpy. He is not Corwin of Falcon’s lodge or the manager of the logging camp.

### Tibor Wester

The individual the party was sent to meet at the loggers’ camp to deliver supplies and collect a hundred-gold reward. His signature is required on the party’s notice of delivery. He is notably missing upon their arrival, along with every other apparent inhabitant of the camp.

## Items

### Magic Ring

A brass ring Falcon says he found among the remnants of Iymrith’s hoard, inscribed with “lightning” in Elvish and Sylvan. Its powers and original owner remain unresolved. Vukradin has its description and inscription for his provenance investigation and plans to consult Ser Kaelen. The party did not take the ring.

### Supply Cart

A cart filled with supplies, including wine, that the party is transporting to the loggers’ camp. Brewbarry is fiercely protective of its contents and repeatedly invokes the wine as a reason to fight. After the skirmish, Shrimpy’s hands are tied to the cart’s handles and he is forced to pull it. The cart reached the loggers’ camp, but there was no one present to accept its contents.

### Walloping Crossbow Bolts

Specialized ammunition used by Valphine during the ambush. Partial cover and the thick forest made the bolts difficult to land accurately, and Valphine’s crossbow attacks repeatedly missed during the encounter.

### Halberd

A long-reaching polearm wielded by Brewbarry during the forest skirmish. Its reach allowed him to threaten enemies before they entered ordinary melee range and potentially strike those attempting to flee without disengaging. Brewbarry’s actual attacks were less reliable, with several dramatic swings cutting only air and branches.

### Notice of Delivery

A formal document required to be signed by Tibor Wester to authorize payment of one hundred gold pieces to the party upon successful delivery of the supplies. Tibor’s absence leaves the party unable to prove completion or collect the reward, motivating them to search the silent camp.

## Spells

### Wild Shape

Soma transformed into a bear during the ambush. She used the form to approach the scout as an apparently natural forest animal, roar at him, climb the tree after him, and bite him. She later leapt onto the brigand, shoved him into position for Moonbeam, and delivered a critical bite and claw attack against Raider 2.

### Mental enchantment (spell name unspecified)

Vukradin charmed Orc Raider 2, forcing him to use his action to make a melee attack against a creature Vukradin mentally chose. Vukradin directed him against the scout, aggravating the raiders’ existing family disputes. Raider 2 later succeeded on a Wisdom save and broke free. The exact spell name was not established.

### Moonbeam

A cylinder of radiant moonlight conjured by Soma after she leapt from the tree and shoved the brigand into position. The beam caught the scout, brigand, and Raider 2. Its initial damage roll was unusually low, inflicting four radiant damage on the scout and brigand and two on Raider 2 after his save. Soma later ceased concentration to return to melee in bear form.

### Phantasmal Force

Employed by Vukradin against Orc Raider 1. The spell created one illusory duplicate of Raider 2 on the opposite side of Raider 1 from the real Raider 2, causing him to believe himself surrounded by two copies of his feuding companion. Raider 1 wasted an action fighting the false orc and ultimately died from the illusion’s psychic damage.

### Sleep

Cast by Vukradin against the fleeing Raider 2 after Brewbarry failed to grapple him. The orc succumbed to the spell and collapsed, allowing the party to capture and bind him without inflicting further damage.

### Prayer of Healing

A restorative prayer used by Valphine to tend to the party’s wounds after the battle in Neverwinter Wood. She also considered healing Shrimpy so that he would remain capable of pulling the supply cart.
