# Chapter 49: Universal Basic Treasure

Date: Jun 23rd, 2026

## Summary
The party made their way back to Phandalin with full hearts and ambitious plans. Brewbarry had become consumed by a new vision — a bathrobe business, born from a deep personal desire to atone for generations of violence by bringing softness and comfort to the people of the world. Vukradin, meanwhile, lamented that despite slaying dragons and clearing cursed mansions, his dream of funding a musical studio remained frustratingly out of reach. The group discussed pushing for direct mayoral elections, the legal redistribution of recovered treasure, and the still-unresolved matter of a holy orc army marching through the mountains in Lathander’s name. Whatever came next, the consequences of their choices were only just beginning to unfold.

The session opened with real-world chatter about Dave’s company being acquired by Fox. Wade quipped that his “soul is for rent” regarding the change of ownership, and Dave noted two people had already quit. The party also joked about Stéphane showing up late (“the bottom two”), and Kostadis feigned giving Dave a hard time before clarifying the comment was “really meant for Stefan.”

Upon arriving in Phandalin, the party was greeted warmly but without the fanfare of their earlier triumphs — yesterday’s heroes, as it were. At the Stonehill Inn, Toblen welcomed them back with open arms, and Vukradin earned the whole house a round of the good beer with a rousing performance. Ser Kaelen, however, had more pressing matters on his mind. He informed the party that additional rightful owners of their recovered treasure had been located — in Neverwinter — and reminded them, with barely concealed frustration, that the mermaid statue belonging to Elara Meliamne had still not been returned. He also noticed Valphine’s golden eyes and, with a look of quiet concern, suggested that when the party reached Neverwinter, Valphine should pay a visit to Brother Aldric Sunmantle at the Spire of the Morning Lord — the priest who first received her conversion — to seek guidance on what Lathander might be trying to tell her through the golden eyes. Ser Kaelen was visibly unsettled by the blessing on a Drow, prompting the party to joke that “there might be a new Mourning Lord in town.”

The party then turned their attention to the town itself. After a failed attempt by Vukradin to pressure Harbin Wester into calling a public meeting (with Valphine’s intimidating presence landing flatly), the townmaster eventually agreed to gather the citizens himself. Vukradin performed on the town green, drawing out residents from every corner of Phandalin — Qelline Alderleaf, Linene Graywind, Big Al Kalazorn, Ser Kaelen, and even Harbin Wester himself. When the crowd was warm and swaying, Vukradin stepped forward and delivered a passionate speech, declaring that treasure hoarded by monsters rightfully belonged to the people, not to adventurers or distant governments. He announced the creation of Universal Basic Treasure — UBT — and the crowd erupted in cheers.

Harbin Wester, sensing the political winds, immediately tried to take credit for the idea, stuttering through an endorsement and claiming the Privy Council had been considering something similar for weeks. He even fumbled the party’s group name, which they still hadn’t settled on — “Slayers of Cryovain” was Dave’s suggestion. Linene Graywind pulled out a Candlekeep text on income distribution and volunteered to lead a subcommittee to draft the formal rules. Jenna Roscoe, appalled by what she called mob rule, stormed out of Phandalin and headed back to Neverwinter ahead of the party — giving her a head start to brief the Lord’s Alliance and other contacts before the party arrived in the city. The party donated the fused dragon gold slag from Cryovain’s hoard to the new UBT fund, though Soma quietly noted a troubling planar anomaly surrounding it — the kind of magic that would require an expert far beyond Phandalin’s reach to unravel. Brewbarry and Soma were declared honorary citizens of Phandalin so they could qualify for the fund, and the crowd cheered as though it were the most natural thing in the world. During the debate over who qualified, the group hammered out residency rules — living within Phandalin’s bounds at least 50 percent of the year, paying taxes, and (per Vukradin’s suggestion) attesting under Zone of Truth.

With the political business settled, the party enjoyed a lengthy wine and cheese tasting hosted by Qelline Alderleaf, during which Brewbarry surprised everyone by selecting the finest creamy blue cheese with genuine discernment — Qelline declared him “a man of taste and vision,” and Big Al admitted, “Neither did I” when she asked if he knew Brewbarry had such class. Afterward, Brewbarry sought out Linene at the trading house and laid out his vision: a bathrobe factory, blessed by Lathander’s priests, bearing Vukradin’s logo, and distributed through the Lion Shield Coster. He told her, “My kind has been spreading violence and pain throughout the land for generations. I think I need to turn that around and bring softness to people.” Linene agreed to serve as his wealth management consultant, wrote him a letter of introduction to the Counting House in Neverwinter, and mentioned a troubling supply chain disruption with the Lion Shield Coster that she hoped the party might investigate. Before departing, Ser Kaelen delivered one final piece of news — a note identifying the Alagondar family as the rightful owners of the necklace of fireballs, their ancestor’s belongings stolen by grave robbers a generation ago, with a man named Perrin Alagondar waiting to be found.

The journey to Neverwinter was uneventful, save for Brewbarry’s relentless monologue about thread counts, embroidery patterns, and the ideal softness of a luxury bathrobe. Upon arriving at the city gates, the party was checked against a writ sent ahead from Phandalin and welcomed through. Almost immediately, a trio of young humans in roughhide cloaks with charcoal tribal markings on their forearms stopped dead in their tracks — they recognized Brewbarry. The fans were starstruck, but when they realized Vukradin was standing right beside him, the debate turned awkward: one insisted Vukradin had sold out, that his early recordings were the real art, the authentic stuff before fame corrupted him. Vukradin, rather than argue, simply pulled out an old song from deep in his memory and played it right there at the city gates, leaving the young musician in stunned, reverent silence as a small crowd gathered around them. One fan also noted that the Common Chord had become impossible to book — weeks of reservations and prices through the roof — and another observed it was “all part of the displacement of the common man from the docks as the protector’s enclave expands.”

As the party moved deeper into the Protector’s Enclave, they passed a carved stone plaque honoring the “liberators of the Ordning” and a Lord’s Alliance herald distributing broadsheets featuring a dwarf named Orsik, Defender of Parnast and Champion of the North. The back of the broadsheet displayed a map of the Lord’s Alliance’s territorial jurisdiction — with a dotted line extending into Neverwinter Wood, Leilon, and Phandalin itself. The party exchanged glances but pressed on, eventually finding a high-end armorer who worked with exotic materials. The proprietor recognized Valphine immediately as the Drow follower of the Morning Lord that Brother Aldric had spoken of, and he was overcome with reverence. The party initially discussed having its dragon scales made into armor for Brewbarry, emblazoned with Lathander’s emblem, while the armorer offered a dramatic discount in exchange for Valphine’s blessing. **Later GM ruling (2026-09-23): the faithful armorer gave the finished white dragon scale armor to Valphine. It belongs to her; Brewbarry instead received her elven chain, and there is no outstanding armor collection.** He launched into a series of racial stereotypes about Drow while enthusiastically declaring that nothing bad could ever come of a Drow embracing the warmth of the sun — to which Dave dryly noted, “Well, nothing bad could ever come of this.” With that, Neverwinter opened before them, full of unfinished business and unanswered questions.

### The Universal Basic Treasure Proclamation
#### Vukradin gathers the citizens of Phandalin to propose a radical new way of distributing adventuring wealth.
- Vukradin attempts to intimidate Harbin Wester into calling a town meeting, but the intimidation lands flatly (rolled a 10). The mayor then agrees to gather the people himself after Vukradin says, “I’ll gather the people myself.”
- The party holds a performance on the town green to draw a crowd of local residents and council members. Vukradin suggests “we could put on a rock concert and get them all out there.”

- Jenna notes, “this has been very well covered in the dialectics of the financials.”
- Vukradin proposes ‘Universal Basic Treasure’ (UBT): unclaimed adventuring treasure should belong to the citizens of Phandalin. He rolled 17 (with advantage), and the crowd cheers.
- Harbin Wester unexpectedly supports the plan, claiming the Privy Council was “waiting for a few more days before we would make a formal announcement,” and stutters over Vukradin’s name.
- When Harbin asks the name of their adventuring group, the party realizes they never settled on one — Dave suggests “Slayers of Cryovain.”
- The party donates the fused slag of dragon gold to the new UBT fund, though Harbin notes it’s “fused inside of a magical containment thing” that needs expert extraction.
- Ser Kaelen calls a sidebar, asking whether the Order of the Gauntlet gets a share and what defines a citizen of Phandalin. The party works out rules: property owners or residents within the bounds, 50-plus percent of the year, paying taxes, and (per Vukradin) attesting under Zone of Truth.
- Linene offers to lead a subcommittee of the Privy Council to draft the rules, but Vukradin insists the people need money faster, and the crowd cheers.
- Harbin awkwardly names Brewbarry and Soma “honorary citizens of Phandalin” through their “unique contributions,” with Big Al and the crowd cheering.
- Jenna Roscoe, appalled, leaves town in a huff; the party calls after her, “Don’t let the door hit you on the way out.”

- The party works out they effectively laundered the blood money through the UBT, keeping 5% as “organic fair trade” treasure.

### Lord Neverember
The ruler of Neverwinter, mentioned as taxing the populace heavily to fund the reconstruction of Castle Never. Vukradin jokes he’ll be “a big fan” of UBT.

### Orsik
A dwarf featured on Lord’s Alliance broadsheets as Defender of Parnast and Champion of the North, with a woodcut portrait described as “blunt-nosed, serious.”

### Perrin Alagondar
Presents himself as heir to the Alagondar royal line and claims the Necklace of Fireballs as a recovered dynastic treasure. **GM note (not yet known to party):** Perrin is a Nasher / Son of Alagondar loyalist — his claim to the necklace is sincere as a restoration symbol, but the provenance documentation was manufactured by a Margaster-connected source. The real beneficiary of the handoff is House Margaster, who wants the necklace as an arson weapon, not Perrin, who wants it as a banner. Ser Kaelen located him in good faith through a compromised source and does not know the match is forged. Ch. 47+ encounter; see `notes/session_prep/20260623_necklace-of-fireballs-neverwinter.md`.

### Ostren Vayle
A high-end armorer and faithful follower of the Morning Lord who operates a shop in the Protector’s Enclave of Neverwinter. He is deeply impressed by Valphine’s status as a chosen of Lathander (“You have the sign of the Morning Lord!”). The party initially discusses dragon scale armor for Brewbarry, but the finished Lathander-emblazoned armor is ultimately his gift to Valphine and belongs to her. He then launches into a series of racial stereotypes about Drow, celebrating the justice of Lathander’s warmth.

### Billy the Kid
One of the young fans at the city gates, dressed in roughhide cloaks with charcoal tribal markings. He recognizes the music playing from a window as Vukradin’s old work.

## Items
### Mermaid Statue
An artifact recovered by the party long ago that the party believes belongs to Elara ‘Seasong’ Meliamne of Neverwinter. Ser Kaelen reminds the party that its return has been long delayed, and Vukradin apologizes for forgetting it. **Urgency:** House Margaster agents have previously been intercepting this statue. The party plans to deliver it during their visit to Neverwinter — delay increases the risk of interception. **GM note (not yet known to party):** Elara Meliamne was killed by House Margaster eight days before the party’s arrival in Neverwinter. Her cousin, **Lord Cassian Meliamne**, now carries her anti-Margaster case and her commercial and genealogical reach in the city. Cassian is the relevant contact for the statue — and is actively building a case against Margaster that a second exposed forgery (the necklace provenance) would strengthen. See `notes/session_prep/20260623_necklace-of-fireballs-neverwinter.md`.

### Dragon Treasure Slag
A massive, fused hunk of gold from Cryovain’s hoard, encased in a magical containment field. The party donates it to the UBT fund, but it cannot be distributed until the magical fusion is resolved. Soma identifies a planar anomaly around it — “This is no ordinary... You remember that stag? Those weird gnome things?” — suggesting an expert in Neverwinter will be needed.

### Creamy Blue Cheese
A fine round of cheese selected by Brewbarry during a tasting session with Qelline Alderleaf. Considered the finest of the cheeses on offer, it impresses Qelline with Brewbarry’s discerning taste — “That you are a discerning customer, Mr. Brewbarry.” A round is set aside for Aletra and her assistants.

### Linene’s Note
A written recommendation from Linene to the Counting House in Neverwinter to assist Brewbarry in securing business capital for his bathrobe venture.

### Dragon Scales
Raw materials harvested from a dragon and brought to a high-end armorer in Neverwinter’s Protector’s Enclave. Although armor for Brewbarry was initially discussed, the faithful armorer ultimately gave the finished suit to Valphine.

### Dragon Scale Mail
Valphine’s custom white dragon scale armor, crafted and given to her by the faithful Morning Lord armorer in Neverwinter. It features a prominent emblem of Lathander on the back. The earlier plan for Brewbarry to receive it was superseded; Brewbarry instead received Valphine’s elven chain.

### Lord’s Alliance Broadsheet
A publication distributed by a herald in the Protector’s Enclave, featuring a wood-cut portrait of Orsik, Defender of Parnast and Champion of the North, and a map showing the Lord’s Alliance’s territorial jurisdiction, including a dotted line extending into Neverwinter Wood, Leilon, and Phandalin.

### Boots of Elvenkind
Held by Vukradin (acquired ch43 at the Woodland Manse). Not featured in this session’s events but remain in his possession.

### Staff of Bird Calls
A magical staff the party considers bartering to help cover the costs of the dragon scale armor commission, though Vukradin notes it’s “blood money” under the new UBT law.

### Necklace of Fireballs
Grave-goods recovered from the Alagondar burial mound at Dragonbarrow (ch33) — the same site where Lady Alagondar’s ghost rose and stared toward the Ice Spires, and where Boney was found. The Alagondars are Neverwinter’s deposed royal dynasty; Neverember rules in the absence of their heir. Ser Kaelen’s recovery office matched the necklace to a claimant (Perrin Alagondar) and the party intends to return it. **GM note (not yet known to party):** the provenance match was routed through a Margaster-connected source; the claimant is a Nasher loyalist and the “rightful owner” designation is a forgery. The necklace is seven beads of Fireball — a portable arson arsenal. The real beneficiary is House Margaster, not the restoration cause. Ch. 47+ setup; see `notes/session_prep/20260623_necklace-of-fireballs-neverwinter.md`.
