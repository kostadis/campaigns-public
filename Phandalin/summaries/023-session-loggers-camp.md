# Chapter 23: Loggers’ Camp

Date: 2025-11-12

## Summary

The party arrived at an abandoned logging camp deep in the Neverwinter Wood, finding signs of recent occupation but no living souls. Vukradin, annoyed by the situation, jogged around the perimeter shouting greetings into the empty air while the rest of the party settled near a fire pit with still-warm ashes. The tents remained standing, and a cart sat idle, but the loggers who had worked here were nowhere to be found. Shrimpy and Corbin remained at camp. Vukradin’s noisy circuit around a grassy knoll disturbed footprints, prompting jokes about making the investigation harder. Three monstrous ankhegs suddenly erupted from the ground beneath the party.

A fierce battle followed. Valphine cast Heroism on Brewbarry, granting immunity to fear and three temporary hit points at the start of each turn. Brewbarry raged, grew to Large size with a twenty-foot reach, and opened with a critical strike against ankheg 3. Soma transformed into a brown bear and concentrated her attacks on the same creature. Repeated acid sprays wounded both front-line fighters, while Valphine moved in to heal them and withdrew again. Vukradin repeatedly cast Sleep on ankheg 1: twice it lost its turn but recovered, and the third casting put it fully to sleep.

Bardic Inspiration helped Brewbarry turn a miss into the strike that split ankheg 3 open. Soma critically wounded ankheg 2, and Vukradin landed an attack for exactly its seven remaining hit points. With the last ankheg asleep, the party healed and arranged themselves on opposite sides to avoid sharing an acid line. Brewbarry struck the sleeping creature, Soma mauled it, and Vukradin used Vicious Mockery. The final exchange included more missed attacks before Soma finished the creature with two hits totaling nineteen damage.

After the battle, Vukradin cut open the ankhegs with his rapier, searching for the missing loggers and joking about recovering their “Fair Trade Gold.” Inside were partially dissolved logger bodies; Tibor Wester was not among the identified remains. Drag marks showed where humanoid corpses had been pulled. Valphine investigated a recently destroyed cabin whose chimney alone remained standing. Inside she found thirteen tiny androgynous stick figures dipped in pig’s blood, marked with lightning bolts, and bound with hair. The party suspected Talos and a curse or summoning, but their Religion checks did not establish the bundle’s meaning. Vukradin’s search of the tents also yielded a silver-scaled pendant shaped like a kraken’s eye, warm to the touch. He wanted to consult Ser Kaelen about it.

A fourth ankheg burst through the floor of another building. Brewbarry renewed his rage and struck it for seventeen damage while Soma dashed toward the fight, still in bear form with only seven hit points. Vukradin’s Vicious Mockery failed. The ankheg bit Brewbarry and attempted to drag him into a tunnel ten feet underground. The initial grapple result was revisited when Valphine pointed out his advantage on checks to escape: the additional roll defeated the grapple, and the DM ruled that the ankheg had not burrowed away. Valphine shot it, and Soma opened a door and killed it with both bear attacks.

Valphine then heard a human voice in a northern room. She listened suspiciously before carefully opening the door, revealing Tibor Wester in hysterics. Faced with a dead ankheg, a very large bloodied and acid-covered barbarian, a drow, a bear peering through the doorway, and Vukradin calling his name, Tibor fainted. Vukradin revived him and immediately explained the supply errand, asking for his signature. Tibor gladly signed; the party discussed putting the supplies inside, but completed delivery was not established.

Brewbarry’s questioning established that the camp’s crew numbered thirteen, including Tibor, matching the stick figures. Tibor claimed to have no enemies and said he had survived by running into his office and locking the door. Valphine noticed his anxiety, while Vukradin speculated that he might have summoned the ankhegs—an accusation, not an established discovery. Persistent questioning revealed that visitors with unusually sharp, strangely designed weapons had argued with the Emerald Enclave the day before the attack. The argument concerned a ritual or activity the Enclave insisted was forbidden and must stop. Tibor had ignored it because he regarded it as Enclave business.

Searching an unexplored portion of the camp, Valphine found correspondence belonging to Joe, an Emerald Enclave agent among the loggers. A message promised that a patrol would come the following day to address the situation. Further writing recorded that, after the patrol arrived, Joe could not find its members at their planned rendezvous. The DM separately stated that the patrol had met somebody before disappearing. Joe himself had been digested by an ankheg. Later, Valphine’s knowledge of Underdark lore supplied a connection between kraken worship and the Kraken Society, whose membership had included aboleths and mind flayers.

The next lead lay up the river, deeper into the Neverwinter Wood. Vukradin wanted to return to Phandalin for his hundred-gold reward and fund his music studio; Brewbarry argued that the injustice deserved investigation, and Valphine wanted allies among all the factions. An Intelligence check reminded Vukradin that the Emerald Enclave could reward information about its missing agents. The session ended without a final decision about pursuing the river lead.
