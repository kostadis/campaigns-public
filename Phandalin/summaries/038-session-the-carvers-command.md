# Chapter 38: The Carver’s Command

Date: Mar 25th, 2026

## Summary
The party pressed onward through biting winds and treacherous slopes, climbing the jagged, snow-covered flanks of Icespire Peak without suffering exhaustion thanks to their successful navigation. The mountain was so immense it could be seen from Phandalin on a clear day, though the fortress atop it was dwarfed by the surrounding heights. Icespire Hold consisted of two structures connected by a stone bridge, half-obscured by clouds. A winding path led to the smaller gatehouse, and the Carver’s trail followed that approach. The party considered climbing a 200-foot sheer ice wall instead, but lacked flying magic and suitable climbing equipment. They initially followed the path, crossing a twenty-foot stretch of rock-slide rubble one at a time. From below, they spotted Cryovain apparently sleeping on the main fortress roof, its white scales blending with the snow.

Unwilling to enter through the obvious gatehouse, the group found a shorter climb: a fifty-foot icy cliff beside the fortress. Soma became a giant wolf spider and carried a rope up for the others. Vukradin offered Bardic Inspiration, and everyone completed the climb. After clarifying that they wanted the ruined corner of the main fortress rather than the bridge, the party moved through earthquake rubble toward a back entrance.

Soma continued up the walls to scout the roof. There she discovered Cryovain immobilized within a summoning circle, bound by magical chains while the Carver conducted a ritual with his purple-glowing obsidian sword. Her first stealth result looked disastrous, but accounting for the spider’s natural stealth and adding Bardic Inspiration brought the total to 16—enough to escape notice. An initial Arcana check failed to explain the chains or whether disabling the Carver would release the dragon. Soma reported what she had seen. A later Animal Handling check confirmed that Cryovain was in such a state of rage that releasing it would endanger everyone nearby.

Inside, the party crossed ruined halls where Cryovain had smashed through doors and walls killing orcs, then found the filthy remains of the orcs’ living quarters. The furnishings had been burned in the fireplace. Remembering that the dragon had driven the orcs out, Vukradin suggested securing their former home so they could return safely. Valphine discovered a secret door with a remarkable Insight check, but the party chose to stop exploring and head upstairs toward the dragon.

Vukradin acted swiftly, casting magical silence over the summoning circle and interrupting the ritual. Combat began. Brewbarry raged and dashed around the intervening walls, while the Carver used a legendary movement action to escape his reach without provoking an attack. Vukradin then cast Hold Person through an arrow slit, dropping Silence as he changed concentration and leaving the human antagonist paralyzed.

The paralysis did not stop the sword. Abandoning its ritual, the Carver attempted direct domination of Cryovain. A voice rang inside the party’s heads: “Coward’s magic, you mute the ritual because you lack the strength to shatter the vessel.” It followed with: “And now, now I shall have everything I always wanted.”

The chains dissolved. Purple energy left the sword and entered the dragon, while the blade became plain black obsidian. Soma’s Arcana result of 13 revealed the truth: the Carver was the sword, and the human had been its vessel. The ritual would have secured the dragon without the uncertainty of a saving throw; interrupting it forced the Carver to gamble on a spell instead. That gamble succeeded, but control remained incomplete. The party had three rounds to kill Cryovain before the Carver’s domination became absolute.

Valphine struck Cryovain with Guiding Bolt for 15 radiant damage. Soma used her spider form to climb into position, then became a bear, preserving the bolt’s advantage for Brewbarry. Wielding the Dragon Slayer Sword, Brewbarry landed blows for 21 and 22 damage. Cryovain answered with freezing breath: 40 damage, reduced to 30 by Stone’s Endurance. The dragon then rose thirty feet above the battlements.

With the melee fighters facing an airborne target, Vukradin searched for an answer behind the fortress’s six-foot wall. He again declined to use Fireball, then landed a critical ranged spell attack for 26 damage and celebrated the hit. The session ended mid-battle, with initiative preserved and the dragon’s progress toward domination needing to be tracked.

## Scenes
### The Ascent to Icespire Hold
#### The party navigates the treacherous, wind-swept slopes of Icespire Peak to reach the stone fortress on its icy northeast spur.
- The party successfully navigates the mountain path, avoiding exhaustion through clever skill use and successful rolls from the ascent.
- The adventurers arrive at Icespire Hold, a fortress consisting of two structures joined by a stone bridge, visible from Phandalin on a clear day.
- The DM emphasizes the scale of the mountain: Icespire Peak is the prominent distant landmark, while the fortress is tiny against it.
- Clouds partly obscure the hold. Cold, howling winds buffet the party as the narrow path corkscrews toward the smaller gatehouse.
- The alternative approach requires climbing a 200-foot sheer ice wall. The DM initially says twenty feet, then explicitly corrects the height to two hundred.
- The party considers Fly, Levitate, climbing forms, crampons, and ice picks. Vukradin has no Fly spell, and Soma’s climbing forms solve only her own ascent without further assistance.
- The Carver’s trail follows the ordinary path rather than the sheer wall.
- A rock slide leaves a twenty-foot stretch of difficult terrain. Crossing it requires moving through the rubble one at a time, but the trail is otherwise safe.
- From their vantage point, the party spots Cryovain sleeping atop the main fortress roof, its white scales blending with the snow.
- Discussion of the quest log recalls the Shrine of Savras as a possible source of the dragon’s location; the party now has direct confirmation of where Cryovain is.

## NPCs
### Cryovain
A young white dragon whose scales blend with the snow, initially seen apparently sleeping on the roof of Icespire Hold. Closer scouting reveals it chained inside a summoning circle while the Carver attempts to consume and dominate it.

Soma’s Animal Handling result of 24 establishes that Cryovain is in a state of extreme rage and would attack everything nearby if released. When the Carver resorts to direct domination, the chains dissolve, but the dragon’s will continues resisting the intruding presence for a three-round window.

Cryovain takes 15 radiant damage from Valphine, 43 damage from Brewbarry’s sword attacks, and 26 damage from Vukradin’s final critical hit. It retaliates against Brewbarry with freezing breath before rising thirty feet into the air.

### The Carver
A sentient black obsidian sword using a human veteran as its vessel. The rooftop ritual is intended to consume and fully dominate Cryovain without relying on the uncertain outcome of a direct domination spell.

Before the transfer, the Carver uses a legendary action to move thirty feet without provoking opportunity attacks. Paralyzing the human does not prevent the sword from acting.

Forced away from its preferred ritual, the Carver gambles on direct domination and succeeds, though its control remains incomplete for three rounds. Its purple energy enters Cryovain, leaving the sword looking like ordinary black obsidian. It addresses the party telepathically as it makes the transfer.

### The Veteran
The human vessel possessed and used by the Carver. Vukradin paralyzes him with Hold Person during the opening confrontation. He subsequently fails another saving throw and remains immobile.

After the purple presence leaves the sword, the veteran is still on the roof with the now-dark blade. The paralysis of this body has not disabled the actual antagonist.

### Sister Kaella
Previously left incapacitated by the party and referenced through information obtained from her. She warned that the Carver had gone mad and advised: “Chop off the head and the monster will fall.”

The table recalls that the party questioned her after incapacitating her and left her alive at one hit point. Corbin remains at camp. Vukradin’s player clarifies that the character does not understand that Corbin is torturing her and still considers reforming Corbin part of his earlier intentions.

## Locations
### Icespire Peak
A massive, jagged, snow-covered mountain visible from Phandalin on a clear day. Cold winds batter its approaches, and clouds partially obscure the fortress on its icy northeast spur.

A narrow winding path provides the safe land approach. An alternative route would require scaling a 200-foot sheer ice wall. Along the path, a twenty-foot stretch of rock-slide rubble forms difficult terrain.

### Icespire Hold
A stone fortress atop an icy spur of Icespire Peak, consisting of a smaller gatehouse and a larger main structure joined by a stone bridge. The Carver takes the gatehouse route, while the party bypasses it by climbing a fifty-foot icy cliff.

An old earthquake collapsed two corners into piles of rubble. Inside are empty halls with arrow slits, a doorway smashed by Cryovain, and filthy former orc quarters whose furnishings were burned in the fireplace. Valphine discovers a secret door there.

The rooftop contains the summoning circle and chained dragon. Six-foot defensive walls and arrow slits influence movement, line of sight, and the party’s spellcasting positions during the battle.

### Phandalin
A settlement visible from Icespire Peak on a clear day, referenced as the landmark from which the mountain can be seen.

The party also recalls having gone directly toward the mountain rather than returning to Phandalin for supplies, including the climbing equipment they now discuss.

## Items
### Fireball Beads
Magical beads from the Necklace of Fireballs held by Vukradin. The party repeatedly suggested using them during the climb and approach, but Vukradin firmly refused each time.

They remain unused during the session. A suggestion to throw beads up the ice wall goes nowhere, and Fireball is considered again when Cryovain takes flight.

### Rope
Used to bring the party up the fifty-foot icy cliff after Soma climbs in giant wolf spider form and drops it down.

The rope makes the ascent a DC 7 Athletics or Acrobatics check for the remaining adventurers. Everyone reaches the top successfully.

### Dragon Slayer Sword
A longsword designed to inflict extra damage on dragons. Brewbarry wields it with two hands against Cryovain, using a d10 for its base weapon damage and adding 3d6 against the dragon.

Guiding Bolt’s advantage helps his first attack connect. The two successful blows deal 21 and 22 damage, respectively.

### The Carver (Black Obsidian Sword)
The sentient weapon that is the true Carver, using the human veteran as a vessel. Its purple glow accompanies the rooftop ritual and disappears when its influence enters Cryovain.

The sword’s ability to act despite the human’s paralysis exposes the distinction between the vessel and the controlling intelligence. Soma’s Arcana check then explains the transfer. Afterward, the physical weapon remains a black obsidian blade while the immediate threat resides in the dragon.

## Spells
### Wild Shape
Soma becomes a giant wolf spider to scale the cliffs, place the rope, and scout the roof. The form is a Medium beast, with a +7 Stealth modifier that proves essential to the scouting check.

During combat, Soma uses the spider’s climbing ability to cross a wall, then becomes a bear. This expends her second ordinary Wild Shape charge. She does not attack that turn, leaving Guiding Bolt’s advantage available for Brewbarry.

### Silence
Cast by Vukradin over the summoning circle to prevent verbal spell components and interrupt the Carver’s ritual.

The effect has a twenty-foot radius and a 120-foot casting range. The Carver moves beyond it using a legendary action. The field subsequently drops when concentration changes to Hold Person, after which the sword attempts direct domination.

### Hold Person
Cast by Vukradin through an arrow slit against the Carver’s human vessel. The veteran fails his Wisdom saving throw and becomes paralyzed, then fails his subsequent attempt to escape the effect.

The spell restrains the human but does not prevent the sentient sword from acting.

### Guiding Bolt
Valphine strikes Cryovain for 15 radiant damage and grants advantage on the next attack against it.

Soma deliberately avoids consuming the benefit. Brewbarry uses it on his first Dragon Slayer Sword attack, turning that attack into a hit.

### Bardic Inspiration
Vukradin offers inspiration to the party before the rope climb.

Soma later adds 6 from the inspiration die to her corrected spider-form Stealth result, reaching 16 and avoiding the Carver’s notice. During combat, Vukradin reports having no uses left. Existing inspiration is discussed when Brewbarry faces the dragon’s breath, but it does not alter that outcome.
