# Chapter 39: A dragon defeated, a bard tempted, a barbarian’s vengeance

Date: Apr 9th, 2026

## Summary
The battle resumed on Icespire Hold’s exposed rooftop battlements with Cryovain already thirty feet airborne and the Carver tightening its control. One round of the Carver’s three-round domination had elapsed, so the party entered round two with little time to kill Cryovain before the possession became absolute. Cryovain’s rage kept him engaged rather than simply flying away.

Valphine called down a third-level Guiding Bolt, but even Bardic Inspiration could not rescue her poor attack roll. Soma checked that Moonbeam’s forty-foot-high cylinder could reach the dragon and cast it at third level, dealing eight damage. Brewbarry considered throwing handaxes before the DM ruled that his Large height, combined with his extended halberd reach, would let him strike Cryovain. The Dragon Slayer Sword could not provide the same reach. Two halberd blows, with rage damage included, left the dragon with fourteen hit points.

Cryovain, furious and wounded, turned his freezing breath on Brewbarry again. Having endured twin blasts across the battle, the barbarian remained standing through physical resilience and stubbornness. This session’s breath threatened forty-five damage, but a successful Constitution save reduced it to twenty-two, and Stone’s Endurance absorbed another eight. Brewbarry survived with six hit points. Cryovain then moved out of the Moonbeam, leaving the DM to lament a dragon that had failed to bring down one character with two breaths.

Vukradin spent Bardic Inspiration to turn a miss into an ordinary hit for twelve damage, reducing Cryovain to two hit points. Soma’s Moonbeam remained a threat, but Valphine finished the dragon first. After her missed Guiding Bolt and further teasing from the party, she cast Sacred Flame. Cryovain failed his save, collapsed, and the Carver’s essence dissipated. Asked whether the Carver was gone for good, the DM confirmed that it was.

The party then turned to the Carver’s former human vessel, still paralyzed by Hold Person. Soma attacked with the intention of dealing nonlethal bear damage and moved Moonbeam onto him, but pleaded against killing a man she regarded as a victim. Brewbarry’s rage had not cooled. His halberd strikes killed the veteran, and when the outcome was briefly described as unconsciousness, he explicitly confirmed that he had intended a kill.

The recovered hoard posed a different problem: roughly three thousand gold pieces in coins, gems, and trade bars had fused into a block of residium-infused ice, their identifying markings destroyed. Despite initially disclaiming her Arcana expertise, Soma rolled sixteen and recognized planar interference. Recovering the treasure would require a planar theorist. Vukradin rejected the notion that destroyed identifying marks made the gold legitimate property, insisting that investigation must precede ownership.

The obsidian sword offered a more personal temptation. Its pommel crystal was a mental amplifier that could project music directly into an audience’s minds, providing perfect acoustics without a physical studio. Vukradin demanded clarification about possible mind control, proposed Legend Lore, and declared the weapon the Bluetooth Sword despite the DM’s protests. The DM explained that ordinary users could amplify music; the Carver’s use of the crystal depended on its unusual nature. Unspecified side effects remained. The sword seemed tailored to Vukradin’s dream of a music studio, but his ethical concern remained that its magical gem should be returned to its rightful owner.

The party discussed the now-empty fortress, the orcs at the Shrine of Savras and Falcon’s Lodge, and a journey to Neverwinter. The DM warned that choosing the dragon had cost them the opportunity to return the lighthouse statue directly to its intended recipient before House Margaster acted. Vukradin maintained that the characters did not yet know her fate and could seek her next of kin. Soma also recognized that Harbin Wester and the Lord’s Alliance would claim credit for the dragon’s defeat unless challenged, but chose not to share that insight: preserving the plane and her understanding of the natural order mattered more than glory. Before exploring farther, the party took a short rest.

Two women then appeared: Syleen Wintermoon, a lean redhead with a ponytail, and her muscular, black-haired sister Runa. They praised Vukradin as a saint who returned stolen treasure and claimed the entire slag block. Vukradin rejected the unsupported claim, while Brewbarry demanded substance behind it. Their confrontation escalated into a fight against six opponents: Syleen, Runa, and four numbered veterans.

Valphine’s Spirit Guardians damaged the claimants before a veteran’s blow broke her concentration. Soma became a bear and used Bardic Inspiration to land both attacks. Meanwhile, opponents repeatedly tried and failed to snatch the treasure from Vukradin. He paralyzed two of them with Hold Person and moved away, leaving Brewbarry to cut down the immobilized enemies. Veteran 4 fled after the first killing; Veteran 2 later disengaged and escaped as well. The remaining held opponent fell to Brewbarry.

Before Brewbarry finished the final held opponent, Vukradin used Sending to tell Ser Kaelen that the dragon and Carver were dead and the party intended to travel to Neverwinter. Kaelen urged them instead to come quickly to Phandalin because of trouble involving the Emerald Enclave. A more elaborate congratulatory reply about their “conflict-free triumph” drew laughter and complaints about its artificial polish; the DM restated the practical message. Vukradin shared the summons, Soma wanted to know which Enclave faction was involved, and Brewbarry needed only the prospect of celebration. The party’s next destination became Phandalin.
