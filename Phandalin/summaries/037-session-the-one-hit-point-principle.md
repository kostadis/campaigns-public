# Chapter 37: The One Hit Point Principle

Date: Mar 18th, 2026

## Summary
With Sister Kaella unconscious, Valphine reminded the others of her arrangement with Kaella while the party considered its next move: return stolen artwork, attend the play, return to Phandalin, question Kaella, or speak to the wounded centaur.

A mention of goodberries unexpectedly stirred Brewbarry’s memories. He recalled finding one after the theft of the artifact he had been guarding, remembered its healing properties, and associated it with the accusations that he had neglected his watch. He insisted that his drinking began only after the theft and his expulsion from the Uthgardtian tribe. The exact sequence remained hazy. The DM identified the stolen object as one of eight artifacts connected to an epic quest of which Brewbarry knew nothing. Brewbarry, meanwhile, preferred discussing how much better beer and wine tasted than his tribe’s fermented milk. Vukradin maintained that the Necklace of Fireballs remained someone else’s property, however useful Brewbarry thought it might be against a dragon.

Soma healed the desperately wounded centaur for 12 hit points. Xanthopoulos—Xanth for short—rose, thanked her, and kissed the top of her bear form’s head. Speaking with a Greek accent, he explained that the white dragon had moved into the Icespire Mountains amid the aftermath of the wars involving dragons, giants, and other peoples. Its arrival displaced the orcs, who moved south and fractured into factions. Some followed Talosian anchorites; others retained their old tribal loyalties. Their expanding control around Neverwinter Wood was driving more people toward Phandalin.

Xanth immediately recognized Soma’s staff as the powerful artifact of his old friend Meril. Its single newly grown leaf marked a connection between Meril and its new bearer, prompting Xanth to pledge his help. The staff once bore many leaves in a prior era; its leaves now grow as the bearer accepts the role and never fall away. Soma described her goals modestly: keeping the world safer, giving people somewhere to live, and preserving peace. Xanth contrasted those practical aims with the Emerald Enclave’s arguments over whether the crisis counted as natural change or intervention. He urged the party to deal with the dragon.

Xanth also explained the Carver’s criminal organization. Its three lieutenants were Borg, Sister Kaella, and Jax. Borg had been killed by Locutus in the forest after fleeing Butterskull Ranch, and Jax had fallen at Dragon Barrow. Xanth’s information came through his forest connections; Soma reminded the others that animals were talkative. When asked about the dragon’s exact location, Xanth indicated Kaella. He wanted her information and expressed no objection to her being killed afterward.

Valphine revived Kaella with a Medicine check, leaving her conscious at one hit point, then intimidated her into answering. Kaella was relieved the party had won: Jax would certainly have tortured her, whereas the party merely might. She reiterated and elaborated on her earlier warning that the Carver had gone mad and was heading to Icespire Hold to possess the white dragon through a ritual and wreak vengeance. The new disclosures concerned her motives and the route. She feared she would be among his first targets. Her opposition to him began after the loss of his muscle and his accusations that she had failed at intelligence gathering. She had approached Valphine because Valphine alone had appeared and because she believed Vukradin struggled with moral ambiguity.

Kaella supplied directions to Icespire Hold. She readily accepted imprisonment in Phandalin, preferred the stronger refuge of Axeholm, and even offered to accompany the party. Whatever arrangement they chose, she insisted that they interrupt the ritual. A direct forced march would cost one level of exhaustion; a detour through Phandalin would cost two. Traveling without pushing themselves left their arrival time uncertain. Xanth first proposed guarding Kaella in the tomb, then agreed to carry party members as far as the foothills near the gold mine. With Xanth, Boney, and Soma’s bear form, they could cover the first thirty-five miles mounted and tackle the remaining twenty without exhaustion.

Kaella was left with Corbin, whose eagerness to keep her subdued suggested an ugly history between them. Valphine recognized the pleasure of someone gaining power over a former tormentor. Vukradin did not understand that Corbin might torture Kaella; when she screamed and cursed upon learning who would guard her, he naively defended Corbin as their friend and her former colleague. The party took a long rest and set out.

Near the mountains, Soma sensed a disruption in the natural order. The Carver’s human footprints cut a perfectly straight line over obstacles, with a mechanical regularity that suggested possession or compulsion. The party followed his route, successfully navigating the difficult approach through their combined survival, athletic, and perceptive abilities.

In a narrow pass, stone giants murmured about rocks that had become wrong. Brewbarry and Valphine understood their language. The giants confirmed that a lone little one carrying an obsidian sword had passed through. Soma used Meril’s staff to reveal psychic damage along his trail: apparently rolling stones were actually shimmering and shifting in and out of phase. The giants nevertheless invoked Hekaton’s guidance and the disastrous giant wars to justify remaining apart from the conflict. They gave the party potions of greater healing and hill giant strength before sending them onward.

The Glacier of Whispers lay beyond. Violet energy stained its ice; frozen faces moved their mouths in silent screams beneath the surface. Psychic light marked the Carver’s footprints, and a whisper reached the party’s minds: *He walks without rest, without breath, without soul.* Despite mixed results navigating the terrain, everyone resisted its psychic resonance. Valphine recalled a local blessing of Lathander that could provide advantage against psychic effects for ten minutes, but they did not need to invoke it.

Higher up, the party found a young white drake frozen dead, its eyes burned out with necrotic energy. The Carver had walked straight through its lair. Its mother circled overhead, confused and enraged. Vukradin played calming music, and Brewbarry joined him. The table suggested Moonlight Sonata and recalled their Lathandrian death chant. The mother settled beside her dead young, allowing the party to move past without fighting.

The final obstacle was a mass of boulders and ice. Soma proposed using Mold Earth on the available soil to shift the stones and construct a passage. The DM accepted a Survival check at DC 15, warning that careless manipulation could cause an avalanche. Vukradin supplied Bardic Inspiration, but Soma reported that her roll succeeded without it. The session ended before the resulting passage or the next stage of the ascent was narrated.

### Jax
A lieutenant of the Carver killed with his ogre in the preceding battle. Kaella believes his victory would have meant her prolonged torture and death.

### Xanth
A centaur also known as Xanthopoulos, speaking with a Greek accent. Soma restores him with Cure Wounds, earning an affectionate kiss on her bear head.

A criminal leader appearing in human form whose lieutenants were Borg, Kaella, and Jax. According to Kaella, he has gone mad and intends to possess the white dragon at Icespire Hold.

He travels alone with an obsidian sword, following a perfectly straight route with unnaturally rhythmic footsteps. Psychic disturbances and phasing stones mark his passage. His route also leads through the lair of a young drake found dead with necrotically burned eyes. Possession or compulsion is suspected but not established.

### Corbin
A party associate and former member of the Carver’s organization, assigned to guard Kaella. His enthusiasm and request to beat her reveal a personal motive that Valphine recognizes. ### Stone Giants
Deeply introverted giants encountered in a narrow mountain pass. Brewbarry and Valphine understand their language. They witnessed the Carver traveling alone with his obsidian sword and noticed disturbances in the rocks.

They invoke Hekaton and the disastrous giant wars to justify avoiding intervention. Even after seeing evidence of psychic warping, they offer supplies rather than join the pursuit.

### Mother Drake
A grieving, enraged drake whose young was killed along the Carver’s route. Vukradin and Brewbarry calm her with music. She settles beside her dead offspring while the party passes without fighting.
