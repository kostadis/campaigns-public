# Chapter 58

Date: 2026-10-13

## Summary

The party emerged from the baths of Lord Neverember's estate scrubbed clean of the sewer stench. They wore fresh clothes he had pressed on them, "not so much for you, but for me," and returned to find dessert waiting. The bath took at least half an hour, with soaps, salts, and lotions, though the party still owned only one dirty, smelly prototype bathrobe. Vukradin was already Mayor of Phandalin by Neverember's appointment earlier in the dinner. The paperwork Aldus had delivered earlier in the dinner charged the party with protecting the town and lending aid in Leilon, and left day-to-day operations to "whomever you so choose." Vukradin balked at being appointed rather than elected and said so. Valphine warned that there were ways to do this "that don't involve poking the bear," and Vukradin answered that he would poke the bear anyway.

Neverember turned to his ever-efficient assistant Aldus for an opinion. Aldus returned with the very clause in the paperwork that let a new mayor call an election himself. The way to the goal was left entirely to Vukradin: "how you choose is totally up to you." Vukradin's reply was, "Not a railroad track in sight." The easy comfort with which everyone accepted the arrangement unsettled Valphine (passive Insight 23), whose drow understanding of power politics told her something was badly wrong. Vukradin said he had theories but would share them only once they had left.

The party then asked about the Neverwinter Commission and the origins of the broken planar manifold. Neverember regarded them with boredom and reminded them that, as shareholders, they needed no permission to ask questions of their own commission. He ordered Aldus to draft a letter of introduction. Vukradin also raised the plight of Ilsbeth Marrow, the matron of Marrow and Sons, a small shipping firm whose harbor fees had quadrupled since the manifold failed. Valphine did not think Ilsbeth important, but Vukradin insisted: "You promised her that we would bring it up. We're bringing it up." Neverember granted the firm temporary harbor relief on the spot and had Aldus write it up before dessert was cleared.

Once they were safely away, Vukradin shared his theory. Some hidden power was promoting the party, spreading kind rumors, and pulling Neverember's strings. His need to "get an opinion" meant he was checking with that power, because "he actually doesn't have any real power in this situation." Soma called it the deep state. Valphine proposed draining the swamp. Vukradin allowed that Neverember "thinks he's doing the right thing."

The party had three options: the letter of introduction to the Commission, the standing invitation to tea with Lord Cassian Meliamne, or fetching Bimble from the Spire of the Morninglord to lead them through the sewers to the device. They chose tea first. Vukradin, joking about Brewbarry's absent player, said Brewbarry would have insisted on the Commission: "I tried to speak up for you, but they forced me to go to tea." Bimble was safe in the Spire, where Valphine's "vengeful Morninglord routine" had shocked the faithful and earned her the title of Bishop of Searing Light.

The returned Blue Quartz mermaid statue sat in Cassian's drawing room, not centered like an altar. Beside it lay papers bearing his late cousin Elara's name; the two called each other brother and sister. Cassian first asked whether the technician Bimble was alive and free, and whether anyone else knew. He was relieved to hear Bimble was alive and in the party's custody, safe in the Spire of the Morninglord under Brother Aldric's protection. Then he laid out his evidence:

- A commercial service contract between the Commission's harbor arm and Lionshield Coster, showing fees and routing responsibilities but nothing about the maker's origin.
- Elara's records request slip, which showed she had been digging into the manifold.
- A forged harbor writ, made by Cullen Sharpe, an associate of House Margaster, and used against him.
- Elara's last note: "The failure is profitable only if somebody knew the road would stay closed."

Soma compared that last note to "shorting the stock."

Cassian said he knew the Margasters had blackmailed him and killed his cousin, and that they had killed her so she could never be raised. Yet, he admitted, "Knowing is not yet a case." He did not know who had seen Elara's query. The party agreed that without hard evidence they could not simply walk into House Margaster. In the DM's words, "not at level seven." Cassian promised a substantial donation to Vukradin's studio fund, and equivalent rewards for the rest of them, if they uncovered proof of the murder. Soma asked about Tortle soup, and Cassian hastily explained that it is called that "because it's made by a Tortle." He suggested they might reach the manifold without going through the Commission, since no one could say what the Commission might do. The party meant to go to the Commission regardless.

The Commission occupied a nondescript building in the Protector's Enclave, near the Counting House. Boney, Lady Alagondar's well-mannered horse, waited outside, as he does anywhere people would not bring a horse. The party was admitted at once and shown to a room where a tea tray held exactly the right number of cups. They had been expected. A figure sat, took a cup, and congratulated "Mayor Vukradin" on his appointment and the shareholders on their recent dividend. He announced that access to the "Model 1486 installation, maintenance register, and harbor continuity file" was granted: "It is already written down." Vukradin corrected him to acting mayor and Soma offered "Mayor Pro Tem." The figure noted that down as "the acting pro tem mayor."

He then handed over a full packet:

- Full inspection access to the manifold enclosure.
- Copies of Bimble's maintenance reports.
- The access ledger, which held no entry for the "rewriter" Bimble had described.
- A continuity file showing that House Margaster had been authorized to take over shipping perhaps within minutes of the manifold's failure.
- A replacement-part research request, already opened in the party's names.
- Permission to bring a technical consultant, name unspecified.

He noted that "the welfare inquiry concerning the former operator remains open." The party told him the operator was alive, and he wrote it down. When he asked whether the former operator needed protection, the party said no.

Every document, from both sides of the contracts, was written in the same precise hand. Pressed on who wrote the register, the speaker said only that it "was written by the appropriate committee." He never said "I." He explained that the Commission was always led by the current factor, and that "the commission through the factor is speaking to you." Vukradin's verdict: "Tautology. I love it." Asked who the previous factor was, he said that information was not readily accessible, then took a note. He was unfailingly helpful the whole time and blocked nothing. Soma reached out with a sense for planar anomalies and found nothing on the man himself. A residue of reality-warping strangeness clung to the writing on the paperwork. The party's suspicions of something creepy were now confirmed. The table floated illithid influence or a hive mind, and the DM firmly denied he was replaying Baldur's Gate 3.

Before leaving the topic, the party confirmed that Brewbarry's loan from the Counting House was secured. The plan remained to start the factory in Phandalin, with Leilon, the gold mine, or the dwarf fortress as alternatives.

With the concert set for that night, the party turned to preparations. Vukradin refused to risk the sewers or the manifold before the show: "What if we get trapped and we miss it? Not taking any chances." In the streets, fans eyed Vukradin and Valphine, some VV shippers and some NoRoms. Rsolk had been promised tickets, and Vukradin's answer was "Put him on the list… Better than tickets."

Soma went alone into the sewers to collect the 250 gold owed by the wererat Rsolk, since everyone else was in the band. Rsolk thanked the party for restoring his routes and asked Soma to pass his regards to Zeleen, the wererat who had guarded the dwarves. Soma offered him a place on the guest list, told him he was the only one on it, and promised him a seat at her own table. Rsolk was ecstatic and ordered his henchmen to smuggle in the finest roses in Neverwinter. Soma returned with the gold and told Vukradin how thrilled the wererat was: "I don't think I've ever seen anybody so excited in their life." Vukradin privately planned to put every friend and associate in Neverwinter on the list too: "But he doesn't need to know that." The DM also reminded them that famous and rich patrons had already sent donations by way of the Moonstone Mask.

At the Common Chord, Vukradin began by claiming 500 gold for the night. The party was reminded that the 500 gold had been the value of Petra's right to use Vukradin's likeness indefinitely, settled by this exclusive performance rather than paid in coin. Their real pay for the concert was half the gross, so a crowded guest list would shrink their take. Vukradin shrugged it off: "It's mostly about the studio fund anyway."

There they met Lorniel, the leader of a cover band. He was a freakishly convincing, wholly non-magical double of Vukradin, imitating the early, arrogant Vukradin. He called his act "an homage to the great art." When he insisted he was "authentically you," Soma dubbed him "the real deal, the genuine imitation." Lorniel had been at the Morninglord performance and lamented how hard it was to find "a drow who wants to be a cleric of Lathander" for the new act. He agreed to sit out the night so the crowd would not be confused, asked "am I still going to get paid?", and was paid 25 gold by Petra.

The party decided to use the show to accuse House Margaster, to call Neverember a puppet without naming whoever held his strings, and to promote the Church of Lathander. Soma's rule was "Don't insult him directly. Insult whoever's controlling him." The DM warned these choices were consequential: name Margaster, and by morning everyone would know. The party chose not to reveal that the manifold might never work again, since they had not yet seen it themselves. They discussed calling out Adabra, but decided against it. They also planned a studio fund collection at the doors, hats passed during the show, and a 50-50 raffle.

The concert was a triumph. Rsolk sat in the crowd with his flowers. Vukradin's Insight of 15 still told him Rsolk was hoping to be called on stage, and Vukradin declared, "The stage is for the band." When the songs named House Margaster, a well-dressed listener rose and left. Vukradin said "Message received" and noted he had made no donation on the way out. Soma sent a familiar after him. It followed him toward the docks, where the Margaster offices stood, rather than into the Protector's Enclave. He spoke to no one and vanished into the darkness, but the party will recognize him again.

The crowd went wild at the "puppet" line and invented dance moves on the spot, chanting "He's the puppet." When Rsolk tried to press a huge bouquet on Valphine, her withering, glowing-eyed stare sent him back to his seat for the rest of the night. The party earned 1,875 gold, their half of a 3,750 gold gross ("All totally clean"), to split however they choose. Vukradin wondered aloud whether he was ready to retire.

The next steps toward the manifold and Leilon lay ahead. The table agreed to finish the machinery investigation next session and then head for Leilon. Loose threads were logged:

- The drow advisor Neverember claims not to have.
- The dead drop from the Speak with Dead.
- The continuing absence of Sister Kaella.

## NPCs

### Lord Neverember

The powerful ruler of Neverwinter who appointed Vukradin Mayor of Phandalin earlier in the dinner, grants harbor relief to Ilsbeth Marrow at the party's request, and provides a letter of introduction to the Neverwinter Commission. Vukradin suspects he is being controlled by an unseen power.

- He insists the party change clothes "Not so much for you, but for me."
- He defers to Aldus on the election question ("I really do need an opinion on this. You should get me one").
- He treats the Commission question with boredom: "You do not need my permission to ask."
- He claims to have no drow advisor.
- The party's concert brands him a puppet, and the line becomes a crowd chant.

### Aldus

Lord Neverember's highly efficient assistant who handles all paperwork, legal clauses, and official letters. He is the one who clarifies the election clause in the mayoral appointment and drafts the harbor relief for Marrow and Sons before dessert is cleared.

- The DM describes him as "his messenger boy, I guess. Like Butler."
- He produced the "updated clause" within minutes of being asked.
- Soma: "Aldus is a guy who gets shit done."

### Ilsbeth Marrow

The proprietor and matron of Marrow and Sons, a small independent shipping company. She previously approached Valphine seeking advocacy with Lord Neverember over quadrupled harbor fees caused by the manifold's failure. Lord Neverember grants her firm temporary harbor relief at the party's urging.

- She runs the firm with her four sons.
- In the previous session she told the party the manifold appeared about ten years earlier through a Commission arrangement.
- Valphine doubted her importance; Vukradin held the party to its promise.

### Lord Cassian Meliamne

A noble seeking justice for his murdered cousin Elara. He shows the party documents that point toward House Margaster but, as he admits, prove nothing yet ('knowing is not yet a case'), and offers a substantial reward to Vukradin's studio fund and equivalent gifts for the party if they find hard evidence of the murder.

- He opens with urgent questions about whether Bimble is alive, free, and known to anyone else.
- He insists "alive" and "in custody" are "important distinctions."
- He calls the Margasters "Bastards" for killing Elara so she "could not be raised."
- He nervously clarifies that Tortle soup is "made by a Tortle."

### Bimble

The technician and former manifold operator currently being kept safe at the Spire of the Morninglord. His maintenance reports are handed over to the party by the Neverwinter Commission, though the official ledger contradicts his claims about unauthorized access by a 'rewriter.'

- The Commission keeps a "welfare inquiry concerning the former operator" open. The party tells the Factor he is alive and declines protection.
- Soma intends to bring him into the manifold.

### Cullen Sharpe

An associate of House Margaster mentioned as having used forged harbor writs against Lord Cassian.

- The DM: "Cullen has been engaged in forgeries."

### The Factor

The mysterious official occupying the seat of the Neverwinter Commission's current Factor, who speaks in the collective 'we' and provides the party with all requested documentation with unsettling ease and foreknowledge. He claims to be the voice of the Commission itself, and the party detects a planar residue on all the paperwork he provides.

- He never says "I."
- He answers procedural questions in tautologies ("The commission considers the task and a decision is made").
- He writes everything down, including Vukradin's preferred title and the news that the operator is alive.
- He is unfailingly helpful and never blocks a request.
- No planar residue is detected on him personally.

### Rsolk

A wererat smuggler who operates in the sewers of Neverwinter. He pays the party a 250 gold piece debt and is overjoyed to be put on the guest list for the concert, ordering his henchmen to smuggle in the finest roses in Neverwinter to throw at the performers.

- He believes he is the only name on the list and that he will sit at Soma's table.
- He hoped to be called on stage.

### Zeleen

A wererat mentioned by Rsolk, who previously guarded the party's dwarves. Rsolk asks Soma to pass along a message to her if they see her.

### Petra

The proprietor of the Common Chord who manages the bookings and financial arrangements for the performers. The party's deal with her entitles them to half the gross proceeds of the concert.

- The earlier 500 gold figure was the value of her right to use Vukradin's likeness indefinitely.

A musician who leads a cover band performing as a tribute act to Vukradin. He freakishly resembles Vukradin without the use of magic and imitates his earlier, more arrogant musical persona. He steps aside for the night at Vukradin's request and is paid 25 gold by Petra.
