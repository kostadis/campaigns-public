# Chapter 55: Combat At Last

Date: 2026-09-08

## Summary

The party arrived at the House of a Thousand Faces, a bustling establishment hiding in plain sight as a secret Harper stronghold. Brewbarry was immediately distracted by a mannequin draped in an exceptionally soft-lined coat, muttering, “This is so soft,” while examining its lining and demanding to know its origin. The rest of the group took in a room whose staff and patrons were carefully trying to appear as boring and ordinary as possible. A bartender greeted them with a rehearsed line about the tavern’s selectively flattering mirrors and its more reliably satisfying drinks, all while the staff quietly evaluated the newcomers with careful, measuring eyes. Valphine recognized the scrutiny and evaluated them in return.

Valphine stepped forward and opened the conversation by saying the party was interested in shipping services. The bartender answered in coded terms: House Margaster delivered on time because everyone else paid for the privilege, while the Harpers delivered late enough that no one could charge the village. Valphine then spoke more plainly about House Margaster profiting from the disappearance of the city’s distribution network and the party’s desire to restore the status quo. The bartender tested her carefully, probing whether she was a true cleric of Lathander. Valphine said that people must understand how to use power. The bartender answered that power should be used only against equally powerful opponents and never for control, expressing Harper ideology. Valphine then declared that those doing evil in the city needed to be stamped out as enemies of Lathander. The tension in the room dissolved, and an elderly human wearing a conspicuous Harper pin came up from downstairs to speak with them directly.

The Harper representative, sharp-eyed and guarded, explained that her organization had been running secret food routes to undercut the economic grip of House Margaster and the Zhentarim. House Margaster’s contracts were designed with hidden details and loopholes that gradually placed clients under the house’s control. Brewbarry was assured that the exceptional fabric he coveted could be supplied, but only through a route that did not involve House Margaster. His customary intimidation failed to impress the Harpers.

The representative revealed that the Manifold—a critical piece of the city’s infrastructure—had been shut down for weeks. The official story from the commission was that it was temporarily closed for repairs, but no repairs had occurred, and the mechanic responsible had vanished. She also confirmed what the party had long suspected: the ninth crate moving through the sewers was almost certainly intended for the missing gnome mechanic, a man the party had taken to calling Bimbo. The Harpers had attempted to follow the crate’s collector but had been thwarted by the creatures now occupying the route.

Those creatures were unnatural spiders that blinked in and out of existence. They had no proper spider heads; instead, their necks ended in gaping mouths full of teeth. Their dry webs shimmered in strange colors, and they moved in ways no natural spider should. The party recognized them as Rift Weavers, having encountered the creatures before in a dwarven citadel.

Valphine raised the matter of a Drow said to be influencing Lord Neverember, though the Harpers claimed no knowledge of such a figure within his inner circle. Valphine kept her suspicion to herself—that the Drow in question was likely her own sister, Aletra—though she did reveal Aletra’s name and her house name, Sotorra. The representative had believed the family to be dead and remarked that some threat assessments would need to be updated. The party responded by mocking the Harpers’ intelligence network for failing to know about Aletra, the cambions allied with House Margaster, or even “Bimbo.” The Harper Seat became briefly huffy, insisting that the organization knew more than it disclosed and had known about House Sotorra’s apparent disappearance and survival before the party entered. The party’s insight suggested that she was deliberately withholding information while attempting to discover how much they knew.

The representative then turned to Soma, encouraging her to give her old friend Lim the benefit of the doubt. Lim had been a Harper agent for years, long before the Manifold’s recent closure, and had originally suspected Soma of developing naturalist sympathies. She had therefore been gathering information from Soma under that assumption. The Harper noted that both Soma and Lim were now very different people from those they remembered. Rather than becoming a naturalist threat, Soma had become a determined interventionist, as demonstrated by her staff and recent actions. Soma observed, “The times make the turtle, not the tortole makes the times,” and admitted that the naturalists had sounded good on paper until it became apparent that they might want the world destroyed. The Harper Seat concluded the exchange by announcing that her allotment of friend therapy for the year was exhausted.

The party was tasked with clearing a sewer junction of the Rift Weavers so that the Harper food route could be reopened. The route passed through Dead Rat territory and had previously been avoided because of a strange magical effect that made travelers profoundly uncomfortable. The spiders had appeared after the Manifold’s mechanic disappeared, transforming the previously obscure passage into an active danger.

The group reviewed its crowded social calendar: dinner with Lord Neverember that evening, a performance at the Common Chord the following night, and tea with Lord Cassian still outstanding. Concerned about arriving at dinner covered in blood and spider remains, the party accepted the Harpers’ assurance that washing facilities, clean clothes, and disguises would be available afterward. The Harper Seat promised that they could make Vukradin look even more incredible.

When Vukradin asked why he had become famous, the representative suggested that he might be getting manipulated but then catalogued the stories converging around him: his refusal to take gold, his singing, his sudden adoption by the Order of the Gauntlet, the unprecedented appearance of a new cult of Lathander, Universal Basic Treasure, the novelty of a Drow cleric of Lathander, and the party’s defeat of a dragon.

Descending into the sewers, the party encountered Rsolk and his gang, the Dead Rats. Rsolk greeted Vukradin with “Found yourself then,” recalling that Vukradin had previously become lost below the city. He was pleased to see him but less pleased that an armed expedition had arrived. Rsolk explained that the spiders had occupied a junction connected to the gang’s passageways and blocked a route that people had already been reluctant to use. He initially offered two hundred gold pieces for clearing them. Vukradin asked whether Rsolk had another party of adventurers waiting to do the job and raised the price to two hundred and fifty gold pieces before announcing that he had a date with a lord and would return soon.

The party sent a familiar ahead through the narrow passageway to scout. Beyond was a large junction with a twenty-five-foot ceiling, a central area slowly filling with foul water, a water-control system on one side, and thick webs across the other. Six Rift Weavers occupied the chamber, including a massive Rift Weaver Prime clinging to a web above the ground. Valphine used her only fourth-level spell slot to place Freedom of Movement on Brewbarry, allowing him to move through water and webbing freely for the next hour.

The battle erupted when one Rift Weaver attacked Brewbarry and missed before phasing away. Soma conjured a Flaming Sphere near one of the spiders, scorching it and beginning the work of igniting the webs. The creature partially evaded the spell, but its vulnerability to fire magnified the damage. Soma then transformed herself into a giant spider and climbed onto the ceiling, though she obeyed Valphine’s emphatic instruction to stay out of the chamber itself.

Valphine unleashed a tide of inky magical darkness over several of the creatures. The spell inflicted psychic damage and weakened the saving throws of those who failed to resist it. The Rift Weaver Prime used its Legendary Resistance to succeed but still suffered half the psychic damage, while its lesser companions took the full effect. The darkness also obscured the party’s targets, forcing Brewbarry to fight from the mouth of the tunnel.

Brewbarry raged, enlarged himself, and used his halberd’s reach to attack without entering the magical darkness. His first two blows would have missed, but Bless turned both into hits, badly injuring one Rift Weaver. Another teleported to the tunnel entrance and struck Brewbarry with a dry, glimmering web. The web restrained him temporarily, but Valphine’s Freedom of Movement allowed him to escape by spending only a small amount of movement. A second Rift Weaver attempted the same maneuver and missed. Another spider approached Brewbarry only to enter the reach of his halberd and die before it could complete its attack.

Vukradin moved forward and cast Spray of Cards, sending a cone of spectral cards into two Rift Weavers. The force damage killed the already wounded spider and severely injured the other, after which Vukradin bravely retreated behind the front line. The Rift Weaver Prime then vanished from its perch and reappeared directly in front of Vukradin, displaying the pack’s peculiar fixation on him. Its attack would have been a critical hit, but Vukradin used his reaction to force the attack to be rerolled. The new result missed, and the session ended at the top of the next round with Soma and another spider on the ceiling, Brewbarry holding the tunnel, two lesser Rift Weavers dead, and the Prime looming over Vukradin.

## NPCs

### The Bartender

A staff member at the House of a Thousand Faces who uses coded language and rehearsed lines to vet visitors and gauge their political and moral leanings. He contrasts House Margaster’s costly punctuality with the Harpers’ slower but cheaper delivery routes. Once Valphine declares her intention to stamp out the city’s evildoers, he decides the party has landed on the acceptable side and summons the Harper Seat.

### Harper Seat

An elderly human woman and high-ranking member of the Harpers who manages shipping routes and intelligence. She is guarded with information and skilled at extracting what others know, presenting ignorance when it suits her and becoming indignant when the party mocks the Harpers’ intelligence network. She explains how Margaster contracts entrap clients, confirms the danger to the secret food route, and tasks the party with clearing the sewer junction of Rift Weavers. She also serves as an unwilling mediator between Soma and Lim, offers the party disguises and clean clothing, and is forced to revise Harper threat assessments after learning that House Sotorra survives.

### Lim

A long-time Harper agent and former acquaintance of Soma. She worked for the organization well before the Manifold closed. Lim once expected Soma to become a naturalist and a possible threat, so she used their relationship to gather intelligence. The Harper Seat vouches for her and emphasizes that both Lim and Soma have changed considerably since they knew one another.

### Bimbo

A gnome mechanic who was supposed to repair the Manifold but has since disappeared into the sewers. The party suspects the ninth crate is intended for him, a conclusion the Harper Seat more or less confirms. “Bimbo” appears to be the party’s name for him rather than one recognized by the Harpers.

### Rsolk

The leader of the Dead Rats sewer gang. He greets Vukradin by recalling his previous experience of getting lost below the city. Although pleased to see him again, Rsolk is less enthusiastic about the armed expedition accompanying him. He needs the Rift Weavers removed from a junction connected to his territory and agrees to pay the party two hundred and fifty gold pieces after Vukradin challenges his initial offer of two hundred.

### Rift Weavers

Extra-planar spiders that blink in and out of existence. They lack traditional heads, possessing instead a mouthful of teeth where a neck should be, and spin shimmering, dry, multicolored webs to restrain their prey. They move in packs, behave with more purpose than natural spiders, and are vulnerable to fire. Their teleportation allows them to bypass terrain, magical darkness, and conventional front lines. They appear to have a particular fixation on Vukradin.

### Rift Weaver Prime

The massive leader of the Rift Weaver pack, possessing Legendary Resistance and the ability to teleport across the battlefield. It begins the fight clinging to webbing high above the floor and expends its Legendary Resistance against Valphine’s psychic spell. It later teleports forty feet directly to Vukradin and nearly lands a critical hit, only for Vukradin to force a reroll that turns the attack into a miss.

## Spells

### Freedom of Movement

A protective ward cast by Valphine upon Brewbarry before combat began, using her only fourth-level spell slot. It lasts for one hour, allows him to move through the flooded, web-filled cavern without being slowed, and lets him swim at his normal walking speed. When a Rift Weaver critically hits him with a dry, glimmering web, Brewbarry is initially restrained but can automatically escape by spending five feet of movement.

### Flaming Sphere

A ball of fire conjured and controlled by Soma. She places it near a Rift Weaver and the surrounding webs, intending both to damage the creatures and burn open the battlefield. Even when a target succeeds on its Dexterity saving throw, the Rift Weavers’ vulnerability to fire magnifies the resulting damage. The sphere can move thirty feet each round, while the fires it starts are expected to spread through the webs more slowly.

### Doom Sphere

A powerful spell cast by Valphine that creates a large radius of inky fog and magical darkness. The sphere inflicts psychic damage, and creatures that fail their saving throws also subtract a die from subsequent saves until the end of their next turn. The cloud slowly wafts eastward and can affect creatures as it enters their space or when they end a turn inside it. The Rift Weaver Prime uses Legendary Resistance to succeed on its initial save and takes half damage, while the affected lesser spiders suffer the full effect.

### Bless

A divine enchantment active during the battle that increases the accuracy of the party’s attacks. Brewbarry initially appears to miss twice with his halberd, but Bless improves both rolls enough to hit, allowing him to deal twenty-eight points of damage to a Rift Weaver without expending his Bardic Inspiration.
