# Chapter 15: Untitled

Date: 2025-09-10

## Summary

The party arrived at a roughly sixty-foot clearing deep in the woods, where something had gone terribly wrong. The trees surrounding the grove leaned forward at unnatural angles and gleamed like obsidian, their bark weeping iridescent sap that shimmered and shifted in the faint, broken light filtering through the canopy above. Pools of that sap glowed among blackened, thorny underbrush that snagged at armor and skin. At the heart of the clearing stood an immense, tree-like figure whose limbs bent at impossible angles, its movements less like those of a living plant and more like a glitch in reality. It was the grove’s former guardian, now twisted beyond recognition by planar energy. Nearby, flickering tears in reality pulsed softly and whispered promises of power and madness, while magical wells radiated dangerous otherworldly energy.

Tangled within thick, thorny vines were members of the Emerald Enclave, trapped so tightly that they could neither move nor perform the somatic components of spells. Their leader, a druid named Lyra, cried out, “My name is Lyra. Help us! We’re of the Emerald Enclave! This thing! It was the Grove’s Guardian, but it has been corrupted. We are trying to sever a planar infection when it turned on us. Please, you have to fight it! Don’t let the corruption spread!” Another captive promised the party resources and information about the forces operating in the region if they could free the Enclave and put the corrupted creature out of its misery.

The battle that followed was chaotic and punishing. Vukradin attempted to compel the blighted vines to drop their captives through sheer force of will, but the magical brambles resisted and held firm. Soma, still wearing the form of a great bear, charged into the thorns and suffered repeated lashes while struggling to land her own blows. After accidentally targeting herself during one attack, she ruefully acknowledged her unfamiliarity with fighting as a bear. Brewbarry grew to an imposing size, giving himself tremendous reach, while Valphine struck the vines with radiant magic and later covered both the brambles and treant in Faerie Fire, making them easier to hit.

The corrupted treant initially offered the party a warning: “Stay out of my grove. If you stay out of my grove and let me be, then I will not attack you.” It regarded the Emerald Enclave as betrayers, however, and grew enraged when the party began freeing them. “You think I’m wrong! You think there is something wrong with me! That I must be changed back!” it screamed, insisting that it was happy to be powerful and dangerous and that its choices should be respected. It later demanded, “Why do you attack me? I… I am but a… I am but a feature of the natural world.” Lyra answered bluntly, “You are not, you are a planar abomination.”

Soma eventually tore through a cluster of vines with her jaws, freeing Lyra and another Enclave member. Lyra explained that her group had been interrupted in the midst of a ritual and caused four ritual stones to appear. She told the party that six successful ritual actions were required before three failures accumulated. The actions drew upon nature or wisdom, arcana, and careful manual work, and each participant must choose a different ritual action from the one they attempted on their own previous turn. Errors carried dangerous consequences.

As the rescue continued, Brewbarry entered the treant’s aura and resisted the madness radiating from it. The treant slammed one Enclave warrior twice and left him clinging to life with a single hit point. Soma tore through another cluster of vines, and an Enclave archer later dealt precisely enough damage to destroy the final bramble, freeing the last captive. During the struggle, the psychic influence of the grove inflicted pain and hindered attacks on those who entered or remained within its reach.

With the Enclave members freed, the fight against the treant intensified. The treant had ensnared Lyra and threatened to kill her friends before she cast her first Moonbeam. Lyra then held the corrupted guardian beneath the radiant pillar, while Soma shed her bear form and began contributing to the ritual. The treant struck one Enclave warrior down to zero hit points before trapping another. Vukradin abandoned ineffective attacks and rushed across the battlefield to heal the fallen warrior, restoring him to consciousness and dragging him away from immediate danger. “Thank you, thank you!” the rescued warrior cried.

Soma then added a second Moonbeam to Lyra’s, subjecting the treant to overlapping pillars of radiant lunar light. Brewbarry battered it in close quarters despite its ability to shift unnaturally in the planar winds and negate an attacker’s advantage. Valphine called down Sacred Flame, Lyra hurled a powerful Guiding Bolt, and the Enclave archers continued firing. Finally, Soma unleashed a caustic cloud of noxious gas that tore through the treant’s remaining strength, and the massive, twisted abomination collapsed and was still.

The party had begun attempting Lyra’s ritual while the treant was still fighting and continued after it fell. Vukradin’s first effort at one of the stones failed, prompting Lyra to ask whether he knew what he was doing. He answered that he was using his proficient skill and simply failing, and Lyra replied, “No need to be snippy. We’re all in this together. Go team intervention.” Soma succeeded at a nature-based ritual action, but failures continued to mount alongside the successes. One failure provoked a psychic blast that tore through most of the group and the Enclave members.

Before the final attempts, Vukradin questioned whether Lyra and her small band had ever possessed the skills necessary to complete six ritual actions alone. Valphine’s scrutiny reinforced the pressure, and Lyra finally admitted, “I don’t think I thought this through very carefully.” She explained that the Emerald Enclave had fractured into two ideological camps. Interventionists believed planar rifts were unnatural abominations that had to be closed, while Naturalists regarded such intrusions as part of the multiverse’s natural order.

Soma briefly misunderstood which faction supported which position and considered sabotaging the ritual, but the party paused to clarify the dispute. Soma then activated her corruption-detecting staff. Every blackened plant, strange well, and planar-scarred feature of the grove “pinged like crazy,” confirming that the corruption was unnatural and that the staff’s judgment aligned with the Interventionists.

Lyra traced the schism to the dispute over the attempted summoning of Tiamat during the War of the Dragons. One faction believed the attempted summoning was an abomination that demanded intervention; the other argued that Tiamat was part of the multiverse and that her potential manifestation belonged to its natural cycle. Those who joined the armies opposing Tiamat were denounced by their opponents as betraying the Enclave’s calling. Though the factions attempted reconciliation after Severin’s defeat prevented Tiamat’s manifestation, the disagreement endured and now shaped their response to the planar scars.

Lyra also explained that the treant had once been a benevolent guardian sustained by sunlight, tending the forest and helping it prosper. Since the planar scars emerged, it had been sustained instead by interdimensional energy and poisoned by an extraplanar force. Remembering the distressed deer they had previously encountered, the party committed to completing the ritual for the right reasons.

Their efforts were not enough. Although Vukradin, Valphine, Soma, and the Enclave achieved several successes—and an Enclave warrior proved unexpectedly adept at the delicate manual component—the third failure ended the ritual. The wells expanded instead of closing, and the Whispering Grove became possessed by the planar rift. “Well, shit,” Soma said. “I don’t think we made the world a better place today, guys.” Lyra, distraught, could only admit, “This, this, this did not work the way I had hoped.”

As the party made their way out of the ruined grove, a deeply unsettling sight stopped them cold. A distorted, partially phased creature with unfocused eyes emerged from one of the planar wells. It moved like a puppet controlled by an unseen master and paid the party no mind. Instead, it repeatedly gathered ingredients from the corrupted ground, harvested material from the fallen treant, and then dove directly into a well and vanished. The well did not close behind it.

From the edge of the woods, the party studied the aftermath. The corruption had not yet spread beyond the surrounding forest, and the wells were no longer expanding, but they remained active. Soma concluded that the planar scars were not random natural events. They were being deliberately motivated and cultivated for a purpose by an unknown force. The party fled with more questions than answers, turning back toward Phandalin and the counsel of Adabra.

## Locations

### Whispering Grove

A roughly 60-foot clearing surrounded by dense woods whose trees lean at unnatural angles and possess glossy, obsidian-like bark. Pools of shifting iridescent sap lie amid blackened thorny underbrush, while faint light filters through a broken canopy. Flickering tears in reality and magical wells pulse with dangerous planar energy and whisper promises of power and madness.

The clearing was once protected by a benevolent treant sustained by sunlight, but the planar scars poisoned it and replaced its natural nourishment with interdimensional energy. A dangerous psychic aura surrounded the corrupted guardian and inflicted mental pain on those who entered or remained within it. After Lyra’s ritual accumulated three failures, the wells expanded and the entire grove became possessed by the planar rift. The corruption had not spread beyond the woods by the party’s departure, but the wells remained active.

## NPCs

### Lyra

The leader of an Emerald Enclave party and a druid of the Interventionist faction. She was trapped by blighted vines while attempting to seal a planar rift and identified the corrupted treant as the grove’s former guardian. After being freed, she summoned four ritual stones, guided the party’s attempts to complete the sealing ritual, and fought the treant using Moonbeam and Guiding Bolt.

Lyra believes the planar scars are unnatural abominations that must be removed. She traced the Emerald Enclave’s ideological schism back to the War of the Dragons, when members disagreed over whether the attempted summoning of Tiamat was part of the natural order or a disruption requiring intervention. Severin’s defeat prevented her manifestation. Under questioning, Lyra admitted, “I don’t think I thought this through very carefully.” She became deeply distraught when the ritual failed and the planar rift possessed the grove.

### Corrupted Treant

The former guardian of the grove, now twisted by planar energies into a towering figure with glossy obsidian bark, iridescent sap, limbs bent at impossible angles, and a voice echoing with overlapping whispers. Its movements resembled glitches in reality, and it could shift unnaturally in planar winds to interfere with incoming attacks.

The treant had once tended the forest, helped it prosper, and sustained itself through sunlight. After the planar scars appeared, extraplanar forces poisoned it and sustained it through interdimensional energy instead. It nevertheless insisted that it was a feature of the natural world, demanded that the party respect its transformed state, and regarded Lyra’s Enclave group as betrayers. It could batter foes with tremendous force, ensnare victims using vines, and project an aura of psychic madness. It was ultimately slain by the party.

### Emerald Enclave Warriors

A group of elven Emerald Enclave members who accompanied Lyra. They were ensnared by blighted vines during the battle and initially could neither move nor cast spells requiring somatic components. After being freed, they supported the party with ranged attacks and ritual actions.

One warrior was struck twice by the treant and left at a single hit point. Warrior Two was later reduced to zero hit points and accumulated two failed death saves before Vukradin healed him and dragged him to safety. Warrior Three was freed from the final vine cluster and was subsequently ensnared by the treant itself. No warrior death was confirmed. One warrior demonstrated significant skill in sleight of hand during the ritual attempt, while another argued that the strengthened treant should be killed before the ritual was completed.

### Phased Creature

A distorted, partially phased, puppet-like entity with warped and unfocused eyes that emerged from the planar rift after the battle. The party realized that it was not acting under its own control; an unseen force appeared to be directing it.

It ignored the party and moved purposefully through the corrupted clearing, repeatedly gathering ingredients from the ground and harvesting material from the fallen treant. It then dove into one of the planar wells and vanished. Its behavior strongly suggested that something within the grove had been deliberately cultivated for later collection.

## Items

### Ritual Stones

Four stones summoned by Lyra with a gesture after she was freed from the blighted vines. They served as the focal points for a ritual intended to seal the planar rift affecting the grove.

The ritual required six successful actions before three failures and drew upon nature or wisdom, arcana, and sleight of hand. Participants could assist one another, but each participant must choose a different ritual action from the one they attempted on their own previous turn. Successful actions could improve later attempts, while failures triggered dangerous psychic consequences. The ritual ultimately failed, causing the planar wells to expand and possess the grove.

### Magical Staff

A staff used by Soma that possesses a once-daily ability to detect unnatural blights and corruptions for several minutes. When activated in the grove, it pinged intensely on all the blackened plants, magical wells, and planar-scarred features, confirming their unnatural character and lending support to the Interventionist position.

Its response helped resolve Soma’s confusion over the Emerald Enclave factions and established that the planar scars were not merely unfamiliar natural phenomena. Further examination suggested that the scars were being deliberately created or motivated for an unknown purpose.

## Spells

### Command

Attempted by Vukradin first in an effort to force the blighted vines to “Drop” the trapped Emerald Enclave members. The vines resisted the mental compulsion and maintained their grip on the captives.

Vukradin later attempted to use Command against the corrupted treant with the instruction “Halt.” The treant failed its Wisdom save, making this use more successful than his first attempt.

### Faerie Fire

Cast by Valphine over a twenty-foot area, outlining the treant and the remaining blighted vines in a shimmering green glow. This granted the party an advantage on attacks against the affected creatures, though the treant could use its unnatural planar shifting to negate that advantage against one attack.

### Guiding Bolt

Used multiple times during the session. Valphine first struck a blighted vine cluster with radiant energy, dealing significant damage and leaving it vulnerable to the next attack. Lyra later cast an empowered Guiding Bolt against the corrupted treant after Vukradin promised to save her fallen companion, dealing substantial damage and leaving the guardian momentarily vulnerable.

### Moonbeam

Conjured first by Lyra and later by Soma, creating twin pillars of radiant lunar light that dealt consistent and significant damage to the corrupted treant. The guardian suffered further damage whenever it remained within the beams, and the stacking of two Moonbeams proved to be one of the encounter’s most effective tactics.

### Sacred Flame

Called down by Valphine as radiant flame-like energy descending on the corrupted treant. The treant’s poor Dexterity made this an effective way to bypass its physical defenses, though it succeeded on a later save against another attempt.

### Poison Spray

A puff of noxious, caustic gas used by Soma to deliver the finishing blow to the corrupted treant, finally bringing the planar abomination down.
