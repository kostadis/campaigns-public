# Chapter 60: All roads lead to Leilon

Date: 2026-10-27

## Summary
After concluding their meeting with Tamsin at the Fallen Tower, the party decided to investigate the mysterious machine housed in a warehouse near the city docks. They collected Bimble from the Morninglord’s church, hoping the gnome mechanic’s knowledge would help explain Soma’s recent passage through the supposedly broken device. During the visit, Valphine’s position as Bishop of Searing Light prompted discussion of Lathander’s growing emphasis on discipline and enforcing his will.

Soma led the party into a dusty warehouse, roughly 60 by 40 feet with a 20-foot ceiling. Dim gray daylight filtered through high windows, illuminating seven tendays of undelivered cargo, including goods labeled for the Lionshield Coster. The machine stood silent in the middle of the floor. Beyond the party’s own tracks, the only marks in the dust were rat paw prints beginning in the middle of the room and leading outside—evidence of Soma’s earlier arrival through the device.

Bimble rushed to examine the machine, a piano-like steampunk contraption with a keypad, buttons, controls, a crank lever, indicator lights, and a receiving bay. It resembled the device the party had encountered in Axeholm. He explained that it was intended as a two-way distribution system, although he had believed its connections were limited to devices in participating stores. Soma’s report of arriving from an empty, unfamiliar realm suggested a much wider reach.

When Bimble pulled the activation lever, the machine unexpectedly came to life. The party teased him about having forgotten to turn it on, but he insisted he had attempted precisely this procedure many times. He produced the 1486 manual and pointed to the relevant page as proof. While he defended his competence, Valphine smelled lavender and bleach drifting through the receiving bay: Aletra’s distinctive scent, acquired through years of working with poisons.

Given only a short opportunity to prepare, the party disagreed about how to receive Valphine’s sister. Valphine considered filling the building with a deadly fog before choosing a more conventional approach. Soma stressed that Aletra had spared her during their recent encounter, wanted books, and had effectively provided her passage home. Vukradin argued for defensive restraint through Hold Person. Soma agreed to help subdue Aletra and transformed into a giant constrictor snake. Bimble hid in the foreman’s office while the adventurers attempted to conceal themselves among the crates.

Vukradin alone gained the initial advantage of concealment, but Aletra resisted his first Hold Person. Brewbarry raged and attacked with his halberd, using Elemental Cleaver to add acid damage. Aletra had anticipated trouble and arrived protected by a powerful Armor of Agathys. Two hits stripped away the frost armor but inflicted 50 cold damage on Brewbarry in retaliation. Aletra’s ensuing necrotic spell struck the nearby combatants, prevented reactions, and knocked Brewbarry unconscious. She then stepped across his fallen body, reached the machine’s keypad, and pressed one key.

Vukradin used Unsettling Words to undermine Aletra’s next saving throw and successfully paralyzed her. Soma bit and constricted the helpless spellcaster, keeping her grappled. Valphine restored Brewbarry with Healing Word but missed with her mace. Brewbarry resumed attacking, Aletra failed to escape the paralysis, and Vukradin dealt further spell damage. Soma delivered the final non-lethal attack, bringing Aletra to unconsciousness while maintaining her coils.

The party searched Aletra and found only ordinary equipment. They bound her hands, debated how to prevent spellcasting while still allowing answers, and revived her with one hit point. She appeared relaxed and almost bored. After urging them to finish killing her, she noticed Valphine’s restraint: “you stopped. That's new.” She followed it with, “Mother wouldn't have.”

Under Vukradin’s Zone of Truth, Aletra evaded questions about whom she served. Asked about the manual, she said she did not need it for repairs but wanted it because it was her property. Vukradin pressed her on why she valued it enough to offer 300 gold pieces. Rather than answer the question truthfully, Aletra killed herself with a hidden poison. At the same moment, the machine broke down. Soma’s medical examination identified poison as the likely cause; ordinary healing could not restore Aletra. Bimble rushed from the office, distraught over the machine.

Cullen Sharpe of House Margaster arrived looking pleased with the situation. He announced that Bimble had signed a contract while left at the church for safekeeping, placing his services under House Margaster. A magical non-disclosure agreement prevented the gnome from explaining the arrangement freely. Cullen also asserted that the house held a separate contract with the commission granting it control over the machine’s repairs.

Brewbarry tore up Cullen’s contract, but destroying the paper did not destroy the agreement: the contract remanifested on another sheet, and Cullen produced another copy from his pocket. He emphasized that the agreement belonged to House Margaster rather than to any individual representative. The party killed him. His death did not release Bimble from the restriction.

Bimble could still explain the technical problem. The Margasters had promised him a way to repair the machine if he signed, and had identified a planar resonator in Leilon as the needed component. He insisted on accompanying the party so he could inspect and physically examine candidate devices. Another Margaster representative, Adrian, arrived to take over Cullen’s business, barely reacting to the bodies. The party killed him as well.

The next visitor was Aldus Hern, aide to Lord Neverember. Sent to investigate reports from the commission, he surveyed Aletra’s body, the two dead Margaster representatives, Bimble staring at the broken device, and Soma still in snake form—and fainted.

After being revived, Aldus reminded the party that Lord Neverember expected them to assist with Leilon’s re-establishment. The Universal Basic Treasure policy would reopen only after that work succeeded, and a caravan was already heading there. The party clarified the title Acting Mayor Pro Tem and argued that restoring the machine served Neverember’s interests too. Aldus agreed to relay their explanation, reiterated House Margaster’s repair contract, and undertook to involve the commission and city guards in cleaning up the bloodbath. The party framed the deaths as the aftermath of a Drow attack. Aldus noticed the resemblance between Valphine and Aletra but dismissed it on the assumption that Drow looked alike.

The planar resonator, the caravan, and Lord Neverember’s mandate all pointed toward Leilon. Bimble intended to accompany the party, House Margaster’s contracts remained in force, and Aletra had died without revealing why the manual mattered so much to her. Whether the machine would ever hum to life again remained, for now, an open question.

## Locations
### The Fallen Tower
The site where the party recently finished a discussion with Tamsin before deciding to investigate the machine house. The session begins with the DM recalling that meeting and the party’s intention to confront Aletra.

### The Warehouse (Machine House)
A dusty building near the docks measuring roughly 60 by 40 feet with 20-foot ceilings. High windows admit dim gray daylight. Its entrances connect toward the docks and the street, with other buildings nearby.

The machine stands in the middle of the main floor, surrounded by approximately seven tendays of undelivered cargo. Dust records the party’s arrival and Soma’s earlier rat tracks, which begin in the room rather than at an entrance. The warehouse becomes the site of Aletra’s capture and death, the killing of two Margaster representatives, and Aldus Hern’s investigation.

### Foreman's Office
A small, abandoned room adjoining the warehouse floor. Bimble retreats here before Aletra arrives and remains sheltered during the battle. He rushes out after her death and the machine’s renewed failure.

### Leilon
The settlement where House Margaster says a replacement planar resonator can be found. Bimble intends to accompany the party to identify the correct device.

Lord Neverember also requires the party to assist with Leilon’s re-establishment, and a caravan is already traveling there. Completion of that work is tied to reopening the Universal Basic Treasure policy.

## NPCs
### Tamsin
An individual the party had just finished speaking with at the Fallen Tower before the session’s main events. No further details were revealed this session.

### Bimble
An eccentric gnome engineer and mechanic whose attention is consumed by the transdimensional machine. The DM confirms that his excitement is sincere and describes him as an engineer’s engineer.

Bimble has repeatedly followed the 1486 manual’s activation procedure without success. Soma’s return through the machine gives him reason to try again, and this time the lever works. He vigorously defends himself against suggestions that he simply forgot to turn it on.

He understands the machine as a two-way distribution system linking participating stores, making its connection to Soma’s strange destination a surprise. During the battle, he hides in the foreman’s office rather than risk his life.

While left at the church for safekeeping, he signed with House Margaster after being promised a means of repairing the device. A magical non-disclosure agreement prevents him from freely explaining the contract, even after Cullen dies. He can reveal that the needed component is a planar resonator in Leilon. He insists on going because he must examine and touch potential replacements himself.

### KP
Described in the table discussion as a diviner helping Aletra know the future. Vukradin speculates during combat that KP may be Aletra’s patron, but the session does not confirm that theory or establish the precise source of her foresight.

The DM also notes that KP’s eventual choices remain dependent on Gary’s decisions for the character.

### Aletra
Valphine’s sister, a Drow spellcaster approximately five feet two inches tall. Years spent working with poisons have left her with a recognizable lavender-and-bleach scent.

She arrives through the machine wearing a powerful, pre-cast Armor of Agathys. Her retaliatory frost and subsequent necrotic magic knock Brewbarry unconscious. During the fight she reaches the keypad and presses one key, whose purpose is not explained.

Once paralyzed and subdued, she is found carrying only ordinary equipment. She remains almost bored in captivity, urges the party to finish killing her, and observes that Valphine has shown restraint their mother would not have shown.

She claims the manual as her property but dies by hidden poison rather than explain its value under Zone of Truth. Her death coincides with the machine breaking down. The exact poison and means of administration remain unidentified.

### Cullen Sharpe
A House Margaster representative who arrives unarmed shortly after Aletra dies. The DM characterizes his manner as that of a smooth mob lawyer.

Cullen reveals Bimble’s contract and its magical non-disclosure agreement, then asserts House Margaster’s separate contractual right to control repairs through the commission. When Brewbarry tears up the document, Cullen produces another copy; destruction of the paper does not destroy the underlying contract.

He stresses that the agreements belong to House Margaster rather than to an individual representative. The party kills him, but Bimble’s restriction persists.

### Adrian
A well-dressed House Margaster representative who arrives after Cullen’s death with another contract. He barely reacts to the carnage and comments that he had warned Cullen to be careful.

He announces that he is taking over Cullen’s business. The party immediately kills him.

### Aldus Hern
An aide to Lord Neverember, dispatched after the commission reports activity at the machine room. He faints upon discovering the bodies and the broken machine.

Once revived, he reminds the party of its obligations in Leilon, the approaching caravan, and the condition attached to reopening the Universal Basic Treasure policy. He agrees to relay their explanation to Neverember and have the commission and city guards manage the aftermath.

He notices the resemblance between Aletra and Valphine without recognizing their familial connection, assuming it reflects a common Drow appearance.

## Items
### The Transdimensional Machine
A large, piano-like steampunk device resembling the machine previously encountered in Axeholm. It includes a keypad associated with an override code, buttons, controls, a crank lever, indicator lights, and a receiving bay.

Bimble describes it as a two-way distribution system connected to devices in participating stores. Soma’s earlier return and Aletra’s arrival demonstrate that it can receive people; outbound operation from this warehouse is not tested this session.

Initially silent, it activates when Bimble repeats the manual’s lever procedure. Why the same procedure now works remains unexplained. Aletra presses one keypad key during combat. The machine breaks again when she dies, and House Margaster identifies a planar resonator in Leilon as the replacement component required for repair. Obtaining the component does not establish that the machine can ultimately be repaired.

### Undelivered Cargo
Approximately seven tendays of goods stranded in the warehouse by the machine’s failure. Some bear Lionshield Coster labels.

The crates are left in enough disorder for Soma to attempt concealment by stretching her giant snake body around them.

### 1486 Manual
The technical guide to the machine. Bimble has read it approximately twenty times and uses its illustrated lever procedure to demonstrate that his earlier activation attempts followed the proper instructions.

The manual was originally in Bimble’s possession before the party took it. Its descriptions help him identify the need for a planar resonator.

Aletra previously offered 300 gold pieces for its delivery. During questioning, she claims it belongs to her and denies needing it simply to repair the machine. Why it matters so much to her remains unanswered.

### Brewbarry's Halberd
Brewbarry’s polearm, used throughout the confrontation with Aletra. During his rage, Elemental Cleaver adds acid damage.

His opening blows strip away Armor of Agathys at the cost of severe retaliatory cold damage. After Valphine revives him, he returns to attacking the paralyzed Aletra.

### House Margaster Contract
Bimble’s agreement placing his services under House Margaster, signed during his stay at the church. It includes a magical non-disclosure restriction that prevents him from explaining its terms freely.

Tearing the contract destroys its current paper but allows the agreement to remanifest on another sheet. Cullen produces another copy after Brewbarry destroys the first.

Cullen also identifies a separate agreement with the commission granting House Margaster control over repairs. Killing its representatives does not release Bimble from his restriction.

## Spells
### Armor of Agathys
Aletra pre-casts this frost protection at fifth level before arriving. Its presence suggests she expected the confrontation.

It grants 25 temporary hit points and inflicts 25 cold damage on Brewbarry each time he hits while the armor remains. His first two successful melee strikes exhaust the protection but cost him 50 cold damage. The spell requires no concentration.

### Hunger of Hadar
Aletra uses a Hadar spell against Brewbarry and Vukradin within a stated ten-foot radius. The table refers to it variously as Armor of Hadar and Hunger of Hadar.

As resolved in play, it calls for a DC 15 Strength save, deals 21 necrotic damage on Brewbarry’s failure, and prevents reactions. The damage knocks him unconscious after his injuries from Armor of Agathys.

### Hold Person
Vukradin’s first attempt fails when Aletra rolls an 18 against the DC 15 Wisdom save.

He later uses Unsettling Words to subtract four from her saving throw and successfully paralyzes her. Aletra subsequently fails another save, allowing nearby attackers to exploit advantage and automatic critical hits.

### Wild Shape
Soma transforms into a giant constrictor snake before Aletra arrives. She attempts to hide her large body around the disordered cargo crates, then bites and constricts the paralyzed spellcaster.

She maintains the grapple through Aletra’s capture and interrogation. By Aldus’s arrival she has released the body but remains in snake form.

### Healing Word
Valphine casts Healing Word after Aletra knocks Brewbarry unconscious, restoring him to nine hit points and allowing him to resume attacking.

The party later considers using healing on Aletra, but the DM confirms that ordinary healing cannot reverse her death from poison.

### Bless
Already active on party members during the battle, Bless improves their attack rolls. Its additional die helps Brewbarry land his second opening strike against Aletra.

Valphine also applies it to her mace attack against the paralyzed Aletra, but still misses.

### Zone of Truth
Vukradin uses Zone of Truth during Aletra’s interrogation. The table identifies its duration as ten minutes and its resistance check as a DC 15 Charisma save.

The party recognizes that the spell prevents deliberate lies but does not prevent evasive answers or compel speech. Aletra avoids questions about her allegiance and claims the manual is her property.

When asked why the book is valuable enough to warrant her 300-gold offer, she chooses poison rather than answer truthfully. Her death coincides with the machine’s failure.
