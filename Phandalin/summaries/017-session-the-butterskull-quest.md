# Chapter 17: The Butterskull Quest

Date: 02-03 to 04-03 of Tarsakh 1495

## Summary
The party planned this one in private the night before, having no intention of being ambushed again. The job came off Harbin’s quest board: orcs had attacked Butterskull Ranch, five miles east of Conyberry along the Triboar Trail, and the posting asked for haste, an assessment of the damage, and help in any way possible. The ranch owner, Alfonse — also known as Big Al Kalazorn, a retired sheriff — could reward them for their efforts. And if he was dead, the notice offered an alternative: return to Townmaster Harbin Wester with proof of Kalazorn’s demise for a reward of 100 gp. Valphine read the terms carefully and noticed what they permitted. The money was good, and on her reading they could travel there, discover the ranch owner was dead, do nothing further, and collect the hundred gold for their trouble.

They left Phandalin heading east on the Triboar Trail and met a merchant along the way, friendly and annoyed in equal measure. Asked where he had come from, he named the town of Conyberry, and described what he had found: “The place was ransacked and a wreck. Nothing was there. Lord Neverember promised to fix that.” This was the wrong thing to say in front of Vukradin, who heard ransacked buildings and immediately found another quest in them — they must go there and quickly secure the property. The merchant tried hard not to laugh. The place had been destroyed years ago and utterly looted; there was nothing left to secure. Vukradin was undeterred and escalated instead: they would find out whose property had been stolen, find the thieves, and return it to the next of kin. The merchant, bug-eyed, managed only “That’s very hard,” and got the reply that defines the chapter — “Just because something is hard, doesn’t mean we don’t do it!”

Two days later they entered Conyberry itself, and Valphine found an unsettling silence hanging over it, broken only by wind whispering through hollowed-out homes. The houses were skeletal remains: roofs long since collapsed inward, wooden frames rotted and blackened by fire and time, wattle-and-daub walls crumbled to dust, the streets overgrown with thorny vines and tenacious weeds. Her private assessment was that all of this devastation was nothing compared to what would happen to a noble house marked for extinction by the Noble Houses of Menzoberranzan.

Turning a corner, they found four brigands. Corbin identified them as members of Carver’s gang — his own former gang — and Vukradin immediately encouraged him to go and talk to them. Corbin tried to explain why this was a bad idea: “You don’t exactly quit a gang without there being consequences.” Vukradin would have none of it, and insisted he go. Corbin’s attempt to bluff his way into an introduction to the Carver collapsed on a roll of 5, and the brigands drew steel. The fight lasted barely a round: Soma mauled one in bear form, Brewbarry killed another, Vukradin put a third to sleep, and Valphine wounded the last before he fled.

The party tied up the sleeping brigand before waking him. Surrounded by a bear and an enlarged barbarian, he disclosed the trap waiting at Butterskull Ranch. The Carver had struck the ranch specifically to generate a Phandalin quest that would draw the party into an ambush; spies in town kept him informed, Sister Kaella ran that network, and Borg the Hammer — a large half-orc who served as the Carver’s enforcer and commander — was probably overseeing the attack. The captive acquired the name Skippy. Corbin, much happier with this assignment than the ambush ahead, remained in Conyberry to guard him.

Approaching Butterskull Ranch from the north, the party found dead humans and orcs, with the orcs showing wounds from something much larger. Vukradin scouted the farmhouse, confirmed that several ogres occupied it, and slipped into the cellar. There he found Big Al tied to a chair and freed him without alerting the occupants. Big Al explained that orcs had first raided the ranch and captured him; Borg and the ogres arrived afterward, killed the orcs, occupied the house, and kept Big Al alive as bait for the party.

Big Al could endure the loss of buildings, but not of Petunia, his prized cow. He offered his own suit of Mithral Chain Mail if the party found her. Soma cast Speak with Animals during the search, and after an hour they recovered Petunia and moved both her and Big Al to the camp where Corbin guarded Skippy. With their rescue objective secured, the party returned to watch the farmhouse from a distant hill and learn where the ambushers went next.

Hours later Borg arrived with the brigand who had escaped Conyberry. Furious that the party had not walked into the trap, Borg checked the empty cellar, killed the failed lookout, and called three ogres out of the house. The party chose tracking over intervention while Borg burned the farmhouse, cornfields, and gardens. After the fire was set, Borg sent the three ogres in one direction and departed in another. The party made its final decision: “I think we follow Borg.”

## NPCs
### The Merchant
Encountered on the Triboar Trail coming from Conyberry; friendly, annoyed, and entirely unprepared for Vukradin. He reports the town ransacked and empty, notes Lord Neverember’s unfulfilled promise to fix it, and tries hard not to laugh at the proposal to secure the property. His last word on the plan to trace stolen goods to their owners’ next of kin is “That’s very hard.”

### Corbin
The party’s aide and a former member of Carver’s gang, which is how he identifies the four brigands on sight. Vukradin overrules his warning that former members cannot simply return, and Corbin’s attempted bluff triggers the fight. He later stays in Conyberry to guard Skippy and is persuaded to keep the captive alive.

### Skippy
The nickname given to the brigand Vukradin puts to sleep and the party captures. He reveals the planned ambush at Butterskull Ranch, Sister Kaella’s role as the Carver’s spymaster, and Borg the Hammer’s place as the Carver’s half-orc enforcer and commander. Corbin guards him while the party goes to the ranch.

### Alfonse “Big Al” Kalazorn
The owner of Butterskull Ranch and a retired sheriff. Vukradin finds him bound in the cellar and brings him out without alerting the ogres. Big Al explains that the orcs captured him before Borg’s group killed them and converted the ranch into an ambush. He offers his Mithral Chain Mail in exchange for Petunia’s return.

### Petunia
Big Al’s prized cow, identifiable by a stamp reading “Big Al’s Cow.” Soma uses Speak with Animals during the search, and the party finds Petunia after an hour and takes her to safety.

### Borg the Hammer
A large half-orc described by Skippy as the Carver’s enforcer and commander. He oversees the ranch ambush with three ogres, kills the brigand who fled the Conyberry fight, and burns the farmhouse, cornfields, and gardens when the party does not appear. The chapter ends with the party following him after he separates from the ogres.

### Townmaster Harbin Wester
Not present. He posted the quest, and is named in it as the person to whom proof of Kalazorn’s death should be returned.

## Items

The notice from Harbin’s quest board, quoted in full in the chapter. Its two-branch reward structure — help Kalazorn, or bring back proof he is dead for 100 gp — is what Valphine notices, and what makes the job attractive to her for reasons the poster did not intend.

Big Al’s own suit of magic chain mail, offered to the party as payment for finding Petunia. It remains inside the farmhouse when the party withdraws to track Borg.
