# Chapter 63

Date: 2026-11-18

## Summary
The party continued its conversation with Cullen Sharpe, the polished and ambitious House Margaster representative who had returned as a devil. He handed them a quest log with an unmistakable air of corporate confidence. A discussion of finding someone to monitor the party’s gold mine had prompted another offer of assistance from the House, which Vukradin rejected. Cullen nevertheless remained available and unoffended.

Vukradin questioned Cullen closely about his career. Cullen explained that he came from a poor family in Neverwinter and that the House offered education, purpose, and a lifestyle otherwise beyond his reach. He described his employer as a firm that took care of its employees and rewarded revenue and influence rather than tenure. His permanent contract extended beyond death; continued satisfaction of the senior partners was the condition of his security. Although he admitted working at least eighty hours a week as a relatively junior associate, he insisted that he viewed the work as pleasure. When Vukradin asked about democratic tendencies within the House, Cullen instead asked whether he was interested in joining. Recruiting Vukradin would, Cullen acknowledged, look very good on his own record.

Vukradin also clarified that his title was acting mayor pro tem and that he had been appointed over his objections. The prospect of an election raised another problem: who would actually run, and whether the wrong person would simply end up in charge again. When the party prepared to leave, Vukradin insisted on stopping in Phandalin rather than proceeding directly to Leilon, citing his responsibilities as mayor. The DM warned that either choice would have consequences.

Before departing that evening, the party took a short rest and received Prayer of Healing from Valphine. Vukradin, who had been at one hit point, recovered fully. Bimbo accompanied them. They had not traveled far before a small troop of kobolds surrounded them and demanded money. The party refused, and Vukradin intimidated one of the would-be robbers into admitting that House Margaster had hired them to be hit at least once. Their payment depended on completing that obligation. The kobolds had signed a contract without a clear understanding of its terms, and one began demanding to see it. Vukradin offered to chop off a finger as a compromise; the kobold accepted, and its screaming followed the severing as the party walked on. Vukradin then regretted not recruiting them.

Early in the journey, Aldus sent word that the Eastern Quay was operating normally, its clerk had returned, House Margaster had filed no complaint, and the Lord Protector wanted to know why the party had visited. Vukradin replied candidly that they had broken into the contract archive and learned that only someone who had not signed with House Margaster could repair the machine. Bimbo’s signature made him ineligible. Aldus sent no further reply.

A rider in Lathander’s colors then galloped up behind them, his horse lathered, carrying a bundle wrapped in altar cloth at arm’s length. Addressing Valphine as bishop, he explained that Brother Aldric had sent it after the book appeared, open and unplaced by any known hand, on the altar at dawn. A sworn brother had been burned when he picked it up. Aldric’s communion with the Morninglord established that it was intended for Valphine alone. She reached into the wrappings without hesitation and received the Tome of the Morning. Its revealed properties included identifying the kind of dragon from which a touched dragon part originated, while other pages remained sealed. The DM connected this recognition to Valphine’s refusal to revert to the drow instincts others had expected of her: even Brother Aldric now had to acknowledge her as Lathander’s chosen.

The second day of travel passed quietly. On the third, a frightened farmer warned that the dead were walking near Triboar Junction with purpose rather than wandering. Brewbarry welcomed the prospect of roadside entertainment, and the farmer fled after trying to emphasize the danger. Near dusk, Rimardo and Corrin appeared with an exclusive preview of their show. Corrin turned a card showing skulls at an inn door, with four slashes and six skulls visible. Rimardo complained that this was supposed to be the second act, announced that they would meet the party at the inn afterward, and explained that their contract excluded the dangerous part. Asked whether that contract involved House Margaster, he stated that they did not sign contracts with fiends. Their cart disappeared after they entered it.

At nightfall, the party reached the Wayside Inn at the junction of the Triboar Trail and the High Road. Undead were attacking both entrances: the smaller northern door was already breached, while the larger wagon entrance was beginning to crack. Two hateful, crowned wraiths hovered among the zombies. Soma transformed into a massive bear and struck one wraith twice. Its answering life drain dealt 22 damage and prompted a discussion of reduced maximum hit points and Wild Shape’s temporary hit points.

Brewbarry entered a rage and infused his weapon with lightning through Elemental Cleaver. His attacks left the wounded wraith nearly destroyed, and Valphine’s first Turn Undead finished it while badly injuring the two initial zombies. The other wraith resisted, failed to penetrate Valphine’s defenses, and withdrew through the inn wall, taking opportunity attacks as it went. Vukradin’s Cloud of Daggers finished the two weakened zombies.

Reinforcements then rounded the corner: an ogre zombie and bloated, lurching zombies whose nature the party could not identify. Soma charged the ogre despite Brewbarry’s suggestion that they keep the unfamiliar creatures at a distance. Vukradin’s Silvery Barbs forced a reroll of the ogre’s apparent critical hit, although the resulting attack still struck Soma for 18 damage. Brewbarry battered the ogre with his lightning weapon and eventually killed it after it also suffered damage from the cloud of daggers.

The bloated zombies grappled and bit Soma, repeatedly dealing 17 damage while she succeeded on the associated Constitution saves. One survived a potentially fatal reaction attack from Brewbarry with a single hit point before Soma finally killed it and briefly freed herself. More ordinary zombies emerged from the inn and attacked Valphine without success.

Meanwhile, Valphine readied Guiding Bolt for the surviving wraith’s next emergence. When its hand reached through the wall toward Vukradin, she struck it for 18 radiant damage, doubled to 36 by its vulnerability. It nevertheless hit Vukradin for 22 damage and withdrew again. Vukradin maintained concentration and moved his cloud of daggers onto the ogre and two zombies.

Valphine later repositioned, taking 5 damage from an opportunity attack, and used Turn Undead again. The wraith resisted, but two zombies suffered 20 radiant damage each and were frightened. The wraith then struck Vukradin a second time, leaving him with one hit point. He initially thought his concentration had failed, but remembering Bless turned the result into a success. He finished the two weakened zombies before the remaining bloated zombie grappled and bit Soma again. The session ended with Soma still in bear form, Vukradin at one hit point, and the wraith and one bloated zombie still active.

## Locations
### Neverwinter
The city where Cullen Sharpe originated from a poor family before being recruited by House Margaster. He credits the House with giving him opportunities, education, and a lifestyle that would otherwise have been unavailable to him.

### Eastern Quay
A location in the city containing a warehouse and a contract archive that the party recently infiltrated.

Aldus reports that it is open as usual, the clerk has returned to his desk, and House Margaster has filed no complaint concerning him. The Lord Protector is asking why the party visited.

### The Road to Phandalin
A forest road where the party encountered a group of kobolds and later received a divine messenger from the church of Lathander.

Vukradin insists on stopping in Phandalin because of his mayoral duties, despite the alternative of proceeding toward Leilon. The party leaves in the evening after resting and healing; the second travel day passes quietly.

### Triboar Junction
The intersection of the Triboar Trail and the High Road, where a wayside inn is currently under attack by a force of undead.

A farmer reports that the dead walk here at night with an apparent destination. Rimardo and Corrin’s roadside preview shortly before the party’s arrival points toward skulls at an inn door.

### Wayside Inn
An oddly shaped building at the juncture of the Triboar Trail and the High Road, featuring a smaller northern entrance and a larger wagon entrance, both of which were being battered by undead forces when the party arrived.

The smaller door is already breached, and cracks are forming in the larger entrance. During the battle, zombies can also be heard pounding on an inner door. The surviving wraith repeatedly uses the walls as shelter between attacks, and additional ordinary zombies emerge from inside.

## NPCs
### Cullen Sharpe
A representative of House Margaster who views his service as a prestigious, permanent career that continues even after death. He is professional and ambitious, describing the House as a meritocracy that rewards results over tenure, and he attempts to recruit Vukradin during their conversation.

- Came from a poor family in Neverwinter.
- Describes himself as a relatively junior associate working at least eighty hours a week.
- Measures advancement in revenue and influence, with security dependent on satisfying senior partners.
- Calls his work pleasure and regards recruiting Vukradin as a valuable career achievement.
- Presents the House as willing to help even after its offers are refused.

### Bimbo
An associate traveling with the party whom Vukradin described to Aldus as ineligible to repair the machine because he has signed with House Margaster.

His continued presence on the journey is confirmed before departure. A brief naming joke follows the DM’s use of Bimble and Vukradin’s preference for Bimbo.

### Aldus
A contact who sends magical messages to the party, informing them that the Eastern Quay is back to normal and that the Lord Protector is asking questions about their recent activities.

He reports the clerk’s return and the absence of a Margaster complaint. After receiving the party’s candid account of the archive break-in and the machine’s repair restriction, he sends no further reply.

### Brother Aldric
A high-ranking member of the church of Lathander who communed with the Morninglord and sent a holy relic to Valphine via a rider, confirming that the tome was meant only for her.

The tome appeared open on an altar at dawn and burned another sworn brother who picked it up. The DM emphasizes that even Aldric must now acknowledge Valphine’s status as Lathander’s chosen.

### Rimardo
A mysterious performer who travels with Corrin. He offers the party a cryptic preview of a show and firmly states that he does not sign contracts with fiends when asked about House Margaster.

He treats the omen as a theatrical performance, complains about the order of the acts, and says that the dangerous part is excluded by contract. He promises to meet the party at the inn afterward and reminds them that the preview is free but the show is not.

### Corrin
A performer associated with Rimardo who uses cards to deliver omens, showing the party a card depicting four slashes and six skulls before the duo vanishes into their cart.

The card is described as showing skulls at an inn door, anticipating the party’s arrival at the besieged Wayside Inn.

### Hateful Wraiths
Malevolent, hovering spectral entities wearing crowns that lead the undead assault on the wayside inn. They are capable of moving through solid objects and draining the life force of the living.

- The DM confirms their vulnerability to radiant damage.
- One is destroyed by Valphine’s first Turn Undead after attacks from Soma and Brewbarry.
- The survivor resists both uses of Turn Undead and repeatedly attacks from within the inn wall.
- Its attempt against Valphine misses; its two successful strikes against Vukradin each deal 22 damage.
- The surviving wraith remains active when the session ends.

### Ogre Zombie
A massive, bloated undead brute that possesses immense strength and unnatural durability, capable of delivering heavy blows and enduring significant punishment from the party.

Its apparent critical hit against Soma is rerolled through Silvery Barbs but still deals 18 damage. Soma, Brewbarry, and Cloud of Daggers wear it down, and Brewbarry delivers the killing attacks. It fails the final survival roll.

### Bloated Zombies
Unusual undead that move with a lurching gait and appear distinctly different from standard zombies, capable of grappling and biting their victims.

The party’s knowledge checks fail to identify them. One survives Brewbarry’s reaction attack with a single hit point before Soma kills it. The other repeatedly grapples and bites Soma, dealing 17 damage on each resolved sequence, and remains active at the stopping point. Soma succeeds on the associated Constitution saves.

## Spells
### Wild Shape
Soma used this druidic ability to transform into a polar bear, allowing her to engage the undead in close quarters with powerful claw and bite attacks throughout the battle at the wayside inn.

The transformation is treated as a bonus action. Her opening two attacks deal 24 damage to a wraith. She later fights the ogre and bloated zombies, remaining in bear form through the final grapple and bite. The DM identifies 21 temporary hit points from the transformation, and the table discusses how these interact with Life Drain.

### Bless
A divine enchantment active on the party that bolstered their resilience and accuracy, which was applied to saving throws during the battle, though Soma still failed her save against the first wraith drain.

Bless helps Vukradin pass his Life Drain saving throws. Remembering its bonus also corrects his apparent failed concentration check after the second wraith strike, allowing Cloud of Daggers to continue.

### Silvery Barbs
A magical distraction cast during combat that granted Brewbarry advantage on his attacks against the wraith.

Vukradin first uses it to force a reroll of a wraith’s attack against Soma, although the attack still hits. He later uses it against the ogre zombie’s apparent critical hit; the replacement attack also hits, dealing 18 damage.

### Turn Undead
Valphine channeled the holy power of Lathander twice during the battle, unleashing radiant energy that destroyed the wounded wraith and weakened zombies; the surviving wraith resisted both uses, while zombies that failed were frightened.

Both uses require DC 15 Wisdom saves against undead within 30 feet. The first deals 15 radiant damage to failed saves, doubled against the vulnerable wraith. The second is resolved at 20 radiant damage to the two ordinary zombies that fail.

The DM rules that the known wraith can be targeted inside the wall because the ability does not explicitly require sight. Successful saves negate both damage and effects.

### Cloud of Daggers
Vukradin conjured a localized vortex of spinning blades to damage multiple enemies clustered near the inn’s entrance. He moved it during combat to maximize its effectiveness, and maintained concentration after both wraith strikes, including a corrected saving throw with Bless.

The spell initially deals 12 damage to each of the two wounded zombies, killing both. Repositioning it onto the ogre and two bloated zombies deals 9 damage to each. The ogre later takes another 10 damage for remaining in the cloud. Near the end, Vukradin uses his action to finish the two ordinary zombies left at 2 hit points each.

### Guiding Bolt
Valphine readied a flash of divine radiant light and triggered it when the wraith emerged from the wall to attack Vukradin, dealing significant radiant damage to the spectral creature.

Her attack roll of 17 hits for 18 radiant damage, doubled to 36 because of the wraith’s vulnerability. The wraith survives and completes its attack. Releasing the readied spell uses Valphine’s reaction.

### Prayer of Healing
Before departure, Valphine supplements the party’s short rest with Prayer of Healing, restoring 16 hit points to everyone. Combined with Vukradin’s Hit Dice recovery, this returns him from one hit point to full health before the journey.
